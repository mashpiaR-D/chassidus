# Quote check

Every manuscript in this collection quotes its sources in Hebrew. When the research was done, each quote was checked against its line in the source text before it was kept, and the manuscripts say so in their opening notes. This page records a second, independent check, made for the release by `check_quotes.py`, against the 37 books packed with the Only One guide. See [README.md](README.md) for what the check does and does not show.

**24,745 quotes in all; 15,198 found word for word in those 37 books (61.4%).** The rest are not found *here*: most quote one of the other books in the full library, which this check did not have.
