# Brief: the reading edition — straight to the unity

The owner, on the first draft (`out/chNN.json`, interlinear with four layers of notes): "Make it a more readable translation, and make it go straight to the point of experiencing the unity — how this relates to unity — clearly just about that, without all the side stuff."

So this edition has ONE job: a reader goes through the Gate of Unity in flowing English and, passage by passage, comes to experience that there is nothing but Him and He is fully here. No scholarly layers.

## Input `out/chNN.json` (the first draft: phrases, translation, plain, one, inner, sitting) and `src/chNN.txt` (the Hebrew). Use the first draft's understanding; rewrite the English.

## Output: `read/chNN.json` for each of your chapters ``` { "chapter": 1, "title": "plain title, 2-6 words (you may keep or sharpen the draft's)", "essence": "one sentence: what this chapter lets you see", "segments": [ { "line": 783, "reading": "the passage in flowing, readable English", "unity": "1-3 sentences: what this passage lets you experience of the unity, here and now", "side": false }, { "line": 860, "reading": "...", "side": true, "side_summary": "one line: what this side point is about (e.g. 'A note on the Kabbalistic name for this channel')", "unity": "" } ], "sitting": "100-180 words, second person, a short hisbonenus that walks the chapter's one idea from the mind into the body and the present moment, ending in one small act", "carry": "one line for the day" } ```

## Rules 1. **Readable and faithful.** Translate the whole passage, but as a person would write it in good plain English today: short sentences, active voice, no bracket clutter. Explain a term inside the sentence the first time ("the Name Elokim, the power that hides Him"), then just use it. Verses: give the words in English; a light citation in the text is fine ("as the Psalm says"), no chapter:verse lists. G-d spelled "G-d"; "Hashem" for the Name. 2. **Straight to the unity.** `unity` is not commentary about the text. It is what the reader can now see or feel: "So this chair is being spoken into being right now." Concrete, present tense, the body and the room, varied from passage to passage, never a slogan repeated. 3. **Side points fold away.** Mark `side: true` for passages whose main content is a side point (a gematria, a Kabbalistic name or channel, a technical answer to another view, a proof-text detail, a scholarly aside) rather than the line of the unity. Still translate them fully in `reading`; give `side_summary` (one line); `unity` may be empty. Most passages are NOT side: when a passage carries the main line, keep it in the flow even if it contains a short technical bit (smooth that bit into the sentence). 4. **Every line counts.** No filler, no praise, no "the Alter Rebbe beautifully says". Reading level 7th-8th grade. 5. Every source segment (every `[line N]`) must appear once, in order. Run `python3 check_read.py read/chNN.json` and fix until OK. 6. Write only your `read/chNN.json` files. Do not commit. Use your own scratch dir /tmp/<your chapters>/ (not the shared scratchpad). Report back in under 100 words.
