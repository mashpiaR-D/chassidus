## The shape of the range

The range starts partway through *HaChodesh HaZeh Lachem* (Shabbat Bo, 8 Shevat 5666) and runs to the first page of *Kedoshim Tihyu* (Shabbat Acharei-Kedoshim, 10 Iyar). The maamarim are:

| Lines | Maamar |
| --- | --- |
| to 2192 | HaChodesh |
| 2193-2311 | Vayolech Hashem Es HaYam (Beshalach, 15 Shevat) |
| 2312-2401 | Vaydaber... Zos Chukas HaTorah (Ki Sisa, Parah, 20 Adar) |
| 2402-2490 | Ki Imcha Mekor Chaim (Vayakhel-Pekudei) |
| 2491-2574 | Adam Ki Yakriv (Vayikra, 5 Nissan) |
| 2575-2671 | Adam Ki Yihyeh Be'or Besaro (Tazria-Metzora, 3 Iyar) |
| 2672-2700 | Kedoshim Tihyu (first page) |

These maamarim form one argument. Each Shabbat's verse is used to take the argument a step further, and the step it takes is metaphysical. The range asks: - What does the word "Ein Sof" actually name? - Why is creation called "from nothing" (yesh me'ayin) when "everything is from You"? - How can the infinite be limited at all?

The answers make this the range where F05 (the Essence beyond both) dominates most completely: 138 of the line entries, twice the next face. The world as nothing before Him (F02) and filling-and-surrounding (F04) come next, because the argument keeps pushing past them. The unity it defends is sharper than the Tanya's "all before Him is as nothing". Here even the source of the worlds, the surrounding light, is as nothing before the Essence. And the Essence is "alone, truly, even now, exactly as before" the heavens were made (2299). Worship (F10, F12, F08) appears only at the ends of maamarim, where a verse is applied.

## HaChodesh (to 2192): two kinds of soul, two kinds of drawing, two heads of the year

The range opens on souls. "Sons" (souls of the name MaH, rooted in Zeir Anpin) and "servants" (souls of the name BaN, rooted in Malchus) both come from Atzilus: > ומ"מ יש בזה ב' בחיי, בחי' בנים שהן נשמות דאצילות, ובחי' עבדים שהן נשמות דבי"ע (line 2064)

The MaH souls stay "fish in the sea" even down in the world. They serve with bitul of existence itself (F06, F08): > שגם בארץ הם בבחי' דגים שביםי, שבטלים ומיוחדים לאלקות בתכלית (line 2079)

Moshe's body was nullified by the revealed light of his soul. The Rashab ties this directly to the unity of "Havayah echad": > שהעולם בטל במציאות באמת, לפי שבאמת מאיר עצמות אוא"ס בגילוי בעולם (line 2091)

The loftiness of such souls (Moshe, Yosef, Rashbi) is not their own. It belongs to the Godliness in them (2104). David, a soul of Atzilus among the BaN souls, turned the evil of the animal soul "wholly to good" (2112, F11, citing Tanya ch. 9-10).

The maamar then explains why Atzilus is called "Adam", from *adameh la'elyon* ("I will be like the Most High"). Atzilus is not a new creation. It is the hidden made revealed: > ולא בריאה חדשה יש מאיןייי להיות כמהות אחר ח"ו (line 2123)

> מ"מ, הגילוי הוא מעין ההעלם ממש (line 2136)

Atzilus is "acquisition" (kinyan), like goods passing from seller to buyer unchanged. Only BYA is "creation" (2126). Yet wisdom "comes from nothing" (Job 28:12). That is true relative to the true surrounding light, and to Ein Sof above the kav, where "the category of these sefirot does not apply at all": > כי בא"ס עצמו לא שייך כלל גדר ספירות אלו (line 2183)

The maamar's resolution is two drawings from Or Ein Sof. One is truly infinite, the surrounding light, infinite "because it is the revelation of the light of His Essence". The other is bounded, the kav (2172). Only the surrounding light can make a "something": > דלהיות התהוות בחי' יש זהו בכח הסובב דוקא (line 2181)

These two drawings become two orders. Nature comes from the kav. Because the kav is "like its source", nature never stops, and in that the power of Ein Sof is seen (F01): > לכן הנהגת הטבע היא תמידית בלי שום שינוי והפסק, שניכר בזה כח הא"ס (line 2191)

Redemption comes from the infinite drawing. So Tishrei is head of the year for creation, and Nissan is "head of months" for the miracle (2191).

## Vayolech (2193-2311): what "Ein Sof" names

The east wind at the Sea opens the maamar, but the work is a long inquiry into the word "Ein Sof". It starts with the kav, which touches Ein Sof itself (2196). The withdrawal in the tzimtzum is not empty. The light fitted to the receiver is found inside the withdrawal itself (F03): > מהסילוק גופא נמצא האור השייך אל המקבל (line 2201)

Why is He called "without end" and not "without beginning"? Only He has no beginning (2205): > שמציאותו מעצמותו ואין לו סיבה אחרת ח"ו (line 2205)

The Kabbalists disagree: - The early Kabbalists say Keter is Ein Sof. - The Ramak says the Essence is. - The Rema of Fano says will is. - The Arizal says Keter holds "the lowest aspect of Ein Sof".

From the Arizal the Rashab concludes that Ein Sof is above will and still is not the Essence itself (2233).

Then comes the core, "I Havayah have not changed". Creation changes nothing in Him, because His Essence is not clothed in the worlds. Only His "speech" is, an outer radiance (F01, F04): > משא"כ מה שהוא ית' מחי' את כולם ומהוה אותם מאין ליש, אין עצמותו ית' מלובש ונתפס בהם ח'"ו (line 2242)

> אני ממש מלא כו', דלית אתר פנוי מיני' (line 2251)

The two senses of "Ein Sof" belong truly only to the Essence (2253). The second sense is the most radical statement in the range. Ein Sof is not an attribute. It is the negation of every attribute, including limitlessness itself (F05, F09): > שלילת הגבול ושלילת הבלי גבול, שלילת החיוב ושלילת השלילה (line 2253)

To the surrounding light, spiritual and physical are equal. That is why creation of the physical comes from it and not from the inner light: > וזהו מה שרוחניות וגשמיות שוין לפניו (line 2261)

The Rashab sides with the Ramak against a primordial will. Every revelation that comes by will "is as though non-existence preceded it" (2282). He then turns against the Ramak too. If Ein Sof were the Essence, the world would come from the Essence and "I have not changed" would fail. Creation is "from nothing" because even the source of all worlds is nothing before His Essence (F02): > שאפי' מקור החיות של כל העולמות נק' אין וכלא חשיב ממש לגבי מהו"ע (line 2292)

> ובאמת שמהו"ע ית' למעלה מעלה גם מלהיות בחי' סובב לעלמין, כי אינו בגדר עלמין כלל (line 2292)

"You are Havayah alone" (Nehemiah 9:6) is literal: > ובאמת אתה הוא לבדך ממש (line 2299)

The resolution: creation comes from the light, which is Ein Sof only because it is "like its source": > ונמצא, שאין ההתהוות מן העצמות ממש, כ"א מן האור, שהוא ג"כ א"ס, מפני שהוא מעין המאור (line 2302)

The splitting of the Sea revealed, at its root, "the hidden essence of Ein Sof", and so prepared for the Torah at Sinai (2310, F10).

## Zos Chukas (2312-2401): light and flow, and the world as engraving

"Or Ein Sof" means the light that is itself infinite. It does not mean the light *of* Ein Sof. The proof is the Etz Chaim's "Ein Sof contracted itself", which cannot be said of the Essence (F03, F05): > דבעצמותו ית' לא הי' צמצום ח"ו, אלא הכוונה על האור (line 2324)

The maamar then contrasts two kinds of giving, "shefa" (flow) and "or" (light): - **Flow** passes the substance of the giver, so the giver is grasped and changed. The examples are a thrown stone and a teacher who has to shape the lesson to the student (2326-2357). - **Light** comes "by itself" and changes nothing in its source, like sunlight (2362) or the soul enlivening the body (2366-2385).

Above there is no necessity; the light shines by will (2384). This analysis is structural, not unity-bearing. The unity shows at its two ends. At the start, contemplation that "in every creature is a divine power that brings it into being, and the main thing is the Godliness" (2337, F01, F12). And the love "for He is your life" draws the substance of the divine light into the soul (F12): > שע"י האהבה מאיר לו באמת האלקות בגילוי בנפשו ובחלקו בהעולם (line 2360)

From the level of light, which is only a cause, the world is not a real "something" but engraving. Through Torah the engravings of heaven and earth are drawn down (F02, F10): > ההתהוות מצד מדרי' זו אינו בבחי' מציאות יש ממש, כ"א בבחי' חקיקה לבד (line 2399)

## Ki Imcha (2402-2490): "He and His Name are one"

The light as it is absorbed in the Essence is not light at all: > דבחי' האור הכלול בעצמותו הוא בחי' יכולת לבד, מה שביכולתו להאיר (line 2416)

The Essence is not "a luminary" in its essence (a gem is a better image than the sun). The chief thing of Godliness is not that worlds come from it: > לא זהו עיקר האלקות מה שהעולמות מתהוים ממנו ומקבלים חיותם והתהוותם מאלקותו ית' (line 2420)

"You are holy" is the Essence, apart even from being a source of the source of worlds. "Your Name is holy" is the surrounding light, to which AK and physical Asiyah are exactly equal (2428). The highest statement of unity in the range is the Shelah's distinction between "You are one and Your Name is one" and "He and His Name are one". In the second, the light absorbed in the luminary is not a second thing: > כי אז הכל בבחי' הוא לבד, שלא יש עוד דבר כלל, גם לא בחי' אור (line 2438)

The Pardes's flint that hides fire does not fit the Essence (2440). It fits only the light absorbed in it. The Zohar's lamp "that spreads in every direction, and when you look you find only one lamp" (2479) says the same thing: > הרי אין כאן שום מהות דבר זולת המאור (line 2479)

The future will reveal this level, and it comes through toil in Torah now (2487, F10 with F07).

## Adam Ki Yakriv (2491-2574): the power to limit is the power of the infinite

The ability in the Essence includes its opposite, the ability not to shine. The power to limit comes only from the source of the light: > כ"א מאין שנמשך האור והגילוי, משם נמשך הכח לצמצם ולהגביל (line 2495)

Hiding is not the lesser power. It is the chief one, the root of the gevurot (strengths) above the chasadim (kindnesses) (F03): > ואדרבה, זהו עיקר בחי' הכח והגבורה להעלים ולהסתיר (line 2503)

"Above without end and below without limit" means revelation and concealment in the light. In the Essence all is ability (F09): > דבעצמות הכל הוא בבחי' יכולת, דביכולתו להאיר וביכולתו שלא להאיר (line 2505)

This is F09 in its purest form. The finite is the infinite's own power of self-limitation. The vessels of Atzilus show it: - The unity of lights and vessels with the Emanator is their bitul. The lights are complete bitul, where "the true Nothing" is felt; the vessels are only bitul of the yesh (2519, 2526, F08). - The limiting done by the vessels is inside Godliness. It is a willed limit, "not outside itself" (2528, 2539), like a hand that writes one aleph. The power that restrains the hand never reaches the paper.

> שההגבלה היא בבחי' עצמות אלקות, לא חוץ ממנו (line 2528)

Tanya ch. 48 is brought in as the model. God's knowing of each creature "is its life and coming into being", yet it is surrounding and infinite (2546). "Wisdom comes from nothing" means "limit from the limitless" (2551). The Temple parallels the kav: "the heavens cannot contain You", yet that light rests in this house (2553, F07). The Tanya's sun, whose light inside the sun "is truly nothing", is the image for the kav within its source (2562). Offerings are brought by "Adam" because he cleaves to his source. The offering refines the animal and raises it to its root (2564, 2571, F11 with F06).

## Adam Ki Yihyeh (2575-2671): the thought that holds all, and the hidden love

The two wills (the decided will hidden in the Essence, and the revealed will it causes without being drawn into it) correspond to Tehiru Ila'ah (upper purity) and Tehiru Tata'ah (lower purity). The will to create was decided through delight in the mitzvot, "He consulted with the souls of the righteous" (F10): > דע"י בחי' הרצון והתענוג בקיום התומ"צ בפו"מ, הוחלט להיות רוצה ומתענג כבי' בבריאת והתהוות העולמות (line 2611)

In the primordial thought of AK all worlds and all six thousand years are surveyed "in one glance". Unlike a man's plan for a building, this thought itself makes them be: > משא"כ למעלה שע"י המח' נעשה ההתהוות ממש (line 2631)

On how creation from nothing actually works, the Rashab admits the limit of understanding (F12): > ואמיתית הענין איך ומה הוא, אינו מובן לנו, כי בנבראים לא יש כלל דוגמת ענין יש מאין (line 2603)

Souls, unlike angels, are "inner" in that thought. So even as souls of BYA "they are Godliness in essence" (2649). The feet of AK reach Asiyah, and must be revealed somewhere below. They are revealed in the hidden love in every Jew (F06, F08): > בחי' אהבה המוסתרת שיש בכל אחד מישראל בניצוץ נשמתו ליכלל ולידבק בה' אחד (line 2651)

AK's surrounding circles give all creatures an unfelt bitul. In Atzilus the unity is felt: > מפני שמושג ומורגש שם איך שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו (line 2653)

Nega'im come to "Adam", one who has already rectified everything, to refine his outermost garment. The cure comes through Aharon, who draws the infinite love from above Atzilus (2670, F11).

## Kedoshim (2672-2700): the start of a new step

The range ends on the question of how "for I am holy" can be a reason for "you shall be holy". Vessels, unlike lights, are existents in themselves. Their non-existence comes only from the light shining on them (2681, F02). The two names of Havayah are introduced (2691). Creation needs a complete contraction of the light of His Essence, because the creature has no comparison to its Creator (2694, F03). The argument continues past line 2700.

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The source of the world is itself "nothing".** Shaar HaYichud 3-6 says the world is nothing before its source. Here the source of all worlds (the surrounding light, His "Name") is nothing before the Essence (2292), and the Essence is not a source or cause at all (2387, 2420). "You are alone even now" (2299) goes beyond "all before Him is as nothing".
- **Ein Sof is not a name of the Essence.** The Tanya uses "Or Ein Sof" freely. The Rashab argues at length that "Ein Sof" names the light. The Essence is beyond even "Ein Sof", which is the negation of limit *and* of limitlessness (2253, 2307, 2324).
- **The light before the tzimtzum is only "ability".** Absorbed in the Essence it is no light and no second thing: "He and His Name are one" (2416, 2438, 2448). The Tanya's sun image (Shaar HaYichud 3) is used, but the Rashab corrects it: the sun must shine, and He need not (2384, 2416).
- **Limit is a power of the infinite.** The Tanya (Iggeret HaKodesh 20) speaks of Ein Sof's power of limit. The Rashab makes the power to hide the *chief* power (2503), and shows that limit is "inside" Godliness and willed (2528, 2539).
- **The hidden love is rooted in AK.** Tanya ch. 18-19 roots the hidden love in the Chochmah of the divine soul. Here it is the revelation of AK's "feet" in Asiyah, the one place below where AK must shine (2651).
- **Creation is incomprehensible.** The Tanya explains creation from nothing through speech and letters. The Rashab says outright that how it works "is not understood by us", since nothing in creation is like it (2603).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **The footnotes.** 378 of 638 lines are the editor's apparatus, and 346 of them are pure references. The 32 structural ones are kept for their substance (the Rebbe's notes at 2210-2211, 2430-2432, 2614; the Tzemach Tzedek at 2269, 2451, 2544).
- **Long technical runs with no face.** Much of Zos Chukas (the stone, the teacher, the soul in the body, 2326-2385) and of Adam Ki Yihyeh (the building parable and the kinds of will, 2591-2629) is analysis that the argument needs but that states no unity. These lines are structural with an empty or single face list. So is most of the AK, reshimu and vessels machinery.
- **Worship faces are rare.** F07 appears on 2 lines, F11 on 4, F12 on 5, F10 on 9. Each shows up only where a verse is applied at a maamar's end.
- **No readings.** Every tie in the range was either made by the text or is structural. Where a link seemed ours (for example, the withdrawal that yields the student's light, 2201, as F03), it was marked structural rather than stated.
- **OCR damage.** Page headers are fused into text lines (2112, 2201, 2251, 2324, 2343, 2428, 2448, 2503, 2531, 2653, 2681). Sentences break across lines and footnotes. Stray "י" and "*" marks sit inside words. Quotes keep all of these. In 2307 the OCR has "אץ" for "אין".

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **What does "Ein Sof" name?** The Ramak says the Essence, the Rema says will, and the early Kabbalists say Keter. The Rashab concludes: the light, not the Essence (2233, 2302, 2324). His own gloss leaves the matter open: > ולפ"ז לכאו' יש מקום לומר דא"ס קאי על העצמות (line 2235)

It ends "this needs study, and may God enlighten my eyes". The Likkutei Torah footnote (2240) supports the reading that "Ein Sof" means the light. - **The Ramak or the Rema?** The Rashab calls the Ramak's path "truer" against a primordial will (2281), then rejects the Ramak's identification of Ein Sof with the Essence (2291). The Tzemach Tzedek's note sides with the Rema: > ורמ"ע סל דאפי' סבה וסובב לא יתכן אמהותו ועצמותו, והאמת עמו (line 2269) - **Does creation come from the Essence or from the light?** The spine's F05 cites Iggeret HaKodesh 20 for "the power to create yesh is the Essence's alone". Here the Rashab reads that same letter as saying creation is from the light, which is Ein Sof "because it is like its source", and calls this the Alter Rebbe's innovation: > וזהו מה שחידש רבינו נ"ע דהאור נק' א"ס מפני שהוא מעין המאור (line 2273)

The two formulations stand. - **Are the vessels of Atzilus truly limited?** The Rashab's parable says no: the limit is willed and "inside". The Tzemach Tzedek (footnote) says Iggeret HaKodesh 20 plainly says yes, and that the Alter Rebbe's last writing deserves more weight, though "the parable still holds": > נראה להדיא שהכלים הן בבחי' גבול ממש (line 2544) - **Is the kav bounded or unlimited?** Both views are explained as "two ways" (2172 against 2553). The editor notes that the Tanya follows the second (2559). - **Tehiru Ila'ah before or after the tzimtzum?** The Rebbe's footnote notes the conflict with 5659 and with R. Chaim Vital, and says it "needs study" (2614-2615). - **The Pardes's flint.** The Rashab says it does not fit the Essence, which is none of the attributes at all (2440), against the Pardes's use of it for the ten hidden sefirot.
