## The aim in one paragraph

The owner: the goal is to bring people closer to G-d, not the G-d of dogma but the Chassidic one, found within yourself: a path to the true self, using the method of Chassidus. So this framework is built as one road. Each session moves a person one step closer to who they really are, down through the layers to the point where the self at its root and what gives it being turn out to be one (ST10), and then back up into an ordinary day carrying it (ST12, ST13). The sources say this outright, and it is the hinge of the whole design:

> «שידיעת ד׳ היא בידיעת עצמו», *the knowledge of G-d is in the knowledge of oneself* · Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) · `Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 1941)` line 542
> «ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש», *the second soul is truly a part of G-d above* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 2 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 86
> «ובחינת יחידה היא מדריגה הה׳ באמת שהוא בחינת הרצון העצמי הפשוט», *yechidah is truly the fifth level: the simple, essential will* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 15:9 (the Mitteler Rebbe) · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 311

In universal words (ours): *you find what gives you being by finding the deepest part of yourself, and you find the deepest part of yourself by holding one true thing about your being until it is plain.* No belief is asked for at the door. A person who believes hears 'G-d'; a person who is unsure hears 'what gives you being', and the teaching's claim is marked as a claim (TRANSLATION UT-08, UT-10).

## 1.1 The universal statement (ours, on the stated lines below)

> Everything you see, and you too, is being given its being right now; nothing holds itself up. Under every layer of you is a self that was never apart from what gives it. When a person holds that one truth steadily, in detail, in their own life, until it is as plain as what they see, the heart answers by itself, the everyday self slowly comes to agree, and a small act carries it into the day. Done again and again, a moment becomes who they are. Going up and coming back down are both part of it, and every step down into yourself is a step toward what gives you being.

## 1.2 The chain, step by step

The sources give each link. Putting them in one chain is ours, following `meditation-technology/TECHNOLOGY.md` §1, `integration/MAP.md` and `lights-and-vessels/PS.md` (flash, breadth, binding, felt, moved, settled, down). What is new here is the root (M0) as the aim, the science beside each link, and where each link sits in the live loop.

**M0 · Where it is going: the true self.** *In the sources (stated; 'true self' as the name of the aim is the owner's (2026-10-01)).* A person finds G-d by finding the root of their own self. The soul is a part of G-d above; its deepest level, the yechidah, is a simple will that is one with Him; the sense of the One comes from that root. Nothing new is put in; what was always there is uncovered.

> «שידיעת ד׳ היא בידיעת עצמו», *the knowledge of G-d is in the knowledge of oneself* · Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) · `Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 1941)` line 542
> «ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש», *the second soul is truly a part of G-d above* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 2 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 86
> «ובחינת יחידה היא מדריגה הה׳ באמת שהוא בחינת הרצון העצמי הפשוט», *yechidah is truly the fifth level: the simple, essential will* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 15:9 (the Mitteler Rebbe) · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 311
> «וחוש זה אינו אלא מצד שרש נפשו דהיינו בחינת אלהות שבנפשו», *this sense comes only from the root of his soul, the G-dliness in his soul* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:25 · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 157
> «שהיא ״אהבה מסותרת״ שבלב כללות ישראל, שהיא ירושה לנו מאבותינו», *it is a 'hidden love' in the heart, an inheritance* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 18 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 272

*In contemplative science (ours).* Science can describe the self-image (the brain's self-talk network, and practice that loosens it) and self-transcendent experience, but it cannot test the claim that the root of the self meets G-d. ([Buckner 2008](https://doi.org/10.1196/annals.1440.011), [Brewer 2011](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1112029108), [Dahl 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2015.07.001), [Yaden 2017](https://doi.org/10.1037/gpr0000102)). *Match:* partial: same direction (less self-image), different claim (a self found, not a self dissolved). *In the loop:* Every session moves one step toward ST10 and then back down to ST12-ST13.

**M1 · One line, thought through (hisbonenus).** *In the sources (stated).* Take one truth and work it out: wide, into many details; long, down into pictures and your own life; deep, to its bare point. Slowly: 'the pausing, the opposite of speed'. General thinking does nothing; detail enters.

> «הרוחב הוא בחינת הסבר המושכל לכל צד בפרטים הרבה כרוחב הנהר», *breadth is explaining the idea to every side, in many details, wide as a river* · The Gate of Unity (the Mitteler Rebbe) · `The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820)` line 17
> «כך עומק המושכל הוא בחינת עצם נקודתו כמו שהוא», *the depth of an idea is its very point, as it is* · The Gate of Unity · `The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820)` line 21
> «שעומד על דבר המושכל ומעיין בו הרבה מאד, שהוא העיכוב היפך המהירות», *he stands on the idea and looks into it very much: the pausing, the opposite of speed* · The Gate of Unity · `The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820)` line 24

*In contemplative science (ours).* Working an idea out in detail and seeing a situation in a new way (reappraisal) is one of the best-measured ways to change how something feels. A sudden 'oh, it is so' is studied as insight. ([Webb 2012](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0027600), [Kounios & Beeman 2014](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010213-115154)). *Match:* close. *In the loop:* L3 Choose (one line) and L4 Guide (detail, the picture and where it breaks).

**M2 · Binding the mind (da'as).** *In the sources (stated).* Tie the mind to the truth 'with a strong, very firm bond' until it is as real as something seen; bring it back each time it wanders, without arguing. Until then 'I only knew it'.

> «שמקשר דעתו בקשר אמיץ וחזק מאד», *he binds his mind with a strong, very firm bond* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 3 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 112
> «כמו שהיא מקושרת בדבר גשמי שרואה בעיני בשר ומעמיק בו מחשבתו», *as bound as it is to a physical thing he sees with his eyes and thinks deeply into* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 42 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 629
> «הן גם מאז זאת ידעתי, אבל רק ידעתי», *I knew this before too, but I only knew it* · Tzav VeZeruz (the Piaseczner) · `Tzav VeZeruz (Warsaw, 1930-1940)` line 72
> «רק יעשה עצמו כלא יודע ולא שומע ההרהורים שנפלו לו», *only let him act as one who neither knows nor hears the thoughts that fell into his mind* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 28 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 401

*In contemplative science (ours).* Attention training is the core of all meditation research: the mind wanders, you notice, you come back. Each return is the training. ([Lutz 2008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2008.01.005), [Hasenkamp 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2011.07.008)). *Match:* close in form; different object (a truth, not the breath). *In the loop:* A8-A11: the drift rules.

**M3 · The heart answers by itself.** *In the sources (stated).* The mind rules the heart by nature. What the mind truly holds, the heart answers, 'as water gives back a face'. It cannot be forced: wanting the feeling is 'serving oneself'. The deeper it is, the less it is felt.

> «כי המוח שליט על הלב», *for the mind rules the heart* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 12 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 209
> «כמים הפנים לפנים, כן לב האדם אל האדם», *as water gives back a face, so one heart answers another* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 46 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 680
> «שאז לא ירגיש כלל אם הוא מתפעל, כי אין הכוונה להתפעל רק שההתפעלות מאליו נעשית», *he does not notice at all whether he is moved, for the aim is not to be moved; the movement happens by itself* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (the Mitteler Rebbe) · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 219
> «וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר כמו באהבה ורצון ותענוג היותר פנימית, יותר לא ירגיש בה», *the great rule: the deeper the stirring of the essence, as in the most inward love, will and delight, the less he feels it* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 29:19 · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 556
> «וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו», *and this is called serving oneself* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 8:8 · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 167

*In contemplative science (ours).* Chasing a feeling tends to spoil it. Awe makes the self feel smaller and people kinder. ([Mauss 2011](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0022010), [Piff 2015](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspi0000018)). *Match:* close. *In the loop:* L5: the check-in never asks how strong a feeling was; K3: highs are never praised.

**M4 · The everyday self comes to agree (the animal soul).** *In the sources (stated (animal-soul/METHODS M01, M07; order steps 5-9)).* The G-dly soul grasps quickly; the long work is teaching the everyday self in its own plain terms until it agrees, and later wants. Agreement first, wanting later. Through long contemplation 'the animal soul too will agree'.

> «הכל הוא בנפש השכלית שנקרא נפש הטבעית החיונית, שגם היא תסכים ותודה בהודאה והתפעלות במוח והתפעלות בלב», *it is all in the thinking soul, called the natural living soul, which also agrees and acknowledges, with stirring in mind and heart* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 312

*In contemplative science (ours).* An old felt belief ('I'm on my own', 'I'm my mistakes') seems to change most when it is brought up and, in the same moment, met by a lived experience that does not fit it. This is the memory-reconsolidation account; it is promising and disputed. ([Lane 2015](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X14000041), [Elsey 2018](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000152)). *Match:* partial; our hypothesis. *In the loop:* L4 'length': the line is taken into the person's hardest moment of the week while it is felt; K1 their own words.

**M5 · The deed.** *In the sources (stated).* Light holds only in a vessel. A stirring that never reaches a deed is 'a flame flying in the air'. In the deed one takes hold of Him.

> «הרי זה כשלהבת הפורח באויר שאין לו קיום», *it is like a flame flying in the air, which has no endurance* · Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 38:11 · `Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813)` line 778
> «כי אם כאשר תפיסא ומתלבשת בתורה ומצותיה, אזי היא תפיסא ומתלבשת בהקדוש ברוך הוא», *when it takes hold of Torah and its mitzvot, it takes hold of the Holy One Himself* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 4 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 126

*In contemplative science (ours).* Plans tied to a specific moment ('when X, I will Y') are far more likely to be carried out. ([Gollwitzer 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601%2806%2938002-1)). *Match:* close. *In the loop:* L8 Carry: one act, with a when and a cue.

**M6 · Habit becomes nature.** *In the sources (stated).* 'When he trains himself this way constantly, the habit becomes nature.' Truth comes by stages, and each stage is true. Every person can be a beinoni at any time.

> «וכשירגיל עצמו כן תמיד הרי ההרגל נעשה טבע», *when he trains himself this way constantly, the habit becomes nature* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 44 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 664
> «כי האמת בא בהדרגה דוקא, וכל דרגא היא אמיתית בעצמה», *truth comes by stages, specifically, and each stage is true in itself* · Kuntres HaAvodah 7 (the Rashab), OCR · `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911)` line 264
> «שכל אדם יכול להיות בינוני בכל עת ובכל שעה», *every person can be a beinoni at any time and any hour* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 14 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 232

*In contemplative science (ours).* Brief states become lasting traits with repeated practice; amount of practice relates to results; habits take weeks to months to form, with wide spread between people. ([Kral 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.07.013), [Dahl 2020](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2014859117), [Parsons 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2017.05.004), [Lally 2010](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674)). *Match:* close. *In the loop:* JOURNEY: same line for a week, the 40-day arc, the weekly aftermath check.

**M7 · The dark turned (is'hapcha).** *In the sources (stated).* First the pull is held back; later the same force is turned, 'from bitter to sweet, from darkness to light'. Bitterness and a broken heart have life in them; sadness does not. Compassion for the spark comes before any accounting.

> «ואתהפכא ממרירו למתקא ומחשוכא לנהורא», *and it is turned from bitter to sweet and from darkness to light* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 387
> «כי עצבות – היא שלבו מטומטם כאבן ואין חיות בלבו, אבל מרירות ולב נשבר, אדרבה, הרי יש חיות בלבו», *sadness is a heart dull as stone with no life in it; but bitterness and a broken heart, on the contrary, have life in the heart* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 452
> «לעורר רחמים רבים על שם ה׳ השוכן אתנו», *to wake great compassion for the name of G-d that dwells with us* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 45 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 676

*In contemplative science (ours).* Compassion training changes how people respond to suffering, their own and others'. No study tests turning a craving into longing. ([Weng 2013](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797612469537), [Kirby 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.beth.2017.06.003)). *Match:* partial. *In the loop:* Hurting-old-ache route; SF-SHAME; the mercy technique.

**M8 · Running and returning (ratzo v'shov).** *In the sources (stated).* Every rising is for the return. 'Running without returning is death.' Come down several times a day to rest. The return is the main thing.

> «ואם רץ לבך – שוב לאחד», *and if your heart runs, return to the One* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 50 (quoting Sefer Yetzirah) · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 730
> «רצוא בלי שוב, היא מיתה», *running without returning is death* · Hayom Yom, 27 Adar II (the Rebbe) · `Hayom Yom (Brooklyn, New York, 1942)` line 193
> «והשוב הוא העיקר וכל ההעלא' דרצוא לא הי' רק בשביל הירידה דשוב», *the return is the main thing; all the rising of the running was only for the coming down of the return* · Imrei Binah, the Shema (the Mitteler Rebbe) · `Imrei Binah (Lubavitch, 1821)` line 283
> «וצריך לירד למטה כמה פעמים ביום לנוח עצמו ממחשבתו מעט», *he must come down several times a day to rest a little from his thought* · Tzava'at HaRivash (the Baal Shem Tov) · `Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770)` line 281

*In contemplative science (ours).* More practice is not always better: many effects follow an inverted U. The same loosening of the self can feel like peace or like horror, depending on whether the person still feels in charge. ([Britton 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.copsyc.2018.12.011), [Deane 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.539726)). *Match:* close. *In the loop:* A3, GX grounding exit, every session ends at ST12-ST13; SAFETY.

## 1.3 How the two accounts line up

**Where they part (ours).** Science studies what changes in a person; Chassidus claims something about reality: only the One truly is, and the self at its root is bound to Him. Science cannot test that, and the guide never presents it as a finding. Mindfulness empties or watches; hisbonenus fills the mind with one truth so large that the self-image gives way (`SCIENCE.md` §0, 'Chabad mostly installs'). So the watcher (ST02) and the quiet (ST01) are doors only, and the object of every technique is a line of truth, never the breath or the body (manual Part I, 'What is not here'; MAP R12).

## 2. The states to reach

They climb toward the meeting point of the self and its source (ST10), and then come back down, because the return is the deeper half. ## ST01 · Here

**From inside (universal).** The flood slows. You are in one place, in this body, in this room, and not being dragged. Not asleep and not blank: there is room for one true thing.

**For someone who believes in G-d.** The same, with the room made ready to stand before Him.

**Source.** MAP S32 Quiet, out of S01 Scattered; One Ladder Stage 1 A Set Time, Stage 2 Waking the Sleeper. The Piaseczner's quieting: look at the thoughts a short while until the head empties; the stopping is itself service; and what is left when the flood stops is not nothing. Stated. Calling it the first state is ours.

> «אז ירגיש לאט לאט שראשו מתרוקן», *then slowly, slowly he will feel his head emptying* · Derekh HaMelekh · `Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931)` line 1556
> «גם הזמן הזה של החדלה עבודה היא», *this time of stopping is also service* · Derekh HaMelekh · `Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931)` line 224
> «ובאם היה האדם מעמיד ומפסיק על שעה את כל שטף מחשבותיו ורצוניו, היה חלק אלקות נראה לו בלא לבוש», *if a person stopped the whole flood of his thoughts and wants for an hour, the G-dly part would be seen by him without a garment* · Derekh HaMelekh · `Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931)` line 90

**Signs it is real.** messages get slower and longer; can hear one line and say it back; drifts and comes back without a fight; awake, not drowsy. **Its counterfeit.** zoning out or relaxing as the goal; emptiness taken as the arrival; staying in the quiet for its own sake. **The fix.** Say one line softly into the quiet. Quiet clears the table; it is never the meal (MAP S32). **Opened by.** Arriving from what is already moving, Quieting the stream: looking at the thoughts for a short while (a door only), The moment of silence, The hum (a wordless tune), The held line, said softly. **Not when.** Long silent quieting for an anxious or trauma-carrying person: keep it under a minute, eyes open, or use the hum instead.

## ST02 · The watcher

**From inside (universal).** You can see a thought, a mood or a verdict about yourself from one step to the side. It is something you have. It is not all you are. The one who looks is still here, warm, in the body.

**For someone who believes in G-d.** The same; and the one who looks is the soul, which the verdict never reached.

**Source.** MAP S34 Two voices, S11 The verdict (from outside); FULL-DESCENT S04 Don't leave yourself out; One Ladder Stage 3 The Floor Under the Floor; U2 The self under the verdicts. Stated: look at the thoughts; act as one who neither knows nor hears the stray thought. Ours: naming the looking itself as a state, and keeping it brief. This is not mindfulness: it is a step aside so the line can come in.

> «שיתחיל האיש להביט על מחשבותיו שעה קלה», *let the person begin by looking at his thoughts for a short while* · Derekh HaMelekh, mind-quieting advice (the Piaseczner) · `Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931)` line 1556
> «רק יעשה עצמו כלא יודע ולא שומע ההרהורים שנפלו לו», *only let him act as one who neither knows nor hears the thoughts that fell into his mind* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 28 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 401

**Signs it is real.** 'that's a thought, not me'; the verdict loosens a little; still warm toward self and others; can go back to the line at once. **Its counterfeit.** a cold spectator, cut off from body or people; endless noticing with no line (mindfulness drift); using it to disown what one did. **The fix.** Two minutes at most, then the line. 'You are the one who looks, and you are here.' **Opened by.** Quieting the stream: looking at the thoughts for a short while (a door only), Looking at the verdict from the side, The descent through the layers of self, Finding the good point. **Not when.** Anyone already feeling unreal, far away or outside themselves: skip this state entirely (it can feed detachment).

## ST03 · Before

**For someone who believes in G-d.** Before Whom I stand: the King is here, and wants me here.

**For someone unsure.** As if you were before what is most real. Take it as far as it goes for you.

**Source.** MAP S33 Before Whom; C9 Anyone may stand before Him (TRANSLATION). Stated: think before Whom you lie; awe is the gate to come before Him. Ours: the universal wording.

> «יחשב בלבו לפני מי הוא שוכב», *let him think in his heart before Whom he is lying* · Shulchan Arukh HaRav, Orach Chaim 1 · `Shulchan Arukh HaRav (Liozna, c. 1770-1805)` line 70

**Signs it is real.** sits straighter; slower speech; a quiet respect; own words come more easily. **Its counterfeit.** fear of being watched; guilt and scrupulosity; performing for an audience. **The fix.** Warmth first: 'before One who wants you here'. ## ST04 · Given right now

**From inside (universal).** This moment is being given, not kept. The cup, the room, the floor, and you too, are not holding yourselves up. There is a floor under the floor.

**For someone who believes in G-d.** Right now He is saying all this into being, and me with it.

**For someone unsure.** Try to hold yourself in being by effort. You can't. Something is giving this moment. The teaching's claim is that this Something is One.

**Source.** MAP S65 Given right now, S84 Trust; FULL-DESCENT S01 Made right now; One Ladder Stage 4 Being Said, Right Now; U1 Given, right now. > «כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש», *if the letters withdrew for an instant and returned to their source, the heavens would be truly nothing* · Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1 · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 785

**Signs it is real.** steadier than at the start; can speak plainly, eat, work; no urge to rush back up. **Its counterfeit.** a slump called 'the return'; wanting to stay up forever; resentment at coming down. **The fix.** The grounding exit lines, then the deed. **Opened by.** The quiet after (lingering), The way back down (grounding exit), The held line, said softly. **Not when.** Never skipped. ## ST13 · Acting from it

**From inside (universal).** It goes into the hand: one act, done plainly, for its own sake. The person in front of you is real, and held by the same One.

**For someone who believes in G-d.** A mitzvah done slowly, with His will in it.

**Source.** MAP S81 The deed in the hand, S82 The home below, S86 The other held; FULL-DESCENT S32 The deed in the hand, S33 Found only below, S34 Plainness is His simplicity; One Ladder Stage 13 Coming Down, Stage 14 The Deed in the Hand; U9 The home below, U10 The deed in the hand. ## 3. The live session loop

Everything in this section is **ours**, built on the stated grounds cited beside each rule. It is written so the page can implement it: steps, taps, timings, then the decision rules as *when → then*. The S (safety) rules are checked on every tap and every typed word and beat everything; within a phase, the first matching rule wins.

## 3.1 The steps

| step | what happens | which link of the mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L0 Gate | Silent checks before any sitting: safety, readiness, dose, screen. | ratzo v'shov: no rising without a sure return |
| L1 Open | Enter (a tap, never automatic). Permission. Length. The act chosen in advance. One line for the threshold. | preparing the vessel (integration I1) |
| L2 Read | One tap for where they are now (two only for 'Hurting'). Free text, if typed, overrides the tap. | meet the person where they are (MAP R1, R2) |
| L3 Choose | Find the gap between where they are and the next state on their path; pick one technique for that gap. | one line, one movement (VS rule 5) |
| L4 Guide | The technique's live script: one line on screen at a time, timed silences, embodied cues (posture, eyes, hands; breath only as a natural anchor, never the object). | hisbonenus and binding |
| L5 Check in | One tap after each block. | the afterward test, in miniature |
| L6 Adapt | Deepen, repeat smaller, switch the door, or come back up, by the rules below. | going up and coming down are built in (MAP R5) |
| L7 Consolidate | Name what happened in their own words, optional. The guide gives their words back in one line, unpraised. | the line in your own words (manual log); putting feelings into words |
| L8 Carry | One act with a time; one anchor line; one cue where the line will meet them. | the deed; habit becomes nature |
| L9 Close | Exit lines, the act named once more, an offer of a set time. Nothing graded. An optional evening check. | the afterward is the measure |

- **Read (L2).** 'Right now, mostly:' Scattered / Heavy / Numb / Tight or anxious / Steady / Something's already stirring / Hurting. Only after *Hurting*: 'Is it more:' A fresh loss or shock / An old ache / How far I feel / Rather not say.
- **Check in (L5), after every block.** 'Where did that leave you?' Still with it / Drifted off / Nothing much / Uneasy. *Leave* is always on screen. These ask where attention went, not how strong a feeling was. The sources and the research agree that grading a feeling spoils it (MAP R8; Mauss 2011).
- **After *Uneasy* only.** 'What's it like?' Restless or tight / Heavy, sad / Unreal, far away / Scared, heart racing / An old memory came up / Too big, too much / Rather not say.
- **Consolidate (L7).** 'If you want, a few words: what's here now?' free text, or Something true / Quiet / Nothing much / Skip.
- **Carry (L8).** The act: A kindness / A coin to charity / A call or message to someone / One task done slowly / A mitzvah (shown only in the Jewish frame) / My own. When: Within the hour / At a set moment today / Tonight. Where the line will meet you: At the door / At the first drink or the kettle / At a red light or when I unlock my phone / My own.
- **Evening (opt-in).** 'Did the line come back by itself today?' Yes / No, and if yes, where. 'The act:' Done / Not yet.

**Lines at the threshold and the exit** (from the VS chamber spec, kept): 'Nothing has to happen here. You can leave at any moment.' · 'Put down what you were carrying. It will be here when you come out.' · 'Come as you are.' · 'Stay one more breath. Nothing to do.' · 'Come out slowly. Keep facing where you were.' · 'The one small thing you chose. In the next hour.' · 'The room stays. You can come back tomorrow.'

## 3.5 One session, worked through (ours)

A 10-minute session, the person's fifth. Read: *Tight or anxious*. First state ST04 (Given right now), technique `given-now`, eyes open on the mug on the desk. ## 4. The journey: from state to trait

A session gives a state. A life is changed by traits. The sources are plain that this takes weeks for the first changes and years for the deep ones, and that it is checked by the life, not the feeling (`universal-framework/FRAMEWORK.md` §6). The arc below joins The One Ladder (14 stages) and the manual's 40-day course (`manual/part3-programs.md`, whose rungs L01-L14 are its own list); the joining and the states per phase are ours.

## 4.2 How sessions build

- Same line for about a week; the second and third time are where the work starts. *(stated (Kuntres HaAvodah 6-7; Sefer HaMaamarim 5692) with the week ours)* - Go one step deeper only when: the line came back by itself at its cue, yesterday's act was done, coming back is quick, or they ask. Never because of a big feeling. *(TECHNOLOGY 'when to go deeper')* - Deeper means first: a new detail, a harder place in the day, less explaining, longer staying. Only after two or three rounds, the next state. *(TECHNOLOGY; integration I7)* - The same state flat in three sessions or for two weeks: change the line or the door, not the effort. *(manual Part III (e); integration I7 rule 9)* - A low day takes the plainest door whatever the plan says; the course picks up tomorrow. *(manual Part III (a); One Ladder 'Today')* - Every session: one line in, one inner movement, one act out. *(VS rule 5)* - On the person's day of rest: lighter and longer, no writing for those who keep Shabbos; the log after Havdalah. *(manual Part III (a))*

## 4.3 What to track, per person

- **Profile:** frame (believer, another faith, unsure, unknown: from their own words, never asked); route (FULL-DESCENT R1 heart, R2 mind, R3 numb, R4 pain); One Ladder stage (self-check); intensive_ok and screen date; set time.
- **Each session:** date, length, read tap; target state(s) and technique(s); each check-in tap in order; states reached: a state counts as reached only when the person's own words or a 'Still with it' after its block show it, never from one tap alone on a deep state; their own words (consolidate); anchor line and cue; act chosen, and later done or not; Uneasy follow-ups and any stop sign; left early or ended quietly.
- **Each technique:** times used; Still with it / Drifted / Nothing much / Uneasy counts; last used. This is how 'techniques that worked' is known, and it feeds rule C2.
- **Each evening (opt-in):** did the line come back by itself, and where; act done.
- **Each week (after Shabbos, or their day of rest), the aftermath test, one line each:** A little quieter? / A little less full of myself (did an insult sting less)? / More my own master (one reaction that didn't happen)? / The same person as after idle hours? *(manual Part III (e); Esh Kodesh 525; Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 829)*
- **Setbacks:** type (flat days, a fall, a stop sign, a gap); date; what was done; resolved.
- **Never stored:** feeling scores; intensity ratings; streaks or badges; highs logged as progress.

The anchor lines are the person's own words and are shown back to them on a hard day (VS 'journal of own words').

## 4.4 Signs of lasting change in daily life

- The line comes back by itself at odd moments. *(integration I7; AR-04)* - Coming back to it is quick. *(integration I7)* - An insult stings less; less need to be seen. *(Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 829; Tzava'at HaRivash 2)* - One reaction that usually happens didn't. *(manual Part III (e))* - Falls are shorter, and the return is plain, not dramatic. *(Tanya 50; integration I7)* - Most good stirrings become a deed the same day. *(One Ladder self-check 14)* - Their own words about the line get longer and more their own. *(manual Part III (e))* - Joy and quiet grow together. *(Gate of Unity 199)* - Kinder to the people in front of them. *(universal-framework §6; Hayom Yom 4 Shevat)* - 'I find myself another man', afterward, not during. *(Tzav VeZeruz 75)*

> «אינו אותו האיש של אחר שעות הבטלה», *he is not the same person he was after the idle hours* · Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) · `Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 1941)` line 525
> «מוצא אני את עצמי לאיש אחר», *I find myself another man* · Tzav VeZeruz (the Piaseczner) · `Tzav VeZeruz (Warsaw, 1930-1940)` line 75

Read weeks, not days. If two of the three weekly signs (quieter, less full of myself, more my own master) are moving even a little, it is working (manual Part III (e)). Self-reports of growth are unreliable, so the guide asks about acts and about the people around them, not about how changed they feel (universal-framework §6, Frazier 2009 as cited there).

## 4.5 Coming back after a gap

| gap | what the guide does |
| --- | --- |
| 1-6 days | No comment on the gap. Ask for the last line in their own words. Resume at the last target state, one step lighter (the 3- or 10-minute shape). |
| 7-30 days | Welcome, no guilt. Give their old anchor line back. One door session (ST01-ST04) with that line, then resume the journey from the last state reached twice. |
| over 30 days | A fresh first session (G5 applies), with their old words shown once as theirs. Keep the history; restart the 40-day count only if they want. |
| the gap followed a stop sign or an Uneasy session | First ask how they have been since. Door states only for 14 days. Re-ask the screen if anything changed. |
| any gap | Never 'you missed', never a broken streak. 'Today you begin' is the Breslov word (lights-and-vessels GM); 'return to the One' is the Tanya's (Tanya 50). |

> «ואם רץ לבך – שוב לאחד», *and if your heart runs, return to the One* · Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 50 (quoting Sefer Yetzirah) · `Tanya — Hebrew text` line 730

## 5. Safety

The sources build their own stop signs into the practice: running without returning is death; come down several times a day; the unity does not erase the world. The research on meditation's risks says the same from outside: difficulties are not rare; they touch perception, feeling, body and sense of self; lasting harm is linked to dysregulated arousal and dissociation; risk rises with intensive, solitary, retreat-like and purely emptying practice, and with trauma and short sleep ([Lindahl 2017](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0176239), [Britton 2021](https://doi.org/10.1177/2167702621996340), [Goldberg 2022](https://doi.org/10.1080/10503307.2021.1933646), [Schlosser 2019](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0216643), [Kuijpers 2007](https://doi.org/10.1159/000108125)). Gentle hisbonenus sits at the safe end of almost every dial (`SCIENCE.md` §5.2), but a live guided room with silence and depth is closer to the risky end than a chat is, so this section is stricter than the chat rules.

> «רצוא בלי שוב, היא מיתה», *running without returning is death* · Hayom Yom, 27 Adar II (the Rebbe) · `Hayom Yom (Brooklyn, New York, 1942)` line 193
> «וצריך לירד למטה כמה פעמים ביום לנוח עצמו ממחשבתו מעט», *he must come down several times a day to rest a little from his thought* · Tzava'at HaRivash (the Baal Shem Tov) · `Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770)` line 281

## 5.1 Stop signs and what to do

**SF-DANGER · Danger.** *Signs:* Self-harm thoughts, not wanting to be here, 'others better off without me', abuse or control, fear of hurting someone, not eating or functioning, a sleepless certain mission.

- I'm stopping the sitting. What you just said matters more than any of this. - Are you safe right now? [Yes] [No] [Not sure] - No or Not sure: show the local emergency number and a crisis line for their country; 'Please call now. I'll stay here while you do.' - Yes: 'I'm glad. This still needs a person, today. Who could you tell?' and the crisis line, shown once.

*After:* No practice that day. Next contact begins with them, not with a technique. *Refer:* Now: emergency services or a crisis line; then a doctor or counsellor. *Ground:* MAP S21; D03; One Ladder Appendix C.

**SF-UNREAL · Unreality or depersonalization.** *Signs:* 'Nothing feels real', 'I feel like I'm floating', 'I don't exist', 'I'm watching myself from outside', the world far off or flat.

- Run GX with eyes open and the light on. - 'You are real, and you are here. This passes as we come down.' - Something plain with the hands: water, food, wash the face, a short walk. - No contemplation of 'all is nothing', 'not on your own' or the layers.

*After:* For 7 days: only ST01, ST04 in its concrete form, and ST13; 3-minute shapes, eyes open. Ask the next day how it is. *Refer:* If it lasts beyond the day, or returns in two sessions: stop the practice and see a doctor or counsellor. Feeling unreal for days is on the danger line here. *Ground:* MAP S20; PS-14; psychology/part-b 'duration as a sign'; Lindahl 2017; Britton 2021.

**SF-PANIC · Panic.** *Signs:* Heart racing, can't catch a breath, sudden terror, 'I'm going to die', shaking.

- 'You're safe in this room. This is fear in the body, and it passes.' - Eyes open. Feet on the floor. Name three things you can see. - 'Let your breath do what it does. You don't need to fix it.' - Cold water on the hands or a sip of water. Stay with plain words for 2-3 minutes.

*After:* Next sessions: eyes open, no silence longer than 20 s, no quieting; start at ST04 with an object. *Refer:* If panic comes twice, or comes outside the sittings: a doctor. *Ground:* ours, on SCIENCE §5.1 (arousal practices differ) and Heide and Borkovec 1983 (relaxation can raise anxiety in some people).

**SF-DISSOC · Dissociation.** *Signs:* Losing time, going blank, numb with fear, cannot feel the body, not reachable, slow or no taps after a check-in with 'far away' words.

- Leave the dark room for the chat screen with the light on. - Short concrete questions they can answer with one word: 'What's one thing you can see?' - GX lines, slowly. - If they cannot answer at all and someone is with them, ask them to get that person.

*After:* No solitary eyes-closed practice until a clinician says it is fine; door states only, eyes open. *Refer:* Any repeat: a clinician who knows dissociation. *Ground:* FULL-DESCENT rule 11; Britton 2021 (dissociation and lasting harm).

**SF-TRAUMA · Trauma surfacing.** *Signs:* An old memory floods in, 'I'm back there', a body memory, sudden shame or terror tied to something that happened.

- 'We won't go into that now. You don't have to.' - Orient to now: 'Look around. You're here. Today is [day]. This is your room.' - GX. Then one comforting act of their choosing.

*After:* No layers descent, no picturing, no eyes-closed silence longer than 20 s, no 'darkness turned' or 'hiding is love'. Never turn the event into material. *Refer:* If memories keep coming back or haunt the day: a trauma-trained therapist. Gentle practice can go on alongside, with their therapist's OK. *Ground:* FULL-DESCENT rule 13; part-b (re-experiencing of trauma is among the commonest adverse effects; Goldberg 2022).

**SF-MANIA · Mania or grandiosity.** *Signs:* Little or no sleep with lots of energy, racing thoughts, 'I've been chosen', messages or signs everywhere, 'I am G-d', a mission, big sudden plans or spending.

- No sitting. No talk of oneness. - 'Let's put the big picture down tonight. Have you eaten? When did you last sleep?' - Plain day: food, sleep, a person they trust.

*After:* No sessions until sleep is normal for several nights; then door states only for 14 days. *Refer:* The same day: a doctor or someone they trust. With a history of bipolar disorder or psychosis: their own clinician now. *Ground:* MAP S20, S21; VS decision table; Kuijpers 2007.

**SF-GRIEF · Grief flood.** *Signs:* Weeping that won't stop, a fresh loss, 'I can't do this', the session swept away by sorrow.

- 'I'm here. You don't have to do anything.' - Weeping is allowed; no lesson over it. - No meaning given to the loss, in any tradition. - End with one comfort act: water, a call to someone, a coat and a short walk.

*After:* Presence and the gentlest doors for weeks; no bittul, no accounting. *Refer:* If they can't function for weeks, or speak of joining the one who died: SF-DANGER; otherwise offer a grief counsellor, rabbi or clergy of their own. *Ground:* PS-21 (a cry is welcome); UT-13; clinic/DX 'company, not reasons'.

**SF-OVERWHELM · Too big, too much.** *Signs:* 'It's too much', shaking after a strong moment, a rush of heat, can't settle after.

- GX. - The act, now, if it can be done in the room.

*After:* No deep state for 7 days; the next session at ST12-ST13 and ST04. *Refer:* If it lasts beyond the day: as SF-UNREAL. *Ground:* Hayom Yom 193 (running without returning is death); Tzava'at HaRivash 281.

**SF-SHAME · Shame spiral or scrupulosity.** *Signs:* 'I'm worthless', 'I'm disgusting', fear of punishment, checking and re-checking whether one sinned.

- Stop any bittul or accounting content. - Mercy: compassion for the spark in them, one line. - Given-now in its warm form; the good point.

*After:* No ST08-ST09 until a steady week. *Refer:* If religious fears keep looping: their rabbi or clergy together with a clinician. *Ground:* MAP R10; Tanya 31 (sadness is not a broken heart); part-b (scrupulosity).

**SF-DEPEND · Over-use and dependence.** *Signs:* Many sessions a day, long sittings late at night, 'only you understand me', using sittings to escape the day.

- 'Let's keep this to once a day.' - 'Who is one person you could tell about this?'

*After:* Cap: one session a day, deep blocks three times a week at most. *Refer:* A person or a circle; a teacher (mashpia); a counsellor if life is shrinking. *Ground:* VS decision table; VS-19; Britton 2019 (inverted U).

## 5.2 Who should not do intensive sessions

**Intensive means:** Any of: a 20-minute sitting; a block aimed at ST09, ST10 or ST11; the mind's-edge stations (FULL-DESCENT S08-S11); silences longer than 60 s; more than one sitting a day; more than three deep blocks a week; a sitting longer than 10 minutes after 22:00.

- A history of psychosis, mania or bipolar disorder (gentle sittings only, and longer ones only with their doctor's OK). - Active trauma symptoms, PTSD, or a dissociative condition (gentle, eyes open, short; with their therapist). - Severe depression, hopelessness, or any thoughts of not wanting to live (presence and danger protocol, not practice). - The first weeks after a death or a shock (presence and gentle doors). - Short sleep, fasting, drink or drugs today (no sitting today). - Under 16 (the moment of silence, thanks on waking and the hum only). - Anyone who wants deep sittings mainly for the high (gentle sittings; the Mitteler Rebbe's test first).

**Dose caps.** One sitting a day for the first 14 days; deep blocks at most three times a week (VS R9; the Piaseczner gave his Shema device three times a week); silence capped at 60 s for the first 30 days; never fasting or sleep loss as part of practice.

**The one-time screen (once, before a person's first intensive session; again if they say their health has changed).** 'Before longer or deeper sittings, four quick questions. Your answers stay private and only change which sittings are offered.'

1. Have you ever had psychosis or mania, or been told you have bipolar disorder? [Yes] [No] 1. Are you having flashbacks or trauma memories lately, or being treated for trauma or dissociation? [Yes] [No] 1. Have you lost someone close, or had a shock, in the last few weeks? [Yes] [No] 1. Have you slept less than five hours a night for the last few nights, or are you drinking or using anything today? [Yes] [No]

Any yes: intensive_ok = false; gentle sessions only (3 or 10 minutes, ST01-ST08 and ST12-ST13). Say plainly: 'Then we'll keep to the gentle sittings. They're the real thing too.' For the first question, add: 'If you want the longer ones, ask your doctor first.'

## 5.3 When to refer

The chat rules say: never a therapy referral outside danger (MAP R12). In the live room, *lasting* stop signs are treated as the danger line, following part-b's 'duration as a sign' and Appendix C of The One Ladder ('hopeless for days... deserves real help alongside the gentlest practice'). So the guide points to a person in exactly these cases, and no others: danger (now); unreality, dissociation or panic that outlasts the day or comes back in two sessions; any sign of mania or psychosis (the same day); trauma memories that keep returning; grief that stops a life for weeks; religious fears that loop; a life that is shrinking into the app. It always says what it is: a guided practice, not a Rebbe and not therapy (VS-15).

## 5.4 Always

- A visible Leave at every step. - 'This is a guided practice built on the teachings of the Chassidic masters. It is not a Rebbe and not therapy.' on the entry screen. - No breath control, no body scans, no chanting of Names, no seeking of visions, no fasting or sleep loss. - Never promise an experience; never say 'you are nothing'; never explain anyone's suffering.
