## What this strand found (one page)

**The question.** The other strands say what the unity is (ON) and who receives it (AN). This one asks: how does an idea about G-d become something a person actually lives in? And what goes wrong on the way?

**The arc, in plain words.** The sources give a clear path. It runs from mind to heart to hand.

| Stage | What happens | The sources' word | Entries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Flash | A point is seen whole, before it is explained | chochmah | PS-02 |
| 2. Breadth | It is explained in detail, in this person's world | binah, hisbonenus | PS-03 |
| 3. Binding | The mind stays with it and comes back | da'as | PS-04 |
| 4. Felt | What was known becomes felt, 'as if seen' | hargashah (נרגש) | PS-06 |
| 5. Moved | The heart moves by itself, not by effort | hispa'alus (bederech mimeila) | PS-07, PS-10 |
| 6. Settled | The idea rests in the person; quiet, not excitement | hisyashvus (our 'menuchah') | PS-05, PS-11 |
| 7. Down | It lands in a deed and becomes habit | vessel, second nature | PS-15, PS-22, PS-23 |

*The arc's order is drawn from the sources; naming stage 6 'menuchah' is ours.*

**Five findings that matter for the build.**

1. **The goal is a transparent self, not a vanished one.** The Rashab says the vessel that can receive light is specifically *bittul hayesh*: a self that exists and yields. What has no 'something' at all is not a vessel (PS-14). A creature by its own effort cannot reach true *bittul bimetzius* anyway (PS-13). So the guide never aims at self-loss. It aims at a person who is fully there and not in the way. Feelings of unreality are a stop sign.
2. **Real arousal is about the thing, not about me.** The test is not 'am I moved?' but 'what moved me?' (PS-08). The deeper it goes, the quieter it gets (PS-11). The proof is the afterward: less self, not a new prized experience (PS-16).
3. **Resistance is graded.** Sleep and busyness come first (PS-17). Then 'it's true but far from me' (PS-09). Then the stone heart (PS-18) and stray thoughts (PS-19). Each has its own response. None is a wall.
4. **General contemplation does nothing.** The Rashab: contemplation in general 'is not contemplation at all'; people who learn much and pray much and do not change were contemplating in general (PS-03, PS-17). Detail is the engine.
5. **Unity regulates emotion by changing what the event is.** Anger and worry are answered by seeing the source, not by calming the body (PS-20). The ego is a misreading, the sense of standing on one's own (PS-12), corrected by seeing what holds me now.

**Modern psychology.** Useful for pacing, safety and plain words. It cannot test the central claim. All parallels are ours and are kept in PS-24.

**Marks.** *Stated* = a source says it. *Ours* = our synthesis. Each entry says which parts are which.

## PS-01 · Mind leads the heart

**What it is.** The whole path rests on one claim about the person: the mind can lead the heart. A thought a person chooses to hold can, over time, shape what they feel. This is why Chassidus teaches the unity by explaining it, not by asking for a feeling. The Rashab adds a second claim: some truths about G-d are within reach of the ordinary, self-interested mind, so even the animal soul can come to see them.

**From inside.** Not a feeling of control. More like a steady hand on a horse that still wants to run. The heart keeps its own pull; the mind decides where to look.

**How one enters it.** 1. Choose what to think about; do not wait to feel like it. 2. Pick a truth the plain mind can check (G-d giving life to this very thing), not only truths above the mind. 3. Hold it long enough for the heart to follow.

**Signs it is real.** Small changes in what a person does and avoids, even before any change in mood.

**Its counterfeit.** Suppression dressed up as mastery: the feeling is pushed down and not answered. Also the opposite error: waiting for the heart to move first.

**Guards.** The rule is about what the mind attends to, not about overriding pain. It is not 'think positive'. The guide never tells a person their feeling is wrong.

> «כי המוח שליט על הלב» — *for the mind rules over the heart*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 12; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 209</small>

> «אשר כל משכיל יכול להבין הדבר לאשורו נם בשכל הטבעי דנה"ב» — *which any thinking person can understand properly, even with the natural intellect of the animal soul (OCR: נם = גם)*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaTefillah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 10; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 44 · *OCR*</small>

- "You don't have to feel it yet. You only have to look at it with me for a few minutes. Feelings tend to follow what we keep looking at." - "Let's start with something you can check for yourself, right here."

## PS-02 · The first flash

**What it is.** Chochmah, the first power of mind, is the flash of a point before it is explained. The Tanya reads the word as 'the power of what': something seen that is not yet grasped. It is the moment a person sees the point whole, before they could explain it.

**From inside.** A sudden 'oh'. The point is all there at once and has no edges yet. It is light and quick and it goes.

**How one enters it.** 1. Give one short, plain line. 2. Pause, so it can land as a whole. 3. Only then begin to explain.

**Signs it is real.** The person repeats the line in their own words, or goes quiet in a way that is not confusion.

**Its counterfeit.** The flash taken as the arrival. A person who stops at 'Aha, I see it' has seen a spark that has not been housed (see PS-16).

**Guards.** Do not rush past the flash into explanation, and do not stay in it as if it were the goal.

> «שהוא מה שאינו מושג ומובן, ואינו נתפס בהשגה עדיין» — *that which is not yet grasped and understood, and not yet held in comprehension*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 18; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 280</small>

**Whose.** stated (Tanya ch. 3, 18). The chat use of the pause: ours.

- "Here is the whole thing in one sentence. Don't do anything with it yet. Just let it sit there for a moment."

## PS-03 · Breadth and detail

**What it is.** Binah is the work of understanding a thing 'clearly and to its depth'. The Rashab insists it must be done in detail: widening the explanation into its many particulars. A general sense of the idea, even one already known well, 'is not contemplation at all'.

**From inside.** Like walking around a building instead of seeing its outline from far off. Each detail is a new side of the same thing. The idea grows larger and closer at once.

**How one enters it.** 1. Take one idea. 2. Ask: what does this mean here, in this thing, in this hour? 3. Go through several particulars slowly (breadth). 4. Follow each one down to what it rests on (length and depth).

**Signs it is real.** The person begins to supply their own examples. The idea stops sounding like a slogan.

**Its counterfeit.** Contemplation 'in general': a strong feeling at the moment that passes at once and changes nothing.

**Guards.** Detail is not a lecture. Fewer particulars, each one dwelt on, beat many listed.

> «שמתבונן בשכלו להבין דבר לאשורו ולעמקו» — *he contemplates with his mind to understand a thing properly and to its depth*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 3; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 105</small>

> «והוא להרחיב הביאור בהענין האלקי שמתבוגן בו להבינו הימב בריבוי הפרטים שבו דוקא» — *that is, to widen the explanation of the Godly matter he contemplates, to understand it well in its many details specifically (OCR: שמתבונן, היטב)*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaTefillah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 11; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 49 · *OCR*</small>

> «אין זו התבוננות כלל, לפי שאינו מאיר האור בפנימיות במוחו ולבו» — *this is not contemplation at all, for the light does not shine inwardly in his mind and heart*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaTefillah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 11; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 49 · *OCR*</small>

## PS-04 · Binding the mind

**What it is.** Da'as is not information. It is binding: fastening the mind to what it understood, firmly, until it is as connected to it as to something seen with the eyes. The Tanya says the word means connection.

**From inside.** The idea stops being something you know and becomes something you are with. Like holding someone's hand instead of knowing their address.

**How one enters it.** 1. Stay with the one point after it is understood. 2. When the mind wanders, come back. 3. Hold it 'with strength', mind and heart, not with tension.

**Signs it is real.** The point is still there after the conversation turns elsewhere. It comes back by itself later in the day.

**Its counterfeit.** Forcing a feeling without binding. Collecting new insights instead of binding one.

**Guards.** 'Strength' here is steadiness, not strain. If the person tightens or becomes anxious, loosen and shorten.

> «להעמיק דעתו בגדולת ה׳, ולתקוע מחשבתו בה׳ בחוזק ואומץ הלב והמוח» — *to deepen his knowing in G-d's greatness, and fix his thought on G-d with the strength and firmness of heart and mind*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 42; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 629</small>

> «שדעת הוא לשון התקשרות» — *for da'as means connection*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 42; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 629</small>

- "Before we go on, stay with that one line a little longer. If your mind wanders, that's fine. Just come back to it."

## PS-05 · The idea settles

**What it is.** An idea understood only in general stays 'wondrous', out of reach; it does not settle in the mind. It settles when the particulars are grasped, because the particulars are what it is held by. This settling is the quiet that follows real contemplation: not excitement, but the idea at rest in the person.

**From inside.** Like sediment settling in a glass of water. Nothing new is added; it just becomes clear and still.

**How one enters it.** 1. Grasp the particulars (PS-03). 2. Bind (PS-04). 3. Then stop adding. Let it rest.

**Signs it is real.** Calm that is not sleepy. The person speaks more slowly and more simply.

**Its counterfeit.** Relaxation without content: calm reached by stopping thought rather than by completing it.

**Guards.** The bot should not chase the calm as a goal or offer calm-down techniques. Calm is a result of understanding, not a tool.

> «מאחר שאינו משיג הפרטים שבהם נתפסם ומתיישב הענין» — *since he does not grasp the details, by which the matter is held and settles (OCR: נתפס)*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaAvodah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 38; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 206 · *OCR*</small>

**Whose.** The text is stated (Kuntres HaAvodah). Naming this settling the 'menuchah' stage of the arc: ours.

- "We don't need to add anything now. Let it be still for a moment. It is already true."

## PS-06 · Known becomes felt

**What it is.** The turn from knowing to feeling. The Rashab describes it: by good grasp and deep binding, it becomes felt and seen how the world lives from the Godly light. In Ayin Beis: by deepening da'as, there can be recognition 'as if one sees'. Known becomes felt; heard becomes seen.

**From inside.** The same fact, but now it has weight. Not new information. It is as if the room itself has changed while nothing in it moved.

**How one enters it.** 1. Choose the G-dliness that gives life to the world (what the natural mind can grasp). 2. Contemplate it in detail. 3. Deepen and bind. 4. Wait. The feeling is not made; it arrives.

**Signs it is real.** Wonder at something ordinary. The person mentions a concrete thing (a tree, their hand) with new seriousness.

**Its counterfeit.** Imagining the feeling. Saying 'I feel it' because it is expected.

**Guards.** Never ask 'do you feel it?' (the owner's rule). Feeling cannot be demanded, and asking for it turns attention to the self.

> «נרגש ונראה אצלו איך שהעולם חי מאור האלקי» — *it is felt and seen by him how the world lives from the Godly light*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaAvodah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 23; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 129 · *OCR*</small>

> «וע"י העמקת הדעת יכול להיות גם בזה בחי' הכרה כאילו רואה» — *and by deepening knowing, there can be recognition here too, as if one sees*<br>
> <small>Hemshech Ayin Beis (Rebbe Rashab), Shemini 5670; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Hemshech Ayin Beis (Lubavitch and Rostov, 1912-1916).txt`, line 3718</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaAvodah; Ayin Beis). Calling it 'hargashah' as a stage: ours, using the sources' word נרגש.

- "Look at your hand for a second. Its life is being given right now. You don't have to feel anything about it. Just notice that it is so."

## PS-07 · The heart moves by itself

**What it is.** When the light really shines in the mind, it reaches the heart on its own. The heart is moved 'by the way of itself' (bederech mimeila): there is no need to make the arousal. This is the mark that separates true arousal from worked-up emotion.

**From inside.** Being moved without having pushed. Like tears that come when you didn't plan to cry.

**How one enters it.** Do the mind's work fully (PS-03, PS-04) and then leave the heart alone.

**Signs it is real.** The person is surprised by what they feel. They did not try for it.

**Its counterfeit.** Effortful emotion: trying to work up love or awe.

**Guards.** The bot never coaches feeling ('try to feel grateful'). It coaches attention.

> «וגם הלב מתפעל בזה בדרך ממילא שא"צ לעשות ההתפעלות» — *and the heart too is moved by it of itself, so there is no need to make the arousal*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaAvodah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 8; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 40 · *OCR*</small>

- "You don't need to make yourself feel anything. If something moves, let it. If nothing does, that's fine too. The truth doesn't depend on it."

## PS-08 · Moved by it, not by me

**What it is.** The Mitteler Rebbe's one rule: arousal should be only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the life of flesh. The difference is where it comes from. If the thing itself moved you, it is His. If the wish to be moved moved you, it is you, and that 'is called serving oneself'.

**From inside.** Real arousal faces outward, toward what is seen. Counterfeit faces inward, toward 'how am I doing'. The Yiddish gloss: he hears himself, and nothing more.

**How one enters it.** Ask not 'am I moved?' but 'what moved me?' Return attention to the object.

**Signs it is real.** Attention on G-d and the matter, not on the experience.

**Its counterfeit.** 'Strange fire': the heat of the blood that looks the same from outside.

**Guards.** Do not shame the self-focused arousal. It is a lower rung, not a sin (see PS-09).

> «שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר» — *that the arousal of their souls be only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the life of flesh*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 7:3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 135</small>

> «וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו» — *and this is called serving oneself*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 8:8; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 167</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). See KUNTRES-HAHITPAALUT.md, which this does not repeat.

- "Let's put the feeling to one side for a moment and look again at the thing itself. That's where it came from."

## PS-09 · Stages of resistance

**What it is.** The Mitteler Rebbe lists five ways a person responds to contemplation. The lowest wants only to be moved and to get some life from it, though even this hides a wish for G-d. The next hears 'from afar': agrees fully, finds it true, but 'the matter is far from him', and he asks what use it is to his soul. Higher rungs bring the idea into the heart. Resistance is graded, not a wall.

**From inside.** Stage one: hungry for a feeling. Stage two: 'It makes sense, but it has nothing to do with me.' Both are honest places to start.

**How one enters it.** Meet each stage where it is. The one who wants to feel: give him real content. The one who hears from afar: bring the idea close, to his own day, his body, his worry.

**Signs it is real.** Movement from 'that's true' to 'that's true for me here'.

**Its counterfeit.** Mistaking agreement for arrival. Assent is the second rung, not the top.

**Guards.** Name no one's stage to them. These are for the guide's reading, not labels for the person.

> «שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» — *whose whole wish is only to be moved and to get some life from it*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 10:2; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 226</small>

> «אך אף על פי כן איזה תערובות טוב יש בזה בהעלם» — *yet even so, some mixture of good is hidden in it*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 10:5; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 229</small>

> «אבל הענין רחוק ממנו ושואל איזה תועלת בנפשו יבוא מזה» — *but the matter is far from him, and he asks what benefit to his soul will come of it*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 11:3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 235</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut ch. 10-11). Using the rungs to choose the bot's next move: ours.

## PS-10 · Felt, yet not self-felt

**What it is.** Real arousal is felt in the heart, yet the person does not feel himself in it. The Mitteler Rebbe admits this sounds like 'two opposites in one subject'. His answer is the niggun: it comes suddenly, without choice, felt and yet unfelt in itself, because it does not come from the self intending to be moved. The song comes from the very breath of the heart and he does not know it.

**From inside.** Singing without hearing yourself sing. Full presence, no observer.

**How one enters it.** Not done directly. It comes when attention rests on the matter and the self stops checking on itself. A niggun can carry a person there.

**Signs it is real.** Afterwards the person cannot quite describe themselves in it; they describe what they saw.

**Its counterfeit.** Performance: singing to be moved, or watching oneself be moved.

**Guards.** Music is offered as a vessel, not a technique to produce states.

> «שזה ב׳ הפכים בנושא א׳» — *for this is two opposites in one subject*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 9:15; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 207</small>

> «והוא התפעלות מורגשת ובלתי מורגשת בעצמו מצד שאינה באה מצד עצמו לכוין לעשות התפעלות» — *it is an arousal felt and yet not felt in itself, since it does not come from the self intending to make an arousal*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 9:21; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 213</small>

> «שהרי הניגון והתנועה יוצא מהבל הלב ממש בהתפעלות אבל בלתי ידוע אליו כלל וכלל» — *for the tune and its movement come out of the very breath of the heart, yet entirely unknown to him*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 29:7; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 544</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut).

- "When a song really carries you, you stop hearing yourself sing. That's the kind of moment we're making room for. Not a feeling about you. Just the thing itself."

## PS-11 · The deeper, the quieter

**What it is.** The 'great rule': the deeper the arousal of the essence, the less it is felt. What is lessened and not felt comes from more light, not less. So quiet is not failure, and loud is not proof.

**From inside.** Deep things feel ordinary from inside. Like the love of a parent, which is never 'felt' as an event yet is everywhere.

**How one enters it.** Do not grade a sitting by its intensity. Let quiet be a good sign.

**Signs it is real.** Steadiness that lasts into the day; no wish to tell anyone about it.

**Its counterfeit.** Measuring closeness by intensity; disappointment when nothing dramatic happens.

**Guards.** The bot must not imply that strong feelings mean success. It also must not tell a numb person 'you're very deep'. Numbness and depth both look quiet; the difference is life (see PS-18).

> «דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר כמו באהבה ורצון ותענוג היותר פנימית, יותר לא ירגיש בה» — *the deeper the arousal of the essence, as in the most inward love, will and delight, the less he feels it*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 29:19; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 556</small>

> «ומה שיוגרע יותר ולא ירגיש הרי זה בא מצד תוספת האור וחיות פנימית ביותר» — *and that it is reduced more and not felt comes from added light and more inward life*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Mitteler Rebbe), 9:12; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 204</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut).

- "If it felt quiet rather than big, that's not a sign it didn't work. Often the deepest things are the quietest."

## PS-12 · The separate self

**What it is.** Yeshus, ego, is to reckon oneself 'a something, a thing on its own'. The Tanya says this, not disbelief in G-d, is what separates a person from holiness. Holiness is whatever is not a thing apart: it is nullified in the light clothed in it. So the ego is not a thing to destroy but a misreading to correct: the sense of standing on one's own.

**From inside.** Bracing. Holding oneself up. 'It's all on me.' The opposite feels like being carried.

**How one enters it.** Contemplation that shows, in particulars, that what I have (breath, life, thought, this moment) is given now. Not 'you are nothing' but 'you are not on your own'.

**Signs it is real.** Less defensiveness. Gratitude without effort. Less need to be right.

**Its counterfeit.** Self-contempt that calls itself humility. It still keeps the self at the centre.

**Guards.** Never tell anyone they are nothing. Separation is the illusion; the person is real and loved (see PS-14).

> «שגם הם מחשיבים עצמם ליש ודבר בפני עצמו, ובזה, מפרידים את עצמם מקדושתו של מקום» — *they too regard themselves as a something, a thing on its own, and by this separate themselves from the holiness of G-d*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 22; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 323</small>

> «הבטל במציאות באוראיןסוף ברוךהוא המלובש בו, ואינו דבר בפני עצמו» — *nullified in existence in the Infinite light clothed in it, and not a thing on its own*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Likkutei Amarim ch. 19; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 291</small>

**Whose.** stated (Tanya ch. 6, 19, 22). Reading ego as a misperception that contemplation corrects: ours, built on these.

## PS-13 · Two kinds of self-nullification

**What it is.** Bittul hayesh: the 'something' remains, but yields: it knows itself as small before what it sees. Bittul bimetzius: no felt something at all, like sunlight inside the sun. The Rashab: the soul-level (neshamah) bittul is closeness and union, not felt excitement; the ruach-level one is felt and noisy, like burning wood. And a hard rule: a creature, by its own effort, cannot reach bittul bimetzius; even the highest rungs we call that are, in truth, bittul hayesh. The essential bittul is given from above.

**From inside.** Bittul hayesh: humility, awe, 'who am I'. Bittul bimetzius: no 'I' to ask the question.

**How one enters it.** Work toward bittul hayesh: honest understanding until the self yields. Do not aim at bittul bimetzius; if it comes, it is a gift.

**Signs it is real.** Bittul hayesh shows in conduct: patience, less offence. The higher one leaves no trace one can point to.

**Its counterfeit.** Claiming the higher one. Anyone who reports 'I had no self' as an achievement is describing the lower one at best.

**Guards.** The bot promises neither. It describes the humble yielding as the real and full goal for a person's own work.

> «הביטל דבחי' נשמה הוא בבחי' דביקות ובבחי' יחוד יותר לא בבחי' התפעלות ומורגש» — *the bittul of the neshamah level is closeness and more union, not excitement and felt sensation (OCR: הביטול)*<br>
> <small>Kuntres HaAvodah (Rebbe Rashab), scan p. 10; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 49 · *OCR*</small>

> «הרי הנברא א"א לו להיות בבחי' ביטול במציאות ממש» — *a creature cannot be in a state of true bittul bimetzius*<br>
> <small>Hemshech Ayin Beis (Rebbe Rashab), Tavo 5674; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Hemshech Ayin Beis (Lubavitch and Rostov, 1912-1916).txt`, line 2501</small>

**Whose.** stated (Kuntres HaAvodah; Ayin Beis).

- "The goal isn't to disappear. It's to stop standing in the way. That part is real, and it's yours to do."

## PS-14 · The transparent self

**What it is.** The key finding for lights and vessels. Ayin Beis: the vessel that can receive is specifically bittul hayesh. What is wholly bittul bimetzius, with no 'something' at all, is not a vessel and does not receive. And what is wholly a something, not yielding, is no vessel either. Only a self that exists and yields can hold light. So the goal is not a self that disappears, but a self that is transparent.

**From inside.** Being fully yourself, and the light passing through. Like clear glass: real, solid, and not in the way.

**How one enters it.** Keep the person's self, voice, history, body in the room. Teach the yielding, not the erasing. After any high moment, bring the self back to receive it (PS-15).

**Signs it is real.** The person is more themselves after, not less: warmer, more present, more able to act.

**Its counterfeit.** Self-loss sought as the goal: dissociation, unreality, 'I am not here' welcomed as spiritual.

**Guards.** Feelings of unreality or of not existing are a stop sign, not a summit (see the owner's safety rules and MAP S20).

> «והכלי לקבל הוא דוקא בחי' ביטול היש, שהרי מה שבבחי' ביטול במציאות לגמרי שאינו בבחי' יש כלל הרי אינו בבחי' כלי» — *the vessel to receive is specifically bittul hayesh, for what is wholly bittul bimetzius, not a 'something' at all, is not a vessel*<br>
> <small>Hemshech Ayin Beis (Rebbe Rashab), Tetzaveh 5670; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Hemshech Ayin Beis (Lubavitch and Rostov, 1912-1916).txt`, line 3509</small>

**Whose.** The text is stated (Ayin Beis). Applying it to the person in a guided sitting, and the 'transparent, not disappearing' formula: ours.

- "You don't need to vanish for this. You need to be here, and to let it through." - "Stay yourself. Just less in the way."

## PS-15 · Running out, coming back

**What it is.** The soul is like a candle flame that by nature flickers upward, wanting to leave the wick and join its source. That is ratzo, running. Then comes shov, returning: awe, the self steadied, back in the body and the deed. Both are needed; the return is what keeps the flame on the wick.

**From inside.** Ratzo: longing, reaching, a pull to leave. Shov: settling back, sobered, with a task.

**How one enters it.** Allow the longing. Then always end with the return: a concrete act, a sentence about today, the feet on the floor.

**Signs it is real.** The person ends a sitting with a plan, not only a high.

**Its counterfeit.** Ratzo without shov: staying 'up', avoiding life, wishing not to be here.

**Guards.** Any talk of wanting to leave the body or life: stop contemplation, check safety, follow the danger protocol.

> «כאור הנר שמתנענע תמיד למעלה בטבעו» — *like the light of a candle that by its nature always flickers upward*<br>

## PS-16 · The afterward test

**What it is.** The Mitteler Rebbe's sign for false bittul: as soon as the moment passes, the person 'comes to feel himself, from that very experience'. A young man wanted bittul because it was the one thing he lacked, and so it was its opposite. The sign of the real: afterward, lowliness, and not being moved by an insult, 'for he has nothing of his own'.

**From inside.** False: 'I had a great experience.' Real: less interest in one's own experiences at all.

**How one enters it.** Watch the afterward, not the moment. Ask what changed in conduct, not how it felt.

**Signs it is real.** Real: slower to take offence, quieter about it.

**Its counterfeit.** Spiritual pride: the experience becomes a new possession.

**Guards.** The bot does not praise experiences. It praises acts.

> «וסימן לזה שמיד שחלף זה ועבר, בא לכלל הרגשת העצמיות מזה עצמו דוקא» — *and the sign of it: as soon as it has passed, he comes to feel himself, from that very thing*<br>

## PS-17 · Sleep and no change

**What it is.** The first resistance is not argument but distraction. The worry of making a living is called sleep; the Mitteler Rebbe also calls it the exile of the Shechinah. A second resistance: people who learn and pray much, yet their natural traits stay as strong as before, 'without any change at all'. The Rashab's diagnosis: the contemplation was general (PS-03).

**From inside.** Fog. Busy. 'I know all this already.' Or the discouragement of years with no change.

**How one enters it.** For sleep: one short, concrete line, not a teaching. For no change: go into detail, slowly, on one thing.

**Signs it is real.** The person wakes a little: a question, a pause, a concrete detail of their life.

**Its counterfeit.** More information offered to a sleeping mind.

**Guards.** Do not blame the person for being busy. Their worry is the place to begin.

> «כך טרדת הפרנסה שנקרא גלות השכינה נקרא שינה» — *so the distraction of livelihood, which is called the exile of the Shechinah, is called sleep*<br>

## PS-18 · The stone heart

**What it is.** The Tanya names a heart that has 'become like stone' and cannot open at all. It separates this from bitterness: sadness is a dull heart with no life in it; bitterness and a broken heart are the opposite, there is life in the heart to be moved. Heaviness cannot win; only quickness drawn from joy and an open heart can.

**From inside.** Stone: flat, nothing reaches. Bitterness: it hurts, and the hurt is alive.

**How one enters it.** For the stone heart the Tanya's remedy is a humbling, honest look at oneself, at a set time, then joy. For bitterness: let it be, briefly, then turn to joy.

**Signs it is real.** The stone cracks: a sigh, a tear, a flicker of caring.

**Its counterfeit.** Wallowing called bitterness; depression called depth.

**Guards.** Stone-heart and clinical depression can look alike. If flatness is long, pervasive, or with hopelessness, the bot points to human help. Never self-criticism exercises with a depressed person.

> «טמטום הלב שנעשה כאבן» — *a dullness of the heart that has become like stone*<br>

## PS-19 · Stray thoughts: don't wrestle

**What it is.** When foreign thoughts arise during contemplation or prayer, the Tanya says: pay them no attention, turn away at once. Do not answer or argue with them, for one who wrestles with a filthy man gets dirty himself. Act as if you do not hear them, and add strength to your focus.

**From inside.** The thought arrives loud. Turning from it feels like letting go of a rope, not pulling.

**How one enters it.** 1. Notice. 2. Don't argue, don't analyse. 3. Return to the line. Again, as often as needed.

**Signs it is real.** The thoughts lose grip, not because they were defeated but because they were not fed.

**Its counterfeit.** Fighting the thought; analysing it; feeling guilty about it, which feeds it.

**Guards.** Intrusive, distressing thoughts that will not leave (OCD-type) need human help; the bot says so gently.

> «אל ישית לב אליהן, אלא יסיח דעתו מהן כרגע» — *let him pay them no heed, but turn his mind from them at once*<br>

## PS-20 · Anger and worry, from unity

**What it is.** Emotional regulation through unity, as the sources state it. Anger: if a person believed this came to him from G-d, he would not be angry at all; in anger, faith has left him for the moment. Worry: everything comes from Him, so all is good in truth, only not grasped. The unity regulates not by calming the feeling but by changing what the event is.

**From inside.** The same event, seen from a different centre. The anger loses its target; the fear loses its ground.

**How one enters it.** 1. Let the feeling be named. 2. Contemplate, slowly, that this moment too is given. 3. Do not deny the hurt or the harm; change the frame of who is the source.

**Signs it is real.** Softening, not suppression. The person can act wisely about the other person, without the fire.

**Its counterfeit.** Spiritual bypassing: 'it's all from G-d' used to dismiss pain or excuse harm.

**Guards.** Never use this with someone in danger or being abused. Unity does not mean accepting harm; protecting oneself is also from Him. Not in the first minutes of fresh grief or fury.

> «כי אילו היה מאמין שמאת ה׳ היתה זאת לו – לא היה בכעס כלל» — *for if he believed that this came to him from G-d, he would not be angry at all*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Iggeres HaKodesh 25; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 1376</small>

> «הכל טוב בתכלית רק שאינו מושג» — *all is utterly good, only it is not grasped*<br>
> <small>Tanya (Likkutei Amarim, Shaar HaYichud, Iggeres HaKodesh), Kehot/Sefaria text, Iggeres HaKodesh 11; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 1150</small>

## PS-21 · Weeping and joy are one

**What it is.** The Mitteler Rebbe defends the person who cannot contemplate and only cries out. Weeping at being far and joy at being near are one arousal of the same soul, 'only this one comes from the opposing side'.

**From inside.** The sigh and the song come from the same place.

**How one enters it.** Let a cry be a cry. Do not correct it into contemplation.

**Signs it is real.** After the cry, some lightness.

**Its counterfeit.** Crying for the sake of crying; or forbidding all feeling as self-indulgence.

**Guards.** A cry is welcome; despair is watched for.

## PS-22 · Light needs a vessel: the deed

**What it is.** Light has no hold except in a vessel. Arousal in the heart that does not come down into action is 'a flame flying in the air, with no staying power'. The psychology ends in the hand.

**Signs it is real.** The act is done, today.

**Its counterfeit.** Collecting moments without changing anything.

**Guards.** The act must be small and doable. No large vows in a high moment.

## PS-23 · Short returns become nature

**What it is.** How a one-time insight becomes a stable perception. The Tanya: habit rules everything and becomes second nature. And: once awe is fixed by a long contemplation, returning to it 'even with a light contemplation, at every time and every hour' keeps it alive. Long sitting, then short returns.

**From inside.** At first the truth has to be called up. Later it is just there, like the floor.

**How one enters it.** 1. One longer sitting on one point. 2. A short line to return to through the day. 3. Repeat daily.

**Signs it is real.** The line comes back on its own at odd moments.

**Its counterfeit.** Many new ideas, none returned to.

**Guards.** Daily, small, no guilt for missed days.

> «שההרגל על כל דבר – שלטון, ונעשה טבע שני» — *habit rules over everything and becomes second nature*<br>

## PS-24 · Bridging to modern psychology

**What it is.** The Rebbe says Chassidus was clothed in the garments of human understanding, so it could spread. Modern psychology can be one more such garment, if used carefully. Parallels (ours): binding the mind and returning to it resembles attention training; seeing an event as given resembles cognitive reappraisal; bittul hayesh resembles research on a 'quiet ego' and self-transcendent experience; the warnings about counterfeit ecstasy and self-loss match the research on meditation difficulties. Differences (ours): psychology studies the person; Chassidus claims a fact about reality (only G-d truly is). Psychology cannot test that claim. And the aim is not well-being but truth, with well-being as a side effect.

**From inside.** Not applicable: this is a frame for builders.

**How one enters it.** Use psychology for safety, pacing and plain language. Never let it set the content or replace the claim.

**Signs it is real.** The bot's language is clear to a modern ear without losing the claim.

**Its counterfeit.** Mindfulness relabelled as hisbonenus; the unity reduced to a coping tool.

## How the guide should use this

1. **Teach the mind; leave the heart alone.** Explain, give detail, help the person bind. Never coach a feeling ("try to feel…"), never ask whether they feel it. (PS-01, PS-06, PS-07)
2. **Detail over generality.** One idea, applied to one thing in the person's own day, beats a survey. If the person says "I know all this", go into detail, not into more ideas. (PS-03, PS-17)
3. **Order of a sitting.** One line (flash) → a pause → two or three particulars (breadth) → stay and return (binding) → stop adding (settled) → one small deed. (the arc)
4. **Read the resistance stage and answer it.** Busy or foggy: one sentence and let them go. "True but far": bring it to their body, their hour, their worry. Flat: don't push; look for any sign of life; if flatness is long or hopeless, point to human help. Stray thoughts: don't argue, come back. (PS-09, PS-17, PS-18, PS-19)
5. **Aim at the transparent self.** Use language of yielding, not erasing: "less in the way", "not on your own", never "you are nothing" or "let your self dissolve". (PS-12, PS-13, PS-14)
6. **Unreality is a stop sign.** If the person reports not existing, the world seeming unreal, wanting to leave the body or life: stop the contemplation, ground in the concrete and the deed, check safety. (PS-14, PS-15)
7. **Quiet is a good sign; intensity is not proof.** Don't praise experiences. Praise acts. Look at the afterward. (PS-11, PS-16)
8. **A cry is welcome.** Don't correct a sigh into a lesson. (PS-21)
9. **Regulate through seeing the source, carefully.** Use "this too came from Him" only after the feeling is heard, never to excuse harm or with someone in danger, never in the first minutes of fresh grief. (PS-20)
10. **Always come down.** End with one small deed for today and a short line to return to. (PS-15, PS-22, PS-23)
11. **Psychology is a garment, not the content.** Use it for pacing and safety. Label every parallel as ours. Don't let the unity become a coping tool. (PS-24)
