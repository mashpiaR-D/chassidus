## The shape of the range

The range opens in the middle of *Nasso Es Rosh* (Shabbat Nasso, 9 Sivan 5666) and runs into the middle of *UMikneh Rav* (Shabbat Matos-Masei, Mevarchim Menachem-Av). The maamarim are:

The previous ranges were metaphysics: what "Ein Sof" names and why the world is "from nothing". This range turns that metaphysics into service. It asks three questions: - What does each kind of commandment reach in God? The prohibitions reach the Essence; the positive mitzvot reach the light that is already drawn down; Torah reaches the Essence and draws it inward. - How is the surrounding light present in a world that does not grasp it? It is present the way a person's will is present in his hand. - What does prayer actually know? Only the radiance, "from behind". The service is to thirst for the Essence by seeing that the radiance is only a radiance.

That is why F10 (Torah and mitzvah) and F08 (bitul) lead the face counts here for the first time, with F05 (the Essence) close behind. The unity is still the Essence beyond all revelation. What is new is the claim that a person reaches it through the parts of service that reveal *least*: the "no" of a prohibition, the child who sees his father only from behind, the servant who has no existence of his own.

## Nasso (to 3406): the "no" reaches deeper than the "yes"

The range opens on a parable. A lofty soul is not drawn after small things it wants. It is stirred from its essence to push away what it hates. So the prohibitions reach what the positive mitzvot cannot (F05, F10): > שזה מגיע בבחי' רוממות העצמות מה שאינו בגדר המשכה כלל (line 3378)

> ומכל הנ"ל יובן איך שע"י מלי"ת נמשך בחי' המקיף הרחוק, והיינו בחי' גילוי עצמות אוא"ס שאינו בגדר השתל' כלל (line 3378)

The positive mitzvot are the "limbs of the King", the attributes of Zeir Anpin. The prohibitions are rooted in the hidden Keter, the "black fire", and at the first root in the Essence of Or Ein Sof before the tzimtzum (3383). But what they give is knowledge by negation. One knows a thing's essence better by what opposes it, yet this is not revelation (F12): > וממילא ע"י הלאו יודעים יותר עצם מהותו (line 3383)

This has a practical side. Most positive mitzvot do not apply in exile, but every prohibition does. Revealed light can be blocked by concealment; the Essence cannot (F03, F05): > אבל בבחי' פנימי' ועצמות א"ס שלמעלה מבחי' המשכה וגילוי, ע'"ז אינו שייך שום מונע ומעכב (line 3398)

So the prohibitions, with the rabbinic fences, can rouse "great love" even "in the last generations" and "the darkness". "Havayah lights my darkness" needs the Havayah that is above the chain of worlds (3394, F11). Gershon ("driving out") carried the curtains, the surrounding light. Kehat carried the ark. Torah has both merits, because through it the Essence is drawn *inward*: > וע'"י התורה נמשך בבחי' או"פ (line 3401)

## UReisem Oso (3407-3491): why the prohibitions guard, and the cleaving that is renewal

Why do the 365 prohibitions "guard" the 248 positive mitzvot? First, the other side has surrounding lights too ("God made this opposite that"). Yishmael "came out" of Avraham as the dross of holy kindness. By rights he should have run out of life, because only what cleaves to its source endures. Here the range gives its clearest statement of F01. The life in creatures cleaves to its source *by being renewed*: > דההתחדשות ממקורו זהו הדביקות במקורו (line 3419)

> אבל בקליפה וסט"א, מאחר שהן נפרדים, שהפרידן מאחדותו ית'א, היה צ'"ל כלה כח חיותם ושפעם לגמרי (line 3419)

The other side lives only from a distant surrounding light, like the frankincense among the incense spices (3421). It has a "shadow" (the surrounding of chayah) but no "shadow of a shadow", the surrounding of yechidah. The yechidah receives from the Yachid, the Single One (F06): > בחי" יחידה היא שמקבלת מבחי' יחיד", והיינו בחי' ההתקשרות עצמיות דנש"י בעצמות א"ס ב"ה (line 3441)

The other side can also want something enough to die for it, but it could want otherwise. In holiness the bond cannot be otherwise, so a Jew gives his life even while he wants to live (3448). Against the Essence there is no "opposite" at all: > דנגד בחי' זו לא יש לעו"ז, דכ"ז שייך רק לגבי בח הגילויים, אבל לא לגבי בחי' העצמות (line 3453)

The second reason is that the mitzvot are clothed in things that were under kelipat nogah. Doing the mitzvah folds that life into "the holiness of the One God" (3455, F11). Refined things need guarding afterwards, like the penitent (3464). The prohibitions guard because they never clothe themselves in anything. Their light is "inner and essential", with nothing to grip (3472). Torah guards the same way. It is "light" in the singular, "You are One and not in number" (3474), and so "a sin does not extinguish Torah": > להיות התורה היא מבחי' פנימיות ועצמות אוא"ס, שאין שם אחיזה ויניקה כלל (line 3474)

The maamar closes on "I remember the kindness of your youth, your going after Me in the desert". The prohibitions are "after Me", known only by negation. In the future the two will join, so that the essential light shines even in the positive mitzvot (3485, 3488).

## Vayikach Korach (3492-3581): sovev as the will in the hand

This maamar holds the range's longest analysis of filling and surrounding light (F04). The Rashab lists three differences, and all three have one root: - The filling light is revealed and grasped by what it fills; the surrounding light acts hidden (3506). - The surrounding light is equal from top to bottom; the filling light makes "above and below" (3515). - The surrounding light is one simple radiance; the filling light divides into levels (3515).

> דאור הממלא הוא מה שנתצמצם ונגבל לפ"ע העולמות (line 3515)

The model is the soul. Nefesh, ruach and neshamah are clothed in liver, heart and brain as light in vessels. The brain's temperament shapes the mind (3517-3531). The will, though, has no organ. The hand moves at once. Food does not strengthen the will, yet the will adds life to the whole body or takes it away. The Rashab calls this "two opposites in one subject" (F09) and explains it as a second kind of bond: > שהעצם נמשך ונמצא כאן, אבל הוא בהעלם (line 3542)

The image is a man and his garment. He holds it and moves it, and it has no hold on him: > וז"ע ההמשכה שבבחי' מקיף, שהוא נמצא ומתלבש בהמשכתו, ותופס בהדבר לפעול בו כו והוא אינו נתפס בהדבר (line 3544)

Above, this is "He grasps all worlds and none grasps Him". The same will governs "the feet of Asiyah in this world": > דאיהו תפיס בכולהו עלמין ולית מאן דתפיס בי' כללי: (line 3552)

> ולית אתר פנוי מיני*, שהוא בכל מקום בשוה ממש (line 3560)

Korach's error follows from this. He argued that a garment all of blue (the far surrounding, the true Keter) needs no tzitzit (the near surrounding). But the far surrounding never shines inwardly by itself: > דמבחי' מקיף הרחוק עצמו אין מזה המשכה בבחי' הפנימיות, וכנ"ל דבחי' עצמות הוא נבדל בערך, שאינו מאיר בבחי' הפנימיות כלל (line 3575)

The Essence becomes inner only through the positive mitzvot, through Aharon's "hair" (3579). A "house full of books" still needs a mezuzah: learning must lead to doing, and more finely to bitul in study (3575, F08).

## Az Yashir (3582-3695): mediators, contractions, and the dwelling below

Creation as such is no wonder. The wonder is the enlivening (F02, F01): > דהנה באמת אין ערוך כלל וכלל בין הנבראים לגבי עצמות אוא"ס (line 3598)

> וע"ז יפלא, איך יתלבש אוא"ס הבלתי בע"ג בנבראים בע'"ג להחיותם (line 3598)

Two mediators solve it. Zeir Anpin is "the last level within Ein Sof", measuring the light into attributes. Malchus, the source of time, is the source of creatures (3600-3609). The "Essence of Or Ein Sof" has none of these attributes, "for He is not a man" (3600). Long parables of teacher and student, heart and speech, and intellect and attribute (3621-3673) set out three or four contractions at each step. These lines are structural, and the unity shows only at their ends. Creatures can receive only from speech, "for if they received from the attributes of Atzilus... they would be nullified out of their existence" (3636). And the light joins the finite only through the union of the two: > וע"י בחי' יחוד זו"נ, ע"י שניהם, נמשך ומתחבר אור המאציל בנבראים, ויש להם חיבור, ובלעדם אין ערוך כלל וכלל ביניהם (line 3638)

Face-to-face, the light passes "as from vessel to vessel", changing place but not essence (3675). That is the definition of the dwelling below (F07): > אבל ענין הדירה בתחתונים הוא להיות גילוי בחי' עצמות אוא"ס למטה כמו למעלה (line 3680)

> דכל כוונת התהוות העולמות הוא להיות לו דירה בתחתונים, וזה נעשה ע"י התורה (line 3680)

Torah is Chochmah, and "no thought grasps Him". So the Torah draws Or Ein Sof only through Israel's simple will, "to You, Havayah, I lift my soul". Sea water becomes a well of living water by breaking through earth. In the same way the soul, which had no such will above, breaks through the body's darkness (3680, F11, F06): > והוא בוקע חשך הגוף ונה"ב להיות בבחי' רצון פשוט לה' לבד (line 3680)

## Ki Merosh Tzurim (3696-3788): distance for the sake of nearness; Israel "one nation"

The ascents of Malchus become a map of prayer. The lowest ascent is the "point under Yesod": bitterness at one's distance from God, only "the absence of the spreading of the yesh", seeing God "from afar" (3728). Yet this is how the highest ascent comes (F09, F03): > כי כך היא המדה בכ"מ, שמסיבת תכלית הריחוק יבוא תכלית שלימות הקירוב (line 3733)

The range's most surprising claim follows. Even contemplation of "all before Him is as nothing" is seeing "from behind" (F12): > וכמו"כ מה שהולך בעקבות אביו בההשגה וההתבוננות דכולא קמי' כלא ובהפלאה ורוממות ה', שזהו רק הראי' מאחוריו לבד (line 3735)

Through the force of this "going after", love grows "above, in the Essence of Or Ein Sof", who turns His face. Then in the Amidah comes "bitul of existence itself" and the revelation of the Essence within the souls (3740). Prayer is "behind" because all contemplation is of the radiance, of "Your kingship" (3753). Two kinds of exaltation follow. "The holy King" is the Essence, beyond any relation to worlds. "The King of judgment" is kingship over worlds. Souls are rooted in the first and worlds in the second (3753, 3762). The world has no worth of its own for His will to rest on. It stands on Israel and the Patriarchs, who draw "the oneness of His Essence" into the earth (F07, F06): > והיינו שממשיכים בחי' אחדות עצמותו בארץ, שז'"ע דירה (line 3772)

> דהנה, אלקים הוא בחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס (line 3772)

## Titen Emes (3789-3878): the nearer, the more nothing; the radiance and the thirst

This maamar gives the range's two finest statements of F02. First, "before Him" is exact: the nearer one is, the more one is nothing. In the kav, light cleaving to its source feels its source: > לזאת נרגש שם איך שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו (line 3795)

> שהוא יותר קמי' ה"ה יותר כלא (line 3795)

Beriah is "darkness" from too much light, the first *yesh* where the divine *ayin* still shines (3792, 3795). Second, "Single, life of the worlds, King": > שהוא יחיד ומיוחד לבדו כמו קודם שנבה"ע, שאינו בגדר עלמין כלל, ומה שהוא חי העולמים, הנה החיות המתפשט בעולמות אינו אלא בבחי' מלך (line 3819)

Kingship joins king and people through distance, so only "a radiance of a radiance" comes through (3809). The contemplation of prayer is exactly this. The root of all worlds is a radiance "that does not touch His Essence at all" (3821). Seeing that brings thirst: > שע"י ההתבוננות בתפלה איך שכל ההתהוות וכל הגילוים הכל הוא בחי' זיו והארה לבד, הבאה ע"י ריבוי צמצומים מאד, ולכן אני הוי' לא שניתייי כלל (line 3829)

The Rashab is careful. One does not contemplate the Essence itself: > לא שעבודתו היא בבחי' עצמות אוא"ס, להתבונן ולידע בחי' הפלאת העצמות ורוממות האוא"ס (line 3831)

"Yaakov My servant" works with the hiding Name Elokim. His work is toil, stray thoughts and refining the animal soul, because the concealment gives the animal soul room (3839, 3847). "Israel" has the Name Havayah revealed. He prays like on Shabbat, "as a spark to the torch" (3849). He sings, for "the soul's powers gather into the inner point of the heart" (3857). His Torah draws and does not need to refine. Rashbi drew "the Essence of Or Ein Sof into all the worlds" as he studied (3863). "Give truth to Yaakov" means giving him the truth above the chain, Or Ein Sof above Atzilus (3877).

## UMikneh Rav (3879-3942): the son and the servant

The son serves by nature because he is "part of God above": > וכ"ז הוא מפני שהוא חלק אלקה ממעל ממש"י, משו"ז יבין אמיתית הדברים כמו שהן (line 3888)

> כ"ז הוא מפני שנש"י הן מבחי' עצמות א"ס, נמשך בהם האור תמיד לעוררם (line 3888)

The servant serves under a yoke, because of the body. What makes his yoke bearable is bitul (F08): > ה'"ה מתבטל בכל עצם מהותו, כאלו אינו במציאות בעולם לעצמו כלל (line 3894)

"Serving Elokim" means going past one's nature: the hundred-and-first review (Tanya ch. 15), the miser's charity. It means repairing the Name Elokim, which equals "nature" in gematria, by subduing a nature that stays unchanged (F11): > שלא נהפך טבעיות שלו כלל, רק שמכניע הלב ועוצר רוחו וכובש יצרו בע"כ, שנק' לאכפי' לסט"א לבד, יש בזה נח'"ר גדול למעלה (line 3935)

Reuven and Gad were souls of the lower chariot, servants who chose to be shepherds. The maamar says (3881) that the servant has an advantage over the son, but its explanation lies past line 3942.

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The prohibitions reach the Essence; the positive mitzvot reach the light.** Tanya ch. 4 and 23 speak of the 248 mitzvot as "limbs of the King" and of Torah and mitzvot as His will and wisdom. Here the 365 prohibitions, from Yud-Heh and the far surrounding, reach higher than the 248 (3378, 3573). They give only knowledge by negation (3383), which is why they alone still bind fully in exile (3398).
- **Surrounding light explained by the will.** Tanya ch. 51 uses the soul in the body, most present in the brain, for the filling light. The Rashab adds the other half: the will, present in essence in every limb, equal everywhere, grasping without being grasped (3535-3560).
- **"All before Him is as nothing" is not the summit of prayer.** Shaar HaYichud 3-6 and Tanya ch. 33 set this contemplation at the center. Here it is "seeing from behind" (3733, 3735), and all contemplation is of the radiance (3753, 3821). The goal is thirst for the Essence that the contemplation cannot reach (3829, 3831).
- **"Before Him" is a condition.** The nothingness is felt only where light cleaves to its source; "the more before Him, the more nothing" (3792, 3795).
- **Renewal is cleaving.** Shaar HaYichud 1-2 says creation is renewed every moment. Here the renewal itself *is* the creatures' cleaving to their source, and that cleaving is why they endure while the kelipah should have died out (3419).
- **The dwelling below is the Essence as it is above.** Tanya ch. 36 speaks of the revelation of Or Ein Sof below. The Rashab defines it through Malchus receiving face-to-face without changing the light (3680), and he makes it depend on Israel's simple will as well as on Torah (3680, 3765, 3772).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **The footnotes.** 321 of 565 lines are the editor's apparatus, and 310 of them are pure references.
- **Long technical runs.** The anatomy of limbs and will (3517-3547), the chain of contractions from intellect to speech (3621-3673) and the four ascents of Malchus (3698-3726) are structural. They build the argument and state no unity until their ends. Most have one or two faces or none.
- **Service without a stated tie.** Much of UMikneh Rav (3890-3938) is the ethics of the servant. Only where it says the servant has "no existence of his own" (3894, 3896, 3914, 3927) or names *iskafya* (3935) is it stated as F08 or F11. The rest is structural F08.
- **No readings.** Every tie in the range is either the text's own or structural. The Tanya comparisons in the section above are ours, and they are kept to this report and out of the `how` fields.
- **Who is the "son"?** At 3888 the Rashab first calls all Jewish souls "sons". He then speaks of "these souls" whose Chochmah and Binah mirror Atzilus and are "entirely separate from the powers of other souls". Tanya ch. 2 gives the "part of God above" to every Jew. It is unclear here whether the grasp of Or Ein Sof belongs to all souls or to a class, so the entry records only what the line says.
- **OCR damage.** Page headers are fused into text lines (3421, 3453, 3504, 3598, 3627, 3680, 3713, 3733, 3751, 3772). Stray "י" marks, asterisks and numerals sit inside words ("מאחדותו ית'א", "כלאיי"). A slash replaces an apostrophe at 3821. At 3441 the OCR has "בחי"" with a double mark. Quotes keep all of these.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Is creation from nothing understood?** In the previous range (2603) the Rashab says how creation from nothing works "is not understood by us". Here he says creation itself is no wonder, because He is all-powerful; the wonder is how the infinite is clothed in the finite to give it life (3598). The two emphases stand side by side.
- **Korach against Aharon.** Korach's position (the far surrounding suffices, 3575, 3579) is stated fairly before it is rejected: the Essence does not shine inward except through the positive mitzvot.
- **Yaakov or Israel?** The maamar ranks Israel (son, revealed Havayah, no toil) above Yaakov (servant, Elokim, toil) (3849, 3877). Then it says "in truth there is a great advantage in Yaakov My servant" (3881-3882). The resolution lies past this range.
- **Rashbi and the other Tannaim.** Their study raised the physical through halachah. His drew Or Ein Sof down even through the revealed Torah (3863).
- **Self-sacrifice: nature or will?** The text says a Jew gives his life because he cannot be separated from God, even when he wants to live (3448). The Rebbe's footnote qualifies this. It holds at the moment of actual self-sacrifice, when the yechidah is revealed. Recalling self-sacrifice to resist the inclination is a matter of *wanting* (3450-3451).
- **How many ascents of Malchus?** The text counts three, with "equal in stature" as the third. The Mitteler Rebbe's text, cited in the footnote, puts "equal in stature" above face-to-face as a fourth, and the Etz Chaim counts four (3597).
- **The union of ZuN.** Likkutei Torah ties the union of voice and speech to Tiferes. Here even the flow from Netzach-Hod-Yesod to Malchus counts as the union, and the Rebbe's footnote reconciles the two without erasing the difference (3635).
- **Positive mitzvot in exile.** The positive mitzvot mostly lapse in exile because revealed light can be blocked, while the prohibitions do not (3398). The blue thread is not used today for the same reason (3488). The Rashab states this as a reason, not a ruling.
