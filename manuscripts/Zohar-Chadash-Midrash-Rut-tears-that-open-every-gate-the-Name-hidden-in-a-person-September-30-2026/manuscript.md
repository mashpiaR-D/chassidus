## What this range is

The Midrash on Ruth is the most human part of the Zohar Chadash. It reads the Book of Ruth twice over: once as the story of the soul (Naomi) and its parts (Ruth the intellectual soul, Orpah the animal soul, Elimelech the soul of the soul), and once as the Shechinah in exile. Around that it gathers many short stories. A sage hears a voice in the field on the mother bird and weeps for joy. A sick man cries and laughs on Shabbat because he saw the angel of death and the Shechinah together. A butcher's son is taught Torah and his father is freed from Gehinnom. Rabbi Perachia buries a stranger and is taken to see Eden. The node closes with ten rules for the table, grace after meals, midnight, the Shema, and the soul given back each morning.

## The unity of G-d

> כל מה שברא הקדוש ברוך הוא בעולמו, לא ברא אלא ליקרא דיליה (ZC Midrash Rut 2, line 3319)
> *All that the Holy One created in His world, He created only for His honor.*

**The Name hidden in a person.** The four letters of the Name are in the soul: yud awakens fear, the first heh teshuvah, the vav Torah, the last heh good deeds. Then:

> כמו שהקדוש ברוך הוא נסתר בעולמו, כך הוא זה השם נסתר באדם (ZC Midrash Rut 424, line 3741)
> *As the Holy One is hidden in His world, so this Name is hidden in a person.*

This is one of the most useful unity lines in the Zohar Chadash for the guide. The same hiddenness that makes God hard to find in the world is also inside the person.

> והוא אור אחד בלא פירודא (ZC Midrash Rut 154, line 3471)
> *And it is one light without separation.* (The soul's lights spread from the yud and remain one.)

**The world stands on His Name sealed in truth.**

> שאין העולם מתקיים, אלא על שמו החתום באמת (ZC Midrash Rut 50, line 3367)
> *For the world stands only on His Name sealed in truth.* (A false oath shakes it.)

> מקום כל העולם כלו, היא נקודה אחת (ZC Midrash Rut 51, line 3368)
> *The place of the whole world is one point* (on which all stands).

**Witness twice a day.** Jacob is set by the throne as witness:

> הוי סהיד עלן, דאנן מיחדי שמיה (ZC Midrash Rut 68, line 3385)
> *Be witness for us that we unify His Name* (that is why we say "Hear, Israel").

**His kindness is in everything.** After a hard question on why the righteous suffer, Rabbi Yudai was shown a verse in a dream and stopped asking:

> דהא חסד ה' בכולא אשתכח (ZC Midrash Rut 681, line 3998)
> *For the kindness of the Lord is found in everything.*

**The alef that waited.** The letters came before God asking to begin creation; the alef held back so as not to take the bet's gift, and God said:

> בך אני מתעלה, להקרא בך שמי אחד (ZC Midrash Rut 633, line 3950)
> *Through you I am raised, to be called by you "My Name is One".*

## States of consciousness

**Tears (a new state).** This node has the fullest teaching on tears in the whole Zohar. Prayer, crying out, and tears; and of them:

> דהא בדמעין אזלא לבא ורעותא וכל גופא (ZC Midrash Rut 228, line 3545)
> *For in tears go the heart and the will and the whole body.*

Not all tears. Tears of anger and tears that hand a friend over to judgment do not enter:

> אלא דמעין דצלותא ודתשובה (ZC Midrash Rut 232, line 3549)
> *Only tears of prayer and of teshuvah* (and of asking out of distress; they break through the heavens).

> ותרעין דדמעין לא ננעלו (ZC Midrash Rut 233, line 3550)
> *And the gates of tears were not locked.*

And the highest gate in heaven, seen by Rabbi Nechunya:

> וכל בעלי דמעה (ZC Midrash Rut 362, line 3679)
> *And all those who weep* (from the pain the nations cause; and "all those who return", all enter by this gate).

We propose (ours): **Z-S20 The tear that goes up.** Weeping in which the heart, the will and the whole body go toward God. The Zohar Chadash sets it apart from angry tears and from tears against another. It is not despair. In the map it sits between S73 (exile inside) and S77 (the lower return) as the moment the one turns into the other.

**Crying and laughing at once.** Rabbi Kruspedai, near death on Shabbat, wept and then laughed:

> וחייכית, כד חמינא שכינתא דלא שבקי ליה (ZC Midrash Rut 245, line 3562)
> *And I laughed when I saw the Shechinah not letting him [the angel of death take me].*

We propose (ours): **Z-S21 Held at the edge.** Fear and joy at the same time, because the danger is real and so is the Presence that holds you. It gives the guide language for people facing illness.

**Surrounded from all sides.** When a person serves with his lower soul (in mitzvot) and his spirit (in Torah), from above:

> וסחרא ליה מכל סטרין (ZC Midrash Rut 351, line 3668)
> *And it surrounds him from every side* (a holy awakening, called neshamah).

**Held by His mercy, even the gnat (S54 Compassion).**

> אפילו על יתושא זעירא בעלמא, רחמין דיליה על כלא (ZC Midrash Rut 103, line 3420)
> *Even on the smallest gnat in the world, His mercy is on all.*

> תבירי לבא, תבירי חילי (ZC Midrash Rut 104, line 3421)
> *Broken of heart, broken of strength* (on them His mercy is awakened).

> מרחוק ה' נראה לי ואהבת עולם אהבתיך (ZC Midrash Rut 93, line 3410)
> *"From afar the Lord appeared to me; with eternal love I have loved you."* (Even the prophets who saw from afar were loved.)

> יש רחוק ונתקרב, וקרוב ונתרחק (ZC Midrash Rut 94, line 3411)
> *There is one far who came near, and one near who grew far.*

**Enough.** Rabbi Bun, after hearing the voice on the mother bird:

> ואלמלא לא אתינא לעלמא אלא למשמע דא, דיי (ZC Midrash Rut 106, line 3423)
> *Had I come into the world only to hear this, it would be enough.*

## Meditation and practice

**The heart is what He wants (ties to 16 and 20 own words).**

> וקודשא בריך הוא לבא בעי (ZC Midrash Rut 221, line 3538)
> *The Holy One wants the heart.*

> ורעותא דבר נש, חשיב קמיה מכל קרבנין (ZC Midrash Rut 222, line 3539)
> *A person's will is dearer before Him than all the offerings* (of the whole world).

> לית תרעא בכל אינון רקיעין, דלא פתח לגביה (ZC Midrash Rut 222, line 3539)
> *There is no gate in all the heavens that is not opened to him* (once he sets his will on teshuvah).

> אין קרוב לפני המקום, כלבו של אדם (ZC Midrash Rut 223, line 3540)
> *Nothing is as near before God as a person's heart.*

**Entrust the soul at night (24 evening look-back).**

> ומפקידים רוחם ביד אדון עולם (ZC Midrash Rut 359, line 3676)
> *And they entrust their spirit into the hand of the Master of the world* (every night when they lie down).

> צריך האדם להפקיד נשמתו ליוצרו (ZC Midrash Rut 655, line 3972)
> *A person must entrust his soul to his Maker* (with a verse of mercy, "Into Your hand I entrust my spirit"). Though it owes much, He gives it back.

We propose (ours): **Z-T20 The night deposit.** At the end of the look-back, hand the soul to God as a pledge with "Into Your hand I entrust my spirit", knowing He returns it though it is in debt. In the morning, receive it back (see 4051 below). It completes Z-T12.

**First bless Him, then greet anyone.** In the morning the soul waits in the nostrils and settles only after one blesses God:

> אסיר ליה לאיניש לברכא לחבריה, עד דמברך למאריה (ZC Midrash Rut 734, line 4051)
> *A person may not greet his fellow until he has blessed his Master.*

> צריך להתנהג במידותיו של הקדוש ברוך הוא (ZC Midrash Rut 656, line 3973)
> *One must conduct oneself by the attributes of the Holy One.*

> כל אבר ואבר נוטל תיבה אחת לעצמו, ומתרפא בו (ZC Midrash Rut 124, line 3441)
> *Each limb takes one word for itself and is healed by it.* (Pairs with Z-T17.)

> ומכוין בפרשיות אלין בכל יומא (ZC Midrash Rut 717, line 4034)
> *And he intends in these sections every day* (and so unifies as he should).

**The table before the King.** Ten rules before grace after meals. Set the table:

> בגין דיתיב למיכל קמי מלכא (ZC Midrash Rut 541, line 3858)
> *For he sits to eat before the King.*

When one washes and blesses, the good measure says:

> עבדו של מקום אתה (ZC Midrash Rut 554, line 3871)
> *You are the servant of God.*

Give the poor the best of what you want:

> מכל שופרי דאיהו תאיב (ZC Midrash Rut 569, line 3886)
> *From the finest of what he himself desires.*

> דהא הקדוש ברוך הוא אתהני מההיא מזונא דאיהו יהיב למסכנא (ZC Midrash Rut 569, line 3886)
> *For the Holy One has pleasure from the food he gives the poor.*

> ואצטריך לברך בחדוא, וברעותא דלבא (ZC Midrash Rut 596, line 3913)
> *And one must bless in joy and with the will of the heart.*

We propose (ours): **Z-T21 The King's table.** Eat as one sitting before the King: set the table even when there is little, wash, give the best piece to someone in need, eat slowly, say a word of Torah, and bless with joy. It turns the most daily act into a place of meeting. It links to F07 (dwelling below).

> כדי שידבקו תמיד בהקדוש ברוך הוא (ZC Midrash Rut 453, line 3770)
> *So that they cleave always to the Holy One* (marked with mitzvot sitting, walking, lying down, rising).

> שלא יהא האדם מצוי בלא תורה ובלא מצות, אפילו שעה אחת (ZC Midrash Rut 456, line 3773)
> *That a person not be found without Torah and without mitzvot even one hour.*

**Learn on the road.** Two sages see a man coming and say:

> שכינתא גבן, קום (ZC Midrash Rut 451, line 3768)
> *The Shechinah is with us; get up.*

> בכל מקום שיש דברי תורה, שכינה שם (ZC Midrash Rut 122, line 3439)
> *Wherever there are words of Torah, the Shechinah is there.*

**A Shabbat home.** When Shabbat comes and the house is not ready:

> אין דירה זו מישראל (ZC Midrash Rut 462, line 3779)
> *This dwelling is not of Israel* (says the bride; there must be a lamp lit and a table set).

> שאפילו חרב חדה מונחת על צוארו של אדם אל ימנע עצמו מן הרחמים (ZC Midrash Rut 318, line 3635)
> *Even if a sharp sword rests on a person's neck, let him not hold back from asking for mercy.*

## The big questions

> אין דנין את האדם לפי צערו (ZC Midrash Rut 20, line 3337)
> *A person is not judged by his pain.* (One who gives charity to a poor man who spoke against God makes peace between him and his Maker.)

> ואינו נתפס על צערו (ZC Midrash Rut 25, line 3342)
> *And he is not held to account for [words spoken in] his pain.* (Said of Job.)

**Why was I sent down?** Lech lecha, read as the soul's journey:

> ומבית, דא שכינתא. אביך, דא הקדוש ברוך הוא (ZC Midrash Rut 13, line 3330)
> *"From the house": this is the Shechinah. "Your father": this is the Holy One.* (The soul leaves Eden, the womb, and the house of its Father to come to the low world.)

> תרין קטרין טבין יהיב הקדוש ברוך הוא בבר נש (ZC Midrash Rut 349, line 3666)
> *The Holy One gave a person two good knots* (nefesh and ruach: the nefesh for mitzvot, the ruach for Torah; above them neshamah, the power of teshuvah).

> יראה, ואהבה, תורה, ומצוה (ZC Midrash Rut 353, line 3670)
> *Fear, love, Torah and mitzvah* (these come from the soul of the soul).

> בשני יצריך, ביצר הטוב וביצר הרע. הכל צריך כאחד (ZC Midrash Rut 151, line 3468)

> בהם האדם מכבד לקונו (ZC Midrash Rut 186, line 3503)
> *By them a person honors his Maker* (two fires every person has).

> איש מסכן חכם, זה יצר טוב (ZC Midrash Rut 259, line 3576)

> אשרי מי שתלמודו מתקיים בידו (ZC Midrash Rut 412, line 3729)

> והחי יתן אל לבו (ZC Midrash Rut 696, line 4013)

> העניים הם כליו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, והם קרובים אליו (ZC Midrash Rut 113, line 3430)

> שצריך להכניס ראשו לתוך רבים (ZC Midrash Rut 86, line 3403)
> *One should put his head among the many* (not stand apart).

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus agrees deeply with this node. "The Holy One wants the heart" (3538) is Rachmana liba ba'i, a pillar of the Baal Shem Tov's teaching. "As God is hidden in the world, so the Name is hidden in a person" (3741) is the Tanya's "second soul, a part of God above truly" (F06) in the Zohar Chadash's words; the letters of the Name mapped to fear, teshuvah, Torah and good deeds give the soul's powers exactly. "Fear, love, Torah and mitzvah" from the soul of the soul (3670) is the Tanya's teaching that love and fear are the wings that lift Torah and mitzvot. The two inclinations both needed (3468) is Tanya chapter 9 and the service "with both your inclinations".

Where Chassidus adds: the Zohar Chadash says "the gates of tears were not locked" (3550). Chassidus warns that tears must not become bitterness or sadness, and that after the tears must come joy. The Zohar Chadash hints at this with Rabbi Kruspedai's laugh (3562). The guide should keep them as a pair.

What the guide should take (ours): Z-S20 and Z-S21; Z-T20 and Z-T21; "the Name hidden in a person" (3741) as a core unity line; lines 3337 and 3342 for anyone ashamed of what they said in pain; 3567 (always forgiving) as a practical key; 3430 for anyone who is poor; 3928-3932 for anyone who feels God is far in hard times. Leave out the gentile body and soul claims (3482-3492), the demons, sorcery and women's witchcraft passages (3597-3625), the claims about converts' taint (3656) and gentiles and Torah (3642-3645), the Gehinnom chamber list (3508-3525, keep only the moral list), the procreation threats (3985-3989), and the halachic material on women and zimun (3909-3911).
