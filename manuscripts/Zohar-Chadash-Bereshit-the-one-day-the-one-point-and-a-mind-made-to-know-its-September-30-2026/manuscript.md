## What this range is

The Zohar Chadash is the "new Zohar": pieces that did not make it into the printed three volumes, gathered in Safed and printed later. Its Bereshit section reads the six days of creation more than once. First it reads them as letters and points (the yud, the heh, the alef), then as the ten sefirot hidden in a nut, then, in the Midrash HaNe'elam, as a teaching on the soul and on how a person comes to know God. It moves through the angels' night song, the hidden light, the souls in Eden, Shabbat, the making of Adam, his sin and his return, and ends with Rabbi Shimon ill and judged by God Himself.

Three things run through all of it. God is one and everything else is as nothing before Him. The human being is made to know Him and to cleave to Him. And He waits for the person to return.

## The unity of G-d

**No day without night, and nothing apart.** The first lines read the days of creation as one fabric:

> דהא לית יומא בלא ליליא, ולית ליליא בלא יומא (ZC Bereshit 6, line 6)
> *There is no day without night and no night without day.*

> יומא קדמאה אזיל עם כולהו, וכולהו ביה בגין לאחזאה דלאו בהו פירודא, וכולא חד (ZC Bereshit 7, line 7)
> *The first day goes with all of them, and all of them are in it, to show that there is no separation among them, and all is one.*

This is the Zohar's way of saying that the many things of the world do not stand apart. The first day, the root of light, runs through every day after it.

**Everything begins from one point, and what is inside the point is not known.**

> נקודה חדא שירותא מתמן למימני (ZC Bereshit 20, line 20)
> *One point is the beginning; from there one starts to count.*

The line goes on to say that what is inside that point is not known. Later, the letter heh with its dot inside it is read the same way:

> ובגין כך איהי בכלא, וכלא בה (ZC Bereshit 69, line 69)
> *And so she is in everything, and everything is in her.*

**The names are shells; the kernel is hidden.** The book of Rav Hamnuna Sava, quoted here, compares the names of God to a body and to garments. Inside the shells of the nut:

> ומוחא סתימאה לגו בגו, דלא אתיידע כלל (ZC Bereshit 115, line 115)
> *And the hidden kernel is inside, within within, not known at all.*

So one who wants to unify the Name has to know what each name stands for:

> ועל דא מאן דאתי לייחדא שמא קדישא אצטריך למנדע (ZC Bereshit 94, line 94)
> *So one who comes to unify the holy Name must know* (what each of the names rests on).

And he must not break the secrets into pieces:

> אסור לאסגאה לון בפרטא, אלא לחברא כלהו, ולייחדא לון כחדא (ZC Bereshit 147, line 147)
> *It is forbidden to multiply them one by one; rather join them all and unify them together.*

**He and His Name are one; all before Him is as nothing.** The Midrash HaNe'elam says it in Hebrew, in words very close to the later Chassidic formulas:

> מלמד שהוא ושמו אחד הוא (ZC Bereshit 256, line 256)
> *This teaches that He and His Name are one.*

> להודיע שהכל נגדו כאין (ZC Bereshit 234, line 234)
> *To make known that all is as nothing before Him.* (Said of the thousand worlds He made before this one.)

> וכלם לנגדו כאין וכלא חשיבין, והוא היה, והוא הוה, והוא יהיה (ZC Bereshit 265, line 265)
> *All of them are as nothing before Him, counted as nothing; and He was, He is, and He will be.*

> וכלהו כלא חשיבין לקבליה (ZC Bereshit 627, line 627)
> *And all of them are counted as nothing before Him.* (After a chain in which each world draws its light from the one above it, "and the Holy One is over all.")

**Why the second day is not called good.** One of the clearest unity lines in the whole Zohar Chadash:

> להורות כי אין שני לנגדו (ZC Bereshit 369, line 369)
> *To teach that there is no second beside Him.*

A child, Chiya, answers the same question from the other side:

> מפני שאין טוב אלא באחד (ZC Bereshit 373, line 373)
> *Because there is no good except in the One.*

**He holds the world up.** The Midrash HaNe'elam builds a chain from the body to God:

> והגוף מתקיים בנפש, והנפש באויר, והאויר בבורא (ZC Bereshit 318, line 318)
> *The body stands by the soul, the soul by the air, and the air by the Creator.* (It ends with "I made, and I will carry, and I will bear.")

**He is everything, and He renews it all daily.** On "let us make man" in the plural:

> לאחזאה דהוא כולא (ZC Bereshit 660, line 660)
> *To show that He is everything.*

> מחדש קודשא בריך הוא מצפרא עד רמשא (ZC Bereshit 636, line 636)
> *The Holy One renews [new decrees] from morning to evening.* (Said against fools who think things come from the spheres.)

> אין לך כל רגע ורגע שאין הקדוש ברוך הוא משגיח באדם (ZC Bereshit 581, line 581)
> *There is no single moment in which the Holy One is not watching over a person.*

**The Shechinah fills the world.** King David asks that the Presence that fills everything be focused in the Temple:

> ששכינתך שהיא מלאה כל העולם כלו (ZC Bereshit 510, line 510)
> *That Your Shechinah, which fills the whole world entirely* (be gathered into Your house).

**The King and the Queen.** Unity is also union:

> ומלכא בלא מטרוניתא לאו מלכא איהו (ZC Bereshit 189, line 189)
> *A King without the Queen is not a King.*

> כולא ברוחא ולא בגופא (ZC Bereshit 125, line 125)
> *All of it in spirit and not in body.*

## States of consciousness

**The lost sense of apartness (S62 Only Him, S63 The world held).** Lines 7, 234, 265, 369 and 627 give the view from which nothing stands on its own. Line 318 gives the felt version: one's own body held by the soul, the soul by the air, the air by God. This is S63 in the Zohar's words.

**Being seen every moment (S33 Before Whom).** Line 581. Not a general providence but "every single moment".

**Longing that consumes (S13 The unnamed longing).** The soul in Eden longs only for the place it was taken from:

> נכספה וגם כלתה נפשי (ZC Bereshit 767, line 767)
> *My soul longs, and even pines.*

**Renewed every morning.** Each night the soul goes up and comes back new:

> חדשים לבקרים רבה אמונתך (ZC Bereshit 771, line 771)
> *New every morning; great is Your faithfulness.*

We propose this as a state (ours): **Z-S11 Returned new.** Waking as one who was given back, not as one who simply woke. Close to S76 and S84 but not the same.

**The soul that outlasts.** A quiet ground for the fear of death:

> מה הקדוש ברוך הוא קיים, אף היא קיימת (ZC Bereshit 421, line 421)
> *As the Holy One endures, so it [the soul] endures.*

**One below as He is One above.** Why was man made one, alone at first?

> למהוי יחיד בהאי עלמא, כמה דאיהו יחידאי לעילא (ZC Bereshit 393, line 393)
> *To be single in this world, as He is Single above.*

We propose (ours): **Z-S12 Single below.** The inner stance of a person who knows himself as the one place in creation that mirrors the One. Not pride. The same text says all is as nothing before Him. It is closer to F06 (the soul as part of the One) than to any state now on the map.

**Shabbat as the time He is known (S76 Shabbos).** The Midrash HaNe'elam reads "He rested" as "He sat on His throne", and explains:

> אזי היה מי שיכיר את כבודו (ZC Bereshit 720, line 720)
> *Then there was someone to recognize His glory.*

On Shabbat even the wicked in Gehinnom rest, because:

> אין לך רשע מישראל שאין לו מעשים טובים (ZC Bereshit 716, line 716)
> *There is no wicked one in Israel who has no good deeds.*

## Meditation and practice

**Look at the sky (ties to 11 seeing the garment).** Animals do not lift their heads. Nebuchadnezzar was a beast until he looked up:

> עיני לשמיא נטלת, ומנדעי עלי יתיב (ZC Bereshit 424, line 424)
> *I lifted my eyes to heaven, and my knowing came back to me.* (Daniel 4:31, read here as the cure for the beast inside a man.)

The Midrash HaNe'elam describes the intellectual soul doing exactly this as it grows:

> רואה נפלאות הבורא (ZC Bereshit 317, line 317)
> *He sees the wonders of the Creator* (and then works to reach a pure knowing, knowing the whole world hangs on His power).

We propose (ours): **Z-T11 Lifting the eyes.** Stand outside, lift the eyes to the sky, and let the sight of it bring back the knowing that the self forgets. The Zohar Chadash returns to this with David in the wilderness (report 5). It is not a mood of calm. It is looking at His work until one remembers Whose it is.

**The daily look-back (24 evening look-back).** Two Hebrew passages in this node say it outright:

> כמה יש לאדם להרהר במעשיו, ולפקוד אותם בכל יום (ZC Bereshit 529, line 529)
> *How much a person must reflect on his deeds and review them every day.*

> אלא להרהר בעבודתו, ולהדבק בו, ולא ילך אחר ההבל (ZC Bereshit 738, line 738)
> *[He was created only] to reflect on His service, to cleave to Him, and not to go after emptiness.*

**The daily voice (12 returning the mind).** God says to man "I am above and you are below":

> הוי זהיר לאסתכלא ביקרי (ZC Bereshit 740, line 740)
> *Be careful to look at My glory.*

> אוי להם לבריות שאינם רואים את כבודי (ZC Bereshit 743, line 743)
> *Woe to the creatures who do not see My glory.*

**Lengthening the Echad (3 one line; 16 love and awe).** The Shema is to tie the knots of faith "with the will of the heart":

> ולקשרא קשרין דמהימנותא ברעותא דלבא (ZC Bereshit 189, line 189)
> *To tie the knots of faith with the will of the heart.*

> כשיעורא דימליך יתה לעילא ולתתא, ולארבע זוויין דעלמא (ZC Bereshit 195, line 195)
> *As long as it takes to make Him King above and below and to the four corners of the world.*

This is the Tikkunim's Z-T02 (crown Him on six sides in the Shema, part 4 report 1), confirmed here in the Zohar Chadash: while saying Echad, pass the mind up, down, and to the four sides, and crown Him in each. It is an old custom; here it is given as inner work, not as a count of seconds.

**Midnight study.** When one rises at midnight to learn, God tells the righteous in Eden to be quiet:

> דהאי ניחא קדמאי, מכל שירתא ותושבחתא (ZC Bereshit 546, line 546)
> *For this is more pleasing to Me than all the songs and praises* (sung above).

> קול העוסק בתורה (ZC Bereshit 758, line 758)
> *The voice of one who studies Torah* (is sweeter to Him than all song).

**From fear to love (16 love and awe).**

> לעושה המצות ביראה בתחלה, עד שירגיל עצמו לעשות מאהבה (ZC Bereshit 462, line 462)
> *To do the mitzvot from fear at first, until he trains himself to do them from love.*

**The soul's own praise.** The instruments of the Temple are nothing:

> אלא הלול הנשמה (ZC Bereshit 476, line 476)
> *Only the praise of the soul* ("let every soul praise God").

**Not the angel, only Him.** Healing and harm come from Him, and people should not set their hearts on the angel:

> אלא כולא בידיה (ZC Bereshit 536, line 536)
> *Rather, all is in His hand.*

## The big questions

> דהא לא אתא בר נש לעלמא, אלא למילף ולמנדע למאריה (ZC Bereshit 162, line 162)
> *A person came into the world only to learn and to know his Master.*

> ויהא תאובתיה ורעותיה לעילאין קדישין (ZC Bereshit 671, line 671)
> *And his longing and his will should be for the holy things above.*

**What is my work in the world?** God made Adam last, and said:

> מכאן ואילך אתה תשתדל בה (ZC Bereshit 289, line 289)
> *From here on, you will labor in it.* ("Until now I was working; from now, you.") The image of God is to go on building the world.

> אין קץ הגלות נתלה אלא בתשובה (ZC Bereshit 351, line 351)
> *The end of the exile depends only on teshuvah.*

**What if I have fallen?** Adam sinned, Shabbat protected him, and when it left:

> כיון דנפק שבת, והרהר בתשובה, ארים ליה קודשא בריך הוא (ZC Bereshit 219, line 219)
> *When Shabbat left and he thought of teshuvah, the Holy One lifted him up.*

> פשט ידו וקבלם, ומחל להם מיד (ZC Bereshit 819, line 819)
> *He stretched out His hand, received them, and forgave them at once.*

> כל המתודה חטאתיו ומתבייש מהן מוחלין לו מיד (ZC Bereshit 836, line 836)
> *Whoever confesses his sins and is ashamed of them is forgiven at once.*

And the accuser knows this, and so he comes at exactly that moment:

> אלא בשעה שהאדם רוצה לחזור למוטב (ZC Bereshit 857, line 857)
> *[He accuses] only at the hour a person wants to return to the good.*

That line is worth a great deal to someone who feels resistance rise just as he tries to change.

**Is God harsh?** Rabbi Shimon, ill, is judged not by a court but by God Himself, and says:

> נטי לזכותא תדיר, והוא כוליה צד רחמנותא (ZC Bereshit 794, line 794)
> *He always leans toward merit; He is entirely the side of mercy.*

**What joy is there in learning?** The hermits in the caves eat grass all week and say:

> כד נהירא לן שמעתא (ZC Bereshit 358, line 358)
> *When a teaching lights up for us* (from great joy that day is to us "it was good").

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus agrees with this node almost line for line on unity. "All is as nothing before Him" (234, 265, 627) is the Tanya's "kulo kamei kela chashiv" in the Zohar's own words; the Tanya quotes that Zohar phrase from the printed Zohar, and here it appears three times in the Zohar Chadash. "No second beside Him" (369) and "no good except in the One" (373) are Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah in seed form. "He renews from morning to evening" (636) is the teaching of constant renewal. The body held by the soul, the soul by air, the air by God (318) is F02 and F10 as one chain.

Where Chassidus goes further: the Zohar Chadash says all is "as nothing" before Him. Chassidus adds that the world's existence is itself a concealment, and that the nothingness is the truth seen from His side. The Zohar Chadash does not draw that out. It stays with "as nothing" and "He is everything".

What the guide should take (ours): the daily look-back (529, 738) and the morning voice (743) as a pair; the lifted eyes (424, 317) as a practice for someone who feels like a beast of routine; line 857 for someone whose resistance flares when they try to change; and line 794 for someone who fears God's judgment. Leave out the long letter mysticism and the gentile soul claims at 413 and 425-432; they are not universal.
