# Subjects of Chassidus — for the Unity Index

**Only One research** · September 29, 2026 · early draft 0.1 · Result [001](../../CONTENTS.md#001) · manuscript 3 of 4 · [Cite](CITATION.bib) · [Quote check](../../verification/catalogue.md#subjects-of-chassidus-for-the-unity-index-september-29-2026)

---
Each subject below is to be traced back to one idea: the unity of God. This file is the subject list only. The passages come in the next stage.

**197 subjects in 16 clusters.** Tie to the unity: stated 129 · reading 60 · thin 8.

**How the list was made.** It draws on the 22 pillars of `Chassidus Concepts/` (each README and architecture, with their rooms and fault lines), `00_CONCEPT_MAP.md`, `00_ATLAS.md` and `00_LIBRARY_BY_CONCEPT.md`, and on the four themes of the Sefer HaArachim synthesis (Achdus, Ohr/Tzimtzum, Osiyos, Human Avodah). It was checked against the contents of the books themselves: the chapters of Tanya, the parsha sections of Likkutei Torah and Torah Or, the list of mitzvos in Derech Mitzvosecha, the maamar headings of Hemshech Ayin Beis, and the subject index (מפתח ענינים) of Likkutei Sichos, where the most frequent entries include תשובה, ביטול, שבת, צדקה, מסירות נפש, גאולה, אהבת ישראל, חינוך, רפואה, בחירה, אומות העולם, טבע, דירה בתחתונים and שינה.

**Search terms.** They are unvocalized, and are matched letters-only (nikud, punctuation and spacing ignored), as `find_he.py` does. Each term was counted over the 160 non-OCR works (Shulchan Aruch, responsa and source-reference volumes excluded). Terms that were too short and matched inside other words (for example א"י, ע"ס, ז"א, ס"א, ק"ש, נה"ב, מח"ז, תש"ר) were replaced. Some terms are generic (שכל, דעת, זמן, שבת, אמת). Use them together with a more specific term of the same subject.

**The tie to the unity**, marked for each subject: - **stated**: a text in the corpus makes the link itself. Where a ref is followed by *(verified)*, the Hebrew was read in context and returned FOUND from `find_he.py verify` at that line in `Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt`. Other refs point to the rooms of the pillar architectures, where the earlier work cites them. They have not been re-verified in this pass. - **reading**: the link is our reading of how the subject follows from the unity. - **thin**: the tie is weak or would be forced. We say so rather than stretch it.

Disagreements between the Rebbeim and the schools are kept in the notes; they are not smoothed.

No passages from OCR works are cited here. When passages are drawn from any work marked "(scans, OCR)", they must carry `"ocr": true`.
