## How the range builds the idea

The range covers three different kinds of writing. The last chapters of **Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah** (841-883) are the Tanya's most technical account of the unity. They define sovev and memale, the simple unity of Essence and attributes, and creation as letters of divine speech coming down to the stone. **Iggeret HaTeshuvah** (887-1014) then takes the same scheme, the letters of the Name Havayah, and reads sin and return through it. Sin cuts the rope that ties the soul to the Name. Return puts the lower hei back in its place and the soul back into "a wondrous union". The first eight letters of **Iggeret HaKodesh** (1018-1120) are pastoral. They ground humility, charity and prayer in the same point, and they contain two of the plainest statements of the Essence in the whole book (1090, 1101).

**SHVE 7 end (841-848).** Sovev is not spatial. His knowledge "מקפת כל נברא ונברא בפועל ממש" (841), because the knowing is the creature's existence. Memale is the life contracted to the creature's measure (842-843). The stone passage gives the book's own G01: "even" comes from the name Ban plus an aleph, a very high name brought down through contractions, "וזו היא נפש הדומם, המחיה ומהוה אותו מאין ליש בכל רגע" (847). The chapter states F01 and F04 together. The source "could have spread without end" (844), so the limit is His own act (F03), and the gloss adds that "He and His attributes are one".

**SHVE 8-9 (849-861).** This is the yachid precision in the Alter Rebbe's words. With the Rambam, Essence, will, wisdom and every attribute are "אחדות פשוטה ממש" (849). The unity cannot be grasped, because we picture attributes as they are in ourselves (850). Lines 851-854 build a ladder in the soul, action to speech to thought to middah to intellect, each "as nothing" to the next. Line 855 turns the ladder on God: chochmah "היא סוף מעשה אצלו". It is not even correct to say He "cannot be grasped", because He is not in the category of grasping at all (858). Chapter 9 closes on the kept disagreement in F12: "והנה, אין לנו עסק בנסתרות" (861). What is revealed to us is to *believe* that He and His attributes are one, "the mystery of faith". This is the Alter Rebbe's side of the dispute. Nothing in this range lets us soften it toward the Rayatz's "to know".

**SHVE 10 (862-867).** The sun analogy is licensed only because "the Torah speaks in human language". A ray in its source "אין לו שום מציאות כלל בפני עצמו" (862), which is the paradigm of bitul. The analogy is then limited explicitly: the attributes are united with Him in that way "and more", but "not literally in that way" (863). Here G06 is done by the text itself. The attributes carry their names only relative to creatures (864). Eliyahu adds that He has "no known justice or mercy" (867). The days of creation show chesed and gevurah included in each other (865-866). The firmament conceals the upper waters "so the dry land would appear and man on it to serve God", the text's own form of G03.

**SHVE 11-12 (868-882).** The Utterances are "utterances" only relative to creatures (868). Hidden and revealed worlds correspond to thought and speech (872). Divine speech is above created wisdom, since man with his intellect was made *by* it (873). A word draws a general life that joins its letters and outweighs them (876), which is the Tanya's own G04. The part ends by taking the one speech down to "אבן" and "עפר" as substitutions of substitutions (882). The book's systematic statement of unity thus ends at the stone, the lowest point. Most of these lines are stated, and the rest are structural steps of the letter theory.

**Iggeret HaTeshuvah 1-3 (887-918): mostly "none".** The halachic frame covers the baraita of atonement, fasting versus return, the Arizal's fast counts, the three-fold count, the ban on harmful fasts and redemption by charity. Twenty-one of these lines are marked "none", and the tie should not be forced. Only a few lines touch the idea. The gloss at 888 says a positive mitzvah draws the radiance of Or Ein Sof (F10). Line 893 defines teshuvah as ceasing "to rebel against His kingship". Line 905 calls fasting a means to reveal the supernal will. These lines are marked "reading" or "stated" as the text warrants.

**IT 4-7 (919-973): the Name in the soul, and sin as cutting.** The soul is "a part of Havayah", *blown* from His inwardness, while angels come from the externality of the letters and so are called "Elokim" (922-926). The four letters of the Name are traced in the soul: hidden intellect, contemplation, love and awe, then Torah and mitzvot in speech and deed (934-937). This passage is F06 and F12 joined. Then the range's plainest ein-od sentence: "אין שום דבר גשמי ורוחני חוצץ לפניו יתברך" (941), "no place empty of Him", "none else". Only sins separate, because they oppose the will that gives life to all (942). Karet is "שנכרת ונפסק חבל ההמשכה משם הוי׳" (943). The rope is woven of 613 cords (945), and a reshimu always remains (946). Chapter 6 extends this to the Shechinah: in exile the flow of the lower hei is clothed in Nogah and gives life even to sinners, and a person's deeds pull the rope downward into the chambers of the sitra achra (948-955). This is F03 and F11 stated in cosmic terms, with the person as the one who moves the divine flow. Lower teshuvah has two parts. The first is compassion for the Name in exile, which grows bitterer the more one contemplates Ein Sof who fills and surrounds all (961). The second is crushing the kelipah, whose whole life is "coarseness and self-elevation" (962, F08). The way to do it is to be one of the "מארי דחושבנא" (966), which is G13 in practice. Line 971 lists the sins the sages equate with idolatry: anger, lashon hara, arrogance, averting the eye from charity. This is G08.

**IT 8-10 (974-996): higher teshuvah as union.** The Thirteen Attributes come from the will above the Name's letters, so they cleanse every blemish, and the lower hei returns to unite with yud-hei-vav (974-975). The soul then returns "ולדבקה בו יתברך ביחוד נפלא" (977), as it was before it was breathed out. This is the Iggeret's answer to what "complete teshuvah" is, and it states F06 and F02 together. Higher teshuvah is Torah and kindness, the word of God "literally in his mouth" (990). A retied rope is "כפול ומכופל" at the knot (987), which is F11 at small scale. Line 992 ties the mitzvot to sovev: supernal holiness cannot clothe itself in worlds "משום ד״כולא קמיה כלא חשיב״", so it surrounds.

**IT 11-12 (997-1014).** The chapter holds submission and joy together (997, F09 stated). It says forgiveness is immediate and unlimited because "ולגבי בחינת איןסוף אין הפרש כלל בין מספר קטן לגדול" (1003), the higher unity applied to mercy. The laws of forgiving between people (999-1000, 1005-1007) are "none". "My sin is always before me" is read as lowliness without dejection (1008). Accepting all events with joy (1009) is read by us as providence, so it is marked "reading". Chapter 12 closes on the shadow's handbreadth moving with the sun's thousand miles (1012), where the lowest deed moves the highest world (G05). It ends with "the reward of a mitzvah is the mitzvah" (1014, G09).

**Iggeret HaKodesh 1 (1018-1029).** The loins that support the head are faith in "the One God, Ein Sof": he fills and surrounds, "לית אתר פנוי מיניה, למעלה עד אין קץ ולמטה עד אין תכלית" (1020). That faith supports the contemplating mind. The strongest line in the letter is 1021. The World to Come is only a radiance, but teshuvah and good deeds "מקרבין ישראל לאביהם שבשמים ממש, למהותו ועצמותו כביכול". The service below reaches the Essence, which is F05 and F07 in the Alter Rebbe's own words. Halacha, the will above wisdom, strengthens the faith (1023). The communal instructions on who leads the prayer (1026-1029) are "none".

**IH 2 (1030-1034).** The letter was written after Petersburg. Nearness is shown as lowliness, derived from the higher unity: "כל שהוא ״קמיה״ יותר – הוא יותר כלא ואין ואפס" (1031). Yishmael's chesed of kelipah swells with pride instead (1033). The derivation of F08 from F02 is stated here.

**IH 3 (1035-1045).** Charity makes the "holes" in the surrounding garment through which blessing flows into this world. Sovev then closes them against the outer forces (1041-1042). The mitzvot join the surrounding and the inner light, "the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah" (1038). This is F04 made operative by deed.

**IH 4 (1046-1060).** The heart has an outer part, love born of contemplation, and an inner point above knowledge. That point is "בחינת ניצוץ אלקות שבכל נפש מישראל" (1049). It is in exile, which is "literally the exile of the Shechinah" (1050). We remove the coarse husk ourselves. God removes the fine one, "for the sake of your life", meaning God alone is all one's life (1052). Charity from one's livelihood, which is one's life, redeems the captive point (1056-1057). It is called "peace" because it redeems souls that are "literally a part of God" (1059). The chapter keeps a tension that belongs in the index. The love from the inner point is "a gift from heaven", beyond contemplation (1054), yet arousal below must come first (1055). Line 1058 even calls service from knowledge alone "hester panim".

**IH 5 (1061-1084).** "Who makes the holy Name each day? One who gives charity" (1061, 1083 "מאן דעביד שמא קדישא"). This world was created with the hei, the breath, and the analogy is to the matter of speech-letters as against their form (1064-1067). This world is too small for the inner form of the letters and receives only their breath (1073). Yet within that breath is clothed the form of the letters, "united with Ein Sof utterly" (1074), which is G05. A ranking is kept: Torah scholars draw light to Beriah and Yetzirah, but only those who spend most of their days in charity draw it into this lowest world (1081-1082). This is the text's own ranking, and the index records it without evening it out.

**IH 6 (1085-1098).** This letter holds the range's plainest statement of the Essence. The differences between worlds, and all the changes of time and events, are changes of letter-combination (1089, F01). But "I, God, have not changed": "שכמו שהוא יתברך מצוי בעליונים – כך הוא ממש בשוה בתחתונים" (1090). As He was yachid u-meyuchad before creation, so He is now. The soul-letter analogy is called only "to soothe the ear", "there is no comparing to You". Yaakov's trait of compassion for the spark comes from this knowledge (1088). The love and awe given from above as a gift are "truth", and all created truth is nothing before it (1094). Charity is the counsel for drawing it (1097).

**IH 7 (1099-1113).** How can one have a "portion" in God, who has no parts (1100)? The answer holds F05 in tension with itself. "אני ממש, דהיינו מהותו ועצמותו כביכול ולא כבודו לבד" fills heaven and earth, yet He is not grasped within them "as a soul in a body" (1101). So creatures live only from "a radiance of His Name" (1102). Line 1101 is the Alter Rebbe's form of "the physical comes from the Essence", and line 1102 is his form of "only a mere radiance". **Both are in the same letter, and the index keeps both.** The radiance divides into 613, and all descends for the sake of man's soul (1104). The particular light given to each soul and generation is "above knowledge", and its image below is the lot (1113).

**IH 8 (1114-1120, the range ends mid-letter).** The revelation in prayer is given "as charity". Upper worlds are "בטלין במציאות וכלא ממש חשיבי קמיה" (1115), and gevurah hides the light unless it is roused from below (1117). This is more than measure for measure, since prayer reaches the light itself and not just the World to Come (1118). The seed analogy follows. The seed rots and "ונתאחדו והיו לאחדים" with the earth's power (1119), and the fruit far exceeds it. This is F08 as the condition of disclosure, and it continues past line 1120.

## Where the tie is thin, and what is kept apart

- Thin: IT 1-3 and much of IT 11 are halachic detail about fasts, charity sums and forgiving people. They are marked "none", 36 lines in all, with the IH 1 communal instructions and the two editorial markers. - Kept disagreement in F12: SHVE 9 (861) says "we have no business with hidden things" and that the union of Essence and attributes is held by faith. IT 7 (966), IT 9 (980) and IH 1 (1021) require deep contemplation. IH 4 (1054) and IH 6 (1093-1094) put the highest love beyond contemplation, as a gift. The three are not reconciled in the text. - Kept tension in F05: IH 7 has "His Essence, not His glory alone" filling the world (1101) next to creatures living from "a radiance of His Name" (1102). IH 1 (1021) and IH 7 (1118) contrast the World to Come as "only a radiance" with teshuvah and deeds, which reach the Essence. - The soul analogy is marked as failing at 863, 1084, 1090 and 1101 (G06), and the index does not treat it as an identity. - Ranking kept: charity-doers draw light into Asiyah where scholars do not (1081-1082). The special care for Torah study in IT (891, 911, 971) points the other way.
