Vayetzei follows Jacob out of Beersheba to Haran and back. The Zohar reads his road as the sun going down to its place, as the soul going down into "the other side" and coming out whole, and as Israel going into exile. Inside it: the ladder set on earth whose head reaches heaven; "surely God is in this place and I did not know"; the well that rises to meet the one it is bound to; the hidden world (Leah) and the revealed world (Rachel); the power of thought over deed; the Torah calling to people who sleep with open eyes; David whose soul cleaves and is held; and the King who sleeps on thorns while His son is in exile. ## 2. The unity of God

**Sun and moon, one without parting.** «שמשא בסיהרא, חדא אינון בלא פירודא, וסיהרא אף על גב דאיהו תחות שמשא, כלא איהו שמשא, בלא פירודא» (Vayetzei 2:4; line 3061): "the sun in the moon are one, with no separation; and the moon, though it is below the sun, all of it is sun, with no separation." Ours: the receiving world is not a second thing beside the Giver; "all of it is sun." Ties: F02, the Tanya's "the light is not other than its source" (Shaar HaYichud ch. 3; memory).

**Two worlds, hidden and revealed, all one.** The upper world is "He", always hidden; the lower is "You", always revealed; we bless Him "from world to world" (line 3289). «ומעלמא דא עד עלמא דא, כלא חד» (Vayetzei 24:8; line 3290): "and from this world to that world, all is one." Ours: the two names in "Havayah, Havayah" are one God seen hidden and seen near. The same pair runs through Chabad's "He" (hu) and "You" (atah) in every blessing.

**All at once, like sparks from a bellows.** «וכלהו בזמנא חדא, כמרזפא דאפיק זיקין לכל סטרין בזמנא חדא» (Vayetzei 23:1; line 3235): "and all of them at one time, like a smith's bellows that sends out sparks to every side at one time." God brings out numberless hosts, each different, in one moment. Ties: line 2259 in Vayera.

**Nothing small below that is not tied above.** «ולית לך מלה זעירא בהאי עלמא, דלא תלייא במלה אחרא עלאה … דכלא אתאחד דא בדא» (Vayetzei 23:6; line 3240): "there is no small thing in this world that does not hang on a higher thing … for all is held one in the other." Ties: S85 (seeing Him in what happens).

**Gate of heaven, all one.** «זה השער לה'. זה שער השמים כלא חד» (Vayetzei 11:5; line 3119): "'This is the gate to Havayah', 'this is the gate of heaven', all is one."

**The Name not whole while she is in exile.** «כל זמנא דכנסת ישראל בגלותא, שמא קדישא לאו שלים הוא» (Vayetzei 20:7; line 3204): "as long as the Assembly of Israel is in exile, the holy Name is not whole." And above «ותעמד ביחודא חד, מכאן ולתתא עלמא דפירודא איהו» (Vayetzei 20:8; line 3205): "stands in one unity; from here down it is a world of separation." Ours: the Zohar lets unity be both a fact above and a work still to be finished below. Chabad keeps both: nothing is apart from Him (Shaar HaYichud), and our mitzvot make Him "a dwelling below" (Tanya ch. 36; memory).

**Living and Blessed, not parted.** «וברוך צורי, כלא חד, חי וברוך, דלא מתפרשי מהדדי» (Vayetzei 39:2; line 3407): "'and blessed is my Rock', all is one, Living and Blessed, which do not part from each other"; the one flows within and the other is filled, and together they are "a well of living water."

**He alone at the end.** When the unclean power is gone «אלא קודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי, וישראל לפולחניה» (Vayetzei 38:20; line 3405): "only the Holy One alone, and Israel to serve Him"; a single King above and below.

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **"God is in this place and I did not know."** Jacob's "I did not know" is read as: «וכי כל האי אתגלי לי ולא אסתכלנא למנדע אנכי, ולמיעל תחות גדפי דשכינתא» (Vayetzei 10:1; line 3109): "all this was revealed to me and I did not look to know 'I' (Anochi), to enter under the wings of the Shechinah." Ours: the state of waking up inside what was always there and grieving that you did not see it. Ties: S62, S85; new state below.
- **Trembling at the holy place.** «מה נורא המקום הזה … ובני נשא לא משגחן ביקרא דביה» (lines 3111-3112): "how awesome is this place", and people do not notice the honor in it. Ties: technique 16 (awe).
- **Cleaving and being held.** «מכאן, לבר נש כד אתא לאתדבקא בקודשא בריך הוא, קודשא בריך הוא אחיד ביה, ולא שביק ליה» (Vayetzei 38:10; line 3395): "from here: when a person comes to cleave to the Holy One, the Holy One takes hold of him and does not let him go." David "did not care for other things of the world, only to cleave his soul and will to Him." Ties: S60 (essential bond), S84. The felt fact: you reach, and then you are the one held.
- **Joy, because there is no sadness before Him.** «כל מה דלעילא, כלא איהו בנהירו … ועל דא תנינן, אין עצבות לפני המקום» (Vayetzei 37:4; line 3385): "all that is above is all in light … and so we learned, there is no sadness before the Place"; so "serve Havayah with joy." Ties: Tanya ch. 26 and 31 on joy (memory); S-states of joy.
- **Seven years like a few days.** Jacob weighed the seven years «כאינון עלאין. דאינון אחדין דלא מתפרשאן» (Vayetzei 16:2; line 3174): "like the higher ones, which are 'one' and do not part." Ours: love gathers time into one; the long wait felt short. Near S13.
- **Sleeping with open eyes.** «חמאן ולא חמאן … אסתכלו למנדע, ואתגלי לכון נהורא דנהיר» (Vayetzei 32:5; line 3356): "they see and do not see … look, to know, and the shining light will be revealed to you." Ties: S73 (exile inside); the opposite of S85.
- **Light out of darkness.** Jacob in Laban's house chose his portion «נהורא מגו חשוכא, חכמתא מגו טפשותא» (Vayetzei 32:6; line 3357): "light from within darkness, wisdom from within foolishness." Ties: Tanya ch. 27 on the praise that rises from the dark (memory).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Good thought climbs the tree of life.** A proclamation to "the children of the Most High": guard against bad thought, for «בגין דהרהורא טבא סליק לעילא, אחיד באילנא דחיי» (Vayetzei 22:6; line 3226): "for good thought rises above and takes hold of the tree of life." «כל מלין דעלמא, אזלין בתר מחשבה והרהורא» (Vayetzei 22:7; line 3227): "all things of the world follow thought and meditation." And so «בצלותא אצטריך רעותא והרהורא לכוונא» (Vayetzei 22:14; line 3234): "in prayer, will and thought are needed to aim." Ours: this is the Zohar ground of Chabad's teaching that thought is a "garment" that draws the soul after it (Tanya ch. 4; memory). Ties: techniques 12 (returning the wandering mind) and 2.

**Deed, speech, and the will of the heart.** «מלין דלעילא, מנהון תליין בעובדא, ומנהון במלולא ומנהון ברעותא דלבא» (Vayetzei 31:1; line 3347): "things above: some hang on deed, some on speech, some on the will of the heart." Some blessings come only through prayer; some only through action. Ties: technique 18 (lips).

**Include yourself among the many.** When you pray, do not say "remember me" alone (line 3328); «יכליל גרמיה בין סגיאין בכללא דסגיאין» (Vayetzei 27:18; line 3329): "let him include himself among the many." The Shunamite said "I dwell among my people": «לא בעינא, למהוי רשימאה לעילא, אלא לאעלאה רישאי בין סגיאין» (Vayetzei 27:19; line 3330): "I do not want to be marked above, only to put my head among the many." New technique below. Ties: Chabad's "I accept upon myself the mitzvah of 'love your fellow as yourself'" before prayer (from the Arizal via the Siddur; memory).

**One blesses, two answer.** Three are needed for a blessing: one blesses, two answer and "acknowledge", so His praise rises in the secret of three (lines 3321-3322). In Torah even two may sit and give "greatness" to Him.

**Torah on the road.** «הא שכינתא לגבן נתעסק במלי דאורייתא» (Vayetzei 39:1; line 3406): "the Shechinah is with us; let us busy ourselves with words of Torah"; whoever does draws her with him. Ties: technique 22 (carrying a line).

**Awakening below feeds above.** «דהא באתערותא דלתתא, אתער לעילא» (Vayetzei 41:1; line 3413): "for by the awakening below, it is awakened above." Whoever feeds a soul, God blesses and wakes food from above for him (line 3414); and so «דלא יזלזל בר נש, לשום בר נש אחרא בעלמא» (Vayetzei 41:3; line 3415): "a person should not belittle any other person in the world." A poor man carrying his child turned out to hold a teaching.

**Charity in every season.** Charity given to the poor rises to Jacob's place and draws blessing «ממבועא דכל מבועין» (Vayetzei 15:12; line 3165), "from the spring of all springs," to every host; therefore "doing charity at every time."

**Evening prayer.** Jacob «קאים לאתקנא לתפלת ערבית» (Vayetzei 38:14; line 3399): "stands to set right the evening prayer", to light the moon. Ties: technique 24 (evening look-back).

## 5. Big human questions

- **Do I doubt God when I say "if"?** Jacob's "if God will be with me" (line 3120) was not doubt of God: «דלא הימין ביה בגרמיה, דילמא יחוב» (Vayetzei 12:6; line 3125): "he did not trust himself, lest he sin." Ours: humility about oneself inside trust in Him. Ties: S59, S84.
- **Why does the righteous go down to dark places?** Adam and Noah went in and were caught; Abraham and Isaac went in and came out; Jacob had to go in, «בגין דמאן דאשתזיב מתמן, איהו רחימא וברירא דקודשא בריך הוא» (Vayetzei 3:6; line 3067): "for whoever is saved from there is the beloved and chosen of the Holy One." Ties: the state "Gone down and not caught" (Lech Lecha).
- **Does thought matter if no one sees it?** Yes: the firstborn's right went where Jacob's thought was, to Rachel (lines 3230-3232); thought is "the root and makes the deed."
- **What about the one who hurts God's world?** The Holy One is like a king whose son died, who overturned his bed and slept on thorns and briars: «כך קודשא בריך הוא, כיון דאתגלו ישראל, ואתחרב מקדשא, נטל כובין ודרדרין ושוי תחותיה» (Vayetzei 27:7; line 3318): "so the Holy One, once Israel went into exile and the Temple was destroyed, took thorns and briars and put them under Himself." That is the burning bush. Ties: S73 and S86: He is in the pain with us.
- **Death.** Job is shown the gates of death «למנדע דהא כלהו ברשותי» (Vayetzei 28:5; line 3335), "to know that all of them are in My power," and all will be swallowed.
- **How do I start to know?** A child knows nothing until it tastes bread; Israel knew God only after the bread from heaven (lines 3270-3271). Ours: knowing starts with tasting.
- **From revealed to hidden.** «מגו דאתגליא, אתי בר נש לסתימאה» (Vayetzei 17:9; line 3185): "from within the revealed a person comes to the hidden." The way in is through what can be seen.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **"All of it is sun" (line 3061) and "from world to world all is one" (line 3290).** Two short Zohar lines for unity.
2. **Thought climbs the tree of life (lines 3226-3234).** The Zohar root of guarding thought in prayer.
3. **Cleave and be held (line 3395).** The felt center of devekut.
4. **No sadness before Him; serve with joy (line 3385).**
5. **"I did not know" (line 3109) and "they see and do not see" (line 3356).** Waking inside what was always there.

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "Surely here, and I did not know."** Inside: waking to see that God was in this place all along, with awe and a small grief for not having looked (lines 3109-3112). How to enter: at a moment of surprise or waking, stop and say "Surely Havayah is in this place"; let the not-knowing turn into looking. Near S62, S85.
- **State, new: "Held after cleaving."** Inside: you reached toward Him and now feel the reaching answered; He holds and does not let go (line 3395). How to enter: turn all will toward Him "caring for no other thing" for a few minutes; then stop reaching and rest in being held. Near S60, S84.
- **Technique, new: "Among the many."** In personal prayer, do not stand alone before the Judge; put your head among your people: ask for yourself inside a request for all (lines 3328-3330). Ties technique 20; the love of Israel before prayer.
- **Technique, new: "Good thought upward."** When a stray or low thought comes in prayer, replace it with a good thought and feel it "rise and take hold of the tree of life" (lines 3223-3227, 3234). Ties technique 12.
- **State, new: "The well rises."** Inside: when you come near what you are bound to, it rises to meet you with joy, "like a wife glad for her husband" (lines 3135-3136, 3167). How to enter: go to the place of Torah or prayer as to a well you belong to, expecting it to rise. Near S65.
