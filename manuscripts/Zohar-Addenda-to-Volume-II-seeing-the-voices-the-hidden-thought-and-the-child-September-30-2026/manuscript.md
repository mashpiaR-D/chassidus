## 1. What the range is

The Addenda are loose pieces that the printers placed after Pekudei. Some are Hebrew, some Aramaic. They are:

- **Sinai** (5210-5216): the people "saw the voices." There were seven voices, and out of them came one word.
- **Twelve commands to do and twelve to guard** (5217-5218). The first is the unity of G-d. The last is to give one's soul over in cleaving.
- **A passage from Sefer HaBahir** (5219) on the offering (*terumah*) lifted in prayer.
- **The Garden, the pillar and midnight** (5226-5231).
- **The staff, Marah and the rock** (5232-5267).
- **Physiognomy**, reading a person from the face, lines and hair (5268-5364). We set this aside. It judges people by their bodies and does not belong in a guide. Two lines in it are kept below because they speak of return and of joy, not of faces.
- **Adam's book, the oral Torah, the Ten Commandments on the sefirot** (5365-5404).
- **Moses and Aaron, the Tabernacle, Enoch and the hidden light** (5406-5418).
- **The cover of the ark, the cherubs, and the signs of favor** (5419-5450).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**One word out of many voices.** The people first saw the voices, "and at the end they heard the word coming out from among all of them" (5213: "ולסוף שמעו הדבר יוצא מבין כלם"). The ten sayings were said as one: "הא למדת דכלהו כחדא, מחדא מלתא אמרינן" (5214), "so you learn that they are all as one; from one word they were said." Why? "ובעבור שלא יטעו ישראל לומר אחרים יעזרוהו מן המלאכים" (5214), "so that Israel would not err and say that others, from the angels, helped Him." Many voices, many powers, and still one Speaker with no helper. This is the Zohar's plain answer to the fear that the sefirot or the angels are many gods.

**An image and not every image.** A king stands wrapped in white before his servants, far off. They hear his voice. Can they see his throat? No. "הא למדת שראו תמונה ולא כל תמונה" (5212), "so you learn they saw an image, and not every image." What is seen of G-d is real and is never all of Him.

**Where to look.** "במופלא ממך אל תדרוש ובמכוסה ממך אל תחקור במה שהרשיתיך התבונן" (5215), "Do not seek what is too wondrous for you, do not probe what is covered from you; look deeply into what you were given leave to." This is the old rule of Ben Sira, placed here by the Zohar itself.

**The first command and the last.** The list of twelve starts with "יחוד" (the unity of G-d) and ends with "למסור נפשו בדבקות" (5217), "to give over one's soul in cleaving." Unity is first known and at last lived by giving oneself to it.

**Soul and body, one.** The Tabernacle was built like heaven and earth, "to join this with this, like soul and body, למהוי כלא חד" (5409), "so that all would be one."

## 3. States of consciousness

- **Anger stilled by a child.** "מאן דאיהו ברוגזא ייתון לגביה נער וישתכך רוגזיה, ויתהדר בחדוה ושוי גרמיה כרבייא" (5446): "One who is angry, let them bring a child to him and his anger will settle; he turns back to joy and makes himself like a child." And "לית רחימו וחביבו בר ברבייא" (5446), "there is no love and fondness except in children." The cherubs had children's faces, set "בחביבו דא בדא אנפין באנפין" (5448), "in fondness one to the other, face to face," and when the Presence rested on them "all was in joy and no judgment was found." G-d wanted Israel "ויהון תדיר בחדוה עמיה" (5448), "to be always in joy with Him." Ties to **S15 Angry** and **S53 Glad**. Proposed as new: **ZS2-22 Softened like a child** (see JSON in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`).

- **Light hands and heavy hands.** When the people were worthy, the priest's hands rose "בלא טורח כלל ואתרמו בחידו וברעו" (5437), "with no effort at all, lifted with joy and good will," and he blessed "ברעותא דלבא" (5437), "with the will of the heart." When they were not, as with Moses in the war with Amalek, "וידוי דמשה אתייקרו ולא יכיל לזקפא לון" (5440), "Moses' hands grew heavy and he could not raise them." Ties to **S04 Low and heavy** as its outward sign, and to **S53 Glad**. (ours) Heaviness in holy work is read here as a message about the whole group's state, not as a verdict on the one who feels it.

- **Sadness lifted by learning.** Of those born under Saturn: "ואינון עציבין תדיר דלא חדאן לעלמין, בר אי לעאן באורייתא וקא אתאחדן בקודשא בריך הוא" (5356), "they are always sad and never glad, unless they toil in Torah and are joined to the Holy One." We do not take the star talk. We take the claim that being joined to Him can undo a lifelong sadness. Ties to **S04**.

- **Joy of the one who makes His name known.** "כי בו אני שמח שיודיע שמי" (5219), "in him I rejoice, for he makes My name known," said of the one "whose heart moves him" to be drawn away from this world. Ties to **S41-S44**.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **The hidden thought given for good.** "ובר נש אית ליה מחשבה סתימאה, דלית דידע בה אלא קודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי" (5399), "a person has a hidden thought that no one knows but the Holy One alone," "ויהבה ביה בבר נש למחשב מילין דאורייתא, מילין דמצוה" (5399), "and He placed it in man to think words of Torah, words of mitzvah." Therefore "מאן דחשיב למעבד מצוה כאילו עשאה" (5400), "one who thinks to do a mitzvah, it is as if he did it," for "ומחשבה דא רישא דכולא" (5400), "this thought is the head of everything." When that thought walks in the ways of Torah, "כדין אתפשט ההיא מחשבה בכלהו דרגין מסטרא דאמת" (5403), "it spreads through all the levels on the side of truth," and "כדין כלהו עלמין מתמלאן ברכאן עלאין" (5404), "then all the worlds are filled with blessings from above." Coveting is the reverse: it clings to "the other side of falsehood" (5402: "כדין אתדבק בסטרא אחרא דשקר"). Proposed as new: **ZT2-16 Setting the hidden thought** (JSON in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`). Close to technique **21** (the act that closes) and **2** (Before Whom), but its object is the first private intention, before any act.

- **The offering lifted in prayer.** "אותה תרומה תרימו אותה בתפלותיכם" (5219), "that offering, lift it in your prayers." Supports **ZT2-05** and technique **20** (own words).

- **Midnight.** The righteous stand before the light of the Garden "ונהנים ממנו עד חצות הלילה" (5230), "and enjoy it until midnight," when the Holy One comes to the Garden. Adds a source to **ZT2-09 Rising at midnight**.

- **Return while strong.** In the physiognomy section, one line on return: "ואי תב בתיובתא בזמנא דאיהו בחיליה" (5285), "and if he returns while he is still in his strength," the knots are undone. (ours) Return is best done now, not saved for weakness.

## 5. The big human questions

1. **Is G-d many?** No. Seven voices, ten sayings, one word, and no angel helped (5213-5214).
2. **Can I see Him?** You see "an image and not every image" (5212). What you see is true and is not the whole.
3. **Where should my mind go?** To what you were given leave to look into (5215), and to the first thought of doing good (5399-5400).
4. **Does intending count if I fail to do?** The Zohar says the thought to do a mitzvah is counted as done (5400). (ours) This comforts the one who is blocked, and it does not excuse the one who could act.
5. **What does one good person matter?** "אפילו אין בעולם אלא צדיק אחד, מעמיד את העולם" (5226), "even if there were only one righteous person in the world, he holds up the world."
6. **Why is my anger so hard to put down?** Look at a child's face (5446). Love and fondness live there.
7. **What is a person without spirit?** "בר נש איהו מעפרא, ועד לא שרא עליה נציצו דרוחא דלעילא, לית ביה ממשו" (5433), "a person is from dust; until a spark of the spirit from above rests on him there is no substance in him," "כיון דשריא עליה, אית ביה ממשו" (5433), "once it rests on him, he has substance." (ours) Worth is given, not earned from the dust.
8. **When suffering seems unjust?** Moses "knew His ways and justified the judgment" (5247: "ידע משה דרכיו והצדיק עליו את הדין").

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **"From one word they were said" (5214)** is a short, strong line for the unity of G-d behind all His forces.
2. **"An image and not every image" (5212)** keeps every experience of Him honest: real, never complete.
3. **The hidden thought (5399-5404)** is a practice anyone can do at any moment: point the first private thought toward a good deed. It fills the worlds with blessing.
4. **The child who stills anger (5446, 5448)** is a gentle, universal way out of anger, and says G-d wants us "always in joy with Him."
5. **Light hands and heavy hands (5437, 5440)** name heaviness in holy work without shame.

Physiognomy (5268-5364) is not used. Star lore at 5356 is not used, only its point about Torah and joy.
