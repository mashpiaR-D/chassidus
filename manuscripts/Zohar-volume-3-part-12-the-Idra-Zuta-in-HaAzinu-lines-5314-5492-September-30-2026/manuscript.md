## The shape of the range

The Idra Zuta ("the small gathering") is Rabbi Shimon's last day. The house fills. Fire surrounds it. Rabbi Hamnuna the Elder and seventy righteous souls come to listen, and so does the Holy One Himself (5314-5321). Rabbi Shimon says he wants to reveal what he held back in the great Idra, so that he will not "enter the world to come in shame" (line 5317). Rabbi Abba writes, Rabbi Elazar repeats, and the rest "whisper in their hearts" (line 5318).

This time Rabbi Shimon speaks alone. He sets out again: - the Ancient One (Atika): the skull, the still mind, three heads, the forehead of will, the open eye, the nose, and the beard (5323-5364); - Father and Mother, and the son called Knowledge (5365-5388); - the Small Face: its eyes, nose, ears, cheeks, beard and lips (5389-5473); - the joining of male and female "face to face" on the eve of Shabbos (5474-5486).

He dies in the middle of the word "life" (line 5487). His face is smiling (line 5488).

## The unity of G-d

**1. One lamp, many lights.** This is the Idra Zuta's main picture of how the many relate to the One:

> ואינון נהורין דמתפשטין, כד יקרבון למנדע לון, לא שכיח אלא בוצינא בלחודוי (Zohar, Idra Zuta 2:2; line 5324)
> And those lights that spread out: when one comes close to know them, nothing is found but the lamp alone.

"So is the Holy Ancient One, the high lamp, hidden of all hidden. Nothing is found except those lights that spread, revealed and hidden. They are called the Holy Name. Therefore all is one" (line 5324). It comes back near the end: "When they look, all the lights that spread out, nothing is found but the high lamp, hidden and not revealed" (line 5391).

**2. Separate and not separate.** On his last day Rabbi Shimon names the Ancient as the One who is:

> פריש ואתפרש מכלא, ולא פריש, דהא כלא ביה מתדבק, והוא מתדבק בכלא הוא כלא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 1:9; line 5322)
> Separate and set apart from all, and not separate, for all cleaves to Him and He cleaves to all. He is all.

*Ours:* this is the plainest ancient form of the sovev and memale (surrounding and filling) pair. It also matches the Tanya's "He is all, and all is He" without losing the "separate."

**3. Light within light, never outside.** Each lamp's light "holds to the light that is inner of inner, and is not separated outward." So "all rises in one level, all is crowned in one word, none is separated from the other":

> ולא מתפרש דא מן דא, איהו ושמיה חד הוא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 23:2; line 5390)
> And one is not separated from the other. He and His Name are one.

"The light that is revealed is called the garment of the King. The light inner of inner is hidden, and in it dwells that which is not separated and not revealed" (line 5390). *Ours:* this is the Zohar's own form of Chabad's "light and its source" (ohr and ma'or).

**4. Nothing ever leaves Him.** All the levels are tied "one to the other, until one knows that all is one":

> עד דישתמודע דכלא חד, וכלא הוא עתיקא, ולא אתפרש מניה כלום (Zohar, Idra Zuta 16:3; line 5360)
> Until it is known that all is one, and all is the Ancient One, and nothing at all is separated from Him.

And his summary of both Idras: "All I have said of the Holy Ancient One and all I have said of the Small Face, all is one, all is one word. No separation hangs on it" (כלא חד, כלא הוא חד מלה. לא תלייא ביה פירודא, line 5368).

**5. Three, two, one.** The Ancient is marked in three heads, then in two, then "is marked and hidden in one, and He is one, and all is one." All the other lamps likewise "are sanctified, bound and returned into One, and they are one" (line 5333).

**6. Not by number, but by the will of the heart.** Above all there is "one who is not known, not recognized, not marked, and He includes all." He is reached:

> וההוא לאו במניינא, ולא בכללא ולא בחושבן אלא ברעותא דלבא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 15:4; line 5356)
> And that one is not in number, not in a general rule, not in a count, but in the will of the heart.

Hence the verse: "I will guard my ways from sinning with my tongue" (line 5356). Of the highest one does not speak.

**7. He and You.** "In the Ancient there is no beginning and end," so He "is not called You, because He is hidden and not revealed. He is called He." Only from the place where a beginning is found, Wisdom, is He called "You" and "Father": "for You are our Father" (line 5361). *Ours:* this is a clear source for why prayer addresses "You" while the Essence is only "He."

**8. The head that is not a head.** The highest head "does not know and is not known... it does not cleave to wisdom or to understanding." Of it the verse says "flee to your place" and "the living beings ran and returned" (line 5328). "Therefore the Holy Ancient One is called Nothing (ayin), for Nothing hangs on Him" (ובגין כך עתיקא קדישא אקרי אין. דביה תלייא אין, line 5329). In Him "all is in joy, and He never changes from mercy" (כלא בחידו, ולא שניא מרחמי לעלמין, line 5330).

## States of consciousness

- **Tied in one knot all one's days.** Rabbi Shimon's opening verse is "I am my beloved's, and his longing is for me." He explains:

> כל יומין דאתקטרנא בהאי עלמא, בחד קטירא אתקטרנא ביה בקודשא בריך הוא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 1:9; line 5322)
> All the days I was bound in this world, I was bound with one knot to the Holy One.

So now "His longing is for me." Maps to S12 (cleaving) and S44. *Proposed state: the single knot.*

- **The soul that burns in Him.** "Every day I looked at this verse, 'In Hashem my soul will praise itself.' Today the whole verse is fulfilled":

> דהא נשמתי ביה אחידא, ביה להטא, ביה אתדבקת ואשתדלת ובאשתדלותא דא תסתלק לאתרהא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 24:3; line 5401)
> For my soul is held in Him, burns in Him, cleaves in Him and strives, and in this striving it will rise to its place.

- **Weeping and laughing at once.** Rabbi Shimon "raised his hands, wept and laughed." He wanted to reveal one more thing. "I have been pained over this all my days, and now they do not give me permission." He sat, whispered with his lips, and bowed three times. Then he spoke to his own mouth: "Mouth, mouth, you have merited all this. Your spring has not dried. Your spring flows and does not stop" (line 5387). Near S53. *Ours:* longing and joy held together, and the humility to accept that some things are not given.

- **Pain for the world.** "I testify about myself that all my days I was pained for the world, that it should not meet the judgment of Tzedek, and that the world should not be burned in its flames" (אסהדנא עלי, דכל יומאי הוינא מצטער על עלמא, line 5398). "In this generation there are righteous ones, but few who stand to protect the world" (line 5398). When a beloved righteous person is in the world, "the world can be saved because of him" even when judgment wakes (line 5396). Maps to S86. *Proposed state: carrying the world.*

- **The open eye that washes all colors white.** The lower eyes have colors: red for judgment, green for showing deeds, and white, from which come "all the mercies and all the good." "All the people of the world cannot wash colors into white, but here, with one glance, they are all joined and washed in the white" (line 5431). When the lids of the lower eyes part, the eyes open "like one who wakes from sleep," see the open eye, "and are washed in its white" (line 5428). This deepens "under the open eye" from part 6.

- **Face to face heals.** "Long of patience" (erech apayim) is read as "healing of the faces," "for there is no healing in the world except at the time when face looks into face" (דהא לא אשתכח אסוותא בעלמא, אלא בזמנא דאשגחין אנפין באנפין, line 5409). *Ours:* this is a teaching on presence. What heals is being turned toward.

- **Half a body.** "The male alone looks like half a body... and so the female. When they join, all looks like one whole body, and all the worlds are in joy" (line 5476). "Blessing does not rest on a lacking thing... half a thing does not last" (line 5477). This is why Shabbos is blessed: "the Queen cleaves to the King and they are found as one body" (line 5477). Maps to S76.

- **Death with a smile.** "After the fire left, I saw the holy lamp... that he had passed from the world. He was wrapped, lying on his right side, and his face was smiling" (ואנפוי חייכין, line 5488). This is the Idra's picture of the end of a life of cleaving. Compare the death "by a kiss" in part 6.

## Meditation and practice

- **Speak the prayer with your lips.** The Idra Zuta gives the reason:

> כל צלותא ובעותא דבעי בר נש מקמי קודשא בריך הוא, בעי לאפקא מלין בשפוותיה, דאי לא אפיק לון, לאו צלותיה צלותא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 34:4; line 5446)
> Every prayer and request a person asks before the Holy One, he must bring the words out on his lips, for if he does not bring them out, his prayer is not a prayer.

"Whatever a person thinks and considers in his heart does not make a thing, until he brings it out on his lips." The word "splits the air, rises and flies, and becomes a voice," and it is carried to the King's ear (line 5445). Ties to `lips` and `own_words`. *Ours:* this is a strong ancient basis for saying one's own words aloud.

- **Hearing slowly.** The ear is bent "so that the voice is held back from entering the mind, and the mind discerns it, and not in haste":

> דכל מלה דהוי בבהילו, לא הוה בחכמתא שלימתא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 34:1; line 5443)
> For any matter done in haste is not in complete wisdom.

"The ear tests words," and "the palate tastes food," because each waits (line 5447). Ties to `discern`. *Proposed technique: the bent ear.* (It fits with part 6.)

- **Holding what is told.** The ear "gathers secrets" and reveals them only to those "who are not crooked in their ways": "the secret of Hashem is for those who fear Him" (line 5450). "The faithful of spirit covers a matter" (line 5452). Ties to `hold`.

- **The three Shabbos meals.** "A person should rejoice in the three meals of Shabbos, for all of faith is found in it" (line 5337). Rabbi Shimon testifies:

> דהא מן יומאי לא בטילנא אלין ג' סעודתי, ובגיניהון לא אצטריכנא לתעניתא בשבתא (Zohar, Idra Zuta 8:2; line 5338)
> In all my days I never skipped these three meals, and because of them I never needed to fast on Shabbos.

They are the meal of the Queen, the meal of the Holy King, and the meal of the Holy Ancient One (line 5338). Ties to `act` and S76.

- **Shabbos Mincha, the hour of will.** When the forehead called Will is revealed, "all prayers below are accepted." "On Shabbos at the time of Mincha... this forehead is revealed and all judgments are subdued... even the fire of Gehinnom quiets in its place, and the wicked rest. Therefore a soul of joy is added on Shabbos" (line 5336). The Small Face's forehead is "red as a rose," and when the Ancient's forehead shines on it, it "turns white as snow, and that hour is called a time of will for all" (line 5422). This confirms "the hour of the will of wills" from part 6.

- **Intending more than you say.** When Moshe prayed after the spies he said only nine of the thirteen attributes, but "the matter hangs on intention": he "intended the holy source (mazal) and mentioned it" with the words "Now let the strength of Hashem be great" (line 5465). Ties to `before_whom`.

- **Tasting the King's sweetness.** "Happy is the one who is careful with the King's sweetness and tastes of it as is fitting. Of this it is written, 'Taste and see that Hashem is good'" (line 5473). Ties to `heart_after`.

## The big human questions

- **What happens to broken first attempts?** The first worlds were "like sparks from a smith's hammer, that flash, shine and go out at once." They failed because "face did not look at face," and there was no male and female yet. Now "all endures" (5405-5406).

- **Is a person's lot fixed by merit?** "Children, life and food do not hang on merit but on mazal (the flowing source)," which is above the realm of judgment (line 5408).

- **Why teach at all?** "Not for my honor, and not for my father's house," but so as not to enter before Him in shame (line 5386). "These words shine in my heart in the fullness of love and awe" (line 5367).

- **Who may hear this?** Only "those who entered and came out, and know the ways of the Holy One, and do not turn to the right or the left" (line 5367). "Whoever did not enter and come out, better for him not to have been created" (line 5400).

- **How does a great life end?** In the middle of a word of blessing, with a smile. The voices say "length of days" and "life he asked of You" (line 5487). At the cave a voice cries: "This is the man who shakes the earth... Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, in whom his Master is praised every day" (line 5492).

## For the guide (ours)

1. The lamp and its lights (lines 5324, 5391) and "He and His Name are one" (line 5390) are the Idra Zuta's gift to the unity layer. They say simply what Shaar Hayichud says at length: the many lights are real, but when you come close there is only the Lamp.
2. "Separate and not separate... He is all" (line 5322) should sit beside "He is all, one without separation" (part 11, line 5262).
3. "Not by number but by the will of the heart" (line 5356) is a clear instruction for how to turn to the Essence: not by counting levels, but by wanting.
4. Two practices are simple and well grounded here: say your prayer aloud (line 5446), and hear slowly (line 5443).
5. "No healing except when face looks into face" (line 5409) and "half a body" (line 5476) are strong human teachings. Wholeness comes from being turned toward another.
6. Rabbi Shimon's last day gives the guide a model of a good death: tied in one knot all one's days, pained for the world, and smiling at the end.
