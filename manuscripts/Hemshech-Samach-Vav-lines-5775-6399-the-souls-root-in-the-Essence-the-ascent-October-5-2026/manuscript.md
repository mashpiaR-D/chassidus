The five maamarim of winter 5668 are really one argument in five steps. That is why F05 (the Essence) and F06 (the soul) dominate the counts here, with F10 (Torah and mitzvah) third. 1. The soul's root is in "letters engraved right through". Those letters are no being of their own. They are one with the light of Ein Sof before the tzimtzum, "He and His Name are one". 2. The soul came down into the lowest body "with force", from the Essence. Only in that descent does its root shine in it. So the descent is for an ascent that could not happen above. 3. The ascent goes beyond the soul's root. Through Torah and mitzvot the soul rises from "pure" (the vessels) to "kodesh", the essence of the light, and then to the essential concealment of Ein Sof, His essential Name. 4. The kinds of service reach different depths. Torah reveals, and the mitzvah done as an act reaches deeper. The servant who accepts the yoke reaches deeper than the son who understands, but the son reveals what the servant reaches. 5. The closing section applies this to Rosh Hashanah. The union of the filling and surrounding lights, which are opposites, can come only from the Essence, "where neither of them takes up place and it includes both".

The previous ranges built the three levels in the light and the parable of engraved and written letters. This range turns them into an account of why a soul is in a body, and of what each kind of service reaches.

## Ein Omdin, its end (5775-5831): letters that are the light itself

The parable of the tablets finishes. Letters engraved partway have a "foothold" and can be filled with ink, which makes them a "something" that darkens the stone. Letters engraved right through cannot be filled. Above, the vessels of Keter and Adam Kadmon are "possible existence", and the hidden sefirot before the tzimtzum are no existence at all. The line on Beriah gives a clean formula for how being relates to the divine Nothing (F01, F05): > וכל מה שמתרחק מן האין האלקי, נעשה בבחי' יש יותר (line 5790)

> והיינו בדרך דילוג ובדרך אין ערוך (line 5790)

Before the tzimtzum the letters shone with no concealment, "because the letters are the light itself" (5798). The souls are rooted there (F06): > שהאותיות הן מיוחדים ממש עם האור בבחיי אחדות ממש (line 5800)

The maamar then closes on prayer. Prayer joins the soul in the body to its root. Sin is a screen between the soul and "your God", which is read as the Godliness inside the soul (F06, F03): > וע'"י התפלה נעשה התחברות דנשמה המלובשת בגוף עם שרש הנשמה (line 5821)

> פי' אלקיכם היינו בחי' האלקות שבנשמה (line 5821)

The remedy is a holy heaviness: submission, and mercy for one's own soul. That lifts the soul "to Me", to His very Essence (F08): > לעצמותו ומהותו ממש (line 5824)

## Vayigash (5832-5928): the soul is real Godliness, and it walks only below

This maamar asks why the soul came down at all. First it says what the soul is. Every creature has no existence before it is made. The soul does, because even as created it has not changed from its being (F06): > וה"ה אלקות ממש, אלא שהאלקות נעשה בבחי' יש (line 5862)

Then the paradox. The soul's root is already in the Essence, so what can the descent add? The answer is that the root shines in the soul only when it is below (F07): > דאמיתית אור הנשמה כמו שהיא מצד שרשה ומקורה בבחי' עצמות אור (line 5881)

To put a soul, "a part of God above", into a wholly separate body is a leap with no proportion. The parables for it are the jumper, the strong man lifting beyond his strength, and the one who blows "from inside". Only the hidden power of the Essence can make such a leap (F05, F06, F09): > דבכדי שיהי" ירידת והתלבשות הנשמה, שהיא חלק אלק' ממעל (line 5901)

Man's body is lower than the animals'. It was dust before it had a soul, while theirs came with their souls. His soul is higher than everything. The two extremes are joined so that he can refine everything, like a lever gripped at its lowest end (F07, F11): > ונמצא, דדוקא בירידת הנשמה למטה נמשך בה כח שרשה ומקורה (line 5909)

What the descent brings is "walking". Angels, and souls above, "stand": their love is measured by the light that fills worlds. Below, the love "with all your might" comes from sensing the Essence, and it leaves every vessel (F05, F08): > לצאת מגדר הכלי ולהכלל בעצמות אוא"ס (line 5922)

## Hakavtzu (5929-6031): hidden only toward the worlds; pure and holy; One and Only

The maamar opens on a hard question from Iggeres HaKodesh 20. Souls of Atzilus are not "real Godliness to create from nothing", so how is Yaakov called "El"? > דאמיתות כח הבריאה וההתהוות הוא בעצמות א"ס דוקא (line 5930)

On the way to the answer, the range says the plainest thing it says about concealment. The Essence is everywhere, and nothing can hide it. It hides itself, and only toward the worlds (F03, F04): > כי אין שום דבר מעלים על עצמות אוא"ס (line 5948)

> דבעצמו ה"ה מאיר בגילוי ממש, רק שבהעולמות אינו נרגש האור (line 5948)

The light is clothed in the vessels and united with them, yet it does not mix with them. That is the sense of "none is holy like God" (F04, F09): > ד[ה]גם שמתלבש בהכלים ומתאחד עמהם, ומ"מ אינו מתערב ממש עמהם (line 5964)

Beyond "kadosh", the radiance drawn into worlds, is "kodesh", which the worlds cannot hold (F05, F03): > שבגילוי בחי' זו היו העולמות בטלים במציאות לגמרי (line 5980)

The unity itself gets three names. "Echad" (One) is the oneness of what spread out and came back together. "Yachid" (Only) is oneness in essence, "One but not in number" (F05, F02): > אבל העצמות הכל אחדות (line 5987)

> אבל בחי' יחיד היינו יחוד בעצם (line 5987)

Torah reaches the inwardness of Atik because "Torah and the Holy One are all one". But the souls' true root is higher even than Torah, and practical mitzvot now will reveal it (F10, F06): > דאורייתא וקוב"ה כולא חד (line 6022)

> והיינו דשרשן הוא בבחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס (line 6022)

> דהיינו גילוי בחי' ההעלם העצמי דא"ס ממש, יהי' ע'י קיום מצות מעשיות דעכשיו (line 6026)

Yaakov is called "El" because through descending and refining he rose into union with the essence of the light. "Gather" means gathering the sparks, and "listen to Israel your father" is the rise that follows (6028, 6031).

## Kodesh Yisrael (6032-6165): three levels before the tzimtzum; He and His Name

The purpose of the dwelling below is not to bring back the light that filled the space before the tzimtzum. That light is only the hidden form of the line, and moving from hidden to revealed is nothing new. The purpose is to draw what is above it (F07, F05): > עד בחי' ההעלם העצמי דא"ס ממש, שזהו בחי" אמיתית הגילוי שיהי' לעתיד (line 6045)

Atzilus is the start of limit but not limit itself. The reshimah stands between nothing and something (F09): > שאינו אין ממש, וגם לא יש (line 6055)

The range then sets out three levels inside Or Ein Sof before the tzimtzum, using the parable of the soul's powers: the essential hyle, the hidden drawing, and the aroused power that is measured out. The deepest point is that the light truly included in the Essence is not light at all (F05): > דהנה, האור הכלול בעצמותו ממש הרי אינו בבחי' אור כלל, כ"א בבחי' יכולת (line 6102)

The three levels are "Only, One and Primordial". The second level is "One", where every revelation is included as one (6111): > וזהו בחי' אחד, שהן בבחי' התאחדות לבד (line 6111)

The line of light is the Shechinah, and Or Ein Sof above it is "the Holy One". So the higher and lower unities are placed in the line and in what is above it (F02): > שהקו הוא בחי' שכינה (line 6125)

True kodesh is His essential Name (F05): > דאמיתית ענין קדש הוא בבחי' שמו העצמי הכלול בעצמותו ממש (line 6149)

The souls are the earth in which Torah and mitzvot are sown, because they are no being of their own. Torah lifts them to the essence of the light. Practical mitzvot, "the King's commands", lift them to the Essence itself (F10, F08): > והיינו מחמת הביטול שיש בנש"י (line 6157)

> אבל ע"י המצות מעשיות, בחי' מצות המלך, העליי היא בבחי' אמיתית העצמות דא"ס (line 6157)

## HaShamayim Kis'i (6166-6355): the essential will, the physical mitzvah, and the servant

The will that made the worlds is an outer will. It is for the sake of something else (tzaddikim, doing good), it is clothed in wisdom, and it can be withdrawn ("God regretted"). The will in the mitzvot is inner and essential (F10, F05): > וזהו הרצון דמצות, שהוא בחי' רצון עצמי, שכל עצמותו כבי' ברצון זה (line 6186)

Because it is not built on reason, it cannot be divided. A part of it is the whole, which is the Baal Shem Tov's principle (cited in the footnote at 6196). So it can come to rest only in a physical thing, which is not a vessel at all (F10, F09, F07): > שכשאתה תופס במקצת מן העצם אתה תופס בכולו (line 6189)

> כ"א בא בדבר גשמי דוקא, שאינו בגדר כלי כלל (line 6189)

> בכל מקום שאני תופס ואוחז בקצה וחלק מחלק האחדות (line 6196)

Torah needed an exodus from limits before it was given. Its whole aim is the limitless light below (6201): > וכל הכוונה בנתינת התורה להמשיך בחי' הגילוי דאוא"ס הבבע"ג למטה (line 6201)

The sections that follow rank the kinds of service (6232-6354). Torah is reason, so its delight is felt. Mitzvot "were not given for enjoyment", so theirs is the unfelt essential delight. Yet Torah can also reach the Essence, when halachah is clarified with the yoke, in awe and not for the sake of the reason (F10, F08): > שזהו אמיתת הכוונה המושרשת בעצמות א"ס, להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 6234)

> וע"י בירור הלכות בבחי' קבלת עומ"ש, מגיעים לבחי' העלם העצמי (line 6241)

Only Torah as it is below has this, so it was given to souls in bodies and not to angels: > ולכן ניתנה התורה למטה דוקא (line 6249)

"Do" before "hear" is the bitul of the yoke, laying oneself down entirely. That reaches the Essence and draws the "essential nothing" of Keter (F08, F05): > שהי' בהם הביטול דקבעומ"ש בבחי' הנחת עצמותם לגמרי (line 6282)

The servants (souls of BaN) reach the Essence, and the sons (souls of MaH, the tzaddikim) reveal it below, like the baal teshuvah and the tzaddik. So Yehudah is truly above Yosef, but Yosef reveals him (F08, F07): > זהו דוקא ע''י העבודה דנשמות דב"ן, בבחי' עבודת עבד דוקא (line 6318)

> ובאמת, בחי' ומדרי' יהודה למעלה ממדרי' יוסף (line 6327)

> להיות הם בבחי' כלים לאלקות מצ"ע (line 6337)

The maamar ends on prayer as the house for all of it, and on the dwelling below as the aim: > ובזה נשלם אמיתית הכוונה דנתאווה הקב"ה להיות לו ית' דירה בתחתונים (line 6349)

A careful self-correction follows. The Rashab suggests that yoke-accepting Torah reaches higher than yoke-accepting mitzvot. He then rejects the idea, because "all Israel have a share in the World to Come" through their deeds (6354): > וכמא'יי כל ישראל יש להם חלק לעוה"ב (line 6354)

## The closing section (6356-6399): Malchiyos, Zichronos, Shofros

The life of all the worlds is only His kingship, "I will reign", an outer radiance that does not touch His Essence. So "I God have not changed" (F04, F05, F01): > שההארה המחי' עולמות ה"ה הארה חיצוניות לבד מעצמותו ית' (line 6372)

Each Rosh Hashanah that kingship goes back into its source and must be drawn down again (6359). Malchiyos is "He and His organs are one", the inwardness of kingship revealed below. Zichronos is the union of the surrounding and filling lights (F04, F02): > שזהו בחיי יחוד סובב כל עלמין בממלא כל עלמין (line 6381)

Shofros comes from the root of both. Surrounding and filling are opposites, and they can be joined only from the Essence, where both are included (F05, F09): > צ''יל שרש ההמשכה מעצמות א"ס, דשם שניהם אינם תופסים מקום וכולל שניהם (line 6390)

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The soul as "real Godliness" even when created.** Tanya ch. 2 calls the soul "a part of God above, truly". Here the soul's root is placed before the tzimtzum, in letters that are "the light itself", and the soul is said to exist before it was made (5862). Tanya does not locate the root this precisely.
- **Iggeres HaKodesh 20 is qualified.** The Alter Rebbe says souls of Atzilus are not real Godliness able to create from nothing. The Rashab quotes this (5930) and does not deny it. But he says that through descent and service Yaakov rose into the essence of the light and so could be called "El" (6028). This is an addition to the Tanya, not a contradiction of it.
- **Descent for ascent, made precise.** Tanya chs. 36-37 say the purpose is the dwelling below. Here the reason is given in terms of the soul: its root shines only below (5881-5885), and below it can "walk" (5913-5928).
- **Deed over study.** Tanya ch. 5 praises the knowing of Torah as a wondrous union, and the Tanya ranks study highly throughout. The Rashab says the act of the mitzvah reaches the essential concealment of Ein Sof, while Torah reveals it (6157, 6215, 6266-6272). He keeps "study is greater because it leads to deed", but only as the revealer. This is a real difference in emphasis, and it is kept here, not smoothed over.
- **The yoke as the summit.** In Tanya ch. 41 accepting the yoke is the root and beginning of service. Here it is what reaches highest, above the love and delight of the tzaddikim (6282, 6318, 6351).
- **The tzimtzum hides only toward the worlds.** This matches Shaar HaYichud 7. The range adds the reason: only the unlimited can hide itself, and so the hiding does not touch the Essence at all (5948).

## Where the tie is thin

- Almost all of the 305 footnote lines are bare references. Only 6196-6197 carry content. - The Yehudah, Yosef and Binyamin genealogy (5832-5860, 6326-6337) is structural. It is about types of soul. The tie to the unity is real (reaching and revealing the Essence), but it comes through the type and not through the story. - The parables of the strong man, the jumper and the kind man (5896-5900, 6082-6099) only set up points. The prayer customs (heaviness of head, charity before prayer, 5820-5825) are tied through F08 and F06, not directly. - F09 (the unity of opposites) is marked sparingly. It is stated at 6390 and 5907. At 6164 ("darkness is like light" from the surrounding light) the tie holds, but the passage is about where the flow goes, not about opposites as such. - Zero rows are marked *reading*. This range states its ties so openly that no reading was needed. The faces attached to structural lines are ours, however, and they follow the section each line serves.

## Disagreements kept

- **Torah above the souls, or the souls above Torah?** 6022 says both. The souls are higher in their root, but Torah is higher in revelation, because "Torah and the Holy One are all one" while the souls became beings of their own. The text states both and does not reduce either to the other.
- **Study or deed?** Rabbi Tarfon and Rabbi Akiva are both called right: deed rouses the Essence and study reveals it. For the future, deed will be greater and the revelation will still come through Torah (6272, 6295).
- **Son or servant?** The servant reaches deeper and the son reveals. The "son who became a servant" may have a greater bitul, but the Rashab refuses to rank those who learn above those who do (6354). He leaves the tension open.
- **Unsure:** the maamar name for 5929 ("Hakavtzu") comes from the opening verse and the later cross-references ("ד"ה הקבצו"). The dates come from the fused page headers. 6356-6399 is treated as the written closing of the whole series, following the footnote at 6366. The range ends in the middle of the Shofros explanation, which continues after line 6399.
