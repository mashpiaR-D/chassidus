## What this slice adds (one page)

The Rebbe Rashab's Sefer HaMaamarim (all years, OCR) takes the depth below 'nothing but Him' in four directions. First, the self: the soul is not a ray that vanishes but His essence become someone, like a child from a father; even yechidah is only a name, and under it is the 'true Something'. Second, the bottom: under understanding and under will is 'the deep that cannot be blocked'; the deepest cry has no sound; the deepest bond is a knot, not a melting, and it shows in the plain mitzvah. Third, the person who feels nothing: the distance itself is a door, and a 'something' that becomes 'nothing' is higher than a born nothing. Fourth, the return: the deepest run already holds the return; joy is 'His will got done'; and He is not 'all things merged' but the Only One, einzig, who carries all and freely chooses us.

1. **You are not a ray.** The Rashab keeps returning to one mashal: a teacher gives light, a father gives himself. A soul comes the second way: 'the essence itself becomes a something'. That is why the soul is not erased when His essence shines, does not change in any world, and alone can draw His essence down (RM-C04, RM-D2). He goes further than DESCENT: even *yechidah* is one of the soul's 'five names'; under it is the 'true Something' (RM-C03, RM-D1).
2. **The bottom is a deep, not a floor.** Understanding can be blocked by sin; the will above reason can stop; the yechidah is 'the springs of the great deep' that never stop and refill even what you never did (RM-C01, RM-D3). The deepest cry has no sound (RM-C02, RM-D4). The deepest connection is a knot, not a melting, and it shows in the plain mitzvah (RM-C14, RM-D6). Real self-sacrifice has 'no delight and nothing sought, only the bond' (RM-C15, RM-D11).
3. **Feeling nothing is a door.** For one who feels nothing even in the Ten Days, the Rashab does not prescribe a feeling. The turn comes 'from the pain of the distance itself', and 'when the something becomes nothing, that is higher' than a born nothing (RM-C11, RM-D7). Mercy 'agrees he is not worthy' and gives anyway (RM-C13).
4. **The return is in the run, and the One is Only One.** The deepest run already holds the return (R. Akiva in and out in peace; RM-C06). Joy is 'that His will got done' (RM-C16, RM-D8). And the unity is not 'all merged in Him' but One who is 'einzig in essence, carrying everything' (RM-C19, RM-D9) and chooses us freely (RM-C20).

**For the guide (ours).** Use RM-D2 before any 'you are nothing' talk. Use RM-D7 for people who report no feeling at all. Use RM-D10 as a guard against spiritual pride. Use the prayer ladder (avodah 1) as the practical frame.

## RM-C01 · Something in me is blocked. I did wrong, and the feeling for G-d dried up.

*Where:* Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah 5672 (Ayin-Beis opening), lines 35474-35503; Tik'u 5651, lines 7783-7785

1. **When you do wrong, the river inside you can dry up. Your mind and heart go dull, like a river whose spring got blocked.** (M11) *How:* The 5651 maamar compares understanding to a river and its source to a spring. Sins block the spring, and the river of feeling goes dry. > והנהר יחרב ויבש בטמטום המוח והלב (line 7785, OCR) - "and the river dries up, in dullness of the mind and the heart"
2. **The fix is not to clean the old opening. You dig deeper than the place the water first came out.** (M6) *How:* The Rashab quotes the known mashal: to bring back lost water you dig deeper into the spring than before. Teshuvah reaches a deeper root than the one the will first came from. > בכדי להחזיר המים שנחסרו צריכים לחפור בעומק המעיין ביותר (line 35475, OCR) - "to bring back the water that was lost, one must dig deeper into the spring"
3. **But even a deeper spring can get blocked again. A strong burst of 'I want G-d', above reason, can still fade.** (M1) *How:* The text tests its own answer: the will above reason comes from the soul already 'narrowed' for the body, so it too can stop. > אך מ"מ גם בח" עומק הזה אפשר שיופסק לפעמים (line 7785, OCR) - "but even this depth can sometimes stop"
4. **There are springs that never stop: the springs of the great deep. They come from the deep itself, not from cracks in the ground.** (M5) *How:* Normal springs come from the veins of the earth and can clog. The 'springs of the great deep' (as in the Flood) burst from the deep itself and push away every block. > יש מעיינות שאינם נפסקים לעולם והם הנק' מעיינות תהום רבה (line 7784, OCR) - "there are springs that never stop, called the springs of the great deep"
5. **In you that deep is the yechidah, the soul's single point. There, nothing can block or stop.** (M8) *How:* The 'deep' in the person is the yechidah, the point of the heart that has no hold in the body's tools. A cry from there cannot be stopped. > ושם אינו שייך שום הפסק וטתימה ח"ו (line 7785, OCR) - "and there no stopping or blocking applies"
6. **From there everything missing fills again, even mitzvos you never did. One hour can 'buy a world'.** (M15) *How:* The text asks how teshuvah can fill a missed positive mitzvah, and answers: when the essence is reached, all the lights come along by themselves. > יש קונה עולמו בשעה אחת (line 7784, OCR) - "one can acquire his world in a single hour"
7. **So after a real return He is not just willing again. He is more pleased than before the sin.** (M4) *How:* Forgiveness means being wanted as before 'and more', because the will now flows from a deeper root than at first. > שיהי' מרוצה כמו קודם החטא ועוד יותר (line 35475, OCR) - "that he be desired as before the sin, and even more"

**Bottom.** The yechidah: the deep under the deep, where no sin and no dullness can stop the flow (stated). **Return.** Every river refills from there: mind, heart and deeds; this is why Rosh Hashanah's work is teshuvah from the heart's point, and the year's work is the mitzvos it then fills (stated; 'the whole river' image is ours).

## RM-C02 · I want to cry out to G-d, and I can't even find words.

*Where:* Tik'u 5651 hemshech, lines 7776-7782; also 5677, line 37082

1. **There is a cry with no words. It comes when the feeling is too big for the mind and heart to hold.** (M2) *How:* A 'simple voice' cannot be built of letters; that itself shows it comes from above the mind. People cry like this from great distress. > ויצעקו אל ה' בצר להם (line 7778, OCR) - "they cried out to G-d in their distress"
2. **But a loud cry still feels itself. Someone is still there, shouting.** (M4) *How:* The Rashab sorts two cries. The loud one, from distress or sudden fear, still has a hold in the inner self; it is felt. > כמו כאשר יתפעל בחרדה פתאום (line 7778, OCR) - "as when one is shaken by sudden terror"
3. **Deeper is the heart's own cry. It goes so deep it cannot even make a sound.** (M5) *How:* 'Their heart cried out to G-d' (Eicha): the heart itself cries. It is touched so deep that the voice cannot come out. > עד שלא יוכל לצעוק בקול כלל (line 7778, OCR) - "until he cannot cry aloud at all"
4. **Then the whole self is moved from its place, without feeling itself at all.** (M8) *How:* This is the yechidah stirring: 'all its essence is moved from its place, with no feeling of itself'. > וכל עצמותה ניזוז ממקומה בלי שום הרגש בעצמו כלל (line 7778, OCR) - "and its whole essence is moved from its place, with no feeling of itself at all"
5. **Picture a spark racing to a flame: still a spark. Then picture a flame melting into the great fire: gone, with no noise.** (M10) *How:* The mashal and where it breaks: the racing spark still exists; the flame dissolving in elemental fire disappears 'without noise, flame or excitement'. > כלה והולך ומתבטל במציאו' לגמרי בלי רעש התלהבות התפעלות כלל (line 7779, OCR) - "it goes on and disappears entirely, with no noise, no flame, no excitement"
6. **That silent cry is the shofar. A small breath goes in at the narrow end, and the shofar makes it great.** (M7) *How:* The tekiah is 'the heart's point fixed in its simplicity'. The voice is not the person's shout; a quiet inner breath enters the narrow end and comes out the wide end as a great voice. > יוצא בחשאי מצד הקצר שבשופר (line 7782, OCR) - "goes out quietly from the narrow end of the shofar"
7. **Then you walk 'in the light of Your face': face turned to face, and new life flows into the year.** (M15) *How:* The return: after the tekiah 'happy is the people... they walk in the light of Your face'; the giver's face turns back and new life comes into Torah, mitzvos and the world. > באור פניך יהלכון (line 7782, OCR) - "they will walk in the light of Your face"

**Bottom.** A cry with no sound and no self-feeling, from the yechidah (stated). **Return.** The Rashab says this stirring often comes from above, 'when the Source is near' in the Ten Days; and the shofar brings it out as a voice, then into the year (stated).

## RM-C03 · Who am I, really, under all of it?

*Where:* Tik'u 5651 hemshech, lines 7749-7760

1. **Each morning you say: 'the soul You gave me is pure.' Those words list four levels of you.** (M3) *How:* The Rashab reads 'pure... You created, You formed, You breathed' as four levels (chayah, neshamah, ruach, nefesh). > נשמה שנתת בי (line 7758, OCR) - "the soul You gave me"
2. **But 'You gave' is more than 'You breathed'. Giving means giving with an open hand, the essence itself.** (M2) *How:* The Midrash: now the soul is 'breathed', in the future it will be 'given'. 'Giving' is 'with a good eye', the essence. So 'that You gave me' points to a level above the four: yechidah. > דענין הנתינה הוא כמארז"ל נותן בעין יפה נותן והיינו בחי' העצמות (line 7758, OCR) - "giving means as the Sages said, one who gives, gives with a good eye; that is the essence"
3. **That essence guards all the rest, the way the sun guards its light. When the sun sets, the light is gone at once.** (M6) *How:* 'You guard it within me': the essence of the soul guards its four lights, like the sun's body guards its rays. > שהרי מיד בשקיעת המאור הי' הזיו כלא הי' ובטל לגמרי (line 7759, OCR) - "for as soon as the sun sets, the light is as if it never was"
4. **Even 'yechidah' is only a name. The Sages say the soul has five names.** (M4) *How:* The Rashab brings the Midrash: five names were given to the soul, yechidah among them. So yechidah too is a name, and the soul's essence is above it. > חמשה שמות נקראו לה נר"ן חי' יחידה (line 7759, OCR) - "five names were given to it: nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, yechidah"
5. **Yechidah is the 'nothing' of the True Something. Your real self is the True Something itself.** (M5) *How:* He draws the line: yechidah is the 'ayin' of the true Yesh; the essence of the soul is the true Yesh. > דיחידה הוא בחי' האין של היש האמיתי ועצם הנפש הוא בחי' יש האמיתי (line 7759, OCR) - "yechidah is the 'nothing' of the true Something, and the essence of the soul is the true Something"
6. **And this did not come by creation. G-dliness itself spread out and became a soul.** (M6) *How:* An angel is created from far away; a soul is 'G-dliness itself spreading and becoming a soul'. > שהאלקות עצמו נתפשט ונעשה בבתחי" נשמה (line 7752, OCR) - "G-dliness itself spread out and became a soul"

**Bottom.** Under every name, even yechidah, is the soul's essence, the true Something, one with His (stated). **Return.** From there comes teshuvah, 'the spirit returns to G-d who gave it' (7761): the bottom is where the turning begins (stated).

## RM-C04 · If everything is nothing before Him, am I nothing too?

*Where:* Noach 5672, lines 35547-35572; Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah 5659, line 14824

1. **A teacher can only give you light. If you have no mind of your own, his teaching will not take.** (M3) *How:* Two kinds of giving: a teacher's teaching is only 'a glow'; it can only shape a mind that is already there. > אם המקבל אין לו שכל בעצמו לא יבין השפעת הרב (line 35551, OCR) - "if the receiver has no mind of his own, he will not understand the teacher's teaching"
2. **A father gives himself. From the drop, a whole new person comes, with a mind of his own.** (M10) *How:* Begetting passes on the father's essential powers, so a whole being with all its powers comes to be. Proof: a blind man fathers a whole child, since his deep powers are untouched. > שהסומא מוליד שלם (line 35552, OCR) - "a blind man fathers a whole child"
3. **So in begetting, the essence itself becomes a 'someone'.** (M2) *How:* The essence does not become a 'source' of something else; it itself becomes a thing, only set apart, like a spark from a torch. > שהעצם גופא נעשה בבחי' מהות ומציאות דבר (line 35552, OCR) - "the essence itself becomes a real being"
4. **Creatures are not like that. If their source shone on them, they would vanish. Even now they are not truly real.** (M4) *How:* Angels and worlds come from a glow; when a higher light shone, angels ceased to be. 'So even while they exist, they are not true existence'. > וא"כ הרי גם בעת מציאותם אינם מציאות אמיתי (line 35566, OCR) - "so even while they exist they are not true existence"
5. **Souls are the opposite. A soul can take in even the essence's own light and not disappear.** (M12) *How:* Because the soul is 'an essential thing', it receives inner and essential lights in Torah, Gan Eden and the future, and is not nullified. > יכולה לקבל כל הגילוים גם אורות עצמיים (line 35570, OCR) - "it can receive all revelations, even essential lights"
6. **A soul stays the same in every world it goes down to. An essence does not run out or stop.** (M13) *How:* The soul does not change its essence in any world; 'the essence does not end, stop, or become nullified, ever'. > והעצם אינו נכלה ונפסק ואינו מתבטל לעולם (line 35571, OCR) - "and the essence does not run out, stop, or become void, ever"
7. **That G-d Himself should be a 'someone' is so hard that the Zohar calls it birth pains.** (M14) *How:* The 5659 hemshech: only the infinite power could make G-dliness itself be a 'yesh'; 'this is the hard labor'. > שז'"ע קושי הלידה כבי' שהאלקות עצמו יהי' בבחי' יש (line 14824, OCR) - "this is the hardship of birth, as it were: that G-dliness itself should be a something"

**Bottom.** You are not a ray that is about to vanish. You are His essence become someone (stated). **Return.** So only souls can bring His essence down, through Torah and mitzvos (35572); and they cannot be ruined: like a nut fallen into filth, washed and still good (35569) (stated).

## RM-C05 · People say the world is 'nothing' before G-d. What kind of nothing?

*Where:* Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah 5659 hemshech, lines 14829-14849

1. **There are two 'knowings'. From above: He is the something, and all is nothing. From below: we are something, and He is the nothing we came from.** (M3) *How:* 'G-d of knowings' (Shmuel I 2): da'as elyon and da'as tachton, two pictures of yesh and ayin. > והאין הוא בחי' האור א"ס שהוא בחי' גילוי העצם (line 14830, OCR) - "the 'nothing' is the light of the Infinite, the revelation of the essence"
2. **The first nothing: His light inside Him is so lost in Him that no name can be put on it at all.** (M2) *How:* Light 'that feels its source' feels that He alone is; so it is 'nothing' in itself, and nothing can be called by any name but 'nothing'. > אינו שייך להניח עליו שום שם כ"א אין (line 14835, OCR) - "no name can be placed on it except 'nothing'"
3. **The second nothing is different. We call our Source 'nothing' only because we cannot grasp it.** (M4) *How:* This 'ayin' is called so from our side: we do not know what it is, so we cannot name it, and it is 'as if it is not'. > ונקרא אין ע''יש שאינו מושג (line 14835, OCR) - "it is called nothing because it is not grasped"
4. **Your being here proves your Source is 'not a thing'. A thing cannot come from a thing forever.** (M14) *How:* Every something is limited, so it must have a start; before it, there was 'not a thing'. 'The creatures, by existing, force us to say the source is nothing, not a thing.' > נמצא שהנבראים במה שהם נמצאים במציאות יש, ה'"ה מכריחים שהמקור הוא בחי' אין ולא דבר (line 14846, OCR) - "the creatures, by existing as something, force us to say the source is 'nothing, not a thing'"
5. **But your being here also proves the Source is real. It truly is; it is not zero.** (M12) *How:* The same proof cuts both ways: the source exists, only not like us. 'It is existent, not utter nothing.' > אבל מ"מ הוא במציאות נמצא עכ"פ, ולא בבחי' אין ואפס לגמרי (line 14846, OCR) - "yet it is existent in any case, not utter nothing"
6. **And the two nothings are not the same. The nothing that is lost in Him cannot be a source of anything.** (M5) *How:* The 'nothing' of the true Something is so empty of being that it 'is not in the category of a source'. So the world comes from a lower nothing, which is 'something' compared to the first. > שהאין של היש האמיתי, מאחר שהוא בבחי' אין ואפס עד שאינו עולה בשם כלל, ממילא אינו בגדר להיות בבחי' מקור להוות (line 14837, OCR) - "the nothing of the true Something, being so void that it has no name, cannot be a source to create"

**Bottom.** Two nothings: the light lost in Him, nameless; and the Source we cannot grasp, which is (stated). **Return.** Back to your own existence: the fact that you are here, limited, is itself the proof that you come straight from Him (stated; the 'proof you can feel' framing is ours).

## RM-C06 · When I feel close to G-d I want to stay there forever. Why must I come back down?

*Where:* Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah 5672, lines 35490-35502

1. **Life inside you beats in two moves: out and back, like a heart. Light does the same.** (M9) *How:* Life in the body is always 'run and return', like the heartbeat's two motions; the light in the worlds is the same. > והו"ע דפיקו דליבא בב' תנועות (line 35490, OCR) - "this is the heart's beat, in two motions"
2. **Light runs upward because it is stuck to its source and can feel it.** (M6) *How:* Light is only a glow, joined to its source, so 'it feels its source' and always wants to be swallowed back in. > לפי שדבוק במקורו ונרגש בו מקורו (line 35491, OCR) - "for it is joined to its source and feels its source"
3. **Love that is only closeness is still just a feeling of Him. It does not quench the thirst, and it does not rest.** (M4) *How:* Even deep love is 'only a feeling of the existence of G-dliness'; so the soul 'does not quench its thirst and is not at rest'. > ולכן בהדביקות שבהאהבה אינו מרווה צמאונו ואינו נח ושקיט בנפשו (line 35496, OCR) - "in the cleaving of love one does not quench one's thirst and is not calm"
4. **A love that only wants to dissolve has no way back. **Quotes.** - ולזאת הנח"ר של האדון הוא הנח"ר שלו (line 21749, OCR) - "the master's pleasure is his pleasure" - כ"א מצד שהפקיר עצמו מכל וכל לגבי אדונו (line 21747, OCR) - "because he gave himself entirely to his master" - וואס עס פירט זעך אויס דער רצה"ע (line 21749, OCR) - "that the higher will gets carried out"

## RM-D9 · Only One, carrying all

**Teaching.** The deepest unity is not that everything is one in Him. He is single, 'einzig', and carries everything. The four praises climb to 'who is like You': His essence (stated).

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Climb the Midrash ladder: Yisro, Naaman, Rachav, Moshe 'even in the empty space' (8247). 2. Then the praises: none compares; none but You; nothing apart from You; who is like You (14496). 3. Drop the picture of all things merging. 4. See instead: One, carrying all, not mixed with them (14484). 5. And you: 'I am He and no other' (14482).

**From inside.** Spacious; carried rather than dissolved (ours). **Where it lands.** Body: the floor holding you. Now: carried. Deed: Shema. **Extends.** Extends D4 and ON's ladder with the Rashab's 'einzig'.

**Quotes.** - לא שכולם בו בבחי' אחדות, כ"א הוא בבחי' יחיד ממש, איינציג בעצם, שנושא את הכל (line 14484, OCR) - "not all in Him as one; He is single, einzig, carrying all" - ומי דומה לך הוא בחי' העצמות ממש (line 14496, OCR) - "'who is like You' is His essence" - משה שמו אף בחללו של עולם (line 8247, OCR) - "even in the empty space of the world"

## RM-D10 · You cannot measure your own depth

**Teaching.** Being good on the outside does not prove you reached the depth. Rabban Yochanan ben Zakai wept; Rebbi wept at a sinner's one hour. So 'be in your own eyes like a rasha', not to despair but to keep gathering yourself (stated).

**Hisbonenus.** 1. List what is good in you; do not deny it. 2. Ask: has all of me gathered into one will? (492) 3. Admit you do not know: 'I do not know my soul' (498). 4. Let this make you humble, not sad. 5. Pray again, step by step (489).

**From inside.** Honest, a little shaky, and free (ours). **Where it lands.** Body: lower the shoulders. Now: unknown. Deed: pray. **Extends.** New guard against spiritual pride; pairs with D14 (no bottom).

**Quotes.** - לא ידעתי את נפשי (line 498, OCR) - "I do not know my soul" - וזה הוראה שיש לו נשמה נמוכה (line 497, OCR) - "a sign he has a lower soul" - שנאספו ונקבצו אצלו כל הכחות אל הרצון פשוט (line 500, OCR) - "all his powers gathered into the simple will"

## RM-D11 · The bond with no feeling

**Teaching.** Real self-sacrifice has no delight and no seeking, only the bond. That want for G-d has no cause, shows by itself or when something opposes it, and never changes (stated).

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice a want for G-d you did not reason into. 2. When something tries to take it, watch it come out (30392). 3. Do not look for a feeling (30411). 4. Rest in 'it never changes' (30392).

**From inside.** Flat and solid, not emotional (stated). **Where it lands.** Body: the spine. Now: unchanged. Deed: keep the mitzvah even when dry. **Extends.** Sharpens D8: the bond itself is felt as no-feeling.

**Quotes.** - דבמסינ בפועל אין בזה שום הרגש עונג, ולא שום מבוקש כלל וכלל, כ"א מצד עצם ההתקשרות (line 30411, OCR) - "no delight and nothing sought, only the bond" - שאין שום סיבה כלל לרצון זה, ולא שום התעוררות כלל (line 30386, OCR) - "no reason at all for this will" - ואין בו שינוי ותמורה לעולם (line 30392, OCR) - "no change, ever"

## Avodah: what to do with it

1. Prayer is a ladder: begin with plain thanks (Modeh ani, Hodu), then wonder at creation (Pesukei Dezimra), then the angels' bittul, then Shema where all your powers gather into one will. Do not skip rungs (stated). > דא"א לבוא לבתי' רצון פשוט שלמעלי מטו"ד אם לא הגיע מתחלה לתכלית שלימות העבודה (line 489, OCR) - "one cannot reach the simple will unless one first completes the service with the revealed powers"
2. Contemplate daily, a little at a time, like a nurse feeding a baby, until it is 'as if you see' (stated). > צריך להרגיל בכל יום בהתבוננות ביטל היש לאין עד שנקבע הדבר בנפשו כאלו רואה (line 37078, OCR) - "train every day... until fixed as if he sees"
3. Use the physical 'to learn and pray with that strength', not to fill desire (stated). > היינו לא למלאות תאות נפשו כ"א שבכח זה ילמוד ויתפלל (line 35593, OCR) - "not to fill desire but to learn and pray with that strength"
4. With forbidden things say: 'I would, but my Father said no' (stated). > אל יאמר אדם אי אפשי בבשר חזיר אלא אפשי ומה אעשה אבי שבשמים גזר עלי (line 35594, OCR) - "I want it, but my Father forbade it"
5. Work on one trait at a time; when one is subdued, start the next (stated). > וכשיפעול בעצמו להכניע את הפרט הזה אז ייגע להכניע פרט שני (line 5680, OCR) - "subdue this detail, then a second"
6. In the day's busyness, turn to Mincha with extra force: 'er reist zich op' (stated). > ער רייסט זעך אֶפ (line 37081, OCR) - "he tears himself away"
7. Give yourself to Torah, not as a side thing: 'er zol zich avekgeben' (stated). > ער זאל זעך אוועק געבין אל התורה (line 8245, OCR) - "he should give himself over to the Torah"
8. Even if you feel nothing in exile, keep working at 'G-d is One'; it brings some bittul (stated). > מ"מ צריך לייגע א"ע בהשגת ה' אחד ובהתקשרות לההשגה (line 27970, OCR) - "one must toil to grasp G-d is One"
9. Bow at Modim; even physical bowing is part of the repair (stated). > דכרע במודים (line 35488, OCR) - "who bowed at Modim"
10. Make room for others: holiness gives room; senseless hatred is just ego (Heichaltzu) (stated). > ה"ה נותן מקום אל הזולת. וענין מה שנותן מקום היינו שהוא מקבל את הזולת ומתאחד עמו (line 16270, OCR) - "gives room to the other and receives him and unites with him"
