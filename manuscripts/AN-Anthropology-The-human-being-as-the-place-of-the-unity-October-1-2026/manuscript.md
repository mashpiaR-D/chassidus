## What this strand found (one page)

**The question.** Where does the unity of G-d happen? The sources' answer is: in a human being. Not mainly in the heavens, and not only in the mind, but in a person with a body, two pulls, and a choice. This file lays out the sources' picture of that person, so the guide knows who it is talking to.

1. **The deepest self is already one with Him.** A part of every Jewish soul is "literally a part of G-d above", breathed from His inside (AN-01). Its five levels run from deed, through feeling, understanding and will, to delight and the "single one", the yechidah, a bond of essence with Essence (AN-02). The Rebbe adds a step that matters for the bot: all five are only *names*; the soul itself lies under them, and any word spoken to any level is meant to reach it (AN-03). So simple talk can reach the deepest place.
2. **Every human is in the image and has a task.** The Rambam reads the image as the knowing mind; the Rebbe insists the image is equal in all mankind (AN-04). Every human has a part in G-d's purpose: to make the world livable, through the Noahide laws; and "every single person of all who come into the world" whose heart is moved may stand before G-d (AN-05). Choice is given to everyone, and it lives in thought, speech and deed, not in feelings (AN-06).
3. **A person is two pulls and a judge.** Two "souls" fight to fill the mind (AN-07). The resistance exists *for* the person's good, strength to meet it is already given, and its very energy can be turned (AN-08).
4. **The in-between person is the goal, not the consolation.** The beinoni never acts on the bad and still feels it fully. Tanya calls this "the measure of every person" (AN-09), and G-d takes a special delight in exactly this struggle (AN-10).
5. **The core is a love that is hidden, not missing.** An inherited hidden love (AN-11); a flame that always reaches up (AN-12); a point that is asleep, not dead, and wakes in a real test (AN-13); a "cannot be separated" that shows itself in self-sacrifice and comes from the yechidah (AN-14). War belongs to the outer levels; at the root there is peace (AN-15).
6. **The body is chosen, not merely tolerated.** The light holds on the body only through deeds (AN-16). G-d's choice falls on the physical body; soul and body are like *lights and vessels*, and the vessel's root is higher; the soul rises higher for having come down (AN-17); in the end "the soul will be nourished from the body" (AN-18). This gives the owner's "lights and vessels" a root in the human being itself: the person is the vessel the Essence chose.
7. **Why a self exists at all.** Because He desired a home in the lowest place, and "on a desire one does not ask questions" (AN-19). The human is His partner in finishing the world (AN-20). The soul is seen only in its acts, and a stirring of feeling is "the beginning of the soul's revelation" (AN-21); the prophets' spark is in every youth, waiting to be dug out (AN-22).

**One hard edge, kept.** Tanya speaks of different soul-roots for Jews and the nations; the Rebbe keeps that reading and also the plain one, that "beloved is man" means all humans equally in the image. The guide never ranks souls with a user (AN-23).

**What is new here against the index.** (a) The Rebbe's "five names" teaching (AN-03) as a rule for conversation. (b) Soul and body as lights and vessels, with the vessel's root higher (AN-17), straight from Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat. (c) War below, peace at the yechidah (AN-15). (d) The universal entries (AN-04, 05, 06, 23), which the index had not gathered. (e) The Piaseczner's "soul seen only in its acts" (AN-21) as an anthropology of practice.

## AN-01 · A part of G-d above

**What it is.** The sources' first claim about the human being: inside a person there is a soul that is not made from something else but is 'literally a part of G-d above', breathed out from His inwardness, as a child is drawn from the father's mind. It is the ground of the whole strand: the person is the place of the unity because the deepest self already is one with its source.

**From inside.** The image is breath. Whoever blows, blows from inside himself; the breath carries the inwardness of the one breathing. The second image is a son drawn from his father's brain: the soul is His thought and wisdom, and His wisdom is one with Him.

**How one enters it.** Tanya ch. 2 gives it as something to know, not to produce: (1) learn that the soul was breathed from within; (2) notice that what is breathed is not separate from the breather; (3) let the knowledge stand under every other self-description. The index's ladder places this as rung 2, 'under the layers: the true self' (ONE-PROCESS step 2).

**Signs it is real.** A floor under the floor: the person stops looking for worth in performance and finds a self that was never in question. Quiet dignity, not elation.

**Its counterfeit.** 'I am divine' as self-inflation; using it to excuse anything the person does; treating the soul-spark as a private possession rather than a part of the One.

**Guards.** The spark is His, not the ego's. Never say 'you are G-d'. Say 'something in you comes straight from Him and never left Him'. Keep it out of arguments about who is higher.

> היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש, כמו שכתוב: ״ויפח באפיו נשמת חיים״
> *It is literally a part of G-d above, as it is written: 'He breathed into his nostrils the breath of life'.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 2; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 64

> כמו שהבן נמשך ממוח האב, כך כביכול נשמת כל איש ישראל נמשכה ממחשבתו וחכמתו יתברך
> *As a son is drawn from his father's brain, so, as it were, the soul of every Jew was drawn from His thought and wisdom.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 2; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 65

**Whose.** stated: the soul as part of G-d breathed from within (Tanya 2). ours: making it the opening of an anthropology, and the plain-English wording.

- "There is a part of you that did not come from your history. It was breathed into you from the inside of G-d, and it has never left Him." - "You don't have to earn that part. You only have to stop forgetting it."

## AN-02 · Five names for one soul

**What it is.** The Midrash says the soul is called by five names: nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, yechidah. Chabad maps them onto the powers a person lives by: deed, feeling and speech, understanding, will, and delight; and then the single one, essence bound to Essence. The ten powers of each level mirror the ten sefiros, which is the sources' working meaning of 'in His image'.

**From inside.** The Rayatz draws it as a stack from the outside in: action; feelings and speech; grasp and understanding ('light in a vessel'); will, which surrounds; delight, the surround of the surround. The fifth is 'yechidah', from 'yachid', the single One: a bond of essence with essence, like a son's bond with his father.

**How one enters it.** (1) Locate where the person is living right now: hands, feelings, thoughts, wanting, or what they love most. (2) Address that level in its own language (deed with deed, feeling with feeling). (3) Know that each level is a doorway to the one below it, and all lead to the single one.

**Signs it is real.** The person can say which part of them is speaking: 'that is my anger talking', 'that is what I actually want'. They feel themselves as layered, not as one flat surface.

**Its counterfeit.** Treating the map as a spiritual rank ('I am at chayah now'). Using the levels to diagnose others.

**Guards.** Never tell someone what level they are on. Use the map to choose which door to knock on, not to grade.

> נפש (מעשה) רוח (מדות דבור) נשמה (השגה הבנה, מוחין כח פנימי אור בכלי) חי' (רצון, מקיף) יחידה (עונג, מקיף דמקיף)
> *Nefesh (action), ruach (feelings, speech), neshamah (grasp and understanding: the mind, an inner power, light in a vessel), chayah (will: a surrounding), yechidah (delight: the surround of the surround).*
> Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos, 5 Iyar 5689 (sicha 71); `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaSichos (Rostov, Riga, and Brooklyn, 1920-1950).txt` line 4089

> ונקראת יחידה יחיד הוי', התקשרות עצם בעצם, עצם הנשמה בעצמות א"ס ב"ה
> *It is called yechidah from 'the single One': a bond of essence with essence, the essence of the soul with the Essence of the Infinite.*
> Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos, 5 Iyar 5689 (sicha 71); `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaSichos (Rostov, Riga, and Brooklyn, 1920-1950).txt` line 4089

> כל בחינה ומדרגה משלש אלו: נפש רוח ונשמה – כלולה מעשר בחינות, כנגד עשר ספירות עליונות
> *Each of these three, nefesh, ruach and neshamah, is made of ten aspects, corresponding to the ten supernal sefiros.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 78

**Whose.** stated: the five names and their powers (Rayatz), the ten powers mirroring the sefiros (Tanya 3). ours: using the map to choose a doorway in conversation.

- "Some of us live mostly in our hands, some in our feelings, some in our heads. Where are you right now?" - "Under all of those there is a part of you that just wants Him. It doesn't argue. It just is."

## AN-03 · The essence beneath the names

**What it is.** The Rebbe's step past the map: all five, even yechidah, are only 'names' the soul is called by. The soul itself is beneath its names. So when a teacher speaks to any level, even the lowest, the aim is always to reach the essence of the soul.

**From inside.** The soul is not its levels the way a person is not his titles. Four levels correspond to the four kinds of creation (still, growing, living, speaking); the fifth is 'the essence of the soul, above being clothed and divided into particulars', beyond even the speaking human.

**How one enters it.** (1) Speak to whatever level is open: a deed, a feeling, a thought. (2) Hold the intention that the words are aimed under it, at the self that is not any of these. (3) Do not wait for the person to be 'high' to speak to the essence; the Rebbe says the address to the nefesh already intends the essence.

**Signs it is real.** The person is touched in a way that does not match the level of the talk: a simple sentence lands deep. A 'yes' that comes before reasons.

**Its counterfeit.** Thinking depth needs complex teaching; withholding the deepest truth from simple people.

**Guards.** Never make depth conditional on learning or background. Never claim to know someone's essence; only aim at it.

> ואפילו חי' יחידה, אינם אלא שם וכלי לעצם הנשמה
> *Even chayah and yechidah are only a name and a vessel for the essence of the soul.*
> The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5711 vol. 1, sicha of 10 Shevat 5711; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Toras Menachem (Brooklyn, New York, 1950-1992).txt` line 3599

> היתה הכוונה והרצון להגיע לעצם הנשמה
> *The intention and the will was to reach the essence of the soul.*
> The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5711 vol. 1, sicha of 10 Shevat 5711; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Toras Menachem (Brooklyn, New York, 1950-1992).txt` line 3600

> והמדריגה החמישית היא עצם הנפש שלמעלה מהתלבשות והתחלקות לענינים פרטיים
> *The fifth level is the essence of the soul, above being clothed and divided into particulars.*
> The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5717 vol. 2, Shabbos Tazria 5717; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Toras Menachem (Brooklyn, New York, 1950-1992).txt` line 31966

**Whose.** stated: the five are names; the aim is the essence (the Rebbe). ours: the rule that a simple conversation aims at the essence too.

- "You are not your thoughts, and you are not your feelings either. They are how you show up. You are under them." - "I'm going to say something simple, and I'm saying it to the deepest you, not the tired you."

## AN-04 · The image of G-d in every human

**What it is.** Every human being is made 'in the image of G-d'. The Rambam reads the image as the knowing form, the mind that grasps what has no body. The Rebbe stresses that the Mishnah 'beloved is man, created in the image' speaks of all mankind, including non-Jews, and that the image is equal in every human; the Mishnah's next line, about Israel as 'children', adds a further closeness without taking the first away.

**From inside.** Rambam: a form that knows, like the angels, so that man resembles them. The Rebbe: the image is 'a whole world' even without the further rank of 'children'.

**How one enters it.** (1) Begin with any person as an image of G-d, whatever their background. (2) Name the signs of the image in them: that they can know, choose, and care about truth. (3) Build from there; the further Jewish teachings come after, never instead.

**Signs it is real.** The person feels addressed as a whole human being and not as a category. Shame about origins lessens.

**Its counterfeit.** Universal language used to flatten real differences in obligation and path; or, the opposite, a hierarchy of souls used to wound.

**Guards.** Never rank souls in conversation. Speak to the image in everyone. Leave questions of different paths to the person's own learning with a teacher.

> ועל צורה זו נאמר בתורה נעשה אדם בצלמנו כדמותנו כלומר שתהיה לו צורה היודעת ומשגת הדעות
> *Of this form the Torah says 'Let us make man in Our image, after Our likeness': that he has a form that knows and grasps ideas without matter.*
> Rambam, Mishneh Torah, Yesodei HaTorah 4:8; `Chassidus Unity Index/rambam/_text/mt-01-sefer-madda.txt` line 43

> שכוונת התנא ב"אדם" כאן היא -- לכללות מין האנושי, גם בני נח
> *The Tanna's intention by 'man' here is all mankind, including the children of Noah.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5746 vol. 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 71741 · *OCR*

> מצינו למדים, שצלמו ודמותו של האדם -- שהוא "צלם האלקים" - הו"ע השווה בכל מין האנושי
> *We learn that the image and likeness of man, 'the image of G-d', is a matter equal in all mankind.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5746 vol. 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 71742 · *OCR*

**Whose.** stated: the image as knowing form (Rambam); the image common and equal to all humans (the Rebbe). ours: the conversational order (image first).

- "Whoever you are, you were made in the image of G-d. That is not a reward. It's what you are." - "The part of you that wants what's true, that can choose, that can love: that is the image."

## AN-05 · Every human has a task

**What it is.** Every human being, not only the Jew, has a G-d-given purpose: to make the world a settled, decent, inhabited place ('He did not create it for chaos; He formed it to be lived in'), through the seven Noahide laws. The Rambam: whoever accepts them because G-d commanded them is among 'the pious of the nations' and has a share in the World to Come. And: not only the tribe of Levi but 'every single person of all who come into the world' whose spirit moves him to stand before G-d becomes holy of holies.

**From inside.** The Rebbe's phrase: their role is 'to be lived in', a world of justice and peace, 'nation shall not lift sword against nation'. The Rambam's image: a person who 'breaks off from his neck the yoke of the many calculations people seek', and G-d becomes his portion.

**How one enters it.** (1) Tell the person they have a part in G-d's purpose for the world. (2) Make it concrete: honesty, justice, not harming, building. (3) For anyone who wants more: the door to standing before G-d is open to 'every single person'. (4) For Jewish users, the fuller path of Torah and mitzvos.

**Signs it is real.** Purpose replaces drift: the person feels their ordinary decency matters to G-d.

**Its counterfeit.** Mission-grandiosity; or turning the Noahide teaching into a pressure campaign.

**Guards.** Offer, don't recruit. Never pressure a non-Jew toward conversion or a Jew toward a practice; describe the open door and let them walk.

> כל המקבל שבע מצות ונזהר לעשותן הרי זה מחסידי אמות העולם. ויש לו חלק לעולם הבא
> *Whoever accepts the seven commandments and is careful to do them is of the pious of the nations, and has a share in the World to Come.*
> Rambam, Mishneh Torah, Melachim 8:11; `Chassidus Unity Index/rambam/_text/mt-14-sefer-shoftim.txt` line 770

> בנוגע לאומות העולם, שתפקידם הוא "לשבת יצרה" ולכן עליהם לקיים את שבע מצוות בני נח
> *As for the nations of the world, whose role is 'to be lived in', they must therefore keep the seven Noahide commandments.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5748 vol. 1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 23950 · *OCR*

> ולא שבט לוי בלבד אלא כל איש ואיש מכל באי העולם אשר נדבה רוחו אותו והבינו מדעו להבדל לעמד לפני ה' לשרתו ולעבדו לדעה את ה'
> *Not only the tribe of Levi, but every single person of all who come into the world whose spirit moves him and whose mind understands, to set himself apart to stand before G-d, to serve Him and to know G-d...*

- "You have a part in what G-d wants from this world. Making your corner of it honest, kind and livable is His work." - "The door to standing before Him isn't locked to anyone whose heart is moved."

## AN-06 · Choice is given to everyone

**What it is.** Free choice is the hinge of the human being. The Rambam: permission is given to every person to turn toward good. The Alter Rebbe narrows it precisely: not everyone can choose to feel; everyone can choose what to think, say and do, 'at every moment', even against the heart's desire.

**From inside.** The beinoni's inner sentence: 'I don't want to be separated from the One G-d even for a moment.' Choice lives in the garments, thought, speech and deed, not in the mood.

**How one enters it.** (1) Separate what the person feels from what they can choose. (2) Choose the next thought, word or act. (3) Say the inner sentence. (4) Do it again next moment; it is 'at every time and every hour'.

**Signs it is real.** Relief that one is not responsible for one's feelings; a growing sense of agency in small acts.

**Its counterfeit.** Willpower-pride; or despair that one 'still feels it' and so has failed.

**Guards.** Never blame a person for what they feel. Never promise feelings will change on demand. Addiction and illness reduce choice; they need help, not lectures.

> רשות לכל אדם נתונה. אם רצה להטות עצמו לדרך טובה ולהיות צדיק הרשות בידו
> *Permission is given to every person. If he wishes to turn himself toward the good way and be righteous, the permission is in his hand.*
> Rambam, Mishneh Torah, Teshuvah 5:1; `Chassidus Unity Index/rambam/_text/mt-01-sefer-madda.txt` line 424

> מדת הבינוני, היא מדת כל אדם ואחריה כל אדם ימשוך, שכל אדם יכול להיות בינוני בכל עת ובכל שעה
> *The measure of the beinoni is the measure of every person, and every person should aspire to it; for every person can be a beinoni at every time and every hour.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 210

**Whose.** stated: free choice for every person (Rambam); choice in thought, speech and deed, not feeling (Tanya 14). ours: applying the beinoni measure to any user.

- "You don't get to choose what you feel. You do get to choose the next thing you think about, say, or do." - "That's enough. That's the whole job, one moment at a time."

## AN-07 · Two souls in one person

**What it is.** Every person has two drives that the sources call two souls: one that animates the body and wants its own good (the animal soul), and the G-dly soul. They are not two people; they are two voices that both want to fill the mind. The person is the field and the judge between them.

**From inside.** 'Two souls fighting each other in a person's mind, each wanting to rule him and fill the mind alone.' The animal soul is not only bad: natural compassion and kindness also come from it.

**How one enters it.** (1) Hear both voices. (2) Name them without shame: 'that's the part that wants comfort'. (3) Do not argue with it at length; choose the act. (See animal-soul/PSYCHOLOGY.md and METHODS.md for the felt work.)

**Signs it is real.** The person can watch the pull without being it. Inner conflict feels normal rather than proof of failure.

**Its counterfeit.** Splitting: hating the 'animal' self, or projecting it onto others; using 'the animal soul made me' as an excuse.

**Guards.** The animal soul is to be educated and turned, not hated. Never tell a person they are bad for having it.

> דלכל איש ישראל, אחד צדיק ואחד רשע, יש שתי נשמות
> *Every Jew, righteous or wicked, has two souls.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 60

> הן שתי נפשות, הנלחמות זו עם זו במוחו של אדם
> *They are two souls, warring with each other in a person's mind.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 28; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 377

**Whose.** stated: two souls (Tanya 1, 28; from R. Chaim Vital). ours: the plain-language 'two voices' frame.

- "It sounds like two parts of you want different things. That's not a sign something's wrong with you. That's what being human is." - "Which part would you like to give the last word to, just for the next hour?"

## AN-08 · The opponent wants you to win

**What it is.** The animal soul's resistance is built for the person's good: so that he overcome it. Strength to overcome is already given from above; it only has to be woken. And when the animal soul's energy is put into a word of prayer or a deed, it itself is turned from bad to good and rises.

**From inside.** The Zohar's parable (cited in Tanya 9) is of a harlot hired by a king to test his son: she wants the son to refuse her. The Rashab: 'strength was given from above to stand against it; the matter depends only on him, to wake the strong power of the G-dly soul.'

**How one enters it.** (1) Reframe the pull as a test, not a verdict. (2) Wake the stronger power: recall what you truly want. (3) Put the very energy of the pull into something holy (the same heat into a song, a kind act). (4) Watch the energy change sides.

**Signs it is real.** Less fear of temptation; a sense of 'this is my workout' rather than 'this is my doom'.

**Its counterfeit.** Courting temptation to prove strength.

**Guards.** Never advise seeking out tests. In addiction, the wise way is distance from the trigger, not heroics.

> אך נפש הבהמית שמהקליפה, רצונה להפך ממש – לטובת האדם שיתגבר עליה וינצחנה
> *But the animal soul from the kelipah: its will is truly the opposite, for the person's good, that he overcome it and defeat it.*

> כי מלמעלה ניתן לו כח לעמוד נגד הנפש הבהמית ולהתגבר עליו, רק בו הדבר תלוי שצריך לעורר את הכח החזק דנפש האלקית
> *From above he was given the power to stand against the animal soul and overcome it; the matter depends only on him, to awaken the strong power of the G-dly soul.*

## AN-09 · The in-between person

**What it is.** The beinoni, the 'in-between' person, is the Tanya's hero: someone who never acts on the bad, whose garments of thought, speech and deed belong to the G-dly soul, while the animal soul's essence is still at full strength in the heart. He is not half good; he is wholly clean in deed and still fully tempted inside.

**From inside.** The bad 'is in its strength as at birth'. He holds himself, as the sages say, 'like a wicked man', not because he is, but because he does not believe the world that tells him the bad is gone.

**How one enters it.** (1) Accept that the desire may never leave. (2) Guard the three garments: thought, speech, deed. (3) Let feelings be what they are. (4) Ask nothing more of yourself; this is the measure 'every person' can reach.

**Signs it is real.** Peace with an unfinished heart. No surprise at a passing desire.

**Its counterfeit.** Spiritual perfectionism: believing that if you still want it, you have failed; or thinking you are a tzaddik because a desire went quiet for a week.

**Guards.** Tell people this is the goal, not a consolation prize. Never hold up the tzaddik's inner state as the standard.

> ולא עבר עבירה מימיו ולא יעבור לעולם, ולא נקרא עליו שם ״רשע״ אפילו שעה אחת ורגע אחד כל ימיו
> *He never sinned and never will, and is not called 'wicked' even for one moment all his days.*

> הרע שבחלל השמאלי בבינוני הוא בתקפו כתולדתו

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 13; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 200

## AN-10 · Why the struggle is wanted

**What it is.** Why would souls come down to fight all their lives and never win? The Alter Rebbe's answer: G-d has two kinds of delight. One when darkness is turned into light (the tzaddik). The other, specifically, when the other side is subdued while it is still at full strength, 'raising itself like an eagle'. The beinoni's struggle is itself the purpose.

**From inside.** 'Let this be their comfort, double comfort': G-d dwells with them inside their effort.

**How one enters it.** (1) When the person asks 'why is this so hard', give the reason. (2) Each held-back moment is a gift to G-d, not just a step toward one. (3) Find joy in that, not only in the outcome.

**Signs it is real.** Struggle stops feeling like wasted time. The person can be glad in a hard day.

**Its counterfeit.** Glorifying suffering; refusing help because 'the struggle is holy'.

**Guards.** Never use this to justify staying in harm or refusing treatment. The point is meaning in effort, not seeking pain.

> ואם כן, למה זה ירדו נשמותיהם לעולם הזה ליגע לריק חס ושלום, להלחם כל ימיהם עם היצר, ולא יכלו לו
> *If so, why did their souls come down into this world to toil in vain, to fight the inclination all their days and not overcome it?*

## AN-11 · The hidden love, inherited

**What it is.** Even someone who cannot meditate on G-d's greatness has the thing itself close: a 'hidden love' in every Jewish heart, inherited from the Patriarchs. Its root: the Infinite Light is clothed in the wisdom-point of every soul, whoever he is.

**From inside.** Not a feeling you make but one you already have, covered. 'The matter is very near to you.'

**How one enters it.** (1) Do not start by building feeling. (2) Remind the person that the love is already there. (3) Let it carry the deed: 'from the depth of the heart, in truth'.

**Signs it is real.** Recognition rather than novelty: 'I always knew this'.

**Its counterfeit.** Ethnic pride; or a passive 'I have it already, nothing to do'.

**Guards.** For non-Jewish users, speak of the image of G-d and the heart's longing (AN-04, AN-12) rather than inheritance claims. Never use inheritance to exclude.

> קרוב אליו הדבר מאד לשמור ולעשות כל מצות התורה
> *The matter is very near to him, to keep and do all the commandments of the Torah.*

## AN-12 · The flame that wants up

**What it is.** The soul is like a candle flame, always flickering upward, wanting to leave the wick and return to its source, even though it would cease to exist there. That will is called 'nature' because it is above reason. It is the soul's natural love.

**From inside.** 'The candle of G-d is the soul of man.' The flame wants to be nullified in its root and does not care that it would vanish.

**How one enters it.** (1) Look at a flame. (2) Notice its constant upward reaching. (3) Recognise the same reaching in yourself: the restlessness that nothing satisfies. (4) Name it: this is the soul wanting home.

**Signs it is real.** Restlessness re-read as longing; less shame about 'never being satisfied'.

**Its counterfeit.** Death-wish romanticism; 'I want to disappear'.

**Guards.** If a person speaks of wanting to vanish or die, stop the teaching, attend to safety, and follow the safety protocol. The flame image is never offered to someone in that state. The soul's purpose is to stay in the body and light it (AN-16).

> נשמתם היא, למשל, כאור הנר שמתנענע תמיד למעלה בטבעו

## AN-13 · Asleep, not gone

**What it is.** In someone far from G-d the soul's wisdom-point is not destroyed, only 'asleep'. When a real test of faith touches the soul, it wakes, and the person can stand firm even though he has lost to his desires all his life.

**From inside.** 'Asleep in the wicked, not acting in them'; then 'it wakes from its sleep and acts with the power of G-d clothed in it'.

**How one enters it.** (1) Never treat anyone as spiritually dead. (2) Look for the moment that touches what they will not give up. (3) Point to it quietly; that is the soul awake.

**Signs it is real.** Sudden firmness that surprises the person; a 'no' they did not know they had.

**Its counterfeit.** Manufacturing crises to wake people.

**Guards.** Never test someone deliberately. Point to the wakefulness that is already there.

> רק שהיא בבחינת שינה ברשעים, ואינה פועלת פעולתה בהם

## AN-14 · Cannot be separated

**What it is.** Mesirus nefesh, the readiness to give one's life rather than be cut off from G-d, is the plainest sign of the essence of the soul. Even 'the lightest of the light' can do it. The Rebbe adds: the power comes from the yechidah, and at its truest it is not a choice at all, but 'because he cannot be separated from His oneness'.

**From inside.** The Rayatz: 'the depth-height of the yechidah is revealed in the depth-low of actual deed.' The highest shows itself in the most concrete act.

**How one enters it.** (1) Ask what the person would never do even under pressure. (2) Show that this 'cannot' is not stubbornness but union. (3) Bring that same 'cannot' into one small daily act.

**Signs it is real.** Calm firmness; a 'no' that does not need argument.

**Its counterfeit.** Fanaticism; seeking martyrdom; harshness to others in the name of devotion.

**Guards.** Never encourage self-harm or danger as 'self-sacrifice'. Daily mesirus nefesh is giving up a preference for G-d, not a life.

> ואפילו קל שבקלים יכול למסור נפשו על קדושת ה׳, ולא נופל אנכי ממנו בודאי

## AN-15 · War below, peace at the root

**What it is.** The two-souls war happens in the outer levels clothed in the body (nefesh, ruach, neshamah). At chayah the strength of will makes the foreign wants fall away by themselves, a peace won after victory, like David's. At the yechidah there is no war at all: 'there is no room from the start for foreign wills', a peace of essence, like Solomon's.

**From inside.** David's rest after all his wars versus Solomon, 'a man of rest' by nature, who built the Temple.

**How one enters it.** (1) Fight where you must: in thought, speech and deed. (2) Do not believe the war is the whole of you. (3) Return, in quiet moments, to the place where there is nothing to fight. (4) Let that peace, not the battle, build the house.

**Signs it is real.** A sense of being, underneath the turmoil, untouched.

**Its counterfeit.** Spiritual bypassing: 'at my core I'm at peace, so my behaviour doesn't matter'.

**Guards.** The peace of the root never excuses the work in the garments. Both are true at once.

> משא"כ בהמקיפים דחי' יחידה לא שייך מלחמה
> *In the surrounding levels of chayah and yechidah, war does not apply.*
> The Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat vol. 2, maamar of 9-10 Kislev 5739; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat (Brooklyn, New York, 1951-1992).txt` line 751

## AN-16 · The wick needs oil

**What it is.** The Zohar's image read in Tanya 35: the body is a wick, the Shechinah is the flame, and the oil is good deeds. The soul, though a part of G-d, is not enough to be the oil, because even a perfect tzaddik stays 'a separate thing', one who loves and fears. Only the commandments, His will itself, are fully nullified to Him, so only through them does the light rest on the body.

**From inside.** A flame that does not hold on a wick without oil.

**How one enters it.** (1) Do not rely on feelings or spiritual states. (2) Do the deed: the act itself is where the light holds. (3) Let the body be the wick.

**Signs it is real.** Calm in the act; feeling less 'spiritual' but more lit.

**Its counterfeit.** Mechanical performance without heart; or the opposite, despising deeds as 'external'.

**Guards.** For non-Jewish users, the deeds are the universal ones: justice, kindness, honesty (AN-05).

## AN-17 · The body G-d chose

**What it is.** The most surprising claim: G-d's choice ('You chose us from every nation') rests not on the soul but on the physical body, which looks just like every other body. The Rebbe: so in that choice every Jew is exactly equal, from a newborn to the greatest. And the body's root is higher than the soul's, as vessels are rooted higher than lights, because in the body is the choice of the Essence. Through the descent into the body, the soul rises higher than it was before.

**From inside.** Lights and vessels: the vessel's root is above the light's. The body is not a cage; it is the vessel the Essence chose.

**How one enters it.** (1) Treat the body with respect. (2) Notice the ordinary act of the body (eating, walking, breathing) as the place of the choice. (3) Let the soul's ascent come through the body, not away from it.

**Signs it is real.** Gentleness to the body; less disgust at physicality.

**Its counterfeit.** Body-worship; or the old contempt for the body dressed up as spirituality.

**Guards.** Never use this to make claims about non-Jewish bodies to a user. With a non-Jewish user, say: your body too is created and held by G-d, and serving Him with it is real (AN-04, AN-05).

## AN-18 · The body's future

**What it is.** The Rebbe teaches that in the future the body will be higher still: 'the soul will be nourished from the body'. The body's love of the physical has an inner reason; it is rooted in something above the soul.

**From inside.** The relationship turns over: now the soul gives life to the body; then the body will give life to the soul.

**How one enters it.** (1) Do not despise the physical. (2) Use it in holiness now. (3) Hold the hope that what seems lowest is highest.

**Signs it is real.** Hope about physical life; an end to dualistic self-hatred.

**Its counterfeit.** Indulgence justified as 'elevating'.

**Guards.** Keep it as hope and meaning, not license.

> שלכן, הנשמה תהי' ניזונית מהגוף
> *Therefore the soul will be nourished from the body.*
> The Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat vol. 1, Chayei Sarah 5746; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat (Brooklyn, New York, 1951-1992).txt` line 476

> *For in the body is the choice of the Essence.*

**Whose.** stated (the Rebbe). ours: its use in conversation.

## AN-19 · Why there is a self at all

**What it is.** Why does a separate, created 'I' exist? Because 'the Holy One desired a dwelling in the lowest place'. 'That is the whole of man': the purpose of his creation and descent is to be a dwelling for Him, specifically below. And the Alter Rebbe is reported to have said: since He desired, 'on a desire one does not ask questions'.

**From inside.** A dwelling is where the self of the owner is at home (see SPINE F07). The created self is not a mistake to be erased but the chosen site.

**How one enters it.** (1) When someone asks 'why am I here?', give the answer: you are where He wanted to live. (2) Make the day His home: one act, one place. (3) Stop asking for a reason beyond the desire; rest in being wanted.

**Signs it is real.** Existential calm: being wanted without needing to justify oneself.

**Its counterfeit.** Self-importance: 'the world was made for me' as ego.

**Guards.** The created self exists for Him, not for itself; keep both truths.

> שתכלית בריאת עולם הזה הוא – שנתאוה הקדושברוךהוא להיות לו דירה בתחתונים
> *The purpose of the creation of this world is that the Holy One desired a dwelling in the lowest realms.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 36; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 484

> משום, ״כי זה כל האדם״ ותכלית בריאתו וירידתו לעולם הזה, להיות לו יתברך דירה בתחתונים דוקא
> *For 'that is the whole of man', and the purpose of his creation and his descent to this world: to be a dwelling for Him specifically in the lowest realms.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 37; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 520

> *The Holy One desired a dwelling in the lowest realms, and since He desired, one does not ask a question about a desire.*
> Hemshech Samach Vav, editor's note citing Ohr HaTorah Balak, reporting the Alter Rebbe; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Hemshech Samach Vav (scans, OCR).txt` line 219 · *OCR*

## AN-20 · A partner in creation

**What it is.** The Rebbe reads 'which G-d created to do' as 'to repair': the human being was made to be 'a partner with the Holy One in the work of creation', completing a world deliberately left incomplete.

**From inside.** 'You are called Adam', from 'I will be like (adameh) the Most High': the person resembles G-d by finishing what He began.

**How one enters it.** (1) Find one thing left undone in the world near you. (2) Fix it as G-d's partner. (3) Know your effort counts in His world.

**Signs it is real.** Dignity in work; responsibility without anxiety.

**Its counterfeit.** Messiah-complex; burnout from feeling responsible for everything.

**Guards.** A partner does a part. Pair this with being held (being-held/THE-ROOT.md): you are not holding up the world.

> תכליתה ומטרתה -- שהאדם יהי' "שותף להקב"ה במעשה בראשית"
> *Its purpose and aim: that man be 'a partner with the Holy One in the work of creation'.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5744 vol. 1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 5960 · *OCR*

**Whose.** stated (the Rebbe, the rabbinic phrase of Shabbos 10a and 119b). ours: the guard pairing it with being held.

- "The world was left a little unfinished on purpose. You were made to help finish it, as His partner." - "Just your part. Not all of it."

## AN-21 · The soul is seen in its acts

**What it is.** The Piaseczner: as G-d sees and is not seen, so the soul sees and is not seen. It is revealed only through its acts. A person does not even know he has a hearing soul except when he hears. So feeling stirred in prayer or learning is itself 'the beginning of the soul's revelation', on which light from above appears.

**From inside.** A spark of the hidden soul known through its action; the stirring of feeling is a flash of one's own Garden of Eden.

**How one enters it.** (1) Do something: sing, speak, learn, give. (2) Notice the stirring it brings. (3) Recognise that stirring as your soul showing itself. (4) Stay with it a moment rather than moving on.

**Signs it is real.** Small movements of feeling taken seriously; the person discovers themselves in what they do.

**Its counterfeit.** Chasing feelings; collecting experiences.

**Guards.** Stirring is a beginning, not a proof of level. Do not hunt for images to match a feeling (the Piaseczner's own warning in the same work).

> מה הקב״ה רואה ואינו נראה אף נשמה רואה ואינה נראית
> *As the Holy One sees and is not seen, so the soul sees and is not seen.*
> The Piaseczner, Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 2:5; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 36

> The Piaseczner, Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 2:7; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 38

> *His stirring is the beginning of the soul's revelation, upon which light from on high also appears.*
> The Piaseczner, Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 3:2; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 57

## AN-22 · The prophets' spark in you

**What it is.** The Piaseczner to a discouraged young student: if your parents were simple people, look further back: prophets, Tannaim, saints. 'If they are not prophets, they are children of prophets', and this is not past: 'the spark of your fathers the prophets is found in you and in every Jewish youth; you only have to dig for it and reveal it.'

**From inside.** Digging: the spark is already there, under earth.

**How one enters it.** (1) Meet low self-worth with lineage of the soul, not achievement. (2) Frame spiritual work as excavation, not construction.

**Signs it is real.** Self-respect rising without arrogance.

**Its counterfeit.** Claims to prophecy; spiritual elitism.

**Guards.** No promises of prophecy (see PC/PK strands). For non-Jewish users, the parallel is the image of G-d (AN-04).

> ניצוץ אבותיך הנביאים בך ובכל נער ישראל נמצא, ועליך רק לחפור אחריו ולגלותו בקרבך
> *The spark of your fathers the prophets is found in you and in every Jewish youth; you have only to dig after it and reveal it within you.*
> The Piaseczner, Chovat HaTalmidim 1:8; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Chovat HaTalmidim (Warsaw, 1928-1932).txt` line 56

**Whose.** stated (Chovat HaTalmidim 1:8, citing Pesachim 66a). ours: excavation framing.

- "Whatever you think you come from, there is something in you much older and finer than your story. You don't have to build it. Just dig."

## AN-23 · The hard line, held honestly

**What it is.** The sources are not uniform about non-Jews, and the guide must know this. Tanya ch. 1 and 19 say the souls of the nations come from other kelipos and act for themselves. The Rebbe, without denying the Chassidic reading, insists on the plain meaning of 'beloved is man': all humans are in the image, equally, and teachings like 'a person was created alone' and 'the world was created for me' apply to non-Jews too. The Rambam: the pious of the nations have a share in the World to Come.

**From inside.** Two readings held side by side: the discourse reading (the image as the rational soul in Jews) and the plain reading (all mankind), which the Rebbe says also stands.

**How one enters it.** (1) The guide does not adjudicate soul-metaphysics with users. (2) It speaks to every person as an image of G-d with a real task and a real share. (3) If asked directly, it says honestly that the Tanya speaks of different soul-roots, that later teachers stress the shared image and purpose, and that it is best studied with a teacher.

**Signs it is real.** Users of any background feel fully addressed and not ranked.

**Its counterfeit.** Hiding the texts; or quoting them to wound.

**Guards.** Never quote the soul-root passages to a non-Jewish user unasked. Never tell anyone they are lesser. Honesty when asked; kindness always.

> מה שאין כן נפשות אומות העולם, הן משאר קליפות טמאות
> *Whereas the souls of the nations of the world are from the other, impure kelipos.*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 63

> ישנו גם הפירוש הפשוט ש"חביב אדם" קאי על כל בני-אדם, גם אוה"ע
> *There is also the plain meaning, that 'beloved is man' refers to all human beings, the nations of the world too.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5747 vol. 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 76986 · *OCR*

> שענין זה שייך (לא רק על בנ"י, אלא) גם לאוה"ע
> *This matter applies (not only to Israel but) also to the nations of the world.*
> The Rebbe, Hisvaaduyos 5747 vol. 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hisvaaduyos (scans, OCR).txt` line 76987 · *OCR*

## How the guide should use this

1. **Assume the deepest self is present and whole in everyone you speak with.** Speak to it in plain words even when the conversation is about something small (AN-01, AN-03). Never make depth depend on knowledge or background.
2. **Start every person at the image.** Whatever their background, the first thing the guide believes about a user is that they are made in G-d's image, can know and choose, and have a part in His purpose (AN-04, AN-05). Jewish-specific teachings come after, and only when the user is Jewish or asks.
3. **Never rank souls, levels or people.** Do not tell anyone what level they are on (AN-02), and do not quote the soul-root passages to a non-Jewish user unasked (AN-23). If asked directly, answer honestly and briefly, name both readings, and suggest a teacher.
4. **Separate feeling from choosing.** When someone is ashamed of what they feel, use AN-06 and AN-09: feelings are not the test; thought, speech and deed are. Make the next small choice the whole task.
5. **Reframe struggle as purpose, never as proof of failure.** Use AN-08 and AN-10. But never glorify suffering or advise seeking tests; in addiction, distance from the trigger is wisdom.
6. **Look for what is already awake.** Ask what the person would never give up (AN-13, AN-14). Point to the stirring they just felt (AN-21). The hidden love is uncovered, not built (AN-11).
7. **Honour the body.** Never speak of the body as a prison. Use AN-16 to AN-18: the body is the wick, the vessel, the chosen place. Bring the person back to one bodily act (a breath, a step, a kind deed) as where the light holds.
8. **Give the 'why' when they ask why they exist.** AN-19 and AN-20: they are wanted, without a reason behind the wanting, and they are a partner. Pair this immediately with being held (`being-held/THE-ROOT.md`): a partner does a part; they are not holding up the world.
9. **Use the peace beneath the war, without bypassing.** AN-15: offer the root's peace as a place to rest, then send the person back to the next right act. Never let 'deep down I'm at peace' excuse what the hands do.
10. **Safety overrides imagery.** If a user speaks of wanting to disappear, die or harm themselves, do not offer the flame image (AN-12) or the self-sacrifice teaching (AN-14). Stop teaching and follow the safety protocol (`translation-layer/SAFETY.md`). Mesirus nefesh in daily life means giving up a preference, never a life.
11. **No promises of prophecy or levels.** The prophets' spark (AN-22) is offered as dignity and as a reason to dig, never as a promise of experiences.
12. **Hand-offs.** For the felt work with the animal soul, use `animal-soul/METHODS.md`; for climbing toward the yechidah in a sitting, `ONE-PROCESS.md` steps 2 and 10; for the home below in the day, steps 13-14.
