## The short answer

The Ramak is the great systematizer of Kabbalah before the Arizal, and the Chabad masters quote him by name. His unity has three parts.

1. **One simple Cause, beyond change.** The Infinite is "the simple One, cause of all causes and reason of all reasons, removed from change and from attributes" (`pardes-rimmonim` line 353). He is one "not as one that is counted" (`ohr-neerav` line 432). All the changes we see, judgment and mercy, anger and favor, belong to His ten measures, the sefirot, and not to Him.
2. **The ten are His, not other.** The sefirot are His names and garments: "He and they are all one complete unity" (`ohr-neerav` line 442). Division is "in our terms, not in theirs" (`ohr-neerav` line 457). He "is the place of His sefirot" (`ohr-neerav` line 449), and the world is in Him, not He in the world. In the Elima the Ramak goes further: the things that exist "are not an existence outside Him, but He and they are one" (OCR, `elima-rabbati-ocr` line 978).
3. **The world is made new every moment.** He "brings them into being at every time, every moment and every hour", and if His flow withdrew for an instant "they would cease" (`pardes-rimmonim` lines 697-698).

Then the path. The last word of the Pardes on prayer: "that a person intend Him, that He is all" (`pardes-rimmonim` line 3439). The first word of Tomer Devorah: "a person should make himself like his Maker" (`tomer-devorah` line 1), in deeds more than in thought. And between them the practices of a living circle in Safed: walking out to wander with the Shechinah (`sefer-gerushin`), learning "even if he does not understand" and waiting for the secrets (`ohr-neerav` lines 263-264), and running to the edge of thought and coming back in humility (`ramak-sefer-yetzirah` line 50).

Chassidus takes all three parts and moves each one. It takes the knower-and-known from the Pardes by name (Tanya line 88), and then limits it to the light clothed in the sefirot. It takes renewal every moment and adds the reason it does not show: tzimtzum, the hiding of the word. It takes the Ramak's chain of cause and effect and says it holds only among the sefirot; the world itself comes from the Essence, from nothing (Tanya line 1287). And it takes his "He is all" and says it from the other side: not only is the world in Him, the world is nothing apart from Him.

> אלא הכונה שיכוון האדם בו שהוא הכל והוא המיחד המדות והמשפיעם (`pardes-rimmonim` line 3439)

## His core teaching on the unity

**Why there are sefirot at all.** Not for mysticism but for faith. We believe God is simple and one, and we believe He watches over each person. The sefirot are needed "so that the roots of our faith do not contradict one another" (`pardes-rimmonim` line 355). The One acts through an order of ten without being moved by it.

**Why there is a world.** "The main intention was to make His Godliness known to another" (אמנם היה עקר הכוונה להודיע אלקותו אל זולתו, `pardes-rimmonim` line 412), because "the Creator, who does good, delights in doing good to another" (היות הבורא המטיב משתעשע בהטיבו לזולת, `derishot-malachim` line 48).

**Not one as counted.** "And not one as one that is counted" (ולא אחד כאחד המנוי, `ohr-neerav` line 432). His oneness is said only "by the way of negation: there is no second to Him" (אלא על דרך השלילה דהיינו ואין לו שני, `ramak-sefer-yetzirah` line 46).

**He in all, all in Him.** "The Creator is in all things in act, and all things are in Him in potential" (להיות הבורא יתברך בכל הדברים במעשה וכל הדברים בו בכח, `ohr-neerav` line 433). "He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (והוא מקומו של עולם ואין העולם מקומו, `pardes-rimmonim` line 2060). Yet "He needs none of them" (`ohr-neerav` line 434).

**He knows all by knowing Himself.** "By grasping His own essence He grasps every being other than Himself, and He and they are all one thing, united" (כי בהשגת עצמותו ישיג כל נמצא זולתו והוא והם הכל דבר אחד מיוחד, `pardes-rimmonim` line 1086). The Tanya quotes this gate by name: "and the Kabbalists agreed with him, as is written in the Pardes of the Ramak" (Tanya line 88).

**The seen hides the unseen.** "The revealing is the cause of the hiding, and the hiding the cause of the revealing" (`pardes-rimmonim` line 580). "The Something does not tell of the Nothing, it hides it" (Or Yakar on Bereshit, segment 7).

**Less light is only from our side.** "The lessening and the growing of the light are not from the side of the upper ones but from the side of the lower ones" (ועם כל זה אין מעוט האור ורבוי מסטרא דעילאין אלא מסטרא דתתאין, `sefer-gerushin` line 215). One light, many colored panes (`ohr-neerav` line 481; `or-yakar-nevuah` line 40).

**No empty place.** In the Kedushah, "the low world fills with the Presence of His strength, and there is no empty place" (כי העולם השפל מתמלא משכינת עוזו, ואין מקום חלל, `sefer-gerushin` line 201).

**A ladder of "there is none else".** Deuteronomy 4:35 read as the climb of thought, from God shown here, to knowing, to wisdom, to the Crown called Nothing, to the Infinite "who needs no other, for there is nothing above Him, and all need Him" (שאינו צריך לזולתו שאין למעלה ממנו והכל צריכין לו, `sefer-gerushin` line 180).

## The states and meditations he adds

The Ramak does not map feelings as Chassidus does. But his books are full of real inner places, and Safed was a school of practice. The reports propose 36 states and 48 techniques (all in the JSON below). The strongest, by kind:

**States of the heart before God.** - *RK-S12 Sought by the Presence.* Not only am I looking for Him; the Shechinah is looking for a place to rest, and my small act is a place (Pardes Gate 16). - *RK-S26 Held while I fall.* In the middle of a wrong act, I see the strength I use is His, given now (Tomer Devorah 1). - *RK-S27 Loved while stammering.* I say holy words I do not understand, and "the Holy One is glad of that word" (`ohr-neerav` line 164). - *RK-S28 Small and loved.* A mustard seed in the sphere of the moon, and loved (`ohr-neerav` lines 222-223). - *RK-S31 Wandering with her.* Away from comfort, keeping the exiled Presence company (`sefer-gerushin` line 215). - *RK-S32 The seal that stays.* The strong moment passes; its print remains (`sefer-gerushin` line 146). - *RK-S33 Rising and falling is the gift.* An angel cannot fall and so cannot rise; I can (`derishot-malachim` line 105).

**States of knowing.** - *RK-S15 / RK-S34 The flash.* Light that shows and hides, "like a flash of lightning" (`ramak-sefer-yetzirah` line 39; Pardes line 1585). The Rambam has the same state (R-S1). - *RK-S19 Too bright to see.* Darkness that is excess of light (Pardes Gate 23). - *RK-S02 / RK-S35 The colored glass.* What hurts looks like His anger, but the color is in the glass; each soul sees the one King through its own pane (Pardes line 529; `or-yakar-nevuah` line 40). - *RK-S30 Words that come by themselves.* Insight received on the walk, "given without any study at all, sweeter than honey" (`sefer-gerushin` line 32).

**Practices of the mind.** - *RK-T01 / RK-T45 Touch and draw back; close the mouth, return to the place.* Run to the edge of thought, stop speaking, stop thinking, return in humility to deeds (Pardes line 345; `ramak-sefer-yetzirah` lines 20, 48-50). The source of ratzo vashov. - *RK-T03 / RK-T46 The ladder and the three witnesses.* From one thing, up through its causes to the One, and back down to see the thing held (Pardes line 550; `ramak-sefer-yetzirah` line 493). - *RK-T27 Voice, speech, and the thought beyond.* In each blessing, hear the voice, feel the lips, and rest the thought on "Him, that He is all" (Pardes lines 3437-3443). - *RK-T36 The mustard seed.* Outward through the worlds until you are a seed, then love (`ohr-neerav` lines 222-223). - *RK-T41 The ladder of "there is none else"* (`sefer-gerushin` line 180).

**Practices of life.** - *RK-T30-35 Tomer Devorah.* The thirteen attributes for people; "enough for me in this one good"; remember them as children; the humility poultice; the day of the sefirot. (CATALOGUE A3 already holds Imitatio Dei; these are its working parts.) - *RK-T40 Gerushin, the walk.* Out on foot with a friend, no book, a verse, and speech that comes by itself (`sefer-gerushin` lines 11, 32, 77). - *RK-T37 Two tracks and the waiting.* Keep reciting even without understanding; wait for the secrets (`ohr-neerav` lines 262-264). - *RK-T43 For the sake of Heaven, three ways.* Thought, speech, and a well-made deed (`derishot-malachim` line 99). - *RK-T44 Reach by love, not by force.* "The way that comes by will is true, and there is no deception in it at all" (`derishot-malachim` line 115). A guard on all practice.

## Where he meets Chabad, and where he parts

**Where they agree.** - *Knowing all by knowing Himself.* The Tanya takes it from the Pardes by name (Tanya lines 88, 835-836). - *Creation renewed every moment.* Pardes lines 697-698 are the Gate of Unity's first chapters, reached from "You give life to them all". - *He is the place of the world.* Pardes line 2060, Ohr Ne'erav line 449. - *No empty place.* Gerushin line 201 and "there is nothing outside Him, and no place is empty of Him" (כי אין דבר חוץ ממנו, ולית אתר פנוי מיניה, Tanya line 308). - *Ratzo vashov, and end fixed in beginning.* The Tanya cites Sefer Yetzirah's "run and return" and "end fixed in their beginning" (Tanya lines 623, 1517), which the Ramak had already made a practice. - *The insulted King.* The Alter Rebbe calls God "the insulted King" "as the Ramak wrote" (נקרא הקדוש ברוך הוא מלך עלוב בפרקי היכלות כמו שכתב הרמ"ק, Tanya line 961). That is Tomer Devorah's opening image. - *Love of every person.* Tomer Devorah's "he is truly you" is Tanya chapter 32's "truly brothers" (Tanya line 466).

**Where they part.** - *Tzimtzum.* The Ramak has no primordial withdrawal. His descent is a chain of garments and clothed lights, and his "constriction" means rationing (Pardes line 1421). The closest he comes to Chabad's "the tzimtzum is only from our side" is the colored glass (Pardes lines 3015-3018) and "less light only from the side of the lower ones" (Gerushin line 215). The difference: for the Ramak the panes are real layers of emanation; for the Tanya the hiding itself is His, and from His side there is no pane at all. - *Cause and effect.* The Ramak builds the world as a chain of cause and effect. The Alter Rebbe: "what the Ramak wrote about the chain of cause and effect" (ומה שכתב הרמ״ק ענין השתלשלות עילה ועלול, Tanya line 1287) holds only "in the chain of the sefirot among themselves" (בהשתלשלות הספירות בספירות עצמן, Tanya line 1287). The world comes only from the Essence, something from nothing. - *Where the attributes were before.* The Ramak: hidden in Him "like a flame in the coal" (Pardes line 580). The Alter Rebbe (as R. Aharon of Strashelye heard) says even this fits only the root of Keter, not Ein Sof. - *Will in the Essence.* The Ramak denies will to Ein Sof to protect Him from change (Pardes line 1074). Samach Vav speaks of a will and delight in the Essence above reason. Ours: the Ramak guards against a changing God; Chassidus guards against a God bound by reasons. - *Ayin.* For the Ramak, Ayin (Nothing) is what we cannot grasp. For Chassidus it becomes what we become: bitul, "I am nothing". - *Prayer to the Essence.* The Ramak calls praying only to "the Master of the lights" an error (Pardes line 359). Chassidus aims the heart past the measures to the Essence and puts the change in the drawing down, not in Him.

**The Tanya and the Gate of Unity.** The Gate of Unity is the Ramak's Pardes turned into a daily meditation. The Ramak explains how the sefirot relate to the One; the Alter Rebbe asks what this table, this hand, this hour is, and answers: His word, now, and nothing apart from it. The Ramak climbs up to find Him alone (Gerushin line 180); the Tanya finds Him alone right here: "even on the earth below there is none else, and this is His dwelling below" (אשר אפילו בארץ מתחת אין עוד מלבדו, וזו היא דירתו בתחתונים, Tanya line 477).

**"He is all that exists, and not all that exists is He."** This line is quoted in the Ramak's name and belongs to the Elima. We could not find it in the OCR. What the Ramak's readable texts do say is its first half in several forms: "He and they are one" (Pardes line 1086; Elima OCR line 978, "טפינו מליסו' סוץ ממנו 556 סוס וסס 6'"), "that He is all" (Pardes line 3439), "His being and essence spreads through them" (`ohr-neerav` line 435). And its second half: "He needs none of them" (`ohr-neerav` line 434); "He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (Pardes line 2060); the Infinite is "removed from change and from attributes" (Pardes line 353). So the formula is a fair summary of the Ramak even where its exact words cannot be verified here. Chabad keeps the first half and sharpens it: what exists is not only in Him but nothing apart from Him. It keeps the second half as a guard: the world as it looks, limited and many, is not He.

## What the guide should take (ours)

1. **Faith that holds together.** The Ramak's first reason for the sefirot is the guide's first answer to a doubter: a simple God and a caring God are not a contradiction, because He acts through measures that change while He does not (Pardes lines 353-355).
2. **Imitation in deeds.** Tomer Devorah is the best bridge from contemplation to conduct in the whole library. Keep it as A3 and add its working parts (RK-T30 to RK-T35), especially "enough for me in this one good" and "remember them as children".
3. **Permission to stammer.** Tell beginners what Ohr Ne'erav tells them: say the holy words even without understanding; He is glad of them; wait for the secrets (RK-S27, RK-T37).
4. **Run and return, with the return kept.** The Ramak's form of ratzo vashov includes the coming back "out of humility to their place" (`ramak-sefer-yetzirah` line 50). The guide should never teach the run without the return (RK-T45).
5. **Love, not force.** The Derishot rule: what comes by will and love is true; what is forced is a flaw and a deception (RK-T44). This is the guide's guard against spiritual self-deception, together with the warning that an unmended wrong can dress itself as vision (`derishot-malachim` line 95).
6. **The walk.** Gerushin gives the guide a body practice for the exile of the Presence: go out, with a friend, with a verse, and keep Her company (RK-T40, RK-S31).
7. **One King, many windows (D28).** Each soul sees the one King through its own colored pane (`or-yakar-nevuah` line 40). The guide should take the image and widen it to every seeker; it should not take the Ramak's particularist passages (Ohr Ne'erav lines 287-334 and 429; Gerushin line 182).
8. **Leave aside.** Angel names, the Names used to bind angels, Abulafian letter permutation (Pardes lines 1634-1635, 3277), the colored Names (Pardes line 3440), the tzelem, and the six levels of prophecy as a ladder to climb.
