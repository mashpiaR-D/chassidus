# Ways for the animal soul: everything is being held

**Only One research** · October 1, 2026 · early draft 0.1 · Result [036](../../CONTENTS.md#036) · manuscript 10 of 10 · [Cite](CITATION.bib) · [Quote check](../../verification/catalogue.md#ways-for-the-animal-soul-everything-is-being-held-october-1-2026)

---
*A catalogue of 60 different ways to come to understand, from many sides, that everything (this body, this breath, this food, this room, my money, my child, my future, my next heartbeat) is being held and given being by G-d right now. Each way is phrased so the animal soul, the natural, bodily self, can take it in. Built 2026-10-01 from the project's verified files; where a way or a picture is our own, it says "ours". Machine-readable twin: `ways.json` (same ids).*

## Why this file exists

The owner: "One of the key parts of hisbonenus is coming up with different ways to understand that everything is being held by G-d, in the human brain, in the animal soul."

The animal soul (the natural soul) is the life of the body. It wants what is good *for me*: life, warmth, safety, pleasure, standing, control. It is not evil, and it can be turned (`Chassidus Unity Index/consciousness-map/MAP.md` line 27). It thinks in concrete, physical, self-interested terms, so it is not reached by abstractions.

The Rebbe Rashab says where to reach it. G-d's filling every thing from inside (memale) is something "any thinking person can understand, even with the natural mind of the animal soul" (Kuntres HaTefillah, line 44; `Chassidus Unity Index/kuntres-hatefillah/lines-1-253.md`; SPINE F04). When the contemplation is of things the natural mind can hold, the animal soul itself comes to understand and feel, and is moved; it is turned, not only overpowered (line 77; `Chassidus Unity Index/KUNTRES-HATEFILLAH.md`). What surrounds everything (sovev), and what is above that, needs the G-dly soul. So this catalogue lives mostly on the lower rungs of the ladder (ONE-PROCESS steps 3-6) and on the way down (12-14). That is by design.

## What makes a way work for the animal soul

- **Concrete.** This cup, this rent, this child. Not "the world". The Rashab calls a general glow "a false imagination"; the Mitteler Rebbe: in general, "from very far off G-d appears to him" (ONE-PROCESS step 3).
- **Sensory.** Something the hand, the eye or the tongue can check now. The Maggid: you can see it with your senses, in the taste and the beauty of a thing (BST file, "No place empty of Him").
- **Personal.** First person, its own life. Length (orech) brings the idea down "until a small child could grasp it" and into the thing the person named (TECHNOLOGY §5).
- **Speaks to self-interest.** Rest, safety, being looked after. The Tanya meets the animal soul's own fears by name, "children, life and food", and answers them from continuous creation (Iggeres HaKodesh 11; sources-chabad "Made from nothing this moment"). The self-interest is the door, not the house: the same letter says "nullify your will".
- **A small lived proof.** Something it already did today without holding it up: it slept, its heart beat, a second arrived. Not an argument, a fact it can't deny.
- **Repeated.** "The main thing is habit": faith is a word of practice, like a craftsman training his hands (Tanya 42, TANYA line 644). A way that worked once needs to be walked again tomorrow.
- **With a body act, as a picture only.** Each "try it now" is a picture done with the hands or eyes (the format of `Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/`, "Mashal (with the body)"). It is not a relaxation or breathing technique. Project rule: no breath-watching, no body scan, no "relax", no emptying the mind (`Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/MASTER-PROMPT.md` line 128; `being-held/psychology.md` §16). Breath and pulse appear only as pictures. Eyes stay open unless the way says otherwise.

## What works for the G-dly soul instead

The G-dly soul wants the truth for its own sake, not for what it does for me. It can stay with an abstraction: nothing before Him, the One alone, the Essence beyond filling and surrounding. Its sign is bitul, less of me, not more safety. The animal soul asks "am I safe?"; the G-dly soul asks "what is true?" and "what does He want?". A way built for the animal soul answers the first question truthfully, and so opens the second. If it stops at comfort, it has turned into a technique sold for its benefits, which the project forbids (THE-ROOT, Guards; psychology.md, "Where psychology and the teaching part ways" 3). Being held is true; that is why it helps, not the other way round.

## How a contemplation moves through the ways

The Mitteler Rebbe gives three dimensions, in this order (Gate of Unity 1:10-11, line 101; TECHNOLOGY §4, §5, §8):

1. **Breadth (rochav).** "The idea to every side, in many details, wide as a river." Go through three to six ways quickly, a few seconds each, from different groups: the heartbeat, the cup on the table, last night's sleep, the money. The same truth, held from many sides, stops being a slogan.
2. **Length (orech).** Bring it down to the one thing the person is actually afraid of: Thursday's meeting, this month's rent, the son who is not answering. In the first person.
3. **Depth (omek).** Only after the other two. Stay on the one way that caught. Drop the examples. Say it in three to six words ("Held. Right now. Me too.") and stay a minute.

Signs it is working: "for a second it is not an idea but so" (ONE-PROCESS step 3). It often goes flat just as the details start; stay, that is where the work starts. One picture at a time; say where it breaks; drop it once the line holds without it (TECHNOLOGY §9). Close, if possible, with one act: a thank-you before eating, one message to a person, one task done from rest (THE-GLASS-WALL.md, step 9).

**Three sample walks** (ours): - *4am money worry:* W12 Held all night → W14 The bucket, not the rain → W27 Tomorrow is made of seconds → stay on whichever caught. - *A frightened parent:* W22 His child first → W23 Even the toenail is fed → W05 The beat you don't make → stay. - *Hospital bed:* W33 The pen and the hand → W34 At the head of the bed → W02 Whole in every part → stay.

## Guards for every way

- Never promise an outcome. The certainty is in being held now, not in how things turn out (THE-ROOT). - Never use a way to explain someone else's pain, and never bring the hiding into fresh grief (TECHNOLOGY §5, "Not when"). - Danger, or a body that needs a doctor, comes first. - For anyone who feels unreal or far from their own life: the world is real and is given. Never "nothing is real". - The feeling may not come. Being held does not depend on feeling it. - Never picture G-d as a soul in a body; where a way uses that picture, its "breaks" line says so (ONE-PROCESS step 4, claim A03). - The Tanya speaks of the Jewish soul; offering these to any person is the owner's reading (D28).

## W01 · The hand you don't run

- **Worry:** "My body is mine to keep going. If I don't manage it, it breaks."
- **See:** Move your fingers. You chose to move them, but you have no idea how you did it. You never built the wiring, you don't feed the cells, you don't tell the blood where to go. Something is running this body far more closely than you ever could, every second, including the parts you have never seen. You live in it; you are not the one holding it up.
- **Try it now:** Move the fingers of one hand slowly for ten seconds. Ask once: who is doing the part I don't know how to do?
- **Picture:** Your own life fills your hand without your seeing it. In the same way the One fills everything, you included. **Breaks:** This is the picture the teaching itself refuses at the root: the world is not His body and He is not a soul inside it. A soul can leave and a body stays behind. If His giving stopped, nothing would be left at all.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4 / F04
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/too-small.md line 382 (moving the fingers; break from Shaar HaYichud); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'No place empty of Him' (line 80; MDL Introduction 7, soul in a body); Chassidus Unity Index/ONE-PROCESS.md step 4, 'The counterfeit' (claim A03)
- **Guard:** Use a hand that works and does not hurt. Not as a promise of health. Never let the picture become 'G-d is the soul of the world'.

## W02 · Whole in every part

- **Worry:** "The weak part of me, the bad knee, the scar, is where I'm on my own."
- **See:** The life in you is not more in your head than in your feet. It is whole in the strong knee and whole in the bad one. The weakest part of your body is not less held than the strongest; the giving does not thin out at the sore spot. Each part gets what it needs from the same whole life.
- **Try it now:** Raise one hand high and leave the other resting on your knee. Ask: am I more in the raised hand than in the one on my knee?
- **Picture:** The soul is in the head and the feet equally; so the One is in every part of you, and of the world, equally. **Breaks:** The One is not like a soul in a body; the picture shows only 'equally'. And it does not say the weak part will heal.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4, 13 / F04, F02
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/illness.md line 270 and Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/grief.md line 298 (Tanya 51: not in the head more than in the feet)
- **Guard:** Not to play down pain or disability. Never as a promise of recovery. Not when moving the arm hurts.

## W03 · Made, not maintained

- **Worry:** "I'm a machine I have to service. Once something is built, keeping it going is all on me."
- **See:** A chair, once the carpenter is done, stands without him. He can go home. You are not like that chair. Your body is not a finished object you now have to maintain alone; it is being given its being right now, the way a sentence is being spoken. The day the maker 'goes home' is a day that never comes.
- **Try it now:** Touch something made: a cup, a chair. It doesn't need its maker any more. Then touch your own forearm and say: this does, right now.
- **Picture:** The silversmith and his cup, used as the wrong picture. **Breaks:** The cup stands without the smith because the silver was already there before he started. Before you were said, there was nothing to stand on.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3 / F01
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Not the silversmith's cup' (line 75; Tanya, Shaar HaYichud 2, TANYA lines 792, 795); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'The word is still being said' (line 32; BST Bereshit 50-51)
- **Guard:** For someone who feels unreal or cut off from their body: stress that the body is real and is given, never 'not really there'.

## W04 · Real, and given

- **Worry:** "If everything is G-d, then my body isn't real and nothing I do with it matters. Or: my body is solid, so it's just mine."
- **See:** Both halves are true at once. From where you stand your body really is solid, separate and yours to care for; that is not an illusion. From the other side it is being given, every second, and has nothing of its own. Look at a printed word: ink marks one way, a meaning the other way, the same page.
- **Try it now:** Look at one printed word near you. See it as ink. Then read it. Same page, two true views.
- **Picture:** A word on a page: ink from one side, meaning from the other. **Breaks:** On a page you see one view at a time. Here both are true at once, and the 'ink' is not a lesser thing to get past.
- **Rungs / faces:** 5 / F02
- **Basis:** Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/truths.json truth 'real_from_here' (clothing 'body'; Tanya 21, Shaar HaYichud 3); Chassidus Unity Index/ONE-PROCESS.md step 5 'Two knowings'
- **Guard:** The guard against 'nothing is real, so nothing matters'. Not for someone with depersonalization as a starting way; for them give only the 'real' half first.

## W05 · The beat you don't make

- **Worry:** "If I stop watching, something will stop."
- **See:** Your heart beat all through last night while you slept, and all through every meeting today, and you never once ran it. If staying alive depended on your watching, you would have died the first night. Today it was given to you about a hundred thousand times without your asking.
- **Try it now:** Two fingers on the pulse at the wrist for ten beats. Not counting, not calming: ask of each beat, did I make that?
- **Picture:** The heartbeat: it is beating and you are not making it beat. **Breaks:** A heart has its own causes, and the teaching does not deny them. The point is that the whole chain of causes is itself being given now. And a pulse is not G-d.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3, 6 / F01
- **Basis:** Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/truths.json truth 'being_made_now' (clothing 'body'); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/love-for-god-gone.md line 286; Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/work-swallowed-life.md line 314; Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'From hearing to seeing' (line 486)
- **Guard:** Not for health anxiety, panic, palpitations or heart patients who check their pulse with fear; give them an object instead. Not a breathing or calming exercise.

## W06 · Breathed from inside

- **Worry:** "My life is a thin thing stuck onto me from outside. It could come off."
- **See:** Warm your hands in winter with your breath. The warmth comes from inside you; you didn't make it, you let it out. Your life is like that: it was breathed into you from the inside of the One who gave it, not glued on from outside. Something that comes from inside the giver is not loosely attached.
- **Try it now:** Breathe out once, slowly, onto the back of your hand, the way you warm your hands in the cold. Once only.
- **Picture:** 'He blew into his nostrils the breath of life'; one who blows, blows from within himself. **Breaks:** The One does not breathe. Your breath leaves you and is gone; the soul does not leave its root.
- **Rungs / faces:** 2 / F06
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/depression.md line 286 and Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/loneliness.md line 313 (Tanya 2:1); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'The rope tied above' (line 348)
- **Guard:** One breath as a picture only. The project rule: no breath-watching or breath technique. Not for anyone prone to panic or hyperventilation.

## W07 · A word being said

- **Worry:** "I'm a solid thing that has to keep itself going."
- **See:** Say the word 'now' out loud. Where is that word? It existed only while you were saying it; it isn't stored anywhere. You, and every breath you take, are like that word: being said, right now. You don't have to keep yourself in existence any more than a word has to keep itself spoken.
- **Try it now:** Say one word aloud, quietly. Listen to the quiet after it.
- **Picture:** A spoken word lasts only as long as the speaker keeps saying it. **Breaks:** Our words leave us and go off into the air. His never leave Him. And He has no mouth and no voice; the picture is only there to settle the ear.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3 / F01
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'The word is still being said' (line 41; Tanya, Shaar HaYichud 1, TANYA line 785) and 'Nothing has changed for Him' (line 208; Tanya 20, TANYA line 302); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/depression.md line 263
- **Guard:** Not in fresh grief. Not for depersonalization ('then I'm nothing'): add at once that the word is real and meant.

## W08 · Where the taste comes from

- **Worry:** "I need this food, these things. Without them I'm nothing, so I must get more."
- **See:** Take a bite and stay with the taste. Strip away the crumbs in your mind and think only of the taste itself: you can't hold it in your hand. It is life arriving through the food, now, for you. What feeds you is the life in the bread, and it is being sent, not grabbed.
- **Try it now:** One bite or one sip, eyes on it. Ask once: where does this taste come from?
- **Picture:** The taste, smell and beauty in a thing are the life inside it, like a soul in a body. **Breaks:** The world is not His body. And the taste is real and yours to enjoy; the point is not that food is an illusion.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4, 13 / F04, F07
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'No place empty of Him' (line 80; MDL Introduction 7; the Besht: when you eat, the taste and sweetness come from the life above)
- **Guard:** Not for anyone with an eating disorder; use a sip of plain water and do not centre the practice on food.

## W09 · Bread becomes your voice

- **Worry:** "I'm a body that runs on fuel, and getting the fuel is on me."
- **See:** The bread on your plate doesn't move; it shows no life at all. In an hour it will be the strength you think and speak with. A hidden life in plain stuff turns into you, and neither you nor the bread did the turning. You are being fed by more than you can see.
- **Try it now:** Hold a piece of bread or a cup. Look at it. Then say one sentence aloud, full voice: that strength came from food like this.
- **Picture:** Bread, which shows no life, feeds the living soul; the voice gets its strength from yesterday's bread. **Breaks:** Bread is not G-d, and eating is not a mystery rite. The picture shows only that life is hidden in plain things.
- **Rungs / faces:** 13, 14 / F07, F10
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/empty-ordinary-life.md line 281; Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/punishing-the-body.md line 372 (Tanya 37:9)
- **Guard:** If the person is hungry or does not have food, meet the need first; no picture before a meal.

## W10 · Spring water, not pond water

- **Worry:** "It could all dry up. I have to stockpile."
- **See:** The glass of water in your hand was running somewhere else an hour ago. A pond can dry up in a hot summer. A spring gives drop by drop and does not stop. Your life is spring water: it keeps coming from a source that is still flowing, and you are still joined to it.
- **Try it now:** Pour a glass of water and take one sip. Ask: where was this an hour ago?
- **Picture:** The stream still joined to its spring; the wide river that can run dry, and the spring that never stops. **Breaks:** A stream can be dammed and real springs do fail over years. The root is never cut off, and the One does not run low. Not a promise of supply.
- **Rungs / faces:** 2, 4 / F06, F04
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'The stream still joined to its spring' (line 174; DEG Masei 5); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/cravings.md line 262 (the Rashab's river and spring); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/lost.md line 324 (Hayom Yom 2 Elul)
- **Guard:** Not to someone without water or food as an answer to the lack.

## W11 · Thank you before the bite

- **Worry:** "I got this. I paid for it, I worked for it, it's mine."
- **See:** Before it reached you, this food passed through a field, rain, a truck and a hundred hands. Yours were only the last. A word of thanks before you eat simply puts your name in the right place on the receipt, as the one who receives. It also tells the part of you that grabs that it is being looked after.
- **Try it now:** Before your next bite, hold the food and say 'thank You' out loud, or the blessing you know.
- **Picture:** The receipt with the right name on it (ours). **Breaks:** Nothing is owed back and G-d is not a seller. The thanks is not payment.
- **Rungs / faces:** 14 / F10, F07
- **Basis:** ours, from Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/THE-GLASS-WALL.md step 9 (a blessing over food as the one act) and Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/empty-ordinary-life.md line 293 (a blessing over lunch as the garment you can take hold of)
- **Guard:** Never pressed on someone who does not keep blessings; 'thank You' is enough.

## W12 · Held all night

- **Worry:** "If I stop thinking about it, it will fall apart. I can't sleep until it's solved."
- **See:** Last night you fell asleep with nothing solved. For hours you held nothing: not your heart, not your bills, not your children. In the morning everything was where you left it, and so were you. You have practised being held every night of your life.
- **Try it now:** Let the chair take your full weight the way the bed did last night. Not to relax; only to remember that you put it all down unsolved.
- **Picture:** The sleeper, held until morning. **Breaks:** Sleep does not prove nothing bad happens at night. It shows only that the holding never depended on you.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3, 6 / F01
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/worry.md line 290; Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/THE-ROOT.md 'Applied' table (worry, 4am math)
- **Guard:** Not a sleep technique. Not for insomnia that needs a doctor, or for someone whose nights are not safe.

## W13 · Given back each morning

- **Worry:** "What if I don't wake up? When I'm unconscious, nobody is in charge of me."
- **See:** Each morning you were handed yourself back. Through the night you were in other hands, and they returned you, whole and on time. You are a deposit being kept, not a possession you have to guard. The oldest morning words are just: thank you for giving it back.
- **Try it now:** On waking, or now: open both hands, palms up, as if someone were handing you something back. Say 'thank You for giving it back.'
- **Picture:** A deposit returned to its owner; 'great is Your faithfulness'. **Breaks:** A returned deposit leaves your hands. Here you give yourself back and keep living, now joined to Him. And He is not a creditor holding you for a debt.
- **Rungs / faces:** 6, 14 / F08, F06
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Handing yourself back' (line 466; Tanya 41); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/too-far-gone.md line 312; Chassidus Unity Index/SPINE.md F01 (renewed every moment, the Rebbe on Modeh Ani)
- **Guard:** Not for someone with thoughts of not wanting to wake up; sleep-and-return pictures can feed them.

## W14 · The bucket, not the rain

- **Worry:** "If I don't hustle every minute, we'll go under."
- **See:** You do the work: you set out the bucket. The rain is not yours. A farmer who ploughs on time is not lazy, and a farmer who stands in the field at midnight shouting at the sky gets no more rain. Your part is today's piece, done well; the living comes through it, not from it.
- **Try it now:** Hold your keys, phone or tool: 'this is my part.' Put it down: 'the rest is not in my hand.'
- **Picture:** A vessel for the rain; a farmer who ploughs in season and trusts for the rain. **Breaks:** A bigger bucket holds more rain; here a small vessel is enough because the blessing fills it. And G-d is not the weather.
- **Rungs / faces:** 13, 14 / F07, F10
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Make the vessel, but do not lean on it' (line 823; Hemshech 5672 §82; Pelach HaRimon); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/anxiety.md line 316
- **Guard:** Not an excuse to skip real steps or refuse help. Real debt needs a plan and a person.

## W15 · The coin nobody else saw

- **Worry:** "Money is luck and grabbing. Nobody is looking out for me."
- **See:** Think of the last time money came in a way you didn't plan: a refund, a lead, a bill someone covered. Many people walked past that chance; you were the one who found it. That wasn't luck. Someone was paying attention to you, by name.
- **Try it now:** Open your wallet or banking app. Find one amount that came unexpectedly this year. Look past the number to the attention behind it.
- **Picture:** A coin found where many walked: their eyes were covered so you would find it. **Breaks:** It does not mean every loss is a punishment, or that more money means more love.
- **Rungs / faces:** 5, 13 / F01, F03
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'The small things too' (line 48; KP Tzav 72; KST 1:36)
- **Guard:** Never used to explain someone's poverty. Not for someone in acute need, who needs help first.

## W16 · Your worry isn't helping carry it

- **Worry:** "Worrying is how I keep us safe. If I stop, I'm being careless."
- **See:** Look at what years of worry produced. Did one bill get paid by it? The One who feeds the whole world is not waiting for your 4am arithmetic to help Him. You have two roads: worry until it works out and then regret the strength you burned, or lean now and later say, good thing I didn't worry.
- **Try it now:** Write tomorrow's one money task on a slip of paper. Fold it, put it in your pocket. Say: 'That one's mine. The rest isn't.'
- **Picture:** 'I made you and I will carry you': like a parent carrying the child he made. **Breaks:** A human carrier gets tired. This One is not strained by you. And it promises no particular outcome.
- **Rungs / faces:** 6, 12 / F12, F11
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Worry is forgetting' (line 723; the Rebbe, Igros Kodesh, Pesach 5711; Isaiah 46:4); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'Worry is a kind of forgetting' (line 405; Chovos HaLevavos; Noam Elimelech Behar 2)
- **Guard:** Not for clinical anxiety as a rebuke. Never 'your worry is the problem' to someone in real money danger.

## W17 · The bottom line

- **Worry:** "The numbers are everything. My worth is my balance."
- **See:** Do every line of the accounts carefully; don't skip any. Then, at the bottom, notice what it all comes to: it was given, line by line, and it is still being given. The detail is your work. The total isn't yours alone.
- **Try it now:** Open the last budget, invoice or receipt. Under the total, write or say one word: 'given.'

- **See:** Praise and shame both reach you through the same hands. Neither adds to what is holding you, and neither takes away. A child carried through a crowd hardly notices who bumps him.
- **Try it now:** Recall one compliment and one criticism from this week. Hold one in each open hand. Notice they weigh the same.
- **Picture:** A child in a parent's arms in a jostling crowd (ours). **Breaks:** A criticism may have something true to learn from, and the sting is real. Evenness is a direction to grow in, not a test.
- **Rungs / faces:** 7 / F08
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'Equanimity: all of it comes from Him' (line 98; Tzava'as HaRivash 2); picture ours
- **Guard:** Never as advice to stay under verbal abuse.

## W48 · The king came down to the heap

- **Worry:** "I'm too low to be held. Not me."
- **See:** Picture a king who walks out of his palace, all the way down, to the one lying on the rubbish heap, lifts him up, and takes him to the innermost room. He comes down to exactly the one who thinks he isn't worth it. You don't climb up to where the special people are; He chose the plain floor.
- **Try it now:** Put your feet flat on the floor: the lowest thing in the room.
- **Picture:** The king and the man on the dunghill; the king who lodges in a commoner's house. **Breaks:** The real thing is greater without end: before Him there is no above and below. 'Low' is only how it looks from here.
- **Rungs / faces:** 6, 13 / F07, F11
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'The King who came down to the dunghill' (line 323; Tanya 46); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/too-small.md line 346
- **Guard:** Not for someone hurt by religious language about being low.

## W49 · This hour isn't thin

- **Worry:** "Real life is later, or somewhere else. Now is just in between."
- **See:** This hour, this kitchen, this Tuesday, is being made right now, as fully as any holy day. There's no place or time that is thinner or further from Him. The present moment isn't a waiting room.
- **Try it now:** Say the day and the time aloud, and add: 'being made now.'
- **Picture:** A king needs a people: space and time are made by his rule. **Breaks:** A human king rules people who exist without him. Here the people, the place and the hour are made by Him now.
- **Rungs / faces:** 11, 13 / F02, F07
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Down here as much as up there' (line 180; Shaar HaYichud 7)
- **Guard:** None in particular; it is gentle.

## W50 · Each note is the song

- **Worry:** "I'm waiting for my life to start."
- **See:** A song isn't waiting for its last note to be a song. Each note is the song while it sounds, and the next one isn't an empty slot; it is already in the song. This month is not the silence before your life; it is being sounded now.
- **Try it now:** Hum one note, and let it end.
- **Picture:** A note exists only while it is being sung. **Breaks:** You hum the note; you don't hum the One. And the note stops; the giving doesn't.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3, 13 / F01, F07
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/waiting.md line 272; Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/truths.json truth 'being_made_now' (clothing 'music')
- **Guard:** For someone waiting for a diagnosis or a child: no promise of how the waiting ends.

## W51 · A full moment

- **Worry:** "This moment is empty. I have to fill it with something."
- **See:** The urge to scroll is the feeling that this moment is empty. It isn't. It is full, being given right now, and you can stay in it because you are held in it.
- **Try it now:** Turn your phone face down. Look at one thing in the room for twenty seconds.
- **Picture:** A room that seemed empty and turns out to be lit. **Breaks:** It is not a rule against phones. It's about the feeling of emptiness, not the device.
- **Rungs / faces:** 3, 6 / F01, F12
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/THE-ROOT.md 'Applied' (scrolling, distraction); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/work-swallowed-life.md line 269
- **Guard:** Not a shaming exercise for someone with attention difficulties.

## W52 · Grey light is sunlight

- **Worry:** "The world is grey. It runs by itself. Nothing good is in it."
- **See:** On an overcast day the whole sky is grey, and every bit of that grey light is sunlight; there is no other light. The cloud lets you look at the day without being blinded. The hiding is part of the kindness; it lets you live under the sun.
- **Try it now:** Look at the daylight in the room, or feel it on your face.
- **Picture:** The sun and its sheath. **Breaks:** The sun is far off and is one thing among others; the One is neither. And the picture explains no one's pain.
- **Rungs / faces:** 5 / F03
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'The hiding is a kindness' (line 151; Shaar HaYichud 4, TANYA line 816); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/depression.md line 275
- **Guard:** In depression, it does not explain the heaviness. Never used to account for grief.

## W53 · The leaf, and how it fell

- **Worry:** "Things just happen. Nobody is minding the details."
- **See:** The leaf turning on the pavement turned when it did and where it did, and even the gust that turned it was sent. If the small leaf is minded, so was the clumsy way your day went. Ask what it is here to teach you.
- **Try it now:** Watch a small moving thing outside, a leaf, a curtain, a bird, for half a minute. Then name one small thing from today and ask what it is here to show you.
- **Picture:** A leaf in the wind, and the wind that turned it. **Breaks:** A leaf doesn't know it is held; you can. And not every event is a code to crack.
- **Rungs / faces:** 5, 13 / F01
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'The leaf, the wind, and how it fell' (line 639; Hayom Yom; Sefer HaSichos; Toras Menachem); note the gap at Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'Gaps and notes'
- **Guard:** Not for scrupulosity or OCD (turning every event into a sign). Never used on a tragedy.

## W54 · A wave is water moving

- **Worry:** "I'm a separate thing out here on my own."
- **See:** A wave looks like a thing, with a shape and a crest. It is water being moved, right now. You are real, with your own shape; and you are being moved, right now, by what you are made of.
- **Try it now:** Pour water from one cup into another and watch the shape of the pour. It keeps its shape only while it pours.
- **Picture:** A wave that knows it is water. **Breaks:** A wave crashes and is gone; you stay you, real, choosing, answerable. And the sea is not G-d.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4, 7 / F01, F08
- **Basis:** Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/truths.json truths 'being_made_now' and 'nothing_on_its_own' (clothing 'nature')
- **Guard:** Not for depersonalization or anyone not wanting to exist.

## W55 · Work from rest

- **Worry:** "If I trust, I'll get lazy. Worry is what drives me."
- **See:** Look honestly: worried work is tight and makes mistakes. Work done from being held has more in it, more energy, better judgement. Your part is real. Do it from rest, not from dread, and watch what happens.
- **Try it now:** Before the next task, put the tool down and rest your open hands on your knees for ten seconds. Then pick it up again as 'my part'.
- **Picture:** None given; the claim is what can be seen. **Breaks:** It doesn't promise success. It says only that the doing works better without the dread.
- **Rungs / faces:** 13, 14 / F07, F10
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Effort without worry works better' (line 862; the Rebbe's letters); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'Doing your part' (line 421; Chovos HaLevavos, Introduction 45)
- **Guard:** For burnout or depression, rest comes first, not more work.

## W56 · Nobody praises the axe

- **Worry:** "It's all my skill. The result depends on me alone."
- **See:** Nobody admires a beautiful building and praises the axe. Your skill is real and yours to grow. But the strength in your hand and the success that comes through it are given. Hold your tool the way you hold it at work: does the pen write, or the hand, with the pen?
- **Try it now:** Hold the tool of your trade the way you hold it at work.
- **Picture:** The axe and the builder; the hammer and the craftsman. **Breaks:** A hammer has no choice and no heart. You do, and you answer for what you do.
- **Rungs / faces:** 7 / F08
- **Basis:** Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/work-swallowed-life.md line 291 (the Rashab's axe); Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md 'You are not the one speaking' (line 128; Tzava'as HaRivash 101, 127)
- **Guard:** Not for someone with low self-worth who needs to own their competence; stress that the skill is real.

## W57 · A patch of sunlight

- **Worry:** "I'm alone out here. It's just me."
- **See:** A patch of sunlight on the floor looks like a thing on its own, cut off from the sun. That's how you look to yourself. But you have never left your source; you're still inside it. You just can't see it from this angle.
- **Try it now:** Find a patch of light (window, lamp) and put your hand in it.
- **Picture:** The ray inside the sun. **Breaks:** A patch really has left the sun; you have not left your source. And the sun is far away; the One is not.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4, 7 / F01, F04
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Sunlight inside the sun' (line 126; Shaar HaYichud 3, TANYA lines 800-803); Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/loneliness.md line 266
- **Guard:** Not for depression, grief or not wanting to exist as the first way; keep the 'you are real' side.

## W58 · Seen in this room

- **Worry:** "Nobody sees me. Nobody knows."
- **See:** In this room, right now, you are not unwitnessed. Every step you took today was seen by the One holding you: not to catch you out, but because nothing of you falls outside His attention.
- **Try it now:** Sit or stand the way you would if someone you respect had just walked in.
- **Picture:** Standing before a king who sees and counts every step. **Breaks:** A person who walks in stays in his own body; here what faces you also fills the room. And the look is not hostile.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4, 6 / F04, F12
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Seen and known' (line 428; Tanya 41-42); Tanya Guided Meditation/universal/engine/truths.json truth 'facing_you' (clothing 'body')
- **Guard:** For someone who feels watched and condemned, or with religious fear: use a parent, not a king.

## W59 · The part you can't feel

- **Worry:** "If I can't feel Him, He isn't here."
- **See:** A stone shows you only weight and cold. That isn't all that's in it. What you feel of being held is the small measured part. The part you can't feel isn't further away. It's the bigger part, and it's right here.
- **Try it now:** Hold a stone, keys or a ring. It shows only weight and cold.
- **Picture:** The earth, which shows only a trace of life and is filled with the whole light. **Breaks:** 'Surrounding' sounds like a circle around you. It isn't about place at all.
- **Rungs / faces:** 4 / F04
- **Basis:** Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/sources-chabad.md 'Around you, and truly inside you' (line 235; Tanya 48, TANYA line 703)
- **Guard:** Not for someone who is numb and being pushed to feel something.

## W60 · Reach one person

- **Worry:** "I have to get through this alone."
- **See:** Being held also comes through other people. Asking for help is part of being held, not a failure of it. One message is enough.
- **Try it now:** Write one person's name. Send: 'Can we talk tonight?'
- **Picture:** A hand held out across a gap (ours). **Breaks:** People can fail us; the holding does not depend on them. But it often comes through them.
- **Rungs / faces:** 14 / F07
- **Basis:** ours, from Chassidus Unity Index/being-held/THE-ROOT.md 'Through people' (reach one person) and Chassidus Unity Index/universal-framework/FRAMEWORK.md 'Loneliness' (guard: ends in one act toward another person)
- **Guard:** If anyone is in danger, call emergency services or a crisis line first.
