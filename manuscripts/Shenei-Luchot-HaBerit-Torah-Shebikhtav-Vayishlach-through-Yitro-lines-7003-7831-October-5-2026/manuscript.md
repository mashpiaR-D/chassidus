This range runs from Jacob's meeting with Esau to the giving of the Torah. Two passages carry most of the weight for the unity. In **Vaera** the Shelah says we were commanded to **know** that G-d is the only power, "with true knowledge", and that faith from tradition "is not enough" (line 7491). He adds that if G-d held back His power "for one moment", every force in the world "would be as if it had never been" (line 7506). In **Yitro** he quotes the Rambam's first law in full and then sets out the Kabbalists: before the Will arose we can say nothing of Him, "only necessary being, and even this by negation and not by affirmation" (line 7758). Between them sit the Joseph chapters, which the Shelah reads as a long lesson in humility, forgiveness, "G-d meant it for good", and all Israel as one soul.

## 1.1 Know it in the heart, not only by tradition

The Ner Mitzvah of Vaera turns the verse "know this day and lay it on your heart" into a command to know, not only to believe (Vaera, Ner Mitzvah 4, line 7491):

> וכן נצטוינו לידע דבר זה בידיעה אמיתית ולא די באמונה מצד הקבלה, רק ידיעה בלב מצד ההשגה

"We were commanded to know this with true knowledge; faith from tradition is not enough, only knowledge in the heart from grasping." What is to be known is that the power is His alone (line 7491):

"For there is none but Him who has power absolutely." Later he says why this matters: "knowing G-d in his heart makes knowledge and a bond between him and G-d" (Vaera, Torah Ohr 43, line 7534):

> כי ידיעת השם בלבו עושה ידיעה וחיבור בינו להש"י

*Ours:* this is almost the program of Tanya ch. 42 and the whole of Chabad contemplation: knowing, not just believing, and knowing "in the heart".

## 1.2 If He withheld for a moment

Every force that rules in the world is caused by Him (Vaera, Torah Ohr 15, line 7506):

"Since You bring it into being, it is caused." And then the line closest to *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 2 in the whole book so far (line 7506):

> ואם רגע אחד כמימרא לא ישפיע בהם הש"י הכח של הממשלה ההיא אז הם כלא היו

"If for one moment G-d did not pour into them the power of that rule, they would be as if they had never been." Nature too is joined to Him "like the sunlight from the sun" (Vaera, Torah Ohr 5, line 7496):

and "the Name Elokim is nature" (line 7493: שם אלהים הוא הטב"ע). The Chabad reader will know both images: the sun's rays and Elokim = hateva (*Shaar HaYichud* ch. 3 and 6).

## 1.3 Nothing exists apart from Him

In Bo, speaking of why even far things have a holy root (Bo, Torah Ohr 43, line 7626):

> כי אין דבר בעולם שלא יהיה נשרש למעלה כי הוא יתברך היוה את כולם ומחיה את כולם

"There is nothing in the world that is not rooted above, for He brought them all into being and gives life to them all." And (line 7626):

> ומה שיש בשמים ממעל ועל הארץ מתחת אין לו עוד מציאות זולתו יתברך

"What is in the heavens above and on the earth below has no other existence apart from Him." In Yitro he quotes the Rambam's first law in full, including "He alone is the truth, and no other has truth like His truth" (Yitro, Torah Ohr 4, line 7734: הוא לבדו האמת, ואין לאחר אמת כאמתתו). Jethro "saw that there was nothing real in any of them" (line 7789: וראה שבכולם אין ממש).

## 1.4 Before the Will: only negation

On the Essence Name, the Shelah lays out three views (the *Pardes*, the Ramaz, *Pirkei deRabbi Eliezer*) and lands here (Yitro, Torah Ohr 28, line 7758):

> וקודם עלות הרצון לא נוכל לדבר שום אות ורמז, רק מחויב המציאות

"Before the Will arose we cannot speak any letter or hint, only 'necessary being', and even this by negation and not by affirmation." This is the Rambam's negative attributes placed exactly where Chabad places them: at the Essence above all revealed Will. The next lines give a rule of the whole structure (Torah Ohr 33, line 7763):

"The higher a thing is, the more it is one." And against any thought that the sefirot or angels are powers of their own (lines 7771, 7774):

> אפילו מחשבה העליונה שהוא הכתר אינו אלוה זולתו של סיבת כל הסיבות

"They depend on Him and He does not depend on them." "Even the high Thought, which is Keter, is not a god apart from the Cause of all causes." *Ours:* this matches the Ramak's rule that the sefirot are His tools, not Him.

## 1.5 Hidden in essence, revealed in acts

In the Joseph chapters the Shelah gives the rule for how we know Him at all (Vayeshev, Torah Ohr 93, line 7206):

> כך המבדיל הקב"ה נעלם מצד מהותו ונגלה מצד פעולותיו

"G-d is hidden from the side of His essence and revealed from the side of His acts." And the ladder (line 7206):

> כי שכל המעשה הוא סולם לעלות אל שכל העיוני ומן הנגלה יבא אל הנעלם

"Practical wisdom is a ladder to contemplative wisdom, and from the revealed one comes to the hidden."

## 1.6 His unity and ours

"Just as G-d is one, so Israel is one", and the bond is through the Torah, "for the whole Torah is His Names" (line 7171):

> וענין הדביקות והקישור הוא באמצעית התורה, כי התורה כולה שמותיו

Jacob's sons, asked at his deathbed, answered him with the Shema (Vayechi, Derech Chayim 17, line 7396):

> כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד כך אין בלבנו אלא אחד

"Just as in your heart there is only One, so in our heart there is only One." G-d "longs to rest His Presence among the lower ones" (line 7236), and "the whole person is a dwelling for the Presence" (line 7525: על כן האדם השלם הוא מדור לשכינה).

## 3.1 Know, do not only believe (proposed: `know_in_heart`)

Take the verse "know this day and lay it on your heart... there is no other" and work it until it is knowledge "in the heart from grasping" (line 7491). The content: He alone has power (7491); every force is caused by Him (7506); if He withheld "for one moment" it would not be (7506); nature is His light like sunlight from the sun (7496). *Tie:* `depth`, `hold`, `heart_after`. *Ours:* this is the same work as Tanya ch. 42. The Shelah gives the goal and the content; Chabad gives the long method.

## 3.2 The nightly search (proposed: `lamp_search`)

The search for chametz becomes a search of oneself: the main time for it is "at night before he sleeps" (Bo, Derech Chayim 4, line 7644: ועיקר זמן הבדיקה בלילה קודם שישן), going limb by limb by the lamp of the soul; and "the main nullifying is in the heart, that he return with all his heart" (line 7644). *Tie:* `cheshbon`. *Ours:* a short review of the day before sleep, by the light of the soul, ending in a whole-hearted turning, not in self-blame.

## 3.3 You can, so do it daily (proposed: `daily_yom_kippur`)

The Accuser's "praise" on Yom Kippur is that Israel can be holy when they want: "it is in their power, so why do they not do so every day" (Vayishlach, Torah Ohr 66, line 7070). With "a person should return to G-d at every time" (line 7109) and "a day's portion each day" (line 7484). *Tie:* `chazarah`, `act`. *Ours:* the proof that you can is that you once did.

## 3.4 Read the turn as Joseph did (proposed: `meant_for_good`)

"But G-d meant it for good" (line 7155), with the warning "heaven forbid to say they were forced; all was by free choice" (line 7156). Then: "so be whole with G-d and set aside your will before His will" (line 7313). *Tie:* `garment_look`, `discern`. *Ours:* two truths held together: the other person chose, and G-d guided. This frees one from both blame and self-pity.

## 3.5 Humility, forgiveness, gratitude (Joseph's practices)

- Credit G-d: "what am I and what is my life, except what G-d shares of His honor with me" (line 7311). - Forgive: "see how much a person must forgive and pass over his traits" (line 7321). - Be grateful, even to strangers: "a person must not be ungrateful" (line 7324). - Ask before you judge: Joseph "should have asked the brothers what they meant" (line 7296). - Let mouth and heart be the same (line 7299). - Move people from a wrong wish "little by little" (line 7302). - Never shame another in public: better "a fiery furnace" (line 7306); your fellow "is in the image of your Maker" (line 7830).

*Tie:* `act`, `lips`. *Ours:* a daily trait list, not a meditation.

## 3.6 Plan a room for G-d (proposed: `room_for_Him`)

"In every act a person does, let him first see in it something for the need above" (Vayigash, Derech Chayim 3, line 7322); when building a house, set a room "to be closed in there for Torah, prayer and being alone" (line 7322). *Tie:* `arrive`. *Ours:* before any project, ask what in it is for Him, and keep one place for being alone with Him.

## 3.7 Prayer: posture, joy, song ahead

"Set his eyes below and his heart above" (line 7681). Both revelry and melancholy block prayer; the cure is joy: "serve G-d with joy to undo Machalat" (line 7686, a play on the name as *machalah*, sickness). Praise before the good comes, "though it has not yet come" (line 7675). Spell out the request even though He knows the heart (line 7096). *Tie:* `before_whom`, `lips`, `own_words`.

## 3.8 Kindness with no reward (proposed: `free_kindness`)

"The heart of kindness is when one does it on condition of receiving no reward, for He does kindness for free" (line 7380). Giving loses nothing, "like lighting a candle from a candle" (line 7392). *Tie:* `act`. *Ours:* this joins R11's `give_like_Him`; merge them.

## 3.9 Go into the depth of the mitzvah

"Doing a mitzvah with a whole heart means going down into the depth of the mitzvah and understanding how far it reaches" (line 7233). Do it "at once" (line 7641). An offering is "offering himself to G-d" (line 7621). *Tie:* `depth`, `act`.

## 4. The big human questions

- **Is belief enough?** No: "faith from tradition is not enough, only knowledge in the heart" (line 7491).
- **What holds the world up?** He does, every moment; without Him "as if it had never been" (line 7506).
- **Can we say what G-d is?** Before the Will, nothing, "only necessary being, and even this by negation" (line 7758). We know Him by His acts (line 7206).
- **Why do bad things happen?** "The bad is for the sake of the good" (line 7284); G-d "meant it for good" (line 7155). But "let a person not be shaken when he sees a righteous man who suffers and a wicked man who prospers" (line 7821): we do not hold the answer.
- **Am I free?** "Everything is in the hands of heaven except the fear of heaven... handed over to human choice" (lines 7207-7208). "One who makes himself holy a little from below is made holy much from above" (line 7208).
- **Body or soul?** Both. The final good is "only in body and soul whole together" (line 7205), with the Ramban against the Rambam (line 7200).
- **Who am I to others?** One soul with them (line 7114); envy "is the opposite of truth" (line 7123); "if there is no peace there is nothing" (line 7421).
- **Is Torah only for one people?** "Whoever wants to merit the Torah, even from the nations, let him come and merit it" (line 7819). The end: "all the peoples will turn to the true faith" and "all the outer ones will become inner" (lines 7216-7217).
- **What if I cannot understand?** "If understanding is hard for him, let him regret his sins, confess and return to G-d" (line 7800).

## 5. With Chabad (kept)

- **Tanya ch. 42 and knowledge.** Line 7491 is the same demand: da'at, knowledge that reaches the heart, beyond faith. A strong agreement.
- **Renewal of being.** Line 7506 ("for one moment... as if it had never been") matches *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 2 (if the letters left, it would return to nothing). Line 7496 (sunlight) and 7493 (Elokim = nature) match ch. 3 and 6.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** Line 7758 accepts them for the Essence before the Will, as Chabad does. Line 7734 quotes *Yesodei HaTorah* 1:1-4. A key agreement. One disagreement: at line 7200 the Shelah takes the Ramban's view that the final reward is for body and soul together, against the Rambam's world to come of souls alone. Chabad sides with the Ramban here too (the resurrection as the peak).
- **The Ramak.** Lines 7771 and 7774 (all depend on Him; even Keter is no god apart from the Cause of causes) match the *Pardes*. Line 7626 ("no other existence apart from Him") is on the side of "He is all that exists"; the Shelah does not add the Ramak's second half here, but lines 7206 and 7758 (hidden essence, negation) keep the distance.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not taken up in this range.
- **Two kinds of return.** Return from love, not "from fear of punishments" (line 7535), is Tanya's *Iggeret HaTeshuvah* ch. 8-9 on higher return.
- **One soul.** Lines 7114-7115 are the ground of Tanya ch. 32.
- **Not carried.** Particularist lines: 7526, 7533 (the nations compared to an ape before a man), 7773, 7776; the mixed multitude (7599-7608); the anti-Esau polemic; Azazel and Samael as a being (7064-7067); martyrdom framing; suffering as purification (7433, 7466, 7474); austerity (7097, 7292, and the "trembling, not comfort" of line 7829); fasts in the revealed teshuvah (7418); prayer abroad not desired (7687-7688); oaths and legal passages; niddah.
