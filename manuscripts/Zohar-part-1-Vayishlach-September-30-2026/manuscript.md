Vayishlach is Jacob meeting Esau and the angel at night. The Zohar makes it a book of prayer: the righteous who do not lean on their merits but on prayer; the prayer of the poor that breaks through every gate; how to order a prayer (praise, then request; spoken words and the words that stay in the heart); the prayer of many that becomes a crown. Around it: the good and evil inclinations as two angels; light that comes little by little "like the eye of a needle"; the limp that remains after the night; the accuser who turns defender; Rachel dying on the road and weeping for her children; Israel as a worm whose strength is its mouth. ## 2. The unity of God

**Mother and daughter one.** When Jacob built the altar and named the place "El Beit El", the lower level took the name of the higher: «בגין דכד אתנהרא כדין כאמה בתה וכלא חד» (Vayishlach 14:1; line 3602): "for when it is lit, then 'as the mother, so her daughter', and all is one."

**All names rise to one.** Jacob, Israel, Jeshurun; "Creator, Former, Maker": «כל אלין דרגין סלקין לחד … וכלהו דרגין אלין על אלין וכלהו חד» (Vayishlach 25:5; line 3689): "all these levels rise to one … all levels one above another, and all one." Ours: many names, one Named.

**Havayah and Elokim, by what the world needs.** «בזמנא דאסגיאו זכאין בעלמא, יי שמיה ואתקרי בשמא דרחמי. ובזמנא דאסגיאו חייבין בעלמא, אלהים שמיה» (Vayishlach 16:3; line 3610): "when the righteous are many in the world, Havayah is His name and He is called by the name of mercy; when the wicked are many, Elokim is His name." Ours: one God whose face changes with us; close to the Chabad teaching that Elokim is the screen and Havayah the Light (Shaar HaYichud ch. 4-6; memory), but here the stress is moral: we call out which name shows.

**Everything below hangs above.** «דהכי כל מלין דהאי עלמא תליין לעילא … וכלא דא בדא תליא» (Vayishlach 9:6; line 3567): "for so all things of this world hang above … and all hang one on the other." Esau's anger softened because his angel had already agreed above.

**He builds the house when we rise to Him.** "If Havayah does not build the house": He is the builder, «כד סלקין רעותין פולחנין מתתא כדקא יאות» (Vayishlach 11:8; line 3579): "when wills and service rise from below as they should." Ties: the awakening from below (Vayetzei line 3413).

**He alone in the end.** «וישתאר הוא בלחודוי» (Vayishlach 25:6; line 3690): "and He alone will remain."

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **Poor before Him.** The prayer of the poor man is the tefillin of the hand: «דעני אתדבק במסכנותיה, כמאן דלית ליה מגרמיה כלום» (Vayishlach 3:21; line 3504): "for the poor man clings to his poverty, like one who has nothing of his own." And «צלותא דמסכנא איהי צלותא דקיימא קמיה דקודשא בריך הוא ותבר תרעין ופתחין» (Vayishlach 3:23; line 3506): "the prayer of the poor is the prayer that stands before the Holy One and breaks gates and doors." Ties: S59 (true lowliness); Tanya ch. 29-31 on the broken heart (memory).
- **Small before all the kindnesses.** «קטנתי מכל החסדים … וכל מה דעבדת לי עד יומא לאו בגין זכותאי הוה אלא בגינך» (Vayishlach 3:26; line 3509): "I am too small for all the kindnesses … all You did for me until today was not for my merit but for Your sake." Ties: S59, S65.
- **Fear always, beside trust.** Jacob was afraid though promised, because he did not think himself worthy of a miracle; and «בעי ליה לבר נש למדחל תדיר ולצלאה קמי קודשא בריך הוא» (Vayishlach 3:7; line 3490): "a person must fear always and pray before the Holy One." And the fear should be of one's own sins, not of dark creatures: «מאן דדחיל מחטאוי דבידיה אית ליה למדחל» (Vayishlach 27:5; line 3700): "whoever fears the sins in his hand has something to fear." Ties: S84 with awe.
- **Light little by little.** For one who lived in darkness «בעיין לאפתחא ליה נהורא זעירתא כעינא דמחטא, ולבתר רב מניה» (Vayishlach 6:2; line 3532): "they must open for him a small light like the eye of a needle, and after that bigger." So healing: «וכן למאן דאתי אסוותא לאו איהו בשעתא חדא, אלא זעיר זעיר» (Vayishlach 6:3; line 3533): "and so for one to whom healing comes, it is not in one hour but little by little." Ties: S77, S78; new state below.
- **The limp after the night.** When rest comes after long exile, people will look back and ache and wonder at what they suffered: «כדין יסתכלון ויכאבון בגרמייהו מכמה בישין וצערין דסבלו ויתמהו עלייהו» (Vayishlach 6:8; line 3538): "then they will look and ache in themselves at the many evils and sorrows they bore, and be astonished at them." Ours: the pain felt fully only when safe. Ties: S73 ending.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Rely on prayer, not merit.** «צדיקייא לא סמכין על זכותייהו, אלא על צלותהון ובעותהון לגבי מאריהון» (Vayishlach 3:2; line 3485): "the righteous do not lean on their merits, only on their prayers and pleas to their Master." And God «אתרעי תדיר בצלותהון דצדיקיא ומתעטר בצלותהון» (same line): "always desires the prayers of the righteous and is crowned with them."

**Praise first, then ask.** «דאצטריך ליה לבר נש לסדרא שבחא דמאריה בקדמיתא, ולבתר יבעי בעותיה» (Vayishlach 3:27; line 3510): "a person must first set out the praise of his Master, and after that ask his request." Then spell the request out clearly: «מאן דצלי צלותיה דבעי לפרשא מלוי כדקא יאות» (Vayishlach 3:28; line 3511): "whoever prays his prayer must spell out his words as they should be." Ties: technique 20 (own words), the order of the Amidah.

**Spoken words and the meditation of the heart.** "The words of my mouth" are words that are spelled out; «והגיון לבי, אלין מלין דסתימן דלא יכיל בר נש לפרשא לון בפומיה» (Vayishlach 3:30; line 3513): "'and the meditation of my heart', these are hidden words that a person cannot spell out with his mouth." Both are needed; the spoken faces the lower level, the heart's words the inner one, «וכלא כחדא איהו» (Vayishlach 3:31; line 3514): "and all is one." New technique below. Ties: techniques 18 (lips) and 14.

**The prayer of many.** «צלותא דסגיאין סליק קמי קודשא בריך הוא ומתעטר בההוא צלותא. בגין דסלקא בגוונין סגיאין» (Vayishlach 3:3; line 3486): "the prayer of many rises before the Holy One and He is crowned with it, for it rises in many colors"; the prayer of one is of one color. Ties: "Among the many" (Vayetzei).

**Thank for a miracle.** «מכאן מאן דיתעביד ליה נסא, איהו בעי לאודאה» (Vayishlach 13:7; line 3601): "from here: whoever has a miracle done for him must give thanks"; as Jacob built an altar "to the God who answered me in the day of my trouble and was with me on the road I walked" (line 3599). Ties: technique 24.

**Strengthen yourself in Him through Torah.** "If you slacken in the day of trouble": «מאן דאתרפי ידוי מקודשא בריך הוא דלא לאתתקפא ביה. והיך יתקף בר נש ביה בקודשא בריך הוא, יתקיף באורייתא» (Vayishlach 18:1; line 3620): "the one whose hands slacken from the Holy One, not to be strong in Him. And how does a person grow strong in the Holy One? He grows strong in Torah."

**Midnight praise.** David rose at midnight «ומשבח ומודה בשירין ותושבחן» (Vayishlach 29:1; line 3708), "praising and thanking in songs and praises," until morning. «ומאן דידע לשבחא ליה לקודשא בריך הוא כדקא יאות, קודשא בריך הוא קביל צלותיה» (Vayishlach 29:7; line 3714): "whoever knows how to praise the Holy One as is fitting, the Holy One accepts his prayer."

**Do not open your mouth to the Satan.** A curse spoken even without intent is taken up by the accuser in the hour of danger: «לעולם לא יפתח בר נש פומיה לשטנא» (Vayishlach 18:19; line 3638): "a person should never open his mouth to the Satan." Rachel died on the road for Jacob's careless oath (line 3638). Ours: guard the tongue; words said in anger are not empty.

## 5. Big human questions

- **Can the enemy inside become a friend?** The humble one does not follow the evil inclination, «וכדין ההוא יצר הרע מתהפך לעבד לו» (Vayishlach 1:17; line 3458): "and then that evil inclination turns into his servant." More: if a person walks in truth, the accuser himself goes up and argues for him (lines 3625-3626). «ועל דא מבעי ליה לבר נש למיהך בארח קשוט ובגין דיהא ליה ההוא קטיגורא, סניגורא» (Vayishlach 18:9; line 3628): "so a person should walk in the way of truth, so that his accuser becomes his defender." Ties: C-current of transformation, Tanya ch. 27 (memory).
- **Why is the beginning so hard?** «כל שירותא תקיף ולבתר נייחא» (Vayishlach 17:4; line 3617): "every beginning is harsh, and afterward rest"; Rosh Hashanah first, then forgiveness.
- **Can what is broken be mended?** Israel is like the silkworm that dies and lives again from its seed (line 3693), and like glass: «דאף על גב דאתבר אתתקן ואית ליה תקנה כמלקדמין» (Vayishlach 26:4; line 3694): "though it breaks, it is mended and can be repaired as before." Ties: S77, S78.
- **Where is our strength?** «וישראל לית לון תוקפא וחילא לנצחאה לון בר בפומהון» (Vayishlach 26:2; line 3692): "Israel has no strength or power to overcome them except with their mouth", like the worm. Prayer and Torah.
- **What happens after death to one who learned?** The ways of Torah known in this life are the roads the soul walks after (line 3649); the Torah learned stands and speaks for him and does not fall silent (line 3654).
- **Sleep.** «לית לך מאן דנאים בליליא בערסיה, דלא טעים טעמא דמותא» (Vayishlach 4:9; line 3524): "there is no one who sleeps at night in his bed who does not taste a taste of death." Ties: the Shema before sleep, "Modeh Ani" on waking.
- **Rachel weeping.** She was buried on the road so that when Israel returns from exile they will stand at her grave and weep, as she wept for their exile (line 3643).
- **Joy.** The curses came «תחת אשר לא עבדת את יי אלהיך בשמחה ובטוב לבב מרוב כל» (Vayishlach 22:10; line 3672): "because you did not serve Havayah your God with joy and goodness of heart from abundance of all."

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **The prayer of the poor breaks every gate (lines 3504, 3506).** For the one who comes with nothing.
2. **Praise, then ask; spoken words and the heart's hidden words (lines 3510-3514).** A plain order for personal prayer.
3. **Light little by little; healing little by little (lines 3532-3533).** For impatience with slow change.
4. **The accuser made defender (lines 3626-3628).** The Zohar's hope for the evil inclination.
5. **"Mended as before" (line 3694).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "Praise, spell it out, then leave the rest in the heart."** First set out His praise; then ask in plain, exact words (what, from whom, why); then stop speaking and let what cannot be said stay as a meditation of the heart before Him (lines 3510-3515). Ties techniques 18, 20.
- **State, new: "Nothing of my own."** Inside: coming before Him as a poor man who has nothing, not even merit; strangely freeing, and the prayer goes straight in (lines 3504-3506, 3509). How to enter: drop the list of what you have done; say "I am too small for all the kindnesses." Near S59.
- **State, new: "Needle's-eye light."** Inside: a very small opening of light after long darkness, not yet enough to live in, but real; knowing it widens little by little (lines 3532-3535). How to enter: accept the small light without demanding the whole day. Near S78, S65.
- **Technique, new: "Thank for the road."** After a rescue, however small, build an "altar": stop and say aloud to whom it is owed and "He was with me on the road I walked" (lines 3599-3601). Ties technique 24.
- **State, new: "The limp after."** Inside: once safe, the old pain is felt and wondered at; not a relapse but a late honesty (line 3538). How to enter: allow it; do not rush to cover it. Near S73.
