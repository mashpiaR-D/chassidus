## The shape of the range

The range starts in the middle of *Kol HaShoneh Halachos* (Emor 5667) and ends in the middle of *Ein Omdin Lehispallel* (Naso 5667). It covers the maamarim from Lag BaOmer season through Shavuos.

The editor notes (5347) that *BeShaah SheHikdimu* and the seven maamarim after it form a unit within the Hemshech, and (5334) that *Im Bechukosai* closes an argument begun at the very start of the Hemshech. The range runs in three movements. 1. **Truth to its truth (5140-5339).** Everything revealed, even the light before the tzimtzum, is "truth" but not "truth to its truth". Only the Essence, whose existence is from itself, is that. Halacha is rooted there, and so is the bitul of "there is none else". The humble who study under the yoke reach it, and it will be revealed below, in the physical world. 2. **Souls and angels (5340-5570).** Angels come from speech, souls from thought. Angels are real creatures, while souls are "Godliness itself that became a something". This leads to the limit of knowledge: from the light we know nothing of the Essence, not even that it is a luminary. Only creation from nothing tells us that it exists from itself. 3. **Written and engraved letters (5571-5774).** Vessels that are "another being" hide the light, like ink on parchment. Vessels of the same substance as the light, like letters engraved in a gem, reveal the Essence itself, and only dim it a little. The Torah at Sinai, the halachos, the soul's root and the sefirot of Keter are engraved letters.

F05 leads the count because nearly every argument ends in the Essence. F06 and F01 are high because two maamarim are about how souls and angels come to be from supernal speech and thought. F11 is low: refining appears, but the range is about the root more than the work.

## Kol HaShoneh Halachos, its end (5140-5192): Eloka and Elokus, and why halacha is from Havayah

The maamar distinguishes Eloka ("God", *got*) from Elokus ("divine-ness", *getlich*). Atzilut is only Elokus, an expansion of a ray. The name Eloka belongs to the Essence alone: > דאלקה הוא בח עצמי, וקאי על עצמותו ית', דמציאותו מעצמותו (line 5140)

Even the vessels of Atzilut are Elokus. Creation passes through them, but they are "like an axe in the hand of the hewer" (5140, F05, F01). The Names come next. Havayah is the Essence and is one, while Elokim is plural because it is light that comes in division (5141-5147). Even "from the side of the worlds He is truly alone": > שגם מצד העולמות הוא בבחי' יחיד ממש (line 5147)

The Rokeach reads the three Names of Shema as "first, last, and besides Me there is no Elokim". The middle time, now, is like before creation: > הכל לפניו ית' כאלו הוא ושמו בלבד, ואין אלקים והסתר כלל (line 5149)

"Elokeinu" is there because Israel draw "one" into the earth through Torah (5149, F07, F10).

Then comes the argument about truth. Havayah is the Name of the necessary existent. Created things are not true existence (5154). Yet the world is not an illusion. The Rashab argues from the Mishnah that if the world did not exist there would be no reward and punishment and no Torah and mitzvot (5175, F02, F03, F10): > דבאמת מציאות העולם הוא מציאות, אלא שאינו מציאות אמיתי ממש (line 5175)

For the Essence there is no world at all, because for Him the tzimtzum does not hide: > כי לגבי' אין הצמצום מסתיר כלל (line 5175)

Even the light before the tzimtzum is only "truth", because what could cease is not true existence (5169, 5175). Only His essential Name is "truth to its truth", "He is His Name and His Name is He" (5163, 5180). Halacha is rooted there: > דמשם שרש ההלכה (line 5185)

That is why the one who learns halachos is a "son" of the World to Come, who inherits everything (5190). The Rashab's asterisked note (5187) sets out a pair that runs through the whole range: "all before Him is as nothing" still leaves something, but "there is none else" leaves nothing at all: > אבל אין עוד היינו שלא יש שום דבר כלל (line 5187)

## Im Bechukosai (5193-5339): the decider, the two knowledges, and the humble

Why does the halacha follow the one who decides between two views? Chesed and Gevurah are both true but oppose each other. Only what bears both is truth to its truth, and only the Essence bears opposites (5208, F09): > דכל התכללות הפכים צ"ל דוקא מצד עצמות אור א"ס הנושא כל ההפכים (line 5208)

The decider does not take a side. He holds a third view that contains both (5220). Levi, "joining", has the same power (5231).

In service, truth to its truth comes through two knowledges (5232-5239). The lower knowledge is the creature's feeling of itself. The higher is that "above is something and below is nothing": > וד"ע היא דעה העליונה, שלמעלה יש ולמטה אין (line 5233) > והאמת הוא כהדעה העליונה (line 5234)

Both knowledges are "in Godliness" (5235). The yesh takes up room only before the ray that is its source, and only because that ray hides itself (5239). Line 5241 then places the two knowledges inside Atzilut, as two arrangements of the sefirot. Line 5243 adds a third level above them, the bitul of "there is none else". That bitul belongs to the essential Name (5251). It is found especially in the creation of *physical* yesh, and it is reached now by the servant under the yoke, "whose whole existence is his master's" (5251, F08): > וכל מציאותו הוא מציאות האדון עצמו (line 5251)

Only the utterly humble can bring out the true halacha, because "the vessel for Ein Sof is only what is nullified" (5253, citing Tanya 35): > דהכלי לאוא"ס הוא מה שבטל דוקא (line 5253)

David the king was humble, and Beis Hillel were gentle and humble. So they, and not the sharper Beis Shammai, were "vessels for the Essence" (5257, 5264). Thanksgiving is essential bitul. Its root is the soul's essence bound to the Essence, which is why Jews acknowledge creation from nothing "with no proof or demonstration" (5262, F06, F12): > דנש"י מודים בחידוש ההשפעה מאין ליש בלי שום ראי' ומופת (line 5262)

The maamar ends in F07. The purpose of the tzimtzum is that through Torah and mitzvot the Essence be revealed in the empty space. The Zohar's "so that they know Me" is only for Atzilut and "is not the true intent": > דתכלית הכוונה הוא שיהי' גילוי עצמות א"ס ב"ה למטה דוקא (line 5330)

So the promised rewards are physical (5336): > דתכלית הגילוי לעתיד יהי' למטה בגשמיות דוקא (line 5336)

## BeShaah SheHikdimu (5340-5481): speech, the screen, and the angel's name

The Torah was given to souls "as they are below", not to angels (5342). Angels come from supernal speech. Of the soul it says "He breathed", and "one who breathes breathes from within" (5344). The long analogy of the 22 letters in the soul (5355-5366) is structural (G06). Its application is stated. The 22 letters of supernal speech "give life to all worlds" (5367, F01). The world is bounded only through the *parsa*, letters out of order that hide the light (5367, F03). The exchanged letters give the two unities (5376, F02): > דאחד הוא בחי' יחו"ע, שהוא בחי' ביטול במציאות ממש (line 5376)

Here *echad* is the higher unity, from the essential light of Atzilut, and *va'ed* is the lower unity, from the ray that is the source of yesh. Every creature has its own letter-combination in the Ten Sayings, and every angel's name is "the combination that gives it life". The angel's "El" is the light of Chochmah without which "they would be as nothing" (5404, 5472): > ואם לא הנהירו עילאה זו, היו כלא היו (line 5472)

The "matter" of the letters makes the yesh. Their "form", which breaks through the screen, makes the yesh nullified (5438). Malchus is the Name Ban, "the spirit that descends", because making the finite from the infinite is "the utmost descent" (5415): > דהנה באמת אין ערוך כלל הנברא לגבי הבורא (line 5415)

Souls know Godliness differently. Souls of the lower worlds know only that Godliness *exists*, even in Gan Eden. Its essence they know only through prayer, Torah and mitzvot (5386, F12, F10).

## Anochi (5482-5570): nothing can be known from the light

Why "who took you out of Egypt" and not "who created heaven and earth"? "I Havayah have not changed": creation changes nothing in Him, because the power that makes worlds is only "the spreading of His Name" (5524). A name is a ray with no nearness to the self. From it: > שמהארה זו אין אנו יודעים את העצם (line 5534)

The light cleaves to the Essence, since nothing interrupts between them (5538). Still, it tells us nothing, not even that the Essence is a luminary: > והעצמות ה"ה נמצא בבלתי מציאות נמצא (line 5542)

Only creation from nothing tells us something, following Iggeres HaKodesh 20 (5548, F05, F01, F12): > הרי דמהתהוות יש מאין אנו יודעים שהעצמות מציאותו הוא מעצמותו (line 5548)

The light cannot of itself be a source of worlds. That would put an "affect" in the Essence, and the Essence has none (5552). The tzimtzum makes it fit to be a source. What passes through the tzimtzum is not the first light, just as the life in hair passes the skull and so feels no pain (5556). Egypt means limits, and leaving limits is only by the unlimited Essence. That is the whole point of Torah: > דהרי זהו כל ענין התורה, להמשיך בחי' אוא"ס שלפני הצמצום, שיאיר בגילוי לאחר הצמצום (line 5558)

What creation reveals of the Essence was not yet revealed at Sinai. It waits for the future: > וזה יתגלה לעתיד, וגלות האחרון הוא הכנה לגילוי זה (line 5568)

## Panim BePanim (5571-5700): the form of a man, and ink against engraving

The residue (*reshimu*) is the power of limit in Ein Sof. Without it, His perfection would lack something (5574, F09): > דא"ס כשם שיש לו כח בבלתי בע"ג כן יש לו כח בגבול (line 5574)

The kav still cleaves to its source after the tzimtzum (5582). The filling light clothes in the vessels and takes "the form of a man", as the soul fills the body (5597). All the tzimtzumim are for the souls (5600, F07, F06): > הנה כללות כל הצמצומים אלו הוא בשביל נש"י (line 5600)

The Rashab goes further. Ein Sof contracts into the ten sefirot at all *because* Israel rose in thought: > ואדרבה, מה שאוא"ס מצמצם עצמו בהכלים דע"ס, זהו לפי שישראל עלו במחשבה (line 5609)

Angels come from the outer side of the vessels, which acts on another. Souls come from the inner side, the *tzelem*, which unites with the light (5628, 5630): > אבל נשמות, בעצם מהותן הם אלקות ממש, וגם כמו שירדו לבי"ע הן בעצם אלקות (line 5630)

Then the parable that governs the rest of the range. Ink letters are another thing laid on the parchment, and so they hide it. The parchment is the surrounding light that bears all (5673, F04). Engraved letters are the gem itself, so "the essence of the light is revealed" (5680). Their slight darkening is only less shining. A hiding that nothing forces is not hiding: > והסתר רצוני אינו הסתר (line 5684)

Line 5688 ranks the hidings. The screen does not hide before the Essence, because Havayah and Elokim are one: > דהוי' ואלקים כולא חד (line 5688)

What really hides is the kelipah, "which separate from His unity". Even that hiding is not forced, and He went down into Egypt Himself (5688, F11, F03). At Sinai the Torah was engraved on the tablets. It was the essence of the light above Atzilut, not a ray (5690). It is called a "sale" because it is acquired by toil. Love and awe in prayer come first, and only then is the light drawn through Torah (5698, F10, F12).

## Ein Omdin Lehispallel, its first part (5701-5774): the Name with no meaning

Every Name of God has a meaning and a secular use except Havayah, because Havayah is not a power (5703, 5708): > אבל שם הוי' שאין לו פי', היינו שאינו בבחי' כח (line 5708)

The vessels are powers and can be partly grasped. The light is simple, and "we do not know it at all" (5710). The Rashab separates the light's "existence of nothing" from the Essence's "existence without an existent". Vessels of Atzilut are Godliness, but "another being", so only a ray clothes in them (5726). In the halachos the garment is Godly, so learning them grasps the very essence of Chochmah (5726, 5752): > מ"מ, בלימוד ההלכות ה"ה תופס במהות ח"ע (line 5726)

The sefirot of Keter and Adam Kadmon are engraved letters, "still wholly united with the Essence of Ein Sof" (5746): > וזהו מה שאותיות עצמיים מגלים את העצם (line 5746)

In Keter the vessel becomes like the light, and "there is no left in this Atika". That is why tzaddikim can turn judgment into mercy (5756, F09, F08). In the future the body too will be Godliness (5765): > אבל הגוף יהי' ג"כ אלקות (line 5765)

Engraving belongs to souls alone, not to angels (5771).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **A ladder of truth above the Tanya's.** Shaar HaYichud 3 ends at "all before Him is as nothing" (the sunlight in the sun). Here that is only "truth". Above it is "there is none else", which the Rashab places in the Essence's own Name and, surprisingly, *in the creation of physical yesh* (5243, 5251).
- **The world is real, and that matters.** Shaar HaYichud stresses that the world is as nothing before Him. This range argues against acosmism: the world is not an illusion, or there would be no reward and punishment and no Torah (5175). It is real existence, only not true existence.
- **Halacha above Chochmah.** Tanya ch. 5 makes Torah the supernal Chochmah and will. Here halacha, the practical ruling, reaches the essential hiddenness *above* Chochmah (5185). Sharpness reaches only the hidden power of Chochmah, and humility reaches the Essence (5264, 5271).
- **Souls as the reason for the tzimtzum.** Tanya 49 says "love compresses" and all tzimtzumim are for Israel. The Rashab cites it (5600) and makes it stronger. The sefirot themselves exist because Israel rose in thought (5609). He also sharpens Tanya ch. 2: even as created, the soul is "Godliness itself that became a something" (5511), unlike an angel.
- **Written versus engraved.** The Tanya's garments of the soul and its "Torah and the Holy One are one" gain a precise tool. A garment hides only when it is another being. Where the revealer is of the same substance (engraved letters, Keter, the halachos, the soul's root), the Essence itself is revealed, only diminished (5680, 5726, 5746).
- **Purpose restated.** Tanya 36 gives the dwelling below. This range rules out "so that they know Me" and "so that His powers be revealed" as the true intent (5330).

## Where the tie is thin

- Over half the range is the editor's apparatus. It is marked none except for nine notes of substance. - The analogies are long: the 22 letters in the soul (5355-5366), thought and speech (5446-5465, 5485-5497), the student absorbed before his teacher (5423), hair and the skull (5556), the cup (5618), ink and gems (5668-5684). These are structural, and the tie lies in how they are applied. - The angelology (5403-5404, 5471-5479) and the letter-combinatorics of Sefer Yetzirah (5391-5393) are building blocks. They serve the unity only through the claim that every creature lives by its letters. - Two lines are marked *reading*. At 5423, the effect that "forgets itself" in its cause is cosmology, and reading it as bitul is ours. At 5716, simple light doing opposite things is about simplicity, and reading it as the unity of opposites is ours. - There are no maamar dates inside Anochi and Panim BePanim other than the page headers "first night" and "second night of Shavuos". The first part of Panim BePanim sits on a page with no header (623-625), and its night is taken from the headers that follow.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **What does Havayah mean?** Line 5154 (Ginas Egoz) seems to say He is called Havayah as Creator, "not from His very Essence". Line 5163 answers that He can create only because He is the true existent, so Havayah is His Name included in His Essence, with no letters. In Ein Omdin, Havayah "has no meaning at all", and "mehaveh" is not what the Name says (5703, 5708). The Rebbe's footnote (5159-5161) says the Hemshech's reading rests on the acronym hint in the Rambam. By the plain sense, "first existent" is God lowering Himself to relate to creatures, and the Havayah hinted there is the Name *with* letters. Tanya's Shaar HaYichud 4 reads Havayah as "mehaveh" without this reservation.
- **"These and these are the words of Elokim", yet halacha is from Havayah.** The Rashab resolves it himself (5264, 5269): the Havayah of halacha has no letters and so is also called Elokim. Both schools reached an essential hiddenness, Beis Shammai that of Chochmah and Beis Hillel that of Ein Sof.
- **Is the Essence a luminary?** The range says we cannot even call the Essence a luminary (5542, 5546). The Rebbe's footnote (5551) notes that the Alter Rebbe's wording in Shaar HaYichud 10 implies that even in the sun there are two things, the luminary and the essence above it. The two are left side by side.
- **How far does service reach?** Line 5519 says a soul reaches its root in prayer, and a light of Adam Kadmon is in every Jew. Line 5386 says lower souls know the essence of Godliness only through prayer, Torah and mitzvot. The Rashab's own note (5734), following Iggeres HaKodesh's Kuntres Acharon, says love and awe reach only the *existence* of Godliness, and Torah and mitzvot draw its *essence*. Line 5698 puts love and awe in prayer *before* Torah as its condition.
- **Angels silent or speaking.** Line 5464 gives two readings. In one, silence is the angels' ascent into their source. In Torah Or's reading, they fall silent when the supernal speech shines and they are nullified, and they speak from longing when it does not. Both are left.
- **The moon verse.** The Gemara reads "the moon's light like the sun's" as the days of Mashiach, when there will be only lights with gradations. Rashi reads "sevenfold" as more than the present light. The Zohar reads it as the future revelation of the essential hiddenness in Malchus (5315, 5321). The Rashab keeps all three at different levels.
- **Angels called by the Name Havayah.** Iggeres HaKodesh says an angel sent from above is called by the Name Havayah dwelling in it. This range says angels are wholly separate from their source. The Rashab says "it needs study" (5512-5514). He offers that souls' service draws Atzilut's "He and His causations are one" even into angels, and he does not claim to have settled it.
