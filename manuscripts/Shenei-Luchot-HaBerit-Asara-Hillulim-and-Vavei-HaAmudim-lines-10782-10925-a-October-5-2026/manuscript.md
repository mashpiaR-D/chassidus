This last range holds two works. The first, *Asara Hillulim* ("Ten Praises"), is the Shelah's short summary of the *Chovot HaLevavot* (Duties of the Heart) in ten gates. He asks that one gate be read every day, "in heart and tongue", and then started again, forever (line 10783). It is the most practical page in the whole book. It ends with a ladder of creation up to the Throne, where all things are "as nothing", and He alone brings them into being and gives them life at every moment (line 10861).

The second work, *Vavei HaAmudim* ("Hooks of the Pillars"), is by his son, R. Shabtai Sheftel Horowitz. Sefaria prints it at the end of the Shelah. It is built on six pillars: Torah, service, kindness, justice, truth and peace. Most of it is Talmudic *pilpul* (sharp textual debate) that was read and not carried. But each pillar ends with a short *mussar* (moral teaching) section, and these hold real gold: a three-step prayer method (line 10895), the four letters of the Name "engraved" on a person's head (line 10905), and a proof of the unity from truth. Falsehood is the one thing G-d did not create, so a liar implies a second creator (line 10918).

## 1.1 In heart and tongue, above and below

The first gate sets the task. Unity is not only a belief. It is to be known and spoken, and it is to be made King in every level of creation:

> המליכוהו למעלה ולמטה בכל הבריאה
> "Make Him King above and below in all creation." (line 10785, Asara Hillulim, Shaar HaYichud 4)

The daily reading itself is to be done "in heart and tongue" (בלב ולשון, line 10783). The gate of contemplation (1.3) and the gate of love (1.2) show how.

## 1.2 He brings all into being, gives all life, always

This is the key passage of the range. In the gate of love, the Shelah climbs from the earth through the spheres and the angels to the Throne:

> וכולם היו כלא היו נגד הכסא
> "And all of them are as nothing before the Throne." (line 10861, Shaar HaAhavah 2)

> והוא סיבת כל הסיבות
> "And He is the Cause of all causes." (line 10861)

> והוא מהוה את כולם ומחיה את כולם ומשפיע בהם תמיד
> "And He brings all of them into being, gives them all life, and pours into them always." (line 10861)

The same idea turns into a fact of every moment in the gate of separation:

> שאלו ה' לא יחשוב בנו לטובה רגע א' אז כל המציאות היה כלא היה
> "That if G-d did not think of us for good for one moment, all that exists would be as if it never was." (line 10858,
> Shaar HaPrishut 4)

And in the gate of humility it becomes the root of patience. Quoting *Tomer Devorah*, he says no one ever sinned against G-d without G-d giving him life "in that very moment":

> שלא יחייהו הוא יתברך באותה רגע ממש
> "That He does not give him life in that very moment." (line 10828, Shaar HaKeniah 4)

## 1.3 One in all things, and all things in His oneness

The gate of contemplation walks through the heavens, the elements, plants, animals, the human body and the embryo in the womb. It ends:

> שאדון יחיד ואין שני מתיחד בהם והם באחדותיך
> "That a single Master with no second is one in them, and they are in Your oneness." (line 10803, Shaar HaBechina 2)

This is two sided. He is one in them, and they are inside His oneness. It is the nearest thing in the range to the Chabad reading of "there is nothing else".

## 1.4 "There is nothing else"

The gate of humility ends with the verse the Chabad map rests on:

> כי במה הוא נחשב נגד ה' הוא האלהים אין עוד
> "For what is he worth before G-d? He is G-d, there is nothing else." (line 10834, Shaar HaKeniah 10)

Here the Shelah uses it for humility, not for metaphysics. The two are the same move. If there is nothing else, the self has nothing of its own to boast of.

## 1.5 Truth is His seal because He is One

The son's pillar of truth gives two new proofs of the unity. The first is from the letters of the word *emet* (truth):

> חותמו אמת להודיע כי הוא אחד
> "His seal is truth, to make known that He is One." (line 10916, Vavei HaAmudim 22:2)

He adds that *aleph* to *mem* is thirteen letters, the number of *echad* (one), and that on "that day" all the Names are one:

> שכל השמות הכל אחד
> "That all the Names are all one." (line 10916)

That is why "truth" is joined to the two Names in the Shema, "so that no one say there are two powers":

> נודע כי הוא אחד ואין שני לו
> "It is known that He is One and has no second." (line 10916)

The second proof is moral. G-d made everything except falsehood:

> הכל ברא הקב"ה בעולמו חוץ ממדת שקר ועולה
> "G-d created everything in His world except falsehood and wrong." (line 10918, Vavei HaAmudim 24:1)

So a person who lies is forced to say that another creator made the lie, and then:

> ואיכא שני רשויות
> "And then there would be two powers." (line 10918)

That is why the sages say that one who goes back on his word is like one who serves idols. The pillar ends with the goal:

> לידע ולהודיע כי י"י הוא אחד וחותמו אמת
> "To know and make known that G-d is One and His seal is truth." (line 10918)

## 1.6 No second power in the offerings, or in the mind

The pillar of service repeats the Talmud's point that the offerings use only the Name of mercy:

> שלא ליתן פתחון פה לומר שיש ב' רשויות
> "So as not to give an opening to say there are two powers." (line 10892, Vavei HaAmudim 6:3)

G-d does not need them: "the whole world is Mine" (לא שאני צריך לקרבנן כל העולם כולה שלי הוא, line 10892). And from the *SMaG* (Sefer Mitzvot Gadol), the first commandment includes a guard on thought:

> שלא לעלות במחשבה שיש שום אלוה זולת י"י
> "Not to let the thought arise that there is any god besides G-d." (line 10893, Vavei HaAmudim 7:1)

In the pillar of peace, Moses tells Israel that one service should match one G-d:

> כשם שהוא יחידי בעולם כך תהא עבודתכם מיוחדת לפניו
> "As He is alone in the world, so let your service be one before Him." (line 10921, Vavei HaAmudim 26:1)

## 1.7 The Name above the head; a Name filled by deeds

From the Zohar (as quoted by R. Avraham Galante), the four letters of the Name rest on a person's head. The *yud* wakes fear, the first *heh* love, the *vav* Torah, and the last *heh* the commandments:

> ובהיות אדם דביק בי"י מופיע ד' אותיות השם
> "When a person cleaves to G-d, the four letters of the Name appear." (line 10905, Vavei HaAmudim 15:1)

> כי אתם דבוקים ממש בשם י"י שחקוק על ראשיכם
> "For you are truly joined to the Name of G-d engraved on your heads." (line 10905)

This is how the son reads "I have set G-d before me always": so that the Name does not leave. The same line says that charity "completes" the Name. The arm is a *vav*, the five fingers a *heh*, the small coin a *yud*, and the poor man's hand the last *heh*. And line 10910 says the Name is "filled" by our deeds: when we do many commandments, "then the Name is filled" (אזי מתמלא השם).

## 1.8 Justice below keeps Him in mercy above

The pillar of justice reads the Zohar on "I will go down now and see" (Gen. 18:21):

> ארדה נא מדרגא דרחמי לדרגא דדינא
> "I will go down from the level of mercy to the level of judgment." (line 10907, Vavei HaAmudim 16:2)

> במקום שיש דין למטה אין דין למעלה
> "Where there is judgment below there is no judgment above." (line 10907)

When we do justice, He stays "in His high place" of pure mercy. That is the sense of "My salvation": it is His own. And one who judges truly is "like a partner of G-d in the work of creation" (כל הדן דין אמת לאמתו כאלו נעשה שותף להקב"ה במעשה בראשית, line 10910).

## 3.1 One gate a day (proposed: `daily_gate`)

The frame of the whole work. Read one gate a day "in heart and tongue" and start again when done (line 10783, בלב ולשון), for "remembering brings to action". *Tie:* `chazarah`, `lips`. *Ours:* the guide can offer the ten gates as a ten-day cycle.

## 3.2 Contemplating creation into oneness (tie: `breadth`, `details`)

The gate of contemplation (line 10803) is a long guided walk: sky, sun, elements, earth, plants, animals, the body, the embryo. It ends in "they are in Your oneness" (1.3). *Tie:* `breadth` and `details`. *Ours:* one level of creation per sitting, ending each time with line 10803.

## 3.3 Tongue not ahead of heart (tie: `heart_after`, `lips`)

> אל יקדים לשונך ללבך
> "Let your tongue not run ahead of your heart." (line 10807, Shaar Avodat Elohim 4)

Before prayer, ask "what am I?" and say the Name with trembling. The line before it widens the rule to the whole body:

> לא תעתיק רגל ויד ותנועת עפעף
> "Do not move a foot, a hand, or an eyelid" (except for Him). (line 10806, Shaar Avodat Elohim 3)

> תמיד תשווה אותו לנגד עיניך
> "Always set Him before your eyes." (line 10806)

*Tie:* `heart_after`, `intent_in_every_act` (R15).

## 3.4 Lying down before Whom (tie: `before_whom`)

> שהמלך הגדול הקב"ה אשר מלא כל הארץ כבודו עומד עליו ורואה במעשיו
> "That the great King, whose glory fills the earth, stands over him and sees his deeds." (line 10833, Shaar HaKeniah 9)

> ובשכבו על משכבו ידע לפני מי הוא שוכב
> "And when he lies on his bed, let him know before Whom he lies." (line 10833)

*Tie:* `before_whom`. *Ours:* a night form of the practice, said lying down.

## 3.5 Trust as rest, and "you are His messenger" (tie: `steward`)

Trust is "in quiet and rest" (בהשקט ומנוחה, line 10810). Money is a deposit. When you give, you give Him His own, "and you are His messenger" (ואתה שלוחו, line 10815). In the gate of accounting:

> וקבל כל המאוראות בחיבה
> "And accept all that happens with love." (line 10852, Shaar Cheshbon HaNefesh 3)

> אין לך לא סומך ולא משען כי אם אבינו שבשמים
> "You have no support and no staff except our Father in heaven." (line 10854)

## 3.6 The ladder to the Throne (proposed: `ladder_to_throne`)

Climb in thought from the earth to the Throne, level by level, and at each level say that it is "as nothing" before the one above, until "all of them are as nothing before the Throne". Then come back down: He "brings all into being, gives all life, and pours into them always" (line 10861). The love that follows is "the soul's longing"; the Shelah asks, "how can the heart not burn with love for Him" (ואיך לא יתלהב הלב לאהבתו, line 10867). And from the gate of love, the joy that "a gnat like me" (מיתוש כמוני, line 10864) is allowed to serve. *Tie:* `depth`, S43, S62. *Ours:* this is the book's plainest form of the ascent and return that Tanya ch. 46 and *Shaar HaYichud* ch. 1 to 3 teach.

## 3.7 The solitude test (proposed: `solitude_test`)

> ובחון נפשך שאם היית מתייחד היית עושה המעשה ההוא
> "Test yourself: if you were alone, would you do that deed?" (line 10823, Shaar Yichud HaMaaseh 8)

If yes, the deed is whole. Line 10824 adds: before each act, ask the good inclination. *Tie:* `discern`. The son's pillar of kindness gives the same test for modesty: "even though you are alone in the inner rooms and no one sees you" (הגם שאתה לבד בחדרי חדרים ואין אדם רואה אותך, line 10901), walk humbly with G-d.

## 3.8 Nightly forgiveness (proposed: `nightly_forgiveness`)

> שרי ליה מארי לכל מאן דמצער לי
> "Master, forgive everyone who hurt me." (line 10832, Shaar HaKeniah 8)

Said at night, as Mar Zutra did. With it, the insult teaching: "and G-d told him, curse" (וי"י אמר לו קלל, line 10827). The one who hurts you is a messenger. And the humble root: "lowliness is one with the height of the soul" (הכניעה היא אחד רוממות הנפש, line 10830); "wherever you find His greatness" you find His humility (כל מקום שאתה מוצא גדולתו, line 10829). *Tie:* `insult_as_atonement` (R15), S15.

## 3.9 Evening return (proposed: `evening_return`)

The gate of return gives four times of atonement: every evening, before Shabbat, before the new month, and Yom Kippur. The point of the daily one is:

> נמצא כל ימיך בתשובה
> "So all your days are spent in return." (line 10848, Shaar Teshuva 14)

It is to be fixed at sunset. With it, a prayer for help: "be with my heart when I think and with my mouth when I speak" (ותהיה עם לבי בעת מחשבתי ועם פי בעת הטיפי, line 10844). *Tie:* `cheshbon`, S77. The son adds David's way (line 10914): G-d has erased the sin, "only I remember my sin always" (רק אני זוכר עוני תמיד), to stay humble, not to despair. *Ours:* the guide keeps the evening review and the prayer. It does not keep the fasting programs.

## 3.10 Heaven, then the Temple: the son's prayer method (proposed: `heaven_then_temple`)

The pillar of service (line 10895) gives three steps from Rabbeinu Yonah. First turn toward the Temple. Then think:

> כאלו עומד בשמים להפשיט גופו מעל נשמתו
> "As if standing in heaven, stripping his body off his soul." (line 10895, Vavei HaAmudim 9:1)

Then, after the body has been quieted, come back down:

> כאלו עומד בב"ה ממש ומתפלל שם
> "As if standing in the Temple itself and praying there." (line 10895)

The hands are held "as if they had no hands" (כאלו אין להם ידים), that is, as if one had no power of one's own. And prayer is great in itself: "if only a person would pray the whole day" (והלואי שיתפלל האדם כל היום כולו, line 10896). Pray in a language you understand, "because prayer depends on the heart" (לפי שהתפלה תלויה בלב, line 10899); "the Merciful One wants the heart" (רחמנא לבא בעי, line 10899). *Tie:* `picturing`, `arrive`. The Asara Hillulim says the early pious did the same: "moving toward the stripping off of the physical" (מניעים להתפשטות הגשמיות, line 10808).

## 3.11 True to the nod (proposed: `true_to_the_nod`)

From *Sefer Chasidim*, quoted in the pillar of truth:

> אף נענוע שלך בראשך יהיה אמת
> "Even the nod of your head should be true." (line 10918)

> וכל האיברים כולם יהיו אמתיים
> "And all the limbs should be true." (line 10918)

And from the Talmud, "that there not be one thing in the mouth and another in the heart" (שלא יהא אחד בפה ואחד בלב, line 10916). *Ours:* since a lie implies a second power (1.5), keeping every word and gesture true is a practice of the unity itself.

## 3.12 Sing at the promise (proposed: `sing_at_the_promise`)

From the Shelah himself, quoted by his son in the Song of the Sea (line 10922). Faith sings as soon as the good is promised, before it comes:

> יקדימו לשורר מיד קודם שיתקיים הדבר
> "They will sing at once, before the thing comes true." (line 10922, Vavei HaAmudim 27:1)

The verse is "sing from the head of faith" (תשורי מראש אמנה). *Tie:* S53, S84. *Ours:* thank for help while still asking.

## 3.13 Two more from the son

- **Torah against the inclination.** "If your inclination comes to ruin you, lean it on words of Torah, and I count it for you as if you created peace" (מעלה אני עליך כאלו בראת את השלום, line 10921). *Tie:* `garment_look`.
- **Mercy inside the verse of judgment** (proposed: `mercy_in_the_verse`). In the Rosh Hashanah debate from the Recanati (line 10913), mercy wins every time "from the very verse that the attribute of judgment brought" (מתוך אותו פסוק עצמו שהביא מדת הדין). The King of kings "holds back anger" (וממ"ה כובש חימה), and remembers mercy "even in His hour of anger" (אפילו בשעת כעסו). *Ours:* when a harsh verse or a harsh day comes, look for the mercy inside that same thing.

## 4. The big human questions

- **Why does anything exist at all?** Because He thinks of it for good at every moment; if He stopped, "all that exists would be as if it never was" (line 10858).
- **Why is lying so serious?** Because truth is His seal and He made everything but the lie. A lie implies another maker (lines 10916, 10918).
- **How do I know my deed is pure?** Would I do it alone? (line 10823).
- **What do I do with people who hurt me?** Forgive them each night; the one who curses is a messenger (lines 10827, 10832).
- **Is suffering a sign that G-d is angry?** Even in His anger He remembers mercy; mercy is found inside judgment's own words (line 10913). And "accept all that happens with love" (line 10852).
- **Why is the exile so long?** The son's answer (line 10924): our commandments are "like a rote command of men, without waking and burning of the heart" (כמצות אנשים מלומדה ולא בהתעוררות הלב ובהתלהבות הלב), and in prayer "the mind wanders in the markets and streets" (והמחשבה משוטטת בשווקים וברחובות). Add baseless hatred and gossip.
- **What holds blessing?** "There is no vessel that holds blessing like peace" (אין כלי המחזיק ברכה כמו שלום, line 10924). Seek it "in your place, and chase it in another place" (בקשהו במקומך ורודפיהו במקום אחר, line 10920).
- **Does a person choose?** "The choice is given into a person's hand" (הבחירה נתונה ביד אדם, line 10911).
- **What is death?** The soul at every hour seeks to leave the body, and returns when the Presence shows itself (הנשמה בכל שעה מבקשת לילך מגופו של אדם, line 10917). The righteous rise and do not return to dust (line 10918).

## 5. With Chabad (kept)

- **"He brings all into being and gives all life always."** Line 10861 is almost the words of *Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah* ch. 1 to 2: creation from nothing renewed at every moment. Line 10858 ("if He did not think of us one moment") is the same idea from the side of His thought. Line 10828 (from *Tomer Devorah*) is the same idea turned into patience.
- **"All things in Your oneness."** Line 10803 fits the Chabad reading that the world is held inside the unity, not beside it.
- **"There is nothing else."** Line 10834 uses the verse for humility; Tanya uses it for the unity. The move is the same.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The first gate lists "He is not a body nor a power in a body" and "the true One has no likeness" (lines 10786-10799). Chabad keeps this for the Essence. The Shelah sets it beside the unity of the ten *sefirot* for "the pure" (line 10785), which is also the Chabad order.
- **Ramak, "He is all, not all is He".** No new statement here. Line 10803 ("one in them, and they in Your oneness") and line 10861 ("as nothing before the Throne") hold both halves: He is in all, and all is nothing before Him.
- **Tzimtzum.** No statement in this range.
- **Love from contemplation.** Line 10861 builds love from fear of His greatness and from contemplation of the ladder of creation. Tanya ch. 41 to 46 teaches the same path.
- **Words as garments; the Name above the head.** Line 10905 (four letters above the head, each waking a power) fits the Chabad teaching that the soul's powers are rooted in the letters of the Name.
- **Not carried.** The fasting programs and sackcloth hours (10842, 10843, 10847, 10857): Chabad teaches return through joy and charity rather than fasts, and the guide never prescribes self-affliction. The detail of sin's damage in the upper palaces (10841). Nearly all of *Vavei HaAmudim*'s *pilpul*: laws of exposed liquids and snake venom (10903), the Rami bar Dikuli debate (10902), sacrifice outside the Temple and vows (10907), the six steps of Solomon's throne (10910), the three books of Rosh Hashanah (10911), burial and impurity of graves (10917), greeting non-Jews (10920), lineage of the nations (10923). The son's rebuke of rabbis who buy their posts (10914) is history, not guidance. Wine and drink in legal examples are law only; the guide never uses drink.

## 6. What the guide should take (ours)

1. **He brings all into being, gives all life, and pours into them always** (line 10861), and if He stopped thinking of us for one moment, all would be as nothing (line 10858).
2. **One Master is one in all things, and all things are in His oneness** (line 10803).
3. **Truth is His seal because He is One; a lie implies a second power** (lines 10916, 10918).
4. **Make Him King above and below, in heart and tongue** (lines 10783, 10785).
5. **Before Whom: standing, and lying down** (line 10833).
6. **The solitude test and nightly forgiveness** (lines 10823, 10832).
7. **Evening return, so all your days are in return** (line 10848), with the prayer "be with my heart" (line 10844).
8. **Heaven, then the Temple: the three-step prayer** (line 10895); prayer depends on the heart (line 10899).
9. **Humility is the soul's height; He gives life even to the one who hurts Him** (lines 10828, 10830).
10. **Sing at the promise; find mercy inside judgment** (lines 10913, 10922).

## 7. Thin places

- *Asara Hillulim* is short and rich; almost every line carries something. It is a summary of the *Chovot HaLevavot* with *Tomer Devorah* and the Zohar woven in (lines 10828, 10858).
- *Vavei HaAmudim* is not the Shelah's own work. It is about nine tenths *pilpul*. It was read in full, and only the *mussar* sections and a few lines were carried. Where the son quotes his father (line 10922), that is marked. - The son says the Shelah finished his book in Jerusalem and studied the Ari's writings there (line 10880). The Ari's teaching on the 216 letters and the "wheel" of Pharaoh's chariot (line 10923) was read and not carried.
- *Siddur Shaar HaShamayim* is announced at line 6110 but is not on Sefaria, so it is not in this study.
