## What this slice adds (one page)

The other Rebbeim teach that there is nothing but Him. The Lubavitcher Rebbe's maamarim keep asking one more question past that point, and his answers move the depth in five directions that DESCENT does not yet have.

**1. Past 'nullified' to 'no second'.** The Rebbe climbs past bitul itself. Speech yields to thought like a chariot to its rider; thought dissolves into the idea; but 'in your heart, only One' means there is no existence besides Him, not even one that is nullified to Him (RB-C05). Even the heart's essential love, the son's cry 'Father!', is still the person's own will. Accepting the yoke is truer and still does not grasp Him. He is grasped only by His own will, which is the commanded deed, and to Him being nullified and being a self are 'exactly equal' (RB-C06). This sharpens D2, D3 and D7.

**2. The Essence is in the lowest, because it has no opposite.** A revelation is one particular thing, so it pushes aside what is not it; the Essence is the essence of each thing, like oil that does not mix and yet soaks into everything (RB-C09). Showing and hiding are one in His 'ability' (RB-C11); the world feels self-standing because its root is the One whose being is from Himself, and the true Being shows in it, 'and in truth also now' (RB-C02). Why a world this low? Not for knowledge (higher worlds would do) but from a desire above reasons, which ran into creation like delight into the bones of the feet (RB-C01). The body is not 'even' precious, it is the main preciousness (RB-C24).

**3. The soul's point that nothing touches.** Chassidus is one point, the yechidah: above all details, not even their source. Modeh Ani is said with unwashed hands because no impurity reaches it; it thanks the Essence with no Name (RB-C07). Only the yechidah feels plainly that the world is being made now from nothing; the work is to recognize, not only understand (RB-C08). From it there was never a war (RB-C22), and in it all Jews are one point (RB-D16).

**4. The bottom keeps you standing.** Real contemplation of the One 'should' make the soul expire; it does not, because the Essence, for whom sea and land are equal, wants souls in bodies. The greater kindness at the Sea was walking on dry land inside it (RB-C15). Faith that is the self cannot deny; an ache for revealed G-dliness even in comfort is higher than self-sacrifice under decree, because it joins the everyday powers to the essence (RB-C16). Free choice, even to choose wrong, is the Essence's own 'not being compelled' (RB-C10).

**5. Plainness and 'only You'.** After all understanding, the height is to do the mitzvah plainly; a Jew's plainness is one with the Essence's simplicity; forgetting the language is a virtue (RB-C18). The clever one takes the King, not even the inner rooms of treasure; wanting Gan Eden for oneself treats a means as the end, the root of idolatry; the Alter Rebbe's 'I want nothing but You alone' is given as a power to every Jew (RB-C12).

The practical floor is constant: every climb ends in a plain physical deed, done for His will, today (avodah list). *Stated: each claim above, with the sources named. Ours: the five-way grouping and the links to DESCENT.*

## RB-C01 · Why is there a world at all, and why such a low one?

*Where:* Melukat, Basi LeGani 5731 (11 Shevat), sections 3-4, lines 1382-1384

1. **The usual reasons for creation are that G-d wants to be known and wants His powers seen.** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The maamar first quotes the Zohar and Etz Chaim reasons and asks whether they really explain a world this low. > בגין דישתמודעון לי' > "so that they will know Him" (Melukat 1382)

2. **But for knowing Him, the higher worlds would have been enough. Down here He is hardly felt at all, and in the physical world not even His existence is felt.** (M4) *How the text gets there:* If the reason were knowledge, the lowest world is the worst place for it, so the reason must lie elsewhere. > הי' מספיק בהתהוות עולמות העליונים > "the creation of the higher worlds would have been enough" (Melukat 1383)

3. **So the real reason is a desire, and a desire is above reasons. You cannot ask 'why' about a longing.** (M14) *How the text gets there:* The rabbis say 'He desired'. The Alter Rebbe's saying settles it: about a desire there is no question. > אויף א תאוה איז קיין קשיא > "about a desire there is no question" (Melukat 1382)

4. **What He desires is a home, and a home is where a person lives with his whole self. Here, in the lowest place, the Essence itself wants to live.** (M6) *How the text gets there:* Because the desire is in the Essence, what it wants is for the Essence: a dwelling, the way a man lives in his house with all of himself. > דענין דירה הוא ע"ד דירת האדם שעצמות האדם דר בהדירה > "a dwelling is like a man's home, where the man's very self lives" (Melukat 1382)

5. **That desire did not stay above. It ran down into the making of this world, the way real delight reaches even the bones of your feet.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The proof of the desire is the low world itself, because the desire flowed into its creation, like delight that spreads through all the body. > ועד שהמרחב דהתענוג נמשך ומתפשט גם בהעצמות שברגל > "until the breadth of the delight spreads even into the bones of the foot" (Melukat 1383)

6. **So even before you do anything, this world already has a link to His Essence. It is ready to become His home from its own side.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* For the home to be made by the lower ones themselves, the lower world must already carry some bond with the Essence. > צריך שתהי' להם איזו שייכות להעצמות (עוד לפני שנעשים דירה) > "they need some relation to the Essence even before they become a home" (Melukat 1383)

**Bottom:** A desire above every reason, living in the Essence itself. **Return:** The lowest world is not an accident or a punishment. It was made from His desire, so your plain work in it is the point (stated).

## RB-C02 · The world seems to stand by itself. Does that mean it hides G-d?

*Where:* Melukat, Basi LeGani 5711 s.4 (line 1252); Basi LeGani 5719 s.3 (line 1290); Zeh HaYom 5741 s.12 (line 77)

1. **Light shows where it comes from. A created thing does the opposite: it hides its source and feels that it exists by itself.** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The maamar contrasts light, which points to its source, with the created 'something', which conceals it. > יש הנברא הנה לא זו בלבד שאינו מגלה בורא > "the created thing not only fails to reveal a Creator" (Melukat 1252)

2. **That very feeling, 'I exist on my own', comes from its root: the Essence, Whose being is from Itself.** (M11) *How the text gets there:* What looks like the block is traced to the highest root: only something rooted in the Essence could feel self-standing. > זהו מפני ששרשו מהעצמות שמציאותו מעצמותו > "that is because its root is from the Essence, Whose being is from Itself" (Melukat 1252)

3. **Only He can make something out of nothing at all. So whatever is solid and 'just there' carries His mark.** (M6) *How the text gets there:* The Igeres HaKodesh is quoted: making 'something from nothing' belongs to the Essence alone. > דרק הוא לבדו בכחו ויכלתו להוות יש מאין ואפס המוחלט > "only He alone, by His power, can bring something from absolute nothing" (Melukat 1290)

4. **In the end, inside the created 'something' its true root will show: the true Something, Him.** (M5) *How the text gets there:* The future revelation is not that the world disappears, but that the real Being is revealed in it. > יתגלה בהיש הנברא שרשו האמיתי, והוא יש האמיתי > "its true root will be revealed in the created thing, the true Being" (Melukat 1290)

5. **And in truth it is so already now.** (M16) *How the text gets there:* The text adds one line, turning a future promise into a present fact. > ובאמת גם עכשיו הוא כן > "and in truth it is so now as well" (Melukat 1290)

6. **So the world is not a dream or a trick of the eyes. It is real, and its realness is His.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* The Rebbe Maharash, in a talk he repeated 'to purify the air of the world', proves from halacha that the world truly exists; the Mitteler Rebbe adds that its true being is the true Being. > דאמיתית מציאותו של היש הגשמי הוא היש האמיתי > "the true existence of the physical thing is the true Being" (Melukat 77)

**Bottom:** The solidity of things is the signature of the One whose being is from Himself. **Return:** Do not try to make the world vanish. Treat its solidity as His, and make it a home by deeds (stated; extends ON-14).

## RB-C03 · Why would the highest show up in the lowest place, and not in the spiritual?

*Where:* Melukat, Basi LeGani 5719, sections 4-5, lines 1291-1292

1. **You would expect the true Being to show in spiritual things, which are 'higher'. Why in physical things?** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The maamar asks it plainly after saying the true Being is found in the created physical thing. > מפני מה ביש הנברא דוקא הוא יש האמיתי ואינו נמצא ברוחניות > "why is the true Being specifically in the created thing and not in the spiritual" (Melukat 1291)

2. **A big torch shows its strength only far away. Its faint light at a distance proves its power.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* First of the Mitteler Rebbe's three parables: up close any candle shines; far away only a great torch still reaches. > במקום הרחוק דוקא, שם דוקא נתגלה בחי' "הגבוה" אשר בהאבוקה > "specifically in the far place the 'height' of the torch is revealed" (Melukat 1291)

3. **A barrel that overflows shows it is full by what spills outside, even though the spill is small.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* Second parable: the stray water, and a sage's small talk, prove a great fullness. > בזה דוקא ניכר אשר החבית מלאה על כל גדותי' > "and by this the barrel is known to be full to the brim" (Melukat 1291)

4. **Teaching passes an idea to a mind that already exists. Only a physical seed brings a new being into the world.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* Third parable: physical giving can make a new essence; spiritual giving cannot. > אשר דוקא ממנה נולד מהות חדש > "that from it alone a new being is born" (Melukat 1291)

5. **So His Essence shows in the physical deed, not only in spiritual work. A pillar holds the roof only when set in hard ground.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* Applied: even in this world, the Essence shows in physical mitzvos, as the Likkutei Torah says pillars need hard soil. > צריכים להיות בעפר קשה דוקא ולא בעפר תיחוח > "they must be in hard earth, not in loose soil" (Melukat 1291)

6. **Even the heart's mitzvos must reach the flesh: fear that tightens the brain, study that adds wrinkles, delight that 'fattens the bones'.** (M7) *How the text gets there:* The Rebbe Rayatz is cited: the commands of the heart are meant to be felt in the physical body. > הרי התענוג צריך להוסיף שמנונית > "the delight must add fat" (Melukat 1292)

**Bottom:** The highest is known only where it is farthest: in the hard, the physical, the done. **Return:** Bring the insight into the body and into one physical deed today (stated).

## RB-C04 · 'I, Havaya, have not changed.' But everything around us is change.

*Where:* Melukat, Basi LeGani 5719, section 8, line 1295

1. **The light comes down world by world and gets smaller. Isn't that change in Him?** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The Rebbe Rashab's question, quoted in full. > והרי זה שינוי בהאור > "and this is a change in the light" (Melukat 1295)

2. **No: the differences come only from what each world can hold. The light itself is whole below as above.** (M4) *How the text gets there:* The first answer moves the change from the light to the receivers. > אבל האור עצמו נמצא בשלימותו ובמילואו למטה כמו למעלה > "but the light itself is found complete and full below as above" (Melukat 1295)

3. **And the receivers' weakness is from a covering, the name Elokim, which is itself holy. An essence does not hide an essence.** (M11) *How the text gets there:* The second answer: even the hiding is His, so from the light's side it is no hiding at all. > שהרי אין עצם מסתיר על עצם כידוע > "for, as known, essence does not conceal essence" (Melukat 1295)

4. **But every creature is different. Isn't that change? No: the word that makes each thing never leaves its source, like sunlight inside the sun.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The third answer uses the Tanya's sun parable: inside its source the ray is 'nothing'. > הרי הם בטלים בתכלית כביטול זיו השמש בהשמש > "they are utterly nullified like the sun's glow within the sun" (Melukat 1295)

5. **Thinking this through makes a person want all his powers and wants to be only for G-d.** (M16) *How the text gets there:* The contemplation is given its fruit: a longing, not just a thought. > לזאת ירצה אשר כל כחותיו ורצונותיו יהיו רק באלקות > "so he will want all his powers and desires to be only in G-dliness" (Melukat 1295)

6. **This you can do now. In the dark of exile the power of self-giving is even stronger than in Temple times.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* The first longing is in a person's own hands now; the second, being fed from the Essence itself, waits for the future. > שהוא מצד כח המס"נ שישנו עכשיו יותר מכמו בזמן הבית > "from the power of self-giving that exists now more than in Temple times" (Melukat 1295)

**Bottom:** Nothing in Him ever changes; the hiding itself is holy. **Return:** Offer your 'blood and fat', your life and your delight, to Him today; the rest He will reveal (stated).

## RB-C05 · 'In your heart there is only One.' What does 'only One' add to total closeness?

*Where:* Melukat, Basi LeGani 5730, sections 8-9, lines 1354-1358

1. **The soul above was in total closeness to G-d. Why add that it had 'no foreign will'?** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The maamar asks what the extra phrase teaches. > מה מוסיף ומחדש שאין לה רצון זר > "what does it add that it had no foreign will" (Melukat 1354)

2. **Speech serves thought like a chariot serves a rider: it obeys, but it is still a separate thing.** (M8) *How the text gets there:* The Mitteler Rebbe's ladder starts from speech and thought in a person. > והוא כמו ביטול המרכבה להרוכב > "it is like a chariot's yielding to its rider" (Melukat 1354)

3. **When you think the idea itself, 'thought within thought', the words are not even felt. They are swallowed in the idea.** (M8) *How the text gets there:* One rung up the inner ladder: letters of thought dissolved in the mind. > במחשבה זו אין האותיות נרגשים, כי הם כלולים ובטלים בהשכל > "in this thought the letters are not felt; they are included and nullified in the idea" (Melukat 1354)

4. **'Only One' means more than that. There is no existence besides Him, not even one that is nullified to Him.** (M5) *How the text gets there:* The brothers' words to Jacob are read as the highest bitul: not a yielding thing, but no second thing at all. > שאין שום מציאות מבלעדו, גם לא מציאות שבטל אליו > "there is no existence without Him, not even an existence that is nullified to Him" (Melukat 1354)

5. **This shines only at the innermost point of the heart.** (M7) *How the text gets there:* 'In your heart' means inside the heart, where simple oneness shines. > שבנקודת הלב מאיר אחדות הפשוטה > "in the point of the heart simple oneness shines" (Melukat 1354)

6. **The brothers, lower souls, could say it equally, because every soul's root is in the Essence, above even the will to create, where all are equal.** (M12) *How the text gets there:* How lower souls share the highest state: at the root, rank does not exist. > כיון שלגבי עצמות אוא"ס (שרש הנשמות) כל הענינים הם בשוה > "since relative to the Essence, the root of souls, all things are equal" (Melukat 1357)

7. **So a soul in a body full of foreign wants has the power to overcome them.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* The height of the root is given as strength for the war below. > יש לה הכח להתגבר עליהם > "it has the power to overcome them" (Melukat 1358)

**Bottom:** Not 'everything bows to Him' but 'there is no second': not even a bowing one. **Return:** That root is in you now, so the distraction in the body has no final hold (stated).

## RB-C06 · What, in a person, can actually reach G-d Himself?

*Where:* Melukat, Tachlis Chochmah 5740 (18 Kislev), sections 2-8, lines 843-853

1. **Learning Torah builds your understanding, your 'self'. Return and good deeds are about yielding. Yet the sages call return and deeds the heart of Torah.** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The maamar sets up the opposition: Torah stresses the self, teshuvah and mitzvos stress bitul. > ובתשובה ומעשים טובים מודגש הביטול דהאדם > "in return and good deeds the person's self-nullification is stressed" (Melukat 843)

2. **Thought cannot grasp Him. The heart's deep wish can, like a son who cries 'Abba!' and moves his father's whole being.** (M9) *How the text gets there:* The Zohar's 'no thought grasps You, but You are grasped by the will of the heart', with the parable of the son. > דכשהבן צועק אבא ניזוז האב בכל מהותו > "when the son cries 'Father!' the father is stirred in his whole being" (Melukat 844)

3. **But even that essential love is still your will. 4. **Even someone low, whose animal soul is fighting him, can have this: by thinking about the yechidah and deciding to live by it.** (M16) *How the text gets there:* Why 'for the many were with me' is the reason for peace. > ע"י המחשבה בענין היחידה > "through thinking about the yechidah" (Melukat 680)

5. **A scholar told the Tzemach Tzedek, 'they step on me in the study hall'. He answered: 'You have spread yourself over the whole study hall, so wherever anyone steps, it is on you.'** (M9) *How the text gets there:* A story of ego as the cause of feeling attacked. > דו ביסט זיך מתפשט איבער דעם גאנצן בית מדרש > "you spread yourself over the whole study hall" (Melukat 688)

6. **Leave even the finest thoughts of self, even the finest of the fine.** (M11) *How the text gets there:* 'Let the man of strength forsake his thoughts', plural. > שצריך לעזוב את מחשבה שבדקות, ואפילו שבדקות דדקות > "he must abandon even subtle thought, even the most subtle" (Melukat 688)

7. **The sparks in the world want to be raised. From the yechidah you see the world helping you, not blocking you.** (M11) *How the text gets there:* 'The many were with me': even Avshalom's men prayed for David. > ויתירה מזה, שהנצוצות רוצים ומשתוקקים להתברר > "moreover, the sparks want and long to be refined" (Melukat 690)

**Bottom:** The point with no opposite, where the war never began. **Return:** In a fight with yourself, stop arguing; think of the yechidah and decide; and shrink the self that gets stepped on (stated).

## RB-C23 · When the Essence shows, a command becomes unnecessary.

*Where:* Melukat, Padah BeShalom 5726 (19 Kislev), sections 3-12, lines 921-936

1. **Prayer asks and may not be answered. A blessing commands, and so it happens, but it only gives what is already in the source.** (M3) *How the text gets there:* Prayer and blessing compared, each with its virtue. > ובברכה שהיא בדרך ציווי – ודאי שתומשך ההשפעה > "and with a blessing, which is a command, the flow certainly comes" (Melukat 921)

2. **Rabbi Pinchas commanded a river to split. Rabbi Shimon only taught Torah, gave no order at all, and the rain came.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* Two miracle stories set side by side. > דהגם שבהדרוש שאמר לא הי' ציווי כלל (גם לא ברמז) > "though in the teaching he gave there was no command at all (not even a hint)" (Melukat 924)

3. **A command is needed only when the one commanded is a separate thing.** (M2) *How the text gets there:* Why the tzaddikim had to command: the world felt separate. > כי ענין הציווי שייך דוקא כשזה שמצווים לו הוא מציאות נבדל מהמצוה > "a command applies only when the one commanded is separate from the commander" (Melukat 936)

4. **When the Essence is revealed, Whose being is the being of all, the rain falls by itself.** (M5) *How the text gets there:* Rashbi's Torah revealed the Essence, so there was no 'other' to order. > ולכן ע"י אמירת הדרוש ירדו הגשמים מעצמם, בלי ציווי > "so by saying the teaching the rains fell by themselves, without a command" (Melukat 936)

5. **In prayer you feel that you are not G-dliness.** (M7) *How the text gets there:* Why 'Your chassidim will bless' and not pray. > נרגש שהאדם המתפלל אינו אלקות > "it is felt that the praying person is not G-dliness" (Melukat 936)

6. **Any part of you that is not G-dliness blocks the revealing. So a chassid's natural powers must become G-dly powers.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* The work required for the deepest revelation. > כשישנה מציאות (בהאדם) שאינה אלקות, הוא סתירה להגילוי > "when there is an existence (in the person) that is not G-dliness, it contradicts the revelation" (Melukat 935)

7. **A chassid serves for G-d's need, not even for his own closeness to G-d.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* 'Who is a chassid? One who is kind with his Maker.' > שעבודתו היא לא בשביל עצמו בכדי שיהי' דבוק באלקות אלא לצורך גבוה > "his service is not for himself, to be close to G-d, but for G-d's need" (Melukat 934)

**Bottom:** The truth of His being is the being of all, so there is no 'other' to command. **Return:** Serve for His sake, not for your spiritual gain, and let your natural powers be His (stated).

## RB-C24 · Where does the deepest cry come out, and what does G-d hold most precious?

*Where:* Melukat, Zeh HaYom 5742 (Erev Rosh Hashanah), s.9 (line 94); Atem Nitzavim 5739 s.3-4 (lines 3056-3058)

1. **The inner cry from the depth of the heart comes out only through the 'stone' in a person: plain acceptance, and the physical deed.** (M7) *How the text gets there:* Not in the mind, not in feelings: in the inanimate part of man. > אלא דוקא בהדומם שבו, העבודה דקבלת עול > "but specifically in the inanimate in him, the service of accepting the yoke" (Melukat 94)

2. **So the shofar must be an animal's horn cut off, a lifeless thing.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* The law points to the soul's way. > שקרן השופר צ"ל תלוש > "that the shofar horn must be detached" (Melukat 94)

3. **The Alter Rebbe: 'You have no idea how precious to G-d the body of a Jew is.'** (M5) *How the text gets there:* A saying passed down by the Rebbe Rayatz for Rosh Hashanah. > מען האט גאר קיין השגה ניט, ווי עס איז טייער בא השי"ת דער גוף פון א אידן > "one has no grasp at all how precious to G-d the body of a Jew is" (Melukat 94)

4. **It does not mean 'even the body'. The main preciousness is in the body. It is beyond grasp because it comes from the Essence.** (M4) *How the text gets there:* The Rebbe reads the saying at its strongest. > אלא שעיקר היוקר הוא בהגוף > "rather, the main preciousness is in the body" (Melukat 94)

5. **The covenant was cut by passing between the pieces of one animal: two who become one flesh. His union with Israel includes the physical body.** (M12) *How the text gets there:* Likkutei Torah on 'to pass you into the covenant'. > כי היחוד דהקב"ה עם בני ישראל הוא גם מצד הגוף הגשמי > "for G-d's union with Israel is also from the side of the physical body" (Melukat 3056)

6. **'G-d will return with your captivity': wherever a Jew is, even at the edge of the heavens, His very Essence is there with him.** (M16) *How the text gets there:* 'Veshav', He returns, not 'He brings back'. > גם אם יהי' נדחך בקצה השמים, נמצא שם עצמותו ומהותו ית' > "even if your outcast is at the edge of the heavens, His Essence is there" (Melukat 3058)

**Bottom:** The body, chosen by the Essence, is where the Essence holds most. **Return:** Do the plain physical deed as the place of your deepest cry; do not despise the body (stated).

## RB-C25 · A home in the lowest: does the lowest have to stop being low?

*Where:* Melukat, VaHashem Amar HaMechaseh 5737, sections 3-5, lines 455-458

1. **A road joins two places far apart, the king's city and the farthest corner.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The Mitteler Rebbe and Rashab on 'the way of G-d'. > שהוא ממוצע שמחבר שני מקומות שמצד עצמם הם רחוקים זמ"ז בתכלית > "a middle that joins two places utterly far from each other" (Melukat 455)

2. **A home in the lowest is like a man living in his friend's house with all of himself, as in his own.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The Rashab's parable of the dwelling. > כמשל אדם הדר בבית חבירו, שכל עצמותו כמו שהוא דר בביתו > "like a man living in his friend's house, his whole self as he lives in his own" (Melukat 456)

3. **The house stays the friend's house. The lowest stays low, and the Essence still lives in it.** (M12) *How the text gets there:* The dwelling does not erase the lower. > שגם לאחרי שהוא דר שם הבית הוא בית חבירו > "even after he lives there, the house is his friend's house" (Melukat 456)

4. **A road must go back too. If you cannot return on it, it is not a road, even from where you started.** (M4) *How the text gets there:* Drawing down is not real unless the lower is lifted to receive. > באם א"א לילך בו בחזרה אזי אינו נקרא דרך > "if one cannot travel back on it, it is not called a road" (Melukat 458)

5. **Real drawing-down soaks into every part of the place until it becomes one with it.** (M5) *How the text gets there:* Only when the lower is a vessel. > עד שמתעצמת עמו > "until it becomes one in essence with it" (Melukat 458)

6. **So give charity with judgment: judge yourself out of luxuries, keep what you truly need, and give the rest.** (M15) *How the text gets there:* 'To do charity and judgment'. > שישפוט את עצמו מהמותרות > "that he judge himself out of luxuries" (Melukat 457)

**Bottom:** The Essence at home in a house that stays the lower's house. **Return:** Judge your luxuries today and give; let the road run both ways (stated).

## RB-C26 · What does a commanded deed testify to?

*Where:* Melukat, Ki Yishalcha 5738 (11 Nissan), sections 1, 4, 6, 9, lines 1909-1922

1. **After Sinai the deed itself is the main thing. Intend the secrets of matzah without eating, and you draw nothing; eat without intention, and you draw the light.** (M7) *How the text gets there:* The difference between the forefathers' spiritual service and commanded deeds. > כשיקיים המצוה בפועל, גם בלי כוונה, ימשיך האור > "when he does the mitzvah in act, even without intention, he draws the light" (Melukat 1909)

2. **The surrounding light cannot be grasped, only known by negation: knowing that it is beyond knowing.** (M3) *How the text gets there:* Three levels and three kinds of mitzvos. > ואור הסובב, אין שייך בו השגת החיוב ורק ידיעת השלילה > "the surrounding light cannot be positively grasped, only known by negation" (Melukat 1914)

3. **But to say G-d 'cannot be grasped' is like saying a deep idea cannot be touched with the hands. Anyone would laugh.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The Tanya's image, pushed further. > הוא כאומר על איזו חכמה רמה ועמוקה שאי אפשר למששה בידים > "is like saying of a lofty deep wisdom that it cannot be felt with the hands" (Melukat 1914)

4. **So even 'beyond the mind' does not apply to His Essence. Mitzvos reach Him only because they are His will.** (M5) *How the text gets there:* Even the chukim, which silence the mind, do not reach the Essence; only the will does. > הוא מפני שהמצוות הם רצון וציווי העצמות > "is because the mitzvos are the will and command of the Essence" (Melukat 1914)

5. **'You are My witnesses': a Jew's very existence testifies to the Essence.** (M9) *How the text gets there:* The soul's root is above even the root of the mitzvos. > עצם מציאותם דישראל מעידה על העצמות > "the very existence of Israel testifies to the Essence" (Melukat 1918)

6. **The wise son's Torah is new every day, so he always stands before Sinai again, like a child just born.** (M16) *How the text gets there:* Why the wise son asks 'what are the testimonies'. > התומ"צ של הבן חכם הם בכל יום חדשים ממש > "the wise son's Torah and mitzvos are literally new every day" (Melukat 1922)

**Bottom:** The Essence, whom not even 'unknowable' describes, testified by a deed and by your being. **Return:** Do the deed even when the meaning is gone; start again each day as one newly born (stated).

## Depths beyond the descent

*Each depth is stated in the sources quoted; the 'hisbonenus' steps are ours, built only from the text's own sequence. 'Extends' names the DESCENT depth it deepens, sharpens or corrects.*

## RB-D01 · Not even a yielding thing

**Teaching.** 'There is nothing but Him' at its height is not 'everything bows to Him'. It is: there is no second existence at all, not even one that is nullified to Him. A bowing thing is still a thing.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice how your words serve your thought: they obey, but they are still something apart. 2. Go inside to thinking the idea itself: the words disappear into it. 3. Now go further: not 'I disappear into Him' but 'there is no I beside Him at all, not even a vanished one'. 4. Do not try to make this happen; notice it is said of the innermost point of the heart, and the brothers of lower rank could say it equally, because at the root all are equal.

**From inside.** Speech serving thought like a chariot its rider; letters swallowed in the idea; 'in your heart, only One'. **Where it lands.** In the heart's inner point; and as strength in a body 'full of foreign wants' to overcome them. **Extends.** Sharpens D3 and D2 (stated).

> שאין שום מציאות מבלעדו, גם לא מציאות שבטל אליו
> "no existence without Him, not even one nullified to Him" (Melukat 1354)

> יש לה הכח להתגבר עליהם
> "it has the power to overcome them" (Melukat 1358)

## RB-D02 · His ability: showing and hiding are one

**Teaching.** Havaya (showing) and Elokim (hiding) are one, not first in the sefiros or even in His will, but in His ability: the ability to shine and the ability not to shine are one. That ability is nothing but that He is able. So darkness itself can be turned to light.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Think: He shows Himself, and He hides Himself. 2. Ask: in what are these one? Not in a plan: in Him being able to do both. 3. Look at yourself: even your evil inclination, rooted in hiding, can love Him. 4. Rest in 'none besides Him': the ability is not a thing besides Him.

**From inside.** Two inclinations both loving; feet that lift the head in the dance. **Where it lands.** In turning one dark thing in yourself rather than only adding light. **Extends.** Extends D4 (neither something nor nothing) with 'ability' (stated).

> דזה שביכלתו להאיר (השרש דהוי') ושביכלתו שלא להאיר (השרש דאלקים) הוא חד ממש
> "that He can shine and can not-shine are truly one" (Melukat 370)

> דענין היכולת אינו דבר כלל זולת העצמות והוא רק שהעצמות הוא יכול
> "ability is nothing besides the Essence; only that the Essence is able" (Melukat 371)

## RB-D03 · Held only by His own will

**Teaching.** Below D7: even the heart's essential love, the son's cry, is still the person's will, an existence. Accepting the yoke is truer, and still does not grasp Him. He is grasped only by His own will: the commanded deed. Bitul matters only so the reaching is seen.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Feel the love that cries 'Father!' and moves Him wholly. 2. Notice: this wanting is still mine. 3. Accept the yoke, doing because He said. Notice even this does not hold Him. 4. Do the mitzvah as His will, and let even the feeling 'I am yielding' go.

**From inside.** The son's cry; the servant who does not even feel he is yielding. **Where it lands.** In the plain deed, done for His will alone. **Extends.** Corrects and extends D7 (simple will and faith) and D12 (stated).

> גם רצון זה הוא הרצון של האדם, מציאות
> "this desire too is the person's own, an existence" (Melukat 846)

> ובכדי לתפוס את העצמות הוא דוקא ע"י שהעצמות רוצה בזה
> "to grasp the Essence is only through the Essence wanting it" (Melukat 846)

> אף שלגבי העצמות ביטול ומציאות הם בשוה ממש
> "to the Essence, nullity and existence are exactly equal" (Melukat 846)

## RB-D04 · Recognize, don't only understand

**Teaching.** The fact that the world is being made from nothing each moment is news to most of the soul, which lives in the worlds. Only the yechidah feels it plainly. The work is to recognize, not only understand, and recognition comes from the yechidah.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Wake and say Modeh Ani: you were just made new. 2. Notice that part of you takes the world for granted; that is natural to the levels that live in it. 3. Let the deepest point speak: plainly, all this is now from nothing. 4. Then nothing else may be mixed into your mitzvah.

**From inside.** Waking as new creation; Reb Yekusiel refusing 'peasant years'. **Where it lands.** At waking, before washing; in doing a mitzvah for no other purpose. **Extends.** Extends D5 and D6 (stated).

> נרגש אצלה בפשיטות שכל העולמות הם אין ואפס המוחלט
> "it feels plainly that all worlds are absolute nothing" (Inyanah 80)

> שיכיר (ולא רק שיבין)
> "that he recognize, not only understand" (Inyanah 93)

## RB-D05 · Faith that is the self, and the ache of exile

**Teaching.** Faith from the soul's 'seeing' is strong but caused; faith from the essence is the person, so he cannot deny. Higher still than self-sacrifice under decree: to be broken that G-d is not revealed, even in comfort, because it touches your being. This joins the everyday powers to the essence.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Ask: is my faith because I have seen, or because it is me? 2. Picture someone who gave his life for years, and in freedom lost the fire. 3. Feel that any corner without G-d limits your own light. 4. Let the ache be daily and quiet: 'may our eyes see'.

**From inside.** Olive oil crushed for the light; the 49th gate missing is sickness. **Where it lands.** In the three daily prayers for return to Zion; in ordinary hours that stay steady 'like the lamp always'. **Extends.** New; deepens D8 (the bond that cannot be let go) (stated).

> שהאמונה באלקות היא העצם שלו, ולכן אי אפשר כלל שיכפור
> "faith in G-d is his essence, so he cannot deny" (Melukat 1728)

> אין ניכר בהם (כ"כ) המס"נ שהי' להם מקודם
> "their former self-sacrifice is not seen in them" (Melukat 1735)

> ועד שזה (גילוי אלקות) נוגע לעצם מציאותו
> "until revealed G-dliness touches his very existence" (Melukat 1734)

## RB-D06 · Dry land inside the sea

**Teaching.** The bottom does not drown you. Real contemplation of the One 'should' make the soul expire; it does not, because the Essence, for whom sea and land are equal, wants souls in bodies. Walking on dry ground inside the sea is a greater kindness than the splitting.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. See creatures of the sea covered by their source; see yourself on land, feeling separate. 2. Let the oneness come like the sea. 3. Notice you are not swept away: you are held to stay. 4. Board the ship: a mitzvah that crosses without drowning.

**From inside.** Fish covered by water; a maidservant pointing 'this'; Moses standing whole. **Where it lands.** In the body that stays; in the mitzvah as a ship. **Extends.** Extends D9 (the bottom sends you back) (stated).

> שבנ"י לא נתבטלו מפני עוצם גילוי האור דעלמין סתימין
> "Israel were not undone by the intensity of the light" (Melukat 2472)

> גוף גשמי דוקא, ששרשו מיש האמיתי
> "a physical body, rooted in the true Being" (Melukat 2470)

## RB-D07 · The world's self-standing is His mark

**Teaching.** The world feels it stands by itself because its root is in the Essence whose being is from itself; in the end its true root, the true Being, shows in it, 'and in truth also now'.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Look at a solid thing: it seems to need nothing. 2. Ask where 'needing nothing' could come from: only from the One whose being is from Himself. 3. Let the thing be real, and let its realness be His.

**From inside.** The Rashab: 'Don't look at the lump; the Infinite Essence brought that lump into being.' **Where it lands.** In treating physical things as real and His, not illusions. **Extends.** Extends D10 and ON-14 (stated).

> ובאמת גם עכשיו הוא כן
> "and in truth it is so now as well" (Melukat 1290)

> קוק ניט וואס ער איז א גולם, דעם גולם האט מהוה גיווען עצמות א"ס ב"ה
> "don't look at it as a lump; the Essence of the Infinite created that lump" (Melukat 3075)

## RB-D08 · The prayer of the rich

**Teaching.** From the Essence, the truth of His being is the truth of all beings, so nothing truly lacks. Then knowing G-d is your whole existence, and you pray for others from fullness, that they too feel they lack nothing.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice understanding leaves room for the opposite. 2. Let knowing become felt, in the essence of the reasoning. 3. Go to where nothing is outside Him: there, nothing lacks. 4. Pray for another's lack from there.

**From inside.** A full barrel; a rich man asking for a ruined province to be rebuilt. **Where it lands.** In prayer for others; then 'man of G-d': refine your traits all day. **Extends.** New; extends D10 (stated).

> הכרת האדם באלקות היא באופן שזה (הכרת אלקות) היא כל מציאותו
> "recognition of G-d is his whole existence" (Melukat 2604)

> שהאמת היא שאינם חסרים מאומה
> "the truth is that they lack nothing" (Melukat 2605)

## RB-D09 · Your face in His water

**Teaching.** The image that appears in the Infinite by our ascent is not a separate thing in it; it is the water itself. A person's root is like a name: only the readiness to turn when called.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Look into still water and see your face. 2. Ask: is the face a thing in the water? No; it is the water. 3. Hear your name called; feel yourself turn with your whole being.

**From inside.** Water reflecting a face; a fainted man waking at his name. **Where it lands.** In refining one trait so the reflection is clear. **Extends.** Extends D11 (called into being) (stated).

> אינם דבר נבדל מאוא"ס אלא הוא אוא"ס עצמו
> "they are not separate from the Infinite; they are the Infinite itself" (Melukat 2302)

> גם כשהאדם מתעלף, ע"י שקוראים אותו בשמו הוא מתעורר מההתעלפות
> "a fainted person wakes when called by name" (Melukat 704)

## RB-D10 · The body is the main preciousness

**Teaching.** The deepest cry comes out only through the 'stone' in a person: plain acceptance and the physical deed. 'You have no idea how precious to G-d is the body of a Jew': not 'even' the body, but mainly.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Find the cry in the heart. 2. Let it out through the plain part: a deed, an acceptance. 3. Know that the body is chosen.

**From inside.** A cut horn sounding; a covenant of one flesh. **Where it lands.** In the body, the deed, the 'four cubits'. **Extends.** Extends D12 (stated).

> אלא שעיקר היוקר הוא בהגוף
> "the main preciousness is in the body" (Melukat 94)

> כי היחוד דהקב"ה עם בני ישראל הוא גם מצד הגוף הגשמי
> "G-d's union with Israel is also from the physical body" (Melukat 3056)

## RB-D11 · Forgetting the language

**Teaching.** Plainness of a Jew is one with the simplicity of the Essence. After all understanding, the height is to do the mitzvah plainly. Even fine intention can dim simple acceptance; forgetting the language is a virtue.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Say 'Baruch Hashem' as a simple Jew means it. 2. Do one mitzvah without a story. 3. Let the words fall away and leave a plain voice.

**From inside.** The prince who forgot his language and cried out. **Where it lands.** In the plain deed and the plain word. **Extends.** Extends D13 (stated).

> והפשטות דישראל הוא חד עם פשיטות העצמות
> "Israel's plainness is one with the Essence's simplicity" (Melukat 117)

> דשכחה זו היא למעליותא
> "this forgetting is a virtue" (Melukat 118)

## RB-D12 · Not even His gifts

**Teaching.** Wanting revelations, even 'Your Garden of Eden', for yourself treats a means as the end, and that is the root of idolatry. The clever one wants only the King. Every Jew can wake at least a trace of 'only You'.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Walk through the rooms of treasure in your mind. 2. Do not stop, even at the inner room. 3. Say: I want nothing but You.

**From inside.** A palace of treasure rooms; the king's chamber. **Where it lands.** In making all other wants 'without heart'. **Extends.** New: corrects any depth that ends in spiritual experience (stated).

> שעיקר הענין דעבודת כו"מ הוא שחושבים דבר שהוא רק אמצעי לעיקר
> "idolatry is thinking a mere means is the main thing" (Melukat 1903)

> איך וויל מער ניט אז דיך אליין
> "I want nothing more than You alone" (Melukat 1901)

## RB-D13 · New each day, before Sinai

**Teaching.** Each day's Torah is incomparably higher, so yesterday's is as nothing; the wise son is always 'before Sinai', like a newborn. Even a slave to Pharaoh is taken out by 'G-d Himself in His glory', a leap from end to end.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice yesterday's understanding. 2. Let it be as nothing next to today's. 3. Stand as a newborn; ask 'what are the testimonies?'

**From inside.** A child at the moment of birth. **Where it lands.** In learning and in the day's first deed. **Extends.** Extends D14 (no bottom: begin again) (stated).

> התומ"צ של הבן חכם הם בכל יום חדשים ממש
> "the wise son's Torah is new each day" (Melukat 1922)

> כתינוק ברגע שנולד
> "like a baby at the moment it is born" (Melukat 1923)

## RB-D14 · Peace where there was never war

**Teaching.** The yechidah has no opposite, so from it there was never room for foreign wants. Even someone low can draw this by thinking of it and deciding by it. Opponents are then seen as helpers.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Stop arguing with the distraction. 2. Think of the point in you that is never apart. 3. Decide to act from it. 4. See the world helping.

**From inside.** The Tzemach Tzedek: 'you spread yourself over the whole study hall'. **Where it lands.** In moments of inner conflict and of feeling stepped on. **Extends.** New; joins D8 (stated).

> שמצד היחידה אין מקום מלכתחילה לרצונות זרים
> "from the yechidah no room from the start for foreign desires" (Melukat 679)

> ע"י המחשבה בענין היחידה
> "through thinking about the yechidah" (Melukat 680)

## RB-D15 · Free choice is His ability

**Teaching.** The possibility of going against His will goes back to the first contraction, which shows He is not compelled to reveal Himself. A person's freedom, even to choose wrong, is the Essence's ability; serving by your own power reveals it.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice you could choose otherwise. 2. Trace that freedom up: to One compelled by nothing. 3. Use it: go against your habit once.

**From inside.** The 101st review; hides softened by treading. **Where it lands.** In the act against habit. **Extends.** New; corrects a reading of 'the turn' as mere surrender (stated).

> שהעצמות אינו מוכרח ח"ו בענין הגילוי וביכלתו להיות (גם) בהעלם
> "the Essence is not compelled to reveal and can be hidden" (Melukat 1391)

> שכח הבחירה דהאדם (גם זה שיכול לבחור ח"ו בהיפך הרצון) הוא מצד יכולת העצמות
> "the power of choice comes from the Essence's ability" (Melukat 1392)

## RB-D16 · One point in all Jews

**Teaching.** What is equal in every Jew, smallest to greatest, is the yechidah; when Jews gather as one, even the unlearned is 'pure', because impurity touches only the revealed powers. The white thread, the soul's simplicity, surrounds the whole person and runs through all his powers.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Think of the plainest Jew you know. 2. Find in him the same point as in the greatest. 3. Let it surround you and run through you.

**From inside.** A white thread around the body; everyone in one sukkah. **Where it lands.** In joining with others; in prayer with the community. **Extends.** New (stated).

> ומחמת ה"יחידה" הרי כל ישראל הם כמו נקודה אחת ויחידה
> "because of the yechidah all Israel are like one single point" (LS 29930)

> עצם הנפש (שלמעלה גם מבחי' יחידה)
> "the essence of the soul (above even the yechidah)" (LS 20548)

## Avodah: what to do with it

- **Wake with Modeh Ani, before washing, and stretch its 'dot' over the whole day.** > צריכים לפושטה (פ¨נ¨נדערשפרייטן) על כל היום כולו "one must stretch it over the whole day" (Inyanah 54)
- **Before prayer, set worries aside as you do every night before sleep.** > דכשהולכים לישון הם מסירים את הטרדות והדאגות "when they go to sleep they remove the worries" (Melukat 1366)
- **When a foreign thought falls in during prayer, do not argue with it: act as one who neither knows nor hears, by strength of will. Reflect on its purpose at another time.** > העצה היא שיעשה עצמו כלא יודע ולא שומע ההרהורים "the advice is to act as one who does not know or hear the thoughts" (Melukat 1370)
- **If someone disturbs your prayer, see it as the Shechinah come down to strengthen you, and pray deeper.** > וזה שירדה השכינה למטה כ"כ הוא בכדי שאחזק עצמי בעבודת התפלה "the Shechinah came down so far so that I strengthen myself in prayer" (Melukat 1368)
- **Before prayer, bitterness that plows the heart: the soul's purpose against how you act.** > נעשה החרישה בקרקע לבבו "the plowing of the soil of his heart is done" (Melukat 3064)
- **Thank Him equally in every measure He gives you.** > בכל מדה ומדה שהוא מודד לך הוי מודה לו "in every measure He measures you, thank Him" (Melukat 1361)
- **In every matter of service, first do it, then understand it.** > צריך לכל לראש לקיימו בפועל (נעשה), ולאחרי זה להבין "first fulfil it in deed, and afterwards understand" (Melukat 2274)
- **Remember your readiness for self-giving in Shema morning and night.** > שקיום התומ"צ תלוי בזכירת ענין המס"נ "keeping mitzvos depends on remembering self-sacrifice" (Melukat 2564)
- **Grab mitzvos; do not wait for calculations or the approval of mockers.** > ולא להתחשב בשום חשבונות ומניעות ועיכובים "not reckoning with calculations, obstacles and hindrances" (LS 12417)
- **Charity with judgment: judge yourself out of luxuries and give the rest.** > שישפוט את עצמו מהמותרות "that he judge himself out of luxuries" (Melukat 457)
- **When obstacles come, decide to fulfil your mission regardless; you will see they have no substance.** > אזי יראה בגילוי בעיני בשר שכל המניעות "he will see with fleshly eyes that all the obstacles" (Melukat 264)
- **While learning, let the Torah be your whole existence.** > בעת הלימוד יהי' זה (לימוד התורה) כל מציאותו "during learning, it should be his whole existence" (Melukat 2228)
- **Make your desires 'without heart'; your main desire: to complete His home.** > שכל הרצונות שלו יהיו בלא לב ולב "that all his desires be without heart" (Melukat 1905)
- **Bring each teaching to a deed, especially with something physical outside you.** > שכל ענין שלומדים בחסידות צריך להביאו בפועל "everything learned in Chassidus must be brought to deed" (Inyanah 107)
- **Do not look at your body or the world as a lump: the Essence made it.** > קוק ניט וואס ער איז א גולם "don't look at it as a lump" (Melukat 3075)
- **Say a hundred blessings a day; through them the hidden 'mah' of the soul, its awe, comes out.** > חייב אדם לברך מאה ברכות בכל יום "a person must say a hundred blessings each day" (Melukat 2899)

## For the guide (ours)

1. The Rebbe's deepening almost always has the same shape: a 'but in truth' that removes the last trace of the self from a good thing (the heart's love, the yoke, even 'nothing but Him'), and then a turn down to a physical deed. Use that shape: one rung up, then the deed (RB-C05, C06, C12, C19).
2. Do not end a 'Go deeper' run in an experience. The Rebbe calls wanting revelations for oneself the seed of idolatry (RB-C12, D12). End in a deed, or in 'only You'.
3. For a person who feels unreal or broken, start from the yechidah that no impurity touches (RB-C07) and from 'the body is the main preciousness' (RB-C24), never from 'the world is nothing'.
4. The brokenness of exile (RB-C16, D05) is for people who already feel the lack; do not induce it.
5. Inner conflict: lead to RB-C22/D14 (peace from the point with no opposite), and the practical rule of RB-avodah 3 (do not argue with the stray thought during prayer).
