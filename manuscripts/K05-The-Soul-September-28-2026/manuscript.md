## What this cluster shows

These twelve subjects are the soul. Here the one idea turns inward: the Essence that is present in everything is present in a person in a special way, as 'a part of God above, literally', breathed from His inwardness (Tanya 2), and at its innermost point, the yechidah, 'always united with the Essence' and giving 'no room to anything besides Him' (Inyanah 11:5, 14:1). The rest of the anthropology follows from where that point stands. The divine soul's nature is bitul (Tanya 19). The animal soul is the 'something' it was lowered into, in order to teach it that Havayah is Elokim (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6), and turning it is where the Essence shows most (Inyanah 19:4). The yetzer is His agent in the concealment (Tanya 9:8; Keter Shem Tov 1:82). The descent is for the dwelling below (Tanya 31:17; Kuntres Umaayan 15). The five levels divide between feeling the worlds as real and feeling them as nothing (Inyanah 14:5-15:1); that this is the lower and higher unity inside one soul is our reading. Free choice is the Essence's own freedom, placed only in man (Likkutei Sichos 31). The tie is stated for nine of the twelve. It is partly stated for the five levels and for tzaddik-beinoni-rasha, and largely our reading for reincarnation, which the Rebbeim receive from the Ari rather than derive from the unity.

## S052 · The soul, a part of God above · חלק אלוה ממעל

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** חלק אלוה ממעל · חלק אלוק · נשמה · נשמות ישראל

> The soul, a part of God above, is the unity of God as His own inwardness breathed into a person: a 'part' of the One who has no parts, so that the whole is present in it.

## Derivation

1. A 'part' of God is a paradox for a God who is yachid and has no parts. Tanya answers with the Rambam's unity: the soul is drawn from His thought and wisdom, and 'He and His wisdom are one' (Tanya 2:2). So the part is not a second thing beside Him. (*stated*)
2. The soul is breathed 'from within', from His inwardness (Tanya 2:1; Iggeret HaTeshuvah 5:1). Iggeret HaTeshuvah 5:1 places the soul's source in 'the inwardness and source of speech, the higher breath'. Setting this against the creatures spoken into being (F01), as blown against spoken, is our framing of that line. (*stated*)
3. The bond holds at every level. Even the soul clothed in the lowest person stays 'bound and united with a wondrous unity' with its first essence, as a nail grows from the father's brain-drop (Tanya 2:6-8). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab, quoted by the Rayatz in Hayom Yom 13 Kislev, goes further: a Jew is 'a part of the Essence', and as the Essence has no measure, neither has the part; 'grasping part of it is grasping all of it'. The text still speaks of a 'part'; reading this as the spine's yachid (Inyanah 5:5) is ours, not the Hayom Yom's. (*stated*)
5. The part is placed in the lowest. The Rayatz: man is joined of dust and 'a part of God above', and so grasps Godliness with a physical mind more than the angels (G05). (*stated*)
6. From the part follows the unity of souls: all have one Father (Tanya 32; see S059). (*stated*)
7. The phrase is shared across schools, but the claims differ in scope: Keter Shem Tov gives it to the tzaddik, Tanya to every Jew, R. Nachman calls Jews 'parts of the Shechinah', the Rashab 'a part of the Essence'. Setting them side by side as different claims is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2:1-8: literally a part of God, drawn from His wisdom which is one with Him, bound to its source even in the lowest. Iggeret HaTeshuvah 5: the rope tied above (S059). Tanya 31:17: why lower 'a part of His light' into a snake's skin (S058).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not quoted on the phrase in this pass. His yechidah as essential bond (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7, S057) and the mazal always attached above (Sha'arei Teshuvah, S059) carry the same point.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz and Matzah 6: the divine soul is a part of God, drawn from His wisdom which is 'Godliness itself', 'not separate at all', and so above the angels, who are yesh (S054, S058).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes by the Rashab): the divine soul, a part of God, always wants to be included, a candle before a torch (S053); its descent is not for itself, since it is a part of God (S058).
- **Rashab.** Hayom Yom 13 Kislev ('my father said'): a Jew is 'a part of the Essence'; Chassidus changes what is apparent and reveals the essence.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 10 (OCR): man is the 'middle creation', dust and a part of God, and grasps Godliness with a physical mind.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 11-14: the yechidah as the part always one with the Essence (S057). He compiled Hayom Yom 13 Kislev.
- **Other schools.** Baal Shem Tov (Keter Shem Tov 1:55:8): 'the tzaddik has a soul, a part of God above' (scope: our reading). The Maggid (67:3): pray not for the body's lack but for what that part of God lacks; the world of speech speaks in him. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 260): Jews are literally parts of the Shechinah, returning to her through self-sacrifice: the part is of the Shechinah, not of the Essence, a real difference in formulation. Me'or Einayim and Toldot Yaakov Yosef use the phrase for the descent and for the soul's final holiness (S058, S062).

## Thin or forced?

No. The tie is stated in so many words (Tanya 2; Hayom Yom 13 Kislev). The one open point is scope (tzaddik or every Jew), which is recorded, not resolved.

## S053 · The divine soul · נפש האלקית

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** נפש האלקית · נפש אלקית · נשמה האלקית · נפש האלהית

> The divine soul is the unity of God as a life with no self of its own: Godliness in a person whose nature is to be nullified into its source.

## Derivation

1. The divine soul is the 'second soul' of Tanya 2, a part of God breathed from His inwardness (S052). (*stated*)
2. Its nature is bitul. Like a flame straining to leave the wick, it longs to leave the body and cleave to its source, 'even though it would become nothing there and be wholly nullified' (Tanya 19:2-4). Bitul (F08) is here not an achievement but the soul's nature. (*stated*)
3. Why: its root is Chochmah, 'in which is the light of Ein Sof'. And the rule of all holiness is that it is only what is drawn from Chochmah, 'nullified in existence to the Ein Sof clothed in it, and not a thing in itself' (Tanya 19:6-7; compare Tanya 22). (*stated*)
4. R. Yitzchak Eizik of Homel says it as phenomenology: the soul's life is 'nothing but the living God', unchanged; its vessels only give that life a shape, and it has 'no self-feeling at all' (Chanah Ariel, Yitro 2:10). (*stated*)
5. The two souls answer to the two unities. The Maharash (notes of the Rashab, Toras Shmuel 5640): the animal soul can reach the nullification of 'something into nothing', the divine soul true nullification; Shema and Baruch Shem are the higher and lower unity 'each according to its measure' (F02). The Maharash offers the mapping to the two souls tentatively ('and it can be said', ואפ"ל), and adds that the lower unity also holds the higher level, hidden. (*stated*)
6. The divine soul therefore sees from above ('before Him', G02). The Rashab: the surrounding light and what is above it come to real grasp only 'in the Chabad of the divine soul', while the filling light can be grasped by the animal soul's natural mind (Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 10, OCR, read in this pass). (*stated*)
7. Its work below is to teach the animal soul that Havayah is Elokim (S054). That the divine soul is the unity's own point of view inside a person is our summary. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2 (part of God); 9:3-4 (its seat in the brain, its love like fiery flames from contemplation); 19 (the flame and 'hidden love'); Likkutei Torah, Ha'Azinu 3:5: the divine soul needs no commandment, 'it believed and knew by itself' in Eden; it descended for the animal soul's sake (S054).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7 and 22: the divine soul's arousal differs in kind from the natural soul's; the natural soul's understanding is only a garment over the divine soul's (S057).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6: souls drawn from His wisdom 'are not separate at all' and would grasp 'the essence of Godliness', unlike angels who are yesh (S054).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes by the Rashab): candle before a torch; true bitul against the animal soul's bitul hayesh.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 10 (OCR): sovev and above grasped in the divine soul's Chabad. Kuntres Umaayan 13-14: the divine soul's root is higher and 'prior', which is why it can prevail (S054).
- **Rayatz.** Not located on the divine soul as such in this pass; see S052 and S058.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 12-14: the four lower levels of the (divine) soul have a 'shape' and so give Godliness 'a place as another thing'; only its yechidah does not (S056, S057).
- **Other schools.** The technical pair divine soul / animal soul is almost wholly Chabad (pillar 19). Homel, a Chabad chassid outside the Rebbeim, gives the sharpest statement (no self-feeling). Outside Chabad the soul is usually 'neshamah' or 'the part of God' (S052).

## S054 · The animal soul · נפש הבהמית

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** נפש הבהמית · נפש החיונית · נפש הטבעית · נפש הבהמיות

> The animal soul is the unity of God as the 'something' in a person that is to be turned: the lowest point, where the Essence's power to transform shows most.

## Derivation

1. Every Jew, tzaddik or rasha, has two souls; the first is from kelipat nogah, 'which also has good in it' (Tanya 1:15-17). It can be refined, not only fought. (*stated*)
2. The Tzemach Tzedek names it by the unity's terms: it is 'yesh', a something, rooted in the realm where the nations call Him 'God of gods' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6). (*stated*)
3. The divine soul was lowered into it 'to turn it and bring it to the nullification of the something'. The soul is revealed in the body 'to teach the animal soul of nogah that Havayah is Elokim' (same passage; G14). The animal soul is where the unity has to be known. (*stated*)
4. Its root divides the Rebbeim. The Alter Rebbe: in its root the animal soul is higher than the divine soul (from the dregs of the Ofanim, the ox face), which is why it is the earth in which the seed grows, and why nearly all the mitzvot were given for it (Likkutei Torah, Ha'Azinu 3:5-6). The Rashab: its root is Tohu, which came first, but the divine soul's root, Tikkun, is rooted in the Essence 'above the root of Tohu', and so it can prevail (Kuntres Umaayan 13:2). (*stated*)
5. Raising it is the purpose. Tanya 31:17: the descent is to raise 'the whole vital animal soul' and its garments, 'which is the purpose of creation' (F07). (*stated*)
6. Its turning shows the Essence. The Rebbe: the lower Chassidus reaches, down to changing the animal soul's nature, the more its essence is expressed (Inyanah 19:4; G05). Hayom Yom 17 Tishrei: when trials reveal the yechidah, the 'yechidah of the animal soul' is itself turned to Godliness. (*stated*)
7. The Maharash: reminded that its root in the Ofanim is nullified to Godliness, the animal soul too is moved toward bitul (Toras Shmuel 5640, notes by the Rashab, S053). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 1 (two souls; nogah), 9 (its seat in the left heart), 29:7-9 (the pure soul is 'given in me', so 'the man himself' is not it; in the beinoni the animal soul 'is the man'), 31:17 (its raising is the purpose). Likkutei Torah, Ha'Azinu 3:5-6: the earth that grows the seed; higher in its root.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 22: the natural soul's arousal is a garment that conceals the divine soul's. Read, not quoted.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6: the animal soul is yesh; the divine soul was lowered into it to teach it that Havayah is Elokim; 'eyes down, heart up' so the animal soul, which contemplates with human intellect, is itself convinced.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes by the Rashab): its root is the ox face of the Ofanim; reminded of that, it reaches bitul hayesh. Hayom Yom 23 Sivan, his answer to the Rashab (1875): the yetzer is called animal soul 'not because it is an animal'; it is sometimes a clever fox.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 2 (degrees of its strength), 13 (Tohu root, divine soul higher), 15 (it is one's enemy). Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 10 (OCR): the animal soul's natural intellect can grasp the filling light.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 17 Tishrei (compiled from his words): the yechidah of the animal soul is turned to Godliness through trials.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 1:8 and 19:4: Chassidus reaches even the intellect of the animal soul, and its essence shows most in changing that soul's nature.
- **Other schools.** The term is Chabad's (pillar 19). The Izhbitz school has a structural parallel: Lot is the yetzer that Avraham, the neshamah, needs to work on (Beit Yaakov, Vayera 37, per pillar 19; not re-verified here).

## S055 · The good and evil inclinations · יצר הטוב ויצר הרע

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F11 Darkness turned to light · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** יצר הרע · יצה"ר · יצר טוב · יצה"ט

> The good and evil inclinations are the unity of God as an opposition He Himself sets up and keeps alive, so that it can be subdued and then turned.

## Derivation

1. Two 'kings' fight over the small city of the body (Tanya 9:5). (*stated*)
2. The opposing will is His own device, for the person's good: the animal soul 'wants the opposite, for the benefit of the man, that he overcome it', like the harlot in the Zohar sent by the king to test his son (Tanya 9:8). The Besht: the yetzer plays the rebel servant, but the wise sense that 'it does the Creator's will and His holy Name is clothed there' (Keter Shem Tov 1:82). Calling this F03 (the concealment as His power, for revelation) is our reading; neither text uses the vocabulary of concealment here. (*stated*)
3. Its life too is from Him. The Ruzhiner: 'even in the evil inclination its main life is only the holy spark of the life of God'; 'I have set God before me always' includes what stands against me (Beit Yisrael, Vayigash 2:2; F01). (*stated*)
4. Subduing it reveals the unity: 'when the other side is subdued below, the glory of the Holy One rises above all' (Tanya 27:5). Turning it is the tzaddik's (Tanya 27:8; S060). (*stated*)
5. The power to turn it is the Essence's alone. The Rebbe: Torah's union with the Essence shows precisely in its being 'spice' for the evil inclination, because revelations can only fight evil; only the Essence, which has no opposite, can turn it (Inyanah 19:7-20:2; F09). (*stated*)
6. The goal is 'with all your heart', both hearts, so that the yetzer too loves Godliness; that is how Havayah becomes 'your Elokim' (the Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5640, notes by the Rashab; G14). (*stated*)
7. In conduct it stays an enemy. The Rashab: the animal soul and the yetzer are one's 'greatest enemies, wanting his spiritual death' (Kuntres Umaayan 15:3). The Maharash: it can be a clever fox or dress as a humble tzaddik (Hayom Yom 23 Sivan). The union of opposites is in Him and is no license to blur them (G10). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 9 (two kings; the harlot), 27 (itkafya and the two delights). Tanya 1:5 cites the Talmud: tzaddikim are judged by the good inclination, the wicked by the evil.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located on the yetzer as such in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not quoted in this pass; his account of the animal soul as yesh (S054) is the ground.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640: both hearts, so the yetzer loves Godliness. Hayom Yom 23 Sivan: the fox.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 15: the yetzer's method (from the fully permitted to the barely permitted) and the call to war; 14: the divine soul's strength is roused by one's own will.
- **Rayatz.** Not located on the yetzer as such in this pass beyond Hayom Yom.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 19:7-20:3: 'I created the evil inclination, I created Torah as its spice': only the Essence turns it; the delight above ('playing before Him') comes from turning darkness to light.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:82; 2:4: learn from the yetzer itself, which always does its Master's will). The Ruzhiner: its life is the holy spark (Vayigash 2:2); the 'level scales', the tzaddik's yetzer grows into a mountain so that choice is kept (Mishpatim 2:2, S061). Pillar 19 records R. Tzadok: it renews itself daily (not re-verified here). Real difference: the Besht and the Ruzhiner stress that it is His agent; the Rashab stresses that it is one's enemy. Both are said in the sources.

> — Tanya 9: two kings fight over the 'small city' of the body.

## S056 · The five levels of the soul · נפש רוח נשמה חיה יחידה

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** נפש רוח נשמה · חיה יחידה · נרנח"י · נרנ"ח

> The five levels of the soul are the unity of God as the two unities inside a person: four levels that live in worlds and feel them as real, and a fifth, above worlds, that feels them as nothing.

## Derivation

1. All the levels, nefesh, ruach and neshamah, from the highest soul to the lowest, are drawn from supernal Chochmah (Tanya 2:6). (*stated*)
2. Nefesh, ruach and neshamah are clothed in vessels (brain, heart); chayah and yechidah remain encompassing (makifim) (Likkutei Torah, Re'eh 7:9; Shir HaShirim 41:10). That this repeats the pair filling and surrounding (F04) inside the soul is our reading. (**reading**)
3. The Rebbe: the four lower levels each live in a world (nefesh in Asiyah, ruach in Yetzirah, neshamah in Beriah, chayah in Atzilus), so what they feel simply is the existence of the worlds; continuous creation is a novelty to them. Only the yechidah, above worlds, feels simply that the worlds are absolutely nothing (Inyanah 14:5-15:1). That the lower and the higher unity (F02) are thus two strata of one soul is our reading; the Rebbe speaks of feeling the worlds as real or as nothing, not of the two unities. (*stated*)
4. Torah has the same five. PaRDeS answers to the four lower levels; Chassidus is the yechidah of Torah. The Zohar lists only four, including the yechidah in 'soul of the soul', and only Chassidus explains the yechidah at length (Inyanah 11:3, 12:1-2; G07). (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe: the divine soul has five levels, the highest the yechidah, an essential bond 'present in every Jew according to his measure' (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:20-22). (*stated*)
6. Other schools read the ladder as attainment. Krakow: 'if he merits more, they give him ruach…', matched to PaRDeS and stopping at chayah (Maor VaShemesh, Yitro 18). The Izhbitz school puts the unblemished point at the neshamah and makes ruach the power of choice (Mei HaShiloach anthology, from Tiferes Yosef). That these differ from the Rebbe's reading on where the soul touches the Essence is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2:6 (all levels from Chochmah); 3 (each level has ten powers); Likkutei Torah, Re'eh 7:9-11 (NRN in vessels, chayah-yechidah encompassing; teshuvah is 'the revelation of the yechidah') and Shir HaShirim 41:10.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:20-26, 23:18: five levels; the yechidah exists 'even in the nefesh of Asiyah'. Sha'arei Teshuvah: chayah and yechidah are the tzelem, the mazal (S059).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located on the five levels as such in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located on the five as a set in this pass; his 'essence of the soul' is under S057.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 21 Sivan (compiled from his words): the ten powers are in 'nefesh, ruach and neshamah that clothe the body'; self-sacrifice comes from the Essence above the sefiros.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 11-15: PaRDeS as NRNC of Torah; the four levels live in worlds; only the yechidah feels them as nothing.
- **Other schools.** Maor VaShemesh (Krakow): the levels are earned. Mei HaShiloach anthology: neshamah never blemished; ruach is choice. Pillar 19 records the Chernovitzer (chayah around the whole person, yechidah around the neshamah) and Dinov's anatomy (brain, heart, liver); not re-verified here.

## S057 · Yechidah and the essence of the soul · יחידה / עצם הנשמה

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F05 The Essence beyond both · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** יחידה · עצם הנשמה · נקודה פנימית · נקודת היהדות

> Yechidah is the unity of God as it stands inside the soul: the point of the soul that is one with the Essence and gives no room to anything besides Him.

## Derivation

1. The yechidah is 'the essence of the soul', from which the four levels are drawn (Inyanah 12:3). That it is to the soul what the yachid is to the spine, not a sum of the levels, is our comparison. (*stated*)
2. It is always united with the Essence, so no blemish or impurity reaches it. That is why Modeh Ani is said before washing, and why it names none of the seven Names: the essence of the soul thanks the Essence, which no Name grasps (Inyanah 11:4-6). (*stated*)
3. It 'gives no room to anything besides Him' (Inyanah 14:1). (Our gloss: here ein od milvado is not a thought but a level of the soul.) (*stated*)
4. It is in every Jew, even the lowest. The Mitteler Rebbe: when a person of small understanding is stirred on hearing about sovev and memale, that sense comes from 'the root of his soul, the Godliness in his soul, the yechidah in him' (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:22-25). It shows in deed: refusing to convert in actual fact (28:26). (*stated*)
5. It is joined with the Essence through simple faith: 'prayer in simple faith joins the essence of the soul with the Essence of Ein Sof' (the Alter Rebbe, Motzaei Yom Kippur 5556, Hayom Yom 11 Tishrei). (*stated*)
6. When revealed it transforms. Trials reveal the yechidah, and through it the animal soul's own yechidah is turned to Godliness (Hayom Yom 17 Tishrei). The Rashab: service from the essence of the soul, above reason, 'waters' the animal soul and turns its folly into holy folly (Kuntres Umaayan 27:2). F11. (*stated*)
7. The Sfas Emes speaks of an 'inner point in the soul of Israel that no stranger touches', never distanced from its root, but not always findable. That this is the same as the Chabad yechidah is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Hayom Yom 11 Tishrei (5556): 'to Him, not to His attributes': the essence of the soul and the Essence of Ein Sof joined in simple faith. Likkutei Torah, Re'eh 7:10: teshuvah is the revelation of the yechidah. Tanya 18-19 locates the 'hidden love' in the Chochmah of the soul, where Ein Sof is, not in the yechidah by name.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:20-26; 23:18; 28:15-26: the yechidah as essential bond, present in every Jew, shining down into the act of refusing to convert.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located on the yechidah by name in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 27: 'the service from the essence of the soul', above reason, transforms the animal soul. Hayom Yom 13 Kislev: a Jew is 'a part of the Essence' (S052).
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 17 Tishrei (compiled from his words): trials reveal the yechidah.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 11-15: the yechidah and Modeh Ani; the yechidah of Torah is Chassidus; Moshiach's point is the yechidah (spine, pillar 17).
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes: the inner point, never distant from its root (Elul 9:4), and the point that is 'the life of all', revealed through circumcision (Vayera 4:3). The Izhbitz anthology places the unblemished point at the neshamah (S056). Difference kept: Chabad calls it the yechidah, one with the Essence; the Sfas Emes calls it a point of Israel's bond, guarded and sometimes lost to view.

> — The Rashab: service from the essence of the soul, above reason, waters and transforms the animal soul's folly.

## Thin or forced?

No. This is the most direct soul-face of the unity; the Rebbe states the tie in so many words (Inyanah 11:5, 14:1).

## S058 · The descent of the soul into the body · ירידת הנשמה לגוף

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F11 Darkness turned to light · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** ירידת הנשמה · ירידת הנשמות · נשמה בגוף · ירידתה

> The descent of the soul into the body is the unity of God as a going down for the sake of the dwelling below: a part of Him clothed in the lowest so that the lowest is raised.

## Derivation

1. The Alter Rebbe states the paradox in the unity's own terms: why would God lower 'a part of His light, which fills and surrounds all worlds and before which all is as nothing', into a snake's skin and a putrid drop? (Tanya 31:17). (*stated*)
2. His answer: 'this descent is for the sake of an ascent', to raise the vital animal soul and its garments, 'which is the purpose of the creation of the world' (Tanya 31:17). Likkutei Torah adds that the ascent happens even while the soul is in the body, not only in reward later (Bamidbar 4:2). (*stated*)
3. The ascent exceeds the soul's first state. The Rashab: the soul clothed in a physical body and natural soul has an advantage over its standing in Eden 'in total unity and bitul', because the purpose of creation is that He desired a dwelling below (Kuntres Umaayan 15:2-3, citing Tanya 36; F07; G05). (*stated*)
4. The Tzemach Tzedek: the divine soul was lowered into the animal soul, below the angels, to turn it to the nullification of 'something' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6). The Maharash: the descent is 'not for its own sake, since the soul is a part of God', but to break the darkness so that even the yetzer loves God (Toras Shmuel 5640). (*stated*)
5. The founding circle says the same with the advantage of light from darkness: the part of God is placed in the darkness of matter 'so that the lower levels rise' (Me'or Einayim, Bereshit 2; F11). (*stated*)
6. The Besht, as handed down by the Alter Rebbe: a soul comes down for seventy or eighty years to do one Jew a favor, material and above all spiritual (Hayom Yom 5 Iyar). That this small purpose is a case of the dwelling below is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 31:17; 36-37 (the dwelling below, spine F07). Likkutei Torah, Bamidbar 4:2 (descent for ascent even now); Ha'Azinu 3:5 (the seed sown in the animal soul). Hayom Yom 5 Iyar transmits the Besht.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not quoted in this pass on the descent as such.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Chametz 6: lowered below the angels to turn the animal soul.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes by the Rashab): the descent is to break the darkness; both hearts.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 6 (the descent is worth the danger, for the ascent and the delight above) and 15 (its advantage over Eden, via the dwelling below).
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 1 Adar I and 15 Cheshvan (compiled from his words): each soul descends for general and particular tasks; the soul waits above to come down, because below it can reach 'delight in Havayah'. Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 10 (OCR): man's mission is to make the lowest world a place for His dwelling.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5738: through teshuvah the soul returns 'to my father's house', higher than before its descent (S060).
- **Other schools.** Me'or Einayim (Bereshit 2): the part of God placed in the darkness of matter, for the advantage of light. The Besht via Hayom Yom. The theme is shared; the dwelling-below framing is Chabad's (spine, F07).

## S059 · The root of the soul · שורש הנשמה

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** שורש נשמתו · שורש הנשמה · מקור הנשמות · חוצבה

> The root of the soul is the unity of God as the one place above where all souls are one, tied to Him by a rope that nothing cuts but what opposes His will.

## Derivation

1. All souls, highest to lowest, come from supernal Chochmah (Tanya 2:6). (*stated*)
2. Their root is 'in the One God'; only bodies divide them. So all Jews are 'literally brothers', and ahavat Yisrael rests on the root (Tanya 32:2). (*stated*)
3. The root is where the unity rests: the aim of the whole Torah is to draw the light of Ein Sof into the source of all souls, 'to be one in One', which cannot happen where souls are divided (Tanya 32:3). (*stated*)
4. The root is one end of a rope tied above (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 5:2). 'In truth nothing physical or spiritual separates before Him', since He fills all and there is nothing else (5:4). Only sin, being against His will, cuts the rope (5:5-6). Reading this as F02 and F03 (from His side nothing separates; the cut exists from below) is ours. (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe: the 'mazal', the encompassing chayah and yechidah called the tzelem, 'is always attached above'; sudden teshuvah beyond reason shines from it (Sha'arei Teshuvah, Shaar HaTeshuvah 6-7). (*stated*)
6. The Besht draws the practical line: include the sinner with yourself 'through the unity that all are one man', and he too returns (Keter Shem Tov 1:59). (*stated*)
7. Reading: the root is the lower unity's many separate souls seen from the higher unity, where they are one. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2:6, 32:2-3; Iggeret HaTeshuvah 5.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Teshuvah (Shaar HaTeshuvah 6-7): the mazal, the encompassing chayah and yechidah, always attached above, called 'tzelem Elokim'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ahavat Yisrael, not located in this pass; the spine records his 'Chassidim one family' (pillar 22), not re-verified here.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located on the root as such in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Not quoted in this pass on the root as such.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:59): 'all are one man'. Pillar 19 and 22 record R. Tzadok on the soul's root and its sins and the Polish grounds for ahavat Yisrael; not re-verified here. In the Polish schools the unity of souls is argued from other grounds; tying it to this face there is our reading (spine, F06).

## S060 · Tzaddik, beinoni and rasha · צדיק בינוני ורשע

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** בינוני · בינונים · צדיק גמור · צדיק ורע לו

> Tzaddik, beinoni and rasha are the unity of God as degrees of how the 'something' in a person stands before Him: turned, held down, or ruling, each of which reveals Him in its own way.

## Derivation

1. Tanya defines them by the two souls, not by counting deeds (Tanya 1, 12). (*stated*)
2. The complete tzaddik has turned his evil to good: the 'sons of ascent', 'who turn darkness to light and bitter to sweet' (Tanya 10:6). F11. (*stated*)
3. The beinoni never lets the evil clothe itself in deed, speech or thought; his divine soul rules alone only at prayer, when he binds his mind to the greatness of Ein Sof (Tanya 12:1-3). This is 'the measure of every man' (Tanya 14:1). (*stated*)
4. Both reveal Him. There are 'two kinds of delight before Him': from the tzaddikim who turn the other side, and from the beinonim who subdue it while it is still strong (Tanya 27:8). When it is subdued below, 'the glory of the Holy One rises above all' (27:5). Perhaps the beinoni was created for this war (27:6). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe opens the gate further: everyone can be a beinoni 'at any time' because everyone has free choice to do teshuvah, and inwardly already regrets (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5745). And the baal teshuvah's delight above exceeds the tzaddik's, since it is a novelty from below (5738). (*stated*)
6. The Besht: the rasha is not outside the unity; include him with yourself 'through the unity that all are one man' and he returns (Keter Shem Tov 1:59). (*stated*)
7. Reading: the three types map onto how the yesh stands before the unity (transformed, subdued, ruling), and onto F08 as degrees of bitul. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 1-14, 27. Tanya 1 rejects the reading of 'beinoni' as half merits and half sins.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located on the three types as such in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha (supplements) summarizes Tanya 12 on the beinoni (read, not quoted).
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 1-2 on the degrees of the animal soul's strength (read, not quoted).
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 20 Cheshvan 5745 (anyone can be a beinoni through the choice of teshuvah); 9-10 Kislev 5738 (the baal teshuvah above the tzaddik).
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:59). The doctrine of the tzaddik as a channel (Noam Elimelech, Polish schools) is a different center; the spine's section 5 finds no stated tie to the unity there, and none is claimed here.

## S061 · Free choice · בחירה

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** בחירה · בחירה חפשית · רשות נתונה · בחירתו

> Free choice is the unity of God as the Essence's own freedom placed in man: a choice nothing compels, which exists below only because He, whom nothing compels, made man in His image.

## Derivation

1. The Rebbe: 'truly free choice belongs only to the Creator Himself, whom nothing compels'; every creature is compelled by its nature, mind or desire. Since free choice belongs only to 'the Essence of Ein Sof', below it has a place only in the man made 'in Our image' (Likkutei Sichos 31, Yisro, letter of 5711). F05. (*stated*)
2. The worlds below Atzilus are 'truly separate and limited, to the point that they have choice to act against His will' (Likkutei Torah, Behar 3:10). That this is the lower unity's real separateness (F02) is our reading. (*stated*)
3. His knowledge surrounds each creature 'in actuality', yet it does not compel, because it is only encompassing and not revealed within (Likkutei Torah, Behar 3:13). The text speaks of the 'higher knowledge' shining only as makif; the phrase 'the gap between sovev and memale' (F04) is ours. (*stated*)
4. What is in choice: deed, speech and thought, even against the heart's desire (Tanya 14:1); being a true tzaddik is not fully in one's choice (14:5). (*stated*)
5. The Ruzhiner: God keeps adding to the yetzer as the good grows, 'so that choice is not cancelled' (Beit Yisrael, Mishpatim 2:2). (*stated*)
6. The Izhbitzer presses the unity against choice: choice is 'only a small thing, like a garlic peel'; all human acts are as He wills (Mei HaShiloach, Vayeilech 3), and Adam came to see 'that his sin too was by the will of God, only above his knowledge' (Mei HaShiloach, Volume II, Bereshit 6). The Chabad sources root choice in the Essence; Izhbitz nearly dissolves it into His will. Kept as a real difference. (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe: every Jew has free choice to do teshuvah, more than for other mitzvot (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5745). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 14 (the scope of choice), 39:1 (angels have no choice); Iggeret HaTeshuvah 6:5; Likkutei Torah, Behar 3:10-13 (choice in the separate worlds; knowledge as makif); Nasso 3:16 (will is above choice and reason).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 14:11: whether the divine soul's strength is roused 'depends on the person's choice' (read, not quoted).
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 24 Iyar (compiled from his words): God's 'pleading' that we walk in His statutes itself gives strength to stand by one's good choice.
- **The Rebbe.** Likkutei Sichos 31 (letter, 5711): free choice only in the Essence and in man in His image. Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5745: the choice of teshuvah. The index to Likkutei Sichos 36 lists 'free choice in Israel because they are rooted in the Essence' (Likkutei Torah 38b); that page was not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Izhbitz (Mei HaShiloach): choice a garlic peel; even sin by His will. Mei HaShiloach, Bereshit 4: all creatures are separate and so have no choice; man holds all their powers (S063). Ruzhin: the level scales.

> **Likkutei Torah, Behar 3:10** (Alter Rebbe, Likkutei Torah; line 1018)
> משא״כ בי״ע הם נפרדים ממש ובעלי גבול עד שיש להם בחירה לעשות ח״ו הפך רצונו ית׳
> — Choice to defy His will belongs to the separate worlds (calling this the lower unity's 'separate thing' is ours).

> **Mei HaShiloach, Volume I, Deuteronomy, Vayeilech 3** (Izhbitzer, Mei HaShiloach; line 468)
> שהיה יודע בטוב שכל מעשי בני אדם אף שנתן הבחירה ביד אדם הוא רק דבר מועט כקליפת השום
> — Izhbitz: human choice is 'only a small thing, like a garlic peel'; all is as God wills.

> **Mei HaShiloach, Volume II, Genesis, Bereshit 6** (Izhbitzer, Mei HaShiloach; line 720)
> שהכיר אחר תשובתו שאין האדם יכול לפעול כלום בעולם הזה רק הכל הוא מהשי"ת, וממילא גם חטאו היה ברצון השי"ת רק שהיה למעלה מדעתו
> — Izhbitz: after his teshuvah Adam recognized that man can do nothing in this world, all is from God, and so his sin too was by God's will, only above his knowledge.

> **Beit Yisrael (Ruzhin), Emet LeYaakov, Mishpatim 2:2** (Ruzhiner, Beit Yisrael; line 249)
> ואזי צריך להוסיף לו עוד מעט יצה"ר כדי שיהי' בשוה עם היצ"ט שלא יתבטל אצלו הבחירה
> — The Ruzhiner: the yetzer is kept level with the good so that choice is never cancelled.

## Thin or forced?

No for Chabad: the tie to the Essence is stated (Likkutei Sichos 31), and Likkutei Torah, Behar states that choice lives in the separate worlds and under a knowledge that is only makif; the mapping to F02 and F04 is ours. Izhbitz states a tie of its own that runs the other way.

## S062 · Reincarnation · גלגול

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F11 Darkness turned to light · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** גלגול · גלגולים · בגלגול · עיבור

> Reincarnation is the unity of God as the soul's returning until it has clothed all of itself in His will: the 613 garments through which a creature can hold the Infinite.

## Derivation

1. The Ari, via Iggeret HaKodesh 29: 'every Jew must come in many incarnations until he fulfills all 613 mitzvot in thought, speech and deed, to complete the garments of his soul'. (*stated*)
2. Why garments: nefesh, ruach and neshamah are created things, and 'no creature can grasp the Creator, Ein Sof'; even after all the contractions the soul cannot bear the light without the garments of Torah and mitzvot (Iggeret HaKodesh 29:5-6). So gilgul serves the union of the soul with Or Ein Sof through His will (F10). (*stated*)
3. Kuntres Acharon 5:8: souls come in gilgul 'to complete the Torah in PaRDeS, in order to refine all the refinements that belong to them' of the 288 sparks (F11, F07). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe: 'all souls now come as a continuation of their previous gilgul, to complete what they lacked then' (Likkutei Sichos 35, letter). He notes that the doctrine predates the Ari, was received by Chassidus and by the Gaon of Vilna, and was rejected by some medieval thinkers (Likkutei Sichos 33). (*stated*)
5. Toldot Yaakov Yosef, from the preacher of Polonoye: after all its incarnations and punishments the Jewish soul must end holy, 'for it is a part of God above' (Nasso 170). Gilgul cannot sever F06. (*stated*)
6. The Besht: one's enemies may carry a tzaddik's spirit in gilgul; prayer for them sweetens it in its root (Keter Shem Tov 1:10). Tie to the unity: our reading (G03). (**reading**)
7. R. Nachman derives gilgul from longing: souls are formed from longing completed in speech, and so they roll from place to place, sometimes into a rasha, who then has thoughts of teshuvah (Likutei Moharan 31:14-15). A different center (longing), recorded as such. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Iggeret HaKodesh 7:14 (every soul must come in gilgul for all 613, yet each has one mitzvah to guard 'more'), 29; Kuntres Acharon 5; Tanya 18:8 cites Sefer HaGilgulim.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Likkutei Sichos 33 (source and reception of the doctrine) and 35 (souls completing a previous gilgul; children who died young completing years).
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:10); Toldot Yaakov Yosef (Nasso 170); R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 31:14, 200: today's tzaddikim are earlier tzaddikim in gilgul).

> — R. Nachman: souls are made from longing completed in speech, and so they 'roll' (gilgul); a different center, longing.

## Thin or forced?

Yes, largely. Gilgul is a doctrine received from the Ari and earlier; the Rebbeim use it but do not derive it from the unity. The tie is stated only through the garments of mitzvot (Iggeret HaKodesh 29) and the refinement of sparks (Kuntres Acharon 5). Beyond that it is our reading.

## S063 · Man as a small world · אדם עולם קטן

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F07 The home below · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** עולם קטן · נעשה אדם · דמות אדם · צלם אלקים

> Man as a small world is the unity of God seen 'from my flesh': the person who contains all creation, from whom one learns that a hidden life sustains the world, and who fixes the world by fixing himself.

## Derivation

1. The Besht: man has ten sefiros 'because he is a small world'; all in the world is in the year and in the soul (Keter Shem Tov 1:58, citing the Raavad; the Maharash and Likkutei Torah cite Avot deRabbi Natan 31). (*stated*)
2. The Maharash uses it to know the world's life: as we know a soul enlivens the body because the body lives, so we know a spiritual life enlivens the world. But of both we know only that it is, never what it is (Toras Shmuel 5631). G06 and F01. (*stated*)
3. The analogy fails exactly where the unity is strongest: soul and body are separate in their root, since the body comes from the parents' drops and food; heaven and earth come wholly from His word and stay nullified in it (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:7). The world is more one with its Source than a body with its soul. (*stated*)
4. Because the world is in him, man carries it. The Alter Rebbe: 'He set the world in their heart'; when a person fixes his four elements 'he fixes the whole world' (Likkutei Torah, Bamidbar 6:1). F07. (*stated*)
5. The Rayatz: man is 'the middle creation', dust and a part of God, unlike upper or lower creatures, and so grasps Godliness with a physical mind (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 10, OCR). (*stated*)
6. The Izhbitzer: every other creature is separate and 'not included in each other'; man, 'the seal of creation', has the power of all of them, and so choice and reward (Mei HaShiloach, Bereshit 4). The Izhbitzer states this about choice, not about the unity; bringing it under the unity is our reading. (**reading**)
7. The Rebbe: Chassidus brings the Torah's hidden part close to the mind 'by analogies from the soul's powers, from my flesh I see God', so that even the animal soul's intellect grasps it (Inyanah 1:8). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Likkutei Torah, Bamidbar 6:1 (the world in man's heart); Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:7 and Tanya 42:12 (where the soul-body analogy fails); Iggeret HaKodesh 12:8 (man, 'called a small world', where good and evil alternate).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631 (Ve'atah harem): knowing the world's life from the soul's; 5627 (Matzos Yei'achel): the narrows of Egypt are also in man, the small world, and only Anochi takes one out (spine F05).
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 15:2 (read): 'man's bodily tendencies are worse than animals', his soul's higher than the angels'.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 10 (OCR): the middle creation.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 1:8 ('from my flesh I see God'); Likkutei Sichos 31 (free choice only in man 'in Our image', S061).
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:58; 1:92: man and Israel as one body). Izhbitz (Mei HaShiloach, Bereshit 4). The Tzemach Tzedek's Or HaTorah and many others use the phrase often; not surveyed here.
