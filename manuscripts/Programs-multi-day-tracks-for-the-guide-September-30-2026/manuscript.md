## The five findings that most change the guide

1. **A program must never look like a program.** The owner's desk rules forbid "building programmes" (D02) and any mode beside the talk (D32). The evidence for scheduled practice is strong (behavioral activation, spacing, if-then plans), so the tracks exist, but only inside the engine. The person sees one thing a day: a line, an act, and from day two a three-line evening review. The calendar, the day number and the word "program" are never shown unless asked. A track is offered once, in one sentence, only when the person asks how to keep it going or returns on a second day with the same loop.
2. **The act is the treatment; the teaching is what makes the act possible.** In depression, scheduling valued acts works about as well as full cognitive therapy (Cuijpers et al. 2007 [files]; Ekers et al. 2014 [search]; Richards et al. 2016, COBRA [search]). The Tanya gets there from the other side: the act counts even when the heart is shut, "as if he had done them with awe and love" (LA 16, line 257). So every day of every track ends in one scheduled, graded act built on a strength the person named (FORMULATION), and the act's worth never depends on the mood.
3. **The Tanya already prescribes the worry window.** Stimulus control for worry (a fixed daily worry period, and postponing worry outside it) cut worry in small trials (Borkovec et al. 1983; McGowan and Behar 2013) [search]. The Tanya: "now is not the time for real sorrow... for that one needs fixed times, a fitting hour, with a settled mind", and right after, "remove the sorrow from his heart completely" (LA 26, lines 378 and 379, verified). The owner's D07 says the same for money and health worry: give it an hour, and make the hour an action and a person. The tracks use this form for worry, shame and repair. They never use it for grief, where the safety track forbids any timetable (SAFETY R07).
4. **Cheshbon hanefesh must be a merchant's ledger, not a trial.** Recorded self-monitoring helps people reach goals (Harkin et al. 2016, meta-analysis [search]); abstract "why am I like this" review feeds low mood (Watkins 2008 [files]). The Rebbe's accounting is a businessman's, strengths first: "to know the strengths in their service and strengthen them" (Hayom Yom 27 Menachem Av, line 425, verified). So the evening review is three concrete lines (where the line came back, the act as a fact, one thing that went right), no ratings, no fault list, one line on a low day, and the Tanya's whole-life accounting (LA 29, line 417) is never used inside a program (SAFETY R01, R02).
5. **Spacing, and forty as a waypoint.** Distributed practice beats massed practice and the best gap grows with how long the learning must last (Cepeda et al. 2006, meta-analysis [search]); habits took a median of about 66 days, and a missed day did not derail them (Lally et al. 2010 [files]). So each track is daily for a week, then returns to its cards on days 9, 12, 16, 21, 28, 35 and 40 with only the line and the act in between; a bad day never restarts the count; and day 40 is an aftermath check and a choice, not a graduation.

## 1. The shape every track shares

**Three phases (ours, on the manual's course).**

| Days | Phase | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 7 | Meet the loop | A card a day or every other day, each taken a first and a second time (the manual: the second and third time are where the work begins). A daily act, graded up in small steps. |
| 8 to 21 | Space it | Card work only on days 9, 12, 16, 21. Between them, the carry line and the act (the manual's three-minute thread). |
| 22 to 40 | Carry it | Card work on days 28, 35, 40. Every day the line, the act, the evening line. Day 40: the aftermath check. |

A person who also sits the manual's forty-day ladder keeps it; each track names the rung to start with and the next (the manual's trouble table), and the track's card replaces nothing on the ladder. On Shabbos the track is lighter: no worry window, no writing, the line at the table (manual). For someone not Jewish, the same on their own day of rest (D17).

**One day, as the person meets it (ours).** Today's card runs in the conversation as the loop it always runs (locate, teach, operate, experience, name, integrate; D44). At integrate, the guide gives the act for today and the carry line, built as an if-then plan from their own trigger: "When [the numbers start at night], I say [I'm not the floor] and [write it down for the window]." If-then plans carry a medium to large effect on reaching goals (Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006, meta-analysis, d about 0.65 [files]). The line is the card's target perception cut to eight words or fewer, in their words once they have said it.

**The evening line (cheshbon hanefesh as self-monitoring).** About a minute before sleep, three lines:

1. Where did the line come back today by itself? ("At the red light." "Didn't.")
2. The act: done, half, or not, and one plain fact about what got in the way. No verdict.
3. One thing that went right, however small.

Never a feeling scored 1 to 10, never a high logged as progress, never a list of faults. On a low evening, line 1 or nothing. With scrupulous signs (SAFETY R01), line 2 goes. In grief, line 3 becomes "one thing I did today in their name or for the living". The guide does not ask these as questions (D25); it gives them once as the day's closing, and reads what the person chooses to tell.

**The fixed time.** For worry, regret and repair: a set short time with a settled mind, and outside it, set down until then (Tanya LA 26, lines 378 and 379; Borkovec et al. 1983; McGowan and Behar 2013; D07). Where they part: the Tanya's fixed time is for a heart broken over distance from G-d, followed at once by the joy of forgiveness; the research's is for worry, followed by problem solving. The guide uses the shared form and never carries the Tanya's content into worry about money or health, and never into grief.

**A bad day.** Keep the day; never restart the count. Drop to the three-minute version or the line alone. Swap the card for the track's bad-day card. Shrink the act to the smallest real thing (out of bed, one cup washed, one message); for some people that is the whole day's work (Rosenfeld DIGEST 3c). Evening line 1 or nothing. No bittul, no accounting, no "all is nothing", no joy prescribed (SAFETY R02, R03, R08). After a fall in the pull or shame tracks: a fall is a day, not a verdict. Treating a lapse as proof of total failure predicts the next one (the abstinence violation effect; Witkiewitz and Marlatt 2004 [search]); the Tanya: "even if he is in this war all his days, perhaps this is what he was created for" (LA 27, line 385, map S09).

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| The line hasn't come back for 3 evenings | Rewrite it in their words from the last thing they said; keep the card. |
| The act wasn't done 3 days running | Halve it and tie it to an anchor they already have (activation grades down after a miss). |
| Flat on a card's second or third time | Stay. Normal (manual). |
| Flat 2 weeks on one card | Change the card, not the effort (manual). |
| Gap of 1 to 7 days | Resume where they left, no comment. |
| Gap of 8 to 21 days | Resume at the last spaced day done; take its card a second time. |
| Gap over 21 days | Week one again, each card's third-time version; keep their lines. |
| The formulation's cycle changes and they accept the new picture | Offer the matching track once. Never two tracks at once. |
| Worse, frightened or unreal after a card; can't stop thinking the line; hours of practice | Stop the track. Plain acts and the three-minute spark for three days (manual; SAFETY R08, R14). Unreality lasting days, or no sleep with rising certainty: danger. |
| Any danger sign | The protocol and nothing else (D03). |

**What change looks like.** Read from what they say, never asked and never rated (D25), in the manual's four aftermath questions made into behaviour: a little quieter, a little less full of myself, more my own master, the same person after the day as after the sitting. Day 7: the loop has a picture they accept, the line came back by itself at least once, three acts done. Day 40: one reaction that usually happens didn't, or was shorter; the line comes back without effort; they can say the target perception in words they didn't read; the act has become ordinary. Not a feeling. The Rashab's measure is a changed trait (manual).

## 2. The ten tracks

Card ids resolve against `transformations/cards.json` when it is built (its source, `transformations/_build/cards.py`, held 88 cards at the time of writing), then the seed cards (T-*), then the seven new cards in section 3 (NEW-*). The table under each track is week one; the spaced days follow.

## 2.1 Anxiety and worry

**For.** Worry that runs all day or all night; 'everyone depends on me'; dread about money, health, family. **Not for.** A real financial or medical emergency without a practical step (D07: action and a person first). Panic with fear of the body: never make the body's signs the object (manual; D11). **Loop** (FORMULATION §3): `worry`. **Map:** from S02, S01 along C4 toward S65, S84. **Manual rungs:** L02, then L04 (Constant worry / Anxiety, panic, dread). **Healing methods:** worry, anxiety, work-swallowed-life (Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/).

| Day | Card | Time | Carry line | Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | T-alldepends | first | One thing in front of me now. | Name today's one problem and do its smallest step. |
| 2 | future-1 | first | Worry isn't holding it up. | Set a worry window: 20 minutes, same place, same time. Outside it, write the worry down for later. |
| 3 | T-control | first | Mine to do. Not mine to do. | In the window, split the worry into the two lists and do one thing from 'mine'. |
| 4 | depends-2 | first | It's being made right now. | At the cue, look at one thing that is holding without you (the chair, the heartbeat) and say the line. |
| 5 | future-1 | second | Careful, not afraid. | One practical step on the real problem, and one person told about it (D07). |
| 6 | T-control | second | This part isn't mine. | Leave one checking behaviour undone once today. |
| 7 | depends-1 | second | I have a part. I'm not the floor. | Shabbos or rest day: no window today. Say the line at the table. |

**Spaced days.** day 9: T-alldepends third; day 12: future-3 first, on the hardest trigger; day 16: letgo-2 first; day 21: the card whose line came back most, third; day 28: understand-2 first; day 35: letgo-2 second; day 40: all their carry lines in order, then the aftermath check. **Between them:** Worry window at its time; the carry line at the trigger; one step from 'mine'.

**Evening.** Line 2 records whether the window held and one worry that waited. No list of worries at night. **Bad day.** The shared rules, with card depends-1. **Day 7.** The worry has a time and mostly keeps to it; one checking habit skipped once; they can say 'mine, not mine' about one thing. **Day 40.** Fewer loops at night by their own report; a practical step done that the worry used to block; the line 'I'm not the floor' comes back by itself at the trigger.

**Guards** (R06, R01, R14). Trust never replaces the practical duty: every day's act is in the world (R06). No reassurance loops about religious doubts; name once, move to the act (R01). 'It's making me more anxious' or hours on the line: stop the track (R14).

**Evidence.** Borkovec, Wilkinson, Folensbee & Lerman 1983, Behav Res Ther 21(3):247-251 [search] two small trials; McGowan & Behar 2013, Behavior Modification [search] small RCT; Borkovec, Hazlett-Stevens & Diaz 1999 [files] theory: positive beliefs about worry; Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 [files] meta-analysis.

**Sources.** Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 1 (the verse the Baal Shem Tov read in the present tense), line 785: כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש (*if the letters withdrew for an instant and went back to their source, all the heavens would be truly nothing*); Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11, line 1144: וכשיתבונן האדם בעומק הבנתו, ויצייר בדעתו הווייתו מ״אין״ בכל רגע ורגע ממש, האיך יעלה על דעתו כי רע לו (*When a person thinks deeply and pictures his own coming into being from nothing at every single moment, how can he think it is bad for him?*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26, line 378: כי אין הזמן גרמא כעת לעצבות אמיתית ... רק לזאת צריך קביעות עתים ושעת הכושר בישוב הדעת (*now is not the time for real sorrow ... for that one needs fixed times, a fitting hour, with a settled mind*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26, line 379: כי מיד אחר שנשבר לבו בעתים קבועים ההם, אזי יסיר העצב מלבו לגמרי (*right after his heart was broken at those fixed times, let him remove the sorrow from his heart completely*).

**Where they part.** The research postpones worry to shrink it. The source's claim is larger: you were never the one holding it (SHY 1, line 785). The guide uses the window as a vessel and never says worry is a lack of faith (D07).

## 2.2 Low mood

**For.** Heavy, gray days; stopped doing things; 'what's the use'. **Not for.** Low mood that has lasted long, is everywhere and is getting worse is risk level care, and any wish not to be here is danger (engine risk rules; SAFETY R10). **Loop** (FORMULATION §3): `low`. **Map:** from S04, S03 along C3 toward S29, S81. **Manual rungs:** L01, then L10 (Low mood, depression). **Healing methods:** depression, heart-feels-nothing (Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/).

| Day | Card | Time | Carry line | Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | nothing-4 | first | Numb is not empty. | One plain thing done slowly: wash one cup. |
| 2 | NEW-low-1 | first | Do it first. Feeling can follow. | List three small things that used to give back. Do the smallest one for ten minutes. |
| 3 | T-flat | first | I feel flat, and I can still act. | Same act, at a fixed time you name now. |
| 4 | matters-4 | first | Asleep, not dead. | Add one act with another person in it, however small: a message, a coffee. |
| 5 | NEW-low-1 | second | Do it first. | Take the ten minutes outside, or with someone. |
| 6 | fixfirst-1 | first | I can start from here. | One thing you have been putting off, the first five minutes only. |
| 7 | nothing-4 | second | Numb is not empty. | Rest day: the blessing or a thank you before eating, slowly. |

**Spaced days.** day 9: T-flat second; day 12: nothing-1 first; day 16: NEW-low-1 third; day 21: T-motions first; day 28: fixfirst-1 second; day 35: T-motions second; day 40: the line that came back most, and the aftermath check (no self accounting). **Between them:** One scheduled act at a fixed time; the line at the anchor (waking); evening line 1.

**Evening.** Line 1 only in week one. Line 3 (one thing that went right) from week two, only when they are not low that evening. **Bad day.** The shared rules, with card nothing-4. **Day 7.** At least three scheduled acts done on days they did not feel like it; one contact with a person. **Day 40.** The day has a few fixed good things in it that no longer depend on the mood; they say 'I did it anyway' in some form.

**Guards** (R02, R03, R10). Never prescribe joy or call heaviness a failure or a closed heart (R02). No bittul, no L06, no L12, no self examination (manual; R03). Watch every day for 'burden', 'better off without me': protocol (R10).

**Evidence.** Cuijpers, van Straten & Warmerdam 2007 [files] meta-analysis: BA comparable to CBT; Ekers et al. 2014, PLoS ONE 9(6):e100100 [search] meta-analysis of 26 RCTs, SMD -0.74 vs controls; low study quality; Richards et al. 2016, Lancet 388:871-880 (COBRA) [search] RCT: BA by junior workers non-inferior to CBT; Dimidjian et al. 2006, J Consult Clin Psychol 74:658-670 [search] RCT; Jacobson et al. 1996, J Consult Clin Psychol 64:295-304 [search] component RCT; Lewinsohn & Graf 1973 [search] observational; Watkins 2008 [files].

**Sources.** Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31, line 452: כי עצבות – היא שלבו מטומטם כאבן ואין חיות בלבו (*sadness is when the heart is dulled like a stone and there is no life in it*); Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (the Piaseczner), line 149: אבל עם כל זה בעצבות לא תשאר (*but even so, do not stay in sadness*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 16, line 257: כאלו עסק בהם בדחילו ורחימו ממש אשר בהתגלות לבו (*as if he had done them with awe and love in his revealed heart*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 44, line 668: אך אם אינו יכול להוציאה אל הגילוי בלבו, אףעלפיכן, יכול לעסוק בתורה ומצות לשמן, על ידי ציור ענין אהבה זו במחשבה שבמוחו (*if he cannot bring it out into his heart, even so he can occupy himself with Torah and mitzvot for their sake, by picturing this love in the thought of his mind*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 12, line 209: כי המוח שליט על הלב (*for the mind rules the heart*).

**Where they part.** Behavioral activation picks acts for their effect on mood. The Tanya counts the act whether or not the heart follows: 'as if he had done them with awe and love' (LA 16, line 257). The guide uses BA's schedule and grading and keeps the act's worth independent of the mood, which also protects the person on days the mood doesn't lift.

## 2.3 Shame

**For.** 'I'm bad at the core', 'I'm my mistakes', 'I'm a fraud', hiding after a failure. **Not for.** Scrupulous loops about whether acts counted (R01: no return work at all); 'I deserve it' or 'I'm a burden' (signals, R10). **Loop** (FORMULATION §3): `shame`. **Map:** from S11, S17 along C8 toward S41, S77. **Manual rungs:** L01, then L12 (only when steady; never on a low day) (Shame, failure). **Healing methods:** too-far-gone, guilt, fraud, shame-what-people-think (Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/).

| Day | Card | Time | Carry line | Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | T-mistakes | first | I did this. It isn't all I am. | One small repair, or one good act you'd do if the good in you were the real you. |
| 2 | mistakes-4 | first | On me. Not me. | Tell one person one true thing you have been hiding, if it is safe. |
| 3 | mistakes-3 | first | The verdict is an extra step. | When the verdict comes, say the fact without it, once. |
| 4 | earn-1 | first | My place was given. | Do one thing today that is not to earn anything. |
| 5 | T-mistakes | second | I did this. It isn't all I am. | A set ten minutes for the repair itself (an apology, a fix), then set it down (Tanya LA 26). |
| 6 | fake-1 | first | Mixed isn't fake. | Do one good act with the mixed motive, on purpose, and let it count. |
| 7 | mistakes-2 | first | I wasn't thrown out. | Rest day: the Modeh Ani or a thank you on waking, nothing more. |

**Spaced days.** day 9: mistakes-4 second; day 12: past-2 first; day 16: earn-1 second; day 21: T-mistakes third; day 28: far-1 first (only if steady; else mistakes-2); day 35: past-2 second; day 40: the aftermath check; L12 only if steady and they ask. **Between them:** The carry line when the verdict comes; one act of repair or good; no review of faults.

**Evening.** Line 3 matters most here (one thing that went right). Line 2 records the act, never the fault. **Bad day.** The shared rules, with card mistakes-2. **Day 7.** They can separate 'I did' from 'I am' in their own words at least once; one repair or one disclosure done. **Day 40.** Less hiding: they told someone, fixed something, or came back after a fall without a long gap. The verdict still visits; it is answered faster.

**Guards** (R03, R01, R10, R13). Never bittul, humility or 'the ego is the problem' (R03). Never reassure ('you're good inside') and never confirm (D05). No return (teshuvah) exercises for a scrupulous person (R01). Harm by a religious authority is R05/R13, not shame work.

**Evidence.** Tangney, Stuewig & Mashek 2007 [files] review: shame to hiding, guilt to repair; Wood, Perunovic & Lee 2009, Psychol Sci 20:860-866 [search] experiments: positive self statements backfire for low self-esteem (supports D05); Kirby, Tellegen & Steindl 2017, Behav Ther 48:778-792 [search] meta-analysis of compassion based interventions; Ferrari et al. 2019 [files] meta-analysis.

**Sources.** Pirkei Avot 2:13, as the Tanya reads it (Likkutei Amarim 1), line 69: ואל תהי רשע בפני עצמך (*do not be wicked in your own eyes*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 2, line 86: ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש (*the second soul in a Jew is truly a part of God above*); Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner), line 541: כי מי שמרגיש עצמו לריק ומנוול מתנהג כריק ומנוול (*whoever feels himself empty and vile behaves empty and vile*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26, line 378: כי אין הזמן גרמא כעת לעצבות אמיתית ... רק לזאת צריך קביעות עתים ושעת הכושר בישוב הדעת (*now is not the time for real sorrow ... for that one needs fixed times, a fitting hour, with a settled mind*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26, line 379: כי מיד אחר שנשבר לבו בעתים קבועים ההם, אזי יסיר העצב מלבו לגמרי (*right after his heart was broken at those fixed times, let him remove the sorrow from his heart completely*).

**Where they part.** Compassion approaches give the self kindness. The source says what is under the failure was never touched by it: a part of G-d above (to a Jew, LA 2), the image of G-d (to anyone, U01). The guide never says 'you're good inside' (that is reassurance, D05); it lets them test the split on their own sentence.

## 2.4 Grief

**For.** A death or a loss that keeps returning; stuck either away from it or inside it. **Not for.** The first days after a death, and shiva for a Jewish mourner: presence only, no program (SAFETY R07). Traumatic loss with danger signs: protocol. **Loop** (FORMULATION §3): `grief`. **Map:** from S14, S10 along C5 toward S55, S60. **Manual rungs:** L04, then L11 (only if they reach for the bond themselves) (Grief). **Healing methods:** grief, survivors (Chabad Library/hisbonenus/healing/).

| Day | Card | Time | Carry line | Act |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | NEW-grief-2 | first | He weeps with the one who weeps. | One act of love in their name: a kindness, a coin, a call to someone who also misses them. |
| 2 | NEW-grief-1 | first | Going to them. Coming back. | When you choose, a few minutes with them (a photo, a letter to them), then one ordinary act of the day. |
| 3 | torn-1 | first | Both are true. | Let one good moment stay good today without apology. |
| 4 | punished-3 | first | I can say all of it to Him. | Say the whole complaint to Him, out loud or on paper, in your words. |
| 5 | NEW-grief-1 | second | Going to them. Coming back. | One task of the new life (a form, a meal, a room) done as your own. |
| 6 | NEW-grief-2 | second | Not alone in this. | Be with one person for an hour, even without talking about it. |
| 7 | torn-1 | second | Both are true. | Rest day: light a candle or set a place in their memory, if that is your custom. |

**Spaced days.** day 9: punished-1 first (only if they asked why); day 12: NEW-grief-1 third; day 16: torn-1 third; day 21: their own words to Him; day 28: one act in their name; day 35: NEW-grief-2 third; day 40: no aftermath check of the grief; only whether the acts in their name continue. **Between them:** The carry line at the hardest hour; one act for the living or in their name.

**Evening.** Line 3 becomes 'one thing I did today in their name or for the living'. No grief score, ever. **Bad day.** The shared rules, with card NEW-grief-2. **Day 7.** Nothing about the grief. The only marker: they both went toward the loss and came back into the day at least once. **Day 40.** Nothing about the grief's size or end (R07: no timetable). Markers: the acts in their name have a place in the week; they can speak of the person and also of today. For a Jew, shloshim falls near here and their own customs lead.

**Guards** (R07, R04, R10). No reason for the death, no 'for the good', 'better place', 'descent for ascent' (R04, R07). Presence first; no contemplation in the first reply of any day (R07). No joy prescribed; no 'all is nothing'; no timetable (R07, R08). 'I want to be with them' is a danger check (R10).

**Evidence.** Stroebe & Schut 1999, Death Studies 23(3):197-224 [search] theory: oscillation between loss and restoration, with respite; Bonanno 2004, Am Psychol 59:20-28 [search] review: resilience is common; Shear, Frank, Houck & Reynolds 2005, JAMA 293:2601 [search] RCT for complicated grief (a clinical treatment; cited only for the loss/restoration structure, not as a model to copy).

**Sources.** Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner), line 585: ושם בוכה ומילל עמו כביכול יחד (*and there He weeps and wails with him, as it were, together*); Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (the Mitteler Rebbe), line 704: אז ישכון בו ממקור כל החיים מקורא דכולא להחיות רוח נדכאים (*then He dwells in him, from the source of all life, to revive the spirit of the crushed*); Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 34 (quoting the Zohar), line 492: בכיה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא, וחדוה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא (*weeping is lodged in my heart on one side, and joy on the other*).

**Where they part.** Grief research describes oscillation and warns against forced 'grief work'. The sources add company, not explanation: He weeps with the one who weeps (Esh Kodesh, line 585), and weeping and joy lodged in one heart (Tanya LA 34 quoting the Zohar, line 492). Neither gives a reason, and neither does the guide. The Tanya's fixed time for sorrow (LA 26) is about sin, not loss, and is not used here.

## 2.5 Loneliness

**For.** Alone even in a full room; nobody understands; nobody is coming. **Not for.** Isolation under control of another person (R05: protocol). ## 4. Where psychology and Chassidus part ways, across the tracks

- **Why the act.** Behavioral activation chooses acts for their effect on mood and measures success by mood. The sources count the act as the thing itself, "the deed in the hand" where the Essence is drawn down (map S81), whether or not the heart follows. The guide takes the schedule and the grading from the research and the act's worth from the sources, which also protects a person whose mood does not lift.
- **Why the fixed time.** The research postpones worry so it shrinks. The Tanya sets a time for heartbreak so that the rest of the day can be glad. The form is shared; the content is not carried across (see §1).
- **What counts as change.** Psychology measures symptoms. The sources measure the aftermath: an insult that stings less, one pull with less grip, the same person after the idle hours as after the prayer (manual). The tracks use only the second, and never a symptom scale, because the owner forbids grading feelings (D09, D25).
- **What the track is for.** A clinical program aims at remission. The sources aim at a person who finds, at the bottom of the trouble, that they were never outside what holds them (FORMULATION §6). The tracks never promise remission and never promise an experience (map R9).
- **Forty days.** The number comes from the manual's course and the tradition's love of forty, not from the evidence. The evidence says habits take longer and vary widely (Lally et al. 2010). Day 40 is a checkpoint.

## 5. WHAT THE GUIDE SHOULD DO

1. **Keep tracks in the engine, never on the page.** Test: no reply contains "program", "track", "day N of 40" or a list of future days unless the person asked.
2. **Offer once, in one sentence,** only when they ask how to keep it going, or return on a second day with the same accepted cycle, and only with the remember switch on. Test: at most one offer per conversation; none on a first message; none in a danger or bad-day turn.
3. **Every day ends in one act and one line.** The act is scheduled (a time or a cue), small, and built on a named strength when there is one; the line is at most eight words, the card's target in their words, tied to their trigger. Test: the integrate step of every program day contains a time or a cue.
4. **Give the evening lines once, as a closing, never as questions.** Test: no reply asks "did the line come back?" or "did you do the act?"; if they tell, the guide reads it into `program.carry_lines`.
5. **Bad days shrink, never restart.** Test: after a bad-day signal, the reply offers the bad-day card or the line alone, an act of one step, and no accounting, bittul or joy words; `program.day` is not reset.
6. **Run the go-back table.** Test: three evenings with no line coming back produce a rewritten line; three missed acts produce a halved act.
7. **Build SAFETY in.** Before each program day, check `formulation.risk.blocks`; drop any card whose tags are blocked and use the track's bad-day card instead. Test: no program day serves a blocked card.
8. **Grief has no clock.** Test: no grief-track reply contains a day count, "by now", "move on", "should be", or a reason for the death; no program runs during the first days after a death or during shiva.
9. **The pull: never advise on a substance or medicine; count turns, not falls.** Test: no pull-track reply tells them to stop or reduce a substance or medicine.
10. **Meaning: answer the question first.** Test: to "what's the point", the first reply gives the teaching with its reason at full depth (D13, D37, D39) and checks once for "I don't want to be here" before any act.
