## The shape of the range

This is the Zohar on the Shema. Vaetchanan holds its fullest teaching on how to say "Hear O Israel" and "You shall love." It also covers: - how to go to sleep, rise at midnight, and stand in prayer (4743-4752); - the mezuzah as G-d standing guard at the door (4812-4859); - the Evil Urge as a guest who becomes the master of the house (4891-4893).

Eikev turns to blessing over food and the ten things done at a meal (4939-5002). Ki Teitzei is almost all Raya Mehemna: - Moshe weeps and asks to be counted as a son, not a doer of favors (5142-5145); - he is told that he is buried outside the land for Israel's sake (5117-5122).

Vayeilech sets out the order of true praise, from below to above, from above to below, and then the knot (5215-5218). It also gives a long teaching on "Amen" (5219-5229). Ha'Azinu opens with "He is all," with the Queen who will not leave her exiled son, and with a book of a wise doctor (5262-5307).

## The unity of G-d

**1. "He" gathers all into One.** Moshe's song names G-d's qualities one after another: great, rock, just, faithful, righteous, upright. Then it closes with one word, "He" (הוא). The Zohar reads that word as the knot:

> הוא כלא. הוא חד בלא פרודא. דאי תימא כל הני סגיאין אינון, חזר ואמר הוא, כלהו סלקין ומתקשרן ומתאחדן בחד (Zohar, Ha'Azinu 8:1; line 5262)
> He is all. He is one without separation. If you say, "These are many," the verse comes back and says "He": they all rise, are tied, and are made one in One.

"And all is: He was, He is, He will be, and He is one" (line 5262). This is the same move the guide makes with the many names. They are real as names, and they rise into one.

**2. As He is made one below, so above.** The Faithful Shepherd gives the commandment of the Shema as a daily unifying of the Name:

> דהא כמה דמייחדי שמא דקודשא בריך הוא לתתא, הכי אתייחד שמיה לעילא. אשתכח קודשא בריך הוא יחידאי עילא ותתא (Zohar, Vaetchanan 9:1; line 4801)
> For as the Name of the Holy One is made one below, so His Name is made one above. The Holy One is found single above and below.

The person saying it "sets his heart and his will on that unity and joins all his limbs in that will, so that all of them are one" (line 4801). Then "all the limbs gather and are joined in one joining... above and below, in the secret of 'Hashem is One and His Name is One'" (line 4803).

**3. Two unities that become one.** "Shema" is one unity and "Blessed be the Name" is another. When each is whole they join, and this is "Hashem, He is G-d" (4818-4823). The Zohar says that this is "all a person needs to know in this world": "You were shown, to know" (line 4823). *Ours:* this is close to the upper and lower unity (yichuda ila'ah and yichuda tata'ah) of Tanya and Shaar Hayichud.

**4. Everything stands on love.** The letters of the Name hold on to one another in love, "like groom and bride":

> היכלא דא, אהבה אתקרי, ובגין אהבה קיימא כלא (Zohar, Vaetchanan 21:11; line 4886)
> This chamber is called Love, and because of love everything stands.

"The yud never parts from the tip above it... because love rests on it." The letters are joined "in love, one with the other." And so, "whoever loves the King is bound in that love" (line 4887). Love here is not only a feeling in us. It is how the Name itself holds together.

**5. Written one way, read another, and not changed.** "Not as I am written am I read": the Name is written YHVH and read Adonai (Lord). "I Hashem have not changed" is true of the writing, "which is its place," while the reading, "outside its place," changes (line 5138). *Ours:* this fits the Idra's "from our side" (part 6). What changes is only how He is read by us.

**6. The heart sees; the eye does not picture.** Prophecy has "many visions, likenesses and sights, and all are known in the eye of the mind of the heart":

> וכלא אשתמודע בעין השכל דלבא, דאתמר בה הלב יודע הלב מבין (Zohar, Ki Teitzei 18:6; line 5128)
> All is known in the eye of the mind of the heart, of which it is said: the heart knows, the heart understands.

"Likeness is not in the image of the eye, but in the mind of the heart," because "To whom will you liken Me?" (line 5128). True sight is "like light shining in the pupil of the eye," the dark center lit from within: "I am black and lovely" (line 5129). *Ours:* this is an early warning against picturing G-d, and an early form of Chabad's "understanding of the heart."

**7. He does not leave them.** Rabbi Yehudah: "I have looked in many places, and the Holy One never removed His love from Israel. Wherever they were, He was among them" (line 5274). Then comes the parable of the Queen (see States below).

## States of consciousness

- **The Queen who goes into exile with her son.** A king is angry with his son and sends him to a far land. The Queen says:

> אנא לא אשבוק ליה, או תרוונא כחדא ניתוב להיכלא דמלכא או תרוונא כחדא ניתיב בארעא אחרא (Zohar, Ha'Azinu 10:3; line 5270)
> I will not leave him. Either we both return together to the King's palace, or we both live together in the other land.

When the king asks for the Queen and she is not there, he says: "Since the Queen is there, both will return" (line 5270). "Who causes Me to hear their trouble? The Queen, whom I remember" (line 5271). Maps to S86 (the other held) and S63. *Proposed state: accompanied in exile.* *Ours:* for someone who feels sent away by G-d, this says the Presence went with him, and that his return is tied to hers.

- **He wanders until she comes home.** A soul is sent into this world "naked," like "a bird wandering from its nest," and "the Shechinah does not move from her." The Holy One too "wanders from His place... and He swears He will not return to His place until He returns her to her place." Whoever returns in teshuvah (return to G-d) "is like one who returns the Holy One and His Shechinah to their place" (line 5084). Maps to S77 and S78.

- **Awake at midnight with the Queen.** At night a person accepts the yoke of the Kingdom "with a whole heart" and first hands over "the deposit of his soul" (לאקדמי למימסר גביה פקדונא דנפשיה, line 4743). Those who rise at midnight join her until day. In the morning a person "arms himself" and comes "with the Queen, to join her with the King" (line 4746). Maps to S76.

- **Clear-minded before the Face.** Other prophets fell on their faces and "went out of their minds." Moshe stood face to face, "head raised without fear," and did not lose himself:

> מיד ושב אל המחנה, למללא עמהון בכל מה דאצטריכאן, ודעתוי מתישבא ביה כקדמיתא, ויתיר (Zohar, Vaetchanan 27:3; line 4906)
> At once he "returned to the camp," to speak with them about all they needed, and his mind was settled in him as before, and more.

Maps to the return current (S68 area). *Proposed state: the settled return.* *Ours:* this is the Zohar's clearest statement that the highest sight leaves a person more settled, not less. It ties to the Idra's "entered and came out."

- **The smile of one who has seen.** Rabbi Yosi, sick, wakes with a smiling face. He saw the reward of those who gave themselves for G-d, and of those who loved their Master. "The highest dwelling of all belongs to those whose love of their Master is bound in them" (ומדורא עלאה דכלא, אינון דרחימותא דמאריהון אתקשר בהו, line 4885). Maps to S41-S46.

- **A son, not a doer of favors.** A chasid (pious one) is "one who does kindness with his Maker," working to redeem the Shechinah (line 5142). But Elijah tells Moshe that his work is more than that: he is "a son obliged to give himself" for his father and mother (line 5144). Moshe bows, weeps, and asks:

> ואף על גב דידענא דכלא ברשותיה, רחמנא לבא בעי (Zohar, Ki Teitzei 22:4; line 5145)
> And though I know that all is in His power, the Merciful One wants the heart.

He asks to be counted as a son "who loves them more than himself... and counts all he has as nothing." G-d comes and kisses him: "Surely you are My son" (line 5145). Maps to S44 (love like a son). *Proposed state: the son who counts himself as nothing.*

- **Not punished for what the will did not choose.** "Whoever does a thing, and does not set his heart and will to the side of evil, though it is bad, since he did not do it by will, punishment does not rest on him" (line 4769). Near S05. A useful comfort for those who condemn themselves for what happened to them.

- **The poor man whose mind has left him.** "Poor" includes one "whose mind was taken from him, like Job," who has "no sin for the words he says" (line 4993). And "all blessings and prayers wait wrapped" until the prayer of the poor goes up (line 4997). Maps to S86.

## Meditation and practice

- **The order of praise: up, down, then the knot.** This is the range's central method:

> במלין ובעובדא הכי … נמי מתתא לעילא, ומעילא לתתא. ולבתר לכוונא בלבא, ולקשרא כלא בחד קשרא (Zohar, Vayeilech 5:8; line 5215)
> In words and in deed, so too: from below to above, and from above to below. Then to aim it in the heart, and to tie all in one knot.

Moshe did this. "I call the name of Hashem" rises to the Shechinah. "Give greatness to our G-d" rises to the King above. "Righteous and upright" comes down through the levels. "He" ties the knot of faith (line 5216). "So a person should order his Master's praise": first raise it "to the place of the deep well," then draw it down "to every level, to the last," then tie the knot (line 5217). Ties to `before_whom`, `walk_down` and `binding`. *Proposed technique: up, down, and the knot.*

- **Unifying with all the limbs.** When saying Shema, put heart and will on the unity "and join all his limbs in that will, so that all of them are one" (line 4801). Lengthen "One." Ties to `binding` and `details`. *Proposed technique: gathering the limbs into One.*

- **Before you sleep, hand over the soul.** Accept the Kingdom with a whole heart and deposit the soul (line 4743). At the end of prayer, "hand over his soul with the will of the heart" (למימסר נפשיה ברעותא דלבא, line 4749). Then the words "rise, split the air and the heavens," and become a crown on the King's head (line 4749). Ties to the earlier "nightly handing over."

- **How to stand.** Feet together, head covered "as one who stands before a king," eyes lowered. "Looking at the Shechinah" means only this: "to know that surely the Shechinah stands before him" (אלא לינדע דודאי שכינתא קיימא קמיה, line 4751). Hezekiah turned to the wall "because there the Shechinah rests," so nothing should stand between a person and the wall (line 4751). Praise comes first, then the request (line 4752). Ties to `before_whom` and `arrive`.

- **Rising: thanks for the soul given back.** On rising, "give praise to his Master who returned his soul," for he was "found in many debts before his Master, and the Holy One did kindness with him and returned it to his body" (line 4912). In the house, lead the family "gently and in joy," without too much fear (line 4911). Ties to `heart_after`.

- **Blessing as drawing life.** Blessing over food "draws life from the source of life." The one who blesses "takes his share first." G-d "desires those who bless Him, and His longing is for the blessing from below" (ותיאובתיה בברכתא דלתתא, line 4945). Bow at "Blessed" (the hidden source), bow the head at "You" (the start of the revealed) (4948-4954). Ties to `lips` and `act`. *Ours:* not blessing is called robbing one's Father (line 4939).

- **Answering Amen.** "Greater is he who answers Amen than the one who blesses." Amen draws blessing "from the spring to the King, and from the King to the Queen" (line 5219). Whoever waits for the blessing in order to answer Amen with intent opens gates above. When he is in trouble, "open the gates," and his prayer is received. At his death "open the gates before him, as he opened gates every day" (5220-5221). Ties to `lips`.

- **Sharpening words; leading yourself.** "Sharpen them to your children" means making Torah keen "like a sword sharp on both sides." "And speak of them" means "lead yourself by them," turning neither right nor left (line 4910). Ties to `chazarah`.

- **Staying at the table for the poor.** Lengthen the meal so the poor may come. "The poor man is counted as dead, and he gives him life; so the Holy One gives him life" (line 4989). Ties to `act`.

- **Prayer from the walls of the heart.** "Who testifies about a person? The walls of his house," read as "the walls of his heart": "Hezekiah prayed from the walls of his heart" (line 5024). Ties to `own_words`.

## The big human questions

- **Why does the Evil Urge take over?** It comes first as a passer-by who does not mean to stay. Then it is a guest for a day or two. Then, "since no one stops his hand," it becomes "the man, the master of the house" (line 4892). *Ours:* the lesson is early refusal. The only thing that breaks it is words of Torah "on your heart," meaning on both inclinations: "the good is crowned by them, the evil is humbled" (line 4893).

- **Does G-d go with us into our worst places?** Yes. "Though they caused themselves to be exiled among the nations, the Shechinah never left them" (line 5269). "Can a woman forget her nursing child?" The answer is that Israel is "held in His Name literally." As He does not forget His Name, He does not forget them (line 5285).

- **What should a doctor do?** The healer is not to say "G-d has seized him, leave him" (line 5302). He must study the illness, guard the sick person "like the pupil of the eye," and not err "even in one word." If he cannot heal the body, he should give "healing to the soul" (line 5306). A doctor of old paid for the medicines from his own pocket, and "did more by his prayer than by his hands" (line 5307).

- **Must a person learn only for pure motives?** No. "A person should always busy himself with Torah even not for its own sake, for from not-for-its-sake comes for-its-sake" (line 5063).

- **What makes a quarrel holy?** Scholars who argue as equals find "love in the end" (line 5096).

- **What of the great one who dies outside his place?** Moshe is buried outside the land "for Israel's sake." Like the sun that sets and still lights the moon and the stars, he lights all the masters of law and Kabbalah, "watering the trees under their roots in hiding" (5117-5122). "Though you lie with your fathers, you stand always to give light" (line 5197).

## For the guide (ours)

1. Lines 5215-5217 give a clear, teachable order for any praise or prayer: rise from below, draw down from above, then tie it in one knot, "He." This should be a named technique.
2. "He is all, one without separation" (line 5262) is a short, strong sentence for the unity layer. It sits with the Idra's "all is one" (part 6) and with Pinchas's "before, within and after" (part 10).
3. The Queen who goes into exile with her son (line 5270) is a picture the guide can hand to someone who feels far from G-d. The Presence did not stay behind.
4. Moshe's settled return (line 4906) and "the Merciful One wants the heart" (line 5145) belong with the map's return current and with love like a son.
5. "In the eye of the mind of the heart" (line 5128) is an ancient source for Chabad's point that the heart must understand, and that G-d is not to be pictured.
6. The Evil Urge as passer-by, guest and master (line 4892) is plain enough for anyone, and it points to a clear practice: stop it at the door.
