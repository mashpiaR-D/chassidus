The Addenda (hashmatot, "omissions") are pieces that printers gathered at the end of Volume I: short Zohar passages on Genesis that were missing from the main text, passages from the Ra'aya Mehemna ("Faithful Shepherd") layer, pieces marked as Sitrei Torah and Zohar Yashan, and some Hebrew fragments drawn from the Sefer HaBahir and a Midrash on Ruth. Much of it is dense sefirotic mapping of the days of creation and the Temple offerings. Among it are clear teachings: "there is no God but Him" and the Cause of all is not lacking in any level; the ladder of Jacob read as prayer; a mitzvah without thought does not rise; how long to prolong the "One" of the Shema; the shofar moving God from the throne of judgement to the throne of mercy; God's rebuke of Noah, "foolish shepherd"; Rabbi Shimon holding back a destroying angel; and "whoever sanctifies himself below is sanctified above." All 391 lines were read in full. Every quote below printed "FOUND near line N" from `find_he.py verify` on the export. A "…" in a quote skips a printed cross-reference or variant in brackets. Ours: because these are gathered fragments of mixed layers, the guide should cite them as "Zohar, Addenda" and not build a teaching on one fragment alone.

## 2. The unity of God

**The Cause of all, no God but Him, not lacking in any.** In a Ra'aya Mehemna passage: the Shechinah is "the image of all," in which He looked and created the world, «וכלל בה עלאין ותתאין בלא פירודא כלל … ועלת כל כלא דאיהו אדון על כלא ולית אלהא בר מניה ולא אשתכח בכל עלאין ותתאין פחות מיניה» (Addenda, Volume I 4:3; line 5302): "and He included in it the upper and lower with no separation at all … and the Cause of all, who is Lord over all, and there is no God besides Him, and He is not found less in any of the upper and lower beings." Ours: a direct source for the Chabad teaching that He is equally present in every level, with no place empty of Him (Tanya ch. 48, Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; memory). Ties: S62, F02.

**Reward and punishment from Him alone.** «וביה תלייא עונשיה או אגריה ולא במלאך ושרף ולא בשום בריה דעלמא» (Addenda, Volume I 4:4; line 5303): "on Him depends his punishment or his reward, not on angel or seraph or any creature of the world"; so "whoever joins the name of Heaven with another thing is uprooted from the world." Ties: S85.

**Evening and morning, one day: unity above and below.** When darkness and light joined, "He makes peace": «כדין יחודא לעילא יחודא לתתא שלמא לעילא שלמא לתתא» (Addenda, Volume I 3:11; line 5284): "then unity above, unity below, peace above, peace below."

**Havayah is in every sefirah; prayer rises to Him everywhere.** To the question why we pray at different levels: «אלא ודאי יהו"ה איהו בכל ספירה וספירה וצלותא איהי שכינתא … וכולא לגבי יהו"ה דאיהו בכל אתר» (Addenda, Volume I 6:8; line 5333): "rather, surely Havayah is in each and every sefirah, and prayer is the Shechinah … and all goes to Havayah, who is in every place." Ours: the Zohar's own guard against praying "to a sefirah"; one addresses Havayah, who is in all.

**His Name receives no impurity.** The shells surround the kernel, but «דא יהו"ה דאיהו לא קביל מסאבו … מה אשא לא מקבל טומאה הכי שמיה לא מקבל טומאה» (Addenda, Volume I 29:3; line 5550): "this is Havayah, who does not receive impurity … as fire does not receive impurity, so His Name does not receive impurity." Ties: Tanya ch. 6-7 on the shells and the Light that is not touched (memory).

**The etrog: with each and with all.** The etrog stands apart from the bound lulav yet the mitzvah stands only through it: «והוא גם כן אגוד עם הכל. שעם כל אחד הוא ועם כולן יחד הוא» (Addenda, Volume I 53:3; line 5655): "and it is also bound with all; for it is with each one, and it is with all of them together." Ours: a small, plain image of how the One is with each thing and with all at once.

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **If I am here, all is here.** Hillel at the water-drawing joy: «אי שכינתא דאקרי אני שרייא כאן. הכל כאן» (Addenda, Volume I 25:93; line 5527): "if the Shechinah, called 'I', dwells here, all is here." Ties: S62; S76.
- **Glad in one's portion.** Of the King's treasure of silver on the right: «ואם שמח אדם בחלקו טוב» (Addenda, Volume I 52:1; line 5649): "and if a person rejoices in his portion, good." Ties: S65, S84.
- **In the seventh year, rich and poor alike.** «וכולהו מסכני ועתירי כולהו שוין בהאי אתר» (Addenda, Volume I 25:99; line 5533): "and all the poor and the rich are all equal in this place." Ties: S86.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Prayer is the ladder, and He unites in the one who prays.** "And behold a ladder set on the earth": «והנה סולם דא צלותא» (Addenda, Volume I 50:1; line 5630): "'and behold a ladder': this is prayer," set on earth where people pray, reaching to heaven. The angels open their wings to carry the Shechinah up in that prayer, and He comes down «ומייחד קודשא בריך הוא ביה בההוא בר נש בצלותיה» (Addenda, Volume I 50:2; line 5631): "and the Holy One unites in that very person in his prayer." Ties: technique 2; Vayetzei's ladder.

**A mitzvah without thought does not rise.** «ומצוה בלא מחשבה לית לה סליקו» (Addenda, Volume I 50:3; line 5632): "and a mitzvah without thought has no rising." Ties: Vayetzei "Good thought upward"; Tanya ch. 38-39 on intention as the wings of a mitzvah (memory).

**The Shema's "One": how long to prolong it.** At "One": aim with the aleph at the hidden Ancient One, with the chet at eight levels, and with the large dalet at the Assembly of Israel joined to them (line 5401). How long? «כשיעורא דימליך יתה לעילא ולתתא ולארבע זוויין דעלמא» (Addenda, Volume I 23:9; line 5406): "as long as it takes to crown Him above and below and in the four corners of the world"; and whoever does so, «כל אינון יומין ושנין עלאין מוסרין ברכאן על רישיה» (same line): "all the higher days and years hand blessings onto his head." Ties: technique 2; Vayetzei line 3278.

**The first in the synagogue.** «ההוא קדמאה דאשתכח בבי כנישתא … זכאה חולקיה דאיהו קיימא בדרגא דצדיק בהדי שכינתא» (Addenda, Volume I 22:4; line 5394): "the first one found in the synagogue … happy is his portion, for he stands at the level of the righteous with the Shechinah." And the one who talks in the synagogue «דאחזי פרודא» (Addenda, Volume I 22:6; line 5396), "shows separation."

**The shofar moves Him to the throne of mercy.** «וקודשא בריך הוא קם מכורסייא דדינא ויתיב בכורסייא דרחמי ורחים עלייהו דישראל» (Addenda, Volume I 10:3; line 5356): "and the Holy One rises from the throne of judgement and sits on the throne of mercy and has mercy on Israel," when they sit before Him in return and blow the shofar.

**Sanctify yourself below.** «מאן דמסאב גרמיה לתתא, מסאב לעילא. ומאן דקדיש גרמיה לתתא מקדש לעילא» (Addenda, Volume I 25:60; line 5494): "whoever defiles himself below is defiled above; and whoever sanctifies himself below is sanctified from above." Ties: technique 17.

**Speak good of His children.** God rebuked Noah after the flood: «רעיא שטייא … כל האי אתעכבית ואמרית לך בגין דתבעי רחמין על עלמא» (Addenda, Volume I 12:1; line 5360): "foolish shepherd … all this time I waited and told you, so that you would ask mercy for the world." And «בגין דניחא ליה לקודשא בריך הוא, מאן דיימר טבא על בנוי» (Addenda, Volume I 12:4; line 5363): "for it pleases the Holy One when one speaks good of His children," as Gideon was sent "with this your strength" because he spoke well of Israel. Ties: "Pleading for another" (Noach).

## 5. Big human questions

- **Can one person hold up the world?** A destroying angel came because there were not thirty righteous; Rabbi Shimon told it: if not thirty, twenty; if not twenty, ten; if not ten, two, «דאינון אנא וברי»; «ואי לית תרין, הא אית חד ואנא הוא» (Addenda, Volume I 17:4; line 5378): "and if there are not two, there is one, and I am he"; and a voice said God decrees above and he annuls below. Ours: never think the world does not depend on you. Ties: S86.
- **Can Torah harm?** «אם זכה בר נש, איהי סם חיים דיליה. ואם לאו, היא מתהפכת ליה לסם המות» (Addenda, Volume I 30:6; line 5565): "if a person merits, it is his elixir of life; if not, it turns into a poison of death for him," like the staff that turned into a snake.
- **Why be made small?** The moon was told "go and make yourself small"; Rabbi Elazar's reading: «לכי ומעטי את עצמך והשתעבדי בגלותא למזכי לך בעלמא דאתי» (Addenda, Volume I 3:22; line 5295): "go and make yourself small and be subjugated in exile, so as to merit in the world to come." Ties: S59, S73.
- **Is argument in Torah a war?** Torah scholars who hear a matter that does not settle in their hearts «מגיחי קרבא דין עם דין» (Addenda, Volume I 3:20; line 5293), "wage war with one another," like swordsmen; and the dispute of Hillel and Shammai is "for the sake of Heaven" (line 5308). Ties: "Dispute of love" (Bereshit I).
- **What is the hidden light for?** The first light was hidden for the righteous; «פעלת לחוסים בך, אותם שחוסים בצלך בעולם הזה … ומייחדים בסתר ובגלוי» (Addenda, Volume I 37:5; line 5583): "'You have made for those who trust in You': those who shelter in Your shade in this world … and unify in secret and in the open."

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "Crowning in the One."** At "echad" of the Shema, hold the word until you have crowned Him above, below, east, west, north, south; then let the dalet be the lowly place that receives it all (lines 5401-5406). Ties technique 2, 18.
- **State, new: "All is here."** Inside: the sense that where His Presence rests, nothing is missing; "if I am here, all is here" (line 5527). How to enter: in a moment of joy in a mitzvah, say it. Near S62, S76.
- **Technique, new: "Speak well of His children."** Before asking anything for yourself, say something good about the people around you before God; it pleases Him and gives strength (lines 5360-5363). Pairs with "Pleading for another" (Noach). Ties S86.
- **Technique, new: "The one who holds."** When a group, a family or a place seems lost, take on in your heart that you are the one it rests on today, and act and pray accordingly (line 5378). Guard: responsibility, not pride. Ties S86.
