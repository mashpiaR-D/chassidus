*Unity Index, the Imrei Binah part: finished 2026-09-29. Imrei Binah ("Sayings of Understanding") is the Mitteler Rebbe's long commentary on the Shema, the tzitzit and the tefillin, printed in 1821. Every line of the text file (603 lines, most of them one printed column of 2,000 to 5,800 characters) was read and asked one question: how does this line express the unity of God? The seven detailed reports are in `imrei-binah/` (listed at the end). Every Hebrew line quoted below was checked with `find_he.py verify` at the line given, and checked again, letters only, to sit on that line itself. Comparisons with the Tanya were checked against the Tanya text the Tanya pass reads (`Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, cited as "Tanya line N").*

## The short answer

The Gate of Unity walks the unity down the chain of worlds, rung by rung. Imrei Binah takes one verse, "Hear, O Israel, Havayah is our God, Havayah is One", and asks how a person saying it makes the unity happen. The book names its subject in its first lines. The Alter Rebbe's whole teaching, the author says, was to fix "the simple unity of God, which is the Essence of the Infinite", in the mind and heart of every Jew (line 3). Then it keeps to that subject for 600 columns. Its answer: **God is yachid, alone, above every "one"; and yet the lowest unity, the world of time and place and the physical mitzvah, reaches Him more than the higher one does, because the end holds the beginning. What reaches Him is not love, which is a "something", but bitul, becoming nothing.**

A line here carries three faces on average, against 1.6 in the Tanya. The Essence leads, as in both Gates. What is new is how close together the other faces run, and which come next: Torah and mitzvah, and opposites joined. This is a book about the Shema, tzitzit and tefillin, so the mitzvah is always at hand. Its favourite phrase is "a thing and its opposite in one subject", so F09 is everywhere. No face is rare. Even the least common, knowing, is on 90 lines.

## How the book builds it, in five steps

**1. The charter (lines 1-8: the author's introduction).** The Alter Rebbe taught only to fix the simple unity, the Essence, in every mind and heart (line 3). The Shema's content is the higher and lower unity: Havayah and Elokim, sovev and memale, the Shema and Baruch Shem. The point of the Shema is that faith above knowing should come into knowing (line 4), and to make the Shema faith alone is "the complete opposite of the truth" (line 5). The kabbalists' many intentions are set aside for one: to bind the soul to His very Essence (line 6). And the unity dwells only where a person does not feel himself at all (line 6).

**2. The first verse, from every side (lines 9-91: Petach HaShaar).** The Shema is read two ways, as ascent (the attributes included in their source) and as descent ("make Him king in heaven and earth"), and "both unities are one matter" (line 11). The Ark stood in a measured room and took no space. Where the Essence itself comes into a bound, the paradox of limit and no limit is gone: "there, these are not two opposites at all" (line 25). Here the book first names the level "Yachid, which is above Echad" (line 32). It also makes its boldest claim early: the lower unity, the letters in each creature, "has a higher root" than the higher unity (lines 70, 72). The four cubits of halacha are His own inner palace (line 77).

**3. Echad and yachid, and why the lower is higher (lines 92-265: Shaar Kriat Shema to §45).** "Echad" is a counted one; "yachid" has no second and no number (lines 120-122). The first Havayah of the Shema is yachid, the Essence before the tzimtzum; the second is echad (line 124). Israel is the intermediary between them (line 129), so after the tzimtzum "there is no change at all between yachid in His Essence and the oneness of God in the worlds" (line 132), and "no difference at all between yachid and echad" (line 150). Then the argument turns. "Baruch Shem", the lower unity in time, has "an extra advantage" over the first verse (line 183). The earth, lowest of all, has the power to create from nothing because the end holds the power of the beginning (line 179). Bare acceptance of the yoke reaches further than understood bitul (line 187). Contemplating how heaven and earth come from nothing "is as if one contemplates the very Essence before emanation" (line 191).

**4. Running and returning, and the letters of "echad" (lines 266-465: to the end of the Shema gate).** Rising toward Him ("running") is only for the sake of coming back down ("returning"): "the return is the main thing" (line 283). The tzimtzum is not judgment but "the essence of kindness" (line 287). Wisdom is oil, "separate from everything and drawn into everything" (line 306). The Infinite rests only in bitul (line 350). The surrounding light is in this world "as in Atzilut itself" now, only hidden (line 354). In the Essence ascent and descent are one (line 418). Then study and deed: which holds more of the Essence? The answer moves. The mitzvah's simple will is higher; study reaches it anyway, because wisdom is "nothing" (line 455); and in the future the mitzvot are the Essence itself, not a vessel (line 464).

**5. Tzitzit and tefillin: turning, and the two seals (lines 466-603).** Tzitzit teach two ways with evil: removing it and turning it. The turning is "the foundation of all Torah and mitzvot" (line 513), and true teshuvah "makes good out of the evil itself" (line 514). The tefillin are read through "Set me as a seal upon Your heart". Love from below is a raised seal, a "something", and it draws down light clothed in vessels. Bitul, the soul become "like a silent stone", is a sunken seal, and it draws down the Essence itself (lines 571-574). The world to come has one vessel only, the physical deed (line 564). The file ends in mid-argument, on the Amidah as "a taste of the future" (line 603).

## What it comes to, for a person

- **One intention.** Not many kavanot and levels, but one: to bind yourself to Him Himself (line 6). The book says God "does not want these, only one intention".
- **Faith is meant to become knowing.** Do not park the unity in faith and walk away. The Shema is where what is above the mind comes into the mind (line 4). Start with the simplest thing: how heaven and earth come from nothing. That, the book says, is as good as contemplating the Essence (line 191).
- **The low place is the high place.** The ordinary, bounded, physical world, the yoke taken on without understanding, the deed done with the body: these reach further than the lit-up moment (lines 179, 183, 187, 564).
- **Love is a door; bitul is the room.** Excitement and love are real and good, but they are a "something", and they bring down something clothed. What brings Him down as He is, is becoming nothing before Him (lines 572, 574). In the Amidah the body bows, and that is higher than the bitul of mind and heart (line 562).
- **The dark is not wasted.** A soul in a body weeps at its distance, and that weeping reaches deeper than the pleasure it had at its source (line 185). Teshuvah does not only push evil away. It makes the evil itself good (line 514).
- **Feel your own darkness.** "The more one recognizes his Creator", the finer the evil he feels in himself (line 477). And be careful: many take natural heat for the flame of God, "and many err in this" (line 517).

## What it adds to the Tanya, the two Gates and Sefer HaArachim

**Yachid and echad, argued in full.** The Gate of Unity ranks yachid above echad in a few lines (Gate lines 493-494). Strashelye draws the same line, and then says even yachid is said only from our side (Sha'arei HaYichud line 447). Sefer HaArachim says the Shema says "echad", not "yachid", on purpose, and that the highest unity is the world's existence one with Him (Sefer HaArachim lines 749, 1205). Imrei Binah argues the question across hundreds of lines, and holds both ends.

- Yachid is above echad. The level before the tzimtzum is "Yachid, above Echad" (line 32). Echad is a counted one; yachid has no partner and no number (line 120). The first Havayah of the Shema is yachid (line 124). Of what has no second one says "not unity but yachid" (line 275). - Yet the two are not two. Israel is "the intermediary between yachid and echad" (line 129), so there is "no change at all" between yachid in His Essence and His oneness in the worlds (line 132), and "no difference at all" between them (line 150). One who gives his soul into the echad is thereby given into the yachid above it (line 126). - Yachid has a consequence. The Essence cannot even be called "holy", since holiness means set apart from something, and He is alone, "with no fellow to be related to" (line 526). Israel is "one nation on earth, as He is yachid" (line 565).

This is close to Sefer HaArachim's point that the Shema's echad is a higher unity than a yachid that leaves the world outside. Imrei Binah gets there by another road: the world's echad equals the Essence's yachid, and Israel is what makes them equal. Neither book says the other's words. The closeness is our reading.

**The lower unity is higher.** The Tanya treats the higher unity as the deeper view: the worlds are nullified before their source "like the light of the sun" (Tanya line 802). The Gate of Unity says the roots of the highest world and ours are "exactly equal" (Gate line 2653). Imrei Binah goes a step further and says so outright. The lower unity's root is higher (line 70). "The main unity is in Baruch Shem" (line 157). The command to know is precisely "Havayah is Elokim", God known through His works (line 158). Baruch Shem has an extra advantage now, more in exile than in Temple times, and it will stand above the higher unity in the future (lines 183, 186, 231). The reason is the end that holds the beginning (line 179): what comes lowest carries the first creating power, and "the bound itself becomes unbounded, which can only be from the Essence" (line 245).

**Where the tzimtzum's strength lies.** The Tanya's Gate of Unity calls God's power to hide "His mighty acts", הן הן גבורותיו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, אשר כל יכול, לצמצם החיות והרוחניות (Tanya line 809). Imrei Binah takes the same words and pushes them: והן הן גבורותיו שיוכל לצמצם ולהסתיר לגמרי כדי שיהי' בריאה יש (line 162). The complete hiding of the second day is higher than the first kindness, and it is why the lower unity surpasses the higher. The tzimtzum is "the essence of kindness" (line 287), and in the future the hiding itself will shine, "with an advantage over the light itself" (lines 38, 40).

**Bitul draws the Essence; love draws garments.** The Tanya builds service on love and awe from contemplation, and says holiness rests on what is nullified. Imrei Binah ranks them by what they bring down. "Spirit brings spirit, just like it" (line 571). Love from below is a "something", so the light it draws comes in vessels: every positive mitzvah now is a garment, "and all this comes through love, the running" (line 574). Bitul in existence, "like a silent stone, with no feeling of himself" (line 572), draws "the light of the Essence as it is" (line 574). The Gate of Unity asked for joy and bitul in equal measure (Gate line 199). Imrei Binah puts bitul alone at the top, and adds that the servant's bitul from awe reaches the Essence "more than the soul's nearness in the most wondrous love and cleaving" (line 424).

**The deed is the Essence's only vessel.** The Tanya makes this world the purpose of creation (Tanya line 506). Imrei Binah says the future revelation of the Essence "has no vessel except the body of the physical deed of the mitzvah below" (line 564), and that then the mitzvot are "the Essence itself, not a receiving vessel" (line 464). Godliness will be revealed "below more than above" (line 439).

**Smaller additions.** - A new east each day: space itself is renewed, not only things (line 64). - The kabbalists' unions of the Shema "do not fulfil the true unity", which is in the Essence (line 43). - The four cubits of halacha are His "inner, essential palace" (line 77), where the world is not His place. The Tanya has the four cubits as His only dwelling since the Temple fell (Tanya line 759). - The hidden fills all equally because it is hidden from all equally (line 494). - A rule for hiddenness: the higher the source, the more hidden and leaping it comes (line 586). - Before the Essence all ten sefirot are equal, each coming from it directly (line 589). - God is called Peace because only the Essence joins far and near, love and fear (line 260). - Oil "is separate from everything and drawn into everything, because its drawing is hidden" (line 306), close to the SPINE's own thesis sentence.

## Where it differs from the Tanya (kept as disagreements)

Each Tanya passage below was checked at its line.

- **Faith or knowing?** The Tanya ends Shaar HaYichud 7 with what is "only to be believed, with a faith above intellect and grasp" (Tanya line 834). Imrei Binah says the whole purpose of the Shema is to bring faith above knowing into knowing (line 4). The two passages speak of different things (His knowledge in the Tanya, sovev here), but the pull is the other way.
- **The soul-body analogy.** The Tanya says the analogy fails at the root, because soul and body are really separate there (Tanya line 821). Imrei Binah uses it openly: the soul in the body models memale, and the soul's essence untouched by the body models sovev (lines 83-85).
- **The craftsman.** The Tanya rejects the craftsman picture: once the vessel leaves the smith it needs him no more (Tanya line 792). Imrei Binah uses the craftsman and the thrown stone at length (lines 197-222). It agrees in substance (the spheres' very bodies are made from nothing, line 199), then goes further the other way: a tzaddik can recognize the maker's essence in any tool (line 222).
- **Mind and heart.** The Tanya calls Chochmah, Binah and Da'at "the mothers and source" of the middot (Tanya line 103). Imrei Binah argues the reverse at length: the middot have their being "far above the intellect", and intellect is only a passage and a lamp (lines 593-595).
- **Fear and love.** The Tanya says one must first awaken the natural fear (Tanya line 598), and that the great love comes as a gift to one "complete in fear" (Tanya line 651). Imrei Binah agrees that the lower fear is the root (line 187). But it puts the servant's bitul above wondrous love (line 424), while in the Shema love is "the main service" (lines 261-262). Both are in the text.
- **Is creation renewed from nothing daily?** The Tanya's Gate of Unity rests everything on continuous creation. Imrei Binah says the heavens' daily renewal "is not new at root", only renewed light, and that true renewal from nothing is in the earth (line 176). Yet line 170 calls the fulfilled will the cause of the daily renewal of creation. The text does not reconcile them.
- **Sin and Sheol.** Sheol is never wholly separate from Godliness (line 541), yet sin is "separated in essence entirely from His unity" (line 542). Here the two books agree: the Tanya calls the garments of sin "worse and lower" than the kelipah itself (Tanya line 340). The tension with "no place is empty of Him" is left as both texts leave it.

## Where it differs from the Gate of Unity (the same author)

- **The advantage of light from darkness.** The Gate of Unity says light in darkness only looks brighter, "not true brightness from its own side" (Gate line 2414). Imrei Binah calls the lower unity's advantage "as light excels from darkness, a real advantage over the light of the Essence" (line 160), and praises the Torah's light for breaking out of the cloud (line 320). Neither book retracts the other. They may speak of different hidings; neither says so.
- **Hidden things.** The Gate of Unity asks everyone to contemplate the Essence before the tzimtzum, "there too in detail" (Gate line 489). Imrei Binah says there is a secret that will never be revealed, and that one must not seek it even for oneself, "for His glory is to be hidden" (line 302). Here the Mitteler Rebbe stands nearer the Tanya's reserve.

## Where the thread is thin (kept honest)

- **Thirteen lines have no tie:** the headings of the gates (1, 2, 9, 10, 92, 466, 546), the signature (8), the colophons (91, 465), two technical notes (212 on the "Asiyah of speech", 293 on the fifty gates) and a closing half-line (321).
- **Letter-maps and anatomy.** The heart and diaphragm (94-100), the geometry of place (15-20), the craftsman and the absent-minded doer (192-215), the shape of the aleph (335), the counts of threads and words, and the Shabbat ascent of worlds (577-578). The unity is why they are there, and the text says so at the end of each section, but most sentences do not. They are marked structural, and 45 lines carry no face.
- **The soul as analogy.** As in the Gate reports, F06 is left off lines where the soul only explains God, and kept where the text speaks of the actual souls of Israel.
- **Five lines, and 15 sentences, are our reading.** The line-level ones are 19 (a teacher's influence "as above" read as the Essence below), 203-204 (dreams as the parable of exile; the teacher contracting as the tzimtzum), 299 (wine hidden for the sake of revelation) and 364 (the wide mind as opposites joined).
- **The file ends mid-argument** (line 603), and line 600 admits the account of black and white tefillin "is not yet settled". The entries supply no answer.

## Where it holds both sides

- **Faith now, knowing later, and knowing now.** The introduction says faith in sovev must come into knowing (lines 4-5). Section 24 says sovev reaches souls now "only as faith", and knowing it is for the future (lines 84, 86). Section 26 then makes "know today" the drawing of sovev into knowing now, through Torah and mitzvot (line 89). The book resolves it by stages; both emphases stay on the page.
- **Equal, or lower higher?** "As they are united above, so below, and not more" (line 162), against "when is He great? in the city of our God" (lines 159, 161). The text sets out the contradiction and answers it by kindness against might.
- **How much does the dwelling weigh?** The end deed "rose first in thought" and is the main will of the chain (lines 245, 271, 283). Yet the pleasure in the refinements is "as nothing" beside the Essence's own simple pleasure, and "all this change between hidden and revealed" belongs only to the receivers (line 273).
- **Study or deed.** Study is greater now, the deed in the future (lines 439, 453). The mitzvah's root is above Torah (line 452), yet study reaches that root because wisdom is "nothing" (line 455). The ranking changes with time and is never collapsed.
- **What "He wraps Himself in light" means.** Concealment (lines 468-469, 499), and the Essence's own light spread as a bright garment (lines 486, 490, 502). "Both readings are true" (line 502).
- **Removing or turning evil.** The white threads, which turn, first seem higher than the techelet, which burns evil away. Then techelet, read as teshuvah that sweetens judgment in its own root, is "a truer sweetening" (line 514), and the penitent stands above the perfectly righteous (line 518).
- **Severity and kindness.** Now severity must be included in kindness, "the foundation of all Torah and mitzvot" (line 545). In the future severity will stand above it. Korach was wrong about the time, not the thing (lines 540, 545).
- **Direct and returning light.** "Heaven and earth were created as one", above both orders (line 529); and the returning light is higher, because it reaches the Essence (line 530).
