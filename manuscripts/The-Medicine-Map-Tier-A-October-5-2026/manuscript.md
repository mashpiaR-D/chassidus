*What the core Chabad works heal, and how. 31 readers over 22 works found 322 problems, 210 programs and 314 reframes, backed by 975 quoted passages. They are grouped here into 43 conditions a person could walk in with, in 11 families. Every quotation was re-checked against its source file by script (975 of 975 found); see CHECKS.md. The same material by work is in WORKS.md.*

Each condition's entry gives: what it looks like from inside; what the library says is really going on; the false conclusions a person draws and what the works set against them; the programs (steps, timing, who for, who not for); the reframes; where the Rebbeim agree and differ; cautions; key passages in Hebrew and English; one to three candidate truths for the guide; and what the core works do not cover.
