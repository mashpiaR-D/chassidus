## What this range is

After Patach Eliyahu the seventy tikkunim begin. Each takes the word בראשית apart a new way (ב' ראשית, "two beginnings"; ירא שבת, "fear Shabbat"; ברית אש, "covenant of fire"; שיר תאב, "a longed-for song") and finds in it a mitzvah or a secret. Tikkunim 1-20 run through the letter bet as the house of the King, the vowel points as the inner life of the letters, tefillin, Shabbat, sukkah and lulav, the order of the Amidah, prophecy, and the palaces of prayer. The Shechinah is on nearly every page: exiled, bound, pleading, rising, coming down with food.

## The unity of G-d

**He is known only in His house.** One who wants to see the King may see Him only in His home:

> ולית ידיעה כלל במלכא ובמלבושיה ובתקוניה לבר נש בעלמא עד דיעול לביתיה ולהיכליה (TZ 18b:7, line 357)
> *There is no knowing at all of the King, His garments and His adornments, for a person in the world, until he enters His house and His palace.*

His house is the Shechinah, the letter bet. No prophet or sage knows anything above except through her: דלית רשו לנביא וחכימא למנדע לעילא שום מדע אלא בה (line 373). All the sefirot pour blessing only through her, because if they poured outside her "there would be separation" (דאי ספירן הוו מריקין לבר מינה הוה פרודא, line 371). The Presence is not a lesser god; she is the single gate through which the One is met.

**Each one sees according to his strength.** God rides the four living creatures of Ezekiel's chariot, and He appears to each person as that person can receive:

> למאן דאיהו אדם אתחזיא ליה בדמות אדם. למאן דאיהו כשאר חיוון אתחזיא ליה בדמות חיון דכרסיא. לכל חד כפום חיליה (TZ 31b:18, line 896)
> *To one who is a "man" He appears in the likeness of a man; to one who is like the other creatures He appears in the likeness of the creatures of the throne. To each according to his strength.*

The printers' Hebrew gloss says the same with a mirror: the Shechinah is שהיא כמו האספקלריאה שכל הפנים נראין בה, "like a mirror in which all faces are seen" (line 1308), ולכל א' נדמה כפי כחו שהיא נשמתו, "and to each one He appears according to his power, which is his soul" (line 1312).

**Giving without losing.** The same gloss explains how the Name comes down into each sefirah and is not less above: כמדליק נר מנר ואינו חסר מן הראשונה, "like lighting a candle from a candle, and the first lacks nothing" (line 1313). This is the classic picture of emanation that Chassidus uses for the soul's descent and for teaching.

**First and last and nothing besides.** On the vowel points inside the vav: וכלא חד. אני ראשון ואני אחרון ומבלעדי אין אלקים, "and all is one: I am first and I am last, and besides Me there is no God" (line 432). And on the hidden letter above all names: He creates and is not created. Water "creates" grass and makes it grow, but water itself was created; אבל האי ברא ולא אתברי, "but this One creates and was not created" (line 1376).

**The light before color.** In the head of the King's power there is a "lamp of darkness" that is "not white, not red, not black, not green, no color at all" (ואיהי לא חוור ולא סומק ולא אוכם ולא ירוק ולא גוון כלל, line 383). Only when it spreads out does it make colors. This is the Zohar's famous opening image (בוצינא דקרדינותא), repeated here twice (lines 380, 1178).

**The lower waters weep.** When the firmament split the upper from the lower waters, the lower waters cried:

> בגין דמים תתאין אינון בוכין ואמרין אנן בעיין למהוי קדם מלכא עלת העלות ובען לסלקא לעילא (TZ 19b:16, line 402)
> *Because the lower waters weep and say: We want to be before the King, the Cause of causes, and we want to go up.*

This is a "dispute for the sake of heaven", which ends in peace and union, until the Cause of causes makes them equal (lines 401-403). Our reading: the separation of creation is felt from below as longing, and the longing is holy.

## States of consciousness

**The seal that stays in the wax (proposed Z-S03, "held by His imprint").** The Shechinah says to God: even though You go up and away, Your likeness never leaves me.

> אע"ג דאנת תסתלק לעילא (מעלמ') דיוקנך לא אתעדי מנאי לעלם (TZ 18a:7, line 335)
> *Even though You withdraw above, Your image never leaves me.*

Like a seal: where it pressed, the mark stays. So Israel says in exile:

> רבון עלמ' אע"ג דאנא בגלותא מרחקא ממך. שימני כחותם על לבך (TZ 18a:15, line 343)
> *Master of the world, even though I am in exile, far from You: set me as a seal on Your heart.*

From inside: He feels gone, and yet I carry His shape. The distance is real; the imprint is also real, and the imprint is how He remembers me (line 343 goes on: "through her You remembered us in exile"). How to enter: tefillin, which are "the seal on the heart and on the arm" (line 337). Ties to S68 The hiding seen as His and S84 Trust, but adds the thing both lack: a felt mark of the absent one.

**The Shechinah's exile felt as one's own grief (ties S73 Exile inside, S54 Compassion; proposed Z-S04 "grieving for Her, not for me").** The range's most famous page (Tikkun 6) describes God looking through the windows of the synagogues, turning "this way and that" to see if anyone will wake up in teshuvah to break open the prison where He and His Presence are held (line 503). He sees no one. They all shout in prayer on Yom Kippur:

> אלא כלהו צווחין בצלותין ביומא דכפורי ככלבים הב הב לנא מזונא וסליחו וכפרה וחיי (TZ 22a:17, line 505)
> *Rather, they all cry out in prayer on Yom Kippur like dogs: give, give us food and forgiveness and atonement and life.*

> דלא אית מאן דקרא ליה בתיובת' דיחזור שכי' לקב"ה דאיהי מרחקא מניה למהדר לגביה (TZ 22a:18, line 506)
> *For there is no one who calls to Him in teshuvah that the Shechinah return to the Holy One, she who is far from Him, that she come back to Him.*

Their kindness is all for themselves: דכל חסד דעבדין לגרמיהו עבדין (line 507). From inside, the state the page asks for is the opposite of "hav hav": standing in prayer for someone else's return, not for my own needs. It is love's grief. Chassidus (the Alter Rebbe's talks, the Rashab's Kuntres HaTefillah) quotes this line again and again as the difference between prayer for the self and prayer for the King. How to enter: before asking, ask for Her; see the next section.

**Bound by love (ties S60 The essential bond).** Why doesn't God simply leave exile? Because He will not leave her:

> קב"ה איהו מלך אסור ברהטים (TZ 21b:16, line 483)
> *The Holy One is a king bound in the tresses (Song 7:6).*

> בגין רחימו דילה איהו אסור בה (TZ 21b:17, line 484)
> *For love of her He is bound in her.*

"A prisoner cannot free himself from prison" (line 484), and her freedom, which is teshuvah, "hangs in their hands" (line 496). The stakes of human return are God's own freedom:

> ווי לון לבני נשא דקב"ה אסיר עמהון בגלותא ושכינתא אסירת עמהון (TZ 22a:8, line 496)
> *Woe to people, for the Holy One is imprisoned with them in exile, and the Shechinah is imprisoned with them.*

> ופורקנא דילה דאיהי תשובה אימא עלאה איהי תליא בידיהון (TZ 22a:8, line 496)
> *And her redemption, which is teshuvah, the Supernal Mother, hangs in their hands.*

**The one who prays is met at the door (proposed Z-S05, "He opens Himself").** If a person raises the Shechinah in his thought in prayer, or in any mitzvah, the King does not wait for a servant to open:

> ולא אמתין לעבד או ממנא דאפתח ליה. אלא איהו ממש אפתח ליה מחביבו ורחימו דאית ליה לגבה כחתן לגבי כלה (TZ 33b:23, line 1005)
> *He does not wait for a servant or an officer to open for him; He Himself opens for him, from the fondness and love He has for her, like a groom for a bride.*

> דכד ב"נ סליק שכינתא בפקודין דיליה לגבי קב"ה. קב"ה נחית עליה בגינה (TZ 34a:5, line 1011)
> *When a person raises the Shechinah through his mitzvot toward the Holy One, the Holy One comes down upon him for her sake.*

And before he finishes speaking she comes out to meet him, like Rebecca (line 1014). If the Shechinah was not in his prayer, he is told "stand outside" and gets his answer through a messenger (lines 1006-1008). From inside: prayer as opening, and being opened to; the sense of being expected. Ties to S33 Before Whom and S80 Prayer.

**Standing with Her in good times and bad; She stands with you (ties S84 Trust, S63 The world held; proposed Z-S06 "She walks with me").**

> זכאין אינון ישראל דאינון רגלין לשכינת' למיקם עמה בין בריוח בין בעאקו (TZ 35a:28, line 1078)
> *Fortunate is Israel, who are legs for the Shechinah, to stand with her in ease and in distress.*

> איהי קיימת בגינייהו בכל דוחקא וצערא בין בארחא בין בישובא בין בימא (TZ 35b:2, line 1081)
> *She stands for them in every strait and pain, on the road, in town, at sea.*

"When you walk it will guide you, when you lie down it will guard you" (Prov. 6:22), and the text adds: in this world, in the grave, and at the resurrection (line 1082). When people do not lift her in prayer, she cries "God has given me into hands I cannot rise from" (line 1058), and she is saddened (אתעציבת בהון, line 1061). The person is her legs; she is his keeper.

**At the head of the sickbed (proposed Z-S07, "the Presence at the pillow").** The word ערש, "bed", reversed is עשר, "ten": the Shechinah, who holds all ten sefirot, rests at the head of the sick:

> דאיהי שריא לרישא דחולה. ובגינה יי' יסעדנו (TZ 35b:10, line 1089)
> *For she rests at the head of the sick one, and for her sake "God will sustain him on his sickbed".*

When she holds the body, a voice comes: "stand on your feet" (line 1091). When she is not there, a storm wind breaks the ship of the body (line 1090). From inside: illness is not abandonment; the Presence has come nearer, to the pillow. Ties to S14 Loss and grief and to being-held.

**Before the King alone, in whisper.** Those who "speak before the King in a whisper and ask Him in a whisper" are יחידים קדם מלכא, "alone before the King" (line 973). One who makes his voice heard in prayer "is of little faith" (line 622). Ties S32, S33.

## Meditation and practice

**1. Ask for Her first (proposed technique Z-T03, "the prayer of the Shechinah").** The practical lesson of lines 503-509. What it is for: to move prayer from "give me" to "let Her come home". How (ours): before the requests of the Amidah, stop and say in your own words, "Let Your Presence return; let what is broken in the world be joined". Then ask for your needs as her needs. Guard (ours): the text does not forbid asking for food and life; it mourns asking only for these. Ties to technique 20 (own words) and to S54.

**2. A fixed place, not a chance meeting (proposed Z-T04).**

> נשמתא דאיהי קבועה בצלותא או באורית' איהי אתר קבוע לשריא בה שכינתא (TZ 21a:9, line 458)
> *A soul that is fixed in prayer or in Torah is a fixed place for the Shechinah to dwell in.*

> אלא אי אזדמנת לה במקרה. הכי איהי שריא עלה במקרה (TZ 21a:9, line 458)
> *But if it meets her only by chance, so she rests on it only by chance.*

What it is for: presence follows constancy. How: the same place and time for prayer and learning. Ties to technique 23 (coming back to the same line, habit).

> זכאה איהו מאן דמתקן לה דירה נאה בלביה. וכלים נאים באיברים דיליה. ואשה נאה דאיהי נשמתיה (TZ 22b:13, line 523)
> *Fortunate is one who readies for her a fine dwelling in his heart, fine vessels in his limbs, and a fine wife, which is his soul.*

(Ours.) This is the Tikkunim's form of the dwelling below (face F07), and it is inward: the heart the house, the limbs the furniture, the soul the one who lives there.

**4. Stretch the Echad up and down (proposed technique Z-T05).** When saying Echad, draw it out:

> וצריך לארכא לה בתנועה (נ"א בתרועה) דאיהו רביע עד אין סוף לעילא. וצריך לנחתא לה בחיר"ק עד אין תכלית לאמלכא לה על תתאין (TZ 25a:10, line 614)
> *One must lengthen it with the tune (another reading: with the teruah) up to Ein Sof above, and bring it down with the point below, to no end, to crown her over the lower ones.*

Later the same act: "turn the sling and stretch the stone" to Ein Sof, "that he may lengthen his days on his kingdom" (line 1357). And the lulav: shake it to six sides, "bring it toward you and away, to Him whose are the four winds; raise it and lower it, to Him whose are heaven and earth" (line 796). What it is for: to feel the one word fill height, depth and every side. Ties to technique 6 and to state S62.

**5. Awe and love are wings (a guard).**

> ואוריתא בלא דחילו ורחימו לא פרחת לעילא (TZ 25b:12, line 637)
> *Torah without awe and love does not fly upward.*

> ותורה ומצוה בלא דחילו ורחימו לא יכילת לסלקא ולמיקם קדם י"ק (TZ 25b:15, line 640)
> *Torah and mitzvah without awe and love cannot rise and stand before the Name.*

This is the exact source the Tanya (ch. 40 and 41) builds on: love and awe are the two wings. Ties technique 16.

**6. Wrapping and tying.** Wrapping in tzitzit "is as if he set up a throne for the Holy One" (line 645). For complete tzaddikim tzitzit and tefillin make a throne; for the middle person (the beinoni) they are like "an ox under the yoke and a donkey under a load" (לבינוניים ציצית ותפלין אינון כשור לעול וכחמור למשאוי, line 659), and on Shabbat "your ox and your donkey may rest". Our note: the Tikkunim does not scorn the beinoni's yoke; the Tanya will make the beinoni the ideal and the yoke the foundation (face F08).

**7. The palaces open to different keys.** All the gates were locked when the Temple fell,

> ודא איהו כל השערים ננעלו. אבל שערי דמעה לא ננעלו (TZ 26b:6, line 674)
> *This is "all the gates are locked", but the gates of tears are not locked.*

There is a palace of tears that opens only with tears; a palace of melody that opens only with melody (ואית היכלא דנגונא דלית לה רשו למפתח אלא בנגונא, line 679), and David drew near to it with his harp; a palace of the light of Torah; a palace of awe; and a palace of the poor that opens only for the poor who wrap themselves in prayer (ואית היכלא דעניים דלא מתפתחא אלא לעניים, line 683). Practice (ours): know which key you are holding today. Tears, a tune, learning, awe, need: each is a door. Ties to technique 19 (melody), S51 The sudden cry, S52 The song that sings itself.

**8. The niggun climbs and comes down.** A melody rises in six (the six words of Shema) and descends in six (the six words of "Blessed be the name"): ונגונא כד סליק סליק בשית. וכד מאיך מאיך בשית (line 727). Ties to technique 19 and S47/S48 (running and returning). Elsewhere, the living creatures "run and return", running in the strong dot and returning in the soft sign (line 449).

**9. The shape of the Amidah.** The first three blessings "order requests for the soul, to enter with them before the Cause of causes, where the soul's life is" (line 940); the middle ones are for the body's needs; the last three receive the answer. Take three steps back so as not to turn one's back to the King (lines 664-668, 958). Those who break off prayer for idle talk are pushed out (line 993).

**10. Lift Her in every deed.** See the state "He opens Himself". The Tikkunim say the same of every limb:

> זכאה איהו אבר דעביד ביה מצוה לקב"ה דבגינה נחית קב"ה לשרייא בכל אבר דיליה ועל האי בר נש מכריזין לעילא הבו יקר לדיוקנא דמלכא (TZ 37b:10, line 1176)
> *Fortunate is the limb with which he does a mitzvah for the Holy One, for through it the Holy One comes down to dwell in every limb of his, and of this person they proclaim above: Give honor to the image of the King.*

Ties to S81 The deed in the hand and technique 21.

**11. Give Her what is dearest.** A gift to the Shechinah must be made "from what is dear to him" (ודורונא דשכינתא צריך למעבד בגינה מההוא חביב דאית ליה, line 1166), and דלית דורונא חביבא קדם קב"ה כדורונא דשכינתא, "no gift is as dear before the Holy One as a gift to the Shechinah" (line 1172). She is poor in exile; kindness done to the poor and to the Torah is kindness done to her (lines 1159-1165).

**12. Kill the snake with prayer, but know why the arrows fail.** A herald cries every day: whoever kills the snake that nests in the high mountains gets the King's daughter, "who is prayer" (line 808). A young tzaddik shoots the arrows of prayer, the words of his mouth, and the snake does not notice them,

> ולאו בגין חלישו דצדיק ח"י עלמין אלא בגין חלישו דההוא דזריק לון (TZ 29b:15, line 813)
> *not because of weakness in the Tzaddik, Life of the worlds, but because of the weakness of the one who shoots them* (when he is not whole).

Only Moses' prayer hits the liver of the snake (line 814). What it is for (ours): an honest guard. When prayer feels powerless against the pull (S09), the Tikkunim do not blame God; they say the archer's hand is not yet whole, and point to the "faithful shepherd", the teacher in whose prayer one prays.

**13. Giving and receiving between people.**

> כד מקבלין דא מן דא בין באורייתא בין בממונא. גרמין לקבלא לעילא דא מן דא (TZ 39a:12, line 1239)
> *When they receive from one another, in Torah or in money, they cause those above to receive from one another.*

(Ours.) Every act of teaching and every act of charity joins the worlds.

**14. Thought, speech, deed.** The crowns of the letters hang on the thought of heart and brain, the vowel points on the speech of the mouth, the letters on the deed of the limbs: תגין תליין במחשבה דלבא ומוחא. נקודין בדבורא דפומא. אתוון בעובדא דאברין (line 1272). The three garments of the soul in Tanya ch. 4 map onto the letters of the Torah. Ties to C3 (mind to heart to hand).

## The big questions

**The soul and God: five likenesses.** The Talmud's five likenesses (Berachot 10a) are brought and read as the soul's root in Binah:

> מה הקב"ה רואה ואינו נראה. הכי נשמתא רואה ואינה נראת (TZ 28a:9, line 743)
> *As the Holy One sees and is not seen, so the soul sees and is not seen.*

> מה הקב"ה מלא כל העולם (נ"א הארץ כבודו) הכי נשמתא מלאה את כל הגוף (TZ 28a:9, line 743)
> *As the Holy One fills the whole world, so the soul fills the whole body.*

And the heart: הלב מבין הלב רואה. הלב שומע, "the heart understands, the heart sees, the heart hears" (line 745). Ties to face F06.

**Why the separation from God is the hardest thing.** "Hard as death is love": תקיפא איהי אפרשותא דקב"ה ושכינתא מישראל כפרישו דנשמתא ורוחא ונפשא מגופא, "the parting of the Holy One and the Shechinah from Israel is as hard as the parting of the soul from the body" (line 339).

**Suffering and redemption.** The burning bush: the Shechinah in the thorns, not consumed (lines 692-695). And a hard teaching: the pressure of exile hastens redemption, and ease delays it (ודוחקא דגלותא דערב רב לישראל ממהר לון פורקנא, line 694). "Take off your shoes" is a hint to strip off the body (הכא רמז דאתפשט מן גופא דיליה, line 697).

**Mercy wakes itself.** Every angel pleads for its birds, but no one pleads for Israel and for the Shechinah in exile (ולית בכו מאן דאוליף זכו על בני דאינון ישראל, line 544). Then God cries out "for My sake, for My sake I will do it" (line 546), and mercy wakes from Him alone. (Ours: providence does not wait for an advocate.) God's love reaches even past the story's lines: to Hagar, whom Sarah drove out, He says אנא רחימנא עלה ועל בנה, "I love her and her son" (line 593).

**Teshuvah goes into exile with us.** Adam sinned in the hidden thought and was stripped:

> ואי לאו דקמת עמיה תשובה וגלת עמיה. הוה אתאביד מכל וכל (TZ 42a:2, line 1368)
> *Had teshuvah not stood with him and gone into exile with him, he would have been lost entirely.*

Even after the worst, "if he returns, it is said of him: he returns and is healed" (line 842). And the whole redemption "hangs only on teshuvah" (דלית מלתא דא תליא אלא בתיובתא, line 520).

**Guests are the Shechinah.** "Fortunate is one who receives guests with a whole will: it is as if he receives the face of the Shechinah" (line 565).

**Against a Torah without depth.** Woe to those "who say the Torah is only its plain meaning and has no other secret in it" (line 1207): they return the world to chaos. Our note: the Tikkunim's polemic is sharp; the guide keeps the claim that the Torah has an inside, not the curse.

## Chassidus and the Tikkunim here (ours)

- **Agrees and builds:** the Tanya's two wings (love and awe) are line 637/640; its image of the Shechinah in exile within the soul extends lines 483-496; the Rashab's Kuntres HaTefillah draws on "hav hav" (line 505) for prayer that is for the King, not for oneself.
- **Moves inward:** in the Tikkunim the Shechinah's exile is mostly history and cosmos, and the task is to raise her by the precise act (tzitzit, tefillin, Shema said slowly, no talking). Chassidus keeps the acts and asks first what the heart is doing in them; the "raising in thought" of line 1005 becomes its center.
- **Where the guide gains:** the Tikkunim give the guide a warm, personal language for being held: she stands with you on the road, in town, at sea, at your pillow (lines 1081, 1089). And a prayer that asks for Her (line 506). These are rarely stated this plainly in Chabad texts.
