This is the second half of the Maharal's commentary on Megillat Esther: Haman's rise, the decree, Mordechai in sackcloth, Esther's fast and her going to the king, the sleepless night, Haman's fall, the second letters, and the setting of Purim. Most of it reads the story line by line. The deep lines are few, but some are among the Maharal's best.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The righteous hold their heart in their power.** "אבל הצדיקים לבם ברשותם" (760): "but the righteous have their heart in their power." In this they are like their Maker. "כי הלב הוא התחלת המחשבה והדעת" (761): "the heart is the start of thought and knowing."
- **Nature is His messenger.** "והטבע הוא שליח" (775): "and nature is a messenger." "ומפני כך נס הגאולה הזאת היה הכל בטבע, מצד שהוא יתברך מנהיג הטבע" (775): "so this redemption was all in nature, since He leads nature."
- **He is straightness itself, and straightness has no end.** "ומפני כי הוא יתברך הוא היושר עצמו, ולפיכך היושר אין לה קץ ותכלית" (969): "since He is straightness itself, straightness has no end." What is straight joins Him and lasts (969).
- **One who knows he lacks is ready.** "ולא כן כאשר האדם רואה עצמו בחסרון, שכל חסר מוכן שיושלם" (670): "when a person sees himself lacking, all that lacks is ready to be completed."
- **Counting as nothing, a person lasts by Him.** "וכיון שאין האדם נחשב לדבר מה, קיום שלו הוא מן השם יתברך" (979): "since a person counts as nothing, his lasting is from G-d." (The Maharal ties this to Purim drinking. The guide takes the teaching only, never the drinking. See the note below.)
- **One people, so the One is with them.** "במה שישראל הם עם אחד, לכן עמהם השם יתברך שהוא אחד" (928): "because they are one people, G-d who is One is with them."

## 2.1 Nature is His messenger

The Megillah has no open miracle. The Maharal says this is because the redemption came through nature, and He leads nature. "והטבע הוא שליח" (775): "and nature is a messenger." "ומפני כך נס הגאולה הזאת היה הכל בטבע, מצד שהוא יתברך מנהיג הטבע" (775): "so this redemption was all in nature, since He leads nature." The omer is brought to Him, "כי העומר מביאין אל הקב" (773), because He is the Leader of nature.

The Maharal ties this to two Names. The four-letter Name makes miracles. The name Adonai, Lord, leads nature: "בו מנהיג עולם הטבע, כמו האדון שהוא מושל" (781): "by it He leads the world of nature, like a master who rules." And a master guards his own: "ואין האדון מניח את עבדו להיות כלה" (783): "a master does not let his servant be wiped out."

Even what looks like chance is not. "לא היה המעשה דרך מקרה, רק שכך היה מחויב כסדר הראוי" (792): "the event was not by chance, but necessary, in fitting order."

## 2.2 "This is my G-d": He must exist

"זה אלי," "this is my G-d." Why "this"? "ונקרא בשם 'זה' כי הוא מחויב המציאות לגמרי" (587): "He is called 'this' because He must exist, fully." "ואין דבר יותר מחויב שיהיה נמצא כמו השם יתברך" (587): "nothing must exist more than G-d." He is the Place of the world: "כי הוא יתברך מעמיד ומקיים הכל, לכך מקום הוא אל הכל" (614): "He keeps all in being, so He is the Place of all."

## 2.3 Above time

What is from Him is not under time. The whole Megillah moves fast at the end for this reason. "כי דבר זה מורה על ענין שאינו תחת המשך הזמן כלל, רק ממעלה שהוא על הזמן" (742): "this shows a thing not under the stretch of time at all, but from a level above time." "כל הנהגת העולם הוא בזמן, אבל דבר שהוא מן השם יתברך, כאשר התחיל לצאת אל הפעל, יוצא במהירות היותר" (805): "the running of the world is in time, but what is from G-d, once it starts to come out into act, comes out most quickly." "כי ההויה כמו זאת שהוא מן השם יתברך, אינו תחת הזמן" (805): "such being, from G-d, is not under time." Yet "a miracle begins a little and at last is done fully" (826).

## 2.4 The end returns to the start

"כי הדבר שהוא שלם, התחלתו וסופו מתחברים באחד" (503): "what is whole has its start and end joined as one," like a ball. "וכל אשר הושלם הוא אל השם יתברך אשר מאתו נברא" (503): "all that is completed goes to G-d, by whom it was made." "והתחלתו בא מן השם יתברך אשר ברא אותו, וכך תכליתו אשר מתחבר אל התחלתו הוא אל השם יתברך" (504): "its start is from G-d who made it, and so its end, joined to its start, is to G-d."

From its own side, everything ends. "כי כל נברא יש לו ביטול מצד עצמו, יהיה מי שהוא" (506): "every created thing has an ending from its own side, whoever it is." "ודבר שהוא מצד השם יתברך, לא מצד עצמם, לדבר זה אי אפשר שיהיה לו הפסק כלל" (506): "what is from G-d's side, not from their own, cannot have a break at all." So even the end is not the end: "ומצד השם יתברך יש להם הקיום הנצחי מצד הסוף" (890): "from G-d's side they have lasting even from the side of the end."

## 2.5 He is straightness itself

At the very end, the Maharal asks why the Megillah must be ruled with straight lines, like a Torah scroll. A straight line goes on without end. "ומפני כי הוא יתברך הוא היושר עצמו, ולפיכך היושר אין לה קץ ותכלית" (969): "since He is straightness itself, straightness has no end." "והדבר שהוא ישר מצטרף אל השם יתברך, שהוא היושר בעצמו, ולכך יש לו קיום עד עולם" (969): "what is straight joins G-d, who is straightness itself, and so it lasts forever."

This is why the Torah lasts: "כי התורה היא מצד העילה, הוא השם יתברך" (972): "the Torah is from the side of the Cause, G-d." Kindness answers the receiver, so "ומצד החסד יש קץ וסוף. רק היושר אין לו הפסק" (972): "from the side of kindness there is an end; only straightness has no break." Sin is bending away: "כי החוטא הוא שיוצא מן היושר לגמרי" (974): "the sinner is one who leaves straightness fully." "ויום זה, יום הכיפורים, הוא היושר בעצמו" (974): "this day, Yom Kippur, is straightness itself."

## 2.6 One people, one G-d

"Yehudi" is one who says He is One. "שהם נקראים יהודים על שם שמודים ביחודו של הקב" (983): "they are called Yehudim because they acknowledge the oneness of the Holy One." "שם יהודי בא על מי שהוא מודה ביחודו יתברך" (989): "the name Yehudi is for one who acknowledges His oneness."

Those who say He is One become one. "וישראל הם מיחדים שמו ערב ובוקר, ובשביל זה הם עם אחד, ודביקים בו יתברך שהוא אחד" (927): "they declare His Name one, evening and morning, and so they are one people, cleaving to Him who is One." "במה שישראל הם עם אחד, לכן עמהם השם יתברך שהוא אחד" (928): "because they are one people, G-d who is One is with them." "ובפרט לישראל שיהיו כאחד, ואז השכינה עמהם" (869): "when they are as one, the Shechina is with them." So gifts to the poor come "מצד שהוא אחיו והם עם אחד" (927): "because he is his brother, and they are one people." And the Megillah was accepted as one: "ולא היו דעות מחולקות" (929): "there were no divided minds."

## 2.7 Two kings, one story

The king's anger "calmed" (shachachah) is read as two calmings: the King of kings and the king of flesh (834). "All things were done below and above" was the first half's rule (82). Here it closes: the whole book is one act seen from below and above.

- **MH-S43 "Heart in my power" (760-761).** Inside: a feeling rises, and I am not swept away. The heart is mine to steer. The wicked "are in the power of their heart"; the righteous "have their heart in their power" (760), and in this they are like their Maker, who "said to His heart." How to enter: when a strong feeling comes, stop and say: this is my heart; I hold it. Then choose what to do with it. It differs from S56 The self quieted, which is a stillness. This state is rule, not stillness. The heart can be full and still be held.
- **MH-S44 "Counting as nothing, held by Him" (979).** Inside: I do not count myself as anything, and I am not afraid. My lasting does not come from me. It comes from Him. "וכיון שאין האדם נחשב לדבר מה, קיום שלו הוא מן השם יתברך" (979). The Maharal reaches this state through Purim drinking. **The guide never does.** We reach it sober, by the thought itself (4.10). It differs from S57 No self to watch (the self gone quiet). Here the self is still there, but it knows it stands on Him. It is close to S62 Only Him, seen from the side of one's own being.

## 4.1 Hold your heart (`mh_heart_in_hand`, new)

When anger, fear or wanting rises, say: "אבל הצדיקים לבם ברשותם" (760): "the righteous have their heart in their power." The heart "is the start of thought and knowing" (761), so whoever holds the heart holds the mind. Name the feeling, then choose. This is MH-S43.

## 4.2 Nature is a messenger (`mh_nature_as_messenger`, new)

Look at something ordinary: rain, a meal, a recovery. Say: "and nature is a messenger" (775). He leads nature (775). Nothing here is by chance (792). This is the way to see Him when no miracle shows.

## 4.3 Do what you can, now (`mh_do_it_now`, new)

"וכל שעה יש לך לראות אם אפשר לך לעשות הצלה" (607): "every hour you must see if you can save." "כי האדם נברא על זה שיוציא אל הפעל מה שאפשר לו להוציא אל הפעל" (617): "man was made to bring into act what he can." "ואם תשתוק עתה, תשתוק גם לעתיד" (617): "if you are silent now, you will be silent later." Ask once a day: what good can I bring out into act today?

## 4.4 Pray for another first (`mh_pray_for_another`, new)

"המתפלל על אחר והוא עצמו צריך לאותו דבר, הוא נענה תחלה" (626): "one who prays for another while he needs the same thing is answered first." When you need something, pray first for someone else who needs it.

## 4.5 Gather to Him (`mh_gather_to_him`, new)

Prayer with others is a gathering to G-d: "כאשר הם מתאספים ומתקבצים אל השם יתברך" (619). "ובודאי כח הכלל יותר מן היחיד" (606): "the power of the whole is more than the single one." In hard times, pray with a group. Acts of kindness also raise: "כי על ידי גמילות חסדים האדם מתרומם אל השם יתברך" (621).

## 4.6 Reach out to receive life (`mh_reach_for_life`, new)

Esther touched the tip of the scepter, because "ואין ראוי שיקבל חיים כאשר הוא עצמו אינו מבקש" (647): "one should not receive life if he does not himself ask." In prayer, ask plainly. Do not wait to be given without reaching.

## 4.7 Three checks before acting (`mh_three_checks`, new)

Before a big act, check three sides: "וזה כאשר המעשה הוא טוב לעושה, גם הוא ראוי אל המקבל, וגם המעשה מצד עצמו הוא ראוי" (852): "it is good for the doer, fitting for the receiver, and right in itself." If you want to be met beyond the letter of the law, go beyond it yourself (852).

## 4.8 Walk the straight line (`mh_straight_line`, new)

Straightness has no end, because He is straightness itself (969). When you feel pulled to bend, a small lie, a clever way around, remember: "ומדברי שקר לא תבא הגאולה" (818): "redemption does not come from words of falsehood." What is straight joins Him and lasts (969). On Yom Kippur, "straightness itself" (974), return to the line.

## 4.9 Not even an hour (`mh_return_today`, extended)

Mordechai would not take off the sackcloth, "שלא רצה לחזור מן התשובה הזאת אף שעה אחת" (580): "he would not turn back from this return even for an hour." And "יפה שעה אחת בעולם הזה בתשובה ומעשים טובים מכל חיי עולם הבא" (986): "one hour of return and good deeds in this world is finer than all the life of the World to Come." Keep one sign of your return in front of you all day.

## 4.10 Count yourself as nothing, sober (`mh_count_as_nothing`, new)

The Maharal reached this through Purim drinking (978-979). **We never do.** Sit quietly and think: from my own side, I count as nothing, "every created thing has an ending from its own side" (506). "וכיון שאין האדם נחשב לדבר מה, קיום שלו הוא מן השם יתברך" (979): "since a person counts as nothing, his lasting is from G-d." Then feel held, not erased. The Maharal also gives the other way, fasting on Yom Kippur: "when a person removes the body by fasting, he has cleaving to G-d" (979). Joy, he says, is to give strength to the soul, "כי אין השמחה רק שיהיה כח לנפש" (979), not to remove the mind. This is MH-S44.

## 4.11 Know that you lack (`mh_know_i_lack`, extended)

"וזה כאשר הוא בחסרון, אז הוא מוכן לקבל השלמה" (669): "when he is lacking, he is ready to receive completion." And the guard: "כל גאון וגבהות יותר מן הראוי, קרוב להיות ניטל" (678): "all pride beyond what is fitting is close to being taken away." Received wholeness is never the last: "רק הם מוכנים להיות להם יותר שלימות" (899): "they are ready to have more wholeness."

## 4.12 Be whole, then give (`mh_whole_then_give`, new)

"כאשר האדם הוא בשלימות לגמרי, הוא גם כן משפיע לאחרים מטובו" (923): "when a person is fully whole, he gives others from his good." "וכאשר עצמו חסר ואינו בשלימות, אין משפיע לאחרים" (923): "when he himself lacks, he does not give." But gifts to the poor are different: they are given "רק מצד חסרון המקבל, שהוא עני" (928): "from the lack of the receiver, who is poor." Give from your fullness, and give to their lack, even when you do not feel full.

## 5. The big human questions

**Where is G-d when everything looks natural?** Leading nature. "Nature is a messenger" (775). The Megillah hides His Name because it was all in nature (775).

**Can I rule my feelings?** Yes. The righteous have their heart in their power (760), and in this they are like their Maker.

**Does everything end?** From its own side, yes (506). From His side, no; even the end returns to the start and to Him (503-504, 890).

**Why be honest when a lie would help?** Because He is straightness itself, and only the straight lasts (969). Redemption does not come through a lie (818).

**Should I be afraid of bad people?** Fear of the wicked would be a lack of trust in Him (701).

**How can I be close to G-d and to people?** Mordechai "was wholly good to Heaven and good to all people" (988). One people that says He is One has the One with them (928).

**Why does it matter if I pray with others?** The whole is stronger than the single one (606). Gathering to pray is gathering to Him (619).

**How far can a person rise?** Very far, but not to be a god: "כי שער חמישים לא נמסר לאדם" (727): "the fiftieth gate was not given to man." Man shares the image with G-d (736), and that is his honor.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Nature and the Names.** The Maharal: the four-letter Name makes miracles; Adonai leads nature (781). Gate of Unity ch. 6 says Elokim is the Name that hides Him in nature (Elokim has the same number as "hateva," nature), and Havayah is above it. The two use different Names for nature, but the idea is one: nature is His, and a Name of His rules it. "Nature is a messenger" (775) is close to the Baal Shem Tov's and the Alter Rebbe's teaching that nothing is outside His providence.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not named. The hidden redemption (775, and 81 in the first report) is the Maharal's version of hiddenness. Chabad says tzimtzum hides Him from us, not from Himself (Tanya ch. 48). The Maharal says the hiding came from a place too high to show. Both say the hiding is not His absence.
- **The Rambam.** "He is called 'this' because He must exist" (587) uses the Rambam's own term, "necessary existence" (Hilchot Yesodei HaTorah 1:1-4). The Maharal agrees with the Rambam here. Their disagreement is on His knowing and attributes (Derekh Chayyim 1164-1173), not on this.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "What is straight joins G-d, who is straightness itself" (969) and "the Torah is from the side of the Cause" (972) show the first half: what is true to Him is one with Him. "From the side of kindness there is an end" (972), because kindness answers the receiver: that is the second half.
- **Counting as nothing** (979) is close to Chabad bittul (Tanya ch. 6, 35: one who does not count himself as anything is a vessel for G-d). Chabad also reads "until he does not know" as a level above reason. Ours: the guide takes the bittul and leaves the drink. Tanya reaches bittul by thought and prayer, and that is the way we use (4.10).
- **One people, one G-d** (927-928) is close to Tanya ch. 32: love of one's fellow comes because all souls are one at the root, and "they are all one, and have one father."
- **Heart in one's power** (760) is close to Tanya ch. 12: "the mind rules the heart by its nature" (moach shalit al halev). The Maharal says the righteous hold the heart. Tanya says every person can, by the mind.
