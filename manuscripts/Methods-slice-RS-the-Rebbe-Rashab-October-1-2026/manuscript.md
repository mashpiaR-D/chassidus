## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

SOURCES.md already has the Rashab's core rule: teach the animal soul through its natural mind, with memale, proofs and time, and it moves from agreement to wanting (P5-P8, P12). This slice adds the rest of his toolkit. It is wider than understanding.

- **Two roads, by the person's state (Umaayan).** The long road: expose each of the animal soul's "follies" with a sober reckoning, and answer its "it's good for you" on its own ground of good (RS-01 to RS-03). The short road, for one who is already caught: holy stubbornness, doing the opposite with no argument at all (RS-04). The short road leads back to the long one.
- **Prayer is where the animal soul is refined, not learning (KT, KA).** Learning Chassidus alone can leave the traits untouched or even swell the ego. Refining happens in prayer: in its spoken words (RS-07), its timing (RS-12), its order of outer then inner carving (RS-23), aimed at one trait (RS-16).
- **Which tool does what.** Fear works by remembering and nullifies the animal soul; only love refines it (RS-09). Love like water weakens it; love like fire, lit from one's own distance, burns its natural fire and raises it (RS-11, RS-13). Coarseness beyond nature and habits from bad company are shed by bitterness and forcing, not contemplation; the animal soul's nature itself needs long work (RS-26). Torah softens it in peace; prayer fights it (RS-22, RS-23).
- **The animal soul must feel it from its own side.** Teshuvah changes it only when the animal soul itself is pained and finds no comfort, after a reckoning of specifics (RS-14). Arousal that stays in the soul's surrounding levels does nothing to it.
- **Body and daily life.** Start with the appetite for food (RS-19); hold back a word (RS-21); business and charity done for their purpose (RS-20); soften the whole animal soul before the particular traits (RS-25).
- **Moods.** Bitterness breaks, joy and song win (RS-24); enter by the yoke when it feels heavy, and the taste follows (RS-17); climb only by true rungs (RS-18).

## 2. The methods

### RS-01. Ask: is this my good? - **Family:** self-interest and good - **What it is:** When the urge says 'this is good for you', answer it in its own terms: is this really my good, given what I am? - **How to do it:** 1) Notice the claim the yetzer makes: 'it is good, it is a delight to the eyes'. 2) Say to yourself: is this my good, at the level of the soul I was given? Was I created for this? 3) Turn its own argument back: because it is a delight to the eyes, that is exactly why to keep far from it, so as not to lower yourself. 4) Put your good in spiritual things, chiefly Torah, which are 'good in truth'. - **Why it works:** The animal soul runs after what it takes to be good. An animal knows no other good, so it follows sweet things. A person who binds himself to lower things becomes like them, and since he has a mind he is lower than the animal (maamar 1 ch. 4). So the argument is fought on the ground of 'good', not against it. - **When / for whom:** Permitted pleasures beyond need, and the first moment of temptation. It is the reasoned answer for one who can still hear reason. **Not when:** When the person is already sunk in the thing and reasoning no longer holds (see RS-04). - **Quotes:** - «וכאשר מסיתו לומר שזה טוב לפניו, זאת ישים אל לבו, הכי זהו הטוב שלי לפי מעלת ומדריגת נפשי הניתן לי» — *When it entices him, saying this is good for him, let him set this to heart: is this my good, by the rank and level of the soul given to me?* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 1 ch. 4. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 29. OCR: no. - «ואדרבא במה שאומר לו שתאוה הוא לעינים, הרי מפני זה עצמו צריך להתרחק מזה בתכלית» — *On the contrary: because it tells him it is a delight to the eyes, for that very reason he must keep utterly far from it.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 1 ch. 4. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 29. OCR: no. - «שהבהמה נמשכת אחרי דברים הטובים והערבים לפי טבעה ואינה יודעת מטוב אחר כי אם מהטוב הזה» — *The animal is drawn after things good and sweet by its nature, and knows of no other good but this.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 1 ch. 2. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 11. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated

### RS-02. Trade up to a higher pleasure - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Do not take pleasure away from the animal soul; move it up a ladder of pleasures, to the pleasure of understanding. - **How to do it:** 1) See the ladder: sweet food (lowest, 'animal'), a beautiful sound, the pleasure of doing kindness guided by mind, and above all the pleasure of grasping wisdom. 2) Give the soul its delight in learning: in the revealed Torah, and in Chassidus, which binds to its inner side. 3) Expect the result: one becomes 'refined' (eidel) in everything one does. - **Why it works:** Pleasure is graded. The pleasure of mind is 'the true and lofty good'. A person, as the choicest creature, should take his pleasure at his own level. - **When / for whom:** For anyone who has some taste for learning. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's pleasure is in G-dliness itself; here the pleasure of the mind is offered as the bridge. - **Quotes:** - «והנה למעלה מכל התענוגים הנ"ל הוא התענוג שבשכל וחכמה» — *Above all these pleasures is the pleasure in mind and wisdom.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 1 ch. 3. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 19. OCR: no. - «וזהו הטוב והתענוג אמיתי והנעלה באמת» — *And this is the true good and pleasure, the truly lofty one.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 1 ch. 3. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 19. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated (the 'trade up' framing is ours)

### RS-03. Expose its arguments, one by one - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** The 'long road'. Kuntres Umaayan lists the animal soul's 'follies' (shtusim) and answers each with a sober reckoning (cheshbon tzedek). - **How to do it:** 1) Name the specific argument: 'it is permitted', 'you are still a good Jew, you are not cut off', 'no one will see', 'my blood is hot', 'he talked me into it', 'business needs all my time'. 2) Answer it with the true account. Example: 'no one will see' is false. People see; a person's chatter and a single needless glance reveal 'the depth of the bad in him'. And G-d sees and reveals. 3) Example: 'you are not cut off'. If he knew that the deed separates him from G-d he would never do it; so the lie must be shown as a lie. 4) Repeat for each argument as it comes. - **Why it works:** The yetzer works by folly that covers the truth. Since the sin of the Tree of Knowledge the natural feeling makes physical good felt very strongly, and that feeling hides one's true good (maamar 26 ch. 2). Coarse, emotional people fool themselves more easily than refined, thinking people (maamar 12). - **When / for whom:** For one in whom the G-dly soul shines enough to hear a reckoning. **Not when:** Not for one already sunk in the matter: he 'cannot overcome it by knowledge and sound reckoning' (maamar 28). Then use RS-04. - **Quotes:** - «ובאמת אינו כן שהכל רואים ויודעים ומכירים» — *In truth it is not so: everyone sees, knows and recognises.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 12. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 253. OCR: no. - «ואלו היה יודע שעל ידי מעשה הרע נעשה נפרד מאלקות לא היה עושה זאת בשום אופן» — *Had he known that by the bad deed he becomes separated from G-dliness, he would not do it in any way.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 2 ch. 1. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 39. OCR: no. - «ואין ביכלתו להתגבר עליו על פי הדעת וחשבון צדק הנ"ל מפני תוקף חומר יצרו» — *He cannot overcome it by the knowledge and sound reckoning above, because of the strength of his yetzer's matter.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 28. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 583. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated

### RS-04. The short road: holy stubbornness - **Family:** above reason (yoke, faith, mesirus nefesh, yechidah) - **What it is:** For one who is caught: no arguments at all. Do the opposite of the urge, by force, from the soul's essence. - **How to do it:** 1) Know the general rule once: these cravings are hateful to G-d. Do not re-open it. 2) Do not enter any claim and counter-claim. Do not check whether you will lose. 3) In the very thing you are sunk in, do the complete opposite, 'mighty as a lion'. 4) Keep it up over time. The same stubbornness applies to fixed times for Torah and to beautifying a mitzvah. - **Why it works:** Reason weakens and limits the will; a stubborn person's will is far stronger because it is not bound by reason. Jewish souls have a holy stubbornness ('a stiff-necked people') from the essence of the soul, the same power that gives one's life rather than worship idols. Acting from it over time weakens the coarse matter and the natural feeling, and the G-dly soul's powers come into the open; then one can live by holy da'as (the long road) after all. - **When / for whom:** One whose G-dly soul does not shine openly, who has been caught by the yetzer, whose powers are weak ('the strength of the body is the weakness of the soul'). **Not when:** Those whose G-dly soul shines are led by reckoning and even find new explanations (maamar 27 ch. 1). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's open powers work by reason; this works from its essence, above reason. - **Quotes:** - «שיתגבר כארי לעשות רק ההיפך מיצרו הרע בלי שום טענה ומענה ובלי שום חשבון כי אם בתוקף גדול» — *That he overpower like a lion, to do only the opposite of his evil inclination, without any argument or reply and without any reckoning, but with great force.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 28. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 585. OCR: no. - «וכידוע שהשכל מחליש את הרצון ומגביל אותו כמה יתפשט» — *As is known, reason weakens the will and limits how far it extends.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 27 ch. 1. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 566. OCR: no. - «וכאשר ככה יתנהג בפועל משך זמן על ידי זה יחליש את חומרו הגס וההרגש הטבעי הבהמי» — *When he behaves so in practice for a length of time, he weakens his coarse matter and the natural animal feeling.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 28. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 586. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated (darkei-hachassidus 3.11 cites line 564; the mechanism lines 566, 585-586 are added here)

### RS-05. Declare war: no to its yes - **Family:** strategy - **What it is:** Treat the animal soul as an enemy who comes with sweet talk. Take a standing counter-position, and watch the small things. - **How to do it:** 1) Know that the animal soul and the yetzer are your worst enemies, who want your spiritual death and come with cunning, step by step from the fully permitted to the barely permitted. 2) Whatever the yetzer says yes to, say no to yourself; whatever it says no to, say yes. 3) This general opposition itself lets the yetzer know you are its enemy and will fight it. 4) Use the tactic of war: attention to small matters. Victory comes from cunning more than from numbers, and the yetzer's own cunning device is self-justification. - **Why it works:** The refining of body and animal soul 'must be by way of war'. The enemy's weapons are the cravings; its trick is the excuse that catches even those who cannot be tempted by sin. - **When / for whom:** General stance for anyone in the work of refining. - **Quotes:** - «אשר בכל דבר וענין אשר היצר הרע אומר הן יאמר הוא לאו לעצמו» — *In every matter where the yetzer says yes, let him say no to himself.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 15 ch. 3. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 314. OCR: no. - «הוא מודיע להיצר הרע שהוא שונאו ואשר ילחום עמדו» — *He lets the yetzer know that he is its enemy and will fight it.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 15 ch. 3. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 314. OCR: no. - «ואחת מטכסיסי המלחמה הוא השימת לב על ענינים קלי הערך» — *One of the tactics of war is attention to matters of little worth.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 15 ch. 3. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 315. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated

### RS-06. Your G-dly soul is older and stronger - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** Answer 'my nature is too strong' with the structure of the two souls: the animal soul came first in time, but the G-dly soul is first in root. - **How to do it:** 1) Admit the facts: the animal soul is there from birth, the G-dly soul only acts from bar mitzvah; habit has made the animal soul's ways a second nature; it is 'an old king' who knows how to entice. 2) Then see the root: tikkun is rooted above tohu, as Jacob was the true firstborn. 3) Understand 'the oath' (the soul is made to swear 'be righteous') as satiation: a giving of strength from the root, like a fed man who is strong and in good spirits. 4) Conclude: if your natural heat is strong, your G-dly soul is surely stronger. It depends on your choice to arouse it. - **Why it works:** Strength over the animal soul is drawn from the G-dly soul's earlier root, and hidden essential powers know no obstacle, as an oath binds a man whatever stands in the way. - **When / for whom:** Against the excuse of temperament (hot blood) and against despair. - **Quotes:** - «וכאשר החום הטבעי שלו חזק ודאי גם הנפש האלקית שלו חזק יותר» — *When his natural heat is strong, surely his G-dly soul is stronger.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 14 ch. 1. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 285. OCR: no. - «מען מאכט איהם זאט» — *[Yiddish:] they make him full (satiated).* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 14 ch. 1. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 283. OCR: no. - «שעל כחות עצמים אין שום מניעה ועיכוב» — *For essential powers there is no hindrance or obstacle.* Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 14 ch. 1. `Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt`, line 284. OCR: no. - **Stated / ours:** stated (darkei-hachassidus 1.9 cites the nearby lines 263, 268-273, 286; the 'oath as strength' lines 283-285 are added here)

### RS-07. The spoken words of prayer refine it - **Family:** speech - **What it is:** Prayer has two parts. The intention is the G-dly soul's ascent. The speech of prayer is the refining of the animal soul. - **How to do it:** 1) Pray with the words, not only with the mind. 2) Know where the words come from: from a person busy all day with his natural powers, who now draws near with those same powers ('my heart and my flesh sing'). 3) Let the day's speech and natural powers be gathered and polished in the words of prayer. - **Why it works:** Letters of Torah come from above; letters of prayer are made by man, from below, 'from the nearness after the distance'. Like gems that shine after being polished, the refined letters become a crown for the King. - **When / for whom:** Every prayer. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Intention (kavanah) belongs to the G-dly soul's binding; the speech belongs to the animal soul's refinement. - **Quotes:** - «והענין הב' בתפלה הוא בירור וזיכוך הנח"ב» — *[OCR הנח"ב = הנה"ב] The second thing in prayer is the refining and purifying of the animal soul.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 1. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 55. OCR: yes. - «והדבור דתפלה זהו מהבירור דנה"ב שבתפלח» — *And the speech of prayer is from the refining of the animal soul in prayer.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 1. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 56. OCR: yes. - «וחיינו בעסקו כל היום בהכחות הטבעים שלו ומתקרב אח"כ בתפלה גם בהכחות הטבעים שלו» — *That is, being busy all day with his natural powers, he then draws near in prayer with his natural powers too.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 1. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 60. ### RS-08. Say the words aloud and clearly - **Family:** speech - **What it is:** The voice wakes the heart. For beginners this is the whole of prayer; for everyone it is the way to learn Chassidus. - **How to do it:** 1) Beginners: pray from a siddur, in a fixed place, very slowly, with the meaning of the words. 2) Pray a little aloud, with clear speech, so the words come out well, until the words feel alive and lit. 3) Learn Chassidus aloud, with desire, not as a burden or by the clock. 4) Know that praying with the meaning of the words beats a general glow: it holds you a little through the day. - **Why it works:** 'The voice arouses the intention of the heart' and adds life to the learning. Words felt from their meaning hold the person; a general excitement passes at once (KA 226). - **When / for whom:** Young men who do not yet know contemplation in detail; also advanced people at times. **Not when:** The advanced must not stop there: the heart-work of love and fear is still required (KT 108). - **Quotes:** - «ויהי' התפלה בקול קצת ובדבור מבורר שיצאו התיבות היטב» — *Let the prayer be a little aloud and in clear speech, so the words come out well.* Kuntres HaTefillah, §14. `Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 108. ### RS-09. Fear comes by remembering, all day - **Family:** habit and repetition - **What it is:** Fear of Heaven and accepting the yoke are not won by long contemplation in prayer. They come from constant remembering, made into a habit. - **How to do it:** 1) Once, ideally in prayer, grasp at length that nothing hides from G-d and He sees every deed. 2) Then train the mind (Tanya 42): whatever the eyes see, remember the life inside it; remember the King stands over you and sees your deeds. 3) Afterwards a short remembering is enough to bring the fear back. 4) Do it all day, not only at prayer. - **Why it works:** Love is born of detailed understanding and lasts only as an imprint after prayer. Fear is rooted in the soul's essence and is natural to Jews, so remembering awakens it. - **When / for whom:** Everyone, at all times. It is the beginning and foundation of the work. **Not when:** It is not the work of refining: fear acts on the animal soul by nullifying it, not by refining it. Refining comes only through love (KA 123-124). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Love requires real contemplation each time; for fear, memory suffices (KA 204). - **Quotes:** - «אלא העיקר הוא ההרגל להרגיל דעתו ומחשבתו תמיד» — *Rather the main thing is habit, to accustom his mind and thought always.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 3. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 101. OCR: yes. - «ובמו"כ מה שפועל בנה"ב הוא ג"כ ענין הביטל שלו (לא ענין חזיכוך וחבירור)» — *[OCR ובמו"כ = וכמו"כ, הביטל = הביטול] So too, what it does in the animal soul is its nullification (not its purifying and refining).* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 3. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 121. ### RS-10. Guard the senses until habit takes over - **Family:** habit and repetition - **What it is:** Close the eyes, ears and mouth as one sits before great people. It is hard only at first. - **How to do it:** 1) Restrict looking, especially gazing; restrict hearing idle and mocking talk; restrict speech. 2) Use the picture: a person at home sprawls and says what comes to mind; seated with great people he holds every limb in check. Feel G-d's yoke that way. 3) Push hard at the start, even if people notice. 4) Expect it to become habit, and to find rest from many disturbances. - **Why it works:** Sight wakes the soul's pleasure; even a 'cold' glance engraves deeply. The yoke works 'by itself', closing the senses without a fight, once it is felt. - **When / for whom:** Everyone; a precondition for any work in prayer. - **Quotes:** - «והכבדות בזה היא רק בעת הראשונה שצריך להכביד ע"ע ולהתגבר על טבעו ורצונו ובמשך הזמן יתרגל כך» — *The heaviness is only at first, when he must burden himself and overcome his nature and will; in time he becomes used to it.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 2. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 70. ### RS-11. Love that moves you away - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Love is a move from place to place. The nearer one comes to G-dliness, the farther one goes from the physical, and the animal soul weakens. - **How to do it:** 1) Contemplate the G-dly life in things (love 'like water') in detail, until closeness is felt. 2) Let the closeness carry you away, as a traveller nearing his goal leaves where he was. 3) Add disuse: natural powers weaken when not used (Rabbeinu Yonah). 4) Repeat daily; one or two prayers do not weaken it in truth. - **Why it works:** After a good prayer a person is 'a different man' for hours or a whole day. The weakening is that the animal soul does not spread into the body's thought, speech and deed. - **When / for whom:** Beginners and everyone; the easiest love and the first step. **Not when:** By itself it only weakens; to make the animal soul understand and want, one must also work on its natural mind (KA 152). - **Quotes:** - «דאהבה היא העתקה ממקום למקום» — *For love is a moving from place to place.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 4. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 140. OCR: yes. - «הנה גם כמה שעות אחר התפלה הוא אדם אחר» — *Even several hours after prayer he is a different person.* Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 4. `Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt`, line 140. OCR: yes. - **Stated / ours:** stated ## 3. What the sources say does NOT work

- **Learning Chassidus without prayer.** It "does not effect the refining" of the natural traits; one can learn much and do the opposite as soon as he stops. Without the work of prayer what he learned "turns into poison" and inflates him. KT 80-81 (OCR): «משא"כ בלימוד התורה גם בלימוד הפנימיות בדא"ח בידיעת והשגת אלקות אינו פועל חבירור והזיכוך במדות הטבעים» — *but learning Torah, even the inner Torah of Chassidus, does not effect the refining of the natural traits* (line 80); «ובהעדר העבודה בתפלה גם מה שלמר וידץ נהפך לו לפה"מ בקרבו» [OCR: שלמר וידץ = שלמד וידע; לפה"מ = לסם המות] (line 81).
- **Torah learned without love and fear.** It makes the learner "a great 'is'", coarse in soul and body. EH 272 (OCR): «נעשה יש גדול מאד ע"י עסק התורה שלו».
- **General contemplation.** It is only a memory of G-dliness, its excitement is "imaginary", and it leaves the animal soul as strong as before; the unrefined trait stays exactly as it was (KA 204, 241; RS-15, RS-16).
- **Noisy outward excitement.** Shouts and outward movements "tear out" the G-dly soul's light; he then wants everything and demands lofty things of himself, while the animal soul is untouched and falls at the first test. KA 253 (OCR): «והסימן ע"ז שבאה בצעקות ונם ברנועות חיצוניגת» — *the sign is that it comes with shouts and outward movements*; KA 255: «ער רייפט אוים דעם נה"א» and «דההתפעלות דנה"א אינו פועל מאומה על הנה"ב» — *the G-dly soul's excitement does nothing to the animal soul*.
- **Arousal from the soul's surrounding (makif) levels.** Even true will above reason, or hidden love, or faith, does not change the animal soul's matter, as the thief prays at the mouth of the tunnel. KA 184 (OCR): «הרי הרצון חזה אינו פועל בהחומריות» and «גנבא אפום מחתרתא רחמנא קרי'». Arousal from contemplating G-d's exaltedness works little on it (KA 178): «אבל כאשר ההתעוררות הוא מצד הנשמה (דהיינו מהתבוננות ההפלאה דאוא"ס ב"ה) אין זה פועל כ"כ על החומריות דנה"ב».
- **Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur excitement without the year's work.** People weep and resolve, and return to their nature unchanged. KA 187, 189: «אך סיבת הדבר הוא.העדר העבודה כל השנה»; «אין זה פועל על החומריות לשנותה באמת». (darkei-hachassidus 4.8 has the theme; these lines add the reason.)
- **A general resolution.** It does not help for the particular trait (KT 104; RS-16).
- **Fear alone.** It nullifies the animal soul; it does not refine it (KA 121; RS-09).
- **Bitterness alone.** Lower teshuvah breaks the animal soul's "is-ness" but does not raise it into holiness (KA 150): «אבל לא שיעלה גם הוא ויהי' נכלל בקדושה».
- **Teshuvah excitement without a reckoning.** "Without aim and without use" (AB 2469; RS-14).
- **Despair at the cooling.** Giving up contemplation because it went cold at the start is "the yetzer's scheming" (KA 228; RS-17).
- **Contemplation for the animal soul's coarse layers.** Gluttony and bad habits are not removed by contemplation (SV 4781; RS-26).
- **The animal soul's one refusal (a jest).** It has agreed to kindness, and lately even to Torah study, since neither stops it; only prayer it will not allow, "because that is directly against it". IK 5745 (OCR): «על הגמ"ח הסכים הנה"ב מכבר, ובשניס אלו הסכים ג"כ על התורה» and «רק את העבודה אינו מניח לפי שזה הוא נגדו ולעומתו ממש».
