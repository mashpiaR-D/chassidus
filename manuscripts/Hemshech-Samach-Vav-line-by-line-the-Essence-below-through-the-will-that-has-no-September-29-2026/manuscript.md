*Unity Index, the Samach Vav part: finished 2026-09-29. Hemshech Samach Vav ("the series of 5666") is the Rebbe Rashab's long series of maamarim, begun on Rosh Hashanah 5666 (autumn 1905) and carried on through 5667 into the winter of 5668 (1908), with a written conclusion he did not deliver. The text is the Kehot edition in uncorrected OCR: 9,229 lines, each a scan paragraph, with the editor's footnotes run into the discourse page by page and the book's appendices and indexes at the end. Every line was read in thirteen ranges and asked one question: how does this line express the unity of God? The thirteen detailed reports are in `samach-vav/` (listed at the end). Every Hebrew line quoted below was copied from those readings, checked with `find_he.py verify` at the line given, and checked again, letters only, to sit on that line itself. The OCR is quoted as it stands, errors and stray marks included; what the author meant is given only in the English. Tanya lines are those of the Tanya pass's text (`Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`), cited as "Tanya line N".*

## The short answer

The Tanya asks how the world can be, if there is nothing but God. The Gate of Unity walks the unity down the chain of worlds. Imrei Binah asks how saying the Shema makes it. Samach Vav asks a sharper question than any of them, and asks it in its first maamar: **why are there worlds at all?** It turns down the classic answers one by one (so that His powers be complete; so that He be known) and gives its own: a desire with no reason, ואין אנו יודעים טעם שכלי מפני מה נשתוקק (line 208), that the Essence, which has no above and below, be revealed below.

Everything else in the series follows from that. **God's will has two layers. The will that made the worlds is His outer will: it has reasons, and it is the root of measure, space and time. The will in the mitzvot is His inner will: it has no reason at all, so it holds no root of limit, and only it can bring the Essence itself into time and space. What reaches the Essence is whatever reveals least: the physical deed, the "no" of a prohibition, the servant who accepts the yoke with no self of his own, the return (shov) rather than the rush upward. And the hiding that seems to stand between is, at its root, the Essence itself: "essence does not hide essence", and in the end "the hiding itself will shine".**

The Essence leads, as in both Gates and Imrei Binah, and by more than in any of them. What stands out is what comes next. In the Gate of Unity the cosmic faces followed (hiding, opposites, knowing). Here the next three are the person's: the mitzvah, bitul and the soul. The series is metaphysics from end to end, but nearly every metaphysical step ends in a question of service: what reaches that level? Knowing and opposites come last. The Rashab says often that the Essence is not known, and he ties the union of opposites to the Essence alone (line 5208).

## How the series builds it, season by season

The series runs as one argument, each Shabbat's verse taking it a step further. The maamarim below are named by their opening verse; the line numbers are the file's.

**1. Why there are worlds (Rosh Hashanah to Noach 5666, lines 75-864).** *Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah* states the thesis at once: the purpose is והו"ע יחוד אוא"ס הסוכ"ע בממכ"ע (line 119), the union of the surrounding light within the filling. It then argues it. Etz Chaim's reason, that His powers be complete, fails, because above, power never lacks act (line 169). The Zohar's reason, "so that they know Me", is fulfilled in Atzilut and cannot explain the lowest world (line 195). What is left is desire alone (line 208), and "a desire has no question" (line 213, in Yiddish). The Essence has no top and bottom, so its main revelation can be below (line 213), and each mitzvah draws a new Name, "your God", because it is made through you (line 237). *Zeh HaYom*: a light has a head and an end, but אבל העצם הוא בכ"מ בשוה, ואין בו ראש וסוף, ולא התחלקות מדריג' כלל (line 290), and between levels one rises only by giving up the former level. *Shuvah Yisrael*: the soul is rooted in the Essence, "and so it went down into a physical body" (line 452). *Lulav va'Aravah*: an essence is indivisible, and Or Ein Sof rests only on one who is nothing before Him (lines 468, 546). *BaYom HaShemini Atzeret* and *VaYechulu* then open the series' central distinction. The will to create has a reason; the will in the mitzvot has none: ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא (line 596). So only the mitzvah-will is drawn "below as above, with no tzimtzum" (line 739), so that even in place and time there is no limit of place and time (line 740). *Eleh Toldos Noach* says it outright: the will of the mitzvot is the inwardness and Essence of Or Ein Sof, above the will for worlds (line 795).

**2. What the heart reaches (Lech Lecha to Vayishlach, lines 865-1509).** "No thought grasps You", not even by negation (line 876), "but He is grasped by the will of the heart" (re'uta deliba, lines 876, 897), the yechidah's own bond, as a son's bond with his father (line 906). *Ki Yedativ* weighs will against delight and holds both as essence (line 959): the simple will of every Jew cannot be parted from God (line 1005), and a hidden delight lies within it. *Ve'Avraham Zaken*: toil is the striking of the flint, reaching what is "hidden even to itself" (line 1125). *Vayiten Lecha*: the Babylonian Talmud's questions hide, and working through them reaches "Torah as it is in His very Essence" (line 1245). *Vayishlach*: Torah is the one intermediary that does not divide (line 1438).

**3. The return (Chanukah to Shevat, lines 1510-2062).** Light runs back to its source; the Essence has no source to run to. So the true will of the Essence is in shov, the coming back down (line 1667), and shov is "planted and rooted in the Essence" (line 2008). *Vayelech Ish* is the Rashab's reworking of the Tanya's Gate of Unity 2-3: the sun's ray is too weak a parable, because creatures are nullified more than a ray (line 1871). *Ki Ka'asher HaShamayim* gives the mechanism, ולכן המשכת בחי' העצמות הוא במצות מעשיות דוקא, שאינו בגדר כלי (line 2005), and the range's most important sentence on the unity: the union of the higher and lower unities is above the higher unity alone (line 2019).

**4. What "Ein Sof" names (Shevat to Iyar, lines 2063-2741).** *Vayolech* asks what the word names and concludes: the light, not the Essence. In the Essence alone the word is true, and there it means שלילת הגבול ושלילת הבלי גבול, שלילת החיוב ושלילת השלילה (line 2253). Even the source of all worlds is "nothing" before the Essence (line 2292), and He is alone even now (line 2299). *Ki Imcha*: the light absorbed in the Essence is only "ability" (line 2416). *Adam Ki Yakriv*: the power to hide is not the lesser power but the chief one (line 2503), and "the power of limit is the power of the infinite" (line 2514). *Adam Ki Yihyeh* admits a limit: how creation from nothing works "is not understood by us" (line 2603).

**5. Havayah and Elokim (Iyar to Shavuot, lines 2742-3332).** *Ki Tavo'u el HaAretz* builds the series' account of hiding in three steps. Elokim, the limiting power, is itself the Essence, and ועצם אינו מסתיר על עצם (line 2891). The soul came down to repair the hiding, because הוי' ואלקים כולא חד (line 2938). And at the deepest level שההעלם בעצמו יאיר (line 2947). *VeAsita Chag Shavuot* maps three hearts (line 3146), sets two contemplations of the higher unity side by side and calls the second, "their existence is Godliness", not lower (line 3173), and finds the point of the heart that never forgets (line 3223). *HaShamayim Mesaprim* tells the Baal Shem Tov's parable of the father who hides to test his son (lines 3255-3278).

**6. The "no" and the back (Naso to Matos-Masei, lines 3333-3947).** The prohibitions reach the Essence; the positive mitzvot reach the light (lines 3346, 3373, 3378). The renewal of creation is itself the creature's cleaving to its source (line 3419). The surrounding light is present like a will in the hand: it moves the limb and is not held by it (line 3544). *Ki Merosh Tzurim* makes its most surprising claim: contemplating "all before Him is as nothing" is only seeing Him "from behind" (line 3735). *Titen Emes*: the nearer, the more nothing (line 3795), and prayer contemplates the radiance so as to thirst for the Essence, not to know it (lines 3829, 3831).

**7. The simple servant (Elul 5666 to Sukkot 5667, lines 3948-4587).** The son, the trusted servant, the simple servant. The last is lowest and reaches highest, because he has no self of his own: שהעבד אינו מציאות לעצמו, ואינו מהות בפ"ע כלל מן האדון (line 3999). His cleaving is "essence to essence" (line 4086). Why does the lowest reach highest? מפני כי נעוץ תחלתן בסופן וסופן בתחלתן (line 4151): "Beginning" can be said only of the Essence (line 4203), and it is wedged in the end. So only refining from below makes the lower thing itself a vessel (line 4290). *BaSukkos* and *Shemini Atzeres* split bitul: Chochmah's is weakness, Keter's is essential (lines 4463, 4470), and bitul reached by contemplation is only acknowledgement, which fades when the contemplation stops (line 4486). Then the Tanya's own formula is ranked: "there is none besides You" is the light; "nothing apart from You" is higher (line 4538).

**8. Torah, toil and truth (Cheshvan to Sivan 5667, lines 4588-5831).** Torah joins "the two essences" (line 4662), yet it is "only light" until toil draws the Essence (line 4708). Every question in the Oral Torah is a hiding, and toil through it reaches the Essence (line 4718). The unlearned reach it too, by the yoke (line 4887). The world is real, only not true (line 5175). "There is none else" leaves nothing at all (line 5187), and it is found precisely in the creation of physical "something" (line 5251). The vessel for Ein Sof is what is nullified (line 5253). From the light we know nothing of the Essence (line 5534); from creation from nothing we know only that its existence is from itself (line 5548). A hiding that nothing forces is not a hiding (line 5684).

**9. The soul, the deed and the conclusion (winter 5668, lines 5832-6497).** The soul is "Godliness that became a something" (line 5862), and its root shines in it only below (line 5909). Nothing hides the Essence; it hides itself, and only toward the worlds (line 5948). Echad is oneness gathered; yachid is "oneness in essence" (line 5987). *HaShamayim Kis'i* sums up the mitzvah: the will of the mitzvot is the essential will, in which "all His Essence" is (line 6186); take part of it and you hold all of it, so it rests only in a physical thing, "which is not a vessel at all" (line 6189). The servant reaches the Essence and the son reveals it (lines 6318, 6327). The written conclusion reads Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur as the calendar of the unity: the union of the surrounding and filling lights can come only from the Essence, where both are included (line 6390), and the shofar reaches the essential concealment (line 6400).

**10. After the Rashab (lines 6498-9229).** The Rayatz's notes explain the opening maamar (hiding needs an "other", and in Godliness there is none, line 6611). R. Dovid Feldman's contents, chapter-heads and summary (lines 7020-7706) restate the whole series maamar by maamar, often more baldly than the discourse. The subject index (lines 7707-8507) glosses the key terms. These are good evidence of what the series says and weak evidence of how it gets there; they are counted apart above.

## What it comes to, for a person

This is the inner side of the series: what a person does with it when he sits, prays and acts.

- **Two wills, and which one you meet in a mitzvah.** The world comes from a will with reasons, and so the world is measured, graded, above and below. The mitzvah comes from a will with no reason at all (line 596). That is why it is not smaller for being small. The will of the mitzvot is "the essential will, that all His Essence, as it were, is in this will" (line 6186: שכל עצמותו כבי' ברצון זה), and the part is the whole: שכשאתה תופס במקצת מן העצם אתה תופס בכולו (line 6189). So it rests only in a physical thing, כ"א בא בדבר גשמי דוקא, שאינו בגדר כלי כלל (line 6189). The coin in your hand is not a vessel that holds a portion of Him. It is where the whole of the will is done.
- **What thought cannot reach, the heart's will does.** No knowing reaches the Essence, not even the knowing that says what it is not (line 876). אבל ברעו"ד נתפס בחי' העצמות (line 897): He is grasped by re'uta deliba, the simple will of the heart. It is not a feeling produced by contemplation. It is the soul's own bond, שהן בבחי' התקשרות עצמיות לאלקות (line 906), the way a son is bound to his father by being his. Its plainest form is "to You, God, I lift my soul" (line 3680), and its test is what a Jew will not give up even when he wants to live (line 1005).
- **Bitul is the vessel, not the prize.** זהו לפי שביטול הוא כלי לגילוי אור (line 368): bitul is a vessel for the light, and דהכלי לאוא"ס הוא מה שבטל דוקא (line 5253). But the series tells two kinds apart. Bitul reached by thinking is only agreeing that there is nothing else, and ולכן כשנפסק ההתבוננות, אינו בבחי' ביטול אז, וחוזר לישותו (line 4486): when the thinking stops, the self comes back. The bitul that holds is silent, "because it is not an existence at all" (line 4493). In practice it looks like the simple servant, who does not carry the work as his own: שהאדון עצמו הוא שעשה את הדבר, אלא שעושה ע"י עבדיו (line 4035). This is not self-crushing. The servant's lowliness is what lets the Master's pleasure be his (line 4027), and the Rashab will not rank the one who learns above the one who simply does (line 6354).
- **The world held is higher than the world erased.** The higher unity alone says the world does not exist, as before it was made (line 2019). Above it is the union of both: the world exists and is, as it exists, one with Him. The Rashab says the second is harder, דבאמת בנקל הרבה יותר להיות בבחינת כלות הנפש מלהיות למטה בקיום התורה ומצות (line 2016): it is much easier to long to expire than to live below keeping the Torah. And he gives it as a contemplation: things exist, והיינו שהן בבחי' מציאות, אלא שמציאותם הוא אלקות (line 3173), so that even busy with physical things a person is not separate from Godliness (line 3173).
- **The hiding is itself the Essence.** When He cannot be seen, the series gives three answers, in order. The hiding is only toward us: ועצם אינו מסתיר על עצם (line 2891); a hiding He chooses is no hiding, והסתר רצוני אינו הסתר (line 5684). The hiding is His strongest power, not His weakest: ואדרבה, זהו עיקר בחי' הכח והגבורה להעלים ולהסתיר (line 2503). And the hiding itself will shine (line 2947). The Baal Shem Tov's father who hides to test his son stands behind it: the wise son runs after him, and the love is revealed "from darkness to light" (line 3261). The series does not use this to explain anyone's suffering, and neither should we.
- **Contemplation sees Him from behind, and that is its use.** Even "all before Him is as nothing" is שזהו רק הראי' מאחוריו לבד (line 3735). Prayer contemplates the radiance, and the radiance, seen as only radiance, makes a person thirst for the Essence (line 3829). One does not contemplate the Essence itself (line 3831). When the feeling is in the Godliness and not in one's own grasp of it, the heart is moved without effort (line 3153: ולכן מאיר ממילא בלב).
- **The point that does not forget.** In every Jew, even in exile, a glow of the surrounding light shines as the point of the heart above knowing (line 3223), and it is never forgotten.
- **The low place, again.** The soul's root shines in it only below (line 5909). The simple servant's refining makes "a new drawing, not by gradation at all" (line 4080). And the unlearned reach the Essence by the yoke (line 4887).

## What it adds to the eight sources

The eight sources read before it are the Tanya, the Gate of Unity, Strashelye's Gates of Unity and Faith, Sefer HaArachim, Imrei Binah, Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, Kuntres HaAvodah and Kuntres HaTefillah (`FOUR-SOURCES.md`).

**Two wills, argued.** The Tanya grounds the mitzvah in the unity (chs. 35-41, "Torah and the Holy One are one") and has the seed in the ch. 41 gloss on the gevurot and chasadim of Atik. Imrei Binah ranks the mitzvah's simple will above study. Samach Vav sets two wills against each other and draws the consequence: the creation-will has a reason and is the root of space and time; the mitzvah-will has none, so only it brings the Essence into space and time (lines 587-614, 739-740, 795). The will of creation is itself a novelty, drawn anew each moment (line 514).

**Why there are worlds, argued.** Tanya 36 names the dwelling below. The Rashab rejects the two classic reasons step by step before giving the desire above reason (lines 169-213), and he repeats the rejection of "so that they know Me" late in 5667 (line 5330).

**"Ein Sof" is not the Essence.** None of the eight draws this line. The Rashab argues at length that "Ein Sof" names the light, that even the source of all worlds is "nothing" before the Essence, and that the Essence is the negation of limit and of limitlessness alike (lines 2253, 2292, 2302, 2324).

**Above the Tanya's formula.** The Tanya's gloss says Ein Sof is שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו, וזו היא מדרגת החכמה (Tanya line 499). The Rashab quotes it and places it: that is "there is none besides You", the bitul of the light. Above it is "nothing apart from You" (line 4538), and "there is none else" leaves nothing at all (line 5187), a level found, he says, precisely in the creation of physical "something": דבחי' אין עוד זהו דוקא בהתהוות היש הגשמי (line 5251). The Tanya's summit becomes a middle rung.

**The union of the two unities, ranked.** Part one and Part two of `FOUR-SOURCES.md` kept four positions on which unity is higher: Imrei Binah (the lower), the Gate of Unity (equal before the Essence), Sefer HaArachim (both), the Tanya (the lower is the purpose, the higher the deeper view). Samach Vav takes a fifth: not the lower above the higher, but the union of both above the higher alone (line 2019), and the contemplation "their existence is Godliness" above "they do not exist" (line 3173). This is closest to Sefer HaArachim's "the world's existence one with Him" (Sefer HaArachim line 1205). That the two say one thing is our reading.

**Hiding, carried to the Essence.** The Tanya calls the power to hide "His mighty acts". Imrei Binah says the hiding will shine. Samach Vav gives the reason: the power to hide is the chief power (line 2503), the limiting Name is itself the Essence (line 2891), and the root of every concealment is the Essence's own hiddenness (line 2947). It stands with Imrei Binah against the Gate of Unity's "only looks brighter" (Gate line 2414).

**Yachid, from the Rashab again.** Part two found the Rashab using "yachid" for the lower unity (Kuntres HaTefillah line 85). Samach Vav says the same, and more plainly: שגם מצד העולמות הוא בבחי' יחיד ממש (line 5147), "even from the side of the worlds He is truly yachid". It also defines the pair: echad is oneness gathered from spreading; אבל בחי' יחיד היינו יחוד בעצם (line 5987).

**What reaches the Essence, widened.** Part two found bitul, not love. Samach Vav agrees and names the forms: the physical deed (line 2005), the prohibition (line 3373), the simple servant (line 3999), toil over a question (line 4718), shov (line 2008), and teshuvah that leaps (line 1492). It adds two rules the others lack: what is lowest has the Essence "wedged" in it (line 4151), and a soul holds the Essence's power in the measure it went down (line 4314).

**Knowing, with the sharpest limit.** Like the four books of Part two, the Rashab stands on the side of knowing: true knowledge that the Essence is beyond knowledge belongs only to one who studies the high levels (line 368). But he sets the tightest limit of any source here: no grasp, not even by negation (line 876); all contemplation is "from behind" (line 3735). He also maps a route (negation, wonder, recognition, then seeing, line 1996). His warning agrees with his own Kuntres HaTefillah (line 71): do not contemplate the Essence itself (line 3831).

**Smaller additions.** - Renewal is cleaving: דההתחדשות ממקורו זהו הדביקות במקורו (line 3419). F01 becomes the reason for F02. - The sun-ray parable is too weak: creatures are nullified more than the ray, because the ray is of the sun's kind and they are not (lines 1871, 7152). - Torah is the one intermediary that joins two essences (line 4662); the Rebbe's footnote limits this to the souls (line 4664). - The soul is rooted above the Torah and revealed only through it (lines 3010, 3080). - The world is not an illusion: דבאמת מציאות העולם הוא מציאות, אלא שאינו מציאות אמיתי ממש (line 5175). - The only thing we know of the Essence from the light is that its existence is from itself, and we know that from creation from nothing (line 5548), which is the Tanya's own Iggeret HaKodesh 20 reason. - A calendar of the unity: filling light on Rosh Hashanah's Malchiyot, surrounding on Zichronot, the Essence on Shofrot and Yom Kippur, and the same three in the Shabbat meals (lines 6402-6452).

## Where it differs from the Tanya (kept as disagreements)

- **Knowing or faith (Shaar HaYichud 7).** The Tanya ends the question of His knowledge with רק להאמין באמונה, שהיא למעלה מהשכל ומהשגה (Tanya line 834). The Rashab says that one who does not study the high levels holds the Essence's unknowability "only by faith, which is not knowledge", and that the true knowing of it belongs to one who studies (line 368); he maps a route to "seeing" (line 1996) and calls the higher knowledge "knowledge of the Essence" that is recognition, not intellect (line 1810). The two are not formally opposed (the Tanya speaks of the content, the Rashab of the knower's route), but the pull is the other way. At the same time the Rashab denies all grasp of the Essence (line 876). Both halves are his.
- **The mind's eye or prophecy (Iggeret HaKodesh 19).** The Tanya says "a sage is greater than a prophet", because wisdom reaches higher levels than prophecy can (Tanya line 1267). Line 1362 says Moshe saw the Essence with the eye and that "you will see My back" is the mind's seeing. The Rebbe's footnote sets them side by side, דבעין השכל רואין במדרי' גבוהות יותר מבעין הנבואה, וכמו שהאריך באגה"ק סי"ט, וכאן כ' להיפך (line 1367), suggests that the sage sees higher levels and the prophet the essence of his level, and still ends "needs study".
- **Where the power to create sits (Iggeret HaKodesh 20).** The Tanya: the light is like the luminary, His Essence, ולכן הוא לבדו בכחו ויכלתו לברוא יש מאין (Tanya line 1295), and the SPINE's F05 reads this as "the power to create is the Essence's alone". The Rashab reads the same letter as saying creation is from the light, which is called Ein Sof because it is like its source, and calls that the Alter Rebbe's innovation (lines 2273, 2302). Elsewhere he says the true power of creation is in the Essence alone (line 5930) and that the Essence is wedged in Malchut (line 4151). The Tzemach Tzedek's footnote also reads Iggeret HaKodesh 20 as saying the vessels are truly bounded, against the Rashab's parable (line 2544).
- **Will or delight.** The Tanya does not rank them. *Ki Yedativ* goes both ways and holds them one in essence (line 959). The editor's footnote records the Rebbe reading the conclusion as הרי המסקנא היא שכח התענוג הוא הכי נעלה (line 1024), and then his handwritten qualification that this is "not as written here". Kept open, as the text keeps it.
- **"He alone is, and there is none besides Him."** The Tanya's gloss (Tanya line 499) is the level of Chochmah; the Rashab agrees and puts "nothing apart from You" above it (line 4538). This ranks the Tanya's formula without denying it.
- **The sun's ray.** Shaar HaYichud 3 makes the ray in the sun the model of nullification. The Rashab says the model understates it (lines 1871, 7152).
- **Which unity is higher.** Shaar HaYichud 1-7 treats the higher unity as the height; the Rashab puts the union of both above it (line 2019). The Tanya's dwelling below (ch. 33, 36) points the same way, but it never ranks them in these words.
- **The positive mitzvah or the prohibition.** Tanya 4 and 35-37 put the drawing of Or Ein Sof in the positive mitzvah. The Rashab, following Likkutei Torah, says the prohibitions reach the Essence and the positive mitzvot do not (lines 3346, 3373).
- **Study or deed; Torah or the soul.** The Tanya calls Torah study a union like no other (ch. 5). The Rashab says the deed reaches the essential concealment and Torah reveals it (line 6157), and that the souls are rooted above the Torah (lines 3010, 3080). He keeps both: the soul is higher in root, Torah in revelation (line 6022).
- **Does the soul rise by nature?** Tanya 19: like a flame, by nature. Line 1623: a pull toward the source needs a felt source, and the rousing comes from above. The Rashab reconciles them himself (line 1568); the emphasis still differs.

## Inside the series (kept, not smoothed)

- **Servant or son?** The servant is above the son (line 3977), and the son's bond is deeper (line 3990). The servant reaches the Essence and the son reveals it (lines 6318, 6327). The written conclusion has the "son who becomes a servant" (line 6405). Feldman's summary ends on the balance: to reveal the Essence the service of revelations is needed too (line 7705).
- **The "no" or the deed?** In Naso the prohibitions reach the Essence (line 3373); in *Ki HaMitzvah HaZos* the practical mitzvot as a whole are rooted "in the very Essence" (line 4250). Not reconciled in the lines.
- **Is creation from nothing understood?** "It is not understood by us" (line 2603); creation is no wonder, the wonder is the enlivening (line 3598).
- **What "Ein Sof" names.** The Rashab concludes "the light", and his own gloss leaves it open: ולפ"ז לכאו' יש מקום לומר דא"ס קאי על העצמות (line 2235), ending "this needs study". The Tzemach Tzedek's note sides with the Rema: ורמ"ע סל דאפי' סבה וסובב לא יתכן אמהותו ועצמותו, והאמת עמו (line 2269).
- **Is the world real?** Real, not true (line 5175); and before the Essence, all is nothing (line 2292). The two knowledges, kept.
- **What alone reaches the unfelt Essence?** The conclusion and the summaries give five answers: Shofrot and Yom Kippur, refining with the yoke, the deed, teshuvah, and toil in Torah. They are not reduced to one.

## With the other books

- **The Gate of Unity.** Light from darkness only looks brighter (Gate line 2414). Samach Vav: the hiding at its root is higher than the revealing (line 2947). Samach Vav sides with Imrei Binah here.
- **Imrei Binah.** Its lower unity higher (Imrei Binah line 183) against the Rashab's union above the higher (line 2019): close, not the same. Both put the servant's bitul above love (Imrei Binah line 424; here line 3999).
- **Kuntres HaHitpa'alut.** The deeper the arousal, the less it is felt (line 556 there). The Rashab's silent, essential bitul (lines 4493, 4542) and his fading contemplative bitul (line 4486) say the same from the other end.
- **Sefer HaArachim.** "Created every moment" is the first rung. Samach Vav agrees and adds that renewal is the cleaving itself (line 3419).

## Where the thread is thin (kept honest)

- **The OCR.** The file is uncorrected OCR of scans. The editor's footnotes are run into the text page by page, so the discourse breaks at every scan page, often mid-sentence (3,594 footnote lines). Page headers are fused onto text lines. Stray marks sit inside words (בבחיי, ית'א, "*" and digits from footnote numbers), and some words are misread: מהאוד for מהאור (line 7188), אץ for אין (lines 2307, 6627), כת לאש for כח לאש (line 7203). Quotes keep them. Lines 6907-7017 are OCR of handwriting and unreadable. Some quotes were shortened to avoid the worst runs.
- **Seven lines and seven sentences are our reading** in the Rashab's text: 302.1 (the rotting seed heard as creation), 536.1, 669, 1119.2 (the flint as creation), 1242.1, 1540, 1556 (the oil image, said here of the intellect, tied by us to the Rebbe's later use for the Essence), 2747, 2834.1 (Lag BaOmer), 2956.1, 3112.2, 4043, 5423 and 5716.
- **The soul as parable.** As in the other reports, F06 is left off lines where the soul only explains God, and kept where the text speaks of the actual souls of Israel.
- **Long technical runs.** The partzufim, the hair and skull of Arich, the letters of the Names, the flow from Z'A to Malchut, the three crowns. They are marked structural, and the tie comes at their ends.
- **Voices other than the Rashab's.** Lines 6498-9229 are the Rayatz's, the Rebbe's, the editors' and the index-makers'. A "stated" tie there means that voice states it. *Correction:* the range report for lines 6400-7270 calls the summary at lines 7020-7270 "the Rebbe's contents, chapter headings and summary". The front matter says the contents, chapter-heads and summary were compiled by R. Dovid Feldman: תוכן, ראשי פרקים וסיכום של ההמשך (נערכו ע"י הרה"ת ר' דוד שי פלדמן) (line 23), and the sections run on from א at line 7020 to עז at line 7271. This overview counts lines 7020-7706 as Feldman's. The "subject index compiled by the Rebbe" label on 144 lines of that range was not checked against the front matter.
- **The subject index, read two ways.** The readers of lines 6712-8119 marked the whole first half of the subject index "none". The reader of lines 8120-9229 marked its glosses "stated" where they state a tie (112 lines). The two methods are not reconciled; the 112 are the index-makers' summaries, and the pages they cite were not read against them.
- **Maamar labels and dates** were taken from opening verses and fused page headers. At a few boundaries in Feldman's chapter-heads (around lines 7452-7504) the label may be off by one maamar. Between lines 4899 and 4900 the dates jump from Marcheshvan to Iyar 5667; the maamarim between are not in this file.
- **The title.** The file is called Samach Vav, but its later ranges hold the continuation of 5667 and 5668 that the printed volume includes. They are counted with the Rashab's text.
