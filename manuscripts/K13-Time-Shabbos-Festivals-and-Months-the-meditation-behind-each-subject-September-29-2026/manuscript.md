## Why a meditation

Every concept in Chassidus points to the unity of God, and that unity cannot be understood by definition. It can only be glimpsed, in contemplation (*hisbonenus*). So behind each subject below is a sitting, and the sitting, not the idea, is what matters.

## How to sit with a section

This is *hisbonenus*, the Chabad way of contemplation, not mindfulness. There is nothing here to watch in the body and nothing to empty out.

1. **Hold** the one line of teaching. It is the object for the whole sitting.
2. **The move.** Think it through slowly: open it detail by detail, bring it down into your own day, take the source's own picture and remember that it is only a picture, and make it particular: you, here, now.
3. **Stay.** Bind the mind to it, the Tanya's *da'at* (Tanya 3): say the line again slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought. The pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.
4. **From inside** describes the glimpse. It is never asked for. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts: what you held in your mind carries the act that follows.
5. **Back into the day.** Say it with your lips in words of Torah or prayer, do the one mitzvah or act of Torah in your own measure, and carry the return line through the day.

**Tie** says whether the move rests on derivation steps the entry marks *stated*, or on our own *reading*; the steps are listed. **Thin** marks subjects whose tie to the unity the entry calls partly thin (2 of 13 here); their sittings are kept modest and say why. **Kept** records a disagreement the entry keeps between the sources; it is left standing, not smoothed. **Before you start** is a safety line where a subject touches health, fasting or sleep.

## S150 · Time · זמן

*Face F02 The higher and the lower unity. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F01 The world said into being now, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Time is something He makes, moment by moment. He is above it, 'was, is and will be in one instant', and yet He is found right here, in this hour.

**Hold.** 'Though He is above space and time, He is found below, within space and time.' (Shaar HaYichud 7)

**The move.** Open it. Past, present and future belong to the made world, as east and west do; the life of each moment, its coming from nothing and its lasting, flows from His kingship (Malchus); and His Name Havayah means 'was, is and will be in one instant'. Bring it down: the hour on the clock, the morning already behind you, this afternoon's appointment, all of it made now, and He is not carried along by any of it. The Shaar HaYichud's picture is the sun's ray, which is nothing on its own inside the sun; only a picture, since the sun is one thing among others and He is not. You, here, in this very hour, are where He is found: as He was alone before the six days of creation, so He is now.

**Stay.** Say the line again, slowly: 'He is found below, within time.' When the mind drifts to the day's list, come back to the line without arguing with the thought.

**From inside.** Like a ray inside the sun: the hour has nothing of its own to hold up, and still it is real, and He is in it. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Havayah reigned, Havayah reigns, Havayah will reign.' Learn one line of Shaar HaYichud chapter 7 today. Through the day, when you check the time, return to: 'was, is and will be'.

**Kept.** Recurrence or novelty? The Chernobyler: each appointed time, when it comes round, happens again as the first time. Iggeret HaKodesh 14: each year's light is new, higher than any that shone before. The contrast is the entry's own; it calls the two compatible.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:5 (line 799): שחיות המקום וכן חיות הזמן, והתהוותם מאין ליש, וקיומם כל זמן קיומם, הוא ממדת מלכותו יתברך SH 7: the life of time, its coming from nothing and its whole duration, is from His Malchus, re-created like any creature. - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:7 (line 801): כי שם הוי״ה מורה שהוא למעלה מן הזמן, שהוא היה הוה ויהיה ברגע אחד SH 7: the Name Havayah means He is above time, was-is-will-be in one instant; the Name of the Essence is not in time. - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:8 (line 802): אףעלפי שהוא יתברך למעלה מהמקום והזמן, אףעלפיכן – הוא נמצא גם למטה במקום וזמן SH 7: though above time He is found below in time too; this is the lower unity (F02).

## S151 · Day and night · יום ולילה

*Face F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart'. Also touches F09 The unity of opposites, F11 Darkness turned to light, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Evening and morning are one day; in the same way the Name that is and the Name that hides are one. Night is not a time He is absent.

**Hold.** 'Just as the day includes the night and both are one: "there was evening and there was morning, one day".' (Likkutei Torah, Vaetchanan)

**The move.** Open it. 'Know today': as the day holds its night and the two are one, so the two hearts, both inclinations of the heart, are to be one, and Havayah, the Name that is, is Elokim, the Name that hides. The order is evening first and then morning, first darkness and then light, as His light comes out of a higher darkness; and each morning's prayer draws down more than yesterday's. Bring it down: last night's dark window and this morning's first light are one day, not two. The picture is creation's own, 'darkness first, then light'; only a picture, since the sky turns and He does not. You, now, in whatever hour it is: this hour belongs to the one day He is making.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'evening and morning, one day.' When the mind goes elsewhere, come back to the line without arguing with the thought.

**From inside.** Like light that comes out of darkness, not after it: nothing broke in the night; it was one day all along. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** At nightfall, say with your lips: 'there was evening and there was morning, one day.' Say the Shema at bedtime tonight, drawing out 'One'. Through the day, return to: 'know today'.

**Kept.** How is night held? The Alter Rebbe: 'know today', for day and night are one. R. Nosson: night is the withdrawal of knowing, and one holds on by faith alone ('Your faithfulness at night'). The Rayatz stands between them: in exile faith is the main thing, but it must be brought inward. Setting these as one disagreement is the entry's reading.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Vaetchanan 7:1 (line 2943): פי׳ וידעת היום כמו שהיום הוא עם הלילה ושניהם אחד כמ״ש ויהי ערב ויהי בקר יום אחד The Alter Rebbe reads 'know today' as: just as day includes night and both are one ('one day'), so the two hearts are to be one, and so Havayah is Elokim. - Likkutei Torah, Vaetchanan 7:13 (line 2955): כברייתו של עולם ברישא חשוכא והדר נהורא שמן החושך נעשה אור כך הוא גילוי אור א״ס ב״ה מחשך עליון The order of evening then morning is the order of revelation: Ein Sof's light comes out of the higher darkness, joined by the 'awesome God' who joins the two opposites. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 14:6 (line 1156): ובפרטי פרטיות, כן הוא בכל יום ויום, נמשכין מוחין עליונים יותר בכל תפלת השחר IH 14: the yearly renewal happens in miniature every day; each morning prayer draws higher mochin than the day before.

## S152 · Shabbos · שבת

*Face F02 The higher and the lower unity. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F01 The world said into being now, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** On Shabbos everything goes back to its source, the way a mind comes back to itself after work, while He goes on making it. Even after creation nothing is separate from Him.

**Hold.** 'Shabbos is the letters of tashev, return: Shabbos and teshuvah are one, the return of things to their source and root.' (Likkutei Torah, Shabbat Shuvah)

**The move.** Open it. Even on Shabbos He renews the world, and if He withdrew for a moment it would return to nothing, so His rest is no absence; what rests is His speech, and the life spread through the six days rises back to its source, as the Name Havayah shows through Elokim after 'and they were finished'. R. Tzadok calls this the higher and lower unity of the Shema and 'Blessed be the Name of His glorious kingdom'. Bring it down to Friday afternoon: the work set down, the table laid. Likkutei Torah's picture is a person who stops working and whose mind comes back to itself; only a picture, since He neither tires nor stops giving. You, at this table, now: the week is going home to its source.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'the return of things to their source.' When the mind runs back to the week's work, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like a mind come home to itself after work; and then a week that is full of Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** This Friday night, say the kiddush's 'and they were finished' slowly with your lips. Stop your work a few minutes before you must, adding to Shabbos. Through the week, return to: 'Shabbos and teshuvah are one'.

**Kept.** Is Shabbos the goal or a pause? Torah Ohr and the Mitteler Rebbe: on Shabbos the powers rise to the Essence. Kuntres Acharon 4: that ascent is 'only for the hour'; the purpose is revelation below, through practical mitzvot. The Mitteler Rebbe's own passage goes on to descend 'down to below', and Torah Ohr says Israel 'make' Shabbos by their weekday work.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Shabbat Shuvah 3:6 (line 3832): שהרי הוא ית׳ מחדש בכל יום מע״ב וגם בשבת מחדש להיות השפעת והמשכת החיות שאילו היה מסתלק כרגע ח״ו היה העולם חוזר לאין ואפס LT Shabbat Shuvah: He renews creation on Shabbos too, or the world would return to nothing; His 'rest' is not a withdrawal (F01). - Likkutei Torah, Shabbat Shuvah 3:8 (line 3834): ולכן שבת אותיות תשב שענין שבת וענין תשובה הכל אחד דהיינו חזרת הדברים למקורן ושרשן Shabbos is the return of all things to their source, like a person's mind withdrawing from a finished work; Shabbos and teshuvah are one. - Peri Tzadik, Sefer Kedushat Shabbat 6:1 (line 59): הוא סוד יחודא עלאה ויחודא תתאה דשמע ובשכמל"ו R. Tzadok (Kedushat Shabbat 6): the secret of Shabbos, 'united in the secret of One', is the higher and lower unity of Shema and Baruch Shem; even after creation nothing is separate from Him.

## S153 · The new moon and the months · ראש חודש ולבנה

*Face F01 The world said into being now. Also touches F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F11 Darkness turned to light, F02 The higher and the lower unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Each month the life of things is given again from nothing. The moon going to nothing and filling again shows that the hiding lasts only an instant.

**Hold.** 'Every month is a renewal of vitality from nothing to something.' (Likkutei Torah, Rosh Hashanah)

**The move.** Open it. The year is like a body with a head and limbs: Rosh Hashanah is the head, and each month is life given afresh, not carried over. R. Nosson: the moon's waning to nothing is the tzimtzum, the space emptied of light, and its filling again the moment it reaches nothing shows that the tzimtzum lasts only an instant. Sfas Emes: renewal comes when it is made clear that everything is His vitality; one sunk in nature says 'there is nothing new'. Bring it down: this month's first thin crescent over your street, this month's plans and bills, all given new. The moon is the sources' own picture; only a picture, since the moon wanes and He does not. You, this month: not a continuation, a new gift.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'from nothing to something, this month.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like the first thin crescent after the dark nights: what went to nothing did not stay there. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'This month is for you.' On Rosh Chodesh, give one coin to charity. Through the month, return to: 'from nothing to something'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Rosh Hashanah 1:2 (line 3697): כי כל חודש הוא התחדשות החיות מאין ליש LT Rosh Hashanah: the month is a renewal of vitality from nothing to something; the year is the 'head' and each month a limb. - Likutei Halakhot, Orach Chaim, Laws for Afternoon Prayer 4:4:1 (line 2509): ועל-כן נתקשה משה בסוד קדוש החדש, כי הוא בחינת סוד חלל הפנוי R. Nosson: the moon's waning to nothing and immediate refilling is the secret of the empty space; the tzimtzum lasts only an instant, which is why Moshe found the new moon hard. - Sefat Emet, Exodus, Bo 1:6 (line 2184): ובשעת הגאולה דהיינו שנתברר שהכל חיות הש"י. ומזה בא התחדשות Sfas Emes (Bo): 'this month is for you': renewal comes when it is made clear that everything is His vitality; one sunk in nature says 'nothing is new'.

## S154 · Rosh Hashanah and Elul · ראש השנה ואלול

*Face F01 The world said into being now. Also touches F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F07 The home below, F05 The Essence beyond both. Tie: stated (derivation steps 2, 4, 5, 6). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Once a year the whole world is made again: the old year's light goes back to its root, a new light comes down through one simple cry, and it waits for what you do to show.

**Hold.** 'Each year's light withdraws to its root on the eve of Rosh Hashanah', and through the shofar a new light comes down that has never shone before. (Iggeret HaKodesh 14)

**The move.** Open it. On Rosh Hashanah 'all returns to as it was', and we ask Him to remember the first thought and create again; the shofar is a simple voice from the inside of the heart, above speech and wisdom, and by it the worlds are renewed from nothing; and the new light is hidden, and its showing depends on the deeds and teshuvah of those below in the Ten Days. Bring it down: the year that is ending, your plans and your habits; none of it rolls on into next year by itself. The picture for Elul is Likkutei Torah's: the King in the field, whom anyone may go out to meet; only a picture, since He is no king of flesh and needs no field. You, this Elul, in your own field, your street and your work: He is there to be met.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'a new light, waiting for my deed.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like meeting the King in the field: near, and open to anyone who goes out. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'the King is in the field.' Each morning this Elul, give one coin before prayer. Through the day, return to: 'the King is in the field'.

**Kept.** Recurrence or novelty? Iggeret HaKodesh 14: each year a new light that never shone before. The Chernobyler: each appointed time happens again as the first time. The entry calls them compatible and the contrast its own.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 14:5 (line 1155): כי אור כל שנה ושנה מסתלק לשרשו בכל ערב ראשהשנה IH 14: each year's light withdraws to its root on the eve of Rosh Hashanah; through shofar and prayer a new light, never before revealed, comes down. - Likkutei Torah, Rosh Hashanah 1:6 (line 3701): תקעו תקיעה הוא קול פשוט היוצא מהבל הלב מפנימיותו LT Rosh Hashanah 1: the shofar is a simple voice from the inner heart, above speech and wisdom; it draws the inner delight and will that renew the worlds. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 14:11 (line 1161): אך גילויו מההסתר הזה, תלוי במעשה התחתונים וזכותם ותשובתם בעשרת ימי תשובה IH 14: the new light is hidden, and its revelation depends on the deeds and teshuvah of those below (F07).

## S155 · Yom Kippur · יום הכפורים

*Face F06 The soul as part of the One. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F02 The higher and the lower unity, F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Underneath everything you did there is a point of you that was never apart from Him. On Yom Kippur it is 'Israel and the King alone'.

**Hold.** '"Before Havayah you shall be purified": before Havayah, specifically.' (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 1)

**The move.** Open it. For some wrongs there is no atonement 'above' until Yom Kippur, 'before Havayah' specifically; on that day the prayers need no angels to carry them, for the Holy One Himself receives them; and the Rebbe: its atonement comes from the essence of the day itself, which reveals the bond of the soul's essence with His Essence, where no flaw ever applied. Bring it down: the year behind you with its wrongs, and under all of it the plain 'I' that stands before Him. The Rebbe's picture is the Holy of Holies, where there is no room even for angels, 'Israel and the King alone'; only a picture, since He needs no room and no house. You, now, at the level Neilah reveals: the single point of the soul, the yechidah, that never went anywhere.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'before Havayah.' When the mind runs to the list of what went wrong, come back to the two words without arguing.

**From inside.** A core no one has ever touched: 'Israel and the King alone', no one standing between. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Before Havayah you shall be purified.' Tonight, say the Shema at bedtime, forgiving whoever wronged you. Through the day, return to: 'Israel and the King alone'.

**Kept.** Kuntres Acharon 4 holds that the ascent of Shabbos and Yom Kippur is 'only for the hour'; the purpose is revelation below through practical mitzvot. So the height of the day is carried down into deeds, not rested in.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part III; Iggeret HaTeshuvah 1:4 (line 859): ״לפני ה׳ תטהרו״ – ״לפני ה׳״ דייקא IT 1: for prohibitions there is no atonement 'above' until Yom Kippur, 'before Havayah' specifically. - Likkutei Torah, Shabbat Shuvah 3:34 (line 3860): אמנם ביוהכ״פ אין צריך שיעלו התפלות ע״י המלאכים כ״א הקב״ה בעצמו מקבלן LT Shabbat Shuvah 3: during the Ten Days Or Ein Sof itself is drawn, not through the chain; on Yom Kippur He Himself receives the prayers, hence no shoes. - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. ש"פ האזינו, שבת תשובה ה'תשכ"ג:15 (line 149): מתגלה ההתקשרות דעצם הנשמה עם העצמות, דבעצמות אוא"ס אין שום נתינת מקום למלאכים The Rebbe: in the Holy of Holies the essential bond of the soul's essence with the Essence is revealed; there is no room for angels, 'Israel and the King alone'.

## S156 · Sukkos, Shemini Atzeres and Simchas Torah · סוכות ושמיני עצרת

*Face F04 Filling and surrounding. Also touches F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity, F06 The soul as part of the One. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The light that no thing contains surrounds you the same on every side, like the walls of the sukkah. On Shemini Atzeres it is taken inside.

**Hold.** 'On Sukkos the joy is revealed as sukkah and makif, surrounding; on Shemini Atzeres it is taken into the inwardness of souls.' (Likkutei Torah)

**The move.** Open it. The wise son understands that his father hides only so that he will be sought, so the hiding of Rosh Hashanah was 'for the day of our festival'; on Sukkos the joy comes from above as the sukkah, a light that surrounds; the four kinds, four sorts of Jew in one mitzvah, 'one bundle', are 'a true unity above division', not parts glued together; and on Shemini Atzeres the surrounding light is taken inside. Bring it down: the sukkah's walls on every side of your table, the open roof of branches over it, or, out of season, your own house around you. The sukkah is the sources' own picture; only a picture, since walls stand in a place and He is in no place. You, sitting here now: surrounded the same on every side, and on the eighth day it is yours inside.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'surrounding, then inside.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Held in a way that has no edge, the same on every side; then, on the eighth day, 'yours alone'. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Every citizen in Israel shall dwell in sukkos.' In the season eat one meal in the sukkah; out of season, sit for a few minutes with a Jew very unlike you. Through the day, return to: 'one bundle'.

**Kept.** The Alter Rebbe's sukkah is light from above, surrounding. R. Nosson's is holiness widened outward from below after teshuvah, bringing even the outside in. A difference of emphasis, kept.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Sukkot 4:11 (line 4088): פירוש בן חכם ישמח אב כשהבן מבין שההסתר שאביו מסתיר א״ע ממנו הוא רק בכדי שיחפשנו LT Sukkot 4: the wise son understands that the father hides only to be sought; the hiding of Rosh Hashanah is 'for the day of our festival', the revelation of Sukkos (F03; the parable is familiar from the Besht, but LT does not name him here). - Likkutei Torah, Shemini Atzeret 5:19 (line 4175): דבסוכות השמחה המתגלה מלמעלה למטה בחינת ישמח ה׳ במעשיו היא בבחינת סוכה ומקיף. ובשמיני עצרת הוא יוצא מבחי׳ מקיף להיות נקלט ונתפס בבחי׳ פנימיות נש״י LT Shemini Atzeret 5: on Sukkos the joy is encompassing (sukkah, makif); on Shemini Atzeret it enters the inwardness of souls (F04). - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. אור לי"ג תשרי (הילולא דאדמו"ר מהר"ש נ"ע), ה'תשמ"ג:7 (line 307): היא אחדות אמיתית שלמעלה מהתחלקות The Rebbe (13 Tishrei 5743): the four species as one mitzvah, four kinds of Jews as 'one bundle', is a true unity above division, not a joining of parts (the match to 'yachid' rather than 'echad' is ours; he does not use those terms).

## S157 · Pesach and the Exodus · פסח ויציאת מצרים

*Face F05 The Essence beyond both. Also touches F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity, F07 The home below, F06 The soul as part of the One. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** What takes you out of your limits is not a light or a level but God Himself, 'Anochi'. What goes out each day is the swollen self, leaving something flat and plain as matzah.

**Hold.** '"Who took you out of the land of Egypt", and not "who created": creation is only from a radiance, but the Exodus is from Anochi, His Essence.' (the Maharash)

**The move.** Open it. Only His Essence takes a person out of limits, so that there be a dwelling below, 'below as above'; every day one must see oneself leaving Egypt, the divine soul going out of the body's prison to be included in the unity of the infinite light, above all through the Shema; and the Tzemach Tzedek: chametz rises and swells, the selfhood of 'something', even the pride of great understanding, while matzah is flat, the self given up. Bring it down: the place where you are puffed up today, the argument you must win, the thing you must be seen to know. Risen dough and flat matzah are the sources' own picture; only a picture, to settle the ear. You, today: taken out by Him, not by anything you reached.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'who took you out'. When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Plain, like matzah: nothing puffed up to keep up, taken out by Him and not by anything you reached. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips the close of the Shema: 'I am Havayah your God, who took you out of the land of Egypt.' Today let go of one argument you only wanted to win. Through the day, return to: 'out of Egypt, today'.

**Kept.** Radiance or Essence in creation? The Maharash, with Torah Ohr (Esther 12:23): creation from nothing is only from a radiance, while the Exodus is from the Essence. Iggeret HaKodesh 20 and the Rebbe: only the Essence can create something from nothing. Kept, not resolved.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלה:3 (line 713): ולא אמר אשר בראתי יש מאין כי הבריאה יש מאין היא רק מהארה בעלמא The Maharash: the first commandment says 'who took you out of Egypt', not 'who created', because creation is only from a radiance. - Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:1 (line 714): שאנכי בחי' עצמותו ית' שאינו בחי' הארה בעלמא, ומצד בחי' זו נמשך להיות אשר הוצאתיך מאמ"צ The Maharash: the Exodus comes from 'Anochi', His Essence, so that there be a dwelling below, 'below as above' (F05, F07). - Derekh Mitzvotekha, To not eat Chametz 1 (line 55): אבל עיסת החמץ מורה על הגבהה והתנשאות שתפח ועולה למעלה והוא רמז על גדלות ההשגה בבחי' יש ודבר מה The Tzemach Tzedek: chametz, which rises, is the selfhood of 'something', even the pride of great comprehension; matzah is the bitul of faith (F08).

## S158 · Counting the Omer · ספירת העומר

*Face F11 Darkness turned to light. Also touches F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity, F09 The unity of opposites, F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** Even the animal side of you, its wants and its tempers, can be refined one day at a time, until it too can give itself up to the One.

**Hold.** 'So that even the animal soul can give itself up in One (echad).' (the Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha)

**The move.** Open it. On the first night of Pesach the light comes from above, leaping, and then withdraws, and the counting is the effort from below to draw it again; the omer is barley, animal food, and the counting refines the seven traits of the animal soul, each containing seven, 'so that all be for God'; and at Sinai, when 'Anochi' is revealed, even the animal soul can give itself up in One. Bring it down: tonight's count, and one trait of yours that runs by itself, a quick answer or a hunger for credit. The barley offering is the sources' own picture; only a picture, to help the ear hold it. You, tonight: one day, one trait, turned toward the One.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'even the animal soul, in One.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The dignity of a small daily step: one trait at a time, counted, so that all be for God. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Tonight, count the Omer with its blessing, with your lips; out of season, say the bedtime Shema aloud, drawing out 'One'. Tomorrow give that one trait one small turn: hold back one hasty word. Through the day, return to: 'so that all be for God'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the explicit tie to the unity rests on one text, the Tzemach Tzedek's 'give itself up in One'; otherwise the Omer ties to the unity one step removed, through refinement and bitul. So this sitting stays modest.

**Kept.** What is the counting for? Chabad: refining the animal soul toward Sinai. Ropshitz: including each trait in the others at their root, like 'love your fellow'. A difference of emphasis.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the Omer 5 (line 61): והתועלת מזה הוא שגם נפש הבהמית תוכל למסור נפשה באחד ע"י גילוי בחי' אנכי The Tzemach Tzedek: the counting draws the hidden light into the omer (animal food) step by step, so that even the animal soul can give itself up in 'echad' when 'Anochi' is revealed at Sinai. - Likkutei Torah, Pekudei 1:53 (line 98): והאתעדל״ת דספירת העומר ידוע שהיא התהפכות השבע מדות דנפש הבהמית וכל מדה כלולה מז׳ שתהיינה כולם לה׳ LT Pekudei: the first night of Pesach is a revelation from above that then withdraws; the Omer is the effort from below, the transformation of the animal soul's seven traits, each including seven, 'so that all be for God'. - Likkutei Torah, Vayikra 2:26 (line 215): וספירת העומר וספרתם לכם בחינת בירור ז׳ מדות דנה״ב LT Vayikra: the omer is barley, animal food, for refining the animal soul, and the counting refines its seven traits.

## S159 · Shavuos · שבועות

*Face F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart'. Also touches F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual, F05 The Essence beyond both, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** At Sinai 'there is none besides Him' was not thought about but seen. And in the Torah He gave us Himself.

**Hold.** '"You were shown to know that Havayah is God; there is none besides Him": shown, with physical sight.' (Tanya 36)

**The move.** Open it. At Sinai the speech came from every direction, above and below; because His inner will was shown without any hiding, they were nullified in existence and their souls flew out; and the Torah they received clothes His will and wisdom, which are one with Him, so that giving it was 'as if He gave us Himself'. Bring it down: the book of Torah on your table, the page you learned last; the words on it are that same will. The Tanya's picture is a gift that is the giver; only a picture, and the Tanya itself says 'as if', since a gift of flesh is separate from its giver and this one is not. You, now, holding a line of Torah: you are not holding a sign of Him.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'shown: there is none besides Him.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like a gift that turns out to be the Giver: not a sign of Him, Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'there is none besides Him.' Learn one line of Torah today before anything else you read. Through the day, return to: 'as if He gave us Himself'.

**Kept.** Which revelation is higher? Tanya 36: Sinai was a foretaste of the messianic revelation. Torah Ohr and Sfas Emes: Sinai's acceptance was forced from above and Purim's came from below, so Sinai was only 'what they began'. The Rebbe: the moment of Sinai was above time and contains all time (offered as 'it may be said').

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:9 (line 492): וגם כבר היה לעולמים מעין זה, בשעת מתן תורה, כדכתיב: ״אתה הראת לדעת כי ה׳ הוא האלהים אין עוד מלבדו״ – ״הראת״ ממש, בראיה חושיית Tanya 36: Sinai was a foretaste of the messianic revelation: 'ein od milvado' seen with physical sight, speech heard from every direction. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:10 (line 493): ולכן היו בטלים במציאות ממש Tanya 36: because His inner will was revealed without concealment in the Ten Commandments, they were nullified in existence, their souls flew out. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 47:3 (line 673): והרי זה כאלו נתן לנו את עצמו כביכול Tanya 47: in giving the Torah, in which His will and wisdom are clothed, He as it were gave us Himself (F10).

## S160 · Chanukah · חנוכה

**From inside.** The sweetness of light that came out of darkness, reaching even the one standing outside. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'a mitzvah is a lamp and Torah is light.' Do one mitzvah today in the least holy place of your day, the office, the street or the train. Through the day, return to: 'darkness into light'.

## S161 · Purim · פורים

The hidden face is the verse's own picture; only a picture, since He has no face, and for Him hiding is not going away. You, now, in ordinary days: what you take on here, unforced, is the higher acceptance.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'I will surely hide My face.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The relief of finding what was never gone: the hiding and the saving were one story. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'I will surely hide My face.' Give one coin to a poor person today. Through the day, return to: 'hidden, not gone'.

**Kept.** Which is higher, Sinai or Purim? Torah Ohr and Sfas Emes: Purim, taken on from below. Tanya 36: Sinai was a foretaste of the messianic revelation. ## S162 · Fasts and the Three Weeks · בין המצרים ותענית

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F11 Darkness turned to light, F06 The soul as part of the One, F02 The higher and the lower unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4). Thin: partly.*

**Before you start.** Fasting is not for everyone: whoever is ill, pregnant or nursing asks a rav first. This sitting asks no one to fast; it holds only the line.

**The glimpse.** What was lost is revealed light gone back into hiding, not His absence; and the soul itself was never in exile.

**Hold.** '"For these I weep": over the withdrawal of the light and the revelation called "these".' (Likkutei Torah, Pekudei)

**The move.** Open it. The destruction is the withdrawal of revealed light back into its source, into hiding, and the weeping is over the hiding; the Rebbe, citing the Rayatz: only the body is in exile, not the soul, which is a part of God, and the descent of exile is for an ascent higher than before; and R. Nosson reads 'Eichah', how, as 'Ayeh', where: even from the lowest place one asks 'where is the place of His glory?'. Bring it down: the fast day or the weeks of mourning, and the place in your life where something that was open has gone quiet. The Rebbe's picture is the verse 'I sleep, but my heart is awake'; only a picture, to help the ear hold it. You, now, wherever you are: the question 'where?' is asked from here.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'where is the place of His glory?' When the mind drifts, come back to the question without arguing.

**From inside.** The weeping of 'for these I weep' is over a hiding, not an absence: the One being looked for is there. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'where is the place of His glory?' Today learn one line of Torah about the Temple. Through the day, return to: 'hidden, not gone'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the Three Weeks and the destruction are tied to the unity by stated texts, but the fast as such only through sacrifice, teshuvah and Likkutei Torah's 'day of will'. So this sitting stays with the hiding, not the fast.

**Kept.** Does exile reach the soul and the Shechinah? The Rebbe, citing the Rayatz: only the body is in exile, not the soul. Likkutei Torah (Shir HaShirim) and R. Nosson: the Shechinah itself falls and is in exile. The entry reads these as two kinds of knowing; no text says so.
