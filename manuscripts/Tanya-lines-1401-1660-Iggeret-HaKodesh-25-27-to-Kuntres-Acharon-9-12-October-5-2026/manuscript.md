## How the range builds the idea

The range closes the Tanya. It holds the last eight letters of Iggeret HaKodesh (25 from its 27th paragraph, through 32) and all of Kuntres Acharon. Two threads run through it. The first is F10, which 109 of the 258 lines carry: the halacha and the practical mitzvah are where the Essence is held, more than in the mind that contemplates it. The second is the divine going down into the lowest place, even into the husks, so that the lowest can be refined (F03, F11, F07). The range also gives the book's last word on knowing versus believing (F12), and that word has two sides.

**IH 25, end (1401-1414): the Shechinah in the voice that disturbs prayer.** The letter explains a saying in Tzava'at HaRivash. Its frame, set before the range, is that anger is "like idolatry" because the angry man forgets that the harm came from God. Within the range, even the husks and the nations live only from the Shechinah clothed in them "in exile" (1401-1402). So when a gentile's talk disturbs one's prayer, the counsel is to see that "ניצוץ הארתה מתלבש בבחינת גלות דרך פרט, בדבור נכרי זה" (1407). Seeing this drives the prayer so deep that he no longer hears. This is G03 stated: once the hiding is known, it serves the revelation. The proof is the lowest matter, "אין לך גשמי כעפר הארץ, ואף על פי כן מתלבשת בו מלכות דמלכות דעשיה" (1410). Then comes a position on F12 that the index must keep. The matter is not hidden Kabbalah, "כי אם – מ״הנגלות לנו ולבנינו״, להאמין אמונה שלימה במקרא מלא" (1411), namely "Do I not fill heaven and earth". It is the simple faith of Israel, held "בלי לחקור בשכל אנושי ענין האלקות, אשר הוא למעלה מהשכל עד אין קץ" (1412). Investigation began only "רק, שחדשים מקרוב באו לחקור בחקירה זו" (1413), and for such people the explanation is given orally. The hisbonenus ROUTER (C35) names this letter as the Chabad contemplation for a grudge and has no path built on it. Lines 1404-1407 are the second half of that contemplation.

**IH 26 (1415-1450): the halacha is the unity, and the object is the husk.** The letter reads a Ra'aya Meheimna passage that seems to put the study of "forbidden and permitted" under the tree of good and evil. Lines 1418-1428 are a chain of proofs against that reading. They are structural, and the Talmudic examples inside them (1421, 1425-1427) are "none". The turn comes at 1429: "דגוף דבר האסור ודבר המותר – הוא מאילנא דטוב ורע, שהוא קליפת נוגה" (1429). The objects belong to the mixed realm, and the Torah that rules on them does not. The permitted can rise, because when a person eats it for God "ונמצא שנעשה אותיות התורה והתפלה העולה לה׳" (1430). The forbidden cannot rise, unless the sages permitted it to save a life (1432). The study itself is the unity. In Atzilus Or Ein Sof is united so that "שהוא ורצונו וחכמתו המלובשים בדבורו שנקרא ״מלכות״ – הכל אחד" (1433). Every halacha is "a spark of the Shechinah, which is the word of God" (1442), fallen into Nogah. Clarifying an obscure ruling therefore lifts it out of the husks, and the Zohar's "שהקושיא היא מסטרא דרע" (1439) names the concealment that study undoes. Only the lower beings can do this, "ולכן באים העליונים לשמוע חידושי תורה מהתחתונים" (1440), which is G05 stated. At the end the order of study turns over. Once the refinement is done, the inner Torah leads, "for the root of the mitzvot is very high in Ein Sof" (1445), and the revealed law is known without effort (1447-1448).

**IH 27 (1451-1469): thin.** This is a consolation on the passing of R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk. The tzaddik's life is "שחיי הצדיק אינם חיים בשרים כי אם חיים רוחניים" (1461), namely faith, awe and love, and after death it spreads to his disciples, "והארה זו היא בהעלם והסתר גדול" (1469). Only one line states a tie: the "feminine waters" of his deeds make a union above (1467). The rest is reading (7 lines) or none (5). The letter also cites the Zohar that without the tzaddikim's prayer "ואלמלא צלותא דצדיקייא בההוא עלמא – לא אתקים עלמא רגעא חדא" (1457). Pillar 03 says Tanya's tzaddik is "a rank, not a channel". This letter and IH 28, where the passing of tzaddikim atones, qualify that. The index records them and does not fold them into the one idea.

**IH 28 (1470-1478): the highest source reaches the lowest impurity.** Why is Miriam's death set beside the red heifer? The heifer's waters draw from the "hidden brain" of Arich Anpin, through which "ו״אתהפכא חשוכא לנהורא״" (1474), so they purify corpse impurity, far below Nogah. The passing of tzaddikim who gave their souls in the Shema reveals "שכל עמל האדם שעמלה נפשו בחייו למעלה בבחינת העלם והסתר, מתגלה ומאיר בבחינת גילוי מלמעלה למטה בעת פטירתו" (1475). It atones "אף גם על הזדונות שהן מג׳ קליפות הטמאות שלמטה מנוגה" (1476), while the altar offerings reach only unintentional sins. F11 and G05 are both stated here.

**IH 29 (1479-1505): the crown of Torah.** The soul needs garments because "ואי אפשר לשום נברא להשיג שום השגה בבורא ויוצר הכל" (1483). Unmediated, the light would nullify it, "ותתבטל ממציאותה כנר באבוקה" (1485), so it is clothed in the mitzvot: concealment for the sake of revelation. The will clothed in the mitzvot is the "back" of His delight (1489). The text then makes G06's move itself: "שהוא יתברך ורצונו אחד, ולא כרצון האדם חס ושלום, לא מיניה ולא מקצתיה" (1490), and the soul-analogy serves only "to soothe the ear". Even the mitzvot of heart and mind were given to the body: "הרי המצוה ניתנה לאדם הגשמי שבעולם הזה דוקא" (1493). They are pillars "נצבים מרום המעלות, הוא רצון העליון ברוך הוא, עד הארץ הלזו החומרית" (1494). The will is hidden in the Written Torah, and the commandments "באים מההעלם אל הגילוי בתורה שבעל פה" (1503). Pillar 09 records the same seed-and-birth image from Torah Ohr. So halachot are "the crown" because they reveal the will, which is above wisdom. The text keeps its own qualification (1495): elsewhere the mitzvot are the *inner* will, because face and back are relative.

**IH 30-32 (1506-1529): charity joins the infinite to the finite and holds the future light.** In IH 30, giving to the poor draws from "the Life of life, Ein Sof" into a world of number (1509). Its "peace" is "הוא דבר המחבר ומתווך ב׳ קצוות הפכיים" (1510), F09 stated. In IH 31 "the Shechinah is sick in exile". All creatures receive life through the souls of Israel, because "דכולא קמיה כלא ממש חשיבין" (1516). When the souls are bound together, the circuit runs "לקשר ולחבר כולן להוי׳ אחד ולדבקה בו יתברך" (1518). The Temple fell "בעון שנאת חנם ופירוד לבבות" (1519). That is F06 as the root of love of Israel, the claim pillar 22 finds in Polonoye ("hatred divides the roots above"). In IH 32, kindness done now stays in this world until the resurrection, "זמן גילוי אלקות ואור אין סוף ברוך הוא מבחינת סובב כל עלמין בעולם הזה" (1522). For that, "וצריך להיות כלי ומכון להתלבש בו אור אין סוף ברוך הוא" (1523), and the vessel is the trait of kindness. F07 and F04 are stated together: the surrounding light is to be revealed "down, down" in the physical world (1528).

**KA 1-3 (1533-1551): why speech and deed, and what blocks ascent.** Thought alone draws nothing down, and that fails the soul's purpose: "מה שירדה נשמתו לעולם הזה רק להמשיך אורות עליונים למטה" (1536). The union "face to face" comes only through practical mitzvot (1539). KA 3 compares Torah and prayer without intention. Study done "to be made great" stays "under the sun", "שמחשבתו וכוונתו הן מתלבשות באותיות הדבור ואינן מניחות אותן לסלקא לעילא" (1546). That selfhood is what blocks the ascent (F08) is our reading. The harmonizations of Zohar passages (1548-1550) are "none".

**KA 4 (1552-1609): the deed holds the essence.** This is the core of the range, with 43 stated lines. Prayer draws Or Ein Sof into creation "לא בדרך התלבשות בלבד, רק האור ממש, לשנות הנבראים מכמות שהם" (1555), since only Ein Sof, "שהוא לבדו כל יכול" (1556), changes what creatures are. The practical mitzvot of the Land are "שהן תכלית ההשתלשלות" (1564). Then comes the central argument. By contemplating His attributes, "איננו דבק אפילו במדות העליונות אלא במציאותן, ולא במהותן" (1566), because "שאי אפשר לנברא להשיג כלום במהות האלקות" (1572). The etrog's life, however, comes from the essence of the vessels of Atzilus, so one who waves it "הרי זה תופס ממש חיותו המלובש בו מנוקבא דאצילות, המיוחדת באור אין סוף המאציל" (1574). Knowing is not dismissed. Knowing the existence of the chain of worlds "היא גם כן מצוה רמה ונשאה, ואדרבה – עולה על כולנה" (1577), "Know today", because it brings a whole heart. But it is one of the 613 (1578). G05 is stated outright: the sparks refined in food are rooted higher than the soul, whose source in Mah is "הוא יוצא מה״מצח״ הארה בעלמא" (1579), while the etrog reaches Sag, "ולא הארה בעלמא כמו שם ״מ״ה״ שממצחו" (1592). The "drop" that carries essence "יש בה כח המוליד ומהווה יש מאין" (1581), and speech does not. The purpose is stated twice, first "וזהו תכלית ההשתלשלות, להתגלות אור עליון למטה, ולא לעלות התחתון למעלה" (1589), then "כי זהו תכלית ההשתלשלות שירד העליון למטה ויהיה לו דירה בתחתונים" (1608), in order to raise the lower realms "to be one in One". The details of halacha are wisdom clothed in physical things. Created things exist "בהסתר והעלם הבורא מהנברא" (1596), but the law shines openly, for "ההלכה עצמה – אינה גשמיות ממש" (1598). When a person learns, he draws Or Ein Sof into this world "להיות נכלל ובטל באורו יתברך, ״כי זה כל האדם״" (1607). Lines 1607 and 1608 hold together the two emphases the spine keeps apart under F07: bitul (the Rashab) and a dwelling for the Essence (the Rebbe). That the two meet here is our reading.

**KA 5 (1610-1619): every detail.** Even a prohibition lives from above, "שאם לא כן לא היה יכול להיות במציאות בעולם בלתי השפעה עליונה" (1611), which is F01 stated of the forbidden. A detail that never happens still exists, "על כל פנים ישנו במציאות, להבדיל, בחכמה עילאה" (1612). The Tzemach Tzedek's gloss in the same line adds a root in Nogah. The reason is G04 in the Alter Rebbe's words, "כי התפשטות חכמה עילאה היא בבחינת ״אין סוף״ המלובש בה בפועל ממש" (1613), and each detail is a "hair" of that wisdom. In the future the prohibitions return to their root as holy severities, "ולהמתיקן בחסדים ברמ״ח מצות עשה, ולכללן יחד" (1618): F09, placed at the end of days.

**KA 6 (1620-1634): the two unities in the Torah itself.** All worlds are "תלוים בדקדוק מצוה אחת" (1622), and the contemplation sees "ואיך כולם בטלים במציאות לגבי דקדוק אחד מדקדוקי תורה" (1625). David was punished for praising the Torah by this, because it is only the Torah's "back". The inner Torah is one with Or Ein Sof, "ולגבי אין סוף ברוך הוא, כל העולמות כלא ממש ואין ואפס ממש" (1628). This is F02, the higher unity, stated of the Torah: it cannot be praised for the life it gives to worlds that count as nothing. The fault is repaired by carrying the ark on the shoulder, "לחבר וליחד את ה״כתפיים״" (1634), joining back to face. The Tzemach Tzedek builds on this letter directly. In Derekh Mitzvotekha (carrying the Ark on the shoulder 2) he quotes it word for word, "וכמבואר שם בכי"ק וז"ל" (Derekh Mitzvotekha, line 88). He then adds IH 29's will and delight: "ומצד בחי' הרצון שבמצות שהוא בחי' חיצונית לגבי התענוג הוא השבח הזה ששיבח דהע"ה" (Derekh Mitzvotekha, line 89). Pillar 18 cites that Tzemach Tzedek passage, and KA 6 is its source.

**KA 7 (1635-1640): the unity in the heart.** In the future, "שיתגלה אז אור אין סוף ברוך הוא ויחודו יתברך תוך פנימית נקודת הלב" (1635), with the heart nullified in His unity. Until then, the spark of Godliness that cannot shine into the heart "הרי זה בבחינת גלות ממש" (1637). Mercy above redeems it, and mercy below rouses mercy above: "ולכן, פעולת הצדקה, היא פעולת ״נחל איתן״ ממש" (1639). F06, F08 and F07 are stated in one short essay.

**KA 8-9 (1641-1660): the last generation's service, then regulations.** KA 8 opens as a rebuke about prayer leaders (1641-1642, none). Inside it is the doctrine of the last generations, "ועיקר העבודה בעקבות משיחא היא התפלה" (1643). The contemplation before the Shema is "והיא חובה של תורה ממש, למביני מדע – תועלת ההתבוננות ועומק הדעת קצת" (1644), so that the hidden love reaches the heart. That is the refinement of the vital soul, "שהוא בחינת אתהפכא או אתכפיא של נפש החיונית לנפש האלקית" (1647), F11 and F12 stated. Earlier generations needed only a moment (1648). KA 9 is communal regulation (1649-1656, none). The book ends on Shabbat. Its inner side is to cleave to the One God (1659). Its "keep" is ceasing from mundane speech, "כמו ששבת ה׳ מיו״ד מאמרות שנבראו בהם שמים וארץ גשמיים" (1660). The Tanya's last line traces human speech back to the divine speech that made the world (G01).

## Where the tie is thin, and what is kept apart

- **Thin.** The tie is thin in four places: IH 27 on the tzaddik's passing (12 of its 19 lines are reading or none), the proof chain of IH 26 (1418-1428, structural or none), the Zohar harmonizations of KA 3 (1548-1550), and the regulations of KA 8-9 (1641-1642, 1649-1656). In all, 29 lines are marked none and 13 reading, and no tie is forced on them.
- **Knowledge or faith (F12): the Alter Rebbe is on both sides.** IH 25 places His presence in everything among "the revealed things", to be believed with simple faith. Investigation is a remedy for "newcomers" (1411-1413). KA 4 says no creature grasps essence (1566-1572), yet knowing the existence of the chain of worlds "exceeds them all" (1577). KA 8 makes contemplation before the Shema "a Torah obligation" for those who understand (1644). These differ in audience and in object (how He fills the world, that it exists, the heart). The text does not reconcile them. The spine's F12 disagreement between Rebbeim therefore has a version inside the Alter Rebbe's own letters.
- **Where the forbidden sits.** IH 26 calls both the forbidden and the permitted object "the tree of good and evil, which is Kelipat Nogah" (1429). Tanya 7 puts forbidden foods "שהן משלש קליפות הטמאות לגמרי" (Tanya 7:9, line 162), and pillar 05 records the same in the Tzemach Tzedek. The rule is the same in both: the permitted rises and the forbidden is bound. Tanya 7 even gives the same reading of "permitted": "שאינו קשור ואסור בידי החיצונים" (Tanya 7:6, line 159). The placement differs, and the index keeps the difference.
- **Radiance or essence (F05).** KA 4 puts creation from nothing in what carries essence: the "drop" begets, and speech does not (1581). It ranks the lowest objects (Sag, "the essence of the lights") above the souls (Mah, "a mere radiance") (1579, 1592). That sides with IH 20 against the Maharash's "creation is only a mere radiance" (spine F05j). But it also ranks speech below the drop, while Shaar HaYichud makes speech the medium of creation, and KA 4:22 (1573) calls the Ten Utterances the vessels of Atzilus, "essence in essence". All of these formulations are kept.
- **Deed above intellect.** KA 4 ranks practical mitzvot and their study above intellectual love and awe (1566). A deed no one else can do overrides even the study of the Chariot (1565). Pillar 13 records Chabad's ranking of intellectual love above natural love. KA 4 compares a different pair and does not contradict that ranking, but it does put the deed above the mind. Reading this with the Rebbe's "only through action is the essence of Chassidus grasped" (G11) is ours.
- **Opposites.** The forbidden cannot rise now (1432). Only in the future are the prohibitions sweetened and "included together" with the positive commandments (1618). The same eating is gluttony on a weekday and a mitzvah on Shabbat (1431). The union of opposites is placed at the end, and conduct keeps them apart.
- **An open question.** The last line has God "ceasing" from the Ten Utterances (1660), while SH 1 holds that the Utterances stand in creation always. The text does not address this, and the index leaves it open.
