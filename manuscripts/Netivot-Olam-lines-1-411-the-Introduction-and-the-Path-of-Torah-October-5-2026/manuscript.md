Netivot Olam ("Paths of the World") is the Maharal's book of character. It has 32 paths, each built on sayings of the sages. This range is the Introduction and the first and longest path, the Path of Torah. It is the Maharal's picture of what learning does to a person, and why.

The main idea is simple. The Torah is not only a book of laws. It is the *order* of the whole world. The world is one because it hangs on one order. A person who learns is joined to that order, and through it to the One who made it.

## 2.1 The Torah is the order, and order is one

The book opens its first path with this: "שאין התורה רק סדר מציאות העולם בכללו" (11, Netiv HaTorah 1:2): "for the Torah is nothing but the order of the whole world's existence." That is why the Midrash says G-d "looked into the Torah and made the world."

From there the Maharal makes a rule about oneness:

- "כל דבר שהוא מסודר הוא דבר אחד מקושר" (12, 1:3): "whatever is set in order is one bound thing." - "כאשר מקיים הסדר בדבר אחד, הוא קיום אל הכל" (13, 1:4): "when one keeps the order in one thing, it holds up everything."

So the world was made in ten sayings, not one, to show that it is many parts bound into one order. Break one part and you shake the whole. Keep one part and you hold up the whole.

## 2.2 Two learners become one, because the Torah is one

The strongest unity text in the range is at line 142 (Netiv HaTorah 6:13). When two people learn and truly listen to each other,

- "הם נעשים אחד לגמרי על ידי התורה, שהיא אחת" (142): "they become wholly one through the Torah, which is one." - "כי השם יתברך שהוא אחד, מתחבר עם התורה שהיא אחת" (142): "for G-d, who is One, joins with the Torah, which is one."

When they do not listen, it is as if the Torah were split, and the Shechina (the felt Presence of G-d) leaves. Oneness between people is the place where the One can rest.

## 2.3 The world is one; the wicked stand outside it

Why do dogs hate each other? Because they are far from the settled world, "אשר העולם כולו הוא אחד" (274): "for the whole world is one." The wicked are the same: "הם רחוקים מן העולם שהוא אחד" (276): "they are far from the world, which is one." To be far from the One is to be split inside and from others.

The positive side is the lone traveler (16). Dangers rule over one who is cut off. A traveler with no company should learn, since then "נחשב זה כאילו היה בישוב העולם" (16): "it counts as if he were within the settled world." Learning puts you back inside the one order.

## 2.4 Awe belongs only to the One

Why may we love a teacher like G-d but not fear him like G-d? Because "יראת השם יתברך הוא מצד שהוא יתברך נבדל מן הכל" (198, Netiv HaTorah 9:3): "awe of G-d comes from His being separate from all." Love can be shared. Awe can not, since He has no partner. This is the Maharal's two-sided picture of G-d: the One who is separate from all (so we stand in awe) and the One who joins with what is whole (so we cling in love).

**Proposed MH-S7 "Inside the order".** Inside: I am not a stray part. What I do in one small place holds up the whole. How to enter: take one small act and do it exactly, knowing it keeps the whole order (13). Source: Netivot Olam 11-16.

**Proposed MH-S8 "Seen and not seen".** Inside: I am with people fully, but I am not lost in them. Like a loose necklace on the neck (103), I rest on the world without being tied into it. How to enter: in company, keep a quiet inner place that watches and is not watched. Source: Netivot Olam 103.

## 4.1 Learn aloud, because you are your speech (`lips`)

"אין המחשבה הוא האדם" (113): "the thought is not the person." A person is a "speaking spirit". Learning only in the mind does not join the learning to the life. Say the words. This matches the technique id `lips`.

## 4.2 Fixed times (`chazarah`, `carry`)

Learning now and then is like a passing relation, not a marriage (90). What is by-the-way has no real being (91). Set a time and keep it.

## 4.3 Learn with a partner and listen (new: `mh_listen_to_become_one`)

The Maharal's promise: two who listen become one through the one Torah (142), and G-d who is One joins them. How: choose a partner. Before you answer, repeat back what they said until they agree you heard it. Then speak. Source: 142, 130-136.

## 4.4 Teach, and it becomes yours (`act`)

"נחשב כאילו עשאו לבן חבירו" (174): "it counts as if he had made his fellow's son." Giving the mind away makes it fully real in you.

## 4.5 Look at the world to know its Maker (`picturing`, `breadth`)

"ולכך האדם מחויב עליו לדעת ולהכיר את בוראו" (313): "so a person is obliged to know and recognize his Creator." The Maharal says it is a duty to study the order of the heavens and nature, since all of it is G-d's work. He even mentions the "new astronomer" (Copernicus) at 314.

## 4.6 Run to the good; completion is rest (new: `mh_run_is_rest`)

"שכל השלמה היא מנוחה לאשר יושלם" (397): "for every completion is rest to the one completed." Even running to a mitzvah on Shabbat is rest, since it completes you. How: when you notice tiredness about a good act, remember that finishing it is the rest you want. Source: 397.

## 5. The big human questions

**Purpose.** "וצריך האדם להוציא אל הפעל הדבר אשר נזרע בו" (328): "and a person must bring out into act what was sown in him." Adam is from adamah, soil. A pure soul was planted in you. Your life is its growing. And "זה האדם תמיד יכול להוציא את עצמו יותר אל הפעל" (329): "this person can always bring himself further into act." That is why a person is "born for toil": not as a punishment, but because there is always more of you to grow.

**Worth.** "אפילו גוי ועוסק בתורה הרי הוא ככהן גדול" (254): "even a gentile who studies Torah is like a High Priest." Worth is in the mind that reaches toward G-d, not in birth. This line suits D28.

**Loss and failure.** "כי לוחות ושברי לוחות מונחים בארון" (255): "for the tablets and the broken tablets both lie in the ark." An elder who forgot his learning through no fault keeps his level. What you reached is still yours.

**The lowly.** "אני אשכון את שפל רוח" (264): "I dwell with the lowly of spirit." G-d Himself fights for the orphan and the widow.

**Knowing and love.** "הידיעה הוא חבור היודע בידוע" (338): "knowing is the joining of the knower with the known." And "כי הדעת מערב ומחבר הכל" (301): "for knowing mixes and joins everything." So the sages say a person's mind should be "mixed with people". To know someone is to be joined to them.

**Joy.** "אין השם יתברך מתחבר רק אל השלם" (400): "G-d joins only with what is whole." So "שאין השכינה שורה אלא מתוך דבר שמחה" (401): "the Shechina rests only out of joy." Joy is the feeling of being whole, and wholeness is where G-d joins.

**The soul in the body.** "הנר יש לו שתי בחינות" (378): "the candle has two sides." It hangs on the wick and it is light. So the soul: it stands in the body and it is not body. So a mitzvah: a deed in matter that gives light.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Torah as the world's order** fits the Tanya's teaching that Torah is G-d's wisdom and will, and that learning joins the mind to Him (Tanya ch. 5). The Maharal speaks of *order* where the Tanya speaks of *wisdom clothed*. Same picture, different words.
- **Two become one through the one Torah** (142) is close to the Tanya's ch. 32 (love of Israel because all souls are one at the root). The Maharal grounds it in the oneness of the Torah and of its Giver rather than the root of souls.
- **Awe from separateness** (198) is not the Tanya's route. The Tanya builds awe from seeing His nearness and greatness. The Maharal builds it from His having no partner. Both are true; the guide can use both.
- **Tzimtzum** does not appear here. The Maharal has no need of it in this range.
