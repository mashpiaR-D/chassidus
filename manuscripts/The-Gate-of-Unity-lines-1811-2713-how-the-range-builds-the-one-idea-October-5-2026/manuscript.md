## The shape of the range

This is the second half of a book whose introduction says what it is about: "the unity of Godliness in every single detail" of the chain of worlds. The range does exactly that, and so most of it is mapping. Line after line places one partzuf, one letter or one Name inside the next. That is why *structural* dominates (84% of lines), and why most structural lines carry no face. The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud states the unity in seven short chapters. Here the Mitteler Rebbe walks it down, rung by rung, from the Essence's first will to the zodiac and the last blade of grass. Then, every twenty or thirty lines, he stops and says what the walk was for. Those stopping points are the *stated* lines, and they almost all say one thing: however far down the chain goes, "all is one" and the end is held in the beginning. That is face F05 (the Essence beyond both, present in the finite), and it leads the count. F03 is second because the range's other long theme is the tzimtzum, the screen and the kingship that needs "someone else".

The faces that belong to the person's life (F06 the soul, F07 the home below, F10 Torah and mitzvah) are rare. This range is cosmology. The person enters only as the one who contemplates it (F12), as the soul used for analogies, and in ch. 53-54 as the one whose self-feeling is the start of kelipah (F08).

## Ch. 32-33 (1811-1861): the end of Ein Sof, and the method

The range opens on a strong claim. Even the outer vessel of the heart of Zeir Anpin (the emotional partzuf of Atzilut) has in it "the simple essential will in the Essence of Or Ein Sof before the first tzimtzum" (line 1813). So it still counts as the end of the infinite:

> נחשב עדיין הכל מסוף עולם האין סוף (line 1815)

The reason is the Zohar's phrase, which the range repeats as its motto:

> משום דאיהו וחיוהי וגרמוהי חד (line 1829)

"He, His life and His causes are one." Lines 1830-1833 define each word. "He" is the Essence, "His life" is the sefirot, "His causes" are the vessels, and "one" means "all still Ein Sof". Ch. 33 then names the method. Contemplation (hisbonenus) means gathering the many details into one whole:

> ההתבוננות אחת מן רבוי כל הפרטים אל כלל א' הכוללם יחד (line 1817)

The parables that follow (a child and an elder of seventy; a map and the globe, line 1847-1849) all make one point: "all is one, only brought from the longest to the shortest" (line 1853). Contraction changes size, not identity. Line 1858 gives the Sefer Yetzirah principle the book returns to again and again: לפי שנעוץ תחלתן בסופן, "their beginning is wedged in their end". Line 1861 carries the one will down to the physical spheres, "the root of mineral, plant, animal and speaker" (F01).

## Ch. 34-36 (1862-2010): Israel and Jacob, the middle line, cedar to hyssop

Ch. 34 is the development of the mochin (mind) in the middot, told through a child's growth from six to nine to thirteen to twenty. Every line of it is structural and carries no face. The unity is not in view.

Ch. 35 gives the range's definition of truth. Kindness and severity limit and cut off each other (lines 1921-1924). Truth is the middle line, which holds both opposites "with nothing opposing" (line 1926, F09 at the level of the sefirot) and does not change from top to bottom:

Emet is alef, mem, tav, the first, middle and last letters: "as its beginning so its end, and that is the essence of truth" (line 1959). This is the book's image for the unity in every detail. We mark it structural, since the text is speaking of Jacob and Tiferet, not yet of the Essence.

Ch. 36 (Joseph, yesod) closes with a parenthesis that applies the method outright (line 1994). Solomon "spoke from the cedar in Lebanon to the hyssop in the wall" (1 Kings 5:13). The cedar is

> בעצם התענוג הפשוט שבעצמות אור אין סוף כשעלה ברצונו הפשוט אנא אמלוך (line 1996)

and the hyssop is the smallest point of ZA's yesod joined to Malchus (line 1997). One verse spans the Essence and the last point. Line 2007 is the one place in these chapters where knowing becomes bitul in the heart: והוא בחינת הביטול שנמשך בהבל חזה הלב.

## Ch. 37 (2011-2071): thought and speech; where the world is made

Leah and Rachel are thought and speech, the two hei's of the Name (lines 2012-2014). The chapter is mostly an anatomy of voice: fire, water and wind in the heart's breath, and how sorrow narrows speech. The unity comes in at the joint between heart and speech. Here the Besht's verse is placed:

> וזה שכתוב לעולם יהו״ה דברך נצב בשמים (line 2038)

Creation from nothing happens in speech, not in the heart. While it stays in the heart, "the heart does not reveal to the mouth", and "no king's word has yet been made from nothing to something" (line 2042). In speech, כי בדבור כבר נעשה, "in speech it is already done" (line 2044). In the heart it can still turn from judgment to kindness (line 2045). This is F01 with a location: continuous creation is the work of Malchus, the divine speech. Thought and speech are "the hidden world and the revealed world" (line 2068). The range later uses this pair for the higher and the lower unity.

## Ch. 38-39 (2072-2165): letters, vessels and garments

The soul "is full of letters" from its very coming into being, before will itself (lines 2075, 2089). Letters are what let one will pass into intellect, then love, then thought, speech and deed, as "a chain of cause and effect from the soul's essence to the end of action" (line 2114). The application is stated. The letters "engraved in the supernal purity" are

> בחינת אותיות העצמיים דמחשבה ודבור שבעצמות אור אין סוף (line 2117)

and they run from "I will reign" down to "the root of the coming into being of our low world" (line 2122):

> כל אשר חפץ יהו״ה בעצמותו ממש, עשה בפועל ממש (line 2123)

Ch. 39 then holds two descriptions of the same letters, and says both are true (line 2161). As *vessels*, letters are one with the essence (באחדות גמור עם העצם, line 2162). As *garments*, they are separate from it and hide it, so that only the letters show, "as if there were nothing outside this form" (line 2147). This is F03 and F05 in one object: the thing that hides Him and the thing that is one with Him are the same letters, seen from two sides. The idea itself is never in its words: "none of them reach its essence at all" (line 2150). Line 2165 carries the analogy up to the Essence.

## Ch. 40-43 (2166-2321): kinds of thought; the Rambam; the large dalet

Ch. 40-42 are careful psychology: will in the nerves as "hidden thought", reasoned thought in Binah, "action, speech and thought within thought", and why a child who cannot yet speak has no letters in his thinking. All structural. Line 2255 states a rule the range will need later. The dividing power looks like descent and contraction, "but its root comes from above".

Ch. 43 applies it all to the Essence (line 2282). Here the range contains its sharpest point against the Tanya. "Action in thought" in the Essence is "the end of the deed, first in thought" (line 2284). Then:

> ודלא כלשון הרמב"ם בידיעת עצמו יודע את הכל (line 2285)

"Not as the Rambam puts it, that knowing Himself He knows all." Rather, He knows the chain of worlds with a knowledge "outside Himself", as one pictures a separate thing, הגם שאין לך דבר חוץ, "though there is nothing outside" (line 2286). See the disagreements below.

"For He said", in the essential thought, "and it was", through the first tzimtzum (line 2289). A parenthesis at line 2294 is the range's one clear F07/F10 statement. The first desire for kindness is roused by the deeds of those below, so their deeds reach

> לתחלת התעוררות הרצון במחשבה העצמית (line 2294)

The chapter ends in the Shema. ZA is called "echad" as the unity of the ten sefirot of Atzilut (line 2311). The large dalet of "echad" is Malchus of Tevunah, and that is why the dalet is drawn out in the Shema:

> שביחודא תתאה דברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד (line 2316)

Line 2317 carries it up to the simple unity in the Essence, "hidden of all hidden, which no thought grasps".

## Ch. 44-46 (2322-2444): Elokim, sovev as knowing, the root of the lowest, nogah

The name Elokim is contraction (line 2325: ובחינת אלהי״ם הזה הוא בחינת הצמצום וההסתלקות). But its power, though it comes as tzimtzum, "is from above" the understanding it contracts (line 2332). F03 is stated here from its top: the hiding comes from higher than what it hides.

Sovev (F04) is given as knowing. Knowledge surrounds what it knows on every side (line 2347). Citing the Tanya's ch. 48, God's knowledge of all creatures "surrounds them" (line 2348). Then comes the range's clearest source for the SPINE's claim that matter's solidity comes from sovev. The "ice", the fully materialized, fixed form, has a higher root than Binah's inner light:

> ואמנם שרש הקרח הזה שבא דוקא בבחינת הגשמה גמורה, הוא מלמעלה מבחינת אור פנימי דבינה (line 2355)

It comes from Binah's surrounding light (line 2356). The same rule is given for parables: "the root of the parable is above the thing it illustrates" (line 2389). Then it is given in general (G05, the highest in the lowest):

> כל שבא בהשגה והסבר בהגשמה ביותר למטה מטה, שם דוקא בחינת העומק והמקור (line 2397)

Ch. 45 explains the breaking of the vessels as letters scattered by too much light, like a word that cannot hold its meaning, or the day's thoughts scattered in dreams (lines 2371-2383). The scattered letters become the "garments of skin" (line 2385). Ch. 46 turns to nogah, and here the range qualifies a Tanya phrase. A candle looks brighter at night, but

> ואין זו הבהקה אמיתית מצד עצמו (line 2414)

"This is not true brightness from its own side." The "advantage of light from darkness" is in the eye of the seer. After repair, the "garments of light" shine "from the light itself" (line 2443). Yet the same chapter states the strongest form of F11. Through the bitul of the new Mah, nogah's felt "something" becomes actual "nothing" (line 2436), and

> וכאשר היש הוא בבחינת אין, הרי הוא יותר נעלה מבחינת אין דמ"ה (line 2437)

"When the something is nothing, it is higher than the nothing that refined it." The refined Ban rises above Mah (line 2439).

## Ch. 47-48 (2445-2509): Malchus, and the king who needs a people

Ch. 47 builds Malchus of Atzilut: the speech that turns each trait into "a separate something on its own" (lines 2459-2461). Line 2476 gives the Besht's verse again. The word "stands in the heavens" because the power of speech is always attached to ZA's chest. Line 2483 prepares the lower unity: "night" is Malchus hidden in the head of Beriah.

Ch. 48 is the range's argument about the tzimtzum. In a person, exaltation is unlike kindness. Kindness flows from the self even with no one to receive it, but exaltation "cannot exist at all without someone to be exalted over" (lines 2492-2495). Its size depends on the subjects' bitul (line 2496). So above: שאין מלך בלא עם דוקא (line 2498). And the Mitteler Rebbe adds that this trait, unlike the others, is

> אבל אינה המשכה ממש מבחינת העצמות (line 2499)

"not really drawn from the Essence". This leads to the question of the whole doctrine: "there is nothing outside Him; over whom would He be exalted?" (line 2502). The answer is the tzimtzum:

> כדי שיהיה דבר חוץ מן העצמות (line 2506)

The first tzimtzum was made "so that there would be something outside the Essence", over which the Malchus of Ein Sof could reign. The same reason is given for the screen between Atzilut and Beriah (line 2508), and for the separation of the nations' roots "from His unity" in darkness (line 2507). Rosh Hashanah renews the kingship "from its source" in the Essence's "I will reign" (lines 2489, 2505). "I am first" in Malchus of Ein Sof, "I am last" in Malchus of Atzilut, "for their beginning is wedged in their end" (line 2509).

## Ch. 49-50 (2510-2578): renewal every moment; oil and vinegar

F01 is stated as daily and momentary renewal from the first source (lines 2518-2519: עד שבכל עת ושעה מתחדש אור ושפע ממקור הראשון). Malchus of Malchus is where flow comes "as the source of the coming into being of something from actual nothing" (line 2544). Ch. 50 grounds nature in letter-combinations. R. Chanina ben Dosa's "He who told oil to burn will tell vinegar to burn" means that the One who combines the name Elokim into oil's nature can recombine vinegar (line 2574). "The tzaddik rules by the fear of Elokim", that is, by its 120 combinations (line 2575). F03's "Elokim is nature" is here made concrete. Line 2578 gathers the whole into one contemplation, "as vinegar burns in actual fact".

## Ch. 51-52 (2579-2645): the screen, and the lower unity

Only "a radiance of a radiance" breaks through the screen; the essential light stays above it (lines 2581-2583). "First darkness, then light" (line 2584). The Mitteler Rebbe then compares the two tzimtzumim. The first was "a hiding and inclusion of the light in His Essence, not a real screen", because "all is still His Essence" (lines 2596-2597). From Atzilut to Beriah, from the unbounded to the truly bounded, "a screen of complete break was needed" (line 2598). Yet:

> שאין הפסק המסך הזה ענין הפסק והבדל אמיתי לעצמו, רק לגבי המקבלים בלבד (line 2599)

The break is real "only toward the receivers", because the screen is made by the one who contracts, "like the snail whose garment is of itself" (line 2600, the Tanya's image). This is F03 in its Alter Rebbe form: concealment only toward creatures.

Then the two unities are defined as places. "Baruch Shem" is the lower unity of Malchus (line 2602). It is Malchus of Atzilut shining in Beriah (line 2603), "so she unites below in the throne of Beriah" (line 2604). The tribes' Shema to Jacob becomes the key:

> כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד בבחינת יחודא עילאה דאצילות (line 2605)

As in Jacob's heart (Atzilut) there is only One, so in theirs (Beriah) there is only One, the lower unity. That is "Havayah is one and His name is one" (line 2606). In general it is Havayah and Adnut, Havayah and Elokim, סובב כל עלמין וממלא כל עלמין (line 2607, F02 and F04 together). Ch. 52 takes the chain through Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah to the zodiac: אין לך עשב שאין לו מזל, "no blade of grass without its mazal" (line 2644), ruled by Malchus of Malchus of Asiyah (line 2645).

## Ch. 53 (2646-2672): the aim of it all

This chapter is the book's summary, and it is almost all *stated*. All the desires, to emanate, create, form and make, were included in His simple will (line 2647). As a man's will "arose all at once" before dividing into steps (line 2648), so relative to the Essence "there is no division of levels" between the desire to emanate and the desire to make (line 2650). Then the range's strongest single line:

> כי שרש האצילות ושרש העשיה שוין ממש לגבי עצמותו (line 2653)

"The root of Atzilut and the root of Asiyah are exactly equal relative to His Essence." Sovev and memale are "all in one equality" (line 2654). And the purpose of the whole contemplation:

> רק להיות נכלל הכל באחדות הפשוטה (line 2657)

The line goes on: "until it is fixed in the soul, also in the order of divine providence in particulars, to the very last detail". F12's aim is stated here as F01's providence, felt. The stars are arranged "according to His will", His simple will in His very Essence (line 2659). All of Asiyah is in "complete bitul of the something to the nothing" (line 2661). So are its angels, the matter of the ofanim (line 2663), and even the seventy princes of nogah in exile, כסוס לגבי רוכב, "like a horse to its rider" (line 2664). In the utmost hiding, "garments of sackcloth", still "Elokim reigns over the nations" (line 2666). Balaam's "I cannot transgress the word of Havayah" is the proof (line 2667). Even the three impure kelipot, which say "my strength", "are subdued at times" (line 2669).

## Ch. 54 (2673-2712): where kelipah comes from

The book ends with the other side. The principle is stated:

> כל דבר מה שבא בבחינת התפשטות מן העצם, ודאי יש כח המנגד אליו שבא גם כן מן העצם (line 2678)

"Whatever extends out from the essence surely has a power opposed to it that also comes from the essence." Pleasure (oneg) and pain (nega) are the same letters (line 2676). "This opposite that God made" (line 2682). It comes through "the contraction and hiding that precede every light" (line 2683). This is F09 and F03 stated from the side of evil.

The practical centre of the chapter is F08. Nogah of Atzilut has no evil at all. It is only "the felt, protruding something", like a person who feels his own kindness or wisdom (lines 2699-2700). From that feeling, branching down, comes evil: kindness done "only for pride and for himself" (line 2701). The example is taken from worship itself (line 2703):

> וכמו שמהתלהבות הבולט בהרגשת עצמו בתפלה

Felt enthusiasm in prayer turns to anger at whoever touches one's honour. Nogah grows worse from world to world, until the three impure kelipot, "utter evil without any good", have "no refinement yet at all, only in the future" (line 2711). The last line is the colophon (line 2712).

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The unity walked through every rung.** The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud states the unity of creation and refutes the literal tzimtzum. It does not go through the partzufim. The Mitteler Rebbe does, and at each station repeats that "all is one, only from the longest to the shortest" (line 1853). This is the book's declared purpose. The SPINE's note that partzuf mechanics state the tie "at the level of method, not in each passage" is confirmed here line by line: 654 of 914 rows carry no face, and the ties come at the gathering points.
- **The root of the lowest is higher.** The "ice" of full materialization (line 2355), the parable (line 2389) and "whatever comes lowest" (line 2397) are each rooted above the inner light. The Tanya has the seed (ch. 48, the knowledge that "surrounds" the earth). Here it is a stated rule.
- **Letters as both vessel and garment.** The same letters are "in complete unity with the essence" and a separate garment that hides it (lines 2161-2163). The Tanya has letters of speech as the medium of creation (SH 1). It does not have this double reading.
- **Kingship as the reason for the tzimtzum.** Line 2506: the first tzimtzum was "so that there would be something outside the Essence" to reign over. Line 2499: kingship alone among the traits is "not really drawn from the Essence".
- **The opposing power comes from the essence too** (line 2678). This is F09 applied to evil, stated as a general law.
- **Felt selfhood as the first step of kelipah**, even in holiness, with prayer's excitement as the example (lines 2699-2703). This gives F08 a practical warning that the Tanya's cosmology does not spell out in this form.
- **The higher and lower unity as places.** The higher unity is Atzilut, "Jacob's heart". The lower is Malchus of Atzilut shining through the screen into Beriah, "the tribes' heart" (lines 2602-2606). The Tanya (SH 7) frames the lower unity as His filling the world through space and time. The two framings fit together, but they are not the same.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Ch. 34 (the child's growth of mind, 1884-1905), ch. 37's anatomy of voice (2016-2031), ch. 40-42's psychology of thought (2176-2281), the six days and six thousand years (2568-2573) and the map of Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah (2618-2640) are structural with no face. The text gives no tie in them. The tie comes only from the book's stated aim. - Five lines are *none*: two signposts and cross-references (1863, 1906, 1913), the "botzina" cross-reference (1852) and the colophon (2712). - One *reading*: line 2224. Exile "likened to sleep" is read as F03. The text only names the likeness. (Line 2405, which says it again inside the Joseph passage, is marked structural.) - F06 appears once, and only as an analogy (line 2118, the soul's yechidah). F07 appears five times, and only once with force (line 2294, the deeds below reach the Essence's first will). F10 appears through Rosh Hashanah's kingship verses (lines 2488-2489, 2505) and the prayer "He arranges the stars" (line 2659). This range is not about the person's life. - The F09 lines in ch. 35 (1921-1927) are about kindness and severity joined in the middle line of the sefirot. The text does not say they are joined in the Essence there, so they are marked structural. - Some face tags on structural lines are judgments. F05 is put on lines that say "as at the head so at the end" or "the same will through all its forms" (e.g. 1928-1930, 2103, 2153). F11 is put on lines about birur (refinement, e.g. 1865, 1908).

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **Does He know the world by knowing Himself?** The Tanya adopts the Rambam: "by knowing Himself He knows all creatures, *not* by a knowledge outside Him" (Likkutei Amarim 48, Chabad Library file line 707: ולא בידיעה שחוץ ממנו כידיעת האדם; again at SH 7, line 834). The Mitteler Rebbe writes "not as the Rambam puts it" and says He knows the chain "with a knowledge outside Himself... though there is nothing outside" (lines 2285-2286). The Alter Rebbe's own gloss at SH 9 (line 860: אבל בלי צמצום והלבשה הנזכר לעיל לא שייך כלל לומר) says the Rambam's formula applies only after the tzimtzum and the clothing in the sefirot. The Mitteler Rebbe is speaking of the Essence's own thought before the tzimtzum. So the gloss may reconcile them. That reconciliation is ours. The wording stands opposed.
- **What is the tzimtzum for?** The Tanya: "the purpose of all the tzimtzumim is to create the physical body of man, and to subdue the sitra achra" (Likkutei Amarim 49, line 713: ותכלית כל הצמצומים הוא – כדי לברוא גוף האדם החומרי). The Mitteler Rebbe: the first tzimtzum was so that there be "something outside the Essence" to be king over (line 2506). The Tanya also has "no king without a people" (SH 7). One can hold that the body and the kingship are one purpose seen from below and from above, but neither text says so. Kept as two answers.
- **The advantage of light from darkness.** The Tanya names it as the purpose (ch. 49, line 713, "the advantage of light from darkness"). The Mitteler Rebbe says light in darkness looks brighter only to the eye, "not true brightness from its own side" (line 2414). He sets above it the light of the repaired garment, which shines from itself (line 2443). He also says the "something" turned to "nothing" is higher than the "nothing" that refined it (line 2437). So he does not deny the advantage of transformation. He denies that the contrast itself is the light. This is a qualification, not a contradiction, but it sits against the Tanya's phrase and is kept.
- **Is every trait drawn from the Essence?** Line 2499 says kingship is "not really drawn from the Essence", unlike kindness and wisdom. The SPINE's F05 speaks of the Essence present in everything. The Mitteler Rebbe keeps the Essence as the root of the will to reign ("I will reign", line 2505) while saying the trait itself exists only toward another. Kept.
