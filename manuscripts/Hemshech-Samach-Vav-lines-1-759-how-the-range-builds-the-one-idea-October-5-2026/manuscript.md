## The shape of the range

This is the opening of the great 5666 series, and it is organized around one question: why are there worlds at all? The answer is stated in the first maamar and never dropped:

> והו"ע יחוד אוא"ס הסוכ"ע בממכ"ע (line 119)

So the leading faces are F05 (the Essence) and F10 (Torah and mitzvot), not the cosmological F01-F02 that lead in the Tanya's Shaar HaYichud. The Rashab's unity is not first "the world is nullified in its source". It is "the Essence, which no light reaches, is drawn below through the mitzvah". F04 (sovev and memale) comes third, because the union of sovev with memale is his name for the purpose. F12 (knowing) and F02 (the higher unity) are comparatively rare. The range rarely asks how the world is nothing before Him. It asks how the Essence, beyond both lights, comes to dwell in it.

## Front matter (1-74)

These lines hold the title page, the publisher's preface and the Rayatz's table of topics for the whole series. Five topic entries are marked structural because they name the book's arc: "the purpose of the creation of worlds and souls" (line 30), "Or Ein Sof is light and not Essence" (line 42), and "memale, sovev and what is above them" (line 72). Everything else here is *none*.

## Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashanah (75-246): why there is a world

The maamar opens on a halachic puzzle: the shofar is not blown when Rosh Hashanah falls on Shabbat. The shofar is teshuvah, and teshuvah is distinguished from re'uta d'liba. Re'uta d'liba comes from closeness, contemplating the Essence "to be included in Or Ein Sof". Teshuvah comes from distance, and its movement is to hand oneself over (line 92, F08). Zichronot is knowing that "Havayah and Elokim are all one" (line 102, F12 with F02):

Rosh Hashanah renews the will to reign, rooted "in the Essence of Or Ein Sof before the tzimtzum" (line 104, F05). He vivifies every creature every moment (line 118, F01).

Then comes the thesis (line 119, above) and its unfolding. The mitzvah's union of sovev in memale is the revelation of the Essence below, 'a dwelling below' (line 129). The first tzimtzum left a vacated space, the root of all vessels (line 129.2, F03). In the future the pre-tzimtzum light will shine in that space; once the worlds exist they can hold it, as a student can hold his teacher's depth once his mind is formed (lines 131, 145).

The range's first sustained argument (lines 146-208) is negative, and it is the strongest thing in the range. Etz Chaim's reason, that His powers be complete, cannot be the true reason, because above, power never lacks act (line 169). The Essence is beyond both power and act:

> דאמיתת המצאו הוא שנמצא בבלתי מציאות נמצא (line 182)

The Zohar's reason, "so that they know Me", is fulfilled in Atzilut, where the vessels are "Godliness itself" (line 195). It cannot explain the lowest world, which was made precisely so that not even the existence of Godliness is felt (line 195.2). So the reason is desire alone (line 208):

> ואין אנו יודעים טעם שכלי מפני מה נשתוקק (line 208)

Then comes the key claim about the Essence below (line 213, F05 + F04 + F07):

> אבל כאשר ההמשכה היא מבחי' עצמות אוא"ס, אין בזה התחלקות מעלה ומטה, ועיקר הגילוי יהי' למטה דוקא (line 213)

Memale is a radiance with a top and a bottom. The Essence has no top and bottom, and so its main revelation can be below. Each mitzvah draws a new name Havayah from the Essence, and it becomes "your God" because it is made through you (line 237):

> ונקרא אלקיך מפני שנעשה על ידך (line 237)

## Zeh HaYom (247-346): Eden no eye has seen, and bitul between levels

Eden is "the King's delight in His Essence", which no eye has seen, not even Moses or Adam. Gan Eden receives only the radiance carried by the "river" (Binah, comprehension), and the river is itself a concealing garment (lines 253, 262, 266; F05, F03). Speech hides the thought it carries: a child says every word and misses the idea (line 266.1, F03 with G06). In the future the Essence itself will be revealed, and Moshiach will teach Torah "as it is in the essence of Ein Sof" (line 262.3):

> כ"א שיומשך ויתגלה בחי' העלם העצמי ממש (line 253)

The central sentence of this maamar is F05 stated as a principle:

> אבל העצם הוא בכ"מ בשוה, ואין בו ראש וסוף, ולא התחלקות מדריג' כלל (line 290)

A light has a head and an end, strong near the source and weak far from it. The Essence does not. The rest of the maamar turns this into F08. Between incommensurable levels one rises only by bitul of the former level: R. Zeira's hundred fasts, the rotting seed, the river of fire and the pillar in Gan Eden (lines 292-335). In prayer, bitterness and lowliness must come first:

> כל שהמרירות מצד הריחוק העצום הוא בעומק יותר, יהי' יותר גילוי האור והשמחה האלקית (line 310)

Line 322 joins this to F11. Refinement in BY"A is itkafya (subduing), but entering Atzilut needs a second, truer bitul.

## Shuvah Yisrael (347-454): 'ad and not including'

"Return unto (ad) Havayah." The word "ad" means both eternity and a limit, and both are true of the Essence (lines 354, 358). Praise of the unbounded never ends, and it also never arrives:

> שבכל ההילולים והתשבחות לא יגיע כלל לבחי' העצמות (line 358)

Line 368 is the range's most important sentence on F12, and it is the Rashab's own position on the SPINE's F12 dispute:

> דאמיתתיי הידיעה בהפלאת העצמית, איך שאמיתות עצמותו אינו בגדר השגה, הוא דוקא במי שעוסק בידיעת מדרי' הנעלות שבאוא"ס (line 368)

Those who do not study the high levels hold the Essence's unknowability only as faith. Bitul is "a vessel for the revelation of light" (line 368.1). In the future the Essence will be grasped: "My servant shall understand" (line 370).

Then the purpose returns in its sharpest form. "The end of the deed rose first in thought" means the practical mitzvah rose before even the beginning of the will to emanate. "With whom did He consult? With the souls of the tzaddikim" (line 375):

> דתכלית הכוונה מה שעלה בבחי' עצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מבחי' מח' ורצון הנ"ל הוא (line 375)

The Temple, Shabbat and Torah are the present foretastes of the Essence below (lines 425-438). The voice of Torah is "from before the tzimtzum", and so it reaches all the way down. Torah is chiefly below because it is above measure:

> אבל באמת אור התורה הוא למעלה מבחי' מדה וגבול, ולכן נמשך ג"כ למטה ממש (line 438)

The soul is F06 stated in this book's own terms. It is rooted in the Essence, and that is why it descends (line 452):

> כי הנשמה היא מושרשת ג"כ בעצמות אוא"ס, ולכן ירדה למטה בגוף גשמי דוקא (line 452)

## Lulav va'Aravah (455-572): essence is indivisible; teshuvah moves the soul's essence

The maamar gives a general principle for F05 (line 468):

> דכל עצם בלתי מתחלק ובלתי מתפשט כמו שהוא בעצמותו (line 468)

The soul is the parable. When its essence (the yechidah) is revealed, as at an only son's wedding, it shines equally in every power (line 469). The text itself carries this up to God (line 505):

> דכאשר מאיר בחי' העצמות דאוא"ס ה"ה בכולם בשוה (line 505)

In teshuvah the soul's essence is "moved from its place". The penitent is turned over "from end to end", down to the axioms his mind rests on (lines 482, 487; F08, F11). The Rashab adds that "relative to the true exaltedness of the Essence all are as sinners" (line 487.1). All comprehension is "far from the essence", and even Moses' Chochmah could not see it (line 495, F12 + F05).

The will of creation is only the outside of the will. The will in the mitzvot is its inwardness, and the practical mitzvah is now a vessel whose revelation waits for the future (line 507.2):

> דבאמת בחי' מעשה המצוה עכשיו נעשה כלי לאלקות, וההתגלות מזה יהי' לעתיד (line 507)

Line 514 states that the will to create is itself a novelty to the Essence, and must be drawn anew every moment by arousal from below:

> דבחי' המשכת הרצון בכל עת ובכל רגע להוות ולהחיות העולמות הוא בבחי' התחדשות (line 514)

The "poor servant" is the one who accepts the yoke by force, when Godliness is hidden and nothing moves him. His lowliness causes the descent above that creates and sustains the worlds (line 536.2). The "string of pearls" is Torah as "the bar from end to end", so that even the world of separation is joined "to His unity". The pearl must be bored hollow (line 546):

> שאין אוא"ס שורה ומתגלה אלא במי שבטל אליו במציאות לגמרי בבחי' אין ממש (line 546)

Honest business and eating for Heaven's sake refine sparks within BY"A. Only Joseph's bitul of existence raises them into Atzilut (line 557, F11 + F08). The halachic opening is answered from the structure of light: on Shabbat the makifim already shine inward, so the lulav does not override Shabbat now (line 569).

## BaYom HaShemini Atzeret (573-664): the will with no reason

The inner will "is not compounded with anything" (line 584). A man's pleasure in his own home has no reason, and so it cannot be explained and is unbounded. Keter's outside is the will to create, which has a reason ("the nature of the good is to do good"). Its inside is the root of the mitzvot (lines 587-596):

> רק הוא בחי' רצון פשוט ועצמי ממש שמצד עצם עצמותו ית' (line 587)

> ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא (line 596)

"The reward of a mitzvah is the mitzvah" (line 596.2). "Doing the will of the Omnipresent (Makom)" means drawing sovev into place itself, and Malchut is the source of space and time (line 614):

> עד שיהי' גילוי אוא"ס בבחי' מקום למטה ממש (line 614)

Lines 627-658 give this book's account of F02 and the chain. The higher never passes its essence to the lower, only its radiance, like sunlight, which is a "garment" and yet never severed from the sun (line 627). The will is only "the soul's inclining" (line 651), and Keter is "the inclining of the Essence to will to emanate", with no existence of its own (line 654):

> וכמו"כ הכתר לגבי עצמות אוא"ס (line 636)

The seventy bulls bring the makif onto the nations for their refinement. Prayer is a war in which the animal soul comes to understand the good of Godliness (line 660.2, F11).

## VaYechulu (665-750): time, ratzo and shov, and the partner in creation

Time begins with Beriah. Atzilut and every Keter are above it (lines 669-680). Time is made by ratzo and shov, and at root it is interruption (line 695):

> אבל כאשר נמשך החיות למטה ה"ה בבחי' התחלקות, דבכל רגע נמשך חיות אחר (line 695)

Ratzo belongs to a light that is far from its source and clothed in a vessel. The will has only "reaches and does not reach", because it is the Essence itself inclining (lines 700-707). R. Shimon bar Yochai's "with one knot I am bound to Him... aflame in Him" is that level in a person (lines 708-711):

> שהי' יודע הפנימיות בכל פשוטי ההלכות (line 711)

The will of creation holds the root of limitation. The will of the mitzvot, drawn through the chasadim of Atik without contraction, draws the Essence "below as above" (lines 731-740):

> להיות נמשך למטה כמו למע' דוקא בלי צמצום (line 739)

> דהיינו שגם בבחי' מקום וזמן לא יהי' ההגבלה דמקום וזמן (line 740)

So whoever says "vayechulu" becomes a partner in creation (lines 742-750, F10 + F07).

## Eileh Toldot Noach (751-759), opening only

The mitzvot are the true "offspring" of the righteous. The kav "makes above and below". Without it all worlds would be equal (line 755):

> דאם הי' מאיר מאוא"ס שלא ע"י הקו, הי' כל העולמות בשוה (line 755)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The purpose is argued, not only stated.** Tanya 36 names the dwelling below. Here the Rashab first rejects, step by step, the two classic reasons: completing His powers (Etz Chaim) and "so that they know Me" (Zohar). The answer is a desire "above reason" (lines 169-213). The Tanya range has nothing like this.
- **Light is not Essence.** The Tanya (SH 7) says His Essence fills the earth below as above. The Rashab keeps a sharp line. Memale, sovev and even Keter are "light and radiance", and memale has levels because it is only a radiance (lines 213, 290, 627, 654). Only the Essence is equal everywhere. So the lowest place can hold it because it is beyond both high and low, not because the light is there.
- **The will to create is a novelty to Him and must be renewed by us.** Line 514: the Essence "is removed from will". The will to create comes through a tzimtzum, and on Rosh Hashanah, and every day, it is drawn anew by service. The Tanya's F01 has the word renewing creation every moment. It does not make that renewal depend on the acceptance of the yoke.
- **Mitzvah and creation come from two different wills.** The creation-will has a reason and is the root of time and space. The mitzvah-will has no reason at all and holds no root of limitation. That is why only the mitzvah reveals the Essence inside time and space (lines 587-614, 739-742). The Tanya ch. 41 gloss on the gevurot and chasadim of Atik is the seed. The range develops it at length (line 731).
- **Bitul as the vessel, and rising by leaps.** Between incommensurable levels one does not climb. One rises only by losing the former level (lines 292, 314, 368). The Tanya's bitul is chiefly the soul before its source. Here it is also the law of every ascent, in Gan Eden and in study.
- **The soul is rooted in the Essence, like Torah, and so goes down** (line 452). This is F06 stated through F05 and F07, where the Tanya says "part of God above".
- **Time** as interruption of the light and as ratzo and shov (line 695) has no parallel in the Tanya range.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 419 lines are editor's footnotes and 74 are front matter. They are all *none*. Four more are running heads with the date (694, 705, 717, 736), and line 325 is a starred cross-reference. - Kabbalistic mapping is marked structural, with a face only where the text itself draws the unity from it. Examples: Adam Kadmon, Akudim and Nekudim (393), the river of fire and the pillar (320-321), Binah and Chochmah as river and Eden (263-264), and the gevurot and chasadim of Atik (731, 737). - The soul-parables (lines 469, 584, 629, 634, 650-651) are *not* tagged F06. They use the soul as a picture of how the Essence relates to its powers (G06). They do not teach that the soul is part of God. F06 is tagged only where the text speaks of the soul's root or its spark (452, 482, 487, 660).
- *Reading* is used where the tie is ours: 302.1 (the rotting seed's "from nothing to something" heard as F01), 536.1 (creation's "descent" heard as F03's hiding) and 669 (the beginning of time tied to the unity). - F09 (unity of opposites) is almost absent. At line 720 the text contrasts ratzo and shov, which are opposites, with "reaches and does not reach", which are not. We did not tag F09 there, because the point is that no opposites arise at that level. - The halachic openings (lines 75-76, 455-456, 573-574) are structural. They become stated only where the text answers them from the doctrine (lines 104, 569).

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **Knowing the Essence (F12).** In SH 7 (Tanya range, line 834) the Alter Rebbe says that the unity of His Essence and knowledge is "only to be believed, with a faith above intellect". The Rashab (line 368) says that true *knowledge* that the Essence is beyond knowledge belongs only to one who studies the high levels of Or Ein Sof. One who does not study holds it "only by way of faith, which is not knowledge". This is the Rayatz's side of the SPINE's F12 dispute, stated by his father. The two are not formally contradictory, since the Alter Rebbe is speaking of the content and the Rashab of the knower's route. The emphasis differs, and we keep it.
- **Is there change in Him?** SH 7 says there is no change in Him before and after creation. Line 514 says the will to create "comes as a true novelty relative to Or Ein Sof", and line 521 says it must be drawn anew each Rosh Hashanah because "the Essence of Or Ein Sof is wholly removed from will". The Rashab places the novelty in the will, which is light, not in the Essence. That fits SH 7 in substance, but the language pulls the other way, and we have not smoothed it.
- **Who can take the light above the worlds.** Tanya 36 says the Torah enables the tzaddikim, and the worlds, to receive the light above the worlds without being nullified. The Rashab agrees (line 145), but he also says that most souls are not vessels for the light above the worlds. They feel its wonder but not the light itself, so their love is ratzo and not "love of delights" (line 310). This matches the SPINE's F02 note that very few truly reach the higher unity.
