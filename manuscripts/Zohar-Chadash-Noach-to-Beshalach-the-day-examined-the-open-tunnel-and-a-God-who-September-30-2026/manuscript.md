## What this range is

Seven short nodes on the Torah portions from Noach to Beshalach. The main thread is the soul: where it comes from, what it is for, how it is helped, and how it comes back when it strays. Noach turns into a long teaching on teshuvah and on speaking good of others. Lech Lecha reads Abraham's journey as the soul's descent into a body. Toldot has the wells Abraham and Isaac dug, read as the door to knowing God. Vayetzei and Vayeshev speak of Jerusalem, of Torah learned daily, and of Shabbat. Beshalach closes with Moshe's staff and the bitter waters sweetened.

## The unity of G-d

This range speaks of unity less in theory and more in how He acts. A few lines carry it.

**The soul is sent down from His throne to light a dark body.**

> באה הנשמה להאיר על הגוף האפל (ZC Noach 23, line 884)
> *The soul comes to shine on the dark body* (to lead it on a straight path).

**He rises from the throne of judgment.** On Rosh Hashanah, when the shofar is blown:

> וקודשא בריך הוא קם מכורסייא דדינא ויתיב בכורסיא דרחמי (ZC Noach 4, line 865)
> *And the Holy One rises from the throne of judgment and sits on the throne of mercy.*

**Mercy inside judgment.** The Flood was judgment, and still:

> וכן את מוצא רפואה זו, בכל דין שהוא עושה (ZC Noach 64, line 925)
> *And so you find this healing in every judgment He makes.* (He always leaves a remnant.)

**He does not want the death of the wicked.** On "He was grieved in His heart":

> אינו חפץ במיתתן, ולא היתה שמחה לפניו (ZC Noach 52, line 913)
> *He does not want their death, and there was no joy before Him.*

**The well is the door to Him.** The wells Abraham and Isaac dug are read as teaching the world to serve God:

> איהו פתחא למינדע ליה לקודשא בריך הוא (ZC Toldot 37, line 1170)
> *It is the door to knowing the Holy One.*

**His Name in the world depends on learning.** When the righteous learn:

> הקדוש ברוך הוא בונה עולמו, ונותן שמו בקרבו (ZC Vayetzei 50, line 1224)
> *The Holy One builds His world and places His Name within it.* (When they stop, He takes His Name out and the world is lacking.)

**Only Him, not an angel.** Moshe asks for God Himself. Later, in the world to come, the soul's garment is His light:

> אנכי מגן לך, אנכי ולא אחר (ZC Lech Lecha 105, line 1119)
> *"I am a shield to you": I, and no other.*

> ואין לו רשות להרהר במה שאינו יכול להשיג (ZC Vayetzei 13, line 1187)
> *And he has no permission to muse on what he cannot grasp.* (One must take on the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven.)

## States of consciousness

**Walking in His ways and being helped (S84 Trust).**

> כשהאדם הולך בדרכי קונו, הרבה מסייעין אותו (ZC Noach 25, line 886)
> *When a person walks in his Maker's ways, many help him.* (His soul, the angels, and the Shechinah.)

**Waited for.** This is the most tender line in the range, and we think it deserves its own place on the map:

> מחכה תמיד אימתי יעשו תשובה (ZC Noach 146, line 1007)
> *He waits always: when will they return?*

The line just before says He delayed the decree seven sabbaticals and seven jubilees "in case she returns". We propose (ours): **Z-S13 Waited for.** The felt knowing that God is not done with me and is waiting, now, for me to turn. It is the opposite of S73 (exile inside) felt from the other side. It differs from S77 (the lower return) because it begins with His waiting, not my effort.

**No sadness in Jerusalem (S76 and joy states).**

> מעולם לא נכנס אדם בירושלים והיה עצב (ZC Vayetzei 70, line 1244)
> *Never did a person enter Jerusalem and remain sad.*

**The Shechinah resting on one who draws near.**

> וכד איהו זכאה, שכינתא שריא עליה, ואוליפת ליה רזין (ZC Vayeshev 10, line 1259)
> *And when he is worthy, the Shechinah rests on him and teaches him secrets.*

**Anger and its cure.** Anger is a kind of idolatry here, and the measure of the righteous is:

> מדת הצדיק, קשה לכעוס, ונח לרצות (ZC Noach 46, line 907)
> *The measure of the righteous: hard to anger, easy to appease.* (The text reads Noach's name, "rest", into it: calm in mind, speech, and walk.)

**Pain is not held against a person.** Noah complained in pain, and:

> אין דנין את האדם לפי צערו (ZC Noach 106, line 967)
> *A person is not judged by [what he says in] his pain.*

This matters for anyone who has said hard things to God in grief.

## Meditation and practice

**The day from waking to sleep (ties to 24 evening look-back, 12 returning the mind, 21 the act that closes).** This is the fullest daily practice in the Zohar Chadash, given in Hebrew and Aramaic side by side:

> כמה יש לאדם ליזהר, ולבדוק עצמו ומעשיו בכל יום ויום (ZC Noach 66, line 927)
> *How much a person must take care and examine himself and his deeds every single day.*

> שיהא לבי נכון ומסור בידי, שלא אשכחך (ZC Noach 66, line 927)
> *[May it be Your will] that my heart be steady and given into my hand, that I not forget You.*

Then he watches his steps as he walks, runs to a mitzvah, and at night:

> ואי צריך יעשה מיד תשובה עליו קודם שיישן (ZC Noach 67, line 928)
> *And if needed, he does teshuvah for it at once, before he sleeps.*

We propose (ours): **Z-T12 The whole day held.** A morning sentence asking not to forget Him; watching the feet through the day; running, not walking, to a good deed; and an honest look back before sleep with immediate return. Technique 24 is only the last part. The Zohar Chadash gives the whole arc, and the first sentence is a prayer against forgetting, which is the core problem of S73.

> עולה נשמתו לשוט בארץ החיים (ZC Lech Lecha 78, line 1092)
> *His soul rises to roam in the land of the living* (when he says the Shema on his bed as it should be said).

**Speak only good of others.** Noah failed to pray for his generation. Gideon was "not righteous, nor the son of the righteous", yet:

> טיבותא דא דאמרת על בני, יהא לך חילא סגיאה (ZC Noach 119, line 980)
> *This good that you said about My children will be great strength for you.*

> דניחא ליה, מאן דאמר טבא על בנוהי (ZC Noach 118, line 979)
> *For it pleases Him when someone speaks good of His children.*

Elijah, who spoke against Israel, is made to swear he will always speak their merit. We propose (ours): **Z-T13 Speaking merit.** Before prayer, and in any hard moment with other people, say one true good thing about them to God. It is not a trick of positive thinking. It is the Zohar's claim that God is pleased by it and that it gives the speaker strength.

> המלמד תורה לתינוקות, דירתו עם השכינה (ZC Lech Lecha 69, line 1083)
> *One who teaches Torah to young children, his dwelling is with the Shechinah.*

**Bring someone back.** The righteous one runs after the wicked:

> אולי יחזירהו בתשובה, ומקרבו ומלמדו (ZC Lech Lecha 48, line 1062)
> *Perhaps he will bring him back in teshuvah; he draws him near and teaches him.*

**Kindness as Abraham's way of knowing God.** Abraham knew his Creator through chesed:

> ועביד טיבו עם בני נשא (ZC Toldot 9, line 1142)
> *And he did good to people* (and never let go of it).

**No wine with worship (the guide's "never drink").** This node gives the Zohar's own reason:

> תרי ענייני אינון דלא מתיישבי כחדא, יינא ופולחן שמיא (ZC Noach 94, line 955)
> *There are two things that do not sit together: wine and the service of Heaven.*

> כל המתעסק בתורה בכל יום, יתחדשו לו סתרים של מעלה (ZC Vayetzei 40, line 1214)
> *Whoever studies Torah every day, secrets from above are renewed for him.*

## The big questions

**Is there still a way back for me?** The parable of the tunnel: robbers were jailed in a tower; the clever ones dug a tunnel and fled. The fool saw the tunnel and stayed.

> רואים דרך התשובה פתוח לפניהם (ZC Noach 83, line 944)
> *They see the way of teshuvah open before them* (and do not want to escape).

> אפתחת תשובה כעינא דמחטא, אנא אפתח לה תרעין רברבין (ZC Lech Lecha 6, line 1020)
> *[If Israel] opens teshuvah like the eye of a needle, I will open great gates for her.*

**When should I return?** Now, while strong:

> בעוד שהנשמה בגוף, והגוף עומד בכח, היא, תשובה מעלייתא (ZC Lech Lecha 40, line 1054)
> *While the soul is in the body and the body stands in its strength, that is the best teshuvah.* (The parable follows of a man who gave the poor man food only after he was full.)

**Whose fault is it?** David, fleeing his son, blamed no one else:

> היה תולה הכל בעצמו, ומיד היה שב בתשובה (ZC Noach 136, line 997)
> *He hung it all on himself, and at once he returned.*

> וגלות האחרון אין לו קץ וזמן, אלא הכל תלוי בתשובה (ZC Noach 144, line 1005)
> *The last exile has no end and no set time; everything depends on teshuvah.*

> אלמלי שמרו ישראל שתי שבתות כהלכתן, מיד נגאלים (ZC Vayeshev 34, line 1283)
> *If Israel kept two Shabbatot properly, they would be redeemed at once.*

> הנשמה היא מורה ומלמדה לאדם (ZC Lech Lecha 16, line 1030)
> *The soul is the one that guides and teaches a person.* (Like a father leading a son.)

**Can one person change a decree?** Rabbi Shimon faces the destroying angel:

> דקודשא בריך הוא גזר למעלה, ואת מבטל לתתא (ZC Vayera 3, line 1132)
> *The Holy One decrees above, and you annul it below.*

**Can bitterness be healed?** At Marah the bitter water was sweetened; the Zohar reads the tree as teshuvah and Torah:

> סמא דחיי שריא באינון מיין (ZC Beshalach 10, line 1307)
> *A medicine of life rests in those waters.*

And Moshe, told he would not enter the land, accepted it:

> הצור תמים פעלו (ZC Beshalach 25, line 1322)
> *The Rock, His work is perfect.*

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus agrees and builds on this range in three places. The soul "sent to shine on the dark body" (884) is the Tanya's descent of the soul for the sake of an ascent. The day held from waking to sleep (927-928) matches the Chabad day from Modeh Ani to the bedtime Shema and cheshbon hanefesh. The line on speaking good of Israel (979-980) is the root of the Chassidic love of every Jew and of the Berditchever's defense of Israel before God.

Where Chassidus adds: Chassidus would say that the waiting God (1007) is also the God who gives the power to return. The Zohar Chadash says it briefly at 1020 (a needle's eye, and He opens great gates). The guide should keep both halves together.

What the guide should take (ours): the whole-day practice (Z-T12) as a better frame than technique 24 alone; "waited for" (Z-S13) for someone who believes they are finished; line 967 for someone ashamed of what they said in grief; line 955 as the Zohar's own backing for "never drink"; and speaking merit (Z-T13). Leave out the angels' names and the gate lists.
