## Part 1. The short answer

The sources describe one movement, done every morning. *(The sequence is stated across the sources; drawing it as one map is ours.)*

1. **Clear and give** (preparation). Put down the day's worries. Give charity. Take on love of the fellow. Make an honest, short reckoning. Bring one idea from learning.
2. **Acknowledge** (the opening). Hand the soul back. Begin with thanks alone. The ladder stands on the ground.
3. **Bring the animal near** (korbanos). The animal soul is not attacked. It is brought close, so that "a strong love is born in the animal soul too".
4. **Feed it, and prune** (Pesukei deZimra). Praise of the physical world is the animal soul's food. It also cuts away what clings from outside: the outer carving of the vessel.
5. **Show it its root** (Yotzer). The angels and holy chayos bow "with a great storm". The animal soul's own root is there. It is subdued by seeing its source bow.
6. **Pity the soul** (Ahavas Olam). Compassion on the spark that fell from a high roof to a deep pit.
7. **Hear that He alone is** (Shema). One in the heavens, the earth and the four directions, as before creation. The self given into the One.
8. **Come back down unbroken** (Baruch Shem). The lower unity: He fills time and place. The world returns as His name.
9. **Love with both hearts** (Ve'ahavta). The animal soul's own desire turned to Him; then love beyond measure. The inner carving: the heart hollowed.
10. **Let it settle** (Emes veYatziv). Confirmed from above. True in the physical as in the spiritual.
11. **Stand** (Amidah). Before the Essence, found in every place. Inner bittul, no longer even "someone who loves".
12. **Ask from nothingness** (the requests). Real needs, asked as His.
13. **Glue** (Tachanun). "To You I lift my soul", so as not to separate during the day.
14. **Go down** (Aleinu). "Know today and return it to your heart." Eyes down, heart up. One particular trait, one decision.
15. **Carry the imprint** (the day). A different person for some hours. The world as the King's garments. The old self returns; tomorrow, again.

The core teaching the owner names, "it doesn't need to be its own self", is stated most directly in three places: the Rebbe on the offerings (the animal soul's wrong turns are "not the true reality"), Torah Ohr on Yotzer (the animal soul is subdued only "through its root", so it is shown its root bowing), and the Alter Rebbe on Ve'ahavta (the animal soul's own love turns to G-d). The Rashab gives the method: the contemplation must be of things "even the natural mind of the animal soul" can understand (KT §1, §8). *(The grouping of these three as "the teaching" is ours.)*

## Part 3. The translation: a Chassidic prayer as a conversation *(all ours)*

**What it is.** A guided conversation of 15 to 40 minutes that keeps the inner movement of the morning prayer without requiring its words. It works for a person of any background. It is not a replacement for the prayer a Jew is obliged in; for a Jew it can be preparation for it, or the "inner" of it. The guide says so when relevant.

**Principles.** - One stage per message. Short. Wait for the person. Do not narrate the map. - The order matters: ground, feed, show, hear, stand, ask, go down. Do not jump to the Shema or to bittul. - Speak to the animal soul as a "part of you" that is invited, never as an enemy. - Offer real words only at three points, and only as an offer: the first verse of the Shema, "Blessed be the name...", and "To You I lift my soul". Give them in English, with the Hebrew transliteration if wanted. - Measure by change, not by feeling (PR-21).

**Short form (the businessman's minute, KT §16).** Steps 1, 5, 10, 14, 17. Five messages. "Every moment more is like buying life for the whole day" (stated).

**Signs the conversation worked.** The person names a concrete decision. They report less self-noise, not a big feeling. The restless part was included, not shamed. *(ours)*

**Guards.** - Not at a crisis peak: presence and one practical step first (animal-soul METHODS step 2). - Skip the reckoning (3) and keep the pity (9) brief for anyone low or depressed. - Dissolving language ("you disappear into the One") is not used; the sources' bittul is a standing before Him, not a loss of the person. Offer "held in the One" instead. - Do not test the person on feeling; the Mitteler Rebbe's counterfeit is wanting to be moved. - Do not present the conversation as the halachic prayer, and do not ask a Jew to skip actual prayer for it. - The Shema verse is offered, never required. For someone not Jewish, it can be heard as Israel's verse that speaks of the One of all. - The silence in step 14 is voluntary and short.

## PR-01 · Prayer as the integration

*Stage: The frame. Whose: stated (the frame and the quotes); the ordering of stages below as one map is ours, built on Kuntres HaTefillah §10 (line 85) and Hayom Yom 5 Kislev.*

**What it is.** In Chabad, prayer is the daily place where what the G-dly soul knows is brought home to the heart and to the animal soul. Learning is the preparation. Prayer is where the knowledge is 'returned to the heart' and where the animal soul is refined. It is a ladder: it stands on the ground in plain acknowledgment and reaches up to self-nullification, passing through the praises, the blessings of the Shema and the Shema.

**From inside.** Mind and heart meet. The Rashab: the main thing is 'the returning to the heart'. The Rayatz: prayer is 'the ladder of the soul's connection with G-dliness'. Without it, knowledge sits outside the person and can even sour into ego.

**How one enters it.** Learn first; then, in prayer, contemplate at length and in detail until the idea is felt; let it reach the heart; then carry it into the day's deeds. The order of the liturgy is the order of the inner work: ground, praise, angels, unity, standing, descent.

**Signs it is real.** The idea that was 'known' becomes felt. Afterwards there is a decision about one particular trait, and it holds in the moment of test.

**Counterfeit.** Learning much Chassidus and never praying with it. The Rashab: then what was learned 'turns into poison' and makes one a 'something'. Also: prayer by habit, fast, 'like everyone'.

**Guards.** Not a test of feeling. The beinoni's prayer-love that fades afterwards is still real for his level (Tanya ch. 13). Do not shame a person who cannot pray at length; the Rashab gives the businessman a shorter way (PR-20).

**Sources.** - «דהעיקר הוא ההשבה אל הלב» — *the main thing is the returning to the heart.* Kuntres HaTefillah §5. `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 62 (OCR). - «תפלה היא סולם ההתקשרות של הנשמה באלקות» — *prayer is the ladder of the soul's connection with G-dliness.* Hayom Yom, 5 Kislev (Rayatz). `Chassidus-txt/R. **In a chat today.** "This is not about saying words right. It is a time to let what you know is true actually reach the part of you that lives your life."

## PR-02 · Clearing the room

*Stage: Preparation (hachanah). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** Before prayer one clears the mind of the day's business and worries, does not rush in straight from the street, and stands in one place.

**From inside.** The Rashab's image: you already know how to set worries down, because you do it every night to fall asleep. If prayer were dear to you, you would do it for prayer too.

**How one enters it.** 1. Do not start the moment you come in; wait the space of 'two doorways'. 2. Set aside every worry, even neutral thoughts. 3. If it will not go, make a reckoning: if I can drop it for my body's sleep, all the more for my soul's life. 4. Stand in one place; do not walk about.

**Signs it is real.** Quiet that is not sleepy. The mind is free for one subject.

**Counterfeit.** Pacing during prayer and calling it 'breadth of mind'. The Rashab: that is only 'broadening of the outer spirit and self-feeling'.

**Guards.** At a crisis peak, do not demand that the person 'drop' a real emergency; first meet the need (animal-soul METHODS order, step 2; ours to apply here).

**Sources.** - «שלא ימהר להתפלל מיד בבואו מהחוץ» — *he should not hurry to pray right when he comes in from outside.* Kuntres HaTefillah §11. `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 88 (OCR). - «הלא בעניניו הגשמיים כמו בשינה עד"ם ה"ה מסיר טרדת לבבו וישן» — *in his physical affairs, sleep for example, he removes the worry from his heart and sleeps.* Kuntres HaTefillah §11. `Chassidus-txt/R. Shalom Dovber Schneersohn (Rebbe Rashab) (1860-1920)/Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt`, line 88 (OCR). - «שאין זה רק התרחבות הרוח החיצוני והרגש עצמי» — *this is nothing but a broadening of the outer spirit and self-feeling.* Kuntres HaTefillah §11. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-03 · Giving before asking

*Stage: Preparation (hachanah). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** Charity before prayer, and taking on the love of one's fellow. Rabbi Elazar gave a coin to a poor man and then prayed. The Alter Rebbe's siddur opens: 'I take upon myself the commandment to love your fellow as yourself.'

**From inside.** Giving life to someone adds life to the prayer. The Rashab explained it with a gesture of his hand from below upward. Love of the fellow is 'the gate of entry' through which one comes to stand before G-d.

**How one enters it.** Give something, even small, before praying. Say or mean: I take on loving the other as myself. The Rashab sometimes went to find a poor man to feed before prayer.

**Signs it is real.** The self loosens before the prayer begins. Coming in as one of many, not alone.

**Counterfeit.** Charity as a transaction to 'buy' an answer; love of the fellow as a formula said and not meant.

**Guards.** Any sum, any person. Not for someone with no money: a kind act or a kind thought is the same movement (ours).

**Sources.** - «רבי אלעזר יהיב פרוטה לעני והדר מצלי» — *Rabbi Elazar gave a coin to a poor man and then prayed.* Tanya, Iggeres HaKodesh (on the time of prayer). `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. ## PR-04 · Washing and the honest reckoning

*Stage: Preparation (hachanah). Whose: stated; placing the mikvah under this heading is ours.*

**What it is.** Lower teshuvah before higher teshuvah. Prayer is 'higher return', so it is preceded by humbling: the reckoning of what in one's thought, speech and deed was 'not for G-d'. The chassidim's mikvah before prayer belongs here as an image: Chassidus 'must wash the flesh' of its habits before one puts on the holy garments.

**From inside.** A sober heaviness (koved rosh), then relief. The Rayatz: precisely through the heaviness and bitterness before prayer, the heart can move in prayer. The Rashab: each person must be 'a vessel and a dwelling' for G-d's light, and a king's house must be clean.

**How one enters it.** 1. Make an honest account, at set times (the Rashab: not every night for most people; privately; best at midnight). 2. Name particulars, not a general 'I am bad'. 3. Then gladden the soul and learn, so as to pray in joy.

**Signs it is real.** Bitterness that is alive and particular, short, and turns into joy. The heart becomes 'broken', which the Rashab calls the true vessel.

**Counterfeit.** Sadness passing for piety: dull, general, constant. Self-reckoning used as self-hatred.

**Guards.** Not for a depressed or very low person, and not at a crisis (animal-soul PSYCHOLOGY part 6; ours). Bitterness has its set time; prayer itself must be in joy. The mikvah is a Jewish custom; offer only its inner shape to others (ours). Our library search found the mikvah before prayer only in passing (Rayatz sichos); the washing image is from Hayom Yom.

**Sources.** - «ומאחר שהתפלה היא בחינת תשובה עילאה – צריך להקדים לפניה בחינת תשובה תתאה» — *since prayer is higher teshuvah, it must be preceded by lower teshuvah.* Tanya, Iggeres HaTeshuvah ch. 10. `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 994. - «שע"י הכובד ראש שקודם התפלה, שמתמרמר בנפשו, עי"ז דוקא יכול להיות אח"כ התפעלות המדות בתפלה» — *through the heaviness before prayer, when he is bitter in his soul, precisely by this the traits can be moved afterwards in prayer.* Hayom Yom, 20 Tishrei. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-05 · Bringing a thought in

*Stage: Preparation (hachanah). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** Learning a subject in Chassidus before prayer, so that prayer has something to contemplate. The Rashab: after putting on tallis and tefillin, think over a subject for half an hour or more. The Rayatz names three contemplations: in study (the idea shines), before prayer (feeling the life of the idea), in prayer (feeling the G-dliness in it).

**From inside.** Before prayer the aim is not new understanding but 'the feeling of the life of the matter'.

**How one enters it.** Choose one idea already understood, e.g. that each thing is made from nothing now. Go over it slowly. Ask what life it has, not only what it means.

**Signs it is real.** The idea starts to feel alive and close.

**Counterfeit.** Studying new material during prayer; the Rashab forbids inventing new readings while praying (§3). General contemplation 'is no contemplation at all'.

**Guards.** Busy people: at least learn a little (§16). The idea must be one the person can hold.

**Sources.** - «שקודם התפלה, ענינה הרגש חיות הענין שלמד» — *(the contemplation) before prayer: its business is feeling the life of the matter he learned.* Hayom Yom, 20 Tammuz. `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hayom Yom (Brooklyn, New York, 1942).txt`, line 377.

**In a chat today.** "Let's take one thought into this. Just one, simple: everything you see is being given existence right now. Don't analyse it. See if it has any life for you."

## PR-06 · Handing the soul back

*Stage: Preparation and the opening (Modeh Ani, the morning blessings, Hodu). Whose: stated; reading 'Hodu' as the acknowledgment that grounds the ladder is ours (Hayom Yom speaks of the beginning of prayer generally).*

**What it is.** The morning blessing 'My G-d, the soul You gave me... You will take it from me' becomes a decision: since it is Yours, I return it to You now, to unite with Your oneness. And prayer begins with acknowledgment alone (hoda'ah): 'Hodu', 'Give thanks'. The ladder stands on the ground.

**From inside.** Not yet understanding or feeling. A simple 'yes, it is Yours'. Thanks before any grasp.

**How one enters it.** On waking, or at the start, say plainly: this life is given; I give it back for this hour. Then begin with thanks, without needing to understand anything yet.

**Signs it is real.** Ease. One begins without needing to feel much.

**Counterfeit.** Waiting to feel something before starting.

**Guards.** For anyone. Thanks is the floor, not a performance.

**Sources.** - «לכן, מעתה אני מוסרה ומחזירה לך לייחדה באחדותך» — *therefore, from now I hand it over and return it to You, to unite it with Your oneness.* Tanya ch. 41. `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (1745-1812)/Tanya__Alter-Rebbe__Sefaria-Kehot/Tanya — Hebrew text.txt`, line 621. - «התחלת התפלה היא בהודאה לבד» — *the beginning of prayer is in acknowledgment alone.* Hayom Yom, 5 Kislev. `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hayom Yom (Brooklyn, New York, 1942).txt`, line 585.

**In a chat today.** "Start with thanks. Not because you feel it yet. Just name three things you didn't make and were given this morning."

## PR-07 · Bringing the animal near

*Stage: The offerings (korbanos). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** 'A person who brings near from you an offering to G-d: from the animal...' The Alter Rebbe reads 'from you' as the G-dly soul's self-giving alone, and 'your offering... from the animal' as the animal soul, to be refined into submission and transformation through contemplation it can follow, until 'a strong love is born in the animal soul too'. The offerings on the altar raise the animal soul to its root in the holy chayos.

**From inside.** The Rebbe: the offering is the drawing-near of one's powers and senses; one contemplates that what sinned was the animal soul and its inclination, 'which is not the true reality of a Jew'. The animal is not destroyed. It is brought close.

## PR-08 · Feeding the animal first

*Stage: Pesukei deZimra (the verses of praise). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** The long praises of G-d in creation are food for the animal soul. 'One may not eat before feeding his animal.' Pesukei deZimra is also 'zemer' as pruning: it cuts away the externals that cling around the soul. The Alter Rebbe's image: a vessel carved from a block of wood needs two carvings, first from outside, then a hollow inside. Pesukei deZimra is the outer carving; the Shema is the hollow.

**How one enters it.** 1. Praise concrete things the animal soul knows. 2. See each one as made from nothing now. 3. Say it aloud, slowly, with a melody if possible. 4. Our generation needs this part longer, because the animal soul hides more.

**Signs it is real.** The animal soul's resistance softens. Interest replaces boredom. The Alter Rebbe: this arousal is only 'the preparation of the block'.

**Guards.** For anyone. Do not stay only here.

## PR-09 · The animal soul listens

## PR-10 · Seeing the angels bow

*Stage: The blessings before the Shema (Yotzer Or). Whose: stated*

**From inside.** The image of ministers bowing before a king: watching them, one catches their awe. The storm of the ofanim is the sound of a 'something' that is overwhelmed by what it senses.

**Counterfeit.** Fear of punishment, or spectacle. The Alter Rebbe calls the angels' bowing 'external' compared with the soul's own bittul in the Amidah (PR-16).

## PR-11 · Compassion on your own soul

*Stage: The blessings before the Shema (Ahavas Olam). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** If the preparations did not open the heart, arouse great compassion on your soul: it came down 'from a high roof to a deep pit'. Its root is higher than the angels; now it is wrapped in a body and an animal soul. The main place for this is the blessing 'Ahavas Olam'.

**From inside.** Not self-pity. Pity for the spark that is held captive. The Rashab: 'this is easy for a person'. And we ask for 'Your great mercies' because we do not know how great our fall is.

## PR-12 · Hear: He alone, everywhere

*Stage: The Shema, first verse. Whose: stated*

The letters of Echad: the ches is the seven heavens and earth, above and below; the dalet the four directions. Echad means He is alone in heaven and earth 'as He was alone before creation'. **Counterfeit.** A general flash of 'all is one' with no detail; the Rashab calls general contemplation an imagination without existence (§2). Merging talk that erases the person.

**Guards.** Not for someone whose sense of self is fragile; dissolving language can harm (ours; see consciousness-map MAP.md cautions). The verse is Torah and may be offered to anyone; a Jew is obliged to say the Shema itself at its time (ours to note).

## PR-13 · Blessed be the Name: the lower unity

## PR-14 · With both hearts, all soul, beyond measure

*Stage: The Shema, 'and you shall love'. Whose: stated*

**What it is.** 'With all your heart' is with both inclinations: the animal soul's desire turned from this world's pleasures to love of G-d. 'With all your soul' is the soul that mediates and joins the G-dly and animal souls (and, in the Tanya and elsewhere, mesirus nefesh). 'With all your might' (me'od) is love without limit, reached only when the animal soul's evil has been turned. This is the inner carving: the point of the heart is hollowed and lifted from its place.

**From inside.** The heart's own wanting becomes love of Him. The Alter Rebbe: 'my heart is hollow within me'; 'my flesh and my heart pine away'. A love that has no edge.

**How one enters it.** 1. Take the animal soul's strongest wanting. 2. Ask what it really wants: life, pleasure, being held. 3. Show it that He is the source of that life. 4. Let the same force turn toward Him. 5. Then, beyond measure: do not count.

**Signs it is real.** The urge itself warms toward G-d instead of being only suppressed. Inner change, not only outer stirring (the hollow, not the outer carving).

**Counterfeit.** Love talked about but the desire unchanged; or the desire 'transformed' only in words.

## PR-16 · Standing before the King

*Stage: The Amidah (Shemoneh Esreh). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** Before the Amidah, think briefly but with full binding: the Essence is revealed, found in every place, where hiding and revealing do not apply, and before Him one stands and speaks face to face. Great awe falls, and inner bittul: 'the main point of the Amidah'. The Alter Rebbe: the love of the Shema leads to inner fear, 'bittul bimetzius', the soul's own bowing, beyond the angels' outward bowing; beyond even the love that runs, which is still 'someone who loves'. The bowings of the Amidah enact in deed the yoke accepted in the Shema in speech.

**From inside.** Silence. No more running. The one who loves is no longer felt. Standing, feet together, quiet.

**How one enters it.** 1. Stop moving. 2. Think: He is here, in this place, fully. 3. Speak to Him as 'You'. 4. Let the self be too small to lift itself.

**Signs it is real.** Quiet awe; no self-commentary. The Mitteler Rebbe's rule: the deeper the arousal, the less one feels it (PR-21).

**Counterfeit.** Intense emotion displayed; a 'mystical state' one is aware of having.

**Guards.** Silence can frighten some people; keep it short and voluntary (ours).

## PR-17 · Asking from nothingness

*Stage: The Amidah's requests (the descent into needs). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** The Amidah is, in halachah, the asking for one's needs: health, livelihood, wisdom. The Rebbe: even while asking, one is in a movement of bittul; this is not two things. The Maggid's reading: the request is that what is lacking above be filled, for the soul is a part of G-d. At the height, 'he needs nothing for himself; all his needs are a need on high'.

**From inside.** Asking without grasping. The needs are real, and they are laid before Him as His.

**How one enters it.** 1. Name a real need, plainly. 2. Then: this lack is also a lack in His world; I ask for it as His. 3. Leave it with Him.

**Signs it is real.** Asking feels clean, not anxious. Afterwards, less clutching.

**Counterfeit.** Bargaining. Or 'spiritual' refusal to ask, as if needs were beneath bittul.

**Guards.** Never tell a suffering person their need is not real (ours).

## PR-18 · The glue: falling on the face

*Stage: Tachanun (nefilas apayim). Whose: stated*

**What it is.** After the Amidah: 'To You, G-d, I lift my soul.' The Siddur im Dach: this is the soul given to be included in the One in complete self-giving, higher than the Amidah, like two things joined with glue: they are counted as one body and are never separated. So that through the day's business he will not separate from G-d's oneness.

**From inside.** Leaning one's whole weight on Him. A bonding that is meant to last past the prayer.

**How one enters it.** 1. Put your head down on your arm. 2. Lift the soul to Him: 'to You'. 3. Ask that this hold through the day.

**Signs it is real.** A sense of being joined, not merely moved.

**Counterfeit.** Collapse or despair taken for surrender.

**Guards.** Not grief-falling; it is the closest closeness (ours).

**Sources.** - «אשא ממש ליכלל באחד במס"נ גמור» — *'I lift', literally, to be included in the One in complete self-giving.* Siddur im Dach. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-19 · Eyes down, heart up

*Stage: Aleinu and the descent into the day. Whose: stated; that Aleinu holds the 'know today' verse is a fact of the liturgy; tying it to KT §5 is ours.*

**What it is.** The prayer ends by turning outward. Aleinu contains the verse the Rashab calls the whole service: 'Know today and return it to your heart, that Havayah is G-d in the heavens above and on the earth below; there is no other.' The Rebbe: even at the height of cleaving, the aim must be to act in the world outside oneself; 'eyes down, heart up'. The Mitteler Rebbe: the return is the main thing; all the rising was for the sake of coming down.

**From inside.** Leaving without losing. The world is the place the prayer was for.

**How one enters it.** 1. Say the verse, or its meaning. 2. Name the first thing you will do today. 3. Name one particular trait to watch in it (the Rashab §13: a general resolve does not work on the particular).

**Signs it is real.** A concrete decision. Wanting to go out, not to stay.

**Counterfeit.** Spiritual hangover: irritation at the world after prayer.

**Sources.** - «גם בתכלית הדביקות והתפשטות הגשמיות שבתפלה צריך להיות המכוון לפעול בעולם שמחוץ ממנו» — *even at the height of cleaving and stripping off physicality in prayer, the aim must be to act in the world outside himself.* Likkutei Sichos vol. 35, Bereishis 2. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-20 · The imprint through the day

**What it is.** After a good prayer a person is 'a different man' for some hours: the animal soul is weakened. Every extra moment in prayer, even for a businessman, is 'like buying life for the whole day'. Through the day, habituate the mind: all you see, heaven and earth, are the King's outer garments (Tanya ch. 41-42). And honestly: in the beinoni, after prayer the evil wakes again (Tanya ch. 12), so one prays again tomorrow.

**From inside.** A light, background awareness. Returning to it in free moments.

**How one enters it.** 1. Pray at least a little more slowly than you would. 2. In free moments, glance at what you see as His garment. 3. Expect the old self to return; that is not failure.

**Signs it is real.** Fewer reactions in the day; a pull back to Him when there is a gap.

**Counterfeit.** Expecting the high to last and despairing when it fades.

**Guards.** The light contemplation is light: not constant brooding (ours, following animal-soul METHODS on set times).

**Sources.** - «הנה גם כמה שעות אחר התפלה הוא» — *even several hours after prayer he is (a different person).* Kuntres HaAvodah ch. 4. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-21 · Godly arousal, not the self

*Stage: Across all stages: the test. Whose: stated*

**What it is.** The Mitteler Rebbe's rule for prayer: the arousal should be 'only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the life of flesh'. The counterfeit is the wish to be moved so as 'to have some vitality, to be a something'; its name is 'serving oneself'. The great rule: the deeper the arousal of the essence, the less one feels it.

**From inside.** The true is quiet and leaves no 'I had a great prayer'. The false is loud inside and needs to be noticed.

**How one enters it.** Ask after any stage: was I moved by Him, or by being moved? Aim at the thing itself, not at the feeling (Mitteler Rebbe, animal-soul METHODS M15).

**Signs it is real.** Less self-feeling; change in a trait; humility afterwards.

**Counterfeit.** Pride in one's prayer. The Rashab: the scholar who prays long and credits himself has fallen below the simple man saying Psalms (darkei-hachassidus 4.5).

**Guards.** Do not make people anxious about every feeling. The first arousal may be 'from the side of the yesh' and refined later (R. Aharon of Strashelye; animal-soul METHODS).

**Sources.** - «שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר» — *that the arousal of their souls be only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the life of flesh.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut. `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 135. - «וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו» — *and this is called serving oneself.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut. `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 167. - «וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר כמו באהבה ורצון ותענוג היותר פנימית, יותר לא ירגיש בה» — *this is the great rule: the deeper the arousal of the essence, as in the most inward love, will and delight, the less he feels it.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## PR-22 · Slowly, with whom

*Stage: Across all stages: the pace. Whose: stated*

**What it is.** Chassidic prayer is slow. The Rayatz as a boy was pained that his father, the Rashab, did not pray fast like everyone; his uncle explained, 'father cannot say the letters quickly'. And the old chassidim, after the Shabbos service, began to contemplate 'with what one prays and with whom one prays'.

**From inside.** Each word weighs something. Two questions frame it: what am I saying, and to whom.

**How one enters it.** 1. Fewer words, slower. 2. Before and after, ask: with what am I praying? With whom?

**Signs it is real.** Words land. Time stretches.

**Counterfeit.** Slowness as display; length without contemplation.

**Guards.** Not a rule for children or the busy; the floor is a little, slowly (Rashab §14, §16).

**Sources.** - «כי אבא אינו יכול להגיד האותיות במהירות» — *for father cannot say the letters quickly.* Sefer HaSichos (Rayatz), Reshimas HaMaasar. `Chassidus-txt/R.
