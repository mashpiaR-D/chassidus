## The shape of the range

Hemshech Ayin Beis is one long series of discourses, not a set of separate maamarim. Each Shabbat or festival adds chapters to a single argument. In this range the argument is about the word *sefirah*. The Rashab reads it four ways: as number (*mispar*), as telling (*sippur*), as sapphire brightness (*sapir*), and as a book or parchment (*sefer*). Each reading answers one question: how does anything limited (a sefirah, a vessel, a world) come from the Ein Sof, and how does it still show the Ein Sof?

So the range's unity is not mainly "the world is nothing before Him", as in the Tanya. It is a theory of revelation. The Essence is not even light (line 719). Light cleaves to its source and would rather rise than shine (706-707). Vessels hide the light, and by hiding it they make it shine (720). Atzilut exists *so that* Godliness can be known in the worlds (737). That is why F05 (the Essence) and F03 (hiding for the sake of revelation) lead the count, and F12 (knowing) comes third. The Tanya's cosmological core, F01 and F02, is present but is placed inside this frame. Two passages state it as strongly as anything in the Tanya: "both knowledges are true" (794) and "the world is Godliness" (1043).

## Shabbat Teshuvah, end (588-601): one will from Atzilut to Asiyah

The first lines finish the three levels of teshuvah (nefesh, ruach, neshamah). Most of it is structural. The neshamah's teshuvah is over the lack of Godly comprehension, and the Rashab cites the Iggeres HaKodesh that knowing the order of worlds is "a great mitzvah" (line 590, F12). The general power of teshuvah is "the very point of his Jewishness" (593, F06).

Chapter 79 states the range's first unity claim. Chochmah of Asiyah is a different essence from Chochmah of Atzilut. Will (keter) is not: > מ"מ אין בזה התחלקו' מדרי' בעצם דהכל הוא בחי' רצון והיינו דהכל הוא מהות א' בעצם (line 595)

> ובהמשכת הכתרים הוא שעצם המהות נמשך בכל מקום והכל הוא מהות א' ממש (line 597)

Torah is the one exception on the side of wisdom. It is the same essence in Asiyah as in Atzilut (596, F10). The simple Jew who is "tam" in deed reaches the aleph, simple will (597). This is G05, the highest in the lowest.

## Sukkot and Shemini Atzeres (602-668): the surrounding light, temimus, and number

**The surrounding light is not divided (602-621).** "Circles" of ten sefirot differ only in how much light shows, not in essence. The inner light is weakened when it is lessened. The surrounding light is not: > משא"כ באו"מ הגם שהוא בבחי' מיעוט ה"ז בבחי' התוקף ממש כמו בבחי' ריבוי האור (line 620)

**Temimus (622-634).** Wholeness belongs to the self, not to what spreads out from it (622). Its bitul is in the point of will above reason. It shows as trust: the businessman keeps his fixed times for Torah and prayer (623, F08). Line 625 gives a clear F04 reading of nature. Memale is the "cause of causes", the source of what goes by nature: > עה"ע הוא בחי' ממכ"ע ומשם נמשכי' הדברים שהולכין כסדרן שע"פ הטבע וכענין הטבע בגימט' אלקים (line 625)

The *tamim* ranks above the *tzaddik*: the tzaddik is memale, the tamim is sovev (626). "Emes" is one will from head to end: > והכל אמת בעצם נקודת הרצון בכל הכחות עד סוף מעשה (line 629)

The sukkah is true surrounding light. "Every native (ezrach) in Israel" is the inner makif that "will shine" (yazriach) in the future, and it is sat in now (631, F07).

**Number (636-668).** Before the tzimtzum there are sefirot "without number", because "You are One, but not in number" (639). Number only reveals what was hidden. True limit belongs only to the lower worlds: > דבבי"ע ההגבלה היא בבחי' התחדשות דהיינו בבחי' מציאות יש ממש (line 640)

The ten are there in the root, "with no distinguishable existence at all" (641). Revelation happens by limiting: "whatever is not limited and shaped is not revealed" (654, F03). Israel draw "without number" into number through Torah and teshuvah: > להיות גילוי אוא"ס בעולם (line 664)

## Noach and Vayera (669-734): providence, the soul as Godliness, and why light needs a vessel

**Providence (671-677).** The stars do not perish. That is not their nature, since anything made of parts comes apart. It is the Creator's will, and "in this the power of Ein Sof is seen by the senses" (672, F01). Line 673 names the F07 doubt: all agree Godliness is in heaven, but "where is their God" is asked of the physical mitzvot. Providence is the creatures' very life (674). It is hidden under the name Elokim for the sake of a physical flow: > וכנודע דבכ"מ שהאור בגילוי הוא מיעוט הישות ובכדי שיהי' בבחי' יש צ"ל התעלמות האור (line 675)

**Souls (678-684).** This is the range's strongest F06. The stars endure only by a power of Ein Sof that stays outside them. Souls are otherwise: > אבל הנשמות ה"ה אלקות ממש בעצם מהותם (line 678)

They cleave by "an unnoticed cleaving" even in the lower worlds (680-681), so their endurance is their own: > וי"ל ג"כ הוא קיים בחי' העצמות שהוא חי וקיים לעד שהרי מציאותו מעצמותו (line 682)

Through souls, Godliness is seen in the world (683).

**Evil included in "all" (691-699).** "He creates evil" is read aloud as "creates all". Even the sitra achra falls under His kingship (691, F03 + F11). True fear is "es Hashem", being secondary to Him with no will of one's own (693, F08). Prayer turns "tzarah" (trouble) into "tzohar" (light) by subduing the animal soul (697), and that reveals the Essence (696).

**Light and vessel (703-734).** Light by nature rises toward its source, like fire (706): > כ"א כל תשוקתו הוא להיות כלול במקורו (line 707)

Vessels draw it down against its nature, because their root is higher (717-718). Here is G05 inside the sefirot: "the power of Or Ein Sof above the lights is precisely in the vessels" (718). The Essence itself is not light: > דבחי' עצמות המאציל הרי אינו בגדר אור כלל וכמ"ש ישת חשך סתרו (line 719)

Only vessels, which hide a little, make it shine (720). Mitzvot are those vessels: "as if you made Me" (714, F10). Holy intellect nullifies because the thing grasped is felt in it. The other side's intellect understands creation ex nihilo and stays a "something" (711, F08). Good comprehension is "how both opposites are true" (712, F09).

## Chayei Sarah and Toldos (735-804): Atzilut so that He be known; the two knowledges

**Why Atzilut (737-744).** This is F12 at its most basic: > דהנה ידוע דכללות ענין האצי' הוא בכדי שיהי' גילוי אלקות בעולמות (line 737)

From creation alone we would know only "that there is a God who creates" (739), because creation is His hiding (737). The Essence is "wholly hidden" (740). Nothing would be revealed of it except through Atzilut (741).

**Negative knowledge (777-787).** Comprehension compels its own opposite. Having grasped a Godly matter, one knows it must be only a radiance (780), and: > כ"א ההשגה היא אשר תכריח את השגת והרגש הבלתי מושג (line 781)

By denying all the levels of the world's wisdom of Him, one gains "recognition of how Or Ein Sof is transcendent in wisdom" (785).

**Higher and lower knowledge (788-804).** This is the range's F02 charter. Lower knowledge says below is "something" and above is "nothing". Higher knowledge says the reverse (788, 792). Then: > הנה באמת מצד האוא"ס ב"ה שניהם אמת, וז"ע אל דעות ה' דש' אל נושא ב' הדעות (line 794)

The light holds both: an existence by the tzimtzum, nothing by its cleaving (795). Creation is lower knowledge. Sinai draws higher knowledge down through Torah and mitzvot (796-797, F10). Separating the dross (Yishmael, Esav) came first, because Torah reveals the Essence (800).

## Vayetze, Vayishlach, Vayeshev (805-914): vessels that reveal, the heap and the pillar, and the unity below

**Vessels are not garments (807-828).** The body is a "united garment" that shows the soul (810-811). The soul's powers hide its essence even as they reveal it (816). The vessels of Atzilut exist to reveal: > כ"א תכלית ענינם הוא לגלות אוא"ס (line 822)

"Let there be light" is the Essence shining through the Chesed of Malchus (823). Before the sin, "all was Godliness" (824). The sefirot are "tikkunim" because through them Or Ein Sof shines in the world, "the ultimate intent of the Emanator" (825, F07).

**Lavan's heap and Yaakov's pillar (829-881).** Holy sefirot are in "utmost oneness" with Or Ein Sof. The lights' union is "yachid" and the vessels' union is "echad" (840, F02). The other side has ten sefirot too, but "in utter separation" (841). Refining needs a light above the measure, "a voice above the firmament", which is Torah (844). Honour is the surrounding shell and the worst: > דכל ענין הקדושה הוא הביטול והנחת עצמותו, ובזה הוא בבחי' הרגש עצמו ביותר (line 845)

Yaakov "turned darkness to light far beyond measure" (847). Against the heap stands the pillar, and the Shach's gematria that "gal" equals "Hashem echad": > דכאשר יש היחוד דה' אחד אין הגל והפרסא מפסיק כלל (line 877)

The large dalet of "echad" is Malchus above the worlds. Before it: > אבל לגבי בחי' סובב אין היש תופס מקום כלל והכל בטל במציאו' (line 873)

The contemplation of the Shema follows: > איך שאני ה' לא שניתי ממש כמו קודם ברה"ע כי גם עתה בשמים ממעל ובארץ מתחת אין עוד כי כולא קמי' כלא ממש חשיבי (line 876)

**The unity below (884-914).** In the Temple, and in Shlomo's time, "the moon was full": the lowest of Malchus shone like its inside (886-890). Only Israel comprehend Godliness in the utmost hiding, "because in their essence they are Godliness" (889). The range's clearest F07 comes next: > דהנה תכלית כוונת יעקב הי' להמשיך שגם למטה בבי"ע יהי' איהו וחיוהי וגרמוהי חד כמו שהוא באצי' (line 894)

The halachah makes the natural intellect a vessel for His wisdom (894). The hand that gives charity becomes a vessel for Or Ein Sof (895). Prayer turns bitter into sweet by contemplating the Godly life in each thing (901-902). Then: > ועי"ז נעשה אתהפכא חשול"נ והיינו שחשך הגשמי של עוה"ז מעשה שמים וארץ שנראים ליש ודבר גשמי יתהפך לנהורא להיות בו גילוי אוא"ס להיות היש בטל אליו ית' ונגלה כבוד ה' למטה כמו שהוא למע' (line 905)

Souls come from the inner vessels, and angels and "something" from the outer (911).

## Miketz (Chanukah) and Vayigash (915-991): the point of bonding, the name of the Essence, and silence

**The point (917-925).** Chochmah is colourless, and so it shows every colour truly (919). In service, the foundation is an essential bitul in the point of the heart: > כ"א בבחי' התקשרו' וביטול עצמי בעצם נקודת לבו ונפשו שמתקשר בעצם אלקו' (line 920)

Only from that point is anything done "in its truth" (921). To the point, contemplation and charity are equal (922).

**The name of the Essence (926-947).** White is the essential colour. Havayah is the "name of the Essence", not added to Him (929). The Rashab cites the Tanya's gloss in ch. 35: > דאוא"ס אחד האמת שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו וזו היא מדרי' החכ' (line 932)

Torah is the lights' union, "entirely one"; mitzvot are "limbs of the King" (941-942, F10). Then comes the range's sharpest statement of the lower unity's status: > שאין זה כפי האמת דהרי באמת אין שום מציאו' דאני הוי' לא שניתי וכולא קמי' כל"ח כו', א"כ ה"ז רק דמיון לבד (line 944)

The Hasmoneans won by self-sacrifice, which draws the Essence (944-945, F08).

**Hod, hadar, silence (958-991).** A king's essential majesty is not taken from the people (960-961, F05). Praise belongs to the spreading. Of the Essence: > ולך דומי' תהלה בבחי' ביטול בתכלית שאין בזה חילוקי מדרי' כלל (line 965)

At inner Keter even negative knowledge does not apply (968). The Torah is "black fire on white fire". The white is the simple Essence (972). A mitzvah is fulfilled only in deed, since the will has no reason (980). From the will's side no mitzvah is light or grave (981, F10).

## Shavuos (992-1073): the dwelling, the two "nothings", and "Kel Olam"

**Why the infinite light is revealed (1003-1010).** The range's F07 is stated in the words of the Midrash: > אמנם הכוונה בגילוי אור זה הוא שיהי' גילוי אוא"ס בעולמו' ויהי' בהם בחי' ביטול שעי"ז יושלם הכוונה דנתאווה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 1003)

The inner light is revealed from the worlds' side, to make them exist. The infinite light is revealed from the Essence's side: > והיינו שגם העולמות יהיו כמו העצמות ובחי' העדר המציאו' (line 1005)

There are two "nothings". The nothing of the true "something" is truly nothing (1007). The nothing of the created "something" is a borrowed name. Actual creation comes only from "a radiance of a radiance" (1008).

**Sinai (1014-1017).** By the order of worlds, Atzilut does not shine below. At Sinai the decree was annulled: > ובמ"ת נתחדש זאת שיהי' גילוי אצי' בבי"ע (line 1014)

**Kel Shaddai and Kel Olam (1018-1052).** The Rashab leans on the Tanya, Part II chs. 3-4 (lines 1018-1019, 1043, 1104). Creatures are "only light and radiance spreading" (1018). Gevurah makes them seem things in themselves (1019): > וכאלו גוף הנברא הוא דבר בפ"ע, ואף שבאמת אינו דבר בפ"ע אלא כמו התפשטו' האור מהשמש (line 1019)

The contraction is "the ultimate kindness" (1021, F03). The Patriarchs' physical lives "were Godliness itself" (1026). Then Avraham's name for God: > שאין העולם דבר בפ"ע כ"א הוא אלקות (line 1043)

> וענין ההתהוות הוא שמהות הנבראים ממש נתהוו מאלקו', והיינו מההארה שמאתו ית' שההארה גופא נעשה מהות נברא (line 1045)

This is put against the error of "His glory is above the heavens", which imagines cause and effect (1043-1045). Hair grows from the mind's life and seems separate. So do creatures (1046). Sinai is the exodus from the "Egypt", the limits, within holiness: > דכל ענין התו' הוא להיות יצי"מ והיינו היציאה מהמיצרים וגבולים דקדושה (line 1051)

Torah study now "plants" the new heavens (1071-1072).

## Behaalotcha, Korach, Chukat (1074-1167): grasping the essence, nature as God drowned, and the world as "nothing"

**Grasping the essence (1076-1100).** The lower worlds know Godliness's existence. Atzilut and Gan Eden grasp its essence (1076). Torah is "the parable of the Ancient One". A parable clothes the very essence of what it stands for (1079, F10). Great scholars hold what is above comprehension "not only by faith but truly in their mind" (1085). A foretaste now: > והיינו שכל הדברים הגשמי' הן כמו משל אל הנמשל להכח האלקי, והמשל הוא הנמשל ממש (line 1094)

This is the completion that the future will bring from today's refining of the body (1095-1096, F11).

**Beriah and the Divine nothing (1106-1110).** Beriah is only "possibility of existence", as close to the Divine nothing as a man is to himself. Then: > ובאמת א"א לחלק כלל בין האין האלקי להיש, שהרי בלתי בחי' האין א"א כלל לבחי' היש להיות בבחי' מציאו' כלל, וא"כ כל ענין מציאו' היש הוא האין המקיים אותו (line 1109)

**Lack, nature, Korach (1120-1133).** Worlds come from a lack of light (1120). "You are He, Havayah, alone" is before the tzimtzum. "All know the Essence, and no one grasps it" (1121). The animal soul grows strong when there is no service (1123). Nature (teva) means that Godliness drowned (nitba) in it: > וז"ע טבע שהוא מל' טבעו בים סוף דהיינו שהאלקו' נטבע בזה (פארטרונקען) ואינו נראה ונגלה כלל (line 1127)

A miracle (nes) raises it up. Korach wanted the division itself, and so the opposite of Avraham's "Kel Olam": > וקרח עשה קרחה מריטת השערות שהוא רצה בעצם המציאו' והישות של העולם (line 1132)

**Speaker and red heifer (1136-1167).** The mineral, plant, animal and speaker levels of intellect are mostly structural. Their point: "the main thing of the speaker is comprehension of Godliness" (1152), comprehension of "the Divine nothing that makes the something" (1153). In service, the speaker is cleaving in "utter oneness" (1155). Shlomo's soul was of Atzilut (1161). He could not grasp the red heifer, the very essence of wisdom. The range ends inside that answer (1166-1167).

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **Both knowledges are true, and the lower is "only a likeness".** Shaar HaYichud gives the higher unity and then the lower unity. Here the Rashab says outright that from Ein Sof's side "both are true", and that Kel "bears both knowledges" (794). Later in the range he says the lower unity "is not the truth, since in truth there is no existence at all… only a likeness" (944). Both statements are kept. The Rashab reconciles them himself only in part: the lower unity is still called "Our likeness" because it too is bitul (944).
- **Hiding is the mechanism of revelation, not only its cost.** The Tanya's F03 is that concealment serves revelation. Here concealment *is* how light becomes light at all: "the Essence is not light" (719), and vessels make it shine by hiding it (720). Contraction is "the ultimate kindness" (1021).
- **Why Atzilut exists: so that He be known.** The Tanya does not say this in the range it shares with the SPINE. Here it is a thesis. Creation alone would give only "general knowledge that there is a God who creates" (737-739).
- **Knowing above knowing.** On the SPINE's F12 dispute, the Rashab in this range stands between the positions. Comprehension itself compels the sense of what cannot be grasped (781). Negative knowledge yields real recognition (785). Great scholars hold such matters "not only by faith" (1085). At inner Keter even negation fails (968).
- **The world is Godliness, not an effect of God.** Part II of the Tanya (chs. 3-4) is the source, and the Rashab cites it. He adds the growing-power and hair analogies and the argument against cause and effect (1043-1046). He adds "one cannot distinguish at all between the Divine nothing and the something" (1109).
- **The dwelling as bitul.** The infinite light is revealed so that the worlds "be like the Essence, without existence" (1005), and this completes "the desire for a dwelling below" (1003). This matches the SPINE's F07e (the Rashab: the purpose of the "something" is that it become batel).
- **Torah and mitzvot are two unions.** Torah is the union of lights, "entirely one"; mitzvot are the union of vessels, "limbs" (941-942). Only the physical deed fulfils a mitzvah (980).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 17 lines are *none*: the maamar headings (602, 635, 669, 700, 735, 767, 805, 838, 882, 915, 948, 992, 1028, 1053, 1074, 1101, 1135). - 87 line entries carry no face. Most are the questions that open each maamar, links in the soul-analogies, or summaries. - Long psychological passages are structural, and the unity tie there is at most one step away. These are the teaching analogies (647, 652-653, 752-756), the mind-and-emotion chains (1055-1070), and the four levels of intellect (1138-1152). The text uses them to build the sefirot. It does not say how each line bears on the unity. - The *reading* rows (12) are places where the face is ours: 653, 658, 885 and 1102 (F09); 810, 811, 903, 1057 and 1151 (F06); 1034 (F01, the mazal over each blade of grass, used only as an analogy); 1160 (F07); 705 (F08). - The kabbalistic mappings (Akudim, Adam Kadmon, Yisrael Saba and Tevunah, the 620 pillars, the partitions) are marked structural unless the line itself names the unity.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Tanya 38 against the Rashab on mineral, plant, animal and speaker.** The Rashab cites Tanya 38 (deed, speech, hidden love, revealed love). He then says that mapping concerns keeping the mitzvot. For "the essence of service" he places the speaker higher, in cleaving with the essence of the mind (1155). Two mappings stand.
- **Tamim and tzaddik.** Here the *tamim* (sovev) is ranked above the *tzaddik* (memale) (626). In the Tanya "tzaddik" names the highest human rank. The terms are used differently, and the ranking is kept as the Rashab gives it.
- **Where creation comes from.** In this range, actual creation is "only from a radiance of a radiance, a separated radiance", from Malchus (1008), and "from revealed light no existence can come" (1120). The inner light's root, though, is still in the Essence (1004). This matches the Maharash side of the SPINE's F05 tension (F05j). It does not state the formula of the Iggeres HaKodesh 20 and the Rebbe, that only the Essence can create a "something" (F05a, F05b). The range does cite the Iggeres HaKodesh 20, for light cleaving to its source being "the root of creation" (1008). Both formulations remain.
- **"Sovev" is relative.** The Tanya (ch. 48) defines sovev as His knowledge that surrounds the world. Here Malchus of Atzilut itself is called "sovev" relative to the lower worlds (872), and Z'A is "only surrounding" them (1112). The same word names different levels. Rows follow the Rashab's usage.
