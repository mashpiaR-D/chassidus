## The shape of the range

The range is one long commentary on the Shema, read word by word, from "Shema Yisrael" to "va'ed". It has four parts.

- **Sections 45-49 (266-281): the first verse and Baruch Shem.** The souls' self-sacrifice in "Shema Yisrael" raises the fallen sparks up to the Essence. From there a new light comes down into Chochmah and Binah ("Havayah Elokeinu"). "Havayah echad" then unites the divided attributes, and Baruch Shem is the lower unity in time. F05, F02 and F09 lead here.
- **Sections 49-53 (278-296): the two paragraphs.** The first paragraph ("with all your might", 42 words) is *running*, the soul leaving its vessel. The second (Vehayah, 72 words) is *returning*, light settling in vessels through the mitzvot. The return is the purpose. Rain, grain, wine and oil are read as the drawing down of Torah. F07, F08, F10 and F03 lead.
- **Sections 54-60 (297-321): wine and oil, secrets, and the two "mazalot".** Oil (Chochmah) is "separate from everything and drawn into everything". There is a secret that will never be revealed. The two mazalot (Notzer and Nakeh), and the early and late rains, are the opposites 'something into nothing' and 'nothing into something'. F09, F12, F05 and F03 lead.
- **Sections 61-69 (322-351): the letters of "echad" and "va'ed".** The aleph is the intermediary between the Essence and Chochmah. The chet is Chochmah received. The large dalet is the power of speech. All three are the higher unity, "He and His life are one". F05, F09 and F01 lead.

The range differs from the Tanya range (lines 561-840) mainly in weight. F05 (the Essence) leads by far, and F09 (union of opposites) is second, where in the Tanya F12 and F10 lead. F01 (continuous creation) and F04 (filling and surrounding) are the rarest faces here. The creation of the world is not this range's subject. Its subject is how the unity is *made* in prayer: by the soul below, through the letters of the Shema, up to the Essence and back down.

## Sections 45-46 (266-268): contraction from greatness, ascent from below

The range opens by saying that any emanation at all out of the Essence is a leap across a gap, "like from nothing to something", not a move from hidden to revealed:

The power to contract comes from the Essence's own greatness. "Where you find His greatness, there you find His humility":

> ויש בכח גדולתו העצמו' כח הגבול לצמצם א"ע (line 266)

The first contraction came from His own goodness. After that it depends on the deeds of the lower ones (line 266.3), and "the hidden pleasure" at the start was that the arousal from above should come through arousal from below (line 266.4). The six words of the Shema are then named as the higher unity, made by the souls:

> דשית תיבין דפסוק זה הוא בבחי' יחו"ע דע"ס דאצי' במאצילן (line 267)

Line 268 adds that even the union of Chochmah and Binah is not self-sufficient. It needs the Essence, as a man and wife need the Shechinah between them:

> דכל יחוד פנימי משפיע ומקבל תלוי בעצמות אא"ס (line 268)

Even "the lowest soul in Israel" can say "Elokeinu" (line 268.2, F06).

## Sections 46-47 (269-274): only the soul in a body; the end in the beginning

A soul without a body does not make this ascent. Only a soul still in a body does, refining the animal soul and the pleasures of this world:

> אין זה העלאת מ"ן רק דוקא כשהנשמה בעודנה בגוף (line 269)

The refining means rising "from separation to unity" (lines 269.1, 270). The divine power goes down into the husks to do it. That descent is a hiding, as in exile, and it is there for the sake of the refining (F03, F11):

> שהוא בחי' הסתר אלקות שמסתתר בע' שרים דנוגה לבררם (line 269)

What is refined rises higher than what stayed above, "the stone the builders rejected" (line 269.3). The soul itself "needs no repair" and came down only to refine (line 270.1), as in Tanya ch. 37. Line 271 names the purpose and cites the dwelling below:

> וכמאמר נתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 271)

Line 273 then sets a limit on this from the Essence's side. Before the breaking and after the repair, all is equal before Him:

> הכל בהשואה א' לפני עצמותו ממש (line 273)

> שכל השנוי הזה בין העלם וגלוי לא שייך רק לגבי המקבלים למטה ולא לגבי עצמותו (line 273)

Even the pleasure in the refinements is "as nothing" beside the Essence's own simple pleasure:

> הכל הוא כלא חשיב לגבי תענוג הפשוט שבעצמותו ממש (line 273)

That limit is kept as a disagreement below. "Yisrael" is the intermediary because the 600,000 souls are bound to the 600,000 letters of the Torah (line 273.4, F06 + F10).

## Section 48 (275-277): 'echad' and 'yachid'; opposites joined by their source

This is the range's clearest statement of the SPINE's precision, 'yachid, not echad'. "Echad" joins divided things. Of what has no second one says "yachid":

> לא יפול לשון אחדות אלא לשון יחיד (line 275)

"Havayah echad" is the ten divided attributes, opposites like kindness and severity, made one before the source that holds them:

> וה"ז כהתכללות ב' הפכי' לגבי העליון הכוללם יחד (line 275)

The parable is Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel, one forbidding and one permitting, who are one before their teacher. When the simple light shines on the attributes, "they are nullified of their existence" and joined (line 275.3, F09 + F08). Baruch Shem is the lower unity, and its mark is time. The higher unity is above time (line 277):

> אבל ביח"ע הוא למעלה מן הזמן (line 277)

In the future, time itself will be eternal, "because what is above time will shine in time" (line 277.1).

## Sections 49-51 (278-293): running for the sake of returning

"With all your might" is self-sacrifice "in the physicality of the body" (line 278). It means leaving the limiting vessel (line 279), and that is the acceptance of the yoke of heaven:

> וזהו הנק' עול מ"ש להיות בטול הרצון מכל וכל במס"נ ממש (line 280)

> והשוב הוא העיקר וכל ההעלא' דרצוא לא הי' רק בשביל הירידה דשוב (line 283)

> שישכון וידור בכלים מכלים שונים (line 283)

The vessel is not to hide as a separate thing, but to be a 'something' nullified (line 283.3). The soul's longing to be absorbed into the Essence is "the reverse of the main will" of the whole chain (line 283.4). The more one runs, the more one can draw down: "each depends on the other in proportion" (line 284).

Then comes F03 as kindness, built on two parables: a bridge built of many small chambers, and rain falling in drops. Undivided, the light "would not be received at all" (line 286). The division that looks like judgment is the heart of kindness:

> ואמנם אינו בחי' דין וצמצום אמיתי אדרבה זהו עיקר החסד (line 287)

The same kindness, with no likeness between its forms (light, water, whiteness, sweetness, the kind man), has "surely one source for them all" (line 288, F04 + F01). Line 290 makes bitul the condition of Torah. Ein Sof clothes itself only in Chochmah, which is bitul, and the one who has it speaks "only as a vessel for God's word". Line 291 extends Moshe's "I will give" to every person who has first said "with all your might". Line 292 defines the mitzvah:

> והוא הנק' עול מצות שהמצוה היא המשכת גלוי רצונו למטה להתלבש בבחי' כלי דוקא (line 292)

## Sections 53-54 (294-306): Torah as food, and the secret that stays sealed

The soul does not know its Father in heaven until it tastes the Talmud:

> וכך בנשמה האלקית אין בה בחי' הדעת להכיר לאביו שבשמים עד שיטעום דגן שהוא התלמוד דוקא (line 294)

The text raises the objection itself (Chanoch knew God before Sinai). It answers that the patriarchs knew only El Shaddai, not the essential Name. "Torah is rooted in the essence of the mind" (line 295.2), so through it Israel knows God as a son knows a father.

Then wine and oil. Oil is Chochmah, and it holds two opposites:

> וזהו שהשמן נבדל מהכל ונמשך בכל לפי שהמשכתו בהעלם (line 306)

"Separate from everything and drawn into everything, because its drawing is hidden." This is very close to the SPINE's own thesis sentence ("separate from every subject and so present in every subject"). Chochmah reaches "even the inanimate" (line 305). Holiness comes through anointing oil because it is drawn "from the Essence itself" (line 306.1).

There is a limit to knowing. Some secrets never come out:

> אבל רזא דרזין דאוריית' גם לע"ל לא יתגלו (line 301)

Of the Essence one cannot even speak of a secret being revealed, since He does not change:

> כי לגבי בחי' העצמות לא יתכן לשון גלוי סוד או סוד דסוד (line 302)

And one must not seek it out, even for oneself:

> שזהו עיקר כבודו להיות בהעל' דווקא (line 302)

## Sections 54-60 (307-321): the mitzvah lamp; two rains and two 'nothings'

Torah's light comes out of hiding only through the mitzvah:

> שע"י נר מצוה דוקא בא האור דתורה מהעלם לגלוי (line 308)

That light is like a shining face, "the revelation of the very essence" (line 308.1). The early rain rots the seed: "the nullification of something into nothing" (line 311). The late rain fills the grain. They are true opposites (line 312), and yet:

> והכל א' ממש גם לאחר התחלקותן משום דכלולים בשרשן (line 319)

Line 313 separates two kinds of 'nothing'. One is the essential nothing of the Keter. The other is the nothing that makes things nothing, and that is bitul, "as one who sees the king" (line 313.1). "You made them all with wisdom": wisdom is the power that makes all things nullified (line 313.2). At Sinai "they saw what is heard": Chochmah and Binah each did the other's opposite work (line 318). Torah came out of cloud and darkness because its root is hidden in the Essence (line 320). The pattern is stated in so many words:

## Sections 61-62 (322-331): feeding the beast first; humility where the greatness is

Sparks rise from the husks "of themselves", as converts do (line 322.1). When a person eats, the food rises "to the One God" through his self-offering (line 322.3). In exile Godliness is so hidden that no light is seen, yet people return by themselves "because the Shechinah is with them" (line 322.4). Pesukei deZimra feed the animal soul first, "I was a beast with You" (line 324), and only then does the soul itself speak in the Shema (line 323.2).

The aleph is the intermediary between the Essence and Chochmah (lines 325-326). Within it, pele (the hidden wonder) and aleph (teaching) are opposites, and yet one depends on the other (line 326.1). The upper point is "the contraction of the Essence of Ein Sof" (line 326.2). Line 329 gives the plain reason for humility above:

> דלפ"ע בחי' האין האמיתי שבעצמו' אא"ס כך הוא בחי' ענוה שלו במה שמצמצם א"ע (line 329)

Humility is "the sign of the utmost bitul" (line 328), and wisdom is not found in the arrogant.

## Sections 64-69 (332-351): the letters of 'echad'; speech and the higher unity

The union of Chochmah and Binah in Atzilut is constant, and the reason is F01's own reason:

> שההמשכה מחכמה דאצי' לבינה שהוא מאין ליש ממש זה צריך להיות בתמידות בלי הפסק כלל (line 337)

The main point of teaching is in the receiver (line 339.1). The main mitzvah of Torah study is in speech (line 340). The "mouth" is the power to refine the Tree of Knowledge (line 341.1). The one who gives that mouth is:

> אלא אנכי שהוא אור א"ס שבעצמות הכתר הנק' אנכי (line 341)

Each letter-combination makes a particular creature, so no two are alike (line 343). "In each hour there is a new combination of the Name Havayah" (line 344.2). Division and inclusion produce each other:

> ונמצא ההתכללו' עושה ריבוי ההתחלקו' ואינו דבר והיפוכו (line 344)

In Chochmah, "they are all united there as truly one" (line 347). The Besht's verse is read at the level of the power of speech, where "there are no changes at all":

> והיינו מ"ש לעולם דברך נצב בשמים פי' דברך בחי' עצם כח הדבור העליון (line 349)

The whole word "echad" is the higher unity, because Ein Sof rests only in bitul:

> שידוע שאין אא"ס שורה רק בבחי' הבטול דמ"ה ואין דחכמ' ולא בבחי' יש כלל (line 350)

> הכל בבחי' יח"ע דאיהו וחיוהי חד (line 350)

The analogy from the soul closes the range. Wisdom, feeling and speech "count as nothing before the essence" of the soul (line 351).

## What this range adds to the Tanya (or says differently)

- **The unity is made in the Shema, by souls in bodies.** The Tanya reads Shema as the higher unity and bitul. Here it is a causal chain. The souls' ascent in "Shema Yisrael", which only a soul in a body can make (lines 269, 278), reaches the Essence. From there a new light comes into Chochmah and Binah, and without it "nothing new would be drawn" (line 274).
- **'Yachid', not 'echad', stated outright** (line 275), and then worked out letter by letter in "echad" and "va'ed" (lines 325-351).
- **How opposites are joined.** Not by merging, but before their common source (Shammai and Hillel before their teacher, line 275). Each does the other's opposite work (lines 317-318). Division and inclusion produce each other (lines 343-344).
- **Tzimtzum as kindness, argued with parables.** The bridge chambers, the raindrops and the teacher all make one point: gevurah within chesed "is the essence of kindness" (line 287). Greatness and humility are proportionate (lines 266, 329).
- **One sentence close to the SPINE's own thesis:** oil "is separate from everything and drawn into everything, because its drawing is hidden" (line 306).
- **Two 'nothings'** (line 313): the essential nothing, and the nothing that makes things nothing, which is bitul. That gives F08 a clear place in F05.
- **Talmud as the condition of knowing God as Father** (lines 294-295), and the mitzvah as the lamp without which Torah's light stays hidden (line 308).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **Two lines are *none*.** Line 293 is a note on which reading applies to the fifty gates. Line 321 is the closing half-line of section 57.
- **Much of the range is kabbalistic mapping with no face.** This covers which of light and vessel belongs to which paragraph (lines 281, 291.2), the shape of the aleph (line 335), the three kinds of kindness (lines 332.1, 333) and the two meanings of "Abba founded" (line 349.2). These are marked *structural*.
- **Long parables and psychology are *structural*.** The bridge and the spout (lines 284-285), the halachic dispute over oil floating on wine (lines 297, 304), wine and secrets (line 298), the mind's two powers of insight and grasp (lines 315-317), the stages of growth from two to seventy (line 351.1) and the soul's three garments (line 347.2). The text uses each of these to teach the unity above, and they are tagged by what they illustrate.
- **Six ties are *reading*, ours rather than the text's.** They are 276.2 (the student's bitul as higher than rule), 299 (wine hidden for the sake of revelation, as F03), 305.1 (oil's two opposites as sovev and memale), 339.1 and 340.2 (completeness in the receiver, and in speech, as the pattern of the dwelling below) and 351.2 (the highest comes last, as F07).
- **F01 and F04 are thin.** Creation is not this range's subject. F01 is stated mainly at lines 337, 343-344 and 349, and F04 at lines 288, 297, 306 and 347.

## Disagreements kept

- **How much does the dwelling below weigh?** The range says two things. Lines 271, 283 and 320.3 make the dwelling the "main will" of the whole chain. They say that "the end deed rose first in thought", and line 320.3 puts it "in the simple pleasure in His very Essence". Line 273 says the pleasure in the refinements concerns "something outside the Essence", "as nothing" beside the Essence's simple pleasure, with "no change before the refinement and after". The Tanya (ch. 36, 49) states the dwelling as the purpose without that limit. The two statements in this range may be the same purpose seen from below and from the Essence, but the text does not reconcile them here. They are kept apart.
- **"Your word stands in the heavens."** The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud 1, following the Besht) reads it as the letters of the Word standing in each created thing. Line 349 reads "Your word" as "the essential power of supernal speech", above the combinations, where there is no change. The two readings are not in direct conflict, but they point to different levels: the Tanya to the letters in each thing, this range to their unchanging source.
- **Knowing and its limit.** Line 294 makes the divine knowing depend on tasting the Talmud, and the text itself notes the tension with "know today" (Chanoch knew God without it). Line 302 then forbids seeking the secret of secrets "even for oneself", because "His glory is to be hidden". Here the Mitteler Rebbe stands nearer the Tanya's "no business with hidden things" than his own Gate of Unity does (which asks for contemplation of the Essence before the tzimtzum "in detail"). This is kept as a tension inside the Mitteler Rebbe's own work.
- **"The advantage of light out of darkness."** Line 320.2 uses it in praise: Torah's light breaks out of the dark cloud, and that is its greater praise. The Gate of Unity (line 2414 there) says the contrast only looks brighter "to the eye". Both texts are the Mitteler Rebbe's. Neither retracts the other.
- **Do the letters dissolve?** Line 350.1 rejects the text's own earlier reading, that on Shabbat the combinations rise into the power of speech. If they did, there would be "a complete nullification of something into nothing", and "the word of our God stands forever". Created letters must remain. This sits beside, not against, the Tanya's "nullified like sunlight in the sun" (Shaar HaYichud 3), which is said from His side.
