Chayei Sara is the Zohar's book of the soul and the body, of death and burial and the resurrection. Its Midrash HaNe'elam sections read Abraham as the soul, Sarah and Rebecca as the body, Eliezer as the angel who restores the body, and the buying of the cave as the soul's care for its body. Around them: the days of a life that "stand" above, the one who makes himself small, the masters of return who come near in one moment, the soul's night, Abraham's prayer at the cave, tefillin as the human chariot, the three kinds of prayer (silent, shouted, wept), and the Shechinah going into exile. ## 2. The unity of God

**He is all, and there is none beside Him.** On "the God of heaven and the God of earth": «שהוא אדון על הכל, בבת אחת וברגע אחד הוא מניע לכל וכלם כאין נגדו. רבי יצחק אומר על שתים אותיות משמו להורות שהוא הכל ואין אחר בלתו» (Chayei Sara 19:13; line 2728): "for He is Lord over all; at once and in one instant He moves everything, and all are as nothing before Him. Rabbi Yitzchak said: on two letters of His name, to teach that He is all and there is none other beside Him." Ours: in plain Hebrew inside the Midrash HaNe'elam stands the very phrase Chassidus lives by ("ein od milvado"), joined to "all are as nothing before Him" (kulei kamei kelo chashivin).

**He and His name are one.** «מאן דידע שמיה על בורייה, ידע דהוא ושמיה חד הוא» (Chayei Sara 19:23; line 2738): "whoever knows His name truly knows that He and His name are one." Ties: F02 in SPINE.md (the higher unity), the Tanya's teaching that the Name and its bearer are not two.

**Unity above, separation below, one all the same.** Sarah's years: "a hundred year" and "twenty year" in the singular are «רזא דיחודא דלא אתפרש … לעלמין» (Chayei Sara 5:2; line 2591): "the secret of unity that never separates"; "seven years" in the plural go out into judgement and mercy by many ways, «ואף על גב דכלא יחודא חדא» (Chayei Sara 5:3; line 2592): "even though all is one unity."

**All creatures sing.** «דכל מה דברא קודשא בריך הוא בעלמא כלהו אמרי תושבחן ושירתא קמיה בין לעילא בין לתתא» (Chayei Sara 6:2; line 2595): "for all that the Holy One created in the world, all of them say praise and song before Him, above and below." The Tanya's and Perek Shirah's world of song.

**The world as one body.** As a person is many limbs that together are one body, so all creatures "stand one on another, and when they are set in order, it is really a body", and so is the Torah (line 2840, the opening of Toldot).

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **The one who makes himself small.** «זכאה איהו מאן דאזעיר גרמיה בהאי עלמא. כמה איהו רב ועלאה בההוא עלמא» (Chayei Sara 4:1; line 2588): "happy is he who makes himself small in this world; how great and high he is in that world." And the rule: «דלא רבי קודשא בריך הוא אלא לדאזעיר, ולא אזעיר אלא לדרבי» (Chayei Sara 4:1; line 2588): "the Holy One makes great only the one who makes himself small, and makes small only the one who makes himself great." Ties: S59 True lowliness, S56 The self quieted.
2. **Near in one moment.** Abraham was drawn near step by step all his life (line 2686). But «זכאין אינון מאריהון דתשובה דהא בשעתא חדא ביומא חדא ברגעא חדא קריבין לגבי קודשא בריך הוא» (Chayei Sara 16:4; line 2688): "happy are the masters of return, for in one hour, one day, one instant they come near to the Holy One," which even the perfectly righteous reach only over years; and they draw «ברעותא דלבא יתיר ובחילא סגיא» (Chayei Sara 16:5; line 2689): "with more will of the heart and great force." Ties: S78 The higher return, S51 The sudden cry.
3. **Sick with love.** The soul says to the other souls: tell Him «שחולת אהבה אני», and Rav Huna: «אותה התשוקה והכסוף שכספתי בעולם על הכל לפיכך אני חולה» (Chayei Sara 8:9; line 2612): "that longing and yearning I yearned in the world above everything, that is why I am sick." Ties: S13, S43 Love like fire.
4. **The "golem".** «אין אדם עושה עבירות אלא מי שהוא גולם ולא אדם, והיינו ההוא דלא מסתכל בנשמתא קדישא» (Chayei Sara 8:3; line 2606): "a person sins only when he is a golem and not a man: the one who does not look at his holy soul," acting like a beast that does not notice. Ties: S12 The flat world, S08 Run by the natural soul. Ours: the way out is to look at the soul, not to fight the body.
5. **Life without sadness.** Torah is «חיין דחידו, חיי בלא עציבו, חיין דאינון חיין» (Chayei Sara 21:1; line 2786): "a life of joy, life without sadness, life that is life", and freedom in this world and the next (line 2789). Ties: S53 Glad, S79.
6. **Comforted by what remains.** When Rebecca entered the tent, Sarah's image appeared there, seen only by Isaac, «ועל דא וינחם יצחק אחרי אמו» (Chayei Sara 24:3; line 2817): "and so Isaac was comforted after his mother." Ties: S14 Loss and grief. Ours: a gentle image of a presence that stays in the home.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **The Shema as a daily giving of oneself.** «כל המכוין לבו בהאי פסוקא כדי למסור נפשו על קדושת שמו מעלה עליו הכתוב כאלו נהרג בכל יום עליו» (Chayei Sara 10:7; line 2628): "whoever directs his heart in this verse ('and you shall love') to give over his soul for the sanctification of His name, Scripture counts it as if he were killed for Him every day." This is the source of the Chabad practice of mesirat nefesh in "Echad" and "ve'ahavta". Ties: S60 The essential bond, S83; technique 16.
- **Say "Echad" rightly.** «מאן דאמר אחד אצטריך לחטפא אל"ף ולקצר קרנא דיליה ולא יעכב בהאי אות כלל, ומאן דיעביד דא יתארכון חייו» (Chayei Sara 4:2; line 2589): "whoever says 'Echad' must say the Alef quickly and shorten it, not lingering on that letter at all; whoever does this, his life is lengthened." (And whoever cuts short the "Amen" is cut short.) Ties: technique 18 (saying it with the lips).
- **Three prayers: silent, shouted, wept.** «שמעה תפלתי יי דא צלותא די בלחש. ושועתי האזינה דא צלותא דארים בר נש קליה בעקתיה» (Chayei Sara 23:6; line 2799): "'hear my prayer' is the silent prayer; 'give ear to my cry' is the prayer where a person raises his voice in distress", and it breaks through the gates; and tears: «ולעולם לא אהדרו דמעין בריקניא» (Chayei Sara 23:6; line 2799): "and tears never return empty." Ties: technique 20 (own words), S51 The sudden cry, S49.
- **In prayer, fear only Him.** Do not pray behind your teacher, «אלא מורא דקודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי ולא מורא אחרא» (Chayei Sara 23:11; line 2804): "only the fear of the Holy One alone, and no other fear." Ties: technique 2.
- **Be careful at the afternoon prayer.** As the sun turns west, judgement wakes; «דיהא בר נש זהיר בצלותא דמנחה» (Chayei Sara 23:14; line 2807): "a person should be careful with the afternoon prayer." David spoke (asichah) in the morning and moaned (ehemeh) in the afternoon (line 2810).
- **Pray in the field.** Abraham went out every day to a field where higher scents rose, saw light from the cave and prayed there, and there God spoke with him (line 2668). Ties: technique 20 (being alone with Him).
- **Tefillin as the chariot.** «זכאה הוא בר נש דמנח תפילין וידע טעמא דידהו» (Chayei Sara 19:41; line 2756): "happy is the person who lays tefillin and knows their reason": the person becomes a chariot; the heart rides below like the lower chariot, the brain above, and the King rests on them both (lines 2758-2762). Ties: S81 The deed in the hand, S82.
- **The traveler's three prayers.** The day's prayer, the prayer for the road, and the prayer to return in peace; all may be included in "who hears prayer" (line 2604).
- **Whoever comes to be purified is helped** (line 2639), and whoever wakes toward impurity draws it (line 2642): «כגוונא דאתער בר נש הכי נמי אמשיך עליה מלעילא» (Chayei Sara 12:5; line 2642): "as a person wakes, so he draws upon himself from above."
- **The first question in the grave.** «כשנכנס אדם לקבר מה דשאלו ליה תחילה אם קבע עתים לתורה» (Chayei Sara 19:24; line 2739): "when a person enters the grave, the first thing asked is whether he set fixed times for Torah." A daily practice given its weight.

## 5. The big human questions

- **What wealth and children are for.** «יהיב עותרא לבר איניש, למה, למיזן עניין ולמעבד פקודוי» (Chayei Sara 8:5; line 2608): "He gives a person wealth: why? to feed the poor and do His commandments"; children, to teach them His ways; if one grows proud in them, he is struck through them.
- **Whose days stand.** A person's days are made above; when they "stand" there, he stands in the world; the days of the righteous are "lives" (lines 2602-2603, 2637). Ties: S65 Given right now.
- **Death and the body.** The soul mourns its body seven days (line 2615); the righteous soul first protects its body, then rises (line 2619); the same body and the same soul will return, "complete in knowledge, to grasp what they did not grasp in this world" (line 2720). Ours: the Zohar honors the body; the guide should too.
- **The Shechinah in exile.** When the Temple burned, the Shechinah went first to see her house, then went with the people; she asks "who gave Jacob to plunder?" (lines 2834-2836). In the end God builds Jerusalem first and then raises her from the dust (line 2837). Ties: S73.
- **Change of name atones.** Hagar returned to good deeds and her name became Keturah: «מכאן דשנוי שמא מכפר חובין» (Chayei Sara 25:1; line 2824): "from here: a change of name atones for sins." Ties: S77.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **"He is all and there is none beside Him" (line 2728)**, in the Zohar's own words.
2. **The Shema as daily self-giving (line 2628).** The Chabad practice in its source.
3. **Near in one moment (lines 2688-2689).** For the person who feels late.
4. **Three kinds of prayer, and tears never return empty (line 2799).**
5. **The one who makes himself small is made great (line 2588).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "Giving the soul in the Shema."** At "you shall love Havayah your God with all your soul", intend to give over your soul for His name, as if today; then live the day as one who has given it (line 2628). Guard: this is an inner offering of will, never a wish for death.
- **State, new: "Near in one moment."** Inside: after long distance, one turn of the heart and you are closer than those who walked for years (lines 2688-2689). Enter: return with the whole force of the heart. Near S78 and S51.
- **Technique, new: "Silent, shouted, wept."** Let a prayer move through three: whispered, then with raised voice in your distress, then with tears if they come (line 2799). Guard: never force tears.
- **State, new: "Sick with love."** Inside: a longing for Him above everything you wanted in the world, so strong it hurts (line 2612). Near S43, S13.
