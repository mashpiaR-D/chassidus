The Zohar on Noah reads the righteous man and the ark as the foundation (Yesod) and the Shechinah; the flood as the lower world falling back into "water in water"; Noah's hiding from the destroyer; Moses who gave his life for his people against Noah who did not pray for his generation; the rainbow; Noah's vineyard; Nimrod; and the tower of Babel as a craft of wisdom turned to "another authority". It includes several Tosefta and Sitrei Torah pieces. All 388 lines were read in full. Every quote below printed "FOUND near line N" from `find_he.py verify` on the export. ## 2. The unity of God

**Thought, voice, speech: one.** The fullest statement in the range of how the hidden becomes revealed without a second: God's will rises from "a hidden thought that is not known", spreads to the place where breath flows always and is called "living God", goes out as one voice (Jacob), and strikes the lips to become speech. «אשתמע דכלא איהו ההיא מחשבה סתימא דהות לגו וכלא חד» (Noach 41:9; line 1521): "and it is understood that all of it is that hidden thought that was inside, and all is one." And when the lower one draws from above, «כדין קיימין עלמין כלהו ברזא חדא ביחודא חד, ולא הוי בהו בכלהו עלמין פירודא» (Noach 41:12; line 1524): "then all the worlds stand in one secret, in one unity, and there is no separation in any of the worlds." Ours: this is the frame the Tanya builds on when it calls creation "the word of God" and speaks of thought and speech in God (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 11-12, from memory).

**You cannot hide from the One who made the hiding places.** «הלא את השמים ואת הארץ אני מלא» (Jeremiah 23:24, cited at line 1384): God tells the rebels who hide in tunnels under His palace: «אנא הוא דעבדית נוקבי פסירין ועבדית חשוכא ונהורא ואתון איך יכלין לאתטמרא קמאי» (Noach 26:2; line 1385): "I am the One who made the tunnels, and made darkness and light; how can you hide from Me?" His filling of all things is the ground of the guide's S85 and of the Tanya's "He fills all worlds".

**He is one above and below.** The three colors that come from the river into the garden show «דיקרא דקודשא בריך הוא אתפשט לעילא ותתא, ואיהו חד בעלאי ותתאי» (Noach 37:1; line 1492): "that the glory of the Holy One spreads above and below, and He is one in the higher ones and the lower ones."

**Only He, even in judgement.** In the land of Israel no appointed power rules, "only the Holy One alone"; even the destroying angel over Jerusalem was He: «הן לטב והן לביש קודשא בריך הוא שליט עלה» (Noach 7:4; line 1232): "whether for good or for bad, the Holy One rules over it."

**What is above hangs on what is below.** «דעלמא עלאה תליא בתתאה ותתאה בעלאה» (Noach 31:9; line 1431): "the upper world hangs on the lower and the lower on the upper."

**Rabbi Shimon's hands raised to the will beyond knowing.** «ארימת ידאי בצלותין לעילא» (Noach 16:1; line 1312): "I have raised my hands in prayer on high": the will above stands upon a will that is not known and never grasped; the thought pursues it and «מטי ולא מטי» (Noach 16:2; line 1313): "reaches and does not reach"; «וההוא מחשבה לא ידע» (Noach 16:2; line 1313): "and that thought does not know." The nine palaces there are "not lights, not spirits, not souls", and all of it is called Ein Sof (line 1315). This matches the top of the palaces of prayer in Bereshit II (lines 885-887).

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **The sea that cannot rest.** The wicked are "like the driven sea": it leaves its bounds like a drunk who cannot sit still, goes up and down, «כי השקט לא יוכל» (Isaiah 57:20), and throws up mud. «בגין כי השקט לא יוכל. דהא עקימו דארחייהו גרים לון למהך בלא תקונא ובלא שכיכו» (Noach 42:6; line 1530): "for it cannot be quiet: the crookedness of their way makes them go without order and without rest." Ties: S01 Scattered, S07 Restless wanting. The Zohar names the root: a crooked way, not a lack of technique.
2. **Hidden in the ark.** In time of judgement, stay hidden: «ערום ראה רעה ונסתר» (Proverbs 22:3), Noah saw the destroyer and hid (line 1403). The Shunammite says "I dwell among my people": «מאן דעייל רישיה בין עמא … לא ישגחון עליה למידן ליה לביש» (Noach 29:11; line 1413): "whoever puts his head among the people, they do not single him out to judge him for bad." From inside: safety in being one among many, not standing out. Ties: S84 Trust, S02 Holding it all up (its opposite).
3. **Longing to know, even when one cannot.** The waves rise "in longing to see", and so they are praised: «מכאן כל מאן דכסיף לאסתכלא ולמנדע אף על גב דלא יכיל … שבחא איהו דיליה» (Noach 29:8; line 1410): "from here: whoever longs to look and to know, even though he cannot, it is his praise." Ties: S13 The unnamed longing, S35 The edge of knowing. This is a comfort the guide can give a person who cannot understand.
4. **Rest that gathers inward.** In the offering, smoke and fire are gathered in «לגו לגו» until the anger rests: «ואתקריב כלא לגו מחשבה ואתעביד רעותא חדא. וכדין ריח ניחח. דנח רוגזא ואתעביד נייחא» (Noach 31:3; line 1425): "and all is brought near into thought and becomes one will; and then 'a pleasing scent': the anger rests and there is rest." Rest is "the rest of the spirit, the joy of all together, the light of lamps, the light of faces" (line 1426). Ties: S32 Quiet, S50 Joy and bittul together.
5. **Sadness that withholds blessing.** «בעצבון … עציבו ורוגזא בלא נהירו דאנפין» (Noach 32:4; line 1441): sadness is "grief and anger without light in the face", the other side "that holds back blessings from the world", and so "the blessing of God adds no sadness." Ties: S04 Low and heavy. The Zohar here stands behind the Tanya's insistence that sadness is not holiness.
6. **The face that keeps the image.** One who does not sin keeps the radiance of the higher image, and all creatures fear him; when a person sins, the image changes and he fears the beasts (lines 1442-1444). Ties: S63 The world held, S86.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **Torah needs the body and the heart.** Rabbi Yehudah will not hear Torah from a man lying in bed: «מלי דאורייתא בעיין תקונא דגופא ותקונא דלבא» (Noach 35:4; line 1466): "words of Torah need the body set right and the heart set right"; "prepare to meet your God" (line 1465). And at midnight, when God enters the Garden to delight with the righteous, all of them listen to the one who rises to learn (line 1467). Ties: technique 2, 18 (saying it with the lips); S79.
- **Midnight rising.** When the north wind wakes at midnight, a flame strikes the rooster and it calls; only the truly righteous rise to Torah, and God and the righteous in Eden listen to their voice (lines 1583-1587, the opening of Lech Lecha, noted here for its practice).
- **Do not travel alone, carry Torah.** «כל מאן דאזיל בארחא ולאו עמיה מלי דאורייתא אסתכן בנפשיה» (Noach 30:1; line 1415): "whoever goes on the road without words of Torah endangers himself." "The Holy One goes before a person on the way" when he is worthy (line 1570); keep the camp holy and the mouth clean (lines 1571-1573).
- **Return while you are here.** «דכד בר נש אתיב ואתנחם על חובוי. קודשא בריך הוא מחיל ליה» (Noach 19:3; line 1340): "when a person returns and regrets his sins, the Holy One forgives him." And the one who sinned in secret and returns, God covers him: «ואי תב בר נש לגביה חפי עליה ומחיל ליה ושביק ליה» (Noach 19:4; line 1341): "if a person returns to Him, He covers him and forgives him and lets it go."
- **The mark of sin, and keeping it before you.** A sin makes a mark above; a second strengthens it; a third spreads it like a stain (lines 1502-1503); only great return erases it. David, though forgiven, kept his sin before him always, «בגין דדחיל עלייהו תדיר» (Noach 39:7; line 1508): "because he feared them always." Ours: a practice of remembering the sin after return, as humility, not as self-condemnation. Guard: S11 The verdict: I'm my mistakes is the counterfeit of this.
- **When pleading for another, make the sin small.** Moses began "Why, O Lord, does Your anger burn": «מאן דמרצה לאחרא לא בעי למעבד ההוא חובא … רב, אלא יזעיר ליה קמיה» (Noach 25:5; line 1373): "whoever appeases for another should not make the sin great, but make it small before him"; to the sinner himself, later, make it great.
- **Give yourself for others (Moses, not Noah).** Moses: «השתא טב לי דאימות ולא ישתצון ישראל» (Noach 25:4; line 1372): "now it is better for me to die, and let Israel not be destroyed." Noah was told he would be saved and «לא בעא רחמין על עלמא» (Noach 25:3; line 1371): "did not ask mercy for the world", so the flood is called "the waters of Noah." Ties: S86 The other held; technique 20 (own words to Him), here for others.
- **Warn, and you are free.** Noah warned his generation every day; whoever warns the wicked "saves himself" even if not heard (line 1381).
- **One heart.** Even rebels, while they were of one heart and one will, could not be touched by judgement; how much more the companions who learn Torah «בלבא חד ורעותא חד» (Noach 44:19; line 1576): "with one heart and one will." And: «מכאן לאנון מארי דמחלוקת לית לון קיומא» (Noach 44:20; line 1577): "from here: people of dispute have no endurance."

## 5. The big human questions

- **The Shechinah goes into exile with them.** Why did the Shechinah not return to the land after the wicked died? Because the righteous were not there; «בכל אתר דאזלו נחתת ושויאת מדורה עמהון» (Noach 27:3; line 1396): "wherever they went, she came down and made her dwelling with them." Being held in exile. Ties: S73 Exile inside; S84.
- **The righteous man as offering.** «ובר נש דאיהו זכאה איהו קרבנא ממש לכפרה» (Noach 16:10; line 1321): "a righteous person is himself an offering, for atonement": the righteous atone for the world.
- **Why do the wicked prosper, and why do some suffer early?** The King's precious vessel: He watched it every day and feared for it, and broke it when His son angered Him (lines 1238-1239). God does not rejoice in destroying the wicked before their time; since the Temple was destroyed «לא הוה חדוה לעילא ותתא» (Noach 8:9; line 1244): "there has been no joy above or below."
- **Whoever comes to be purified is helped.** «אתא בר נש לאתדכאה, מסייעין ליה» (Noach 10:3; line 1253): "a person comes to purify himself, they help him," and the holy soul is added; the one who does not come is left to his own way. The Lech Lecha opening repeats it: «כל מאן דאתער לאתדכאה מסייעין ליה» (Lech Lecha 4:3; line 1596).
- **Joy that belongs only to those who are close.** The King who wants to feast with his beloved first feeds the guards and officials, then sits alone with him and gives him all he asks (line 1300): Shemini Atzeret. Ours: an image of intimacy after duty.
- **Answering Amen.** When Israel answer "Amen, yehe shemei rabba" with a loud voice, God is filled with mercy and even those in Gehinnom are given an hour and a half of rest (lines 1257-1258).

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Longing that cannot reach is itself praise (line 1410).** The best one-line comfort for a person who feels shut out of understanding.
2. **Thought, voice, speech: all one (lines 1519-1524).** A clear, early form of the teaching the guide uses for "the world is His speech".
3. **Moses against Noah (lines 1370-1377).** The question every seeker faces: save yourself, or pray for others. The Zohar is clear.
4. **Sadness without light in the face withholds blessing (line 1441).**
5. **One heart among companions (lines 1576-1577)**: a guard against dispute in any group.

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "The driven sea."** Inside: cannot be quiet, rising and falling, throwing up mud in speech; the cause is a crooked way (lines 1529-1531). Way out: straighten the way, not only calm the mind. Near S01 and S07; the Zohar adds the moral root.
- **State, new: "Longing that is praise."** Inside: you strain to see and know and cannot, and the straining itself is counted as praise (line 1410). Near S13, S35.
- **Technique, new: "Pleading for another."** Ask mercy for others as Moses did, putting yourself on the line; make their fault small before God (lines 1372-1374). Guard: do not decide who is beyond help.
- **Technique, new: "Remembering the forgiven sin."** After return, keep the sin before you as David did, from fear of God, not from self-hatred (lines 1502-1508). Guard: if it turns into "I am my mistakes" (S11), stop and turn to the forgiveness (line 1504).
