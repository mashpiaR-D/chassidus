## The shape of the range

The Third Gate is one long argument, and nearly all of it is about the unity. Its tools are the Zohar's Idra Rabba and Idra Zuta and the Etz Chayim: Arich Anpin and Zeir Anpin, and the skull, brain, hair, eyes, nose, forehead, mouth and beard of each. The argument does not use them as a map of the upper worlds for its own sake. It reads each feature as a way His one power is drawn to creatures, and it ends every time on the same line from the Idra: "Zeir Anpin is Atika, all is one, and from our side they differ." That is why *stated* dominates. The author tells us the tie himself, again and again.

Three faces lead. F03 (the hiding) leads because every feature of Zeir Anpin is explained as a contraction toward creatures. F02 (the higher and lower unity) comes next because every such explanation ends with "but to Him all is equal". F05 (the Essence) is third because the author keeps insisting that nothing, not even "wisdom" or "will", can be said of the Essence itself. F09 (the unity of opposites) is unusually strong. The phrase "two opposites in one subject" and its variants run through the whole gate. F04 never appears. The author does not use the words sovev and memale here; Arich and Zeir do that work.

The gate announces three problems at the start (line 563): particular providence against the verses of "hiding the face"; the Idra's descriptions; and knowledge against free choice. It answers them in order. The Idra material takes chapters 1-30. Hiding the face is answered in chapters 31-34, and knowledge and choice in chapter 38.

## Ch. 1-2 (563-574): three assessments; yachid and echad

The first "assessment" is F01 and F05 together. The worlds live only from the power of His Essence, and the "kav" is a drawing of the Essence itself, not just of light: > ואלו היה הסתלקות כוחו יתברך חס ושלום מהעולמות אפילו רגע אחד היו מתבטלים לגמרי והיו כלא היו (line 564)

The second assessment splits in two. In the first half (Arich Anpin) every level is before Him with no hiding and He is "yachid". That every difference comes from Him and yet He is yachid is "two opposites in one subject", called "pele": > איך יהיו שני הפכים אלו בנושא אחד (line 565)

In the second half (Zeir Anpin) He is revealed as divided powers and is "echad" in them: > אשר לשון התאחדות פירושו הוא הגם שנגלו לכוחות מחולקים אף על פי כן הוא אחד בהם (line 566)

The parable of the sage (568-569) sets up the gate's key claim: the greater the wisdom, the lower it can reach, and the small thing does not hide the sage. So in Arich "the yesh does not hide before Him at all" (line 570). Zeir contracts so that each creature can appear as itself, "each in its own way and essence" (line 572). Vessels are what turn one essence into another (573-574), and so they belong only to Zeir.

## Ch. 3-4 (575-583): every difference is in the "face"

The human parable breaks at one point, and the text says so (576). A person's pupil and his own brain are separate from his soul, but before God they are not: > אבל לגביה יתברך אשר הנבראים גם כן אינן דבר נבדל ממהותו יתברך (line 576)

The Idra's "from our side they change" is read with emphasis on "our": > מסטרא דילן דווקא דהיינו מצידינו כאשר נגלה אצלינו משתנים ההמשכות אבל לגביה יתברך הכל הוא המשכה אחת לבדה בלי שום שינוי כלל (line 580)

Arich's "open eye that never sleeps" is providence without pause over every detail (581, F01). Zeir's "brows" let a complete, separate yesh appear, "the very opposite" (582). Its "sleep" is when only the created vessels appear and His unity does not. The long line 583 reads the nose as "life" and "life of life", and the second is the creatures' bitul into His Essence: > וחיין דחיין הם התאחדותם וקישורם וביטולם לעצמותו יתברך (line 583)

The gloss in 583 also names F07. His delight (Atik Yomin) is completed "by the deeds of the lower ones".

## Ch. 5-8 (584-598): the purpose, and our side of it

He is one before and after creation (584, F05). Why create, then? The answer is F07 with a precision that recurs through the gate, "from their side": > אבל מחמת שרצונו יתברך היה בבריאות עולמו בכדי להתגלות יחודו בנבראים דייקא מסטרא דלהון (line 585)

Since nothing exists besides Him, creating a world of hiding is "the opposite of reason" (586). So it is called will, and will is the mean between two "opposed opposites" (587). The three heads of the Idra Zuta are "one, when you look at them" (593). Line 595 names the two unities in the Zohar's terms: > רק בבחינת המשכתו לנבראים נקרא זעיר אנפין יחודא עילאה והתגלות יחודו בבחינת נבראים נקרא שכינה ויחודא תתאה (line 595)

Then comes the person's side (F12 and F08, 596). The Shema is contemplation of the higher unity carried into the lower, until nothing below is separate, and from that comes self-sacrifice: > הכל אין דבר זולתו להיות בחינת נפרד ומחמת זה יהיה הביטול בבחינת מסירות נפש (line 596)

Torah and mitzvot "add lights to the vessels" (597, F10). The addition is in the vessels and never in Him (598).

## Ch. 9-13 (599-628): the two faces shine into each other

Arich and Zeir "shine into each other" when, and only when, the lower ones unite them by service (600, F07). Anger, "the eyes of God" and "you weakened the Rock" all concern the drawing and not the Essence (601). The hair, the arms (nature is "the hand of God", miracle is "the great hand", 610), the legs, the mouth and the ears are each read the same way. Line 618 sets the future ("they will see eye to eye") beside Sinai, where the souls flew out and the dew of Torah revived them: > כי בחינת טל תורה הוא התקשרותו מצד עצמותו אשר מצד עצמותו שוין היש והאין (line 618)

The same revelation at Babel and Sodom nullified the levels, for the need of the hour. Arich has no ears because hearing takes in something from another (621), yet every prayer reaches Him, "He who plants the ear, shall He not hear?". Life is from Him, but "if it were revealed to us no creature would exist" (622). Chapter 13 closes with the gate's plainest F09 statement and its F12 consequence: > וכל ההפכים אצלו יתברך ביחודא חד בלי שום פרודא כלל (line 627) > כי אם להאמין באמונה שלמעלה מהשכל (line 627)

## Ch. 14-16 (629-654): "above intellect"; the wonders of soul and body

"Above intellect" is defined carefully (630). It is not the Rambam's "depth of the concept". It is "the antecedent of intellect", outside the category of wisdom. Ibn Ezra denied that Shamor and Zachor were said at once "because the light of Kabbalah did not shine on him" (631). To God wisdom is a made tool (633-634), yet "the power of the maker must be in the made" (635, F01). The soul-body bond is "umafli la'asot", a wonder (636-644). The three wonders are finally one: > וכאשר תסתכל יותר הכל הוא פלא אחד שהוא כל יכול (line 644)

"The head that is not a head" (653) leads to the gate's rule for the knower: > דהיינו לקשר ההתגלות אליו יתברך בבחינת אמונה למעלה מן הדעת (line 654)

## Ch. 17-20 (655-679): hiding as the condition; the breaking as room for limit

The hairs grow through the skull, a bone that reveals nothing. That is the hiding of His will, without which no divided level could ever appear (657): > איך יומשך ממנו הזולת והבעל גבול אך אין לומר על זה כי אם כך עלה ברצונו יתברך (line 657)

As the growing power is hidden in the dust (661), so the revelation of His Essence needs "the great hiding" called ayin (662). Line 669 reads the breaking of the vessels in the same way. The light withdrew to make room for "the power of limit that is within the power of Ein Sof": > הוצרך להיות בחינת שבירה דהיינו שיסתלק האור שהוא הגילוי מבחינת אין סוף ברוך הוא בכדי שיהיה מקום לגילוי כח הגבול (line 669)

The author then states that limit itself is His: > ושורש הצירוף וההתחברות הוא מכח עצמותו ברוך הוא השווה אשר כח היש הוא עמו בהשוואה גמורה (line 673) > אשר מצד השוואתו ושלימותו יתברך משתוה הגבול עם בלי גבול בהשואה גמורה (line 678)

The head's hair joins His will to the outside of the vessels. The beard's hair, from the front, reveals His unity inside them and nullifies them to Him (665, 677).

## Ch. 21-24 (680-705): the thirteen attributes; black and red hair

The thirteen attributes of mercy arise because the vessels are one with Him in root and far from Him in revelation, like a father and a son who were once one (684). "Truth" is the attribute that says so: > כי מצד אמיתיותו יתברך הכל הוא עצמותו ברוך הוא אפילו מצד הכלים (line 684)

"As if two brains join ... and become one brain": the "as if" is ours, and the oneness is true (690). Zeir's six extremities are one even in the opposite, "as above, unlimited, so below, in limit" (702). The other side takes hold where details are revealed. But: > ומצד כח גילוי יחודו יתברך מתמתקין ומתהפכין מחשוכא לנהורא וממרירו למתיקא (line 703)

Nukva's hair is red, neither white nor black (704-705). White would allow no division. Black would leave creatures unable to join, so that "each would swallow the other alive". Red means divided and still joined. "His main will is that through the division of levels the essence of His glory be revealed" (705).

## Ch. 25-27 (706-718): turning the middot; two kinds of bitul; bitul within the yesh

Line 708 is the gate's clearest F11 and F12 together. The wisdom in nature can show His unity, or it can become "the wisdom of Egypt" that says "I and none else": > אשר חכמתם הוא לכחש בהוי"ה וביחודו כי אם שאומרים אני ואפסי עוד (line 708) > ולאהפכא חשוכא לנהורא דהיינו מחכמת טבעיות הנבראים ישיג חכמת אלקו"ת (line 708)

Two kinds of bitul are distinguished (712). Zeir's beard lacks the attribute "truth", because its bitul is bitul within vessels, not bitul as it is in truth: > דהיינו כי לא נגלה הביטול כמו שהוא מצד האמת אשר אין זולתו כלל כי אם שנגלו לכלים ובבחינת כלים נגלה הביטול בהם (line 712)

Arich's beard may not be touched. The bitul there is only "by faith above knowledge" (713). Yet when Israel serve with self-sacrifice, "through their grasp in wisdom they are nullified above wisdom" and Zeir's beard turns white (714). Then comes the gate's central F07 statement. Nukva has no beard, because her work is bitul within the yesh itself: > אבל בבחינת נוקבא הוא דייקא הכוונה שיהיה נגלה הביטול בבחינת היש דייקא (line 715)

The parable of the gem and the talking bird explains why this is the delight (717): > אבל שיהיה נמשך הביטול אפילו בבחינת יש וגבול בבחינתו אשר מהות היש הוא היפוך מהביטול ואף על פי כן יהיה בטל בזה הוא חידוש בבחינת תענוג (line 717)

Torah and mitzvot are "the revelation of His will precisely within the yesh" (716, F10).

## Ch. 28-33 (719-755): yachid and echad; the hidden face explained

Line 719 restates F01 in the same words as line 564. Then comes the gate's clearest statement of the precision the SPINE puts first: > ואין להעריך בכח הזה אפילו בחינת אחד כי אחד הוא התאחדות מצד כוחות מחולקים (line 722) > ובחינה זו נקרא אחד דהיינו התאחדות הכחות (line 724)

Arich is yachid, with "no second at all". Zeir is echad, the joining of divided powers. "The 'I' of His Essence is the 'I' in all the powers of the worlds" (723). The whole purpose is "to reveal His equality in all levels ... in heaven above and on the earth below, there is none else" (734).

The problem from line 563 is then answered. Those who follow only the outside of the worlds oppose His power (742). That was the sin of the Baal worshippers (743). Hiding the face means leaving people to "chance", to the princes and the stars, and yet: > כי באמת כל הנמשך בעולמות הכל הוא עצם כחו לבדו אפילו שבעים שרים וכוכבים ומזלות עצמם (line 745)

The two kinds of verse are reconciled (746). Troubles that come from the hiding alone are not intended punishment. Punishment follows real transgression, and even it does not come "from the mouth of the Most High". Line 748 states F11 as the goal of the whole creation, including the other side: > כי זהו תכלית כוונתו יתברך שיהיה שולטנותיה עילא ותתא ומכל סטרין דייקא שיהיה התגלות כחו יתברך אפילו מן ההיפוך (line 748) > שיהיה תחיית המתים דהיינו בחינת המת שאין למטה הימנו ואף על פי כן יתגלה בו חיותו (line 748)

Chapter 33 ranks providence by service (751-755). Service above intellect draws "the eye of God toward those who fear Him", one eye. Service through understanding draws Zeir's two eyes, for good. Rote service brings "sleep". Transgression makes the eyes "roam" to judge. The highest rung is reached only through the worlds: > והתגלות זה הוא על ידי כח העולמות דוקא כי שלא בבחינת עולמות הלא רצונו יתברך אינו בערך גילוי כלל (line 753)

## Ch. 34 (756-757): the great gloss

Line 756 is a single gloss that sums up the gate. It sets four rungs of service. The first is to turn from the opposite, and the second is to accept the yoke. The third is to "look at the inwardness of the worlds" and turn darkness to light even in eating. The fourth is not to look at the outward at all, "for in truth, when you look, you will see that all is He alone". Its sharpest line makes the other side the stage of "ein od": > וגילוי הזה בבחינת אין עוד הוא דווקא על ידי בחינת סטרא אחרא האומרת אני ואפסי עוד (line 756)

Even punishment is within the one will (756.9). It ends: "when you look, all is His Essence alone" (756.13). Line 757 applies this to the prayer "yehi ratzon": it asks that His will be revealed, not changed, because > כי הרע אינו רק מצד ההסתרה המסתיר רצונו יתברך בבחינת יחוד (line 757)

## Ch. 35-37 (758-770): nature and miracle; the dwelling in the vessels

His will is revealed either through nature (Solomon's Temple, where Godliness lived "in wood and stones, silver and gold", 758) or by changing nature (the sea, the vinegar). The Names and their combinations make every nature (761, 764). Service by wisdom follows the set order, since He is known only from His acts. Self-sacrifice above wisdom can change the combinations (764). Adam Kadmon's feet reach physical Asiyah, and nothing was made except by the primordial thought (765). Both ways are "words of the living God" (766-767). The sea came before Sinai: first one goes beyond the vessels, then one draws His will into them (769). Line 770 ties prayer, Torah and the Temple to F07: > וזהו עיקר הכוונה שיהיה לו דירה בתחתונים דייקא שהוא בבחינת הכלים דייקא (line 770)

## Ch. 38 (771-778): knowledge and free choice

The Sages never say "He knows"; they say "He showed Adam" and "all is foreseen" (771). In the hidden thought all that was and will be is one power, and yet it is there in every detail, "two opposites, truly a wonder". The "souls of the tzaddikim" He consulted are His own power of equality (771.3). His will does not compel; it includes the choice (772): > אבל לא שיהיה חס ושלום כחו מכריח אדרבה בכח כוונתו ורצונו זה נכלל גם כן בחינת בחירה הזאת (line 772)

Every detail, even among the wicked, is particular providence (775). At the end all will be refined (776), and choice is "the purpose of creation" (777). The gate ends on the Book of Adam (778), which is the root of each soul, revealed through good deeds.

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **Yachid and echad, stated outright.** The SPINE's first precision (the Rebbe's Inyanah 5:5) appears here in 1820 as the difference between Arich and Zeir (lines 565-566, 722, 724, 725). Arich is "yachid", with no second at all; Zeir is "echad", divided powers joined. The Tanya ranges read so far do not draw this line.
- **Limit belongs to the Infinite.** The breaking makes room for "the power of limit within the power of Ein Sof" (669). "The power of yesh is with Him in complete equality" (673), and limit and the limitless are equal in His perfection (678). The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud nullifies the yesh before its source. Here the yesh and its limit are themselves His power, waiting to be revealed.
- **The aim is bitul inside the yesh.** Zeir's unity nullifies the yesh. Nukva's aim is that "the bitul be revealed within the yesh precisely" (715), and that is the delight: a stone that shines, a bird that talks (717). This is a third formulation beside the two the SPINE keeps under F07. For the Rashab the purpose of the yesh is that it become batel (F07e). For the Rebbe the created yesh is itself the dwelling (F07h). Strashelye's version keeps the yesh as yesh and asks that it be batel as it stands. Reading it as a middle position is ours.
- **The other side as the stage of "ein od".** The Tanya (ch. 22) calls kelipah "I and none else". Here that very claim, when subdued and turned, is where "there is none else" is revealed (756.6, 748, 703). The power of the opposite "was also in His will" (748.2).
- **Revelation follows service.** The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud states the unity as a fact to contemplate. Here what is revealed of it depends on how a person serves (600, 744, 751-756). The rungs run from rote service, which brings "sleep" and chance, through understanding, which brings two eyes for good, to self-sacrifice above intellect, which brings one eye and changes nature. His providence never leaves (754: "for Him all is particular providence"), but its being revealed is ours to bring about.
- **Three old problems answered from the unity:** the hiding of the face (745-746), "yehi ratzon" (757), and knowledge and choice (771-772). None of these is in the Tanya range read so far.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- One entry is *none*: line 629, a chapter plan. Sub-entry 563.1 (title and plan) is also none. - Eleven line entries are structural with no face. These are parables set up for the next line (613, 762, 768), kabbalistic mapping (612, 645, 729) and plans or summaries (640, 641, 672, 685). The parable lines that carry a face (568, 578, 604, 659, 666, 671, 681, 701, 747) are also kept structural. The text itself draws the tie only in the next line.
- *Reading* is used three times, all in the last chapter. Lines 773 and 774 (all souls included in Adam's; potential leaders hidden by sin) are tied to F06 only by us. Line 778 (the Book of Adam as the root of each soul) is thin; the text speaks of the soul's root, not of God. - The Adam Kadmon, Abba and Imma, and "120 combinations of Elokim" passages (735, 756.7, 765.3) are kept structural where they only name levels. - F04 is absent, and F06 and F10 are rare. The gate is about how God is drawn to the world, not about the soul or the mitzvah as such. Where Torah and mitzvot do appear (597, 611, 618, 620, 716, 744, 769-770), the tie is stated.

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **What "the higher unity" names.** In the Tanya's Shaar HaYichud 3 the higher unity is creatures nullified like sunlight in the sun, and the lower unity (ch. 7) is His kingship, where space and time appear. Line 595 uses the Zohar's terms differently. The higher unity is Zeir Anpin, His drawing to creatures as it is within His power. The lower unity is the Shechinah, His unity revealed in creatures. The two maps overlap, but they are not the same map.
- **Which is higher, the bitul above the worlds or the bitul within them?** In service the author ranks bitul above the vessels highest ("whom have I in heaven", 753, 756.4). In purpose he ranks bitul within the yesh as the main intent and the delight (715-717, 770). He does not resolve the two rankings, and neither do we. The Tanya ch. 36-37 range holds the dwelling below as the purpose; the Shaar HaYichud range holds the higher unity as the truth.
- **Faith or knowledge.** The Alter Rebbe says the unity of His Essence and knowledge is known only by faith (Tanya SH 7, SPINE F12b-c). This gate agrees on the Arich level: "secret of faith" (627), "flee to your place ... faith above knowledge" (654), and Arich's beard "only by faith" (713). But it also insists on knowing. "Through their grasp in wisdom they are nullified above wisdom" (714), and 764 says He is known only through His acts, so service by wisdom is needed. That is closer to the Rayatz's "toil to understand, then believe the rest" (F12e) than to "we have no business with hidden things". In the model range the Alter Rebbe holds both sides too.
- **God's knowledge.** The Tanya (SH 7) adopts the Rambam: He is knower, known and knowledge, beyond our grasp. This gate uses that formula once (576), but for free choice it steps away from the word "knowledge". The Sages "never say He knows" (771), and what is in His power is "not in the category of knowledge at all" (772). This is a different road to the same conclusion, and it is kept as different.
- **Punishment within the one will.** The gate itself holds a tension. Line 746 says evils do not come from the mouth of the Most High and "your evil chastises you". Line 756.9 says the power of punishment was within His will, because through it His perfection is revealed more. The author settles it with "one simple will that includes the opposite" (748, 772). Whether that settles it is not ours to decide.
