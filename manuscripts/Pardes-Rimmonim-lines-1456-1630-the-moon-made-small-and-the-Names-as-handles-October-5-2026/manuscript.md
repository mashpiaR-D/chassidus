# Pardes Rimmonim, lines 1456-1630: the moon made small, and the Names as handles

---
Four gates. Gate 17 reads the Zohar on the angel who struck Jacob's thigh, and uses it to say something the guide needs: the shells never enter the divine world; their "entry" is only an accusation that makes mercy turn its face. Gate 18 is the Talmud's story of the moon who said "two kings cannot share one crown" and was told "go, make yourself small". The Ramak works through three readings and a hard question: how can there be complaint inside holiness? Gates 19 and 20 are on the Names of God: why the four-letter Name has no meaning in any human word, why the Names are only "handles" for speaking of One who has no name, why the calf was the sin of splitting "Who" from "These", and how Israel drew the Names together with tears.

## 2. The unity of God in this range

**No name holds Him.** The strongest theme of these gates. The four-letter Name is called "the Name of the essence", but the Ramak warns: this does not mean the essence of Ein Sof, "for there is nothing that can bound His essence, His oneness, His being" (כי אין דבר שיגביל עצמותו ואחדותו ומציאותו, line 1532). A name is a handle: "as the name Yitzchak is a handle to that man, by which speech can take hold of him" (כמו ששם יצחק הוא בית יד אל האיש ההוא שבו תהיה תפיסת הדיבור, line 1532), so the sefirot are the handle by which we can speak of Him at all. Even "Cause of causes" is "a borrowed name" (אבל הוא שם מושאל, line 1541). And "the one who thinks there are real letters in the sefirot makes Him bodily" (כי החושב כן מגשים, line 1545). The four-letter Name itself "is awesome; who can explain it? It has no explanation at all" (line 1531): every other Name comes from a human verb (strong, lord, enough, hosts); this one does not.

**Hidden, but not absent.** Keter is like the kamatz, a closed fist: no one knows what is inside. But a fist may be empty, and this one is not: "because Ein Sof is hidden inside, a person must not deny His existence" (כי לא מפני העלם האין סוף שבתוכו יכחיש האדם מציאותו, line 1541). Ein Sof is "hidden of all hidden" (סתימא דכל סתימין, line 1596): hidden even from the hidden things near Him.

**Who and These: never apart.** The Zohar's opening: the hidden Binah is called Mi, "Who?", "a question without an answer"; when she brings out the seven attributes she makes Eleh, "These", which one can point to with a finger. Joined, Mi + Eleh spell Elokim. "And this was the sin of the calf: they split Mi from Eleh" (וזה היה עון בעלי העגל כי הפרידו בין מ"י לאל"ה, line 1597), saying "these are your gods" with no "Who" behind them. The Ramak adds the other half: "just as Eleh cannot be without Mi, so Mi cannot be without Eleh" (כמו שא"א לאל"ה בלא מי כן א"א למי בלא אלה, line 1597), "for the Shechinah in the lower ones is a need on high" (כי שכינה בתחתונים צורך גבוה, line 1597). The hidden God is bound to the revealed world, and the world is never without its hidden "Who".

**Hiding and showing cause each other.** "The hiding is the cause of the showing, and the showing is the cause of the hiding" (כי ההעלם סבת ההתגלות והגילוי סבת ההעלם, line 1612). When Tiferet comes out, Binah is covered by it; the son walks before his mother. Every revealing covers something higher.

**The part holds the whole.** Each letter of the Name, filled out, holds the whole Name: "so that the part is in the whole and the whole in the part, to show more of the oneness" (להיות החלק בכל והכל בחלק, line 1537). "The Names are the sefirot and the sefirot are the Names, and there is no difference between them" (line 1627).

**Beyond time.** On the order in which Tiferet and Malchut were emanated: "there is no before and after here, for it is all in one instant... for divinity is not under time" (שאין האלהות תחת הזמן, line 1499). So the schools that said heaven came first, and earth first, and both together, are all right: "the words of all are true, the words of the living God" (line 1499).

**The shells never get in.** "Let no one think the shells enter Atzilut, as many kabbalists of our generation thought" (line 1473). Their entry is "not truly entering, but their claim and their pleading of debts" (ולא ממש שיכנסו, אלא תביעתם ולימודם חובות, line 1473), which wakes judgment and makes mercy hide its face. The damage is real, but it is done from outside. And the shell is finally undone "by drawing near to purity" (כי אבוד הקליפה הוא בקורבתה אל הטהרה, line 1519).

## 3. States of consciousness

**Lightning that hides again (RK-S15, proposed).** Chesed is the first place anything can be grasped at all, and even there it comes "like lightning, shining for a moment and then hidden again, running and returning" (כענין הברק מאיר לפי שעה וחוזר ונגנז ברצוא ושוב, line 1585). The Zohar: "as soon as it comes out and shines for a moment, at once it is hidden". Inside: something opened for a moment, a clear sight of kindness, and then it closed. It was real, and it is gone. How to enter: do not try to hold the flash. Thank for it and go back to work; it comes again. This is close to S19 (After the high: the flame in the air), but the Ramak makes the flash-and-hide the normal shape of knowing, not a letdown. Compare the Tanya, where Chochmah is "like a point and like lightning that flashes on the mind" (שהוא כנקודה וכברק המבריק על שכלו, Tanya line 1229).

**Held by opposites made one (from Gate 17).** To "increase peace", Netzach, a branch of kindness, draws from judgment, and Hod, a branch of judgment, draws from kindness, "until judgment and kindness become one thing to them" (עד היות הדין והחסד ענין א' להם, line 1474). This deepens RK-S09 (Whole in opposites). No new id.

**Hidden fist, not empty.** In dark times the guide can use line 1541: I cannot see what is inside the closed hand, and I do not therefore say it is empty. This is S36 (Faith above knowing) with a picture.

**Fear, then love.** "One must put fear, from the side of judgment, before love, which is mercy" (line 1557), for "we go up in holiness and not down". The 365 prohibitions are awe, the 248 commandments love. Tied to S45 (Lower awe) before S41-S44 (love); the map already has this order.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Turn to El when judgment presses (RK-T13, proposed).** Sefer HaOrah (quoted and accepted): the camps of judgment spread through the world; above them is El, kindness; "and if there is a tzaddik in the world who knows how to direct his heart to this attribute called El" (ואם יש צדיק בעולם שידע לכוין אל המדה הזאת הנקראת א"ל, line 1579), the camps scatter. "For this reason we say 'El melech yoshev', to quiet the power of judgment" (line 1579). The Zohar: "were it not that this El wakes in the world, the world could not stand one hour" (line 1580). And in the prayer before the Thirteen Attributes, God "acts with piety, leaning toward kindness, though we are not worthy of mercy" (ומתנהג בחסידות שמטה כלפי חסד אע"פ שאין אנו ראוין לרחמים, line 1582). What it is for: when harshness is all around, to turn the mind on purpose to the kindness above the harshness. How: say "El melech yoshev al kisei rachamim" slowly, holding "El" as kindness, not as power. Guard: this is not a spell; it is turning the heart. Ties to `before_whom`, `choose_line`.

**Remember with the mouth, pour out the heart (RK-T14, proposed).** The Zohar on "These I remember and pour out my soul": "I remember them with my mouth, and I pour out my tears with the will of my soul, to draw these letters" (אדכרנא בפומאי ושפיכנא דמעאי ברעות נפשא בגין לאמשכא אתוון אלין, line 1599). The Ramak: on the festivals "Israel prayed for their union with these letters, with tears of the heart, with prayer and pleading" (היו ישראל מתפללים על יחודם באותיות אלה בדמע הלב ובתפלה ובתחנונים, line 1604), "to show that this union and drawing was great labor and strain". What it is for: some joining above does not happen by thought alone; it needs the mouth and the tears. How: name what you long to see joined, out loud, and let the longing come up; then close "with the voice of song and thanks" (line 1599). Ties to `own_words`, `lips`, `heart_after`.

**Direct every word to the Name.** "One who prays with kavanah must bring his intention in every word of prayer to the four-letter Name" (חוייב המכוין להביא כוונתו בכל מלות התפלה אל שם בן ד', line 1561). The same letters can be holy or not by intent, "and as one must have intention to draw down a soul at the time of union, so the scribe must have intention" (line 1561). Ties to `before_whom`; the guide's form: whatever Name or word is said, the heart points to the One. And a promise: through such kavanah "a person is saved from sin, and his heart is firm, trusting in God" (ובזה ניצול האדם מעון ונכון לבו בטוח בה', line 1545).

**Keter means wait.** "Keter" is read as "wait for me a little": "do not go deep into it, for it cannot be grasped" (כתר לי זעיר ואחוך. והכונה אל תעמיק בו שאין בו השגה, line 1564). Ehyeh means "here I stand, ready to be" ( line 1564). A guard for RK-T01 (Touch and draw back): the top is for waiting, not for grasping.

**Where not to speak holy words.** Torah may not be spoken in a filthy place, because the letters' spirit is clothed in the air of this world by one's breath, and there "one joins the pure to the impure" (נמצא מרכיב טהור בטמא, line 1531). A plain rule with a reason: speech is a real thing.

## 5. The big human questions

**Why is there a world?** "So that through the emanation His greatness would be revealed" and those in the world could be guided (כדי שעל ידו יתגלה גדולתו, line 1498); "the main thing that rose in His will was Malchut, which governs the world" (line 1498). The purpose of all the upper worlds is the lowest one's relation to Him.

**Why does the moon wane? Why am I small?** The moon asked to rule by her own nature; she was made small and put "at the feet" of the others. The Ramak does not hide how strange this is: "how can there be complaint and quarrel in the pure, holy world?" (line 1522). His answer: Malchut draws from strict judgment, and like judgment she did not want to be weighed and decided. She was lowered, not as punishment only, but to rule the lower worlds, until "the shell is undone and death swallowed forever, and all creatures know that He alone is God" (ויהיו יודעים כל הברואים כי הוא אלהים לבדו, line 1525), and she returns as before. And through all of it, "the union never changed and will never change" (כי מעולם לא נשתנה היחוד ולא ישתנה לעולם ועד, line 1526). Smallness is a place in the story, not the end of it.

**Nature and miracle.** "From the side of judgment everything goes by nature" (line 1480); miracles come from Chesed. So the ordinary course of things is not godless; it is God as judgment and order. Livelihood is hard because food comes through judgment (line 1482).

**Suffering and providence.** The Zohar reads "the eyes of the Lord your God are always upon it" as "not turning the eye away for one moment" (דלא תסתלק מעינא רגעא חדא, line 1607). When the male light keeps its eye on its partner, no suspicion or jealousy can enter; when that watching is withdrawn, harsh judgment spreads below. Ours: the guide can read this as the ground of trust, that He does not look away, and as the model for how we hold those in our care.

**Love.** The vowel segol is from segulah, a treasure: "a person hides away the thing he longs for, to be his treasure" (line 1542). Love as keeping close and hidden.

**Building.** "There is no building except by kindness" (אין בנין אלא בחסד, line 1507). Whatever lasts is built from giving.

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **Elokim as sheath.** The Ramak (line 1578, 1606): YHVH is the sword, Elokim its sheath; outside its sheath the sword cuts, in its sheath it is mercy. The Gate of Unity: "this Name Elokim is a shield and sheath for the Name YHVH" (הוא מגן ונרתק לשם הויה, Tanya line 815), hiding the light that makes the world from nothing, so the world seems to run by nature. Same image, different job. In the Ramak the sheath softens judgment; in the Tanya the sheath hides being-from-nothing. The guide should keep both: a hidden God protects and also conceals.
- **Nature = Elokim.** Line 1480 and 1104 give the Ramak's version; Tanya line 815 gives the gematria and the reason. Full agreement on the fact; the Tanya adds that the "nature" is only how it looks.
- **Names do not reach the Essence.** The Ramak is firm (lines 1532, 1541): even the four-letter Name names the sefirot, not Ein Sof. Chassidus keeps this, and then says something the Ramak does not: the Essence is found in the lowest and in the deed, below all names. The Ramak's "handle" picture is the right warning for the guide against treating any word as God.
- **Lightning.** The Ramak puts the flash of grasp in Chesed (line 1585); the Tanya puts it in Chochmah, the first point of insight (Tanya line 1229). Both know that the first grasp is a flash, and that it must widen or it is gone.
- **Mi and Eleh.** The Ramak's "Mi cannot be without Eleh... the Shechinah below is a need on high" (line 1597) is close to Chabad's dwelling below. But the Ramak argues it to keep the world standing; Chabad makes it the purpose.
- **Shells outside.** Line 1473 is a strong guard that Chabad shares (the shells get their life only as from outside, from the "back" of holiness). The guide should keep the Ramak's plain version: evil does not get inside God.
