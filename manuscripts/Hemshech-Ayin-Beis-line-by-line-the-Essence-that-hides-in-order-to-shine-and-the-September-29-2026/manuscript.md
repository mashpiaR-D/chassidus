# Hemshech Ayin Beis, line by line: the Essence that hides in order to shine, and the one who knows it

*Unity Index, the Ayin Beis part: finished 2026-09-29. Hemshech Ayin Beis ("the series of 5672") is the Rebbe Rashab's longest series of maamarim. He began it on Shavuot 5672 (1912) in Lubavitch and carried it, Shabbat by Shabbat and festival by festival, to Vayera 5676 (autumn 1915). Then comes a long written continuation that the file heads "a written continuation, not spoken" (line 4682). The file's title gives Lubavitch and Rostov, 1912-1916; the continuation carries no date in the text read. The text is a clean digital edition, not OCR: 5,750 lines, each a printed paragraph, under 144 maamar headings, with the series' own chapter numbers running from 1 to 421 (as far as Mishpatim 5675) and none after. The winter of 5674 is not in the series: after Vayera 5674 (line 1819) the next maamar is Bamidbar (line 1859). Every line was read in ten ranges and asked one question: how does this line express the unity of God? The ten detailed reports are in `ayin-beis/` (listed at the end). Every Hebrew line quoted below was copied from those readings, checked with `find_he.py verify` at the line given, and checked again, letters only, to sit on that line itself.*

*The owner has said that contemplating the unity is not a second thing added to understanding it: it is "part and parcel of the same process". Ayin Beis says the same in its own words, and more sharply than any book the index has read (see the short answer). So this overview gives the two together. For each movement of the series it says what the movement teaches about the unity, and then what that teaching is when a person sits with it: the movement in the mind, one line to hold, what opens inside, where the mind reaches its edge, and which step of `ONE-PROCESS.md` it deepens. No line-by-line meditative reading of Ayin Beis exists yet (`meditative/` has none), so the contemplative notes are built from the range readings and from the Rashab's own instructions for contemplation, which are many. Where an instruction is the Rashab's, the line is given. Where the arrangement is ours, it says so.*

## The short answer

Samach Vav asks why there are worlds at all. Ayin Beis asks the next question, and keeps asking it for four years: **how does anything come from the Essence and still show it?** How does light come from what is not light, many from what is simple, a "something" from what is no thing? Its answer turns the usual picture of hiding around. **The hiding is not the price of revelation. It is how revelation happens.** The Essence is not light at all, דבחי' עצמות המאציל הרי אינו בגדר אור כלל (line 719). Light shines only through vessels that hide it a little (line 720). The tzimtzum is itself "for the sake of revelation" (line 2578), and the aim is not to undo it but that "the tzimtzum itself not hide" (line 3657). At the end the concealment will shine of itself: שהלילה עצמה תאיר (line 4688). The power of hiding is itself the Essence (line 4693), so the created "something" is where the Essence shows most: ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי (line 4454). That is why the only name that fits the Essence is "Creator" (line 543). The book ends on one verse holding both unities: "the lower unity is a union just like the higher" (line 5750).

And the book says, in its own words, that knowing the unity and contemplating it are one process. The higher unity, "all before Him is as nothing", can be understood by any mind, and that is not yet knowing it: זהו רק ההשגה לבד, אבל שיהי' בדרך הנחה במוחו ובהרגש הענין ומכ"ש שיהי' הביטול באופן כזה, א"ז בנבראים מכח עצמן (line 1551). "That is only the understanding. That it be settled in his mind, felt, and still more that the bitul be of that kind, is not in created beings by their own power." It comes when it is given from above, through the Torah (lines 1552-1556). The knowing the book counts is the one that settles, is felt and nullifies, and it maps the road there: the details, the extract, recognition, and then "confirmation", והתאמתות היא הנחה בשכל מה שלמעלה מן השכל לגמרי (line 4665), "placing in the mind what is wholly above the mind". Contemplation that stays cold stays in the mind (line 5647). Every contemplation should reach toward sight, even of the higher unity (line 2183).

The Essence leads, as in every book the index has read, and here by the widest margin. The range reports warn that the count overstates it: many F05 tags mark "the essence of the soul" or "the essence of the light" inside a parable that the Rashab then carries up to the Divine Essence. What stands out is the second and third places. In Samach Vav they were the mitzvah and bitul. Here they are bitul and **hiding**, and knowing comes sixth, not last. Ayin Beis is a book about how the hidden becomes known, and about the person who knows it.

## How the series builds it: twelve movements, the unity and its contemplation together

The series is one argument. Each week the Rashab takes it up where he stopped ("to understand this, first recall what was explained"). The movements below follow its seasons. Each gives what the movement says about the unity, then what it is when contemplated. The step named at the end of each is a step of the ladder in `ONE-PROCESS.md`; the pairing of a movement with a step is ours.

## 1. Will, the ten hidden in the One, and "one" defined (Shavuot to Elul 5672, lines 1-509)

**What it says.** The series opens with Keter as will, and with the first great claim: the power to create is the Essence's alone, and "Havayah is one" means the worlds, after they came to be, are wholly nullified (line 39). Then it studies the ten sefirot hidden in Ein Sof before the tzimtzum, and asks how ten can be in the One without making it many (line 118). Chapter 29 gives the index's own precision in the Rashab's words: ההפרש בין אחד ליחיד דאחד הוא התאחדות הפרטים (line 193), אבל יחיד הוא שמיוחד בעצם שאין כאן פרט כלל (line 193). "There is nothing else" is found "precisely in the existence of the something" (line 356). The surrounding light is not above the worlds; it is within every creature, their main life (line 352), and the whole source of the "something" (lines 426-428). The Essence is drawn only by "the absence of a vessel", the bitul above reason of a small child before a great man, which is the acceptance of the yoke on Rosh Hashanah (lines 409-411).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* From the thing to what surrounds it, and from what surrounds it to what is inside it. The mind first pictures the surrounding light as a sky above the world, then is made to turn the picture inside out: what "surrounds" is what holds this thing up from within. - *One line to hold* (line 352): > איהו תפיס בכולהו עלמין שנמצא בתוכם ופנימיותם והוא עיקר החיות שלהם > *He holds all the worlds: He is found within them and their inwardness, and He is their main life.* - *What opens inside.* The thing in front of you stops being a surface with God somewhere past it. It is held from inside and all around at once. The Rashab's own picture for "one" is the power of motion in a hand before it moves: gathered, with no parts yet (line 193). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At the vessel. The mind wants a vessel to hold what it has seen, and is told the vessel for the Essence is having none: דהעדר הכלי זהו כלי לעצמות אוא"ס (line 409). Past that is the child's bitul, which every Jew has, "because they are rooted in the Essence" (line 412). - *Step deepened:* 4, Held.

## 2. The name of the Essence, and both knowledges true (Rosh Hashanah to Vayigash 5673, lines 510-991)

**What it says.** The Essence cannot be named, not "Ein Sof" (line 383) and not even "Luminary" (line 542). ומה שאנו יכולים לכנות בעצמות הוא רק בשם בורא (line 543): the only name that fits is "Creator", because creation shows a being that is from itself, and the created thing is made "in the likeness of the Essence" (line 544). The winter turns to revelation. The Essence is not light (line 719); vessels make light shine by hiding it a little (line 720); Atzilut exists so that Godliness be revealed in the worlds (line 737), because creation alone is hiding. Souls, unlike stars, "are Godliness itself in their essence" (line 678). Then the charter of the two unities: the lower knowledge says below is "something" and above "nothing", the higher says the reverse, and הנה באמת מצד האוא"ס ב"ה שניהם אמת (line 794). Later in the range the Rashab says the lower unity "is only a likeness" (line 944), and of the Essence itself only silence is praise (line 965).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Holding two true sentences without letting either swallow the other. First "the world is something and He is hidden"; then "He is the true Being and the world is nothing"; then the turn: from His side, both. - *One line to hold* (line 794): > הנה באמת מצד האוא"ס ב"ה שניהם אמת, וז"ע אל דעות ה' דש' אל נושא ב' הדעות > *In truth, from the side of the Infinite Light, both are true. This is "a God of knowledges is Havayah": the > name El bears both knowledges.* - *What opens inside.* Relief. The world does not have to be argued away for Him to be everything, and He does not have to be pushed back for the world to be real. Comprehension itself opens onto what it cannot grasp: כ"א ההשגה היא אשר תכריח את השגת והרגש הבלתי מושג (line 781). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At praise. Every word is a word about the spreading. Of the Essence: ולך דומי' תהלה בבחי' ביטול בתכלית שאין בזה חילוקי מדרי' כלל (line 965), silence is praise, a bitul with no grades in it. - *Step deepened:* 5, Two knowings, and the hiding is His.

## 3. A ladder of contemplation, and the higher unity as a gift (Shavuot to Ki Tavo 5673, lines 992-1570)

**What it says.** The infinite light is revealed so that the worlds "be like the Essence, without existence" (line 1005), and this completes the dwelling below (line 1003). The world is not an effect of God: שאין העולם דבר בפ"ע כ"א הוא אלקות (line 1043), and ובאמת א"א לחלק כלל בין האין האלקי להיש (line 1109). Nature is God "drowned" in it (line 1127). "There is nothing outside His Essence" (line 1223), yet creatures feel themselves a "something", and that feeling too comes from His power of limit (line 1224). A tzimtzum that is willed is no tzimtzum (line 1402). Torah is itself the Essence (line 1432). The bitul of "there is nothing else" belongs precisely to a "something" (line 1458). And then the range's most sober teaching (lines 1548-1558): the higher knowledge is Ein Sof's own, "and how can the something grasp the nothing?" (line 1548). A creature can understand it, but holding it is given.

**Contemplated.** This is the one place in the index where a maamar lays out the whole ladder itself (chapter 150, lines 1179-1189), and the ladder is the Rashab's, not ours. - *The movement in the mind.* Step by step, in his order. A "something" cannot come from an endless chain of somethings, so it comes from the Godly nothing (line 1179). It is composite, so it cannot exist from itself (line 1180). It lives, so its life is Godliness (line 1184). All of that is only a radiance beyond comparison to the Essence (line 1188). And the Essence, apart from everything, is for that very reason below as above (line 1189). Chapter 151 then warns that the logic only "surrounds" the heart; what moves it is the Godly feeling, "for the divine soul by its essence is drawn to Godliness" (line 1193). - *One line to hold* (line 1184): > וכמו"כ יבין בעולם שרואה שהעולם חי הרי יש בו חיות המחי' אותו והחיות הוא אלקות > *So let him understand of the world: he sees that the world is alive, so there is a life in it that > gives it life, and the life is Godliness.* - *What opens inside.* The Rashab names it: sometimes bitterness, "that what is seen in the world is the complete opposite of the truth" (line 1187), and then the running, to be included in the Essence itself (line 1188). He adds that the bitterness belongs before prayer and the service itself is in joy (line 1187). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* Exactly where the ladder reaches the higher unity. The mind can say "all before Him is as nothing", and the Rashab says that is "only the understanding" (line 1551). The bitul of it is not in creatures of themselves, כ"א כאשר נותנים מלמע' ידיעה והשגה זו וביטול זה (line 1552), and it comes through the Torah (line 1554), even to souls of Atzilut (line 1556). - *Step deepened:* 3, Being said, right now (and it marks the edge of step 7).

## 4. The will that does not change, and the bitul that does not feel itself (Tishrei and Cheshvan 5674, lines 1571-1858)

**What it says.** Teshuvah is not God changing His mind; it reaches the essential delight above the will of the mitzvot (line 1585), "because Jewish souls are rooted in the Essence" (line 1586). The soul's bond with Him is a will that has no pleasure in it and never changes, "even amid many physical pleasures" (line 1649). The distance of sin is only outward: even an iron partition does not separate Israel from their Father (line 1642). The vessel for Godliness is bitul (line 1717). Incense is the inner heart that wants only to be included in the Essence (line 1733). True bitul does not feel itself (line 1744). The bond with Israel is "essence to essence" (line 1784), and the cause of the great hiding of exile is the essential love (line 1834).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Downward, under the feelings. Past what you enjoy in the service and what you do not, to the will that stays when enjoyment is gone. The test the Rashab gives is self-sacrifice: what you would not give up (line 1645). - *One line to hold* (line 1642): > ובאמת גם הריחוק הזה הוא רק בחיצוני' דהרי גם מחיצה של ברזל אינה מפסקת בין ישראל לאביהם שבשמים > *In truth this distance too is only outward, for even an iron partition does not separate Israel from > their Father in heaven.* - *What opens inside.* A floor that does not move. The incense prayer, "whom have I in heaven", is a wish with nothing in it but Him: כ"ז לא חפצתי רק להכלל בבחי' עצמות אוא"ס (line 1733). And the depth of the soul reaches the depth of the Infinite (line 1812). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At its own watching. The bitul that counts is וכ"ה בקדושה ביטול עצמי ופנימי הוא שאינו מרגיש כלל שהוא בטל (line 1744): it does not feel itself at all. Looking for it ends it. - *Step deepened:* 10, The heart's simple will.

## 5. Giving place, yachid above echad, and three kinds of "nothing besides Him" (Bamidbar to Nitzavim 5674, lines 1859-2558)

**What it says.** Existence begins with the tzimtzum (line 1892). Why can the exalted not bear another beside him? Not because the other exists, but because the exalted one is himself an existence. Essential exaltation has none, and before it "truly all is as nothing" (line 1894). The same exaltation gives everything its place, and the place is given, not owned (line 1916). So the higher unity is true while the worlds exist: they exist only because He gives them place, and in His knowledge they are not (line 1918); in the unity from above, "their existence is Godliness" (line 1919); this is the truth of "Havayah is one" (line 1920). Yachid is set above echad (lines 2004, 2006). Contemplation of "Havayah and Elokim are one" must return to the heart (line 2062). And the three levels: *ayin* (the light is nothing), *ein zulatcha* ("without You there is nothing, but with You there is"), *efes biltecha* ("even with the Essence there is nothing") (lines 2278, 2294).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* From "I am here" to "I am given a place". The mind watches a great person make room for a small one, and sees that the small one takes up no room of his own; then carries it up. - *One line to hold* (line 1916): > דזה שנותן לו מקום אין זה שהדבר תופס מקום אצלו, דבאמת אין הדבר מצ"ע תופס מקום כלל רק מה שהוא נותן לו מקום וערך > *That He gives it a place does not mean the thing takes up place with Him. In truth the thing, of itself, > takes up no place at all; only that He gives it place and worth.* - *What opens inside.* Less full of yourself, and not crushed: held in a place you did not have to earn. The Rashab asks that every soul, not only the high ones, try for the higher unity "as sight", as far as it can (line 2183). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At the third "nothing": ואפס בלתך הוא שגם עם העצמות אין שום מציאות כלל (line 2294). The mind can say it; it cannot stand there. The true bitul of existence belongs to the Da'at that faces inner Keter, "which is not a source at all" (line 2386). - *Step deepened:* 7, Before Him, nothing.

## 6. Four ways of coming-to-be, and one and many not opposites (Rosh Hashanah to Shemini Atzeret 5675, lines 2559-2785)

**What it says.** Part II opens by grading how a thing comes from its source: hidden to revealed, essence to spreading, power to act, and "something from nothing". Only the last is real renewal (line 2603), and it shows the Essence's power most, ובאמת ענין ההתהוות זהו רק בכח העצמות כידוע ומבו' באגה"ק סי' ך' (line 2619). The first tzimtzum too is for revelation (line 2578); without it the light meant for the worlds would not exist (line 2579). The name Elokim that hides is also Godliness (line 2680). Then the central point of the range: creation from Havayah through Elokim shows that oneness and multiplicity are not opposites (lines 2708, 2709). The shells are separate from His oneness, and so they are no thing: כי אלקות הוא דבר (איז א זאך) ומה שנפרד מאלקות אינו במציאות דבר כלל (line 2732).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Holding the many as many, and the One as one, and not letting either cancel the other. The four species in the hand, four kinds joined (line 2721), are the Rashab's own picture. - *One line to hold* (line 2709): > אלא בהכרח שהאחדות והריבוי אינם הפכים כלל > *Rather, necessarily, oneness and multiplicity are not opposites at all.* - *What opens inside.* The world stops being a distraction from the One. The Rashab calls it "the simple faith in every Jew in the oneness of God, that there is nothing separate from Godliness" (line 2730). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At the curtain. From it we know only that something above intellect exists (lines 2617-2618). The mind sees the many come from the One and cannot see how. - *Step deepened:* 8, Weeping and joy in one heart (the opposites that meet in Him).

## 7. Sitting and standing, hearing and seeing (Bereishit 5675 to Mishpatim 5675, lines 2786-3460)

**What it says.** "Havayah is one" is said of the light that relates to worlds; of the Essence one cannot even say "one", for He is yachid (line 2806). The Shema is still hearing (line 2832); sight is certainty that reaches even the deed (line 2835). The created "something" is itself something essential and originating (line 2932). Torah is called truth because the Essence is drawn in it "equally below as above" (line 2966), and in its words "you take Me" (line 2975). The essential point of the heart is always awake (line 2995), and the soul's essence is never in exile (line 2998). Two things grasp the Essence, Torah and the heart's will, and not equally (line 3042). Drawing from above comes only from the Essence's own desire, and needs no vessel (lines 3102, 3104). The Shema teaches that the worlds, *as made*, are nullified (line 3212); a person separates a thing only when he takes it as his own pleasure (line 3213). And again, "one" is below yachid (line 3319).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* The difference between hearing a thing from someone and seeing it yourself. The Shema is said sitting: the mind listens to the truth that He is one. The Amidah is said standing: the self goes quiet before what it sees (lines 2822, 2832-2835). - *One line to hold* (line 2832): > וכמו"כ ענין שמע ישראל כו' שההתבוננות איך ה' אחד הוא עדיין אצלינו בבחי' שמיעה כמו ששומע הענין הזה מזולתו ולא שהוא רואה בעיניו ממש > *So too "Hear, O Israel": the contemplation of how Havayah is one is still, for us, hearing, as one hears a > thing from someone else and does not see it with his own eyes.* - *What opens inside.* Honesty about where you are. The Rashab says even now the world shows Godliness "to one who pays attention" (line 3317), and the point of the heart is awake under everything (line 2995). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At the word "one". Above the chain of worlds, כי למעלה מהשתל' שמים וארץ לא שייך לו' עליו ית' ל' אחד כ"א יחיד שהוא מיוחד בעצם (line 3319). The mind that says "one" is still counting. - *Step deepened:* 9, Alone: where the mind stops.

## 8. The tzimtzum turned, and the face in the water (Terumah to Shavuot 5675, lines 3461-3941)

**What it says.** Every screen is two things: hiding from the giver's side, bitul from the receiver's (line 3512). The Essence is below as above, because nothing hides from it; the aim is that it be so "in our knowledge as in His" (line 3544), and only physical things, which are not vessels, hold it (line 3545). The tzimtzum does not reach the Essence (line 3646); the infinite light was not hidden by it but "hidden and included in its Luminary" (line 3648). The aim is not that the tzimtzum be cancelled, but that the tzimtzum itself not hide (line 3657), and existence stays, in utter bitul (line 3653). The Essence itself requires the many (line 3691); limit and the limitless are both the Essence, "or neither is" (line 3692). The face in the water is the face of the one who looks (line 3783), so the will to create is the Essence itself (line 3787), and the light after the tzimtzum is the light before it (line 3788). Both knowledges are in Godliness itself (line 3871). "One" is known only through evening and morning (line 3911), and two lines are "of the Essence's perfection" (line 3915).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Stop trying to get behind the cover. Look at the cover and let it be what it is for. The Rashab's picture: a face bending over water sees one face, its own (line 3783). - *One line to hold* (line 3657): > ולא שיתבטל הצמצום לגמרי כ"א שהצמצום גופא לא יסתיר ויעלים > *Not that the tzimtzum be cancelled entirely, but that the tzimtzum itself not hide and conceal.* - *What opens inside.* Tasting bitter as sweet: saying of the hard thing too "this also is for good", and then "there are no longer two flows" (line 3884). The lowest soul, bitter over its distance, draws the simple mercy that flows by nature to the lowly (line 3887). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At "His knowledge and ours". The mind can know that from His side nothing hides (line 3544). It cannot make its own knowledge His; that is the work, and the gift. - *Step deepened:* 5, Two knowings, and the hiding is His.

## 9. The vessel first, the end above the start, and the true "something" (Shavuot 5675 to Rosh Hashanah 5676, lines 3942-4471)

**What it says.** In the chain light comes before vessel; in the thought the vessel comes first, because the vessel is the aim (line 3962). But a vessel that is a "something" hides the light, the opposite of the aim (line 3963). Everything runs and returns; even Atzilut is only a glow (line 3978). The light changes nothing in the Essence: "I have not changed" (line 4007). The "something" lives from the nothing only by its bitul to it (line 4154). Upper Eden admits only one who is "nothing" in essence, not feeling himself (line 4107). The finished deed, well received, rises into the essence of delight (line 4362). Havayah and Elokim are "truly one" (line 4411). Then Rosh Hashanah 5676: whatever came into being is not true existence (line 4441), because it exists only by its source being hidden (line 4442). Yet only the created "something" proves the true Being (line 4454), the Essence's power is precisely in it (line 4455), and at that level its existence is true existence (line 4456). So a practical mitzvah done with acceptance of the yoke draws from the Essence (line 4456), and the shofar must be a deed, because the deed reaches the Essence (line 4457).

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Two verdicts on the same thing, a few lines apart, both the Rashab's. Look at a thing and see it is not truly there; look again and see that it, and only it, points to the One who truly is. - *One line to hold* (line 4454): > ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי > *So it is precisely the created "something" that proves the true Being.* - *What opens inside.* The deed stops looking small. The mind has no picture for it; the deed does not need one. - *Step deepened:* 13, The home below (and 14, the deed in the hand).

## 10. Two "nothings", and the discipline of knowing (Rosh Hashanah to Vayera 5676, lines 4472-4681)

**What it says.** The line of light has two ends, and so two "nothings": its essence is the nothing of the true Being, its outside the nothing of the created being (line 4474). "I have not changed": nothing differs before and after creation (line 4476). A creature exists only because it does not grasp its source (line 4479). The higher knowledge is the giver's (line 4482); the Essence itself does not come into the light (line 4493); and the true power of the Essence is in making the "something" (line 4504), so forgiveness comes only from the higher knowledge, where no blemish reaches (line 4515). The hiding is not a true hiding (line 4587). Knowing's end is to recognize the unknowable (line 4589), and the soul's essence recognizes the Essence "because it is its root" (line 4590). In the many hidings His providence is seen most (line 4618). What the Essence's "infinity" means is the negation of everything, of limit and of the limitless alike (line 4633). Seeing never sates (line 4655); to see a "something" is to see the power that makes it (line 4657). The higher knowledge is "a secret to the world" (line 4680).

**Contemplated.** Here the Rashab gives the discipline in so many words (lines 4664-4665). - *The movement in the mind.* Grasp the details at length. Reach the extract, the point of the matter. Stay on it until recognition comes. Then confirmation: not the sense that it is true, but the truth of it settling in the mind "as if seen with the eyes". - *One line to hold* (line 4665): > והתאמתות היא הנחה בשכל מה שלמעלה מן השכל לגמרי > *Confirmation is placing in the mind what is wholly above the mind.* - *What opens inside.* Lovesickness. What is grasped satisfies; what is seen does not, משא"כ בראי' מה שהוא רואה אלקות אינו מרווה צמאונו בשום ענין (line 4655). One who sees the King cannot turn his mind away (line 4667). - *Where the mind reaches its edge.* At its own goal: אך אמיתי' ענין ד"ע הוא כמא' תכלית הידיעה שלא נודע (line 4589), "the end of knowing is that we do not know". What recognizes there is not the mind but the soul's essence (line 4590). - *Step deepened:* 6, It is so: binding the mind until the heart answers.

## 11. The night that shines of itself (the written continuation, first half, lines 4682-5017)

**Contemplated.** - *The movement in the mind.* Not "let light come into the dark place", but "let the dark place itself shine". ## 12. Three sweetenings, and one in One (the written continuation, second half, lines 5018-5750)

The truth is the same now as it will be: דבאמת כן הוא גם עכשיו רק שעכשיו אין זה נרגש בהיש (line 5614). What is missing is not the fact but the feeling of it. ## What it comes to, for a person

This is the inner side of the series: what a person does with it when he sits, prays and acts. Each point is the Rashab's; where the application is ours, it says so.

- **Understanding it is not yet knowing it.** Anyone can follow the argument that the world is nothing before Him. The Rashab says that is "only the understanding" (line 1551). Knowing it means it settles in the mind, is felt, and makes you less. That part is given, through Torah (lines 1552-1556). So the honest posture is not "I have got it" but "I have heard it" (line 2832), and learning is where the gift comes.
- **Contemplation has a road, and its end is not the mind.** Details, then the extract, then recognition, then confirmation (lines 4664-4665). The feeling that moves the heart is not the argument but the Godly feeling in it (line 1193), and a contemplation done coldly stays in the head (line 5647). Its end is not a better thought: אך אמיתי' ענין ד"ע הוא כמא' תכלית הידיעה שלא נודע (line 4589), and what recognizes is the soul's essence, "because that is its root" (line 4590).
- **You are held in a place you did not earn.** The world, and you in it, take up no room of your own; you exist because He gives you place (lines 1916-1918). Said to oneself, this is not a verdict of worthlessness. It is the reason one need not defend one's place.
- **Having no vessel is the vessel.** The Essence comes where there is nothing to hold it: a small child before a great man, the yoke taken on Rosh Hashanah (lines 409-411), a broken heart (line 3942). The bitul that counts does not feel itself (line 1744).
- **Your will is already bound.** Beneath the feelings there is a will that never changes, even among many pleasures (line 1649), and an iron wall does not separate it from Him (line 1642). The essential point of the heart is always awake (line 2995), and simple will is in every Jew equally (line 3225).
- **The hiding is how He shows Himself.** The tzimtzum is for revelation (line 3924), and the aim is not an end to hiding but a hiding that no longer hides (line 3657). In the many hidings His providence is seen most (line 4618). The Rashab does not use this to explain anyone's suffering, and neither should we.
- **The hard place is not only to be overpowered.** Kindness that overpowers severity changes nothing in essence (line 5116). The deepest change is the hard thing sweetened in its own root, and only that completes His home (line 5160). In practice (ours): do not only push the bad trait down; ask what good it is the twisted form of.
- **The thing in your hand is where He shows most.** The created "something" proves the true Being (line 4454), His will is for the "something" itself (line 4713), and the deed reaches the Essence (line 4457). The Rashab also holds that Torah is the Essence (line 1432); see the disagreements.
- **One in One.** The last lines give the Shema's two halves one weight: the lower unity, the world as it is and one with Him, is a union just like the higher (line 5750). The world held is not a lesser form of the world erased.

## What it adds beyond Samach Vav and the other sources

The sources read before it are the nine of `FOUR-SOURCES.md` (the Tanya, the Gate of Unity, Strashelye, Sefer HaArachim, Imrei Binah, Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, Kuntres HaAvodah, Kuntres HaTefillah, Samach Vav), and the Piaseczner's books on method (`PIASECZNO.md`).

**Echad and yachid, defined in the Rashab's own words.** The Gate of Unity and Sefer HaArachim rank yachid above echad; Samach Vav gives a one-line gloss (Samach Vav line 5987). Ayin Beis defines both: echad is "the uniting of particulars", yachid is "one in essence, with no particular at all" (line 193), and it adds a reason: singleness itself denies existence, since whatever exists is by nature many (line 1371). Then it places the Shema: "one" is said of the light that relates to worlds, and of the Essence one cannot say even "one" (lines 2806, 3319, 5428). And it ends by joining them: "the lower unity is a union just like the higher" (line 5750).

**Only "Creator" names the Essence.** Samach Vav argued that "Ein Sof" names the light. Ayin Beis agrees (line 383) and goes one step further: not even "Luminary" fits, דהגם שהאור נמשך ממנו אין העצמות בבחי' מאור (line 542). The one name that fits is "Creator" (line 543), because creation shows a being from itself, and the created thing is made "in the likeness of the Essence" (line 544). The same formula Samach Vav gave for the Essence as the negation of limit and of the limitless (Samach Vav line 2253) returns here: ומה שעצמות הוא א"ס היינו שלילת הכל והוא שלילת הגבול ושלילת הבל"ג שלילת החיוב ושלילת השלילה (line 4633).

**The absence of a vessel is the vessel.** Samach Vav says the Essence rests in the practical mitzvah, "outside the category of a vessel" (Samach Vav line 2005). Ayin Beis says the same of the person: דהעדר הכלי זהו כלי לעצמות אוא"ס (line 409), the small child's bitul above reason, which every Jew has (lines 411-412).

**Both knowledges true, from Ein Sof's side.** The Tanya gives the higher and lower knowledge as two views of one truth. Ayin Beis says both are true from the Infinite's side (line 794), that both are "in Godliness itself" (line 3871), and at the end that in the Essence they are one (line 5738).

**The higher unity understood, but held only as a gift.** No source before it sets this limit. Samach Vav says contemplation sees Him "from behind" (Samach Vav line 3735). Ayin Beis says something different and more practical: a creature can understand the higher unity, but it cannot hold it, feel it, or be nullified by it from its own power; that comes from above, only to the divine soul, through the Written Torah (lines 1551-1556). Even souls of Atzilut need Torah for it (line 1556).

**The Torah as the Essence.** Samach Vav ranks the deed as what reaches the Essence and Torah as what reveals it. Ayin Beis says: דבאמת התו' גופא הוא בחי' עצמות א"ס (line 1432); even the wisdom of Torah, its knowing and grasping, is the Essence (line 1505); it is truth because the Essence is in it equally below as above (line 2966); in its words "you take Me" (line 2975). It holds the other side too (see the disagreements).

**Hiding as how revelation happens.** The Tanya: hiding serves revelation. Samach Vav: the hiding is the chief power and will shine (Samach Vav lines 2503, 2947). Ayin Beis explains the mechanism at every level. The Essence is not light, and vessels make light shine by hiding it (lines 719-720). Creation is hiding (line 737). The first tzimtzum is for revelation (line 2578). The Essence is felt through contraction, not spreading (line 2869). The worlds exist so that the light can be hidden and so received (line 3307). Absence comes before being (line 3939). The aim is the tzimtzum turned, not undone (lines 3657, 3664). And its last word: the hiding is the Infinite's power as much as the revealing, דבחי' ההסתר הוא כח הא"ס כמו כח הגילוי (line 5739).

**Why the higher unity is true while the world exists: giving place.** Tanya II has the sun and its ray. Ayin Beis adds a new analysis: essential exaltation has no existence of its own, and so gives place without being crowded (lines 1894, 1916-1920).

**The Essence requires the many.** The Tanya says the many are nullified before the One. Ayin Beis says the Essence's true simplicity itself requires multiplicity (line 3691), that one and many are not opposites (line 2709), and that limit is the Essence as much as the limitless, "or neither is" (line 3692). It rejects the Pardes's answer that division comes from the vessels (lines 3686-3690).

**"There is nothing else" belongs to the "something".** Samach Vav found "ein od" in physical "something" (Samach Vav line 5251). Ayin Beis argues it through the whole series: in the existence of the "something" (line 356), the bitul of a something that sets itself aside (line 1458), the true power of the Essence in making the "something" (line 4504), the "something" itself becoming nothing (line 5237).

**Three sweetenings.** The Tanya has itkafia and ithapcha. Ayin Beis gives three: kindness overpowering severity (itkafia), light coming in its place, and severity sweetened of itself; only the third completes the dwelling (lines 5114-5160).

**The vessel above the light, and the vessel turned to light.** Vessels come from the Essence, lights only from "Ein Sof" (line 4920); the goal is the vessel itself turned to light (line 4977); in the future the body will give life to the soul (line 4957).

**A method of knowing.** Hearing grasps existence and sight grasps essence (line 2180); the Shema is hearing (line 2832); every contemplation should reach for sight (line 2183); details, extract, recognition, confirmation (lines 4664-4665). The Kuntres HaAvodah and Samach Vav name the route (negation, wonder, recognition, seeing); Ayin Beis gives the inside of each step and says what the last one is.

## With Samach Vav (the same author, six years earlier)

- **Torah or the deed.** Samach Vav: the Essence is drawn "precisely through the practical mitzvot" (Samach Vav line 2005), and the heart's will grasps it: אבל ברעו"ד נתפס בחי' העצמות (Samach Vav line 897). Ayin Beis: Torah itself is the Essence (line 1432), Torah is above time and the mitzvot are bound to it (line 1373), and of the two that grasp the Essence, דתורה תופסת בבחי' פנימיות ועצמות א"ס ממש, ורעו"ד תופסת רק בבחי' החיצוניות דאוא"ס ב"ה (line 3042). Yet Ayin Beis also has Samach Vav's side: the drawing of the Essence is by the physical act (line 128), the deed reaches the Essence (line 4457), and souls, and teshuvah, are above Torah (lines 4857, 4860). And in the written continuation the mitzvot draw only outer light while Torah is the light itself (line 4995). Kept as the Rashab keeps it: the two books weigh the pair differently, and each book holds both sides.
- **Itkafia or ithapcha.** Samach Vav: the simple servant who subdues his nature without changing it gives great pleasure above, שלא נהפך טבעיות שלו כלל, רק שמכניע הלב ועוצר רוחו וכובש יצרו בע"כ, שנק' לאכפי' לסט"א לבד, יש בזה נח'"ר גדול למעלה (Samach Vav line 3935), and the servant with no self reaches highest. Ayin Beis opens the same way, וע"כ באמת ביטול הכלים מגיע למעלה מביטול האורות (line 71), the itkafia of the vessels. But it then calls itkafia "only" what the divine soul does to the animal soul, and ithapcha the gift from above (line 443); and in the written continuation itkafia is the first and lowest sweetening, which changes nothing in essence (line 5116), while only severity sweetened in its root completes the dwelling (line 5160). Not reconciled in the lines.
- **What knowing reaches.** Samach Vav: no grasp reaches the Essence, not even by negation (Samach Vav line 876). Ayin Beis agrees that "contemplation does not reach the Essence" (line 1444), yet has negation yield real recognition (line 785), great scholars hold what is above grasp "not only by faith" (line 1085), and confirmation places in the mind what is above it (line 4665). The same tension as the SPINE's F12 note, with the weight moved toward knowing.
- **Which unity is higher.** Samach Vav: the union of the two above the higher alone (Samach Vav line 2019). Ayin Beis inside itself: the lower unity "is only a likeness" (line 944), and at the end "just like the higher" (line 5750). Its last word agrees with Samach Vav.
- **Where the power to create sits.** Samach Vav reads Iggeret HaKodesh 20 as creation from the light (Samach Vav lines 2273, 2302). Ayin Beis cites the same letter for the opposite: creation "only by the power of the Essence" (line 2619; also lines 1180, 1217). It also says the worlds come "only from Your kingship, not from the King's essence" (line 2543), and that the light creates only by its cleaving to the Essence (line 5400). Kept.

## Inside the series (kept, not smoothed)

- **Is the "something" true?** "Not true existence" (line 4441) and "true existence" at the level where the Essence's power is (line 4456), fifteen lines apart.
- **Does the tzimtzum hide?** Toward the Essence nothing hides (lines 3544, 5536); yet revealing the Essence would nullify existence (line 3653), and the worlds exist only because the Creator is hidden (line 5724). The Rashab's answer: hiding is real toward the worlds and nothing toward the Essence (line 5738).
- **One "nothing" or two?** If nothing hides, there is one "nothing" and no creation (line 5740). Two drawings, joined before the tzimtzum and split by it (lines 5741-5747); and then, toward the Essence, all is "as in potential" (line 5748). His own tension.
- **Can a low soul have the higher unity?** Every divine soul, "even the lowest", is nullified like a candle before a torch (line 1558); souls of Asiyah lack the higher unity's bitul (line 1559).
- **Outer Torah: bound or one?** Only its inner delight is "all one" (lines 1489, 1503); then even outer Torah is one, because its wisdom is the Essence (line 1505).
- **Torah above Israel, or Israel above Torah?** Both midrashim affirmed (lines 5464, 5476).
- **Comprehension or sight?** Future comprehension of the essence above sight (lines 1851-1853); sight "far above comprehension" (lines 2248-2250).
- **Gift or work?** Manna, Shabbat's inner delight and the higher knowledge are gifts (lines 1552, 4266); yet "if he had not given Him pleasure He would not have given" (line 4270).
- **Loose ends the Rashab marks himself.** A missing explanation "in the author's manuscript" (line 1569); a Pardes passage he cannot find (line 5412); a reference not yet checked (line 5102); a reading that "does not fit what was said above" (line 5162); a point to square with Tanya 37 (line 5663).

## Where the thread is thin (kept honest)

- **The contemplative layer is ours in its arrangement.** No line-by-line meditative reading of Ayin Beis exists yet. The instructions quoted in each movement are the Rashab's (lines 1179-1193, 1551-1556, 1744, 2062, 2183, 2832-2835, 3213, 3234-3235, 3884, 4655-4667, 5084, 5647-5654). The choice of one line per movement, the description of "what opens inside" where no line is cited, and the pairing of each movement with a step of `ONE-PROCESS.md` are ours.
- **The Essence overcounted.** Every range report says F05 overstates how often the Divine Essence itself is the subject: many tags mark "the essence of the soul", "the essence of the light" or "essential delight" inside a parable the Rashab carries upward himself.
- **Readers differ.** The first range (lines 1-587) was read most strictly: 34% stated, 366 structural, and F12 on only 27 lines. Later ranges run 42-66% stated. Part of the rise across the series is the text (the written continuation's long paragraphs reach their point), and part is the readers. The kinds were not re-balanced.
- **25 lines are our reading only:** 299 (the oil that does not mix, heard as the Rebbe's later image for the Essence), 653, 658, 885, 1102, 1935 (opposites), 705, 1359 (bitul), 810, 811, 903, 1057, 1151, 1186, 1906 (the soul), 1034, 1160, 1864, 2442, 2464, 2465, 3914, 5351, 5352, 5594, and one sentence (3491.1).
- **Long structural runs.** The soul-parables (the thrown stone, Hillel and Shammai, the teacher and student, the face and the king), the psychology of the emotions (lines 4734-4791), and the Kabbalistic mechanics (Adam Kadmon, Akudim, the fillings of the Name, the atbash exchange) are marked structural; the tie comes at their ends.
- **Labels.** Chapter numbers were read by script from the series' own markers and checked to run in sequence; from Terumah 5675 on there are none. The file's own section labels sometimes name the wrong parsha (the Shoftim and Ki Tetze maamarim of 5674 labelled Re'eh; the Pinchas maamar of 5675 labelled Balak); the rows name the maamar from its heading. The written continuation has no headings, and its rows name the verse under discussion.
- **Gaps.** The winter of 5674 is not in the series (lines 1819-1859). The written continuation is undated in the text read.
- **Verification.** In one range (1168-1742) the `opens` fields were copied by script from the file rather than each verified; every `quote` in all ten ranges was verified.
