This is chapter 4 of Pirkei Avot in the Maharal's commentary. It holds Ben Zoma's "who is wise, strong, rich, honored," the call to be "very, very lowly of spirit," the three crowns and the crown of a good name, "this world is a corridor," and the last mishna: "those born are to die, and the dead to live."

Five teachings matter most for the guide:

- **None judges alone but the One.** A person must not judge alone, "because He is One, apart from all beings, and has no partner" (831). Being alone belongs only to Him.
- **People are parts; only He joins them.** "כי בני אדם מצד עצמם הם פרטים מחולקים" (871): "people, from their own side, are separate parts." A gathering lasts only "because He joins the whole, and binds and unites the whole" (871).
- **Moving toward Him is being with Him.** One hour of return in this world is better than all the World to Come, because "כי התנועה אל הדבר כאילו הוא אחד עמו" (961): "moving toward a thing is as if one is one with it."
- **Return makes a person one with Him.** "כי על ידי תשובה ומעשים טובים נעשה האדם עם השם יתברך אחד לגמרי" (1058): "through return and good deeds a person becomes fully one with G-d," and there is no judgment on one who is one with Him.
- **He is all, so all is from Him.** "כי אין מצד הבריאה עצמה כלום, רק הכל מן השם יתברך, כי הוא יתברך הכל" (1059): "for from the side of the creature itself there is nothing; all is from G-d, for He is all."

## 2.1 Only the One judges alone

"Do not judge alone, for none judges alone but One." The Maharal reads this as a teaching on His oneness. He alone may judge alone "מפני שהוא אחד נבדל מכל הנמצאים, ואין לו שתוף עם אחר" (831): "because He is One, apart from all beings, and has no partner." A person is not one in that way. He is bound up with others, so "two are better than one." To insist "accept my view" is to act as if one were the One.

## 2.2 People are parts; He is what joins them

"Every gathering for the sake of Heaven will last." Why? "כי בני אדם מצד עצמם הם פרטים מחולקים" (871): "people, from their own side, are separate parts," and a gathering of parts would come apart. It lasts "רק מצד כי הוא יתברך מחבר הכלל והוא מקשר ומאחד הכלל" (871): "only because He joins the whole, and binds and unites the whole." The word for gathering, knesiyah, ends with the Name Yah (873).

## 2.3 The 613 are one light

"שהתורה היא אחת" (745): "for the Torah is one." Each mitzvah is a candle. In the Torah "שהתחברו הנרות ונעשו אור אחד גדול" (745): "the candles joined and became one great light." And "כי כל דבר שהוא אחד אינו נחלק כלל" (746): "whatever is one is not divided at all." So one mitzvah pulls the next: it is half of a whole that wants to be whole.

## 2.4 The name is the essence

"כי השם הוא גלוי של בעל השם" (778): "the name is the showing of the one who bears the name." And "כי השם הוא בא על מהות הדבר ועצמו שבו הוא נבדל מזולתו" (779): "the name is given to the essence of a thing, the self by which it stands apart from others." Desecrating His Name touches the level above the body, where there is no "by mistake"; that is why intent and error are alike there (768-781). His Name is His self shown, "אשר כבודו מלא העולם" (766): "whose glory fills the world."

## 2.5 A perfect Maker and a world that begins lacking

"כי כפי מדריגת הפועל, כך הוא פעולתו" (949): "as is the level of the maker, so is his work." Then why is this world a corridor, and not the hall itself? Because the world is caused: "שהרי היא נמצאת אחר שלא היתה" (952): "for it exists after it was not." What comes from not-being starts with lack, like a child who grows. This world is where the lack is filled.

## 2.6 He is all, so all is His

Why can no one hide from Him? "כי אם היו הם מסולקים מאתו, ולא היה הכל שלו, היה שייך בזה העלמה" (1049): "if they were removed from Him and all were not His, there could be hiding." Why can He take no bribe? The word for bribe (shochad) is read as becoming one (chad) with the judge. He cannot be bribed, because all is His already. And the last line of the chapter: "כי אין מצד הבריאה עצמה כלום, רק הכל מן השם יתברך, כי הוא יתברך הכל" (1059): "for from the side of the creature itself there is nothing; all is from G-d, for He is all." "ומאחר שהוא הכל, לכך הכל הוא ממנו יתברך" (1059): "and since He is all, all is from Him."

## 2.7 One with Him through return

The only "bribe" He takes is return and good deeds, because they make a person one with Him: "כי על ידי תשובה ומעשים טובים נעשה האדם עם השם יתברך אחד לגמרי" (1058): "through return and good deeds a person becomes fully one with G-d." "ואין הדין על מי שהוא דבק בו יתברך ונעשה עמו אחד לגמרי" (1058): "and there is no judgment on one who cleaves to Him and becomes fully one with Him." This matches the mikveh teaching in the Drashot (DM 139).

- **MH-S37 "Moving toward is being with" (959-961).** Inside: I am far from where I should be, and I am turning. In the turning itself I am already with Him, more than if I were resting in a reward. How to enter: in a moment of return, stop asking "how far am I?" Say: I am moving toward Him, and so I am with Him (961). It differs from S48 Returning, which is the act of coming back. This state is the closeness felt in the motion, before arriving. It also differs from MH-S32 Still becoming, which is peace with being unfinished; here the stress is on Him as the one I move toward.
- **MH-S38 "The treasure of the free gift" (935).** Inside: something good has come that I did not earn, from His side alone, and I let it be that. I do not turn it into a wage. How to enter: when good comes, do not ask "what did I do to deserve it?" Say: this is from the treasure of the free gift, from His side alone. It differs from S65 Given right now (the gift of this moment). This is the knowing that a gift is not a wage, which the Maharal calls the greatest treasure.

## 4.1 Despise no one (`mh_each_has_an_hour`, new)

"אין לך אדם שאין לו שעה ואין לך דבר שאין לו מקום" (754): "there is no person who has no hour, and no thing that has no place." Each person "כל אחד משלים אל העולם" (754): "each one completes the world," as each mitzvah completes the one Torah. Practice: when you look down on someone, say: he has an hour, and he completes the world.

## 4.2 Very, very lowly (`mh_lowly_to_the_end`, new)

"צריך שיהיה שפל בתכלית, עד שאי אפשר יותר" (762): "he must be lowly to the end, until no more is possible," and lowly before every person, even the lowest. "אין מדה נבדלת מן הגשמית רק הענוה" (852): "no trait is separate from the bodily except humility." Guard: this lowliness is for doing, not for despair (see DC 434 in the 420-717 report).

## 4.3 Honor others to be honored (`mh_share_honor`, new)

"שהמכובד חולק כבוד לאחר" (735): "the honored one shares honor with another." G-d is "King of glory" because He shares glory. "המתחבר אל הכבוד, מקבל הכבוד" (817): "one who joins honor receives honor." Practice: each day, honor one person on purpose, in word or deed.

## 4.4 Do not judge alone (`mh_not_alone_in_judgment`, new)

Only the One judges alone (831). Practice: before a hard decision about another person, ask counsel. When you feel "accept my view," stop: that belongs to Him.

## 4.5 The mitzvah goes in before Him (`mh_advocate`, new)

A mitzvah is an advocate: "והטוב הוא נכנס לפני השם יתברך" (862): "and the good itself goes in before G-d." A sin can put out a mitzvah, but not the Torah, "כשם שאין כבוי לאור" (858): "just as light cannot be put out." Practice: after a mitzvah, picture the good you did going in before Him on your behalf.

## 4.6 Return is a shield (`mh_return_shield`, new)

"שהתשובה נבראת קודם שנברא העולם" (864): "return was created before the world was created." Return and good deeds join a person to a level above the world, "ובזה יש לאדם חוזק בלתי מקבל התפעלות כלל" (864): "and by this a person has a strength that cannot be moved at all." Practice: when afraid, turn in return and do one good deed, and stand on that strength.

## 4.7 Greet first (`mh_greet_first`, new)

"הפותח בשלום, אצל זה נמצא השלום בעצם" (942): "one who opens with peace has peace in its very self." Greet every person first, even one you do not like (944). This is pursuing peace in the smallest way.

## 4.8 One hour of return (`mh_move_toward`, new)

"ואשר הוא מתנועע אל דבר הוא נחשב עמו לגמרי ביותר" (959): "one who is moving toward a thing counts as fully with it." Practice: set aside a short time for return. Do not measure the distance. Turn, and know you are with Him in the turning (961). This is MH-S37.

## 4.9 Wait for the calm (`mh_wait_for_calm`, new)

Do not try to calm a person while he is angry, or comfort him while his dead lies before him. "כל מי שמתגבר בדבר אחד, אם בא אדם כנגדו לבטלו מזה, הוא מוסיף" (973): "whoever is gripped by one thing, if someone comes against him to stop it, he adds to it." Practice: be there, say little, and speak when the wave has passed.

## 4.10 No joy at a fall (`mh_no_joy_at_fall`, new)

"שאין השם יתברך שמח במפלתן של רשעים" (980): "G-d does not rejoice at the fall of the wicked." "ואין שמחה לכל דבר שהוא קלקלה" (980): "there is no joy in anything that is ruin." And "כי השמח ברע הוא חפץ ברע" (985): "one who rejoices at bad wants bad." Practice: when an enemy falls, say: I want the evil to end, not the person to be ruined.

## 4.11 Envy, desire and honor as one (`mh_one_wanting`, new)

Three powers take a person out of the world: envy, desire and honor (1004-1017). "כי כל תוספות הוא העדר וחסרון" (1009): "for all excess is absence and lack." Of envy: "כי למה אל האדם לקנאות על דבר שאינו שלו" (1010): "why should a person envy what is not his?" All three come from one wanting, "כמו שהנפש היא אחת, כוללת שלשה כחות המחולקים" (1022): "as the soul is one, holding three separate powers." Practice: when one of the three stirs, name the wanting under it. Then say "תמים תהיה עם ה' אלהיך" (1017): "be whole with the L-rd your G-d," and turn the wanting to Him.

## 5. The big human questions

**Who am I, really?** A name is the essence, the self by which one stands apart (779). The good name, earned by deeds, is above all crowns (898, 911).

**Why is the world unfinished?** Because it is caused: it exists after it was not (952), so it starts lacking and grows. That is why this world is a corridor (945-954).

**Why can't I rest?** Because here all being is becoming, and being that is not at rest is sorrow (966). Rest is in the World to Come (965). But the moving toward Him is itself being with Him (961).

**Can I be close to G-d after I have sinned?** Yes. Return was made before the world (864). Through return a person becomes fully one with Him, and there is no judgment on one who is one with Him (1058).

**Why be humble before everyone?** Because each person has his hour (754), and humility is the only trait that is apart from the body (852).

**Should I be glad when my enemy falls?** No. G-d is not glad at the fall of the wicked (980). Want the end of evil, not the ruin of a person.

**Must a good thing be earned?** No. The greatest treasure is the free gift, from His side alone (935).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **"He is all"** (1059). Here the Maharal says it plainly: from the creature's side there is nothing; all is from Him, for He is all. This is close to Gate of Unity ch. 3 and 6 (nothing but Him; the creature is as nothing before Him). The Maharal's step is from "He is all" to "all is from Him"; he does not say the creature is an illusion.
- **The Ramak's formula.** Not stated, but 1059 and 1049 come near its first half ("He is all"). The second half ("all is not He") is carried by 871: people from their own side are separate parts; only He joins them.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not in this range. The corridor teaching (952) gives the Maharal's own reason for a lacking world: the caused starts after not-being. Chabad would add that this lack is the hiding of tzimtzum. They agree that the purpose is to fill it in this world.
- **The Rambam's attributes.** "He is One, apart from all beings, and has no partner" (831) is close to the Rambam. But the Maharal speaks freely of His not rejoicing (980) and His wanting (1058). He does not keep to negation.
- **Return before the world** (864) matches Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah ch. 4 and on: return reaches a level above the world and above the sin. **One with Him through return** (1058) meets Tanya ch. 29-31 and the teaching of the "essential bond."
- **Moving toward is being with** (961) is close to the Chabad teaching of the baal teshuvah, whose longing from far away is stronger than the tzaddik's (Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah ch. 7-9). The Maharal grounds it in motion: to move toward is to be with.
- **Humility, the only separate trait** (852), meets Tanya ch. 30 on being lowly before every person, and Chabad's bittul.
