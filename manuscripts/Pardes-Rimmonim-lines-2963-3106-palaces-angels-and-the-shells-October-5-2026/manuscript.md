# Pardes Rimmonim, lines 2963-3106: palaces, angels, and the shells

*Range:* lines 2963-3106: Gate 24 (Heichalot, the palaces), Gate 25 (Temurot, the shells), Gate 26 (Heichalot HaKelipot, the palaces of the shells)

---
These three gates move from God's own levels down to the worlds below them. Gate 24 describes the seven palaces of holiness, with their angels and gates. Gate 25 asks where evil comes from and why it exists. Gate 26 describes the seven palaces of the "other side". The Ramak says plainly that most of this is copied, not his own. The palaces carry "no new matter from the side of contemplation at all" (ואין בענין ההיכלות האלו ענין מחודש מחלק העיון כלל, line 2964). Of the names of the shells he says: "apart from this we have no part and no knowledge in it" (ובזולת זה אין לנו בו חלק ולא ידיעה, line 3075). The angel names, gate-keepers and demons are **not for the guide**, and this report leaves them out.

But in chapters 10 to 12 of Gate 24 and chapters 1 to 3 of Gate 25 he thinks for himself, and these are among his clearest pages on the unity. One light passes through four panes of glass. A lamp burns in the middle of a great hall. Grain is clean before it is eaten. There is the parable of the harlot sent by the king. And the evil urge can itself become good.

## 2. The unity of God in this range

**One light, four panes.** The whole of existence "from the upper point to the lower point is like a ray of the sun that passes through a window with four panes of glass" (כמשל אל נצוץ השמש העובר דרך החלון ולו ד' עששיות, line 3015). One pane is clear, one less clear, one a little cloudy, one fully cloudy: the four worlds. "The light that flows is the light of Ein Sof, the King of kings, the root essence that spreads and shines to the last point" (עצם השורש המתפשט ומאיר עד הנקודה האחרונה, line 3017). "And the light did not change, except in relation to the receivers" (והנה האור לא נשתנה אלא בערך המקבלים, line 3018). It is the same line he gave on the sefirot (line 2445), now carried all the way down.

**Many windows, one lamp.** A great lamp burns in a hall with many narrow windows. The rays look many, but "if the windows were made one, the light would be one" (שאם יתיחדו הארובות יתיחד האור, line 3018). The many angels are only many openings. The soul teaches the same thing from the body: "the soul is not a thing divided into parts at all" (כי אין הנשמה עצם מתחלק לחלקים כלל, line 3018). It seems divided only because the body has limbs. This is why the palaces are united in prayer. It is not for the angels' sake but for the one light clothed in them.

**Nothing is without life.** Everything flows down from God, rung by rung, "until you will not find in this world a thing without some spirituality and life" (עד שלא תמצא בעה"ז דבר שלא יהיה בו רוחניות וחיות קצת, line 3005). Stones and water also have it, each in its measure. Fire proves it: "it will not rest or be still until it rises to its root" (לא ישקוט ולא ינוח עד עלותו אל יסודו, line 3006). The closer a thing comes to the lamp, the more it shines: "the parable of a great lamp burning in the middle of the hall" (המשל אל עששית גדולה דולקת באמצע הבירה, line 3006). And so, he adds, "the pious were careful not to speak badly of any created thing" (ולכן היו נזהרים החסידים שלא לספר בגנות שום א' מהנבראים, line 3002), because all of it came from a high place.

**No evil above.** Where does evil come from, if everything comes from the pure? The Ramak answers from his teacher with grain. "Before it was eaten, the food was cleaner" (לפי מציאת המזון קודם אכילתו היה הדבר הנאכל ההוא יותר נקי, line 3048). The waste appears only in digestion, as the good is separated out. So "above, in the place of Atzilut, no evil thing comes down from heaven" (כי למעלה במקום האצילות אין דבר רע יורד מן השמים, line 3049). Evil is the leftover of the descent. It is not a second source. He warns hard: one who thinks the shells enter Atzilut "owes his head to his Maker" (כי החושב זאת חייב ראשו לקונו, line 3076). "Above, in the Tree of Life, there are no shells" (לעילא באילנא דחיי לית קליפין, line 3076).

**Change is not in Him.** "Changes are not in Him, blessed be He" (כי ענין השנויים אינם בו יתב', line 3014). Judges, scribes, witnesses and punishers belong to the lower worlds, "close to the deeds of men". He does not change. We see Him through changing glass.

**The accuser does the King's will.** The Zohar's parable: a king wants to test his beloved son and sends a beautiful harlot to tempt him. If the son refuses, the father brings him into the inner palace with gifts. Does the harlot deserve praise? Yes, from every side: "rather, surely, she does her Master's will" (אלא ודאי רעותא דמאריה עביד, line 3057). "Happy are those who met him and were saved from him" (זכאין אינון דאערעו ביה ואשתזיבו מניה, line 3057). So the Ramak concludes that "the shell is a need of the Most High, fitting for the world, fitting for the righteous" (כי הקליפה צורך גבוה נאה לעולם נאה לצדיקים, line 3058). Even the other side is not outside His rule.

## 3. States of consciousness

**Tested by the King's own messenger (RK-S22, proposed).** From the harlot parable (line 3057). Inside: the pull toward wrong is real and sweet. But I know who sent it. It is the King testing how I love Him, and if I push it away He brings me in closer than before. How to enter: when temptation comes, say inside, "You were sent. I will not go", and feel the door to the inner room open. Near S10 (Torn in two). What is new is that the struggle itself is inside His love and not against it. The Tanya uses the same parable twice (see §6).

**Counted though I could not (RK-S23, proposed).** In the palace of Nogah there are watchers who look "also on those who thought thoughts of a mitzvah, though it did not come into their hands because they could not" (וכן על אותם שחשבו מחשבות מצוה עם היות שלא עלה בידם מפני שלא יכלו, line 2975). Others look "on those who comfort the poor, or pain themselves over them, though they have nothing to give" (line 2975), and "on those who thought to do evil and conquered their urge and did not" (line 2975). In the palace of Merit they watch "deeds done with a good heart and good intent, even though they are not done as they should be" (וכן משגיחים באותם המעשים הנעשים בלב טוב ובכונה טובה ואע"פ שאינם במעשה כראוי, line 2978). Inside: I wanted to do the good and could not, or did it badly. It is still seen and written. Nothing true in me is lost. How to enter: at the end of a day, name the good you meant and could not do, and hand it up. It answers S11 (The verdict: I'm my mistakes), and it sits near S54 (Compassion for the soul).

**Friday evening (named).** In the palace of Love, four keepers come down with the added soul on Friday evening "and take from all Israel all anger and all worry and all sadness, and gladden them with joy" (ומעבירים מכל ישראל כל רוגז וכל דאגה וכל עצבות ומשמחין אותם בשמחה, line 2979). On Shabbat the river of fire goes quiet: "a herald goes out and calms the fire and its tremblings, and the whole world is at rest" (כרוז יוצא ומשכך האש והרעשותיו וכל העולם בנחת, line 2978). This is S76 (Shabbos) with a specific content: the three things that are taken away are anger, worry and sadness.

**Love from below fills the palace (named).** "When they wake love from below, this palace is filled with many goods" (וכאשר מעוררים האהבה מלמטה אז מתמלא ההיכל הזה מכמה טובות, line 2979). With it: the righteous souls are "mayin nukvin", the waters from below that wake the union (line 3079). Ties to S41 (The hidden love wakes). Our love is not only received above. It is what fills the palace.

**The joy of leaving.** "If all the righteous knew this, they would rejoice on the day they are taken from this world" (אלו הוו ידעין דא כלהו צדיקייא הוו חדאן ההוא יומא דמטא לון לאסתלקא מהאי עלמא, line 3032), "for the Holy One delights only in the souls of the righteous". A ground for S84 (Trust) in the face of death.

**Wound and healing held together.** Gavriel's blows are "sufferings of love", "for blow and healing are bound to each other" (כי מכה ורפואה אחוזים זה בזה, line 2980). And Rafael "reminds Heaven for healing of all the sick who were forgotten in their sickness" (line 2982). This is near S49 (Weeping and joy in one heart), applied to illness.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Lift what you eat (RK-T23, proposed).** The Ramak traces a ladder. Earth rises into the plant, the plant into the animal, and "when a man eats the meat, the meat rises to a finer level" (וכאשר האדם יאכל הבשר ההוא יעלה הבשר אל מדרגה משובחת, line 3011), close to the soul lit by the King of kings. "No grass below lacks an angel above that strikes it and says: grow" (אין לך עשב מלמטה שאין לו מלאך מלמעלה ומכה אותו ואומר לו גדל, line 3010). The warning is sharp: "for this reason an ignoramus may not eat meat" (ולזאת הסבה עם הארץ אסור לאכול בשר, line 3011), because he cannot raise it. What it is for: making eating part of the unity. How: before eating, remember you are the next rung for this food. Eat with a blessing and with the intent to use its strength for good. Afterward, do one good thing with that strength. Guard: it is not a diet rule. It is also not a license. He who cannot lift the great should lift the small ("whoever can live on barley bread...").

**Return while the nut is soft (RK-T24, proposed).** The husk of the nut is easy to peel "when the nut is soft", and hard when it is dry. So God tells a person "to return in repentance in his youth, before the evil urge grows old in him" (לאחזרא בתיובתא בבחרותיה קודם דיצר הרע יזקין ביה, line 3076). What it is for: habits that are still forming. How: when you catch a new fault, turn from it that day, before it dries into who you are. For an older person the same rule applies to anything new: return now, before the shell hardens. Guard: this is not a verdict on old habits. It is a push to act while acting is easy.

**The evil urge becomes good.** Not only can it be beaten: "surely the evil urge is purified and becomes a good urge" (ובודאי שיצה"ר נטהר ונעשה יצ"ט, line 3060), like the firstborn donkey redeemed with a lamb. When the upright person brings his urge into a mitzvah, "he returns it to holiness". The wine that makes drunk becomes the wine that gladdens. The reason it can be done is that "by the actions below, through the branches, the roots act" (כי בפעולות התחתונות בסוד הענפים יפעלו השרשים, line 3059). This extends RK-T22 (Draw the rind near) and belongs with "binding" and "act".

**Grind the words.** The third husk sticks to the kernel like bran to wheat, and is separated only by grinding: "one must grind with them the words of Torah until they are like fine flour" (דצריך למטחן בהון מילי דאורייתא עד דיהון כקמח סלת נקייה, line 3076). Then the soul is fed as the body is fed by bread. This fits "chazarah": grind the same passage until it is flour.

**Tears and the healing gate.** "All gates were locked, and the gates of tears were not locked" (כל השערים ננעלו ושערי דמעה לא ננעלו, line 2967). There is also a gate of healing for the prayers of the sick (line 2975). This goes with RK-T20 (The heart's cry).

**Guard the mouth before holy words.** Foul words are held, and when the person later says holy words, "they defile the holy words with the impurity he let out of his mouth before" (line 3092). Things thrown in anger are taken up as "an offering to us" (line 3092). This is a guard for "lips": clean the mouth before prayer.

**Kindness in a hard year.** "When there are doers of kindness in Israel in a time of famine" (וכאשר יש בישראל גומלי חסדים בזמן הרעב, line 3097), the powers of ruin do not rule. Kindness is not only a virtue. It is protection. This fits RK-T16 (Become the chariot of the trait you practice).

## 5. The big human questions

**Why is there evil at all?** For free choice. If all were like angels, "bound in their deeds", there would be no reward. So a person is made of two urges and the tempter is sent (line 3057). The shell is "a need of the Most High" ( line 3058). And there is a second reason. The first man spread the damage, and so his children are the ones who repair it by making the good urge rule (lines 3059-3060).

**Does evil touch God?** No. Evil is the leftover of the descent, as waste is the leftover of clean grain (line 3048). "There are no shells in the Tree of Life" (line 3076). Where the Zohar seems to say the snake reached the Shechinah, the Ramak explains that it happens only in the outer world of Metatron, "and the shells never enter holiness" ( line 3082). "After the great anger, the end is mercy and favor" (ולכן תמצא כי אחר הרוגז הגדול סופו רחמים ורצון, line 3081).

**Am I lower than the angels?** No. An angel is a vessel of light and nothing more. A human has a soul that, when it rules the body, "does not need to receive through any of the levels, but through itself" (ואינו מצטרך לקבל ע"י שום אחת מהמדרגות אלא ע"י עצמו, line 3012). "On the contrary, the angels draw flow from the righteous" (ואדרבה המלאכים שואפים שפע מהצדיקים, line 3011). The angels of song "are not seen until Israel below open and say the song" (עד דישראל דלתתא פתחי ואמרי שירתא, line 2991).

**Can I hide?** From the destroyer, a person can hide at home. "But from before the Holy One one cannot hide" (אבל מקמי קב"ה לא יכיל לאסתתרא, line 3041). This cuts both ways: nothing bad is hidden, and nothing good is lost (RK-S23).

**What does bowing do?** "Bowing is drawing down the flow" (והכוונה כי ההשתחויה היא המשכת השפעה, line 3027). When the creatures came to bow to Adam, he sent them past himself: "Come, let us bow before the Lord our Maker". Bowing is an opening, not flattery.

**Is it only for Jews?** In the palace of the other side there is "a sweet place for the pious of the nations who did no harm to Israel" (והוא מקום מתוק לחסידי אומות העולם שלא הרעו לישראל, line 3099). There is honor for kings who protected the weak. The Ramak's map has room for the good of every nation (D28).

**What about when you run on empty?** Abraham, three days after his circumcision and in pain, still ran to the guests, "so as not to lessen what he used to do before" (ואע"ג דהוה כאיב ממילה רהיט אבתרייהו בדיל דלא למגרע מה דהוה עביד מקדמת דנא, line 3040). Keep the good habit through the pain, as far as it is safe.

**What discourages us?** Among the forces of the other side are those that come "to people who learn Torah and do mitzvot, so that they will not rejoice in it and will be sad with it" (כדי שלא ישמחו בה ויעצבו עמה, line 3095). Sadness in holy work is itself the enemy's work. And the voice in the study hall that says "you are wasting the days of your youth on learning" (ומפסיד ימי בחרותך בעסק הלימוד, line 3096) is named for what it is.

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **The harlot parable.** The Tanya uses the same Zohar parable. The animal soul "wants the opposite, for the good of the person, that he overcome it, like the parable of the harlot in the holy Zohar" (לטובת האדם שיתגבר עליה וינצחנה כמשל הזונה שבזהר הקדוש, Tanya line 188), and again in chapter 29 for doubts in faith. The Ramak and the Tanya agree fully. The Tanya turns it into the daily work of the beinoni.
- **Kelipat nogah and food.** The Ramak's fourth, nearly-holy husk (line 3086) and his ladder of eating (line 3011) are the Tanya's "fourth shell called kelipat nogah" (שהיא קליפה רביעית הנקראת קליפת נוגה, Tanya line 154). The Tanya gives the same practice: meat and wine eaten for the sake of Heaven are refined, "and rise to God as a burnt offering and a sacrifice" (נתברר חיות הבשר והיין שהיה נשפע מקליפת נוגה ועולה לה׳ כעולה וכקרבן, Tanya line 157). This is a direct line from the Pardes to Chabad.
- **Tzimtzum and the four panes.** The Ramak's picture has one unchanging light seen through clearer and cloudier glass (lines 3015-3018). This is the closest he comes to Chabad's "the tzimtzum is only from our side". The glass changes, the light does not. But for the Ramak the panes are real layers of emanation. For the Tanya, the concealment itself is His, and from His side there is no pane at all.
- **Soul above angel.** Both hold it. The Tanya says the soul is "a part of God above", and the Ramak says it receives "through itself" (line 3012). The Tanya adds that this is so because the soul's root is in the Essence. The Ramak roots it in Atzilut.
- **Evil as leftover.** The Ramak's grain (line 3048) has no parallel in the Tanya's language. The Tanya grounds the kelipot in the Divine will that there be an other side for the sake of choice. The two meet in the harlot.
