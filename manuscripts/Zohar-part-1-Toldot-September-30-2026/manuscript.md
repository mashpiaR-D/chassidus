Toldot is the Zohar's book of Jacob and Esau, of the wells, and of the stolen blessings. Inside it: the world, the body and the Torah as one body of limbs; the moon that shines only when it sees the sun; those who stand in the house of God at night; God pairing a hard one with a soft one so the world can bear itself; a Midrash HaNe'elam on the heart and the liver; the righteous tested so their heads are raised; the wells as the work of faith; hidden wisdom in silence; trust that does not dictate to God; and Jacob's blessings kept back for the last days. ## 2. The unity of God

**One body.** «דהא כמה דבר נש איהו מתפליג שייפין, וכלהו קיימין דרגין על דרגין מתתקנין אלין על אלין וכלהו חד גופא, הכי נמי עלמא» (Toldot 1:3; line 2840): "for as a man is divided into limbs, and all stand level on level, fitted one on another, and all are one body, so too the world." The Torah too is limbs and chapters that, fitted together, "became one body." Ours: the Zohar's picture of unity is not a blank; it is many parts that belong to one living whole. The one who learns Torah holds the world together.

**Source and well are one.** «ולית לאפרשא בין מקורא ובירא וכלא חד» (Toldot 14:5; line 2947): "and there is no separating between the source and the well, and all is one." The spring that never stops and the well it fills are called by one name. Whoever looks into this well looks into "the high secret of faith." Ours: the giver and the receiving vessel are one; this is the Zohar's form of the Tanya's teaching that the flow and its Source do not part (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 2-3; cited from memory).

**The moon sees the sun.** «סיהרא לא אתנהירת, אלא כד חזיא ליה לשמשא, וכיון דחזיא ליה, אתנהיר» (Toldot 1:10; line 2847): "the moon does not shine except when it sees the sun, and once it sees it, it shines." Ours: the lower world, and the soul, have no light of their own; the light comes in the looking.

**Hard and soft paired.** «קודשא בריך הוא מזווג זווגין בעלמא, חד תקיף וחד רפיא בגין לאתתקנא כלא ויתבסם עלמא» (Toldot 2:4; line 2855): "the Holy One pairs couples in the world, one hard and one soft, so that all is set right and the world is sweetened." Judgement and kindness are one work.

**One King in the end.** When Esau's kingdom falls, «ותהא דיליה כלא» (Toldot 19:17; line 2992): "and all will be His"; now He has given each nation a portion to use, then "He will be one and His name one."

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **Knowing in the heart as if seeing.** In the time to come the righteous will know Him in their hearts: «וכדין יסגי סכלתנו בלבהון כאילו חזו ליה בעינא» (Toldot 5:8; line 2877): "then understanding will grow in their hearts as if they saw Him with the eye." Ties: S35 and the Tanya's "seeing" of the mind (re'iyah); a foretaste of it is what Chabad contemplation aims at.
- **Fed by the radiance.** The feast of the righteous is seeing: those less worthy "enjoy" the radiance, those worthy "are fed" by it until they grasp fully, like Moses who ate no bread for forty days (line 2878). Ties: S60, S76.
- **Joy of learning needs the evil inclination.** «הכי אצטריך יצר הרע לעולם כמטרא לעולם, דאלמלא יצר הרע, חדוותא דשמעתא לא ליהוי» (Toldot 6:11; line 2894): "the evil inclination is needed in the world like rain; were it not for the evil inclination there would be no joy of learning." Ours: the heat that could pull down is the same heat that makes Torah sweet. Ties: C-current of the animal soul turned (Tanya ch. 27, "when the other side is subdued"; memory).
- **The body struck so the soul may rule.** «מחי לגופא בגין דתשלוט נשמתא» (Toldot 11:8; line 2927): "He strikes the body so the soul may rule." Read with care: a teaching about the righteous who are "tested" to raise them (line 2924), never a reason to seek pain. Ties: S59.
- **Trust that does not dictate.** See section 4. Ties: S84.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Standing at night.** Those whose blessing is a blessing are «אלין אינון דקיימו בפלגות ליליא ואתערי למקרי באורייתא» (Toldot 1:14; line 2851): "those who rise at midnight and wake to read Torah"; then the Holy One comes to delight with the righteous. Ties: the half night practice proposed in Lech Lecha.

**Tzitzit and tefillin as daily strength of faith.** «דכל יומא ויומא אתתקף בר נש בציצית דאיהו מצוה» (Toldot 13:4; line 2941): "each and every day a person is strengthened by tzitzit"; and in tefillin on head and arm, for God is found with the one crowned in tefillin and wrapped in tzitzit. Without them, «וצלותיה לאו צלותא כדקא יאות» (Toldot 13:5; line 2942): "his prayer is not a proper prayer." Ours: the wells the fathers dug are now the daily acts that hold faith. Ties: technique 2 (before Whom), 11 (seeing the garment).

**Trust as leaning, not demand.** «לא יתתקף בר נש דיימא, קודשא בריך הוא ישזבינני, או איהו עביד לי כך וכך, אבל ישוי תוקפיה ביה בקודשא בריך הוא דיסייע ליה» (Toldot 16:3; line 2961): "a person should not make himself strong by saying, the Holy One will save me, or He will do such and such for me; rather let him put his strength in the Holy One, that He help him" as he works at the mitzvot and walks in truth. «דכיון דאתי בר נש לאתדכאה, מסייעין ליה ודאי» (same line): "once a person comes to be purified, they surely help him." And "paths in their heart": a heart made ready «בלא הרהורא אחרא» (same line), "with no other thought." Ours: this is the Zohar's balance on bitachon. The Chabad teaching "think good and it will be good" (Tzemach Tzedek; memory) is not denied here, but the Zohar puts the stress on leaning, not on naming the outcome. Ties: S84; new technique below.

**For His name.** «דכל מאן דאתעסק באורייתא ולא אשתדל לשמה, טב ליה דלא אתברי» (Toldot 16:4; line 2962): "whoever engages in Torah and does not strive for its own sake, better he had not been created"; the Torah learned for His name raises Him and makes Him "one" in the world. Jacob's «כל עובדוי הוו לשמא דקודשא בריך הוא» (Toldot 16:5; line 2963), "all his deeds were for the name of the Holy One," and so the Shechinah never left him.

**Silence and asking.** Highest wisdom opens «וכלא בחשאי דלא אשתמע לבר לעלמין» (Toldot 14:8; line 2950), "all in silence, never heard outside"; only when it becomes voice is it "outside", and «מכאן בעי בר נש לאתקנא בעבידתיה … ולשאלא» (Toldot 14:9; line 2951): "from here a person must set right his work and ask." Ours: there is a place inside that is only silence, and a place where questions belong; the guide should not push words into the first. Ties: technique 14 (edge of understanding).

**The two inclinations as two witnesses.** Two angels go with each person, right and left, the good and evil inclinations (line 3007). When a person comes to purify himself, the good one «אתתקף על ההוא יצר הרע ואשתלים בהדיה ואתהפיך ליה לעבדא» (Toldot 19:33; line 3008): "grows strong over the evil inclination, makes peace with it, and it turns into his servant." This is "even his enemies make peace with him." Ties: technique 17, S77.

## 5. Big human questions

- **Why do the good suffer?** Because God desires their prayers (line 2858); because He tests them «בגין לארמא רישיהון» (Toldot 11:5; line 2924), "to raise their heads", not because He needs to know; because the soul rules when the body is broken (line 2927). He is patient with the wicked so they will return and live in both worlds (line 2923).
- **Is cunning ever right?** Jacob was whole: with the kind he was kind, with the crooked he went crooked (lines 2863, 2919). The Zohar reads his deceit as taking back from the serpent the blessings the serpent took from the world (line 2978). Ours: this is a teaching about meeting evil on its own ground, not a license to lie.
- **Where is the soul in the body?** The heart thinks Torah; the liver hunts food and says "think of eating"; the body's limbs eat only after the heart has thought of it, as servants eat after the master (line 2899). A Midrash HaNe'elam psychology of desire.
- **Can return come too late?** «בתשובה יתקדם כלא» (Toldot 8:7; line 2907): "by return all is preceded"; return was before the world. And those who wait for salvation "every day".
- **What of the tears of the unworthy?** Esau's tears for his father's blessing bought him long rule; they will be answered only when Israel returns «בבכיה ובדמעין» (Toldot 19:70; line 3045), "with weeping and tears." Tears are never lost, even the enemy's.
- **The body honored.** Body and soul are «דודים ורעים זה עם זה» (Toldot 5:3; line 2872), "lovers and friends to each other"; the soul's greatest joy in the time to come is to be in the body, the two of them knowing their Creator (line 2877).

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Source and well are one (line 2947).** A plain image of unity between the flow and its Source.
2. **Trust as leaning, not dictating (line 2961).** For the one who prays "He must do this for me."
3. **The evil inclination as rain for the joy of learning (line 2894).** Desire is not the enemy of the sacred; it is its fuel once turned.
4. **Knowing in the heart as if seeing (line 2877).** The goal of contemplation in the Zohar's words.
5. **Tzitzit and tefillin as daily wells of faith (lines 2940-2942).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "Leaning, not naming."** Before a fear or a need, drop the sentence "He will do X for me." Put your weight on Him that He help you, then take the next right step in the mitzvot and in truth; let the heart be a cleared road "with no other thought" (line 2961). Near S84; ties technique 20 (own words).
- **State, new: "Seeing in the heart."** Inside: the knowing of God grows in the heart until it is as sure as sight (line 2877). How to enter: long contemplation until the thought is felt; in this world a foretaste. Near S35, S60.
- **Technique, new: "Drinking at the well."** Look at the well (a text, a mitzvah, one's own heart) as the place where the unending spring enters, and hold that the spring and the well are one (lines 2940, 2947). Ties technique 11.
- **State, new: "Enemy made servant."** Inside: the pull that used to fight you now works for you; the heat that drove the wrong thing now drives the good (lines 2894, 3008). How to enter: keep coming to purify; the good inclination grows strong and the other makes peace. Near S77, C-current of transformation (itkafya to ithapcha).
