The Zohar on Abraham is the Zohar's portrait of the seeker. Abraham weighs every power that rules the world and cannot reach the one at the center; when God sees his longing, He reveals Himself. Around that story the range sets the soul's ascent (nefesh, ruach, neshamah, read through the lamp), the soul's life at night and after death, "go down to Egypt and come up", midnight study, the blessing that needs a vessel, Rabbi Yosei ben Pazi who came to Torah for money and found love, and a long night of teachings at a circumcision feast. ## 2. The unity of God

**No power apart from Him.** «כי מי אל מבלעדי יי» (Psalm 18:32): «מאן הוא שליטא או ממנא דיכול למעבד מדי מבלעדי יי, אלא מה דאתפקד מעם קודשא בריך הוא, בגין דכלהו לא ברשותייהו קיימי» (Lech Lecha 30:1; line 1905): "which ruler or appointed power can do anything apart from Havayah, except what it is commanded by the Holy One? For none of them stands in its own authority." And "Rock" is not alone: «דהא צור לאו איהו בלחודוי אלא כלא חד» (Lech Lecha 30:11; line 1915): "for the Rock is not by itself, but all is one", as it says "know this day … that Havayah is God."

**Abraham's search and the One at the center.** Abraham, given a spirit of wisdom, weighed all the powers that rule the parts of the settled world, and could weigh them all, until he reached the center point and "could not": «וחמא דהא חילא עלאה דעלה לית ליה שעורא עמיק וסתים» (Lech Lecha 5:4; line 1602): "and he saw that the higher power over it has no measure, deep and hidden," and all other powers come from it and hang on it (line 1603). «כיון דחמא קודשא בריך הוא אתערותא דיליה ותיאובתא דיליה. מיד אתגלי עליה» (Lech Lecha 5:8; line 1606): "when the Holy One saw his arousal and his longing, at once He revealed Himself to him." A second telling (lines 1801-1802): Abraham worshipped the sun by day, the moon by night, and in the morning saw the dark go and said: «ודאי כל אלין מלכא אית עלייהו ושליט דאנהיג לון» (Lech Lecha 22:14; line 1802): "surely there is a King over all these, who rules and leads them." Ours: this is the Zohar's model of the path from the world to its Maker, and it ends not in proof but in being answered.

**Above the stars.** Abraham saw by astrology that he would have no child. God took him outside: «צא מאצטגנינות דילך» (Lech Lecha 29:4; line 1900): "go out of your astrology." «והאמין ביי ולא בככביא ומזלי» (Lech Lecha 29:6; line 1902): "and he trusted in Havayah, not in stars and signs." And God "changes" what the stars show (line 1906). The unity here is practical: nothing, not even fate, stands apart from Him.

**One without separation above and below.** "I, I am He": twice "Anochi", once at Sinai and once at creation, «בגין לאחזאה דלא הוי פירודא בין עילא ותתא» (Lech Lecha 24:13; line 1827): "to show that there is no separation between above and below."

**All souls are one.** «כל נשמתין דעלמא … כלהו חד ברזא חד, וכד נחתי לעלמא כלהו מתפרשין בגוונין דכר ונוקבא» (Lech Lecha 21:10; line 1782): "all the souls of the world, … all are one in one secret; and when they come down to the world they separate into male and female," and God alone joins them again (line 1785).

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **The seeker answered.** Weighing, testing, reaching the edge where the power "has no measure", and longing; then being spoken to (lines 1600-1606, 1801-1802). Ties: S35 The edge of knowing, S22 Hearing from afar. New: see below.
2. **Nefesh, ruach, neshamah: the whole lamp.** The soul's levels are "one level": nefesh rules by night, ruach by day, and the neshamah over them (lines 1730-1731). When all are joined «כדין ההוא בר נש אקרי קדוש שלים מכלא. רעותא חדא לגבי קודשא בריך הוא» (Lech Lecha 17:3; line 1732): "then that person is called holy, whole in everything: one will toward the Holy One." The Zohar gives the picture: the nefesh is the dark light clinging to the wick, it becomes a throne for the white light (ruach), which becomes a throne for the hidden light (neshamah) that is not seen (lines 1736-1737). Ties: the guide's 1.3 (five levels of the soul), S66 Filled from inside. This is the Bereshit III flame (line 990) turned into a map of the person.
3. **Going down without being caught.** Abraham went down to Egypt «ולא אתדבק בהו, ותב לקמי מריה» (Lech Lecha 15:6; line 1719): "and did not cling to them, and returned before his Master," not seduced like Adam or Noah (line 1720): «דסליק ולא נחית, ותב לאתריה» (Lech Lecha 15:8; line 1721): "he went up and not down, and returned to his place." And without going down first «לא יהא חולק עדביה בקודשא בריך הוא» (Lech Lecha 15:9; line 1722): "he would not have been the Holy One's portion." Ties: C5 Running and returning; S82 The home below; C7.
4. **Love that enters the belly.** Rabbi Yosei ben Pazi came to learn for wealth; one day «עאל חמידו דאורייתא במעוי» (Lech Lecha 26:16; line 1864): "the love of Torah entered his insides," and he wept that he was trading the world to come for this. Ties: S39 The heart answers, S41 The hidden love wakes. The Zohar's own case of "from not for its sake comes for its sake."
5. **The soul's night.** At night the nefesh leaves and seeks to rise; if it was not defiled by day it rises and longs «למחמי בנועם מלכא ולבקרא בהיכליה» (Lech Lecha 16:5; line 1728): "to see the pleasantness of the King and to visit His palace"; this is "my soul longed for You in the night" (line 1729). Ties: S13, S84.
6. **The soul testifies.** «כל מה דבר נש עביד בכל יומא נשמתיה אסהידת ביה בבר נש בליליא» (Lech Lecha 33:3; line 1937): "all that a person does each day, his soul testifies about him at night." And God does kindness and the soul returns (line 1940). Ties: technique 24 (the evening look-back).

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **Arousal from below, and help for whoever begins.** «כל מאן דאתער לאתדכאה מסייעין ליה» (Lech Lecha 4:3; line 1596): "whoever wakes to be purified is helped." Abraham set out for Canaan on his own, and only then "Go for yourself" came. And the flame again: «נהורא אוכמא לא אתאחיד בנהורא חוורא עד דאיהי אתערית בקדמיתא» (Lech Lecha 4:4; line 1597): "the black light does not join the white light until it wakes first." Ties: technique 1.
- **Prepare the vessel.** «ברכאן דלעילא לא משתכחי אלא במה דאית ביה ממשא ולאו איהו ריקניא» (Lech Lecha 25:1; line 1839): "blessings from above are found only in something that has substance, not in what is empty": the widow's little jar of oil. So: "I am to my beloved" first, "and his desire is for me" after: «אני לדודי לאתקנא ליה דוכתא בקדמיתא» (Lech Lecha 25:8; line 1846): "'I am to my beloved': to prepare a place for him first." Ours: begin with the small thing you have.
- **Midnight Torah.** At midnight God enters the Garden to delight with the righteous, and all listen to the one who rises to learn; a thread of kindness is drawn over him by day (lines 1941-1945). Rabbi Abba's host had a water clock that rang at midnight (lines 1946-1948). The companions of that village: «בכל ליליא פלגותא אנן ניימין, ופלגותא אנן עסקין באורייתא» (Lech Lecha 35:32; line 1994): "every night we sleep half, and half we are busy with Torah"; and in the morning "the scent of the field and the rivers of water light up the Torah for us."
- **Washing the hands with a full measure.** Whoever washes properly draws blessing on his hands; the student who washed with much water grew rich, fed the poor daily and «מסבר לון אנפין נהירין» (Lech Lecha 24:24; line 1838): "greeted them with a shining face."
- **Leave a bad companion.** «דכל מאן דיתחבר לחייבא סופיה למיהך אבתריה ולאתענש בגיניה» (Lech Lecha 19:3; line 1754): "whoever joins a wicked man will end up following him and being punished for him." The holy spirit left Abraham while Lot leaned toward sin, and returned when Lot left (line 1769).
- **Pray for yourself, then others.** «בקדמיתא לבעי בר נש רחמין עליה ולבתר על אחרא» (Lech Lecha 35:43; line 2005): "first a person should ask mercy for himself, and then for another." (Set this beside Moses in the Noach report: both hold.)
- **Do not withhold teaching.** Rabbi Shimon reproves Rabbi Abba for keeping good words to himself: "do not withhold good from its owner" (line 2052).
- **The soul's daily war (Abram and Lot).** Every day the soul and the evil inclination quarrel and all the limbs suffer between them; the soul says: «כי אנשים אחים אנחנו, יצר טוב ויצר הרע קריבין דא בדא» (Lech Lecha 10:20; line 1664): "'for we are brothers': the good and the evil inclination are close to each other," and bids it go (line 1665). Ties: S34 Two voices, S10 Torn in two. The Tanya's two souls, in the Zohar's picture.

## 5. The big human questions

- **Why the descent?** Abraham had to go down to Egypt and be refined, and Israel after him, to become His (lines 1719-1723). Descent for the sake of ascent is here in its source.
- **Merit that stays.** «דכל מאן דמזכה לאחרא ההוא זכותא תליא ביה ולא אעדי מניה» (Lech Lecha 9:4; line 1639): "whoever brings merit to another, that merit hangs on him and does not leave him."
- **Held from before birth.** Every soul stands before God in the very form it will have in the world (lines 1909-1914); every match is made above (lines 1924-1928), and matches are "as hard as the splitting of the sea" (line 1933). Ours: a person was known and shaped before he came.
- **Fear and worry.** Abraham after the war feared he had lost his merit; God: «אל תירא אברם אנכי מגן לך» (line 1848). And Rabbi Shimon to the worried: «משמועה רעה לא יירא נכון לבו בטוח ביי» (Lech Lecha 35:89; line 2051): "he shall not fear an evil report; his heart is firm, trusting in Havayah." Ties: S84 Trust.
- **Before the world, return.** God tried to build the world from the secrets of Torah and it would not stand, «עד דברא תשובה דאיהי היכלא פנימאה עלאה» (Lech Lecha 27:14; line 1888): "until He created teshuvah, which is the inner, supernal palace." Return is older than the world. Ties: S77, S78.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **The seeker's path (lines 1600-1606, 1801-1802).** Weigh everything, reach what cannot be weighed, long, and be answered. The guide's universal path (D28) has its oldest Jewish form here.
2. **The lamp of the soul (lines 1730-1737).** Nefesh, ruach and neshamah as one flame with three lights: a picture a person can hold.
3. **Going down without being caught (lines 1719-1722).** For the person whose work or life takes him into lower places.
4. **Blessing needs a vessel (lines 1839-1846).** Begin with what little you have.
5. **Teshuvah before the world (line 1888).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "The seeker answered."** Inside: you have weighed and searched every power you can see, reached one you cannot measure, and your longing has become the whole of you; and you are spoken to. Enter: honest search to the edge (lines 1600-1606). Near S35 and S22.
- **State, new: "Gone down and not caught."** Inside: you are in a low place for a reason, you see it clearly, and it does not take you; you return to your place. Enter: go down with the purpose held, come back at once (lines 1719-1722).
- **Technique, new: "The lamp of the soul."** Before a lamp: the dark light clings to the wick (the nefesh in the body), the white light rests on it (ruach), and over it the hidden light that is not seen (neshamah). Ask which light you are living in now, and let the lower become a throne for the higher (lines 1733-1737). Ties the flame technique of Bereshit III.
- **Technique, new: "The half night."** Sleep half the night; rise at midnight for Torah and praise (lines 1941-1948, 1994). Guard: health first; the Zohar's companions slept half.
