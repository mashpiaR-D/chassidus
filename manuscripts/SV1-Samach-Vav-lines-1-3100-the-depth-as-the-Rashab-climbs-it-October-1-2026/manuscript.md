## What this slice adds (one page)

The first third of Samach Vav (Rosh Hashana to Sivan 5666) is the Rebbe Rashab's long argument about why there is a world and what reaches G-d's Essence. Read through, it does not climb only upward. It climbs to the Essence and then insists, again and again, that the Essence is found lowest: in the plain physical mitzvah, in teshuvah from the bottom, in the day after prayer. Seven things it adds to "Go deeper" (all stated):

1. Under every "why" is a desire, not a reason. G-d made this low world because He longed for it ("His legs" = "He longed"); "about a desire there is no question" (C01, D01). The mitzvos come from His inner will, with no reason at all, not even a hidden one; that is why the plain deed brings the Essence itself, unlimited (C06, D13).

2. Every step deeper costs letting go. Each higher level needs the last joy forgotten, "as if it never was"; nullification is the vessel for light. And the higher you climb, the more wondrous He becomes: "up to and not including" (C02, D02, D03).

3. Thought cannot grasp the Essence, not even by negation; the heart's bond does, like a son with a father: "when the son cries 'Abba', the father is moved in his whole being". That bond is a will that never changes, even in the lowest Jew; "the bad is added, the essence is good" (C09, C10, D04, D05).

4. The bottom is pain, and pain is power. Bitterness of distance reaches deeper than the delight of closeness, as a drawn bow or a pressed spring. Teshuvah leaps with abandon; the strongest pull comes from feeling no connection at all (C03, C04, D06).

5. The return is higher and harder than the ascent. "It is much easier to expire in love than to live below and keep the Torah properly." The return is rooted in the Essence; the soul shows it when it cannot rest until it does a G-dly deed (C15, D07).

6. The hiding is His, and it will shine. The world hides G-d like a sun's sheath; the work is to feel the G-dly life in each thing until the world becomes a parable that shows Him; and the hiding itself will shine, because "essence does not hide essence" (C08, C16, D08). G-d is not "nothing": He is the true Being, alone "even now"; the "nothing" is His light (C18, D11).

7. The law of gift and toil. Longing comes suddenly from above each morning and passes in moments; it opens the heart for your own work. The highest love is a gift given only after you have done all you can. Toil strikes the flint; obstacles in exile are the striking (C11, C13, C14, D09, D10). And the deepest delight is the one you do not feel (Yom Kippur, the mitzvah done dry) (C12, D12).

For the guide (ours): this slice gives the guide its strongest returns. Every chain here bottoms out in something a person can do this hour: an honest minute before prayer, a mitzvah done because He asked, the next ordinary duty after a high moment, mercy on one's own blocked soul.

### SV1-C01 · Why did G-d make a world at all? The usual answers: so that His powers would be complete, or so that He would be known. *Yom Tov shel Rosh Hashana / Zeh Hayom (lines 146-213, 374-386)*

1. **The usual reason, that G-d made the world to complete His powers, cannot be the real reason. With Him, the moment He willed it, it already was.** (M1) The text says a power that never acts is lacking only for creatures. Above, there is no gap between willing and being, so nothing was missing. > תיכף כשעלה ברצונו להוות הרי מיד נתהוו — "the moment it rose in His will to bring into being, at once they came to be" (line 169, OCR)

2. **'So that He would be known' cannot be the reason either. Knowing Him happens fully in the high worlds. This low world hides Him, yet this world is the purpose.** (M1) If the aim were to be known, the higher worlds would have been enough. The low, hiding world would not have been needed, let alone be the main point. > ומאחר שתכלית הכוונה הוא בהתהוות עולם התחתון דוקא — "and since the aim is the making of the lowest world specifically" (line 205, OCR)

3. **The real reason is a longing. The Midrash reads 'His legs' (shokav) as 'the world He longed (nishtokek) to create'.** (M2) The text takes the verse 'His legs are pillars of marble' and reads the word as longing: not need, not completeness, only desire. > שוקיו זה העולם שנשתוקק הקב"ה לבראותו — "'His legs': this is the world the Holy One longed to create" (line 207, OCR)

4. **A longing has no 'why'. We do not know, and cannot know, a reason for it.** (M14) The Rebbe quotes the Alter Rebbe's Yiddish saying: you cannot ask a question about a desire. Asking 'why at all' ends in a want with no reason under it. > אויף א תאווה איז קיין קשיא — "about a desire there is no question" (line 213, OCR)

5. **Because the longing comes from His very Essence, up and down mean nothing to it, and the main showing of it will be below.** (M12) Light has higher and lower levels. The Essence has none. So what comes from the Essence can show most in the lowest place. > אין בזה התחלקות מעלה ומטה, ועיקר הגילוי יהי' למטה דוקא — "in this there is no division of above and below, and the main revelation will be below specifically" (line 213, OCR)

6. **The plain deed of a mitzvah was 'first in thought', before even the first stir of the will to create. Everything else came for its sake.** (M5) 'The end of the deed rose first in thought' is read as: before the head of thought. G-d 'consulted with the souls of the righteous', meaning their physical mitzvos are why He willed at all. > סוף מעשה בקיום התומ"צ בפו"מ, הוא שעלה במח' תחלה — "the end of the deed, keeping Torah and mitzvos in actual deed, is what rose first in thought" (line 375, OCR)

7. **So when His will is done in deed, it is 'a pleasure to Me'. The pleasure of the Essence lives in the deed.** (M15) Since the deed is the inner aim, doing it is what draws His own simple delight. > נח"ר לפני שאמרתי ונעשה רצוני — "a pleasure before Me, that I spoke and My will was done" (line 383, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): a desire in the Essence with no reason, 'no question about a desire'. Nothing beneath it to ask about.
- **Return.** Return (stated): the plain physical mitzvah, today, is the purpose itself; Rosh Hashana is 'the beginning of Your works' because man, who does mitzvos, was made then (line 386). Landing (ours): your small deed is not a step toward the point; it is the point.

### SV1-C02 · Souls in Gan Eden climb from level to level. Why is there no rest, and how does one climb? *Zeh Hayom / Shuva (lines 290-373)*

1. **Every light has a head and a foot: strong near its source, weaker far away. Only the Essence is the same everywhere.** (M3) Mashal of a candle, a torch, the sun: near the source the light is strong, across the house it is weak. The Essence has no near and far. > אבל העצם הוא בכ"מ בשוה, ואין בו ראש וסוף — "but the essence is equal in every place, with no head and no end" (line 290, OCR)

2. **So the joy of Gan Eden, which is only a light, has endless levels. That is why 'the righteous have no rest, not in this world and not in the next'.** (M2) Because it is light and not essence, it has levels; because it is a light of the infinite, the levels never end. > צדיקים אין להם מנוחה לא בעוה"ז ולא בעוה"ב — "the righteous have no rest, not in this world and not in the world to come" (line 290, OCR)

3. **To rise to a higher level you must let go of the lower delight completely, as if it never was.** (M4) The levels are not comparable. A small delight held onto blocks a far greater one, so the first must be truly forgotten. > עד שישכח לגמרי בחי' העונג ההוא, והי' כלא הי' ממש — "until he completely forgets that delight, and it is as if it never was" (line 292, OCR)

4. **We see it in learning. Rabbi Zeira fasted a hundred fasts to forget the Babylonian Talmud so he could receive the Jerusalem one. A seed must rot before it sprouts.** (M9) In study a small idea usually leads to a bigger one. But for a different kind of wisdom you must first empty out. The seed loses its whole form, and only then does new life come. > וכמו רקבון הגרעין בארץ — "like the rotting of the seed in the earth" (line 302, OCR)

5. **Being nothing is itself the vessel for light.** (M2) Why does emptying bring you up? Because 'bitul is the vessel for revealed light', the same as the bitterness before prayer. > שביטול הוא כלי לגילוי אור — "for nullification is a vessel for revealed light" (line 368, OCR)

6. **The higher you climb, the more He is beyond you. Only someone who studies the lofty levels truly knows that He cannot be known; others hold it only by faith.** (M5) Bounded levels lead into each other ('up to and including'). Infinite ones never reach ('up to and not including'), and the Essence is never reached at all. > כל מה שעולה יותר, יותר יפלא ממנו — "the more he rises, the more wondrous He is to him" (line 368, OCR)

7. **The Essence's own delight is reached not by climbing but by doing physical mitzvos.** (M15) After the endless ladder, the text turns down: what the souls never reach by rising comes through the plain deed. > ובכדי לבוא לבחי' העונג העצמי, זהו ע''י מעשה המצות בעשי' גשמי' דוקא — "to come to the essential delight is through doing mitzvos in physical action specifically" (line 374, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): 'up to and not including': no level ever reaches the Essence; the climb is endless by nature.
- **Return.** Return (stated): the essential delight comes into the plain physical mitzvah. Landing (ours): when you feel you have stopped growing, let the old good feeling go; and do the next mitzvah, which does what climbing cannot.

### SV1-C03 · How does a person come to real closeness in prayer? *Zeh Hayom (lines 303-312)*

1. **Prayer is coming close to G-d: either His light shines in you, or you burn to reach Him.** (M2) The text names two kinds: closeness and cleaving when you grasp G-dliness, or flames of longing. > דכל ענין התפלה הוא בחי' קרבת הוי' — "the whole matter of prayer is closeness to G-d" (line 304, OCR)

2. **If the light itself shines in you, you feel joy and delight. If you only sense how far beyond He is, you run toward Him in thirst.** (M7) When the light is felt inside, love is 'love in delights'. When only the wonder is felt, from outside, it becomes ratzo, the run. > ה"ה בבחי' רצוא לעלות וליכלל באוא"ס — "he is in a state of running, to rise and be included in the Infinite Light" (line 310, OCR)

3. **Both start with bitterness. 'One stands to pray only from heaviness of head': an honest reckoning of how far you are.** (M3) Before either joy or flames, the person must first feel low, through a true accounting of his distance. > ועושה חשבון צדק בנפשו איך שהוא מרוחק לגמרי — "and makes an honest reckoning in his soul of how utterly distant he is" (line 310, OCR)

4. **The deeper the bitterness, the more light comes after: 'the reward matches the pain'.** (M2) The depth of the pain sets the measure of the joy that follows. > לפום צערא אגרא — "according to the pain is the reward" (line 310, OCR)

5. **In real teshuvah a person cries until his whole form is undone and he becomes truly nothing. From that narrow place comes the wide answer.** (M4) 'From the narrow place I called to G-d; G-d answered me with breadth.' The narrowness is total nullification, and that is what brings the breadth. > עד שנפסד כל צורתו לגמרי, ונעשה כאין ממש — "until his whole form is completely lost and he becomes truly as nothing" (line 310, OCR)

6. **The same is true in learning: a person reaches the depth of wisdom only after toiling until he almost gives up.** (M9) Learning is not easy; it comes after great effort and sorrow, almost despairing of ever understanding, and then wisdom comes with a shining face. > עד היותו כמו מיואש בנפשו לעמוד על החכמה — "until he is almost in despair of ever grasping the wisdom" (line 312, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): becoming 'truly nothing' is what makes room for the light.
- **Return.** Return (stated): then light shines in prayer, and wisdom comes 'with a shining face'. Landing (ours): before you pray, take one honest minute about where you really are; do not skip it to get to the good feeling.

### SV1-C04 · What does teshuvah reach that a good life does not, and how? *Shuva Yisrael; Vayishlach (lines 450-487, 1473-1496)*

1. **Forgiveness can only come from above the whole order of worlds, because inside that order a flaw is real.** (M2) Within the ordered levels, damage stays damage. Only what is above them can fill it. > דסליחת עוונות הוא דוקא מאוא"ס שלמעלה מסדר השתלשלות — "forgiveness of sins is specifically from the Infinite Light above the order of the chain of worlds" (line 450, OCR)

2. **True teshuvah moves the very core of the soul from its place, like a deep pain shakes a person's whole being.** (M9) When the distance touches him deep inside, it is not his mind or heart that moves, but his very self. > עד שכל עצמותו ניזוז ממקומו במרירות עצומה מאד — "until his entire essence is moved from its place in very great bitterness" (line 482, OCR)

3. **In that bitterness the pull toward G-d is really there, but he does not feel it; he feels only the pain of being far, like someone running away from death.** (M7) The pull is hidden inside the pain. It is a silent cry of the heart. He is fleeing the place of death and so runs to the place of life. > ה"ה בורח מן הרע, היינו ממקום המיתה — "he flees from the bad, that is, from the place of death" (line 482, OCR)

4. **The strongest pull comes from a despair that does not sense G-d at all: 'how could someone like me have anything to do with Him?'** (M11) There are two despairs. One comes from sensing how high G-d is. This one comes from feeling one's own lowness, and it pulls the hardest. > ואיך שאין לו שום שייכות לאלקות כלל — "and how he has no connection at all to G-dliness" (line 482, OCR)

5. **Where returners stand, the perfectly righteous cannot. A returner draws G-d 'with greater force'.** (M1) The question: the righteous serve with the delight of their whole soul, and pain and delight are balanced. How can pain go deeper? > משכין לי' בחילא יתיר — "draws Him with greater force" (line 1473, OCR)

6. **Anything pressed into a narrow place bursts out with force beyond its size: springs under the earth split rocks, a sling throws farther, a bow pulled back shoots far.** (M3) The rule of narrowness: the more the pressure, the more force that is not 'in its measure'. Even the shofar is blown from the narrow end. > וכמותח ביתר וקשת — "and like one who draws the string and the bow" (line 1492, OCR)

7. **Teshuvah is a leap, not a climb. Like a man who must cross a wide river: if he jumps with total abandon, he jumps far beyond his strength.** (M10) The righteous go step by step. The returner jumps from furthest to nearest. A jump measured by your strength stays small; one with abandon goes past it. > כ"א כאשר יתן כל כחו בהפקירות גדולה שלא בהדרגה כלל — "only when he gives all his strength with great abandon, not step by step at all" (line 1492, OCR)

8. **Then he returns to Torah and good deeds, and they shine with a new light: 'Take words with you and return'.** (M15) Teshuvah comes before good deeds, and after it the Torah and mitzvos themselves carry the light reached in the depths. > קחו עמכם דברים ושובו אל ה' — "take words with you and return to G-d" (line 1496, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): the 'depth of depths' of the heart, reached by bitterness that touches 'the very point of his Jewishness'.
- **Return.** Return (stated): Torah and good deeds after teshuvah light up from that depth. Landing (ours): the place you feel least connected is the springboard; do not measure the jump by your strength.

### SV1-C05 · How much of G-d does our understanding actually hold? *Lulav va'Arava (lines 489-505)*

1. **When a new idea first strikes you, it is a flash of lightning: bright, but you cannot yet put it into words.** (M9) The text describes the moment of discovery from our own minds: the light is there, but it is not yet held in letters. > ונקרא ברק המבריק — "and it is called the flashing lightning" (line 489, OCR)

2. **As the idea becomes clear understanding, the light inside it hides more.** (M4) The more it is grasped in detail, the more you feel the content and the less the light. Understanding hides the light it holds. > כאשר באה לידי השגה מתעלם בה האור יותר — "when it comes to comprehension, the light hides in it more" (line 495, OCR)

3. **The joy is greatest at the moment of discovery, like Rabbi Eliezer's face shining when he found a new teaching. Once it gets a little old, the joy stops.** (M9) This shows the delight in the mind is only a glimmer of the soul's delight; it changes and fades. > וכשנתיישן מעט נפסק העונג שבה — "and when it grows a little old, its delight stops" (line 495, OCR)

4. **So every understanding of G-d, even a great one, is only a glimmer; understanding always stands far from the essence.** (M5) Even Moshe, who saw with wisdom, was told 'My face shall not be seen'. > דכל השגה היא בריחוק מן העצם דוקא — "for every comprehension is at a distance from the essence" (line 495, OCR)

5. **When the essence itself shines, it shines in every power equally, mind and deed alike.** (M12) A glimmer is stronger in wisdom than in understanding; the essence makes no difference between them. > כאשר מאיר בחי' העצמות דאוא"ס ה"ה בכולם בשוה — "when the essence of the Infinite Light shines, it is in all of them equally" (line 505, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): no comprehension reaches the essence; even wisdom's sight sees only 'My back'.
- **Return.** Return (stated): the essence, which shines equally, comes in the mitzvah deed (line 507: the will in mitzvos holds the essential delight). Landing (ours): do not chase the old flash; let it go and do the act it pointed to.

### SV1-C06 · What is the difference between G-d's will in making the world and His will in the mitzvos? *Bayom Hashmini; Vayechulu; Eleh Toldos Noach (lines 584-614, 801-849)*

1. **Every person has an outer will and an inner will. A merchant's outer will is the travel and work; his inner will is the profit, and it does not show.** (M9) The mashal from business: all his visible effort serves something hidden that is the real point. > אבל מה שהוא תכלית חפצו ורצונו הוא הריוח — "but what is the goal of his wish and will is the profit" (line 584, OCR)

2. **A man wants a house for good reasons. But deep inside he simply delights in this home, with no reason, and if every reason argued against it, his inner wanting would not weaken.** (M7) Outer will is tied to reasons. Inner will is not, which is why reasons cannot touch it. > שגם אם השכל והטעם ינגד לזה — "that even if reason and logic oppose it" (line 584, OCR)

3. **Inner will is not mixed with anything; it comes from the self alone, so it cannot be explained and has no limit.** (M2) Whatever will is clothed in limits it. The inner will is clothed in nothing. > פנימיות הרצון הוא שאינו מורכב בשום דבר כלל, כ"א הוא מצד העצם לבד — "the inner will is not compounded with anything at all, but comes from the essence alone" (line 584, OCR)

4. **G-d's will to create comes with a reason: 'because He desires kindness', the nature of the good to do good. That is His outer will.** (M6) Creation is a will dressed in wisdom and kindness, like the man's reasons for a house. > מפני שטבע הטוב להטיב — "because it is the nature of the good to do good" (line 587, OCR)

5. **The mitzvos come from a will with no reason at all, not even a hidden reason or a taste.** (M5) Even 'Be silent, so it rose in thought' has a hidden reason; even a will from pleasure has a cause. The root of the mitzvos has neither. > ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כלל — "the true inner will is that there is no reason for the will at all" (line 596, OCR)

6. **The mitzvos have exact measures, but the measure does not shrink the light. It only says where the Essence will be found. If He had willed chopping wood, we would have to chop wood.** (M10) The worlds are the reason for creation's measures. Tefillin are not a reason for His will; so the measure only marks the place, and what comes is the Essence without limit. > ואם הי' הרצון לחטוב עצים, היינו מוכרחים לחטוב עצים — "if the will had been to chop wood, we would have had to chop wood" (line 822, OCR)

7. **So the deepest way to do a mitzvah is because He willed it, in acceptance of His yoke, and to find great life in His will being done.** (M15) 'Mitzvos do not require intention' is read: not the secret meanings, only that this is His will; and he has great life and delight that His will is fulfilled. > ויש לו חיות גדול בעשיית המצוה, בזה שמקיים רצה"ע — "and he has great life in doing the mitzvah, in that he fulfills the Supreme Will" (line 849, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): a will in the Essence with no reason, not even a hidden one.
- **Return.** Return (stated): the plain physical mitzvah, done because He wills it. Landing (ours): next time you do a mitzvah you do not understand, notice that this is exactly where it reaches deepest.

### SV1-C07 · Why is G-d's life in the world a 'running and returning', and what is above that? *Vayechulu (lines 681-739)*

1. **The root of time is the running and returning of life. Like breath: each moment life comes from the soul, and at once goes back up, and new life comes.** (M9) The text uses breathing as the picture for the life in all creatures. > וכמשל נשימת רוח החיים שבאדם, שבכל רגע נמשך החיות — "like the breath of life in a person, that every moment the life is drawn" (line 682, OCR)

2. **Time itself is a break. If life flowed without stopping, there would be no 'an hour or two'.** (M2) What withdrew is past, what spreads is future; the pause between is what we call time. > כי ענין הזמן הוא ההפסק — "for the matter of time is the break" (line 695, OCR)

3. **You only run when you are far. A light runs to leave its vessel because it is distant from its source.** (M2) Running belongs to something that has become a separate thing. While it runs, it is still in the vessel. > ורוצה לצאת מן הגבול ולהתכלל במקורו — "and wants to leave the limit and be included in its source" (line 697, OCR)

4. **G-d's will never left Him, so it does not run. It 'reaches and does not reach'. Its rising and spreading are not opposites.** (M4) Running is the light's own effort. In the will, the drawing back comes from the source shining in it, not from distance. > בשניהם הוא נמצא, ובשניהם הוא בבחי' העדר המציאות — "in both it is present, and in both it is without separate existence" (line 720, OCR)

5. **Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai said: 'In one knot I am tied, held to Him, aflame in Him'. His fire did not come from his own understanding but from the Source.** (M7) 'In G-d my soul will glory, like a flame that rises by itself.' This is the service that is a gift: the flame comes out a little from the coal and goes back in. > שניתנה מן המקור להלהיב הנפש, ולא שתתלהב מעצמה מהשגתה — "given from the Source to set the soul aflame, not that it is aflame by itself from its own understanding" (line 711, OCR)

6. **The will in the mitzvos is the Essence itself. Like water that flows down from high to low and does not change, it can come all the way down unchanged.** (M10) Fire's nature is to rise and needs contraction to come down. Water's nature is to come down. So the mitzvos bring the Essence into place and time below. > כמים היורדים ממקום גבוה למקום נמוך, שלא נשתנה מהותם — "like water coming down from a high place to a low place, whose nature did not change" (line 731, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): the Essence, where there is no running and no returning, 'a day that is all long'.
- **Return.** Return (stated): through mitzvos the Essence comes into time and place, and saying 'Vayechulu' on Shabbos makes one G-d's partner. Landing (ours): your breath, right now, is the picture: it comes, it returns. The mitzvah is the water that does not go back up.

### SV1-C08 · Do creatures really exist next to G-d? *Eleh Toldos Noach; Ki Imcha; Shmos; Ki Savo'u (lines 762, 1857-1894, 2292-2302, 2868-2891, 2938)*

1. **A craftsman makes a vessel and walks away; it does not need him, and it even has a use he lacks. A ray of sunlight is different: it needs the sun every moment.** (M10) The vessel was made from material that was already there. The ray has no other cause than the sun. > צריך הוא תמיד לשמש, ואינו נפרד בפ"ע — "it always needs the sun and is not separate by itself" (line 1857, OCR)

2. **And the ray is stronger inside the sun itself, where it does not even count as light. So creatures need Him every moment, and add nothing to Him.** (M12) Two nullifications, and they are opposites: needing Him always is closeness; adding nothing to Him is not taking up any place. > ובטל במציאותו לגבי המקור — "and nullified in its existence before its source" (line 1857, OCR)

3. **Even now, from His side, the contraction hides nothing. The worlds are truly one with Him, as before they were made.** (M4) Creation only means they came out and were shown 'outside' Him; for the Infinite Light the tzimtzum conceals nothing. > כי לגבי אוא"ס אין הצמצום מעלים ומסתיר — "for to the Infinite Light the contraction does not hide or conceal" (line 762, OCR)

4. **Higher still: He Himself is not even 'surrounding' the worlds. You are alone, truly, even now, after the heavens were made.** (M5) Even the surrounding light is only 'His Name'. The Essence is not in the category of worlds at all. > ובאמת אתה הוא לבדך ממש, בבחי' הבדלה לגמרי, גם עתה — "and in truth You are alone, utterly apart, even now" (line 2299, OCR)

5. **He is not 'nothing'. He is the real Being, the true 'Yesh'. What we call the 'Nothing' that makes the world is His light.** (M4) One might say G-d is 'ayin'. The text corrects it: the Essence is the true existent; the 'ayin' is the light that is nothing before Him. > הוא הנמצא האמיתי, ונק' יש האמיתי — "He is the true existent, called the true Being" (line 2300, OCR)

6. **From our side it looks reversed: we feel ourselves as real and the source as 'nothing'. That is why we do not even feel that our own life is G-dliness.** (M9) In every creature the thing is seen and the power making it is hidden. > ולכן אין אנו מרגישים גם בעצמינו שהחיות הוא אלקות — "therefore we do not feel even in ourselves that the life is G-dliness" (line 2874, OCR)

7. **'G-d's truth to the world' means that creatures will feel that there is G-dly life in them and that the G-dliness is the main thing.** (M16) The aim is not to erase the world but for the world to feel what it is. > שיש בהם אור וחיות אלקי, ושהעיקר הוא האלקות — "that there is G-dly light and life in them, and that the main thing is the G-dliness" (line 2876, OCR)

8. **A person who works on this finds the world no longer hides G-d. Through the world he sees G-dliness more tangibly, as a parable shows its meaning.** (M15) By training himself to feel the G-dly life in each thing, the hiding becomes a showing. > דבאמת העולם וכל הדברים שבו ה"ז משל על אלקות — "for in truth the world and everything in it is a parable for G-dliness" (line 2938, OCR)

- **Bottom.** Bottom (stated): He alone is, 'even now', and He is the true Being, not a nothing.
- **Return.** Return (stated): the 'servant of Elokim' works with the physical, feeling the G-dly life in it, until the world becomes a parable that shows Him. Landing (ours): hold one object today and ask: what is the life in this, and is that the main thing?

### SV1-C18 · What does 'Ein Sof', 'without end', really mean, and is G-d a light? *Ki Imcha Mekor Chaim; Adam Ki Yakriv (lines 2253, 2416-2512)*

## Depths beyond the descent

### SV1-D01 · No question about a desire Under every 'why' about the world and your life is not a reason but a longing in G-d Himself: He longed for this low world. A longing has no question. (stated: lines 207-213)

1. Think of the best reasons for a world: so His powers would be complete, so He would be known.
2. See that both are already complete above, without this world; with Him, the moment He wills, it is.
3. So this low, hiding world adds nothing that a reason needs. Why it, then?
4. Only because He longed for it. 'His legs': the world He longed to create.
5. A desire has no question. There is nothing under it to ask.
6. And He longed for it below, where His Essence is to be found, in the deed.

**From inside:** The text's own image is the Yiddish proverb, 'about a desire there is no question', and the Midrash reading 'shokav' as 'longing'. **Lands:** Body and now: the deed in your hand is the thing longed for (stated: 'the end of the deed rose first in thought', line 375). **Extends:** Sharpens D11 and D12: the command and the deed rest on a desire with no reason, not on a plan.

> שוקיו זה העולם שנשתוקק הקב"ה לבראותו — "'His legs': this is the world the Holy One longed to create" (line 207, OCR)

> ואין אנו יודעים טעם שכלי מפני מה נשתוקק — "and we do not know a rational reason why He longed" (line 208, OCR)

> אויף א תאווה איז קיין קשיא — "about a desire there is no question" (line 213, OCR)

### SV1-D02 · Let it go to go up Every real step deeper costs forgetting the joy of the step before. The soul crossing from one Gan Eden to the next dips in a river of fire to forget its first delight. Nullification is the vessel for light. (stated)

1. Notice a past spiritual high you still hold onto.
2. See that the next level is not more of the same; it is a different kind, with no measure to the first.
3. Holding the first blocks the second, the way earthly pictures confuse prayer.
4. So let it go completely, as if it never was, like a seed that rots in the ground.
5. Then the new growth comes 'from nothing to something', a complete newness.

**From inside:** Images: the river of fire, the pillar between the Gardens, Rabbi Zeira's hundred fasts, the rotting seed, astonishment 'for one hour'. **Lands:** Now: when a good feeling fades, do not chase it; it is the price of the next step (ours). **Extends:** Extends D9 and D14 (the return is deeper; each run begins again): the text adds that each new run requires forgetting the last one.

> עד שישכח לגמרי בחי' העונג ההוא, והי' כלא הי' ממש — "until he completely forgets that delight, and it is as if it never was" (line 292, OCR)

> ישכח ויבוטל ממהות התענוג הראשון — "he forgets and is nullified from the essence of the first delight" (line 314, OCR)

> שביטול הוא כלי לגילוי אור — "for nullification is a vessel for revealed light" (line 368, OCR)

### SV1-D03 · Up to and not including With bounded things, each level leads into the next. With the Infinite, no level reaches the next, and none ever reaches the Essence. So the higher you go, the more wondrous He becomes; only one who studies the lofty things truly knows He cannot be known. (stated)

1. Learn something real about how high G-d is.
2. Notice: standing on this rung, you cannot even see where the next one ends.
3. Go higher, and the knowledge becomes more abstract until it is hardly knowing.
4. See that this 'not knowing' is itself the knowing: He is beyond.
5. Without learning, you would hold this only as belief; with learning, you know it.

**From inside:** 'Up to and not including'; Gan Eden's ascents 'without end'; even the Patriarchs and Moshe 'did not reach'. **Lands:** The mind, today: study the hard material; its end is wonder, not answers (ours). **Extends:** Sharpens D5 (where knowing ends): the end of knowing is reached only by going through knowing; it is earned, and it grows the more you learn.

> שלא תגיע לעולם לאורו האמיתי — "that you will never reach His true light" (line 358, OCR)

> כל מה שעולה יותר, יותר יפלא ממנו — "the more he rises, the more wondrous He is to him" (line 368, OCR)

> ורק בדרך אמונה שאין זה ידיעא — "only by way of faith, which is not knowing" (line 368, OCR)

### SV1-D04 · Abba Thought cannot grasp G-d's Essence, not even by negation. A Jew's bond with Him is like a son's bond with a father: with no reason, essence to essence. That bond grasps the Essence. It is always there; it wakes when you remember 'He is my Father', or when you are far and in pain. (stated: lines 876-917)

1. Try to think what G-d is: see that even 'what He is not' does not reach Him.
2. Think of a friend you love for good reasons: the love reaches only those reasons.
3. Think of a child and a father: the child does not need reasons.
4. Hear the child cry 'Abba!' and the father move in his whole being.
5. Set to heart: He is my Father. Or feel how far you are, and cry from there.
6. Then do what He asks without asking why: 'we will do and we will hear'.

**From inside:** The son's cry, the father moved 'in all his essence'; 'for You are our Father'; the heart that grasps what the mind cannot. **Lands:** Heart and mouth: one word, said from inside, and then one deed done simply because He asked (ours: the landing). **Extends:** Extends D6 and D8: under every name is not only a point but a relationship of son to Father; the bottom bond is filial and can be woken.

> אז עס איז גאר ניט דאס — "for it is not that at all" (line 876, OCR)

> כאשר הבן צועק אבא ניזוז האב בכל עצם מהותו — "when the son cries 'Father!', the father is moved in his entire being" (line 906, OCR)

> ולכן בלב דוקא יש בחי' רעו"ד, שתופס בזה בחי' העצמות ממש — "therefore in the heart specifically is the will of the heart, which grasps the Essence itself" (line 915, OCR)

### SV1-D05 · The will that never changed Pleasure cannot change what a person truly wants. Every Jew's deepest will is to be close to G-d, and it never changes, even when he is sunk in pleasures. Even the lowest, at a happy moment, says he wants to be close, and it is not a lie. In a Jew, the bad is something added; the essence is good. (stated)

1. Remember a time you said 'I want to be close to G-d' and doubted you meant it.
2. See: pleasure covered the will; it did not change it. 'Many waters cannot put out the love.'
3. See that G-d wants you the same way a father wants a son who is not going right: the self, not the behavior.
4. The bad in you is added on; under it, the essence is good.
5. Let the will show, as it does at moments of joy.

**From inside:** 'Many waters'; 'the lost ones in the land of Ashur will come'; the father who wants his straying son. **Lands:** Now: speak the wish out loud; it is not hypocrisy (stated: 'it is no lie'). **Extends:** Extends D8 (the bottom is a bond): the bond is a will that survives sin and pleasure; it cannot be lost.

> ואין זה שקר — "and this is no lie" (line 996, OCR)

> מים רבים לא יוכלו לכבות את האהבה — "many waters cannot put out the love" (line 996, OCR)

> דבישראל מה שנמצא רע ח"ו ה"ז דבר נוסף על העצם, אבל העצם הוא טוב — "in Israel whatever bad is found is added onto the essence, but the essence is good" (line 1046, OCR)

### SV1-D06 · From the narrow place The pain of being far reaches deeper into the soul than the joy of being close, because pressure makes force beyond measure. Teshuvah does not climb; it leaps, with abandon, from furthest to nearest. Its strongest pull comes from a despair that feels no connection at all. (stated)

1. Feel honestly where you are far from G-d.
2. Do not soften it: let it touch the 'point of your Jewishness'.
3. Notice that inside the pain is a pull, hidden, a cry of the heart not heard aloud.
4. Remember the bow: the further back it is drawn, the further it flies.
5. Jump, not by your measure, but like a man leaping a river with everything he has.
6. Then take your Torah and good deeds with you; they will carry this light.

**From inside:** Springs splitting rocks; the sling; the bow; the shofar's narrow end; leaping the river; the Raavad whose soul left in weeping. **Lands:** Body: the tightness in the chest is the narrow place; that is where to call from (ours). **Extends:** New: DESCENT has a turn and a return, but not a downward bottom of pain that becomes the strongest upward force; this extends D9.

> ואיך שאין לו שום שייכות לאלקות כלל — "and how he has no connection at all to G-dliness" (line 482, OCR)

> זהו הכלל בכל דבר הבא מן המיצר והדוחק ביותר — "this is the rule for anything that comes from great narrowness and pressure" (line 1489, OCR)

> כ"א כאשר יתן כל כחו בהפקירות גדולה שלא בהדרגה כלל — "only when he gives all his strength with great abandon, not step by step at all" (line 1492, OCR)

### SV1-D07 · Harder to live it than to die for it It is much easier to burn out in love of G-d than to live below and keep Torah and mitzvos properly. The return to the world is rooted in G-d's own Essence, with no reason. A soul shows this root when it cannot rest until it does some G-dly act in speech or deed. (stated)

1. Picture the highest moment of love in prayer, wanting only to be included in Him.
2. Notice that staying there would be easier than coming back to dishes, work, and people.
3. See that coming back is His will: 'not for chaos He created it, for dwelling He formed it'.
4. Lay yourself down before that will, even against your own wish to stay above.
5. Then go do: a word of Torah, a coin of charity. Only there does the soul rest.

**From inside:** Rabbi Akiva who 'entered in peace and left in peace' versus Ben Azzai; the servant at the king's feast whose awe stays with him in joy. **Lands:** Deed, now: the next mitzvah of the ordinary hour is the deeper devotion (stated: lines 2008-2010). **Extends:** Extends D9 and D12: the return is not only deeper; it is harder, and its root is higher than the ascent.

> דבאמת בנקל הרבה יותר להיות בבחינת כלות הנפש מלהיות למטה בקיום התורה ומצות כדבעי — "in truth it is much easier to be in expiry of the soul than to be below keeping Torah and mitzvos properly" (line 2016, OCR)

> שלא יוכל למצוא מנוח לנפשו, אם לא שיעסוק ויעבוד — "that he cannot find rest for his soul unless he engages and works" (line 2010, OCR)

> וואס ער לייגט זיך אוועק בכל עצמותו צוא עצמות א"ס — "that he lays himself down with his whole being before the Essence of the Infinite" (line 2025, OCR)

### SV1-D08 · The hiding itself will shine G-d hides behind His name Elokim like the sun in its sheath, so the world can exist. The soul's work is to feel the G-dly life in each thing and that it is the main thing; then the world stops hiding and becomes a parable that shows Him. And the hiding itself comes from the Essence: 'essence does not hide essence'; in the end the hiding will itself shine. (stated)

1. Look at one ordinary thing.
2. Ask what gives it being and life. It does not make itself.
3. Say: that life is G-dliness, and it is the main thing; the thing without it would decay.
4. Now the thing is a parable: through it you see Him more tangibly than without it.
5. Then go further: the very hiddenness is His; 'the cloud and the darkness, and it lit up'.

**From inside:** The sun's sheath; the tanned hide ('oved', like tanning); the cloud and darkness that lit up. **Lands:** The world, today: what hides G-d is the material for seeing Him (ours: the landing). **Extends:** Extends D10 (the world given back as His): it is given back not only as kept, but as the very parable and as the shining of the hiding.

> דבאמת העולם וכל הדברים שבו ה"ז משל על אלקות — "in truth the world and everything in it is a parable for G-dliness" (line 2938, OCR)

> ועצם אינו מסתיר על עצם — "and essence does not hide essence" (line 2891, OCR)

> שההעלם בעצמו יאיר — "that the hiddenness itself will shine" (line 2947, OCR)

### SV1-D09 · The gift and the work The longing for G-d comes from above, suddenly, each morning, in every soul. But it passes in moments. Its purpose is to open the heart so that you do your own work of thinking and loving. The highest love is a gift too, but it is given only after you have done all you can. (stated)

1. Notice the sudden pull that comes, with no preparation.
2. Know it was sent, and that it will pass.
3. Use the open moment now: think about G-d slowly, until it settles in you.
4. When the heart is closed, have mercy on your own soul; that opens it.
5. Do your part fully; then the gift of love comes 'like a flame that rises by itself'.

**From inside:** The father walking the child to school the first day with gifts; 'whoever toiled on Friday eats on Shabbos'; 'open for Me like the eye of a needle and I will open for you like the Temple hall'. **Lands:** Now: the minute after a sudden spiritual stir is the minute to act (ours). **Extends:** New: DESCENT's D2 says the deeper stillness is given; the text adds the law of the gift: given suddenly, kept only by one's own toil, and the highest given only after it.

> שבכל בקר אור יש התעוררות פתאום בכל נשמה — "that every morning at light there is a sudden awakening in every soul" (line 1645, OCR)

> היא חולפת ועוברת במעט זמן — "it passes and goes away in a short time" (line 1674, OCR)

> אי לאו דעביד לי' נייחא לנפשי' לא הוה יהיב — "if he had not given him satisfaction, he would not have given" (line 1774, OCR)

### SV1-D10 · Strike the flint A quick mind brings out what already exists, like blowing on a coal; it runs out. Toil reaches what does not exist even to itself, like fire in a flint; it comes only by striking, and it never runs out. Exile's service is trials and obstacles, and that is how the deepest is reached. (stated)

1. Take something hard to understand, and do not settle for the first look.
2. Ask questions until it becomes dark and wondrous.
3. Stay with it. Before you know, sense the 'rightness' dawning.
4. See every obstacle as the striking that brings the fire.
5. Stand against each obstacle to the mitzvah, firmly.

**From inside:** Coal and flint; digging to living water; 'he who works his land will be sated with bread'; Avraham blessed 'with everything' through trials. **Lands:** Today: the obstacle is not in the way; it is the way (ours). **Extends:** New: no D treats effort itself as the path to the Essence.

> צור החלמיש א'"'א להוציא ממנו האש כ"א ע'י יגיעה והכאה — "from a flint stone one cannot bring out fire except through toil and striking" (line 1119, OCR)

> שאינו מסתפק בהשגת הדבר בהשקפה ראשונה — "that he is not satisfied with grasping the matter at first glance" (line 1101, OCR)

### SV1-D11 · He is the true Being 'Nothing' is too small a word for G-d. The Nothing from which the world comes is His light. He Himself is the true Being, alone, even now, not even 'surrounding' the worlds; 'Ein Sof' is the negation of every description, even of 'unlimited', and so everything is in Him. (stated: lines 2253-2302)

1. Think: the world comes 'from nothing'. Ask: is G-d that nothing?
2. No: the nothing is His light, which is nothing before Him.
3. He is the true Being; we are the borrowed being.
4. He is not even 'around' the worlds; that is only His Name.
5. You are alone, even now, after the heavens were made.
6. And because He is not limited and not unlimited, everything is in Him.

**From inside:** The sun's ray that is 'not even called light' within the sun; a person's name when no one calls him; the gem that shines. **Lands:** Mind: rest on 'He is'; it is enough (ours). **Extends:** Sharpens D3 and D4: D4 says 'neither something nor nothing'; the text says the Essence is the true Something (yesh ha'amiti), and 'nothing' is His light.

> ובאמת אתה הוא לבדך ממש, בבחי' הבדלה לגמרי, גם עתה — "and in truth You are alone, utterly apart, even now" (line 2299, OCR)

> הוא הנמצא האמיתי, ונק' יש האמיתי — "He is the true existent, called the true Being" (line 2300, OCR)

### SV1-D12 · The delight you do not feel There is a felt delight of the Essence (Shabbos: eat, it is all good) and an unfelt one (Yom Kippur: no food, affliction even of the soul). In the mitzvos now, only the will shows; the delight is hidden. A martyr goes from simple will, with a hidden taste he does not feel. (stated)

1. Notice a mitzvah you do without feeling anything.
2. Know that 'mitzvos were not given for enjoyment': the will shows, the delight hides.
3. Think: the deepest delight is not felt; the felt kind is lower.
4. On a dry day, remember Yom Kippur: removing felt pleasure, to reach the unfelt.
5. Keep doing; the hidden delight is there.

**From inside:** Shabbos and Yom Kippur; the martyr's hidden 'taste' ('a gishmak'); the son who suffers when his father suffers. **Lands:** Body: the empty feeling is not absence (ours). **Extends:** Extends D13 (plainness is deepest) and D7: the plainest, unfelt service touches the Essence's own delight.

> שיבוטל ממנה ע'"י העינוי כל בחי' עונג מורגש — "that through the affliction all felt delight is removed from it" (line 1459, OCR)

> מצות לאו ליהנות ניתנו — "mitzvos were not given for enjoyment" (line 1073, OCR)

> שנמצא בהעלם בנפשו עונג כזה (אזא גישמאק) — "that there is hidden in his soul such a delight (such a taste)" (line 1026, OCR)

### SV1-D13 · The will with no reason, in the deed Creation comes from G-d's outer will, which has reasons. The mitzvos come from His inner will, with no reason at all. Their exact measures do not limit the light; they only say where His Essence is found. 'If He had willed chopping wood, we would chop wood.' (stated)

1. Think of something you want deeply for no reason you can explain.
2. See that no argument could weaken it: that is inner will.
3. Know that G-d's will in each mitzvah is like that, with no reason, not even a hidden one.
4. So the tefillin's exact shape is where He chose to be found.
5. Do it because He wills it, and take life from His will being done.

**From inside:** The merchant's hidden profit; the man who delights in his house for no reason; chopping wood. **Lands:** Hand: the plain physical mitzvah, exactly as prescribed (stated: lines 822-849). **Extends:** Extends D12 (the deed in the hand): explains why the deed is deepest: the will in it has no reason, so nothing limits what it brings.

> שאין טעם לרצון כלל, אף לא טעם כמוס — "that there is no reason for the will at all, not even a hidden reason" (line 596, OCR)

> ואם הי' הרצון לחטוב עצים, היינו מוכרחים לחטוב עצים — "if the will had been to chop wood, we would have had to chop wood" (line 822, OCR)

> אבל מה שנמשך בזה הוא בחי' אור העצמי כמו שהוא בלי מדה וגבול — "but what is drawn by this is the essential light as it is, without measure or limit" (line 825, OCR)

it passes quickly. > היא חולפת ועוברת במעט זמן — "it passes and goes away in a short time" (line 1674, OCR) - When mind and heart are blocked, arouse mercy on your own soul. > ולכן העצה לזה היא לעורר ריר על נפשו — "therefore the advice for this is to arouse mercy on his soul" (line 1674, OCR) - Do mitzvos because He willed them, in acceptance of the yoke, and take life from fulfilling His will. > ויש לו חיות גדול בעשיית המצוה, בזה שמקיים רצה"ע — "and he has great life in doing the mitzvah, in that he fulfills the Supreme Will" (line 849, OCR) - Even with no desire, a person can force himself into Torah and mitzvos; this comes from the soul's own giving. > להכריח א"ע בעסק התורה וקיום המצות גם שאין לו רצון — "to force himself into Torah and mitzvos even when he has no desire" (line 2014, OCR) - Eat only what you need, for the sake of serving; the table refines and atones like the altar. > נהמא אפום חרבא — "bread 'on the mouth of a sword'" (line 1916, OCR) - Do business honestly, without even a trace of wrongdoing, and without being sunk in it. > כשעושה מלאכתו — "when he does his work" (line 526, OCR) - In Torah, do not settle for the first glance; ## Notes for the guide (ours) - Use C01/D01 when a person asks "why at all" (why the world, why me, why mitzvos): the honest bottom is a desire, and it lands in their deed. - Use C04/D06 only after the person feels heard. It is for someone who already feels far; do not hand "the pain is power" to someone in acute grief or despair as an explanation. - Use C10/D05 for someone who doubts their own sincerity ("I say I want G-d but I don't mean it"): the text says it is not a lie. - Use C15/D07 for someone who loves the high moments and resents ordinary life. - Use C13/D09 when someone reports a sudden spiritual stirring: tell them to use it now. - C18 and D11 are for strong, intellectual seekers; for others, give only the landing: "the hiding is His strength". - Pairing with DESCENT: D01 sits before DESCENT's D11-D12; D02-D03 sharpen D5 and D9; D04-D05 deepen D6-D8; D06 is a new bottom (pain) beside D8; D07 deepens D9-D12; D08 deepens D10; D11 sharpens D3-D4; D12-D13 deepen D12-D13. - Disagreement kept: in this slice the deed (mitzvah) and teshuvah each reach "higher" than Torah in some passages, while Torah toil reaches "above even the hiddenness of wisdom" in others (C11, C12). The text holds both: Torah reaches by refining (Bavli), teshuvah reaches the unfelt delight. Do not flatten.
