## The shape of the range

These maamarim have one thread, stated at the start: there are three levels, "*yesh*, *ayin*, *yesh*". The first *yesh* is the true Yesh, the Essence. The second is the created *yesh*. Between them is the *ayin*, and the *ayin* itself divides in two. From that the Rashab builds the two knowledges. The **higher knowledge** (*da'at elyon*) is the giver's view: "above is *yesh*, below is *ayin*". The **lower knowledge** (*da'at tachton*) is the receiver's view: "below is *yesh*, above is *ayin*". The Tanya says the two knowledges are views of the one truth. The Rashab says both are **in Godliness itself**, in the light, before any creature looks. Then, in the written continuation, he turns to the most daring claim of the range. The concealment itself (Elokim, the vessel, Malchut, the physical body) is rooted higher than the revelation. In the future the concealment itself will shine.

1. **Rosh Hashanah and Shabbat Teshuvah (4472-4516).** Two kinds of *ayin*. The light "takes no place" before the Essence, so "I, God, have not changed". Four kinds of the light's *bitul*. Forgiveness comes only from the true *ayin*, which does not hide.
2. **Sukkot and Shemini Atzeret (4517-4571).** Atzilut is the higher knowledge, yet also a "world". The ten sefirot are garments. The four species draw the higher knowledge into BYA. The inner and outer *kav*. The Name before creation and the thought "I will reign". The eighth day is for Israel alone.
3. **Bereishit to Vayera (4572-4681).** In man, the two knowledges are the two unions of Havayah and Elokim. Daat is recognition. "Bread from the earth" and "bread from heaven". "Havayah is Elokim in heaven above and on earth below", made so by practical mitzvot. The infinite light over the measured light. Black fire and white fire. Lovesickness from seeing. Teshuvah heals the partition.
4. **The written continuation, first half (4683-4870).** "There is none else": Elokim itself will shine, and "night will shine like day". "I am first and I am last". Gan Eden and the world to come. Innate, felt and understood emotions. Wholeness of heart. The higher will and the "forehead". Love "with all your might". Souls above Torah. Teshuvah above Torah.
5. **The written continuation, second half (4871-5017).** The trees of God are sated. Joy breaks bounds. The *reshimu* is above the *kav*, and the vessels are above the lights. Mitzvot are vessels, and the exchanged letters *yud-heh*. The vessel turned to light. The future body above the soul. Harsh and sweetened severities. "His adversaries will be broken" from inner Keter.

F05 (the Essence) leads by far. F10 (Torah and mitzvah) and F06 (the soul) come next, because the written continuation asks what in the soul and in the mitzvah can reach the Essence. F07 (the home below) is higher than in the earlier ranges, because the future, when "bodies will be Godliness itself", is the goal of the whole argument.

## Rosh Hashanah and Shabbat Teshuvah (4472-4516): the two *ayins*

**The *kav* and the two *ayins* (4472-4475).** The top of the *kav* touches Ein Sof and the bottom does not. So its upper part is the *ayin* of the true Yesh and its lower part the *ayin* of the created *yesh*: > דעצם האור הוא בחי' האין של היש אמיתי, וחיצוני' האור הוא בחי' האין של היש המתהווה (line 4474)

**"I have not changed" (4476-4478).** Before the Essence the light "takes no place", so nothing changed when the worlds were made: > ולכן אני ה' לא שניתי שלא יש שום שינוי כלל בין קודם ההתהוות לאחר ההתהוות (line 4476)

The source of the created *yesh* is "*ayin*" only because it is "not a thing" and cannot be grasped. The creatures themselves prove that their source is *ayin*: > וזהו שהנבראים עצמן מכריחים שהמקור הוא בחי' אין (line 4477)

**Something from nothing is the reverse of cause and effect (4479-4481).** An effect comes into being by grasping its cause. A creature comes into being only because it does *not* grasp its source: > ואם הי' משיג את האין האלקי לא הי' בבחי' נברא כלל (line 4479) > אבל ביש מאין הוא להיפך שאם יהי' התגלות האין בהיש שיהי' מושג בהיש לא יהי' התהוות הנברא בבחי' יש (line 4480)

After creation the knowledge can come, and in the future the *yesh* will grasp its source: > לעתיד שהיש יהי' אלקות ויהי' בו השגת מקורו בבחי' השגת המהות ממש (line 4481)

**The two knowledges (4482).** > דד"ע הוא דעת המשפיע שלמעלה יש ולמטה אין היינו שהאור הוא אין ממש לגבי העצמות דכולא קמי' כלא חשיב (line 4482)

**Might and the throne (4483-4488).** Only the infinite can make something from nothing. The shofar draws the might of the Essence into Malchut, so that Malchut's throne of judgment becomes a throne of mercy: > ההתהוות דמל' בבחי' יש מאין שזהו דוקא בכח הא"ס הכל יכול (line 4483) > ולאתקנא כורסיא הוא להמשיך בחי' כח וגבו' דעצמות א"ס להיות תיקון הכסא (line 4484)

**"I, I erase your sins" (4489-4516).** The two knowledges are in the light itself. The light is nullified in itself because it cleaves to and feels the Essence (4492-4500). Yet "the Essence does not come into the light at all": > דבחי' העצמות ממש אינו בא בהאור (line 4493)

We learn what "no comparison" means only from the created *yesh*, which only the Essence can make: > שהרי בכדי להיות מציאות יש זהו מבחי' העצמות דוקא (line 4494)

The four kinds of the light's *bitul* are these. The first is feeling the Essence. The second is existing only by cleaving, like sunlight, which is *ein zulatecha* (4501-4503). The third is *efes biltecha*, "even with the Essence there is only the Essence" (4503 s.1). The fourth is holding the Essence's own true knowledge (4505). One note overturns the usual order of rank: > לפי המבו' כאן י"ל מפני שאמיתת כח העצמות הוא בהתהוות היש דוקא (line 4504)

The deepest "there is none else" belongs to the created *yesh*, not to the light. Then the *ayin* of the created *yesh* is shown to be a glimmer of a glimmer, the name of action (Elokim) clothing the name of essence (Havayah) (4506-4511). "Looking" is from afar and "seeing" is from near, the hidden God in every creature (4512). In the higher knowledge the world's concealments hide nothing: > אבל בד"ע לא יש שם העלם ואין שם יניקה כו' כי אינו מתעלם לצורך ההתהוות וכל ההעלמות דהעולם אינם מעלימים ומסתירים כלל (line 4513)

So forgiveness comes only from there, drawn by teshuvah from the depths: > אבל בבחי' ד"ע אין הפגם נוגע ומשם נמשך להיות נושא עון (line 4515)

## Sukkot and Shemini Atzeret (4517-4571): Atzilut, the four species, the Name before creation

**Atzilut is the higher knowledge (4519-4522).** Atzilut comes as the hidden revealed, not as something from nothing, so all of it is one with the Ein Sof light. Within Atzilut there is *ein zulato*, and above it *efes bil'ado*: > ובאצי' הוא בחי' אין זולתו ולמעלה מאצי' הוא בבחי' אפס בלעדו (line 4520)

Yet Atzilut is also a "world" (4520 s.1). Its sefirot are defined, and even "He" in "He and His life are one" is light, not the Essence (4522).

**Why ten (4523-4527).** The One, "not in number", would spread without end: > דלהיותו חד בבחי' יחיד ע"כ בחי' התפשטותו הוא בבחי' ספי' אין קץ (line 4524)

Ten come so that bounded worlds can be made. Why not make creatures straight from the tzimtzum? Because the intent is revelation: > והרי הכוונה היא שיהי' ביטול היש ושיהי' גילוי אלקות בעולם (line 4526)

**The soul's garments (4528-4536).** Intellect and emotion are "essential garments" of the soul, not its essence, since the essence does not change (4531). Contemplation binds the soul to its root (4530). The sefirot are the Ein Sof light's garments, and it hides in them because they are defined (4534). The lights of Atzilut are like branches: the fruit grows on them, not on the trunk (4536). Every mediator holds both *ayins* (4537).

**The four species (4539-4542).** Torah too has outer and inner, lower and higher Chochmah (4539). The waving draws the higher knowledge into Malchut and BYA. The four species were chosen for their oneness, and oneness comes from *bitul*: > וידוע דאחדות הוא מצד הביטול (line 4540) > דכמו שהן בגשמיות יש בהם בחי' האחדות, ועי"ז יהי' המשכת בחי' ד"ע במל' ובעולמות בי"ע (line 4542)

**The inner and outer *kav* (4545-4553).** Even Atzilut is "for the worlds" (4545-4546). Revelation "from" the Essence lights the worlds. Revelation "of" the Essence only reveals the Essence: > וגילוי העצם הוא מה שעצמות מגלה אור לפי אופן העצמות כו', ואין ענינו להאיר העולמות רק לגלות עצמותו (line 4550)

**"He and His Name alone" (4554-4568).** Before creation "His Name" was also simple oneness (4555). A name is for others, and a creature can call only the Name, not the Essence (4556). Man's thought makes nothing. His thought, "I will reign", made everything, in one thought, "all seen in one glance" (4558-4560). But kingship is a great descent: > כי על מי הוא מולך אחרי שכל העולמות במח' א' נבראו וכולא קמי' כלא חשיב (line 4562)

"His place" is the power of thought, united with the Essence (4563, 4566). The outer *kav* does not show the worlds as nothing. On the contrary, they take place: > שאינו מאיר בזה איך שהעולמות הן כלא חשיב כ"א אדרבא שהעולמות תופסים מקום (line 4567)

The inner *kav* goes to Jewish souls and Torah (4568). Kingship itself is drawn only by souls below, because of their root in the Essence (4568 s.1).

**The eighth day (4570-4571).** The seventy bullocks refine the nations with the outer light. The true *ayin* is above refinement and belongs to Israel alone: > אבל בחי' פנימי' האור בחי' האין האמיתי הוא למעלה מענין הבירורים (line 4571)

## Bereishit to Vayera (4572-4681): the two unions, heaven and earth, seeing

**The two knowledges in man (4574-4578).** They are the higher and lower unions of Havayah and Elokim. Daat is recognition, not information. "Know the God of your father" asks for recognition (4575-4576). Even the lower knowledge's hiding is a drawing, from mercy (4577). The soul's work is to light the dark: > וכידוע ונת' ג"כ לעיל דתכלית ירידת הנשמות למטה הוא להאיר את החשך שלמטה (line 4578)

**Thought, speech and the letters (4579-4586).** Thought is one with the soul. Speech is a separate garment and divides. So too Z'A's vessels are "colors of the light", while Malchut's become things (4585).

**Havayah in Adonai, and Adonai in Havayah (4587-4592).** The hiding is not a true hiding: > וגם זאת יבין איך שההעלם אינו העלם אמיתי (line 4587)

The true higher knowledge is recognition of the Essence by the soul's essence, because it is its root: > אך אמיתי' ענין ד"ע הוא כמא' תכלית הידיעה שלא נודע והוא להכיר ולהרגיש את הא"ס שלא נודע בבחי' השגה כלל (line 4589) > וכמו"כ היא ההכרה דעצם הנפש שמכיר ומרגיש עצמות א"ס מפני שזהו שרשו (line 4590)

Nature and miracle are the lower and higher knowledge (4593). Creation is told in the Torah because refinement from below also needs Torah's light (4594).

**Heaven and earth, and practical mitzvot (4596-4623).** "Heaven above and earth below" answers the "two authorities". It also answers the nations, who grant that spiritual service draws Godliness but deny it of physical mitzvot. That view comes from reason, while faith is the soul's "*mazal* that sees" (4598-4599). The Essence, removed from all worlds, shines equally in BYA and in Atzilut: > דאוא"ס עצמות המאציל להיותו מובדל בערך מן העולמות גם מעולם האצי' וע"כ הוא מאיר בבי"ע כמו באצי' בשניהם בשוה (line 4601) > הרי הרצון דאוא"ס ב"ה מאיר בגילוי בהמצוה בלי שום הסתר פנים (line 4602)

Man is lowest because his root is highest (4616). The physical mitzvah becomes a vessel (4617), and the light's very substance is drawn (4617 s.1). In the many concealments His providence shows most: > אבל באמת הוא טעות גדול שהרי אדרבה בריבוי ההסתרים רואין השגחתו ית' ביותר (line 4618) > ואדרבה עיקר ההמשכה הוא ע"י העבודה למטה במצות מעשיות שזהו אמיתי' רצונו ית' (line 4619)

Beit Hillel says "earth first": the deed draws light even into Torah (4622).

**The infinite light and the measured light (4625-4650).** The tzimtzum teaches that its own aim is revelation (4628). If the infinite light were revealed in the void, it would be taken as simple and existence would seem the novelty (4629), and so it will be in the future (4629 s.1). The infinite light surpasses even the inner *kav* in being unmeasured. Yet only the Essence's "infinity" negates everything: > ומה שעצמות הוא א"ס היינו שלילת הכל והוא שלילת הגבול ושלילת הבל"ג שלילת החיוב ושלילת השלילה (line 4633)

Black fire lights the dark by hiding, and white fire is above the dark (4643-4646). The smell of prayer and teshuvah reaches the Essence (4647). Surrounding light revealed in filling light is redemption: > וכאשר גילוי סוכ"ע בממכ"ע עי"ז הוא הגאולה (line 4648)

**Seeing and lovesickness (4653-4667).** Grasp satisfies. Seeing never does: > משא"כ בראי' מה שהוא רואה אלקות אינו מרווה צמאונו בשום ענין (line 4655)

Something from nothing has no likeness in man or in angels, so to see a *yesh* is to see the power of the infinite: > והרי כאשר אנו רואין מציאות יש הרי אנו רואין בזה כח הא"ס המהווה את היש (line 4657) > ומה שנראה מהעולם הוא בחי' עצמות א"ס (line 4659)

The discipline of contemplation is this: grasp the details, reach the extract point, then recognition, then "confirmation", which places in the mind what is wholly above mind: > והתאמתות היא הנחה בשכל מה שלמעלה מן השכל לגמרי (line 4665)

One who sees the King cannot turn his mind from Him. The golden calf came from falling from sight to hearing (4667).

**Teshuvah heals the partition (4668-4681).** The world is placed in man's heart: > דתכלית הכוונה הוא בכדי להמשיך גילוי אלקות בעולם (line 4670)

Only drawing from the Essence heals the source of the sickness (4672-4673). In the future the two Havayahs will be joined and every partition healed: > וכאשר יהי' חיבור הב' שמות הוי' ולא יהי' הבדל ביניהם כלל אז יתרפאו כל המחיצות עד גם המחיצה שבין העליונים והתחתונים (line 4675)

Circumcision and Avraham's trials removed the covering. The higher knowledge is "a secret to the world": > אבל ד"ע למעלה יש ולמטה אין דכולא קמי' כלא חשיב זהו סוד לגבי העולם (line 4680)

## The written continuation, first half (4683-4870): "there is none else"

**Havayah and Elokim are one (4683-4685).** Each holds the other's intent: > ובהצמצום דש' אלקים כלול הש' הוי' דכוונת הצמצום אינו בשביל להעלים ולהסתיר כ"א בשביל הגילוי (line 4683)

**"Night will shine like day" (4686-4693).** This is the pivot of the range. After the refinements, Elokim itself will shine. Not the moon receiving from the sun, but the concealment shining of itself: > אך מ"ש אין עוד הוא שלאחר גמר הבירורים יאיר ש' אלקים עצמו והוא דש' אלקים שהוא בחי' העלם יהי' בבחי' גילוי כמו ש' הוי' ממש (line 4686) > אמנם מ"ש ולילה כיום יאיר הוא שהלילה עצמה תאיר והיינו שההעלם יאיר מצ"ע לא מצד שיקבל מבחי' האור והגילוי (line 4688)

This never was, not even before the tzimtzum. Then only the light shone, through the *reshimu* letters. In the future the letters themselves will shine, and so the body of creatures will be Godliness: > אבל ההתחדשות שיהי' לעתיד הוא שהאותיות דרשימו הן עצמן יאירו (line 4691) > ממילא גם גוף מציאות הנבראים יהיו אלקות כו' שזהו עיקר החידוש דלעתיד שהגוף הגשמי יהי' אלקות ממש (line 4692)

The reason is that the power of concealment is itself the Essence: > ודוקא כח ההעלם דש' אלקים הוא בחי' עצמות א"ס שז"ע ומבלעדי אין (line 4693)

**Gan Eden and the world to come (4698-4712).** The reward is for the deed, not its spiritual side (4701). The Essence is drawn only into what is not a vessel: > וכמ"ש במ"א בענין המשכת העצמות שזהו דוקא בדבר גשמי שאינו בבחי' כלי (line 4703)

"I am first and I am last": the Maker of Chochmah from nothing and the Maker of the physical from nothing are the same, without change (4709). Worlds come only from Malchut, which is separate. Whatever comes from the essence comes as separate: > דכן הוא בכל דבר שבא מן העצמי ה"ז בא בבחי' נבדל דוקא (line 4712)

**The dwelling and the delight (4713-4724).** The will of Ein Sof is for the *yesh* itself: > כי הרצון דאוא"ס ב"ה הוא בהתהוות היש דוקא (line 4713)

The Keter of Malchut of Ein Sof may be the delight in Israel's Torah and mitzvot and in the dwelling below (4723). And to the Essence no tzimtzum hides: > ולגבי עצמות אוא"ס אין הצמצום הראשון שלפני אצי' וצמצום האחרון שלפני ברי' ויצי' מסתיר כלל וכלל (line 4724)

**Innate, felt, understood (4734-4793).** This is a long psychology of the emotions. Most of it is *structural* and tagged F06. The unity ties that come out of it are these. First, wholeness of heart is essential and so cannot be weakened: > משא"כ התמימות שבלב אין בזה שום מיעוט וחלישות להיות שזהו עצמי והעצמי אינו מתבטל ואינו מתחלש משום דבר (line 4753)

Second, inner mind contemplates the Essence as holy and separate, and its light shines in the heart unfelt (4769-4770). Third, Chochmah is *ayin* and its task is to make every *yesh* into *ayin*: > והוא שהחכ' הוא בחי' אין ומה בעצם וענינה הוא לעשות כל יש לאין (line 4781)

Fourth, both readings of Shammai and Hillel are true, since Chochmah's *ayin* holds both (4783). Fifth, the higher fear comes from how the Essence is found below as above (4785).

**The higher will and love "with all your might" (4803-4870).** Rabbi Akiva's martyrdom shows a delight above even hidden reason (4805). In Arich there is no change: "I have not changed" (4807). Love "with all your might" is the soul's essential love. Its first level is thirst to be included in the Essence: > המדרי' הא' היא כאשר הוא בתוקף וההתפעלות בנפש בצמאון גדול להיכלל בעצמות אוא"ס ב"ה שרשו ומקורו (line 4829)

Godliness becomes one's own self, "God is my portion, says my soul" (4838). Then Torah laws show their secret, as Rashbi saw (4841-4842), and mitzvot draw the Essence into Malchut (4843). Souls at their root are above Torah: > והגם דאורייתא וקוב"ה כולא חד (ועמשנת"ל ד"ה שופטים הב') כ"ז הוא בבחי' הגילוים מן העצמות (line 4857) > ונמצא דמעלת התשובה היא למעלה הרבה מהתורה (line 4860) > וכ"ז רק מצד מעלת הנשמות מצ"ע שהן מושרשים בעצמות א"ס (line 4862)

Self-sacrifice is yechidah, one will only: > והוא בחי' יחידה שבנפש דפי' וענין יחידה הוא שהיא בבחי' רצון א' לבד ואין לו שום רצון זר כלל (line 4865)

## The written continuation, second half (4871-5017): joy, the *reshimu*, the vessel turned to light

**Sated (4871-4892).** The Temple and Eden reveal Havayah (4871). Satiety is drawing from the Essence, which is no source at all (4879-4880). Torah "baked" in fiery love becomes truly one with the soul (4883). There is a Torah above refinement, given as a gift, in which one becomes truly one and the essential shines (4885-4886).

**Joy breaks bounds (4895-4916).** The essence of a power is unbounded. Only its revelations are bounded (4897). Joy in the soul's essence lifts it above all its powers: > כמו שעצם הנפש מושללת מכל הכחות גם מהכחות העצמיים רק מה שהיא נושאה בעצמה כל הכחות שאינם שם בבחי' מציאות כלל וכלל (line 4898)

So the supreme joy, "God rejoices in His works", breaks the "line of measure" and reveals light down to the lowest (4901-4902). In the future this will be nature, not wonder: > שכל הנשמות גם הנשמות שהן בבחי' יש מאין כו' וגם בהתלבשותן בגופים כו' יהיו כלים לגילוי עצמות א"ס (line 4905)

The joy below causes the joy above to be revealed (4908). Joy without a mitzvah is only ascent. The mitzvah is the vessel: > והמצוה היא כלי להכיל כל בחי' פנימיות ועצמות דאוא"ס שבהעלם (line 4907)

**Vessels above lights (4917-4934).** Here is the reason behind the whole written section. Vessels come from the Essence, lights only from "Ein Sof". "Infinite" is a description of light, not of the Essence: > כי שרש ומקור שיתהוו בחי' כלים בנאצלים הוא בחי' עצמות ומהות א"ס ב"ה, ושרש ומקור האורות הנאצלים הוא מבחי' א"ס בלבד (line 4920)

The *reshimu* keeps the whole light as it was before the tzimtzum (4926). Its point may be rooted in the Name contained in His Essence as "ability" (4930). The proof is from reason. That light comes from the Essence we grasp. That a "thing" comes from it we accept only because He is all-powerful: > ומציאות הכלים מהעצמות מוכרחים אנו לזה שהוא ית' כל יכול להוות ג"כ מציאות דבר (line 4931)

**Mitzvot, halachot and the exchanged letters (4935-4959).** The deed is the inner essence of the supreme will, so it draws down (4936). Halachot clothe the substance of the supreme Chochmah (4940-4947): > נמצא כשתופס האתרוג ומנענעו כהלכתו ה"ז תופס ממש חיותו המלובש בו מנוק' דאצי' המיוחדת באוא"ס המאציל ב"ה (line 4943)

In the word *mitzvah*, the letters *yud-heh* of Havayah are hidden by exchange (at-bash, into *mem-tzadi*). They are "the hidden things". Yet the whole Essence is in them: > ויש בזה באמת כל בחי' העצמות רק שבא בבחי' התלבשות (line 4954)

In the future all concealment will be revealed. The body will stand above the soul: > וכידוע שלעתיד יהי' הגוף למעלה מהנפש עד שהנפש יקבל אור וחיות מהגוף (line 4957) > אבל בחי' המל' תהי' בבחי' התגלות העלם העצמי ממש כו', וזהו עיקר הענין שהכלי תהי' בגילוי מפני שיתגלה בה שרשה העצמי (line 4959)

**The world depends on Israel (4960-4975).** The inner union, drawn by mitzvot, gives the outer flow of abundance, as a soul gives life to a body (4960). Now, in exile, the flow depends only on physical mitzvot (4962). Without the inner intent of creation, the outer would not exist at all: > והיינו מפני שתכלית הכוונה הפנימיות בהתהוות העולמות הוא שיהי' תוס' אור וגילוי בעולם, ולולא הכוונה הפנימיות הזאת לא הי' ההמשכה דבחי' החיצוניות כלל (line 4963) > ואדרבא עיקר הגילוי יהי' בהגשמי עצמו (line 4965)

**The vessel turned to light (4976-5000).** A vessel that draws light gets only outer light. The Essence comes when the vessel itself becomes light: > אך הענין בזה הוא שזהו ע"י שהכלי גופא נהפך לאור והיינו שההעלם נעשה גילוי (line 4977)

This needs souls, who are also vessels, to reveal their hidden essence (4978-4979). "You" is the Essence, where hiding and revealing do not apply. "He" is the light hidden in itself (4981). What is beyond revelation cannot be reached by any raising from below. From there comes only truly new light (4991-4992). Mitzvot are outer and draw the outer; Torah is inner (4993-4996). The higher the light, the lower it reaches (4998). And teshuvah from love reveals the hidden essence: > תשובה מאה"ר שהרע עצמו נהפך לטוב והו"ע שהזדונות נעשים כזכיות כו' וכמשנת"ל, שעי"ז נמשך ומתגלה בחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס (line 4999) > והוא שהגופים יהיו אלקות ממש (line 5000)

**"His adversaries will be broken" (5001-5017).** Holy severities, overstrict, give rise to harsh ones. Heat of love and heat of anger come from one cause: > הענין הוא דהכל בא מסיבה אחת והוא בחי' חום הטבעי שברתיחת הדמים אך שכלול מדבר והפוכו (line 5012)

Kindness overcomes judgment only by rising above both lines, to inner Keter, where the adversaries have no hold: > דמבחי' פנימיות הכתר דשם אין להם אחיזה כלל ואין משם שום הסתעפות כלל וכלל (line 5016)

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The two knowledges are in God, not only in us.** Tanya II 7 gives the higher and lower knowledge as two ways of knowing the one truth. The Rashab places both in the divine light itself, as the two *ayins* (4492, 4512, 4519-4520). The lower knowledge is how the glimmer that makes the *yesh* "sees" itself.
- **The deepest "there is none else" is in the *yesh*.** The Tanya's *bitul* ladder puts the light above the creature. Here the true power of the Essence is in making *yesh*, so the true "none else" belongs to the *yesh* (4494, 4504).
- **The vessel above the light.** Tanya 35 and Iggeret HaKodesh 20 give the light's precedence and the vessels' union with it. The Rashab says the vessels' root is the Essence and the lights' root is only "Ein Sof" (4920, 4931). The goal is not more light in the vessel but the vessel turned to light (4977).
- **The concealment itself will shine.** Tanya 36 says the future is revelation below. The Rashab says it will be Elokim, the *reshimu*, Malchut and the body themselves shining (4686-4693, 4957-4959, 5000). The body will give life to the soul.
- **Souls above Torah.** Tanya 4 and 23 stress "Torah and the Holy One are all one". Here that is true "in the revelations", but in the Essence souls are above Torah, and teshuvah proves it (4855-4862).
- **Joy as the power that breaks the line of measure.** The Tanya (chs. 26, 31, 33) makes joy a condition of service. The Rashab makes joy of a mitzvah the cause of joy in the Essence, which breaks the ordered chain (4901-4908).
- **Recognition and "confirmation".** Tanya 3 and 42 ask for Daat, binding the mind. Here recognition is the soul's essence knowing its root (4590, 4664), and "confirmation" places in the mind what is above mind (4665).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 8 lines are *none*: the headings (4489, 4517, 4543, 4572, 4595, 4624, 4651, 4682). - Most of 4734-4762 and 4773-4791 is a psychology of emotions (innate, felt, understood; nursing and bar mitzvah; the two cries). It is marked *structural* and tagged F06 or F08. The Rashab ties it to unity only at its ends, in the Essence as holy and separate (4769), wholeness as essential (4753), and Chochmah as making *yesh* into *ayin* (4781). - Kabbalistic mechanics without a direct tie: Atzilut's worlds and palaces (4521), the fillings of the Name and the horse of Sag (4815), the two unions of Abba and Imma (4794-4796), the *at-bash* exchange (4948-4951), and the souls of the Baal Shem Tov and the Ari (4876-4878). - The anatomy of evil men and harsh severities (5004-5007) is structural. The tie comes only at 5014-5016. - F05 (200) overstates, as in the earlier ranges. Many tags mark "the essence of the soul", "the essence of the power" or "the essential light" inside a parable, where the Rashab applies it to the Divine Essence. - 5017 breaks off mid-sentence, on God "sitting" to judge.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Is the *ayin* of the created being truly nothing?** "Toward the Essence the light is truly nothing" (4491). The source of the created *yesh* is called *ayin* only because it is "not a thing" and ungraspable (4478, 4506). The same word carries both meanings, and the Rashab keeps both.
- **Which *bitul* is higher?** *Efes biltecha* in the light is "far higher" than *ein zulatecha* (4503). Yet the true *ein od* is in the *yesh* alone (4504). Both are said, three lines apart.
- **Mitzvah or soul?** Mitzvot are the essential will, a vessel for the whole Essence (4907, 4954). Yet mitzvot alone draw only outer light, and only the soul's self-sacrifice turns the vessel to light (4978, 4993-4997). In the same way, the deed alone gives the world to come (4702), but upper Eden needs intent (4937).
- **Torah or mitzvah?** Mitzvot are rooted above Torah, as vessels are above lights (4935). Mitzvot are the outer skull and Torah the inner brain, so mitzvot draw only outer light (4993-4995). The Rashab says both, a few pages apart.
- **The patriarchs.** Their mitzvot were spiritual, so "the sons surpass the fathers" (4701). Yet the patriarchs had deeds, and their deeds were the supreme mitzvot themselves, and they tasted the world to come (4936, 4966).
- **Seeing now or only in the future.** Souls in bodies can see the divine *ayin* now (4481), and one may "see the King" (4667). Yet the eye is never sated until the future (4655, 4666).
- **Chochmah's *ayin* and Binah's *yesh*.** Binah's task is to make the emotions real (4781). Yet the highest love "with all your might" belongs to Binah (4822, 4837). The Rashab solves this by the depth of the mind, and the tension stays in the text.
