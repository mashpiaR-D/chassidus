## The shape of the range

The range starts at the last page of *UMikneh Rav* (Matos-Masei 5666) and runs through the High Holiday and Sukkos maamarim of 5667, stopping in the middle of *BaYom HaShemini Atzeres*.

The range is one long argument in three stages. 1. **The servants (3948-4088).** There are three ways to serve: the son, the trusted servant (who serves from love) and the simple servant (who serves only under the yoke). The simple servant, the lowest, reaches highest. He has no existence of his own, so his work is his master's own work. 2. **Why the lowest reaches highest (4089-4400).** "Their end is wedged in their beginning." The Essence ("the Beginning") is found in the end, in Malchus, in the earth and in the practical mitzvah, more than in the "spreading head". So only Malchus, and only the refining done from below by toil, draws "an actually new light" from the Essence itself. 3. **Two kinds of nothing (4401-4518).** Chochmah's bitul is only weakness. It gives way before what is above it. Keter's nothing is "bitul in actual existence", because Or Ein Sof itself shines there. Sukkos and Shemini Atzeres draw this essential bitul inward.

F08 (bitul) and F05 (the Essence) lead the face counts by a wide margin, with F11 (refining) and F09 (the end in the beginning) next. The range's claim is that the Essence is reached by the self that is *least* present, and in the place that is *lowest*.

## Eileh HaDevarim and Acharei (3948-4088): the servant who is no one

The Rashab sets out the grades. Souls of Atzilus are actually united with Ein Sof, "He and His life and His causes are one": > דאיהו וחיוהי וגרמוהי חד (line 3960)

Souls of Atzilus are "not a something or a separate thing at all, but Godliness" (F06, F02): > שאינן בבחי' יש ודבר נפרד כלל, כ"א בחי' אלקות (line 4045)

Souls of BYA cannot be united like that. The best of them, the "trusted servants", know the exaltedness of Or Ein Sof and give themselves to it in love: > שיודעים ומשיגים בחי' הרוממות וההפלאה דאוא"ס, איך שאוא"ס מרומם ומופלא מעולמות (line 3952)

Then the reversal. Service from love, however high, "is not called service", because the servant enjoys it and it varies. Service means doing what is against one's nature (3984). The simple servant under the yoke has no share of his own in the work: > שהעבד אינו מציאות לעצמו, ואינו מהות בפ"ע כלל מן האדון (line 3999)

His whole existence is the master's existence, so the master's pleasure is his pleasure: > מאחר שהוא הוא עצמו של האדון, שאינו שום מציאות לעצמו כלל, וכל מציאותו הוא רק מציאות האדון (line 4027)

His work is therefore called by the master's name. It is the master's own work: > שהאדון עצמו הוא שעשה את הדבר, אלא שעושה ע"י עבדיו (line 4035)

That is why "walking after God", the lowest level, leads to "and to Him you shall cleave". It is the cleaving of essence to essence (F08, F05): > שזהו דביקות עצם הנפש בעצמות אוא"ס, דביקות עצם בעצם (line 4086)

The simple servant's toil makes something new. The labour turns coarse nature "from one essence into another" (4070). His refining is "making something from nothing", and it draws down "a new drawing-down not by gradation at all" (F11, F01): > שמחדש דבר ועושה יש מאין. ועי"ז ממשיך בח" התחדשות מאין ליש, דהיינו המשכה חדשה שלא בהדרגה כלל (line 4080)

On the way, the Rashab asks whether the yoke is not also a power from above. His answer is that a king's awe falls only on those who accepted him. The kingship of the Essence, "exalted in itself", needs no people. Kingship over a people needs their acceptance (4063): > זהו בחי' מלוכה מצד עצמותו ית' כמו שהוא מרומם ומנושא מצ"ע (line 4063)

## VeHayah Ki Savo (4089-4180): bread from the earth, and the end wedged in the beginning

"By the sweat of your brow you shall eat bread" is a curse, and it is also the source of the greatest blessing (4109-4110). The general flow of livelihood comes down ready-made from Rosh Hashanah. The second flow "is renewed every moment really from nothing to something", and only toil brings it (F01): > שמתחדשת בכל עת ושעה מאין ליש ממש, והוא ע"י עבודה ויגיעה דוקא (line 4103)

The seed must rot before it sprouts. Newness from nothing needs the nothing first: > שא"א להיות כ"א ע"י ביטול היש לאין בתכלית, כרקבון הזריעה (line 4108)

A blessing only draws down what already exists in the root. Prayer asks for "a new will not by the chain of worlds" (4125). Here the range gives its sharpest definition of what is new. Anything hidden and then revealed is not new, not even the source of the line (kav) itself. Only the light that comes from the Essence without passing through hiding and revealing is new from its root (F05): > אבל אור הנגלה מבחי' עצמות אוא"ס שלא ע"י בחי' העלם וגילוי (line 4134)

Why can only Malchus create from nothing, and not Chochmah? Chochmah to Binah, and intellect to emotion, is only cause and effect, revealing what was already included above (4143). Malchus has a power the head of Atzilus lacks: > מפני כי נעוץ תחלתן בסופן וסופן בתחלתן (line 4151)

The physical earth shows the same thing. The heavens have not added a single star since creation. The earth brings forth something new every year (4153-4172). The maamar ends with the dwelling below: > היינו שיהי' גילוי עצמות אוא"ס למטה (line 4175)

> שעל ידי זה ממשיכים גילוי בחי' עצמות א"ס למטה, שז"ע דירה בתחתונים (line 4178)

## Ki HaMitzvah HaZos (4181-4251): "Beginning" means the Essence

This maamar gives the metaphysics behind the whole range. A person's delight in the finished building was already there at the first stirring of his will. At the end of the deed the will "returns to his very essence" (4201). "Beginning" (techilah) can be said only of the Essence: > שזה שייך רק על העצמות, דמה שאינו עצמות אינו שייך לומר שהוא קודם לכל (line 4203)

The Essence is not a source. It is that from which everything is found, and it stays utterly separate from what it brings into being (F05, F01, F04): > אלא שזהו בחי' העצמי שממנו נמצא הכל, דמאמיתת המצאו נמצאו כל הנמצאים (line 4209)

> אבל הוא בבחי' הבדלה לגמרי, והיינו שהעצמות מובדל לגמרי מהדברים הנמצאים ממנו (line 4209)

So "Or Ein Sof" cannot be called "the light of the Beginning". It is not true that giving light is all His Essence is: > אבל בעצמותו ית, הרי לא זהו כל העצמות מה שהאור נמשך ממנו (line 4224)

Everything in the "spreading head", even the lights before the tzimtzum, "has nothing of the Essence at all" (4232). Malchus is rooted in the very Essence (4226), and to create something from nothing takes the power of the Essence alone: > דענין התהוות יש מאין הוא בכח העצמות דוקא, שמציאותו מעצמותו, ואינו עלול ח"ו (line 4240)

The application is that the practical mitzvot are "not wondrous and not far", because they are not in the lights at all: > כ"א שרשן הוא בבחי' העצמות ממש (line 4250)

> דאם היו בבחי' האורות והגילוים לבד, לא היו יורדים כל כך למטה (line 4250)

## Tiku and Kadosh Atah (4252-4400): the lower thing itself a vessel

There are two ways to refine. Tzaddikim refine from above. Light shines and the lower thing ceases to exist by itself. The simple servant refines from below. He clothes himself in the thing, and "the spark itself is roused to rise" (4258, 4264). Only the second fulfils the intention of the dwelling below, because only it makes the lower thing itself a vessel: > היינו שהדבר התחתון ממש יהי' כלי לאלקות (line 4290)

> היינו שהדברים התחתונים מתבטלים ממציאותם, ולא שנעשו כלים לאלקות (line 4295)

The refining starts with the Godly soul lowering itself to the animal soul's level. It contemplates what natural intellect can hold (F01, F12): > איך שבאמת יש אור וחיות אלקי בכל דבר, והעיקר הוא האלקות (line 4260)

The mixing of the names says the same about the future. Adnut inside Havayah means the lower world keeps its existence and still grasps the Essence (F02): > והיינו שיהי' מציאות המטה, והמטה ישיג בחי' עצמות א"ס (line 4305)

A soul holds the Essence's power in the same measure that it went down: > דוקא כמו שירדה למטה מטה, יש בה כח העצמות דוקא (line 4314)

Kadosh Atah names the level these refinements reach: the light "included in His very Essence". That light is not a being of light at all. It is "He and His Name are one". Only the yoke reaches it: > שאין זה בבחי' מהות ומציאות אור כלל וכלל (line 4392)

The prayer "there is no God besides You" becomes a statement about the tzimtzum. The spreading light is hidden by the contraction. When the essential hiddenness is revealed, nothing can hide it (F05, F03): > ולזאת אין אלק' מבלעדיך, שאין כאן שום העלם וצמצום כלל, ומאיר האור בגילוי למטה מטה (line 4399)

Along the way comes the distinction between the "mitzvot of Havayah" (as they are in the chain of worlds) and "My mitzvot" (as they are in the Essence), which only the simple servant draws down (4383, 4387). The good name also rises above the three crowns, into "His essential Name" (4341).

## BaSukkos Teshvu and Shemini Atzeres (4401-4518): weakness against essential nothing

The last stage returns to bitul and splits it. Chochmah's bitul is real. It is why the mind can hold two opposites (4444, 4450), and why the attributes of Tikkun make room for each other where those of Tohu broke (4453, 4461). But it is bitul from weakness: > דלהיות שבכתר מאיר גילוי אוא"ס ממש, ה"ה בבחי' העדר המציאות בעצמו (line 4463)

> אדרבה, הוא בחי' תוקף האור והגילוי, והביטול הוא מצד העצם (line 4470)

The sukkah reveals Keter's nothing. Sitting in it is: > שהו"ע ההתכללות והביטול בכל מציאותו בעצמות אוא"ס (line 4472)

The Shemini Atzeres maamar then contrasts contemplative bitul with essential bitul. Contemplative bitul is only "acknowledging that there is nothing besides Him", and it fades once the contemplation stops: > ואינו אלא בבחי' הודאה, שמודה ומסכים דאין עוד מלבדו (line 4486)

> ולכן כשנפסק ההתבוננות, אינו בבחי' ביטול אז, וחוזר לישותו (line 4486)

Essential bitul is silent, "because it is not an existence at all" (4493), and in practice it is self-restraint that comes from the Godly power itself, not from reasoning (4495). The range ends inside the image of the letter Yud. Filled out, it faces Binah as "another". Alone, it is the essence of Chochmah. A bare point without the Yud "shows the total absence of existence" (4510).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The lowest service reaches highest, and says why.** The Tanya praises *iskafya* and the beinoni (the person between the righteous and the wicked). Here the Rashab ranks the simple servant, with no love, no light and no pleasure, above the trusted servant and even the son, *because* he has no existence of his own. The unity is reached by the absence of the self, and the love that seems higher still leaves a self in place.
- **"Their end is wedged in their beginning" as the reason for the dwelling below.** The Tanya (ch. 36) states the desire for a dwelling below. This range grounds it in the claim that the Essence, "the Beginning", is present in the end (Malchus, earth, the practical mitzvah, the refining done by toil) and not in the lights. The Tanya's creation from nothing (Iggeres HaKodesh 20) is cited and pushed further: only Malchus creates, *because* the Essence is wedged in it.
- **A precise meaning of "new".** Nothing revealed from something hidden is new, not even from the source of the kav. Only a light from the Essence that did not pass through hiding and revealing is new. This is a more exact tool than anything in the Tanya's Shaar HaYichud.
- **Two kinds of bitul.** Chochmah's bitul is weakness. Keter's is essential. The Tanya's bitul in Shaar HaYichud ch. 3 ("like the light of the sun in the sun") is bitul from contemplation. Here that kind is called outer ("acknowledgment"), and it returns to selfhood once the contemplation stops.
- **Refining from below versus from above.** Light from above makes the lower thing cease to exist. Refining from below makes it a vessel while it still exists. The Tanya's "bitul" of the world before God (the higher unity) is here kept distinct from the goal, which is the lower thing itself as a vessel.

## Where the tie is thin

- About half the range is the editor's apparatus. It is marked none except for seven notes of substance. - The parables are long: servants and masters, the minister and the king, a building, the rainbow, gems, the spiral staircase, the child's temper. Parable lines are marked structural. The tie is in the application, not the image. - One line is marked *reading* (4043). The pleasure in what one earns by toil is tied to the dwelling below by us, a line before the Rashab draws the link himself. - The long sections on the three crowns (4329-4341), direct and returning light (4342-4357) and the colours of Chochmah (4511-4512) are building blocks. They serve the unity only through the claim they support, that Malchus rises into the Essence.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Servant or son?** Line 3977 puts the servant above the son, because the servant binds himself by his own choice. Line 3990 says the son's bond is deeper ("actually one essence") and even his bitul purer, since he does not care who does his father's will. The Rashab keeps both: the son is higher in the bond, the servant in the pleasure he adds above.
- **Trusted servant or simple servant?** Eileh HaDevarim first praises the trusted servant as "trusted in all My house", drawing added light into all of Atzilus (3962, 3976). It then says his service "is not service at all" (3984). By 4381 the simple servant draws from the Essence itself, while all the trusted servant's added lights are "revelations only".
- **The simple servant's pleasure.** Line 3995 says he has no pleasure in his work. Line 4027 says he does, since the master's pleasure is his, but not as his own. The Rebbe's footnote (4028) says this feature exists also in the trusted servant and the son.
- **Moshe.** Moshe is the model trusted servant (3962), yet he failed at the rock (3999). Later he is the model of the essential bitul of Chochmah, "and what are we" (4477). Here Moshe stands for the "son / trusted servant" rank and also for the essential bitul that the range places highest.
- **Tzaddikim's refining.** Tanya ch. 28 says tzaddikim refine the world. The range grants this (4137-4141) but says their refining happens "by itself" from above and draws no new light from the Essence (4256, 4273). That comes only from refining under the yoke.
- **"Beginning" in Rambam.** The Rashab's own marginal gloss (4215) says that Rambam's "beginning" (Moreh I:16) is by this maamar's terms only "kedem", the hidden sefirot, not the Essence. It then notes that Moreh II:30 gives a sense that fits.
- **The previous range.** The earlier range (3378-3942) placed the Essence in the *prohibitions*, the "no", over the positive mitzvot. Here the *practical* mitzvot as a whole are rooted "in the very Essence" (4250), and "My mitzvot" are drawn by the simple servant's doing (4383). The two emphases are not reconciled in these lines.
