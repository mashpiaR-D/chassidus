## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

SOURCES.md (P1-P20) already has the Tzemach Tzedek's two lines: human intellect belongs to the animal soul (DM 54), and the omer draws light into it "slowly" (DM 61). This slice adds a lot more.

1. **Da'at is a felt sense, like feeling you are alive.** The Tzemach Tzedek says the animal soul turns only when G-dly life is *felt* the way a man feels his own life. Knowing it, even knowing it well, is "only faith", and a believer can still be a thief (DM 151).
2. **A ladder of three servants.** The Canaanite slave obeys from fear of the stick. The Hebrew slave contemplates and longs, but his traits stay as they were. The bondwoman studies deeply (iyun) and her traits change. Only deep study changes the essence (DM 157).
3. **The animal soul cannot hear "One".** The Maharash and R. Hillel both say: the contemplation of "Hashem is One" (the higher unity) does not move it. It needs "Blessed be the Name" (the lower unity), creation from nothing, and the story of the angels bowing, because its root is in those angels (TS 6108, 6112, 10864; PR 356, 782; MA 181, 486).
4. **Speak to its self-interest.** It wants what is good for it. Show it that G-d is the source of all pleasures (OHT 1207); that its loves are a child's pebbles and nuts (TS 330); that one should want the Maker, not the thing made (TS 12910); that its "life" is imagined and is dying (PR 421).
5. **Tell it who it is.** R. Hillel: the G-dly soul explains to the animal soul that its root is higher than the G-dly soul's own. A higher stone falls further. From this comes pity and a cry, and the two wills unite (PR 650, 656).
6. **Time and stages.** Love born of long contemplation lasts, like a baby carried nine months (OHT 2633). R. Yitzchak Eizik gives three stages: womb, nursing, grown (CA 46).
7. **Deed, habit, body, voice, joy.** Act joyful until it becomes true (IK 243). Faith becomes "second nature" by habit (PR 107). Charity lifts the animal soul (DM 258). The body must be "crushed and ground" like olives, or contemplation does not work (IK 1202). Pray aloud, together, for at least an hour and a half (IK 1203).
8. **The maggid.** The masses do not understand the Hebrew of the prayers. So the maggid speaks to them, "brings it close to the intellect", and his words work as much as he himself lives them (IK 410-413).

## 2. The methods

### TZ01. Show it with the eyes of flesh - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** In prayer, look "down" and grasp how the world's existence is nullified, until G-d's unity is true for you "below, in eyes of flesh". - **How to do it:** "Heart up": contemplate G-d's essence, beyond all worlds. "Eyes down": contemplate creation and its nullity, the level the animal soul can see. Do it daily ("Exodus every day"), until reaching "Hashem is One" in Shema. - **Why it works:** The animal soul grasps with human intellect, which is its own. The soul came into the body "to reveal knowledge to the animal soul" that Hashem is G-d. What was seen with eyes of flesh holds when the world tempts. - **When / for whom:** Daily prayer. Pesach gives extra strength to fix this faith "like a peg". - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's eye will later see G-d's *essence*. The animal soul is shown only the nullity of existence. - **Quotes:** - «כשנתן עיניו למטה בתפלה והשיג ביטול היש לאשורו לא יפול ח"ו כי הרי נתאמת אצלו אחדות ה' למטה בעיני בשר» — *when he puts his eyes down in prayer and grasps the nullity of existence properly, he will not fall, for G-d's unity has become true for him below, in eyes of flesh.* DM 54 (Mitzvah of eating matzah). Not OCR. - «שהנשמה נתגלית בגוף לגלות דעה את נפש הבהמית דנוגה כי הוי' הוא האלקים» — *the soul is revealed in the body to reveal knowledge to the animal soul of noga, that Hashem is G-d.* DM 54. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Tzemach Tzedek).

### TZ02. Make it felt like your own life - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Da'at means feeling G-d's life as fully as a person feels his own life. You never saw your soul, but you know it is there. - **How to do it:** (1) Notice your own life: in sleep it withdraws; when you wake it returns and you feel it in the body. (2) Do the same with the world: when you see living things, know there is life in them from the Source of life. (3) Deepen da'at until you feel the life flowing onto *you*. (4) Then the traits turn in order: anger at the evil, then mercy on your own soul, then love. - **Why it works:** Good and evil are mixed in a person. Only da'at sorts them. Intellect without da'at is "only faith". The world stops hiding when you feel it is nothing next to the life in it. - **When / for whom:** For "seed of beast" souls, who understand much but do not feel. Moshe gives da'at to them ("grass for your beast"). - **Contrast:** Souls of the "seed of man" have this feeling and serve easily. The others serve "with more difficulty". - **Quotes:** - «שמרגישים חיות האלהות בהרגשה גמורה כמי שמרגיש חיי נפשו» — *they feel G-dly life with full feeling, like one who feels the life of his own soul.* DM 151 (Hebrew slave). Not OCR. - «וכנראה בעליל שכאשר ישן מסתלק החיות וכשניעור משנתו תשוב אליו רוחו ונפשו ונרגש פעולתה בגוף» — *as is plainly seen: when he sleeps the life withdraws, and when he wakes his spirit returns and its working is felt in the body.* DM 151. Not OCR. - «אבל כשיעמיק דעת בזה וירגיש החיות השופע עליו מה' מקור החיים שזהו גילוי אלהות כשמרגיש חיי נפשו כנ"ל אזי יתהפכו מדותיו באמת» — *but when he deepens da'at in this and feels the life flowing onto him from G-d, the Source of life … then his traits truly turn.* DM 151. Not OCR. - IK 1186 (uncertain recipient), the same teaching as a habit: «דהיינו להרגיל עצמו ולהתבונן ברוחניות המחיה» — *to accustom oneself to contemplate the spirituality that gives life*; «כאשר נדמה לנו כשאנו רואים האדם אינו מושג לנו רק הבשר, ובאמת עיקר האדם הוא החיות שאין בו תמונה ודמיון» — *when we see a person we grasp only flesh, but the real person is the life, which has no image.* IK 1186. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated.

### TZ03. Speak of the physical world it knows - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** The animal soul is moved only by creation from nothing (יש מאין), the "lower eye" that sees that things exist. So the prayer dwells on physical things. - **How to do it:** In Pesukei DeZimra, go slowly over the physical creatures: "Praise Hashem from the earth, sea monsters and all depths…", "You made the heavens". R. Yitzchak Eizik: look at the sun, moon and stars and their order. The long story of G-d's praises moves even human intellect. - **Why it works:** «נה"ב לא יתפעל רק מבחי' השגה יש מאין» (MA 181). In the Tzemach Tzedek's reading of the mandrakes (dudaim), G-d as the life that fills things (memale) can be "known and grasped" by the natural soul, "from my flesh I see G-d". Sovev is faith only (OHT 4935). - **When / for whom:** Every prayer, before Shema. It also explains why we are given seventy years: to refine the animal soul. - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul is moved by grasping the *essence* ("upper eye"). That eye opens only after the animal soul is refined. - **Quotes:** - «וכדי שיתפעל נה"ב מאלקות זהו דוקא ע"י עין דלתתא בחי' השגת המציאות של בריאת יש מאין דוקא לפי שנה"ב לא יתפעל רק מבחי' השגה יש מאין» — *for the animal soul to be moved by G-dliness, it must be through the lower eye, grasping that creation from nothing exists, for the animal soul is moved only by grasping creation from nothing.* MA 181 (Vayechi 5615). Not OCR. - «וזהו שמאריכין בפסוד"ז שהוא הכל בדברים גשמי' כמו הללו את ה' מן הארץ תנינים וכל תהומות» — *this is why we dwell at length in Pesukei DeZimra, which is all about physical things, like "praise Hashem from the earth, sea monsters and all depths".* MA 181. Not OCR. - Homel: «ולכונה זו נתקן פסוד"ז באריכות סיפור שבחי גדולת הבורא ית' עד שגם השכל אנושי שבאיש הישראלי ע"י התבוננות זו דריבוי שבחי המקום ב"ה. יתפעל באהבה או ביראה» — *for this Pesukei DeZimra was set as a long telling of the Creator's praises, so that even the human intellect in a Jew is moved to love or fear by this contemplation.* CA 49 (Vayishlach). Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Tzemach Tzedek; R. Yitzchak Eizik).

### TZ04. The lower unity, not the higher - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** "Hashem is One" (the higher unity, everything is nothing before Him) does not reach the animal soul. "Blessed be the Name of His glorious kingdom" (the lower unity) does: all worlds are only a ray of a ray of His Name. - **How to do it:** Maharash: contemplate that worlds come only from "the Name of the glory of His kingdom", a mere ray. A ray is nothing next to its source. Deepen this: the source of all worlds is "nothing", so how can the "something" we see be anything? R. Hillel: in Pesukei DeZimra, contemplate how life flows into every detail, and that "the concealment itself is G-d's essence". The Tzemach Tzedek: the animal soul can be moved only by "Your back" (achorayim), where contemplation applies. - **Why it works:** Every trait has a mind that gives birth to it, also in the animal soul. That mind can understand that one must love G-d, but not the "One". R. Hillel: the "One" only cancels the animal soul's power to hide; it does not turn it. - **When / for whom:** Everyone whose animal soul is not yet refined. R. Hillel: earlier generations with higher souls did not need this step. - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul is moved by "One" itself and reaches self-sacrifice there. - **Quotes:** - «וע"י השכל יכול להבין גם הנה"ב שצריך להיות אהבה לה', אך ההתבוננות דאחד לא יכול לקבל הנה"ב» — *through the intellect even the animal soul can understand that there must be love of G-d, but the contemplation of "One" the animal soul cannot receive.* TS 10864 (Ivdu es Hashem b'simcha, 5639; Rashab's notes). Not OCR. - «עי"ז יבוא הנה"ב נפש השכלית שבו ג"כ לביטול היש ויהיה לו אהבה לאלקות ולכה"פ שלא יהי' מונע ומעכב ויסכים לביטול דנה"א» — *through this the animal soul, the intellectual soul in it, will also come to nullify its self, and have love for G-dliness, or at least not block, and agree to the G-dly soul's nullification.* TS 10864. Not OCR. - R. Hillel: «ע"י ההתבוננות איך שההסתר הוא עצמות הוי' כנ"ל ממילא נתהפך גם הנה"ט לבחי' אלקות» — *through contemplating how the concealment is itself G-d's essence, the natural soul too is turned to G-dliness by itself.* PR 361 (Tazria). Not OCR. - «ולכן כדי להיות נרוצה שנינו דהיינו שגם נה"ב תתפעל ע"י שכל המתבונן בגדולת ה' זהו רק בבחי' אחריך» — *so for "we will run, both of us", that the animal soul too be moved by the mind contemplating G-d's greatness, this is only at the level of "after You" [His back].* OHO 92844 (Shir HaShirim). OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Maharash; R. Hillel; Tzemach Tzedek).

### TZ05. Let it watch its own root bow - **Family:** understanding (with fear) - **What it is:** Before Shema we tell how the angels, the ofanim and chayos, rise "with great noise" and are nullified to G-d. The animal soul's root is in these angels. Seeing its root bow, it bows too. - **How to do it:** Say the blessing Yotzer Or slowly, as a story. Dwell on the ofanim, then the chayos, then the seraphim. Let fear fall on you from their fear. The Maharash's parable: a commoner does not grasp the king's greatness. He sees great ministers kneel before the king, and dread falls on him too. - **Why it works:** "Judgments are sweetened only at their root" (MA 486). The animal soul is physical and cannot grasp the spiritual "how and what". But it can copy those like it. - **When / for whom:** Before Shema, daily. It prepares "with both your inclinations" and "with all your soul". - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul is moved by the "One" itself. The animal soul takes its self-nullification from watching others. - **Quotes:** - «וה"ז כמשל איש המוני כשרואה את המלך הנה להיותו המוני אינו מבין כ"כ גדולת המלך כ"א ממה שרואה שרים רבים ונכבדי ארץ שכורעים ומשתחוים לפני המלך» — *it is like a common man who sees the king: being common he does not understand the king's greatness, except from seeing many ministers and nobles kneeling and bowing before the king.* TS 6112 (Basi L'gani 5633). Not OCR. - «לכן בחי' ביטול דמס"נ דבכל נפשך נלקח ממה שמתבונן איך שהאופנים וחיות הקדש הם ברעש גדול» — *so the self-sacrifice of "with all your soul" is taken from contemplating how the ofanim and holy chayos are in great noise.* TS 6112. Not OCR. - «ולכן מזכירין תחלה ביטול המלאכים, ומשמיעים ביראה כמו והאופנים וחיות הקודש ברעש גדול ועי"ז יומתקו הגבורות דנה"ב» — *so we first mention the angels' nullification … the ofanim and holy chayos with great noise, and through this the severities of the animal soul are sweetened.* MA 486 (Ko amar 5615). Not OCR. - R. Hillel: «והנה ביטול הנה"ב א"א להיות על ידי ק"ש כי זה אין יכולה לשמוע» — *the animal soul's nullification cannot come through Shema, for this it cannot hear.* PR 782 (Basukkos teishvu). Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Tzemach Tzedek; Maharash; R. Hillel).

### TZ06. Tell it its high root, and pity it - **Family:** feeling and the senses (pity) / speech - **What it is:** R. Hillel: the G-dly soul *explains* to the animal soul that the animal soul's root is far higher than the G-dly soul's root. The higher stone falls further, so it fell lowest. It could be the King's palace, and it is the one that drives the King out. - **How to do it:** (1) Explain its lofty root. (2) Explain its fall. (3) Let great pity come, on both souls. (4) A cry comes from a broken heart. (5) From this a strong will to leave the darkness is born, and the two wills become one. - **Why it works:** "Judgments are sweetened only at their root." Pity aroused below arouses unlimited mercy above. Compare DM 151: after anger at the evil, "arouse mercy on his own soul", then love. - **When / for whom:** The omer (barley is animal food). R. Hillel ties it to the waving of the omer. - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul ("Adam") is the one who waves and offers the animal soul. - **Quotes:** - «והיינו שתסביר לה אשר שרש נה"ב גבוה מאד מאד למעלה הרבה משרש נה"א» — *that is, she [the G-dly soul] explains to it that the animal soul's root is very, very high, far above the G-dly soul's root.* PR 650 (Usfartem lachem). Not OCR. - «וכידוע המשל שהאבן הגבוה נופל למרחוק יותר» — *as the known parable: the higher stone falls further.* PR 650. Not OCR. - «כשתסביר הנה"א לנה"ב גודל הרחמנות עלי' איך שתוכל להיות פלטין ודירה של מלך מה"מ הקב"ה» — *when the G-dly soul explains to the animal soul how great the pity on it is, how it could be a palace and dwelling for the King of kings.* PR 656. Not OCR. - «ואז נתאחד הרצון דנה"ב והרצון דנה"א» — *and then the will of the animal soul and the will of the G-dly soul become one.* PR 650. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (R. Hillel). Tying it to DM 151 is ours.

### TZ07. G-d is the source of all pleasures - **Family:** self-interest and good - **What it is:** The desiring power wants pleasure and life. Show it that G-d is the source of all pleasures and the life of all life. Then it wants Him. - **How to do it:** Through Pesukei DeZimra, Yotzer and Ahavas Olam, contemplate deeply, until Shema. The desire in the heart is burnt and turns. Homel: the natural soul's nature is to want what is good *for it*. Show its human intellect that nearness to G-d is "the true and eternal good", the main thing, and all else secondary. - **Why it works:** Its nature is to choose good for itself. When the bad angel sees this truth, he "answers amen against his will". - **When / for whom:** Shema and its blessings. - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul's love is natural, "like water". This love is born of contemplation, "like fiery coals". - **Quotes:** - «שגם כח המתאווה שבנה"ב שבאדם המתאווה לדברים גשמיים וצרכי גופו ותענוגות בנ"א ישוב אל ה' ויחפוץ בקרבת אלקים כי טוב שהוא מקור כל התענוגים וחיי החיים» — *that even the desiring power of the animal soul, which desires physical things, the body's needs and human pleasures, will return to G-d and want nearness to Him, since He is the source of all pleasures and the life of life.* OHT 1207 (Zos chanukas hamizbe'ach). Not OCR. The Maharash repeats this almost word for word (TS 3269). - «הנה מלאך רע בע"כ יענה אמן ומסכים ומתרצה להודות על האמת כי הוא ית' חיי החיים ומקור התענוגים» — *the bad angel against his will answers amen, agrees and consents to admit the truth, that He is the life of life and the source of pleasures.* OHT 1207. Not OCR. - Homel: «אזי מתפעלת גם נה"ט שבאדם שטבעה לחפוץ בדבר הטוב ולבחור בו שתחפוץ בקרבת אלקים» — *then the natural soul too is moved, whose nature is to want the good and choose it, to want nearness to G-d* (CA 316, Devarim); «וע"י ההתבוננות יושג לה שזה טוב לה לפי שזה הוא העיקר והכל טפל» — *through contemplation it grasps that this is good for it, since this is the main thing and all else secondary.* CA 316. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated.

### TZ08. The child's pebbles and the treasure - **Family:** understanding (mashal) / self-interest - **What it is:** The Maharash: the animal soul's mind is like a child's mind. It understands only small things. The intellectual soul shows the traits where to point: to G-d, the true good. Every other love is a child longing for pebbles and nuts. - **How to do it:** Tell it the picture: a child wants clay shards and nuts; an adult laughs. Then the second picture: everyone knows to love a treasure. The intellect tells the traits *what* the treasure is: love of G-d, Torah and mitzvos. "Seek it like silver, search for it like hidden treasure." - **Why it works:** Every Jew has love asleep inside. It "does not know what to love". The mind shows it the place. - **When / for whom:** General. - **Contrast:** Not stated here. - **Quotes:** - «השכל שבנפש החיונית אינו מבין רק דברים קטנים הן עניני עוה"ז ההוה ונפסד» — *the mind in the vital soul understands only small things: the matters of this passing world.* TS 330 (V'haya lecha l'os 5627). Not OCR. - «וכל האהבות זולתו ית' הם ע"ד שהקטן מתאוה לחרסי האדמה ולצרורות ואגוזים» — *all loves other than Him are like a child craving clay shards, pebbles and nuts.* TS 330. Not OCR. - «רק שבמשל ידוע תחלה שיש לאהוב אוצר, ובנמשל השכל מודיע שאהבת השי"ת ותומ"צ זהו האוצר» — *in the parable one already knows to love a treasure; in the lesson the mind tells that the love of G-d, Torah and mitzvos is the treasure.* TS 355. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Maharash).

### TZ09. Why want the thing made? Want the Maker - **Family:** self-interest and good / understanding - **What it is:** The Maharash: the animal soul is the power of desire. It *can* love G-d. "They just paint a bad picture in it." Its mind shows it how the desire is good for it, though it is a lie, like too much honey: sweet, and bitter at the end. Because its evil comes from a mind, it can be turned by a mind. - **How to do it:** Contemplate "How many are Your works … He calls them all by name": everything has a root and source. So why want the thing that came to be and is good for you? Want the One who makes it be. - **Why it works:** Its traits follow its reasoning. Change the reasoning and the trait follows. - **When / for whom:** General. But beware: even after turning, "don't trust yourself". - **Contrast:** The G-dly soul's mind is set on G-dliness by nature ("lift your eyes and see"). - **Quotes:** - «נה"ב הוא כח המתאווה ויוכל לאהוב אלקות ג"כ רק שעושין בו הציור רע» — *the animal soul is the power of desire, and it can love G-dliness too; only they make a bad picture in it.* TS 12910 (V'ahavta 5640; Rashab's notes). ### TZ24. ### TZ25. Be a servant: clean the house - **Family:** deed and body / above reason - **What it is:** There are two ways: son and servant. The son's way is the soul's pure expiry into G-d, as the hosts of heaven bow. Because of the animal soul, no one can reach that. So become a servant: sweep the King's palace daily, throw out the rubbish, set the table, lamp, bed and chair so the King will dwell there. - **How to do it:** Throw out foreign thoughts with force. Put your mind into the letters of Torah and prayer, even if you do not intend the meaning of the words; they are vessels for G-d's presence. Keep tzitzis, tefillin and charity properly. A daily hour of contemplating your lowliness and G-d's greatness, with Zohar or Reishis Chochmah. - **Why it works:** The King has more pleasure from a house cleaned of filth than from one always clean. - **When / for whom:** For one who cannot feel G-dliness. - **Contrast:** The "son" level belongs to the soul alone; the animal soul blocks it. - **Quotes:** - «אך מחמת נפש הבהמית אי אפשר, שאין שום אדם יכול להשיג זה» — *but because of the animal soul it is impossible; no person can attain this.* IK 1196 (uncertain recipient, Letter 3). Not OCR. - «וישים מחשבתו באותיות התורה והתפלה, אף שאינו מכוון בפירוש המלות רק שהם כלים להשראת הקב"ה» — *and put his thought into the letters of Torah and prayer, even if he does not intend the meaning of the words, only that they are vessels for G-d's presence.* IK 1196. ### TZ26. A maggid who speaks its language - **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering) / speech - **What it is:** A long letter explains the maggid. The heart is not in a man's control except by deepening da'at. The prayers say all that is needed, but they are in Hebrew and the masses do not understand, or say the words without listening. Speaker: must himself be careful in deed and in traits. Words from the heart enter the heart. Chassidim prefer one great maggid heard rarely over a lesser one heard often. Homel: it is easier to receive from a teacher before whom one is humble (CA 46). - **Why it works:** The power of the words matches the speaker's own care and good heart. - **When / for whom:** The general public, who do not contemplate on their own. - **Contrast:** Not stated. - **Quotes:** - «כי הלב אינו ברשות האדם להטותו לכל אשר יחפוץ, כי אם על ידי העמקת הדעת ועיון השכל» — *for the heart is not in a man's power to turn it as he wishes, except through deepening da'at and study of the mind.* IK 410 (Letter 33). Not OCR. - «ולפי ערך הזהירות וטוב לב הדורש כך ערך פעולתו לפעול בלב השומעים» — *according to the care and good heart of the preacher, so is his effect in the hearts of the listeners.* IK 413. ### TZ27. Eat, then pray with that strength - **Family:** deed and body (eating) - **What it is:** First one does physical things, eating and drinking, from the natural soul's wants. Then he prays and learns for G-d's sake with the strength of that food. Then all the good in the food is sorted out and rises, "like an offering". Like a plant: first leaves and husks, then the fruit. - **How to do it:** Eat with the intent to pray and learn with that strength. - **Why it works:** The good hidden in the food is refined by being used for G-d. - **When / for whom:** General (but see OHT 1225: not before morning prayer). - **Contrast:** Not stated. - **Quotes:** - «שעושה מתחלה דברים גשמיי' אכילה ושתי' מצד רצונות נה"ט ואח"כ כאשר מתפלל ולומד לשמה בכח האכילה ההיא אז מתברר כל הטוב שהי' מובלע בהמאכלים» — *he first does physical things, eating and drinking, from the natural soul's wants; afterwards, when he prays and learns for its sake with the strength of that eating, all the good hidden in the food is refined.* OHT 1156 (Ko sevarchu). Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated.

### TZ28. Bitterness at night, joy in prayer - **Family:** fear and bitterness - **What it is:** The Maharash: night is when the body and animal soul hide G-d's light and pull to this world's pleasures. Contemplating this, he is embittered over his distance and humbled. This darkness comes before the light. Then prayer, "nearness to G-d", is in great love and only in joy. - **How to do it:** At night (Tikkun Chatzos time), contemplate your distance; be bitter and humble. In the morning, pray with joy. - **Why it works:** Darkness precedes light; humility makes room. - **When / for whom:** Night and morning. - **Contrast:** Not stated. - **Quotes:** - «וכאשר יתבונן בדברים אלו יתמרמר נפשו על ריחוקו ויכנע לבבו להיות בבחי' הכנעה ושפלות» — *when he contemplates these things his soul will be embittered over his distance and his heart humbled.* TS 1982 (Vayached levavenu 5629). Not OCR. - «שהתפלה היא בחי' קרבת אלקים באהבה רבה בשמחה דוקא» — *prayer is nearness to G-d, in great love, and in joy specifically.* TS 1982. Not OCR. - **Stated / ours:** Stated (Maharash).

### TZ29. Keep the wick oiled - **Family:** habit and repetition (picture) - **What it is:** In the lamp, the wick is the natural soul (the desiring power in the heart). The oil is the G-dly soul's wisdom, contemplation not yet in the heart. The light is the G-dly flame. Without oil the flame "jumps off" the wick, because the natural soul is a coarse spirit and does not take the light inside. - **How to do it:** Keep anointing the wick with oil: steady contemplation, again and again. - **Why it works:** The flame holds the wick only through the oil. - **When / for whom:** Prayer (lighting the lamps = raising the seven traits). - **Contrast:** Not stated. - **Quotes:** - «כ"א לא הי' החכ' הי' האור קופץ מעל נה"ט מפני שהיא רוח הגסה ומסכסכת מאד» — *if not for the wisdom, the light would jump off the natural soul, for it is a coarse, very troubling spirit.* OHT 1294 (B'haaloscha). ### TZ30. Four floors of prayer (Homel) - **Family:** other (a ladder) - **What it is:** R. Yitzchak Eizik's four "prayers". Asiyah: by fixing da'at on the Root of all worlds, *force* the body and animal soul to accept the yoke in deed. ### TZ31. ### TZ32. Let it see the land is good - **Family:** self-interest and good / feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Homel, on the spies. ## 3. What does not work, and warnings

1. **Intellect without da'at.** Even one who knows G-d's greatness well, if he does not feel it, has "only faith". Such a believer can still be a thief "at the mouth of the tunnel calling on the Merciful". «משא"כ ע"י השכל לבד לא יתברר הטוב שבנפשו מן הרע» DM 151.
2. **The "One" contemplation.** It does not move the animal soul (TS 10864, 6108). R. Hillel: it only puts the natural soul to sleep; later, walking in this world's darkness, it wakes and he becomes "another man": «שזה אינו פועל רק ביטול הכח בנה"ט שלא יוכל להסתיר ואינו פועלת פעולתה רק הוא בבחי' שינה» and «ולכן אח"כ כשהולך בחשכת עוה"ז יכול להתהפך לאיש אחר מצד נה"ט שניעורה משינתה» PR 356. Also: «כי ענין אחד ואהבת אינו בחי' אתהפכא עדיין רק בחי' אתכפייא לבד» PR 334; turning is when the natural soul's love has "sweetness and delight" (PR 334).
3. **The G-dly soul's natural love.** It does not refine the animal soul. Only love from contemplation does: «ומה שהנה"ב תתברר אינו ע"י אהבה הטבעית כ"א ע"י אהבה שכלית» OHT 4431; «אמנם אהבה זו היא רק נוגע לנה"א» OHO 92923 (OCR). Even with natural love, the animal soul must at least agree (OHT 4431).
4. **Rushing.** Starting at Shema and being moved at once gives a love that is "passing" (OHT 2633). Contemplation that passes after prayer does not reach the heart's blood (OHT 4232). Without contemplation "his nature does not budge"; when it leaves, nothing remains (CA 46). When the mind withdraws, only a trace (reshimu) remains, and the natural soul follows its heart again (CA 318).
5. **The Hebrew-slave trap.** Real longing in prayer while the traits stay unchanged (DM 157). And even deep study (binah) does not turn binah's own coarseness, "a man wise in his own eyes" (DM 157).
6. **Imagery of human intellect.** A grasp built of human images and letters is "the very opposite" of the true grasp of G-dliness: «כי בחקירה אנושית אי אפשר בשום אופן כי הוא המנגד» IK 1187. Philosophers grasp G-d with human intellect and are not moved: «שהפיליסופים אינם מתפעלים מאלקות שמשיגים ושוה אצלם ידיעת אלקות כידיעת תכונת הגלגלים» CA 201.
7. **Proofs and wonders are medicine for the sick only.** «כמ"כ עניני מופתים הם שכל אנושי מק"נ כו' וא"צ כלל לסמים אלא למי שנתקלקל» OHT 3961: they are human intellect from kelipat noga; only one confused by doubts in faith needs this "drug".
8. **Without crushing the body**, contemplation does not act (IK 1202). **Without contrition**, the natural soul's agreement is imagined (CA 316).
9. **Do not trust it after it turns.** «ואף גם זאת מ"מ נק' רע דאל תאמין בעצמך ואפשר שיחזור לקלקולו» TS 12910.
10. **Melancholy.** No sad melodies in prayer (IK 244). The animal soul itself casts melancholy to confuse one's service (SHS 1163, Rayatz retelling).
11. **Doing mitzvos coldly, by rote.** «ולא בקרירות דכשעושה בקרירות ע"ד מצות אנשים מלומדה ה"ז כאלו עושה» TS 12959 (but compare TZ15, where "cold" Torah study still redeems).
12. **Know its disguises.** The Alter Rebbe and Mitteler Rebbe made chassidim wise «שחסיד יוכל להכיר את הנפש הבהמית בכל לבושיה» SHS 31 (Rayatz retelling, OCR), "the false ones and all its clever ways".
