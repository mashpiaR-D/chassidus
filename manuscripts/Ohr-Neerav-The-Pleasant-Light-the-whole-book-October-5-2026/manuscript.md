Ohr Ne'erav is the Ramak's short door into the inner Torah. The Pardes is the house. This book stands at the gate and talks to three kinds of people who stay outside: those who say there is nothing inside, those who say it is too high for them, and those who are afraid of making a mistake. Then it says why a person must come in (Part II), how and when (Part III), what it gives (Parts IV and V), a short course in the basics (Part VI), and a small dictionary of names (Part VII).

Its heart is warm. The Ramak keeps saying: come in even if you stammer, even without a teacher, even if you do not understand yet. God is glad of the stammering word.

## 2. The unity of God in this book

**He is one, but not like the number one.** "And not one as one that is counted" (ולא אחד כאחד המנוי, line 432). He is in all things, and all things are in Him: "the Creator is in all things in act, and all things are in Him in potential" (להיות הבורא יתברך בכל הדברים במעשה וכל הדברים בו בכח, line 433). If the work stops, the Worker does not stop, "and He needs none of them" (ובהתבטל הפעולה לא יתבטל הפועל, עם היותו בלתי צריך לאחד מהם, line 434). And plainly: "in truth His being and essence spreads through them" (והנה באמת מציאותו ועצמותו מתפשט בהם, line 435).

**The sefirot are His names, not other things.** "The sefirot and the names are names of the Ein Sof according to His actions" (והספירות והשמות הם שמות האין סוף כפי פעולותיו, line 438). The whole needs the parts and the parts need the whole "by the power of the true unity, and it is a wonder" (נמצא כלל צריך לפרט ופרט צריך לכלל בכח היחוד האמתי, והוא פלא, line 438). The ten "are from His essence, united in Him, and He and they are all one complete unity" (שהם מעצמותו מתיחדות בו, והוא והם הכל אחדות שלימה, line 442).

**He is their place.** "The truth is that the Ein Sof is the place of His sefirot" (אלא האמת הוא כי האין סוף מקום לספירותיו, line 449). All need Him, "and He needs none of them" (ואין סוף כלם צריכין לו והוא אינו צריך לשום אחד מהם, line 448). We cannot even say "blessed be He" of Ein Sof, for He is the One who blesses (לא יצדק אמרנו בו לא יתברך, line 441).

**Division is only in our eyes.** "All of emanation is one complete unity, and what is said of separation and cutting is in our terms, not in theirs" (אלא כל האצילות אחדות שלימה, ומה שנאמר בו פרוד וקצוץ, הוא בערכנו לא בערכם, line 457). Emanation does not make the Source less: "their earlier being will not be lacking" (שמציאותם הקודם לא יחסר, line 486). The light of Ein Sof "is clothed in them through the emanated light" (ואור אין סוף מתלבש בהם על ידי האור הנאצל, line 490). The picture: "the secret of the light, HaVaYaH, and the lamp in which are the colors, Adonai" (והיינו סוד האור יהו"ה והעששית שבה הגוונים אדנ"י, line 481). One light, many colored panes.

**Why it matters to a person.** People who deny the inner Torah "want to believe only in the wondrous unity" (ואין רצונם להאמין אלא באחדות הנפלא, line 2), and so miss what the unity means. The Ramak says the unity must be known, not only believed (§5).

## 3. States of consciousness

- **Loved while stammering** (new, RK-S27). A person wants to learn and has no one to teach him. He stumbles through words he does not understand. The Zohar quoted here: he "stammers in it with a stammer, not knowing" (ומגמגם בה בגמגומא דלא ידע, line 162), and every word rises, "and the Holy One is glad of that word and receives it" (וקב"ה חדי בההיא מלה, וקביל לה, line 164). "In her love you will always stray" (באהבתה תשגה תמיד, line 166). Close to S13 (the unnamed longing) and S41 (the hidden love wakes), but its mark is that not knowing and being received are felt together.
- **Small and loved** (new, RK-S28). The mustard seed state (§4, RK-T36). "Who am I, like a mustard seed inside the sphere of the moon" (וכי מי אני כגרגיר חרדל בתוך גלגל הירח, line 222), and then the turn: "and by this he will add great love in his soul and die in His oneness" (ובזה יוסיף אהבה גדולה בנפשו וימות באחדותו, line 223). Smallness that ends in love, not in fear alone. Links S45 (lower awe), S46 (higher awe), S59 (true lowliness), S62 (only Him).
- **The walls of darkness split** (new, RK-S29). The Zohar passage in Part V: all the King's faces are hidden behind darkness, and those who know how to unify the holy Name "split all those walls of darkness, and the King's faces are seen" (מבקעין כל אנון כותלי חשוכא, ואנפין דמלכא אתחזיין, line 395). Close to S68 (the hiding seen as His) and S74 (seen eye to eye). Its feel is a break: the dark gives way from inside prayer, not by argument.
- **Fear and joy at once.** "Fear and joy must join together in the learner" (צריך שתשתתפנה אל הלומד היראה והשמחה יחד, line 237). Even reciting the Zohar makes "joy in the soul in the fear of God" (line 426). This is S49 and S50 in a Kabbalist's voice.
- **One who never looked.** "One who did not look at the soul, how will he look in the future at the soul of the soul" (ועתה אחי הודיעני מי שלא הסתכל בנשמתא איך יסתכל לעתיד בנשמתא דנשמתא, line 83). Not a denier, "only one who never saw light in his days" (כי יש חלוק גדול בין מי שהכחישה למי שאינו מכחישה אלא שלא ראה מאור מימיו, line 83). This is S12 (the flat world) named without blame.
- **A picture of God as an old man.** "The poor man thinks God is an old man" (והנה העני חושב כי האלוה הוא זקן, line 221). A named mistake; S23 before it becomes S24.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**RK-T36. The mustard seed** (new). Lines 222-223. Picture the earth inside the heavens, the heavens inside the higher worlds, and yourself as "a mustard seed inside the sphere of the moon" (line 222). First comes awe: "from there he will add strength to his fear". Then love, because the One so great chose to love you (the text cites "I have loved you, says God"). Goal: "die in His oneness" (line 223), that is, the self given over into the One. Tanya chapter 42 and the Gate of Unity ask the same move of the mind; see §6.

**RK-T37. Two tracks and the waiting** (new). Learn in two ways. One is to "sharpen his tongue many times" (לשנן לשונו פעמים הרבה, line 262), fluency, going over the words. The other is depth at the good hours. Do not stop reciting when you do not understand: "even if it seems to the student that he does not understand, all the same he should not stop saying them from his mouth" (ואף אם יראה אל המעיין שאינו מבין, עם כל זה לא יפסיק גרסתם מפומיה, line 263). The Ramak adds: "I have tested this more times than can be counted" (ודבר זה נסיתיו פעמים אין מספר, line 263). And when a question stays open, wait: "if something in this wisdom is in doubt, let him wait, for in time it will be revealed, and the main reward of this wisdom is the waiting for the secrets" (ואם יסתפק לו ענין בחכמה הזאת ימתין כי במשך הימים יתגלה לו הענין, ועיקר שכר חכמה זאת היא המתנת הסודות, line 264). Links chazarah (review).

**RK-T38. Do a good deed to open a doubt** (new). "When a person does a mitzvah, or something like it, and then studies the hidden, the doubtful thing is revealed" (כי בזמן שיקיים האדם מצוה או כיוצא לעסוק בנסתר אז מתגלה המסופק, line 239). When stuck, stop thinking and go do one kind act, then come back. Links act.

**RK-T39. The tested hours** (new). The Ramak's best hours for deep study: "in long nights from midnight on" (בלילות ארוכות מחצות ואילך, line 235), Shabbat, Friday afternoon, festivals, Shavuot, the sukkah. "I speak from testing" (ודרך נסיון אני מדבר, line 235). Before starting, "let him purify his thought from the vanities of the time" (ואחר יטהר מחשבתו מהבלי הזמן, line 231). Links arrive and quieting.

**Fear and joy together** (line 237) and "humility rises above all" (והענוה עולה על הכל, line 238). Not a new technique. It is the guard on all the others.

**Pray for the Shechinah's sake.** The child asks for his own needs, the elder asks "if he intends in his requests the benefit of the Shechinah" (אם יכוין בשאלותיו לתועלתא דשכינתא, line 359). Some pray as sons: "there are sons whose whole intent in prayer is to unite Malchut with Tiferet" (יש בנים שכל כוונתם בתפלתם ליחד המלכות עם התפארת, line 119). Links RK-T21 and binding.

**Intent guards itself.** "One who aims his prayer in the way of truth, it is helped by itself, and the outer forces have no power to break it with idle thoughts" (כי המכוון בתפלתו על דרך האמת הדבר מסתייע מעצמו ואין כח בחצונים להפסיקו במחשבות בטלות, line 428). A comfort for S01 (scattered).

**Gerushin** (line 427): the Ramak and R. Shlomo Alkabetz went out to the fields "to engage with verses of the Torah suddenly, without study, and new things came" (לעסוק בפסוקי התורה פתאום בלי עיון, והיו הדברים מתחדשים, line 427). The full practice is in Sefer Gerushin; see that report.

## 5. The big human questions

**Can I come close if I have no teacher and do not understand?** Yes. "Even if a person cannot find someone to teach him properly, he should not hold himself back because of that" (כי גם אם לא ימצא האדם מי שילמד לו כראוי שלא מפני זה ימנע עצמו, line 184). Errors made out of longing count as good: "from much longing he looks at hidden things and cannot help stumbling, and it is counted to him as righteousness" (ומרוב חשקו צופה בדברים נסתרים ואי אפשר שלא יכשל, והוא נחשב לו לצדקה, line 114).

**Am I too small for this?** The Ramak answers with the soul: "my soul is a part of God above, and I am fit for the word of God like one of the ministering angels" (נשמתי חלק אלוה ממעל, וראוי אני אל דבר ה' כאחד ממלאכי השרת, line 89). And "every soul has its own portion in the Torah" (כי כל נשמה יש לה חלק מיוחד בתורה, line 208). No one else can learn your part.

**Is it enough to believe?** No. The first commandment is "to know that there is a First Being" (לידע שיש שם מצוי ראשון, line 212), "which means real knowing and grasping of God as far as human understanding reaches" (משמע שצריך ידיעה ממש והשגת האלהות כפי כח דעת האנושי, line 213). Love and fear grow from knowing.

**What happens to me after I die?** The soul goes where it gave light: "when a person's soul leaves, it will surely cleave to the place it lit up while alive" (כי בהסתלק האדם נשמתו בלי ספק תתדבק אל המקום שהאיר בהיותו בחיים חייתו, line 385). And "as much as a person works in the low world, so his soul cleaves in the upper world" (שאין ספק שלפי העסק שיעסוק האדם בעולם השפל כן ידבק נפשו בעולם העליון, line 339).

**Does God change when I pray?** Prayer with only the plain meaning risks "thinking that God has change and will and love" as a person does (בחשבו היות האל יתברך בעל שנוי ורצון ואהבה, line 358). The change is below and in the sefirot, never in Him. Yet what we do matters: "every union needs a waking from below" (ואולם כל יחוד צריך התעוררות תחתון, line 464), and "it is in our hands to repair it and draw it down and unite them" (ובידינו לתקנו ולהמשיכו וליחדם כראוי, line 459). Words said below repair above (כי כמו שהאדם מוציא הדברים מפיו ומתקנן כן המדות מתקנות למעלה, line 384).

**Does all this make me big?** No. The Ramak compares himself to the earlier sages "like the light of a dim star before the sun" (כאור הכוכב החשוך לפני החמה, line 345). And the loud ones who boast, "their voice roars like the waves of the sea and they do not have a quarter measure" (כי קולם יהמה כגלי הים ואין להם כדי רביעית, line 259). Nor may learning the hidden replace ordinary duties (אבל לא שיתרפה ממלאכתו מלאכת שמים עסק הדינים, line 346).

**How should I be with people?** Walk in God's ways: "like the trait of mercy, the trait of generosity, and the trait of silence" (כמדת הרחמנות, ומדת הנדיבות, ומדת השתיקה, line 320). The trait of silence is worth noting: God bears in silence, so may we.

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **Ohr Ne'erav is the Tanya's "knowing".** Line 213 (real knowing, as far as a human mind can reach) is the Ramak's version of Tanya chapter 42: "the main thing is to deepen his mind in God's greatness and fix his thought on God" (אלא העיקר הוא, להעמיק דעתו בגדולת ה', Tanya line 629). Both reject faith that stays in the head as a report. The Tanya adds the word bond (daat as connection) and makes it a daily practice before prayer.
- **The mustard seed and "fills all worlds".** RK-T36 moves from world to world outward until the self is a seed. The Tanya makes the same climb with the angels and worlds without end: "Ein Sof fills all worlds to give them life, and in each world are creatures without end" (אין סוף ברוך הוא ממלא כל עלמין להחיותם, Tanya line 736). The Ramak ends in love and "dying in His oneness". The Tanya ends in bittul (being nothing before Him) and in joy.
- **The Alter Rebbe quotes the Ramak.** In Iggeret HaTeshuvah the Tanya calls God the insulted King "as the Ramak wrote" (נקרא הקדוש ברוך הוא מלך עלוב בפרקי היכלות כמו שכתב הרמ"ק, Tanya line 961). This is Tomer Devorah's opening image, so the link between the two schools is written into the Tanya itself.
- **"He is all, and not all is He."** Ohr Ne'erav line 433 ("He is in all things in act, and all things are in Him in potential") and line 435 ("His being spreads through them") come near to the famous line. Line 434 ("He needs none of them") is its second half: the world cannot hold Him. The words "He is all that exists and not all that exists is He" are in Elima Rabbati, not here (see that report).
- **Tzimtzum.** Not in this book. The Ramak's picture is the lamp: one light, colored panes (line 481). Division is "in our terms" (line 457). Chabad agrees that the change is only from our side (Gate of Unity chapter 7), and reads the tzimtzum the same way: not literal. The Ramak gets there without the word tzimtzum.
- **Who may learn.** Lines 266-334 and 429 limit the inner Torah to the worthy and to Israel. Chabad in the Rebbe's time spread Chassidus to everyone. The guide follows D28 (universal) and does not take these limits.
- **Stammering received.** Line 164 (God glad of the stammering word) fits Tanya chapter 5 on learning's union with His will, but the Ramak's tone is softer and more personal. This is a gift to the guide's beginners.
