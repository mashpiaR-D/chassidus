## The shape of the range

The gate asks one technical question: why the sefirot are exactly ten, "ten and not nine, ten and not eleven" (line 282). Answering it takes the author through the whole structure of the worlds. At each step he stops to say that the structure is ours, not His.

- **Lines 282-309** build an anatomy of every created thing. Each has reason (mushkal), nature (mutba) and felt body (murgash). Each of these has three lines, which makes nine, and the form that shows them is the tenth. This part is almost all *structural*.
- **Lines 310-383** lift that anatomy into the worlds and into Atzilut. Here the doctrine is dense. There is one power, His. What makes it look many is Malchut, the power of "likeness and image" (tziyur vedimyon), together with the parsa (curtain) that hides. Tzimtzum, hiding and even the breaking of the vessels are only "toward us".
- **Lines 384-421** return to the count. Z'a is nine because division is nine. The unity is ten because Keter, pele (wonder), both brings the nine out and binds them. Malchut does not make eleven because she has nothing of her own.

F05 leads the count, as it did in the Introduction (lines 75-96). F02 is second, because the author's refrain is the two-sided sentence "toward us... but toward Him" (לגבי דידן... אבל לגביה). F09 is high because pele is his name for the joining of opposites. F06 is absent: the soul as part of God does not come up in this range.

## SG 1-4 (282-309): the anatomy of a creature, and why ten

Line 283 sets the frame for everything after it. From His Essence no attribute applies: > כי אין זולתו ואפס בלעדו ולית בר מיניה (line 283)

All sefirot and names are "from the side of creation". "Atzilut" has two meanings. It means separation, since attributes cannot be ascribed to His being (line 284). It also means "etzlo", beside Him, since the powers are united in Him: > כי אם מתייחד בהם ביחוד גמור (line 285)

Then comes a long analysis of creatures. It is building material, not a statement of the unity. Every fruit, animal, person and heavenly sphere has reason, nature and felt body (lines 286-290). A fourth aspect, form (temunah), has no being of its own. Yet it reveals all the others and is what tells one essence from another (line 293). This is the model for Malchut later (lines 335-338). The author uses it on purpose, so it is marked structural. Da'at is defined as "recognition" that can exist without understanding (line 297). That is the building block F12 needs, and the link to F12 is ours. The count comes to nine plus one, ten (line 301): > נמצא אין דבר במציאות שלא יהיה כלול מעשר בחינות (line 307)

Line 295 lists the fourfold pattern up to "the four letters of Havayah". It is marked structural F01, because the text lists the pattern and does not draw the conclusion.

## SG 5-6 (310-324): three worlds as a ladder of bitul

Asiyah is the world of the felt, where everything is grasped as "the tangible yesh" (line 310). Yetzirah is the world of nature. There His middot "bring into being, give life to and sustain" each creature (line 311, F01). In the same line, the love and fear of every creature is a longing for bitul: > שנכספים ליבטל למקורם ברוך הוא (line 311)

Even sweetness is, at root, "the sweetness of nullification to the Creator" (line 311). In Asiyah the Godly life is hidden, and in Yetzirah it shows in the middot (line 315, F03 and F04). Beriah is the world of intention, and it states the purpose of creation for each creature (F07): > אשר לכל נברא יש כוונה מיוחדת מהבורא יתברך להיות נגלה בהם יחודו יתברך (line 316)

Beriah is called "throne" (kisei) because it covers what is below it. It also carries F01 one world up: if its intellect withdrew "for a moment they would be as if they never were" (line 321). Line 323 says that His being is not revealed at all, and His Godliness is revealed only "from the side of the making, as creatures". Line 324 then says something the Tanya does not say in these words. Even bitul is a creature-side word: > כי בבחינת עצמותו יתברך אינו נופל לשון ביטול מאחר שאינו מושג כלל ואין זולתו (line 324)

## SG 7-9 (325-342): the unity of what cannot be compared; Atzilut

Head, body and feet cannot be compared, yet they make one man. Their joining is pele: > וקישורם ויחודם הוא בחינות פלא שהוא יתברך מפליא לעשות (line 327)

The worlds are joined the same way, "through His power, equal in all" (line 328). Line 329 is the gate's central claim, and it has two sides. The levels are real: he rejects those who think the division only seems so to us (ולא כאשר יש חושבין בזה שנדמה לנו לחילוק דרגין). Yet "although all the differences are from His Essence", they are one power, and that is what Atzilut means. Havayah is the one "who brings all the aspects into being from nothing" (line 330).

Inquiry is limited as it is in the Tanya: "before Atzilut we have no business" (line 331). Line 332 goes further. Of the Essence one cannot say "even pele". "Like the appearance of a man" (Ezekiel 1:26) supplies the analogy: a person is many levels and one man (line 333). The word "like" marks where the analogy fails (line 334, G06). The soul is changed by its body because both are created, but He is not changed: > ואפילו כל בחינות הנבראים בפנימיותם וחיצוניותם הכל הוא לבדו יתברך (line 334)

Z'a means "small face", because His power divided into levels "is called smallness relative to His Essence" (line 335). Malchut gives each power form, as letters give form to a thought. Without her, "all would be one essence and power" (line 338).

Lines 340-342 repeat the text of lines 334-337 almost word for word. The repeat looks like a copying or printing duplicate. They are indexed with the same ties and marked as repeats.

## SG 11 (343-350): Malchut, the name, and tzimtzum only toward us

Revelation grows with contemplation and with service. Whoever nullifies Asiyah to Him through love sees Yetzirah, and whoever understands the nullification of creatures sees Beriah: > וזהו תלוי בעבודה של ישראל (line 345)

"All unity depends on her" (line 346). Line 347 names the paradox openly. Each creature appears as its own thing "although there is none besides Him": > ודבר זה הוא אינו בערך השגה איך יהיה שני הפכים אלו (line 347)

Malchut is "name", and a name exists only for another. That is why He is called Havayah "from the side" of the creatures (line 347). Then tzimtzum is placed on the creatures' side. A tzimtzum in Ein Sof would mean a change in Him (line 348): > אבל לגביה יתברך אין צמצום והסתר מעלים לפניו יתברך (line 348)

To make levels in the Essence is forbidden, "for the essence of the unity depends on this" (line 349). The analogy of a person, who contracts in thought but not in soul, fails again (line 350).

## SG 12-15 (351-376): the curtain, the parable, and "all existence is His Essence"

The parsa exists "to hide the light of His Essence... so that creatures may be revealed" (line 351). Without it there would be "no place for bounded beings" (line 353). The author's main analogy is a sage who must hide his wisdom in a parable fitted to a student. His wisdom "did not change". Only the garment differs, and only as the receiver sees it (line 363). This analogy does the work in this gate that the sun and its rays do in the Tanya. The result is strong: the worlds are not copies of the sefirot but the same sefirot. > השתנותם אינו מצד העצם כי הכל כח אחד להם בלי שינוי הוא כוחו יתברך ושינוייהם הם מצד ההסתרה והדמיון (line 355) > והם הם העשר ספירות דאצילות והם הם העשר ספירות דבריאה ממש (line 359)

The breaking of the vessels came from light and vessel being "two opposed opposites" (line 365). The parsa is the railing that stops it happening again. In Atzilut, chesed cannot even be called chesed, "for chesed is only toward another" (line 366). And then: > אפילו כח היש הוא עצמותו יתברך (line 367)

Line 373 is the fullest line in the range. It holds F05, F02, F04, F09 and F12 together: > וכל העולמות וכל המציאות הכל הוא עצמותו לבדו ברוך הוא (line 373)

Memale, sovev, "outside" and "around" are one power. The gloss traces two paths. Going inward, one passes Malchut, then Z'a (the higher unity), then Arich (pele), to where one cannot say "even pele". Going outward, "the more external the yesh, the more His power is revealed", down to the physical earth, "the place of the world" and the makif of Atik: > שם נגלה כח עצמותו ברוך הוא אשר אינו בערך דרגין כלל (line 373) > כי כאשר כוחו בלי גבול ככה כוחו בגבול באין הבדל כלל (line 373)

These two sentences state, in 1820, what the SPINE attributes to later Rebbeim: F05's "as He has power in the unlimited, so in the limited", and F04's "the somethingness of matter comes from sovev". The line ends with F12: צריך לדעת.

The purpose follows (lines 374-376). The glory should be revealed "without difference even toward us". This happens through prayer, where the yesh is nullified to its source with self-sacrifice, and through Torah and mitzvot, where "His will is revealed even in limit" (line 374, F07, F08, F10). In the future the yesh "will not hide His Essence", and this will happen "precisely in physical doing" (line 376). Now the unity must be "in knowing and in your heart" (line 376, F12).

## SG 16 (377-383): the literal-tzimtzum dispute; breaking and tikkun

Line 378 states F03's kept dispute from the Alter Rebbe's side. He writes "for you" (לכם), meaning that the curtain divides toward us, not toward Him. Then: > ולא כמו שחשבו רבים להעריך הצמצום כפשוטו ממלכות דאין סוף (line 378)

He places the main tzimtzum at the parsa between Atzilut and Beriah, not above it. Anyone who takes the levels as separate essences "is lowered beneath her" (line 379): > שכל חילוקי הדרגין הוא רק מצד האור וגילוי אבל לא מצד העצם (line 379)

The breaking is in the revealing (BaN), not the essence (MaH) (lines 380-382). Tikkun comes "when His unity is revealed through the yesh", by nullifying the yesh through Torah and service (line 383, F11).

## SG 17-19 (384-399): Z'a, Asiyah as the aim, and the three states

Z'a is "smallness" relative to Ein Sof (line 384). Its core is the middot, and the world is led through Yetzirah. There the Mishnah's categories are "the main intention of His drawing into the worlds" (line 387, F10), and "the whole of service is to unite His middot" (line 387). Line 390 answers the obvious objection. The aim is still Asiyah, "because there is the full yesh and the other": > כי באמת עיקר כוונתו יתברך להיות כבודו ואלק"ותו יתברך בבחינת עשיה דוקא (line 390)

The king's parable adds "the farther, the greater his glory" (line 391). Above the chain of worlds, "the yesh which is His Malchut is principal" (line 392). The three states of Z'a map onto three ways of knowing. In ibur the worlds are only Asiyah. In yenika the unity is known only through understanding: > אבל היש מצד עצמו עדיין נערך למהות בפני עצמו רק מצד ההבנה הכל אחד אבל לא מצד הנגלה (line 398)

In gadlut, understanding is no longer needed: > כי בחינת בינה אין שייך אלא במהותים נבדלים בגדרם (line 398)

All three depend on service below (line 399). Deed and the yoke of heaven draw ibur. Contemplative love with self-sacrifice draws yenika. Bitul even of the physical, "as in the time of the Temple", draws gadlut.

## SG 20 (400-404): Malchut as one point; exile and the wicked kingdom

Malchut's one point is "that He created the world and is King over it and the world is nullified to Him" (line 400). She grows to ten through Israel's likening of "the form to its Maker" (line 401): > ובה תלוי כל עיקר היחוד על ידי ישראל המקשטין למטרוניתא (line 402)

When Israel cleave to yesh, the nine withdraw and the worlds live by bare pele (line 402). In exile, even the sitra achra's life and its "wicked kingdom" come from holy Malchut, "for nothing is outside Him" (line 403). In the future the wicked kingdom is annulled, "like smoke" (line 403). Line 404 is a cross-reference (*none*).

## SG 21-23 (405-421): ten not nine, ten not eleven

"The whole essence of the unity depends on the sefirot, whose unity is precisely because they are ten" (line 405). Two errors are ruled out. The first is a Chochmah separate from Him: "there is no greater separation than this" (line 406). The second is a world that exists by necessity. Instead: > ואין העולמות מחוייבים המציאות כי אם הכל ברצון ובנדבה (line 406)

The first emanation is Keter, pele. It bears opposites in one subject, the SPINE's F09 wording almost exactly: > ומדה זו סובל כל המהותים וכל ההפכים בנושא אחד (line 407)

Keter brings the sefirot out and unites them ("You are He who binds them and unites them", line 412). "All unity is precisely through ten" (line 412). Malchut is not an eleventh sefirah. The image she gives is His power, unlike a human's image of a thing (line 414). "There is no separation between the Holy One and His Shechinah" (line 415). Eleven belongs to the sitra achra, whose "Chochmah" is a knowing "to grasp the yesh precisely" (line 416, F12 inverted). Its powers exist: > מטעם להפכם ולאכפיים (line 417)

The half-shekel reading adds that Ein Sof is not counted among the sefirot (line 418). Pele is emanated and does not apply to the Essence: > לכן אין שייך לייחס אליו פלא מאחר שאין דבר ומהות אחר זולתו כלל (line 421)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say, or says differently

- **One set of sefirot in every world.** In the Tanya, Malchut gives rise to space, time and the lower unity (SH 7). Here Malchut, the power of likeness, is the only source of all difference between worlds. The sefirot of Beriah are "the very" sefirot of Atzilut (lines 355, 359). The Tanya does not make this claim.
- **Pele is not the Essence.** The joining of opposites is Keter, and Keter is emanated. Of the Essence one cannot say "even pele" (lines 332, 373, 421).
- **Bitul is a creature-side word** (line 324). The Tanya's higher unity describes creatures as nullified "before Him" (SH 3). R. Aharon says that from the Essence even "nullified" does not apply.
- **The levels are real.** He attacks those who think the levels only appear divided (line 329). He also says that division is only "from the light, not the essence" (line 379). He holds both on purpose.
- **Explicit statements of later doctrine.** "Even the power of yesh is His Essence" (line 367). "As His power is without limit, so in limit" (line 373). The physical earth as the place where the Essence's power is revealed and where Atik's outermost makif lies (line 373). The SPINE draws these from the Tzemach Tzedek, the Rebbe Rashab and the Rebbe. Here they are stated in 1820.
- **The unity depends on Israel.** "In her depends the whole essence of the unity, through Israel" (line 402). The states of Z'a depend on the kind of service (line 399). Tanya SH 1-7 does not tie the unity to service this way. Likkutei Amarim does, through F07.
- **Understanding is outgrown.** Binah is needed only while things appear separate. In gadlut the unity is revealed and Binah is not needed (line 398). The Tanya puts what is beyond understanding under faith (SH 7).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Lines 286-309 are anatomy (reason, nature, felt body, three lines, four elements, nine spheres). They are marked structural, mostly with no face, even though the gate later uses them for the unity. - Lines 340-342 are duplicates of lines 334-337. They are not independent evidence. - Line 404 is a cross-reference (*none*). The gloss on the order of terms at line 287.2 is *none*. - The one *reading* is 391.1. The king who defeats his enemies is read as F11, but the text applies the parable to distance. - F11 appears in two different ways. Line 403 annuls the wicked kingdom ("like smoke"). Line 417 says its powers exist "to overturn and subdue them". Both are kept. - F06 does not appear, and F04 and F10 are few. No line has been stretched to include them.

## Disagreements with the Tanya (and the SPINE), kept

1. **F09's subject.** The SPINE says "the perfection of His Essence bears all opposites". R. Aharon uses the same words, "bears all opposites in one subject", but applies them to Keter (line 407). He denies that pele can be said of the Essence (lines 332, 421). The Tanya (SH 6) says "You bind and unite them" of Him. R. Aharon reads the same Patach Eliyahu text as said of Keter, the "Cause of causes" (line 412).
2. **Real levels against "yesh only to eyes of flesh".** Tanya SH 3 makes the creature's separateness a matter of limited sight. Line 329 insists that each level is "a real power", and he rejects "those who think" the division is only apparent. Line 379 then brings him close to the Tanya again.
3. **Faith against revelation beyond understanding.** For the Alter Rebbe, what is beyond comprehension is held by faith (SH 7). For R. Aharon, understanding gives way in gadlut to revealed unity, which depends on service (lines 398-399).
4. **Where tzimtzum sits.** Both reject a literal tzimtzum (line 378; Tanya SH 7). R. Aharon also places the main tzimtzum at the parsa between Atzilut and Beriah, with none between Adam Kadmon and Atzilut. The Tanya does not locate it this way.
