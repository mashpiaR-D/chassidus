## The shape of the range

This is not the part of Tanya where the unity is taught directly. That happens in chapters 20-25 and 33-37 and in Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah. Chapters 1-18 are anthropology and method. They describe the two souls, the soul's faculties and garments, kelipah and nogah, the five types of person, and the beinoni. The unity comes in at a few points and carries weight there. Most lines are *structural*, meaning they are definitions and steps the later chapters need. The dominant faces follow from that. F06 is the soul as a part of God. F10 is Torah as one with Him. F12 is contemplation as the tool. F11 is subduing and transforming the animal soul. F01, F02 and F04, the cosmological faces, appear only a few times, each as a single sentence given as the content of contemplation.

## Front matter (lines 1-64)

The title page, approbations and printing notices have no tie, and they are marked *none* without forcing one. There are two exceptions. The title verse "for the thing is very near to you... to do it" (line 18) is the frame that chapters 17-18 fulfil. The foreword (line 54) states F10 outright, saying that every soul, "down to the lightest spark", is bound to Torah, and that Torah binds it to the Holy One: > כולהו מתקשראן באורייתא, ואורייתא – היא המקשרת אותן להקדושברוךהוא (line 54)

Line 56 roots "these and these are the words of the living God" in the three lines of soul-roots. Calling that a unity of opposites (F09) is our reading.

## Ch. 1 (68-85): the question

The oath "be a tzaddik... and be in your eyes like a rasha" and the five types are all structural. Two lines matter later. The animal soul comes from kelipat nogah, which "contains good" (line 84), and that is the premise for F11. Line 85 says the nations' souls come from kelipot "with no good at all". Read alone, it cuts against the index's claim that the point is present "in everything". The text does not reconcile the two here. Line 148 (ch. 6) later implies that even these live only from Him, which is our reading and not a statement in ch. 1.

## Ch. 2 (86-99): the soul as part of the One (F06, stated)

This is the strongest stated stretch for F06: > היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש (line 86)

The chapter makes the Rambam's unity of God with His wisdom the ground of the soul (line 87). The Ari's gloss (line 88) adds F03 and F05: Or Ein Sof is clothed through many contractions, and the Essence is so far above Chochmah that Chochmah counts as physical action before Him. The toenail analogy (lines 92-95) is G04 and G06 at work. The lowest soul stays "bound and united in a wonderful and mighty union" with its first essence: > עודינה קשורות ומיוחדות ביחוד נפלא ועצום במהותן ועצמותן הראשון (line 95)

Line 89 already sides with the Alter Rebbe in the F12 dispute: the unity of knower and known "cannot be grasped by man".

## Ch. 3 (100-114): knowing the unity makes love and awe (F12)

This is a faculty psychology, and most of it is structural. Its one stated content line is the first thing Tanya gives the mind to contemplate, which is the unity in three forms (F04, F02): > איך הוא ״ממלא כל עלמין״ ו״סובב כל עלמין״ ו״כולא קמיה כלא חשיב״ (line 107)

Da'at, "binding" one's thought to the greatness of Ein Sof, is what turns that knowledge into feeling. Without it there are only "false imaginings" (lines 112-113). This is F12's "return it to your heart" in the Alter Rebbe's own terms.

## Ch. 4-5 (115-141): Torah and mitzvah as the unity made actual (F10)

This is the densest stated stretch for F10. The garments of Torah and mitzvot are higher than the soul because "Oraisa and Kudsha Brich Hu are all one" (line 119). The Essence that no thought grasps (line 120) contracted His will into the 613 mitzvot so that it could be grasped, and F03 is there for the sake of revelation: > וצמצם הקדושברוךהוא רצונו וחכמתו בתרי״ג מצות התורה (line 121)

Torah descends like water down to ink on parchment (G05, lines 123-124). Line 126 comes closest to F07 in the range. One hour in this world outweighs the World to Come, which holds only a radiance, because the Essence is grasped only as clothed in Torah and mitzvot: > יפה שעה אחת בתשובה ומעשים טובים בעולם הזה מכל חיי עולם הבא (line 126)

Ch. 5 makes a hypothetical civil ruling the place of union, "a wonderful union, like which there is no other" (line 134). Line 138 says the soul's vitality from Torah is Ein Sof itself. Linking the soul-level inner/surrounding pair of line 141 to memale/sovev is marked *reading*.

## Ch. 6 (142-153): bitul as the condition of presence (F08); kelipah still from Him (F01)

The sitra achra is defined as "the other side": whatever is "not for God" (line 145). Then comes F08, stated: > ואין הקדושברוךהוא שורה אלא על דבר שבטל אצלו יתברך (line 146)

Line 148 is the range's strongest statement that nothing stands outside Him. The separate thing lives from His "back", clothed there "in exile", and only so that it not "return to nothing": > שלא יחזור להיות אין ואפס כבתחלה מקודם שנברא (line 148)

Lines 149-150 hold both knowledges side by side (G02). This world "is called the world of kelipot", and the gloss says Or Ein Sof "fills all this lowest earth": > ולכן נקרא עולם הזה ומלואו, עולם הקליפות וסטרא אחרא (line 149) > ונמצא אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא מלא כל הארץ הלזו התחתונה (line 150)

The list of what the impure kelipot vitalize (line 153) is halachic taxonomy. It is marked structural, with a thin tie.

## Ch. 7-8 (154-180): refining the permitted, and its limits (F11)

Nogah can rise, and permitted meat and wine eaten for Shabbat or for Torah rise "like a burnt offering" (line 157, F11 with G12). "Heter" is read as "not bound" (line 159). The only way out for what is forbidden is teshuvah from great love, which turns sins into merits (lines 162-163). The limits are kept as limits and not smoothed. Teshuvah without love does not raise the vitality (line 164). "What is crooked cannot be made straight" (line 169). Forbidden food eaten unknowingly does not rise (line 170). The eschatological and punitive details in lines 173-177 are *none*. For the nations' wisdom used "as an axe to cut with" (line 180), the text states that it may be used, and reading that use as refinement is ours.

## Ch. 9-10 (181-196): the two kings; turning darkness to light; the dwelling below

The divine soul wants every limb "nullified to it" and a chariot for it, and wants its love to spill into the left side "to change and turn" the lust (line 185: F11, F08, F12). "Love in delights" turns the evil "completely good, like the good inclination itself" (line 186). The animal soul's opposition is "for the person's good" (line 188, F03 by way of G03). Ch. 10 names F11 in the Zohar's words: > די חשוכא מהפכן לנהורא (line 194)

It then names F07, the only explicit "lower realms" goal in the range: > לייחדא קודשא בריך הוא ושכינתיה בתחתונים (line 195)

Line 196 joins the two: refining nogah draws "His Godliness from above to below to be clothed in the lower beings".

## Ch. 11-13 (197-231): the rasha and the beinoni

Most of this is typology and is marked structural. The stated ties are these. At Shema and prayer the beinoni binds his ChaBaD to the greatness of Ein Sof, and the evil is then "bent and nullified" (line 207). The light of God is what drives out the darkness: "the advantage of light over darkness" (lines 210, 221). Itkafia comes by way of Binah (line 224). The oath "like a rasha" is answered as a sober judgment and not as self-loathing (line 222). The following are marked *reading* because the text does not make them: the divine soul standing as makif over the unrepentant rasha (line 203), God as the "deciding judge" (line 220), and Yaakov's "middle bar" of truth running through every level (line 230).

## Ch. 14 (232-239): sin as separation from the One (F08)

This is the practical key of the range: > כי אינני רוצה להיות מובדל ונפרד חס ושלום מה׳ אחד בשום אופן (line 232)

What the beinoni refuses is separation from "the One God", and every Jew can refuse it at any moment. Joy comes from contemplating the greatness of Ein Sof "even in imagination" (line 238).

## Ch. 15-16 (240-262): contemplation as the beinoni's work

Changing one's nature takes contemplation of God's greatness (line 249). Reading "teva" here beside Elokim = hateva is ours. Line 256 is the fullest use of the higher unity in the range: > דכולא קמיה כלא חשיב ממש (line 256)

From that, the soul should long to expire into Him (F08). Since He is grasped only through Torah and mitzvot, the longing is turned into embracing Him in action (F10, G11). Line 257 has contemplation giving deeds "wings". Line 259 has the Holy One "joining" hidden love to the deed. Calling Him "the joiner" in the sense of F09 is our reading.

## Ch. 17-18:10 (263-280): "very near"; Ein Sof in every soul

"To do it" is near to anyone with a mind who contemplates Ein Sof (line 265). "Today is the world of action" (line 266); tying that to the dwelling below is *reading*. Sin draws the soul's divine spark into kelipah, which is the exile of the Shechinah, and a broken heart raises it (lines 268-270). Ch. 18 then grounds the hidden love of every Jew in the unity itself. Even the lowest soul-level contains, nested within it, the Chochmah of Atzilut, where "Or Ein Sof literally shines" (line 276). Then: > כי איןסוף ברוךהוא מלובש בבחינת חכמה שבנפש האדם – יהיה מי שיהיה מישראל (line 277)

That is F06 for every Jew, stated. It stands against the Keter Shem Tov wording that the SPINE records, which ties the "part of God" soul to the tzaddik. Line 280 gives the reason. Chochmah is "ko'ach mah", what is not yet grasped, so Or Ein Sof, which no thought grasps, is clothed in it: > ולכן מתלבש בה אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא דלית מחשבה תפיסא ביה כלל (line 280)

The range stops there, before line 281 draws the conclusion that this is why every Jew believes and gives his life for "the One God".

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Front matter, the typology of ch. 1 and 11, and the physiology of ch. 9 are *none* or plain structural. - The eschatology and halachic taxonomy of ch. 7-8 (lines 153, 166-167, 173-177) are *none* or structural with no face. - Line 85 (nations' souls with no good) is not tied to the unity by the text. We note the tension and do not resolve it. - The cosmological faces (F01, F02, F04, F05) appear only at lines 88, 107, 120, 126, 148, 150, 256, 276-277 and 280. Elsewhere in this range the idea works through its human faces (F06, F08, F10, F11, F12).
