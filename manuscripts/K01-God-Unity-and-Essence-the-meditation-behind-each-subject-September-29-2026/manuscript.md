## S001 · The unity of God · אחדות ה'

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F12 Knowing the unity **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5)

**The glimpse.** He was alone before anything was made and He is alone now, with everything made. The world adds nothing to Him, and every detail of it shows His oneness.

**Hold.** 'This world and all the upper worlds make no change at all in His unity by being created from nothing' (Tanya, ch. 20).

**The move.** Open it one piece at a time: this world, the upper worlds, every detail in them came from nothing, and not one of them added to Him or changed Him, because He is one without parts, not a whole made of pieces. So the commandment is more than 'one God, not two'; the Tzemach Tzedek writes that it is to know that He is one and there is nothing besides Him. You, here, now, detail by detail as the Mitteler Rebbe teaches: this kitchen, this bill, this child in front of you; none of them makes a second beside Him.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'no change in His unity'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** A quiet oneness under the busy day: the details do not crowd Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the Shema with your lips, drawing out 'One' (Echad): on its ches think that He reigns in the heavens and the earth, on its dalet that He alone rules the four directions. Then learn one short passage of Torah, as your time allows. During the day, return to: 'no change in His unity'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 20:2 (line 278): כי, עולם הזה וכן כל העולמות העליונים – אינם פועלים שום שינוי באחדותו יתברך בהבראם מאין ליש - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:12 (line 806): אבל הקדושברוךהוא – הוא אחדות פשוט בלי שום הרכבה וצד ריבוי כלל - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 1 (line 119): ומצווים במצות היחוד לידע האמת כי הוא אחד וזולתו אין עוד

## S002 · Echad and Yachid: one and only · אחד ויחיד

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F02 The higher and the lower unity **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3)

**The glimpse.** 'One' (echad) is the many gathered and set aside in Him; 'only' (yachid) is Him alone, above even being the one who gathers them. He is alone after creating exactly as before.

**Hold.** 'Yachid means literally alone, above the joining of separate things, which is called echad' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 10).

**The move.** First 'echad': the Rayatz reads its letters as the seven heavens and the earth and the four directions, all gathered into the aleph, the One, the many set aside in Him. Then 'yachid', which is past that: literally alone, where there is nothing besides Him of which 'joining' could even be said. You, here, now: the walls of this room, the street outside, the sky over it, all gathered into the One, and He is as alone now, with all of it made, as before anything was. The letters are only a way for the mind to hold it; He has no parts to gather.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'yachid, literally alone'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Simplicity: nothing to count and nothing to hold together, and the room still standing, not as a second thing. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the first verse of the Shema with your lips, slowly, giving 'Echad' its time. Then do one mitzvah in your measure, a coin to charity or a blessing said slowly. During the day, return to: 'alone, as before'.

**Disagreement kept.** The words shift between teachers: the Mitteler Rebbe keeps 'echad' for the joining of separate things, while the Rayatz and Radzin use 'echad' for the higher term, against 'first'. The doctrine agrees; the words do not.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - The Gate of Unity 10:12 (line 493): בחינת יחיד משמעו לבדו ממש … והוא למעלה מהתאחדות נפרדים שנקרא אחד - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 20:3 (line 279): שכמו שהיה הוא לבדו הוא יחיד ומיוחד קודם הבראם, כן הוא לבדו הוא יחיד ומיוחד אחר שבראם - Kuntres Limud HaChassidus, printed p. 3 (line 103): והוי' אחד פירושו מיוחד, והוא אחד העצמי דענינו יחיר … ומה שנאמר בלשון אחד ולא יחיד, כי בא להורות על היחוד ואחדות הנאצלים

## S003 · The Essence · עצמות ומהות

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F07 The home below **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 3, 4, 5, 7)

**The glimpse.** What is here in the plain physical is not a light or a level of Him but He Himself; that is why a plain place can be a dwelling for Him.

**Hold.** 'I prayed with the lectern: the physical comes into being from the Essence' (the Alter Rebbe, as the Rayatz tells it).

**The move.** Open it: lights and levels show only that He is there, never what He is; He Himself is above hiding and showing, and even the light that surrounds all worlds is one detail beside Him. The Mitteler Rebbe had prayed with the bowing of the highest level, 'every stature shall bow before You'; his father answered that he had prayed with the lectern, the plain wood in front of him, because the physical comes into being from the Essence. The wood is not Him; the story says only where its being comes from. You, here, now: the table in front of you, the floor under it; the Rebbe says a dwelling is where the owner himself is found, and this plain world is to be a dwelling for His Essence.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the physical comes into being from the Essence'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Plainness that is not empty: the plainest thing in the room points to Him Himself. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before you pray or learn today, say with your lips the Alter Rebbe's answer: 'the physical comes into being from the Essence'. Then pray or learn one piece at that table, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'from the Essence'.

**Thin.** The tie is stated throughout Chabad; outside Chabad 'Essence' is rarely a term of doctrine, so this meditation keeps to the Chabad sources and claims nothing for the others.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 7 ¶12 (line 46): אני התפללתי עם הסטעגדער, דהתהוות הגשמיות הוא מהעצמות - Hemshech Ayin Beis, חלק שני / בס"ד. ש"פ וירא, העת"ר / תשכח:1 (line 2911): כי העצמי ממש ה"ה למע' מהעלם וגילוי ואין האור מגלה מהות העצם רק מציאותו - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. יום ב' דראש השנה ה'תשמ"א:16 (line 77): דירה לעצמותו ית' [וכידוע דענין הדירה הוא מה שהעצם נמצא בהדירה]

## S004 · Ein Sof and the Infinite Light · אין סוף / אור אין סוף

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F10 Torah and mitzvah **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 6)

**The glimpse.** He is bound by nothing, so nothing can stand beside Him as a second; and His unbounded light comes down into a plain act done with the hand.

**Hold.** 'Because He is without end, there must be nothing else: were there another, there would be a difference between them, and whatever is distinct is bounded' (Keter Shem Tov).

**The move.** Take it step by step: whatever is separate from something else has an edge where it stops, so a second beside Him would give Him an edge, and He has none. Even the name 'Ein Sof', without end, is only His name and not what He is, and His light is above every kind of mind, even the highest wisdom. You, here, now: the table ends at its rim and the room at its wall, and not one of them stands as a second beside Him. And that endless light comes down into this world through a mitzvah; the Tanya says doing it draws the revelation of the Infinite Light into the physical.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'without end, and nothing else'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Nothing pressing on Him from outside, and so no outside to be in. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'ein od milvado', there is nothing besides Him. Then do one mitzvah with your hand today, a coin for charity or a blessing over food, as a place His light comes down. During the day, return to: 'without end, and nothing else'.

**Disagreement kept.** The Rebbe says 'Ein Sof is the essential'; the Rashab, after Pelach HaRimon, says 'Ein Sof' names His name and not His Essence. The sources state both; the entry's way of reconciling them is its own reading.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Keter Shem Tov 1:55:5 (line 88): פי' שהוא מקומו ש"ע דהוא א"ס א"כ צ"ל אין עוד דא"כ צ"ל הפרש וכל נפרש מוגבל - Hemshech Ayin Beis, חלק שלישי / בס"ד. המשך בכתב, שלא נאמר / א'שמה:3 (line 5230): וה"ז כמ"ש בפלח הרמון בענין שם א"ס דאין ר"ל מהותו ועצמותו אלא על שמו ית' - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:2 (line 496): כי בעשייתה, ממשיך האדם גילוי אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא מלמעלה למטה להתלבש בגשמיות עולם הזה

## S005 · Nothing besides Him · אין עוד מלבדו

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F07 The home below · F08 Bitul **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5)

**The glimpse.** Nothing stands on its own before Him, not even the ground under your feet; and the ground is real, not an illusion. Both are true together.

**Hold.** 'Even in the earth below there is nothing besides Him, and that is His dwelling below' (Tanya, ch. 33).

**The move.** Ask what the Shaar HaYichud asks: nobody thinks there is a god in the waters under the earth, so 'there is nothing else' must be about the world itself. The picture to put down is the soul filling a body: do not think heaven and earth are a separate thing He fills that way; from His side even the solid earth, which looks like a full 'something', is not 'another' thing at all. You, here, now: the floor under you is real, not a dream or a trick of the eye, because it comes from the truth of His being; it only does not stand on its own. And this plain ground of your home is His dwelling.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'even here below, nothing besides Him'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Lightness, like a ray inside the sun with nothing of its own to hold up, and still firm ground under you. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'ein od milvado'. Then kiss the mezuzah as you go out, knowing whose dwelling you leave and come back to. During the day, return to: 'nothing besides Him, even here'.

**Disagreement kept.** The Yismach Moshe reads the verse more plainly, no other ruler or power beside Him, and holds that this is what every Jew must grasp; the bare 'nothing else' he keeps for a time of full revelation. Radzin adds that one who truly hears 'Hashem echad' loses the power of choice.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 33:6 (line 455): הוא יחוד ה׳ האמיתי, אשר אפילו בארץ מתחת – אין עוד מלבדו, וזו היא דירתו בתחתונים - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:5 (line 791): שגם הארץ החומרית שנראית יש גמור לעין כל, היא אין ואפס ממש לגבי הקדושברוךהוא - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. יום ב' דראש השנה ה'תשמ"א:16 (line 77): שמציאות העולם וכל מה שנברא בו הוא לא דמיון ואחיזת עינים כ"א הוא מציאות באמת

## S006 · Higher and lower unity · יחודא עילאה ויחודא תתאה

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 7)

**The glimpse.** There are two true ways to see the world: from His side all of it is nothing before Him, and from our side it is real and He fills it in its time and place. You live in the second and are asked to touch the first.

**Hold.** 'Hear, Israel … is the higher unity; Blessed be the name of His glorious kingdom forever is the lower unity' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 1).

**The move.** Open the two lines: in 'One', everything is seen from His side, and even the earth is nothing before Him; in 'Blessed be the name', His Essence fills the earth itself, in time and place, through His kingship, which keeps time and place from dissolving. The Maharash makes them two knowledges: the lower sees the world below as something and its source as nothing, the higher sees all as nothing before Him. You, here, now, live and serve in the lower one, with a clock on the wall and walls around you, and the Rashab says everyone must still touch the higher, because the lower alone slides easily into 'I am something'. Radzin says more: in truth there is only the higher, and He set up a place for the lower so that there could be service, which is why the second line is said quietly.

**Stay.** Say the two lines again slowly, the first aloud and the second quietly. When the mind drifts, come back to them without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The lightness of a ray inside the sun; then the room again, not lost, but full of Him and ruled by Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the bedtime Shema tonight with your lips, the first line aloud and 'Blessed be the name' quietly, with both unities in mind. Before it, give a coin to charity. During the day, return to: 'One, and His kingdom here'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:1 (line 755): ד״שמע ישראל כו׳״ – הוא ״יחודא עילאה״, ו״ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד״ – הוא ״יחודא תתאה״ - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:8 (line 802): וזהו יחודא תתאה [שילוב הוי״ה באדנות ברוךהוא], דהיינו, שמהותו ועצמותו יתברך, הנקרא בשם ״איןסוף״ ברוךהוא – מלא את כל הארץ ממש בזמן ומקום - Sod Yesharim, Yom Kippur 13:1 (line 156): וזהו כל הענין שהציב השי"ת מקום ליחודא תתאה אף שאין באמת רק יחודא עלאה

## S007 · Filling and encompassing all worlds · ממלא כל עלמין וסובב כל עלמין

**Face:** F04 Filling and surrounding. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7)

**The glimpse.** He is inside each thing to its own measure and around all things equally; neither is a place, and neither is yet Him Himself.

**Hold.** 'He surrounds all worlds … no one leaves His domain … He fills all worlds' (the Zohar, cited in Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 7).

**The move.** Filling: the life clothed inside each thing, cut to its measure, so a plant lives as a plant and a person as a person. Surrounding: the light not revealed inside anything, which is not 'above' in any place, and from which, the Tzemach Tzedek says, the very 'somethingness' of solid matter comes. You, here, now: the life in you is measured to you, and the solidity of the chair you sit on comes from the light around all things equally; no one leaves His domain. The chassidim held on to both, and for Radzin, thinking only 'He surrounds' and not 'He fills' was the reverse of Torah; and both together are still one detail beside Him Himself.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'no one leaves His domain'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Being carried: held from inside and from all around, with no edge to the holding. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'He fills all worlds, He surrounds all worlds'. Then say one blessing slowly today, over food or over something new. During the day, return to: 'no one leaves His domain'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:17 (line 811): איהו סובב כל עלמין כו׳, ולית מאן דנפיק מרשותיה לבר, איהו ממלא כל עלמין - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:20 (line 814): ״ממלא כל עלמין״ – היא בחינת החיות המתלבשת תוך עצם הנברא, שהיא מצומצמת בתוכו בצמצום רב - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 2 (line 120): רק שבאמת מ"מ עיקר החיות הוא מהסובב

## S008 · Creation something from nothing · יש מאין

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F01 The world said into being now · F08 Bitul **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5)

**The glimpse.** The plain way a thing just stands there, as if nothing made it, is the mark of the One who is not made: only He could make something that feels it has no cause.

**Hold.** 'Only He, whose being is from Himself and caused by nothing, has the power to create something from absolute nothing' (Iggeret HaKodesh 20).

**The move.** Open it: an effect by nature melts back into its cause and does not stand as a thing of its own, so a solid 'something' cannot come from a chain of causes, only from the One who can do anything. The Rebbe adds that a created thing feels uncaused, simply there, precisely because it comes from the Essence. You, here, now: the bread on your table is just there and does not feel like anyone's effect; that plain thereness is His trace. And the work runs back the other way: the Maggid says heaven and earth were made something from nothing, and the righteous make the something into nothing before Him, even in eating.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'only He can make something from nothing'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Ordinary solidity no longer seems cut off from Him; it is where only He could be at work. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the blessing over the next thing you eat with your lips, slowly, and eat it for strength to serve Him, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'something from nothing, only He'.

**Disagreement kept.** The Maharash holds that creation from nothing is 'only from a mere radiance', and that what comes from the Essence is the going out of limits; Iggeret HaKodesh 20 and the Rebbe tie creation to the Essence. The entry keeps both.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:11 (line 1261): הוא מהותו ועצמותו של המאציל ברוךהוא, שמציאותו הוא מעצמותו, ואינו עלול מאיזה עילה שקדמה לו חס ושלום, ולכן הוא לבדו בכחו ויכלתו לברוא יש מאין ואפס המוחלט ממש - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשי"ט:5 (line 1290): דזה שבהרגשתו אין לו עילה וסיבה שקדמה לו ח"ו, הוא לפי שהתהוותו הוא מהעצמות - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 6:2 (line 18): כי מעשה שמים וארץ הי' יש מאין וצדיקים עושים מיש אין

## S009 · Continuous re-creation · התהוות תמידית / מחדש בכל יום

**Face:** F01 The world said into being now. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** The world did not just happen once; it is, only while His word stands in it, this moment and the next.

**Hold.** 'Forever, God, Your word stands in the heavens' (Psalms 119, as the Baal Shem Tov explains it in Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 1).

**The move.** The Besht reads it plainly: the words 'let there be a firmament' stand in the heavens now and keep them in being, and the same holds for the earth, for stone and dust; a thing's Hebrew name is the letters that keep it here. The picture to put down is the smith, who makes a vessel and walks away while it stands; creation is not like that, and being made in the six days is not enough for it. You, here, now: the stone on the path, the cup in your hand and you yourself are being brought into being this moment, not left standing from before. The Rebbe says why: it comes from the Essence, before whom the worlds take up no room, so their whole existence is new each moment.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'Your word stands'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Being held rather than holding yourself up; His word never leaves Him the way ours leave us. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the verse with your lips: 'L'olam Hashem, d'varcha nitzav bashamayim'. Tomorrow, say Modeh Ani on waking and the morning blessings slowly, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'Your word stands, now'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:3 (line 757): כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:2 (line 777): החיות הנשפע בכל רגע ממש בכל הברואים ממוצא פי ה׳ ורוחו, ומהוה אותם מאין ליש בכל רגע - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 18:1 (line 96): מכיון שההתהוות היא מעצמות אוא"ס ב"ה – שאין העולמות תופסים מקום לגבי' כלל, הרי אין שום נתינת מקום לכל ענינם, וכל עיקר מציאותם מתחדשת מאוא"ס ב"ה

## S010 · Particular providence · השגחה פרטית

**Face:** F01 The world said into being now. **Also touches:** F12 Knowing the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** Nothing moves by itself: each small movement is His doing and belongs to the whole purpose of the world.

**Hold.** 'Even a lone straw rolling in the marketplace, our master the Baal Shem Tov says, is by particular providence' (the Rayatz).

**The move.** Take it slowly: this straw, which way it rolls, where it stops; each is moved by Him, and each small movement bears on the whole purpose of creation, a ring in the chain of His will. It is the same fact as the world being made new each moment: He renews it, and that is its whole existence. You, here, now: the leaf turning on the pavement, the bus that pulled in as you reached the stop; each is moved by Him. What makes it look like chance is a covering, which the Besht calls the shell that hides His watching.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'even the straw'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** A day that is not a scatter of accidents; each small thing in it is meant. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'Shivisi Hashem l'negdi tamid', I have set God before me always. Then give a coin to charity at the first small chance the day brings you. During the day, return to: 'even the straw'.

**Disagreement kept.** The Rambam limits providence in the case of a falling leaf; Radzin answers that from God's side all is providence, and from creation's side it is received by the vessel. That this is the two knowledges is the entry's reading.

## S011 · He is the knower and the known · הוא היודע והוא הידוע

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F12 Knowing the unity · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2)

**The glimpse.** He knows you not by looking at you from outside but by knowing Himself; knowing everything adds nothing to Him and makes no crowd in Him.

**Hold.** 'He is the knowledge, the knower and the known; knowing Himself He knows all creatures, not by a knowledge outside Him as a person knows' (Tanya, ch. 48, after the Rambam).

**The move.** Take the three words one at a time: the knowing, the one who knows, the thing known; in a person they are three, and in Him they are one. When you know a thing, your knowing stands outside it and your mind now holds one more thing; He does not know that way, since all creatures are from Him and set aside before Him, so knowing them adds nothing to Him. You, here, now, are known in that way: in His knowing of Himself, with nothing added. The Tanya says a person cannot fully understand this; it is held by faith above the mind.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the knower and the known'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Being known from within His own knowing, not looked at from outside. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before one blessing today, say with your lips: 'He is the knower and the known'. Then make the blessing slowly. During the day, return to: 'the knower and the known'.

**Thin.** The tie is stated, but the subject lives almost entirely in the Alter Rebbe's school; this meditation keeps to those sources and makes no wider claim.

**Disagreement kept.** The Maharal and the Tosafos Yom Tov differ with the Rambam: knowledge is His act, not His Essence. The Tzemach Tzedek holds both true: the Essence is above knowledge, and the Rambam's formula holds once the light is clothed in the vessels of wisdom.

## S012 · Omnipotence and the joining of opposites · כל יכול / נושא הפכים

**Face:** F09 The unity of opposites. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 7)

**The glimpse.** Limit and the limitless are not rivals in Him: the power that has no end is the same power that draws the edges of your day. What cannot meet in things is borne together in Him.

**Hold.** 'As He has power in the limitless, so He has power in the limited' (Avodat HaKodesh, cited by the Rebbe).

**The move.** Open it: for anything bounded some things are impossible, but for Him there is no 'impossible' at all, and that is not a great amount of power but no limit on it. So He has power in limitation too, and to deny Him that would take something from His perfection; even His hiding of the light in things is this same power. You, here, now: the hours of your day, the walls of this room, the one task you can do at a time are His work, not a place where His power stops. In His Essence two opposites are in one subject, borne together; in you they stay two, as the Alter Rebbe told a young prodigy that the spiritual and the physical are opposites in their essence.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'power in the limited too'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The edges held by what has none; the limited and the limitless together, neither one denied. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the line with your lips: 'as He has power in the limitless, so in the limited'. Then give a coin to charity before you start the one task in front of you, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'power in the limited too'.

**Thin.** Stated in Chabad and Strashelye; outside them the formal argument is thin, and the Maggid's and the Besht's 'joining' are only near it, so this meditation keeps to the Chabad argument.

## S013 · A dwelling in the lowest realm · דירה בתחתונים

**Face:** F07 The home below. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F11 Darkness turned to light **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 6)

**The glimpse.** He wanted a home in the lowest, plainest place; it is true already now, and plain deeds of Torah and mitzvot make it show.

**Hold.** 'The purpose of creating this world is that the Holy One desired a dwelling in the lowest realms' (Tanya, ch. 36).

**The move.** Open it: not the high heavens but this world, the lowest, with nothing lower than it; and nothing changes in Him by it, since the change is only in those who receive. The Rebbe says the plain physical is exactly a dwelling for His Essence, to be revealed in the future and true already now. You, here, now: your kitchen, your doorstep, this ordinary day are the place He wanted. The Rayatz says what makes it show: lighting the dark of the lowest world with Torah and mitzvot, and making it a place for Him to dwell.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'a dwelling in the lowest'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The joy of the plain man whose king lives with him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Kiss the mezuzah as you come home today and say with your lips 'dirah b'tachtonim', a dwelling below. Then do one plain mitzvah in the house, a coin in the charity box or setting the Shabbos table. During the day, return to: 'a dwelling in the lowest'.
