## What this slice adds (one page)

The Mitteler Rebbe adds four things to the full depth. (1) The deepest point is a PLACE and a REST, not an experience: the yechida rests in the four cubits, in plain bodily Shabbos rest, where great and simple are equal; the true Being rests only in plain being. (2) The bottom is a CIRCLE: pushed or lowered to the bottom, you are at the top; stepping back from the King is nearer than nearness; a son's cry from afar turns the Father's face; the treasure of the heart opens only when one feels 'as dead'. (3) He gives exact TESTS against fake depth: the wish 'to feel that I live' is the root that holds desire and even spiritual fervor; pain at a loss measures the hold; an insult reveals false humility; real nothingness is 'not made'. The soul's true life is the feeling of not-being. (4) The RETURN is concrete: He holds the world by calling its name and we call His Name; exile is His sleep, and deed and speech (not intention alone) wake Him; joy 'only because it is His will' reaches the Owner of the will; prayer (Yiddish 5587) runs from 'no one there to want', through falling on the face like a dead man, to being lifted by Him and carrying the King's print into the day. Faith from far, and the child's plain 'Abba', hold more than sight and understanding.

**Coverage (honest).** Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (2,467 long lines, about 2M letters): read in full the deepest maamarim by density and the famous ones: Atah Echad 5587 (1580-1600), Igulim veYosher 5575 (2156-2205), Shaashuei HaMelech (2375-2422), Kuntres Inyan HaTefillah 5587 in Yiddish (2423-2467), Sameach Tesamach/sovev umemaleh (1077-1110), Yafeh Sha'ah (1843-1871), Zevach Pesach and Avadim Hayinu (44-69), Se'u Marom (143-155), Mi Yitencha I-III and VeHaya Mispar (201-261), Kabbalas HaTorah (262-271), Shema (708-720); plus every Yiddish-glossed line across the whole work (about 200 lines, which are where the text speaks of inner experience), parts of Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (1653-1657, 1757-1760) and Poke'ach Ivrim (2207-2227). The long wedding drushim (873-1579) and the Moshiach kuntres (1892-2137) were swept, not read through. Sha'arei Teshuvah and Ner Mitzvah were outlined and ranked; read in full: ST 126-152, 196-201, 249-252, 293-310, 445-448, 507-518, 693-699, 715-718, 728-731; NM 24-45, 336-346, 453-457. **ours**

**For the guide (ours).** Three cautions from the texts themselves: do not offer 'feel it' as the goal (the wish to feel oneself is the root of Amalek, 1849); do not take an overcome desire as gone (ST 303); judge a deep moment by the day after (1760). And one comfort: if the person feels far or 'dead', the texts say that is exactly where the hidden treasure opens and where the Father turns His face (ST 447, 718).

## MR3-C01 · G-d is good. But is goodness His nature, the way giving life is the soul's nature?

*Where:* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai, 'Atah Echad' (Kislev 5587), lines 1580-1600 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **His goodness is not a nature He is stuck with. He is good because He can do anything, and so He can also be good.** (M10) *How:* The text takes the parable of the soul, whose nature is to give life to a body, and breaks it: the soul is bound to that one nature (put it in a plant and it is in exile). The Infinite is bound to nothing. So His kindness comes from His being able to do all. > ועיקר שם בחי' א"ס הוא שהוא כל יכול > "The main meaning of 'Infinite' is that He can do everything." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1586)

2. **Because His kindness comes from no limit, it has no limit either: it reaches the good and the bad equally.** (M5) *How:* If the kindness came from a measured nature, it would be measured. It comes from the Infinite, so it flows all the way down, to souls in Eden and to bread for every nation, the same. > לטובים ולרעים בשוה ממש > "To the good and to the bad, exactly equally." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1587)

3. **In every Jew there is a point of Jewishness that no sin touches. Even someone who broke everything can come back from there.** (M8) *How:* The text names the yechida, the soul's single point, above mind and heart. It is why even the lowest gives his life for G-d, and why 'return, Israel' works for one who damaged the whole Name. > הוא בחי' עצם נקודת יהדות שנמצא בכל נפש מישראל אף בפחותים וקלים > "It is the very point of Jewishness found in every Jewish soul, even the lowest and lightest." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1597)

4. **That deepest point does not live in your head or your heart. It rests in your four cubits, the space around you, the place farthest from thought.** (M4) *How:* Step by step the text rules out places: not the brain and heart, not the aura over the head, not the clothes, not even the house. Only the four cubits, 'the farthest, with no distance beyond it.' > כ"א דייקא בד' אמות הרחוקים בתכלית בריחוק אשר אין ריחוק יותר ממנו שם דוקא ישכון הארת בחי' יחידה > "Only in the four cubits, the farthest of all, with no farther distance, there the light of the yechida rests." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1598)

5. **The rule behind it: the true Being rests only in plain, solid being. The most hidden place is the only place that can hold Him as He is.** (M12) *How:* Even the 'nothing' of wisdom is not a vessel for the true Yesh. Only the full 'something', the last concealment, can carry it. > בחי' יש האמיתי לא ישרה כ"א דוקא ביש גמור > "The true Being rests only in complete being (yesh gamur)." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1598)

6. **So Shabbos rest of the body, plain rest, is where the yechida rests. There the great tzaddik and the simplest person are exactly equal.** (M15) *How:* Shabbos forbids the soul's 'work' too, the effort of mind and heart. What is left is the soul at rest in itself, and there everyone is the same. > ואדרבה במנוחה גשמיות שם דוקא תהי' השראת בחי' יחידה > "On the contrary: in physical rest specifically, there the yechida rests." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1599)

**Bottom:** The deepest point of the person is not an experience but a place of rest: the plain body resting in His day. **Return:** Down into Shabbos: stop working on yourself; sit, eat, rest; 'let no man leave his place' (1599). The rest itself is 'from You, from Your very Self' (1600), and it is where one would give his life for G-d.

## MR3-C02 · Where does a new insight come from?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Igulim veYosher' (before Rosh Hashanah 5575), lines 2162-2180 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Notice: the delight of a new idea comes after the idea. Yet the delight is what produced it.** (M9) *How:* The text calls this 'a full proof': you find a deep insight and then feel a great pleasure. It seems the pleasure is only a result. In truth the insight came out of a deeper delight, and only shows itself second. > מבחי' התענוג שמתענג האדם ממה שחידש איזה שכל עמוק ביותר > "From the delight a person has when he discovers some very deep idea." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2162)

2. **Every small delight you feel is a ray falling from a bottomless delight that you do not feel.** (M6) *How:* The particular pleasure is 'a ray of a ray' from the very power of delight, the hidden source of all ideas. > הנה הוא בא בבחי' אור תענוג פרטי היורד מכח עצם התענוג > "It comes as a particular light of delight that descends from the power of the essence of delight." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2163)

3. **In the Essence itself there is no higher and lower at all. Head and end are equal.** (M5) *How:* Levels and steps belong only to light that spreads out. The Essence, from which the light comes, has no top or bottom. > אלא שוה ומשוה ראש וסוף בלי ימצא ראש וסוף מעלה ומטה כלל וכלל > "Rather, head and end are equal, without any head or end, up or down, at all." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2171)

4. **To make a world of steps, He 'stepped back', the way a craftsman lets go of the whole picture in order to work on one part.** (M3) *How:* Mashal: the craftsman first sees the whole vessel at once. To plan its parts he must put the whole picture aside, and the last part, the feet, comes to mind first. That letting-go is the tzimtzum. > הרי מוכרח לסלק ולצמצם המח' הכללי' כשמתחיל לצייר במח' בפרט > "He must remove and contract the general thought when he begins to picture the details." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2174)

5. **And the higher a thing is, the more it can only show itself through its outermost edge.** (M10) *How:* Kasha: the circle-light is higher, so why is it called 'soul-level' and the straight line 'spirit'? Answer: the greater cannot pour its inside into a small receiver. Moshe's face was like the sun, so the people got him only through Yehoshua. > שכל הגבוה גבוה במדריגה יותר לא יוכל לבא ממנו בגילוי למטה רק מבחי' חיצוניות > "Whatever is higher in level cannot be revealed below except from its outer side." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2178)

6. **That is why the highest things come down lowest: the Torah is in physical matters, and the clouds of glory took the stones and arrows.** (M15) *How:* The text draws the rule to the ground: what comes from higher clothes itself in what is more outward and more physical. > וכן ענני כבוד שקיבלו אבנים וחיצים > "And so the clouds of glory took the stones and arrows." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2180)

**Bottom:** The source of everything, delight itself, never shows; it shows only as the latest and lowest thing. **Return:** So the outer and plain things in front of you (the deed, the physical Torah, the edge of a feeling) are where the highest has come down. Do not wait for the inside to shine first.

## MR3-C03 · In exile we mostly just keep going, without feeling. Is that worth anything?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Igulim veYosher', lines 2181-2205 (seraphim and ofanim) · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Love that comes from understanding fades; the doing goes on, carried by a small ray of the old love.** (M9) *How:* The text describes ordinary life honestly: a love that was born from thinking cannot burn forever. The person keeps doing the kind deed 'coldly', and a little life of the love is still in it. > וזה ברור שא"א שתבער האהבה באותו [ה]דבר שחפץ בו לעולם בלי הפסק > "It is clear that love for a thing cannot burn forever without a break." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2182)

2. **That is our state in exile: the 'small' state, just walking after G-d by doing, with mind and heart hidden inside the deed.** (M2) *How:* Like a baby in the womb with its head between its knees: the feeling is folded inside the walking. 'Walk after Hashem your G-d.' > שבנ"י בגלות הן בבחי' הקטנות > "The Jewish people in exile are in the 'small' state." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2186)

3. **Bowing only your head seems like nothing. But bowing the head leads to lying flat on the ground, the real giving up of self.** (M15) *How:* Outer surrender can be without inner feeling, the text admits. Still the end is planted in the beginning: the bend of the head brings the full prostration. > אך מ"מ כפיפת הראש מביאה לידי השתחווא' אמיתי' בשיטוח על הארץ על פניו > "The bowing of the head brings one to true prostration, spread on the ground on one's face." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2187)

4. **When the teacher is beyond you completely, you stop measuring yourself at all. That bittul is deeper than the bittul of someone who understands.** (M10) *How:* Mashal of two students: one grasps his master and bows in proportion. The other cannot grasp him at all and so 'does not see himself as anything'. > אז בחי' ביטולו הוא רק ביטול העצמי' שאינו רואה את עצמו לכלום לגבי ערך רבו > "His bittul is a bittul of self: he does not see himself as anything next to his master." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2192)

5. **A wheel has no top. What is up goes down and what is down comes up, and that is how the deepest surrender looks.** (M10) *How:* A person standing and bowing still has his head above his feet. The ofan, the wheel-angel, turns: its top becomes bottom. In surrender above knowledge, 'no one knows which is higher.' > הכל הוא בחי' ביטול שלמעלה מן הדעת שאין שם בחי' ראש וסוף ולא נודע מי [ה]עליון > "It is all a bittul above knowledge, where there is no head and end and it is not known which is higher." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2195)

6. **And the same part that rises is the part that comes back down, like a flame leaping from a coal and returning.** (M7) *How:* The seraphim's running and returning is like breathing: a new breath each time. The ofanim's is like the flame, the same one goes out and comes back. > משא"כ באופנים שאותו חלק העליון ממנו שעולה הוא עצמו שיורד > "With the ofanim, the very part that goes up is the part that comes down." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2197)

7. **So the hidden glory itself will be seen below, 'below exactly as above'.** (M15) *How:* The ofanim say 'Blessed is the glory of Hashem from His place', asking for the hidden Essence to come out and be revealed in the lowest world. > אז יהי' למטה כמו למעלה ממש > "Then it will be below exactly as above." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2205)

**Bottom:** The bottom is the wheel: a surrender with no head and foot, carried by the plain deed when feeling is gone. **Return:** Back to the day: keep walking after Him in the small state; bow the head even without feeling; the deed carries the hidden love.

## MR3-C04 · How does the Infinite reach a world that is nothing next to Him?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Shaashuei HaMelech', lines 2383-2410 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Ordinary causes make things that then stand on their own: cut the apple off and it stays an apple.** (M3) *How:* The text first lays down how cause and effect work: one 'something' makes another 'something', and the effect stays separate even after the cause stops. > אבל אם יופסק בחי' קריא' והמשכ' דשמות העליוני' אין להנבראים קיום ומציאות כלל והי' כלא הי' > "If the calling of the higher names stopped, creatures would have no existence at all, as if they had never been." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2394)

2. **But G-d holds the world the way you hold a person by calling his name.** (M9) *How:* Call a person 'Reuven!' and his whole self turns to you, even though his name has none of his life in it. > כאשר יקראו לאדם בשמו ראובן או שמעון וכה"ג מיד יפנה > "When they call a person by his name, Reuven or Shimon, at once his whole self turns." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2389)

3. **A name turns a person more easily than grabbing his body.** (M10) *How:* The text stops on the wonder: alone, you forget your name; it adds nothing to you. Yet called, you turn faster than if someone took hold of you. > כי יותר בקל יפנה ע"י קריאת שמו משיאחזנו בגופו > "He turns more easily by being called by his name than if someone took hold of his body." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2389)

4. **A name is like the handle of a cup: not the cup, but how it is picked up and brought to the mouth.** (M10) *How:* The handle holds no water and is not 'the vessel'. Without it no one could lift the cup to drink. > וה"ז כמו עד"מ בית יד של הכלי > "It is like the handle of a vessel." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2389)

5. **So the world is both: given up into Him and still really there, because He keeps calling.** (M12) *How:* If He stopped calling, the world would vanish; if the calling became a solid 'something from something', the world would be cut off. So the creature is a middle: 'its bittul is not total, its being is not separate.' > ואין הבטול בטול לגמרי ואין היש בחי' יש נפרד לגמרי > "The nullification is not total nullification and the being is not a fully separate being." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2397)

6. **The deepest delight is not pleasure in a thing. It is the self's delight in being itself, like a person's love of his own life.** (M8) *How:* Delight in a son or a song is still delight in 'something outside'. Essential delight is in the soul itself, the yechida. > כמו באהבת עצמו שאוהב חיי נפשו > "As in a person's love of himself, that he loves his own life." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2408)

7. **Above, there is nothing besides Him to enjoy. So His delight is the King's delight in Himself, and the world comes from that.** (M14) *How:* The why of creation: before anything, 'He and His Name alone' (2413), a running and returning inside the Essence. > כי אין שום דבר זולתו שיתענג ממנו כמו שעשועי האב בבנו > "For there is nothing other than Him for Him to delight in, as a father delights in his son." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2410)

**Bottom:** The world exists because He keeps saying its name, and His saying comes from His delight in Himself. **Return:** Prayer: in each blessing we call Him by His Name, and 'why does Israel cry out and not get answered? Because they do not call by the Name' (2390).

## MR3-C05 · Why is G-d silent in exile?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Shaashuei HaMelech', lines 2401-2421 (sleep, exile, and the deed) · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **A person can be so absorbed in himself that he does not turn when called. Sleep is the fullest form of this.** (M8) *How:* The text climbs: deep in thought you stop feeling your feelings; deeper, even the first thought is lost; deeper, will and delight are gathered in. In sleep everything returns to the self and only a thread of life stays in the limbs. > וה"ז דומה בכלל לענין השינה > "This is like sleep in general." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2401)

2. **Exile is His sleep.** (M4) *How:* What looks like absence is withdrawal into His own depth. > וזהו עיקר ענין הגלות לפי שבזמן הגלות הוא בחי' שינה לפני המקום > "This is the main point of exile: in the time of exile it is a state of sleep before the Omnipresent." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2416)

3. **A sleeper does not feel a fly walking on his clothes or even on his face.** (M10) *How:* So the nations dance in His palace and He is silent: from the Essence, all the worlds take up no space at all. > וכמו מי שהוא ישן שאינו מרגיש בזבוב שהולך על לבושיו וגם על פניו לא ירגיש > "Like a sleeper who does not feel a fly walking on his clothes, and on his face too he does not feel it." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2416)

4. **His silence is His strength: 'these are His mighty deeds, that nations dance in His palace and He is quiet.'** (M11) *How:* What blocks (His silence) is turned into a sign of His depth: the rising into His own Essence has no end. > וזהו שא' הן הן גבורותיו שגוים מרקדים בהיכלו והוא שותק > "That is why it is said, 'These are His mighty deeds': nations dance in His palace and He is silent." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2416)

5. **What wakes Him toward us is not mainly intention. It is the deed and the spoken word.** (M15) *How:* A thought, even a holy intention, rises up; it does not draw down. To draw the light into vessels you need action and speech. > היינו ענין מעשה המצות בעובדא ובמלולא דוקא ולא ע"י כונה ורעד"ל בלבד > "Specifically the doing of the mitzvos in deed and in speech, not by intention alone." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2419)

6. **Intention alone can even get in the way of the drawing-down.** (M12) *How:* Because it is a rising, it is the opposite motion. Both are needed: rising in prayer, drawing down in deed. > ולא די שאינו מועיל אלא מונע ומעכב > "Not only does it not help; it holds back and prevents." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2420)

7. **In prayer the two meet: bowing at 'Blessed' is the running up, standing straight at the Name is the coming back down.** (M15) *How:* The body itself acts out the run and return. > שהרי בחי' הבטול במציאות כשכורע בברוך הוא בחי' רצוא וכשזוקף בשם הוא בחי' ההמשכה > "When he bows at 'Blessed' it is the running, and when he straightens at the Name it is the drawing down." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2421)

**Bottom:** The bottom is His sleep: a silence that is not absence but depth. **Return:** Wake Him with the hand and the mouth: the mitzvah done, the word said. 'And Hashem awoke like a sleeper' (2418).

## MR3-C06 · Why pray at all, if there are 613 commandments?

*Where:* Maamarei, Kuntres Inyan HaTefillah 5587 (Yiddish), lines 2425-2450 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Prayer is like the spine: not one of the limbs, yet it holds them all together.** (M10) *How:* The 248 limbs could all be there; without the spinal cord the body is dead. Prayer is what joins the commandments to G-d. > ומזה המשל יובן ג"כ ענין פעולת התפלה > "From this parable we understand what prayer does." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2426)

2. **Prayer can also dry up, the same today as yesterday and as tomorrow. That is a spine drying out.** (M1) *How:* The text warns that lifeless prayer slowly cuts a person off. > גאר אן איין שום לעבדיקייט וויא נאכטין אזוי היינט און אזוי מארגין > "Without any life, like yesterday so today and so tomorrow." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2433)

3. **Begin from Him alone: there is nothing to want outside Him, and no one there to want.** (M2) *How:* The first entry into prayer is with 'small' knowing, as before creation: 'Master of the world, who reigned before anything.' > בכן איז ניט דא גאר קיין זאך וואס צו וועלין חוץ ממנו אחרי שמלבדו הוא און עס איז ניט דא ווער זאל וועלין > "There is nothing at all to want outside Him, since He alone is, and there is no one there who would want." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2446)

4. **That small knowing is like a man who is tied up and still has to do business.** (M9) *How:* He does not know how it will end; wanting does not help; he just has to act. Do not worry 'what will become of me'. > למשל וויא דא איינער איז צו גיבונדין מי מוז האנדלין אין מו"מ > "Like one who is tied up and has to do business." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2446)

5. **And it is like a child proud to say 'that is my father' without understanding who his father is.** (M16) *How:* 'How fortunate we are, how good our portion' (Ashreinu): our portion is the Infinite Himself. That pride softens the ground like rain before plowing. > למשל וויא איינער איז זיך מתפאר דאס איז מיין פאטער בדרך כלל > "Like a person who boasts, 'That is my father,' in a general way." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2448)

6. **Then the songs of praise: like a man with boils going to see a great king. He wants nothing for himself; he only goes to see the king, and loses himself.** (M8) *How:* He knows he will get nothing; so he has no angle. Just seeing the king, he forgets himself entirely. The detailed praises plow up the self. > למשל וויא אמוכה שחין גייט זעהן אמלך גדול ונורא > "Like a man covered with boils who goes to see a great and awesome king." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2449)

7. **At Shema it all comes together at once, like a long Talmud discussion that suddenly lights up.** (M13) *How:* After the long give-and-take, all the pieces gather to one point and 'all at once it became bright'. That is the inner union. > איז מיט אמאהל ליכטיג > "All at once it became bright." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2450)

**Bottom:** The bottom of prayer: 'nobody there to want', the Infinite alone as before creation. **Return:** See next chain: the return through the Amidah, falling on the face and getting up again.

## MR3-C07 · After 'He alone', what happens to the world and to me?

*Where:* Maamarei, Kuntres Inyan HaTefillah 5587 (Yiddish), lines 2435-2467 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **There are two kinds of work: tilling and tanning.** (M3) *How:* Tilling brings out what is hidden in the soil, but the soil stays soil. Tanning changes the hide itself into leather. > אבער עס איז פראן עוד אופן עבודה מלשון עורות עבודים, למשל דיא זאך אליין ווערט אנדערש > "There is another kind of avodah, from 'tanned hides': the thing itself becomes different." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2435)

2. **A Jew can feel in his own heart that there is nothing but Him, just as before creation.** (M16) *How:* This is not proved by the mind; it is an inheritance from the fathers, held like seeing with the eyes. > א איד קען אין הארצין דער הערין וויא ער איז אין עוד מלבדו > "A Jew can feel in his heart how there is nothing besides Him." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2437)

3. **When we forget, the life-force goes silent, like a sheep being sheared. We keep living as if we were on our own.** (M11) *How:* 'Like Rachel, silent before her shearers': the hidden power keeps us alive in hiding and says nothing. > איז דאס וויא אשעפש מי שערט > "It is like a sheep that is being sheared." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2443)

4. **And yet the vessels and the hiding are also Him. The hiding itself is G-dliness, for the good of the creatures.** (M11) *How:* 'If your heart runs, return': we want to leave the vessels for Him, but the vessels too come from the true Being. The concealment is so that we can choose and earn. > ווען דיא הסתרה גופא איז אלקות בכדי להיטיב > "When the hiding itself is G-dliness, in order to do good." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2455)

5. **After the Amidah, fall on your face like one who wants nothing, like a dead man, 'like a lion lying on its prey'.** (M8) *How:* Leaving the king one may slip into one's own affairs; so one hands oneself to death, wanting nothing at all. > דהיינו ער זאל [גאר] ניט וועלן ווי א מת בר מינן > "He should not want anything at all, like a dead man." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2463)

6. **But staying down is also a lack. You must be lifted again, and what lifts you is His understanding, not yourself.** (M15) *How:* 'Who will raise her?' Mi is Binah: understanding G-dliness lifts him back to being something, 'but not from himself'. > מי איז בינה דהיינו בינתו ית' וואס ער לייגט זיך אריין צו פאר שטיין אין אלקות דאס הויבט עם ווידער אוף > "His understanding, as he puts himself into grasping G-dliness, lifts him up again." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2465)

7. **Leaving the king's house, keep the impression of the king himself; it guards you all day.** (M15) *How:* Aleinu: a reshimah (a print) of the prayer stays and carries the bittul into eating, business and Torah. > מוז בייא אים בלייבין ארשימה אכח > "There must remain with him an impression, a power." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2467)

8. **Without prayer first, even Torah is not fully Torah.** (M15) *How:* Prayer is the spine that runs from end to end; then the mitzvos become the King's limbs. > אבל האומר אין לי אלא תורה אפי' תורה אין לו > "One who says 'I have only Torah' does not even have Torah." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2467)

**Bottom:** Falling on the face like a dead man, wanting nothing. **Return:** Lifted again not by yourself; carry the print of the King into the day; 'today to do them' turns the physical thing into His dwelling.

## MR3-C08 · Who wants the closeness more, G-d or us?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Sameach Tesamach: sovev umemaleh' (wedding of his daughter), lines 1081-1089 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **The giver wants to give more than the receiver wants to receive.** (M9) *How:* The rabbis' saying: more than the calf wants to suckle, the cow wants to nurse. > וידוע שיותר ממה שהעגל רוצה לינק הפרה רוצה להניק > "More than the calf wants to suckle, the cow wants to nurse." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1089)

**Bottom:** His love comes from outside and turns us to face Him. **Return:** The deed in the body is where His Essence is moved most.

## MR3-C09 · Why does the Torah curse serving G-d without joy, even when the service is perfect?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Sameach Tesamach: sovev umemaleh', lines 1090-1110 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Joy breaks the walls a person normally lives inside.** (M9) *How:* A king usually sits in his palace and speaks little. At his feast he goes out to the street and talks with the lowest servants. > להיות ידוע בטבע השמחה שהוא פורץ גדר הטבעי > "It is known that the nature of joy is to break through the natural boundary." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1091)

2. **In his joy, the king even sets rebels free from prison.** (M10) *How:* Joy changes a thing into its opposite: from hatred to love, from judgment to kindness. > שבשעת השמחה יצוה המלך להוציא מבית הכלא גם המורדים ופושעי' בו > "At the time of joy the king will order even the rebels and sinners against him freed from prison." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1091)

3. **Joy reaches the inner self: in joy a person tells secrets he would not tell even his dearest friend.** (M9) *How:* That is the proof that joy comes from the soul's own depth. > ע"י השמחה יבוא לידי גילוי בדיבור אשר לא יגלוהו לשום אדם אפי' לאוהבו אשר כנפשו > "Through joy he will reveal in speech what he would not tell anyone, not even his friend who is like his own soul." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1091)

4. **The kasha: perfect service with love and awe, but without joy, brings curses. 'This would make anyone's hair stand on end.'** (M1) *How:* The text states it plainly and lets it sting. > וזה הדבר באמת פלא גדול מאד אשר תשיר שערות ראש כל איש עובד ה' בתומו > "This is truly a very great wonder, which would make the hair fall from the head of every simple servant of Hashem." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1103)

5. **Joy of a mitzvah is joy 'only because I did His will', not because I understood it. So it reaches the One whose will it is.** (M2) *How:* The mitzvah itself reaches only His will. The joy of doing it, for no reason but that He wanted it, reaches the Owner of the will, His own delight. > א"כ ה"ז מגיע בבחי' בעל הרצון > "So it reaches the level of the Owner of the will." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1107)

6. **And one who is not happy himself cannot make another happy.** (M15) *How:* He rejoiced in Adam from His own joy, before anyone did anything. So we ask: rejoice with us as then. > עד"מ מי שאינו שמח בעצמו אינו יכול לשמח לזולתו > "As one who is not happy himself cannot make another happy." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1110)

**Bottom:** Joy for no reason but His will, which reaches past every screen. **Return:** Do the mitzvah glad that it is His will. That joy, not the depth of understanding, sweetens judgment below (1109).

## MR3-C10 · What really holds a person to a desire?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Yafeh Sha'ah Achas', lines 1843-1856 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **What makes a desire stick is not the desire. It is the wish to feel alive in it.** (M2) *How:* The text glosses da'as in Yiddish: 'he wants to feel that he lives.' > כי הדעת הוא הרגשת החיות (בל"א ער וויל הערין ווי ער לעבט) > "Da'as is the feeling of being alive ('he wants to feel how he lives')." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1844)

2. **That is why two people eat the same food and one is lost if he misses a meal while the other does not mind a fast.** (M9) *How:* The difference is where the da'as is. 'Life you cannot let go of.' But if his da'as is that G-d's word gives life through the food, the fast does not reach his soul. > בל"א דעם לעבין קען מען דאך ניט לאזין > "Life, after all, you cannot let go of." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1846)

3. **The head of it all is the soul's craving to feel itself.** (M5) *How:* Proof: a man who hates someone forgets the hatred at his son's wedding. The particular desires can be traded. But the wish to feel oneself does not change. > וראש דדעת דעמלק הוא בחי' תאות ותשוקת עצמיות הנפש להרגיש עצמותה > "The head of Amalek's da'as is the soul's craving to feel its own self." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1849)

4. **Gehinnom is not a punishment sent from outside. It is your own thoughts, the things you burned for, standing between you and the light.** (M11) *How:* After death the soul sees and longs; its own passions surround it and block it. That is the burning. > כי בחי' גיהנם הוא תיסרך רעתך והאדם בעצמו הוא העושה כל מיני גיהנם > "Gehinnom is 'your own evil will punish you'; a person makes every kind of Gehinnom himself." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1851)

5. **So the worries and poverty of this world are a mercy: they make a person forget his pleasures here instead of there.** (M11) *How:* What blocks is turned: the furnace of poverty and confusion refines now. > ולכן מחסדי ה' עלינו מאיר ומזכך אותנו בכור העוני והבלבול בעוה"ז > "So by G-d's kindness He cleanses us in the furnace of poverty and confusion in this world." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1852)

6. **For the very root, the wish to feel oneself, there is no cleaning. There is only losing it, a despair until one prefers death to life.** (M4) *How:* Knowledge and good deeds do not reach it. Only a bitterness so deep that he asks for his soul to go back to G-d. > שהוא ביאוש החיות עד שיבחר המות מן החיים ממש > "A despair of life, until he would choose death over life." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1855)

7. **That is the red heifer burned to ash.** (M10) *How:* The image of the end: nothing of the old self remains. > וזהו שריפת הפרה עד שנעשה אפר > "This is the burning of the heifer until it becomes ash." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1856)

**Bottom:** The bottom is the end of 'I want to feel myself': ash. **Return:** See MR3-C11: from the ash, the deed that shines, and the life that is felt as no-life.

## MR3-C11 · What is the higher Paradise, and why do we need deeds after teshuvah?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Yafeh Sha'ah Achas', lines 1858-1871 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **There are two Paradises. One is a ray of the Shechinah, for service felt by a 'me'. The other is a true 'nothing', for one whose only wish was to be included in his Source.** (M3) *How:* Reward is the deed itself: whoever served with felt excitement receives felt light; whoever served out of disgust with his own life, wanting only Him, receives the true Ayin. > רק רצונו הי' להבטל וליכלל אל המקום אשר משם לוקחה > "His only wish was to be nullified and included in the place from which he was taken." 2. **The sun through windows varies with each window. If the sun's ball itself came in, no window would change it.** (M10) *How:* Rays have degrees. The Essence has none. 3. **Teshuvah without deeds is light without a vessel, like the sons of Aharon who ran and did not return.** (M12) *How:* 'If your heart runs, return to One.' The aim is a home below. > שהתשו' בלי תומ"צ הוא בחי' אור בלי כלי והוא כמו בחי' מיתת בני אהרן > "Teshuvah without Torah and mitzvos is light without a vessel, like the death of Aharon's sons." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1870)

4. **How can 'nothing' be clothed? Because the feeling of having no life is itself the G-dly soul's life.** (M12) *How:* 'He kills and gives life': the death, the feeling of not-being, is the life. > פי' המיתה היינו הרגשת העדר החיות הוא בעצמו החיות > "The death is the feeling of the absence of life, and that itself is the life." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1870)

5. **So the deed after that bitterness is a garment that shows that the garment is gone.** (M15) *How:* 'I will clothe you in fine garments': a garment that reveals the taking-off of the garment. > וזהו והלבש אותך מחלצות פי' לבוש המראה חליצת הלבוש > "A garment that shows the removal of the garment." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1871)

**Bottom:** Feeling oneself as nothing, which is the soul's true life. **Return:** Good deeds 'shine' with it; 'not with himself at all' (1861) while doing them.

## MR3-C12 · Can a person know G-d, or only that He exists?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Ve'amartem Zevach Pesach' and 'Avadim Hayinu' (Pesach 5577), lines 50-68 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **From our own wisdom we learn only that there is wisdom above, not what it is. Like a blind man told about light.** (M10) *How:* You can explain light and dark to a blind man in every detail; he knows that it is, not what it is. One who sees needs no proofs. > וה"ז כמשל סומא שלא ראה מאורות מימיו > "Like a blind man who never saw light in his life." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 50)

2. **Standing outside the palace and thinking about the king makes you humble by measure. Seeing the king himself undoes you.** (M7) *How:* Bittul from contemplation has degrees. Bittul from seeing the essence is total. > משא"כ כשנכנס להיכל המלך ורואה את עצם המלך > "But when he enters the king's palace and sees the king himself." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 60)

3. **Feeling is not lower than thinking. It comes from the soul's essence; the mind is only the pipe.** (M4) *How:* People think emotion is below the intellect because it comes from it. The text reverses it: the soul is moved because it was taken from G-d. > והשכל אינו אלא דרך מעביר > "The mind is only a passageway." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 65)

4. **In great joy you clap your hands without choosing to. The essence of joy shows in your fingers.** (M9) *How:* It is not the nerve of movement obeying the will; it is joy itself coming out in the body. > שבעת שהוא בשמחה רבה אזי מוכרח לספק ביד ואין בו בחירה להיות מונע א"ע שלא לספק > "When he is in great joy he must clap his hands, and he has no choice to hold himself back." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 65)

5. **Six hundred thousand left Egypt with no reason and no understanding. That was the soul's essence showing.** (M8) *How:* They had no grasp of G-d, yet they would not stay sunk in impurity. That plain going was the preparation for Sinai. > שהרי יצאו ממצרים ת"ר אלף רגלי אנשים וטף בלי טעם ודעת > "They left Egypt, 600,000 men on foot, women and children, without reason and knowledge." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 67)

6. **That is why Kiddush, the 'big' state, comes before matzah, the 'small'. Like a person returning from the depths, who first grasps beyond his level and only then grows step by step.** (M15) *How:* The baal teshuvah's arousal is not in proportion to his soul; afterward he goes in order. > וה"ז כמשל בע"ת שהי' מקושר בעומק רע בתחלה > "Like a baal teshuvah who was first bound in the depth of evil." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 68)

**Bottom:** The essence moving without reason, the way 600,000 walked out of Egypt. **Return:** Start where you are; the big moment may come first; then go slowly, step by step.

## MR3-C13 · If G-d's kindness turns to judgment, does He change?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'LeHavin Shoresh Kabbalas HaTorah' (Shavuos 5577), lines 264-268 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Love that comes from reasons can change; love that is part of you cannot.** (M7) *How:* Love for a friend found guilty can turn to anger. A father's love for a son, or David and Yonasan's, is essential and does not change. > כי האהבה אשר מצד השכל והטעם יוכל להשתנות > "Love that comes from reason and taste can change." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 265)

2. **A father who strikes his son: even at the moment of the blow, the essential love has not moved.** (M10) *How:* The rebuke itself comes from the love; he wants the son on the straight way. > גם בשעת ההכאה האהבה עצמיו' לא ניזז מאתו > "Even at the time of the blow, the essential love has not moved away from him." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 268)

3. **So the destruction and the exile are from His essential love for Israel.** (M11) *How:* What looks like the end of the bond is its expression. After the rebuke, 'as a father desires his son', the kingdom returns. > וכמו"כ ענין חורבן בהמ"ק וזמן הגלות אינו רק מחמת אהבה עצמיו' שיש מאא"ס לישראל > "So too the destruction of the Temple and the time of exile are only because of the essential love the Infinite has for Israel." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 268)

**Bottom:** A love that does not change because it is not based on anything. **Return:** Torah after the argument does not change either: the mitzvah, once clear, is His unchanging will (265-66).

## MR3-C14 · Who is closer: the one who never fell, or the one who came back?

*Where:* Maamarei, 'Mi Yitencha' (5577), lines 205-222 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **A person pities his own handiwork like his own burning house, because some of his power is in it.** (M9) *How:* So G-d pities His world. > ולכן האדם חס על מעשה ידיו כמו על שריפת ביתו > "That is why a person pities his handiwork as he would the burning of his house." ## MR3-C24 · How does distance become nearness?

*Where:* Sha'arei Teshuvah, lines 715-718 and 731 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **A father and son turned their backs and walked far apart.** (M10) *How:* When the son, from far away, cries with a bitter voice until it touches the point of his soul... > שכאשר יתעורר הבן מרחוק וצועק בקול מר עד שנגע לנקודת נפשו > "When the son wakes from afar and cries out with a bitter voice until it touches the point of his soul." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 718)

2. **...the father, standing far off with his back turned, turns his face to him.** (M11) *How:* The nearness came because of the distance; had he been close, he would not have been so bitter. > מתעורר גם הוא להחזיר פניו אליו > "He too is roused to turn his face to him." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 718)

3. **A covenant is one thing cut in two; the two are halves of one body. Even if one does ugly things, the other cannot take revenge.** (M10) *How:* That is the bond that the Thirteen Attributes call on. > ואפי' אם א' יפעל רעה לרעהו או יעשה מעשים מכוערים > "Even if one does evil to the other or does ugly deeds." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 731)

**Bottom:** The covenant, which no deed can undo. **Return:** From the far place, cry plainly. He turns.

## MR3-C25 · How do I know if my excitement in prayer is real?

*Where:* Maamarei, Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, lines 1653-1760 · **stated** (wording ours)

1. **Every Jew, even of low level, is moved when he hears a true explanation of G-dliness. That is real.** (M9) *How:* It comes from the soul's root, like a spark pulled after the flame. > כאשר ישמע איזה דבר בענין ההתבוננות באלקות כמו ביאור דסובב וממלא וכה"ג תתפעל נפשו ביותר > "When he hears something about contemplating G-dliness, such as sovev and memaleh, his soul is very moved." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1653)

2. **There is also a fake fervor: shouting, heat, 'serving oneself'.** (M1) *How:* It looks the same from outside. It is blood heating up, and it ends in arrogance. > וזהו הנק' עובד א"ע > "This is called serving oneself." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1655)

3. **The test: when insulted, does it make you sick?** (M9) *How:* If so, the 'humility' was the deepest pride. > והמופת לזה כשיבטשוהו בבזיון > "The proof of this is when they humiliate him." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1757)

4. **Real nothingness is not made by counting your faults; it simply is.** (M4) *How:* 'Nishtikeit' that is 'not a made-up nishtikeit'. > הנקרא נישטיקייט ער איז נישט בעצם ניט גימא[כטע נישטיקייט > "Called nothingness: he is truly nothing, not a manufactured nothingness." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1759)

5. **And the sign of the real thing: all day you are drawn to G-d in deed.** (M15) *How:* Not in the high moment, but in how the day goes. > הוא בחי' ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהי' במעשה דוקא > "The drawing of the soul all day after closeness to G-d, specifically in deed." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1760)

**Bottom:** Nothingness that is not manufactured. **Return:** Judge the prayer by the day after it.

## MR3-D01 · The love inside the blow

**Teaching (stated):** His love for you is not based on anything, so nothing you did and nothing that happened can change it. Even the exile, even the blow, comes from that love.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Think of love that has reasons: it can turn when the reasons go. 2. Think of a father's love for his child: it has no reason, so it does not turn. 3. See the father at the moment he strikes the son: the love has not moved; the blow is from it. 4. Now look at what is hard in your life and say: even this is inside His love, not outside it.

**From inside:** A son being rebuked who knows, even while crying, that he is his father's son. **Where it lands:** In the body, in the hard moment itself; then in the deed of keeping the mitzvah anyway. **Extends:** D8 (the bond that cannot be let go), seen from His side. (ours)

> גם בשעת ההכאה האהבה עצמיו' לא ניזז מאתו
> "Even at the time of the blow, the essential love has not moved away from him." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 268)

> וכמו"כ ענין חורבן בהמ"ק וזמן הגלות אינו רק מחמת אהבה עצמיו' שיש מאא"ס לישראל
> "The destruction and exile are only because of His essential love for Israel." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 268)

## MR3-D02 · The farthest place holds the deepest point

**Teaching (stated):** Your deepest point does not live in your mind or heart. It rests in your four cubits, in plain rest, in the most solid 'something'. The true Being rests only in plain being.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Notice that your thoughts and feelings change from day to day. 2. Ask where the point is that gave its life for G-d in every generation, in great and simple alike. 3. The text answers: not in head, heart, clothing or house; in the four cubits around you. 4. Rest. On Shabbos, simply rest your body, and know that this rest is where the point lives.

**From inside:** Sitting still at the Shabbos table, with nothing to achieve, equal with everyone. **Where it lands:** In the body at rest; in 'let no man leave his place'. **Extends:** D12 and D13 (the deed and plainness), sharpened: the place, not only the act. (ours)

> כ"א דייקא בד' אמות הרחוקים בתכלית בריחוק אשר אין ריחוק יותר ממנו שם דוקא ישכון הארת בחי' יחידה
> "Only in the four cubits, the farthest of all, there the light of the yechida rests." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1598)

> בחי' יש האמיתי לא ישרה כ"א דוקא ביש גמור
> "The true Being rests only in complete being." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1598)

> ובבחי' יחידה [עצמה] שווין הכל ממש
> "In the yechida itself everyone is exactly equal." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1595)

## MR3-D03 · Called by name

**Teaching (stated):** He holds the world by calling it, the way a name turns a whole person. And we reach Him the same way: by calling His Name.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Remember being called by name across a room: your whole self turned. 2. Your name has none of your life in it; alone you forget it. Yet it turns you more easily than a hand on your arm. 3. He keeps saying the world's name; if He stopped, it would be as if it never was. 4. In each blessing, call Him by His Name, plainly, as a child calls.

**From inside:** Turning toward someone who calls you; a cup lifted by its handle. **Where it lands:** In speech: the Name said in each blessing. **Extends:** D11 (the self as the one addressed): it is mutual; He turns when called. (ours)

> כי יותר בקל יפנה ע"י קריאת שמו משיאחזנו בגופו
> "He turns more easily by being called by his name than if someone took hold of his body." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2389)

> מפני מה ישראל צועקי' ואינם נעני' מפני כשקוראי' אין קוראי' בשם
> "Why does Israel cry out and not get answered? Because when they call, they do not call by the Name." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2390)

## MR3-D04 · His sleep

**Teaching (stated):** G-d's silence in exile is not absence. It is His withdrawal into His own Essence, like sleep, where the whole world is like a fly on a sleeper's coat. What wakes Him toward us is the deed and the spoken word.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Picture someone so absorbed that he does not hear his name. 2. Picture sleep: every power gathered into the self, only a thread of life in the limbs. 3. Exile is like this: nations dance in His palace, and He is silent, and that silence is His might. 4. Do not wait for Him to speak first. Do the mitzvah with your hand; say the words with your mouth.

**From inside:** A sleeper who does not feel the fly; then 'Hashem awoke like a sleeper.' **Where it lands:** In the deed and speech, not intention alone. **Extends:** new (ours)

> וזהו עיקר ענין הגלות לפי שבזמן הגלות הוא בחי' שינה לפני המקום
> "In the time of exile it is a state of sleep before the Omnipresent." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2416)

> וזהו שא' הן הן גבורותיו שגוים מרקדים בהיכלו והוא שותק
> "'These are His mighty deeds': nations dance in His palace and He is silent." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2416)

> היינו ענין מעשה המצות בעובדא ובמלולא דוקא ולא ע"י כונה ורעד"ל בלבד
> "Specifically the doing of the mitzvos in deed and speech, not by intention alone." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2419)

## MR3-D05 · The bottom of the circle is its top

**Teaching (stated):** In a circle there is no up or down. When you are pushed, or lower yourself, all the way to the bottom, you are level with the highest point. Real lowliness has no measure, because it is simple.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Make an honest count, where every hair looks like a mountain. 2. Look at your good deeds and say: 'only from the lips out'. 3. Look at an inner stirring and say: 'it was for a moment; truth does not stop'. 4. Stand there at the bottom, without pretending; that is the top of the circle.

**From inside:** A servant walking behind his master; an empty cup. **Where it lands:** In the body: heaviness of head before prayer; then walking after Him all day. **Extends:** D9 (the bottom sends you back), sharpened: the bottom is not left behind; it is the top. (ours)

> שאין בעיגול מצד עצמו בחי' מעלה ומטה כלל
> "A circle in itself has no up and down at all." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 129)

> דהיינו עד שגם כל חוט השערה נדמה לו כהר
> "Until every hair looks to him like a mountain." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 135)

> כי אין לך דבר עומד בפני תכלית ההכנעה בלב האמיתי'
> "Nothing stands before the utmost submission in a true heart." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 142)

## MR3-D06 · Faith from far away

**Teaching (stated):** A spark near a flame is pulled to it; no wonder. Faith pulls the soul from far away, as from Egypt. That is a deeper bond than seeing, and it holds everything up when all the light of love and awe has run out.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Notice the times you felt close; that was the spark near the flame. 2. Notice the times you felt far, and still did not leave. 3. That not-leaving is faith, the 'kindness of your youth', which He remembers even in the future. 4. Say plainly: I believe. No feeling is needed.

**From inside:** Walking after Him in the desert, in a land not sown. **Where it lands:** In plain faithfulness on dry days; in eating matzah, bread without taste. **Extends:** D7 (simple faith), deepened: faith is stronger than seeing, not a substitute for it. (ours)

> אין זה חידוש כלל בהיות הניצוץ קרוב ורואה בעין שיומשך
> "It is no wonder that the spark, when close and seeing, is drawn." (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 31)

> ויצאו משם באמונה פשוטה הרי כח אמונה זאת היא המושכת את הנפש בבחי' התכללות גם מרחוק
> "The power of this faith draws the soul even from afar." (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 31)

> באמונתם יחיו חיי עולם
> "By their faith they live eternal life." (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 33)

## MR3-D07 · The child's 'Abba'

**Teaching (stated):** A small child's word 'Abba' holds more of the truth of his father than a grown man's understanding, the way a short law for a child holds all of the Talmud. Smallness is not lower.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Think of a child who knows his father without knowing how he was born. 2. Think of a teacher giving a child a one-line law that holds the whole debate. 3. Say 'Avinu, our Father' without explaining it. 4. Let that one word carry everything you cannot understand.

**From inside:** A child asking: 'Abba, give me bread.' **Where it lands:** In speech: 'Avinu Malkeinu', said simply. **Extends:** D13 (plainness) and D7 (simple faith). (ours)

> ומבקש לחם ומים אבא האכילני לחם
> "'Abba, feed me bread.'" (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 43)

> ובאמת יש יתרון מעלה בקטנות הלבשה זו יותר מגדלות ההשגה שאח"כ
> "There is an advantage in this smallness over the greatness of grasp that comes later." (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 44)

## MR3-D08 · The wish to feel myself, and the life that is no-life

**Teaching (stated):** What holds a person is not desire but 'wanting to feel that I live'. Even in holiness this can creep in. The deepest life of the G-dly soul is the opposite: the feeling of not being, which is itself its life.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Ask what you want in your spiritual moments: Him, or to feel yourself feeling Him? 2. See that the particular wants can be traded, but the wish to feel oneself stays. 3. Know that no knowledge cleans this; only a bitterness that is willing to give it up. 4. Then do the deed 'not with yourself' at all, and see that this not-being is alive.

**From inside:** The red heifer burned to ash; a garment that shows the garment is gone. **Where it lands:** In plain deeds after deep regret, done without self-watching. **Extends:** D2 (no one left to watch), sharpened: names the hidden motive that keeps the watcher. (ours)

> וראש דדעת דעמלק הוא בחי' תאות ותשוקת עצמיות הנפש להרגיש עצמותה
> "The soul's craving to feel its own self." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1849)

> פי' המיתה היינו הרגשת העדר החיות הוא בעצמו החיות
> "The feeling of the absence of life is itself the life." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1870)

> בל"א נישט באיי זיך בתכלית
> "Not with himself at all." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1861)

## MR3-D09 · Distance nearer than nearness

**Teaching (stated):** Sometimes the most real moment is stepping back. When the King Himself shines, you cannot stand and you back away; each step back is a deeper giving up. And the son's bitter cry from far away is what turns the father's face.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Notice the urge to step back from G-d, to say 'I can't'. 2. Do not fight it as failure. It may be the King's own awe. 3. From that far place, cry plainly, until it touches the point of you. 4. Know that He turns His face because you are far.

**From inside:** Standing far off at Sinai; a son crying from a distance. **Where it lands:** In tears and plain words, then the return to deeds. **Extends:** D9 and D14 (the turn and no bottom). (ours)

> עד שכל פסיעה ופסיעה בפרט שנסוג אחור מורה ביטול העצמות ביותר
> "Each step that he retreats shows a greater nullification." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 201)

> מתעורר גם הוא להחזיר פניו אליו
> "He too is roused to turn his face to him." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 718)

## MR3-D10 · Pain as the test of what I still hold

**Teaching (stated):** Overcoming a desire in prayer does not remove it; it comes back. The honest test of what you still hold is how much its loss hurts.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Name a thing you think you have let go of. 2. Imagine losing it. Notice the pain. 3. The pain shows the size of the hold, exactly. 4. Work there; aim for 'Hashem gave and Hashem took'.

**From inside:** Honey on a radish: sweet until the honey is gone. **Where it lands:** In the day, when something is taken; bless on it. **Extends:** D8 (what you cannot let go), turned into a diagnostic. (ours)

> אבל מיד כאשר יסתלק אור וכח הרצון האלקי אז כח הרצון הזר חוזר וניעור כמו שהיה תחלה
> "The foreign will wakes again as before." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 303)

> ע"כ תלוי ריבוי ומיעוט הצער לפי ערך ריבוי ומיעוט העונג והרצון
> "The pain depends on the amount of pleasure and will." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 306)

## MR3-D11 · Joy only because it is His will

**Teaching (stated):** The joy of doing a mitzvah 'only because I did His will', without needing to understand it, reaches past every screen to the One whose will it is. Joy breaks walls, like a king who leaves his palace at his feast.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Pick one plain mitzvah. 2. Do not look for its reason. 3. Be glad simply that He wanted it and you did it. 4. Know that this gladness reaches Him as He is, and sweetens judgment below.

**From inside:** A king in the street at his feast, freeing prisoners. **Where it lands:** In the deed, with gladness. **Extends:** D12 (the deed in the hand), with the key of joy. (ours)

> וכידוע בענין שמחה ש"מ שאינה רק מפני שעשה את רצונו ולא מצד בחי' ההשגה והטעם
> "Joy of a mitzvah is only because he did His will, not because of grasping the reason." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1107)

> א"כ ה"ז מגיע בבחי' בעל הרצון
> "So it reaches the Owner of the will." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1107)

## MR3-D12 · The treasure that opens when you are 'dead'

**Teaching (stated):** The heart's deepest point does not show in good prayer. It opens when you have fallen so far you feel cut off; then a will not to be cut off rises. The long exile is for this.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. If you feel spiritually dead, do not despair of it. 2. Notice the small 'I will not be cut off' inside. 3. That is the treasure, spent only when life is at stake. 4. Let it lead to one plain act of return.

**From inside:** A man emptying his whole treasury to save his life. **Where it lands:** In the moment of greatest distance, then deeds. **Extends:** D8 (the bond), found from the bottom. (ours)

> עד שנפסק חבל התקשרות נשמתו וכמת יחשב אז יתמרמר ביותר
> "Until the cord is cut and he is counted as dead; then he becomes very bitter." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 447)

> ולא שנתעכב זמן הגלות מצד חטא
> "Not that exile was delayed because of sin." (Sha'arei Teshuvah (Lubavitch, c. 1817-1826).txt, line 448)

## MR3-D13 · The real and the fake fervor

**Teaching (stated):** There is a true being-moved, which every Jew has, and a fake heat that 'serves oneself'. The test is not the high moment but the insult afterward and the day after.

**Hisbonenus (steps ours, content stated):** 1. Notice when you are truly moved by an idea of G-d. 2. Notice when you are heating yourself up. 3. Test: does an insult make you sick? Does your fervor make you angry at whoever touches your honor? 4. Look for a nothingness that is not manufactured, and a pull to G-d in deeds all day.

**From inside:** Raw meat and cooked meat; blood heating versus a heart that has heard. **Where it lands:** In the day after prayer. **Extends:** new (a guardrail for the descent) (ours)

> וזהו הנק' עובד א"ע
> "This is called serving oneself." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1655)

> וכמו שמהתלהבות הבולט בהרגשת עצמו בתפלה יכעס על המנגדו להכותו ולרודפו כשנוגע בכבודו
> "From fervor that stands out in feeling oneself in prayer, he will be angry at one who touches his honor." (Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt, line 457)

> הוא בחי' ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהי' במעשה דוקא
> "The drawing of the soul all day after closeness to G-d, specifically in deed." (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1760)

## 3. Avodah: what to do

- **Begin prayer from Him alone: 'there is nothing to want outside Him and no one there to want'. Do not worry 'what will become of me'.** > בכן איז ניט דא גאר קיין זאך וואס צו וועלין חוץ ממנו אחרי שמלבדו הוא און עס איז ניט דא ווער זאל וועלין (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2446): "There is nothing to want outside Him, and no one there who would want."

- **In the songs of praise go 'like a man with boils to see the king': wanting nothing, only to see Him.** > למשל וויא אמוכה שחין גייט זעהן אמלך גדול ונורא (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2449): "Like a man with boils going to see a great king."

- **Call Him by His Name in each blessing; bow at 'Blessed', stand straight at the Name.** > שהרי בחי' הבטול במציאות כשכורע בברוך הוא בחי' רצוא וכשזוקף בשם הוא בחי' ההמשכה (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2421): "Bowing at 'Blessed' is the running; straightening at the Name is the drawing down."

- **After the Amidah, fall on your face like one who wants nothing; then let His understanding lift you, not yourself.** > וזה נק' כארי' דשכיב על טרפי' (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2463): "This is called 'like a lion lying on its prey'."

- **Keep the print of the King when you leave prayer; carry it into eating and business.** > מוז בייא אים בלייבין ארשימה אכח (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2467): "An impression must remain with him."

- **Do the deed and say the words; intention alone does not draw down.** > היינו ענין מעשה המצות בעובדא ובמלולא דוקא ולא ע"י כונה ורעד"ל בלבד (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2419): "In deed and speech, not intention alone."

- **Serve with joy 'only because it is His will'.** > וכידוע בענין שמחה ש"מ שאינה רק מפני שעשה את רצונו ולא מצד בחי' ההשגה והטעם (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1107): "Joy only because he did His will."

- **Before prayer: heaviness of head, falling away from yourself; an honest count where a hair looks like a mountain.** > הנק' בל"א אראפ פאל פון זיך (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 148): "Falling down from oneself."

- **Each morning, look at the plain world and see that it lives from G-d now; keep the contemplation near.** > הענין הוא כי ענין התפלה הוא להיות דבר ההתבוננות קרוב אליו (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 151): "The contemplation should be close to him."

- **On Shabbos, rest the body and the soul's work; that rest is where the yechida rests, equal for all.** > ואדרבה במנוחה גשמיות שם דוקא תהי' השראת בחי' יחידה (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1599): "In physical rest the yechida rests."

- **First thought on waking: a thought of return; not a 'made' brokenness. A true baal teshuvah is in joy.** > אדרבה איין בעל תשובה דארף זיין אין שמחה (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 2222): "A baal teshuvah must be in joy."

- **Test your fervor by how you take an insult, and by being drawn to G-d in deed all day.** > הוא בחי' ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהי' במעשה דוקא (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 1760): "All day after closeness to G-d, specifically in deed."

- **Never think you have arrived.** > כמו אם לומד ומתפלל לא ידמה בנפשו שהגיע לתכלית העבודה (Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (Lubavitch, 1813-1827).txt, line 233): "He should not imagine he has reached the goal of the work."
