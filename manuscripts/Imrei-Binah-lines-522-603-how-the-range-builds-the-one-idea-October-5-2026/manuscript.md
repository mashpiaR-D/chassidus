## The shape of the range

The whole range is built on one image: the seal. "Set me as a seal upon Your heart, as a seal upon Your arm" (Song 8:6). A raised seal makes sunken letters in the wax, and a sunken seal makes raised ones. The Mitteler Rebbe uses this to ask one question many times: **when a person reaches up, what comes down, and in what form?** His answer has two halves. Love and excitement from below, the "running" (ratzo), are a "something". They draw down a "something": light clothed in vessels, which is what every mitzvah is now. Bitul, becoming like a silent stone before the King, is a "nothing". It draws down the Essence itself, with no vessel, as it will be in the future. The rule behind both is "spirit brings spirit, just like it".

So the Essence (F05) leads, as in the earlier ranges. Here it is paired with bitul (F08) far more than before, because bitul is the one thing that reaches it. F09 is equally strong, since the book keeps turning opposites into one: raised and sunken, severity inside kindness, fear inside joy, "the end fixed in the beginning". F07 (the home below) is the future almost every time. The future is when the Essence will be seen without a garment, and the physical deed done now is its only vessel. F12 (knowing) is the weakest stated face in this range. That is not neglect. The range says outright that knowing and love do not reach the Essence (561), and near the end it argues that the heart's middot are rooted above the intellect (593-595).

The Tzitzit part (522-545) is mostly *stated*. Its long paragraphs reach their point before they end. The Tefillin part is more *structural*, because it builds three long kabbalistic models before it says what they are for: ascent by clothing versus ascent by inclusion (577-581), a ladder of letters, hairs and writing (584-588), and the middot's root above intellect (591-595).

## Tzitzit 18-19 (522-528): 'I' and 'Nothing', and the holiness of the one who is alone

Siman 18 asks what "I am Havayah" means, when nothing knows Him. What is known in any world is only a glimmer of a glimmer: > אין זה עצם מהותו רק בחי' הארה דהארה מעצמו' אורו הפשוט (line 522)

Keter is both "Ani" (I) and "Ayin" (nothing), and it stands between the Essence and the emanated (line 522). The soul analogy (G06) comes next. Will and delight are not held in any organ, yet the whole body moves by them equally. So Keter is the general sovev: > וזהו את השמי' ואת הארץ אני מלא בשוה שזהו בחי' סובב הכללי (line 523)

Before the Essence, though, even Keter is only the last rung (523.3). The Essence is reached only by self-sacrifice of the soul's very point, not by the will of delight (524.1, F08). Then the book states F05, F07 and F10 in one line. "I am Havayah" means I in My very Self, and through physical mitzvot: > שורה למטה כמו למעלה בעצמותו ממש בלי הפרש בין מעלה ומטה כלל (line 524)

Hidden "Ayin" and revealed "Ani" are exactly equal before the Essence (524.3). He lowered Himself to be called Havayah only so as to be "your God" in the deed (524.6). That lowering is what caused "in the place of His greatness, great humility" (line 525).

Siman 19 gives the range's clearest statement of the SPINE's "yachid". "Holy" means set apart from something lesser. It cannot apply to the Essence itself, which is alone: > אבל כשהוא יחידי ואין לו חבר להתייחס עמו (line 526)

The Essence's own holiness is its rising without end, and its descent without end is "blessed" (526.3, F05 + F09). Mitzvot hold only a tip of that holiness now. It will be revealed in the future, and it comes from the physical deed done now, because: > הענין הוא מטעם דנעוץ תחלתן בסופן דוקא (line 527)

"Mitzvah" is read as tzavta, joining: the Essence itself is joined in the mitzvot (527.4). The line ends with the light stored in the last physical act: > הגנוז ומלובש בסו' מעש' במצו' דעכשיו דוקא (line 528)

## Tzitzit 20-21 (529-536): white and tekhelet; death turned to life; the foot moves at once

The white threads are direct light from above. The tekhelet is returning light from below. The view that "heaven and earth were created as one" belongs to a level above both, where there is no giver and no receiver (529.2, F09 + F05). The white threads reach down "to light the darkness even in the lowest depths" (529.3). Then comes this range's strongest F11. The Essence's holiness is reached only by the teshuvah that turns evil itself into good: > שזהו כמו שהמות עצמו נעשה חיים שהוא יותר נעלה מגוף אור החיים (line 530)

This is the root of the resurrection. It is also why returning light rises higher than direct light: it "brings below as it is above" (530.3). The hidden darkness reveals the Essence more than the garment of light does: > לפי שבחי' החשך הנעלם הנעלם דישת חשך סתרו יאיר אור העצמות ממש (line 531)

Joy and fear are opposites, and they are joined. The joy of teshuvah, "from death and evil turned to life and good", wakes the joy above. In the very place of that joy there must be trembling (531.3-4).

Siman 21 returns to sovev with a medical analogy (533, G06). Damage to the foot does not disturb thought. Yet the foot moves the instant one wills. So the essence of will is present in the foot as much as in the brain, only hidden. Above, memale gives each world its own measure. Sovev is present equally in every detail: > כך משגיח בארץ סוף הכל בלי הפרש כלל (line 534)

"He is the place of the world, but the world is not His place" (534.3). Above both is the Essence, rising without limit and descending without end, "and both are one": > ואמנם שניהם ענין א' הוא (line 535)

Only through Torah and mitzvot does the Essence rest below as above (535.3). "The whole earth is full of His glory", and glory is Torah. It comes out of the Essence like hair from an overflowing mind: > כך התורה מצד שרשה בעצמות ממש שלא יכילנו כל כלי (line 535)

At Sinai the Essence's inner wisdom was revealed "by a leap" (line 536). The 32 tzitzit threads come from the surround of the surround, the tallit, and divide into the 613.

## Tzitzit 22-23 (537-545): Korach, Aharon, Sheol, and severity inside kindness

Aharon's great love, which is above reason, can wake sudden teshuvah even in people sunk in sin (538.1). He walked in the middle line, which joins left into right: > וזהו התכללות גבורות בחסדים לאכללא שמאלא בימינא (line 538)

Great mercy runs from the upper edge to the lower and lights "even the coarsest darkness" (538.3). Korach's sin is named in these very terms. He refused the unity of opposites: > ורצה שיהי' בחי' הגבורות נבדל ונפרד מן החסד ולא שיוכלל הגבורה בחסד (line 540)

Then the depths. The keter of kelipah is "afel" (gloom), the letters of "pele" (wonder) reversed (541.2). Sheol too is held by God, in utmost hiding: > לא יש שם פרוד גמור מאלקות (line 541)

But the next column says the sin itself is worse than Sheol, because it is > ונפרד בעצם מכל וכל מיחודו ית' (line 542)

(See "Disagreements kept" below.) Fear of punishment, rightly understood, is fear of God and not of the pain (542.2). How deep the soul is cast shows how precious the thing it damaged was. The prohibitions are rooted higher than the positive mitzvot, because what He hates shows His inner self (543.1). Knowing the Essence "by negation" returns at 561. The descent into Sheol is kindness, and the ascent matches the fall: > וא"כ עלייתו יותר נעלה כל שירידתו יותר למטה (line 544)

Korach's error was one of timing. Severity above kindness belongs to the future, when "the Levites will be Kohanim". Now severity must be included in kindness, and this is > ונכללים הגבורות בחסדים שזהו לאכללא שמאלא בימינא דוקא שזהו יסוד כל התו"מ (line 545)

Hillel and Shammai are "both the words of the living God" because both are held in the middle line (545.4).

## Tefillin 24-27 (546-560): two seals, the hidden love, something from nothing, and 'as if you made Me'

God's own tefillin say "who is like Your people Israel, one nation". Knesset Yisrael asks to be engraved on His heart (547.1). The divine love hidden in the animal soul rises in prayer like a raised letter, like a mountain (548.1). Line 548 adds that light seen from inside darkness stands out more, like a rainbow in a clear cloud. That is marked *reading* for F03 (see below). The soul's root is never cut: "though you go here and there, your image remains with Me" (548.3).

Siman 25 reads "Shema" as gathering. The sparks scattered among foreign desires are gathered and rise to their root (549.1). "Havayah Elokeinu" is the constant union of Chochmah and Binah, "renewing the work of creation every day" (549.4, F01). Creation must be something from nothing. If it were something from something, the Creator would change: > באין שנוי כלל בין קודם שברא (line 549)

Malchut gives life to the worlds by "letters", which are separate from its essence: > שאינו ממהותו כלל כמו בחי' אותיות שהן נבדלין מן העצם (line 550)

"With all your might" means self-sacrifice to cleave to the Essence itself, above even the source of souls (550.5). Line 551 argues that Israel have a root in the very Essence, because every Jew can give up his life, and self-sacrifice is only ever for the Essence: > ואין זה רק מצד שרשם בעצמות ממש (line 551)

All the bitul of the Shema and its blessings is bitul of a "something", not the essential nothing (553.1). The silent Amidah is that nothing: > והוא בחי' בטול פנימי דעצם אין דחכמה שנק' בטול במציאו' (line 554)

The nearer one is, the less one's bitul shows (554.2). Moshe could only speak by the King's strength: "open my lips" (554.3). The vessels above are made from the physical body of the mitzvah below (554.4). The Essence in itself is none of the attributes (555.2). He contracted Himself into the line only because > שנתאו' להיות לו בחי' דירה בתחתוני' בתו"מ (line 555)

That is why a physical act can wake the Essence to emanate anew: "as if you made Me" (555.4-5). Mitzvot are called "tzedakah" because they give life to the lowly. The Essence clothes itself in the sefirot out of that same mercy, and > וכל שיותר רחוק בערך יותר יומשך ר"ר (line 559)

## Tefillin 28-31 (561-573): known by negation; the stone that bows; miracles clothed in nature; the image on His heart

Love and yearning never grasp the Essence. A king is known by what he hates more than by what he wants, which is "knowledge by negation" (561.2). Only becoming like a silent stone makes one a vessel: > שבזה הוא נעש' כלי לגלוי עצמו' אלקי' ממש (line 561)

So the Amidah's bitul must show in the body, and bowing the physical head is higher than inner bitul: > שזה דוקא יותר מעלה מבטול הפנימי שבמוחו ולבו (line 562)

"Our signs we have not seen" (Psalms 74:9) is explained through F03 and F02. Elokim equals "hateva", nature. A miracle is the hidden world shining in the revealed one, and in its own place that light is nothing new (562.3). Now it comes "through holes in the screen" (563.1). In the future: > שיתגלה למטה כמו שהוא למעלה ממש בלי שינוי המהות (line 563)

Even the Torah's wisdom is, for now, a garment the Essence wears (563.4). Line 564 gives the range's most striking F07 + F10. The world to come, where the Essence above all His inner wills is revealed, has only one vessel: > אין לו כלי רק דוקא גופה של מעשה המצוה שלמטה בפ"מ (line 564)

That is why all Israel have a share, even without intention. God's tefillin hold Israel because Israel's bitul makes the raised seal above: > שלכך נק' גוי אחד בארץ כמו שהוא יחיד (line 565)

The parchment is untouched by whatever is written or erased on it, yet it bears every letter equally (566.1, F04). "His right hand embraces me" means a surround that keeps the heart from separating (567.2). The names of Israel are engraved on His heart, even in exile and in the struggle for a living: > שרש עצם רצונם חקוק ודבוק בלבו למעל' (line 568)

Now every flow depends on arousal from below. In the future it will come from Him alone: > אבל לע"ל יהי' גלוי אלקות למטה כמו למעלה בשוה ולא מצד אתדל"ת כלל (line 569)

"He desires kindness" is His own essential, simple desire (570.1), and "each depends on the other" (570.2). Siman 31 corrects the seal image. It is not a mechanical inversion. It is "spirit brings spirit, just like it" (571.1). Bitul below draws down > נמשך מבחי' העצמות שבאא"ס שאינו מתיישב בכלי ויש כלל (line 572)

## Tefillin 32-35 (574-583): why mitzvot hide now; two loves become one; ascent by inclusion; letters that create every moment

Every mitzvah is now a garment, because the arousal from below that draws it is a "something" (574.1). Bitul draws the Essence as it is, which is "your eyes shall see" (574.3). The prohibitions give a taste of this now, since a prohibition is only the absence of a vessel (574.4). Israel's rising love and the love coming down are the two "or" (light) in "ve-ahavta": > והיו לאחדים ממש (line 575)

"Emet veyatziv" is the chamber of will. It comes from > מצד בחי' התקשרות עצמיו' דנשמות באלקות (line 575)

That bond needs no command. The redemption reaches even one "who has only a nefesh of Asiyah" (575.3). "For His name's sake" equals "ratzon" (will): "and all is one" (line 576).

Simanim 33-34 are kabbalistic and mostly structural. There are two kinds of ascent. In ascent by clothing, the lower dresses the outside of the higher, which is the Shabbat ascent (577-578). In ascent by inclusion, the lower is absorbed as an embryo is in its mother: > כשעולה שם נכלל ומתאחד בו ונתבטל מהותו שם כאילו אינו במציאות בפ"ע כלל (line 580)

Inclusion draws entirely new light that has no garment ("silence is a fence for wisdom", 581.2). That is the second seal: > בחי' גלוי אור חדש מעצמו' המאציל באצי' שלא בא בבחי' כלי ולבוש כלל (line 581)

Siman 35 is F01. A letter reveals the thing as it is but is not the thing (582.2), and > דאותיות יהי אור מהווה את האור מאין ליש בכל רגע (line 582)

The letters are intermediaries (582.4). They are vessels that limit, yet only they join one level to the next, so the coming into being is constant: > להיות התהוותם תמידי' מאין ליש (line 583)

## Tefillin 36-41 (584-603): hairs and writing; the sefirot equal before the Essence; the heart above the mind; black and white; the Amidah as the future

Lines 584-585 build a ladder: engraved letters, spreading letters, speech let out into the air, writing, and hairs. Hairs are cut without pain but hurt when pulled, because each is tied to its root. The rule that follows is this range's addition to F03 and F05: > דכל שהשפע הולך ממקום הגבוה במדרגה ביותר יותר יבא בצמצו' והעלם בדילוג הערך ביותר (line 586)

The letters that create the worlds are the last stage of speech, breath already let out of the supernal mouth (586.3). What comes through hair or writing comes from the innermost Essence: > אדרבה היא הנותנת שבאה השפע והאור ממקום גבוה ועליון מפנימית ועצמות המאציל ביותר (line 587)

The root of Torah is "the ancient parable" (587.3). Line 589 states F05 in a new way. In their first point of being, all ten sefirot come straight from the Essence, not one from another: > ולכך שוין הכל בלי חילוק כלל לגבי העצמו' (line 589)

Simanim 38-39 argue that the middot are not born of the intellect. Intellect only lights the way. Middot born of contemplation can outgrow it and defy it: > שיש למציא' מדו' בעצם תחלת התהוותם למעלה מעלה מן השכל (line 593)

The middot pass through intellect "only as a passage" (595.2). At the sea no reason could tell Israel from Egypt. Only the essential will could decide, the place where Israel rose: > ששם דוקא עלו ישראל להיות בבחי' חסד וטוב בלא טעם כלל (line 596)

In the Essence all of this is one: > הוא ורצונו וחכמתו ומדותיו הכל א' בבחי' עצמיות ממש (line 597)

Siman 40 asks why the straps and housings are black. A surround clothed in the outside comes black ("He made darkness His hiding place"). A surround shining as its own inwardness comes white (599.1). The Torah is "black fire on white fire" (599.2). Line 600 ends by admitting the question is not settled. Siman 41 matches the sitting prayer to the hand tefillin (light hidden in vessels, "it is good to store the light") and the Amidah to the head tefillin: > לפי שבש"ע הוא בבחינת גלוי עצמות אלקות (line 601)

Essential kindness has no vessel, so it does not thicken on the way down. It reaches penitents even in Sheol (602.2). Because the "something" was nullified below, the light comes without a vessel (602.3). The book's last column makes the Amidah a taste of the future: > דבש"ע הוא מעין דלעתיד (line 603)

In the future Malchut rises above Zeir Anpin, and the mitzvah itself becomes the name Havayah: > היינו דמצו' עצמ' יהיה שם הויה (line 603)

Then God's head tefillin come first, "the sun out of its sheath": > שיתגלה שם הוי' דז"א בלי לבו' והסת' דשם אלקי' (line 603)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say, or says differently

- **Love draws garments; bitul draws the Essence.** The Tanya (chs. 41-50) builds love and awe from contemplation as the core of service, and it says holiness rests only on what is nullified (ch. 6, ch. 35). This range ranks the two. Love, the "running", is a "something", so what it draws comes clothed in vessels. Only bitul of existence draws the Essence without a vessel (561, 572, 574, 581, 602). This is the rule of "spirit brings spirit" applied without softening.
- **The physical deed is the Essence's only vessel.** The Tanya (chs. 36-37) makes this world the purpose. Line 564 goes further. The world to come has no vessel except "the body of the physical deed". The reasons for the mitzvot belong to the lesser days of Moshiach. The Tanya's range does not rank the deed above its reasons in this way.
- **A rule for hiddenness.** "The higher the source, the more hidden and leaping it comes" (586-587): hair and writing carry more of the Essence than ordered letters do. The Tanya's F03 says hiding serves revelation. This gives a gradient for it.
- **Holiness cannot apply to the one who is alone** (526). The Tanya does not use "holy" this way. The line is a clean textual anchor for the SPINE's distinction between "yachid" and "echad".
- **The sefirot are equal before the Essence** because each comes from it directly, not one from another (589). The Tanya's range teaches the chain; this line puts the chain second.
- **Knowledge by negation, and the prohibitions above the positive mitzvot** (543, 561, 574). The Tanya range used as the model for this report does not state this.
- **The future is described concretely.** Malchut rises above Zeir Anpin ("a woman of valour is her husband's crown"), the mitzvah itself becomes Havayah, God's tefillin go head first, and Elokim no longer covers Havayah (563, 569, 603). The Tanya's F07 names the end. This range describes the reversal.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- One line is *none*: the Gate of Tefillin heading (546). - Nine lines carry no face. Four are openings or objections (532, 573, 579, 584). Five are models not yet applied: Korach's tallit and the oil on Aharon's beard (537), the Shabbat ascent of worlds (577-578), and the ladder of speech, writing and hair (585). Their ties arrive later (580-581, 586-587) and are entered there. - The long medical analogy of nerves and muscles (533), the hairs of kelipah and the shaving of the Levites (541.1), the halachic detail on straps and parchment (565.3, 590), and the order of the prayers (549.3, 553.2) are marked structural. - Simanim 38-39 (591-595) argue about psychology: whether middot come from intellect. The unity comes into it only at 596-597, and those lines are stated. The lines before them are marked structural with F12, since the tie there is a step on the way.
- *Reading* is used once. At 548.2 the rainbow and "He lights my darkness" are the text's own points about how the soul's light shows. Reading them as the hiding that serves revelation (F03, G03) is ours. - The book ends unresolved twice. Line 600 admits the black-and-white account "is not yet settled", and line 603 breaks off mid-argument. The entries record this and do not supply an answer.

## Disagreements kept

- **Is anything outside the unity?** Line 541 says Sheol is never wholly separate from Godliness: "You are there", and Jonah saw light from its gates. Line 542, the next column, says the sin itself is "separated in essence entirely from His unity" and is worse than Sheol. The text holds both and does not reconcile them. The Tanya (ch. 24) also calls sin a separation worse than the kelipot, so the two books agree here. The tension with "no place is empty of Him" (524, 534) is left as the text leaves it.
- **Mind and heart.** The Tanya (ch. 3) makes Chochmah, Binah and Da'at the "mother and source" of the middot. This range argues the reverse at length: the middot's being comes from the simple will above intellect, and intellect is only "a passage" and a lamp (593-595). The same sits in tension with the Tanya's F12 (ch. 42, da'at as binding) and with this book's own earlier gates, where detailed contemplation is the way to the unity. Here love and yearning "do not grasp" the Essence (561), and bowing the body is higher than the bitul of mind and heart (562).
- **Prayer and Torah.** Line 603 grants that Torah is "eternal life" and prayer "temporary life", so a person whose Torah is his craft is exempt from prayer. Then it says prayer's root is higher, and in the future it will rise above Torah. The Tanya's range puts Torah study as the highest union (ch. 5, ch. 23). Both are kept.
- **Severity and kindness.** Now severity must be included in kindness, "the foundation of all Torah and mitzvot" (545). In the future severity will stand above kindness and "the Levites will be Kohanim". Korach was wrong about the time, not about the thing. F09 here is not symmetrical, and its order reverses with time.
- **Direct and returning light.** Line 529 puts "heaven and earth created as one" above both orders, where there is no above or below. Line 530 then says returning light is the higher of the two, because it reaches the Essence. The book keeps both in the same passage.
