## 1. The system in one paragraph

Chassidus is not only the idea that there is nothing but G-d. It is also a way of making that idea into the person, and the sources call that way the whole point: «כל ענין החסידות הוא לשנות טבע מדותיו» (*The whole point of Chassidus is to change the nature of one's traits.* The Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus §1, line 7). The way is a **daily loop**. The night before, you prepare. In the morning you learn one thing **in detail**, **bind** your mind to it until it is real, and take it into **prayer**, which is the vessel that carries it from the head into the heart and the body. Inside prayer you make a **resolution about one trait**, not about being good in general. You **carry one thought** through the day, keep a few **anchors**, and when a pull comes you **hold back** a little (itkafia). At midday you check whether the morning's imprint is still there; at night you **account** for the day honestly and without sadness; and you **begin again**. The loop turns on bigger wheels (the week and Shabbos, the month, the year, a life), it works on the person from the outside in (thought, speech and deed; then the traits; then the nature of the traits; then the essence), it takes **years**, it is done **with others** (a friend, a guide, the farbrengen, the Rebbe, love of other people), and it is tested by one plain question: «הן בעסער ווערין אין יראת שמים, און הן אין מדות טובות, טוט ער זיך ניט אַריר» (*Whether in growing better in fear of Heaven or in good traits, he does not stir.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5504)

## 2. The engine: the core loop of internalization

*The steps and their order are ours; each step's content is the sources'. Times in minutes are ours unless a source is named.*

## Step 1 · Prepare the night before

- **What you do.** Before sleep, pick tomorrow's one subject to learn and one trait to work on. Set a waking time and mean it. Go to sleep as someone who serves, not only as someone who is tired.
- **When.** Bedtime, right after the accounting (step 11). **How long.** Two to five minutes (ours).
- **Why.** The Rayatz begins his model day at night: «אַז מען שלאָפט ווי אַ איד, שטייט מען אויף ווי אַ איד» (*If you sleep like a Jew, you get up like a Jew.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5685, Simchas Torah, line 569) The Rebbe: «שהכנה זו (קודם השינה) פועלת על השינה, שגם השינה היא שינה של עבד» (*This preparation (before sleep) works on the sleep, so that even the sleep is a servant's sleep.* The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5712, line 7327) And the old Chassid R. Gershon Dov after a real bedtime Shema: «מען באַדאַרף אויפשטיין גאָר אַן אַנדערער» (*One must get up an entirely different person.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5706, Sukkos (R. Gershon Dov), line 6123)

## Step 2 · Wake and hand the day back

- **What you do.** The first words are thanks. Then one sentence: this soul is Yours; I give it back to You for today.
- **When.** The first moment awake. **How long.** Under a minute.
- **Why.** The Tanya turns the morning blessing into a decision: «לכן, מעתה אני מוסרה ומחזירה לך לייחדה באחדותך» (*So from now I hand it over and return it to You, to unite it with Your oneness.* Tanya ch. 41, line 621) Hayom Yom: nothing spoils it, «דער מודה אני בלייבט גאנץ» (*The Modeh Ani stays whole.* Hayom Yom 11 Shevat, line 84) The BeSHT's school: «יקום בזריזות משינתו כי נתחדש ונעשה אדם אחר» (*Let him rise from sleep briskly, for he has been renewed and become another person.* Tzava'at HaRivash, line 38)

## Step 3 · Learn one thing in detail

- **What you do.** Learn a short passage about G-d's oneness (for Chabad, a maamar of Chassidus; ONE-PROCESS gives the rungs). Go into its details and examples, not only its headline. Read it twice.
- **When.** Before prayer. **How long.** The Rashab: half an hour or more; for the busy, at least a little.
- **Why.** «הנה הזמן המסוגל ללימוד דא"ח הוא בבקר קודם התפלה» (*The propitious time for learning Chassidus is the morning before prayer.* Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim ch. 25 (OCR), line 348) «טוב לחשוב קודם התפלה איזה ענין בדא"ח כחצי שעה או יותר» (*It is good before prayer to think over a subject in Chassidus for half an hour or more.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §11 (OCR), line 88) A general idea, even a well-known one, stays *around* you and fades; the Rashab calls the flash it gives «אבל הוא דמיון כוזב בלבד ואין לו קיום כלל» (*It is only a false imagining and has no staying power at all (of general, not detailed, contemplation).* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §2 (OCR), line 49)

## Step 4 · Bind the mind (da'at)

- **What you do.** Take one point from the learning. Stay on it. When the mind wanders, come back, without fighting the stray thought. Say the point in your own words. Come back to it several times.
- **When.** Right after learning, and at the start of prayer. **How long.** Start with five to ten minutes (ours). For the long road the Rashab asks «שצריכים לזה אריכות זמן כשעה ויותר» (*This needs a length of time, an hour or more.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §4 (OCR), line 57) and trains it outside prayer too: «שיעשה לו עת וזמן להתבודד ולהעטיק» (*That he set himself a time to be alone and go deep.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §6 (OCR), line 67)
- **Why.** Knowing is not yet knowing: «אך עיקר הדעת, אינה הידיעה לבדה» (*The main thing in da'at is not knowing alone.* Tanya ch. 42, line 629) The Tanya's binding is «ויתקע מחשבתו בחוזק בגדולת אין סוף ברוך הוא, ואינו מסיח דעתו» (*He fixes his thought firmly on the greatness of the Infinite and does not take his mind off it.* Tanya ch. 3, line 112) It is always possible, because «כי מוחו ברשותו ויכול להתבונן בו ככל אשר יחפוץ» (*His mind is in his own hands; he can contemplate with it whatever he wants.* Tanya ch. 17, line 265) The Rayatz on why understanding stays barren: «שיאריך בההתבוננות ויתבונן כמה פעמים באריכות, ואז יוקלט הענין אצלו בכי טוב» (*Let him dwell at length and contemplate it several times at length; then it will be well absorbed.* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 435) This is ONE-PROCESS step 6 ("It is so"), and the manual's Stage 6.

## Step 5 · Prayer as the vessel (the four parts)

- **What you do.** The Rayatz's four parts: «א) דער לימוד החסידות פאַר'ן דאַוונען, ב) הכנה צו דעם דאַוונען, ג) די התבוננות בתוך התפלה, ד) דער פירוש המלות» (*(a) learning Chassidus before davening, (b) preparing for davening, (c) contemplation within the prayer, (d) the meaning of the words.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5708, Pesach, line 7956) And above all: «אַז מען דאַרף טאַקע זאָגן די ווערטער מיט אַ חיות» (*One must actually say the words with life.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5708, Pesach, line 7956)
- **When.** Morning prayer. For someone not Jewish: morning words to G-d, Psalms or one's own (section 9). **How long.** The Alter Rebbe asks those who can «להאריך בתפלת השחר ערך שעה ומחצה לפחות כל ימות החול» (*To lengthen the morning prayer about an hour and a half at least, every weekday.* Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 1, line 1027) and everyone on Shabbos. The Rebbe's graded floor, for anyone: «ולכל הפחות התבוננות קודם התפלה, או לכל הפחות תפלה מלה במלה (מבלי לחטוף)» (*At least contemplation before prayer, or at least praying word by word, without grabbing.* The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5711, line 6891) The Rashab's floor for beginners: «יתפללו בקביעות מקום מתוך הסידור במתינות גדולה ולכוין פי' המלות» (*Let them pray in a fixed place, from the siddur, very slowly, with the meaning of the words.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §14 (OCR), line 108) The Rayatz's minimum before starting: «מי הוא המתפלל ולפני מי הוא מתפלל» (*Who is praying, and before Whom is he praying.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 4789)
- **Why.** Before prayer the truth is in the head only: «כי קודם תפלה הארה זו הוא רק במוחו לבד» (*Before prayer this light is only in his mind.* Tzemach Tzedek, Maamarim 5615, line 287) «אבל ע"י התפלה מתפשטת בגוף ג"כ שמרגיש ג"כ בגוף» (*But through prayer it spreads into the body too, so he feels it in the body too.* Tzemach Tzedek, Maamarim 5615, line 287) Hayom Yom: «עבודת התפלה היא המביאה השגת המוח בהרגשת הלב, ושניהם יחד בעבודה בפועל» (*Prayer brings the mind's grasp into the heart's feeling, and both together into practice.* Hayom Yom 21 Cheshvan, line 568)

## Step 6 · The one-trait resolution

- **What you do.** Inside prayer, name one trait that is doing damage (anger, a craving, envy). Wake up its holy opposite. Resolve about the real situation you will meet today ("when he interrupts me at the meeting I will not answer sharply"), not "I will be good".
- **When.** Inside prayer, after the contemplation (placement ours). **How long.** A minute, repeated day after day on the same trait.
- **Why.** This is the Rashab's missing gear. «צריך לעורר בתפלה ביותר המדה דקדושה שלעומת המדה רעה» (*He must arouse in prayer especially the holy trait opposite the bad one.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §8 (OCR), line 77) «והסכם הכללי אינו מועיל על הפרט» (*A general resolution does not work on the particular.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §13 (OCR), line 104) «צריכים לייגע כמח פעמים בכמה תפלות על מדה א' פרטית» (*One must toil many times, in many prayers, on one particular trait.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §13 (OCR), line 104) In a letter: «ומעט מעט דוקא אגרשנו» (*Little by little, specifically, I will drive it out.* Rashab, Igros Kodesh vol. 1 (OCR), line 1240)

## Step 7 · Carry one thought

- **What you do.** Take one line from the morning into the day. Know a few holy lines by heart. Go back to the line in every gap: walking, waiting, on hold.
- **When.** All day. **How long.** Seconds, many times.
- **Why.** The BeSHT's school: «אפי' כשהולך לעסקיו יקיים שויתי ה' לנגדו תמיד» (*Even when he goes about his business he should keep 'I have set G-d before me always'.* Keter Shem Tov (the BeSHT's school), line 257) The Piaseczner's unit of integration is one teaching a day: «וְהַדָּבָר שֶׁקָּרָאתָ תַּעֲבֹד בְּכָל הַיּוֹם, אוֹ שְׁנַיִם וּשְׁלֹשָׁה יָמִים» (*What you read, work with it all day, or two or three days.* Piaseczno, Bnei Machshava Tova, Daily Instructions, line 273) Hayom Yom: «מוז האבען עפעס אויסגעלערנט בעל פה» (*One must have learned something by heart.* Hayom Yom 11 Teves, line 40) Stray thoughts come from «די פוסטקייט פון קאפ» (*The emptiness of the head.* Hayom Yom 16 Cheshvan, line 562) And every extra moment spent in the morning «הרי זה כקונה חיים לנפשו על כל היום» (*It is like buying life for his soul for the whole day.* Rashab (quoting the Mitteler Rebbe), Kuntres HaTefillah §16 (OCR), line 115)

## Step 8 · Anchors in the day

- **What you do.** A few fixed hooks. A coin to someone in need before praying. A book in your pocket. Renew the morning intention each hour. Stop for the afternoon prayer in the middle of work. Under pressure, one sentence to G-d in your own words.
- **When.** Set points. **How long.** Seconds to minutes each.
- **Why.** «וע"י שנותנים צדקה לעני קודם התפלה ומחיים אותו, ניתוסף חיות הרבה בתפלה» (*By giving tzedakah to a poor man before prayer and giving him life, much life is added to the prayer.* Hayom Yom 2 Kislev (the Rashab), line 582) «לכל בעל עסק צריך להיות, בעמדו בחנות או בשוק, ספר בכיס» (*Every business person, standing in the shop or market, should have a book in his pocket.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 736) «בכל שעה ושעה על כל פנים» (*At least every single hour (renew the intention).* Tanya ch. 41, line 623) Karlin: «יזהר להתפלל מנחה ומעריב בכוונה גדולה כי זה צירוף כל היום» (*Pray Minchah and Maariv with great intention, for this is the refining of the whole day.* Karlin, Beit Aharon (R. Asher I), line 41) Piaseczno, for the busy man: «חוֹב עָלָיו שֶׁיֹּאמַר בְּאֶמְצַע טִרְדָּתוֹ זֹאת אֵיזֶה פְּעָמִים בַּיּוֹם» (*He must say, in the middle of his pressure, a few times a day (a sentence to G-d).* Piaseczno, Bnei Machshava Tova, Amendments, line 328)

## Step 9 · The moment of itkafia (with the Tanya's "delay")

- **What you do.** When a pull rises (to eat now, to say the cutting word, to keep a thought going), do a small thing against it. Delay a meal. Hold back one word. Push a thought away the moment it comes. Say inside: I do not want to be separated from G-d.
- **When.** The moment it rises. **How long.** Seconds; the delay up to an hour.
- **Why.** The Tanya gives the three exact acts: «כגון שחפץ לאכול, ומאחר סעודתו עד לאחר שעה או פחות, ועוסק בתורה באותה שעה» (*He wants to eat, and delays his meal an hour or less, and studies Torah in that time.* Tanya ch. 27, line 390) «וכן אם בולם פיו מלדבר דברים שלבו מתאוה מאד לדברם» (*Or if he holds his mouth from saying things his heart very much wants to say.* Tanya ch. 27, line 391) and for thoughts, «מיד בעלייתו לשם, דוחהו בשתי ידים» (*The moment it rises there, he pushes it away with both hands.* Tanya ch. 12, line 215) The sentence: «אינני רוצה להיות מובדל ונפרד חס ושלום מה׳ אחד בשום אופן» (*I do not want to be separated from the One G-d in any way.* Tanya ch. 14, line 232) Why small is enough: «אדם מקדש עצמו מעט למטה – מקדשין אותו הרבה מלמעלה» (*A person sanctifies himself a little below; he is sanctified much from above.* Tanya ch. 27, line 392) The Rebbe gives the reason it opens the door: «כאשר המדות הם בחלישות אזי שייך שתהי' בהם פעולת השכל» (*When the traits are weakened, then the mind can act in them.* The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5715, line 22897) The early Chassidim's pact: «שכל דבר המותר, אך שיש לו איזה רצון וחמדת הלב לזה לא יעשנו» (*Any permitted thing he feels a craving for, he does not do.* Hayom Yom 27 Shevat, line 107) It is hard at first only: «והכבדות בזה היא רק בעת הראשונה» (*The difficulty is only at first.* Rashab, Kuntres HaAvodah ch. 2 (OCR), line 70)

## Step 10 · The midday gauge *(name ours)*

- **What you do.** At the afternoon prayer, or midday, ask two questions. Is the morning's imprint still with me? When today's test came, did the trait move?
- **When.** Afternoon. **How long.** One minute.
- **Why.** The sources name the thing being measured, not the check. R. Yitzchak Eizik of Homel: the animal soul may rule the body «עד שיבטל בחי' הרשימו שראוי להיות נשאר מן ההתפעלות בתפלה על כל היום» (*Until it cancels the imprint that should remain from prayer's arousal all day.* R. Yitzchak Eizik of Homel, Chanah Ariel, line 373) Karlin: the first Chassidim stayed an hour after prayer «שישאר החיות והיראה של התפילה על כל היום» (*So that the life and awe of the prayer remain all day.* Karlin, Beit Aharon (R. Aharon II), line 95) The Rayatz's test of davening: «הן בעסער ווערין אין יראת שמים, און הן אין מדות טובות, טוט ער זיך ניט אַריר» (*Whether in growing better in fear of Heaven or in good traits, he does not stir.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5504)

## Step 11 · Bedtime accounting, without sadness

- **What you do.** Go over the day's thoughts, words and deeds. Be honest, and don't defend yourself. Be sorry briefly about one detail, never about everything. Then put the sadness down entirely, and plan tomorrow.
- **When.** At bedtime, with the bedtime Shema. If not nightly, at least once a week before Shabbos. **How long.** A few minutes nightly (ours); the Alter Rebbe's full form is «שעה אחת בכל יום או לילה לפני תיקון חצות» (*One hour every day or night, before Tikkun Chatzos.* Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah ch. 7, line 966)
- **Why.** «לעשות חשבון עם נפשו, מכל המחשבות והדיבורים והמעשים» (*To make an accounting with his soul, of all his thoughts, words and deeds.* Tanya ch. 29, line 417) The Rayatz's condition: «באמיתית אָן אהבת עצמו» (*Truthfully, without self-love.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5685 (the model day), line 576) What it does: «צוברעכט דעם קיר המפסיק בין המוח אל הלב» (*(The first davening after an honest accounting) breaks the wall between mind and heart.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5506) The limits: the Rashab, «והנה עגין המרירות והשפלות הנ"ל צ"ל רק לעתים» (*The bitterness and lowliness should be only at times.* Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah §11 (OCR), line 89) and the natural moment is «היא בשעה שהוא עצב בלאו הכי ממילי דעלמא» (*The right time is when he is sad anyway over worldly things.* Tanya ch. 31, line 454) The Tanya: «אזי יסיר העצב מלבו לגמרי» (*Then let him remove the sadness from his heart entirely.* Tanya ch. 26, line 379) Breslov, the same rule: «רק בשעה מיחדת יהיה לו לב נשבר» (*Only in a set hour should he have a broken heart.* Breslov, Likutei Moharan II 24, line 6343)

## Step 12 · Renewal

- **What you do.** Forgive yourself completely. Decide that tomorrow will be a little finer. Begin again in the morning, even for the thousandth time.
- **When.** The end of the accounting, and every waking. **How long.** A breath.
- **Why.** «אין הפרש בין פעם אחת לאלף פעמים» (*There is no difference between once and a thousand times.* Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah ch. 11, line 1003) «הואיל ובכח נפשם האלהית לחזור ולעורר בחינת אהבה זו לעולם» (*Since it is in the power of their Divine soul to re-arouse this love always.* Tanya ch. 13, line 229) «דער מארגען זאל זיין א סך שענער ווי דער היינט» (*Tomorrow should be much finer than today.* Hayom Yom 1 Iyar (the Rashab), line 252) «שצריכין ממש בכל יום להתחיל מחדש» (*One must truly begin anew every day.* Breslov, Likutei Moharan 261, line 5215)

## 2b. The wall between mind, heart and deed, and what crosses it

The Rayatz's plainest picture of the problem. For worldly wants there is no wall: you understand, you want, you do. In the service of G-d there is: «קיר איז אַ וואַנט וואָס איז מפסיק בין מוחין למדות» (*Kir is a wall that separates mind from heart.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, 12 Tammuz, line 5501) «אין עבודה איז פאַראַן אַ הפסק בין מוחין למדות ובין המדות אל הפועל, וואָס עס איז פאַראַן אין דעם אַ שהיות זמן» (*In avodah there is a gap between mind and heart, and between heart and deed, and it takes time.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, 12 Tammuz, line 5503) His portrait of the person stuck behind it: he davens, learns, does favours, «אָבער ער רירט זיך ניט פון אָרט» (*But he does not budge from his place.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5503)

**What crosses from mind to heart** (theirs, each named by a source): - **Detail and lingering**: step 3-4. «שיאריך בההתבוננות ויתבונן כמה פעמים באריכות, ואז יוקלט הענין אצלו בכי טוב» (*Let him dwell at length and contemplate it several times at length; then it will be well absorbed.* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 435) - **An honest accounting, then prayer**: step 11 into step 5. «צוברעכט דעם קיר המפסיק בין המוח אל הלב» (*(The first davening after an honest accounting) breaks the wall between mind and heart.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5506) - **Prayer itself**: «אבל ע"י התפלה מתפשטת בגוף ג"כ שמרגיש ג"כ בגוף» (*But through prayer it spreads into the body too, so he feels it in the body too.* Tzemach Tzedek, Maamarim 5615, line 287) - **Weakening the trait first**: step 9. «כאשר המדות הם בחלישות אזי שייך שתהי' בהם פעולת השכל» (*When the traits are weakened, then the mind can act in them.* The Rebbe, Toras Menachem 5715, line 22897) - **You are commanded the thinking, not the feeling**: «דורך התבוננות קען מען צוקומען צו אהבה, און דער ציווי איז אויף דער התבוננות» (*Through contemplation one can come to love; the command is on the contemplation.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691 (the Maggid), line 5581)

**What crosses from heart to deed:** - **Hidden love is enough.** You do not wait for the fire: «וזה כלל גדול בעבודת ה׳ לבינונים» (*This is a great rule in the service of G-d for the in-between people.* Tanya ch. 16 (hidden love is enough to act), line 252) The goal: «ופיו ולבו שוין» (*His mouth and his heart are the same.* Tanya ch. 16, line 257) - **A specific resolution**: step 6. - **Binding the heart all day by itkafia**: «שגם לבו יהי' "קשור" ומשועבד (עכ"פ בדרך אתכפיא) במשך כל היום» (*That his heart too be 'bound' and subject (at least by itkafia) all day long.* The Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos vol. 39, line 35478) - **Habit**: «ועוד, שההרגל על כל דבר – שלטון, ונעשה טבע שני» (*Habit rules over everything and becomes second nature.* Tanya ch. 14, line 239) - **Stubbornness of holiness**, when thinking has lost to habit: «שיתגבר כארי לעשות רק ההיפך מיצרו הרע בלי שום טענה ומענה» (*Let him overcome like a lion, doing only the opposite of his yetzer, without argument.* Rashab, Kuntres Umaayan 28, line 585) «וכאשר ככה יתנהג בפועל משך זמן» (*And when he behaves this way in practice for a period of time.* Rashab, Kuntres Umaayan 28, line 586)

**The test runs backward.** «שהלב אינו מתפעל שזוהי הוראה שהשגתו אינה השגה» (*That the heart is not moved shows his grasp is not grasp.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5703 (OCR), line 4008) And the mind is shown in the deed: «והיינו דהשכל שבמוח מאיר בלב במדות טובות בפועל ממש» (*The mind's understanding lights up the heart as good traits, in actual deed.* Hayom Yom 4 Shevat, line 74)

*The two walls are the Rayatz's; the list of crossings is ours, gathered from the sources.*

## 4. The layers of the person, in the order they are reached

*The four layers are named by the sources; putting them in one column, outside to inside, is ours.*

**1. The garments: thought, speech, deed.** This is where the work begins, and it is always in your hands. «התחלת העבודה איז משעבד זיין די לבושי הנפש מחשבה, דיבור ומעשה» (*The beginning of avodah is to enslave the soul's garments: thought, speech and deed.* Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5641, line 14786) «וכבסו שמלותם - לבושי מחשבה דבור ומעשה, וואס דאס דארף מען אליין» (*'Wash their garments': thought, speech and deed, which one must do oneself.* Hayom Yom 5 Sivan, line 308) «דבר הנעשה רק על ידי עבודה פנימית יוס יומית בכל שלושת לבושי הנפש» (*A thing done only by inner, day-by-day work in all three garments of the soul.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 574) *Acts on it:* step 9 (itkafia), guarding the senses («עוצם עיניו מלראות ברע ואוטם אזנו משמוע דבר רע» (*He shuts his eyes from seeing evil and stops his ears from hearing evil.* Rashab, Kuntres HaAvodah ch. 2 (OCR), line 66)), the carried line (step 7), pushing stray thoughts away.

**2. The middos (traits).** What changes is which trait wins in the real moment. «ראשית העבודה הוא תיקון המדות לבררם» (*The beginning of the work is repairing the traits, sifting them.* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 684) Two kinds: «וכמו מדת הגאוה הכעס שקר והדומה, שאין שייך בזה ענין התיקון כלל» (*Like pride, anger, lying and the like, which cannot be repaired at all (only broken).* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 683) The good ones are washed and put under the mind: «וכאשר השכל מנהיג את המדות אז ישפיע רק להראוי» (*When the mind leads the traits, he gives only to the fitting.* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 687) *Acts on it:* the one-trait resolution (step 6), the friend and the guide (section 6), the accounting (step 11).

**3. The nature of the middos.** Not only what you do, but what you *want*. Itkafia (holding back) comes first; ishapcha (turning) later, built on it: «ומי שמרח בע"ש בעבודה שע"פ טו"ד בבחי" אתכפי' יאכל בשבת» (*Whoever toiled on Friday, by reason, in itkafia, will eat on Shabbos.* Rashab, Kuntres HaAvodah ch. 5 (OCR), line 197) Habit becomes nature: «ועוד, שההרגל על כל דבר – שלטון, ונעשה טבע שני» (*Habit rules over everything and becomes second nature.* Tanya ch. 14, line 239) The Rashab told the Gerer Rebbe: «וברוב רגילותי הוטבע ונעשה טבע, עד אשר הנני מאמין להגוף» (*By much habit it became nature, until I trust my body.* Rayatz quoting the Rashab, Sefer HaSichos 5685, line 709) Against temperament: «הגם ער איז בטבע אַ קר הנה המצות צריך לקיים בחמימות» (*Though by nature he is cold, he must do the mitzvos warmly.* Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, line 373) One rule for life: R. Shmuel Munkes resolved before his first yechidus «אַז אַ ענין גשמי וואָס עס ווילט זיך זאָל ער ניט טאָן» (*A bodily thing that he wants, he should not do.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691 (R. Shmuel Munkes), line 5549) *Acts on it:* the whole loop, kept for years.

**4. The essence.** «שעל ידי התשובה אמיתית מתהפך מהותו» (*Through true teshuvah his essence is turned.* Rashab, Kuntres Umaayan 14, line 292) «קבלת עול איז משנה דעם מהות» (*Kabbalas ol changes one's essence.* Hayom Yom 3 Adar II (the Tzemach Tzedek), line 159) But the Tanya keeps a floor under the hope: in the beinoni the animal soul's essence may not change at all, «ואדרבה – נתחזק יותר בהמשך הזמן» (*On the contrary, it grew stronger over time (the animal soul's essence).* Tanya ch. 13, line 222) So the sources promise the first three layers to anyone who works, and treat the fourth as given, not scheduled. In ONE-PROCESS this layer is steps 10-11, the heart's simple will.

## 5. Growth over time

**What changes first.** The garments, then which trait wins, much later the wanting. «אבל אחר התפלה… הרי הרע חוזר וניעור בחלל השמאלי» (*But after prayer the evil wakes again in the left chamber.* Tanya ch. 12, line 208) is the normal state of the beinoni, and he can still re-arouse love every day (step 12).

**Start small.** «ובתחלה הוא במעט ואח"כ מופיף» (*At first it is a little, and then he adds and goes on.* Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim ch. 18 (OCR), line 323) «דתחלת העבודה צריך להיות מהמוסר שבחסידות» (*The work must begin from the mussar within Chassidus.* Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (OCR), line 138) «לפני חסידות צריך להיות תיקון המדות» (*Before Chassidus there must be repair of the traits.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 2261)

**Truth comes slowly and in order.** The Rayatz's well: first stones, then pebbles, then soft earth. «כל כיסוי וכיסוי דורש עבודה מיוחדת להסרתו וגם סדר מסודר» (*Each covering needs its own work to remove it, and an ordered order.* Rayatz, Igros Kodesh vol. 14, line 3461) «נאר עס פליסט טיפין טיפין» (*It flows drop by drop.* Rayatz, Igros Kodesh vol. 14, line 3463)

**When thinking has lost.** For a habit that reasoning no longer touches, the Rashab says stop negotiating and do the opposite "like a lion" for a period (section 2b).

**Relapse.** Expected, and not the end. «עדיין עליו לבקר היטב ולהשגיח על עצמו שלא יהיה חוזר וניעור» (*He must still check well and watch himself that it does not come back.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 560) Where it starts: «ראשית הירידה, ר"ל, הוא העדר העבודה בתפלה» (*The beginning of the descent is the lack of avodah in prayer.* Hayom Yom 23 Iyar, line 292) The BeSHT: «והירידה הוא צורך עלי'» (*The descent is for the sake of ascent.* Tzava'at HaRivash, line 119) Breslov: «ואף אם לפעמים הוא רואה שמקים הנהגה זו ימים ושנים» (*Even if he sees he has kept this practice for days and years (and is not yet close).* Breslov, Sichot HaRan 68, line 406) and speaking to G-d works «כמו מים היורדים על אבן» (*Like water falling on stone.* Breslov, Likkutei Etzot, Hitbodedut, line 343) After a fall, the danger is self-punishment: «צריכיסם להתחזק בכל מיני חיזוק» (*They must strengthen themselves with every kind of strengthening.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 5450)

**The tests of real change** (theirs): 1. Have you moved from your place? «הן בעסער ווערין אין יראת שמים, און הן אין מדות טובות, טוט ער זיך ניט אַריר» (*Whether in growing better in fear of Heaven or in good traits, he does not stir.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 5504) 2. Does it show in traits, in deed? «והיינו דהשכל שבמוח מאיר בלב במדות טובות בפועל ממש» (*The mind's understanding lights up the heart as good traits, in actual deed.* Hayom Yom 4 Shevat, line 74) 3. Does arousal leave a result? «כל התפעלות כשהיא תולדה משכל, מביאה לייבכןיי» (*Every arousal born of the mind brings a 'therefore'.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 2259) 4. Past your habit: «הפעם המאה ואחת, היתרה על הרגילות» (*The hundred-and-first time, beyond the habit.* Tanya ch. 15, line 249) 5. Praise and insult land the same: «הכל שוה אצלו בין בענין שמשבחין ב"א אותו או מבזין אותו» (*It is all the same to him whether people praise him or insult him.* Tzava'at HaRivash, line 3) 7. Wednesday, not only Simchas Torah (section 3).

## 6. The companions

The sources are clear that no one does this alone. The Rayatz's R. Aizik: had King Solomon gone to a Rebbe, «וואָלט ער האָבן חסידישע חברים, און זיין אויף חסידישן פאַרברענגען» (*He would have had Chassidic friends and been at a Chassidic farbrengen.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691 (R. Aizik), line 5559) And the warning for those who guide themselves: «יפה ומבריק מבחוצ ורקוב מבפנים» (*Beautiful and shiny outside, rotten inside (self-made education).* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 3769)

**The chaver (friend).** How it works: «אז הן שתי נפשות אלקיות כנגד נפש בהמית אחת» (*They are two G-dly souls against one animal soul.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (the Mitteler Rebbe's chaver), line 3814, OCR) Why: «כי אין אדם רואה נגעי עצמו מצד אהבת עצמו» (*A person does not see his own blemishes, because of self-love.* Rashab, Kuntres Umaayan 14, line 289) Karlin's practice: «יזהר שיהי' לו חבר טוב ונאמן רוח ומכסה דברים שידבר עמו הגם חצי שעה בכל יום» (*Have a good, faithful friend who keeps secrets, to talk with even half an hour a day.* Karlin, Beit Aharon (R. Asher I), line 40) Lizhensk: «לספר בכל פעם לפני המורה לו דרך השם, ואפילו לפני חבר נאמן» (*To tell it each time to the one who guides him, or even to a faithful friend.* Lizhensk, Tzetl Katan 13, line 35) The story: R. Pesach hugged the great thinker R. Zalman at a farbrengen and wept: «אַך ווען דיין האַרץ וואָלט געווען אַ כלי אמיתית צו דיין קאָפּ» (*If only your heart were a true vessel for your head.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691 (R. Pesach to R. Zalman), line 5556)

**The mashpia (guide).** What people brought him: «אצלו שאלו עצה איך להיפטר ממדה רעה ואיך להתרגל במדה טובה» (*They asked him how to be rid of a bad trait and grow used to a good one.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 4700) He chooses your method with you: «אז יתייעץ עם המשפיע באיזה מהן לבחור» (*Then let him consult the mashpia which to choose.* The Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos vol. 34, line 17033) He must work on himself first: «אלא עליו לעבוד עס עצמו במדה שישפיע גס על הזולת» (*He must work with himself to the degree that it affects the other.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 4727) He speaks «כשמדבר בנחת דווקא» (*Specifically when he speaks calmly.* R. Hillel of Paritch, Pelach HaRimon, line 82) His real job is not to give a taste of ideas but «אַז די תלמידים זאָלן דערהערן דעם ניט געשמאַק פון זיך» (*That the students sense their own untastiness.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5707, line 7031) Through him one becomes «און דאַן ווערט מען אַ מקבל פנימי» (*And then one becomes an inward receiver.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5708, line 8329) The check on zeal comes from a friend: R. Aizik to R. Hillel, «כי מעקרי העבודה הוא ללכת בדרך המיצוע» (*One of the main things in avodah is to walk the middle path.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos (R. Aizik to R. Hillel), line 10163)

**The farbrengen (the table of friends).** «באַ חסידים איז פאַרברענגען איינע פון די יסודי דרכי החסידות» (*Among Chassidim, farbrengen is one of the foundations of darkei haChassidus.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, 19 Kislev, line 4976) «אַ חסידישער פאַרבריינגען איז אַזוי ווי אַ קעסיל» (*A Chassidic farbrengen is like a kettle.* Rayatz quoting the Rashab, Sefer HaSichos, line 7558) «אַ חסידישער פאַרברענגען וואַשט אויס די וועלטיקע בלאָטע» (*A Chassidic farbrengen washes out the worldly mud.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5689, line 3868) How: «המשפיע היה שבור בינו לבין עצמו, המאזין היה שבור משברונו של המשפיע» (*The mashpia was broken within himself; the listener was broken by his brokenness.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 5794) «אבל עס העוקצ כלפי עצמו» (*But with the sting pointed at oneself.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 5796) «אשר איש את רעהו הוכיחו באהבה ובחיבת גדולה» (*Each rebuked the other with love and great affection.* Hayom Yom 24 Tishrei, line 531) What to talk about: «עיקר הדיבור צ"ל בענין תיקון המדות ואהבת רעים» (*The main talk should be on repairing the traits and love of friends.* Rayatz, Igros Kodesh vol. 16, line 5904) When someone is low, Karlin: «ופעם כשנזדמן שאינו טוב לו. ע"ז טוב הקיבוץ שיתדברו ביניהם» (*When things are not good for him, the gathering helps: let them talk together.* Karlin, Beit Aharon (R. Aharon II), line 131) Why a little drink is there at all: «אַז עס ווערט ווייכער דער עור הבהמה פון נפש הטבעי» (*So that the natural soul's animal hide softens.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5691, line 4977) (the Rayatz; the manual's guard stands: never drink as a practice).

**The Rebbe and hiskashrus.** «אַז דער עיקר מהות פון חסיד איז התקשרות» (*The essential being of a Chassid is hiskashrus.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5708, line 8213) In practice it is done by learning: «ההתקשרות האמיתית היא ע"י לימוד התורה» (*True hiskashrus is by learning Torah.* Hayom Yom 24 Sivan, line 337) «למד את החסידות שאני אומר ותהיה למקושר» (*Learn the Chassidus I say and you will be bound.* Rayatz quoting the Rashab, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 510) Yechidus is «לברר את מצבו, ולקבוע אופן עבודתו בסור מרע ולקרב את המדות הטובות» (*To clarify one's state and fix one's manner of avodah.* Hayom Yom 10 Elul, line 446) And a blessing never replaces the work: «בעטען א ברכה אויף עבודה איז דאס אל ישעו בדברי הבל» (*Asking for a blessing for avodah is 'heeding empty words'.* Hayom Yom 24 Tammuz (the Tzemach Tzedek), line 382) «שבברכה בלבד לא נעבור את העולס» (*With a blessing alone we will not get through the world.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 576) Hayom Yom's three steps: «שרי' - ווייקען זיך אין דברי הרב, מליחה - יחידות, הדחה - ניגון» (*Soaking: in the Rebbe's words; salting: yechidus; rinsing: a niggun.* Hayom Yom 26 Elul, line 473)

**Ahavas Yisrael (love of others).** Where to begin: «אַז אָנהויבן דאַרף מען פון אהבת ישראל» (*One must begin with ahavas Yisrael.* Rayatz quoting the Rashab, Sefer HaSichos 5706, line 6485) The gate: «מצות אהבת ישראל היא שער הכניסה» (*Love of Israel is the gate of entry.* Hayom Yom 2 Tammuz, line 350) The daily test: «ווען עס גייט דורך אַ טאָג און ער איז ניט מחי'» (*When a day passes and he has not revived (a Jew).* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5707, line 6895) Its rank: «אַ טובה טאָן אַ אידן בגשמיות איז העכער ווי פאַרשטיין אַ טיפן ענין אין לערנען» (*Doing a Jew a material favour is higher than understanding a deep matter in learning.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5707, line 6850) How: «לראות גם בהזולת צד הטוב, ולהבין זה מעצמו» (*To see the good side in the other too, and understand it from oneself.* Rayatz, Igros Kodesh vol. 14, line 1085) and when resentment comes, «יהדפנה מלבו כהנדוף עשן» (*Let him push it from his heart like smoke.* Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 22b, line 1349) The Apter: «ועוד שאהבת ישראל נקשרת עם אהבת השי"ת. ושניהם הם אחד» (*Love of Israel is bound to love of G-d, and the two are one.* Apta, Ohev Yisrael, line 64) The Rayatz's summary: «עיקר החסידות הוא תיקון המדות ולייקר יהודי פשוט» (*The essence of Chassidus is repairing the traits and holding a simple Jew dear.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 5447)

**Stories and niggun.** «סיפורי החסידים איז די הטבה כללית וואָס מאַכט פאַר אַ בעל מדות טובות» (*Chassidic stories are the general remedy that makes one a person of good traits.* Rayatz quoting the Rashab, Sefer HaSichos 5707, line 6787) R. Hillel gave a young man a saying, then a niggun: «וממילא נדבקה האימרה בניגוו הראשון» (*So the saying stuck with the first singing.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (R. Hillel) (OCR), line 4726) But: «ניגון איז אַ הכנה צו עבודה, דאָס איז נאָך ניט עבודה» (*A niggun is a preparation for avodah; it is not yet avodah.* Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5706, line 6511)

**Where they agree.** The goal is the same unity lived in every act; prayer is the centre of the day; the proof is the changed person, not the feeling; joy, not sadness; some form of holding back; a friend or guide.

**Where they differ (kept).** - **The door.** Mind (Chabad), speech (Breslov), voice (Karlin), habit and picture (Lizhensk), attachment in every act (the BeSHT), attentive looking (Izhbitz). - **Body first or mind first.** The BeSHT's school: «צריך לעורר עצמו בתחלה בגוף שלו בכל כחו כדי שתאיר בו כח הנשמה» (*He must first wake himself in his body with all his strength, so the soul's power shines.* Tzava'at HaRivash, line 111) Chabad starts with the mind. - **Is hisbodedus the road?** Breslov: «ואי אפשר לבוא לידי בטול, כיאם עלידי התבודדות» (*One cannot come to self-nullification except through hisbodedus.* Breslov, Likutei Moharan 52, line 2696) The Mitteler Rebbe counts it as one of three handles («התבודדות, עיון והתעכבות» (*Withdrawal, close study and lingering.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (the Mitteler Rebbe) (OCR), line 1533)) and adds «למרות מעלת וגדולת ההתבודדות -- אין היא כוונת ירידת הנשמה בגוף» (*For all its greatness, hisbodedus is not the purpose of the soul's descent into the body.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 1541) - **Depth or simplicity.** Breslov prefers simplicity to "cleverness"; sources D notes its target is sophistication, not Chabad's contemplation of the unity, so the two are not simply opposed. - **The gaps of the day.** Chabad fills them with da'at (Tanya 42); Lizhensk with a pictured act of self-giving; Breslov and Piaseczno with a word to G-d; Piaseczno also with a word of Torah held in the street.

*Ours:* the loop in section 2 has room for each door. A person whose mind is the door leans on steps 3-4; one whose speech is the door leans on step 5's "own words" and the sentence in step 8; one who needs habit leans on step 9 and the forty days.

## 9. For anyone

The owner applies the practices to everyone (D28: the Tanya's "part of G-d above" does not deny a soul to others, so the work applies to all). The sources speak to Jews; the universal forms beside them are **ours**, keeping each Jewish practice under its name. The sources do give several things as open to every soul: «והוא דבר השוה לכל נפש מקטן ועד גדול» (*It is a thing equal for every soul, from small to great.* Breslov, Likutei Moharan II 25, line 6352) «כל אחד ואחד לפום שיעורא דילי' יכול לגשת אל הקדש לעבוד את הוי' במוחו ולבו» (*Each person, by his own measure, can come near and serve G-d with mind and heart.* Hayom Yom 17 Kislev, line 606) «שכל אדם יכול להיות בינוני בכל עת ובכל שעה» (*Every person can be a beinoni at every time and every hour.* Tanya ch. 14, line 232) «כל עבודה ופועל, איזה שיהיו, בכוונה אמיתית די ומספיק: א ברכה בכוונה» (*Any service or act, with true intention, is enough: one blessing with intention.* Hayom Yom 2 Adar I, line 115)

## 10. What AI can do at each point, and what it must not do

*All of this section is ours, built on the sources' own warnings.*

**Its place.** A companion and a reminder. Not a mashpia, not a community, not a Rebbe. The sources' reasons: a guide has to work on himself to reach you («אלא עליו לעבוד עס עצמו במדה שישפיע גס על הזולת» (*He must work with himself to the degree that it affects the other.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 4727)), the choice of method belongs to a human guide («אז יתייעץ עם המשפיע באיזה מהן לבחור» (*Then let him consult the mashpia which to choose.* The Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos vol. 34, line 17033)), and a program a person builds alone tends to go wrong (section 6).

| Point in the loop | What AI can do |
| --- | --- |
| Step 1, the night | Ask for tomorrow's subject and one trait; set the waking reminder. |
| Step 3, learning | Offer a short passage; walk its details; ask the person to say it back in their own words. |
| Step 4, binding | Keep time; bring them back to the one line without scolding. |
| Step 5, prayer | Give the four parts as a short checklist; on hard days offer the floor: word by word. |
| Step 6, resolution | Help name one trait and one real situation today. Refuse general vows gently. |
| Steps 7-8, the day | Send the carried line at the person's own anchor times, and nothing else. |
| Step 9, itkafia | On request, "wait with me ten minutes": a delay, not a lecture. |
| Step 10, gauge | Two yes/no questions at midday. |
| Step 11, accounting | A short guided review with a time limit; always end with one good point and tomorrow's plan. |
| Growth | Show the one trait over weeks; name relapse as normal; suggest the next trait only when the first holds. |
| Companions | Encourage a real friend, a guide and a weekly table; help prepare what to tell them. |
| Counterfeits | Gently name a sign (sadness, sighing, showing off) and point to its cure. |

**What it must not do.** - **Not rule.** No halachic rulings; send to a rav. - **Not guide in the mashpia's place.** No choosing a person's path of avodah; point to a human guide. - **Not replace people.** No "I am your chaver" or "this is your farbrengen". - **No guilt.** No sin scores, no streak-shaming, no long lists of faults. The Rebbe to one who was tormented: «במשך איזה שבועות יסיח דעת מלחשוב אודות מצבו ברוחניות» (*For some weeks, let him stop thinking about his spiritual state.* The Rebbe, Igros Kodesh vol. 4, line 15630) «והעצה לזה ת"ת ולא עצבות» (*The remedy for this is Torah study, not sadness.* The Rebbe, Igros Kodesh vol. 4, line 15630) - **No harshness.** No fasts, sleep loss or self-affliction; the sources call that the animal soul's trap («ומקבליס עליהס כמה עניניס של סיגופים ועינוי הגוף» (*And they take on self-mortifications and afflicting the body.* Rayatz, Likkutei Dibburim (OCR), line 5449)). - **No pushing for experiences**, and no asking how it feels (the guide's standing rules, GUIDE-LAYER.md). - **Privacy.** Confessions and the accounting stay private: store the least, never share, let the person delete. - **Danger protocol.** Talk of self-harm, abuse or crisis: stop the practice, give the crisis line, urge real help now. (Same as the manual's guard.)

## 11. Gaps

- **Rosh Chodesh**: no practice in the four files.
- **Clock times**: apart from the Rashab's yeshiva hours, the Alter Rebbe's hour and a half, "half an hour or more", "an hour or more" and "an hour" on Shabbos Mevarchim, minutes here are ours.
- **The midday gauge** is our name for a check the sources imply (the imprint, Mincha) but do not set.
- **Home and marriage as avodah**: almost nothing beyond running the home calmly («שלא ברוגז וכעס» (*Without irritation or anger (running the home).* Rayatz, Igros Kodesh vol. 16, line 5904)) and Karlin's «ליזהר מאד מגאוה וכעס אף לדבר מצוה» (*Beware greatly of pride and anger, even for a mitzvah.* Karlin, Beit Aharon (R. Aharon the Great), line 24)
- **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim ch. 29-33** (learning in order to act; prayer) are missing from the file.
- **The Rayatz's "lamplighter" sichah** and the 1936-42 sichos are missing.
- **OCR**: much of the Rashab, the Rayatz's Likkutei Dibburim (itself a Hebrew translation of Yiddish) and Kuntres Toras HaChassidus are uncorrected scans.
- **Karlin's outcry** is tradition, not a written instruction in Beit Aharon.
- **The seven-year passage** in Kuntres Bar Mitzvah: which maamar it belongs to is unconfirmed.
- **Non-Jews**: the sources address Jews; applying the system to everyone is the owner's decision (D28).
- **Mental health**: the sources do not address when practice itself harms; the danger protocol is ours.
- **Not read for this file**: Me'or Einayim, Toldot Yaakov Yosef, R. Nosson's works, the full Igros of the Rebbe and the Rashab.
- **A source slip corrected**: sources D quotes Beit Aharon line 95 as «…של התפלה…»; the file reads «התפילה». The corrected string is used here.
