## 1. Structure of the range

My range starts partway through **Achdus Hashem (4)** and runs to the end of the volume. The volume's table of contents (lines 4088-4099) gives the section names used below.

**A. Achdus Hashem (4): "The unity is recognizable in the nature of the world"** (end of §3 and all of §4; lines 2051-2095, scan pp. 124-126) - *§3, natural facts that go against the laws of nature (end).* The heat of summer and the cold of winter cannot come from the sun's tiny change in distance, so God sends them through the heavens. Some real measures, such as the side of a square of 50 and the diagonal of a unit square, can only be approximated. A thrown stone rises against both its natures and still exists. The entry reads each of these as a sign that a thing's nature is a garment over the divine power that is its true being. - *§4, the unity is recognizable even in nature's concealment.* The hiding of exile brings out self-sacrifice (mesirus nefesh). The entry concludes that the inner reality of a concealment is revelation.

**B. Achdus Hashem (5): "From the side of the intent"** (the full heading adds: above the unity that comes from creation and the unity that comes from the condition; lines 2096-2242, pp. 127-135; entry pp. רלז-רנד) - *Introduction.* There are three ways of saying the world is one with God: it is created anew every moment; it was created on the condition that Israel accept the Torah; it was created for the sake of Israel and the Torah. Each gives a deeper degree of nullification. - *§1, the difference in degree of nullification.* Continuous creation gives only bitul hayesh (nullification of selfhood), because the creating power is a limited power. The condition gives nullification of existence (bitul bimetziyus), but not total, since in any conditional act the act remains the main thing. The intent gives complete nullification. Above, thought itself creates, so the world's true reality *is* the intent: Israel and Torah. - *§2, which level.* Sometimes this is said even of the level of speech: the Rebbeim "saw not the ceiling but the word of God that creates it". Mainly it belongs to the will for Torah and mitzvos, which is above the will for worlds. Most precisely it belongs to Torah, not mitzvos: a mitzvah is an instruction for acting in the world, so it gives the world standing, while Torah is His will and wisdom and relates to nothing outside Him. - *§3, why a condition was needed as well.* Creation as the creatures experience it comes from the divine "action", not the divine "thought". The condition makes their inner content Israel and Torah on that level too. - *§4, how it shows in halachah.* Several laws go by a thing's intended purpose and not by its outward form: "the need of the Most High cancels the need of the ordinary person" when terumah is burned on Yom Tov; a vessel is secondary to the food carried in it; "this whole verse was said for measures" about the seven species.

**C. Achdus Hashem (6): Yichuda Ila'ah and Yichuda Tata'ah, the higher and the lower unity** (lines 2243-3640, pp. 136-214; entry pp. רנה-תיב). This is the bulk of the range. It has a long introduction and 23 sections: - *Introduction.* The higher/lower distinction has two senses: how far the unity is *felt* by creatures, or a difference in *the unity itself*. It maps onto bitul hayesh and bitul bimetziyus, onto Echad and Va'ed (the Shema and Baruch Shem), and onto the lower and higher knowledge. The entry gives four explanations of the difference, three levels of who can feel it, and a series of pairs of "source" and "above source". - §1, external or internal (bitul only in the outer powers, or bitul that comes only through effort). - §2, from the creature's side or from Godliness' side. - §3, two divisions in the lower unity: taking up place, and feeling oneself a separate thing. These follow the letter exchange Echad→Va'ed, the two effects of the Name Elokim, and the two sides of the lower knowledge. - §4, the source makes room for the selfhood. - §5, three levels: in angels, in souls, in lofty souls. - §6, Va'ed (the lower unity) as eternity. - §7, the lower unity is in all creatures. - §8, the higher and the lower knowledge. - §9, both knowledges are true. - §10, the "something" of the lower knowledge does not contradict His unity. - §11, surrounding and filling (sovev, memale). - §12, Havayah and Elokim. - §13, the lower and the higher Havayah. - §14, in the sefiros and the worlds. - §15, the difference as seen from the thing created. - §16, seeing and hearing. - §17, the higher unity is only for the chosen few. - §18, everyone must still strive a little for the higher. - §19, three levels: lower, the higher shining in the lower, higher. - §20, why the lower unity is needed. - §21, which is harder. - §22, joining the two. - §23, their links to other pairs: image and likeness, blessing and thanks, sea and land, the two eyes, Yaakov and Yisrael, the double loaves, bones and flesh, seeing and gazing, from above and from below, revealed and inner Torah, one witness and two.

**D. Supplements to the notes (Miluim) for the entry's earlier sub-entries** (lines ~3638-4086, pp. 217-253). These are additions to footnotes of Achdus in creation, Achdus in humankind, Achdus through keeping the "boundaries", and Achdus Hashem (1)-(6). The ones that bear on the unity: the world is not an illusion; "no existence at all" was newly revealed in Chassidus and is now obligatory to learn; the line between shituf (partnership) and idolatry; *yachid* versus *echad* (from the Tzemach Tzedek); the lower unity completes the purpose of creation.

**E. Table of contents and dedication** (lines 4088-4101).

## 2. Key teachings on the unity

**T1. The inner reality of a concealment is revelation.** Exile hides God, yet exile is when self-sacrifice comes into the open. A thing's true reality is the purpose it was made for, and a concealment exists for the sake of a greater revelation. So the inner content of the hiding is itself the revealing of Godliness. The entry cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 19 p. 192ff, Toras Menachem vol. 3 pp. 193-4 and Likkutei Sichos vol. 17 p. 96. > שענינו הפנימי דההעלם גופא הוא שיהי' גילוי אלקות (line 2093)

**T2. Nature is a garment over the divine power.** A creature's natural properties are a covering that lets the eye of flesh see it at all. The entry's example: if the eye were allowed to see the vitality within, "the physicality of the creature, its matter and its substance, would not be seen at all". The entry cites Sefer HaMaamarim 5711 p. 292ff, the Siddur (Shaar Lag BaOmer) and Or HaTorah Nach vol. 2 pp. 946-7. > לא הי' גשמיות הנברא וחומרו וממשו נראה כלל (line 2082) > שהענין ד,קיץ וחורף" נובע לא מחוקי הטבע (line 2060)

**T3. Three grounds for the unity, in rising order.** Continuous creation gives only "nullification of selfhood", because the power that creates is limited. Even the eternity that creatures get from the Ein Sof power does not enter their inner being. > הוא רק ,ביטול היש", כי הכח האלקי המהווה את העולם הוא כח מוגבל (line 2116)

The Torah condition goes further: if the condition had failed, the world would be undone retroactively, past included. The intent goes furthest, and this nullification is complete. > ביטול העולם לאלקות מצד זה שכוונת הבריאה היא בשביל ישראל ובשביל התורה - הוא ביטול בתכלית (line 2115) > אמיתית המציאות של העולם (כפי שהוא מצד הכוונה) הוא (לא עולם, אלא) ישראל ותורה, אלקות (line 2191)

The entry cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 19 pp. 192-3, vol. 6 p. 92, and D"H Shuvah Yisrael 5746.

**T4. "There is nothing else besides Him" is fully true at the level of Torah, not of mitzvos.** A mitzvah tells a person how to act in the world, so the person and the world have standing; at that level there remains, "most subtly", a reality that is not Godliness. Torah is His own will and wisdom, which relates to no world. The world exists so that Torah's laws are also fulfilled in fact. The entry reads "halichos olam lo" as "do not read *halichos*, but *halachos*". It cites D"H Shuvah Yisrael 5746 and the Hadranim on the Rambam p. 146. > אמיתית הענין ד,אין עוד מלבדו" שאין שום מציאות מלבד אלקות הוא בהדרגא דתורה שלמעלה מהדרגא דמצוות (line 2196)

**T5. A halachic mirror.** Laws that judge a thing by its purpose show that its "reality" as it looks outwardly gives way to its true reality. The entry's examples are burning terumah on Yom Tov (Tosafos: the need of the Most High cancels the ordinary need) and a vessel carried with food. It cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 19. > אמיתית מציאותה של ,שריפת תרומה" הוא זה שמצוה לשרוף תרומה טמאה (line 2233)

**T6. The Rebbeim's way of seeing.** > שאין רואים את התקרה (דעם באלקן) ורק את הדבר הוי' שמהווה את התקרה (line 2179)

The entry cites a sicha of 15 Shevat 5737 and D"H Shuvah 5746.

**T7. What the unity means, and the two senses of higher and lower.** In general, His unity according to Chassidus is that He is the only existent and there is no existence besides Him. > הוא ית' הוא המצוי היחידי ואין שום מציאות זולתו ית' (line 2272)

On the second reading of the distinction, "the worlds are not existences in themselves" is the *lower* unity. The higher unity is that the worlds "have no value and take up no place at all": "all before Him is as nothing". > כל העולמות אין להם שום ערך ותפיסת מקום (line 2273)

A footnote adds a point that matters for the index. The teaching that nothing exists besides Him because He creates everything each moment is itself one of the things revealed in Chassidus, and yet within Chassidus it is only the *lower* unity. > אלא שבהענינים דאחדות ה' שבתורת החסידות גופא, ענין זה הוא יחודא תתאה (line 2276)

**T8. The four explanations of lower and higher.** The lower bitul (1) stays in the creature's exterior, or (2) comes from the creature's own grasp, or (3) is that of the sun's ray outside the sun, or (4) is contained in the source but still counts for something before it. The higher is the opposite in each case. For (3) the image is the ray *inside* the sun, where the name "something" does not apply at all: "ayin" (dependent) against "efes" (contained). The entry cites Hemshech Rosh Hashanah 5694 ch. 31 and Sefer HaMaamarim 5663. > בדוגמת אור השמש, שבהיותו כלול בהשמש אין נופל עליו שם יש כלל (line 2314)

**T9. Who can reach which level.** The first level can be felt even by angels. Being contained in the source can be felt only by souls, because souls are Godliness in essence and an angel that reached it would cease to be a creature. The true higher unity ("as nothing") is given from above, and as a rule only to lofty souls, though the gift can reach most souls. The entry cites Hemshech 5672 vol. 1 ch. 189 (pp. 385-7) and Kuntres HaAvodah ch. 1 p. 5. > אבל הביטול דכלא חשיב, אמיתית הביטול דיחודא עילאה הוא שנותנים להם מלמעלה (line 2499)

**T10. Bitul the creature makes is still "something".** Bitul that comes from the creature's own understanding, feeling, or even self-sacrifice is only bitul hayesh, because the bitul is itself a "something". Rabbi Akiva's soul went out "in Echad" because a light of the true One was drawn onto him, and that folded his self-sacrifice into the higher unity. The entry cites Toras Menachem Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat vol. 3 p. 159 n. 31 and Torah Or Vayeshev 28b. > ע"י שנמשך עליו הארה מבחינת אחד האמת (line 2416)

**T11. Contemplation and intention.** The higher bitul comes of itself; the lower comes through contemplation. > הביטול דיחודא עילאה בא ממילא, והביטול דיחודא תתאה בא ע"י התבוננות (line 2353)

In the lower service, a person aims in doing mitzvos to bring his soul close and reveal Godliness in himself. In the higher, he aims for the soul's cleaving to Ein Sof and for drawing Ein Sof into the world. > שיהי' דביקות נפשו באוא"ס (line 2359)

**T12. Echad and Va'ed, and Va'ed reaching Yachid.** Va'ed has the letters of Echad exchanged, so its unity is lower. > האחדות ד,אחד" היא יחודא עילאה, והאחדות ד,ועד" - יחודא תתאה (line 2285)

Yet "va'ed" is the word for eternity and belongs to Atik, which is *yachid*, above *echad*. So the lower unity reaches above the higher. > שייכת לבחינת יחיד שלמעלה גם מבחינת אחד (line 2537)

The tribes' "just as in your heart there is only One, so in ours" is praise. Their lower unity reaches the *yachid* above Yaakov's *echad*. > הוא מגיע בבחינת ,יחיד" שלמעלה מ;אחד" (line 2556)

This is mainly through refining the physical world, which lies below even the lower unity. > כי יתרון האור הוא מן החושך דוקא (line 2580)

On Yom Kippur this root is revealed, so Baruch Shem is said aloud. The entry cites Or HaTorah Beshalach p. 567, Sefer HaMaamarim 5735 p. 309 (Hayoshevet BaGanim) and 5748 pp. 169-170.

**T13. The lower unity is in every creature, as acknowledgment.** The lower unity comes from Malchus, which spreads everywhere, so every creature has it, but only as hoda'ah (acknowledgment), the lowest grade of bitul. The spheres always turn toward the west, bowing to the Shechinah. The three impure shells have not even this, so they must be removed. The entry cites Sefer HaMaamarim 5567 p. 290 and Sefer HaMaamarim 5670 pp. 306 and 435. > אלא שביטול זה הוא רק בבחינת הודאה בלבד (line 2587)

**T14. The two knowledges, and what counts as "real".** The lower knowledge: creation is "something from nothing", the world is real and its source is "nothing". > דעת תחתון היא שהבריאה היא מאין ליש (line 2294)

The higher knowledge: above is the true "something" and below is nothing. There are two "nothings": the source that the creature cannot grasp, and the light that is as nothing before its own source. The two knowledges differ over *what counts as existence*. A child counts only what he can touch as real; a thinker counts an idea as real; a person refined by long work counts only Godliness as real, because what is defined is not truly existent. The entry reads the Rambam's second halachah this way: even someone who knows that everything exists "from the truth of His existence" may still think of Him as "not an existent", because He is undefined. The Rambam answers that He alone truly exists. The entry cites the Hadran on the Rambam 5749 §§8-9. > שאדרבה, הוא ית' הוא המצוי האמיתי (line 2671)

**T15. Both knowledges are true, but the main truth is the higher.** Even the creatures' knowledge that the world is a real "something" is true. The last words of creation, bara Elokim la'asos, end in the letters of *emes* (truth). > גם זה שהעולם הוא מציאות יש (דעת תחתון דהנבראים) הוא אמת (line 2696)

The reasons given: - The tzimtzum is real above, so the contracted light truly gives creatures room. The midrash says "Truth said, let it not be created; Kindness said, let it be created": truth belongs to the light above the tzimtzum and kindness to the contracted light. > אמת הוא בדרגת האור שלמעלה מהצמצום (line 2724) - Both knowledges are rooted in the Ein Sof, which is at once "above without end" and "below without limit". They are true only when that root is openly recognized in them, and that is why they must be joined. > דוקא כשניכר בהם בגילוי (line 2733) - Still, the main truth is the higher knowledge, because the lower is true only in a certain place (after the tzimtzum), while the higher is so in itself and everywhere. > ולכן עיקר ענין האמת הוא בדעת עליון (line 2768) - Beyond this, through the world's yesh a truth higher than the higher knowledge is revealed.

For the person, the higher knowledge arrives as acknowledgment. He sees the world as real and keeps the mitzvos in it as real, yet at the moment of action he acknowledges that it is nothing. > בשעת מעשה הוא מודה שהעולם הוא אין וכלא חשיב (line 2679)

The entry cites Hemshech 5666 p. 436ff, Toras Menachem Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat vol. 2 pp. 105 and 111, the Tzemach Tzedek's Sefer HaMitzvos 54b, and Or HaTorah Shir HaShirim 199a.

**T16. Surrounding and filling.** Being (hisHavus) comes from sovev and vitality from memale. Relative to memale the world is a real but secondary thing, like a body to its soul. Relative to sovev it is "not even a secondary existence". "Ein od" in its full sense ("not even a secondary thing") holds only relative to sovev. The entry cites Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 6, Likkutei Torah Emor, the Tzemach Tzedek's Sefer HaMitzvos 60b, Hemshech 5666 p. 273ff, and Inyanah shel Toras HaChassidus §16. > העולם אינו גם מציאות טפילה, יחודא עילאה (line 2831)

The entry also gives a warning. The nations, who know Godliness only as memale, think God and the world are like soul and body. > חושבים שהקב"ה והעולם הוא כמו נשמה וגוף (line 2839) > ולא כגוף לנשמה (line 2840, a footnote citing Sefer HaMitzvos and Inyanah §16)

**T17. Havayah and Elokim.** Relative to Havayah ("was, is and will be as one", above worlds) creatures are as nothing: the higher unity. Relative to Elokim creatures take up place: the lower unity. Elokim does two things. It contracts Havayah's light into a source, which gives creatures place, and it hides even that contracted light, which makes them feel separate. Yet the very being of the yesh comes from Havayah, and Elokim conceals nothing from Havayah's side. So in truth even the creatures that became "something" are nullified in existence. The entry cites Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah chs. 4 and 7, Hemshech 5666 pp. 223-6, Sefer HaMaamarim 5657 p. 51, and Kuntres Eitz HaChaim p. 29. > גם הנבראים שנעשו מציאות יש, הם בטלים במציאות (line 2968)

**T18. The lower and higher Havayah; echad and yachid.** On one reading, the lower Havayah is *echad*: the heavens, earth and four directions exist but are utterly nullified, and that is the higher unity. The higher Havayah is *yachid*, above even the higher unity. The entry cites Hemshech 5666 p. 224 and Or HaTorah Beshalach p. 431. > והוי' דלעילא הוא בחינת יחיד, שלמעלה גם מהביטול דיחודא עילאה (line 2999)

The Supplements quote the Tzemach Tzedek (Sefer HaMitzvos 124b): > עצם מהות הא"ס ב"ה אין שייך לקרותו אחד רק יחיד (line 3912)

In the entry's reading, relative to the Essence as it is in itself the worlds were not "nullified as if they were not"; they were not created at all.

**T19. Hearing and seeing.** One view: the higher unity is seeing (chochmah) and the lower is hearing (binah). What one only understands leaves room in the soul for doubt; what one sees does not. > לא נשאר אצלו שום מקום בנפש שתהי' אחיזה לסתירה (line 3137)

The more common view: the higher unity is only "hearing", since the nullification of the sefiros of Atzilus cannot be pictured, while the lower is "seeing", since the bowing of the spheres is visible. Yet a sage outranks a prophet: knowing that the higher exists is worth more than seeing the essence of the lower. > חכם עדיף מנביא (line 3181)

The entry cites Or HaTorah Vayeira 104b and the Or HaTorah derushim for Pesach p. 786. The men of Jericho did not say Baruch Shem, so as not to interrupt between Echad and v'ahavta. The halachah does not follow them, because where seeing is possible it must be used.

**T20. Most souls serve in the lower unity, but everyone must taste the higher.** Following the Rashab's Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (pp. 30-35), most souls today serve through the lower unity, and very few truly belong to the higher. > והעבודה הנדרשת מכל אחד היא רק העבודה בהביטול דיחודא תתאה (line 3199)

But without at least a little of the higher, the lower service is not true. It cannot stand against obstacles, and it falls easily into full selfhood. > צריך כל אדם לייגע ולהרגיל את עצמו לבוא מעט מזעיר עכ"פ (ואפילו בדרך דמיון) (line 3208) > גם עבודתו ביחודא תתאה אינה אמיתית (line 3208)

**T21. And the reverse: those who serve in the higher need the lower.** "In prayer his heart should be above and his eyes below" (the entry cites Yevamos 105b and Shulchan Aruch OC 95). > שבתפלה צריך להיות לבו למעלה ועיניו למטה (line 3293)

The lower unity has advantages the higher lacks: - It refines the kelipos (birur), while the higher only overwhelms them for a time. The entry cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 9 p. 57ff. > לא בדרך בירור אלא בבחינת ביטול (line 3304) - It is the person's own work, it produces a stronger arousal, and it is the novelty that draws a new light. - The purpose of creation, the dwelling below, is completed in it. > וכוונה זו נשלמת ביחודא תתאה (line 3326, footnote) - The higher alone can fall after prayer. The "nefilim" were lofty souls drawn straight from Z"A, not through Malchus, and they fell, because the higher bitul "does not act on the selfhood of creatures". The entry cites the Tzemach Tzedek's Biurei HaZohar vol. 2 p. 610. > כי הביטול במציאות שמצד ז'א אינו שייך ולכן אינו פועל בהישות דהנבראים (line 3336)

**T22. Which is harder.** The higher unity, ceasing to be an existence of one's own, belongs to every Jew by his soul, and he is always ready for it. > זה שייך לכל יהודי מצד נשמתו (line 3344)

The lower, remaining an existence while that existence is nullified to God (for example "all your deeds for the sake of Heaven"), needs special preparation, and in fact needs a little of the higher. So the daily offerings begin with the olah, which is wholly burnt, before the offerings that are eaten. Because the body and animal soul hide the soul, even the higher needs a special empowerment, and that comes from Moshe. The entry cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 6 p. 233ff.

**T23. Joining the two.** In the joining, the lower unity stays lower, yet the higher bitul shines in it. The chief novelty is when the bitul becomes higher while the person's awareness stays lower. > שיחודא תתאה (מצד ענינה) נשארת יחודא תתאה, ואעפ"כ מאיר בה הביטול דיחודא עילאה (line 3366)

The joining draws a light above both: - the Name Kel ("Kel de'os": one Name holding both knowledges; Torah Or Yisro 68); - the Ein Sof's "above/below" as one; - finally the capacity (yecholes) of the Essence alone.

The joining goes first from below upward (Shema) and then from above downward (Torah). The Ark that "took up no space" is the entry's model of the three: - measuring the Ark as 2½ cubits is the lower; - measuring ten cubits on each side of it is the higher; - holding both at once, equally, is the joining.

The entry cites Toras Menachem Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat vol. 2 p. 105ff and the Rebbe's Igros Kodesh vol. 2 p. 392ff. On Shabbos, by the inner Torah, the two challos lie side by side, because relative to the revealed Essence neither unity outranks the other. > אין יתרון ביחו"ע על יחו"ת, ושניהם שוים ממש (line 3572)

**T24. Revealed and inner Torah.** The revealed Torah is the lower unity and the inner Torah the higher. Learning the revealed Torah alone, without the love and awe that the inner Torah brings, makes one "a something", even to the point of learning for one's own honor. > ובהכרח שיהי' גם הלימוד דפנימיות התורה (line 3626)

**T25. Blessing and acknowledgment; image and likeness.** The higher unity is *brachah*, Godliness openly present as if seen. The lower is *hoda'ah*: one does not see it but believes and acknowledges that it is true. > ורק שמאמין ומודה שכן הוא האמת (line 3469)

The entry cites Likkutei Torah Va'eschanan 5c and Eikev 16d. "In our image" (tzelem, the thing's own form) is the higher unity, and "after our likeness" (demus, a resemblance) is the lower. Only man was made with both. The higher unity is found only in man. The lower is in all creatures, but in man it is higher, because it passes through understanding.

**T26. From the Supplements.** The world is not an illusion (dimyon). Its existence is "strong" and is a reality "according to Torah". The meaning of the unity revealed in the inner Torah is "no existence at all", not even the most subtle. > שמבלעדו ית' אין גם מציאות הכי דקה (line 3823)

Earlier generations had no flaw in their faith for lacking it, but now that it is revealed: > חובה על כאו"א מישראל ללמוד ענין זה ולהאמין בזה (line 3826)

(the entry cites Likkutei Sichos vol. 35 with vol. 7). Someone who treats his business schemes as the main source of his livelihood, needing God only "also", falls into *shituf* (partnership); thinking the business alone is the source falls into idolatry. > נכלל זה ב,שיתוף" (line 3768; the entry cites Or HaTorah Yisro p. 918)

## 3. What the range clarifies, sharpens or corrects in the SPINE

**Thesis precision "yachid, not echad": confirms and sharpens.** The entry sets up three rungs, not two: 1. the lower unity (Va'ed: the worlds are not existences in themselves); 2. the higher unity (Echad: the worlds exist but count for nothing); 3. Yachid (relative to the Essence the worlds were not created at all). See T18 (lines 2999, 3912) and the footnote at line 2283.

So the SPINE's "yachid" is above even yichuda ila'ah and must not be treated as the same thing. The entry also adds a paradox the SPINE should carry: the *lowest* rung (Va'ed, and the physical world below it) is what touches *yachid* (T12, lines 2537, 2556).

**F01, the world said into being now: sharpens, and partly re-ranks.** The entry places continuous creation as the *lower* unity. It gives only bitul hayesh, because the creating word is a limited power (lines 2116, 2276). A Supplement note (line 3959) records the Rebbe's remark that Tanya ch. 20 grounds "as nothing before Him" without the argument from continuous creation. The notes at lines 3905-3906 go further: He is alone even in the "place" of creatures, even if creatures did not need constant renewal. The guide can keep F01 as a face, but it should not present continuous creation as the peak or the ground of the unity. It is the first rung.

**F02, the higher and the lower unity: confirms, and greatly sharpens.** Six points the SPINE lacks: - The distinction has two senses: degree of feeling versus the unity itself (line 2273). - The entry gives four explanations and three levels of who can feel them (T8, T9). - The lower knowledge exists in Godliness too, as the source's "giving place", and not only in creatures' perception (lines 2445-2446, 2624-2625). - Both knowledges are true, but the main truth is the higher (T15). - The higher alone is not enough (T21), and the lower alone is not true (T20). - The SPINE says "the second exists for the sake of the first". That is incomplete. The entry says the lower also reaches *above* the higher (Va'ed→Yachid; a higher truth revealed through the world's yesh; the lower is the novelty that draws a new light; it completes the dwelling). **Partial disagreement:** the guide should state both directions.

**F03, the hiding for revelation: confirms.** Exile's hiding reveals mesirus nefesh (T1). Elokim conceals only toward creatures, and the yesh itself comes from Havayah (T17). **Sharpens:** Elokim does two separate things, giving creatures "place" and making them feel separate, and these must not be blurred (lines 2438-2440).

**F04, filling and surrounding: confirms and sharpens.** Sovev is the root of the higher unity and memale of the lower. "Ein od, not even a secondary thing" holds only relative to sovev (T16). This agrees with the SPINE's Tzemach Tzedek anchor that matter's being comes from sovev (lines 2880-2881). The entry adds that memale's light also sets how coarse each world's yesh is (Beriah is not yet full yesh, Asiyah is), at lines 2865-2872.

**F05, the Essence beyond both: confirms.** The two knowledges are joined only by a light above both, finally the capacity of the Essence (T23). **Reading:** the SPINE's Maharash tension (whether creation comes from a mere radiance or from the Essence) is eased by the entry's formula at lines 2966-2967: creation works *through* Elokim, but its "body and essence" is from Havayah. The entry does not address the Maharash there.

**F06, the soul as part of the One: confirms and sharpens.** Only souls can be "contained in the source", because they are Godliness in essence; angels cannot, or they would cease to be creatures (lines 2489-2491). The higher unity "belongs to every Jew by his soul" and is always ready (line 3344). **Sharpens:** it is ready in the soul, yet as experience it is a gift from above, fully so only for lofty souls (T9, T20).

**F07, the home below: confirms, and resolves the SPINE's "pull apart".** The purpose (yesh together with bitul hayesh) is completed in the lower unity, and "the dwelling below" means the higher bitul revealed *within* the lower (footnotes at lines 3326 and 3330). The joining keeps the lower as lower while the higher shines in it (line 3366). This reconciles the Rashab's emphasis (the yesh nullified) with the Rebbe's (the yesh itself as the dwelling) better than the SPINE's "two emphases" note does. That the entry answers the SPINE's question is our **reading**; the texts are stated.

**F08, bitul: sharpens.** Bitul that comes from the creature's own grasp, feeling, or even self-sacrifice is still bitul hayesh. The higher bitul comes from Godliness itself, as a gift (T10). Every creature has at least acknowledgment, the last grade, and only the kelipos lack even that (T13).

**F09, the unity of opposites: confirms.** Both knowledges are true, and so are both conducts, nature and miracle. They are one inwardly and two outwardly, so they must be joined (lines 2741-2751). The Infinite "bears two opposites" (line 2676). This agrees with the SPINE's rule that the opposites stay distinct in conduct.

**F10, Torah and mitzvah: sharpens, and corrects a flat reading.** "Ein od" is fully true at the level of Torah, not mitzvos. A mitzvah gives the world standing and Torah does not (T4). The world's true reality is the intent, Israel and Torah (T3). The revealed Torah is the lower unity and the inner Torah the higher, and learning the revealed alone breeds selfhood (T24). This confirms the SPINE's Rashab warning in F10 from another source.

**F11, darkness turned to light: confirms strongly.** Refining the darkness of the physical world, which is below even the lower unity, is what reaches Atik (line 2580). The lower unity refines (birur), while the higher only overwhelms for a time (line 3304).

**F12, knowing the unity: sharpens.** Knowing the unity means changing what one counts as "real", not only learning that everything depends on Him (T14, Rambam). For most people the higher truth arrives as acknowledgment, not as sight (T15, T25). "Hearing" the higher outweighs "seeing" the lower (T19).

**Grammar:** - **G02 (hold both knowledges): confirms and deepens.** Each knowledge alone looks false, and they are true only when joined and traced to their root (line 2733). - **G03 (concealment serves revelation): confirms** (T1). - **G04 (particular, whole, beyond): confirms.** "Source" and "above source" are relative: each level is a source relative to a higher one (line 2340). - **G05 (the highest in the lowest): confirms** (T12, T21). - **G06 (analogy from the soul, and where it fails): confirms, with a named error.** Taking God and the world as soul and body is the nations' mistake (T16). - **G08 (bitul): sharpens** (T10). - **G09 (purpose): confirms and adds.** The intent is the thing's true reality, and halachah follows it (T3, T5). - **G10 (opposites joined in Him): confirms** (T23). - **G11 (end in action): confirms.** The lower unity is where action and birur happen (T21). - **G14 (Havayah is Elokim): confirms**, with two effects of Elokim to keep apart (T17).

## 4. Distinctions the guide should get right, and warnings

1. **Three rungs, not two.** Va'ed (lower unity), Echad (higher unity), Yachid (the Essence, relative to which nothing was created). The lowest rung touches the highest (lines 2537, 2999, 3912).
2. **Two senses of "higher and lower".** How far the unity is *felt*, or a difference in the *unity itself* (lines 2272-2273). Say which one is meant.
3. **Bitul hayesh and bitul bimetziyus**, in four explanations (T8). The sun image works both ways: the ray outside the sun is dependent (ayin), the ray inside it is "not even called something" (efes).
4. **Two senses of the creature's "somethingness" in the lower unity**: *taking up place* before its source, and *feeling itself separate*. These are Elokim's two effects, the letter exchange's two effects, and the two sides of the lower knowledge (§3).
5. **Two "nothings".** The "nothing" of the created something is the unknown source; the "nothing" of the true something is the light that is nothing before its own source (lines 2632-2638).
6. **From His side and from ours.** The lower knowledge exists in Godliness too, as the source giving place, and there it is true. As the creatures' sense of being separate things, one view says it is not true, while another says it is true on its own level (lines 2694-2702). The guide should give both views and not pick one silently.
7. **Source and above-source are relative.** The same light is "above source" relative to one level and "source" relative to a higher one (line 2340). So "the higher unity" can name different levels in different texts.
8. **Understanding, acknowledging, seeing.** Hearing and seeing, understanding and acknowledgment, brachah and hoda'ah (T19, T25). Most people hold the higher truth by acknowledgment.
9. **The four areas.** In this range: creation (nature, the intent, T1-T3); the Name (Echad/Va'ed, Havayah/Elokim, lower and higher Havayah); Torah (Torah above mitzvos, halachah following the intent, revealed and inner Torah); Israel (souls against angels, man as image and likeness, Yaakov and Yisrael, the tribes, "most souls today").

**Warnings the entry gives against mistaken readings:** - The world is not an illusion. The tzimtzum is real, "Kindness said, let it be created", and the lower knowledge is true (T15, T26). - Continuous creation is not the whole unity. Within Chassidus it is only the lower rung (line 2276). - Do not picture God and the world as soul and body; that is how the nations err (lines 2839-2840). - Do not take one's own bitul, even self-sacrifice, for the higher unity (T10). - The lower service alone "is not true" and falls easily (line 3208). The higher alone can fall after prayer and does not refine the world (T21, the nefilim). - The revealed Torah without the inner Torah breeds selfhood (line 3626). - Treating the means as the main source of livelihood, needing God only "also", is shituf (line 3768). - The higher unity cannot be forced by one's own effort. It is given from above, as a rule to lofty souls. Everyone should still reach for "a little, even imaginatively", from time to time (T9, T20).

## 5. For the guide: sentences a person could be told

1. Chassidus teaches two unities. In the lower, the world is real but exists only because God keeps creating it. In the higher, before Him the world counts for nothing at all. Both are true, and each needs the other.
2. That God re-creates the world every moment is the first step of the unity, not its summit. Beyond it, the world counts for nothing before Him, and beyond that is the Essence, before which the world was never created at all.
3. Most of us live and serve in the lower unity, and that is our real task. But without tasting a little of the higher, even in imagination, our service is not truly one with Him and slips back into self.
4. The higher unity also needs the lower, "heart above, eyes below". It is the lower unity that refines the physical world and makes it a home for God, and through it the lowest reaches the highest.
5. The unity asks us to change what we count as real. Before Him only God truly exists, and every defined thing, however solid it looks, is secondary. Usually we hold this by acknowledging it, not by seeing it.
6. A hiding is never only a hiding. Its inner purpose is a greater revelation, which is why exile brings out a Jew's readiness to give everything for God.

## Summary

The heart of the range is Achdus Hashem (6), the higher and the lower unity: 23 sections that set out the doctrine carefully and in layers. Its main lessons for the index: - Continuous creation, the SPINE's F01, is only the *lower* unity. It is the first rung, not the summit (line 2276). - The unity has three rungs, lower unity, higher unity and Yachid. The SPINE's "yachid" is above even the higher unity (lines 2999, 3912). - The lowest rung, refining the physical world, is what reaches Yachid and completes the dwelling below. This qualifies the SPINE's "the lower exists for the sake of the higher" (lines 2537, 2580, 3326). - Both knowledges are true, with the main truth in the higher, and they must be joined. Most souls live in the lower, and everyone must taste the higher; the higher alone falls, and the lower alone is not true. - Achdus Hashem (5), just before it, adds that the world's true reality is its purpose, Israel and Torah. "There is nothing besides Him" holds fully at the level of Torah, not mitzvos, and the halachah itself follows this.
