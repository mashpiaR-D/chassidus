## The shape of the range

The written continuation began (line 4683) by asking what "there is nothing else" (אין עוד) adds to "Havayah is Elokim". This range is its long second half, and it ends by answering that question. The Rashab moves by chains of verses and parables. The paragraphs are long, some run past 1,800 characters, and many parts end with a gloss that opens a new question. The thread is **how darkness, the *yesh* and the lower worlds are joined to the Essence. Is it by light coming in their place, or by their own turning? And what makes the lower unity (יחודא תתאה) as true as the higher?**

F05 leads by far. Almost every argument climbs past memale and sovev to "the essential hiddenness of Ein Sof" (העלם העצמי דא"ס). F11 comes second. The middle of the range is a sustained treatment of darkness turned to light, with three distinct ways of sweetening severity. F06 is high because the last third asks why souls, and only souls, can do this. F02 is not frequent early but dominates the last 150 lines, where the range ends on the Shema's two unities.

## 1-3. The adversaries broken, the horn raised (5018-5078)

**What is essential is not divided.** Sitting in judgment, only the outer mind reaches down. Standing, the essential mind is drawn, and that same light reaches the lowest:

> וכמשנת"ל דהמשכת אור עצמי לא יש בחי' התחלקות דמעומ"ט ונמשך עד למטה מטה כו' (line 5018)

The accusers ("sons of God") are rooted in the first tzimtzum. So only a drawing from the Essence before the tzimtzum undoes them (5022-5023). Two ascents answer two descents: memale rises into the Essence above the chain, then into the Essence above sovev and memale both (5024, 5036). Even sovev is only a glow. The contemplation of Tanya Part II is applied to creatures:

> ולא כמו שנראה בעיניו לבחי' יש כ"א באמת הן בבחי' ביטול כו' (line 5032, sentence 1)

**Even hidden, it is the Essence.** Now the Essence comes hidden (the Yud-Heh of the mitzvot is in exchanged letters). Still, "it is the Essence that is drawn", so the accusers are nullified, as Torah's words take no impurity (5039-5040). This is the range's first form of a principle it will return to at the end: the hiding does not make what is drawn less than the Essence.

**Breaking the accuser, or turning him?** "God turned the curse into a blessing" is more than refusing to listen. It comes through repentance from love, and through the "no" that reaches the Essence more than the "yes" (5043-5046). The drawing is chiefly into Malchut, the "dark essence", because Malchut alone is rooted in the Essence (5047). The servant's bitul is bitul "in the very being of the yesh" (5050-5051). At the resurrection:

> והיינו שהמת עצמו יקום ויחי' והגוף יהי' אלקות ממש כו' (line 5054)

**The corner.** "Keren" is a corner, born only where two walls meet. It adds nothing but the joining, so it is "ayin" (5059). Malchut joins Atzilut and Beriah, two opposites, by a power rooted above both, in the Malchut of Ein Sof (5065). Its main joining runs upward:

> אך עיקר ההתחברות הוא בבחי' מלמטלמ"ע והוא להיות ביטול הנבראים והתאחדותם באלקות שזהו ע"י בחי' מל' דוקא כו' (line 5066)

## 4. The lamp of God: darkness turned to light (5079-5181)

**Nature is Godliness.** Man is made to light a doubly dark world. Everything lives from divine power, yet nature's steady order hides it (5079). And yet:

> הטבע גופא הוא אלקות דהטבע בגימט' אלקים כו' (line 5080)

The constancy of nature shows the infinite itself, since whatever is limited must change and stop (5080). Faith alone is not light: the thief believes and still breaks in (5083). One needs grasp that nothing makes itself, and that the renewing power is always in the thing:

> והכח אלקי הוא תמיד בו שאם הי' מסתלק כרגע הי' חוזר לאין כו' (line 5084)

**Driving out darkness or turning it.** Darkness is a creation of its own, not only an absence. So "to light the darkness" means that darkness itself becomes light (5092-5093). Refinement adds light, but the sparks do not shine of themselves (5105-5106). Only complete tzaddikim with love of delights, and true penitents, reach real transformation (5110).

**Three sweetenings.** (1) Kindness overpowering severity, like good water poured into bitter or radish cooked in honey. Nothing changes in essence. This is itkafia (5114-5116). (2) Severity turned by a light incomparably higher, "the kindness of truth" of Atik. The accuser sees the King's face and becomes a defender (5134-5135). Here the light comes in darkness's place. This is the Tree of Life, Torah's "luminary", and the tzaddik's love of delights. Mah comes down as physical *yesh* "equal to the ayin", like the manna (5145-5149). (3) Severity sweetened of itself, "only in its root": incense, myrrh, the animal soul's own repentance (5150-5158). Only the third completes the dwelling below:

> וכמ"ש במ"א שבזה דוקא נשלם הכוונה דלהיות לו דירה בתחתונים, מפני שבזה הרי הדברים התחתונים עצמן נעשים כלים לאלקות כו' והיינו שהן עצמן נעשים אלקות כו' (line 5160)

Its source is the reshimu's "air", a dark essence, "His essential Name, Havayah without letters". Its power is the black flame that consumes the wick rather than lighting the room (5179-5180).

## 5. The rock and the single King (5182-5216)

"There is nothing else, even in His void" is tied to the "place" of the reshimu (5183). The Creator is "the beginning of all" without being a source in line with things (5183:1). Gevurah turns out to be overflowing strength, not only withholding. Only by it does the flow reach without end and to the lowest (5185-5191). The perfection of the Essence is to be divided and infinite at once:

> דוקא שיהי' בבחי' התחלקות ובבחי' א"ס דשני הענינים אינם הפכים בבחי' העצמות כו' (line 5195)

Mercy from exaltation is equal to all, since everything is low before the King (5209-5210). But the Essence itself is not even "exalted over" anything, because nothing is besides Him:

> ונק' מלך יחיד שיחיד ומיוחד לגמרי ואין עוד מלבדו כו' שלא יש שום מציאות כלל כו' (line 5213)

The bitul of *yesh* into *ayin* reaches above the first *ayin* (5214-5215).

## 6-7. Two repentances, and "night like day" (5217-5248)

The tzaddik draws light down, from *ayin* to *yesh*. The penitent raises darkness, from *yesh* to *ayin*, and reaches the essential hiddenness (5217-5218). In the future Mah will come down all the way and Ban will rise all the way:

> וזהו שיהי' הוי' אחד ביחוד דמ"ה וב"ן למטה ושמו אחד ביחוד דמ"ה וב"ן למעלה (line 5228)

Eternity belongs to time. The true union of Havayah and Elokim is time itself above time, as the ark took no space (5231-5234). "Everlasting light" (אור עולם) means the world itself is light (5235:1). "There is nothing else" now gets its first answer. It is Elokim itself becoming light, and the bitul of the *yesh* itself:

> ועיקר ענין אין עוד הוא מה שיש מצ"ע נעשה בבחי' אין שזהו מיש לאין להיות בבחי' אין ממש כו', וזה מגיע בבחי' עצמות א"ס כו' (line 5237)

## 8. The lamp of the mitzvah; Malchut has nothing of its own (5249-5306)

The wick, the oil and the flame model the divine soul, the animal soul's intellect and the divine light (5250-5256). The Holy One and Israel "complete" each other. Neither light nor vessel is whole alone (5246). The aim of all of Atzilut is the creatures:

> דזה תכלית הכוונה להיות התהוות הנבראים בבחי' יש ושיהי' ביטול היש לאין ויהי' גילוי אלקות למטה כו' (line 5253)

Kingship alone of the traits needs another to wake it. It applies only to servants and "separate" subjects (5299-5302). And here the Rashab himself marks where the parable fails:

> משא"כ למעלה הרי באמת אינן נפרדים והכל הוא מאתו ית' ועל מי ימלוך כו', וגם מה תופס מקום העלאת מ"ן של האדם למטה כו' (line 5306)

## 9-10. Moon, gem, eye; yachid and echad (5307-5444)

Scripture comes from so high that it can be revealed only as story and bare deed, in Assiyah (5311-5314). The Mishnah decrees "valid, invalid" by the eye of wisdom. The Talmud, working in darkness through questions, reaches wisdom's hidden source (5360-5391). Creation shows more of Ein Sof's wonder than Atzilut does, because it is genuinely new (5399). The light creates only by its cleaving to the Essence:

> אמנם מה שהאור מהווה אין זה בכח עצמו כ"א מפני הדביקות של האור בהעצמות ויש בו כח העצמות שהוא לבדו בכחו ויכולתו להוות כו' (line 5400)

"None compares to You in this world": the power of the Essence is seen in this world specifically (5397). Then comes a direct statement of the index's own precision:

> וכידוע בענין יחיד ואחד דיחיד הוא כמו שאוא"ס יחיד ומיוחד מצ"ע כו', ואחד הוא כמו שבא בבחי' התלבשות בעולמות (line 5428)

The Shema's "echad" is read as the oneness from the side of the worlds (5428:1). Of the Essence there is only acknowledgment, which is bitul of one's whole being (5427, 5430).

## 11-12. The pupil of His eye; prolonging echad (5445-5546)

Souls are rooted above Torah, in "yachid", His essential Name (5470-5476). "You shall be holy" means that souls make even the Essence "holy" (5478). Providence by Havayah reaches souls without intermediaries (5486). Then comes the Shema's first meditation, "prolonging echad". The letters of speech, from which the many worlds come, are in the letters of thought, and those are in the great Name:

> ובאמת כמו ששמו הגדול הוא בבחי' אחדות כן ממש הוא גם בבחי' אותיות הדבור עליון שהן ג"כ בבחי' אחדות גמורה ממש כו' (line 5531)

Then the principle that closes the book is stated for the first time:

> הרי עצם אינו מסתיר על עצם כו', ומאיר באמת בחי' עצמות אוא"ס בגילוי ממש לאחר הצמצום כמו קודם (line 5536, sentence 1)

> וכאשר באמת מאיר גילוי אוא"ס גם לאחר הצמצום הרי באמת הכל בטל במציאות והכל כלול בעצמותו כמו קודם שנתהווה כו' (line 5538)

The meditation climbs through the hidden sefirot to His essential Name:

> וממילא הרי היחוד בעולמות הוא בבחי' יחיד ממש כו' (line 5540)

Echad is kept distinct from yachid: "what is included in the essence is like the essence", where not even "oneness" applies (5528:1).

## 13-14. The attributes above; the Shema's two unions (5547-5618)

Against the Guide (1:54) and the Kuzari, the attributes above are real but essential, not reactions to creatures (5557-5558). Change occurs only in Malchut clothed in Metatron (5560). Evil does not come down from above (5562-5564). The 248 words of the Shema reach the Holy One "to the highest". The Shema's union from above says:

> והיינו דכל מה שנתהווה באמת הכל הוא אלקות ולא יש שום דבר עוד מבלעדו ית' כלל כו' (line 5612)

> וא"כ בעת מציאותו מהו מציאותו הרי כל מציאותו הוא רק האין האלקי המהווה אותו ומבלעדו באמת אין שום מציאות כלל כו' (line 5613)

The *yesh* does not feel this now. The future differs only in feeling it:

> דבאמת כן הוא גם עכשיו רק שעכשיו אין זה נרגש בהיש ולעתיד יהי' נראה ונגלה איך שזהו מציאותו כו' (line 5614, sentence 1)

In the higher knowledge, even the light is "nothing else":

> והיינו שהכל הוא רק בחי' העצמות ואין עוד מלבדו כו' (line 5617)

## 15-17. Honey, the desert, the lamp (5619-5705)

The Shema's love must have delight. Cold contemplation stays in the mind (5647-5652). Its content:

> והוא ע"י ההתבוננות בה' אחד איך שעצמות א"ס הוא היש האמיתי וכולא קמי' כלא חשיב ממש (line 5653)

> ותהי' אהבתו לה' כאדם האוהב את חייו ממש היינו האהבה לעצם החיים שלו כו' שאהבה זו מעצמות הנפש כו' (line 5654, sentence 1)

The soul below is "black and comely". Distance brings a thirst it did not have above. Only below does the incomparability become felt, because only here is there a *yesh* (5681-5683). True life is the Essence, alive of itself. Everything else, even the soul, lives by cleaving:

> ואתה מחי' את כולם אתה דוקא שהוא הוא ית' חיות כל העולמות ואין עוד מלבדו ואפס זולתו והיו כלא היו כו' (line 5688, sentence 1)

The natural intellect must itself understand before the animal soul can love. Two opposite souls joined into one being are "among God's wonders" (5699-5703).

## 18. A God of knowledges: one in One (5706-5750)

The book ends on the Shema. "God is One" is the higher unity, "Blessed be the name" the lower:

> דז"ע ה' אחד שהוא יחיד ומיוחד בתכלית גם לאחר שנתהוו העולמות כמו שהוא יחיד קודם שנבראו העולמות כי הכל בטל בתכלית בלי שום מציאות כלל ואין עוד זולתו ית' כו' (line 5707)

> דלגבי מדרי' זו העולמות הן בבחי' מציאות דבר רק שהן בטלים בבחי' ביטול היש כו' (line 5707, sentence 1)

Creation is from nothing, not cause and effect. So it makes no change in Him (5708-5713). Higher knowledge says "above is *yesh*, below is nothing"; lower knowledge says the reverse (5730). The lower unity is self-nullification by knowledge, still a *yesh* (5735). But the Essence holds both knowledges as one:

> הנה בעצמות א"ס ב' הדעות אחת הן ששניהם אמת ושניהם מיוחדים בא"ס ובבחי' ביטול ואין ממש כו' (line 5738)

> אבל לגבי עצמות א"ס הרי הצמצום והפרסא אינו מעלים ומסתיר כלל ולא יש כאן שום הפסק וריחוק כלל כו' (line 5738, sentence 1)

> דבחי' ההסתר הוא כח הא"ס כמו כח הגילוי כו' (line 5739, sentence 1)

The objection follows: then there is only one *ayin*, and no creation. The answer is that the two *ayin* are two drawings from Ein Sof, one light before the tzimtzum, split only by it (5740-5747). And the last word:

> כמו"כ הוא גם באבי"ע לאחר הצמצום ה"ה כמו בהשערה בכח בלי שום מציאות כלל (line 5748, sentence 1)

> וענין הבריאה הוא בחי' התגלות בלבד (line 5749)

> כי הצמצום אינו מפסיק כלל לגבי העצמות כו' (line 5750)

> וממילא כמו"כ גם כל מה שנתהווה הכל הוא בחי' המשכה מן העצמות ומיוחד בו ית' בתכלית היחוד כו' (line 5750, sentence 1)

> ושניהם הם בפסוק א' הוא למהוי אחד באחד שגם יחו"ת הוא בבחי' יחוד כמו יחו"ע ממש כו' (line 5750, sentence 2)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The lower unity is as true as the higher.** Tanya (Shaar HaYichud 6-7) sets the higher and lower unity side by side, and the lower is what the world sees. Here the Rashab proves they are one "before Him". The *ayin* that is source of the *yesh* is a drawing from the Essence as much as the *ayin* of the true *yesh*, "for the tzimtzum does not interrupt at all toward the Essence" (5750). The Shema holds both in one verse, "one in One". The Tanya does not reach this conclusion in these terms.
- **"Ein od" belongs to the *yesh*.** Light cleaving to its source is "as nothing". The *yesh* is "nothing else", truly not existing (5613:1, 5614). The strongest bitul is at the lowest point, not the highest.
- **Echad and yachid distinguished explicitly.** The Shema's "echad" is the oneness from the side of the worlds (5428). Yachid is the essential light, "like the essence", where not even "oneness" applies (5528, 5530). This is the SPINE's precision, stated here outright.
- **Three sweetenings.** Tanya 9-10 has itkafia and the complete tzaddik's itapcha. The Rashab adds a third, severity sweetened "of itself" by the animal soul's own repentance. It is above the tzaddik's transformation and alone completes the dwelling (5150, 5160).
- **Creatures reveal more of the Essence than light does** (5616), and creation shows more of Ein Sof's wonder than Atzilut does (5399). Tanya Part II grounds the unity in the light (the word that speaks things into being). Here the light is itself "incomparable" and created in the same way as the *yesh* (5615-5617).
- **The attributes above are essential, against the philosophers** (5557-5558). The Tanya (Part II ch. 8-9) says the attributes are "His Essence"; the Rashab argues it against Rambam's Guide by name.
- **Gevurah as overflowing strength.** The Essence's perfection is to be both divided and infinite (5195). Gevurah of Atik, not its kindness, is the true source of all emanation (5196).
- **Souls above Torah.** Souls are rooted in "yachid", above even the inner Torah, and give to it (5470-5479). The Tanya ranks Torah as the union "unlike any other". The Rashab keeps that (5464) and puts the soul's root above it.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 10 lines are *none*: historical and source notes on the Mishnah and Talmud (5342, 5343, 5359), kabbalistic source lists (5403, 5409, 5410, 5286), a practical example (5117) and two parable details (5298, 5444). - 3 lines are *reading*: 5351 and 5352 (Binah's study making truth manifest, applied by us to knowing the unity) and 5594 (spiritual space and time in Atzilut). - 52 line entries carry no face. They are inner steps of long parables (sight, moon, gem, lamp, digestion, the teacher), rabbinic history and Kabbalistic mechanics (Metatron's two levels, the Sanhedrin and the "wondrous one"). - Stretch 9 (5307-5391, Scripture, Mishnah, Talmud) and stretch 15's angelology are mostly F10 or structural. Their tie to the unity runs through the claim that Torah draws Ein Sof into the world. That claim is stated (5456, 5457), but most lines there are about the mechanics of transmission. - F05 (237) is high partly because the Rashab names "the essential hiddenness" or "the Essence" in nearly every argument's last step. Some tags mark "essential" traits and lights by analogy, not the Divine Essence itself. - The 50 sub-entries capture second ties with their own quotes. Other long lines hold further ties that are named in `how` without a quote of their own.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Does the tzimtzum hide?** "No contraction toward Ein Sof", "an essence does not hide an essence" (5536). Yet the lower worlds exist only because the Creator is hidden from the creature (5724). And the lower unity is only bitul of *yesh* (5735). The Rashab holds both: hiding is real "toward the worlds" and nothing "toward the Essence" (5738).
- **One *ayin* or two?** If the partition hides nothing, there is one *ayin* and no creation (5740). The Rashab answers with two drawings joined before the tzimtzum and split by it (5741-5747). Then he says that toward the Essence even after the tzimtzum all is "as in potential, without existence" (5748). The tension is his own.
- **Does He change?** Nothing changes in the Essence, nor in the light, nor even in Malchut as it creates (5711-5713, 5723-5728). Yet sins blemish above, "the Shechinah's limbs" (5719). Malchut "turns according to lower deeds" (5573), and deeds reach even the vessels and lights of Atzilut (5726). He distinguishes creation (no change) from service (it registers), and keeps both.
- **Which is higher, hidden or revealed?** Beriah's hiddenness and Keter's "Holy" are higher, yet revelation "has the advantage, because the intent is revelation" (5408, 5414, 5431).
- **Israel above Torah or Torah above Israel?** Both midrashim are affirmed: the soul in the body is bound to God through Torah (5461, 5464), and souls at their root are above Torah and give to it (5460, 5476-5479).
- **Light in darkness's place, or darkness itself?** The tzaddik's transformation, the Sinai revelation and the mitzvot are light coming in darkness's place. Only the penitent's turning is darkness itself becoming light (5141, 5160, 5217). Yet both are called "darkness turned to light", as Tanya 10 calls the tzaddik's.
- **Loose ends the Rashab marks himself.** He cannot currently find the Pardes passage he cites (5412). He has "not yet looked into" a reference (5102). His reading of striking and speaking to the rock "does not fit what was said above" (5162). He leaves open whether the higher penitent survives by his own nature or by merit (5226), and he notes that one point needs squaring with Tanya 37 (5663).
