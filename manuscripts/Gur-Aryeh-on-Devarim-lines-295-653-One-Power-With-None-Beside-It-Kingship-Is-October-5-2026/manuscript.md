This is the last part of Gur Aryeh: the laws of food and tithes, the festivals, judges and kings, prophets, witnesses, war, the laws between people, bikkurim, the curses, Nitzavim, the song of Haazinu, Moshe's blessings, and his death. A large part is law, war, and passages on nations, women and unions, all left out under D28. What remains closes the whole work with its clearest words on oneness.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **One power, and none beside Him.** Why is the merit of the Shema alone enough? "לפי שהם דבקים בכח אחד, שהאחד מורה על שאין מבלעדו" (403). "ומאחר שאין מבלעדו, נמצא כי הוא גובר על הכל, עד שאין הכל נחשב אצלו, רק כח אחד" (403).
- **Kingship is oneness.** "וזהו מפני כי המלכות הוא האחדות" (628). When Israel is one, "מלכותו עליהם" (628); "וכאשר נחלקים, ואין כאן אחדות, מלכותו" (628) is not on them.
- **He returns with us.** The verse says He "will return," not "will bring back," "להורות שהדבר הוא כאילו הוא יתברך נגאל עמהם" (589): "to show that it is as if He Himself is redeemed with them."
- **No bad thing comes from Him; comfort comes from inside the trouble.** "כי אין דבר רע יוצא מאתו" (561). "כי הנחמה תהיה מתוך הצרה" (561).
- **He is all, the blow and the saving.** "כדי להראות להם מזה כי הוא יתברך הכל, ומאתו בא להם הפורענות והישועה גם כן" (618).
- **Moshe reached the level with no division.** He climbed all the steps in one: "שהיה בא למדריגה אשר אין שם חלוק כלל" (644).

## 2.1 One power, none beside Him (403)

(Rule only; the battle frame is not used.) Before battle the kohen says "Shema Yisrael." Why? Even the merit of the Shema alone is enough, "לפי שהם דבקים בכח אחד, שהאחד מורה על שאין מבלעדו" (403). "One" does not mean one among others. It means there is none beside Him. "ומאחר שאין מבלעדו, נמצא כי הוא גובר על הכל, עד שאין הכל נחשב אצלו, רק כח אחד" (403). "כמו שהוא יתברך אחד על הכל ומנצח הכל בכח אחדות" (403). Whoever cleaves to the One shares in the power of oneness.

## 2.2 Kingship is oneness (628)

"And He was King in Yeshurun, when the heads of the people gathered, the tribes of Israel together." The Maharal: "וזהו מפני כי המלכות הוא האחדות" (628). This is why the Shema is called accepting His kingship. So when Israel is joined in one bond, "מלכותו עליהם" (628). "וכאשר נחלקים, ואין כאן אחדות, מלכותו" (628), which is oneness, is not on them. (This is the same teaching as Bamidbar 696: "oneness is kingship.") Our unity with each other is where His oneness is seen as kingship.

## 2.3 The altar stands alone for the One alone (349)

Why no tree or house beside the altar? "שהרי ראוי להיות המזבח במיוחד, כדי להורות על מי שנבנה לו המזבח הוא מיוחד" (349). To plant something beside it "הרי הוא משתף יחד העבודה זרה למי שהוא יחיד בעולמו" (349). The place of His service must show that He is alone.

## 2.4 He is all: the blow and the saving (618)

"See now that I, I am He." The Maharal: the verse "where is their god" is spoken "כדי להראות להם מזה כי הוא יתברך הכל, ומאתו בא להם הפורענות והישועה גם כן" (618). There is no second power behind the hard times. The One who struck is the One who saves.

## 2.5 No bad thing comes from Him (561)

Why do the curses in Vayikra end in comfort, and those in Devarim do not? Because His own sufferings are for good: "כי אין דבר רע יוצא מאתו" (561), "כי אם לנקותם מן החטא להטיב להם באחריתם" (561). And "כי הנחמה תהיה מתוך הצרה" (561): comfort grows out of the trouble itself, as the redemption from Egypt came out of the slavery. Where there is a count and an end, "כי דבק בהן הרחמים, שהרי יש בהן קץ וסוף" (561).

## 2.6 He returns with us (589)

"Hashem your G-d will return your captivity." Not "bring back," but "return." For other peoples He says "I will return," since exile breaks the order He set. For Israel the exile touches His very glory: "אבל ישראל, שהגלות שלהם מגיע לעצם כבודו, שהשם יתברך נקרא" (589) "G-d of Israel." So when they go back, "שזה בודאי כאילו נגאל ושב אתם" (589), "להורות שהדבר הוא כאילו הוא יתברך נגאל עמהם" (589). And He cannot part from them: "אחר שאי אפשר לו להבדיל ממך" (579).

## 2.7 The receiver and the Giver (610)

"You weakened the Rock." The Maharal is careful: not Him, but the power that comes from Him. "ודע, כי ענין זה, שכאשר יש מקבל יש נותן" (610). "וכאשר אין מקבל, יסולק כח הנותן" (610). He does not change. What changes is whether there is someone to receive. (This is the same teaching as Devarim 146: the change is only on the side of the receivers.)

## 2.8 The level with no division (644)

Moshe went up from the plains of Moav to the peak, many steps, in one step. Why? "שהיה בא למדריגה אשר אין שם חלוק כלל" (644). Steps and parts belong to what has a body. "אבל משה היה דבק במעלה שאין שם גשם" (644). Where there is no body, there are no steps between. This is the last unity teaching in Gur Aryeh.

## 2.9 Every creature in its own measure (511)

When He made the world, He "מדד ושקל ופלס לכל אחד ואחד קיומו בפני עצמו, שלא יהיה כל אחד נוגע בחבירו כלל אפילו כחוט השערה" (511). "וכן כל הנמצאים בעולם משוערים, והוא השיעור האלקי אשר שיער את הכל" (511). So one who cheats in weights and measures breaks the divine measure, and the enemy comes in past its limit.

- **MH-S73 "None beside Him" (403).** "לפי שהם דבקים בכח אחד, שהאחד מורה על שאין מבלעדו" (403). Inside: I face something much stronger than me. I say "Hashem is One," and I feel that "One" means there is nothing beside Him, so this force too is nothing before Him. How to enter: in a struggle, say the Shema slowly and think: "There is none beside You. I hold to You." It differs from MH-S70 One now, which sees lent powers. This is holding to the One in the middle of a fight.
- **MH-S74 "He returns with me" (589).** "להורות שהדבר הוא כאילו הוא יתברך נגאל עמהם" (589). Inside: when I come back from a far place, He is coming back too. My going out touched His glory, and my return is His return. How to enter: when coming home from a long or hard time away, say: "You went out with me, and You come back with me." It differs from MH-S59 He is with us in exile, which is about the time away. This is the return.
- **MH-S75 "Comfort from inside" (561).** "כי הנחמה תהיה מתוך הצרה" (561). Inside: I do not wait for the trouble to end and something else to begin. I look for the comfort inside the trouble itself, since both come from Him: "ומאתו בא להם הפורענות והישועה גם כן" (618). How to enter: in a hard time, for yourself only, ask: "Where in this is the seed of the good?" It differs from S17 The ruin as a beginning, which sees ruin as a new start. This finds the comfort already present in the trouble.
- **MH-S76 "Whole with Him" (376).** "ואתה מקבל כל דבריו בתמימות" (376), "אלא פירושו שאז תהיה עם הקדוש ברוך הוא ולחלקו" (376). Inside: I do not reach for signs and fortunes. I take His words simply, and so I am with Him, His portion. How to enter: when you want to know the future, stop. Say: "I will be whole with You," and do what is in front of you. It differs from S11 The simple one, which is simplicity of heart. This is simplicity toward the unknown future.
- **MH-S77 "One level, no steps" (644).** "שהיה בא למדריגה אשר אין שם חלוק כלל" (644). Inside: at rare moments the steps fall away, and what I climbed toward is simply here. How to enter: this is not a state one makes; it is given. One can only prepare by cleaving to what has no body, Torah and prayer. It differs from S5 Undivided now, which is inner wholeness. This is the level of Moshe at his end, noted for the map, not as a practice.

## 4.1 Shema in the struggle (`mh_shema_in_struggle`, new)

The practice for MH-S73 (403). When you face something too strong, a fear, an illness, a hard person, say the first verse of the Shema slowly. On "One," think: "There is none beside You." Then act.

## 4.2 Make peace so He is King (`mh_one_for_kingship`, new)

"וזהו מפני כי המלכות הוא האחדות" (628). Practice: before saying the Shema in the morning, think of one person you are apart from. Resolve to make peace with them today. Our oneness is where His kingship rests. See also 4.8.

## 4.3 Turn back the evil thought (`mh_turn_back_thought`, new)

"כי לעולם תמצא כי המחשבה מתהפך על המחשב רע" (395). "לפי שכאשר יחשוב לעשות רע, והוא שלא כדין, הוא עושה רושם בעצמו" (395). "כל החושד בכשרים לוקה בגופו" (395). And "שיותר טוב להיות מקולל, מאשר יהיה מקלל אחרים" (395). Practice: when a thought of harm or suspicion against someone comes, stop it before it grows. It leaves its mark on you first. Better to be cursed than to curse.

## 4.4 Stop the quarrel while it is small (`mh_beyond_the_line`, extended)

"כך הריב גם כן, כי כאשר יתחיל מעט, סוף שיהיה גדול עד שלא תוכל להשקיט אותו" (488). A quarrel is a crack in a dam. Add to the practice from Bamidbar: fix a quarrel at the first drop. And "גדול השלום אפילו בשעת מלחמה צריכין שלום" (408).

## 4.5 Keep the measure (`mh_keep_the_measure`, new)

"והוא השיעור האלקי אשר שיער את הכל" (511). Practice: in money and work, keep exact measures: the true weight, the full hour, the fair price. Each thing has its measure from Him. Keeping it keeps the world in its order.

## 4.6 Give with love, and praise (`mh_gift_as_offering`, extended)

"שנתתי המעשרות לשם הקדוש ברוך הוא, ונתתי אותם באהבה, ושבחתי עליו הקדוש ברוך הוא" (540). Add to the practice: when you give tzedakah or maaser, give it for His Name, give it with love, and thank Him for being able to give.

## 4.7 Name the good (`mh_name_the_good`, new)

The bikkurim speech is so that "שאינך כפוי טובה" (522). "והוא טובה אצלי, ואני מודה עליו" (522). And "מזכיר חסדי המקום" (526). Practice: when you receive something good, say out loud what it is and that it came from Him. Do not let it pass as nothing.

## 4.8 Make yourself glad (`mh_make_yourself_glad`, new)

"כי מן רוב כל שהיה לכם היה לכם לעבוד בשמחה" (565). And joy is something one brings on oneself: "כי דרך האדם להביא עליו השמחה, ומשמח עצמו" (570). Practice: before a mitzvah, stop and think of three good things you have. Let them make you glad. Then do it with that joy. Guard: this is never with drink.

## 4.9 Comfort from inside, for oneself (`mh_return_today`, extended)

"כי אין דבר רע יוצא מאתו" (561). "כי הנחמה תהיה מתוך הצרה" (561). Add to the practice: in a hard time, ask where the comfort is already growing inside it. Guard: this is for oneself only. It is never said to another person about their pain.

## 4.10 Be whole with Him (`mh_whole_with_him`, new)

The practice for MH-S76 (376). When you worry about what will be, do not seek signs. Say: "I will be whole with You." Do the next right thing. Trust that "תשועתך תלויה בו יתברך" (643): your salvation hangs on Him.

## 4.11 Leave as you came (`mh_leave_as_you_came`, new)

"שתהא יציאתך מן העולם כמו ביאתך לעולם בלא חטא" (557). Practice: at night, before sleep, return from anything you did wrong today, so that the day ends as clean as it began.

## 5. The big human questions

**What does "G-d is One" mean?** There is none beside Him (403). He is all, the blow and the saving (618). Oneness is kingship (628).

**Where is G-d's kingship seen?** Where we are one with each other (628).

**Does anything bad come from G-d?** No. What He sends is to cleanse and to do good at the end (561).

**Where is the comfort?** Inside the trouble itself (561).

**Where is G-d when I come home from exile?** Coming home with me (589). He cannot part from me (579).

**Why does G-d's flow seem to stop?** Not because He changed. Because there was no one to receive (610).

**What happens when I think harm of someone?** The thought turns back and leaves its mark on me first (395).

**Why stop a small quarrel?** It is a crack in a dam (488).

**Why keep exact measures?** Each thing has its measure from Him (511).

**How should I face the future?** Whole with Him, not seeking signs (376).

**What is the soul, under the sin?** Pure in itself; the flaw is only from its joining to the body (490).

**What is the Torah?** Black fire on white fire: good and pleasant to do, and true when you think it through (627).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Tzimtzum.** "When there is a receiver there is a giver; when there is none, the giver's power is held back" (610) is close to the Chabad teaching that the light is always there, and what changes is the vessel (Tanya ch. 35-36; Iggeret HaKodesh 20). Chabad calls the hiding tzimtzum and says it is only from our side. The Maharal says the same of the "weakened Rock": not Him, only the flow.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "The level where there is no division" and "no body" (644) is the Rambam's first principle: G-d has no body and no parts (Yesodei HaTorah 1:7). The Maharal applies it to the highest human level: Moshe at his death cleaved to what has no body, so for him there were no steps.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "He is all, and from Him came both the punishment and the saving" (618) is the first half. "One power, and none beside Him, so all counts as nothing" (403) is close to Shaar HaYichud ch. 3 and 6: all is as nothing before Him.
- **Kingship is oneness** (628) is a teaching Chabad develops at length: "there is no king without a people," and the Shema is the acceptance of His kingship (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; Tanya ch. 41 on kabbalat ol). The Maharal adds that Israel's own unity is where His kingship rests, which fits Tanya ch. 32 on love of every Jew as the gate to His service.
- **He returns with us** (589) is the verse Chabad reads with the Talmud: the Shechina is in exile with Israel and returns with them (Megillah 29a; Tanya ch. 17; Iggeret HaKodesh 4 and 25).
- **No bad thing comes from Him** (561) fits Tanya ch. 26 and Iggeret HaKodesh 11: all is good, even what looks bad is hidden good. The Maharal's "comfort from inside the trouble" matches the Chabad teaching that the descent is for the sake of the ascent.
- **The soul is pure in itself** (490) fits the prayer "the soul You gave me is pure" and Tanya ch. 2 and Iggeret HaTeshuva ch. 4: the soul's root is never touched by sin.
