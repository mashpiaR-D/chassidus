**The claim of this cluster.** Tzimtzum, light and the chain of worlds are the part of Chassidus where the one idea is most exposed to being misread as its opposite. They seem to be a map of God's distance from the world: a withdrawal, a void, a ladder of worlds, screens. The sources read every element of that map as the unity itself, seen from below. The contraction hides only toward the creatures (SH 6:8). The trace in the void holds all of Him in hint (Shaar HaYichud 13:55). The line reaches every creature (IH 20:21). The vessel is His own power to limit, so that 'He and His vessels are one' (Shaar HaYichud 29:32). The whole chain exists for the lowest world (Tanya 36:5). This is face F03 (hiding for revelation) together with F05 (the Essence holds limit as well as the limitless) and F02 (nothing before Him, a something to itself).

**Where the cluster is thin.** The technical Lurianic stages (Adam Kadmon, the reshimu, the screens) are tied to the unity by their role, not in every passage, and outside Chabad and Strashelye they are rarely developed. Angels serve mostly as a foil. Breslov reads the void in the opposite direction, from unity to faith over paradox. These points are kept in each entry.

## S014 · Tzimtzum

**צמצום** · search: צמצום · הצמצום · צמצום הראשון · צמצום כפשוטו

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F02 The higher and the lower unity; F05 The Essence beyond both; F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity (primary first). **Grammar:** G03, G02, G08.

> Tzimtzum is the unity of God as a concealment He works on His own light, so that finite worlds can exist and receive Him; it hides only toward the creatures, never before Him.

1. The light that comes from Him resembles the luminary and is without end. Had the worlds come from it without contraction, this finite world could not exist at all (Tanya 48:1; Derekh Mitzvotekha, Emunah 6). *(stated)*
2. So He contracted. The contraction is His gevurah, the Name Elokim. It hides the vitality so that the creature seems a thing in itself, and that power to hide is His omnipotence, not an absence (SH 4:5-6, 6:1). *(stated)*
3. The contraction took place in the light, not in the Essence. The withdrawn light is 'included in the luminary', so nothing changes in Him (Tzemach Tzedek, DM Emunah 6; Strashelye, First Gate 22:2). *(stated)*
4. Therefore the contraction and hiding exist only for the lower beings. Before Him all is as nothing, like sunlight in the sun (SH 6:8). To read the tzimtzum literally is an error (SH 7:15), 'a grave offense' (Strashelye 21:6). *(stated)*
5. The hiding is itself kindness and exists for revelation. The Maggid calls the love the contraction (MDL 33). SH 6:2 calls it gevurah included in chesed. The Rashab, the Rayatz and the Rebbe say its intent is revelation (Hayom Yom 27 Adar II; Rayatz 5689; Basi LeGani 5737). *(stated)*
6. The first tzimtzum is a complete removal, not a dimming. Only a clean break reveals the power of limit (the reshimu, root of vessels), and through it even the limitless can enter limit (the Rebbe, Basi LeGani 5737:18-21). *(stated)*
7. In the person: 'as water reflects a face', just as He set His great light aside out of love for lower man, so a person should set aside all he has in order to cleave to Him (Tanya 49:5). That tzimtzum is thus the pattern of bitul (F08) is how the Rebbe reads it in Basi LeGani 5737:18; as a general rule for the subject it is our reading. *(reading)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 4, 6-7; Tanya 48-49; IH 20. The tzimtzum is not literal (SH 7:15-16). It is gevurah included in chesed (SH 6:2) and exists only toward the lower beings (6:8). Its purpose is the physical human body, and making light out of darkness (Tanya 49:4). The person mirrors it by setting everything aside (49:5). The mechanics ('how and what') are left unexplained (48:3).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7, 12-15. Understanding the first tzimtzum and the reshimu in detail 'is the essence of the true unity in the Essence of Or Ein Sof', until a person can direct his will with mesirus nefesh to the Essence alone (12:3-5). The contraction is 'only a limitation within the Essence so that all be drawn into revelation' (13:74).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Emunah 6. The tzimtzum happened in the light, which is called 'Ein Sof' because it resembles the luminary. It did not happen in the Essence. The withdrawn light is included in the luminary. His image is the closed eye: sight does not vanish when the lid shuts but is gathered into the soul.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5633 (VaPharaoh Hikriv 130). A reshimu remains 'because a holy thing is never wholly uprooted'. Without the prior tzimtzum, the worlds could not stand even after the line is drawn back into them.
- **Rashab.** In the talk cited in Hayom Yom 27 Adar II (via the Rayatz): 'the first tzimtzum's intent is revelation', and the way to it is self-nullification, 'not what the intellect says'. In Hemshech Ayin Beis (opening maamar), the removal of light has to come first for worlds to exist.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (BaSukkos Teshvu 51): the first tzimtzum works by removal, the later ones by diminution, 'all of them for revelation'. Yud Kislev 5692: the contraction is 'the foundation and essence of kindness'. His mashal is the teacher who must first silence his own mind before he can teach. The same power turns Elokim into 'Elokeinu'.
- **the Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5737: the first tzimtzum is a complete removal whose intent is revelation, above all so that vessels can come to be; he cites the Maggid's teaching that the tzimtzum is for Israel, like a father who narrows his mind for his young son (5737:23). In a letter (Likkutei Sichos 35, Bereshis additions 234:4, read in context, line 21228) he writes that even the view that the tzimtzum is literal, held because 'He is omnipotent', does not contradict the command to know God and serve with ChaBaD. It is no less incomprehensible than continuous creation from nothing. This softens the polemic of SH 7 without retracting it; that it softens it is our reading.

- **Baal Shem Tov.** Tzava'at HaRivash 137:3 speaks spatially ('cleared a place in Himself'), as a meditation for the state of katnut. Whether this is meant literally is not stated. Mekor Mayim Chayim (1938, citing Magen Avos) reads the Besht as holding that tzimtzum is concealment and so holds 'only toward the creatures'. That is a later reading.
- **Maggid.** The love is itself the contraction; without it Godliness could not be revealed in the worlds (MDL 33:2). In 110:1 the tzimtzum is 'touching and not touching' at every moment.
- **Strashelye.** Takes the hardest line: the literal reading is 'a grave offense', and the tzimtzum appears 'only from our side' (Sha'arei HaYichud I 21:6, 22:2).
- **Breslov.** Likutei Moharan 64 holds the void as two opposites: God is absent there, and yet truly present. It cannot be understood until the future. The questions born of it have no answer, and only faith crosses them. This is a real difference. For the Mitteler Rebbe, contemplating the tzimtzum leads to the true unity. For R. Nachman, it is the place where contemplation must stop.
- **Berditchev.** Kedushat Levi, Re'eh 8: all giving is by limit and contraction. The receiver's side is to make himself 'as nothing' (see S019).

> — The Alter Rebbe names the literal reading (He removed His Essence from this world) as the error of 'some who are wise in their own eyes'. *[stated]*

> — The Tzemach Tzedek: the contraction happened in the light, not in the Essence; the withdrawn light is simply included in the luminary, so nothing changes in Him. *[stated]*

> **Tzava'at HaRivash 137:3** (Baal Shem Tov (and Maggid circle), Tzava'at HaRivash; line 286)

> — Kept disagreement: the Besht's circle speaks spatially, 'He cleared a place in Himself', as a meditation of katnut; that this wording conflicts with SH 7 is our reading. *[reading]*

> — Breslov: the void must be spoken of as two opposites, He is absent and yet truly present, and cannot be grasped until the future. The unity is affirmed, but as paradox, not as comprehension. *[stated]*

> — The Rebbe: the first tzimtzum is a complete removal of the light (not a dimming), and its intent is revelation. *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** None. The tie is stated in so many words by the Alter Rebbe, the Mitteler Rebbe, the Tzemach Tzedek, the Maggid and Strashelye. The live disagreement (literal or not; comprehension or faith) is kept above.

## S015 · Line and impression

**קו ורשימו** · search: קו אור · הקו והחוט · רשימו · מקום פנוי · חלל

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F05 The Essence beyond both; F04 Filling and surrounding; F01 The world said into being now (primary first). **Grammar:** G04, G03, G05.

> The line and the impression are the unity of God as the trace He leaves inside His own concealment: the reshimu holds all of Him in hint within the 'empty place', and the line draws Him, measured, into every world down to the end of Asiyah.

1. After the first tzimtzum a remnant (reshimu) stayed in the void, and from it the line (kav) was drawn, as surrounding and inner light (Shaar HaYichud 7:17-18; DM Emunah 6). This is received Lurianic doctrine. *(stated)*
2. The reshimu is like writing that has been erased, a mere trace (DM). Yet it 'contains everything in concealment', and it is what lets everything come into revelation through the line down to the end of Asiyah (Shaar HaYichud 13:54-55). *(stated)*
3. So the whole of it is 'only a limitation within the Essence so that all be drawn into revelation' (13:74). Void, trace and line are the Essence limiting itself; nothing else enters. *(stated)*
4. The line ends at the feet of Adam Kadmon in Malchus of Asiyah, and shines as 'a radiance of a radiance of a radiance' in every creature (IH 20:21; DM Achdut 2). This is F01, the word standing in each thing. *(stated)*
5. The reshimu is the root of vessels and limit, the line the root of lights (Shaar HaYichud 29:29-30; DM Emunah 6; the Rebbe 5737). Subjects S017 and S019 unfold this pair. *(stated)*
6. Contemplating this in detail brings one 'to the true unity', binding the soul to the life of all life (Shaar HaYichud 13:76-77). *(stated)*
7. From below, the void is where the question 'where is He?' lives. R. Nachman: He surrounds even the void and is truly there, but it is crossed by faith alone (LM 64:2). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 20:8 and 20:21: the line clothed in the souls of Atzilus, 'like the first tzimtzum making a void', and its light reaching every creature. The Alter Rebbe mostly leaves the kav/reshimu mechanics to 'those who know' (SH 9:5-6).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** The fullest treatment (Shaar HaYichud 7, 11-17). The reshimu is the craftsman's sketch, from which alone he can build the whole building (13:61). The point holds two opposites, concealment and revelation (13:69-70). The line is a vav whose head is a yud (13:72). He states the purpose: to come to the true unity (13:77).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Emunah 6: the erased-writing image; vessels come from the reshimu, lights from the line. DM Achdut 2: the line passes through the vessels of Atzilus and breaks through the screen into every creature (memale).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5633: the trace remains 'because a holy thing is never wholly uprooted'.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 127:3: the line is 'a line made of points'. The sefirot stay simple in it until they are clothed in vessels.
- **Rayatz.** Yud Kislev 5692: the line is light contracted to the measure of the worlds, and it measures them. So 'the tzimtzum is the cause of revelation', though in itself it is concealment.
- **the Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5737: only the complete removal reveals the reshimu, 'the root of vessels', which then receive the light of the line.

- **Breslov.** Likutei Moharan 64: the void (chalal ha-panui) stands between memale and sovev. He surrounds even it, and 'truly there is Godliness there', but no inquiry finds Him there. It is reached by faith, not contemplation.
- **Baal Shem Tov (later reading).** Mekor Mayim Chayim, Vaetchanan 6 (citing Magen Avos): Moshe 'placed Him in the void of the world' ('ein od'), while Rachav left the void. See spine anchor F02n.
- **Maggid and others.** In my search I found no technical treatment of kav and reshimu in the Maggid, Keter Shem Tov or the Polish masters. They use tzimtzum, but not this pair.

## S016 · Light and luminary

**אור ומאור** · search: אור ומאור · המאור · זיו והארה · אור השמש

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity; F05 The Essence beyond both; F04 Filling and surrounding (primary first). **Grammar:** G06, G02.

> Light and luminary are the unity of God as the ray that has no existence apart from its source: the sefirot, and all that is emanated, are one with Him as sunlight is one with the sun.

1. Because Torah speaks in human language, the sages were permitted to call the sefirot 'lights', so that we might grasp a little of how He is one with His attributes (SH 10:1). *(stated)*
2. In the sun, the ray is one substance with the body of the luminary and has no existence of its own (SH 10:1). *(stated)*
3. In the same way, and more, His will, wisdom and attributes in Atzilus are one with His Essence (SH 10:2). The effect is nullified in its cause 'as the ray of the sun in the sun' (IH 20:3). *(stated)*
4. Before Him the whole world is nullified in the same way, 'as sunlight in the sun' (SH 6:8). This is F02, the higher unity. *(stated)*
5. The analogy fails, and the sources say where. It holds 'not really in this way, but in a way far beyond our grasp' (SH 10:2). And above, light does not issue by necessity as a ray does; it is willed (Rashab, HAB 676:1). This is G06. *(stated)*
6. The light 'is like the luminary' and so is infinite (DM Emunah 6), 'His very Essence' (IH 20:11). That is why, clothed in vessels, it can create yesh. Kept tension (F05): the Maharash calls creation 'only a mere radiance' (spine F05j). *(stated)*
7. What the light reveals is 'there is none besides You', since there is no light without a luminary. What it cannot reveal, that nothing compares to Him ('ein aroch'), is shown only by the creation of the yesh (HAB 676:2). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 6:8, 10:1-3; IH 20:3, 20:11. The mashal is used and then limited.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Emunah 5-6: the light is called 'Ein Sof' (without end) because it resembles the luminary, while the Essence is also without beginning. The sun does not change when more or less of its ray is drawn out.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631 (Ki Yesharim): Elokim is the shield and sheath of the sun. Without it the worlds would be nullified 'as a ray in the sun'. The Maharash uses the phrase 'zeiv ha-shemesh' on 47 lines of this edition (find_he count).
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 365-366: light and luminary are one (devekus, not a binding of two), and that is the model for how Torah and God are one. At 676 the light shows 'there is none besides You' but not 'nothing compares to You', and it is willed, not necessary.
- **Mitteler Rebbe, Rayatz, the Rebbe.** The sun mashal appears in their works (e.g. Rayatz Sefer HaMaamarim, 13 lines; Toras Menachem, 18). I did not read a passage from them on this subject closely enough to cite it.

- **Baal Shem Tov.** The Besht's circle uses a different image for the same claim: the snail whose garment is of itself (Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3; see S026).
- **Homel.** Chanah Ariel uses the sun-ray mashal (13 lines), in the Chabad manner.
- **Other schools.** Outside the Chabad circle I found no systematic use of the light-and-luminary mashal as an account of the unity.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 10:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 834)

## S017 · Lights and vessels

**אורות וכלים** · search: אורות וכלים · אור וכלי · התלבשות האור · כלים

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both; F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F09 The unity of opposites; F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual (primary first). **Grammar:** G10, G03, G04.

> Lights and vessels are the unity of God as limit held within the limitless: the vessel is His own power to conceal and measure, and where it is wholly nullified to the light, 'He and His vessels are one'.

1. The contraction and hiding of the vitality is called 'vessels'; the vitality itself is called 'light' (SH 4:8). The vessel is a function of His concealing power, not a second thing. *(stated)*
2. The reshimu is the root of the vessels; the line is the root of the lights (DM Emunah 6; Shaar HaYichud 29:29-30; the Rebbe 5737). *(stated)*
3. The power of limit belongs to the Omnipotent. The vessels' limit comes from Ein Sof itself, 'and that is why He and His vessels are one' (Shaar HaYichud 29:28-32). This is F05 and F09. *(stated)*
4. So the vessels of Atzilus are Godliness that can create yesh from nothing, like Ein Sof. They are not merely a chain of cause and effect (IH 20:1-2). *(stated)*
5. Vessels divide. Because creation passes through vessels, creatures are many, divided and finite (IH 20:24). Only when clothed in vessels do the sefirot become 'something' (Rashab, HAB 127:3). *(stated)*
6. What makes a vessel one with its light is bitul: tikkun-vessels are 'wholly nullified' to Or Ein Sof, while a broken vessel becomes a thing in its own right (LT Matot 2:5). This leads into S022 and F08. *(stated)*
7. Mitzvot are vessels. Measured as they are, through them the limitless is drawn into limit (Tanya 38:12 after Etz Chaim; Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5640). This is F10. *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 4:8; IH 20:1-2, 20:12, 20:24; LT Matot 2. Vessels are concealment, divide the one light into many creatures, and are Godliness where they are nullified.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 29:26-32: the limiting power is the Omnipotent's own, so the vessels of Atzilus, though they truly limit, are one with Him.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Emunah 6-7: vessels come from the reshimu and lights from the line. The sefirot are divine attributes yet 'not His Essence', because they were emanated through tzimtzum.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640: mitzvot are 'wide vessels' for the supernal 'me'od'; the question how a limit can hold the limitless is answered by tohu and tikkun.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 127-128: the sefirot are simple in the line and become 'something' in the vessels. HAB 876:2: vessels exist in every world, and their origin is in Adam Kadmon.
- **the Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5737: the first tzimtzum's complete removal is chiefly for the sake of the vessels.
- **Rayatz.** Yud Kislev 5692: the line measures 'the lights and vessels in each world'. I found no fuller passage.

- **Strashelye.** Sha'arei HaYichud I 21:6: 'body' and 'vessels' of Adam Kadmon are borrowed names given 'from the side of the worlds'. This is a stronger nominalism than Tanya's (our reading of the difference).
- **Izhbitz.** Beit Yaakov, Miketz 10:3: the worlds of tohu took more light than their vessel could hold (see S022).
- **Other schools.** Outside the Chabad circle the technical pair appears mostly in Lurianic citation. I found no independent development.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:8** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 783)
> והנה, בחינת הצמצום והסתר החיות – נקרא בשם ״כלים״, והחיות עצמו – נקרא בשם ״אור״
> — The Alter Rebbe's definition: the contraction and hiding of vitality is called 'vessels', the vitality 'light'. The vessel is a function of His concealing power. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:1-2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1252)
> איך ה״איןסוף״ חד עם ״גרמוהי״ הן הכלים, הרי הכלים הן בבחינת גבול ותכלית … אמנם, הכוונה היא, לומר שהן אלקות לברוא יש מאין כמו ה״איןסוף״
> — The problem and its answer: how can the limitless be one with limited vessels? They are Godliness, able to create yesh from nothing like Ein Sof. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:24** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1274)
> ומאחר שהבריאה היא עלידי הכלים, לזאת הם הנבראים בבחינת ריבוי והתחלקות וגבול ותכלית
> — Why the world is many: because creation passes through vessels, creatures are multiple, divided and finite. *[stated]*

> **The Gate of Unity 29:28-32** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 1630)
> כי כל יכול יש בו כח המגביל גם כן … והיינו דאיהו וגרמוהי חד, מכח דאין סוף עצמו
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: the Omnipotent has the power to limit too; the vessels' limit is from Ein Sof itself, and that is why 'He and His vessels are one'. *[stated]*

> **Likkutei Torah, Matot 2:5** (Alter Rebbe, Likkutei Torah; line 2600)
> שגם הכלים הם אלקות ממש כי הם בבחי׳ ביטול לגמרי ממש לגבי האור א״ס המלובש בהן
> — The Alter Rebbe: in the ten sefirot of tikkun the vessels are Godliness because they are wholly nullified to the Or Ein Sof within them (bitul is what makes a vessel one with its light). *[stated]*

> **Hemshech Ayin Beis, second night of Rosh Hashanah 5673, 127:3** (Rebbe Rashab, Hemshech Ayin Beis; line 512)
> רק כאשר מתלבשים בהכלים דע"ס דאצי' ה"ה נעשים בבחי' מציאות דבר מה כו', שהרי הכלים הם בבחי' מהות ומציאות דבר
> — The Rashab: the sefirot are simple in the line; only clothed in vessels do they become 'something', divided and ranked. *[stated]*

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Basi LeGani 5737:19** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1448)
> הרשימו [שהוא שרש הכלים] היא בגילוי … ועי"ז נעשה התהוות הכלים ומקבלים בתוכם את אור הקו
> — The Rebbe: the complete removal of the first tzimtzum first reveals limit, the reshimu, root of the vessels; then vessels come to be and receive the light of the line. *[stated]*

> **Toras Shmuel 5640, LeChol Tachlah Ra'isi Ketz 874:1** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel; line 13261)
> מפני שמצות הם כלים רחבים למאוד העליון … איך אפשר להיות שהם כלים לבלי גבול
> — The Maharash: mitzvot, measured as they are, are 'wide vessels' for the limitless; he asks how that is possible (answered by tohu-lights in tikkun-vessels). *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** Stated in Chabad. The pair is technical, and outside Chabad and Strashelye its tie to the unity is not drawn.

## S018 · Inner and surrounding light

**אור פנימי ואור מקיף** · search: אור פנימי · אור מקיף · אור הפנימי · אור המקיף · מקיפים

**Faces:** F04 Filling and surrounding; F02 The higher and the lower unity; F05 The Essence beyond both (primary first). **Grammar:** G06, G04, G02.

> Inner and surrounding light are the unity of God as two relations of the one light to each thing: measured and grasped within it, and present in it equally but not revealed.

1. The vitality clothed inside each creature, contracted to its measure, is 'filling', or inner light (SH 7:20). *(stated)*
2. The light not so contracted, whose influence is not revealed inside the creature, is 'surrounding'. This is not 'above' in place (Tanya 48:4-6). *(stated)*
3. So all of Or Ein Sof is called surrounding the physical earth, 'although it is truly within it' (48:7). *(stated)*
4. The surrounding light holds all four worlds equally; there is no place empty of Him even in physical earth (IH 20:22). Through the vessels it gives them the power to create yesh (20:23). *(stated)*
5. The division into higher and lower comes from the filling light. But 'the main vitality is from the surrounding light' (Tzemach Tzedek, DM Achdut 2). *(stated)*
6. The whole line, head to foot, is one detail within the encompassing circle, and the circle is one detail beside the Essence (Shaar HaYichud 7:35-38). This is G04 and F05. *(stated)*
7. The soul-analogy: will is surrounding light and acts in foot as in head; the other faculties are inner lights in their vessels (Rashab, HAB 3:3, 128:1). This is G06. *(stated)*
8. Breslov's different structure: the void lies between memale and sovev. He surrounds the void, but only faith crosses it (LM 64:2). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 48; SH 7:17-20; IH 20:22-23. IH 23:5: the Shechinah resting on ten Jews is a surrounding light, too great for a finite soul to hold inside.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:18 and 7:35-38. At 12:9-12, 'the heavens' is the general surrounding light after the tzimtzum, and 'I desire nothing on earth besides You' refers to the line, the general filling light.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Achdut 2: memale makes levels; sovev shines on all equally and is the main vitality. It alone gives matter its somethingness (spine F04d).
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis opens with will as surrounding light (3:3). At 127-128 the surrounding light is 'a revelation of the Essence resembling the Essence', equal in all worlds; the great circle before the line gives the vessels their bitul. Kuntres HaTefillah (spine F04e): filling light can be grasped even by the natural mind.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (BaSukkos Teshvu 50:3): the filling light, inner light, comes 'through the break of the tzimtzum'.
- **Maharash, the Rebbe.** Not checked for this subject.

- **Breslov.** LM 64:2: the void surrounds the world and He surrounds the void. There must be 'a difference between the filling and the surrounding', or all would be one. Chabad has no such intervening void; its equality of sovev is precisely that nothing intervenes.
- **Homel.** Chanah Ariel, Tazria 3:2: Or Ein Sof fills all equally, and the change is only from the receiving worlds.
- **Other schools.** The spine counts the pair outside Chabad at about a ninth of the Chabad rate.

> אלא מקיף עליהם מלמעלה, ונקרא – ״סובב כל עלמין״ … ואין הפירוש סובב ומקיף מלמעלה בבחינת מקום חס ושלום
> — Surrounding light is the light not clothed in revelation; it is not spatially above. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:7** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 681)
> וכל אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא נקרא סובב עליה, אף שהוא בתוכה ממש
> — All of Or Ein Sof is called 'surrounding' the physical earth although it is truly within it. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:22** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1272)
> וסובב כל עלמין בשוה … ו״לית אתר פנוי מיניה״ אף בארץ הלזו הגשמית, רק, שהוא בבחינת מקיף וסובב
> — The Essence of Or Ein Sof is not in place, surrounds all worlds equally, and no place is empty of Him even in the physical earth. *[stated]*

> — The Mitteler Rebbe: the whole line joined head to foot is one detail within the encompassing circle, and the circle itself is one detail beside the Essence (G04, F05). *[stated]*

> **Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 2** (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; line 120)

> — The Tzemach Tzedek: division into higher and lower comes from the filling light; but the main vitality, the very somethingness of things, is from the surrounding light. *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** None in Chabad. Outside Chabad the pair is rarer, and Breslov restructures it.

## S019 · Limit and limitlessness

**גבול ובלי גבול** · search: גבול ובלי גבול · בלי גבול · בל"ג · כח הגבול

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both; F09 The unity of opposites; F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation (primary first). **Grammar:** G10, G05.

> Limit and limitlessness are the unity of God as the Essence's perfection that holds both: as He has power in the limitless, so He has power in the limited.

1. Creatures are limited ('from earth to heaven five hundred years'), and the light without contraction is limitless (SH 7:20; Tanya 48:1-3). *(stated)*
2. Between them 'there is no ratio at all'; the limited is as nothing beside the limitless (Tanya 48:3). *(stated)*
3. Yet the power of limit comes precisely from the Essence of Or Ein Sof, and the two lines are equal in infinity (Shaar HaYichud 15:53-58, read in context). *(stated)*
4. 'As He has power in the limitless, so He has power in the limited.' To deny Him the limited would lessen His perfection, and the power of limit within the Essence is itself limitless (Avodat HaKodesh, cited by the Rebbe, Basi LeGani 5737:21). This is F05. *(stated)*
5. So the limitless itself can come into limit. Once the tzimtzum reveals the power of limit, it limits even the limitless Or Ein Sof, and the vessels receive it (ibid.). *(stated)*
6. Unity and multiplicity are not opposites in the Essence (Rashab, HAB 675:4). This is F09. *(stated)*
7. In service: mitzvot are measured, yet they are vessels for the limitless (Maharash 5640). The receiver's measure is his bitul (Kedushat Levi, Re'eh 8). That the second is the human side of this unity is our reading. *(reading)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 48:1-3; IH 20:12: a finite yesh made by an infinite power, through Or Ein Sof clothed in the vessels of Atzilus. IH 10: tzedakah, 'without limit or measure', draws the higher kindness where sacrifices (measured) could not.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 15:53-58 and 29:26-32: two equal lines in Ein Sof, the expanding and the limiting; the Omnipotent has the power to limit too.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Emunah 6: the tzimtzum leaves only a tiny radiance, 'as nothing' beside the limitless, 'as limit against the limitless'.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640: a limit cannot be a vessel for the limitless, which is why tzimtzum was needed; yet mitzvot draw tohu-lights into tikkun-vessels.
- **Rashab.** HAB 675-676: 'the greatness of Ein Sof' is seen precisely in the power that creates the yesh, not in the light; unity and multiplicity are not opposites in the Essence.
- **the Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5737:21 (Avodat HaKodesh): power in the limited is part of the Essence's perfection. This is the anchor of F05 in the spine (F05d).

- **Homel.** Chanah Ariel, V'Zot HaBerachah 1:2: wherever the light spreads, even into measure and form after many contractions, it is Or Ein Sof in itself.
- **Berditchev.** Kedushat Levi, Re'eh 8: all giving is through limit and contraction, and the receiver's gevurah is making himself 'as nothing'. The limit is read in the giving and the receiving, not in the Essence.
- **Other schools.** The formula 'power in the limited' is Chabad's. I did not find it in the Polish or Galician masters.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:3** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 677)
> בכדי שיהיו בבחינת גבול ותכלית, שהיא הארה מועטת מאד וממש כלא חשיבי לגבי בחינת הארה בלי גבול ותכלית, ואין ביניהם ערך ויחס כלל
> — Limit is a tiny radiance, as nothing beside the limitless; there is no ratio between them. *[stated]*

> **The Gate of Unity 15:53** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 887)
> וכן מעצמות אור אין סוף דוקא בא כח הגבול והמדה אשר בבחינת רשימו שבקו
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: the power of limit and measure comes precisely from the Essence of Or Ein Sof; the two lines (extending and limiting) are equal in infinity. *[stated]*

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Basi LeGani 5737:21** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1450)
> דגם כח הגבול הכלול בעצמותו הוא בלי גבולי ואינו אלא משלימות עצמותו … אלא כשם שיש לו כח בבלי גבול כך יש לו כח בגבול
> — The Rebbe, citing Avodat HaKodesh: as He has power in the limitless, so He has power in the limited; the power of limit in the Essence is itself limitless. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:12** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1262)
> וכדי שיהיה היש הזה הנברא בכח האיןסוף בעל גבול ומדה – נתלבש אוראיןסוף בכלים די׳ ספירות דאצילות
> — To make a finite yesh by an infinite power, Or Ein Sof clothed itself in the vessels of Atzilus. *[stated]*

> **Hemshech Ayin Beis, second night of Sukkot 5670, 675:4** (Rebbe Rashab, Hemshech Ayin Beis; line 2708)
> וזה מגלה לנו איך שהאחדות והריבוי אינם הפכים בבחי' העצמות
> — The Rashab: that the utterly simple Havayah creates the many shows that unity and multiplicity are not opposites in the Essence (F09). *[stated]*

> **Toras Shmuel 5640, LeChol Tachlah Ra'isi Ketz 875:1** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel; line 13263)
> וע"י מצות ממשיכים אורות דתוהו בכלים דתיקון שזהו מאד דתיקון
> — The Maharash: through mitzvot, measured vessels, the unlimited lights of tohu are drawn into vessels of tikkun. *[stated]*

> **Chanah Ariel, V'Zot HaBerachah 1:2** (R. Yitzchak Eizik of Homel, Chanah Ariel; line 381)
> עד שבכל מקום שנתפשט שם הוא בבחינתו בחי' אא"ס אחר ריבוי הצמצומים להתפשטותו הנמצא במדה וגבול ותמונה
> — Homel: wherever the light spreads, even after many contractions into measure, limit and form, it is in itself Or Ein Sof; its infinity does not end. *[stated]*

> **Kedushat Levi, Re'eh 8** (R. Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev, Kedushat Levi; line 978)
> כי הנה ידוע בענין השפעת המשפיע למקבל שהוא על ידי בחינת גבול וצמצום … היינו מה שמשים עצמו כשירים בבחינת ביטול כאין ואפס
> — Berditchev: all giving is by limit and contraction, and the receiver's gevurah is to make himself as nothing. That this is the limit in His giving answered by bitul is our reading of its tie to the unity. *[reading]*

**Thin or forced?** None for Chabad. For Berditchev the tie to the unity is our reading.

## S020 · Adam Kadmon

**אדם קדמון** · search: אדם קדמון · עיגולים ויושר · עיגולים

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both; F04 Filling and surrounding; F06 The soul as part of the One (primary first). **Grammar:** G05, G04.

> Adam Kadmon is the unity of God as the first will for worlds, still Ein Sof, whose 'feet' reach the bottom of Asiyah so that the end of all is embedded in its beginning.

1. After the tzimtzum, Malchus of Ein Sof becomes Keter of Adam Kadmon, and so on down to Malchus of Malchus of Asiyah. 'The end of all is embedded in the beginning of all' (Shaar HaYichud 7:20-25). *(stated)*
2. Adam Kadmon is 'still in the aspect of Ein Sof' (Rashab, HAB 128:2). Strashelye: the Ari spoke only from Malchus of Adam Kadmon down, to show how the levels are united in His Essence, and 'body' and 'vessels' of Adam Kadmon are borrowed names (Sha'arei HaYichud I 21:6). *(stated)*
3. The 'straight' of Adam Kadmon ends at the bottom of Asiyah. Beneath its feet shines the surrounding Or Ein Sof, with only Adam Kadmon's circles between, and the line at its feet shines back upward as returning light (IH 20:26-28). *(stated)*
4. That is why the lowest element, earth, shows the power to make yesh from nothing 'in tremendous revelation', more than the heavenly hosts (IH 20:25). This is G05; its link to the dwelling below (F07) is our reading. *(stated)*
5. The purpose of Adam Kadmon's tzimtzum, as of all the contractions, is to clothe the light in vessels. 'We have no business with hidden things', only faith that He and His attributes are one (SH 9:6-7). *(stated)*
6. Noam Elimelech, via Piaseczno: the world called 'Klal Yisrael', always whole, is called Adam Kadmon. Its generality is not parts joined together but 'the unity before it branched into parts' (Derekh HaMelekh). That this matches the Rebbe's yachid/echad (Inyanah 5:5) is our reading. *(reading)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 20:21-28 (the feet of Adam Kadmon and the power of the earth); SH 9:6-7 (the tzimtzum of Adam Kadmon; the limit of inquiry).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:20-26, 8:21, 12:8: Adam Kadmon as the first link from Malchus of Ein Sof; 'the days of old'. The term appears 74 times in this work.
- **Rashab.** HAB 128:2: Adam Kadmon is still Ein Sof, so each world needs its own Keter (particular will). HAB 876:2: vessels begin in Adam Kadmon.
- **Tzemach Tzedek, Maharash, the Rebbe.** The term appears (Or HaTorah 5; Toras Shmuel 11; Toras Menachem 11) but I did not read those passages.

- **Strashelye.** Sha'arei HaYichud I 21:4-6: from Malchus of Adam Kadmon upward it is forbidden to speak at all; all naming of Adam Kadmon is borrowed, from the worlds' side.
- **Noam Elimelech / Piaseczno.** Derekh HaMelekh, Shabbat Teshuvah 1:9-11: Adam Kadmon is the root soul of Israel, 'Israel rose first in thought', the first of all revelation; a Jew binds himself to that wholeness before a mitzvah (the 'le-shem yichud... in the name of all Israel'). This is a real reinterpretation of the Lurianic stage as the community's root.
- **Other schools.** Rare elsewhere: Agra DeKala 4 lines, Ohr LaShamayim 3; I did not read them.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:20, 7:24** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 308)
> ואחר כך נמשך על ידי צמצום, מלכות דאין סוף להיות כתר דאדם קדמון … ונמצא סוף הכל שהוא מלכות דמלכות דעשיה, נעוץ בתחלת הכל
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: after the tzimtzum, Malchus of Ein Sof becomes Keter of Adam Kadmon; so the end of all (Malchus of Asiyah) is embedded in the beginning (G05). *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:25-26** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1276)
> היא מראה כחה ויכלתה ביסוד העפר הגשמי בגילוי עצום … משום דרגלי ״אדם קדמון״ מסתיימים בתחתית עשיה, ותחת רגליו מאיר אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא הסובב כל עלמין
> — The Alter Rebbe: the earth shows the power to make yesh from nothing more than the heavenly hosts because the feet of Adam Kadmon end at the bottom of Asiyah, and beneath them shines the surrounding Or Ein Sof. *[stated]*

> **Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah, First Gate 21:6** (R. Aharon of Strashelye, Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah; line 251)
> ולא דיבר רק ממלכות דאדם קדמון ולמטה הנצרך לקישור הדרגין איך מתייחדים כל הדרגין בעצמותו יתברך … מכנה להמשכה זו גוף וכלים בבחינת השאלה
> — Strashelye: the Ari spoke only from Malchus of Adam Kadmon down, as needed to show how all levels are united in His Essence; 'body' and 'vessels' of Adam Kadmon are borrowed names from the side of the worlds. *[stated]*

> **Hemshech Ayin Beis, second night of Rosh Hashanah 5673, 128:2** (Rebbe Rashab, Hemshech Ayin Beis; line 514)
> וגם דא"ק הוא בבחי' א"ס עדיין
> — The Rashab: Adam Kadmon is still in the aspect of Ein Sof, which is why each world needs its own particular will (Keter). *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 9:6-7** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 832)
> וצמצום אדם קדמון, וסוד הדיקנא, שסוד כל הצמצומים לצמצם האור שיתלבש בבחינות כלים די׳ ספירות
> — The Alter Rebbe names the purpose of all the contractions, Adam Kadmon's included: to clothe the light in vessels; and then (9:7) 'we have no business with hidden things', only faith that He and His attributes are one. *[stated]*

> **Derekh HaMelekh, Holidays, Shabbat Teshuvah 1:9-10** (R. Kalonymus Kalman of Piaseczno, Derekh HaMelekh (citing Noam Elimelech); line 924)
> היינו שבחי' כללות ישראל הוא בחי' אדם קדמון. וצריכים לדעת, שכללות אין הפי' פרטים רבים שחברו ונעשו מהם כלל, רק האחדות טרם שנסתעף לפרטים
> — Piaseczno, citing Noam Elimelech: the world called 'Klal Yisrael', always whole, is Adam Kadmon; and generality means not parts joined but the unity before it branched into parts. *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** Yes, partly. Adam Kadmon is a technical Lurianic stage that most sources refuse to open ('no business with hidden things'). The tie to the unity is stated through its role: it is still Ein Sof, and its end is set in its beginning. Piaseczno's reading ties it to Klal Yisrael, not directly to the unity.

## S021 · The chain of descent

**סדר השתלשלות** · search: סדר השתלשלות · השתלשלות העולמות · עילה ועלול · ממדרגה למדרגה

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F07 The home below; F05 The Essence beyond both (primary first). **Grammar:** G04, G09, G11.

> The chain of descent is the unity of God as one will descending through garments that conceal, whose purpose lies not in the higher worlds but in the lowest one.

1. Before creation He alone filled the space, and so He does now 'before Him'; the change is only in the receivers, through many garments (Tanya 36:3). *(stated)*
2. The chain is that descent from level to level by garments that hide the light, down to this physical world where the darkness is doubled (36:4). *(stated)*
3. Cause and effect alone would never reach this world; it needs contractions (48:1). That the physical comes from the spiritual 'is above the chain, the ability of the Omnipotent alone' (LT Shir HaShirim 23:2). In Malchus the power to create yesh is revealed 'not by cause and effect' (IH 20:14). This is F05. *(stated)*
4. The purpose of the chain is not the higher worlds but this lowest one, where light shines in place of darkness (36:5; 49:4). This is F07. *(stated)*
5. Contemplating every detail from Malchus of Ein Sof to Malchus of Asiyah draws all of it to the first source (Shaar HaYichud 7:29). This is G04. *(stated)*
6. The Maggid: nothing below is without a root above; the root of roots is the primordial will, and 'the power of the maker is in the made' (MDL 50:1). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 36, 48-49; IH 20:3, 20:14; LT Shir HaShirim 23: the chain hides, cannot produce matter by itself, and exists for the lowest.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:13-40 and 8: the chain in order, as matter for contemplation that joins every detail to the whole and the whole to the Essence.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Achdut 2: the line passes through Atzilus and the screen into every creature, and 'the main vitality is from sovev', i.e. not from the chain.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (52:1): in the chain, what descends is not the higher level's essence but its outer aspect, the lower level included in it.
- **Rashab.** HAB 675-676: the creation of yesh reveals 'ein aroch', which the chain of lights does not.

- **Maggid.** MDL 50:1: root above for everything below; the primordial will; the power of the maker in the made.
- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3: the chain's walls and gates are an illusion of the king (see S025).
- **Other schools.** The term 'seder hishtalshelus' is mostly Chabad's; the Polish masters speak of worlds and rungs without the systematic chain.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:4-5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 487)
> וזהו ענין השתלשלות העולמות וירידתם ממדרגה למדרגה, על ידי ריבוי הלבושים המסתירים האור … והנה, תכלית השתלשלות העולמות וירידתם ממדרגה למדרגה – אינו בשביל עולמות העליונים
> — The Alter Rebbe: the chain is descent by many garments that hide the light; its purpose is not the higher worlds but this lowest one. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 675)
> ואילו היתה השתלשלות העולמות מאוראיןסוף ברוךהוא בלי צמצומים, רק כסדר המדרגות ממדרגה למדרגה בדרך עלה ועלול – לא היה העולם הזה נברא כלל
> — Cause-and-effect alone would never reach this finite world; the chain needs contractions. *[stated]*

> **Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 23:2** (Alter Rebbe, Likkutei Torah; line 4753)
> אך בודאי זה גופא שמהרוחני יתהוה הגשמי הוא למעלה מהשתלשלות שהוא רק יכולת הקב״ה שהוא כל יכול
> — The chain does not itself produce the physical: that the physical comes from the spiritual is above the order of descent, the ability of the Omnipotent alone (F05). *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:14** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1264)
> כי בה נגלה כח אוראיןסוף לברוא יש מאין שלא עלידי עילה ועלול … וזהו שכתוב: ״נעוץ תחלתן בסופן״
> — Malchus is 'the revealed world' because in it the power of Or Ein Sof to create yesh is revealed, not by cause and effect; the beginning is embedded in the end. *[stated]*

> **The Gate of Unity 7:29** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 315)
> בהתבוננות היטב בכל פרטי ההשתלשלות מריש כל דרגין שהוא מלכות דאין סוף עד מלכות דעשיה דוקא, אזי יומשך הכל אל המקור הראשון
> — The Mitteler Rebbe's method: contemplating every detail of the chain from Malchus of Ein Sof to Malchus of Asiyah draws all to the first source, the Essence before the tzimtzum (G04). *[stated]*

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 50:1** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 118)
> אין דבר למטה שאין לו שרש למעלה … ושורש כל השרשים הוא רצון הקדמון שעלה ברצונו לברוא את העולמות
> — The Maggid: nothing below lacks a root above; the root of all roots is the primordial will to create the worlds, and 'the power of the maker is in the made'. *[stated]*

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:4** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 691)
> ותכלית כל הצמצומים הוא – כדי לברוא גוף האדם החומרי, ולאכפייא לסטרא אחרא … כי זה תכלית השתלשלות העולמות
> — The purpose of every contraction, and of the whole chain: the physical human body, subduing the other side, light from darkness (F07, F11). *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** None. The chain is the most explicitly purposed subject here: Tanya 36:5 and 49:4 say its purpose outright.

## S022 · Tohu and tikkun; the breaking of the vessels

**תהו ותיקון / שבירת הכלים** · search: עולם התהו · שבירת הכלים · עולם התיקון · מלכים שמתו

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light; F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity; F09 The unity of opposites; F07 The home below (primary first). **Grammar:** G08, G10, G09.

> Tohu and tikkun are the unity of God as the passage from separateness to inclusion: lights too great for vessels that each stood alone broke into the 'something', and the task is to draw those lights into vessels that are one.

1. In tohu each sefirah was a separate point, 'each by itself'; in tikkun they are included in one another (Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5633; the Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos 35). *(stated)*
2. The worlds broke because 'each said, I will reign', and took more light than its vessel could hold (Izhbitz, Beit Yaakov). That self-assertion is the root of the breaking is stated there; that this is the same root as in Tanya 22 ('I and none else') is our reading. *(reading)*
3. The broken vessels, scattered letters, became a thing in their own right, and from them came the separate yesh of the lower worlds. Tikkun-vessels are nullified to the light (LT Matot 2:3-5). This is F03. *(stated)*
4. The sparks that fell must be raised. The Besht's circle calls this 'the purpose of a Jew's service in Torah and mitzvot and in intention while eating' (Keter Shem Tov 1:40). R. Chaim of Chernovitz: all creation was for repairing the breaking (Be'er Mayim Chaim). This is F11. *(stated)*
5. Through mitzvot the lights of tohu are drawn into the vessels of tikkun (Maharash 5640). They join only through a light higher than both (Maharash 5633). This is F09. *(stated)*
6. The goal, tohu-lights in tikkun-vessels, is 'the ultimate unity'; the breaking was 'destroying in order to build' a world of inclusion (the Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos 35, Vayera 2). *(stated)*
7. Kept mood: R. Nachman leaves the catastrophe standing. From the breaking come the husks and the external wisdoms (LM 64:2). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** LT Matot 2 (the yesh from the breaking); Torah Ohr, Esther 11:9 (the sparks of the breaking are higher than the world of tikkun, and so man is nourished from food); IH 26 (Malchus of Atzilus clothed in noga to refine the 288 sparks).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 20:2 (the sefirot of tohu were points only), 45:9 (tohu = scattering of letters), 54:2 (kelipah from the breaking). I read only the headings of these, not the full context.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5633 and 5640: no inclusion in tohu; mitzvot as the way tohu-lights enter tikkun-vessels; a higher light joins them.
- **Rashab.** HAB 876:3: in tohu there were no screens, and so it broke; the screen is part of tikkun.
- **the Rebbe.** Likkutei Sichos 35, Vayera 2 (Sodom as tohu, 'mine is mine'; its return in the redemption as tohu-lights in tikkun-vessels). Toras Menachem uses 'breaking of the vessels' 79 times; not read here.

- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:40: raising the sparks from the worlds He 'built and destroyed' is the service of a Jew.
- **Izhbitz.** Beit Yaakov, Miketz 10:3: the fault of tohu is 'I will reign'; tikkun begins with Joseph, who knew that no good is in one's own hand. The Izhbitz reading is moral and psychological.
- **Chernovitz.** Be'er Mayim Chaim, Genesis 1: creation is a 'kindness of truth' to the dead kings, for their repair. The mood is purpose, not catastrophe.
- **Breslov.** LM 64:2: the external wisdoms come from the breaking, and their heresy can be answered, unlike the heresy from the void.

> **Likkutei Torah, Matot 2:3-5** (Alter Rebbe, Likkutei Torah; line 2598)
> אך הוא ע״י שהיה שבירת הכלים דתהו שכשנשברו ונסתלקו מהם האורות ונפלו השברי כלים למטה בנוגה נעשו בחינת יש
> — The Alter Rebbe: the separate yesh of BYA comes from the breaking; broken vessels (scattered letters) became a thing in their own right, as a broken jar is no longer subordinate to its wine. *[stated]*

> **Likkutei Sichos vol. 35, Vayera 2, 74:4-5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos; line 20089)
> שבתהו "כל א' בפני עצמו, ענפין מתפרדין" … אין בזה ענין של פירוד ושבירה, אלא
> — The Rebbe: tohu is separateness, 'each by himself'; its breaking is 'destroying in order to build' a world of inclusion and unity; the goal is tohu-lights in tikkun-vessels, 'the ultimate unity'. *[stated]*

> **Toras Shmuel 5633, VaPharaoh Hikriv 131:1** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel; line 6178)
> שמתחברים אורות דתהו שהם בלי גבול עם כלים דתיקון שהם שורש ומקור הגבול זהו ע"י שמאיר הארה יותר גבוה משניהם … משא"כ התהו שלא הי' בהם התכללות כ"א נקודות זת"ז כל א' בפ"ע
> — The Maharash: tohu had no inclusion, only points one under another, each by itself; tohu-lights and tikkun-vessels can join only through a light higher than both (F09). *[stated]*

> **Beit Yaakov, Miketz 10:3** (R. Yaakov Leiner of Izhbitz, Beit Yaakov; line 966)
> כי עלמין דאתחרבו שכל אחד אמר אני אמלוך, והיו מקבלים אור יותר מכפי הכלי קיבול שלהם, לזה היה שבירת הכלים
> — Izhbitz: the worlds were destroyed because each said 'I will reign' and took more light than its vessel; tikkun began with Joseph, who knew that the good is only God's (bitul, F08). The link to the unity is our reading. *[reading]*

> **Be'er Mayim Chaim, Genesis 1:1:11** (R. Chaim of Chernovitz, Be'er Mayim Chaim; line 11)
> כי ידוע שתכלית בריאת העולם היה בכדי לתקן שבירת הכלים ומיתת מלכין קדמאין
> — R. Chaim of Chernovitz: the purpose of creating the world was to repair the breaking of the vessels and the death of the primordial kings, a 'kindness of truth' to the dead. The purpose is stated; its tie to the unity is our reading. *[reading]*

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:40:1** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 57)
> ענין ברורי ניצוצות הקדושות שנפלו בעת שהיה הקב"ה בונה עולמות ומחריבן … וזהו תכלית עבודת איש ישראל בתורה ומצות וכוונת אכילה
> — The Besht's circle: raising the sparks that fell when He 'built worlds and destroyed them' is the purpose of a Jew's service, even in eating (F11, G12). *[stated]*

> **Likutei Moharan 64:2:1** (R. Nachman of Breslov, Likutei Moharan; line 3399)
> כי מחמת רבוי האור נשתברו הכלים, ומשם נתהוו הקלפות כידוע, וחכמות חיצוניות באים משם
> — Breslov keeps the catastrophe: from the breaking come the husks and the external wisdoms, whose heresy (unlike the heresy from the void) can be answered. *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** SUBJECTS.md marked this 'reading'. The sources state more than that. The Rebbe calls tikkun 'the ultimate unity', and the Maharash says a higher light joins tohu and tikkun. Outside Chabad the tie is to bitul (Izhbitz) and purpose (Chernovitz, the Besht's circle). The link to the unity there is our reading. Both moods are kept: catastrophe (Breslov) and purpose (Chernovitz, the Rebbe).

## S023 · The four worlds

**אצילות בריאה יצירה עשיה** · search: אבי"ע · אצילות בריאה יצירה · ארבעה עולמות · ארבע עולמות

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F02 The higher and the lower unity; F04 Filling and surrounding (primary first). **Grammar:** G02, G04, G05.

> The four worlds are the unity of God as degrees of one concealment: the same ten sefirot of Atzilus, Godliness itself, hidden further at each screen, while to Him first and last are the same.

1. The hiding of Or Ein Sof through the chain is, in general, three great contractions for three worlds, Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah. Atzilus is Godliness itself (Tanya 49:1). *(stated)*
2. Each world is 'Ein Sof' to the one below it; the light can clothe itself below only through contraction and concealment (49:2-3). A world is a degree of hiding, not a place. *(stated)*
3. In truth it is only the ten sefirot of Atzilus in the three lower worlds, His power alone without change. The division into worlds comes only from Malchus and the screen (Strashelye, Sha'arei HaYichud II 13:2). *(stated)*
4. From the Essence all the particulars of the four worlds are united with Him, and 'to Him there is no difference between first and last' (Shaar HaYichud 8:38-39). This is F02. *(stated)*
5. In one glance the four worlds are surveyed as one. In Atzilus the lower worlds are hidden, and in the lower worlds Atzilus is hidden (Homel, Chanah Ariel). *(stated)*
6. In the soul: love and awe rise into the sefirot of Beriah and Yetzirah, within which Atzilus is clothed and united with Ein Sof (Tanya 39:8). The Besht's circle walks the four worlds in prayer 'from room to room' (Keter Shem Tov 1:184). That this is one ascent of attachment is our reading. *(reading)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 39 and 49; Tanya 2:9 (the soul descends through the four worlds from His wisdom).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 8: one wisdom and one will create, form and make, descending world by world, to Solomon's wisdom and the Ark in the Holy of Holies; no difference to Him between first and last.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631: the curtain between Atzilus and Beriah is rooted in Elokim (see S024).
- **Rashab.** HAB 876:1-2: the garments of Beriah reveal, those of Yetzirah hide like speech, those of Asiyah hide entirely; screens exist in every world.

- **Strashelye.** Only one set of ten sefirot; the forty sefirot of the worlds are counted 'by likeness', not in essence.
- **Homel.** Chanah Ariel, Tazria 3:2: all worlds receive from Or Ein Sof equally in their inner aspect; the difference is concealment.
- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:184: the worlds as stations of prayer.
- **Piaseczno.** Derekh HaMelekh, Toldot 14: angels in Yetzirah, souls in Beriah, chayah and yechidah in Atzilus. The pillar architecture (08, room 5) records his 'four worlds = rungs of the soul' in Chovat HaTalmidim; that passage was not re-read here.

## S024 · Atzilus and the screen

**אצילות / פרסא** · search: עולם האצילות · פרסא · איהו וגרמוהי חד · מסך

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity; F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F12 Knowing the unity; F05 The Essence beyond both (primary first). **Grammar:** G14, G03, G02.

> Atzilus and the screen are the unity of God as the border where 'He and His vessels are one' ends and real hiding begins: a screen that hides only toward the created, and that the light of Malchus breaks through.

1. In Atzilus 'He and His life are one, He and His vessels are one'. The lights and the vessels are all Godliness, and this is not so in the three lower worlds (IH 20:1). *(stated)*
2. How He unites with His attributes is beyond comprehension. The sefirot are called 'the secret of faith' (SH 9:7). This is F12's faith above reason. *(stated)*
3. Created minds cannot receive from Atzilus, which is not contracted (Tanya 39:12). The concealment is the screen (parsa) between Atzilus and Beriah (Strashelye, Sha'arei HaYichud II 13:1). *(stated)*
4. The screen is rooted in the Name Elokim, the sheath of Havayah, and 'outside the curtain too is holy' (Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5631). This is G14. *(stated)*
5. The line clothed in Atzilus reaches the screen, and the light in the vessels of Malchus breaks through it and shines in the lower worlds 'as in Atzilus itself' (IH 20:20). Strashelye: Malchus 'breaks the screen'. *(stated)*
6. Screens exist in every world, even above Atzilus, and their absence in tohu caused the breaking (Rashab, HAB 876:2-3). That the screen is therefore a form of the kindness in the hiding (F03) is our reading. *(reading)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 20:1, 20:20; SH 5:2 and 9:7; Tanya 39:12, 49:1. Atzilus is 'Godliness itself', and the screen is where hiding in earnest begins.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 29:31-32: the vessels of Atzilus truly limit, yet they are one with Him through the Omnipotent's power to limit (see S017).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM Achdut 2: the line clothed in the vessels of Atzilus 'breaks through the screen' into the lower worlds.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631: the curtain and the screen are rooted in Elokim, and outside the curtain is holy too, like the sun seen through its shield.
- **Rashab.** HAB 876: screens in all worlds; tohu had none.

- **Strashelye.** Sha'arei HaYichud II 12-14: the screen is 'His concealment', a garment that hides the power of Atzilus; the differences between the worlds are only 'likeness' (dimyon) and concealment, not in essence.
- **Homel.** Chanah Ariel, Tazria 3:2: the screens between worlds come through the refinement of the kings.
- **Other schools.** Outside the Chabad circle I found no treatment of the screen as the border of the unity.

> — The definition of Atzilus: lights and vessels are both Godliness; not so in the three lower worlds. *[stated]*

> — How He unites with His attributes is beyond comprehension; the sefirot are called 'the secret of faith' (F12: faith above intellect). *[stated]*

> **Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah, Second Gate 13:1** (R. Aharon of Strashelye, Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah; line 358)

## S025 · Concealment and revelation

**העלם וגילוי** · search: העלם והסתר · הסתר פנים · גילוי אלקות · העלם וגילוי

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation; F12 Knowing the unity; F09 The unity of opposites; F11 Darkness turned to light (primary first). **Grammar:** G03, G14, G12.

> Concealment and revelation are the unity of God as one power seen from two sides: the gevurah that hides Him in the creature is His own, and once one knows He is hiding there, it is no hiding.

1. He fills the place now as before creation; the change is only in the receivers, through garments (Tanya 36:3). *(stated)*
2. The hiding is His gevurah. Vitality hides in the creature so that it seems a thing in itself, though it is like light from the sun, and this hiding is the power of the Omnipotent (SH 4:5-6). *(stated)*
3. The same Name that hides reveals. The yesh comes precisely through Elokim that hides Havayah, and through that very hiding the greatness of Havayah is seen, as letters hide the intellect and so convey it (Rashab, HAB 675:4). The contraction is 'the foundation of kindness' (Rayatz). *(stated)*
4. The point holds two opposites, concealment toward what is above it and revelation toward what is below (Shaar HaYichud 13:69-70). This is F09. *(stated)*
5. The Besht: the king's walls were an illusion, and 'once one knows that the Holy One is hiding there, it is no hiding' (Keter Shem Tov 1:39, 1:55). This is F12. *(stated)*
6. R. Nachman: there is a hiding, and a hiding within the hiding, where one does not know one is hidden. Even there He is clothed (in the letters of Torah), and knowing this turns the hiding into da'at and Torah (LM 56:3). This is F11. *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 4-6; Tanya 36:3; Torah Ohr (pillar 09): what is concealment for us is return to source from His side.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 13:69-70: the point as concealment and revelation together.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** DM (pillar 09, room 5, not re-read here): a trial is divine vitality in its deepest concealment.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631: Elokim is the shield through which the sun can be received; 'through the shield the light is tempered'.
- **Rashab.** HAB 675-676: the hiding of Elokim is the condition of the greatness of Havayah; Hayom Yom 27 Adar II: the first tzimtzum's intent is revelation.
- **Rayatz.** Yud Kislev 5692: the tzimtzum is the root of mercy; Elokim becomes Elokeinu.
- **the Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5737 (see S014); Inyanah 29:5 (spine F03n): even at the lowest point He dwells with us.

- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3, 1:55:9: the palace of illusions; knowledge ends the hiding.
- **Breslov.** LM 56:3: the double concealment, and da'at as its turning.
- **Lublin, Radzin, Ger, Sochatchov.** Pillar 09 records R. Tzadok's 'the real fall is not knowing you are hidden', the Radzin parable of the prince beaten out of his father's sight, and the rules of Ger and Sochatchov. These were not re-verified in this pass.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:5-6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 780)
> כי אם בהסתר פנים, שהחיות מסתתר בגוף הנברא, וכאילו גוף הנברא הוא דבר בפני עצמו … הן הן גבורותיו של הקדושברוךהוא, אשר כל יכול
> — The concealment: vitality hides in the creature so that it seems a thing in itself, and the hiding is His own omnipotent gevurah. *[stated]*

**Thin or forced?** None. This subject is F03 itself, stated in every school.

## S026 · Angels and the heavenly hosts

**מלאכים** · search: מלאכים · שרפים · חיות הקדש · צבא השמים

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity; F11 Darkness turned to light; F06 The soul as part of the One; F02 The higher and the lower unity (primary first). **Grammar:** G08, G02, G10.

> The angels are the unity of God as a ladder of creatures that stand before Him as a 'something' and dissolve at a greater revelation, and so show what only the soul in a body, choosing, can do.

1. Angels are created from nothing and live from the externality of the vitality, the letters, 'the breath of His mouth'. So they are called 'Elokim', while the soul comes from the inwardness and is part of Havayah (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 4:6-8). *(stated)*
2. Even the bodies of the angels of Atzilus are a yesh, not wholly nullified as an effect is in its cause (IH 20:7). *(stated)*
3. Their love and fear are natural and they have no choice. Souls rise above them because, by choice, they subdue the other side (Tanya 39:1, 39:7). This is F11. *(stated)*
4. An angel among ten Jews would be nullified from existence by the Shechinah resting on them (IH 23:7). This is F06. *(stated)*
5. Michael and Gavriel appear as a yesh only from our side; before Him all is one power (Strashelye). The angel of water and the angel of fire are at peace through bitul before a higher light (Maharash). This is F02 and F09. *(stated)*
6. The Besht: all angels and palaces are made 'as it were of His Essence, like the snail whose garment is of itself' (Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3). *(stated)*
7. Piaseczno: the angels are from the good of tohu that did not break. Their self-nullifying service is holier than man's choice, yet man, choosing, is greater (Derekh HaMelekh). *(stated)*

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 39; Iggeret HaTeshuvah 4; IH 20:7, 20:35, 23:7; Kuntres Acharon 4:58 (the service of angels is only ascent, not drawing down; angels are created even from Torah studied without intent).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5633: Michael and Gavriel at peace through bitul before the King.
- **Mitteler Rebbe, Tzemach Tzedek, Rashab, Rayatz, the Rebbe.** Not checked for this subject.

- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3: angels made of His Essence; and 1:55:6: angels created by a tzaddik's mitzvot sing above.
- **Strashelye.** Sha'arei HaYichud II 12:7: the angels' separateness is only from the side of revelation.
- **Piaseczno.** Derekh HaMelekh, Chayei Sara 3:16 and Toldot 14-15: angels from unbroken tohu; they are made in Yetzirah from the light of Israel's souls.

| Is the tzimtzum literal? | Alter Rebbe (SH 7:15) and Strashelye ('grave offense'): no; concealment toward creatures only. Tzava'at HaRivash 137: spatial wording. The Rebbe (Likkutei Sichos 35, letter): even the literal view 'because He is omnipotent' does not contradict avodah in ChaBaD. Breslov (LM 64): two opposites, yes and no, not graspable until the future. | S014, S015 |
| Is the void an object of comprehension or of faith? | Mitteler Rebbe: understanding tzimtzum and reshimu in detail 'is the essence of the true unity' (12:3, 13:77). Alter Rebbe: 'we have no business with hidden things' (SH 9:7). R. Nachman: no inquiry finds Him in the void; only faith crosses it (LM 64:2). | S014, S015, S020, S024 |
| Does anything stand between filling and surrounding? | Chabad: sovev holds all equally and 'is truly within' (Tanya 48:7; IH 20:22). Breslov: the void stands between memale and sovev (LM 64:2:10). | S018 |
| Is the breaking a catastrophe or a purpose? | Breslov: husks and external wisdoms come from it. Chernovitz and the Besht's circle: all creation is for its repair. The Rebbe: 'destroying in order to build', ending in 'the ultimate unity'. Izhbitz: its cause is 'I will reign'. | S022 |
| Is the light the Essence or a mere radiance? | IH 20:11: 'the light is like the luminary, His very Essence'. DM Emunah 6-7: the attributes are Godliness but not His Essence. Maharash (spine F05j): creation is 'only a mere radiance'. Rashab: light is willed, not necessary, and reveals 'none besides You' but not 'nothing compares'. | S016, S017 |
| Who is greater, angel or man? | Tanya 39: the righteous, by choice. Piaseczno: angels' self-nullification is holier than man's choice, yet man is greater. | S026 |
