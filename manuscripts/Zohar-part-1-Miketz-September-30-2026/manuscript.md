Miketz is Joseph raised from the pit to rule Egypt, and his brothers coming down for bread. The Zohar uses it to teach how a person lives inside God's hidden plan: all Israel held on one tree of life; the good that is done "in its time"; rejoicing in whatever comes; the small sins a person tramples underfoot without noticing; not watching one's own prayer to see if it is answered; the evil inclination that lives only where there is wine-joy and pride; and above all, not repaying evil. The peak is a story: a traveler saved from a snake and a falling rock, because every night he forgave everyone who hurt him before he went to bed. All 269 lines were read in full. ## 2. The unity of God

**One tree, many parts, all one.** «אילנא דחיי, איהו בכמה דרגין מתפרשן דא מן דא, וכלהו חד» (Miketz 1:5; line 4009): "the tree of life is in many levels, distinct one from another, and all are one"; branches, leaves, bark, trunk, roots, «וכלא הוא אילנא» (same line), "and all of it is the tree." All of Israel holds some part of it; «מאן דאשתדל באורייתא, איהו אחיד בכלא» (Miketz 1:6; line 4010): "whoever busies himself with Torah holds all of it." Ours: a picture of unity with room for every kind of person.

**All is in Him; do not lean on a man.** Joseph asked the butler "remember me", and the Zohar calls it an error: «ואיהו טעה בהאי, דהא ביה בקודשא בריך הוא הוי כלא» (Miketz 1:7; line 4011): "and he erred in this, for in the Holy One is everything." Ties: S84, the Tanya's reading of trust (Shaar HaBitachon via Chovot HaLevavot; memory).

**Only He binds and frees.** «בגין דהא לית מאן דאסיר ופתח, בר קודשא בריך הוא … דהא כלא ביה» (Miketz 4:10; line 4045): "for there is none who binds and frees except the Holy One … for all is in Him."

**He descends when there are righteous.** When no righteous are in the world «איהו אסתלק מנייהו, ולא אתגלי להו» (Miketz 4:14; line 4049): "He withdraws from them and is not revealed to them"; when they are, «קודשא בריך הוא נחית בדרגוי לקבלהון דתתאי, לאשגחא על עלמא, לאוטבא להו» (same line): "the Holy One descends by His levels toward those below, to watch over the world, to do them good." Ours: His hiding and showing follow us.

**Redemption and prayer, hand and head: see that all is one.** «אין מפסיקין בין גאולה לתפלה, כמה דלא מפסיקין בין תפלה של יד לתפלה של ראש. דבעי למחזי דכלא חד» (Vayigash 1:9; line 4282): "we do not interrupt between redemption and prayer, as we do not interrupt between the hand tefillin and the head tefillin, for one must see that all is one." (This line opens Vayigash, but it answers Miketz's close.) Ties: technique 2.

**Good joined to Israel, "that all be one."** «אך טוב לישראל, בגין לאדבקא ביה … למהוי כלא חד» (Miketz 15:31; line 4272): "'only good to Israel', so as to cleave to it … so that all may be one." (Ours: here "Israel" means those "pure of heart" who do not defile themselves with evil; the guide reads it universally as the one who clings to the good.)

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **Rejoice in all that comes.** «כי אם לשמוח, בכל מה דייתי עלוי, ולמיהב הודאה לקודשא בריך הוא» (Miketz 4:24; line 4059): "only to rejoice in all that comes upon him, and to give thanks to the Holy One"; even if a bad thing came from his own act, «אית ליה למחדי ביה, ולאודאה עליה» (same line): "he should rejoice in it and give thanks for it." Ties: S85 (seeing Him in what happens), Tanya ch. 26 and Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (memory).
- **The heart that waits for His kindness.** "Havayah desires those who fear Him, who wait for His kindness": those who learn at night with the Shechinah, «וכד אתי צפרא, אינון מחכאן לחסדו» (Miketz 4:2; line 4037): "and when morning comes, they wait for His kindness"; a thread of kindness is drawn over them by day. Ties: S84.
- **The spirit that comes and goes.** "His spirit was troubled": «דהוה אתי רוחא ואזיל, ואתי ואזיל. ולא הוה מתיישבא עמיה» (Miketz 3:2; line 4032): "the spirit would come and go, come and go, and did not settle with him"; so Samson's spirit began. Ours: the beginning of inspiration is unsteady; it is not a failure. Ties: C-current of ratzo vashov; S35.
- **Sadness drives out the added spirit.** When Joseph was lost, Jacob lost «ההוא תוספת רוחא» and the Shechinah left him, «בגין דעצבונא דהוה ביה גרמא ליה» (Miketz 6:9; line 4096): "because the sadness in him caused it." Ties: Tanya ch. 26 (memory).
- **The broken spirit that the evil inclination cannot stay in.** «דהא יצר הרע לא שריא אלא באתר דאשתכח חדוה דחמרא, וגסותא דרוחא. וכד אשתכח רוחא תבירא, כדין אתפרש מניה» (Miketz 12:6; line 4201): "the evil inclination dwells only where there is joy of wine and haughtiness of spirit; when there is a broken spirit, it departs." «יצר טוב בעי חדוה דאורייתא» (Miketz 12:7; line 4202): "the good inclination wants the joy of Torah." Ours: two joys: one of wine and pride, one of Torah; and brokenness that is a door, not a pit. Ties: S59, the Tanya's pairing of broken heart and joy (ch. 31; memory).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Forgive everyone before you sleep.** Rabbi Abba saw a sleeping traveler saved from a snake and then from a falling rock, and asked what his deeds were. «כל יומאי לא אשלים לי בר נש בישא בעלמא, דלא אתפייסנא בהדיה, ומחילנא ליה. ותו, אי לא יכילנא לאתפייסא בהדיה, לא סליקנא לערסי, עד דמחילנא ליה, ולכל אינון דמצערו לי» (Miketz 11:24; line 4192): "all my days, no one did me harm in the world that I did not make peace with him and forgive him. And if I could not make peace with him, I did not go up to my bed until I forgave him, and all who pained me." And more: «דמההוא יומא ולהלאה, אשתדלנא למעבד עמהון טבא» (same line): "from that day on, I strove to do them good." Rabbi Abba wept: this is greater than Joseph, who forgave brothers (line 4193). New technique below. Ties: the bedtime "Master of the world, I forgive all who angered me"; technique 24.

**Do not repay evil; hope to Him.** «אפילו למאן דאשלימו ליה בישין, לא אית ליה לאשלמא בישא, חלף ההוא בישא דשלימו ליה. אלא, קוה ליי ויושע לך» (Miketz 11:17; line 4185): "even to the one who paid him evil, he should not repay evil for that evil; rather, 'hope to Havayah and He will save you.'" Joseph not only did not repay, «אלא דעבד עמהון טיבו וקשוט» (Miketz 11:20; line 4188), "but did them kindness and truth." And love among people keeps the right side strong (line 4182).

**Do not watch your prayer.** «דלית ליה לבר נש לאסתכלא בבעותיה לגבי קודשא בריך הוא, אי אתי, אי לא אתי» (Miketz 13:1; line 4213): "a person should not keep looking at his request before the Holy One, whether it comes or does not"; for then accusers look into his deeds. Instead: «מאן דבעי דקודשא בריך הוא יקבל צלותיה, ישתדל באורייתא» (Miketz 13:3; line 4215): "whoever wants the Holy One to accept his prayer, let him busy himself with Torah." Ties: S84; new technique below.

**The man of faith asks for his daily bread.** Rabbi Yeisa the Elder, though he had food for the day, would not prepare it «עד דשאיל מזוניה קמי מלכא קדישא» (Miketz 9:13; line 4146): "until he asked for his sustenance before the holy King"; he would say, «לא נתקין, עד דינתנון מבי מלכא» (same line): "we will not prepare, until they give from the King's house." New technique below. Ties: technique 20.

**Rouse the good against the evil; then Torah; then the day of death.** «לעולם ירגיז אדם יצר טוב על יצר הרע … ואי לאו, ישתדל באורייתא. דהא לית לך מלה לתברא יצר הרע אלא אורייתא. אי אזיל, מוטב. ואי לאו, ידכר ליה יומא דמותא» (Miketz 12:4; line 4199): "a person should always rouse the good inclination against the evil … if not, let him busy himself with Torah, for nothing breaks the evil inclination but Torah; if it goes, good; if not, let him remember the day of death." Ties: technique 17; Tanya ch. 29 (memory).

**Search out the sins under your heel.** Some sins a person "treads on with his heels" and does not notice, and so fears without knowing why (lines 4118-4119). David searched them out before battle and so pursued his enemies; Asa searched less and could only chase; Jehoshaphat could only sing; Hezekiah could only lie in bed while God fought (lines 4124-4128). «זכאין אינון צדיקייא, דידעין לאסתמרא מחוביהון, ואינון מפשפשין תדיר בעובדייהו» (Miketz 8:16; line 4133): "happy are the righteous who know to guard against their sins and search their deeds always." Ties: technique 24.

**Judge every person favorably.** A man walked wrapped in a tallit and armed and did not return their greeting; Rabbi Chiya suspected him; «הא חסידי עליונין אמרו, הוי דן לכל בר נש לזכו» (Miketz 15:23; line 4264): "the highest pious ones said: judge every person to the side of merit." He had been reviewing his learning. And one who goes on the road should prepare for three: a gift, battle, prayer (same line).

**Where words of Torah are said, the Shechinah is.** After a night of Torah with a stranger: «קודשא בריך הוא אתרעי בהו, דהא שכינתא לא אעדי מהכא» (Miketz 9:31; line 4164): "the Holy One desired them, for the Shechinah did not leave here." Ties: technique 22.

## 5. Big human questions

- **What is the point of all these turns of fate?** «כל מה דעבד קודשא בריך הוא, כלא איהו לגלגלא גלגולין, בגין דבעי לקיימא קיומא» (Miketz 5:9; line 4070): "all that the Holy One does is to turn the wheel of events, because He wants to fulfil what He decreed." And His way: «בקדמיתא בארי … אסוותא, ולבתר מחי» (Miketz 5:11; line 4072): "first He creates the cure, and afterward He strikes." Ties: S85.
- **Is anything done after death?** «בכחך … דא נשמתיה דבר נש» (line 4078): do it with your strength, which is your soul, now; «אי לא זכי בר נש בהאי עלמא, לא יזכי ביה לבתר בההוא עלמא» (Miketz 5:19; line 4080): "if a person does not merit in this world, he will not merit afterward in that world." Provisions are packed here.
- **Can I come back after sin and still be strong?** «דאף על גב דאיהו חטי, אי יהדר מניה בתיובתא שלימתא, הא תקיף איהו, ויתתקף ביה בקודשא בריך הוא, כאילו לא חטא» (Miketz 12:9; line 4204): "even though he sinned, if he returns from it in full return, he is strong, and strengthened in the Holy One, as if he had not sinned." But «דחובוי דבר נש מתברין לביה, ולית ליה חילא כלל» (Miketz 12:10; line 4205): "a person's sins break his heart, and he has no strength at all" (why the guilty brothers feared).
- **Why does good often take long?** "Hope deferred makes the heart sick": the request passed hand to hand through many officers, each examining; "a tree of life is desire come" is what He gives at once (lines 4216-4217).
- **What can I control?** «דתרין מנהון, ואינון עיינין ואודנין, לא קיימין ברשותיה דבר נש, ופומא איהו ברשותיה» (Miketz 4:18; line 4053): "two of them, the eyes and ears, are not in a person's power, but the mouth is in his power." So «לעולם אל יפתח בר נש פומיה לביש» (Miketz 4:26; line 4061): "let a person never open his mouth for evil."
- **The Shechinah weeping.** When the Temple burned she went up to the roofs and wept over her home and her children (line 4223); she will be raised from the dust, and they will return "with weeping" (line 4228).
- **A healing sun.** The hidden first light, put away from the wicked, healed Jacob's limp (line 4239); «ומרפא בכנפיה, דההוא שמשא, יתסון כלהו» (Miketz 14:12; line 4240): "'and healing in its wings': by that sun all will be healed."

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Forgive everyone before sleep, then do them good (line 4192).** A practice with a story that stays in the mind.
2. **Rejoice in all that comes and give thanks (line 4059).**
3. **Do not watch your prayer; learn instead (lines 4213-4215).**
4. **The broken spirit that the evil inclination cannot live in, and the joy of Torah (lines 4201-4202).**
5. **One tree, many parts, all one (lines 4009-4010).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "No one goes to bed unforgiven."** Each night before sleep, name each person who hurt you today; make peace if you can; if not, forgive in your heart before lying down; tomorrow, do one good thing for one of them (lines 4192-4193). Ties technique 24; the bedtime Shema's "I forgive."
- **Technique, new: "Ask before you eat."** Before preparing a meal (even when food is already there), ask God for it in a few words, and only then prepare it, "from the King's house" (line 4146). Ties technique 20; S84.
- **Technique, new: "Pray, then let go into Torah."** After making a request, do not keep checking whether it came; turn to learning, trusting the request to Him (lines 4213-4215). Ties S84.
- **State, new: "Glad in all that comes."** Inside: a thankful joy that does not wait to see if the thing was good; even one's own mistakes are received and thanked for (line 4059). How to enter: say "this too is from Him" and give thanks aloud. Near S85, S65.
- **State, new: "Waiting for His kindness."** Inside: after a night of learning or prayer, a quiet expectancy that kindness will be drawn over the day (lines 4036-4037). Near S84, S65.
