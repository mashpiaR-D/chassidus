The second half of Tiferet Yisrael reads the Ten Commandments word by word, then turns to the lasting of the Torah: why it cannot change, why it seems forgotten, why the world to come is not written in it, how mitzvot are paid, and why the Torah is split into written and oral. It ends with "the four cubits of halacha" and the offering as the return of the caused to its Cause.

Four teachings in this half matter most for the guide:

- **"Anochi" is His very self.** He is our G-d not through an attribute (mercy, justice), which comes and goes with His acts, but from Himself. The many attributes bring no many into Him.
- **He alone is being.** "He alone is being, and there is nothing besides Him, as is the truth" (553). Only He is fully one. Any two things that are joined testify to the One who joins them.
- **No go-between.** Serve "His very self, fully, and no go-between in the world" (565). Faith is not one more way of cleaving; it is the essence of cleaving (816).
- **Change is on the receiver's side.** His voice at Sinai "lasts for ever"; He gives the Torah always, with no change; "the change is only on the side of the receivers" (844). He is apart from the world, and because He is apart He acts in it (869).

## 2.1 "Anochi": His very self, not an attribute

The Torah says "Anochi" (I), not "Ani". "Anochi" points at the speaker's very self. "אין השם יתברך אלהי ישראל מצד התואר, רק מצד עצמו יתברך הוא אלהיהם" (542, 37:25): "G-d is not their G-d by way of an attribute; He is their G-d from His very self." Attributes change: "כי אם היה אלהותו יתברך על ישראל מצד התואר בלבד, ואינו מצד עצמו, הרי התארים הם מצד הפעולות אשר יפעל" (542): "for if His being their G-d were only by an attribute and not from Himself, attributes come from the acts He does", and acts are at one time and not another. So a bond with His self cannot be forgotten: "ומאחר שדבקים בו מצד עצמו, אין שכחה לדבר זה" (543, 37:26): "and since they cleave to Him from His very self, there is no forgetting it."

The many attributes do not make Him many: "ואם יש כאן רבוי תוארים, אין זה מביא רבוי ושנוי בעצמו, כי כל אלו התוארים מצד הפעולות" (545, 37:28): "and if there are many attributes, this brings no many and no change in Himself, for all these attributes come from the acts." He was seen as a warrior at the sea and as an elder at Sinai, and so the Mekhilta has Him say: "אני הוא שעל הים, אני שעל היבשה, אני לשעבר, אני לעתיד" (545): "I am the One at the sea, I am the One on dry land, I in the past, I in the future."

## 2.2 He alone is being

"Lo yihyeh" is the second word because one who worships another does not deny G-d but lessens Him: "כי ראוי שיאמין" "שהוא יתברך המציאות בלבד, ואפס זולתו, כאשר הוא האמת" (553, 38:1): "for he should believe that He alone is being, and there is nothing besides Him, as is the truth." "והעושה אלהים אחרים, כיון שעושה אלהים, כאילו יש דבר זולתו" (553): "and one who makes other gods, since he makes a god, it is as if there is something besides Him."

G-d is the root ("Ikkar"): "כן השם יתברך, הכל נמצא ממנו, וחי ממנו" (569, 38:17): "so G-d: all exists from Him and lives from Him." When He is named for a real need, He is not apart from the world: "כי בודאי העולם הוא תלוי בו יתברך, וממנו הכל, וכל אשר הוא בעולם הוא ממנו" (597, 39:14): "for surely the world hangs on Him, and all is from Him, and all that is in the world is from Him." And after Sinai: "אני בשמים וכבודי בארץ" (696, 46:1): "I am in heaven and My glory is on earth"; so making an idol on earth "הרי זה מעוט מציאותו יתברך, שהוא יתברך הכל" (696): "lessens His being, for He is all."

## 2.3 Only He is fully one; the One who joins

Shabbat complained: "You gave every day a partner, and me none." The answer: the Congregation of Israel is your partner. Why must Shabbat have a partner? "שאי אפשר שיהיה נמצא דבר שהוא אחד לגמרי, רק השם יתברך שהוא אחד שלם" (628, 40:28): "for there cannot be a thing that is fully one except G-d, who is wholly One." "שאם היה אחד והוא שלם, היה הוא אלוה. כי הוא יתברך הוא אחד, ואין זולתו" (628): "for if it were one and whole, it would be a god; for He is One and there is none besides Him." (So too Adam was given a partner, so that he would not seem a god below.)

Then the three testimonies: Shabbat and Israel testify that He is One. "וכל שני דברים שהם מתחברים ומזדווגים יחד, אי אפשר שלא יהיה חבור שלהם מן אותו שהוא אחד" (631, 40:31): "and any two things that join and pair together, their joining must come from the One." "ואותו שהוא מחבר אותם הוא אחד, שאם אינו אחד, איך יחבר את שנים להיות אחד" (631): "and the One who joins them is one; if He were not one, how could He join two to be one."

## 2.4 No go-between

"על פני" (before My face) means before His very self: even one who sets up only a go-between between himself and the First Cause breaks this command, "כי אין לעבוד רק לעצם עצמו לגמרי, ולא לשום אמצעי בעולם" (565, 38:13): "for one must serve only His very self, fully, and no go-between in the world." Even an image made to serve G-d Himself is forbidden: "וכל דבר שהוא אמצעי בינו למי שהוא עובד לו, הוא עבודת אלילים" (698, 46:3): "and anything that is a go-between between him and the One he serves is idolatry", "ואתה עושה תמונה למי שאין לו תמונה" (698): "and you make an image for One who has no image."

## 2.5 Three holinesses, and apart yet acting

The angels say "Kadosh" three times. The Maharal reads: "קדושה אחת, שהוא יתברך אינו גשם. הקדושה השנית, שאינו כח בגשם. הקדושה השלישית, שאינו מקושר בקשר המציאות" [עם גשם] (541, 37:24): "one holiness: He is not a body. The second: He is not a power in a body. The third: He is not tied in existence with a body." (In man, the body is a body, the soul is a power in the body, and the mind is tied to the body in existence only.)

Yet being apart is what lets Him act: "כי ה' אש אוכלה" means "שהוא נבדל לגמרי מן העולם. ובמה שהוא נבדל מן העולם הוא פועל בעולם, כי הדברים אשר יתחברו ושוים ביחד אינם פועלים זה בזה" (869, 59:3): "that He is fully apart from the world; and because He is apart from the world He acts in the world, for things that are joined and alike do not act on each other." And: "כי הוא יתברך, עם שהוא נבדל, אינו נבדל מן עולם הזה, שהרי הוא יתברך פעל אותו ומנהיגו" (858, 57:13): "for He, though He is apart, is not apart from this world, since He made it and leads it."

## 2.6 The change is in the receivers

"A great voice that did not cease": "מלמד שקול של הקב"ה קיים לעולמי עולמים" (844, 56:28): "this teaches that the voice of the Holy One lasts for ever and ever." "כי הוא יתברך משפיע התורה והחכמה תמיד בלי שנוי כלל" (844): "for He pours out the Torah and wisdom always, with no change at all." "רק השנוי הוא מצד המקבלים" (844): "the change is only on the side of the receivers." That is why the blessing says "who gives the Torah", in the present. Even the six days of creation were for the receiver: "אמנם מה שהיה השם יתברך פועל העולם בזמן, הוא מצד המקבל" (604, 40:4): "but that G-d made the world in time is from the side of the receiver." The Torah itself has two sides: "אי אפשר שלא יהיה" "כאן בחינה מיוחדת מצד הנותן, ובחינה אחרת מצד המקבל" (659, 43:6): "it cannot be that there is not one side from the Giver and another side from the receiver."

## 2.7 Full joining needs one

After Sinai: "I sold you My Torah, and I was sold with it." A king whose only daughter married said: "לפרוש ממנה איני יכול" (705, 46:10): "to part from her I cannot", "אלא בכל מקום שאתם הולכים, בית אחד עשו לי שאדור אצליכם" (705): "but wherever you go, make Me one house so I may dwell near you." Why is the bond complete? Because the Torah is one: "כי אי אפשר שיהיה חבור גמור לשני דברים מחולקים" (709, 46:14): "for there cannot be a full joining with two separate things"; "ולכך השם יתברך יש לו צירוף וחבור גמור בלתי מחולק" [אל] [התורה שהיא אחת] (709): "so G-d has a full, undivided joining [with the Torah, which is one]." And on our side too the commands are joined in the one person: "כי האדם שהוא המקבל הוא אחד, ואליו נאמרו המצות המחולקות" (686, 45:2): "for the person who receives is one, and to him the separate commands were said."

## 2.8 The caused returns to its Cause

The book ends with the offering. The angel Michael is said to offer above: the offering is the return of the caused to the Cause. "וכאשר העלול הוא שב אל עלתו, אל זה נמשך הכל, עד שהוא יתברך יחיד ואין זולתו" (1037, 70:14): "and when the caused returns to its Cause, everything is drawn to this, until He is alone and there is none besides Him." The Midrash says the offering is the souls of the righteous, "וזה מפני שהצדיקים מקריבים עצמם אל השם יתברך" (1038, 70:15): "because the righteous offer themselves to G-d." Study of the laws of offerings is the same return.

**Proposed MH-S21 "Faith is the cleaving".** Inside: not a belief about G-d held at a distance, but the bond itself. The 613 commands were condensed by David to eleven, by Isaiah to six, by Micah to three, by Isaiah to two, and by Habakkuk to one: "the righteous lives by his faith". Every good deed joins a person to G-d in one respect: "וכל אחד לפי המעשה הטוב שלו קונה הדבקות בו יתברך. כי אין ראוי שיהיה הדבקות אחד לדברים מחולקים" (802, 54:2): "each by his good deed gains cleaving to Him; for cleaving should not be one thing for separate deeds." Faith is different: "כי אין עצם האמונה רק הדבקות בו יתברך, שהוא מאמין בו, ומתדבק בו יתברך באמונתו" (816, 55:6): "for the essence of faith is only cleaving to Him: he believes in Him and cleaves to Him by his faith." "והאמונה היא עצם הדבקות, ולא נחשב זה דבקות במה" (816): "and faith is the essence of cleaving; it is not a cleaving in some one thing." How to enter: when you cannot do much, or cannot feel much, say "I believe in You" slowly, and know that saying it is itself being joined. Source: 801-816. Map: close to MH-S1 Faith as solid ground (Gevurot Hashem), but here faith is the joining itself, not the ground under it.

**Proposed MH-S22 "Nothing between".** Inside: standing before His very self with no screen, image or helper in between. "ה' מי יגור באהלך" asks "מי הוא שאפשר להתדבק עם השם יתברך, ולא יהיה לו דבר שמונע החבור, ונחשב כאילו הוא דר באהלו" (804, 54:4): "who can cleave to G-d with nothing holding back the joining, as if he dwelt in His tent." Only after this comes the second stage, "ומי ישכון בהר קדשך": "כלומר שיהיה לו התעלות אל השם יתברך" (804): "that is, that he will rise up to G-d." The same rule is behind 565 ("His very self, and no go-between") and 698 (no image even for Him). How to enter: at the start of prayer, set aside every picture and every middle helper, and turn to Him directly: "You." Source: 565, 698, 804. Map: near S60 and MH-S9 Nothing shared.

## 4.1 His tent, then His mountain (`before_whom`; new: `mh_tent_then_mountain`)

Two stages from Psalm 15 (804). First remove what stands between: in body (whole), in soul (doing justice), in mind (truth in the heart), in speech; then with others; then with G-d (806-809). Only then rise. How: in prayer, first a short, honest check: "Is anything between us today?" Clear it with a word of return. Then go on to praise. Source: 804-810.

## 4.2 Rise for the Shechina (`act`; `mh_rise_for_the_shechina`, from the first half)

The second half adds why Rav Yosef said it of his mother: "ומתחברת לבנה כמו השכינה שמתחברת לנבראים" (634, 41:2): "she joins her son as the Shechina joins the creatures." Parents are a particular cause, as Shabbat shows the general Cause (636-637), and honoring them shows that even each one's coming into being is not by chance. How: stand when a parent comes in, and silently name whom you stand for. Caution (ours): this honors parents; it does not ask anyone to accept harm.

## 4.3 Drag it to the study house (`own_words` / `act`; new: `mh_drag_to_the_study_house`)

"כל הגדול מחברו, יצרו גדול ממנו" (735, 48:11): "whoever is greater than his fellow, his inclination is greater than he." The Maharal explains the inclination as "כי היצר הרע באדם הוא החסרון וההעדר הדבק בנמצאים" (739, 48:15): "the lack and absence that clings to beings", which "renews itself against him every day and seeks to kill him, and if not for the Holy One helping him, he could not stand against it" (739). The counsel: "אם פגע בך מנוול זה" "משכהו לבית המדרש. אם אבן הוא - נמוח, ואם ברזל הוא - מתפוצץ" (736, 48:12): "if this ugly one meets you, drag him to the study house. If he is stone he dissolves, if iron he shatters." How: when a pull you do not want comes, do not argue with it; open a page of Torah, or say a verse you know by heart, and ask G-d for help, since without His help no one can stand (739). Source: 735-739.

## 4.4 Do what you can (`act`; new: `mh_do_what_you_can`)

The porter's parable (829). A man with little money says, "I will never be rich", and so he wastes all he has. A wise man says: "If I cannot be rich, I can be middling, and at least" "מכל מקום לא אהיה חוזר על הפתחים" (829, 56:13): "at any rate I will not go begging from door to door." So with learning in a weak generation: "כאשר ידעו בעצמם כי לא יגיעו אל המעלה שהגיעו אליה הראשונים בחכמתן, מפני קוטן שכלם, נתיאשו מן הדבר לגמרי" (829): "when they knew they would not reach the level the first ones reached, because their minds were small, they gave up on the thing entirely." Against this: "אם ראית דור שנתייאשו מן התורה, עמוד התחזק בה, ואת מקבל שכר כלם" (842, 56:26): "if you see a generation that has given up on the Torah, stand and hold fast to it, and you receive the reward of them all." How: choose a small, fixed portion you can keep and review. "וכלל הדבר, כל מה שלמד האדם לא יוסר ממנו ולא יאבד מה שטרח וקנה, ובזה יהיה עולה מדרגה אחר מדרגה" (842): "the rule: let nothing a person learned leave him; so he rises level after level." End with his prayer: "והוא יתברך המשלים חסרון ברואיו, ישלים חסרוננו" (842): "and may He, who completes what His creatures lack, complete our lack."

## 4.5 One mitzvah, and the light one (`act`; new: `mh_light_as_heavy`)

"כל העושה מצוה אחת מטיבין לו" (773, 51:2): "whoever does one mitzvah is done good to": for a single person, a single mitzvah is a whole. And no one knows which mitzvah joins most: "כלל הדבר, במה שהמצות מביאים אל האדם החבור והדבקות בו יתברך" [לכך לא] "נוכל לדעת ולהבין איזה מצוה" [סבה לדבקות הוא יותר] (896, 61:8): "since the mitzvot bring a person joining and cleaving to Him, we cannot know which mitzvah does it more." The effort still counts: "לפום צערא אגרא" (891): "the reward is by the effort." How: once a day, do one small mitzvah as if it were the great one, with full attention. Source: 773, 889-896.

## 4.6 Join the order when alone or uneasy (`act`; new: `mh_join_the_order`; guard)

"One who walks on the road with no companion should busy himself with Torah." The road is away from settled life, so danger; learning joins him to the order of being: "וכאשר יעסוק בתורה יש לו חבור ודבוק אל סדר המציאות, והוא השומר אותו מכל רע" (908, 62:12): "and when he busies himself with Torah he is joined and cleaves to the order of being, and it guards him from all harm." The same for aches of head, throat, bowels, bones and body: "והיא סם החיים לכל גופו" (911): "it is an elixir of life for his whole body." Guard (his own): this is said only of one who "feels an ache", "כי לא אמר רק 'חש', והיינו קודם שחלה" (912, 62:16): "that is, before he fell ill"; for real illness "צריך כאן זכות גדול לשנות הטבע, והוא כמו נס" (912): "great merit is needed to change nature, and it is like a miracle." (Ours) So this practice is never instead of medical care; the guide gives no health advice.

## 4.7 Learn at night (`act`; new: `mh_night_joining`)

"Who stand in the house of G-d by night" are those who learn at night: "לפי שהלילה מיוחד להתחברות עם השם יתברך ביותר" (1036, 70:13): "because the night is set apart for joining with G-d the most." How: keep a short time of learning after dark, even a few lines. Moses learned the written Torah by day and the oral by night (1004). Caution (ours): not at the cost of needed sleep.

## 4.8 Offer yourself (`heart_after`; with `mh_back_to_beginning`)

The offering is the caused returning to the Cause (1037), and the righteous "offer themselves" (1038). Learning the laws of an offering is offering it (1032-1035): "וכשהוא עוסק בתורה, דבר זה הוא הסתלקות הגוף, ואז אין נמצא החטא שהוא מצד החומר" (1034, 70:11): "and when he busies himself with Torah, this is the body stepping aside, and then sin, which comes from matter, is not found." How: at the end of learning or prayer, say: "I return to You what came from You." Caution (ours): the offering of oneself here is inner and in learning; it never means harming oneself (see the guard at Netzach Yisrael 252).

## 5. The big human questions

**Does G-d love me only when I am good?** "Anochi" means He is our G-d from His very self, not through mercy that comes and goes (542). So "there is no forgetting it" (543).

**Why is there so much dispute, even among the wise?** Disputes of the sages are like thin leaves of a great tree: "לא יחייב באילן שהוא בעל שרשים הרבה מאד, ונופו חזק ועב מאד, שלא יהיו לו גם כן עלים ושרוגים דקים חלשים" (1016, 69:4): "a tree with very many roots and a strong, thick crown will surely also have thin, weak leaves and twigs." The Torah's own aim is the opposite of strife: "שכל ענינה לעשות קשר וסדר ואחדות בין הבריות" (1018, 69:6): "whose whole aim is to make bond, order and oneness among people."

**Is there peace in this world?** "כי השלום אינו מענין העולם הזה, רק הכל במחלוקת" (764, 50:6): "for peace is not of this world's nature; here all is in dispute." Peace, like the Torah, rain and mercy, comes as a gift (762-770). "ומצד עצמו של אדם אין לו תשועה" (765): "and from his own side a person has no salvation."

**If I have failed badly, is there a way back?** The golden calf and David's sin were not decreed; what was decreed was that return should exist: "רק היתה הגזרה מן השם יתברך על התשובה שתהיה נמצאת, לא על החטא כלל" (729, 48:5): "rather G-d's decree was that return should exist, not about the sin at all." Return means "כי אחר שחטא האדם, ישוב אל התחלתו כאשר היה בראשונה" (731, 48:7): "that after a person has sinned, he returns to his beginning as he was at first." And leaving the bodily "comes only from the upper world" (851), so the one who returns stands where the fully righteous cannot (851).

**What stays of me after death?** Burial and shrouds show that nothing of a person is fully erased: "כי כבוד האדם וצלמו האלהית לא יקבל העדר גמור" (790, 52:9): "for a person's honor and his divine image do not receive full absence."

**Why serve without a promise?** "כי ראוי לעבוד למלך גדול שלא על מנת לקבל פרס" (861, 58:3): "for one should serve a great King not in order to receive a reward." What good we get in this world is His kindness, not wages: "כל מה שעשיתי בעולם - עשיתי עמך חנם, אבל שכרך מתוקן לעולם הבא" (879, 60:2): "all I did for you in the world I did for free; your reward is prepared for the world to come."

**Am I alone?** "כי כלל מין האדם נחשב כמו אדם אחד" (722, 47:13): "for all humankind counts as one person"; each great person is like a limb of Adam.

**Why must I finish what G-d made?** "כי כל הדברים הם שיושלמו על ידי האדם השכלי" (1023, 69:11): "for all things are to be completed by man, who has a mind." Wheat must be ground; the oral Torah clarifies the written.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **"He alone is being, and there is nothing besides Him"** (553) and **"He is all"** (696) are the closest the Maharal comes to the Tanya's "ein od milvado" (Gate of Unity ch. 6). He says it as a matter of faith and of the second commandment. He does not build it on creation by speech renewed every moment, as the Tanya does.
- **"Anochi" as His very self, beyond the attributes** (542-545) is close to Tanya ch. 20 ("Anochi" and "Lo yihyeh" contain the whole Torah) and to the Chabad teaching that the essence is above all attributes. The Maharal's reason (attributes come from acts, acts are in time) is the Rambam's.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The three holinesses (541) are a clear use of negative description: not a body, not a power in a body, not tied to a body. The Maharal keeps the Rambam here while elsewhere (463-477) refusing to read "He came down" as mere figure.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not present. The nearest is 869: He acts in the world because He is apart from it. The Tanya would say He acts through concealment of His light; the Maharal says through distance of kind. Keep them distinct.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). 696 ("He is all") and 858 ("though He is apart, He is not apart from this world") together come near both halves. They are still two sentences, not one.
- **"The change is only on the side of the receivers"** (844, 604, 659) is the same principle as in the first half and in Netzach Yisrael 719, and the same as the Tanya's Gate of Unity ch. 7.
- **No go-between** (565, 698) matches the Tanya's warning against making any middle power into a lord, and the Chabad stress on turning to the Essence.
- **Faith as the essence of cleaving** (816) is close to the Chabad teaching that faith reaches higher than understanding.
