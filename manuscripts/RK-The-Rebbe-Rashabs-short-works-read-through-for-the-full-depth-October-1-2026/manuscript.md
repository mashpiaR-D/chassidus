## What this slice adds (one page)

The Rebbe Rashab's short works add the inside of the climb: how a person actually gets from knowing 'nothing but Him' to living it, and why it so often fails. Five findings. (1) The hiding is His. Nature is not water over a sunk thing; He made the cover, so G-d in nature is G-d beyond nature, and solidity itself is made of His hiding, which is His kindness (Umaayan 23-25). (2) Near is quiet. Fire-love cries 'Abba' from a distance; when one feels that nothing hides Him and time and space are not real limits, love becomes water: full, still, whispered, as in the Amidah (Eitz HaChaim 2). (3) Reach above your level. The lower unity alone slips into 'somethingness'; everyone must touch the upper unity a little, and it is true for that hour (Eitz HaChaim 7-10). (4) The essence moves only through the narrow place. Love is the soul's own pull, like a son's, not a product of thinking; but great arousals change nothing unless the pain reaches the animal soul itself and daily work prepared the ground (HaAvodah 5). Fear, the plain 'one may not', touches the Essence; love touches lights (HaAvodah 3). (5) Under reason is a spring. Where you are stuck, don't argue; act from the soul's stubborn strength, which outlasts reason, and understanding returns (Umaayan 26-28). Toras Shalom in this file is the Rebbe Rashab's halachic responsa; it adds little to depth beyond one line: before the Essence wisdom and speech are equal (OC 4).

**For the guide (ours).** Three things the descent did not have. First, a reason the hiding is safe to look at: it is His, made by Him, and kind (RK-C01, C02). Second, the sound of nearness is silence, not intensity; when a person's 'fire' quiets, that may be arrival, not loss (C03). Third, an honest permission: 'this is not my level' is not a reason to stop; touching the top for one hour is true and keeps the lower work from falling (C05). For someone stuck, the Rashab's counsel is not more thinking but a plain, stubborn 'no' from under reason (C12), and patience with one trait (C10, C18).

## RK-C01 · Kuntres Umaayan, maamar 25, ch. 1-3 (lines 514-536)

1. **There is G-d hidden in nature and G-d out in the open. The difference is only for us. For G-d it makes no difference at all whether He wears nature's clothes or not.** (M12) *How:* The text reads 'You are a G-d who hides' and sets two opposite levels, nature and above nature, then says they differ only from the creature's side. «דלאלקות אין הבדל כלל אם הוא בלבושי הטבע או שהוא גלוי בלא לבוש הטבע» — *for G-dliness there is no difference at all whether it is in the garments of nature or revealed without the garment of nature* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 517

2. **The word for nature, teva, means 'sunk', as in 'they sank in the Red Sea'. A thing sunk in the sea is covered by water so you cannot tell it is there.** (M3) *How:* It lays a foundation from the plain meaning of the word before going further. «שהדבר שנטבע בים הנה מי הים מכסים עליו שאינו ניכר כלל» — *a thing that sank in the sea: the sea water covers it so that it cannot be seen at all* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 518

3. **But covering is not erasing. The sunk thing is fully there, as real as if it lay in the open.** (M4) *How:* It turns the picture: what looks like absence is only cover. «אין הכיסוי מבטל מציאותו כלל» — *the covering does not cancel its existence at all* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 518

4. **Then the text breaks its own parable. The sea did not make the sunk thing, and the sunk thing did not make the sea. G-d made the very clothes that cover Him, and He keeps them alive.** (M10) *How:* It says outright 'this is not a true parable' and makes the failure of the image the lesson. «לא המים יצרו את הדבר הנטבע בהם» — *the water did not create the thing sunk in it* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 519

5. **So the G-d dressed in nature is the very same G-d who is above nature. Nature itself is beyond nature.** (M12) *How:* Because He made and enlivens the cover, the covered and the uncovered are one. «האלקות המלובש בטבע הוא עצמו האלקות שלמעלה מהטבע» — *the G-dliness clothed in nature is itself the G-dliness above nature* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 519

6. **Why don't we feel this? Habit. The sun rises in the east and sets in the west every day, so it stops moving us. Only slow, deep thinking shows that nature itself is a G-dly thing.** (M16) *How:* The idea is not new; what changes is moving it from known to felt, against the dulling of repetition. «מצד הרגילות והתמידות שהשמש זורח במזרח ושוקע במערב» — *because of habit and constancy, that the sun shines in the east and sets in the west* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 520

7. **Now it lands in your workday. Your business is only a garment, a messenger. If your whole mind goes into the deal and pushes out prayer and Torah, you have bowed your head to the garment.** (M15) *How:* From 'the stars are only messengers' the text draws the deed: overwork is a quiet bowing to the tool. «כשמניח שכלו ראשו ורובו בעסק משא ומתן עד שמונע אותו מתורה ועבודה, הרי זה גם כן ענין השתחואה ברוחניות» — *when he puts his mind, head and most of himself into trade until it keeps him from Torah and prayer, that too is a bowing in the spirit* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 536

**Bottom:** Nature itself is G-d beyond nature, only covered, and the cover is His own (stated, U519-520).

**Return:** Make a vessel for livelihood, but a small one; keep prayer and fixed learning first; know who gives through the garment (stated, U525-541).

## RK-C02 · Kuntres Umaayan, maamarim 23-24 (lines 479-512)

**Start:** Why does good come to us through nature and the stars?

1. **For anything to become physical, the light must be hidden, layer under layer. Every 'something' is made by hiding.** (M14) *How:* It asks why blessing must pass through so many garments, and answers with a general rule. «וכידוע דכל ענין המציאות נעשה על ידי הסתר» — *as is known, all existence comes about through concealment* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 481

2. **When light shines openly there is no 'something' at all. Open light cancels existence.** (M4) *How:* The reverse side of the rule: revelation and solid existence cannot sit together. «דכאשר האור מאיר בגילוי אז אין שום מציאות» — *when the light shines openly there is no existence* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 483

3. **So the more hidden, the more solid. How real a thing feels is a measure of how much is covering it.** (M13) *How:* All the levels of worlds are gathered into one scale: more cover, more 'yesh'. «וכל שההסתר יותר נעשה יותר בחינת מציאות הישות» — *the more concealment, the more it becomes the existence of 'somethingness'* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 483

4. **The laws of nature and the stars are the thickest cover, like sackcloth. They hide so well that people mistake the garment for the giver.** (M11) *How:* This is the root of idol worship and of overwork: the cover is real, but its power is borrowed. «שהמזלות הם בבחינת לבוש שק» — *the constellations are like a garment of sackcloth* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 507

5. **But the hiding itself is His kindness. Without it we could not receive a good that fits our size.** (M11) *How:* The thing that seemed to block G-d is shown to be His love: a good above nature would not fit creatures in nature. «רק צריכים לידע שזהו החסד עליון הנקצב לנו מאת ה' דוקא הוא אשר נתלבש בלבושים הנ"ל אשר זה עצמו הוא מחסד ה' עלינו» — *one only needs to know that it is the higher kindness allotted to us by G-d that dressed itself in these garments, and that this itself is G-d's kindness to us* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 508

6. **A father who wants to play with his small son makes himself small and plays with nuts, even with the empty shells. The child does not know the fruit inside; to him the shell is the kindness.** (M9) *How:* A picture from every home makes the hiding feel like love, not distance. «כמו לשחק עמו באגוזים ועוד בקליפות האגוזים» — *like playing with him with nuts, and even with the nutshells* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 499

7. **So don't praise the tool. The stars are an axe in the builder's hand; no one admires the axe for the building.** (M15) *How:* It returns to the person: thank the hand, not the instrument. «הם רק כגרזן ביד החוצב בו» — *they are only like an axe in the hand of the one who hews with it* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 475

**Bottom:** The world's solidness is His hiding, and His hiding is His kindness (stated, U481-508).

**Return:** Use the physical good for Torah and mitzvos, which is why it was given (stated, U500-503); in every good thing, look past the tool to the hand.

## RK-C03 · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim, ch. 2 (lines 138-157) [OCR]

**Start:** All the worlds are only a glimmer of Him.

1. **Start from the distance: all worlds, with all their space and time, are only a glimmer, with no comparison to His Essence.** (M5) *How:* The first kind of love comes from feeling how far the worlds are from Him. «וכאשר יתבונן בכ"ז איך שכ"ז חוא הארה לבד ואין ערך כלל לגבי עצמות» — *when he contemplates how all this is only a glimmer, with no comparison to the Essence* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 146 (OCR)

2. **Even space is held inside Him: 'here is a place with Me'. Place is nullified to Him; He is not in place.** (M6) *How:* From things in place, to the place itself, to the One who holds place. «חנה מקום אתי שחמקום במל אליו ית'» — *'Here is a place with Me': place is nullified to Him* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 146 (OCR)

3. **From that distance a fire is lit: 'Whom do I have in heaven? With You I want nothing on earth', neither spiritual nor physical pleasure.** (M7) *How:* The heart moves from what it enjoys to the One beyond all enjoying; this is love like flames of fire. «מי לי בשמים ועמך לא חפצתי בארץ» — *whom do I have in heaven, and with You I want nothing on earth* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 146 (OCR)

4. **But in truth, time and space down here are not real limits at all.** (M4) *How:* The text overturns the first picture of 'far' and 'glimmer'. «והזמן ומקום שלממה אינו בבחי' חנכבלת זמן ומקום כלל באמת» — *the time and space below are not truly bounded by time and space at all* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

5. **Proof: in the Temple the place of the Ark took up no space; and Adam was shown every generation in a short time. That is how the whole world truly is.** (M3) *How:* It lays a foundation from two known cases where the hiding was lifted. «מקום הארון אינו מן המדה» — *the place of the Ark was not part of the measurement* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

6. **There is truly nothing that hides from Him. The Infinite shines openly even here below.** (M4) *How:* The limits are real only from our side, because the light is not felt in us. «אבל באמת אין שום דבר חמעלים ומפתיר כלל לגבי' ית'» — *but in truth nothing at all hides or conceals before Him* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

7. **When you feel that nearness, you don't burn or thirst. You become still. This is love like water: it cools the fire.** (M16) *How:* The same truth, now felt, changes the shape of the love from reaching to resting. «אינו בבתי' צמאון ובכחי' רשפי אש כלל כ"א בבחי' ביפול במציאות לגמרי» — *it is not thirst or flames of fire at all, but complete self-nullification in being* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

8. **A son who is still a little far from his father cries 'Abba, Abba!' with great feeling and thirst.** (M9) *How:* A picture every person knows from childhood. «שצועק אבא אבא בהתפעלות גדולה ובצמאון גדול» — *that he cries 'Father, Father!' with great excitement and great thirst* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

9. **Right next to his father, his heart is full of love, but nothing shows outside the heart.** (M10) *How:* The parable turns: more love shows less. Nearness is quiet. «תוכו רצוף אהבה אבל .אינה נראית ונגלית חוץ מן הלב» — *its inside is lined with love, but it is not seen or revealed outside the heart* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

10. **That is why the Amidah is whispered. In Pesukei d'Zimra you are still far and burning; in the Amidah you stand before the King, say 'Blessed are You', bow, and go quiet.** (M15) *How:* The inner state is mapped onto the order of prayer. «שמו"ץ שבלחש בחשאי» — *the Amidah, which is in a whisper, in silence* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 150 (OCR)

11. **After the Amidah you go back to how you were. The fix is Torah study, even the laws about plain physical things, which keeps that stillness nailed in all day.** (M15) *How:* It does not stop at the peak; it asks how the peak survives the day, and answers with study of practical law. «קבוע. ונטוע במסמרות בל ימוט לנצח כל היום בעפקו בתורה» — *fixed and planted with nails, never to move, all day in his Torah study* · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR).txt, line 153 (OCR)

**Bottom:** Nothing hides Him, so nearness is silent: the cry of 'Abba' gives way to a full, quiet heart (stated, E150).

**Return:** Say the Amidah as a whisper before the King; then learn practical law so the stillness stays all day (stated, E150-153).

## RK-C04 · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim, ch. 6 (lines 196-211) [OCR]

## RK-C05 · Kuntres Eitz HaChaim, ch. 7-10 (lines 212-257) [OCR]

## RK-C06 · Kuntres HaAvodah, ch. 5 (lines 161-176) [OCR]

**Start:** Love for G-d comes from thinking about Him.

1. **The word for love, ahava, comes from 'avah', will. Will is deeper than feelings and can even change them: one who fasts on Shabbos because fasting is his pleasure does not feel the hunger as pain.** (M2) *How:* It gives the inner meaning under the plain word. «וכנידץ בענין תעגית הלום בשבת מפגי שהוא תענוג לו» — *as known about a dream-fast on Shabbos, because it is a pleasure to him* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 162 (OCR)

## RK-D10 · The moisture of the deep (Rebbe Rayatz, letter of 1931 printed with Kuntres HaAvodah)

**Teaching.** There is a love that does nothing and still works: like the deep springs that never come to the surface, yet because they are there the earth is moist and things can grow. The eternal love works by being there, not by acting. (stated by the Rebbe Rayatz, in the letter appended to Kuntres HaAvodah; not the Rebbe Rashab's own text)

**Hisbonenus (as the text gives it; steps ours in wording).**

1. Picture deep springs far under the ground, never seen.
2. See the soil above them: damp, able to grow.
3. There is no action between them, like two neighbors in one house who affect each other just by living there.
4. So the deepest love in you does not need to 'do'; its being there keeps you moist.
5. Trust it on dry days.

**From inside.** 'Feichtkeit', moisture of the earth (A280).

**Lands.** Plant mitzvos in the moist ground; the growth comes from the deep (ours, from A280).

**Extends.** D8 (extends: the bond you cannot let go works silently, by presence)

- «מעינות תהום רבה הנוזלים עמוק עמוק בבטן האדמה» — *the springs of the great deep that flow far down in the belly of the earth* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 280 (OCR) - «הנה טבע הארץ להיות רטובה» — *the nature of the earth is to be moist* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 280 (OCR) - «הגה מעצם מציאות היותה נמצאת במציאות נמצא היא פועלת תמיד» — *by the very fact that it exists, it is always acting* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 281 (OCR)

## RK-D11 · Strip the picture, keep a thin veil

**Teaching.** You come to a G-dly matter through examples and details; then you put them down and come to the thing itself, which has no such shape. But some thin veil always stays. At the top you know Him by what He is not, and you bow. (stated, Kuntres HaAvodah 1)

**Hisbonenus (as the text gives it; steps ours in wording).**

1. Explain the matter to yourself with every example you have.
2. Now set the examples down one by one.
3. Tell yourself: it is not like this picture.
4. Notice the thin veil that remains, and accept it.
5. Let the knowing turn into a bow.

**From inside.** The natural senses falling quiet (A40); taking a G-dly matter 'altogether differently' (A53).

**Lands.** The bow of the Amidah (A53); then everyday understanding changes too.

**Extends.** D5 (sharpens: the end of knowing is reached by stripping pictures, and it ends in a bow, not a blank)

- «ואח"כ שולל ומפשיט ומניח את הענינים שבהם הוא משיג את הענין ובא אל עצם הדבר המושג» — *then he negates, strips and sets aside what he grasped it through, and comes to the thing itself* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 42 (OCR) - «כי כאמת אין ביכולתו לתפוס ולהשיג ענין אלקי בלי שום לבוש» — *for in truth he cannot grasp a G-dly matter without any garment* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 46 (OCR) - «והו"ע ודיעת השלילה והכרת ההפלא' ובהי' ההתאמתו' דראיית עין השכל» — *the knowledge of negation, recognizing the wonder, the mind's eye seeing* · Kuntres HaAvodah (Lubavitch, 1911).txt, line 51 (OCR)

## RK-D12 · Feeling is knowing

**Teaching.** Daas is not information; it is feeling the thing. A gifted child knows everything and feels nothing; a cruel person knows the poor man's pain and doesn't feel it. Feeling G-d's height is the same act as feeling the lowness of the bad, and only that gives real choice. (stated, Kuntres HaTefillah 5-9)

**Hisbonenus (as the text gives it; steps ours in wording).**

1. Take one truth you 'know' about G-d.
2. Ask: do I feel it, or only understand it?
3. Bind your mind to the thing itself, not to the explanation; let the explanations become a passage.
4. Let the heart hear it by itself.
5. Then look at your one bad trait in that light, and feel it as low.

**From inside.** 'Add knowledge, add pain' (T78): feeling hurts and heals.

**Lands.** Many prayers aimed at one trait until it bends (T104).

**Extends.** new (a missing step between 'knowing nothing but Him' and change: knowing must become feeling to move the will)

- «וכמו תינוק חכים שמבין הכל ומ"מ אינו מרגיש עצם הענין» — *like a clever child who understands everything yet does not feel the thing itself* · Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt, line 61 (OCR) - «וכמו יופיף דעת יוסיף מכאוב» — *as 'he who adds knowledge adds pain'* · Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt, line 78 (OCR) - «וכמה שמרגיש את העילוי דאלקות ה"ה מרגיש את השפלות והפחיתות של הרע» — *as much as he feels the height of G-dliness, he feels the lowness of the bad* · Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt, line 80 (OCR)

## RK-D13 · The highest fell furthest

**Teaching.** A stone from the top of a wall lands furthest away. Man got the coarsest body because his soul is the highest, and only it can refine the coarsest stuff. So a person can be worse than a beast and higher than an angel. (stated, Umaayan 15)

**Hisbonenus (as the text gives it; steps ours in wording).**

1. Look at what is coarsest in you.
2. Don't conclude you are low; conclude how high you fell from.
3. Know you were given this body because you can refine it.
4. Grasp one G-dly truth inside this coarse world; that lifts the world, which angels can't do.
5. Turn one piece of darkness into light today.

**From inside.** Stones falling from a wall (U305).

**Lands.** The body and its appetites are the material (U309); refine them in eating and dealing (U108).

**Extends.** D12 (extends: the deepest lands lowest because it fell from highest)

- «וכל אבן שהיא גבוה גבוה יותר הנה כשנופלת היא בריחוק מן הכותל ביותר» — *the higher the stone, the further from the wall it lands* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 305 - «לפי שבכחו לברר ולזכך את החומר היותר עב והגס ביותר» — *because he has the power to refine the thickest, coarsest matter* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 306 - «ולאהפכא חשוכא של הגוף הגס לנהורא» — *to turn the darkness of the coarse body into light* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 309

## RK-D14 · Before the Essence, high and low are equal

**Teaching.** Before the Essence, wisdom and speech are the same, and wisdom is as 'physical' as deed. Hiding and revealing don't apply to Him. So the simple Jew who says Tehillim without understanding, in pure acceptance of the yoke, stands as near as the scholar, or nearer. (stated, Toras Shalom OC 4; Kuntres HaTefillah 11; Umaayan 15)

**Hisbonenus (as the text gives it; steps ours in wording).**

1. Rank your spiritual 'levels' in your mind.
2. Then learn: before His Essence, the highest wisdom is like a physical act.
3. So no level is nearer by itself; He is not more hidden in the low.
4. Say a few words of prayer simply, even without understanding them.
5. Small and great are equal in pouring out speech to Him.

**From inside.** The simple Jew, ashamed, still singing his Tehillim (U322).

**Lands.** Plain prayer and plain deed; no credit taken (U331-336).

**Extends.** D13 (extends: plainness is nearest because before the Essence the high is as low as the low)

- «דלגבי עצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מסדר השתלשלות הרי החכמה והדבור שוין» — *before the Essence of the Infinite Light, beyond the order of worlds, wisdom and speech are equal* · Toras Shalom (Lubavitch, 1898-1919).txt, line 81 - «שאינו שייך בו ח"ו העלם וגילוי כלל וכלל» — *to whom hiding and revealing do not apply at all* · Kuntres HaTefillah (Lubavitch, 1900).txt, line 91 (OCR) - «קטן וגדול שוים בלי שום חילוק כלל» — *small and great are equal, with no difference at all* · Toras Shalom (Lubavitch, 1898-1919).txt, line 52 - «הרי זה אצלו בלי שום הנאה כלל, כי אם כל זה אצלו בדרך קבלת עול לבד» — *for him it is without any pleasure, only acceptance of the yoke* · Kuntres Umaayan (Lubavitch, 1903).txt, line 320
