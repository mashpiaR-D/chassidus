## The shape of the range

This is not a book about the unity. It is a book about how knowing the unity is made real in a person, and prayer is where that happens. That is why F12 ("know today and return it to your heart") runs through almost every line. It is also why so many lines are *structural*: they build the psychology the F12 teaching needs (Da'at as feeling, mind drawing the heart, "surrounding" against "inner" light, the vessel). The SPINE already warned about works like this (section 5, on Kuntres HaHitpa'alut): the purpose is stated, but most passages are about the mechanics of arousal. Here the purpose is stated more often than there. The Rashab names the content of contemplation at several points: memale and sovev, creation from nothing, the Shema's unity, the Essence before which one stands.

The second strong face is F08, from both sides. Bitul is the goal of contemplation (lines 44-45, 91). Selfhood (yeshut) is named as what poisons learning (lines 72, 73, 81). F11 is the third face. The soul came down to refine the animal soul, and prayer is where this happens (lines 77, 80, 104, 108). The cosmological faces (F01-F05) are few, but they come at the key points: the catalogue of §1, the order of prayer in §10, and the preparation for the Amidah in §11.

## Opening and §1 (41-47): what one contemplates, and where it ends

The occasion is a question: how does one pray (line 41)? The first answer is a list of what is contemplated. It begins with memale, which "any thinking person can understand, even with the natural mind of the animal soul", and goes on to sovev and what is above it, and the Shema (line 44, SPINE anchor F04e): > הן בענין בחי' טמכ"ע אשר כל משכיל יכול להבין הדבר לאשורו נם בשכל הטבעי דנה"ב (line 44)

(OCR: טמכ"ע = ממכ"ע, נם = גם.) The goal of the highest contemplation, "gazing at the King's glory", is that the senses are nullified and the inner heart "sees and feels the essence of Godliness as it is, truly above comprehension": > שעצם פנימיות לבו רואה ומרגיש עצם האלקות כמו שהוא למעלה מהשנה ממש (line 44)

(OCR: מהשנה = מהשגה.) He becomes "like a silent stone" (line 45). Then comes the section's peak, F09 with F05, naming the unity as *yachid* exactly as the SPINE asks (SPINE anchor F09): > עד מה ששלימות עצמותו נושא כל ההפכים והכל בבחי' יחיד ממש (line 46)

## §2-§4 (48-59): contemplation must be detailed and inward

Study is only preparation. The main thing is contemplation in prayer (SPINE anchor for F12): > כי כל הלימוד והידיעת הקודמת היא כהכנה אל העיקר (line 49)

General contemplation "is no contemplation at all", because the light stays "surrounding", over the mind, and never inside it (line 49). Such contemplation does not reach practice and does not subdue the animal soul (line 50). Study alone does not refine evil, except in lofty souls (line 50.2). §3 warns against inventing new readings of the prayer words during prayer (lines 52-55). The link to the unity there is only through method: one should receive, not invent. §4 describes the true toil. It takes an hour or more of attached contemplation, until the light shines in the mind and flows to the heart: > ויאיר האור בגילוי ממש במוחו. ומהמוח נמשך אל הלב שנחקק ונרגש האור כלבו (line 57)

Line 57 also says, of an idea, that "the essence brings its own revelation". Hearing F05 in that phrase is our reading.

## §5-§6 (60-69): Da'at is feeling; the garment as a passage

Da'at is defined as feeling, not only attachment (line 61). The analogies are human: the child, the cruel man, the merciful man. The face that follows is stated. Feeling the Godly matter itself moves the heart. Nearness and greatness give love. Two contemplations give awe, and they are the higher and the lower unity: > וכן כשמתבונן איך שכולא קמי'. כלא חשיב כו' או בהתבוננות איך שאוא"פ נמצא למטה כמו למעלה כו' מתעורר מדת היראה כו' (line 62)

(OCR: אוא"פ = אוא"ס.) Then the F12 verse itself, read as the main point: > ולכן כתי' וידעת היום והשבות אל לבבך דהעיקר הוא ההשבה אל הלב להיות שזהו העבודה העיקרית (line 62)

§6 separates two kinds of arousal. In the lesser kind the heart is moved only by the leftover conclusion, "Godliness is good and wonderful". In the true kind the garments of understanding are "like a passage", and the Godliness itself is felt: > אבל הלבושים הן כעין מעבר והעיקר נרגש אצלו האור האלקי (line 66)

Reading this as F03 (the garment serves the revealing) is ours. Line 67 says this work is near to everyone who has "a mind and a heart".

## §7 (70-73): the warning against "deep matters only"

This is the SPINE's G21 anchor, and it matters for the index as a whole. Some deal only in what is above Atzilut and "the Essence before the tzimtzum", and call creation from nothing, memale and sovev "low matters" (line 71). The Rashab answers: > אשר באמת עיקר העבודה עכשיו הוא. בענינים אלו ביותר (line 71)

The highest matters are "above comprehension itself" and are known only in the heart's understanding (line 72.1). Those who innovate by their own power turn them upside down. Some make them coarse: > ומגשיטים את הדברים מאד בזה שמביאים אותם בישות דהשגה ובלבושי הגשמה (line 72)

For this index the lesson is plain. F01 and F04 are the working faces of prayer. F05 is held back from the grasping mind.

## §8-§9 (74-83): without prayer, knowledge of God turns to selfhood

Prayer is "the life of Torah and mitzvot" (line 75). Knowledge of God lasts only if it is engraved in prayer (line 75.2), and then it lasts "with an eternal existence" (line 77). The soul came down to refine the animal soul: > אשר ע"ז הוא תכלית ירידת הנשטה בנוף בכדי לברר את הנה"ב לזכך את המדות טבעיים ולאכפיי' לסט"א (line 77)

(OCR: הנשטה בנוף = הנשמה בגוף.) Line 77.4 is the tract's F11 at its fullest. When the contemplation is of matters the natural mind can grasp, the animal soul itself comes to understand and feel the Godly matter, and is moved to love. It is turned, not only overpowered. §9 then makes the sharpest claim in the range: > ולבן יכול להיות שגם אם לומד דא"ח הרבה והוא רחוק בעצמו לגמרי מאלקות ואדרבה להיפך כו' (line 80)

Without prayer, what he learned becomes poison (SPINE anchor F08): > ובהעדר העבודה בתפלה גם מה שלמר וידץ נהפך לו לפה"מ בקרבו ר"ל שנעשה עי"ז יש וגסה"ר ביותר (line 81)

## §10 (84-86): the order of prayer as a ladder of the unity

This is the densest cosmological passage in the range. Pesukei deZimra covers the greatness of Ein Sof in bringing things into being, and the nullification of the outer side of the worlds (F01). The blessings of the Shema cover the angels' nullification (F08). Shema Yisrael covers the lower unity, called *yachid*: > איך שהוא אחד בז' רקיעים וארץ וד' רוחות העולם בבחינת יחיד ממש (line 85)

Baruch shem teaches that all the worlds come from Malchut, "a glow of a glow of a glow" (line 85.5, F02 + F01). The ladder ends in "one will for God alone" and a life near to Godliness "in all his affairs". That last phrase is F07 on our reading.

## §11 (87-93): the vessel and the dwelling; standing before the Essence

Here F07 is stated, at the scale of one person. Each person must be clean like a king's dwelling: > שכאו"א צ"ל כלי ומשכן לאור ה' וקדושתו (line 89)

If he is unrefined, the Torah he learns is in exile in him, and he causes the exile of the Shechinah above (line 89.2, F03 + F11). If he is broken-hearted, "then he becomes a vessel for God's light to dwell within him" (line 89.3). The soul's "root of its root" is in the essence of Ein Sof (line 90.2, F06 + F05). Before the Amidah he contemplates that even the highest levels are nothing before Him (line 90.4). Then comes the key line of the range for F05: > ועצמות המאור הוא בהתגלות שנמצא בכל מקום ומקום ממש (line 91)

Hiding and revealing "do not apply to it at all". Standing before this, he is in inner bitul, "the main point of the Amidah" (line 91.2; OCR בימול = ביטול).

## §12-§18 (94-123): diagnoses, the yoke, and the close

The rest is mostly structural and F11: the student who is "whole in himself" (line 100), the man who serves but has not refined his particular traits (line 104), the young (line 108), businessmen (line 115), and halacha study (lines 117-120, marked *none*). Three points are stated. First, a true feeling of Godliness makes evil felt as its opposite (line 104). Second, "the true vessel for inner Godliness is the broken heart" (line 100). Third, the yoke of heaven means knowing a Master "who makes His kingship one over each person" (line 111): > שיש לו אדון הוא ה' אלקינו שמייחד מלכותו יח' על כאו"א בפרט (line 111)

The close returns to F12, "to learn to know God", but in order to do (line 122).

## The subject index (124-253)

All 130 index lines are *none*: they are lists. A few entries point to unity passages ("the unity of God" at line 128, "the Essence is revealed in every place" at 177, memale and sovev at 185, the Shema at 224 and 248). They are noted in the "how" field and add no tie of their own.

## What this book adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **Where the unity must be known: prayer, not study.** Tanya 41-42 place contemplation before every mitzvah and in Da'at in general. The Rashab makes prayer the one place where knowing becomes real. Study of Chassidus without prayer can leave a man "far from Godliness, and the very opposite" (line 80), and turns into poison (line 81). The SPINE keeps this as the dispute "prayer or action and spreading outward" (Rashab against the Rebbe). This range gives the Rashab's side in full.
- **Torah's power to refine is limited.** Tanya 40 and 52-53 make Torah the dwelling of the Shechinah below. The Rashab says refinement "from above" through Torah works only in lofty souls. For most souls it must come "from below", through prayer (lines 50, 80). This does not contradict the Tanya, but the stress is different.
- **The F12 mechanism in detail.** The Tanya says Da'at is binding the mind "with a strong and firm bond" (Tanya 42). The Rashab adds that Da'at is *feeling*, a brain of its own. He adds how the mind draws the heart, and how the garments of understanding become "a passage" (lines 61-66). This is F12's psychology spelled out, and the Tanya has no such detail.
- **The animal soul turned, not only subdued.** Contemplating memale, which the natural mind can grasp, lets the animal soul itself feel the Godly matter and love (line 77.4). This joins F04 to F11 in a way the Tanya's Part I only sketches (Tanya 9 subdues; its turning, in ch. 53, is for the tzaddik).
- **"Yachid" for the lower unity.** The Rashab calls God's oneness "in the seven heavens and earth" *yachid* (line 85). Line 46 uses *yachid* of the Essence that bears all opposites.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Most of the tract is the mechanics of prayer: distractions, standing still, the siddur, halacha study, businessmen's schedules. These lines are *structural* or *none*. The tie is carried by the frame (F12), not by the line. - The eight *reading* ties are ours: "the essence brings its revelation" as F05 (57.2), surrender to the Rebbeim's words as bitul (72.2), "near to Godliness in all his affairs" and the day's "impression" as F07 (85.6, 115.2), makif and pnimi as F04 (88.1), the teaching that "does not become one with him" as F08 (100.1), physical matters "in the divine mind" as F07 (111.2), and "whole in every detail" (122.2). - F07 in its full sense (the Essence's dwelling in the lowest world) is never stated. It appears only in the person as "vessel and dwelling" (line 89). The "purpose of the descent" (lines 77, 104, 108) is stated as refinement (F11). Reading it as the dwelling is ours. - The OCR is heavy in places. Lines 14-15, 26-39 and 76 are unreadable, and the index columns are merged. No tie was read into garbled text.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Knowledge or faith (F12).** The Alter Rebbe ends Shaar HaYichud 7 with belief "above comprehension". The Rashab too puts the Essence above comprehension (line 72). But his remedy is not faith. It is deeper, inward comprehension felt in the heart ("known in the inner Binah", line 72; "sees and feels the essence of Godliness", line 44). He also warns against reaching for the Essence with the mind at all (line 71).
- **Who can do it.** Line 67 says true contemplation is "near to anyone with a mind and heart". Line 50 and §12-§14 say most people fail, and that the young should not attempt it before their time. The tract holds both, as the SPINE's "who can hold the higher unity" dispute does.
- **Study against prayer.** The SPINE records that the Rebbe puts action and spreading outward first. This tract puts prayer first and study below it. The range gives only the Rashab's side, stated strongly (lines 75, 80-81).
- **The kitzurim are the Rayatz's.** They sometimes sharpen the Rashab. The G21 anchor (line 73) is in fact the Rayatz's summary of lines 71-72, not the Rashab's own words. The Rashab's own sentence is line 72.3, which this report quotes above.
