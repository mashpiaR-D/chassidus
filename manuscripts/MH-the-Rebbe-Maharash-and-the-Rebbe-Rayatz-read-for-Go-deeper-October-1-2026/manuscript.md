## What this slice adds to the full depth (one page)

The two Rebbeim of this slice keep asking one question that DESCENT left open: **what kind of 'nothing' is meant, where is it true, and who can reach it?** Their answers push the depth downward and inward, not higher.

1. **'Nothing' has two meanings.** From His side, the world comes from a ray that takes no room, so nothing in Him changed. From our side, 'nothing' means what we cannot grasp, like our own soul: nearer than anything, certain, never seen. We call it nothing 'only because we do not know what it is' (Maharash, MH-C11, MH-D1). *stated*
2. **The strongest 'nothing else' is true only below.** 'All counts as nothing' is a candle at noon: still there. 'There is nothing else' is true only of the lowest created things, because their whole being is the Essence (Rayatz, MH-C01, MH-D2). The deepest unity is in the table, not above it. *stated*
3. **The soul is a part, not a ray; distance strengthens its pull.** A ray rises only while attached; a separated part longs more. The soul's essence is hidden, which is exactly why it can live in a body. 'I love you as you are and where you are' (MH-C05, MH-D3). *stated*
4. **Under yechidah is a spark that is G-dliness itself.** Even yechidah is 'only a name'. Under it is a spark from 'the lowest level in the Creator'. Teshuvah reaches it and fills every gap (MH-C08, MH-D4). *stated*
5. **The one who feels nothing has a road.** Lofty souls climb by understanding; most of us cry from the heart over the distance, 'with no feeling at all for the Infinite', and that leap is the truest drawing of the Essence (MH-C03, MH-D5). *stated*
6. **Only below does the soul walk.** Everything above, even the surrounding light, is measured; only the Essence is not, and only the soul in a body, through physical mitzvos, moves in it. To the Essence there is no higher and lower; resting in physical things takes the Essence itself (MH-C06, MH-C17, MH-D8, MH-D9). *stated*
7. **Knowing has three floors.** Understanding (can be argued away); the 'laying-down' where something settles, 'it is so'; and the heart's will, which alone grasps the Essence, like a son's cry 'Abba' moving the father's whole being. A child's 'Abba' already carries all the adult knows (MH-C02, MH-C12, MH-D6, MH-D10). *stated*
8. **The return is room, brightness, and the plain deed.** Bowing in the Temple made room for everyone; going home, 'all physical things look brighter and cleaner' (MH-D11). The guard: feeling love in prayer can still be 'completely outside' if self-love is not cleaned (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, MH-D12). *stated*

**For the guide (ours):** after D3-D5, offer the 'two nothings' (MH-D1) before going higher; when someone says they feel nothing, go to MH-D5, not to more ideas; when the descent reaches the bottom, bring the return through MH-D2 (the plain thing is where 'nothing else' is truest) and MH-D11 (room for the other person).

## MH-C01 · Rebbe Rayatz, Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, d"h Tzohar Taaseh LaTeivah (Shabbos Noach), sections 41-44, lines 232-249

**Start.** 'There is nothing but Him' is usually said as one sentence. This maamar splits it in two and shows that the stronger half is true only down here.

1. **If He is the only true 'something', why do we call creation 'something from nothing'? It sounds backwards.** (M1) *How the text gets there:* The text asks: the Infinite alone is the true being, and everything made is as nothing before Him; so how can we call the world the 'something'? > «והא"ס הוא דוקא בחי' יש האמיתי, דהיינו אמיתת המצאו, וכל מה שנברא ונתהווה הכל בבחי' אין ואפס לגבי'» — *The Infinite alone is the true 'something', the truth of His existing; everything created is nothing and zero before Him.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 237

2. **His greatness shows most where He makes something totally new: in the lowest world that comes from nothing.** (M2) *How the text gets there:* Only the Essence can make real newness from nothing, so His 'greatness without end' is seen in the power that makes the plain world. > «המהווה מאין ליש בבחי' התחדשות גמורה, דהתהוות זו הוא מכח העצמות דוקא» — *It brings into being from nothing, as a complete newness; this coming-into-being is from the power of the Essence alone.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 237

3. **There are two kinds of 'nothing before Him'. One says: it counts for nothing. The other says: there is nothing else at all.** (M3) *How the text gets there:* Before going further the text lays down a rule: two levels of bittul, and the second is far higher than the first. > «הא' הביטול דכולא קמי' כלא חשיבא, והב' הביטול דאין עוד מלבדו» — *The first: the bittul of 'all is counted as nothing before Him'; the second: the bittul of 'there is nothing else besides Him'.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. 4. **'Counts for nothing' is like a candle at noon. It still burns; it just takes up no room next to the sun.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The mashal: a candle in daylight is not seen and gives no light, yet it exists. So 'counted as nothing' still leaves a something. > «והמשל בזה כמו שרגא בטיהרא» — *The parable for this is a lamp at noon.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 244

5. **But even 'takes up no room' only works between things of the same kind. You cannot say a fly 'takes up no room' next to a man. It is not even in the running.** (M10) *How the text gets there:* The mashal breaks: 'not taking up place' needs some likeness. Light can be 'nothing' before a higher light, but before the Essence it is not even comparable. > «וכמו שאינו שייך לומר דזבוב ואבן אינו תופס מקום לגבי אדם שהרי אינו מערכו ומהותו כלל» — *Just as one cannot say a fly or a stone 'takes up no place' next to a man, since it is not of his measure or nature at all.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 244

6. **So 'there is nothing else' is true only of the created things down here. Their whole being is the Essence itself.** (M4) *How the text gets there:* The turn: the higher bittul belongs not to the lofty lights but to the lowest creatures, because they have no being of their own at all; their being is His. > «דהנבראים אינם שום מציאות כלל לעצמן וכל מציאותם הוא העצמות ובמילא הרי הביטול הוא בבחי' אין עוד מלבדו» — *Created things are no existence at all of their own; all their existence is the Essence, and so their bittul is 'there is nothing else besides Him'.* · `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 244

7. **When you really see this, it is like solving a riddle. You want to shout: it is not the way it looks!** (M16) *How the text gets there:* The ofanim grasp that the truth is the opposite of what the eyes see; like a man who cracks a riddle, they cry out with great noise. > «יצעק בקול גוואלד עס איז ניט אזיי ויא איהר זעהט» — *He will cry out 'Gevald! Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt` line 247

**Where it bottoms out.** 'There is nothing else besides Him' is true right here, even in the empty space the world stands in: the lowest things are nothing but His own being.

**How it returns down.** The return is prayer that makes the knowing near, and the slow work on one's own goat, ox and sheep (the stubborn, the kicking and the soft traits) so the world becomes His home.

## MH-D1 · Two nothings: the ray that changes nothing, and the soul you cannot see

**Teaching.** 'Nothing' (ayin) means two different things, and Chassidus needs both. Looking from Him down, the world comes from a ray so small it takes up no room at all, so nothing in Him changed when the world began. Looking from us up, 'nothing' means what we cannot grasp, like our own soul: closer to us than anything, sure beyond doubt, and never seen. The descent's 'even with Him, nothing' (D3) is the first; the second is new and gentler: the 'nothing' you live from is the most real thing in you, only unseen.

**Hisbonenus, as the texts give it (arrangement ours).**

1. Feel that you are alive right now: the hand moves, the eyes see, you are thinking.
2. Ask what is doing this. Not the brain as meat: a dead body has a brain and thinks nothing (TS 3457).
3. So there is a soul. You know it without any doubt. Now try to see it, to say what it is. You cannot.
4. That is what 'nothing' means from below: not 'not there', but 'not grasped'. We call it nothing only because we cannot name it (TS 3458).
5. Now turn it upward: the whole world lives the same way, from a life no one can see.
6. And from His side: all of that comes from a ray that takes up no room in Him. You are before the world and after it, unchanged (TS 3456).

**From inside.** The Maharash's images: the line in the middle of an aleph joining two yuds; a deep spring dripping between the great abyss and the rivers; a seed that rots before it sprouts (TS 3454, 3462-3463).

**Where it lands.** In the body: at the sea, 'the hidden nothing of the soul shone in their bodies', and each pointed with a finger, 'this is my G-d' (TS 3467). In the present moment: the life in this breath.

**Extends.** D3, D4 (sharpens: two meanings of 'nothing', one about Him, one about our knowing); D5

## MH-D2 · 'Nothing else' is true only down here

**Teaching.** There are two sentences: 'all counts as nothing before Him' and 'there is nothing else besides Him'. The first is like a candle at noon: still there, just taking no room. It only works for things of the same kind, like a small man next to a great one. The second, stronger sentence is true only of the lowest created things, because they have no being of their own at all: their whole being is the Essence. So the deepest unity is not found by leaving the world but in the plainest physical thing.

**Hisbonenus, as the texts give it (arrangement ours).**

1. Light a candle in your mind and carry it into bright noon. It still burns; it just disappears (SHM 244).
2. That is 'counted as nothing': you are still something, only you take up no room.
3. Now notice: you cannot say a fly 'takes up no room' next to a person. It is not in the same running at all.
4. Before the Essence, nothing is in the running. So even 'takes up no room' is too much to say.
5. Then say it of this table, this hand: it has no being of its own; its being is His.
6. If you feel like crying out 'it is not the way it looks!', that is the right feeling (SHM 246).

**From inside.** The ofanim's great noise; a riddle suddenly solved; a person who cries 'gevald, it is not as you see' (SHM 246).

**Where it lands.** On the lowest, plainest object in front of you, and on the empty space between things: 'nothing else, even in the empty space of the world' (SHM 245).

## MH-D3 · A part, not a ray: the farther, the stronger the pull

**Teaching.** A ray rises toward its source only while it is attached; cut off, it has no pull and even falls. The soul is not a ray but a part of the Essence. A part that is separated longs to return more, not less. So distance is not the opposite of the bond; it is where the bond is felt most. And the Essence in the soul is hidden, which is exactly why it can live in a body.

**Hisbonenus, as the texts give it (arrangement ours).**

1. Picture a spark flying up to join a great torch: it rises because it is fire (SHM 1453).
2. Now see that sunlight on a wall has no wish to return to the sun; it only lasts while connected.
3. You are not like the light on the wall. You are a part of the fire itself (SHM 1457).
4. Where do you feel far from G-d right now? Do not push the feeling away.
5. Read it as the pull of a part that is separated. The farther, the stronger (SHM 1457).
6. Then hear: 'I love you as you are and where you are' (SHM 1464).

**From inside.** The spark running fast to the torch; malchus 'never silent'; the father's love 'as you are and where you are'.

## MH-D4 · Under yechidah: a spark that is G-dliness itself

**Teaching.** DESCENT reaches 'a point under all your names' (D6). The Rebbe Rayatz names it: even yechidah, the highest name of the soul, is only a name. Under it is a tiny spark that is actual G-dliness, from 'the lowest level in the Creator', clothed in a created spark called yechidah. That is why thoughts of teshuvah come suddenly, and why teshuvah reaches where Torah does not.

**Hisbonenus, as the texts give it (arrangement ours).**

1. Remember a time when a thought of return came out of nowhere, with no preparation (SHM 414).
2. Ask where it came from. Not from your effort; it dripped down from your soul above you.
3. List your soul's names: life, spirit, soul, living one, single one. Each is a name.
4. A name is not the self. Under the last name is a spark that is G-dliness itself (SHM 415).
5. So the one who returns is not only you; it is His own spark in you returning.
6. Rest there: nothing you did can stain that spark, and from it every gap is filled (SHM 429).

**From inside.** Daniel's companions who saw nothing yet trembled, because 'their mazal saw' (SHM 415).

**Where it lands.** In teshuvah right now: a sudden turning, then breaking one's self-satisfaction ('zich tzu brechen', SHM 429).
