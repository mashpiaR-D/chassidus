This range covers the laws of Mishpatim and the building of the Mishkan (the desert sanctuary). It is one of the richest stretches of the book for the unity. Three things stand out. First, the Shelah's own statement of **tzimtzum**: the Endless, "by His great power without limit, brought limit into being", "contracted Himself" for this, and "is within them and surrounds them without end" (line 8095). Second, the **hidden and revealed**: He "sees and is not seen" (lines 7990-7992), and "the Name of the Essence is not grasped" (line 8119). Third, the **dwelling below**: "what is below is dearer than what is above, for He left what is above and came down into what is below" (line 8370). Around these sit strong practical teachings: judge like Him, leaning to kindness; guard the "fire" of anger; clothe the soul when you dress; keep a small Shabbat every day.

## 1.1 Tzimtzum in the Shelah's own words

Before the Shema we mention the renewal of the world, "to show the absolute oneness" (Terumah, Torah Ohr 23, line 8095: להורות על אחדות המוחלט). Then (line 8095):

> והוא ברוך הוא מבלי סוף ומבלי תכלית, אין לו התחלה ולא גבול

> וכביכול צמצם בעצמו להמציא גבול כי מלא כל הארץ כבודו

> הוא בתוכם ומקיף אותם בלי סוף ומבלי תכלית, וגילוי כבודו בכל הנמצאים הוא צמצם בערך הנבראים

"He is without end and without limit; He has no beginning and no border. By His great power without limit He brought limit into being. As it were, He contracted Himself to bring limit into being, for the whole earth is full of His glory. He is within them and surrounds them without end or limit, and the showing of His glory in all beings He contracted to the measure of the creatures."

*Ours:* read closely, this is the Chabad reading of tzimtzum, not the literal one. The contraction is "as it were" (*kivyachol*); it is for the sake of limit; it is "to the measure of the creatures" (*be'erech hanivra'im*), that is, from their side; and He remains "within them and surrounding them without end". That is the *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 7 position: tzimtzum is a hiding for the receivers, not a change in Him. The next line applies it to the Mishkan: "G-d contracted His Presence between the two poles of the ark, though the heavens and the heavens of heavens cannot hold Him" (line 8096).

The source of it: creation "shows G-d's existence and His absolute oneness, that He is the Cause of all causes" (line 8094):

> על כן חידוש העולם מורה אמציאות הש"י ואחדותו המוחלט, שהוא עילת כל העילות וסיבת כל הסיבות

## 1.2 Hidden in Himself, revealed in His acts

"Before G-d created His world there was He and His Name alone, hidden and concealed in absolute concealment" (line 8098):

> וקודם שברא הקב"ה את עולמו היה הוא ושמו בלבד נסתר ונעלם בהעלמה המוחלטת

> ומציאותו, מצד עצמו הוא נסתר, ומצד פעולותיו שהוא חדושו של עולם הוא נגלה, והנגלה בא מהנסתר

"His existence, from His own side, is hidden; from the side of His acts, the renewal of the world, it is revealed; and the revealed comes from the hidden." "He brought all being into being and keeps all being in being" (line 8099: והוא היוה כל הויה ומקיים כל הויה). From His side the hidden comes first; "but from our side the revealed comes first and then the hidden" (line 8423). This is the path of contemplation: from His acts to the hidden.

## 1.3 He sees and is not seen

In Mishpatim, on "He who dwells in the hidden place of the Most High" (lines 7988-7992):

> אבל הקדוש ברוך הוא מקומו של עולם, הוא יושב בסתר בלתי נראה

> הוא יתברך רואה, רצה לומר משגיח בו בפרטות ביתר שאת, אבל אינו נראה, רק הוא נסתר ונעלם

"G-d is the place of the world; He dwells hidden and unseen." Even in the Temple "He sees, that is, He watches over it in detail all the more, but He is not seen; He is hidden and concealed." Angels have the world as their place because they take on its air; He does not (line 7990).

## 1.4 The Essence is not grasped; no image of Him

On the *Shiur Komah* (the mystical "measure of the body"), the Shelah quotes a teacher and agrees (line 8117):

> וקבלתי קבלה אמיתית כי שיעור זה אינו אלא על מטטרון הנברא

> אבל ממנו ולמעלה אמר הנביא (ישעיה מ, יח) אל מי תדמיון אל וגו', והאומר הפוך זה לא ראה אור

"This measure is only of Metatron, who is created. From there upward the prophet said, 'to whom will you liken G-d'. One who says the opposite has not seen light." And (line 8119):

> אבל שם העצם שהוא שורש כל האצילות הנסתרות והנגלות אינו מושג

"The Name of the Essence, the root of all emanation hidden and revealed, is not grasped."

## 1.5 No go-between

The Rambam's account of Enosh: he did not deny G-d but "brought in a go-between". Shabbat, on which "G-d alone is exalted", "shows our being one with Him with no go-between" (Vayakhel, Torah Ohr 44, line 8403):

The golden calf too "was not denial of Him, only they sought a go-between" (line 8403). "There is no judge in essence; only G-d is the true Judge" (line 8027). "We are not forced under any power but G-d alone" (line 8252).

## 1.6 His dwelling below, and in us

The Presence was "mainly below" at creation, "so G-d fills from above to below" (line 8073, quoting *Shaarei Orah*). Then the core line (Vayakhel, Torah Ohr 11, line 8370):

> ולא עוד, אלא שחביבין כל מה שלמטן משל למעלן

"What is below is dearer than what is above: He left what is above and came down into what is below." And where He dwells most: "'among them' means: in the thought of those who make it I will dwell, which is the inner, hidden Mishkan" (line 8103).

## 1.7 Souls a part of G-d above

In Terumah, on "you are children to the Lord" (line 8138):

"The souls are a part of G-d above. So He is truly a Father and we are truly His children." This is the very phrase of Tanya ch. 2. He adds, as the Father's word: "I am ready at every time and every moment to be so, when you return" (line 8139).

## 1.8 One with judgment and mercy

"Havayah is Elokim, the secret of the oneness of judgment with mercy" (line 8004); "one G-d created them all, and from Him are judgment and mercy" (line 8107). Bribery breaks this oneness in a judge: leaning to one side "is the opposite of oneness", but "when both are equal in his eyes, this is the secret of oneness" (line 7893).

## 3.1 Judge like Him: lean to kindness (proposed: `lean_to_kindness`)

Judges must "begin with compromise and not go down to the depth of the law", "all as G-d judges the world", who "joins mercy to judgment, for without this the world could not stand" (line 7886). "That they act as He acts, for He leans toward kindness" (line 7891). Explain your reasons, so the other "will accept it with love" (line 8036). *Tie:* `act`. *Ours:* in any judgment of another person, start from compromise and lean to kindness.

## 3.2 Guard the fire (proposed: `guard_the_fire`)

"Here is a hint to the fire of quarrel and of anger: one must guard it extra carefully so it does not escape from him even without intent" (line 8059). And again: "a person must be careful never to kindle" it, "and all the more on Shabbat" (line 8451). *Tie:* S15 (anger), `discern`, `quieting`. *Ours:* treat anger like a fire you are liable for even when it "got out" by accident.

## 3.3 Clothe the soul (proposed: `clothe_the_soul`)

"Let a person think this when he clothes the body's nakedness, and wake himself to clothe the soul's nakedness" (line 8282). *Tie:* `garment_look`, `arrive`. *Ours:* each morning, while dressing, one line: "what will my soul wear today?"

## 3.4 Eat as an offering (proposed: `eat_as_offering`)

"With this thought a person makes holy even the necessary food he eats on weekdays, that his eating be like an offering" (line 7966). *Tie:* `act`, S82. *Ours:* keep this without the book's austerity (see Not carried).

## 3.5 Think while doing (proposed: `mind_in_the_deed`)

Bezalel built while holding in mind the letters by which heaven and earth were made (line 8097). "While doing the mitzvot let his thought roam in their secrets and reasons, with wisdom, understanding and knowledge" (line 8352). "The heart of holiness is in thought" (line 8386). *Tie:* `depth`, `hold`. *Ours:* close to Chabad's *chochmah, binah, da'at* as a way of doing, not only of study.

## 3.6 A small Shabbat every day (proposed: `daily_shabbat`)

"Every day a person must make himself holy at some time of the day and rest from bodily work; only Shabbat is holy all of it" (line 8407). "The weekday of Israel is a weekday kept in the purity of holiness" (line 8407). And on Shabbat itself: "let it be in his eyes as if all his work were done" (line 8387). *Tie:* `quieting`, `arrive`. *Ours:* a fixed short time each day set apart from work, as a small Shabbat.

## 3.7 Generosity as partnership in creation (proposed: `give_like_Him`, merge with R11/R12)

"He created the world as a free gift; He has no need of His world" (line 8105). One who gives with a willing heart becomes His partner, and "G-d, who knows thoughts, joined his good thought to the deed" (line 8106). Money is made whole only by giving: "let him wake his heart to be broad in giving" (line 8142). Give bread "with a kind face", remembering that He "gives bread to all flesh and gives with a kind face" (line 8156).

## 3.8 Wear His traits (Tie: `act`)

"That they clothe themselves in G-d's traits: to be merciful, gracious, slow to anger, abundant in kindness and truth" (line 8355). With the middle way of the Rambam's *Shemonah Perakim* ch. 4: "good deeds are the even deeds, in the middle between the two extremes" (line 8159), and if one leaned to one extreme, go to the other for a time, then back.

## 3.9 Bless the bad as the good

"Just as we bless for the good, so we bless for the bad" (line 8440). The moon "sanctifies the Name even when it is lacking, and does not change its task" (line 8441). *Tie:* S84. *Ours:* when you are "lacking", keep your task.

## 3.10 Other practices in this range

- **Wake first.** "Just as you are with Him, He is with you; by the waking of the lower the upper wakes" (line 7960).
- **Pray more.** "When he prays more, he is answered more" (line 8272). Join the inside to the outside in prayer (line 8258).
- **Tears.** "The gates of tears are not locked" (line 8064).
- **Arrive.** Entering a synagogue or study hall, "the Presence rests in these holy places, and a person should take this to heart" (line 8068).
- **Speech.** "Speak speech that is a real word and not empty breath" (line 8385); everyone who serves G-d "is like a priest... let him make his mouth extra holy" (line 8284).
- **Account.** Moses gave an account of every coin; "let every person learn to be clean in the eyes of people, and not think 'I am one of the trustworthy'" (line 8453).
- **Zeal with joy.** Start a mitzvah "even when he cannot finish it all", and "with joy" (line 8052).
- **Raise your whole self.** "Let him raise himself up to purify his whole stature", and "I will dwell among them" is kept in him (line 8454).

## 4. The big human questions

- **How can the Infinite make a finite world?** "By His great power without limit He brought limit into being", contracting "to the measure of the creatures", while "within them and around them without end" (line 8095).
- **Can I know Him?** From His acts, yes; His Essence, no (lines 8099, 8119). No image fits above the created (line 8117).
- **Where is G-d?** He "is the place of the world", "sees and is not seen" (lines 7990-7991); He prefers the lower world (line 8370); He dwells in the thought of those who serve (line 8103).
- **What am I?** "A part of G-d above", truly His child (line 8138); the soul "is the coin and imprint of G-d" (line 7962).
- **Why do I suffer?** He hides His face "so that Israel will return" (line 8000); "out of the darkness will come the advantage of light" (lines 8023, 8204). Trust "even when He judges" (line 8004).
- **Is punishment forever?** No. Punishment is "from our side", limited "as we are limited"; reward is "from His side", endless (line 8332).
- **Should I seek to suffer for G-d?** The Torah did not record Abraham's self-sacrifice in Ur "so that later generations would not learn from him to do as he did" (line 8266).
- **Why serve?** Not for reward: Hillel served "not for reward even in the world to come, only 'blessed is the Lord day by day'" (line 8372). "The reward for doing the mitzvot is to know their spirit and cling there" (line 8444).
- **Must I be drunk on Purim?** No. The Shelah reads the saying as a warning "not to get so drunk" (line 8249), and the feast as "not revelry, foolishness and madness" but the joining of body and soul (line 8247). *Ours:* the guide never includes drinking.
- **Can two opposed views both be true?** "Every keeping of a mitzvah has a rank of clinging above, and one enters and clings more than another" (line 8427).

## 5. With Chabad (kept)

- **Tzimtzum.** A key agreement. Line 8095 has all four of Chabad's points: "as it were"; for the sake of limit; "to the measure of the creatures"; and He is still "within them and surrounds them without end". This is *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 7 and Tanya ch. 48 in outline. The Shelah does not use the Ari's language of the vacated space here.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** Line 8119 (the Essence not grasped) and 8117 (no likeness above the created) fit the Rambam and *Shaar HaYichud* ch. 9. Line 8403 takes the Rambam's history of idolatry (go-betweens) and uses it to explain Shabbat. Line 8159 takes his middle way.
- **The Ramak.** "He is the place of the world and the world is not His place" (line 7990) and "He sees and is not seen" (line 7991) keep the Ramak's second half: the world is in Him, but He is not the world. Line 8095 ("within them") and line 8073 ("fills from above to below") keep the first half.
- **Souls.** "A part of G-d above" (line 8138) is Tanya ch. 2 word for word.
- **Dwelling below.** Line 8370 ("what is below is dearer") is the root of Tanya ch. 36.
- **Awakening from below.** Lines 7960 and 8201 match Tanya ch. 46. But line 8203 adds a limit Chabad also keeps: the world's existence and bread come from Him without any preparation of ours, "for His kindness is forever".
- **Not carried.** The legal count (7832-7879, 8070-8072, 8162-8168, 8286-8296, 8358-8359); the Esau, Amalek and Samael polemics (7941, 8267-8268, 8432, 8442-8443); rankings that put the pious of the nations lower (7982, 8412, 8449); martyrdom framing (8053, 8271, 8440 in part); mourning in sackcloth (8353); "better with no food at all" (7964) and separating from the permitted as a rule (8241, 8442); the reading of wine on Purim as spiritual (8232-8233), which the guide does not carry in any form; the Zohar on women at funerals (8450); Shiur Komah measures and gematria chains (8116-8118, 8300-8335).

## 6. What the guide should take (ours)

1. **He brought limit into being, contracting "to the measure of the creatures", and is still within and around all** (line 8095).
2. **He sees and is not seen; He is the place of the world** (lines 7990-7992).
3. **The Essence is not grasped; no image fits Him** (lines 8117, 8119).
4. **What is below is dearer to Him** (line 8370); **He dwells in the thought of those who serve** (line 8103).
5. **The soul is a part of G-d above; He is ready for your return at every moment** (lines 8138-8139).
6. **Guard the fire of anger and quarrel** (lines 8059, 8451).
7. **Keep a small Shabbat every day** (line 8407).
8. **Clothe the soul when you dress** (line 8282).
9. **Lean to kindness when you judge; wear His thirteen traits** (lines 7891, 8355).
10. **Punishment has a limit; reward does not** (line 8332). **Serve for no reward** (line 8372).
