This is the second half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Shemot: the judges, Sinai and the Ten Commandments, the laws, the Mishkan and the priests' garments, the golden calf, Moshe's prayers, the Thirteen Attributes, and the building of the Mishkan. Much of it is law. But three of the Maharal's great teachings on oneness are here: the Torah is one word, true glory spreads into all beings, and He does not change even after sin.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The whole Torah is one word.** The Ten were said in one utterance "לומר כי כל התורה ענין אחד ודבור אחד" (512): "to say that the whole Torah is one matter and one word." "דהיא כמו נקודה אחת, ואין לה מקצת" (512): "it is like one point, and has no part." "ואין דבר קרוב אל האחד רק התורה" (512): "nothing is near to the One except the Torah."
- **The upper world is the world of oneness.** "כי העולם העליון נקרא עולם האחדות, וכל שקרוב אל השם יתברך, הוא האחד האמת, הוא יותר באחדות" (512): "the upper world is called the world of oneness, and whatever is nearer to the Name, who is the true One, is more in oneness." "שעולם הזה השפל, הרחוק מאתו יתברך, הוא עולם הנפרד" (512): "this low world is the world of parting."
- **True glory is spreading into all.** "You will see My back." The face is "הכרת אמיתת כבודו יתברך, שבו נבדל מזולתו, והוא עצם כבודו" (914): "knowing the truth of His glory, by which He is set apart; it is His very glory." And "כי זהו אמיתת הכבוד" (914), "התפשטות הכבוד בזולתו" (914): "this is the truth of glory: glory spreading into what is other than it," "בכל הנמצאים" (914), "into all beings."
- **He does not change, even after sin.** Why is the Name said twice? "כי הוא יתברך לא ישתנה לעולם, כי אף אם חטא האדם נגדו" (916) "עדיין הוא ברחמים" (916): "He never changes; even if a man sinned against Him, He is still in mercy."
- **The act below brings the act above.** The menorah seemed to make itself. "וכל הפעולות שבאים מלמעלה צריך שיעשה האדם למטה" (726): "every act that comes from above needs a person to act below." "וכאשר התחיל לעשות" (726), "גמר השם על ידו" (726): "when he began, the Name finished it through him."
- **Torah joins the Giver and the receiver.** "כי אין התורה גזירת המלך בלא טעם" (865): "the Torah is not a king's decree without reason." "כי התורה היא בין השם יתברך ובין האדם, משלמת רצון השם יתברך ונותנת לאדם שלימות" (865): "the Torah is between G-d and man; it fulfills His will and gives man wholeness."

## 2.1 The Torah is one word, like one point

The Ten Commandments were said in one utterance. "לומר כי כל התורה ענין אחד ודבור אחד" (512). "דהיא כמו נקודה אחת, ואין לה מקצת" (512): "it is like one point, and has no part." That is why denying one verse denies the whole: a point cannot be half kept.

## 2.2 Nearer to the One, more one

"כי העולם העליון נקרא עולם האחדות, וכל שקרוב אל השם יתברך, הוא האחד האמת, הוא יותר באחדות" (512). "שעולם הזה השפל, הרחוק מאתו יתברך, הוא עולם הנפרד" (512). "ואין דבר קרוב אל האחד רק התורה" (512). The Maharal draws a scale. The farther from Him, the more things are split. The nearer, the more they are one. The Torah is the nearest thing, so it is one word.

## 2.3 Glory spreading into all beings

Moshe asked to see His glory. G-d said "you will see My back, but My face will not be seen." The Maharal: "פירוש 'פנים' הוא הכרת אמיתת כבודו יתברך, שבו נבדל מזולתו, והוא עצם כבודו" (914): "'face' means knowing the truth of His glory, by which He is set apart from all else; it is His very glory." No one sees that. The "back" is what Moshe saw: "כי זהו אמיתת הכבוד" (914), "התפשטות הכבוד בזולתו" (914): "this is the truth of glory: glory spreading into what is other than it." "שרוצה לומר התפשטות הכבוד בזולתו בכל הנמצאים" (914): "meaning the spreading of glory into all other beings." This is "the whole earth is full of His glory."

So His glory has two sides. In itself it is set apart. As it spreads, it fills all beings. The second is what we can know.

## 2.4 He does not change

The Thirteen Attributes open "Hashem, Hashem," the Name twice. Rashi says: I am He before a man sins and after he sins and returns. The Maharal: "כי הוא יתברך לא ישתנה לעולם" (916): "He never changes." "אבל השם יתברך הווייתו נצחית, ואף אחר שחטא" (916) "הוא ברחמים" (916): "His being is eternal, and even after he sinned He is in mercy." A person changes when he is wronged. He does not. And more: "שהשם יתברך מנקה אותו מן החטא ומן העונש" (918): "the Name cleans him of the sin and of the punishment."

## 2.5 The Torah joins Giver and receiver

G-d and Moshe went over the law together. Why? "כי אין התורה גזירת המלך בלא טעם" (865). "אבל מפני כי הדברים הם מוכרחים על פי החכמה והדעת" (865): "the things are necessary by wisdom and knowledge." "כי התורה היא בין השם יתברך ובין האדם" (865): the Torah stands between them and makes both whole. And "כי המתדבק בתורה מתדבק במדריגה אשר אין שם חסרון רק שלימות" (531): "one who cleaves to Torah cleaves to a level where there is no lack, only wholeness."

## 2.6 The Torah is necessary

The mountain was held over them. The Maharal: "לומר כי התורה היא הכרחית לקבלה" (507). "וידוע, כי דברים המוכרחים להיות הם חשובים במעלה יותר, שאי אפשר מבלעדם, ואין קיום לנמצא בזולתם" (507): "things that must be are higher in rank, for nothing can be without them." And it is ours from both sides: "הרי התורה לישראל מצד הנותן שרוצה בזה, ומצד המקבל שהם ישראל" (507).

## 2.7 The parts go under the root

"כי החשבון הפרטי יכנס תחת חשבון הכללי, שהוא סכום חשבון, כמו שיכנסו כל החלקים תחת השורש, שהוא ראש להם" (833): "the particular count goes in under the whole, as all parts go in under the root, which is head to them." The same idea holds the Mishkan: "שכל כלי המשכן הם כמו כלי אחד, שהם צריכים זה לזה, ואין זה בלא זה" (732): "all the vessels are like one vessel; none is without the other." And the middle ties the parts: "כי האמצע מקשר כל החלקים לפי שהוא באמצע" (541).

## 2.8 The form never ceased

The anointing oil, the menorah and the beams did not end; only what carried them was lost. "כי אין חשיבות המשכן הדברים החומרים, רק עצם הציור שהיה מסודר מן השם יתברך, ואל דבר זה לא הגיע בטול ושינוי" (846): "the worth of the Mishkan is not the material things, only the form set in order by G-d, and no ending or change reached that." "ומכל מקום התבאר לך כי הנרות יאירו אל מול פני המנורה לעולם" (846): "the lamps will shine toward the face of the menorah forever."

## 2.9 The book is the form of all beings

Moshe said "erase me from Your book." "ודעת רז"ל הוא כי הספר הזה הוא ציור הנמצאות" (893): "the sages' view is that this book is the form of all beings." To be in the book is to have a place in His picture of the world.

## 2.10 The judge joins creation

"כי כאשר ברא הקב"ה את העולם בראו בדין" (471): "when the Holy One created the world, He created it by judgment." So one who judges truly "מתחבר במדה שנבראו בה מעשה בראשית" (471): "joins the very measure by which creation was made." "ואין לך דבר אהוב לפני השם יתברך כמו משפט אמת" (545).

## 2.11 The Shechina among them is the witness

"אבל השכינה בישראל הוא עדות, שאין אחד דר אצל שונאו כי אם אצל האוהב" (957): "the Shechina among Israel is a witness, for no one lives beside his enemy, only beside one who loves." After the calf, His dwelling with them was the proof He had forgiven.

- **MH-S52 "One word" (512).** "לומר כי כל התורה ענין אחד ודבור אחד" (512). Inside: the many laws and verses stop feeling like a long list. They are one word from One, like one point. How to enter: before learning, say: "all of this is one word from Him." When learning one small law, think: this is the whole Torah seen from here. It differs from S62 Only Him, which is about Him. This is about His word, seen as one.
- **MH-S53 "Glory in all" (914).** "שרוצה לומר התפשטות הכבוד בזולתו בכל הנמצאים" (914). Inside: I cannot see His face, His glory in itself. But I can see His glory spread into everything that is: a tree, a person, a day. How to enter: say "the whole earth is full of His glory" slowly. Then look at one thing in front of you and say: His glory has spread even to here. It differs from S63 The world held, where He holds the world up. Here the world shows His glory.
- **MH-S54 "Mercy that does not change" (916).** "כי הוא יתברך לא ישתנה לעולם, כי אף אם חטא האדם נגדו" (916) "עדיין הוא ברחמים" (916). Inside: after I did wrong, I expect Him to have turned cold. But He has not changed. He is the same, and He is in mercy. How to enter: after a fall, say "Hashem, Hashem" and think: before I sinned, You. After I sinned, the same You. Then return. It differs from S48 Returning, which is my turning back. This is knowing He never turned away.

## 4.1 All beginnings are hard (`mh_all_beginnings_hard`, new)

"אם עתה תקבלו עליכם עול המצוות, אף על גב שהוא כבד עליכם, יערב לכם מכאן ואילך, שכל התחלות קשות" (496): "if now you take on the yoke of the mitzvot, though it is heavy on you, it will be sweet from then on, for all beginnings are hard." Practice: when you start a new mitzvah, a new learning, or a new habit, and it feels heavy, say: this is the start, and all starts are hard. Keep going for a set time before you judge it.

## 4.2 Serve because you want to (`mh_serve_from_will`, new)

"כי העבודה צריך שיהיה עובד לו מרצונו, ואז נקרא 'עובד'" (537): "service must be from his own will; then he is called a servant." "וזה צריך הודאה בלב, לא כמלך בשר ודם המכריח את האדם שיודה במלכותו" (537). Practice: before a mitzvah you do out of habit, stop for a moment and say: "I want to do this for You." Let the thanks be in the heart, not only on the lips.

## 4.3 Begin, and He will finish (`mh_act_below_first`, new)

"וכל הפעולות שבאים מלמעלה צריך שיעשה האדם למטה" (726). "וכאשר התחיל לעשות" (726) "גמר השם על ידו" (726). Practice: when a task seems too big, do not wait until you can see how to finish it. Do the first real step with your hands, and ask Him to finish it. This pairs with the Bereishit teaching that He helps and man completes.

## 4.4 A good eye is wealth (`mh_good_eye_rich`, new)

"אבל העשיר הוא שיש לו עין טוב ולב טוב" (698): "the rich one is the one who has a good eye and a good heart." "כי הרע עין הוא חסר" (698): "the one with a bad eye is lacking." Practice: when you give, give with a glad eye. When you see another's good, be glad for it. Count yourself rich by that, not by what you hold.

## 4.5 Join the far ones in prayer (`mh_join_the_far`, new)

A fast without the sinners of Israel is no fast, like incense without the galbanum. "ואין הכנעה ממי שהוא כבר קרוב אליו כמו הצדיקים" (850). "כי כאשר הוא רחוק מן השם יתברך ההכנעה הוא יותר" (850): "when one is far from G-d, the humbling is greater." Practice: when you gather to pray, do not keep out those who have gone far. Their humbling brings the mercy. And when you feel far yourself, know that your broken prayer has great power.

## 4.6 Come face to face (`mh_face_to_face_return`, new)

"וכשבא אליו ומודיע חטאתו והוא מוחל לו, הוא גמר כפרה" (785): "when he comes to him and makes his sin known and he forgives him, that is full atonement." Practice: in return, do not stop at feeling bad or at sending a message through others. Speak to Him yourself, in your own words: "I did this. Please forgive me." With a person you wronged, go to them yourself.

## 4.7 Cry out; He answers now (`mh_return_today`, extended)

"ובא לומר שמיד שהוא צועק" (647) "נענה" (647): "as soon as he cries out, he is answered." Add to the practice: after a return face to face, trust that the answer has already begun. He does not change (916), and He cleans the sin and its punishment (918).

## 4.8 Remember Shabbat all week (`mh_always_remember_shabbat`, new)

"תנו לב לזכור תמיד" (524): "set your heart to remember always." Practice: when something fine comes your way in the week, a good food, a good thought, a good word of Torah, set it aside for Shabbat. So the week leans toward Shabbat.

## 5. The big human questions

**Why so many laws?** They are not many. The whole Torah is one word, like one point (512).

**Why is the world so split?** It is far from Him. The nearer to the One, the more things are one (512).

**Can I see G-d?** Not His face, His glory in itself. But His glory spreads into all beings, and that you can see (914).

**Does G-d stop loving me when I sin?** No. He never changes. After the sin He is still in mercy (916). He cleans both the sin and its punishment (918).

**Is the Torah just rules He decided?** No. It is not a decree without reason. It is wisdom, and it makes both sides whole: His will is done and man is made whole (865).

**Why is starting so hard?** All beginnings are hard; then it grows sweet (496).

**Do my small acts matter?** Every act from above needs an act below. Begin, and He finishes (726).

**What lasts?** The form He set in order does not end, even when the material is lost (846).

**Who is rich?** The one with a good eye and a good heart (698).

**Can one far from G-d help others?** Yes. The humbling of one who was far brings mercy (850).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **The Torah as one word** (512) is close to Tanya ch. 4-5 and 23: the Torah is His will and wisdom, one with Him, and "He and His will are one." The Maharal says nothing is as near the One as the Torah (512). Tanya says the Torah is not only near; it is one with Him, and the one who learns is joined to Him in a total union (ch. 5).
- **Glory spreading into all** (914) is close to Tanya's two sides of G-dliness: the light that fills all worlds (memale) and the light that surrounds all (sovev) (Tanya ch. 48). The Maharal's "face," His glory in itself, set apart, fits sovev; the "back," His glory spread into all beings, fits memale. Chabad adds that even memale is hidden from us, and that "the whole earth is full of His glory" is really true, not a figure of speech (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7).
- **Tzimtzum.** The low world is "the world of parting," far from Him (512). Chabad says the parting is only how it looks to us (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7, Tanya ch. 48); there is no place empty of Him. The Maharal's scale (nearer means more one) is how things are for the creature. Chabad agrees, and adds that from His side even the far is not far. This is a real difference in stress: the Maharal gives the parting more weight.
- **He does not change** (916) matches "I G-d have not changed," which Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 uses to say that creation adds no change in Him. The Maharal uses the same truth for mercy: sin makes no change in Him.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The face is His glory "by which He is set apart" (914): no one knows it. This is the Rambam's teaching that His essence is not known (Guide 1:54, on this very verse). The Maharal and the Rambam both read "My back" as what can be known through His acts. Chabad keeps this and adds the essence that is above both knowing and not knowing.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). Glory spreading into all beings (914) is the first half. The face that no one sees (914) is the second half.
- **The act below** (726) is the Chabad teaching of "arousal below brings arousal above" (Tanya ch. 4 and 46; Iggeret HaKodesh 4).
- **Serve from will** (537) fits Tanya ch. 41: the servant who serves without love is still called a servant, but the higher way is from love and will.
- **The far one's humbling** (850) is close to Tanya ch. 7 and the teaching that returning from far reaches higher than the righteous (Berachot 34b, used in Tanya ch. 7 and Iggeret HaTeshuva ch. 7).
