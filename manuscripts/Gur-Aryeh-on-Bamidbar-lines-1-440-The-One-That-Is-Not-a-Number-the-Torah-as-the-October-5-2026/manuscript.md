This is the first half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Bamidbar: the census and the camps, the levites, the nazir, the priestly blessing, the gifts of the princes, the menorah, the cloud, the complainers, the spies, the libations and challah, tzitzit, and Korach. Much is law, and the sotah and other passages are left out under D28. But this half holds some of the Maharal's clearest words on how a person is joined to G-d.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The one is the root of number and is not a number.** The prince counted with Moshe stands like the one in counting: "והיו הנשיאים דומים כמו האחד בחשבון, שהוא יסוד המספר, ובעצמו אינו מספר" (38). "The princes were like the one in counting, which is the root of number and is not itself a number."
- **A person cleaves to G-d through a middle, and the middle is the Torah.** "דע כי האדם הוא דבק בשם יתברך, ודבקתו הוא על ידי שלשה דברים" (333). "וכל דבר שהוא דבק בדבר, אי אפשר רק על ידי דבר אמצעי ביניהם, הוא המחבר הדברים" (333). "והחבור שהוא אל אדם עם בוראו היא התורה, שהיא אמצעי בין האדם לבוראו" (333).
- **Every act of G-d needs a doer below.** Moshe struck the gold, and the menorah came out by itself. "וכך הוא כל מעשה שפועל השם, הכל צריך פועל למטה, והשם יתברך גומר על ידו" (144).
- **Making a "head" of what is under Him is the root of idolatry.** The spies said "let us make a head." "לפי שרצו לעשות ראש וסיבה ועיקר" (278), "rather it is under the Holy One": "אלא שהוא תחת הקדוש ברוך הוא" (278).
- **Quarrel is all strict law; beyond the line there is no quarrel.** "להודיע כי המחלוקת הוא גוף הגיהנם" (391). "כי יש נמצאים נכנסים לפנים משורת הדין, ואין באלו אנשים מחלוקת" (391).
- **He counts each one out of love.** "מפני חיבתן מונה אותם כל שעה" (1). And each one is worth the whole Torah: "שהרי כל אחד מישראל חשוב כחמשה חומשי תורה, ואם מת הרי כאילו נשרף ספר תורה" (155).

## 2.1 The one that is not a number

Why is the prince counted with Moshe and Aharon? The Maharal answers with a rule about number. The one is where number starts, but the one is not itself one of the many: "והיו הנשיאים דומים כמו האחד בחשבון, שהוא יסוד המספר, ובעצמו אינו מספר" (38). He says this about the princes. But the rule is the rule he uses for G-d in all his works: G-d is One as the root of all counting, not as the first item in a list. (Ours: this is the same shape as the Rambam's "One, but not like one of the counted.")

## 2.2 The three bonds, and the Torah as the middle

This is the main unity passage in the range. Why is idolatry like denying the whole Torah? Because a person is joined to G-d by three bonds: "דע כי האדם הוא דבק בשם יתברך, ודבקתו הוא על ידי שלשה דברים" (333). G-d is his G-d, which is the yoke. The person is His servant, which is the brit, the seal on the one who receives. And there is a middle that joins the two. The rule: "וכל דבר שהוא דבק בדבר, אי אפשר רק על ידי דבר אמצעי ביניהם, הוא המחבר הדברים" (333). "Anything that cleaves to another can do so only through a middle between them, which joins them." That middle is the Torah: "והחבור שהוא אל אדם עם בוראו היא התורה, שהיא אמצעי בין האדם לבוראו" (333). So the idolater denies all three, and also "וכופר גם כן בחבור אשר הוא בין העילה ובין העלול, והוא התורה" (333): "he also denies the bond between the Cause and what is caused, which is the Torah."

## 2.3 Korach: "we need no middle"

Korach's claim is the reverse of 2.2. A tallit all of blue needs no blue thread; a house full of books needs no mezuzah. So, he said, we need no kohen for deeds and no Moshe for learning: "כי אינם צריכים שום אמצעי" (362). "והיו סבורים הרשעים כי אינם צריכין לשום אמצעי להיישיר אותם בשני אלו דברים" (362). The Maharal calls this wicked. The middle is not a wall between us and G-d. It is the joint.

## 2.4 A head under Him is not a head

The spies said "let us make a head and go back to Egypt." The Maharal reads it as idolatry: "לפי שרצו לעשות ראש וסיבה ועיקר" (278), to make a head, cause and root of something that is not a head, "אלא שהוא תחת הקדוש ברוך הוא" (278): "rather it is under the Holy One." There is one Head. Anything else we make a head is under Him.

## 2.5 Every act of G-d needs a doer below

The menorah was hammered from one piece. Moshe could not do it. So G-d said: throw the gold into the fire and strike it, and it came out by itself. "וכך הוא כל מעשה שפועל השם, הכל צריך פועל למטה, והשם יתברך גומר על ידו" (144). "והיה פועל כפי מה שהיה יכול, והקדוש ברוך הוא גומר על ידו" (144). "He did as much as he could, and the Holy One finished it through him."

## 2.6 The seven as one

"Shivat hanerot," the seven lamps, is said of the whole menorah, "כיון שכל שבעת הנרות מנורה אחת, ואי אפשר לאחד בלא השני" (141). A whole is named by what its part does, "כמו שיאמר האדם" (141) "the knife cuts," "אף על גב שאין חותך רק חוד הסכין" (141): "even though only the edge of the knife cuts." Many parts, one thing.

## 2.7 The Mishkan and the world

"כי מלאכת המשכן שקול כמו בריאת העולם, וכל דבר שהיה בבריאת העולם היה במלאכת המשכן" (133). The gifts of the princes hint at the foundations of the world, "לדמות משכן אל כלל העולם" (133): "to liken the Mishkan to the whole world." The small house of G-d holds the order of the whole.

## 2.8 The middle is like the whole

A smaller rule from the camps: "כי האמצעי לעולם דומה אל הכלל, לפי שהאמצעי הוא נוטה לב' הצדדין" (60). The middle leans to both sides, so it stands for all of them. And the camps stood in a circle around the Tent, "כי העגולה היא נוכחית לנקודה" (17): "for a circle faces its center point."

## 2.9 Above mazal; the Land's rank

"דאין מזל לישראל" (272): "Israel has no mazal." They receive from G-d directly, not through the stars. And the Land has "מעלה עליונה אלהית" (263): "ואם כן יש בארץ מעלה עליונה אלהית" (263), a high, divine rank.

- **MH-S61 "Counted out of love" (1, 155).** "מפני חיבתן מונה אותם כל שעה" (1). "וזהו החיבה, שאינו סומך על האומד" (1). Inside: I am not a number in a crowd, or an estimate. He counts me, one by one, again and again, because He loves me. And I am worth the whole Torah (155). How to enter: say your own name before Him, slowly, and think: "You counted me today too." It differs from S86 The other held, which is about seeing others. This is being seen.
- **MH-S62 "Joined through the middle" (333).** "והחבור שהוא אל אדם עם בוראו היא התורה, שהיא אמצעי בין האדם לבוראו" (333). Inside: when I learn, I am not studying about G-d from far away. I am holding the joint between me and Him. How to enter: before learning, stop and say: "This is the bond between me and You." Then learn. It differs from S60 The essential bond, which is the bond itself, beneath all. This is the bond felt through the Torah in the hand.
- **MH-S63 "Beyond the line" (391).** "כי יש נמצאים נכנסים לפנים משורת הדין, ואין באלו אנשים מחלוקת" (391). Inside: I could demand my full right. I let some of it go, and the quarrel has nowhere to stand. How to enter: in a dispute, find one thing you have a right to, and give it up freely. It differs from S59 True lowliness, which is an inner state. This is lowliness acted out in a quarrel.

## 4.1 Hear your name counted (`mh_counted_by_love`, new)

The practice for MH-S61 (1). Once a day, say your name before G-d. Think: "You counted me today. Not as a guess." When you see another Jew, think of 155: this one is worth the five books of the Torah. And like the verse that speaks briefly "מפני חיבתן, שלא לדבר בחטאם" (155), do not name their sin.

## 4.2 Learn as holding the bond (`mh_torah_as_bond`, new)

The practice for MH-S62 (333). Before learning, say: "This is the middle between me and You." Learn one line. At the end, say: "I was joined to You through this." Do not skip the Torah to reach G-d "directly." Korach said "we need no middle" (362), and that was his fall.

## 4.3 Do your part; He finishes (`mh_act_below_first`, extended)

Add 144 to the practice from Shemot. "והיה פועל כפי מה שהיה יכול, והקדוש ברוך הוא גומר על ידו" (144). Practice: when a task is too big for you, do the part you can, with full effort. Then leave the finish to Him. And do not rely on a miracle instead: "ואין זה בלתי מאמין, שאדרבה, אין לסמוך על הנס" (250), "ויש להשתדל לעשות כל דבר שראוי לעשות" (250).

## 4.4 Drop the "but" (`mh_no_but`, new)

The spies' sin was one word, "efes," "but." The land is good, "but" the people are strong. Every "but" "הוא למעט מענין הראשון" (250): "comes to lessen what was said first." Practice: when you speak of a person or of G-d's gift, notice when you add "but." Stop there. Let the good stand alone.

## 4.5 Help your brother (`mh_help_your_brother`, new)

One who does not help his brother: "כלומר שאין עוזרו, הרי נאכל חצי בשרו" (244): "it is as if half his own flesh were eaten." Practice: when someone close needs help, do not ask "is it my job?" Their loss is half of you.

## 4.6 Go beyond the line (`mh_beyond_the_line`, new)

The practice for MH-S63 (391). Quarrel is "הכל דין" (391), all strict law, "שאין ויתור לו" (391), giving nothing up. Practice: in any dispute this week, give up one thing you have a right to. Say nothing about it. Watch the quarrel lose its ground.

## 4.7 Eager is glad (`mh_do_it_now`, extended)

"והזירוז הוא שמחה בכל מקום" (421): "eagerness is joy everywhere." Add to the practice: when you do a mitzvah quickly, that quickness is itself joy. And do not rejoice at another's fall: "אין הקדוש ברוך הוא שמח במפלתן של רשעים" (421).

## 4.8 Keep from wine (`mh_silence_for_mind`, extended as the never-drink guard)

"כל הרואה סוטה בקלקולה יפרוש עצמו מן היין" (102). The sages tie wine to the loss of self-control. Add to the guard: the guide never suggests drinking, and it uses this line only as a reason to keep away.

## 5. The big human questions

**Does G-d see me as one of many?** He counts each one, each hour, out of love (1). Each one is worth the whole Torah (155).

**How am I joined to G-d?** By three bonds: He is my G-d, I am His servant, and the Torah is the middle that joins (333).

**Do I need the Torah, or can I go straight to G-d?** Anything that cleaves to another does so through a middle (333). Korach said "we need no middle," and fell (362).

**What is idolatry at root?** Making a head of what is under Him (278).

**I cannot finish this. What do I do?** Do what you can. G-d finishes it through you (144). But do not sit and wait for a miracle (250).

**Why do quarrels never end?** A quarrel is all strict law and gives nothing up (391). Go beyond the line, and it has nowhere to stand.

**What is a mitzvah for?** Not for G-d's sake, as if He needs it. For the doer's good, to make him "נפש טוב" (440): "כדי שיהיה האדם נפש טוב, ולא יהיה נפש רע" (440). "אבל המצות אשר נתן לנו הקדוש ברוך הוא כולם הם לתועלת המקיים המצוה" (440). "ובודאי טעם יש בכל מצוה ומצוה" (440).

**Is being exempt a relief?** No. "שיותר טוב שיהיה חייב" (160): "it is better to be obligated." One who could not do a mitzvah still lacks its merit: "שכל מי שהוא פטור מן המצוה מחמת שהוא אונס, גנאי הוא לו שלא עשה המצוה" (160).

**What is a person, at the core?** "כיון דעיקר האדם הוא דעתו" (148): his mind. So one draws a person only with words (355).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Tzimtzum.** The Maharal does not speak here of G-d hiding. He speaks of a middle. Anything that cleaves needs a joint (333), and the joint is the Torah. Tanya says the Torah is His will and wisdom, and one who learns it holds Him, like embracing a king (Tanya ch. 4-5). Both make the Torah the place where the person and G-d meet. Chabad adds that the tzimtzum is why a middle is needed: the light is hidden, so it must come dressed in Torah and mitzvot. The Maharal says the middle is needed because two things join only through a third.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "The one is the root of number and not a number" (38) matches the Rambam: G-d is One, but not one of the counted (Yesodei HaTorah 1:7; Guide I:57). The Rambam says it to deny that G-d is like anything. The Maharal uses it to say something positive: the one is the root from which all counting comes. Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 speaks the same way of His unity as more than "one and not two."
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "The Mishkan weighs as much as creation" (133) and "the seven are one menorah" (141) are the first half: the many hold one order. "A head that is under Him" (278) is the second half: nothing in the world is a head beside Him.
- **The tzaddik as the middle.** Korach's "we need no middle" (362) is exactly what Chabad answers about the Rebbe and the tzaddik: the soul of Moshe in each generation joins the people to G-d (Tanya ch. 42, Iggeret HaKodesh 27). The Maharal says the middle is Moshe for learning and the kohen for deeds. Tanya says Moshe's spark is in each Jew, so the middle is also within.
- **Every act needs a doer below** (144) is close to Chabad's "arousal from below" (itaruta diletata) drawing an arousal from above (Tanya ch. 46; Iggeret HaKodesh 4). The Maharal's picture is exact: the person strikes, the menorah comes out by itself.
- **Mitzvot for the doer's good** (440) is the Maharal. Chabad adds the other side: the mitzvot are also for His sake, to make Him a dwelling below (Tanya ch. 36). Both can stand. The mitzvah makes the doer good, and the doer's goodness is His home.
- **Beyond the line** (391) fits Tanya ch. 32 on loving every Jew, and Chabad's teaching on bittul in dealing with others.
