This range closes the *Aseret HaDibrot* part of the book. Sukkah is the pillar of peace. Tamid (the daily offering) holds the Shelah's longest teaching on prayer. Megillah joins Purim to charity. Three findings matter most for the guide. First, the **middle way of body and soul**: the Creator joined a high soul to a dark body "so that both would live together", and a joy aimed at His Name turns even food and drink into service. Second, a full **manual of prayer**: sit silent first, think of His greatness and your smallness, add your own words, and stop when a stray thought comes. Third, the clearest statement in the book of the **three levels of G-d's attributes**, where the Shelah says the philosophers who deny attributes are right about the Infinite itself, and right only there.

## 1.1 The Infinite takes no attribute; the attributes live in Atzilut

This is the key passage of the range for the question of the Rambam and Chabad. The Talmud tells of a prayer leader who piled up praises and was rebuked. The Shelah brings the Rambam (*Guide* 1:59) and the *Ikkarim*: every positive attribute is a lack in Him, and we know Him only by negation or by His acts (lines 6059-6061). Then he gives his own answer in three levels (line 6062):

> לא יצדק בו שום תואר ולא שם ולא אות ולא נקודה כי הוא פשוט תכלית הפשיטות
> (no attribute fits Him, no name, no letter, no dot, for He is simple, the utmost of simplicity; line 6062)

> ובו יצדק מאמר הפיליסופים השוללים התוארים
> (and here the saying of the philosophers who deny attributes is correct; line 6062)

> בחינת האצילות ששם יצדקו התוארים
> (the level of Atzilut, where the attributes are true; line 6062)

The third level is knowing His Names through His acts. So "eye, ear, hand" and the other terms are inner, spiritual things said of Atzilut, the ways the Infinite watches over the world (line 6062). To say them of His Essence itself is an insult. That is why one uses only the praises the Men of the Great Assembly fixed (line 6063). *Ours:* this is the same structure as *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 9-10 and the Ramak: the Rambam is right about the Essence, the sefirot are where attributes apply, and He is in them without being defined by them.

## 1.2 Souls are one above; division below cuts above

The secret of the pillar of peace (line 5919):

> מכל מקום למעלה בסוד נשמתם הם אחדות אחד
> (still above, in the secret of their souls, they are one unity; line 5919)

> וכשיש פירוד למטה ביניהם, כביכול עושים פירוד וקציצה למעלה
> (and when there is division among them below, as it were they make division and cutting above; line 5919)

Branches that want to return to their root must look like the root, one below as well. And His Name is "Peace". This carries the Ramak line from the Yoma range (5249, 5793) into daily life: the unity of G-d is kept or cut in how people treat each other.

## 1.3 Without Him not one moment

In the three prayers of the day, one thanks Him that He supplies all needs, "and without Him we cannot exist and stand even one moment" (וזולתו אי אפשר לנו להתקיים ולעמוד אפילו רגע אחד, line 6013). Hands clasped like one bound, who has no power "to bring near the helpful and keep away the harmful" except with His help (line 6013). The Shema's "One" means "you shall have no trust in an angel or in a star" (לא יהיה לך מבטח לא במלאך ולא במזל, line 5981), "only in G-d alone, for He is one and there is no second to Him" (רק בה' לבדו כי הוא אחד ואין שני לו, line 5981). *Ours:* the renewal of being (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-3) stated as a prayer.

## 1.4 The joy of the unity

The Shema moves from fear to love: "let him pass from fear into love, so that the joy of faith in the unity is in his heart" (יכנס מהיראה לאהבה להיות שמחת אמונת היחוד בלבו, line 5991). Crown Him over every limb: "no limb of his may be empty" (ולא יהיה אבר מאיבריו פנוי, line 5999). "Truth" after the Shema means He is first, last, and there is no other power in between (line 6000).

## 1.5 The world was made so His G-dliness would be known

In the prayer for children: "You created Your world so that Your G-dliness would be known" (ובראת עולמך בגין לאשתמודע אלהותך, line 6091). And the flame: "Israel are like the wick to the Glory" (כן ישראל הם כדמיון הפתילה אל הכבוד, line 6099), "and they prepare it to shine" (והם מתקנים אותו להאיר, line 6099), from *Avodat HaKodesh*. The blue flame consumes whatever clings to it, but those who cling to G-d live (line 6100). When no one below clings, the last *heh* of the Name becomes a poor *dalet* (line 6101).

## 1.6 Aim to the Infinite

From the Zohar (Vayakhel): in prayer "his will clings like the flame to the coal" (אדבק רעותיה כנורא בגחלתא, line 6119), and the Shelah's rule: "that he must aim in his thought up to the Infinite" (שצריך לכוין במחשבתו עד אין סוף, line 6119), and unite all to its source.

## 3.1 Joy for His Name (proposed: `joy_for_the_name`)

Hillel at the water drawing said "if I am here, all is here" (line 5856). The Shelah reads "I" as G-d: "when we aim our joy to the Name of G-d, then G-d will be with us and His Presence will dwell among us" (כשנכוין בשמחתינו לשם ה', אז יהי ה' אלקינו עמנו ושכינתו תהיה שרויה בינינו, line 5856). "We will rejoice in Him", not in the day (line 5857). *Tie:* `heart_after`, `act`. *Ours:* this is the positive side of the guide's no-drinking rule. The Shelah himself warns against drunkenness here (line 5859) and at Purim (line 6138).

## 3.2 The joy ladder (proposed: `joy_ladder`)

The Tur: the pious, when happy, bless and praise even more (line 5860), "and let a person say in his heart in his time of joy and pleasure: if this is the joy of this world" (ויאמר האדם בלבו בעת שמחתו והנאתו, אם כך היא שמחת העולם הזה, line 5860), what will the joy of the world to come be. *Tie:* `length`, `heart_after`. *Ours:* a simple move for any good moment: thank, then ask what the source of this good must be.

## 3.3 The middle way of body and soul

From *Emek Beracha* (lines 5847-5850). Some afflict themselves "and this is not a straight path, for this is not the Creator's intent" (line 5847). The gluttons are worse. The middle: "for G-d joined the high spiritual soul to a dark and murky body so that both would live together" (כי הש"י הרכיב הנשמה העליונה רוחנית בגוף אופל ועכור שיחיו שניהם יחדיו, line 5850), and each gets its share; "one who does not walk this way cancels the purpose of creation" (line 5850). *Tie:* S82. *Ours:* a strong support for the guide's rule against harsh practice.

## 3.4 Preparation before prayer (proposed: `sit_first`)

The seventh of ten aids (line 5947): "let him sit a little, idle and silent, and remove his thoughts from his heart" (ישב מעט בטל ודומם ויסיר מחשבותיו מלבו). Then think of the greatness of the One who spoke and the world came to be; then of the good He has done for you; "and let him think of his own lack and smallness" (ויחשוב בגריעות ופחיתות עצמו); only then stand and pray. The same before the Amidah: "the greatness of G-d and the lowness of man", for "wherever we find His greatness, we find His humility" (line 6012). And in the rebukes (line 6116): "let him train himself not to start praying or blessing at once" but be silent first. *Tie:* `arrive`, `quieting`, `breadth`, `before_whom`. *Ours:* the order matters: silence, greatness, kindness, smallness. The kindness step keeps smallness from becoming shame.

## 3.5 When a stray thought comes, stop

"Let him be silent until it leaves his heart" (ישתוק עד שתצא מלבו, line 6116), be a little ashamed before the One who knows thoughts, and begin again. If the mind will not clear, "let him think that the Creator stands opposite me, watches what leaves my lips, and tests the thoughts of my heart" (line 5938). *Tie:* `before_whom`, `edge`. *Ours:* simple and kind: pause, do not fight.

## 3.6 Your own words, and the short prayer (proposed: `short_prayer`)

In each blessing add something new of your own, "for these prepare the heart the most" (כי ביותר הם מכינים את הלב, line 5942). In "who hears prayer" ask everything and confess, "for He hears the prayer of every mouth, fit or unfit" (line 6041). Each person must pray for his own needs "and let him not trust in his diligence and his intelligence" (line 6079), and "after the prayer let him do this business and put his trust in G-d" (line 6079). Before each task: "let him make a short prayer habitual on his tongue, to cast his burden on G-d... Master of the worlds, make my way succeed, for all is from You" (line 6090). *Tie:* `own_words`, `lips`, S84.

## 3.7 The Rokeach's six questions (proposed: `six_questions`)

"Think on the unity: in whom you trust, whom you serve, who sees you, who knows your thoughts and deeds, and to whom your spirit returns" (חשוב היחוד במי אתה בוטח ולמי תעבוד מי רואה בך מי יודע מחשבותך ומעשיך ולמי תשוב רוחך, line 6114). And before: "I will tear open my closed heart and come to His doors in awe and humility" (line 6114). First lowliness, which is fear; then His greatness "with a good heart", which is joy (line 6114). *Tie:* `before_whom`, `breadth`.

## 3.8 The ladder of praises

The Shelah's brother, R. Yaakov Horowitz (line 6116): one who puts to heart the meaning of the praises will find them enter "little by little, day after day", then awe, then love, "until he reaches the inner awe, which is the last completeness" (יראה הפנימיית שהיא השלמות האחרון). *Tie:* `chazarah`, `depth`. *Ours:* a slow path, not a technique for one sitting.

## 3.9 The weeping voice and the poor man's prayer

The gates of tears are not locked; if one cannot cry, speak low and broken "as if he were weeping" (כאלו היה בוכה, line 6014). The Zohar: "this is a whole prayer, which is in the heart" (line 6117). The poor man's prayer (line 6015): make yourself poor before Him, "and I and He, alone together". *Tie:* `own_words`, S33. *Ours:* keep this soft. The guide does not push tears.

## 3.10 Peace as practice

Greet everyone first and increase peace "even with a gentile in the market" (ואפי' עם גוי בשוק, line 5892). Seek peace where you are and pursue it elsewhere (line 5896). Guard another's money more than your own (lines 5893-5895). Anger is the root of strife (line 5900). The Shelah retells Aaron the peacemaker but prefers finding a way without lying (line 5898). *Tie:* S86, `act`.

## 3.11 Trust and rooms for seclusion

The sukkah teaches "that a person not put his trust in the height of his house" (line 5921), "but put his trust in the One who spoke and the world came to be" (line 5921). Build not palaces but "rooms to be alone in for Torah and for return" (line 5923). *Tie:* `seclusion` (proposed in the Yoma report), S84.

## 3.12 Raise the ordinary

Even what one does for oneself, feeding one's children: "if he can raise this thing to the honor of His Name, let him take it to heart, wake up to it, and do it with joy" (line 6145). *Tie:* `work_as_means` (proposed earlier), `act`.

## 3.13 Less, with intention

"Better a little with intention than much without intention" (טוב מעט בכוונה מהרבות שלא בכוונה, line 6064). Against people who race through Psalms. *Tie:* `details`.

## 4. The big human questions

- **Should I enjoy this world?** Yes, with the soul: the middle way (lines 5847-5850). One who takes no share "cancels the purpose of creation".
- **Where is real joy?** "There is no joy greater or finer than to gladden the heart of the poor, orphans, widows and strangers", and one who does "is like the Presence" (line 6137). Locking the door and feasting alone is only "the joy of his belly" (line 5886).
- **Why pray if He knows?** Because trust in one's own cleverness is the mistake (line 6079), and because prayer is a gift to the King (line 6120). He hears "every mouth, fit or unfit" (line 6041).
- **What if my mind wanders?** Stop, be quiet, begin again (line 6116). Confused thoughts come from the soul's past wrongs, and study at night clears them (line 6121).
- **Who is G-d, can I describe Him?** Not in His Essence (line 6062). Use the fixed words; pour out your own heart in confession and need, and there "whoever tells much is praiseworthy" (line 6063).
- **Why do people hurt each other, and does it matter above?** Division below cuts above (line 5919).

## 5. With Chabad (kept)

- **Negative attributes.** Line 6062 is the Shelah's direct ruling on the Rambam. He accepts the negative attributes for the Infinite in itself and places the positive ones in Atzilut. This matches *Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah* ch. 9-10 and *Likkutei Torah*'s use of the Rambam: the Rambam is right about the Essence, and the sefirot are His will to be known. It also matches the Ramak: "He is all that exists", yet the attributes are not the Essence.
- **The renewal of being.** "Without Him we cannot stand one moment" (line 6013) matches Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-2.
- **Joy.** The joy of faith in the unity (line 5991) and joy with the poor (line 6137) match Tanya ch. 26, 31-33 (joy of the soul, joy in the unity) and the Alter Rebbe's warning against sadness.
- **Prayer.** Contemplating His greatness and one's smallness before prayer (lines 5947, 6012) is the Chabad order of *hitbonenut* before the Amidah (Tanya ch. 41-42, *Kuntres HaTefillah*), though here in a simpler form.
- **Unity of souls.** Line 5919 matches Tanya ch. 32: all souls are one at the root, and love of a fellow is the gate.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not discussed in this range.
- **Not carried.** The Psalms-to-sefirot map (lines 6065-6078); the Chanukah and Purim kavanot (6102-6109, 6161-6162); the martyrdom prayer and fasts in the personal prayers (6088-6092, 6094-6095); drinking at Purim and the Ari's drinking kavanah (6138, 6162); tithe law (6147-6151); Chanukah and Purim halacha; the seventy bulls framed as the nations blessed only through Israel (5907); Aaron's white lies (5897).
