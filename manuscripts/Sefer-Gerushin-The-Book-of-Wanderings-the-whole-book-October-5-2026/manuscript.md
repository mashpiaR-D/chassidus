In the winter of 1548 the Ramak, his teacher and brother-in-law R. Shlomo Alkabetz, and a few friends began to go out of Safed on foot. They walked to ruined study houses, caves and the graves of the sages of the Mishnah. On the way they opened a verse, with no book and no preparation, and spoke what came. They called these walks gerushin (wanderings, or exiles), because they went out into exile together with the Shechinah (the Presence of God in the world, who is also in exile). This book is the Ramak's diary of what came to them, section by section, walk by walk.

Most of it is dense reading of verses through the sefirot. What matters for the guide is the practice itself, how it felt, and a handful of lines on unity that are among the clearest the Ramak wrote.

The text is a typed copy from grimoar.cz. It has not been checked against a printed edition, and it keeps the editor's brackets. ## 2. The unity of God in this book

**"There is none else", as a ladder.** Line 180 reads "You were shown, to know, that the Lord is God; there is none else besides Him" (Deuteronomy 4:35) in three ways. The third is a path for the mind: this verse teaches "how a person lets his thought climb from below upward" (כיצד ישלשל האדם מחשבתו מעליו לעיל, line 180). Start with "You were shown": God seen in Malchut, the place where He shows Himself. Rise to "to know": Daat (knowing, the hidden bond). Rise to "the Lord is God": Chochmah and Binah (wisdom and understanding). "And after that to Keter, which is Ayin" (ואח"כ אל הכתר שהוא האי"ן, line 180): Keter (the Crown), called Ayin (Nothing). And last, "there is none else": the hidden Ein Sof, "who needs no other, for there is nothing above Him, and all need Him" (שאינו צריך לזולתו שאין למעלה ממנו והכל צריכין לו, line 180).

**No empty place.** The Kedushah (the "Holy, holy, holy" prayer) draws holiness down from the Crown to "the whole earth is full of His glory", "for the low world fills with the Presence of His strength, and there is no empty place" (כי העולם השפל מתמלא משכינת עוזו, ואין מקום חלל, line 201).

**Less light is only from our side.** When the light seems small, "the lessening and the growing of the light are not from the side of the upper ones but from the side of the lower ones" (ועם כל זה אין מעוט האור ורבוי מסטרא דעילאין אלא מסטרא דתתאין, line 215).

**We and the Presence are one.** On "and You are within us" (ואתה בקרבנו, line 130): the divine is clothed in souls as a soul is clothed in a body. So "we are truly the Shechinah, for the Shechinah is the soul of Israel, and it is all one thing" (כי אנו ממש השכינה, כי השכינה נשמתו של ישראל, והכל דבר אחד, line 130).

**Even the pull to wrong goes home.** In the time to come the yetzer will "enter and be drawn into its holy source, which is Gevurah, and become a holy spark" (שיכנס וישתאב במקורו הקדוש שהוא הגבורה ויהיה ניצוץ קדוש, line 19). Nothing stays outside forever. Holiness is so strong that "it is the nature of holiness to purify and consume every kind of shell in the world" (כי מטבע הקדושה לטהר ולכלות כל מיני קליפה שבעולם, line 75). In Egypt "the Holy One Himself, in His glory, was clothed inside the shell" to end it (היה הקב"ה בעצמו ובכבודו מתלבש בתוך הקליפה, line 189).

## 3. States of consciousness

- **Words that come by themselves** (new, RK-S30). "The words of Torah were shining in us, and the words were said by themselves" (והיו דברי תורה מאירים בנו, והדברים נאמרים מפי עצמם, line 11); "there the words were shining on their own" (כי שם היו מאירים הדברים מעצמם, line 15). At the end of a day: "they are all from above, given without any study at all, and they are sweeter than honey, a gift of the Queen to those who wander and go into exile with her" (הם כולם עליונים, מושפעים בלי עיון כלל, והם מתוקים מדבש. מתנת המלכות אל המתגרשים ומתנודדים עמה, line 32). Close to S52 (the song that sings itself) and S79 (learning: mind clothed in His wisdom). Its mark is that the insight is received, not worked out, and it comes while walking with others.
- **Wandering with her** (new, RK-S31). The walker is in exile on purpose, keeping the Presence company. The Shechinah goes from place to place looking for one fitting person, "for the righteous one goes wandering and roaming for her sake, and he unites her" (כי הצדיק הולך נע ונד בעבורה והוא מיחדה, line 215). What he suffers there "is suffering of love, real love" (ואלו הם יסורין של אהבה, אהבה ממש, line 215). Close to S73 (exile inside), but turned outward: my loneliness becomes her company.
- **The seal that stays** (new, RK-S32). After a union, the two go back to their places, and yet the mark remains, "like a seal: when it is lifted from the place it stamped, still its form stays there" (כחותם שכאשר יוסר ממקום שחתמו בו, עם כל זה ישאר שם מציאותו, line 146). The Ramak says it of Tiferet and Malchut. Ours: it is also the state after a real moment in prayer. The moment passes; the print does not. Links S27 (knowing that holds) and S26 (it goes flat), as the answer to S26.
- **A short prayer from the walls of the heart.** At Meron: "there I prayed a short prayer from the walls of my heart" (ושם התפללתי תפלה קצרה מתוך קירות לבי, line 41). S55 (own words to Him).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**RK-T40. Gerushin: the walk** (new). Go out on foot, with one or two friends, away from your usual place. Carry no book. On the way someone says a verse, and each says what the verse opens in him, "suddenly, without study" (Ohr Ne'erav line 427). When the words come by themselves (line 11), write them down that day. The Ramak's group walked to ruins and to graves; "we went out to the field, to the rocks, to wander" (ויצאנו השדה אל הסלעים להתגרש, line 77). Some walked partly barefoot in summer, "without a vow" (בלא נדר נצא קצת יחפין בסוד השכינה, line 28). The inner meaning: you go into exile with Her, so She is not alone. Links arrive and letters.

**RK-T41. The ladder of "there is none else"** (new). Line 180, as a sitting. Say Deuteronomy 4:35 slowly, one phrase at a time, and let each phrase lift the mind one step. "You were shown": God as He shows Himself here, in this world, in this hour. "To know": the bond under the showing. "The Lord is God": the wisdom and understanding behind it all. Then the Crown, Ayin, where thought stops. Then "there is none else": the One who needs nothing and whom all need. Stay there. Links edge, before_whom, and S62 (only Him).

**RK-T42. Blessing as paying back** (new). Line 168: one who enjoys the world's flow without a blessing takes from "father and mother". "So one who eats and enjoys must bless, to set right what he bent" (ולכן האוכל ונהנה לתקן את אשר עותו צריך לברך, line 168). The blessing returns the flow with more. Before food, stop and say the blessing as a repayment: I took; I give back. Overlaps CATALOGUE A2 and RK-T23. The Ramak's line adds the moral weight: taking without thanks is a kind of theft.

**Prayer that never stops.** "Would that a person pray all day long" (הלואי שיתפלל אדם כל היום כולו, line 169), because each request brings new flow into the world.

**Weekday by deed, Shabbat by intent.** On weekdays the union comes through action. On Shabbat it comes "in the spiritual, by intention and the full light" (אלא ברוחני ע"י הכונה והאור השלם, line 46). The servant is moved by force; the King's daughter only by "speech and word and intention, that is, by gentleness" (אלא דבור ואמירה וכונה והיינו בפיוסא, line 48). Ours: the higher the place, the softer the touch.

**Not for the guide.** The Names and letter-counts in lines 30-32 and 104. Lines 182 and 187 on converts and nations as "shells" are particularist; the guide follows D28 and does not take them. Line 205 points the other way: when the shells end, all peoples will turn "with one speech, all to call on the Name of God and serve Him together" (שפה אחת לקרוא כלם בשם ה' ולעבדו שכם א', line 205).

## 5. The big human questions

**If I am not worthy, are my prayers wasted?** No. The Ramak brings the Zohar: a person who is not fit, "all his prayers are gathered under the hand of one angel until he returns" (שכל תפלותיו מתקבצות תחת יד מלאך אחד עד שובו, line 44), and then they are all received together. And about himself at Meron: "even though I am not fit for them to be crowned through me because of my bad deeds, still they will be crowned through words of Torah, for words of Torah do not take on impurity" (כי דברי תורה אינם מקבלים טומאה, line 43).

**Are my good deeds lost if I fall?** No. Even the harshest cutting off reaches only the lowest soul, "so that this poor man will not lose all the good deeds he did" (כדי שלא יאבד העני הזה כל מעשיו הטובים אשר עשה, line 199).

**Does my small act matter above?** Yes. "The light of deeds makes a channel for the flow" (כי אור המעשים עושה צנור לשפע, line 43). When people keep adorning the Presence at every moment, "Tiferet will never leave, and He will dwell in the world through the Shechinah" (הנה לא תסתלק הת"ת לעולם, ויהיה שוכן בעולם ע"י השכינה, line 105). Without the human side, "if you were not like her soul, doing deeds to unite, they would never be united" (ואם לא (היתה) [היית] אתה כענין נשמתה, שאתה עושה מעשים כדי להתייחד, לעולם לא היו מתיחדים, line 180).

**Where can I find the hidden God?** Only in the revealed. "If a person tries to go further in than Malchut he will not grasp Wisdom, for it is grasped only in her" (ואם יכנס אדם לפנים מהמלכות לא ישיג החכמה, כי אינה מושגת אלא בה, line 117). The way up is through the world, the word, the deed, never around them.

**What is the body for?** The body is a garment given after the first sin, and the task is to refine it "and bring it into holiness as it was before the sin" (ולהכניסה בקדושה כמו שהיתה קודם חטאו, line 40). Meanwhile "the righteous are clothed in this world in words of Torah" (כי הצדיקים מתלבשים בעה"ז בדברי תורה, line 40).

**Why is my joy bigger with others?** When souls shine into each other, "all the souls are included in each other" (ונמצא מזה כל הנשמות נכללות אלו באלו, line 25), and the light multiplies like combinations: "two stones build two houses" (ששני אבנים בונים שני בתים, line 26), three build six, four build twenty-four. That is why many doing a good deed together is not like a few.

**Do we need a building to hold God?** In the end, no: "we will be a throne for the pleasantness, and will not need the Tabernacle" (כי נהיה אנחנו כסא אל הנועם ולא נצטרך אל המשכן, line 207).

**Who is rich?** "Who is rich? One who is glad in his portion" (איזהו עשיר, השמח בחלקו, line 174), which the Ramak reads as bringing the joy of Binah down into one's own portion.

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **"There is none else."** The Ramak turns Deuteronomy 4:35 into a ladder that climbs to Ein Sof, who needs none (line 180). The Tanya reads the same verse as a seeing: at Sinai "'You were shown', really, with the senses' sight" (הראת ממש, בראיה חושיית, Tanya line 514). And the Tanya brings it down: "even on the earth below there is none else, and this is His dwelling below" (אשר אפילו בארץ מתחת אין עוד מלבדו, וזו היא דירתו בתחתונים, Tanya line 477). The Ramak climbs up to find Him alone; the Tanya finds Him alone right here. Both are true. The guide can use the Ramak's ladder going up and the Tanya's line coming back down.
- **"No empty place."** Line 201 (the low world fills, "there is no empty place") is the Tanya's "there is nothing outside Him, and no place is empty of Him" (כי אין דבר חוץ ממנו, ולית אתר פנוי מיניה, Tanya line 308). Same teaching. The Ramak ties it to the Kedushah; the Tanya ties it to God's speech that never leaves Him.
- **"We are truly the Shechinah."** Line 130 is close to Tanya chapter 2's "part of God above, truly" (Tanya line 86, checked in an earlier report). The Ramak says it of the whole people, as one soul of the Shechinah. D28: the guide says it of every human soul.
- **Less light only from our side** (line 215). This is the Chabad reading of tzimtzum in one sentence: the hiding is for us, not in Him (Gate of Unity chapter 7). The Ramak never says tzimtzum here, but he says the same thing.
- **Exile of the Shechinah.** Gerushin makes the Tanya's idea of the Shechinah in exile (Iggeret HaTeshuvah) into something the feet do. Chabad keeps it inside: the "exile" of the divine soul in the body, and teshuvah that raises it. The Ramak's walk is a body practice for the same truth.
