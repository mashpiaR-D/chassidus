## What this range is

Yitro is the second largest node in the Zohar Chadash and it has three parts. The first (to about line 1506) is a long physiognomy, like the Tikkunim's Tikkun 70: hair, forehead, eyes, nose and lips read as signs of a person's soul, with the constant note that teshuvah changes the signs. The second (about 1507 to 1530) is a passage on thought, speech and deed that reads like Patach Eliyahu and belongs with it; it is the unity core of the whole Zohar Chadash. The third (1654 to 1853) is a commentary on Ezekiel's chariot and on the Ten Commandments given "in one word".

## The unity of G-d

**No place and no creature outside His Name.**

> לית אתר ולית בריה בעלמא, דלא שליט שמיה עליה (ZC Yitro 10, line 1352)
> *There is no place and no creature in the world over which His Name does not rule.*

And the famous picture of the rose, whose petals are read as the letters of the Name:

> דלית אפילו עשבא דלא אתברי בשמא (ZC Yitro 11, line 1353)
> *For there is not even a blade of grass that was not created with the Name* (so no one could say another god made it).

**Thought, speech and deed are not cut apart.** This is the heart of the node. The soul is thought, the spirit is speech, and the lower soul is deed, and none has power without the Shechinah:

> לית מחשבה דאית לה חילא לחשוב בלא שכינתא עילאה (ZC Yitro 166, line 1508)
> *There is no thought that has the power to think without the upper Shechinah.*

> הכא לית קיצוץ ופירוד במחשבה ודבור ומעשה (ZC Yitro 170, line 1512)
> *Here there is no cutting and no separation in thought, speech and deed.*

**He changes His works, and in Him there is no change.**

> איהו משנה עובדוי, וביה לית שנוי (ZC Yitro 176, line 1518)
> *He changes His works, and in Him there is no change.*

**He placed His unity in the sefirot, so one who divides them divides Him.**

> וכל מאן דאפריד בשום ספירה (ZC Yitro 178, line 1520)
> *And whoever separates any sefirah* (from another is as if he separated Him).

**The Zohar Chadash's own "leit asar panui".** This is the line that stands beside Patach Eliyahu:

> לית אתר דלאו איהו תמן, לעילא עד אין סוף, ולתתא עד אין תכלית, ולכל סטרא לית אלוה בר מניה (ZC Yitro 179, line 1521)
> *There is no place where He is not: above without end, below without limit, and on every side there is no god besides Him.*

> ואם הוא אסתלק מנייהו, לית לון קיומא (ZC Yitro 179, line 1521)
> *And if He withdrew from them, they would have no existence* (no knowing and no life).

**Wise, but not with a known wisdom. One, but not in number.**

> דאיהו חכים, ולא בחכמה ידיעא (ZC Yitro 181, line 1523)
> *He is wise, but not with a known wisdom.* (And so for understanding, kindness, might, and kingship.)

> איהו אחד, ולא בחושבן (ZC Yitro 182, line 1524)
> *He is one, but not in number.*

The same line says He carries the upper and lower worlds:

> ולית מאן דסביל ליה (ZC Yitro 182, line 1524)
> *And there is no one who carries Him.*

> הוא תפיס בכל מחשבתין, ולית מחשבה ידיעא ביה (ZC Yitro 185, line 1527)
> *He grasps every thought, and no thought knows Him.*

**Why the sefirot, then?** For us, not for Him:

> ובגין דינדעין למקרי ליה באתר ידיע (ZC Yitro 186, line 1528)
> *So that they will know how to call Him in a known place* (He set the sefirot to be known through them).

**He is in every place, and yet shows Himself more in some.**

> דאף על גב דבכל אתר איהו קודשא בריך הוא (ZC Yitro 118, line 1460)
> *Although the Holy One is in every place* (there is a place where He is revealed and a place where He is revealed less).

> וקודשא בריך הוא לא אתגלי באורייתא, אלא לכל חדא כפום עובדוי (ZC Yitro 119, line 1461)
> *And the Holy One is revealed in the Torah only to each one according to his deeds.*

**No likeness in the soul's root.** The neshamah comes from Binah:

> ובה, לית דמיון, ולית צורה (ZC Yitro 111, line 1453)
> *And in it there is no likeness and no form* ("for you saw no image").

**The chariot: it is and is not.** Ezekiel's vision:

> הא איהו, והא לאו איהו (ZC Yitro 345, line 1687)
> *Here it is, and here it is not.*

And the Israelites at Sinai saw the letters only:

> כמאן דחמי מגו עששיתא (ZC Yitro 500, line 1842)
> *Like one who sees through a lamp-glass.*

> בר נש לא יכיל למחמי לראשיה (ZC Yitro 459, line 1801)
> *A person cannot see his own head* (even though it is he; how much less the One hidden within him).

## States of consciousness

**Ratzo vashov in the heart (a map state link: S30 Holding back, and the running and returning of love).**

> וכמא דאינון רצוא ושוב, אוף הכי בלבא ודעתא (ZC Yitro 415, line 1757)
> *And as they run and return, so too in the heart and in the mind.*

This is the source text for the Chassidic "ratzo vashov" as an inner rhythm, not only a vision of angels.

**Leaving bodily thoughts to see within.** The prophets saw when:

> ונפקו ביה מכל רעיוני גופא (ZC Yitro 343, line 1685)
> *They went out, in it, from all the thoughts of the body* (and saw within, in a whisper).

> חיזו בלחישו (ZC Yitro 355, line 1697)
> *A vision in a whisper* ("a still small voice").

We propose (ours): **Z-S14 Seeing in a whisper.** A quiet inner seeing that comes when bodily thoughts are set aside; not an image, and not blankness. The Zohar Chadash is careful: even then, "it is and it is not" (1687). The guide should present this as a state some people touch in prayer, not as a goal to chase.

**Carried.** The angels strain to know and cannot, but:

> אבל כל אינון דמתדבקאן בקדושה עילאה, אינון נטלין לאינון דנטלין לון (ZC Yitro 429, line 1771)
> *But all those who cleave to the upper holiness, they are carried by those who carry them.*

We propose (ours): **Z-S15 Carried.** The one who cleaves does not have to hold himself up; he is borne. Close to S84 Trust, but in the place of cleaving rather than worry.

> לאתדבקא תדיר בשמים (ZC Yitro 387, line 1729)
> *To cleave always to the heavens* (the way of the eagle, whose longing is to rise and cleave).

**The Ten Commandments kissed.** Each person at Sinai:

> כמאן דנשיק בחביבו דרחימותא (ZC Yitro 486, line 1828)
> *Like one who kisses in the fondness of love* (kissed the word in a whisper).

**Joy against the mood of Saturn.** The Zohar Chadash has a striking line against astrology and sadness. Israel has no share in the stars:

> אלא בקודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי (ZC Yitro 290, line 1632)
> *Only in the Holy One alone.* (So on Shabbat, Saturn's day, they must show joy, eat, and wear white.)

And those born to sadness are always sad:

> בר אי לעאן באורייתא (ZC Yitro 292, line 1634)
> *Unless they learn Torah* (and cleave to the Holy One).

> באתר דעציבו דשבתאי, לאחזאה חדוה (ZC Yitro 143, line 1485)
> *In the place of Saturn's sadness, to show joy* (a lamp where it is dark, delight where there was fasting).

This gives a real teaching to someone who feels they were "born sad": the Zohar Chadash says there is such a thing, and that cleaving and learning undo it. It ties to S76 Shabbos.

## Meditation and practice

**Thought, speech and deed each given to Him (ties to 3 one line, 18 lips, 20 own words).**

> ליחדא שמיה עד אין סוף ועד אין תכלית (ZC Yitro 172, line 1514)
> *To unify His Name to no end and no limit.* (This is what thought is for.)

> צייר פומא, למללא באורייתא (ZC Yitro 174, line 1516)
> *He formed the mouth to speak Torah* (the eyes to look at its light, the ears to hear).

We propose (ours): **Z-T14 Three garments given back.** Take a few minutes and give each power back to its purpose: the thought to unify His Name without end, the speech to words of Torah or prayer, the hands to a single deed of kindness. Tanya chapter 4 names the three garments. This passage from the Zohar Chadash says what each was made for, and that the Shechinah is the power inside each one (1508). It is a way to make "one" felt in the body.

> כי בכל יומא אפיק לן מחילא דמסאבו, ואעיל לן בחילא דדכיו (ZC Yitro 4, line 1346)
> *For every day He takes us out of the power of impurity and brings us into the power of purity.*

> והבן שואל מאביו (ZC Yitro 157, line 1499)
> *And the son asks of his father* (in the middle blessings of prayer).

**Returners hidden under His wings.** On "the hands of a man under their wings":

> ימינא דקודשא בריך הוא, פרישא לקבלא כל אינון דתייבין לגביה (ZC Yitro 392, line 1734)
> *The right hand of the Holy One is spread out to receive all those who return to Him.*

> דהא מאריהון דתיובתא צניעו אצטריך (ZC Yitro 392, line 1734)
> *For those who return need modesty* (so that no one else will know of them).

That second line is a gentle rule for the guide: a person's return is private.

## The big questions

> לאו אינון חיים, וכל שכן אי אינון יומין בלא תורה ומצוה (ZC Yitro 73, line 1415)
> *They are not life, and all the more so if they are days without Torah and mitzvah.* (Said of days lived in poverty, pain and pressure.)

**Am I ruled by my stars?** Teshuvah raises the soul above its star:

> ובגין דא אין מזל לישראל (ZC Yitro 76, line 1418)
> *And for this reason Israel has no mazal* (no star that rules them).

**Why does God seem to rest or not rest?** Not because He tires:

> דלא אשתכח צדיקא, דיהא לה נייחא ביה (ZC Yitro 98, line 1440)
> *Because no righteous one was found in whom she [the Shechinah] could rest.*

**Why do people do good things for a name?** The physiognomy points to the builder of synagogues:

> וכלא למעבד להון שם (ZC Yitro 48, line 1390)
> *And all of it to make a name for themselves* (and not for the Name of God).

> מי שאין תוכו כברו (ZC Yitro 14, line 1356)
> *One whose inside is not like his outside.*

**What about the unanswered questions?** In the upper academy:

> לית תמן פרכי וקושיין (ZC Yitro 258, line 1600)
> *There are no refutations or difficulties there.*

**How should I give?** David received as a poor man:

> הכי יהיב למסכני (ZC Yitro 307, line 1649)
> *So he gave to the poor.*

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

This node is where the Zohar Chadash most directly supports the Chabad reading of unity. Lines 1508-1528 say: no power in thought without the Shechinah; no cut between thought, speech and deed; He changes His works and does not change; whoever separates a sefirah separates Him; no place where He is not; if He withdrew they would not exist; wise but not with a known wisdom; one but not in number; He carries all and nothing carries Him; He grasps every thought and no thought grasps Him; the sefirot are for us. The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud and the Alter Rebbe's Torah Or say these things, mostly quoting Patach Eliyahu. Here the Zohar Chadash says them again in its own words.

Where Chassidus goes further: "if He withdrew they would have no existence" (1521) is the Zohar's form of the Baal Shem Tov's teaching that the creative word must stand in each thing at every moment. Chassidus turns it into a daily contemplation. The Zohar Chadash states it once.

What the guide should take (ours): the three garments given back (Z-T14); "carried" (Z-S15) and "seeing in a whisper" (Z-S14) as named states; line 1734 on the privacy of return; lines 1632-1634 and 1485 for someone who feels born to sadness. Leave out the physiognomy as a method of reading others, the omens (1547-1565), and the amulet at 1573. Keep only its moral: marks on a face change with teshuvah.
