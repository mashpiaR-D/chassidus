## The short answer

Volume 3 of the Zohar says two things about the One, and it keeps both.

**First, G-d is One and does not change.** He "surrounds all worlds and fills all worlds." He "grasps all and nothing grasps Him." Every name is only a title for His deeds toward us. The Idra gives the rule behind all of this: the Ancient One and the Small Face are "all one," and "it is from our side that they differ."

**Second, His Name is made whole or left broken by what happens below.** "On that day Hashem will be One. And if you say: now He is not One? No." Every Shema, every blessing, every Amen, every honest prayer from the poor ties the knot again. The Zohar says "as it were" every time. It never says that He Himself is less.

The Idra Zuta holds both in one picture. There are many lights, and they are real. But "when one comes close to know them, nothing is found but the Lamp alone."

For a person this comes down to a few acts: - give the soul over at night and again after prayer; - rise from below to above in praise, draw down from above to below, and then tie it all in one knot, "He"; - say the prayer aloud; - hear slowly; - let the heart, not a picture, do the seeing; - love, because "everything stands on love."

## The core unity teaching

**1. He surrounds and fills (Pinchas).** This is the Zohar's own wording for the pair that Chabad builds on:

> איהו סובב על כל עלמין, ולית סובב לון לכל סטרא, עילא ותתא, ולארבע סטרין, בר מניה. ולית מאן דנפיק מרשותיה לבר. איהו ממלא כל עלמין (Zohar, Pinchas 40:11; line 4065)
> He surrounds all worlds, and there is none that surrounds them on any side, above or below or on the four sides, except Him. And none goes out of His domain. He fills all worlds.

> איהו שליט על כלא, ולית מאן דשליט ביה. איהו תפיס בכלא, ולית מאן דתפיס ביה. ואיהו לא אתקרי ידו"ד, ובכל שמהן, אלא באתפשטות נהוריה עלייהו (Zohar, Pinchas 40:10; line 4064)
> He rules over all, and none rules over Him. He grasps everything, and nothing grasps Him. He is not called by the four-letter Name or by any name, except as His light spreads over them.

Behar says it too: "You are outside all and inside all" (line 1934). In Him "there is no change, partner, number, image or likeness" (ולית ביה שנוי ושותפו וחושבן ותמונה ודמיון, line 4182).

**2. All one, and different only from our side (Idra Rabba).**

> כללא דכל מלין, עתיקא דעתיקין, וזעיר אפין, כלא חד. כלא הוה. כלא הוי. כלא יהא. לא ישתני. ולא משתני (Zohar, Idra Rabba 41:1; line 2617)
> The sum of all these words: the Ancient of Ancients and the Small Face are all one. All was, all is, all will be. He does not change and is not changed.

> ומסטרא דילן הוו שניין דא מן דא (Zohar, Idra Rabba 41:3; line 2619)
> It is from our side that they differ one from the other.

Day and night are "only from our side" (line 2484). In the Ancient "there is no left, all is right" (line 2351).

**3. The Lamp and its lights (Idra Zuta).**

> ואינון נהורין דמתפשטין, כד יקרבון למנדע לון, לא שכיח אלא בוצינא בלחודוי (Zohar, Idra Zuta 2:2; line 5324)
> And those lights that spread out: when one comes close to know them, nothing is found but the lamp alone.

The Ancient is "separate and set apart from all, and not separate, for all cleaves to Him and He cleaves to all. He is all" (line 5322). Each light "holds to the light that is inner of inner": "He and His Name are one" (line 5390). Rabbi Shimon's last summary of both Idras is: "all is one, all is one word. No separation hangs on it" (line 5368).

**4. The names are only from His deeds (Pinchas, Raya Mehemna).** "All the names are His titles, named after His deeds" (line 4698). He is "before every being, within every being, and after every being" (line 4705). The soul-in-the-body parable holds "only in her rule over the limbs," because the soul changes and He does not (line 4708).

> הוא כלא. הוא חד בלא פרודא. דאי תימא כל הני סגיאין אינון, חזר ואמר הוא, כלהו סלקין ומתקשרן ומתאחדן בחד (Zohar, Ha'Azinu 8:1; line 5262)
> He is all. He is one without separation. If you say, "These are many," the verse comes back and says "He": they all rise, are tied, and are made one in One.

**6. And yet: not One now.** This is the other side, and the Zohar says it just as plainly:

> ביום ההוא יהיה יי' אחד וגו'. ואי תימא השתא לאו הוא אחד. לא, דהא השתא חייבי עלמא גרמו, דלא אשתכח חד (Zohar, Achrei Mot 63:1; line 1384)
> "On that day the Lord will be One." And if you say: now He is not One? No: for now the wicked of the world have caused it, that He is not found one.

The ten crowns are "He is them and they are He, like a flame held in a coal, and there is no separation" (line 1259). But the joining of the King and the Queen is broken by exile and sin, and it is mended from below: "as the Name is made one below, so His Name is made one above" (line 4801). The Shema is one unity and "Blessed be the Name" another. When each is whole they become one, "Hashem, He is G-d," and "this is all a person needs to know in this world" (4818-4823).

**7. Love holds it together.** "This chamber is called Love, and because of love everything stands." The letters of the Name hold to one another "like groom and bride," and "whoever loves the King is bound in that love" (4886-4887). "There is no service like love of the Holy One" (line 4878).

**8. Above all: Ein Sof, Ayin, "He."** The will of the offering rises "to Ein Sof... where the will of all wills is," and in Ein Sof there are "no wills, no lights, no lamps" (lines 461-462). The Ancient "is called Ayin (Nothing), for Nothing hangs on Him" (line 5329). Because He has no beginning or end, He is not called "You" but "He." Only from Wisdom on is He called "You" and "Father" (line 5361). He is reached "not by number, not by count, but by the will of the heart" (line 5356).

## The states added

Twenty-two states are proposed across the thirteen reports. They are listed with ids in the JSON below. Grouped by kind:

- **Far, and still His.** - the seeker in the dry land (365); - grief for the Presence (377); - accompanied in exile, the Queen who will not leave her son (5270); - found when small (3446-3450); - small enough to be held (4555).
- **Seen with love.** - seen from behind the wall (2022-2024); - under the open eye, the eye that is always open, always smiling and washes all colors white (2377, 5428-5431).
- **Gathered.** - made one (1444-1449); - the settled spirit (2328); - the fine silence (524, 534); - the settled return, Moshe coming back to the camp "settled as before, and more" (4906); - the single knot (5322, 5401).
- **Burning and giving.** - the lit flame (3954); - the son who counts himself as nothing, "the Merciful One wants the heart" (5145); - the hind at dawn, fed by giving (4531-4537); - carrying the world (5396-5398).
- **Hard places held rightly.** - the dry middle (2893-2900); - counting judgment as kindness (2311-2312); - the poor man's complaint (3520-3522); - joy with the parents present (3957-3964); - accepting for oneself and weeping for others (Addenda 112-116); - the speaking soul (Addenda 28-32).

## The meditations and practices added

Eighteen techniques are proposed. They are listed with ids in the JSON below. Four of them together make a full day:

1. **Morning.** Walk through the day with the morning witnesses (3170-3172). Offer yourself (131). Stand as the priest entered, letting the heart settle before words (1209-1211). Take the four postures of prayer (3525-3535). Gather the limbs into One at the Shema (4801-4803).
2. **After the Amidah.** Give the soul over, "not as a deposit, as at night, but as one who has departed the world" (2117-2119). The Addenda add: "the Holy One counts it as if He took his soul... and that hour stands as atonement" (Addenda 195).
3. **In praise.** Go up, go down, then tie the knot (5215-5217).
4. **At night.** Hand the soul over (593, 2086, 4743). Rise at midnight (369-372; Addenda 78).

Around these sit smaller practices: - naming the purpose aloud (941); - the Kedushah inclusion (1644-1651); - receiving a holy day as a guest (1665-1669); - the Shabbos Mincha hour of will (2355, 5336, 5422); - turning to the Patient One (2390); - praying without watching the result (2965); - confessing into His hands (4194-4196); - whispering her out (4619-4622); - the bent ear, hearing slowly (5443-5447).

## Agreement with Chabad

- **Sovev and memale.** The Zohar's own words, "surrounds all worlds, fills all worlds" (line 4065), "outside all and inside all" (line 1934), and "separate and not separate... He is all" (line 5322), are the texts Tanya (chs. 41, 48) and Shaar Hayichud lean on.
- **"From our side."** The Idra's "they differ only from our side" (line 2619) is the frame of Shaar Hayichud: the many are real to us, and nothing is added from His side.
- **Light and source.** The Lamp and its lights (5324, 5390-5391), and the flame held in the coal (1259), are the Zohar forms of Chabad's "He and His life-force are one."
- **The soul parable, limited.** Pinchas limits the soul-body parable to "rule" and not to change (line 4708). That is exactly the Tanya's caution.
- **Ein Sof above the lights.** "No wills, no lights" (line 462) and "called Ayin" (line 5329) fit Chabad's Essence above all revelation.
- **Two unities.** "Shema" and "Blessed be the Name" as two unities that join (4818-4823) is the upper and lower unity of Shaar Hayichud and Imrei Binah.
- **Heart understanding.** "The eye of the mind of the heart" (line 5128) and "not by number but by the will of the heart" (line 5356) match Chabad's demand that understanding reach the heart, and its ban on picturing G-d.
- **Speech in prayer.** "If he does not bring the words out on his lips, his prayer is not a prayer" (line 5446). The Tanya also makes speech, not thought alone, the body of the mitzvah.

## Disagreement and difference with Chabad (kept, not smoothed)

- **Where the One is seen from.** The Zohar in Vayikra and Achrei Mot speaks from the relation. The One is a union that deeds complete or break, and "now He is not One" (line 1384). The Tanya's Shaar Hayichud speaks from His side, where nothing ever changes (ch. 7). Chabad holds both, calling one the higher unity and the other the unity drawn below. But the weight falls differently: the Zohar gives the broken Name far more space. *Ours:* the guide should teach both, and never let "your deed completes the Name" become "G-d is lacking."
- **Joy in exile.** Bamidbar says: "There is no joy except when Israel dwell in the holy land." In exile, "rejoice with trembling" (2067-2070). And "since the day Israel were exiled... there is no joy before the Holy One" (line 4878). Chassidus makes joy a duty at all times, exile included. This is a real difference in tone and instruction.
- **Who may hear.** The Idras restrict the deepest teaching to "those who entered and came out" (lines 2616, 5367). They warn that one who reveals secrets has a soul "not from the body of the King" (Idra Zuta line 5452). Chabad set out to spread these teachings in plain language to all. The Idras themselves also reveal, and Rabbi Shimon teaches so as not to "enter in shame" (line 5317). But the default is different.
- **Thought and deed.** The Addenda say "a mitzvah without thought has no rising" (Addenda 166). The Tanya grants this (intention is the "wings," chs. 38-40), but insists the plain deed is the main thing even without intention. The Zohar here leans the other way.

## What the guide should take (ours)

1. **Two sentences for the unity layer.** "He is all, one without separation" (line 5262). "When one comes close, nothing is found but the Lamp" (line 5324). Set them beside "surrounds all worlds, fills all worlds" (line 4065) and "they differ only from our side" (line 2619).
2. **The knot as a method.** Rise, draw down, tie the knot (5215-5217). This is the clearest teachable order for praise in the volume.
3. **The daily giving of the soul.** At night as a deposit, after prayer as one who has departed, in love (593, 2117-2121, 4743, 4749; Addenda 192-196). Give it with its guard: not "with a distant heart" (line 2119).
4. **Offer the hard-place states with care.** - The poor man's complaint makes honest, even angry, prayer a form of closeness (line 3521). - Accompanied in exile tells someone who feels sent away that the Presence went with them (line 5270). - Found when small and small enough to be held are for the shrunken self (lines 3446, 4555). - The dry middle is for the person whose practice has gone dead (2893-2900).
5. **Warmth in being seen.** Replace the fear of being watched with the open, smiling eye (line 2377) and with the One behind the wall who watches with longing (line 2024).
6. **Plain rules for prayer.** Say it aloud (line 5446). Hear slowly (line 5443). Heart up, eyes down (line 4161). Do not watch yourself pray (line 2965). Name only the sins you remember (line 3526).
7. **Love as the ground.** "Everything stands on love" (line 4886). "No service like love" (line 4878). "The Merciful One wants the heart" (line 5145).
8. **Keep the tensions visible.** Joy in exile and the Idras' secrecy should be named as differences, not hidden.
