## Summary

Group-06 has thirty-six works. Twenty-seven are from the Chabad Rebbeim or the Chabad library; nine are from other schools (Breslov, three by R. Tzadok of Lublin, Polonne, Ujhel, the Besht anthology, Ruzhin, Ger). The Chabad maamar collections carry the one idea as their stated center, each from its own side. The Rebbe's Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat has the most 'Essence' and 'dwelling below' in the group. It grounds 'ein od' in the Essence, which alone 'can' both shine and hide (371), and it states in a maamar that Chassidus is the yechidah of Torah, reaching the creature itself (957). The strongest new warrant for the spine is in the Mitteler Rebbe's Igros Kodesh, letter 36. There he says that everything the Alter Rebbe taught was 'to establish the simple unity of God, which is the Essence of Ein Sof, in the mind and heart of each person', on the two unities (838). That is a statement of the one-point thesis for a whole teaching, a century and a half before Inyanah. The Mitteler Rebbe's Pirush HaMilos gives the spine's precision in his own words: 'You alone' is 'yachid above echad' (347). His Maamarim put sovev 'in every single detail' (884), and his Yiddish kuntres on prayer says 'in truth there is no separate thing' (2441). The Tzemach Tzedek appears as Kabbalist (Yahel Or, Biurei HaZohar) and as philosopher (Sefer HaChakirah). He keeps F05's tension (radiance or Essence) inside one Psalm note (438) and in a letter (306). The Rayatz's Kuntres Maamarim 5682 puts creation in a radiance but resurrection and the turning of evil in the Essence, a third position that is kept as it stands. The Rashab's Kuntres HaTefillah has the F09 anchor ('the Essence bears all opposites, all truly yachid', 49) and the warning the spine takes as its own limit (74). The Alter Rebbe's letters name the controversy with the Vilna Gaon as literal 'memale kol almin' (2733). Outside Chabad the idea is stated plainly by R. Tzadok (the bet of creation 'shows two'; 'no breaking, no sin, from the depth of His will', an antinomian edge that is kept), by the Ujhel Rav (who cites Tanya 34 by name), in Likutei Tefilot (held by faith, 'You are hidden within all the hidings'), in the Besht anthology and Toldot (the Besht's 'no hiding once you know', whose own center is the tzaddik), in Sichat Shedim (continuous creation as 'the cornerstone of faith'), in the Ruzhin sayings and in Chiddushei HaRim. Thin, and said plainly: Piskei HaSiddur, Toras Shalom (the Rashab's halachic responsa), MiShulchan HaAri, Tikkunei Mikvaos, Seder Tenaim, She'eris Yehudah and Meshiv Tzedek (halacha); Toldos Chabad beRussia HaTzaris (history); Ma'aneh Lashon (grave-side liturgy); Otzar Minhagei Chabad (customs, with a few reasons from the Essence); and the Tzemach Tzedek's, Mitteler Rebbe's and Rayatz's letters outside a handful of teaching letters. Boneh Yerushalayim is unreadable Rashi-script OCR, and its counts are noise.

## Faces

- **F05 The Essence beyond both** (primary): עצמות 502 lines (10.9/10k), the highest count of any work in the group; with עצמותו 225 and אוא"ס 405. The Essence is the working term throughout.
- **F07 The home below** (primary): דירה בתחתונים 112 lines (2.4/10k) and נתאוה 39: the most of any work in the group; 'dirah la'atzmuso' is the recurring end-point (373, 3056).
- **F11 Darkness turned to light** (secondary): אתהפכא 93, אתכפיא 82: transformation of darkness is the second axis, with the last generation as the site.
- **F02 The higher and the lower unity** (secondary): Havayah and Elokim 'kulla chad' as the route to 'ein od milvado' (369-371); אין עוד מלבדו 17.
- **F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity** (secondary): ביטול 388 lines, ביטול היש 24.

## Signature (stated)

The Rebbe's maamarim take the one idea from the Essence's side and bring it to the lowest point. His recurring move is to ask why two things that seem opposite are one (Havayah and Elokim, revelation and concealment, the soul's bond and the body's refinement) and to answer that their common root is the Essence, which alone 'can' do both. Knowing that Havayah is Elokim leads to knowing 'there is none else', because the ability to shine or not shine is nothing apart from the Essence (371). The purpose is stated as a dwelling for the Essence below. What happens in the body, not only in the soul, is the point: 'that the body see Godliness' (3075). He also states in a maamar that Chassidus is the yechidah of Torah, enlivening its four levels down to the point where the creature itself feels 'ein od' (957). This matches Inyanah and is the spine's own thesis in maamar form. Calling the whole collection one expression of the idea is our reading, but each maamar states its part of it.

**Structure.** Four parts, in calendar order: I (Tishrei-Cheshvan), II (Kislev-Shevat, including Yud-Tes Kislev and Yud Shevat maamarim), III (Adar-Sivan), IV (Tammuz-Elul). Each maamar opens with the Rayatz's or Rashab's text for the day and ends in avodah; the Yud Shevat 'Basi LeGani' series (from 1286) states the dwelling-below purpose most directly.

- F05's kept tension appears inside the Rebbe's reading: creation yesh me'ayin is 'in the power of the Essence alone' (2993, 3075, citing the Rashab), against the Maharash's 'mere radiance'. The Rebbe does not smooth the Maharash's wording away; he builds on the Rashab's.

## Signature (stated)

Yahel Or reads the Psalms as a map of how the One becomes a world and how the world returns to Him. Its angle is structural: every verse is placed in the sefiros (Malchus, Z"A, Abba and Imma), and the question it keeps asking is how a separate yesh can come from One before whom all is nothing. The answer is given in kabbalistic terms, as Malchus that cannot receive directly from Ein Sof and must be mediated so that there be 'no king without a people' (1870). It also keeps both readings of 'with You is the source of life': a 'mere radiance' from which yesh is made, and 'with You literally', above revelation (438). That is the F05 tension held open by the Tzemach Tzedek himself. The purpose is stated through the Alter Rebbe: the world was made far from its source so that it would be 'yesh and batel' (583). This work adds F02 and F05 in the language of the Zohar and Etz Chaim more than in the language of avodah.

**Structure.** Psalm by psalm (139 sections), each divided into numbered notes, many with a short summary (קיצור); additions at the end. Many notes are cross-references to Torah Ohr and Likkutei Torah, so the book works as an index of the Alter Rebbe's teaching arranged by psalm.

- F05: 'yesh me'ayin... from a mere radiance called ayin' and 'with You literally, above revelation' are both given as readings of one verse (438); the Tzemach Tzedek does not choose between them.
