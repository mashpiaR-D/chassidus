Gur Aryeh is the Maharal's commentary on Rashi. Most of it is close work on words: why Rashi said this, why the verse wrote that. Many lines are grammar or law. But every few pages the Maharal stops and opens a large idea in a few lines. This report gathers those lines from the first half of Bereishit, from creation to Avraham's servant going to find a wife for Yitzchak.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The words about G-d name His acts, not changes in Him.** "כלל הדבר כי הלשון אינו מונח רק על הפעולות" (206): "the rule: the words are set only on the actions." When the Torah says He was sad, regretted, wept, went or came, "אבל כל אלו התארים הם על הפעולה הבאה מאתו מבלי שיהיה הוא יתברך מקבל שום התפעלות" (206): "all these descriptions are of the act that comes from Him, without His receiving any change."
- **His glory fills all; a revelation is a screen taken away.** "אבל העיקר כי יש מסך בינו ובין ברואיו" (206): "the main thing: there is a screen between Him and His creatures." Sinai was "הסרת המסך המבדיל" (206): "the removal of the dividing screen."
- **The first day had no twoness.** The Torah says "one day," not "first day," because "שלא היה ביום הזה שום שניות לגמרי, רק אחדות גמור" (22): "on that day there was no twoness at all, only full oneness."
- **Man ties the worlds and makes them one.** "והנה הקושר העולמות שעל ידו יתאחדו העליונים והתחתונים הוא האדם" (62): "the one who ties the worlds, through whom upper and lower become one, is man."
- **Oneness fits only Him.** Man was not left alone, "שאין ראוי האחדות אלא ליחיד" (106): "oneness fits only the Single One," "שהוא האחד האמיתי" (106): "who is the true One."
- **Made from the middle, so return is easy.** "כי החטא הוא בעבור שהוא נוטה אל אחד הקצוות, ואחר שהאדם נברא מן המצוע נקל הוא לשוב אל האמצע" (94): "sin is leaning to one of the edges; since man was made from the middle, it is easy to return to the middle."

## 2.1 One day, and no twoness

Why does the Torah say "one day" and not "first day"? Rashi gives one reason. The Maharal adds a deeper one: "שלא היה ביום הזה שום שניות לגמרי, רק אחדות גמור" (22): "on that day there was no twoness at all, only full oneness." The first day carries the mark of the One who made it. Twoness came later, with the second day.

## 2.2 The Name of essence

"ועוד ששם בן ד' הוא שם העצם" (16): "the four-letter Name is the Name of the essence." And what is that essence? "ואמתתו היא הטוב הגמור המשפיע לכל הנמצאים" (16): "His truth is the full good that gives to all that exist." So the world was made with judgment, yet "קיום שלו אינו רק ברחמים" (15): "its lasting is only by mercy."

## 2.3 The words name His acts

Line 206 is the longest teaching on G-d in this range. The verse says "He was saddened in His heart." How can the One who lacks nothing be sad? The Maharal's answer: human words were never set on the inner change of the speaker. They were set on the act. "כלל הדבר כי הלשון אינו מונח רק על הפעולות" (206). When a man is sad, the word "sad" names what flows out of him, whether or not he is moved. So with G-d: "אבל כל אלו התארים הם על הפעולה הבאה מאתו מבלי שיהיה הוא יתברך מקבל שום התפעלות" (206). This is why the sages could say "G-d weeps." "Sleep" means that help does not flow. "Went" and "came" mean that His glory was revealed in a place.

He is sharp against those who explained all of this away: "אלו החכמים אשר מקרוב באו" (206): "these sages who came lately," who climbed a ladder to heaven with their heads on the ground. He says: "שאין בכל אלו גשמות כלל" (206): "there is no bodiliness at all in any of these." So there is no need to run from the words.

## 2.4 The screen

Someone asks: His glory fills the world, so how can it "come" to Sinai or "leave" the Temple? The Maharal answers that to say the Temple was the same before and after would be to deny the Torah: "כל האומר זה סותר פינת התורה" (206). The answer: "אבל העיקר כי יש מסך בינו ובין ברואיו" (206): "there is a screen between Him and His creatures." "והגלות הכבוד על הר סיני או בזולתו מן המקומות" (206): "the revealing of the Glory at Sinai or in other places" "הוא הסרת המסך המבדיל" (206): "is the removal of the dividing screen." Nothing moves in Him. "הכל נאמר שהשם יתברך פעל הגלוי שנגלה לשם" (206): "all is said because G-d made the revealing that was revealed there." And sadness belongs only to our side: "רק מצד עולם הזה יש התעצבות" (206).

## 2.5 Oneness fits only Him

Why "it is not good for man to be alone"? Because a single man, alone below, would look like a god below. "שאין ראוי האחדות אלא ליחיד" (106): "oneness fits only the Single One," "שהוא האחד האמיתי" (106). "כי הוא יתברך הוא הטוב האמתי" (106): "He is the true good." This is why "one day" was His mark (22), and why man is made to need another.

## 2.6 Man ties the worlds

"והנה הקושר העולמות שעל ידו יתאחדו העליונים והתחתונים הוא האדם" (62). Man is made from above and below, "והוא עושה שלום וקשור בין עליונים והתחתונים, ומאחד הכל" (62): "he makes peace and a bond between upper and lower, and makes all one." The Maharal cites the Rambam's Guide here (1:72), the world as one person. And man is "all," not a part: "כי מפני שהאדם נברא מכל האדמה, אם כן הוא הכל" (92).

## 2.7 He includes all, and He makes peace between opposites

Avraham says "my lords" in the plural. Why? "ודרך כבוד אומרים לו לשון רבים שהוא כולל הכל" (525): "out of honor we speak to Him in the plural, since He includes all." He is "שהשם יתברך שעושה שלום בין ההפכים" (107): "G-d, who makes peace between opposites." Opposites wholly apart can live in peace, like fire and water in the angels. Opposites that share one thing fight: "ואם ההפכים מחוברים משתתף להם דבר" (166). The middle holds both, "כי האמצעי משותף לשני הקצות שהם הפכים" (166), and "והכל הולך לדבר אחד ולמקום אחד הוא עולה הכל, והכל אמת" (166): "all goes to one thing and to one place all rises, and all is true."

## 2.8 What is His alone

"כי נקרא 'קנין' הדבר שהוא לגמרי שלו" (434): "a thing wholly his is called his acquisition." Torah, heaven and earth, Avraham and Israel are called His, "אמנם אלו ארבע דברים הם אחד ואין שתוף להם עם זולתם" (434): "these four things are one and share nothing with others." What is one belongs to the One.

## 2.9 The root of idolatry

Idolatry did not start from denying G-d. It started from thinking Him too high: "אי אפשר לעבוד לו, ובחרו בכוחות שהם אינם קדושים" (527): "it is impossible to serve Him, and they chose powers that are not holy," since those seemed close to low man. This is a warning for anyone who feels G-d is too far to talk to.

## 2.10 Nothing hidden from Him

"אין דבר נעלם, ויכול לעשות הכל" (549): "nothing is hidden, and He can do all." "ואין דבר רע יורד מלמעלה" (609): "no bad thing comes down from above." "שאין השם יתברך מביא על האדם דבר שהוא רע לגמרי" (143). "ועיקר הוא הטוב" (208): "the good is the main thing."

- **MH-S45 "Cleaving that does not move" (213).** Noach walked "with G-d." The Maharal says: "שמי שדבק באחד אי אפשר לו לזוז ממנו ולחטוא" (213): "one who cleaves to the One cannot move from Him and sin." Inside: a steady feeling that I am held in place. I am not fighting to stay good. The tie itself keeps me. How to enter: before a hard moment, think: I am joined to the One, and what is joined to the One does not slide. Then act from that. It differs from S27 Knowing that holds, which is a truth held in the mind. Here it is the bond that holds, not the thought. It differs from MH-S43 Heart in my power, which rules the heart from strength. Here there is no struggle at all. (Avraham, the Maharal adds, stood by his own strength without that help, 213. Both are good.)

## 4.1 Three ways to plead (`mh_three_approaches`, new)

When Avraham pleaded for Sodom, he had three aims in one plea: "שג' כוונות היו לו בדברים אלו" (566). "כי נגד שם 'אלקים' שהוא מדת הדין דבר קשות" (566): to the Name of judgment, a hard argument. "ונגד שם 'אל' הוא מדת החסד" (566): to the Name of kindness, soft words. And "המיוחד אליו התפלה והרחמים" (566): to the four-letter Name, prayer and mercy. These match "the Great, the Mighty and the Awesome." Practice: when you pray for something hard, say it three ways. First, the honest case: why it is fair. Second, the gentle ask: please, as a kindness. Third, plain prayer: I ask Your mercy. All three go to the One.

## 4.2 Back to the middle (`mh_return_to_middle`, new)

"ואחר שהאדם נברא מן המצוע נקל הוא לשוב אל האמצע" (94). Sin is not your core. It is a lean to one side. Practice: after a fall, do not ask "what is wrong with me?" Ask "which way did I lean?" Then take one step back toward the middle. The middle is where you were made. It is easy to go home.

## 4.3 Never call it too great (`mh_not_too_great`, new)

"שאין ראוי לחוטא לומר אל השם שעשה חטא גדול שאי אפשר למחול לו" (169): "a sinner should not say to G-d that he did a sin too great to forgive." That is to argue against oneself before Him. His right hand is "פתוחה לקבל שבים" (82): "open to receive returners." And He opens a door on purpose: He spoke gently to Adam, "שלא יהיה נבהל להשיב ואולי יאמר 'חטאתי' וישוב בתשובה" (132). Practice: when the thought "this is too much to be forgiven" comes, stop it. Say instead: I sinned. Your hand is open. Then say what you will do.

## 4.4 Birth yourself by good deeds (`mh_deeds_as_offspring`, new)

"These are the offspring of Noach: Noach." Rashi says a righteous man's main offspring are his good deeds. The Maharal: "ואין לך תולדות יותר מזה, שהרי הוא כאילו מוליד עצמו" (210): "there are no offspring greater than this, for it is as if he gives birth to himself." And "שהמעשים הטובים אשר יעשה האדם הוא חיות וקיום הנפש" (663): "good deeds are the life and lasting of the soul." Practice: at night, name one good deed of the day. Say: this is who I am becoming.

## 4.5 Your own prayer frees you (`mh_pray_yourself_free`, new)

The rule is that a prisoner cannot free himself. But the Maharal says: "דתפילה לא שייך ביה 'אין חבוש מתיר עצמו מבית האסורים'" (654): "in prayer, 'a prisoner cannot free himself' does not apply," because in prayer it is G-d who frees him. So a sick or stuck person's own prayer is the best (654). Practice: when you feel trapped, do not wait for someone else to pray for you. Pray yourself, in your own words. You are not freeing yourself. He is.

## 4.6 Take each word with love (`mh_word_with_love`, new)

He did not tell Avraham the place at once. Why? So the word would be dear, and "ומפני כך כל דבור ודבור שהוא מקבל מן השם יתברך מקבל אותו מאהבה" (356). Practice: before you learn or pray, wait a moment and want the word. Then take it as a gift. Pair it with 668: "מפני שהצדיקים חפצם לעשות רצון קונם": "the righteous want to do their Maker's will."

## 4.7 Give, and it is good (`mh_whole_then_give`, extended)

"שלא יקרא 'טובה' רק מה שנותן לאחר" (85): "only what is given to another is called good." And the gift that turns judgment: "חוץ מן מתנות עניים שהוא מהפך מדת הדין" (551). Add to the practice: when things feel heavy, give something to someone who needs it.

## 4.8 Keep the mind, never drink (`mh_silence_for_mind`, extended as a guard)

"ודע כי השכל הוא הדבוק בה' יתברך, ועל ידי השכרות יאבד הדבוק ההיא" (305): "know that the mind is the cleaving to G-d, and by drunkenness that cleaving is lost." "כי כל זמן אשר האדם שכלו עליו הוא נטע נאמן" (305): "as long as a person's mind is on him, he is a faithful planting." This backs the guide's rule: never drink. The cleaving lives in a clear mind.

## 4.9 Faith as the ground (`mh_silent_trust`, extended)

"כי יסוד הכל הוא האמונה" (450): "faith is the base of all." Avraham's faith was "וזה היה האמנה בה' ובוטח לגמרי והולך בתומו ובאמונתו" (450). Add: trust fully, and walk simply. Do not keep checking.

## 5. The big human questions

**Does G-d feel? Is He sad, angry, glad?** The words name His acts, not changes in Him (206). He is not moved. What flows from Him to us is real, and the words tell us what it is.

**If He is everywhere, why is He hidden?** Because of a screen between Him and us. A revelation is the screen taken away (206). He was there all along.

**Why was I made to need other people?** Because oneness fits only Him (106). A person alone would seem to be a god below.

**What is my job in the world?** To tie upper and lower and make them one (62). I am made from all, and I am "all" (92).

**Can I come back after a bad fall?** Yes, and it is easy. You were made from the middle, and sin is a lean to one edge (94). Do not call your sin too great (169). His hand is open (82).

**Why does bad happen?** Nothing wholly bad comes from Him (143). No bad thing comes down from above (609). The good is the main thing, and the bad is mixed in (208).

**Is G-d too high to talk to?** That thought was the root of idolatry (527). Speak to Him directly.

**What will last of me?** The deeds I do are my offspring (210) and the life of my soul (663). The righteous are called living even in death, "כי החיות הוא דבר שאין לו הפסק כלל" (347): "life is a thing that has no break at all."

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Tzimtzum and the screen.** The Maharal: His glory fills the world, and there is a screen between Him and creatures; revelation is the screen's removal (206). This is very close to Tanya ch. 48-49 and Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 7: the tzimtzum hides Him from the creature, not from Himself, and He is found in all places equally. Both say the change is on our side. One difference: the Maharal insists that places really differ in holiness (Sinai, the Temple), and that to deny this "denies a corner-stone of the Torah" (206). Chabad agrees that revelation differs by place, while the essence is equal everywhere. So the two meet.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The Rambam (Guide 1:52-58) allows attributes of action and reads all else as negation. The Maharal also says the words name the act (206), so here he is close to the Rambam. His quarrel is with those who "came lately" and felt they must translate away every such word (206). He says there is no bodiliness in them to begin with, so the plain words may stand. Gate of Unity ch. 8-9 also says His will and acts are not a change in Him. Ours: the Maharal keeps the Rambam's rule and drops the Rambam's fear of the words.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "He includes all" (525) is the first half. "Oneness fits only Him" (106), and man must not be alone lest he seem a god below, is the second half.
- **Man joins the worlds** (62) is close to Tanya ch. 36-37: the purpose is a dwelling for Him below, and man's service joins the lower world to Him.
- **Return from the middle** (94) is close to Tanya ch. 29-31 (do not sink into sadness over sin) and the Chabad teaching that a Jew's core is never cut off.
- **Cleaving that does not move** (213) is close to Tanya ch. 18-19: the hidden love that holds a Jew to the One. Chabad says every Jew has it. The Maharal names it in Noach and says it keeps one from sin.
