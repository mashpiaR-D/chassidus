## The shape of the range

This range is not like the Gate of Unity ranges, where the rungs of the worlds are mostly *structural* and the tie comes up every twenty or thirty lines. Here the long paragraphs are arguments about practice: Torah study against the deed, tzitzit, joy and fear, teshuvah. Almost every column reaches its tie before it ends. So *stated* dominates the line entries (68 of 84). The honest count is really the sub-entries: about two in three of them are stated. The rest are the analogies the Mitteler Rebbe builds first: growth of a tree and a child, a king's decree, a thought clothing an idea, a cup with a hole.

Two faces lead, and they lead together: the Essence (F05) and Torah and mitzvah (F10). The whole range is one question asked from several sides: *where does the Essence itself rest: in the wisdom of Torah, in the simple will of the mitzvah, or in the physical deed?* The answer moves. Now it rests in wisdom. In the future it rests in the deed. And always it rests where there is bitul and 'nothing'. F11 is the third great face. The Gate of Tzitzit is largely a treatise on two ways of dealing with evil: removing it (techelet, black fire) and turning it (white threads). Then comes a third that turns out higher than both, teshuvah, which makes the darkness itself shine. F09 runs through all of it, because the book keeps saying 'both are true'. F02 (the higher and lower unity of Shema) is nearly absent, even though the range begins inside the Gate of Keriat Shema. It appears only twice, both times as "before Him darkness is as light".

## Keriat Shema, simanim 95-96 (438-442): the future deed, and where Torah and mitzvah are rooted

The second paragraph of Shema is read as prophecy. Israel will stray, be exiled, return in teshuvah, and in Moshiach's days the deed will be greater than study. The reason given is F05 in its plainest form: the physical deed of the mitzvah is rooted in the Essence of the will. > שע"י גוף מעשה המצו' למטה ששרשם בעצמו' הרצון (line 438)

The same line gives the reason for exile. The kelipot draw from the inner side of holiness only through sin. Exile pays them their share below, so their root above is cut off. That is marked *structural* F11: the darkness is used to remove the darkness, not yet turned. Line 439 then states F07 outright, and in its strongest form: not "below as above" but below *more* than above. > מפני שיהי' גלוי אלקו' למטה יותר מלמעלה (line 439)

The line closes on F09: "both opinions are true", study greater now and the deed greater then. Siman 96 (440-442) maps the worlds onto the Name. Malchut of Atzilut is called "Asiyah of Atzilut" because it is the source of creation from nothing (line 440, *structural* F01). In Atzilut nothing is yet created; it is "still Godliness itself" (line 441). A king's word is the model for creative speech. Only a king's decree becomes fact: "let there be a firmament, and it was so" (line 442). The mitzvot are "the King's commands", and their inner root is His inner will and wisdom: > שבמעשה המצות הרי שרשם מפנימי' רצונו וחכמתו (line 442)

## Simanim 97-98 (443-449): Torah from the Essence's own wisdom

Torah is rooted in "the primordial wisdom in the Essence itself" (line 444). The key claim is G05 in the form of an argument about skill. Torah's root is above good and evil, *and that is why* it can go down and repair the lowest: > יותר יוכל להשפיל כח חכמתו לתקן היותר מקולקל (line 444)

Line 445 gives the halachic side of the unity. A ruling of 'kosher, permitted, pure' is the right hand that draws a thing near, so that Godliness can dwell in it. A ruling of 'forbidden' pushes the thing away: > שיוכל לשכון בדבר זה גלוי אלקות כשהוא כשר ומותר וטהור (line 445)

Every law holds both sides, לאכללא שמאלא בימינא (line 445, F09). Siman 98 (447-449) is the long growth analogy. A tree grows by water; a child's limbs grow by the water in the blood, and bodily growth stops when the mind stops growing. The analogy itself carries no face. Its application is *structural* F10: Torah's light is what builds and widens the vessels of the mitzvot into all their details (line 449).

## Simanim 99-100 (450-456): the mitzvah above Torah, and why study still wins

Now the direction reverses. The mitzvot have a root higher than Torah: the essential point of the supernal will, "far above wisdom" (line 450). A will with a hidden reason is contrasted with a will with no reason at all, "be silent, so it rose in thought" (lines 450-451). The will with no reason is counted with the Essence itself: > דכתר שבכתר נחשב מבחי' עצמות המאציל (line 452)

In the future the King's command will rise above Torah (line 452, F07). Siman 100 holds both sides: אלו ואלו דא"ח ושניהם אמת (line 453). Then comes the range's most distinctive argument (lines 454-455). The sages ruled that study is greater even at the root. They could, because study draws the Essence of Or Ein Sof into the simple will of every mitzvah. Wisdom can do what will cannot, because wisdom is 'koach mah', 'nothing', bitul: > שעצמות אא"ס הוא בחי' אין האמיתי ואינו שורה רק בבחי' אין (line 455)

Will is a narrow channel (tzinor) that cuts the light. Wisdom is a broad vessel where the whole Essence can shine. So learning the laws of sukkah draws the Essence into the mitzvah itself: > כשלומד הל' סוכה ואתרוג מביא וממשיך עצמות אא"ס ברצון העליון הפשוט (line 455)

This joins F05, F08 and F10 in one sentence. The Essence rests in study *because* study is bitul. Line 456 draws the conclusion: ולכך ת"ת כנגד כולן.

## Simanim 101-102 (457-464): sin, the Essence's equality, and the two Temples

Why was idolatry forgiven in the First Temple, but neglect of Torah not forgiven in the Second? First the text asks what a sin's blemish is. The light is never lessened. The vessel is holed, like a cup that leaks. The 248 mitzvot are vessels "for the light of the Name Havayah in each mitzvah" (line 457). The yud of the Name is blemished in the soul by a mind "hard as stone", which comes from feeling oneself too much (line 458, F08 from its negative side). The hard question follows: how can inner Godly light reach the kelipot at all? Everything, the border between holy and unholy included, is measured by the kav (line 458). Before the tzimtzum there is no border: > קמי' ממש כחשיכה כאורה שניהן שוין ממש (line 459)

Here is the range's darkest use of the unity. Sin does not send light out through the kav. It sends light out because the light withdraws toward the Essence, and there everyone can receive, since nothing takes up 'place' before Him: > רק מצד הסתלקות האור לגבי העצמות דשם הכל יכולים לקבל (line 459)

So the Essence's equality toward light and dark (F02, F05, F09) is also the reason the exile is long. The negaim make the same point (line 461). Their sign is the lack of bitul, וסימנו הוא שאין בו בחי' בטול ממקומו. In the First Temple Torah restored the light of wisdom at once, because "the main revelation of Godliness is in wisdom". The Torah learners drew "a new light from the very Essence" (line 462). Without Torah there was no remedy. Then the argument turns to the future. Food keeps a man alive because its root in tohu is higher than his root (line 462). Once all the sparks are refined, the deed will be greater (line 463). And then: > ויהי' נגלה למטה כמו למעלה בשוה (line 464)

Line 464 also makes the strongest F05-F10 claim in the range. Wisdom is only a vessel for the true 'nothing'. The mitzvot are His essential wills, and once revealed they are the Essence itself: > הרי הן בחי' העצמות ממש ולא בחי' כלי בית קיבול לבד (line 464)

## Tzitzit 1-2 (467-473): the image of God, the garment of light, and Israel as the joiner

"Let us make man in our image" raises the question of how the finite can be the image of the infinite (line 467). The tzimtzum and the curtain are the known answers, and they are not enough. The true answer is that the image is the soul's light when its mind and middot are shaped ע"י תו"מ דוקא (line 467). "He wraps Himself in light as a garment" is then explained through the human analogy. An idea is grasped only once it is clothed in the letters of thought, and that garment has an advantage over the bare idea (lines 468-469). Tzimtzum as mere absence would not be enough: > אך הוצרך להלביש את אור העצמות בלבוש (line 469)

That light is the Torah: אור זה אור דתורה הוא שנתלבש בה אור א"ס בעצמו (line 469). Inner Torah is unbounded. Revealed Torah is measured, because there Or Ein Sof shines "in an outer, limiting garment" (lines 469-470, F03 + F10). Only Israel can join the two, because Israel rose in His thought in His inner Essence: > לפי שישראל עלו במח' ג"כ בבחי' הפנימית והעצמות דאא"ס (line 471)

The four faces of the chariot are the four ways the 'something' rises to the 'nothing'. The face of Man includes them all "through the bitul of wisdom" (line 471). A person who binds his will, mind, speech and deed to Him resembles the Essence clothed in His will (line 471). Tzedakah imitates the upper act of creating from nothing (line 471, F01). From above, great mercy reaches "the inner Essence more than love and fear" and stretches down to the lowest (line 472). The tallit is the makif. These flows reach Israel only as makif, "because they come from the power of Ein Sof itself" (line 473, F04 + F05).

## Tzitzit 3-5 (474-484): removing evil and turning it; who makes Him 'I' and 'Holy'

Techelet is Malchut, "the fire that eats and consumes". It is the yoke of heaven accepted in awe: > שאז מתבער הרע ואתכפי' סט"א (line 475)

The soul burns "to rise and cleave to the One God" (line 475, F06 + F08). White threads come from where there is no evil at all (line 475). They do not remove evil. They turn it: > אתהפכא חשוכא לנהורא ממילא ומאיליו (line 476)

Line 477 is this range's F12. It is not F12 as comprehension, but as sensitivity: "the more one recognizes his Creator", the more one feels even the finest evil in oneself (וכל שיכיר את בוראו יותר). "You shall not stray" means taking delight in God alone, להתענג על ה' לבדו ואין זר אתו (line 477). The same point returns at 478, 519-521.

Siman 4 (479-481) asks how the Essence can say "I", when it is "removed and exalted above all" (line 479). Keter mediates between them. Its hidden side is 'Ayin'; its revealed side is 'Ani', the same letters reversed. The analogy is the will, which rests "in every limb, great and small, above and below, and in two opposites, all equally" (line 480, F04 + F09). So "I am first and I am last" are one: ואני אחרון אחר האצי' הכל בהשוואה א' (line 481).

Siman 5 (482-484) is the range's most original statement about the unity. In Himself He cannot even be called 'separate' or 'holy', because there is no one for Him to be hidden from: > דממי הוא מופלא ונעלם אחר שהוא ושמו בלבד (line 482)

Only when He consulted with the souls of Israel did 'Ayin' become 'I', and He came to be called Holy. So "you shall be holy" means Israel make Him holy: וא"כ ישראל הם המשיכי' גם לאני (line 482). Then the holiness is "of the Essence itself" (line 483). "My mitzvot" (as against "mitzvot of Havayah") are the Essence's own will. The word 'mitzvah' is read as companionship: ופי מצותי לשון צוותא וחיבור (line 483). Joy and fear are reconciled in sequence. When evil perishes there is song (באבוד רשעים רינה ושמחה, line 484), and that song reaches "His essential place, where there is no evil at all" (line 484, F05 + F11).

## Tzitzit 6-9 (485-496): bitul as the channel, the hidden 'I' in everything equally, the end in the beginning

The 32 threads are the bitul of koach mah, "only through it is the upper makif drawn to receivers" (line 485, F08). Line 486 keeps an important turn. Here "He wraps light" is *not* hiding but "the strength of the essential light shining in the face". The text itself says this is the reverse of the reading in lines 468-469. Siman 6's "without wisdom no fear" defines upper fear as bitul in essence: > משום דגלוי אא"ס תמיד בחכמ' שנק' אין בעצם (line 487)

Godly wisdom "does not dwell in the haughty, who make of it a 'something' for themselves" (line 487). Siman 7: when the Godly light is revealed beyond the measure of hiding, the kelipot "are nullified from existence, and the good in them is included in holiness" (line 488). The sparks rise "by bitul and inclusion in the Shechinah" (line 489). The white threads draw "added light from the Essence of the Emanator into the emanated" (line 489). The first garment, between the Emanator and the emanated, "is all bright light" (line 490), and in the future this light will be revealed below (line 490, F07).

The strongest F04 sentence in the range is at line 494. The hidden 'I' fills heaven and earth equally *because* it is hidden from everything equally: > משום שנסתר מהכל בשוה ע"כ התלבשותו בהעלם בכולם בשוה (line 494)

This contraction into the Name comes about "only through the deed of the mitzvot" (line 494). Siman 9 gives the order of the verses: techelet's bitul first, then the joy of "My mitzvot", רק חדות ה' במקומו (line 495). Line 496 separates two purities. The general makif ("I fill all") is the lower one. The upper one is the Essence, "as only He and His Name". Then it reverses the order: > הוא ע"י סוף מעשה דנשמות בגופים שמבערים הרע (line 496)

What makes the true 'Ayin' become 'I' is the end-deed of souls in bodies, the souls that remove evil. That is F07 and F11 joined at the root.

## Tzitzit 10-14 (496-511): one Torah; the Essence in measure; the makif; the yechidah

"Nuvlot" (fallen fruit) is said in praise. Malchut's counted letters renew creation daily without end (line 497, F01). What comes lowest shows the highest root. Hidden and revealed Torah are one: > והכל א' ולא ב' תורות הן (line 497)

The measuring itself comes from inner wisdom (line 497). Inner and outer Torah are "one level, root and power, from the Essence" (line 498). The eagle's mercy reaches keter of keter. There it is essential mercy, like a father's for his child, "equal to His Essence" (line 498), and it reaches down "to raise from the lowest pit" (line 498). Siman 10 contrasts the worlds with man. The worlds come only from the garment's glimmer, but man is in the image "as He is in His very Essence" (line 499). Even the one thought that surveys everything does not grasp Him, "because the Essence is utterly simple, above thought" (line 499). Without the garment there would be no order of worlds at all (line 499).

Simanim 11-12 (500-503) settle the two readings of "He wraps light". Thought holds "two opposites, hiding and revealing" (line 500). The upper hiding holds both: ונמצא יש בו ב' הפכים להסתיר ולהעלים ממש ולהיות מזה מקור לגלוי אור (line 501). Line 502 states it outright: שני הפי' אמת. Torah is "the light of the Essence itself" (line 502). How can the unbounded and the bounded be in one place (line 503)? Israel join them (line 504): > להיות לאחדים ממש למטה כמו למעלה ממש (line 504)

because "Torah and the Holy One are all one", and Israel are bound into that knot (line 504, F10 + F06). Sovev itself is "not from His inner Essence". The Essence dwells in measure and at the lowest level just as above: > גם בגבול ושעור ובתחתי' המדרגה הוא שורה כמו למעלה ממש (line 504)

The proof is the Temple: כמו השראת עצמות אא"ס בהיכל ק"ק במקום מדה וגבול (line 505). The tallit is the makif "in the inner Ein Sof" (line 506). The face of Man is highest. One who binds thought and speech to halacha and nullifies his will "truly resembles the Most High, the Essence of Or Ein Sof" (line 507). Tzedakah imitates the wisdom in Malchut that renews creation daily from nothing (line 507). Israel, clothed in physical mitzvot, arouse from a higher place than the Patriarchs (line 508). Mercy joins the highest end to the lowest, "to the lowest souls, like this last generation, called the heels" (line 509, F11 + F09). The Essence as it is can shine only as makif (line 510). In each soul: > שבחי' יחידה שבנפש הוא כח העצמות שבנפש ממש (line 510)

## Tzitzit 14-17 (511-521): transformation, teshuvah, and 'darkness as light' again

Techelet removes evil by force. White threads, from the Tree of Life, can turn evil to good (line 511). The same is true of Torah: ולכך יש ביכולת אור התורה להפוך רע לטוב (line 511). The soul's essential part, "cleaving above", despises evil without force. The part in the body has to choose (line 512). The two are one mitzvah (line 513), and line 513 names the foundation: > וזהו יסוד כל התו"מ לאהפכא חשוכא לנהורא (line 513)

Then the ranking turns over. Kindness sweetens the harsh judgments until evil "has no place, for it has become good" (line 514). Techelet does something truer. It sweetens the judgment in its own root, by judgment, like the incense that stopped the plague: > וזה כח התשוב' אמיתי' שעוש' מן הרע עצמו טוב (line 514)

This is the resurrection (line 514). The returning light rises above the direct light (line 515). Siman 16 grounds this in F02 and F05. Air is above light, and in the hidden air of elemental fire darkness and light are equal: > גם חשך לא יחשיך כחשיכה כאורה שוין שם (line 516)

So only fire rooted there can turn darkness itself to light, and in the future "night will shine like day". The soul as a lamp pushes darkness away only for a time (line 517). The soul as "the flame of Yah" eats the alien fire until he "becomes another man" (line 517). The text warns against confusing that flame with natural heat: ורבים טועים בזה (line 517). Purity is above the soul's holiness, "because there darkness and light are equal" (line 517). The mitzvah's lamp is rooted above Torah's light (line 518). The penitent is above the tzaddik, "because even the darkness itself shines" (line 518). The range ends on the practical side of the unity (519-521). The heart is to be one: לב' א' יהי' לה' לבד ואין זר אתו (line 521). Trust means knowing that He gives all one's livelihood, not nature or the stars (line 520, F01 + F03), and this is within every person's power (line 521).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say, or says differently

- **Where the Essence rests is a live question with a moving answer.** In the Tanya range, Torah and mitzvah are both His will and wisdom, "one with Him" (Tanya lines 568, 591). Here the Mitteler Rebbe asks which one carries more of the Essence, and gives three answers in turn. The mitzvah's simple will is higher (line 452). Wisdom holds the Essence more broadly, because it is 'nothing' and will is a narrow channel (lines 454-455). And in the future the mitzvot are the Essence itself, not a vessel (line 464). Bitul is the thread that runs through all three.
- **The Essence's equality has a dark side.** The Tanya range uses "even darkness does not darken" for the body not blocking the soul's union (Tanya line 689). Here "before Him darkness is as light" also explains how sin lets the kelipot feed from the inner light (line 459) and why the exile is long. That is F02/F05 used to explain exile, not only union.
- **Holiness is conferred by Israel.** In Himself He cannot be called separate or holy, since there is only He and His Name (line 482). This sharpens the SPINE's 'yachid' point. The Tanya range does not say it.
- **Sovev is ranked below the Essence.** The Tanya's ch. 48 calls all of Or Ein Sof "sovev" (Tanya line 703). Here sovev is "not from His inner Essence", an outer garment. The Essence is found in the Torah light and in the lowest measured deed (line 504).
- **The hidden fills all equally *because* it is hidden** (line 494). This is F04 grounded in F05, a step the Tanya range does not take.
- **F12 as sensitivity.** Knowing is less comprehension here than the power to feel one's own darkness (line 477). There is also a caution about false fervour (line 517).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Two lines are *none*: the colophon (465) and the gate heading (466). Line 460 is a heading sentence and 474 a one-sentence opening; both are *structural* with no face. - The growth analogy (447-448), the chayah-yechidah and will analogies (480, 492-493), the cantillation aside (451), and the craft-intellect aside (472.1) carry no face, or only the face of what they explain. They are marked *structural*. - The kabbalistic maps are *structural*: the four worlds (440-441), Z"A as Torah's root (443), the three curtains (490-491), and the counting of 32 and 8 threads (473, 511). - Line 491 cites "Forever, God, Your word stands in heaven" (the SPINE's F01 anchor), but only to name the firmament between Atzilut and Beriah. It is tagged F03, not F01. - Line 438's account of exile, where the kelipot are paid below so their root above is cut, is *structural* F11. It removes evil and does not turn it. - F02 appears only twice (459, 516), and never as the Shema unity, although the range begins inside the Gate of Keriat Shema. - No entry is *reading*. Where the text did not say the tie, the entry was left *structural* or given no face, rather than a face of ours.

## Disagreements kept

- **Within the range: what "He wraps light as a garment" means.** At lines 468-469 and 499 it is concealment, and what comes through is only "a glimmer of a glimmer". At lines 486, 490 and 502 it is the essential light spread as a bright garment, "not hiding". The text says both are true (lines 501-502) and applies the pair to inner and outer Torah (line 503). The two are not merged here.
- **Study or deed.** The range holds three positions and does not collapse them. Study is greater now; the deed is greater in the future (439, 453). The mitzvah's root is higher than Torah (450-452). Yet study reaches that root (454-455). And at the end the mitzvah's lamp is again rooted above Torah's light (518). The Tanya range's line 591 ("the will clothed in the mitzvah is itself the halacha he studies") treats the two as one. Imrei Binah ranks them, and the ranking changes with time.
- **Transformation against removal.** In the Tanya range the tzaddik turns evil to good, and the beinoni subdues it (Tanya line 762). Here the white threads (turning) at first seem higher than techelet (removing by force). Then techelet, read as teshuvah that sweetens judgment in its own root, is called "a truer sweetening" (line 514). The penitent stands above the perfectly righteous (lines 515, 518). This ranking is kept as the text's own, not smoothed into the Tanya's.
