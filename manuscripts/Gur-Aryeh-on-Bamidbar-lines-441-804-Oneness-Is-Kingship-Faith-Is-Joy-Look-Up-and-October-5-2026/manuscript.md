This is the second half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Bamidbar: the red cow, the death of Miriam, the waters of strife, the death of Aharon, the copper snake, Sichon and Og, Balak and Bilam, Pinchas, the new census, the daughters of Tzelofchad, the daily and holiday offerings, vows, the war on Midian, the journeys, and the cities of refuge. Much is law, war, or about nations and women, and is left out under D28. What is left includes two of the richest passages in all of Gur Aryeh: the sin at the rock (474) and the moon made small (696).

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **Oneness is kingship.** Why is the Shema called accepting His kingship? "רק כי אין אחדות אלא למלך, שהוא יחיד בעמו, ועל ידו יתקשר הכל להיות כלל אחד" (696). "וזה כי האחדות הוא המלכות" (696). "ויש לך לדעת, כי המלך הוא המקשר את מלכותו עד שהכל אחד" (696).
- **Faith is joy; anger is its opposite.** Moshe's sin at the rock was anger. "כי האמונה הוא מי שבוטח בו יתברך, אין לו בו רק שמחה" (474). "ועם הבטחון השמחה. אבל עם הכעס אין כאן אמונה" (474).
- **He wants all things to follow Him willingly.** "והיה הוא יתברך רוצה שיהיו כל הנמצאים נמשכים אחר דבור השם יתברך לעשות רצונו מעצמם, לא על ידי הכרחי" (474).
- **Look up and bind the heart.** The copper snake did not heal. The looking up did. "שאי אפשר מי שמביט למעלה שלא יהיה רואה ומכוון לבו לשמים" (501).
- **Cleave to the One who renews the light.** On Rosh Chodesh "ובזה הנמצאים דבקים בו יתברך, במה שהוא מהוה ומחדש האור, לא במה שהוא ממעט האור" (696). And "שהחדוש מורה על מעלה עליונה מן ענין הראשון" (696): renewal is higher than the first state.
- **A mitzvah is a divine deed.** "לפי שמי שעושה מצוה פועל פעולה אלקית נפלאה" (598).

## 2.1 Oneness is kingship (696)

This is the fullest unity passage in the range. The two great lights were made in one act, as equals. The moon said: two kings cannot use one crown. The Maharal explains: a king must be single, because the king is the one who ties his kingdom into one. "ויש לך לדעת, כי המלך הוא המקשר את מלכותו עד שהכל אחד" (696). Two cannot do it: "כי איך יתקשר להיות אחד על ידי שנים, כי שנים הם מחולקים" (696). This is why the Shema is called accepting His kingship: "רק כי אין אחדות אלא למלך, שהוא יחיד בעמו, ועל ידו יתקשר הכל להיות כלל אחד" (696). And in short: "וזה כי האחדות הוא המלכות" (696). "Oneness is kingship."

So "G-d is One" is not only a fact about Him. It says that He ties all things into one whole. And when we say it, we accept being tied in.

## 2.2 All is ordered by Him as one

People ask: how can G-d "need atonement" for making the moon small? The Maharal answers that "kapara" means wiping away and "chet" means a lack. The lack was needed for the order of the world. No creature has a claim on Him, "כי מתוך חסדו בראם" (696): "for He created them out of His kindness." With a person, sin and repair are two acts. With G-d, "אבל אצל השם יתברך הכל מסודר ממנו בענין אחד" (696): "all is ordered by Him as one thing": the making, the lessening, and the repair.

## 2.3 Oneness, not many (577)

Bilam built seven altars. The fathers built seven together, not seven each, "שאם כן היה הכל רבוי, ולא אחדות" (577): "for then it would all be many, and not oneness." They cleaved to Him in every part: "ומפני שיש להם דביקות בו יתברך בכל, ולא דביקות בחלק בדבר מה" (577). Bilam thought the opposite, "ומזה הצד אמר כי יותר חשוב הרבוי מן האחדות" (577): "that many is worth more than oneness."

## 2.4 One beginning (445)

The red cow atones for the golden calf. Why? The idolater does not deny that G-d is the beginning of all. "שמודים שהוא יתברך התחלת הכל, רק שאומרים שיש לעשות התחלה אל האדם" (445): "they admit that He is the beginning of all, but they say one must make a beginning for man." The Maharal answers: "ואין התחלה לאדם רק השם יתברך" (445). The cow is burned to ash, "עד שחוזר אל יסוד הפשוט" (445), to show that what they called a beginning goes back to the simple dust it came from.

## 2.5 The non-bodily is small and in the middle (521)

(Universal rule only; the nations frame is left out.) What has a body spreads out. What has no body has no extent, so it is pictured as small: "וכל זה כי הדבר הבלתי גשמי אין לו רוחק, והקטנות אין לו רוחק גם כן, לכך הבלתי גשמי יתואר בקטנות" (521). And it stands like the middle point: "אבל הבלתי גשמי לא שייך בו התפשטות רחקים, ולפיכך הוא דומה לאמצעי שבין שני הקצות" (521). Those who cleave to G-d take on this trait, "שהם דבקים בו יתברך שאינו גוף" (521). The same rule returns in 696: the righteous are called by the small moon, "כלל הדבר, כי הצדיק הוא מי שהוא אינו נוטה מן היושר, ואינו נוטה מן הצדק, ונשאר על הנקודה, ובאמצע" (696), "והקטן בעבור שאין לו התפשטות ראוי לזה" (696).

## 2.6 All beings follow Him willingly (474)

Why speak to the rock and not strike it? "והיה הוא יתברך רוצה שיהיו כל הנמצאים נמשכים אחר דבור השם יתברך לעשות רצונו מעצמם, לא על ידי הכרחי" (474). Then Israel "ויראו כי כל הנמצאים נמשכים אחריו ברצון" (474): "would see that all beings follow Him willingly." The world is not forced to obey. It is drawn.

## 2.7 Every creature is straight in its place (521)

"שכל הנבראים אשר בראם השם יתברך, יש בהם מן היושר בבריאתם" (521). "וזה כאשר כל בריאה עומדת במדריגתה הראויה לה בבריאתה" (521). Evil starts when a thing leaves its place.

## 2.8 The whole is the head of the parts (755, 713)

A count is called "head," "מפני כי הכלל הוא עיקר וראש לפרטים, שהפרטים יכנסו תחת הכלל" (755). And Moshe's prophecy is higher because it is of the whole: "כי הענינים הכוללים הם מופשטים מן החומר, ומושכלים ביותר" (713). "אבל משה רבינו עליו השלום נתן תורה כללית ונצחית, לא ישתנה דבר, וזה נקרא הויה כוללת" (713).

- **MH-S64 "Trust that is glad" (474).** "כי האמונה הוא מי שבוטח בו יתברך, אין לו בו רק שמחה" (474). "שעם כל אמונה שמחה" (474). Inside: when I truly trust Him, there is no room for anger. The anger I feel tells me my trust is thin: "כי מי שאינו מאמין ובוטח בו יתברך בבטחון גמור, הוא בכעס ובמכאובות" (474). How to enter: when anger rises, stop and ask: "Do I trust Him here?" Say: "You are doing this. I trust You." Let the joy of trust push out the anger. It differs from S84 Trust, which is calm reliance. This is trust that shows itself as gladness, and is measured by the lack of anger.
- **MH-S65 "Looking up" (501).** "שאי אפשר מי שמביט למעלה שלא יהיה רואה ומכוון לבו לשמים" (501). Inside: my eyes go up, and my heart follows. I see what hurt me, and I pray harder. How to enter: in pain or fear, lift your eyes, even to the sky. Think: "You gave the healing." Bind your heart to Him. It differs from S39 The heart answers. Here the body leads: the eyes go up first, and the heart comes after.
- **MH-S66 "The renewed light" (696).** "ובזה הנמצאים דבקים בו יתברך, במה שהוא מהוה ומחדש האור, לא במה שהוא ממעט האור" (696). Inside: after a time of lessening, I do not cling to the dark part. I cling to Him as the One who renews. And the renewal is higher than before: "שהחדוש מורה על מעלה עליונה מן ענין הראשון" (696). How to enter: at Rosh Chodesh, or after a hard time, say: "I hold You as the One who makes new light." It differs from MH-S58 Back to the first standing. That is return to how it was. This is new light, higher than before.
- **MH-S67 "Small, at the point" (696, 521).** "כלל הדבר, כי הצדיק הוא מי שהוא אינו נוטה מן היושר, ואינו נוטה מן הצדק, ונשאר על הנקודה, ובאמצע" (696). Inside: I do not spread out or push. I stay at the center point, straight, not leaning to either side. Being small here is not a loss. How to enter: when pulled to one side, picture a point in the middle and stand there. It differs from S26 The middle point, which is the inner core of the heart. This is the stance of the righteous, small and straight.

## 4.1 Measure trust by anger (`mh_trust_without_anger`, new)

The practice for MH-S64 (474). "ועם הבטחון השמחה. אבל עם הכעס אין כאן אמונה" (474). When you feel anger, even at someone who deserves it (Moshe's anger was at Israel), treat it as a sign. Ask: "Where is my trust?" Say: "You are in this. I trust You." Wait until some gladness comes back before you act. Guard: this is not a denial of feeling. It is noticing what the anger shows, and turning back to trust.

## 4.2 Look up (`mh_look_up`, new)

The practice for MH-S65 (501). Healing came "כאשר היו מסתכלין אל הקדוש ברוך הוא, ומאמינים בהקדוש ברוך הוא אשר נתן להם רפואה, והיו משעבדים את לבם" (501). When you are sick or afraid, lift your eyes. Name what hurts you before Him, and pray for it. Say: "You give the healing." This is prayer alongside the doctor, not in place of one.

## 4.3 Hold the one who renews (`mh_cleave_to_renewal`, new)

The practice for MH-S66 (696). At Rosh Chodesh, stop for a moment. Think of what grew small this past month. Then say: "I do not hold You as the One who lessened. I hold You as the One who makes new light." End with the Maharal's own words: "והוא יתברך יאיר עינינו במאור תורתו" (696).

## 4.4 Say Shema as accepting the one whole (`mh_only_one_in_heart`, extended)

Add 696 to the Shema practice. "וזה כי האחדות הוא המלכות" (696). When you say "Hashem is One," think: "You tie everything, and me, into one whole. I accept it." One is not a count. One is His kingship holding all.

## 4.5 Pray even after the decree (`mh_pray_still`, new)

There are three levels: a thing rises in thought, it is decreed, it is decreed with an oath (475). Before the decree, a little prayer is enough. After it, "אבל כשנגזרה, אי אפשר שתתבטל אלא על ידי תפילה וצעקה גדולה וזכות גדול" (475). Moshe was told to go up the mountain and die, and still "דהרי היה סבור לבטל בתפילה" (679): "he still thought to undo it by prayer." Practice: when something bad seems settled, do not stop praying. Pray more, with a real cry.

## 4.6 A mitzvah is strength (`mh_mitzvah_as_strength`, new)

"לפי שמי שעושה מצוה פועל פעולה אלקית נפלאה" (598). "והם המצות, אשר הם מעשה אלקים" (598). And learning: "כי התורה היא אלקית, וכאשר לומד ומתגבר בתורה, היא הגבורה עם הדברים האלקיים" (598). Practice: when you get up in the morning and reach for a mitzvah, think: "I am about to do a divine deed." Rise to it like a lion.

## 4.7 Make peace in two voices (`mh_peace_two_ways`, new)

Aharon made peace by speaking to each side in its own way: "כי מי שהוא רוצה לעשות שלום בין שנים, צריך לדבר ביניהם בענינים מתחלפים" (489). Practice: when you try to make peace, do not give both people the same speech. Find what each one needs to hear.

## 4.8 Remember the road (`mh_remember_the_road`, new)

The journeys are listed like a father telling his son: here we slept, here we were cold, here your head hurt. "כך השם יתברך היה מטפל עם ישראל בשביל אהבתו אליהם, ובשביל כך יזכרו ישראל הטובות שעשה להם, ויעבדו אותו בכל לב" (776). Practice: once a week, list the places your life has passed through. At each one, say: "You were caring for me there." Then serve Him with that memory.

## 4.9 Do it with joy, now (`mh_do_it_now`, extended)

Moshe was told to take revenge on Midian, and knew he would die after it. Still: "עשה בשמחה ולא איחר" (734): "he did it with joy and did not delay." Add to the practice: when a mitzvah costs you something, do it anyway, gladly, and at once.

## 4.10 What the mind makes lasts (`mh_learn_to_do`, extended)

"כי כל דבר שכלי יש לו קיום" (442). "וכל דבר הנמשך אחר השכל יש לו קיום" (442). And learning the laws of a mitzvah is how one reaches its core: "כי ההלכות שיש למצוה מעמידין את האדם על עיקר מהות המצוה" (461). Add to the practice: learn the laws of the mitzvah you are doing. What you understand, lasts.

## 5. The big human questions

**What does "G-d is One" mean?** He is the King who ties all into one whole. "Oneness is kingship" (696).

**Why do I get angry?** Anger shows that trust is thin. With full trust there is only joy in Him (474).

**Does G-d want to force the world?** No. He wants all things to follow His word willingly (474).

**How does healing come?** When the eyes look up and the heart is bound to Him (501).

**Is being small a loss?** No. The small stands at the middle point, and the righteous are called by it (696, 521).

**Can a bad decree be changed?** Yes, by prayer, a great cry and merit. Moshe did not stop praying (475, 679).

**Why did G-d make things lessen?** For the order of the whole, out of kindness, and He renews the light, higher than before (696).

**Does G-d remember what I went through?** Every step, like a father telling the whole road (776).

**What does a mitzvah do?** It is a divine deed done by a person (598).

**What is the Torah of Moshe?** The whole, lasting order that does not change (713).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Oneness is kingship** (696) is close to the Chabad teaching that malchut is the source of the worlds: "there is no king without a people" (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; Iggeret HaKodesh 20). The Maharal says the king is the one who ties the many into one. Chabad says the worlds exist so that His kingship can be revealed. Both make the Shema a statement about His rule over one bound whole.
- **Tzimtzum.** The moon made small (696) is a picture of a lessening needed for the order of the world, done out of kindness, and followed by renewal. This is close to the Chabad reading of tzimtzum as a hiding for the sake of creation, done out of kindness (Shaar HaYichud ch. 4, 7). The Maharal adds that we cleave to Him "in that He renews the light, not in that He lessens the light." Chabad would say: from His side there is no lessening at all.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "What is not bodily has no extent" (521) is the Rambam's first negation: G-d is not a body (Yesodei HaTorah 1:7-8; Guide I:55). The Maharal turns it into a trait for people: those who cleave to Him who is not a body are small and stand at the middle point.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "All beings follow Him willingly" (474) and "every creature is straight in its place" (521) are the first half: all is His, and all is drawn to Him. "Not in that He lessens the light" (696) and "one beginning" (445) guard the second half: nothing in the world is a beginning beside Him.
- **Faith is joy; anger is idolatry.** The Maharal's "with anger there is no faith" (474) matches the Chabad teaching, from the Zohar and the Rambam, that one who is angry is like one who serves idols, since he forgets that all is from G-d (Iggeret HaKodesh 25). And "with all faith there is joy" fits Tanya ch. 26 and 33 on serving in joy.
- **Moshe's prophecy of the whole** (713) is close to Tanya's teaching that the Torah is His will and wisdom as one (Tanya ch. 4, 23), not a report of events.
- **A mitzvah is a divine deed** (598) fits Tanya ch. 23 and 35: the mitzvah is His will, and the limb that does it becomes a chariot for Him.
