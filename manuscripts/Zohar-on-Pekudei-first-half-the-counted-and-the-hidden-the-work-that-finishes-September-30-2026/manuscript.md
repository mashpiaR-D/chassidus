## 1. What the range is

Pekudei, "the accounts," closes the book of Exodus. In the Zohar it is very long, because it holds the two sets of **Heikhalot**, the seven palaces of holiness and the seven of impurity. This first report covers the part before them (lines 4251-4720):

* the daily voice that calls "guard yourselves" and the light hidden for the righteous (lines 4253-4258); * the census and the question of blessing on what is counted (lines 4262-4347); * the building that finished itself, and Betzalel "in the shade of God" (lines 4288-4309); * the nine palaces of thought that are "grasped and not grasped" (lines 4351-4356); * the letters of the Torah at Sinai, the hooks and pillars, the Name hidden and shown (lines 4358-4380); * the priestly garments, the breastplate, the tzitz, Urim and Tumim (lines 4412-4460); * Jonah and his sailors as honest seekers (lines 4439-4445); * David who lowered himself (lines 4480-4485); * the measuring line and the future Temple (lines 4486-4511); * Moses who alone could raise the Tabernacle, and the other side broken by it (lines 4533-4674); * the burnt offering that goes up to Ein Sof (lines 4599-4619); * the levels of the other side, the kinds of anger, and "sufferings of love" (lines 4674-4720).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**Ein Sof is beyond will and light.** The strongest passage of the range. R. Elazar asks how high the joining of the offering rises. R. Shimon: up to Ein Sof, דרעוא דכל רעוין ביה, אין סוף לא קיימא לאודעא, ולאו למעבד סוף, ולאו למעבד ראש, "where the will of all wills is; Ein Sof does not stand to be known, nor to make an end, nor to make a beginning" (line 4610). And: לאו רעותין, לאו נהורין, לאו בוצינין, בההוא אין סוף, "there are no wills, no lights, no lamps in that Ein Sof" (line 4611). All the lights hang on it to exist, but it is not there to be cleaved to by one who knows or does not know; לאו איהו אלא רעו עלאה, סתימא דכל סתימין, אין, "it is only the high will, most hidden of all hidden: *Ayin*, Nothing" (line 4611). When the highest point and the world to come rise, לא ידעי בר ריחא, כמאן דארח בריחא ואתבסם, "they know only a scent, like one who smells a scent and is sweetened" (line 4612).

**Grasped and not grasped.** The nine palaces of thought "are not lights, not spirits, not souls, and no one stands in them." They are held in thought and chased by it, תפסין בה ולא תפסין, "they grasp and do not grasp" (line 4352), and לאו הכא מחשבה ולאו רעותא, "here there is no thought and no will" (line 4352). The light from which the highest thought shines: ומחשבה אתעטר באין סוף, ההוא נהירו דנהיר מניה מחשבה עלאה, אקרי אין סוף, "and thought is crowned in Ein Sof; that light from which the highest thought shines is called Ein Sof" (line 4353). **(ours)** This is the Zohar's apophatic summit, and the root of the Chabad teaching that *Atzmut* is above even the will of Keter.

**Hidden and shown in everything.** קודשא בריך הוא סתים וגליא, "the Holy One is hidden and revealed" (line 4376). The revealed is the court of judgment; the hidden is where all blessings come from. וכל מלין דעלמא בין דעלמא דין, ובין דעלמא דלעילא, כלהו איהו טמיר וגליא, "all things of this world and of the world above are hidden and revealed" (line 4438). The Name is written *YHVH* and read *Adonai*: the one hides in the other.

**Voice and speech never apart.** Urim and Tumim, face and back, voice and speech: ותדיר דא בדא סלקן, "they always rise together" and never separate (line 4435).

**All to be one.** Every measure of the Tabernacle and Temple exists לאתקשרא האי עלמא בעלמא דלעילא, למהוי כלא חד ברזא חדא, "to bind this world to the world above, so that all is one in one secret" (line 4511).

**Even judgment is love.** The judgment God desires is דינא דקודשא בריך הוא אתרעי ביה, איהו דינא בריר, "clear judgment," דאתער רחימו וחדוה, "which wakes love and joy" (line 4312). It is not the "filthy judgment" of the wicked.

**Nothing is lost.** Even Balak and Balaam's offerings were not forgotten: וכלא איהו מתתקן קמיה דקודשא בריך הוא, ולא אתאביד מלה, "everything is set in order before the Holy One, and nothing is lost" (line 4316).

## 3. States of consciousness

**Held and not held (proposed ZS2-21).** From lines 4352, 4610-4612: the mind reaches toward what is beyond will and light, catches it and does not catch it, and is left with only a "scent" that sweetens. Inside: you do not arrive and you are not turned away. The reaching itself is the contact. Near S35 (The edge of knowing) and S36 (Faith above knowing), but new: the Zohar describes a sweetness at the edge, not a wall. **(ours)**

**Lowered and lifted (fills in S59).** David praised himself when he ruled and called himself poor when pursued, and always lowered himself before God, דכל מאן דמאיך גרמיה קמי קודשא בריך הוא, איהו זקיף ליה על כלא, "for whoever lowers himself before the Holy One, He raises him above all" (line 4483). אית ליה לבר נש למהוי נבזה בעיניו, "a person should be lowly in his own eyes," למהוי איהו מאנא דקודשא בריך הוא אתרעי ביה, "to be a vessel the Holy One desires" (line 4485).

**Raised like one who has fallen.** "Moses raised the Tabernacle": מאן דהוה מאיך אוקמיה, כמאן דאוקים למאן דנפיל, "what was low he raised, like one who raises one who has fallen" (line 4620). Israel may fall, but דהא קודשא בריך הוא אקים לה תדיר, "the Holy One always raises her" (line 4653), as Micah says: "though I fell, I rose." A line for S11 (The verdict: I'm my mistakes).

**The quiet anger.** Of four kinds of anger that come from the other side, the worst is the silent one, which "is called the silent destroyer": האי קשיא מכלהו, בגין דאיהו כגוונא דחויא, דשתיק תדיר, וקטיל לבתר, "this is the hardest of all, for it is like a snake, always silent, and kills later" (line 4692). The first kind is האי איהו רוגזא דשלטא ואתקף בעלמא, ועאל בגו בני נשא, "the anger that rules the world and enters into people" (line 4686). **(ours)** A sharp point for S15 (Angry at someone): the anger that is held in silence and nursed is more dangerous than the anger that breaks out and is over.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Begin, and the holy work finishes itself.** When the craftsmen began the Tabernacle, it completed itself: אינון שראן, ואיהי אשלימת עבידתא, "they began, and it finished the work" (line 4294). So with the Temple built of whole stones: וכן בכל עבידתא דאיהי קדישא, איהי אשתלימת מגרמה, "and so with every holy work: it is completed by itself" (line 4296). And God to Moses, who was troubled by the vision: משה, את בסימניך, ואני בסימני, "Moses, you by your signs, and I by Mine" (line 4267). **(ours)** A practice of beginning without having to finish: in study, prayer, or any good task, do your part and leave the completing to Him. Close to technique 21 (The act that closes).

**Set the point first.** In raising the Tabernacle, Moses first raised the hidden point that was "dark and sunk in its place." וכיון דהאי נקודה אתקמת ואתנהירת, כדין, כל שאר תקונא אחרא אתתקן, "once this point was raised and lit, all the other repairs were made" (line 4667). And שארי בקימה, וסיים בקימה, "he began with rising and ended with rising" (line 4673). **(ours)** In any contemplation, first set the one central point (technique 3, Choosing the one line), and begin and end standing up inside.

**Burn the thought that rises.** "This is the law of the *olah* (the rising offering)": *olah* is also the bad thought that rises in a person's will to turn him from the truth; היא היא דסלקא, ואסטיאת ליה לבר נש, בעי לאוקדא ליה בנורא, "it is the one that rises and leads a person astray; one must burn it in fire" (line 4617), so it has no room. Fills in technique 12 (Returning the wandering mind): do not argue with the stray thought; put it in the fire of the altar and go on.

**Keep some things hidden.** כל מלוי דבר נש דאינון בסתימו, ברכאן שריין עליה, "all of a person's matters that are hidden, blessings rest on them" (line 4376); what is on display draws judgment. And ברכאן שריין במה דלא קיימא בחושבנא, "blessings rest on what is not counted" (line 4278). **(ours)** A quiet practice: do not count your good deeds, and keep your inner work to yourself.

**Fixed times with faith.** One who sets times for Torah needs *emunah*: דרעותא דיליה יתכוין לקודשא בריך הוא, יתכוין לשם שמים, "that his will be directed to the Holy One, for the sake of heaven" (line 4300).

**Walk with words of Torah.** On the road: בגין דשכינתא לא תשרי עלן, אלא מגו מלי דאורייתא, "the Shekhinah rests on us only through words of Torah" (line 4480).

## 5. The big human questions

**Why does wisdom go to the wise?** דקודשא בריך הוא לא יהיב חכמתא, אלא למאן דאית ביה חכמתא, "the Holy One gives wisdom only to one who has wisdom" (line 4306), the understanding of the heart that can hold it. **(ours)** Start with the heart's readiness; the rest comes.

**Why do good people suffer?** The righteous who have the merit of the fathers are not touched by the illnesses that chase the wicked. Where then do their illnesses come from? "The Lord desired to crush him": ויי' חפץ, דכאו, אתרעי למחאה ליה, "the Lord desired to strike him," to bring him to the world to come, and not from the other side; ואלין אקרון יסורין דאהבה, "and these are called sufferings of love" (line 4718). **(ours)** A traditional answer that the guide can offer only with care, and never to explain someone else's pain.

**Does God's watching mean I will be rich?** "The eyes of the Lord are on the righteous," yet many righteous cannot find bread. R. Elazar: it means that ובאתר דשלטא ההיא אשגחותא דסטרא דקדושה, לא אשגח עליה סטרא אחרא, "where the watching of the holy side rules, the other side does not look at it" (line 4339). Providence is protection of the soul, not wealth.

**Is the Shekhinah only for Israel?** Jonah's sailors questioned him honestly, then all converted and became great sages, because when God's name is made holy in the open, שמיה דאתכסיא, אסתליק על כורסי יקריה, "His hidden name rises onto His throne of glory" (line 4445). And the voice goes out each day: אסתמרו בני עלמא, טרוקו גלי חובין, "guard yourselves, people of the world; close the breaches of sin" (line 4253), to all people.

**Why does God come in a small tent?** A *mishkan* (dwelling) is not a *bayit* (house): כמלכא דאתי לגבי רחימיה, ולא מייתי כל אכלוסין דיליה עמיה, בגין דלא לאטרחא עליה, "like a king who comes to his friend and does not bring all his troops with him, so as not to burden him" (line 4647). **(ours)** God comes to us in a size we can host.

**Why do the letters matter?** Moses alone could raise the Tabernacle because the will that rose on the letters was given to him alone (line 4525). Betzalel knew the combinations, but not the will. **(ours)** Technique without the will behind it cannot stand the structure up.

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **"No wills, no lights, no lamps in that Ein Sof ... only the most hidden will, *Ayin*" (lines 4610-4611)** is the Zohar's clearest line on the hidden essence. Pair it with the Gate of Unity on *Atzmut*.
2. **"They began, and it finished the work" (lines 4294-4296)** lifts the weight of having to complete everything.
3. **"He comes like a king to a friend, with few troops, so as not to burden him" (line 4647)** is a gentle image of God's nearness.
4. **"The silent anger is the hardest" (line 4692)** is useful for anger work.
5. **"Blessing rests on what is hidden" (line 4376)** supports quiet, uncounted service.

Proposed for the map (details in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`): ZS2-21 *Held and not held*.

Set aside: the census and plague as numbers (lines 4334-4347), Elisha and the bears (lines 4320-4323), the star patterns and letter mechanics (lines 4381-4411, 4486-4508, 4514-4524), the golden calf as sorcery of the other side (lines 4556-4578), the goat hair in tefillin and gifts to the other side (lines 4579-4587), the camel dream-sign, and the anatomy of the other side's levels (lines 4674-4720, used only for the anger passage).
