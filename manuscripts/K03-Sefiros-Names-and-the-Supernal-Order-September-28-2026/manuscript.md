## What this cluster shows

These twelve subjects are the supernal order: the sefiros, their parts, their names and the will beneath them. A system of emanations looks like the opposite of a single point, since it has ten measures, partzufim, unions and names. The sources' answer, stated again and again, is that the order is the unity itself, spread into measures: 'He, His life and His vessels are one' (IH 20; Shaar HaYichud 27, 33). The order exists so that the finite can come from the Infinite (IH 20:12) and so that there be a people for a King (SH 7). Every part points back past itself: Keser to the last level of Ein Sof, Chochmah to 'He alone and nothing besides', the attributes to their inclusion in one another, Malchus to the Essence's power, the names to one Being who is not His names, will and delight to an Essence separate even from delight. The tie is stated for every subject. The cluster's risk is the one the spine names in section 5: most working passages on sefirah mechanics do not restate the tie, and applying it passage by passage is our reading (S027).

## S027 · The ten sefiros · עשר ספירות

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** עשר ספירות · ספירות · עשר ולא תשע · עשר ספירות בלימה

> The ten sefiros are the unity of God as the one Infinite Light clothed in ten measures so that finite things can come from it, while He Himself is not one of the ten.

## Derivation

1. Before the sefiros is the Essence. The Mitteler Rebbe says that in the Essence itself one cannot yet speak of ten sefiros (Shaar HaYichud 10:14), and that the 'One' of Shema is the simple unity 'one, but not in the count of the ten sefiros' (7:31-32). (*stated*)
2. Why ten at all: so that finite creatures can come from infinite power, the Infinite Light clothes itself in the vessels of the ten sefiros of Atzilus and unites with them completely (IH 20:12). (*stated*)
3. So the sefiros are not a second thing beside Him. 'He, His life and His vessels are one': the lights and even the vessels of Atzilus are Godliness, able like Ein Sof to create something from nothing (IH 20:1-2). (*stated*)
4. Their unity shows in how they stand toward each other. SH 6:3 states it: the attributes include one another because they are one with Him ('You bind them and unite them'; under S031). In Tohu the ten do not unite or include one another, and they fall (Shaar HaYichud 20:17-18); in Tikkun each makes room for the others (the Rayatz, Sefer HaSichos 5707, under S031). Shaar HaYichud 20 and the Rayatz describe Tohu and Tikkun without themselves restating the tie to His unity; that tie comes from SH 6:3. (*stated*)
5. Whether the ten are 'in' the Essence divides the sources. The Mitteler Rebbe: even in the Essence, 'alone', the ten are included in Himself, hidden (10:16, 11:52). The Rashab, citing the Alter Rebbe: in the Essence there are no sefiros at all, and those of Atzilus are a new entity, made through tzimtzum (Kuntres Umaayan 21). Each position is stated; calling it a disagreement is ours. (*stated*)
6. The sefiros exist to lead the worlds. What is above them is above nature, and the service is to draw that into nature (the Rebbe, 19 Kislev 5715). (*stated*)
7. The soul's ten powers mirror the sefiros (Tanya 3; the Besht: man is 'a small world'), and that is how a person understands them 'from my flesh'. But a Jew's inability to be separated from God comes from the Essence, above the sefiros (Hayom Yom 21 Sivan). The analogy stops there. (*stated*)
8. That learning the sefiros serves the unity (joining each detail to the whole and the whole to the Essence, G04) is the Mitteler Rebbe's stated aim for his book. Applying it to every passage about sefiros is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 20: the ten sefiros of Atzilus, lights and vessels, are Godliness, and the Infinite unites with their vessels to create limited beings. Tanya 3: each soul-level has ten powers corresponding to the ten sefiros. Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 8:3 (under S031): the attributes belong only to the vessels, yet are ascribed to Him Himself.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud defines its subject as 'the unity of Godliness in every detail' of the ten sefiros of ABYA (Introduction). Shema is above the count of ten (7:31-32). The hidden ten are in the Essence (10:16; 11:52). Tohu is sefiros that do not unite (20:17).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located on the ten as such in this pass. His Keser (S028) and his definition of which Malchus is called Shechinah (S034) belong here.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel uses the term about 30 times. None was read for this subject.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 21: in the Essence there are no sefiros at all; the sefiros of Atzilus are a new entity; Atzilus has no measure relative to Ein Sof, more so than Asiyah relative to Atzilus.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5707 (Yiddish): in Tohu each sefirah is a strong light unrelated to the others; in Tikkun each makes room for the others (S031). Hayom Yom 21 Sivan, compiled from his words: self-sacrifice comes from the Essence, above the sefiros.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, 19 Kislev 5715: 'You brought forth ten tikkunim to lead the worlds'. Above the sefiros is above nature, and the Essence's power must be drawn into nature itself.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:58): a person has ten sefiros as a small world, and he reads suffering as Malchus and delight in service as Yesod. That is the analogy of G06 made into a map of the person. The Maggid counts ten names 'from which all is drawn' (under S036). R. Nachman: the worlds in the vacated space are His attributes (S031). Pillar 08 records Strashelye reading the Ari's 'ten hidden sefiros' as saying there is nothing to find there; that was not re-verified here.

## Thin or forced?

Partly. The tie is stated at the level of doctrine (IH 20, Shaar HaYichud, Kuntres Umaayan). Most working passages about sefiros in the maamarim describe how the sefiros work and do not restate it (the spine, section 5, says the same). Tying each such passage to the unity is our reading.

## S028 · Keser: Atik and Arich · כתר / עתיק ואריך

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding · F07 The home below **Search terms:** כתר עליון · עתיק יומין · אריך אנפין · עתיק ואריך

> Keser is the unity of God as the point where the Infinite touches what it emanates, the last level of the Emanator and the root of the emanated in one.

## Derivation

1. Ein Sof is not defined as wisdom or kindness, so for it to become sefiros there must be something between (the Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha, Vidui 2; IH 20:14). (*stated*)
2. That middle is Keser, which has two sides: the last level of Ein Sof and the root and head of the emanated (IH 20:14; Likkutei Torah, Tzav 4:10). (*stated*)
3. The two sides are two partzufim. Atik is the lowest level of the Emanator; Arich is the root of the emanated (Tzemach Tzedek). In the soul's terms they are delight and will (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 17:17-21). (*stated*)
4. Keser is infinitely above the particular sefiros and holds them together. Ein Sof reaches each vessel only through it (Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 8:7). The Alter Rebbe adds that the Ari's 'last level of Ein Sof' means the light, not the Essence (8:4-5). (*stated*)
5. 'Their end is embedded in their beginning'. Keser is also 'Keser Malchus', because the last level of Ein Sof is its Malchus, and Malchus of Atzilus becomes Atik of Beriah (IH 20:14-15). The highest shows at the lowest point (G05). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe, from a Rashab maamar, names the essential point of Chassidus as the drawing down of inner Keter and inner Atik, 'the Ein Sof present in RaDLA' (Inyanah 2:2). The Rashab places the substance of a person's physical food and life in Atika Kadisha, 'the inner essence of Ein Sof' (Kuntres Umaayan 24). (*stated*)
7. From below, Berditchev: whoever sees that he is nothing and clings to his root becomes a 'garment' for Atik Yomin. That this is the human counterpart of Keser as the hinge (G08) is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 20:14-15: Keser mediates and holds the last level of Ein Sof, and is 'Keser Malchus'. Likkutei Torah, Tzav 4:10 and Shir HaShirim 8:3-7: two sides of Keser. The 'last level' is the last of the light, from which Atik of Atzilus is drawn through the kav.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 17:15-21: Atika Stima is Keser of Adam Kadmon, will and delight still hidden; Atik Yomin and Arich of Atzilus are delight and will revealed. 19:41-46: the kesarim of the sefiros are revealed will; only Malchus of Adam Kadmon becomes Keser of Atzilus.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Vidui 2: Keser divides into Atik and Arich, and will is the mediator, since what is willed is at once included in the will.
- **Maharash.** Not located for this subject.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 24:1: material bounty comes in substance from Atika Kadisha and is drawn through the ten sefiros of Atzilus that Israel's service reaches. Kuntres Umaayan 18-19: the five gevuros of Atik at Ne'ilah (S032). His maamar is the basis of Inyanah 2:2.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, Shemini Atzeres (read, not quoted): the 'wondrous intellect', the Chochmah of Atik, is wholly above even the source of the Chochmah of Atzilus; it is the essence of light, not light as revelation. Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (S036): the thirteen attributes draw 'the power that comes from Atika Kadisha'.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 2:2-3: Chassidus is the drawing down of inner Atik, the Ein Sof in RaDLA; because Ein Sof is the Essence, every virtue of Chassidus branches from it.
- **Other schools.** Berditchev (Kedushat Levi, Ki Tisa 13): the person who knows that he has nothing of himself becomes a garment for Atik Yomin, and 'Israel sustain their Father'. Pillar 18 records Strashelye's order (delight, then will, then will revealed); it was not re-verified here.

## S029 · Chochmah and Binah above · חכמה ובינה / אבא ואימא

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** חכמה עילאה · אבא ואימא · כח מה · חכמה ובינה

> Chochmah and Binah above are the unity of God as a nothing that the Infinite fills, and a something that learns to be like that nothing.

## Derivation

1. Chochmah is koach mah, what is not yet grasped. That is why the Infinite Light, which no thought grasps, is clothed in it (Tanya 18). (*stated*)
2. The Alter Rebbe, quoting his teacher: Ein Sof unites with Atzilus only by first clothing itself in Chochmah, because Ein Sof is the true One, 'He alone and nothing besides', and that is the level of Chochmah (Tanya 35, gloss). (*stated*)
3. So Chochmah is ayin, the formless root of everything, and a thing can change only when brought back to it (Maggid 47). Binah, where understanding takes shape, is yesh (Likkutei Torah, Nasso 6:23). (*stated*)
4. The union of Abba and Imma is 'peace above': supernal Da'at (da'as elyon, here the sefirah that joins them, not the 'higher knowledge' of S030) joins them so that the yesh of Binah is like the ayin of Chochmah (Likkutei Torah, Nasso 6:23). Peace is 'the mediator that joins two opposites' (F09). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe refines this. Chochmah is 'yesh' because the true Yesh rests in it, and 'ayin' because it is nullified to it. As the source of Binah it is the ayin of the created yesh (lower knowledge); in itself it is the ayin of the true Yesh (higher knowledge) (Melukat, Vayeshev 5715). (*stated*)
6. The soul is drawn from His wisdom as a son from his father's brain, and He and His wisdom are one (Tanya 2). Hence even the simplest Jew gives his life rather than deny the One, from the Chochmah above understanding (Tanya 18). (*stated*)
7. Every union of Abba and Imma draws from Arich, Atik and on up to Ein Sof. What is born from it is 'the revelation of the Infinite Light itself' (IH 20:16). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2, 18 and 35 (gloss); IH 20:16; Likkutei Torah, Nasso 6:23; Torah Ohr, Terumah 1:3 (seen, not quoted): Havayah is in Chochmah, and through Chochmah the Infinite reaches all nine lower sefiros.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 6:13 (seen in outline): 'the koach mah of Chochmah in concealment'. Not quoted.

## S030 · Upper and lower knowledge · דעת עליון ודעת תחתון

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F12 Knowing the unity · F04 Filling and surrounding **Search terms:** דעת עליון · דעת תחתון · מלמעלה למטה · מלמטה למעלה

> Upper and lower knowledge are the unity of God as known from two sides: from above, the world is nothing and He is the true Yesh; from below, the world is yesh and its source is 'nothing'.

## Derivation

1. 'A God of knowledges is Havayah': there are two knowledges. The higher joins Chochmah and Binah; the lower joins the attributes, so that they include one another and are not 'separated branches' (Likkutei Torah, Matot 7:6). (*stated*)
2. The Alter Rebbe places them by source. Higher knowledge is from Ein Sof itself, called encompassing, because it cannot be clothed in worlds or in the attributes. Lower knowledge is the filling light that spreads into the attributes and, through countless steps, becomes human knowledge (Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 23:4-5). (*stated*)
3. The lower lives from the higher. The limited knowledge would run out, but the encompassing light shines in it constantly (Shir HaShirim 23:8). (*stated*)
4. The Maharash (as recorded by the Rashab) and the Rebbe state them as two views. Lower knowledge sees below as yesh and above as ayin: creation from nothing. Higher knowledge sees below as ayin and above as the true Yesh (Toras Shmuel; Melukat, Matos-Masei 5713). (*stated*)
5. Each has its own bitul. The lower only nullifies the yesh (bitul ha-yesh). The higher nullifies existence (bitul bi-metzius), which is Moshe's level and the 'mah' in every Jew (Melukat 5713). (*stated*)
6. Both are true, because both are rooted in the Essence, which is both 'below without end' and exalted 'above without end'. Joining them is miracle revealed within nature (Melukat, 19 Kislev 5715). (*stated*)
7. That this pair is the same as IH 20's knowledge 'from above downward' and 'from below upward', and as the spine's G02, is our reading, though the Chabad vocabulary matches closely. (**reading**)
8. Outside Chabad the phrase mostly means Bilaam's 'knowing the knowledge of the Most High' (Likutei Moharan 36, 43; Sefat Emet, Balak). In the Sfas Emes it can also be the revelation that makes the disputing tribes one. The two-knowledge doctrine as such is Chabad's. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 23 (by source: encompassing and filling); Matot 7:6 (Chochmah-Binah and the attributes); Torah Ohr, Vayakhel 1:32 (seen: 'knowledge' means will and choosing); Torah Ohr, Vayera 2 (seen: God's foreknowledge belongs to the higher knowledge, which is why it does not compel choice).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** The term occurs twice in Shaar HaYichud and 8 times in Pirush HaMilos. Not read for this subject.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha (seen, not quoted): two knowledges, the lower clothed in Zeir Anpin's attributes and the higher, which Moshe draws down.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes by the Rashab): lower knowledge, below yesh and above ayin; higher, below ayin and above the true Yesh. 'To give those who love Me yesh' is the higher knowledge shining in the lower.
- **Rashab.** Recorded the Maharash's maamar above. Hemshech Ayin Beis uses the term 5 times; not read.
- **Rayatz.** Two occurrences each in Sefer HaMaamarim and Sefer HaSichos; not read.
- **The Rebbe.** Melukat, Matos-Masei 5713: the higher knowledge is 'sensory sight' that above is the true Yesh, and its bitul is of existence itself. Melukat, 19 Kislev 5715: both are true and rooted in the Essence; Havayah is the higher knowledge, Elokim the lower (S037).
- **Other schools.** Likutei Moharan and the Sefat Emet use the phrase mostly for Bilaam. The Sfas Emes (Vayigash 15:4): when higher knowledge is revealed, all the tribes, each with its own way, are nullified into one. That is a real unity-teaching, but of a different kind. The Besht (Mekor Mayim Chayim, Bereshit 154:4) uses 'higher knowledge' for the thought joined to a physical deed (S035).

## Thin or forced?

Not thin in Chabad, where the doctrine is explicitly about two views of the unity. Inside Chabad the frame shifts: the Alter Rebbe's Likkutei Torah defines the pair by source (encompassing and filling light), while the Maharash and the Rebbe define it by viewpoint (which side is yesh and which ayin). Calling that a disagreement rather than two framings is our reading. Outside Chabad the term is mostly Bilaam's verse, and the Sfas Emes passage's tie to the unity is our reading.

## S031 · The emotive attributes and Zeir Anpin · מדות עליונות / זעיר אנפין

**Faces:** F09 The unity of opposites · F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** זעיר אנפין · ז' מדות · שבע מדות · מדות עליונות

> The supernal attributes are the unity of God as kindness, judgment and mercy that include one another because each is one with Him.

## Derivation

1. His attributes are one with Him ('He and His causes are one'). The sign is that they include one another: kindness contains judgment, and judgment kindness (SH 6:3). (*stated*)
2. The Mitteler Rebbe carries this down to the vessels of Zeir Anpin, 'in the breath of the heart': 'He, His life and His vessels are one', and all of it is still Ein Sof (Shaar HaYichud 33:13-17). (*stated*)
3. The attributes do not describe His Essence. In it 'He is none of these attributes', and they are nullified (Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 8:8). Yet they are ascribed to Him Himself: He is the Merciful and the Kind (8:3). Both are held (G02). (*stated*)
4. What makes them one is the joiner: 'You bind them and unite them, and apart from You there is no unity above' (SH 6:3). The lower knowledge joins the attributes so they are not 'separated branches' (Likkutei Torah, Matot 7:6). In Tohu they stand alone and fall (Shaar HaYichud 20:17); in Tikkun each sefirah makes room for the other, even its opposite, and Tiferes unites kindness and judgment (the Rayatz). The Rayatz's sicha does not itself tie this to His unity; reading it so rests on SH 6:3. (*stated*)
5. The same holds in a person. Attributes attached to physical things are scattered branches (one does not fear what one loves). Attached to God, they are one, because ayin unites them all (Maggid 47). (*stated*)
6. R. Nachman: kingship is grasped only through the attributes, so the worlds were made in the vacated space, and 'the worlds are His attributes' (Likutei Moharan 49). He states that the worlds are the attributes, not that the attributes are one with Him; the tie to the unity is our reading. (**reading**)
7. One's own attributes are the analogy (Tanya 3; Hayom Yom 21 Sivan), which is G06. Likkutei Torah itself marks where it fails: the light clothed in the sefiros works no change in His Essence, 'I Havayah have not changed', and this 'is not like the soul, which itself is clothed in the body' (Shir HaShirim 8:3). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 6:3-4: the inclusion of the attributes shows their unity with Him, and His attributes are His names (S037). Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 8: nullified in the Essence yet ascribed to Him. Tanya 3: the soul's seven attributes as analogy.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 33:13-18: from the will hidden in the Essence down to the vessels of Zeir Anpin's heart, all is one and still Ein Sof. 20:15-21: in Tohu, will-driven kindness cannot join its opposite, judgment.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel (seen in search, not quoted): will is the inner side of the attributes and their life.
- **Rashab.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos, 12 Tammuz 5707: Tohu has many lights and few vessels, each sefirah unrelated to the others; Tikkun has few lights and many vessels, and each sefirah makes room even for its opposite.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 16:7-17:1 (S033, S035): the attributes above Malchus are beyond space and time; their union with Malchus reveals the Ein Sof above worlds in the world.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (47): ayin unites the attributes. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 49): the worlds are His attributes, made in a vacated space whose wording is spatial ('to the sides'), against the Alter Rebbe's rule that the tzimtzum is not literal (spine F03). Pillar 08 records the Piaseczno Rebbe mapping the worlds onto the soul; not re-verified.

## S032 · Kindness, judgment and the sweetening of judgments · חסד וגבורה / המתקת הדינים

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F09 The unity of opposites · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** המתקת הדינים · חסדים וגבורות · דינים · חסד וגבורה

> The sweetening of judgments is the unity of God as a judgment that, brought back to its root, turns out to be kindness.

## Derivation

1. Judgment (gevurah, the name Elokim) is what hides the life so that creatures can exist as things in themselves. That same contraction is the kindness by which the world is built: 'gevurah included in chesed' (SH 4:5-6, 6:2). (*stated*)
2. Why a judgment can be sweetened: judgments are separate only as they spread out. The Maggid: the five organs of speech are dark and separate; when one breath enters them, the life in them is simple unity, and the judgments are sweetened. (*stated*)
3. Hence the rule that judgments are sweetened only in their root (Tzava'at HaRivash 132; Keter Shem Tov 1:57, 1:107; Likutei Moharan 180). (*stated*)
4. The means differ from source to source. The Besht: find the root of kindness inside the judgment, or simply know that you are in smallness. Tzava'at HaRivash: love and joy raise anger to its source. Tanya 41: Torah and mitzvot reveal the supernal will, which unites the attributes, and the judgments are sweetened of themselves. Breslov: the pidyon, money given with hands laid on it (Likutei Moharan 180, 'the segulah of the pidyon'); midnight prayer, 'like a pidyon' (149); taking counsel from the sages (143). (*stated*)
5. The Rashab: the five gevuros of Atik received at Ne'ilah guard the kindness from outsiders and measure it out to each creature. Judgment serves kindness. That this is G03 (concealment for the sake of revelation) is our reading. (**reading**)
6. The Maggid states that when one breath enters the separate organs of speech their life is simple unity and the judgments are sweetened; SH 6 states that the concealing gevurah is itself chesed. That the judgment was never a second power, and that sweetening is the unity showing through, is our summary of the two. The Besht's 'knowing sweetens' runs parallel to his 'once one knows He is hiding there, it is no hiding' (Keter Shem Tov 1:55); that parallel is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 4-6: gevurah included in chesed, Elokim as the sheath of Havayah. Tanya 41, gloss: the gevuros are sweetened in the chassadim through the revealed supernal will, from 'the whiteness of Arich Anpin', the source of kindness.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 20:19-21 (read, not quoted): in Tohu, will-driven kindness will not join judgment, its opposite.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha (seen): 'mishpat is mercy', the middle line drawn from knowledge. Not quoted.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel uses 'sweetening of judgments' 4 times; not read.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 18-19: the five gevuros of Atik at Ne'ilah seal and apportion the kindness of the year.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5707 (S031): in Tikkun, kindness of Atzilus makes room for judgment and Tiferes unites them. His Sefer HaMaamarim uses 'sweetening of judgments' once; not read.
- **The Rebbe.** Melukat uses the phrase twice (seen: the sweetening by the 'hair of His head' is above the sweetening of judgments). Not quoted.
- **Other schools.** The Besht and Maggid circle is the heart of this subject (Keter Shem Tov, Tzava'at HaRivash, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov). Noam Elimelech (13 occurrences) and Likutei Moharan (10) make sweetening a work of the tzaddik and of practice (Breslov: the pidyon, midnight prayer, seeking counsel). There the organizing center is the act and the tzaddik, and the tie to the unity is our reading. Noam Elimelech (Bechukotai 2:6) keeps the Maggid's mechanism, the judgments raised to Chochmah where they 'become ayin', and distinguishes a tzaddik who sweetens judgments from one who draws pure mercy (Tetzaveh 2:4).

## S033 · Malchus: no king without a people · מלכות

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** מלכות · אין מלך בלא עם · רוממות המלך · מלכותך מלכות

> Malchus is the unity of God as a kingship that needs a people apart from it, and so makes space, time and the separate world while remaining one with His Essence.

## Derivation

1. The purpose of the world is His kingship revealed, and there is no king without a people. 'Am' is from 'omemus': things separate, foreign and distant from the king. Many sons or ministers do not make a king (SH 7:2). (*stated*)
2. Malchus, the name Adnut, is the attribute that makes the world be what it now is, a full yesh. If it withdrew, the world would return to its source (SH 7:3). Space and time come from Malchus alone (SH 7:4-5; Inyanah 16:7). (*stated*)
3. The separate yesh comes from Malchus of Atzilus as it becomes Atik of Beriah, and the multitude of creatures comes from the letters of speech that issue from Malchus (IH 20:13). (*stated*)
4. Yet Malchus is united with His Essence, so space and time are nullified before Him (SH 7:6). This is the lower unity of Baruch Shem, 'Your kingship is the kingship of all worlds' (SH 7; Shaar HaYichud 7:33-34). (*stated*)
5. Its power to create something from nothing is the Essence's, above any relation to worlds (Inyanah 17:2). 'Their end is embedded in their beginning': Keser is 'Keser Malchus' (IH 20:14-15). (*stated*)
6. The separateness is for the mitzvot. He is king over the separate ones when they keep His commandments (IH 20:18). The Maggid: first in His will was the delight He would have when Israel crown Him (Maggid 48). (*stated*)
7. Breslov starts from the same premise (Likutei Moharan 49: no king without a people, and therefore the tzimtzum and the worlds) and builds from it a practice of the heart: its infinite yearning must contract into attributes. The premise is shared and stated; that it is the same doctrine as SH 7 is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 7:2-6 (kingship, Adnut, space and time, united with the Essence); IH 20:13-18 (Malchus of Atzilus becomes Atik of Beriah; 'Keser Malchus'; king over the separate ones through mitzvot).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:33-34: Baruch Shem is Malchus of Ein Sof after the tzimtzum, down to Malchus of Asiyah, 'I am first and I am last'. 47-49 (seen): Malchus as the tenth of each of Zeir Anpin's ten sefiros.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha uses 'no king without a people' 14 times; not read. His definition of Malchus as Shechinah is under S034.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel uses the phrase 25 times; not read.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 18-24 (S028, S032): Malchus of Atzilus receives the kindness of Atik on Rosh HaShanah and the gevuros at Ne'ilah. Hemshech Ayin Beis uses the phrase 18 times; not read.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim (seen): Malchus as 'the word of the King is rule' includes ten, yet the world was made with ten utterances. Not quoted.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 16:7-17:2: Malchus is the Godliness related to the world, source of space and time; creation comes through Malchus, but its power is the Essence's; recognizing that belongs to the yechidah.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (48-49): the world was made for Israel's crowning Him; no king without a people. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 49): the same premise, applied to the heart. Likutei Moharan also calls Malchus 'poverty and submission' (219) and 'dalet', having nothing of its own (seen in search): a real difference in emphasis.

## S034 · The Shechinah · שכינה

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** שכינה · השראת השכינה · שכינתא · השראת שכינה

> The Shechinah is the unity of God as His dwelling in what He fills: revealed where a thing is nullified to Him, and present even in exile.

## Derivation

1. The Shechinah is named for 'dwelling and clothing itself in all worlds to give them life and keep them in being' (Tanya 41:16; Inyanah 29:5). (*stated*)
2. The revealed resting of the Shechinah is the Infinite Light revealed in a thing, and it rests only on what is wholly nullified to Him. Even a perfect tzaddik remains 'a thing' who loves, so it rests on him through the mitzvot, His will, in which there is no concealment (Tanya 35:4-6). (*stated*)
3. Its level changes. In the First Temple, Chochmah clothed only in Malchus of Atzilus and of Beriah, which united with the Infinite Light are called Shechinah, rested in the Holy of Holies 'not by way of the chain of worlds'. In the Second Temple, and with one who learns Torah now, it comes down through the chain of Malchus (Tanya 53:2-3). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe collects three definitions (Basi LeGani 5711). The Alter Rebbe: the Shechinah is the beginning of the revelation of Ein Sof. The Mitteler Rebbe: even the kav is 'Shechinah' relative to the Essence. The Tzemach Tzedek: Malchus of Atzilus is called Shechinah only as it becomes Atik for Beriah; within Atzilus it is one with the sefiros. 'Shechinah' is relative: whatever dwells in what is below it. (*stated*)
5. The Rayatz's maamar (Basi LeGani 5710): the main Shechinah was in the lowest realm, and seven tzaddikim brought it back down. The Rebbe, expounding it, adds the task: the seventh generation must complete drawing down not the Shechinah only but its essence, in the lowest realm (Basi LeGani 5711). The words of the task are the Rebbe's. (*stated*)
6. The Besht: the Shechinah includes all worlds and creatures, good and evil, and is the true unity; evil is a throne for the good (Keter Shem Tov 1:17). The Rebbe: the Shechinah went into exile with Israel, and even at the lowest point He dwells with us and gives the power to end the exile (Inyanah 29:5). (*stated*)
7. Radomsk: one who prays should set aside his own lack and set his heart on the pain of the Shechinah's exile. The pathos is shared, but that text centers on her suffering and the redemption, not on 'ein od'. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 35 (rests on what is nullified), 41 (the name), 53 (levels in the two Temples); cited by the Rebbe as 'the beginning of the revelation of Ein Sof'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Cited by the Rebbe (Basi LeGani 5711): the light of the kav, relative to the Essence of the Infinite Light, is called Shechinah. His own text was not read.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Cited by the Rebbe: Malchus of Atzilus is 'Shechinah' only as it becomes Atik of Beriah. His own text was not read.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel carries 'the main Shechinah was below' (grep hit); not read.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis carries 'the main Shechinah was below' (grep hit); not read.
- **Rayatz.** Basi LeGani 5710, the maamar the Rebbe expounds: the main Shechinah was below, it withdrew through sins, and seven tzaddikim brought it back down, Moshe to the earth.
- **The Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5711 (Melukat): the definitions, and the task 'the essence of the Shechinah, in the lowest realm'. Inyanah 29:5: the Shechinah is with us in exile.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:17): the Shechinah holds good and evil in one subject. Radomsk (Tiferet Shlomo): prayer for the Shechinah's exile over one's own need. Pillar 17 records the Sfas Emes ('the root of exile is redemption') and the Yismach Moshe; not re-verified. The Polish and Galician focus on the Shechinah's pain is a real difference from Chabad's focus on the dwelling.

## S035 · The union of the Holy One and His Shechinah · יחוד קודשא בריך הוא ושכינתיה

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** יחוד קוב"ה ושכינתיה · יחודים · זיווג · לשם יחוד

> The union of the Holy One and His Shechinah is the unity of God as the encompassing and the filling light made one by a person's act.

## Derivation

1. The formula said before Torah or a mitzvah means drawing His light onto the source of one's own soul and of all Israel's souls, the Shechinah, which gives the power of this speech or this act (Tanya 41:16-18). (*stated*)
2. The two joined are sovev and memale. The Holy One, source of Torah and mitzvot, is the encompassing light; the Shechinah, source of the divine soul, is the filling light. The upper union in Atzilus is made by the arousal from below (Tanya 41:21). IH 3:4 says it outright: the surrounding light drawn by mitzvot unites with the inner light, and that is 'the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah'. (*stated*)
3. It works because Torah and mitzvot are His will, in which the Infinite is clothed; the same union also sweetens the judgments (Tanya 41:19). (*stated*)
4. The Besht, as the Komarna works quote him, puts it into every act. Thought is Havayah and deed is Adnut, and joining deed to thought is the union 'face to face' (Mekor Mayim Chayim, Bereshit 154:3), even over a drink of water (154:5). 'All matters and deeds of this world are unions' (Keter Shem Tov 1:108). Of the Mekor Mayim Chayim lines, only 154:5 (and 154:4) name the Besht; 154:1 and 154:3 say 'I heard from my teacher', and the anthology (1938) places them under the Besht. (*stated*)
5. Its ground is the unity: 'if you do not believe that in every thing there is the being of God, and that every movement and word leaves an impression above…' (Mekor Mayim Chayim, Bereshit 154:5, naming the Besht; the same in 154:1, from 'my teacher'). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: the union of Malchus with Yesod is the Ein Sof above the worlds revealed in the Godliness clothed in the world (Inyanah 17:1). That this is the higher and lower unity joined (F02) is how we name it. (*stated*)
7. Radomsk turns the union toward the exile: Moshe's prayer for Miriam was for 'the last heh' of the Name, to make the union above, and then she would be healed of itself. The unity here serves the Shechinah's healing; the tie to 'ein od' is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 41:16-21 (the formula explained; sovev and memale united). IH 3:4: the surrounding light drawn by mitzvot unites with the inner light, and that is called the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 4 (seen): the many particular unions in each sefirah, which the contemplation of the higher unity must pass through. Not quoted.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha (seen): joining the higher will and the lower will, with higher knowledge as the joiner. Not quoted.
- **Maharash.** Not located for this subject.
- **Rashab.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 16:7-17:1: the union of Malchus and Yesod.
- **Other schools.** The Besht, through the Mekor Mayim Chayim anthology citing Komarna works (Chanoch unifying with every stitch) and Keter Shem Tov 1:108. The Komarna lines are late and partly anonymous: 154:4-5 name the Besht, 154:1-3 say only 'my teacher'. Pillar 17 records Noam Elimelech, R. Nosson and R. Tzadok on the same formula; not re-verified. Chabad reads the union as sovev and memale made one through mitzvot (a matter of contemplation and will); the Besht's line reads it as an act in every deed. This is a difference of emphasis, not of doctrine.

> וזהו פירוש ״לשם יחוד קודשאבריךהוא ושכינתיה בשם כל ישראל״

## S036 · The thirteen attributes of mercy · י"ג מדות הרחמים

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F11 Darkness turned to light · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** י"ג מדות · שלש עשרה מדות · מדות הרחמים · י"ג מכילין

> The thirteen attributes of mercy are the unity of God as a mercy that comes from where all is nothing before Him, measured so that the world, sins included, can receive it.

## Derivation

1. The thirteen attributes come from the supernal will, hinted in the thorn of the yud, above the letters of the Name. That is why they clean every blemish (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 8:1-2). (*stated*)
2. Why they come from so high: the great mercy stems from sovev, where everything is nothing before Him, even the upper worlds. Like a king whose height makes him pity everyone below him (Likkutei Torah, Eikev 1:2). (*stated*)
3. The thirteen are the contractions through which that boundless mercy can be received by the worlds (Likkutei Torah, Eikev 1:4). (*stated*)
4. They are humility: descending even to the place of sin, carrying iniquity and lifting it, so that darkness turns to light. They are rooted in the inner supernal delight (Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 4:37-38). (*stated*)
5. Their effect is a union: the lower heh returns to be united with yud-heh-vav (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 8:2). (*stated*)
6. The Maggid: love, contraction and the ayin that joins opposites, joined with the ten names, make the thirteen rules by which Torah is expounded, and in Elul the thirteen 'measures of mercy' open (Maggid 50). Through Berditchev and the Maharash he adds that the thirteen rules and the thirteen attributes are one. (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe: the Elul revelation of the thirteen, the king in the field, depends on the unity of Israel (Melukat, Elul 5746). That is the unity of the people, not the text's statement of His unity; linking the two (F06, one Father) is our reading. (**reading**)
8. Berditchev reads 'rachum' as 'gezeirah shavah': mercy is the rich man making himself equal with the poor, 'I am with him in distress'. That this is the unity coming down (F07, F11) is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Iggeret HaTeshuvah 8; Likkutei Torah, Eikev 1, Nitzavim 4, Re'eh 6 (seen: the thirteen shine in Elul so that the soul rises).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Two occurrences in Shaar HaYichud; not read.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** He arranged the Elul discourses in Likkutei Torah (the Rebbe, Elul 5746). Two occurrences in Derekh Mitzvotekha; not read.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5640 (notes): quotes the Maggid, 'Kel' is the first of the thirteen attributes and kal va-chomer the first of the thirteen rules, 'in truth one thing'. The root of the thirteen rules is in the thirteen attributes.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 24 (seen): in Elul the thirteen attributes shine, quoting the Besht via the Alter Rebbe, 'to arouse a reckoning'.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, 'Vaya'avor': the thirteen draw down 'the power that comes from Atika Kadisha' (Zohar), and the conduct of the world is by mercy.
- **The Rebbe.** Melukat, Elul 5746: the king in the field, face to face, depends on Israel's unity. Melukat, 13 Tammuz 5742 (seen): the thirteen rules reveal new will in Torah.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (50); Berditchev (Kedushat Levi, Ki Tisa 13-19); Me'or Einayim (seen: 'cling to His attributes' means cling to the Torah expounded by thirteen rules). The Bnei Yissaschar (54 occurrences) ties them to the calendar; not read. Hasidic schools agree here to an unusual degree, because the Maggid's teaching reached both Chabad and Berditchev.

## S037 · The divine names · שמות הקדושים / הוי' ואלקים

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** שם הוי' · הוי' ואלקים · שמות הקדושים · שם אדנ"י

> The divine names are the unity of God as one Being called by many names, which are His attributes and one with Him.

## Derivation

1. The names are the attributes: 'He and His name are one, for His attributes are His names' (SH 6:4). (*stated*)
2. Havayah means He brings everything into being from nothing, now and always (SH 4:2). Elokim is the name of contraction, equal to 'nature', the sheath that shields Havayah (SH 4:1, 6:1). (*stated*)
3. Therefore 'Havayah is Elokim'. The concealing name is itself kindness and conceals nothing before Him (SH 6:4-8). The Rashab makes this the commandment of unity (Eitz HaChaim p. 32). This is G14. (*stated*)
4. Adnut is the name of Malchus, which makes the world a full yesh (SH 7:3). (*stated*)
5. The names are vessels, and Havayah is the light within all of them. That is why Havayah joins every other name: 'Havayah Elokim', 'Havayah Tzevaos' (Torah Ohr, Bo 1:4-5). (*stated*)
6. A name does not touch its bearer's essence. The ten sefiros are 'the secret of the Holy Name', like a man's name, which he does not need for himself (Torah Ohr, Terumah 1:2). The four names belong only to the vessels (Shaar HaYichud 27:9-14). The Essence is beyond (F05). (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe: Havayah is the higher knowledge and Elokim the lower. Miracle comes from the one and nature from the other, and the aim is to join them (Melukat, 19 Kislev 5715). (*stated*)
8. The Besht: the Lord of the world hides in the letters of adversity, in a name that equals the union of Havayah and Adnut. Once one knows this, there is no hiding (Keter Shem Tov 1:55). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 4, 6, 7; Torah Ohr, Bo 1 and Terumah 1; Torah Ohr, Supplements, Vayechi (seen): Adnut interwoven with Havayah when Beriah is nullified. Pillar 08 also cites Torah Ohr: He becomes 'wise' by being named.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 27:9-14: the four names (AB, SaG, MaH, BaN) and 'the Holy Name' belong only to the vessels; from the side of the lights, the sefiros are 'without what'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim p. 32 (OCR): the commandment of unity is 'Havayah is Elokim'; Elokim does not truly conceal, so creatures are nullified in existence.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (S036): 'Havayah' is the attribute of mercy (Sifre), said twice, before and after sin.
- **The Rebbe.** Melukat, 19 Kislev 5715: Havayah and Elokim as higher and lower knowledge, miracle and nature.
- **Other schools.** The Besht: Keter Shem Tov 1:55, and the union of Havayah (thought) with Adnut (deed) in every act (S035). The Sfas Emes (seen): 'all the Torah is names of the Holy One'. The Radomsk Rebbe's 'last heh' (S035).

## S038 · Supernal will and delight · רצון ותענוג העליון

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** רצון העליון · תענוג העליון · שעשועי המלך · רצון ותענוג

> Supernal will and delight are the unity of God as a desire and a pleasure that are one with Him, which the mitzvot carry into the world without concealment.

## Derivation

1. The simple delight in the Essence before the tzimtzum is 'the King's delight in Himself', and the ten essential sefiros all come from it (Shaar HaYichud 24:2-3). (*stated*)
2. Yet the Essence is separate even from simple delight; it only shines through delight, as the soul follows its pleasure (Shaar HaYichud 24:5-6). This is F05. (*stated*)
3. Keser divides into delight (Atik) and will (Arich) (S028). The mitzvot are the supernal will, and the inside of will is delight (the Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ark 3). (*stated*)
4. The world comes from His will through many concealments, so that it can exist as a separate yesh. The mitzvot are His inner will without concealment, and what lives in them is one with it (Tanya 35:4). (*stated*)
5. The 613 mitzvot with the seven rabbinic ones equal 'Keser' (620): the supernal will clothed in His wisdom, united with the Infinite Light (Tanya 53:5). (*stated*)
6. The world was made only for His delight in the deeds of the mitzvot, which lies in a person's burning wish to please Him (Maggid 63), and in Israel crowning Him (Maggid 48). (*stated*)
7. He and His will are one, so a mitzvah has no purpose beyond itself; only the yechidah feels this simply (Inyanah 16:1-3). This is G09. (*stated*)
8. From the person's side, the Rashab: the love that consumes the animal soul wants no pleasure, physical or spiritual, but God alone, the source of all delights (Kuntres HaAvodah). Learning Torah, His wisdom and will, one is united with it (Kuntres Umaayan 1:3). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 35 and 53; Tanya 41, gloss (the mitzvot from the whiteness of Arich, the supernal will). Pillar 18 cites Torah Ohr: felt delight is twice contracted from an essential delight with no object.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 17 and 23-24: delight and will are inseparable ('if there is no delight there is no will'); the King's delight in Himself; the Essence separate even from delight.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ark 2-3: will is outer and delight inner; David called the statutes 'songs', praising the will's sameness, and was faulted. Also Ark 2: all the worlds are nullified relative to one detail of a mitzvah.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel (seen, not quoted): will is the inner side of the attributes and what keeps them alive.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 1 (the ladder of delights, with intellect highest among them, and union with His wisdom and will); Kuntres HaAvodah (OCR): a love that wants nothing but Him.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5692 (seen): intellect is rooted in delight, a hidden source.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 15:4-16:3: every mitzvah, even one with a reason, is the supernal will above reason; He and His will are one.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (48, 63): the world was made for His delight. Pillar 18 records the Chernobyler ('a constant delight is no delight'), the Apter, Berditchev and R. Tzadok on human delight; not re-verified. Pillar 18's fault line is real: the founders speak plainly of God receiving delight, while the Alter Rebbe (per that pillar) holds that He delights only in giving. It is kept here, not resolved.

> ומכיון שכל המצות, ענינם הוא – רצון הקב"ה, שהוא ית' ורצונו אחד, הרי כשם שבו ית' אין שייך לומר שהוא בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת ח"ו, כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת
> — The Rebbe: He and His will are one, so a mitzvah has no purpose beyond itself; returning a deposit is its own goal.

> **Kuntres HaAvodah, scan p. 27 ¶1** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaAvodah; line 145 · *OCR*)
> שלא תתלכש בשום דבר הנאה ותענוג גשמי או רוחני לאהבה ולא לחפויץ כלל שום דבר בעולם בלתי ה' לבדו מקור החיים של התענוגים
> — The Rashab: the love that consumes the animal soul wants no pleasure, physical or spiritual, but God alone, the source of all delights (OCR תתלכש = תתלבש, לחפויץ = לחפוץ).
