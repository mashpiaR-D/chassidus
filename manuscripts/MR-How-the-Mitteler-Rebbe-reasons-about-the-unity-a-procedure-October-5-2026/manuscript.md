## 1. Summary (one page)

The Mitteler Rebbe (R. Dovber, 1773-1827) does not mainly argue that G-d is one. He teaches how a person comes to KNOW it, and how to tell whether he has. His three books on this are one method seen from three sides. The Gate of Unity (Shaar HaYichud, 1820) calls itself "the tract on contemplation" and gives the method and the map. Imrei Binah (1821) applies it to the Shema, where faith is meant to become knowing. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (1813) is the field guide to what contemplation produces in a person, and to its counterfeits. **stated**

Five things are distinctive about how he reasons. (ours: the five; each is stated in the lines cited.)

1. **Knowing is built, not announced.** A truth about G-d is not "known" when it is understood at first glance. It must be stopped on (iyun), spread to every side (breadth), brought down in parables until a child could hold it (length), and only then reached at its narrow point (depth). Then da'as binds one's feeling to it, and tevunah carries it into a separate matter: prayer, the heart, the deed (Gate 10-112).

2. **Detail first, then gather.** Contemplating the unity "in general" feels close and is far: "from very far does G-d appear to him" (Gate 124). So he climbs: one detail of how a thing comes to be from nothing, then the level above it, world by world, until the details are gathered into the general, and then he asks what that general is itself a detail of (Gate 126-148, 321-323). At the end of the long chain, he makes "the short out of the long": in one moment the lowest physical thing is seen as the place where the Infinite rests "from the beginning of all" (Gate 329-332).

3. **Every contemplation has two faces, and they test each other.** What is made (the something) can be grasped, and grasping it brings joy. What makes it (the Godly nothing) cannot be grasped; it only flashes, and it brings bitul, the absence of feeling oneself. "As much joy, exactly that much bitul" (Gate 199). Joy without bitul means you grasped nothing; bitul without grasp is "an empty fantasy" (Gate 201-202). Weeping and joy are likewise one force in two equal lines (Gate 228).

4. **Contradictions are resolved by locating them, not by blurring them.** He states the hard question at full strength (if the world changes daily, does G-d change? Gate 428-443), then finds the hinge: the tzimtzum. No change in the Essence; change "from the side of the receivers only" (Gate 448). He tests his own parables and says where they break (Gate 738-741).

5. **The proof is in the person.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut gives the tests: what were you after, the feeling or Him? Did the feeling come late? Does it leave you feeling yourself afterwards? How do you take an insult? Does it reach the deed? "The deeper the essential arousal, the less he feels it" (Kuntres 556). Imrei Binah adds: the unity dwells only where a person does not feel himself at all (Imrei Binah 6), and faith in what is above knowing is meant to come INTO knowing; to make it faith alone is "the complete opposite of the truth" (Imrei Binah 4-5).

**What this gives an AI (ours).** Not a set of conclusions to paste, but a procedure with checks. Before saying "G-d is in everything", the guide should be able to (a) name the detail it is talking about and how that detail comes to be from nothing, (b) name the rung above it, (c) say what is grasped and what only flashes, (d) say where its parable breaks, and (e) say how the person could tell whether the thought has reached them. If it cannot, it is speaking "from afar".

## 2. Terms

*Definitions and relations are our plain wording of what the cited lines state. Misreadings are ours unless a line is quoted.*

## MR-T01 · Contemplation (התבוננות (hisbonenus))

**Definition.** A long, strong gaze at one idea about G-d, staying on it until you know it in all its parts, inside and out. **stated**

**Relations.** *is the opposite of* girsa, the quick first-glance understanding that flows on without stopping; *uses as its vessel* MR-T02 iyun (the stopping); *must pass through* MR-T03 breadth, length and depth; *is completed by* MR-T04 da'as (binding feeling to the idea) and MR-T05 tevunah (carrying it down); *always produces, if real* MR-T09 joy and bitul together.

**Common misreadings.** A pleasant spiritual mood, or thinking about G-d 'in general'. Visualizing or picturing G-d. Knowing the conclusion ('all is nothing before Him') and repeating it. The text says such a person can only tell the gist, 'not with a fixed eye'.

**Sources.** - «ההסתכלות החזקה בעמקות הענין ולעמוד עליו הרבה» — *the essence of contemplation is a strong gaze into the depth of the matter, standing on it a long time* · Gate line 10 - «הבנת הדבר בהשקפה ראשונה, שהולכת ונובעת במהירות» — *(girsa, quick study) is only understanding the thing at first glance, flowing quickly with no stopping* · Gate line 12 - «רק כלליות ענין אותו הדבר שראה בהעברת העין ולא בטביעת עין כלל» — *he can only tell another the general gist of what he saw with a passing eye, not with a fixed eye* · Gate line 14 - «היחוד אלקות בכל פרט ופרט בסדר ההשתלשלות» — *the unity of Godliness in every single detail of the chain of worlds* · Gate line 1

## MR-T02 · Staying on it (iyun) (עיון)

**Definition.** Holding the idea still, against the pull to move on quickly; it is the tool that lets you reach depth, not the depth itself. **stated**

**Relations.** *is* the opposite of speed; *is a vessel for* MR-T03 depth; *is often mistaken for* MR-T04 deepening da'as.

**Common misreadings.** Calling iyun 'deepening the knowing'. The text says the world calls it that, 'and it is not so'; iyun spreads out, da'as gathers in.

**Sources.** - «שהוא העיכוב היפך המהירות» — *(iyun) is the holding back, the opposite of speed* · Gate line 24 - «ונמצא בחינת העיון הזה הוא רק בחינת כלי לבחינת ההעמקה דבינה עצמה» — *so this iyun is only a vessel for the deepening of understanding itself* · Gate line 28

## MR-T03 · Breadth, length, depth (רוחב, אורך, עומק)

**Definition.** Three dimensions of any idea: breadth is explaining it in many details to every side; length is bringing it down through parables until a child could grasp it; depth is its one narrow point, the source the others flow from. **stated**

**Relations.** *depth is the source of* breadth and length; *the measure of depth sets* the measure of breadth and length; *depth is reached only after* breadth and length; *is treated in* DEPTH-ENGINE M17 (not repeated here).

**Common misreadings.** Depth as more and more examples (that is breadth). Depth as a long or lofty word (it is narrow and short).

**Sources.** - «הרוחב הוא בחינת הסבר המושכל לכל צד בפרטים הרבה כרוחב הנהר» — *breadth is explaining the idea to every side, in many details, like the breadth of a river* · Gate line 17 - «ואורך הוא עוצם ירידות ההשכלה להלביש את המושכל במשלים שונים עד להביאו בהשגות התינוק קטן» — *length is the great descent of the idea, clothing it in different parables until a small child can grasp it* · Gate line 18 - «והעומק הוא כעומק הנהר שמשם מתרחב ובעצמו אינו רחב כלל» — *depth is like the depth of a river: from it the river widens, while it itself is not wide at all* · Gate line 19 - «שלפי ערך העומק כך יהיה ערך הרוחב והאורך» — *as the measure of the depth, so will be the measure of the breadth and the length* · Gate line 44 - «ודווקא אחר האריכות והרוחב במשלים בעיון רב יבא לבחינת העומק» — *only after length and breadth, in parables with much looking, does one come to depth* · Gate line 101

## MR-T04 · Knowing, bound (da'as; deepening da'as) (דעת, העמקת הדעת)

**Definition.** Da'as is recognizing and feeling the idea, binding yourself to it; deepening da'as gathers all of your mind onto this one idea, so that it reaches the idea's root. **stated**

**Relations.** *is distinct from* MR-T02 iyun, which spreads out; da'as contracts and gathers; *brings* the understanding (binah) to its depth; *when weak, leaves a person* easily talked into the opposite, like a child; *is the meaning of* 'know today' (וידעת), applied to memale.

**Common misreadings.** Da'as as information. The text defines it as 'recognition and feeling of the idea, in binding'. Da'as as an emotional surge. Its sign is a tightening and gathering of the mind, not a spill of feeling.

**Sources.** - «אך הדעת הוא בחינת התקשרות הרגשתו במושג ביותר» — *da'as is the binding of one's feeling to the grasped idea, more and more* · Gate line 46 - «וסימן להעמקת הדעת הוא אשר נראה כענין צמצום וכיווץ כלי המוח» — *the sign of deepened da'as is something like a contraction and tightening of the mind's vessels* · Gate line 50 - «שהוא הקיבוץ והאסיפה מכל כח שכלו להתקשר רק במושכל זה» — *it is the gathering and collecting of all the power of his mind to bind itself to this one idea only* · Gate line 52 - «ובחינת הדעת הוא בחינת ההכרה והרגשה במושכל בהתקשרות» — *da'as is recognition and feeling of the idea, in binding* · Gate line 103 - «ועל זה נאמר וידעת» — *and of this it is said 'know (today)'* · Gate line 104 - «שאין בו כח המרגיש והתקשרות כלל לדבר זה שמבין או רוצה בו» — *(a child of light da'as) has no power of feeling and no binding at all to the thing he understands or wants* · Gate line 48 - «על כן יתפתה להיפוכו» — *so he is easily talked into its opposite* · Gate line 49

## MR-T05 · Carrying it down (tevunah) (תבונה)

**Definition.** The power that takes what you grasped and brings it into something separate from the grasp itself: a ruling, the heart, prayer, the details of the world. **stated**

**Relations.** *flows from* binah, the grasp itself; *is the mother of* love and fear in the heart; *when missing, leaves* a grasp that stays in the head, and the question 'what use is this?'.

**Common misreadings.** Thinking that understanding a teaching well in class is the same as being able to use it in prayer.

**Sources.** - «או לחיוב או לזכות או להתפעל בלב» — *(tevunah carries the grasp down) either to a ruling of guilty or innocent, or to be moved in the heart* · Gate line 69 - «ולא ידע מה לעשות בה» — *(without tevunah) he will not know what to do with it* · Gate line 110 - «עד שישאל מה לעשות בכל זה ולאיזה תועלת צריכים לזה» — *until he asks: what is to be done with all this, and what use do we have for it?* · Gate line 112

## MR-T06 · General and detailed contemplation (דרך כלל / דרך פרט)

**Definition.** General contemplation takes the whole idea at once (e.g. 'all is revealed from the hidden'); detailed contemplation takes each world, creature and level one at a time and climbs from each to the one above it. **stated**

**Relations.** *general alone produces* a feeling of closeness that is really distance; *detail produces* real closeness, and leads up to the general; *each needs the other* the detail needs the general and the general needs the detail; *for a beginner* the general comes first, then the detail little by little.

**Common misreadings.** Detail as a goal in itself, for learning's sake. The text: aim the detail at the Essence, 'to Him and not to His attributes'. General contemplation as 'higher' because it is wider.

**Sources.** - «כי בהתבוננות דרך כלל יוכל להטעות את עצמו עד שנדמה לו כי מאד קרוב אליו הדבר» — *in general contemplation he can fool himself until it seems to him the thing is very close to him* · Gate line 123 - «ובאמת מרחוק מאד יהו״ה נראה לו» — *and in truth, from very far does G-d appear to him* · Gate line 124 - «הרי יוקבע בנפשו בבחינת הקירוב ביותר» — *(each detail grasped in a detail) is fixed in his soul in the greatest closeness* · Gate line 125 - «וממנו יבוא להשגת דבר פרטי העליון הימנו בהדרגה» — *and from it he comes to grasp the detail above it, step by step* · Gate line 126 - «דפרט אצטריך לכלל וכלל אצטריך לפרט» — *the detail needs the general and the general needs the detail* · Gate line 150 - «הנה גם בעיונו בפרטיות יכוין הכל אל המחשבה הכללית דעצמות דוקא» — *even in his looking at details he directs everything to the general thought of the Essence* · Gate line 152 - «אליו ולא למדותיו» — *to Him and not to His attributes* · Gate line 153 - «ולא לכוון העיקר רק בכוונה של הפרט, כמו בשביל איזה לימוד לעצמו» — *and not to aim mainly at the detail's own intention, as if for some learning for himself* · Gate line 155 - «רק החדשים יותר טוב להם להעמיק דרך כלל תחלה» — *but for newcomers it is better first to go deep in the general way* · Gate line 160

## MR-T07 · Particular unity and general unity (יחוד פרטי / יחוד כללי)

**Definition.** A particular unity joins one level or trait to its source; a general unity joins a whole chain; and every general unity is itself a detail before something higher, up to the Essence, which is above even being a 'general'. **stated**

**Relations.** *each general is* a detail relative to the level above it; *the top of the ladder is* the Essence before the tzimtzum, not a general of anything.

**Common misreadings.** Stopping at a high level (Atzilus, sovev) as if it were G-d Himself.

**Sources.** - «ליחד יחוד כללי ולא יחוד פרטי» — *to unite a general unity and not a particular unity* · Gate line 139 - «שענין היחוד האלקי הוא בחינת עומק ההשגה בביטול היש לאין» — *the Godly unity is a depth of grasp in the nullifying of the something into the nothing* · Gate line 140 - «וגם זה פרט יחשב לגבי עצמות בחינת המלכות» — *and this too is counted a detail compared to the essence of Malchus* · Gate line 146 - «אינו רק כמו פרט אחד לגבי בחינת הכלל הכוללם יחד» — *(all this joining of head to end) is only like one detail compared to the general that includes them* · Gate line 321 - «וגם הוא פרט אחד יחשב לגבי עצמיות אור אין סוף שלפני הצמצום» — *and that too is counted one detail compared to the very Essence of the Infinite light before the tzimtzum* · Gate line 323 - «שבאמת אין הפרש בין איהו וחיוהי חד לאיהו וגרמוהי חד» — *in truth there is no difference between 'He and His life are one' and 'He and His causes are one'* · Gate line 1601

## MR-T08 · The something and the nothing (yesh and ayin) in every contemplation (ביטול היש לאין; האין האלקי המהווה)

**Definition.** Every contemplation of creation has two faces: the bounded 'something' and how it comes to be from nothing, which can be fully grasped; and the Godly 'nothing' that brings it into being, which is separate in value and cannot be grasped, only glimpsed like a flash. **stated**

**Relations.** *the something is grasped by* binah, called 'hearing'; it yields joy; *the nothing is glimpsed by* chochmah, called 'seeing'; it yields bitul; *the nothing is* necessarily implied by the grasp of the something, though its nature is hidden; *the Godly unity itself is* 'a depth of grasp in the nullifying of the something into the nothing'.

**Common misreadings.** The 'nothing' as emptiness or absence. It is the Godly source, 'nothing' only because no mind can hold it. That the made thing IS the maker (pantheism). The maker is 'separate in value'.

**Sources.** - «הא' בחינת ביטול היש לאין, שזהו השגת ערך הבעל גבול תחילה באיכות אופן התהוות מציאותו מאין» — *the first is the nullifying of the something into the nothing: grasping the bounded thing first, how its existence comes to be from nothing* · Gate line 166 - «והב' ענין בחינת האין האלה״י המהווה אותו ואיך שהוא נבדל בערך» — *the second is the Godly nothing that brings it into being, and how it is separate in value* · Gate line 168 - «וזהו הנעלם מעיני השכל בהשגה גמורה» — *and this is hidden from the eyes of the mind as a full grasp* · Gate line 169 - «רק מכל מקום יוברק כמו ברק בסקירה בעלמא במוחו» — *yet its truth flashes like lightning in his mind, as a mere glance* · Gate line 170 - «רק שממילא הוא מוכרח בהשגה» — *but it is necessarily implied in the grasp* · Gate line 186 - «לא יושג אף בדמיון שכל כי אם בהעלם לבד» — *it is not grasped even by an image of the mind, only in hiddenness* · Gate line 192 - «שענין היחוד האלקי הוא בחינת עומק ההשגה בביטול היש לאין» — *the Godly unity is a depth of grasp in the nullifying of the something into the nothing* · Gate line 140

## MR-T09 · Joy and bitul (שמחה וביטול)

**Definition.** The two responses that real contemplation must produce together: joy from what you grasp, and bitul (no feeling of self) from what you cannot grasp, in exactly equal measure. **stated**

**Relations.** *joy comes from* MR-T08 the grasped something; *bitul comes from* MR-T08 the ungrasped nothing; *they are* two equal lines of one force; *their balance is* the test of every contemplation (MR-M05).

**Common misreadings.** Bitul as sadness or self-erasing. The text defines it as 'only the absence of feeling oneself at all'. Joy as the whole goal of contemplation.

**Sources.** - «ועל כן ההתפעלות מזה אינו רק בחינת הביטול שהוא רק העדר הרגשת עצמו מכל וכל» — *so the arousal from this is only bitul, which is only the absence of feeling oneself at all* · Gate line 173 - «שההתפעלות מזה הוא השמחה במורגש וטוב לב» — *the arousal from this (the grasp) is felt joy and a good heart* · Gate line 175 - «כל שמשיג יותר, יותר טוב לו» — *the more he grasps, the better it is for him* · Gate line 176 - «כל שיעמיק יותר, יותר יפלא ממנו» — *the more he goes deep, the more wondrous (beyond him) it is* · Gate line 177 - «והסימן לזה הוא הנולד מזה, בחינת הביטול ביותר» — *and the sign of this is what is born of it: the greatest bitul* · Gate line 195 - «בהכרח שבאים כאחד דוקא» — *they necessarily come together* · Gate line 196 - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199 - «וזהו כלל גדול בכל ההתבוננות בפרט ובכלל» — *and this is a great rule in all contemplation, in detail and in general* · Gate line 200

## MR-T10 · Weeping and joy (בכיה וחדוה)

**Definition.** Joy in revealed Godliness and weeping over its hiding are one force split into two equal lines; now, in exile, the joy breaks through by way of its opposite. **stated**

**Relations.** *weeping measures* how much joy there was, and the reverse; *a higher weeping comes from* not being able to grasp what is beyond; *in Kuntres HaHitpa'alut* the cry and the song are 'one', only from the opposing side.

**Common misreadings.** Weeping in prayer as a sign that something is wrong. Joy and sadness as rivals where one must win.

**Sources.** - «גם שהן ב' הפכים הרי באים כאחד ממש מהתבוננות אחת דווקא» — *though they are two opposites, they come together from one contemplation* · Gate line 209 - «הרי כח אחד הוא ממש, רק שנחלק לב' קווין שוין ממש ושקולין» — *it is truly one force, only divided into two lines, exactly equal and weighed the same* · Gate line 228 - «שהוא הבכיה על העדר גלוי האור בנפשו מצד הסתר אלהו״ת» — *the weeping over the lack of revealed light in his soul, because Godliness is hidden* · Gate line 231 - «והנה יש בכיה למעלה מזו, והוא הבא מהעדר ההשגה מחמת עוצם הפלאת והבדלת הערך» — *there is a weeping higher than this: from not being able to grasp, because of how far beyond it is* · Gate line 240 - «אבל עכשיו כל זה בוקע ויוצא על ידי המנגד» — *but now all this breaks through and comes out by way of its opposite* · Gate line 263 - «ונמצא אחת היא רק שזה מצד המנגד» — *so it is one; only this comes from the opposing side* · Kuntres line 662

## MR-T11 · Filling and surrounding (memale and sovev) (ממלא כל עלמין / סובב כל עלמין)

**Definition.** Memale is G-d's power in each made thing, making it from nothing, which the mind can grasp in detail; sovev is the light that surrounds all worlds equally, which now reaches us mainly as faith and is meant to come into knowing. **stated**

**Relations.** *memale is* the Godly power of the maker in the made; the name Elokim; *sovev is* the level faith is about, above memale; *before sovev* darkness and light, spiritual and physical, are equal; *and even in memale* there is no change in Him; *sovev is drawn into knowing* on earth, through Torah and practical mitzvos.

**Common misreadings.** Sovev as G-d 'outside' the world and memale as G-d 'inside', in a spatial sense. Sovev as unknowable and therefore irrelevant.

## MR-T12 · Higher and lower unity (יחודא עילאה / יחודא תתאה)

## 3. Moves

*The move names and the numbered steps are ours; what each step does is stated in the lines cited.*

## MR-M01 · Stop and stay

**What it does.** Turns a quick understanding into contemplation by holding one idea still until all its parts are seen.

**When it is used.** At the start of every contemplation, and whenever a person 'knows' a teaching but can only give its gist.

**Steps.** 1. Pick one idea, small enough to hold (e.g. how this one thing comes to be from nothing). 2. Notice the first quick understanding and refuse to stop there. 3. Go through its inner and outer parts, one by one. 4. Check: could you explain it to someone in detail, not just 'the gist'?

**From the text** (stated). The text sets the glance (girsa) against the fixed look (iyun): the glancer can only tell 'the general gist of what he saw with a passing eye'. - «ההסתכלות החזקה בעמקות הענין ולעמוד עליו הרבה» — *the essence of contemplation is a strong gaze into the depth of the matter, standing on it a long time* · Gate line 10 - «הבנת הדבר בהשקפה ראשונה, שהולכת ונובעת במהירות» — *(girsa, quick study) is only understanding the thing at first glance, flowing quickly with no stopping* · Gate line 12 - «רק כלליות ענין אותו הדבר שראה בהעברת העין ולא בטביעת עין כלל» — *he can only tell another the general gist of what he saw with a passing eye, not with a fixed eye* · Gate line 14 - «שהוא העיכוב היפך המהירות» — *(iyun) is the holding back, the opposite of speed* · Gate line 24

**Done badly.** Memorizing the conclusion and calling it contemplation. Moving on as soon as it 'makes sense'.

## MR-M02 · Measure depth by what flows from it

**What it does.** Uses breadth and length to reach depth, then checks the depth by how much breadth and length it can feed. (Extends DEPTH-ENGINE M17.)

**When it is used.** When a person has many examples but no center, or a center with nothing under it.

**Steps.** 1. Breadth: give the idea to every side in several details. 2. Length: bring it down in parables until a child could hold it. 3. Gather (da'as): pull the whole mind onto the one point. The sign is a tightening, not a spreading. 4. Check: does this point generate the details, or is it just one more detail? 'As the measure of the depth, so the measure of breadth and length'.

**From the text** (stated). Depth comes 'only after length and breadth', and da'as gets there by gathering, 'the collecting of all the power of his mind to bind itself to this one idea only'. - «ודווקא אחר האריכות והרוחב במשלים בעיון רב יבא לבחינת העומק» — *only after length and breadth, in parables with much looking, does one come to depth* · Gate line 101 - «שלפי ערך העומק כך יהיה ערך הרוחב והאורך» — *as the measure of the depth, so will be the measure of the breadth and the length* · Gate line 44 - «וסימן להעמקת הדעת הוא אשר נראה כענין צמצום וכיווץ כלי המוח» — *the sign of deepened da'as is something like a contraction and tightening of the mind's vessels* · Gate line 50 - «שהוא הקיבוץ והאסיפה מכל כח שכלו להתקשר רק במושכל זה» — *it is the gathering and collecting of all the power of his mind to bind itself to this one idea only* · Gate line 52

**Done badly.** Endless breadth taken for depth; or a 'deep' slogan with no breadth or length under it.

## MR-M03 · Climb from detail to general, rung by rung

**What it does.** Builds the knowledge of unity from the lowest concrete detail up through the chain of worlds, then gathers all the details into the general.

**When it is used.** Whenever the unity is being contemplated, and especially when a person feels 'G-d is everywhere' vaguely and warmly but it changes nothing.

**Steps.** 1. Start with one low detail: how the spiritual source of the spheres and stars comes into being from nothing. 2. **Done badly.** Staying general (warm, vague, 'from afar', even 'false imaginings'); or collecting details as learning for oneself.

## MR-M04 · Split the contemplation into what is made and what makes

**What it does.** Separates the graspable face of the idea (how the something comes to be) from the ungraspable face (the Godly nothing that makes it), and lets each give its own response.

**When it is used.** Every contemplation of creation, memale, or any level of the worlds.

**Steps.** 1. Grasp the something: how this bounded thing comes to be from nothing, with explanations and parables, until it is certain in the mind. 2. From that grasp, see that the Godly nothing that makes it is necessarily implied, yet its nature is hidden; it only flashes. 3. Let the grasp give joy ('the more he grasps, the better'). 4. Let the flash give bitul ('the more he goes deep, the more wondrous it is'). 5. Hold both at once; they come 'from one contemplation'.

**From the text** (stated). Contemplating the spheres: how they come to be is grasped, and one is moved by the Creator's greatness; but the Godly nothing of Malchus of Asiyah that makes them 'is hidden from grasp, only necessarily implied'. - «הא' בחינת ביטול היש לאין, שזהו השגת ערך הבעל גבול תחילה באיכות אופן התהוות מציאותו מאין» — *the first is the nullifying of the something into the nothing: grasping the bounded thing first, how its existence comes to be from nothing* · Gate line 166 - «והב' ענין בחינת האין האלה״י המהווה אותו ואיך שהוא נבדל בערך» — *the second is the Godly nothing that brings it into being, and how it is separate in value* · Gate line 168 - «וזהו הנעלם מעיני השכל בהשגה גמורה» — *and this is hidden from the eyes of the mind as a full grasp* · Gate line 169 - «רק מכל מקום יוברק כמו ברק בסקירה בעלמא במוחו» — *yet its truth flashes like lightning in his mind, as a mere glance* · Gate line 170 - «שההתפעלות מזה הוא השמחה במורגש וטוב לב» — *the arousal from this (the grasp) is felt joy and a good heart* · Gate line 175 - «כל שמשיג יותר, יותר טוב לו» — *the more he grasps, the better it is for him* · Gate line 176 - «כל שיעמיק יותר, יותר יפלא ממנו» — *the more he goes deep, the more wondrous (beyond him) it is* · Gate line 177 - «רק שממילא הוא מוכרח בהשגה» — *but it is necessarily implied in the grasp* · Gate line 186

**Done badly.** Picturing the 'nothing'; or collapsing the two faces so that the made thing is called the maker.

## MR-M05 · Weigh the two lines

**What it does.** Tests a contemplation by whether its two opposite results are present and equal.

**When it is used.** At the end of any contemplation, and when a person reports a strong spiritual feeling.

**Steps.** 1. Ask: is there joy from what was grasped? Is there bitul before what was not? 2. Ask: are they equal? 'As much joy, exactly that much bitul.' 3. If only joy: by the text's rule, nothing was grasped. If only bitul: 'an empty fantasy'. 4. Apply the same scale to weeping and joy: one force in two equal lines. 5. Ask: does it survive a little opposition (distraction, feeling oneself)? If it is torn away at once, nothing was fixed.

**From the text** (stated). 'As much joy, exactly that much bitul ... and this is a great rule in all contemplation, in detail and in general.' - «ואינו רוצה לצאת ידי חובתו להטעות את עצמו» — *(for one who) does not want to fulfil his duty by fooling himself* · Gate line 164 - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199 - «וזהו כלל גדול בכל ההתבוננות בפרט ובכלל» — *and this is a great rule in all contemplation, in detail and in general* · Gate line 200 - «לא השיג כלום בוודאי בלי ספק» — *(one who thinks he grasps the coming-to-be but has no bitul before the Maker) has surely grasped nothing, without doubt* · Gate line 201 - «דמיון שווא הוא ואין לו בחינת ביטול כלל» — *(one who imagines bitul without grasping how the thing comes to be) it is an empty fantasy, and he has no bitul at all* · Gate line 202 - «ורבים מאד יטעו בעצמם» — *and very many deceive themselves* · Gate line 205 - «הרי זה סימן גמור שלא נקבע בנפשו כלום» — *this is a full sign that nothing at all was fixed in his soul* · Gate line 211 - «ובמעט דבר המנגד כמו בטרדא גדולה או בהרגשת עצמו ליש ביותר» — *at a little opposition, like great distraction or feeling himself a something, (it) is torn from his heart* · Gate line 212 - «הרי כח אחד הוא ממש, רק שנחלק לב' קווין שוין ממש ושקולין» — *it is truly one force, only divided into two lines, exactly equal and weighed the same* · Gate line 228

**Done badly.** Counting one strong feeling as success. Using the test to condemn instead of to correct.

## MR-M06 · Ask what it is for (tevunah)

**What it does.** Carries a grasp out of the head into something separate from it: prayer, the heart, the details of the world, the deed.

**When it is used.** When someone understands a teaching well and asks 'so what?', or cannot bring it into prayer.

**Steps.** 1. Take the grasp you have (e.g. memale: the Godly power in the made thing). 2. Bring it into a separate matter: this world, this creature, how it comes to be from nothing now. 3. Bring it into the heart (love and awe) and into prayer. 4. Bring it into a deed: 'light has hold only in a vessel'.

**From the text** (stated). Without tevunah 'he will not know what to do with it ... until he asks: what is to be done with all this, and what use do we have for it?' - «או לחיוב או לזכות או להתפעל בלב» — *(tevunah carries the grasp down) either to a ruling of guilty or innocent, or to be moved in the heart* · Gate line 69 - «ולא ידע מה לעשות בה» — *(without tevunah) he will not know what to do with it* · Gate line 110 - «עד שישאל מה לעשות בכל זה ולאיזה תועלת צריכים לזה» — *until he asks: what is to be done with all this, and what use do we have for it?* · Gate line 112 - «להיות ידוע שאין לאור אחיזה ותפיסה רק בכלי» — *for it is known that light has hold only in a vessel* · Kuntres line 769

**Done badly.** Leaving the grasp in the mind; or skipping the grasp and going straight to feeling.

## MR-M07 · Make the short out of the long (one moment)

**What it does.** After the long detailed chain, compresses it so that one look at a physical thing holds the whole chain and the Infinite in it.

**When it is used.** After MR-M03 has been done often enough to become habit; in prayer; when looking at the world.

**Steps.** 1. Learn the chain in detail and practise it 'all the days of his life', until it is habit. 2. Then make the short from the long: look at something physical, 'lift your eyes and see who created these'. 3. See that 'in this very thing rests the Infinite, from the beginning of all'. 4. Remember why: by count it is far from head to end; in truth it is close, with nothing hiding in between.

**Done badly.** Taking the moment without the chain (it becomes a slogan); or living in the chain and never arriving at the moment.

## MR-M08 · State the contradiction fully, then find the side it belongs to

**What it does.** Resolves an apparent contradiction about G-d by finding the hinge concept and placing the change on the receivers' side, not in Him.

**When it is used.** Any question of the form 'if X is true of G-d, how can Y happen?' (change, prayer, judgment and kindness, renewal).

**Steps.** 1. State the question at full strength from verses and experience. (If Malchus of Asiyah changes between judgment and kindness, and it is one chain, then the Essence changes; and we pray 'may it be Your will', a new will.) 2. Find the hinge: the tzimtzum, and the difference between light and cause-and-effect. 3. Place each side: no change in the Essence; after the tzimtzum, change 'from the side of the receivers only'. 4. Return it to practice: we say 'may it be Your will' about the change toward actual doing. 5. Add the duty: every person must know this and contemplate it.

**Done badly.** Softening the question; answering 'it is a mystery' (faith only); or letting the change into G-d.

## MR-M09 · Test the parable and say where it breaks

**What it does.** Gives a parable, checks what it implies against the thing it explains, admits where it fails, and moves to a better one.

**When it is used.** Every time a parable carries weight (tzimtzum, light, soul and body, speech).

**Steps.** 1. Give the parable (a teacher who says a law briefly still holds all its depth). 2. Ask what the parable implies and whether the thing explained implies the same. 3. Say where it fails: 'in truth this is not a true likeness', because the trace 'shows that everything withdrew'. 4. Move to a better parable (the craftsman's short mark that includes the whole building). 5. State the principle under all the parables: He can do everything, including limit.

**From the text** (stated). **Done badly.** Pushing one parable to its literal end (e.g. G-d left, like a teacher who forgot). Never saying where a parable breaks.

## MR-M10 · Every summit is a detail

**What it does.** When a general unity is reached, asks what it is a detail of, until the Essence, which is above being a general.

**When it is used.** When a contemplation settles on a high level (Atzilus, sovev, 'the Infinite light') as if it were G-d Himself.

**Steps.** 1. Name the general unity you reached (head joined to end of the chain). 2. Ask: compared to what is this one detail? (The line's unity is a detail before the surrounding light.) 3. Ask again: the surrounding light is a detail before the Essence before the tzimtzum. 4. Stop at the Essence, which is above being a general of details at all; aim 'to Him, not to His attributes'.

**From the text** (stated). '(All this joining of head to end) is only like one detail compared to the general ... and that too is counted one detail compared to the very Essence of the Infinite light before the tzimtzum.' - «אינו רק כמו פרט אחד לגבי בחינת הכלל הכוללם יחד» — *(all this joining of head to end) is only like one detail compared to the general that includes them* · Gate line 321 - «וגם הוא פרט אחד יחשב לגבי עצמיות אור אין סוף שלפני הצמצום» — *and that too is counted one detail compared to the very Essence of the Infinite light before the tzimtzum* · Gate line 323 - «וגם זה פרט יחשב לגבי עצמות בחינת המלכות» — *and this too is counted a detail compared to the essence of Malchus* · Gate line 146 - «אליו ולא למדותיו» — *to Him and not to His attributes* · Gate line 153

**Done badly.** Mistaking a level for G-d; or using 'it is only a detail' to dismiss the level instead of climbing through it.

## MR-M11 · Bring faith into knowing, and keep the hidden hidden

**What it does.** Refuses to park the unity in faith; brings what is above the mind into the mind through what can be grasped, while keeping a place for what is grasped only in hiddenness.

**When it is used.** When a person says 'that is above reason, I just believe it', or, the reverse, claims to grasp the Essence.

**Steps.** 1. Name what is held by faith (sovev, the Essence). 2. Say why it must not stay there: faith alone in the Shema is 'the complete opposite of the truth'. 3. Bring it down through the graspable: creation from nothing, which is 'like contemplating the Essence before emanation'. 4. Bring it down through deeds on earth: Torah and practical mitzvos. 5. Keep the limit: some is grasped only in hiddenness; 'His main glory is to be hidden'. Sovev is known 'from afar', and that is why its love is without limit.

**From the text** (stated). **Done badly.** 'I just believe' as a stopping place; or claiming to know the Essence directly.

## MR-M12 · Diagnose the arousal

**What it does.** Tests whether a feeling in prayer or study is Godly or self-made, by its source, its timing, its aftermath, and its fruit.

**When it is used.** When a person reports a spiritual high, a flood of feeling, or a sense of bitul.

**Steps.** 1. Source: were you after Him, or after being moved? (Wanting only the feeling is the lowest rung.) 2. Timing: did the feeling come from the idea itself, at once, or a moment later? Delay shows the Godliness was very hidden in it. 3. Self: was it felt without feeling yourself in it? 'The deeper, the less he feels it.' 4. Aftermath: afterwards, do you feel yourself 'from that very experience'? 5. Insult: how do you take a shaming remark? Real bitul is not moved, 'because he has nothing of his own'. 6. Fruit: is the soul drawn all day after closeness to G-d, in deed?

**From the text** (stated). 'He comes to feel himself, from that very experience' is the sign of the counterfeit; the sign of the real is that the soul 'is drawn all day after closeness to G-d, in deed'. - «שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» — *all he wants is only to be moved and to get vitality from it* · Kuntres line 226 - «מזה מורה על היות שבחינת אלהות שבהשגה זו מוסתר בה מאד» — *this shows that the Godliness in this grasp is very hidden in it* · Kuntres line 372 - «וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר» — *this is the great rule: the deeper the essential arousal, the less (he feels it)* · Kuntres line 556 - «בא לכלל הרגשת העצמיות מזה עצמו דוקא» — *he comes to feel himself, from that very experience* · Kuntres line 808 - «והמופת לזה כשיבטשוהו בבזיון» — *and the proof: when they shame him with an insult* · Kuntres line 813 - «מפני שאין לו מצד עצמו כלום» — *because he has nothing of his own* · Kuntres line 829 - «הוא בחינת ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהים במעשה דוקא» — *the soul is drawn all day after closeness to G-d, in deed* · Kuntres line 833 - «ורבים טועים בזה» — *and many err in this (taking natural heat for the fire of G-d)* · Imrei Binah line 517

**Done badly.** Banning arousal ('let no one forbid it'); crowning arousal ('making the arousal the main thing and not Godliness'); or chasing bitul as a prize.

## MR-M13 · Place the person on the ladder, and honor the rung

**What it does.** Names which response a person is on, shows the next step, and refuses to despise the lower rungs.

**When it is used.** When someone says 'I don't feel anything', or 'I feel so much'.

**Steps.** 1. Name the rung: assent from afar; good thought that reaches deed; moved heart; intention; simple will. 2. Honor it: assent from afar with real wanting is 'the true beginning of those who seek G-d'. 3. Show the next step: make the thought touch you 'like a matter that touches yourself'; toil in thought until the heart moves. 4. Remember the limit: all five are the natural soul ('serving G-d with the body'); the Godly soul's arousal is different in kind, and at its root the simple and the scholar are equal.

**From the text** (stated). Five rungs from 'hearing from afar' to simple will, then: 'all this is called serving G-d with his body ... for the soul in itself is a Godly power, literally'. - «שהוא ענין השמיעה מרחוק» — *which is 'hearing from afar'* · Kuntres line 233 - «זהו עיקר התחלת המבקשים ודורשים אלהים באמת ובתמים» — *this is the true beginning of those who seek G-d in truth and wholeness* · Kuntres line 241 - «וכך יובן בהתבוננות אלהות במחשבה כזאת שנדבק היטב בה כענין הנוגע לעצמו» — *so in contemplating Godliness with such a thought, which cleaves to it well, as a matter that touches himself* · Kuntres line 252 - «לייגע את עצמו במחשבה כל כך עד שיתפעל הלב דוקא» — *to toil in thought so much that the heart is moved* · Kuntres line 271 - «וכל זה נקרא עובד ה׳ בגופו ולא נקרא עובד ה׳ בנשמתו» — *all this is called serving G-d with his body, not serving G-d with his soul* · Kuntres line 312 - «כי בחינת הנשמה מצד עצמה הרי היא כח אלהי ממש» — *for the soul in itself is a Godly power, literally* · Kuntres line 313 - «על כן גם עמי הארץ ותלמידי חכמים שוין ממש בזה» — *so the unlearned and the scholars are exactly equal in this* · Kuntres line 623

**Done badly.** Telling everyone they must feel; or treating the heart's flare as the top.

## MR-M14 · The lowest holds the highest

**What it does.** When asked which is higher, checks whether the end holds the power of the beginning, and finds the advantage of the low place.

**When it is used.** Questions about the worth of the physical, ordinary life, bare obedience, the deed, or exile.

**Steps.** 1. Note the usual ranking (higher unity over lower; love over yoke; spirit over earth). 2. Apply the rule: the end is fixed in the beginning, so the lowest holds the power of the first. 3. Find the advantage: the earth creates from nothing; the lower unity has 'an extra advantage'; the higher awe depends on bare acceptance of the yoke. 4. Keep both: the higher is not denied; before the Essence 'the root of Atzilus and the root of Asiyah are exactly equal'.

**From the text** (stated). 'The earth is the end of all, with nothing below it, so the power of the beginning is found in it.' - «הוא מבחי' כחו של אא"ס שבתחלה כי הארץ סוף הכל שאין עוד למטה הימנה לכך נמצא בה כח התחלה» — *it is from the power of the Infinite at the beginning: the earth is the end of all, with nothing below it, so the power of the beginning is found in it* · Imrei Binah line 179 - «מעלה יתירה שיש ביח"ת דבשכמל"ו על יח"ע דפסוק ראשון» — *the extra advantage of the lower unity of Baruch Shem over the higher unity of the first verse* · Imrei Binah line 183 - «דחילו עילאה בטול במציאות ממש תלוי בדחילו תתאה שהוא רק קבלת עול» — *the higher awe, bitul of very existence, depends on the lower awe, which is only accepting the yoke* · Imrei Binah line 187 - «כי שרש האצילות ושרש העשיה שוין ממש לגבי עצמותו» — *the root of Atzilus and the root of Asiyah are exactly equal before His Essence* · Gate line 2653

**Done badly.** 'The low is fine as it is' with no refinement; or the reverse, despising the higher.

## 4. Guardrails

*Each 'never' is our wording; the reason is stated in the lines cited.*

## MR-G01 · Never conclude that the world or the made thing IS G-d.

In every contemplation the Godly nothing that makes a thing is 'separate in value' from it. What the text says of the lowest thing is that the Infinite 'rests' in it, not that it is the Infinite. - «והב' ענין בחינת האין האלה״י המהווה אותו ואיך שהוא נבדל בערך» — *the second is the Godly nothing that brings it into being, and how it is separate in value* · Gate line 168 - «לא יושג אף בדמיון שכל כי אם בהעלם לבד» — *it is not grasped even by an image of the mind, only in hiddenness* · Gate line 192 - «שבזה ממש שורה בבחינת אין סוף דוקא מבחינת תחלת הכל» — *that in this very thing rests the Infinite, from the beginning of all* · Gate line 332

## MR-G02 · Never take the tzimtzum literally (He left), and never erase it (nothing is hidden).

'Not that He withdrew and hid completely'; yet toward what is below it is 'a complete and true tzimtzum'. Say: hidden toward the receivers; nothing missing in Him. - «ולא שנסתלק ונתעלם לגמרי» — *and not that He withdrew and hid completely* · Gate line 662 - «כי הוא מורה שנסתלק משם הכל» — *for (the trace) shows that everything withdrew from there* · Gate line 739 - «זהו ודאי נקרא צמצום גמור ואמיתי» — *this is surely called a complete and true tzimtzum* · Gate line 792 - «ואחר הצמצום יש בחינת שינוי, וזהו מצד המקבלים לבד» — *after the tzimtzum there is change, and this is from the side of the receivers only* · Gate line 448

## MR-G03 · Never say that G-d changes, or that prayer changes Him.

'There is no change at all in G-d's Essence'; change is 'from the side of the receivers only'; 'may it be Your will' asks for change toward actual doing. - «על כן אין בעצמות אלהו״ת שום שינוי כלל» — *so there is no change at all in G-d's Essence* · Gate line 446 - «ואחר הצמצום יש בחינת שינוי, וזהו מצד המקבלים לבד» — *after the tzimtzum there is change, and this is from the side of the receivers only* · Gate line 448 - «אומרים יהי רצון מלפניך, בשינוי לענין עשיה בפועל ממש» — *we say 'may it be Your will' about a change toward actual doing (from judgment to kindness)* · Gate line 481

## MR-G04 · Never picture the Essence or the Godly 'nothing', even as an image in the mind.

It is 'not grasped even by an image of the mind, only in hiddenness'; it 'flashes like lightning'. - «לא יושג אף בדמיון שכל כי אם בהעלם לבד» — *it is not grasped even by an image of the mind, only in hiddenness* · Gate line 192 - «רק מכל מקום יוברק כמו ברק בסקירה בעלמא במוחו» — *yet its truth flashes like lightning in his mind, as a mere glance* · Gate line 170 - «וזהו הנעלם מעיני השכל בהשגה גמורה» — *and this is hidden from the eyes of the mind as a full grasp* · Gate line 169

## MR-G05 · Never treat a warm general sense of closeness as arrival.

General contemplation alone can fool a person: 'from very far does G-d appear to him', and it can end in 'false imaginings'. - «כי בהתבוננות דרך כלל יוכל להטעות את עצמו עד שנדמה לו כי מאד קרוב אליו הדבר» — *in general contemplation he can fool himself until it seems to him the thing is very close to him* · Gate line 123 - «ובאמת מרחוק מאד יהו״ה נראה לו» — *and in truth, from very far does G-d appear to him* · Gate line 124 - «עד שיוכל ליפול בדמיונות כוזבות» — *until he can fall into false imaginings* · Gate line 137

## MR-G06 · Never accept a one-sided result as success.

Joy without bitul: 'he has surely grasped nothing'. Bitul without grasp: 'an empty fantasy'. - «לא השיג כלום בוודאי בלי ספק» — *(one who thinks he grasps the coming-to-be but has no bitul before the Maker) has surely grasped nothing, without doubt* · Gate line 201 - «דמיון שווא הוא ואין לו בחינת ביטול כלל» — *(one who imagines bitul without grasping how the thing comes to be) it is an empty fantasy, and he has no bitul at all* · Gate line 202 - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199

## MR-G07 · Never seek the arousal or the bitul for its own sake.

Wanting only to be moved is the lowest rung; it is 'serving oneself'; making the arousal the main thing is the error; bitul sought because 'it was the only thing he lacked' is its opposite. - «שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» — *all he wants is only to be moved and to get vitality from it* · Kuntres line 226 - «וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו» — *and this is called serving oneself* · Kuntres line 167 - «עד שיעשה עיקר מן ההתפעלות ולא מן האלהות כלל» — *until he makes the arousal the main thing and not Godliness at all* · Kuntres line 548 - «יראה שלא יעשה יש ודבר מהתפעלות לבו ולא ירגישנו כלל» — *let him see that he does not make a something of his heart's arousal, and not feel it at all* · Kuntres line 741 - «שרצה להגיע למדריגות הביטול כי לא יחסר לו רק זה» — *he wanted to reach the levels of bitul because that was the only thing he lacked* · Kuntres line 814

## MR-G08 · Never forbid or shame real feeling, and never let the feeling cut the thinking short.

'Let no one err to forbid or reject this arousal'; 'only let him see that it does not cut short the depth and the length'. - «ולא יטעה הטועה לאסור ולפסול התפעלות זו חס ושלום» — *and let no one err to forbid or reject this arousal, G-d forbid* · Kuntres line 743 - «רק שיראה שלא תקצר לו העומק והאריכות» — *only let him see that it does not cut short the depth and the length* · Kuntres line 744 - «והקבלה בנפשו בהתפעלות אם במוח ומכל שכן בלב זה איסור גמור אצלו» — *(the opposite error) receiving it in the soul with arousal, in the mind and still more in the heart, is completely forbidden to him* · Kuntres line 126 - «הנה בזו הרגע שמתפעל בלב מתקצר כל המשך אריכות הענין האלהי שמחמתו נתפעל» — *at the moment the heart is moved, the whole length of the Godly matter that moved it is cut short* · Kuntres line 290

## MR-G09 · Never leave the unity as 'faith only', and never accept 'who am I?' as an exemption.

Faith-only in the Shema is 'the complete opposite of the truth'; 'who am I to come to deep contemplation?' is 'nothing but the counsel of the inclination'; 'even one who only knows how to read must toil'. - «והיא היפוך גמור מן האמת» — *(to make the Shema faith alone) is the complete opposite of the truth* · Imrei Binah line 5 - «ולא יטעו בו לומר שהאמונה בבחי' סובב למעלה מן הדעת לבד» — *and let them not err to say that faith in sovev is above knowing only* · Imrei Binah line 4 - «מי אנכי שאבא למעלות ומדרגות ההתבוננות העמוקות» — *who am I to come to the heights and levels of deep contemplation?* · Imrei Binah line 5 - «אין זה רק עצת היצר» — *this is nothing but the counsel of the inclination* · Imrei Binah line 5 - «גם אם אינו רק יודע ספר צריך לייגע עצמו בכל יכולתו» — *even one who only knows how to read must toil with all his ability* · Imrei Binah line 5

## MR-G10 · Never describe bitul as the self being erased or worthless.

Bitul is 'only the absence of feeling oneself'; the soul is 'a Godly power, literally'; the bond with the Essence is already there in every Jew. What is given up is the felt something (yeshus), which is the seed of evil even in prayer. - «ועל כן ההתפעלות מזה אינו רק בחינת הביטול שהוא רק העדר הרגשת עצמו מכל וכל» — *so the arousal from this is only bitul, which is only the absence of feeling oneself at all* · Gate line 173 - «כי בחינת הנשמה מצד עצמה הרי היא כח אלהי ממש» — *for the soul in itself is a Godly power, literally* · Kuntres line 313 - «שהדביקות הוא חיות והתקשרות הנפש בחיי החיים עצמות אור אין סוף ברוך הוא» — *cleaving is the life and bond of the soul with the Life of life, the Essence of the Infinite light* · Kuntres line 144 - «וישנו גם בכל אחד מישראל» — *and it is in every one of Israel* · Kuntres line 154 - «וכמו שמהתלהבות הבולט בהרגשת עצמו בתפלה, יכעס על המנגדו» — *as from a flaring excitement in prayer that feels itself, one gets angry at whoever opposes him* · Gate line 2703

## MR-G11 · Never pile up mystical intentions or secrets for one's own sake.

'G-d does not want these, only one intention: to bind his soul to the truth of His Essence'; the one who says 'my secret is mine' cannot taste it; details are not 'for some learning for himself'; 'His main glory is to be hidden'. - «כי לא חפץ ה' באלה כ"א בכונה אחת לבד לקשר נפשו אל אמתת עצמותו ית' דוקא» — *G-d does not want these, only one intention: to bind his soul to the truth of His Essence* · Imrei Binah line 6 - «ולהיפך מי שמגביה עצמו לומר רזי לי» — *and the opposite: one who lifts himself up to say 'my secret is mine'* · Imrei Binah line 6 - «ולא לכוון העיקר רק בכוונה של הפרט, כמו בשביל איזה לימוד לעצמו» — *and not to aim mainly at the detail's own intention, as if for some learning for himself* · Gate line 155 - «שזהו עיקר כבודו להיות בהעל' דווקא» — *for this is His main glory: to be hidden* · Imrei Binah line 302

## MR-G12 · Never leave a light without a vessel.

'Light has hold only in a vessel'; arousal that does not reach the deed 'is like a flame flying in the air'. - «להיות ידוע שאין לאור אחיזה ותפיסה רק בכלי» — *for it is known that light has hold only in a vessel* · Kuntres line 769 - «ואם יאיר אור התפעלות בלב הרי זה כשלהבת הפורח באויר שאין לו קיום» — *and if the light of arousal shines in the heart (without deed), it is like a flame flying in the air, which does not last* · Kuntres line 778 - «הוא בחינת ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהים במעשה דוקא» — *the soul is drawn all day after closeness to G-d, in deed* · Kuntres line 833

## 5. Worked examples

*The question, the order of steps and the answer are ours. Each step names the move and term it uses; the quoted lines are stated.*

## MR-E1 · "I understand all of it. I could teach it. I feel nothing. What's wrong?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **Place it on the ladder (MR-M13, MR-T18).** This is 'hearing from afar': true, valued, but 'the matter is far from him, and he asks what use will come of it'. With real wanting it is 'the true beginning of those who seek G-d'. - «עד שישאל השואל אחר שגמר וסבר, איזה תועלת בנפשו יבוא מזה» — *until the asker asks, after he has finished and understood: what use will come of this to my soul?* · Kuntres line 125 - «שהוא ענין השמיעה מרחוק» — *which is 'hearing from afar'* · Kuntres line 233 - «אבל הענין רחוק ממנו ושואל איזה תועלת בנפשו יבוא מזה» — *but the matter is far from him, and he asks what use will come of it to his soul* · Kuntres line 235 - «זהו עיקר התחלת המבקשים ודורשים אלהים באמת ובתמים» — *this is the true beginning of those who seek G-d in truth and wholeness* · Kuntres line 241 2. **Name what is missing (MR-T05, MR-M06).** Not more understanding but tevunah: the grasp stays in the head, 'until he asks: what is to be done with all this?' - «ולא ידע מה לעשות בה» — *(without tevunah) he will not know what to do with it* · Gate line 110 - «עד שישאל מה לעשות בכל זה ולאיזה תועלת צריכים לזה» — *until he asks: what is to be done with all this, and what use do we have for it?* · Gate line 112 3. **Make it touch you (MR-M04, MR-T04).** A cold thought 'does not touch him'; a good thought cleaves 'as a matter that touches himself'. Take one detail: how this thing in front of you is coming to be from nothing; then let da'as gather on it. - «שנקרא מחשבה קרה שאין זה נוגע ושייך אליו כלל וכלל» — *called 'cold thought', which does not touch him or belong to him at all* · Kuntres line 247 - «וכך יובן בהתבוננות אלהות במחשבה כזאת שנדבק היטב בה כענין הנוגע לעצמו» — *so in contemplating Godliness with such a thought, which cleaves to it well, as a matter that touches himself* · Kuntres line 252 - «הא' בחינת ביטול היש לאין, שזהו השגת ערך הבעל גבול תחילה באיכות אופן התהוות מציאותו מאין» — *the first is the nullifying of the something into the nothing: grasping the bounded thing first, how its existence comes to be from nothing* · Gate line 166 - «אך הדעת הוא בחינת התקשרות הרגשתו במושג ביותר» — *da'as is the binding of one's feeling to the grasped idea, more and more* · Gate line 46 4. **Expect toil, not a switch (MR-M13).** 'In serving G-d, arousal of the heart does not easily follow'; the mitzvah is 'to toil in thought so much that the heart is moved'. - «אבל בעבודת ה׳ לא בקל יומשך התפעלות בלב מהתפעלות המחשבה» — *but in serving G-d, arousal of the heart does not easily follow from arousal of the thought* · Kuntres line 274 - «לייגע את עצמו במחשבה כל כך עד שיתפעל הלב דוקא» — *to toil in thought so much that the heart is moved* · Kuntres line 271 5. **Test the result (MR-M05).** Look for joy at what you grasp and bitul at what you cannot, together. - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199

**Answer a guide could give** (161 words, ours).

> What you describe has a name in Chassidus: "hearing from afar." You see it is true, it matters to you, but it does not feel like it is about you. The Mitteler Rebbe does not scold this. He calls it "the true beginning" for anyone who really wants G-d. What is missing is usually not more knowledge. It is a step he calls tevunah: carrying what you know out of your head into something else. So take one small thing, like the cup in your hand. Ask how it is being made from nothing right now. Stay on it longer than feels needed. Let it be your business, the way good news about your own life is. Don't wait for a rush of feeling. He says that in serving G-d the heart does not follow the mind easily. It takes work. Watch for two quiet things together: gladness at what you understand, and a softening of yourself before what you can't.

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Keep it to one object and one minute; skip the terms; say 'it is okay to start from far'. - *Advanced:* Name the rung (hoda'ah vs machshava tova) and the missing faculty (tevunah); suggest the detailed climb from one detail up a world. - *Doubter:* Do not ask for feeling; ask only for the detail: how does a thing come to be from nothing? Let the argument do the work (see MR-E8).

## MR-E2 · "Is G-d really here, in my messy kitchen, in a traffic jam? It feels like He is in shul, not here."

**Reasoning.** 1. **Refuse the general answer (MR-G05).** 'G-d is everywhere' said in general can feel close and be 'from very far'. - «כי בהתבוננות דרך כלל יוכל להטעות את עצמו עד שנדמה לו כי מאד קרוב אליו הדבר» — *in general contemplation he can fool himself until it seems to him the thing is very close to him* · Gate line 123 - «ובאמת מרחוק מאד יהו״ה נראה לו» — *and in truth, from very far does G-d appear to him* · Gate line 124 2. **Climb from the detail (MR-M03).** Start low: how this physical thing comes to be from nothing, then the level that gives it life, world by world. - «וממנו יבוא להשגת דבר פרטי העליון הימנו בהדרגה» — *and from it he comes to grasp the detail above it, step by step* · Gate line 126 - «באיכות התהוות שרש שפע ברוחניות דגלגלים ומזלות» — *(first contemplate) how the spiritual source of the flow to the spheres and stars comes into being* · Gate line 127 - «כאשר יתחבר כל העיון מכל הפרטים אל הכלל, אז יוקבע יותר בנפש בחינת הכלל» — *when all the looking at all the details is joined to the general, then the general is fixed more firmly in the soul* · Gate line 132 3. **Join head and end (MR-M07, MR-T15).** After the climb, in one moment: 'in this very thing rests the Infinite, from the beginning of all'. By count far; in truth close, nothing hiding in between. - «עד שברגע א' ממש בהתבונן בנפשו בסוף הכל» — *until in one moment, when he contemplates the end of all* · Gate line 330 - «שבזה ממש שורה בבחינת אין סוף דוקא מבחינת תחלת הכל» — *that in this very thing rests the Infinite, from the beginning of all* · Gate line 332 - «רחוק מאד מאד מראש לסוף, אבל באמת קרוב מאד בלי הפסק והסתר כלל באמצע» — *by the count of the chain it is very, very far from head to end, but in truth it is very close, with no break and no hiding in between* · Gate line 337 4. **The lowest holds the highest (MR-M14).** The end holds the power of the beginning; before His Essence the root of the highest world and of ours are exactly equal. - «הוא מבחי' כחו של אא"ס שבתחלה כי הארץ סוף הכל שאין עוד למטה הימנה לכך נמצא בה כח התחלה» — *it is from the power of the Infinite at the beginning: the earth is the end of all, with nothing below it, so the power of the beginning is found in it* · Imrei Binah line 179 - «כי שרש האצילות ושרש העשיה שוין ממש לגבי עצמותו» — *the root of Atzilus and the root of Asiyah are exactly equal before His Essence* · Gate line 2653 5. **Guard (MR-G01).** The kitchen is not G-d; the Infinite rests in it. - «והב' ענין בחינת האין האלה״י המהווה אותו ואיך שהוא נבדל בערך» — *the second is the Godly nothing that brings it into being, and how it is separate in value* · Gate line 168

**Answer a guide could give** (158 words, ours).

> The Mitteler Rebbe would say yes, and then ask you not to settle for saying it. "G-d is everywhere," said in general, can feel close and still be far. So start low and specific. Take one thing on the counter. It exists because it is being brought into being from nothing, now. That power comes from a level above it, and that from a level above that, all the way up. Then he says to do something surprising. After the long climb, come back down in one moment and look at the plain physical thing. In it, he says, "rests the Infinite, from the beginning of all." The chain is long when you count it, but there is no gap in it. He even notes that Psalms praises G-d mostly through sky and earth, not the high worlds, because the end holds the beginning. Your kitchen is not G-d. But He is not less there than in shul.

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Use only the first and last step: one object, made now, and He rests in it. - *Advanced:* Walk the chain explicitly (spheres, ofanim, Malchus of Asiyah, Yetzirah...) and end with 'in one moment'. - *Doubter:* Present it as a claim about dependence: what keeps this here now? Keep MR-G01 explicit: this is not pantheism.

## MR-E3 · "If G-d never changes, what's the point of praying for something? Am I trying to change His mind?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **State the question fully (MR-M08).** The text asks it itself: if there is renewal every day and we pray for 'a new will', there seems to be change in the Essence. - «אם כן יש בחינת חידוש גם בעצמיות אור אין סוף» — *(question) if so, there is something new even in the very Essence of the Infinite light* · Gate line 438 - «ואיך אנו אומרים יהי רצון מלפניך, רצון חדש» — *and how do we say 'may it be Your will', a new will?* · Gate line 442 2. **Find the hinge (MR-T14).** 'The answer to all this is the matter of the tzimtzum.' - «והתירוץ לכל זה הוא ענין הצמצום» — *the answer to all this is the matter of the tzimtzum* · Gate line 444 3. **Place the change (MR-M08).** No change in the Essence; after the tzimtzum, change 'from the side of the receivers only'. - «על כן אין בעצמות אלהו״ת שום שינוי כלל» — *so there is no change at all in G-d's Essence* · Gate line 446 - «ואחר הצמצום יש בחינת שינוי, וזהו מצד המקבלים לבד» — *after the tzimtzum there is change, and this is from the side of the receivers only* · Gate line 448 4. **Return to prayer.** 'May it be Your will' asks for change toward actual doing, from judgment to kindness, in what flows down. - «אומרים יהי רצון מלפניך, בשינוי לענין עשיה בפועל ממש» — *we say 'may it be Your will' about a change toward actual doing (from judgment to kindness)* · Gate line 481 5. **Guard (MR-G03).** Never say prayer changes Him. - «על כן אין בעצמות אלהו״ת שום שינוי כלל» — *so there is no change at all in G-d's Essence* · Gate line 446

**Answer a guide could give** (147 words, ours).

> The Mitteler Rebbe asks your question himself, and sharply. We say G-d does not change. Yet the world changes every day, and in prayer we say "may it be Your will," as if asking for a new will. His answer is the tzimtzum: G-d's hiding of His light so that a limited world can receive it. Because what reaches the world comes through that hiding, "there is no change at all in G-d's Essence." Change is real, but it is "from the side of the receivers only." So prayer does not change His mind. It changes what reaches you. His will does not move, but how it comes down into actual events can turn from judgment to kindness. That is what "may it be Your will" asks for. You are not moving G-d. You are opening the place where His unchanging goodness comes down into your life.

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Use a simple image: the sun does not change; what changes is the window. Then say where the image breaks (MR-M09): the window here is also His. - *Advanced:* Add light versus cause-and-effect (Gate lines 445-446) and that every person must contemplate the tzimtzum (Gate line 451). - *Doubter:* Grant the question's full force first; then show the answer is a distinction (Essence vs receivers), not an escape into mystery.

## MR-E4 · "I had an incredible high in prayer this morning. How do I know it was real and not just me?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **Ask what you were after (MR-M12).** Wanting only to be moved is the lowest rung; Godly arousal comes from the Godliness, not from the wish to feel. - «שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» — *all he wants is only to be moved and to get vitality from it* · Kuntres line 226 - «שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר» — *that the arousal of their souls be only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the life of flesh* · Kuntres line 135 2. **Timing and self (MR-M12, MR-T19).** Did it come from the idea itself? Was it felt without feeling yourself? 'The deeper, the less he feels it.' - «מזה מורה על היות שבחינת אלהות שבהשגה זו מוסתר בה מאד» — *this shows that the Godliness in this grasp is very hidden in it* · Kuntres line 372 - «אף על פי שההתפעלות מורגשת מאד בלבו לא נקרא מורגש בעצמו» — *though the arousal is strongly felt in his heart, it is not called 'felt in itself'* · Kuntres line 555 - «וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר» — *this is the great rule: the deeper the essential arousal, the less (he feels it)* · Kuntres line 556 3. **Aftermath and insult (MR-M12).** Do you now feel yourself 'from that very experience'? How would an insult land? - «בא לכלל הרגשת העצמיות מזה עצמו דוקא» — *he comes to feel himself, from that very experience* · Kuntres line 808 - «והמופת לזה כשיבטשוהו בבזיון» — *and the proof: when they shame him with an insult* · Kuntres line 813 - «מפני שאין לו מצד עצמו כלום» — *because he has nothing of his own* · Kuntres line 829 4. **Weigh the lines (MR-M05).** Was there bitul in equal measure to the joy? - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199 - «לא השיג כלום בוודאי בלי ספק» — *(one who thinks he grasps the coming-to-be but has no bitul before the Maker) has surely grasped nothing, without doubt* · Gate line 201 5. **Watch the seed (MR-T21).** Excitement in prayer that feels itself can turn to anger at whoever crosses you. - «וכמו שמהתלהבות הבולט בהרגשת עצמו בתפלה, יכעס על המנגדו» — *as from a flaring excitement in prayer that feels itself, one gets angry at whoever opposes him* · Gate line 2703 6. **Fruit (MR-G12).** Does it reach the deed? 'Light has hold only in a vessel.' - «הוא בחינת ההמשכה של הנפש כל היום אחר קרבת אלהים במעשה דוקא» — *the soul is drawn all day after closeness to G-d, in deed* · Kuntres line 833 - «להיות ידוע שאין לאור אחיזה ותפיסה רק בכלי» — *for it is known that light has hold only in a vessel* · Kuntres line 769

**Answer a guide could give** (151 words, ours).

> Don't throw it away, and don't frame it. The Mitteler Rebbe warns against both: against forbidding real feeling, and against making the feeling the main thing instead of G-d. He gives a few honest checks. First, what were you after: G-d, or the high? Second, did it rise from what you were thinking about, or did you push it? Third, the strangest one: the deeper a feeling goes, the less you feel yourself in it, like a song that sings itself. Fourth, look at afterward. If you now feel like a bigger person "from that very experience," it was mostly you. If you feel lighter, and an insult would barely land, that is a good sign. Last, and most important: does it carry into what you do today? He says light holds only in a vessel. A high that does not reach your hands is "a flame flying in the air."

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Give only two checks: what were you after, and does it reach a deed today. - *Advanced:* Add the full Kuntres list (delay, aftermath, insult, constancy) and Gate line 2703 on self-feeling in prayer. - *Doubter:* Frame it as an honest test of one's own experience, which the text itself demands; the tradition is suspicious of highs too.

## MR-E5 · "I often cry when I pray. I feel far from G-d and sad. Is something wrong with me?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **One force, two lines (MR-T10).** Joy and weeping are 'one force, only divided into two lines, exactly equal'; the weeping is over the hiding of the light. - «הרי כח אחד הוא ממש, רק שנחלק לב' קווין שוין ממש ושקולין» — *it is truly one force, only divided into two lines, exactly equal and weighed the same* · Gate line 228 - «שהוא הבכיה על העדר גלוי האור בנפשו מצד הסתר אלהו״ת» — *the weeping over the lack of revealed light in his soul, because Godliness is hidden* · Gate line 231 2. **Now it comes through the opposite.** In this time, joy 'breaks through by way of its opposite', the weeping. - «אבל עכשיו כל זה בוקע ויוצא על ידי המנגד» — *but now all this breaks through and comes out by way of its opposite* · Gate line 263 3. **The cry counts (Kuntres).** The cry and the song are one arousal, 'only this comes from the opposing side'. - «ונמצא אחת היא רק שזה מצד המנגד» — *so it is one; only this comes from the opposing side* · Kuntres line 662 4. **Days differ (Toras Chaim).** Some mornings a person rises with a broken heart 'for no reason at all'; this is real humility; and 'the one who falls does not fall completely'. - «ולפעמים יקום בבוקר בלב נשבר ושפלות ועוצב והכנעה בלי טעם כלל» — *and sometimes he gets up in the morning with a broken heart, low and sad and humbled, for no reason at all* · Toras Chaim line 4257 - «וזהו השפלות והענוה להיות נפשו כעפר לכל באמת» — *this is lowliness and humility: that his soul be like dust to all, in truth* · Toras Chaim line 4257 - «שלא יפול הנופל ברוחניות וגשמיות לגמרי» — *(so) the one who falls, in spirit or body, does not fall completely* · Toras Chaim line 4257 5. **Higher weeping.** There is a weeping higher still, from not being able to grasp what is beyond. - «והנה יש בכיה למעלה מזו, והוא הבא מהעדר ההשגה מחמת עוצם הפלאת והבדלת הערך» — *there is a weeping higher than this: from not being able to grasp, because of how far beyond it is* · Gate line 240 6. **Guard (ours).** This is not a reading of clinical depression; if sadness is heavy and constant, a guide should also encourage real help.

**Answer a guide could give** (167 words, ours).

> Not necessarily. The Mitteler Rebbe teaches that joy in G-d and weeping over His hiddenness are one force split into two equal lines. You can only cry over a distance if something in you knows the closeness. He even says that now, in our time, the joy usually breaks through by way of its opposite, through the weeping. In another book he defends a simple man who cannot contemplate and only cries out in prayer. His cry and another's song, he says, are one arousal, just from the other side. He also writes that some mornings a person wakes with a broken heart for no reason at all. That is real humility. And the one who falls does not fall all the way. So your tears are not a failure. Let them be about Him, not only about you, and watch for the other line, the quiet gladness, to come. If the sadness is heavy and does not lift, please also get help from someone you trust.

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Lead with 'your tears are a kind of closeness'; skip the higher weeping. - *Advanced:* Add the three grades of weeping and the role of Tikkun Chatzos as preparation (Gate 6). - *Doubter:* Present it as a model of emotion (pleasure and pain measure each other), which the text itself argues from experience.

## MR-E6 · "If everything is 'nothing' before G-d, am I even real? And why does the world look so separate?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **Split the two faces (MR-M04, MR-T08).** The something and how it comes to be from nothing is real and graspable; the Godly nothing that makes it is hidden and only flashes. - «הא' בחינת ביטול היש לאין, שזהו השגת ערך הבעל גבול תחילה באיכות אופן התהוות מציאותו מאין» — *the first is the nullifying of the something into the nothing: grasping the bounded thing first, how its existence comes to be from nothing* · Gate line 166 - «והב' ענין בחינת האין האלה״י המהווה אותו ואיך שהוא נבדל בערך» — *the second is the Godly nothing that brings it into being, and how it is separate in value* · Gate line 168 - «רק מכל מקום יוברק כמו ברק בסקירה בעלמא במוחו» — *yet its truth flashes like lightning in his mind, as a mere glance* · Gate line 170 2. **Locate the separateness (MR-M08, MR-T14).** The hiding is the tzimtzum; change and separation are 'from the side of the receivers'. - «ואחר הצמצום יש בחינת שינוי, וזהו מצד המקבלים לבד» — *after the tzimtzum there is change, and this is from the side of the receivers only* · Gate line 448 3. **Test the parable (MR-M09).** Not 'He left', not 'nothing is hidden': the teacher parable breaks; the craftsman's mark holds the whole building. - «ולא שנסתלק ונתעלם לגמרי» — *and not that He withdrew and hid completely* · Gate line 662 - «אך הנה באמת אין זה דמיון אמיתי» — *but in truth this is not a true likeness* · Gate line 738 - «אם כן הדמיון האמיתי הוא מרושם וציון קו קצר שעושין האומנין» — *so the true likeness is the short mark and sign that craftsmen make, which includes the whole length of the building* · Gate line 741 4. **Close, not far (MR-T15).** By count far; in truth close, nothing hiding in between. - «רחוק מאד מאד מראש לסוף, אבל באמת קרוב מאד בלי הפסק והסתר כלל באמצע» — *by the count of the chain it is very, very far from head to end, but in truth it is very close, with no break and no hiding in between* · Gate line 337 5. **Guard the self (MR-G10).** Bitul is the absence of self-feeling, not of the self; the soul is a Godly power; the bond is already there. - «ועל כן ההתפעלות מזה אינו רק בחינת הביטול שהוא רק העדר הרגשת עצמו מכל וכל» — *so the arousal from this is only bitul, which is only the absence of feeling oneself at all* · Gate line 173 - «כי בחינת הנשמה מצד עצמה הרי היא כח אלהי ממש» — *for the soul in itself is a Godly power, literally* · Kuntres line 313 - «שהדביקות הוא חיות והתקשרות הנפש בחיי החיים עצמות אור אין סוף ברוך הוא» — *cleaving is the life and bond of the soul with the Life of life, the Essence of the Infinite light* · Kuntres line 144

**Answer a guide could give** (152 words, ours).

> You are real. The Mitteler Rebbe never says the world is nothing in the sense of fake. He splits every contemplation in two. One side is the "something": you, the world, and how you are being brought into being from nothing, right now. That side you can understand, and it brings joy. The other side is the Godly "nothing" that makes you. No mind can hold it; it only flashes. Before it, you feel small, and that is bitul, which he defines as not feeling yourself, not being erased. Why does the world look separate? Because G-d hides His light so that a limited world can exist and receive. The hiding is real from our side, not in Him. He says the chain looks very long when you count it, but in truth it is very close, with nothing hiding in between. And your soul, he says, is "a Godly power, literally."

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Answer only: 'you are real, and you are being made right now; that is how close He is'. - *Advanced:* Add the reshimu as the power to limit (Gate line 736) and the point-and-line resolution (Gate line 795). - *Doubter:* Show the structure: one claim (dependence) is argued, the other (what the source is) is admitted to be beyond grasp. Nothing is asked to be pictured.

## MR-E7 · "I'm a simple person with a job and kids. Deep contemplation isn't for people like me."

**Reasoning.** 1. **Name the thought (MR-G09).** 'Who am I to come to deep contemplation?' is called 'nothing but the counsel of the inclination'. - «מי אנכי שאבא למעלות ומדרגות ההתבוננות העמוקות» — *who am I to come to the heights and levels of deep contemplation?* · Imrei Binah line 5 - «אין זה רק עצת היצר» — *this is nothing but the counsel of the inclination* · Imrei Binah line 5 2. **Everyone, by ability.** 'Even one who only knows how to read must toil with all his ability.' - «גם אם אינו רק יודע ספר צריך לייגע עצמו בכל יכולתו» — *even one who only knows how to read must toil with all his ability* · Imrei Binah line 5 3. **Start general, and simple (MR-M03).** Beginners start with the general; contemplating creation from nothing is 'like contemplating the Essence'. - «רק החדשים יותר טוב להם להעמיק דרך כלל תחלה» — *but for newcomers it is better first to go deep in the general way* · Gate line 160 - «ה"ז כמו שמתבונן ממש בעצמות אא"ס שלפני האצילות בלי שינוי כלל» — *(contemplating creation from nothing) is like truly contemplating the Essence of the Infinite before emanation, without any change* · Imrei Binah line 191 4. **The low has an advantage (MR-M14).** Higher bitul depends on the plain acceptance of the yoke. - «דחילו עילאה בטול במציאות ממש תלוי בדחילו תתאה שהוא רק קבלת עול» — *the higher awe, bitul of very existence, depends on the lower awe, which is only accepting the yoke* · Imrei Binah line 187 5. **At the root, equal (MR-M13).** At the soul's essence 'the unlearned and the scholars are exactly equal'. - «על כן גם עמי הארץ ותלמידי חכמים שוין ממש בזה» — *so the unlearned and the scholars are exactly equal in this* · Kuntres line 623

**Answer a guide could give** (151 words, ours).

> The Mitteler Rebbe heard this a lot, and he did not accept it. He calls the thought "who am I to do deep contemplation?" a trick of the yetzer hara, dressed up as humility. Everyone, he says, even someone who can barely read, is meant to work at this as much as he can. But he also makes it reachable. Beginners should start with the general picture, not the fine details. And the simplest contemplation, how the world around you is being made from nothing, is, he says, "like contemplating the Essence" itself. Take two minutes before you pray: look at your kids' shoes by the door and remember they exist right now only because G-d is making them. He adds that plain obedience, simply accepting G-d's will, is the ground the higher things stand on. And at the soul's deepest point, the simple person and the scholar are "exactly equal."

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* This answer is already for a beginner; give one two-minute practice. - *Advanced:* Not applicable as asked; for an advanced person who feels 'too busy', use Kuntres 640 (trade blocks the toil) and suggest a fixed time. - *Doubter:* Make it an experiment: two minutes a day on one detail, then judge by the tests in MR-M05.

## MR-E8 · "I'm not sure I believe any of this. Isn't it all just faith in the end?"

**Reasoning.** 1. **Do not retreat to faith (MR-M11, MR-G09).** The text itself says making the Shema faith alone is 'the complete opposite of the truth'; faith is meant to come into knowing. - «והיא היפוך גמור מן האמת» — *(to make the Shema faith alone) is the complete opposite of the truth* · Imrei Binah line 5 - «שיבא כח האמונה זו שלמעלה מן הדעת לידי גלוי בדעת והבנה» — *that this power of faith above knowing should come into revelation in knowing and understanding* · Imrei Binah line 4 2. **Start with what can be grasped (MR-M04).** How the bounded thing comes to be from nothing is made close to the mind by explanations and parables 'until it is certain'. - «הא' בחינת ביטול היש לאין, שזהו השגת ערך הבעל גבול תחילה באיכות אופן התהוות מציאותו מאין» — *the first is the nullifying of the something into the nothing: grasping the bounded thing first, how its existence comes to be from nothing* · Gate line 166 3. **The source is implied, not pictured.** The Godly nothing is 'necessarily implied in the grasp', but not grasped; it is not grasped 'even by an image of the mind'. - «רק שממילא הוא מוכרח בהשגה» — *but it is necessarily implied in the grasp* · Gate line 186 - «לא יושג אף בדמיון שכל כי אם בהעלם לבד» — *it is not grasped even by an image of the mind, only in hiddenness* · Gate line 192 4. **Faith as recognition (Toras Chaim).** Underneath reasons there is a recognition like a child's for his father; 'every essence does not change, and every combination of reason changes'. - «אלא רק הכרה והרגשה עצמי'» — *only a recognition and feeling from the essence* · Toras Chaim line 2505 - «וכל עצם אינו משתנה וכל הרכבות שכל וטעם משתנה וחולף» — *every essence does not change, and every combination of reason and logic changes and passes* · Toras Chaim line 2505 5. **Test, don't assume (MR-M05).** Judge by what it does in you: joy and bitul together. - «ולפי ערך השמחה כך ערך הביטול ממש» — *as much joy, exactly that much bitul* · Gate line 199

**Answer a guide could give** (159 words, ours).

> Not in the way you might think. The Mitteler Rebbe is unusual here. He says that treating the oneness of G-d as "just faith" is "the complete opposite of the truth." The whole point of the Shema, he says, is for faith to come into knowing. So he starts where your mind can go. Look at anything limited, a stone, a thought, yourself, and ask how it comes to exist and keep existing. He works this through with reasons and examples until it is solid. Then he says the source that makes it is "necessarily implied" by that understanding, even though no mind can picture what it is. He is honest that some of it stays beyond reach. He also says that under all reasons there is something like a child's recognition of his father, which reasons come and go around. You do not have to start there. Start with the question, and see what it does to you.

**How it shifts.** - *Beginner:* Not the main audience; if a beginner asks, shorten to 'start by asking how anything keeps existing'. - *Advanced:* Discuss the Tanya's 'only by faith' (Shaar HaYichud 7) against the Mitteler Rebbe's 'must know the tzimtzum' (see GATE-OF-UNITY.md, 'Know it, or believe it?'). - *Doubter:* This answer is for the doubter; do not push the child-recognition point as proof, only as a description.

## 6. Tensions kept (not smoothed)

- **'From afar' is a fault and a gift.** In the Gate, general contemplation is faulted because 'from very far does G-d appear to him' (Gate 124). In Toras Chaim, sovev is known 'only from afar', and that is exactly why the love it gives is without limit (Toras Chaim 4616). Same words, two uses: far because the method is lazy, or far because the object is beyond. **stated** (both); the contrast is **ours**.
- **Joy equal to bitul, or bitul above all?** The Gate asks for them in exactly equal measure (Gate 199). Imrei Binah ranks the bitul of awe above the most wondrous love (Imrei Binah 424). See `IMREI-BINAH.md`. **stated** - «שבטול הנפש דמצד היראה יותר מגיע בעצמות מקירוב הנפש באהבה ודביקות היותר נפלאה» — *the soul's bitul from awe reaches the Essence more than the soul's nearness in the most wondrous love and cleaving* · Imrei Binah line 424
- **Heart or mind?** Kuntres calls heart arousal the main revelation of Godliness, yet ranks intention in the mind above the heart's flare, which 'cuts short' the length (Kuntres 290). See `KUNTRES-HAHITPAALUT.md`. **stated** - «הנה בזו הרגע שמתפעל בלב מתקצר כל המשך אריכות הענין האלהי שמחמתו נתפעל» — *at the moment the heart is moved, the whole length of the Godly matter that moved it is cut short* · Kuntres line 290
- **Do traits come from contemplation?** Toras Chaim: the size of the traits grows with the mind, and the mind can lead them against their nature, but the natural traits exist from birth, before any contemplation (Toras Chaim 81). So contemplation shapes and rules feeling; it does not create it from nothing. This sharpens MR-T05 and MR-M13. **stated** - «כי הרי המדות הטבעיים שבלב כבר ישנם במציאות' ומהותם» — *for the natural traits of the heart already exist in their being and nature* · Toras Chaim line 81 - «עד שהמוח שליט על המדות הטבעיים להנהיגם היפך טבעם» — *until the mind rules over the natural traits, to lead them against their nature* · Toras Chaim line 81
- **Detail for everyone, or general for beginners?** Detail is required (Gate 161), yet newcomers should start general (Gate 160), and Kuntres says those absorbed in trade cannot bear the toil 'in general and still more in detail' (Kuntres 640). **stated** - «להעמיק דעתו בממלא וסובב גם דרך כלל וכל שכן דרך פרט» — *(those absorbed in trade cannot bear the toil) to deepen their knowing in memale and sovev, in general and still more in detail* · Kuntres line 640
- **Contemplating hidden things.** The Gate asks for detail even before the tzimtzum (Gate 489); Imrei Binah keeps a hidden core, 'His main glory is to be hidden' (Imrei Binah 302). **stated** - «גם שם דרך פרט דוקא» — *there too, in detail* · Gate line 489
