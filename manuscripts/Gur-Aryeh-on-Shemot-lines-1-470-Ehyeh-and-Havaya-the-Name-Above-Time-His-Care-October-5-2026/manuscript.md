This is the first half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Shemot: the slavery, the burning bush, the plagues, Pesach, the sea, the manna, Amalek, and Yitro's coming. Most lines are close reading of Rashi. But some of the Maharal's clearest words on the Names of G-d are here. He explains what each Name tells us, and what no Name can tell.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **Two Names: present to all, and set apart from all.** At the bush G-d says "Ehyeh" (I will be). The Maharal says: "כי הוא יתברך נקרא לפי פעולותיו" (65): "He is named by His acts." Ehyeh means "שהוא נמצא אל כל הנמצאים אשר הם צריכים לו יתברך" (65): He "is present to all beings who need Him." But the four-letter Name, Havaya (the Name of being), shows "שהוא נבדל מכל הנמצאות ולא נתלה בשום נמצא" (65): He "is set apart from all beings and hangs on no being." And "וזולתו אפס" (65): "apart from Him there is nothing."
- **The Name is His very self, in all times.** "כי זה השם מורה על אמתת עצמו יתברך שמו" (117): "this Name shows the truth of His very self." It holds past, present and future, "והוא אחד בהם לא ישתנה דעתו" (117): "and He is one in them; His mind does not change."
- **To know the Name is to know His essence.** Moshe asked "what is His Name?" This means "איך מורה שמו על עצמותו" (133): "how does His Name show His essence?" The fathers walked in simple faith and did not ask.
- **The mitzvah is above time.** Why must the matza not sour? "והענינים השכליים פעולתם שלא בזמן, לפי שהם אינם תחת הזמן" (268): "things of the mind act outside of time." So "כי דבר ה' הוא מוטל עליו לעשות בלי זמן" (268): "the word of G-d is laid on a person to do without time." And each generation leaves Egypt, since "הסבה לאותן שיצאו הוא הסבה לכל דור ודור, ואין כאן חלוק" (312): "the cause for those who went out is the cause for every generation."
- **His oneness is His kingship.** "כי המלכות בעצמו הוא האחדות, ואין אתו אחר" (439): "kingship itself is oneness, and there is no other with Him." He is whole in Himself, "אך אין השם בשלימות נמצא אצלינו" (439): "only the Name is not found whole with us" while the world still has twoness.
- **The mind's delight is the Shechina's radiance.** "כך עין השכל גם כן נהנה בראות הזיו וההוד מן השכינה, שזהו הנאת השכל" (465): "so the eye of the mind delights in seeing the radiance and splendor of the Shechina; this is the mind's delight."

## 2.1 Named by His acts: Ehyeh

The Maharal's rule: "כי הוא יתברך נקרא לפי פעולותיו" (65): "He is named by His acts." "Ehyeh" (I will be) begins with the letter alef, the letter of one who speaks of himself. And "כי כל מדבר הוא מדבר אל זולתו ונגלה אליו" (65): "every speaker speaks to another and is shown to him." So Ehyeh is the Name of turning toward: "שהוויתו שהוא נמצא אל כל הנמצאים אשר הם צריכים לו יתברך" (65): "His being is that He is present to all beings who need Him." "Ehyeh asher Ehyeh" means He is present in this trouble and will be present in the next.

## 2.2 Set apart: Havaya

The four-letter Name, Havaya (the Name of being), is the other side. "כן שם הויה מורה שהוא נבדל מכל הנמצאות ולא נתלה בשום נמצא" (65): "so the Name Havaya shows that He is set apart from all beings and hangs on no being." "ושם בן ארבע אותיות נאמר על שהוא ההויה שאין לו התלות בזולתו, וזולתו אפס" (65): "the four-letter Name is said because He is the Being that does not depend on anything else, and apart from Him there is nothing." "ולפיכך נקרא בשם הויה שהוא ההוה בלבד בעצמו" (65): "He alone is the One who is, in Himself."

So the two Names hold two truths at once. He is present to all who need Him (Ehyeh). And He needs nothing, and nothing is beside Him (Havaya).

## 2.3 One in all times

"לומר כי הווייתו בכל הזמנים שהם עבר הוה עתיד, והוא אחד בהם לא ישתנה דעתו" (117): "His being is in all times, past, present and future, and He is one in them; His mind does not change." "כי זה השם מורה על אמתת עצמו יתברך שמו" (117): "this Name shows the truth of His very self."

## 2.4 To know the Name

The fathers knew His Name only as a call. Moshe wanted more. "כי השם יורה על אמתתו של הקב"ה" (133): "the Name shows the truth of the Holy One." "כי שאלת 'מה שמו' פירושו איך מורה שמו על עצמותו" (133): "the question 'what is His Name' means: how does His Name show His essence?" "כי זה השם ידיעת עצמותו יתברך" (133): "this Name is the knowing of His essence." The Maharal does not say anyone grasps the essence. He says this Name points to it, as no other Name does.

## 2.5 "With him I am in trouble"

G-d said to Moshe, "I am with him in trouble." The Maharal is careful: "אין הפירוש כלל שהוא בצרה, חס ושלום לומר כך" (58): "it does not mean at all that He is in trouble, G-d forbid." Rather, "שהוא יתברך אסור בזיקים בישראל, והוא עמהם תמיד" (58): "He is bound in chains with Israel, and He is with them always." And "אכן ראוי לך לדעת כי כל ענין זה מצד העולם הזה" (58): "you should know that this whole matter is from the side of this world." In Himself nothing changes. In the world, His kingship is bound up with His people.

## 2.6 Three things the plagues taught

The plagues came in three stages (185). First, that He exists. Second, that His care reaches each detail. The Egyptians "לא נתנו להשם יתברך שהשגחתו פרטית בתחתונים, כי זה מעלה יתירה ורוממות גדול להשגיח בפרטים" (185): "they did not grant that His care reaches each detail below, for it is a great height to watch over details." "ובשביל כך נראה שהשגחתו פרטית לא כללית" (185): "so it was seen that His care is for each detail, not only for the whole." Third, "אבל שאין כמוהו והוא יחיד על הכל לא היו יודעים" (185): "that there is none like Him and He is single over all, they did not know."

## 2.7 Oneness is kingship

"כי המלכות בעצמו הוא האחדות, ואין אתו אחר" (439): "kingship itself is oneness, and there is no other with Him." "כי אחדותו הוא מלכותו" (439): "His oneness is His kingship." So the Shema is taking on His kingship. And the Maharal guards the words: "ואין הפירוש חס ושלום שאין השם שלם בעצמו, חס ושלום לומר כך, כי שלימתו בעצמו" (439): "it does not mean, G-d forbid, that the Name is not whole in itself, for He is whole in Himself." "אך אין השם בשלימות נמצא אצלינו" (439): "only the Name is not found whole with us."

## 2.8 Whole above and below: why Yitro came

Yitro heard of the sea and of Amalek. The plagues were each a "finger," a part. But the sea is the root of all waters (350), so the whole of the lower world was moved. The sun stood still, so the whole upper world was moved. "כי השמש משמש לכל העולם, והיה נס זה בכלל העולם" (440). "כי ה' הוא האלקים בעליונים ותחתונים" (440): "G-d is the G-d above and below." And the Torah is higher still: "והתורה מדריגתה על השמים" (440). "וצריך שנודע לו כי אין בלעדו למעלה מן השמים" (440): "he needed to know there is none but Him above the heavens too."

## 2.9 The upper Temple and the lower

Two Names built two Temples (381). Below, "מצטרף הוא יתברך אל הנמצאים והוא אדון להם" (381): "He joins the beings and is Master to them." Above, "פעל אותו השם המיוחד, שבו נבדל מן הנמצאים, כי בשם המיוחד אין לו צירוף אל הנמצאים כלל" (381): "the special Name made it, by which He is set apart from the beings; in that Name He has no joining with beings at all." Then (382): heaven and earth are each a part, made with one hand. "אבל המקדש הוא הכל" (382): "but the Temple is the all," made with two.

## 2.10 Great without end; "Master" is only a title

Yitro said "Now I know G-d is greater than all gods." The Maharal: "כי גדול ה' בלי תכלית" (462): "G-d is great without end." Why is "Master" not His essence? "לפי שהוא אדון מנהיג את הנמצאות, ודבר זה אינו דבר עצמי לו, רק שם התאר" (462): "He is the Master who leads the beings, and this is not of His essence, only a title." "אבל כבוד עצמו הוא בלי תכלית" (462): "but His own glory is without end."

## 2.11 What is set apart has no parts

Moshe was weighed against all Israel. "כי כל דבר נבדל אין בו חילוק פרטי כלל" (446): "anything set apart has no division into parts at all." And "ish" (a man) names an essence: "וכל עצם הוא עומד בעצמו, אין לו צירוף אל זולתו" (457): "every essence stands by itself and has no joining to another."

## 2.12 He makes the people one

Pharaoh plotted against the oneness of the people. But "והוא יתברך המאחד את העם שעושה אותם תמיד גוי אחד" (7): "He is the One who makes the people one, always making them one nation." "וזהו השם יתברך המאחד אותם" (7).

- **MH-S49 "He is present to me" (65).** "שהוויתו שהוא נמצא אל כל הנמצאים אשר הם צריכים לו יתברך" (65). Inside: I need Him, and in my need He is here, turned toward me, as one who speaks turns to the one he speaks to. How to enter: in a hard hour, say "Ehyeh asher Ehyeh" in plain words: "You are here with me now, and You will be with me in the next thing too." It differs from S33 Before Whom, which is standing before Him in awe. Here He turns to me because I need Him.
- **MH-S50 "The mind's delight" (465).** "כך עין השכל גם כן נהנה בראות הזיו וההוד מן השכינה, שזהו הנאת השכל" (465). Inside: a quiet joy when the mind sees something true about G-d, like the eye's joy at beauty. It is called radiance "מפני שהוא רחוק מהגשמות העב והעכור" (465): "because it is far from thick, murky bodiliness." How to enter: learn one true thing about Him slowly with others who love it, as at a meal with sages. Let the joy come from the seeing itself. It differs from S53 Glad, a gladness at what He gave. This is gladness at Him.
- **MH-S51 "Song rising on its own" (360).** "כי כאשר יגיע השמחה בלב הצדיקים עולה בלבם השירה" (360). Inside: song is not pushed out by the mind. "ולא שהיו מכריחים עצמם אל השירה על ידי שכלם" (360). It rises when joy reaches the heart. How to enter: after a salvation, do not rush to thank Him with set words only. Stay with the joy until words or a tune rise by themselves. It differs from S53 Glad in that the gladness has become song.

## 4.1 Do not let the mitzvah sour (`mh_do_it_now`, extended)

"ואם הוא מחמיץ את המצוה פוגם את המצוה כאילו היתה דבר שהוא תחת הזמן" (268): "if he lets the mitzvah sour, he harms it, as if it were a thing under time." Add to the practice the reason: His word is above time, so the answer to it should not wait. When a mitzvah comes to your hand, do it now. And "כיון שקבלו עליהם לעשות נחשב כאילו עשאו" (278): "once they took it on to do, it counts as if they did it." So when you cannot do it yet, take it on now in your heart.

## 4.2 Leave Egypt today (`mh_every_day_leaving`, new)

"וכל הדברים אשר הם על הזמן כל ענין הזמן שוה, ואינו בזמן זולת זמן" (312): "for things above time, all of time is the same." Practice: each morning, when you say the verse of the Exodus in the Shema, say to yourself: the same cause that took them out is here for me today. Name one narrow place in your life and ask Him to take you out of it.

## 4.3 Bread in the basket (`mh_bread_in_basket`, new)

"כי מי שיש לו פת בסלו ואומר מה אוכל לאחר זמן הוא מחוסר אמונה" (418): "one who has bread in his basket and says 'what will I eat later' lacks faith." Practice: when worry about tomorrow comes, look at what you have today. Say: "Today's bread is here. He who gave today will give tomorrow." Then do today's work. This is not a reason to stop earning. It is a way to stop the worry from eating today.

## 4.4 Ask about the next step, not against the past (`mh_ask_forward`, new)

Moshe said "why did You do harm to this people?" The Maharal says a question about the past is complaint. "ואילו היה אומר לשון עתיד 'למה תרע לעם הזה' אין זה הרהור כלל" (109): "had he said it about the future, it would not be complaint at all." It is prayer. Practice: when you want to ask G-d "why did this happen?", turn it forward: "Please do not let this go on. What do You want me to do now?"

## 4.5 Know the Name (`mh_know_the_name`, new)

"כי שאלת 'מה שמו' פירושו איך מורה שמו על עצמותו" (133). Practice: before prayer, choose one Name that comes in the prayer. Ask: what does this Name tell me about Him? Ehyeh: He is present to me (65). Havaya: He hangs on nothing, and apart from Him there is nothing (65). Adon (Master): this is how He leads, but His own glory is without end (462). Then pray with that Name in mind.

## 4.6 Let the song rise (`mh_song_from_joy`, new)

"מפני שהפעל של השירה היא בלב" (360): "the act of song is in the heart." Practice: when something good happens, sit with the joy before you speak. When a tune or a word comes up by itself, sing it to Him. If nothing comes, say thanks plainly. Do not force it.

## 4.7 Speak gently to one short of breath (`mh_gentle_to_short_breath`, new)

The people did not listen to Moshe "from short breath." The Maharal: "וכל מי שהוא מחשב על דבר קשה" (129), "הוא דואג ומציר לו, ונשמתו קצרה" (129): "whoever thinks about a hard thing worries and is pained, and his breath grows short," so he cannot hear. Practice: when someone is in a hard place, do not load them with big words. Speak little and gently, and do what helps first. When it is you who cannot hear good news, know why: the hard thing has filled you. Ask Him for room in the heart.

## 4.8 Pray short when the hour needs action (`mh_do_it_now`, extended)

At the sea, Moshe prayed long, and G-d said "why do you cry to Me?" "כי עתה לא עת להאריך" (341): "now is not the time to be long." Add to the practice: when someone needs help now, say a short prayer and act.

## 5. The big human questions

**Who is G-d?** He is the Being who hangs on nothing, and apart from Him there is nothing (65). And He turns to everyone who needs Him (65). Both are true.

**Can I know Him?** Not His essence. But the four-letter Name points to it (133). Everything else we call Him is a title for how He leads (462).

**Is G-d in pain when I am in pain?** Not in Himself; He does not change (58, 117). But He is bound with His people and with them always (58).

**Does G-d care about small things?** Yes. His care reaches each detail, and this is His greatness, not a lowering (185).

**Why do the old stories still matter?** Because the cause that took them out is the cause for every generation (312). The mitzvah is above time (268).

**What is life?** Receiving the upper blessing without a break. "כי הדבר שיש לו הפסק הוא המיתה, ודבר שאין לו הפסק הוא החיים" (83): "what has a break is death, and what has no break is life."

**Why can't I hear good news when I'm down?** Worry makes the breath short (129).

**What is the highest joy?** The mind's delight in the Shechina's radiance (465).

**Why do I worry about tomorrow?** Faith is not asking "what will I eat later" while today's bread is here (418).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Ehyeh and Havaya** (65) are close to Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 4 and 7: the four-letter Name means He brings all into being from nothing at every moment, and all is null before Him. The Maharal reads Havaya as "set apart, hangs on nothing, apart from Him nothing" (65). Chabad reads it as "the One who makes all be." The two meet in "וזולתו אפס" (65): Chabad would say "nothing apart from Him" because all is made by Him each moment; the Maharal says it because He depends on nothing.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not named here. But "this whole matter is from the side of this world" (58) and "only the Name is not found whole with us" (439) say the lack is only on our side. Tanya (ch. 48-49 and Shaar HaYichud ch. 7) says the same: the hiding is for us, and He does not change. "והוא אחד בהם לא ישתנה דעתו" (117) matches "I G-d have not changed," which Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 uses for this very point.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "Master is only a title, not His essence" (462) and "He is named by His acts" (65) are the Rambam's attributes of action (Guide 1:52-54). The Maharal keeps the four-letter Name as the one Name that points to the essence (133), as the Rambam does (Guide 1:61). Chabad agrees and goes further: the essence is above even the Name (Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 20).
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). The two Temples (381) hold it well: below, He joins the beings as Master (He is in all); above, by the special Name, He has no joining with them (all is not He). The Maharal gives the formula a place in the world: the Temple is where both are true together (382).
- **Care for each detail** (185) is the Baal Shem Tov's teaching of particular providence, which Chabad holds for every leaf (Shaar HaYichud ch. 2). The Maharal says it is His greatness, not a lowering.
- **Above time** (268, 312) is close to Tanya ch. 47: in every generation and every day one must see himself as leaving Egypt, which Tanya reads as leaving the body's limits.
- **Oneness is kingship** (439) fits Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 and Tanya ch. 41: taking on the kingship of Heaven in the Shema.
