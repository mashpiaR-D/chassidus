## NEW-01 · The visit from another dimension

**Theme:** Seeing from there: Chassidus as teaching from a higher view; the aim of the guide **Teaching (stated):** We take each thing to be a solid thing on its own only because our eyes do not see the life and word of G-d that keeps it in being. If the eye were allowed to see that life, the thing would show itself as nothing apart from it. Chassidus dresses this higher seeing in human understanding so we can taste it. **Source:** Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 3 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 772 > אילו ניתנה רשות לעין לראות ולהשיג את החיות ורוחניות שבכל נברא > *if the eye were allowed to see and grasp the life and spirit in every creature* (FOUND)

**Also:** Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (the Rebbe), section 26 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (Brooklyn, New York, 1965).txt` line 133 > שתורת החסידות נתלבשה בלבושי הבנה והשגה > *that the teaching of Chassidus was clothed in garments of understanding and grasp* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** Picture beings who live on a flat sheet: they know forward and back, left and right, but 'up' is not a direction they can even point to. A ball passes through their sheet. They see a dot, then a circle that grows, then shrinks, then is gone, and they argue about the strange changing circle. When the ball tries to tell them what it is, every word gets flattened into their terms ('a circle stacked on circles?'). Only if one of them is lifted off the sheet for a moment does he see the ball whole, and, looking down, sees his whole world at once, even the inside of closed rooms.

- life on the flat sheet → ordinary seeing, where each thing looks like a thing by itself - the changing circles → the world met one slice at a time: events, people, things that come and go - the ball itself → the world as it is from His side: the life and word of G-d in each thing - descriptions flattened into flat words → teachings of Chassidus heard in ordinary terms ('G-d is everywhere' heard as 'a big invisible gas') - being lifted for a moment → a taste of seeing from there: contemplation, prayer, a moment when the eye is 'allowed to see' - seeing the inside of closed rooms → seeing what keeps each thing in being, not only its surface - going back to the sheet, having seen → living in the same world, knowing what it is - the one who explains how it looks from above → Chassidus, the higher view dressed in human understanding; the guide

**Where it breaks (ours):** G-d is not one more dimension, not a bigger space, and not 'higher' in place; the ball is one more shape among shapes, and He is not a shape or a thing in any space. The flat world is not erased or shown to be fake when seen from above: it is seen as His, which is the point (a home below). The ball stands outside the sheet; nothing stands outside Him, and He is in every point of the sheet too. The picture is of the seeing, not of what He is: it shows how our view can change, not His nature. And the lift in the picture is done to a person by force; here it is a gift ('if the eye were allowed') that a person also prepares for by contemplation.

**Plain (for anyone):** "Picture someone who lives on a flat page and has never known 'up'. If a ball passes through his page, he sees only a circle that grows and shrinks, and every description of a ball sounds like nonsense to him. The deepest teachings are like that: they describe the world from a view we don't usually have, and the aim isn't more facts but, for a moment, to see from there."

**Laws met:** PR-L01, PR-L06, PR-L07, PR-L10, PR-L12, PR-L14, PR-L15 · **Kin:** SB1-132 (A blind man told about light); SB1-104 (The child lifted to see); SB2-PIA-08 (The short-sighted man and the palace); SB1-018 (Sunlight under the open sky); SB2-MHS-01 (The map and the land); SB2-MHS-02 (Everything below is a mashal for above) **For whom:** The opening mashal of the guide. Thinkers, engineers, students of math and science, and anyone who says 'I can't picture G-d' or 'this sounds like words'. **Risk:** Turning into science fiction theology ('G-d is the fourth dimension'); a feeling of being stuck 'down here'; pride (the lifted ones and the flat ones). The idea of a flat world is an old, well-known thought experiment; it is retold here in our own words, with nothing quoted.

## NEW-02 · The screen that redraws itself

**Theme:** Being made right now (continuous creation) **Teaching (stated):** Every creature, even a stone, is being brought into being now by the word of G-d. If that word were withdrawn for a moment, the thing would return to utter nothing. **Source:** Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 1 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 758 > אילו היו מסתלקות ממנה כרגע חס ושלום האותיות מעשרה מאמרות שבהן נבראת הארץ בששת ימי בראשית, היתה חוזרת לאין ואפס ממש > *if the letters of the Ten Utterances by which the earth was created were withdrawn from it for a moment, G-d forbid, it would return to utter nothing* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** The photo on your phone looks like it is just sitting there. In fact the screen is redrawing it sixty or a hundred and twenty times every second. Cut the power and the picture does not 'stay': it is gone. What looks still is constant making.

- the still picture → the solid, ordinary world - being redrawn many times a second → being given being every moment by His word - the power → the word of G-d, the 'letters' that make each thing - the picture gone when the power stops → 'it would return to utter nothing'

**Where it breaks (ours):** When the power stops, the phone's glass and chips are still there; if His word stopped, nothing at all would be left, not even a dark screen. The screen redraws in steps with gaps between them; His giving has no gaps. The phone's maker is in a factory far away; He is in the thing He makes. And He is not electricity, or any force inside the world: the picture is of the world's dependence, not of Him.

**Plain (for anyone):** "The picture on your screen looks still, but it's being redrawn over and over every second. Everything around you is like that: it isn't just sitting there. It is being given, right now."

**Laws met:** PR-L04, PR-L06, PR-L07, PR-L10, PR-L13 · **Kin:** SB1-030 (The sea held up by the wind); SB2-RSB-11 (The stone thrown upward); SB1-029 (The craftsman's vessel); SB2-TZ-28 (The spring that never stops) **For whom:** Anyone with a phone; a good first mashal for 'being made right now'. **Risk:** Hearing G-d as a power company or 'energy'; hearing the world as mere pixels or illusion. Say: the world is real, and it is real as something given.

## NEW-03 · Live, not recorded

**Theme:** Being made right now: the word stands in the thing **Teaching (stated):** The Baal Shem Tov taught that the very words 'let there be a firmament' stand inside the heavens forever, clothed in them to give them life. The world is not a thing G-d made once and left; His word is in it now. **Source:** Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 1 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 757 > תיבות ואותיות אלו, הן נצבות ועומדות לעולם בתוך רקיע השמים, ומלובשות בתוך כל הרקיעים לעולם להחיותם > *these words and letters stand forever within the firmament of heaven, clothed in all the heavens forever to give them life* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** A recorded video keeps playing even if the person who made it is asleep, or gone. A live stream is there only while someone is broadcasting; the moment they stop, there is nothing to watch. The world is live, not a recording.

- a recording that plays on its own → the mistaken picture of a world made once and left to run - the live broadcast → creation as speech going on now - the broadcaster speaking at this moment → His word standing in each thing - nothing to watch when the stream stops → without His word, nothing

**Where it breaks (ours):** A stream travels through cables and servers that exist apart from the one broadcasting; His word needs nothing in between. A broadcaster is far from his viewers; He is in what He says. And we are not viewers watching the stream from outside: we are part of what is being said.

**Plain (for anyone):** "A recording keeps playing after its maker goes home. A live stream exists only while someone is broadcasting. The world is live."

**Laws met:** PR-L04, PR-L07, PR-O02, PR-L10 · **Kin:** SB1-029 (The craftsman's vessel); SB1-030 (The sea held up by the wind); SB2-RSB-01 (Breath in, breath out) **For whom:** People raised on 'G-d made the world long ago' (the watchmaker picture); people who watch or make streams. **Risk:** Making G-d a performer and the world 'content'; making creatures feel less real. The point is closeness, not unreality.

## NEW-04 · The stadium wave

**Theme:** Nothing but Him; all counts as nothing before Him **Teaching (stated):** As He was alone and one before He created the worlds, so He is alone and one after creating them, because everything before Him counts as nothing at all. **Source:** Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 20 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 279 > כן הוא לבדו הוא יחיד ומיוחד אחר שבראם, משום דכולא קמיה כלא חשיב > *so He alone is single and one after He created them, because all before Him counts as nothing* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** A wave sweeps around a stadium. It looks like a thing: it has a shape, a speed, it races past you. But no one moves sideways. There is nothing there except people standing up and sitting down in turn. Ask where the wave is, apart from the people, and the answer is: nowhere.

- the wave that looks like a moving thing → the world, which looks like a thing on its own - people rising and sitting → His power giving each thing life, moment by moment - no wave apart from the people → nothing apart from Him - still a real wave, which you can see and cheer → the world is real, as His doing, not a trick

**Where it breaks (ours):** The crowd is many separate people, each with a will of his own, and the wave is a pattern made of parts; He is one, simple, without parts, and the world is not made of bits of Him. The people exist before and after the wave without needing it; that part holds, and it shows that He does not need the world. But the people are not affected by the wave, while in the picture someone might think G-d is 'the sum of everything': He is not the crowd and not the world.

**Plain (for anyone):** "A stadium wave looks like something racing around the stands, but there's nothing there except people standing up and sitting down. Look at anything around you and ask: what is here besides the life that is giving it to be?"

**Laws met:** PR-L04, PR-L06, PR-L07, PR-L12 · **Kin:** SB1-009 (One word against the whole speaker); SB2-TZ-16 (One letter and the speaking soul); SB1-018 (Sunlight under the open sky); SB2-RYZ-01 (A candle at noon) **For whom:** Sports fans; anyone who likes a picture they can see in a crowd. **Risk:** Pantheism (G-d equals the sum of the world); splitting Him into parts. State the break.

## NEW-05 · The heartbeat on the monitor

**Theme:** Run and return: life given fresh every moment **Teaching (stated):** Each hour a new flow of life comes from above to give life to the lower worlds, and the flow of the hour before returns to its source: 'run and return'. **Source:** Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 41 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 601 > בכל שעה ושעה היא המשכה אחרת מעולמות עליונים להחיות התחתונים, והמשכת החיות שבשעה שלפניה – חוזרת למקורה > *every hour there is a new flow from the upper worlds to give life to the lower ones, and the flow of life of the hour before returns to its source* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** Beside a hospital bed, the monitor draws a line that jumps up and falls back, jumps and falls. Each beat is a new push; the line is never simply 'on'. A perfectly flat line would not mean calm. Put two fingers on your wrist and you will feel the same thing in yourself.

- each new beat → each new flow of life from above - the line falling back → the flow before returning to its source - the rhythm itself → 'run and return' - a flat line → what would be if the flow stopped - your own pulse → the run and return of the soul itself, rising in prayer and coming back to the day

**Where it breaks (ours):** A heart can weaken and stop; the flow from Him does not. The heartbeat is the body working; the flow of life is given. And a heartbeat repeats the same beat, while each flow from above is a different flow, new each time.

**Plain (for anyone):** "Put two fingers on your wrist. Your life isn't a steady hum; it comes in beats, each one new. The life in everything comes like that: given fresh, again and again."

**Laws met:** PR-L09, PR-L04, PR-L07, PR-L13 · **Kin:** SB2-RSB-01 (Breath in, breath out); SB1-166 (The spring that never stops); SB2-TZ-28 (The spring that never stops) **For whom:** Nurses and doctors; anyone who has sat by a bed; people who like a mashal they can feel on their own body. **Risk:** It can wake memories of illness or a death; for someone grieving or afraid, use the pulse on the wrist, not the monitor, or choose another mashal.

## NEW-06 · The hum you stop hearing

**Theme:** Personal providence: training the eye to see constant renewal **Teaching (stated):** The Rebbe: the work of a person, by the teaching of Chassidus, is to train himself to see personal providence, how in His goodness He renews the world and every creature every moment. **Source:** Hayom Yom (the Rebbe), 29 Sivan — `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Hayom Yom (Brooklyn, New York, 1942).txt` line 345 > להרגיל את עצמו לראות ענין השגחה פרטית, איך כי בטובו ית' מחדש את העולם והנבראים בכל רגע > *to train oneself to see personal providence: how in His goodness He renews the world and the creatures every moment* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** A kitchen fridge hums all day, and you stop hearing it. You notice the hum only when it clicks off and the room is suddenly quiet. What is always there is exactly what we stop noticing. You can practice listening for the hum while it is still on.

- the steady hum → the constant renewing of everything - not hearing it → habit; the eye used to the world - noticing only when it stops → waking up only in a crisis or a near miss - listening for it on purpose → 'training oneself' to see providence

**Where it breaks (ours):** When the fridge stops, the kitchen is still there; if His renewing stopped, there would be no one left to notice a silence. So we must learn to hear it while it is on, without waiting for a loss. And the hum is a noise from a separate machine in the room; His renewing is the very being of the room and of the listener.

**Plain (for anyone):** "You stop hearing the fridge until it clicks off. The fact that everything is being kept going is like that hum: always there, so never noticed. Practice hearing it while it's on."

**Laws met:** PR-L09, PR-L13, PR-L07, PR-L14 · **Kin:** SB2-RYZ-02 (Too busy to feel hungry); SB1-074 (Your own matters feel bigger); SB1-170 (Looking without seeing) **For whom:** Busy people; beginners; people who meet G-d only in emergencies. **Risk:** Too light for a deep teaching; can suggest that G-d is noticed only in loss. Use the 'practice while it is on' line.

## NEW-07 · The river in the game

**Theme:** Nothing apart from Him: the world given being from nothing, always **Teaching (stated):** What makes each creature is the life and word of G-d in it; it alone brings the creature out of nothing into being, always. If so, there is truly nothing apart from Him. **Source:** Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 3 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 772 > הוא לבדו המוציאו תמיד מאפס ואין ליש ומהוה אותו, אם כן, אפס בלעדו באמת > *it alone brings it out constantly from nothing into being and makes it; if so, there is truly nothing apart from Him* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** In a video game the river looks wet and the tree looks solid. Underneath, the program is running every frame; there is no river 'behind' the code, and if the program stops there is no tree left lying around. Everything on the screen is the program running right now.

- the river and the tree on screen → the creatures of the world - the program running every frame → His word giving being, always - no river behind the code → nothing apart from Him - program stopped, nothing left → without Him, nothing

**Where it breaks (ours):** G-d is not a programmer at a desk outside the machine, and there is no computer, power or code apart from Him. The world is not fake, not a 'simulation' run by someone else for fun: the river is truly real, as His. A program is written once and then runs by itself; He gives being by will, fresh each moment. And a game character has no soul and no real choice; a person has both.

**Plain (for anyone):** "In a game, the river on the screen is nothing but the program running right now; there's no river behind it. Everything you see depends on being given, moment by moment, and even more than that."

**Laws met:** PR-L06, PR-L07, PR-L10, PR-L11 · **Kin:** SB1-029 (The craftsman's vessel); SB2-RSB-35 (Sea water that covers is not like nature); SB1-018 (Sunlight under the open sky) **For whom:** Coders and gamers, who will think of it anyway; best used to correct the picture they already have. **Risk:** High: it invites simulation theory ('so nothing is real', 'so we are puppets'). Use only together with the break, and strip it quickly.

## NEW-08 · The sourdough starter

**Theme:** The soul is truly a part of G-d above **Teaching (stated):** The second soul in a Jew is truly a part of G-d above. **Source:** Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 2 — `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Tanya (Liozna, 1786-1796).txt` line 64 > ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש > *the second soul in a Jew is truly a part of G-d above* (FOUND)

**Mashal (ours):** A baker keeps a sourdough starter alive for years; some bakeries keep one going for generations. Every loaf rises from a spoon of that same living culture. The spoon in your jar is not a copy of the old starter: it is that starter, still alive.

- the old living starter → the source of life - the spoon in your jar → your soul - not a copy but the same living thing → 'truly a part', not a likeness - the loaf rising from it → the soul giving life to body and deeds - feeding the starter → Torah and mitzvos, which keep the soul's life awake

**Where it breaks (ours):** A spoon of starter is cut off: it lives on its own and can be lost or die. The soul is never cut off from its root and never dies. A starter is many tiny creatures; He is one and has no parts, so 'part' is itself only a mashal for a soul that stays one with Him. Say this break aloud, because 'a piece of G-d' is easy to take literally.

**Plain (for anyone):** "A spoon of a hundred-year-old sourdough starter isn't a copy of the old one; it's the same living thing. Something in you is like that: not a copy of the source of life, but of it, and still joined to it."

**Laws met:** PR-L04, PR-L06, PR-L07, PR-L13 · **Kin:** SB1-001 (The child from the father's mind); SB2-RYZ-13 (A spark rising into the torch); SB1-008 (The flame that pulls upward) **For whom:** Bakers, cooks; people who feel worthless or 'only human'. **Risk:** 'A part' can be heard as G-d being divided. The Tanya's line speaks of the soul of a Jew; a guide speaking to everyone should not claim more than the source says and should use the plain version with care.

## NEW-09 · The glassblower's breath

**Theme:** The soul blown from within **Teaching (stated):** The Zohar says: one who blows, blows from within himself, from his inside and inwardness. So the soul comes from G-d's 'inside', not from the outside of His speech. **Source:** Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 2 — `Chassidus-txt/R. **Where it breaks (ours):** A songwriter wrote for himself; the Psalms were given for every soul. A song moves you by memory and mood; prayer is speaking to Someone. And you do not need to suffer to pray like this: the Piaseczner teaches to find your own lack and longing in the words.

**Plain (for anyone):** "There's a song you played a hundred times, and then one day it's about your life and you sing it like it's yours. Old words you've said a thousand times can become yours like that."
