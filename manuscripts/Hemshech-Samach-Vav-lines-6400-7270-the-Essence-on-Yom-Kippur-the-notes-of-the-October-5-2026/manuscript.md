## The shape of the range

The range has three parts that speak about the unity, with a dead zone in the middle.

1. **The end of the series (6400-6497).** The Rashab wrote this conclusion but did not deliver it. It ranks three levels. Malchiyos and Zichronos draw the filling and surrounding light as they are rooted before the tzimtzum. Shofros draws the Essence itself, above both. Rosh Hashanah begins that drawing and Yom Kippur completes it.
2. **The appendices of the Rayatz and the Rebbe (6498-6711).** The Rayatz's notes are mostly about the grades of love. Their theology comes in a short run of notes (6586-6649): surrounding light creates, filling light gives life, and concealment is not hiding. The Rebbe's talk of 5730 explains why the last and darkest generation receives the most inward Torah.
3. **The Rebbe's summary of the series (7020-7270).** This is the densest part of the range. In 75 numbered sections it condenses the opening maamarim of 5666: the purpose of creation, the inner and outer will, why the mitzvot are unbounded, Eden and the World to Come, teshuvah, running and returning, what "Ein Sof" names, the two Names Havayah, and Yaakov and Yisrael.

F05 (the Essence) leads the face counts by far: 150 line entries against 96 for the next. Nearly every stated line of the conclusion and the summary makes one move. It sets up a ladder of revelations (filling, surrounding, the light before the tzimtzum, the essence of that light) and then says what alone reaches past the top rung. The range gives five different answers to that question, and they are kept apart below.

## The conclusion (6400-6469): Shofros and Yom Kippur draw the Essence itself

The shofar's inner cry, "the voice that is not heard", moves the essence of the soul. So it reaches the essential concealment of Ein Sof (F05): > כי בחי' השופר ה"ז מגיע בבחיי העלם העצמי דאין סוף ממש (line 6400)

The ram's horn is also the bitul of Rosh Hashanah, from the essence of the soul and beyond reason (F08): > והיינו בחי' הביטול והנחת עצמותו שנתונים לה' שלא עפ טו"ד כלל (line 6400)

Why are the Shofros verses said *after* Malchiyos and Zichronos? Because a union of two draws what is higher than both. The union of surrounding and filling light draws the Essence (F04, F05, F09): > כמו"כ ע"י היחוד דבחי' סובב כל עלמין וממלא כל עלמין, נמשך גילוי בחי' עצמות א"ס ממש (line 6402)

> אבל ענין אמירת שופרות הוא בחי' גילוי ההעלם העצמי דא"ס ממש (line 6405)

This drawing needs no arousal from below, "yet it comes only after every possible revelation is complete" (6405). Its home is Yom Kippur, "Shabbat of Shabbatot". Shabbat's pleasure is essential but felt. The Essence's own Name is not drawn at all and is not even called light (F05): > אבל בח שמו העצמי היינו מה שאינו בבחי' המשכה כלל וכלל, עד שאינו נקרא בשם אור כלל (line 6417)

> שההמשכה היא מבחי' עצמות אין סוף ממש (line 6423)

The three Shabbat meals trace the same ladder. Friday night is the filling light. The day meal is the surrounding light. The third meal, at the time of favour, is the Essence above both, which "includes both": Zachor and Shamor were said in one utterance (F05, F04, F09): > וזהו ענין זכור ושמור בדבור אחד נאמרו*, להיות שזהו בחי' עצמות אין סוף שלמעלה מבחי' סובב כל עלמין וממלא כל עלמין, וכולל שניהם (line 6439)

The word *oneg* (pleasure) gives the same three in its letters. Gan (garden) is the filling light, nahar (river) the surrounding light, and Eden the Essence above both: > ועדן זהו בחי' עצמות א"ס שלמעלה מבחי' סובב וממלא (line 6452)

Two conditions for drawing the inward light are stated here. The first is the unity of the souls. In the Temple, priests, Levites and Israelites were "bound together in one band", and only then could the shofar draw the inwardness of the mitzvot (F10, F06): > כולם אחודים וקשורים יחד באגודה אחת (line 6454)

The second is Torah. The mitzvot only arouse the light; the verses of Torah bring it truly below. Everything, even the highest revelation of the future, was already hidden in the giving of the Torah (F10): > דכל ההמשכות, גם מה שיהי' לעתיד במדריגה היותר גבוה, הכל נמשך בהעלם במ"ת (line 6462)

The conclusion ends on the dwelling below (F07, F05): > וזהו אמיתית הכוונה המושרשת בעצמות אין סוף ממש, דנתאווה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 6468)

The Rashab's notes for this conclusion add that the essential concealment is reached by refining (birurim), "the main purpose of the soul's descent" (F11): > וע"י הבירורים שזהו עיקר הכוונה בהירידה כנ"ל:י מגיעים לבחי' העלם העצמי (line 6494)

## The Rayatz's notes (6498-6649): love, knowing, and the surrounding light that creates

Most of these notes (6500-6583) sort the grades of love. Reasoned love, great love and love in delights are set against three grades of knowing: positive knowledge, negative knowledge, and grasp of the essence. At the third grade the knower "loses his whole existence" (F08, F12): > דכל פעולת ועיון שכלי הנה הוא התאחדות השכל עם המשכיל (line 6553)

> אבל מציאותו מתבטל בהשגה ההיא ממש, כלומר שאינו שום מציאות (line 6558)

Two points reach the unity directly. The first is that in Godly levels each includes the other and is completed by the other (F09): > דזהו מדריגות אלקיית מה שכל מדריגה כלולה ממדריגת זולתה (line 6577)

The second is that Zichronos means knowing that God is all one, known in detail but from the essence of the soul (F12): > משא"כ בזכרונות דהעבודה הוא לידע את ה' כו' כולא חד (line 6583)

The theological run (6586-6649) explains the Rashab's opening maamar. In Godliness the life is itself the creating. Without it nothing would remain, not even the vessel (F01): > דזהו מדריגות אלקות שהחיות הוא התהוות, דבהעדר החיות ח"ו יבטל הדבר לגמרי מכל וכל מבלי שישאר אף הכלי (line 6603)

Life and creation are still opposites. Life is nearness, particular light fitted to each creature (filling). Creation needs distance, a creator set apart (surrounding). Because Godliness includes all, each still includes the other (F04, F09): > דסוכ"ע וממכ"ע ה"ה בעצם ב' כחות ואורות הפכים זמ"ז (line 6603)

> הנה כולם נתהוו מאין ליש גשמי מאוא"ס הסוכ"ע (line 6610)

Concealment (he'elem) is not hiding (hester). Hiding comes from "another", and in Godliness there is no other. Only His intent that the worlds exist makes something like an other (F03, F02): > מובן הדבר שלמעלה באלקות אינו שייך ענין הזולת כלל (line 6611)

From creation we know the surrounding light only incidentally. We know it by a rule: whatever is set apart is essential (F04, F05, F12): > ולעולם דבר שהוא נבדל הוא עצמי (line 6617)

## The talks (6650-6711): the darkest generation and the Shechinah in each one

The Rayatz's Yiddish talk and the biography have no stated tie. The Rebbe's talk of Acharon shel Pesach 5730 has one. The inner Torah is revealed more in each later generation because the last generations must finish the refining and "turn the doubled darkness into doubled light" (F11): > להפך את החשך כפול ומכופל - לאור כפול ומכופל (line 6688)

In exile the Shechinah "dwells and is clothed within all worlds". It dwells with us even at the lowest point, "within each and every one" (F04, F06, F07): > שנקראת ,בשם שכינה ע"ש ששוכנת ומתלבשת תוך כל עלמין" (line 6706)

## The Rebbe's summary (7020-7270): the series in 75 sections

**Purpose.** Creation exists to draw the light of Ein Sof into the worlds through Torah and mitzvot (F07, F10): > תכלית כוונת הבריאה - להמשיך גילוי אוא"ס בעולמות ע"י תומ"צ (line 7020)

The summary rejects "to reveal His powers" and "to be known" as the final purpose. Above, power never lacks its act, and He is complete before creation (F01, F05): > כי, למעלה אין הכח חסר פועל (line 7023)

What remains is a desire with no reason: that the Essence, above the division of above and below, be drawn below (F07, F05): > שיומשך למטה מבחי' העצמות שלמעלה מהתחלקות מעלה ומטה (line 7024)

**Eden and the ascents.** Every ascent in Gan Eden is within a glimmer, and each one needs a bitul of the former self (F08). The essence is equal everywhere, so the future has no ascents: > משא"כ העצם הוא בשוה, ולכן לעת"ל לא יהיו עליות (line 7029)

The essential pleasure comes only through doing the mitzvot (F10): > ה"ז בא ע"י קיום המצוות בפועל ממש דוקא (line 7036)

Before the tzimtzum the light shone everywhere equally. Torah and the mitzvot are rooted there, so they have "no beginning and no end" (F04, F10): > משא"כ לפני הצמצום הי" מאיר אוא"ס בכל מקום בשוה (line 7037)

> שאין בהם תחלה וסוף, להיותם מבחי' אוא"ס שלמעלה מהצמצום (line 7039)

**Outer and inner will.** The will that created the worlds is only outer will, "for the sake of Israel" (7043). The will in the mitzvot is inner will, with no reason, from His Essence (F10, F05): > ופני' הרצון שמצד עצמותו הוא שורש המצוות (line 7053)

Because it is unbounded, it is drawn everywhere. This is surrounding light in filling light, the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah (F04, F02): > דכיון שפנימיות הרצון הוא בל"ג, לכן נמשך בכל מקום (line 7055)

> שיומשך הרצון דעצמות למטה כמו למעלה (line 7069)

> ולכן ע"י המצוות נמשך אוא"ס שלא ע"פ המדידה דמעלה ומטה, ונמשך למטה כמו למעלה (line 7074)

Without the one line (kav), the worlds would be "nullified in existence and wholly united". The line is what lets them be (F03, F02): > אבל בטלים במציאות ומיוחדים לגמרי (line 7071)

Two kinds of bitul are set side by side. Refining is bitul of the "something" and raises only to the lower worlds. Prayer and mitzvot are bitul of existence itself and raise to Atzilus (F08): > ביטול היש, שעי"ז נעשית העלי' רק לבי"ע, לעבודה בתפלה ותומ"צ, שהו"ע העלי' לאצילות ע"י ביטול במציאות (line 7048)

**Knowing.** Thought cannot grasp the Essence, not even by negation (F12): > משא"כ בעצמות אוא"ס לא שייך גם השגת השלילה (line 7078)

The will of the heart can. It is the essential bond of the yechidah, like a son's bond with his father (F06, F08): > ולכן רעו"ד תופס בהעצמות (line 7081)

**Toil in Torah and teshuvah.** Toil and the clearing of dross in study reach above the essential concealment of Chochmah, "Torah as it is in His Essence" (F10, F11): > והעיקר - שע"י בירור הפסולת מגיע למע' מבתי' העלם העצמי דחכמה - אמיתת התורה כמו שהיא בעצמותו ית' (line 7111)

The unfelt essential pleasure is above even the first root of Torah and mitzvot. It is reached by teshuvah, which "leaps" and reaches the Essence itself (F11, F05): > ומגיע בבחי' העצמות ממש (line 7124)

**Something from nothing, and where the parable fails.** The angels burn because they see that the created always needs its source (F01): > שהנברא צריך תמיד אל המקור שמהוה אותו מאין (line 7150)

The sunlight parable holds only for this dependence. It fails because sunlight is of the sun's kind, while creatures have no relation to their source at all. So the bitul is greater still (F01, F02): > משא"כ הנבראים שאין ערוך להחיות המהוה אותם לגבי מקורו (line 7152)

> ועי"ז מגיע להביטול דק"ש - ביטול במציאות (line 7155)

**Running and returning.** Running belongs to the radiance. Returning is "planted and rooted in the Essence, which desired a dwelling below" (F07). In the end the bond of the soul's essence with the Essence is so hidden that it must work through one's own acceptance of the yoke (F06, F08): > משא"כ השוב - שייך נטוע ומושרש בהעצמות, שנתאוה להיות דירה בתחתונים (line 7166)

> ובאמת ה"ז מצד התקשרות עצם הנפש בעצמות אוא"ס (line 7168)

**What "Ein Sof" names.** The summary sets out the Kabbalistic dispute. The early Kabbalists say Ein Sof is Keter. The Ramak says it is the Essence, "the negation of all descriptions, limit and limitless... and so everything is in it" (7185, F09). The Rashab's own position is that creation comes from the *light*, which is naught before the Essence and yet carries its infinite power (F05): > ולכן, דעת רבינו שההתהוות היא מהאוד, שהוא אין ואפס לגבי העצמות (line 7188)

> והנה, כללות האור שלפני הצמצום הוא אור הכלול בעצמותו (line 7197)

> אלא כלול ומיוחד בעצמות המאור ממש (line 7200)

> וזהו החילוק בין הוא ושמו אחד (ר"ת הוא) - עצם האור (line 7201)

His power includes the power not to shine. "Your great power" is chiefly the power to hide, and the light is "endless above and limitless below": concealment and revelation together (F03, F09): > שעיקר הכח והגבורה הוא להעלים ולהסתיר (line 7209)

> שהו"ע ההעלם והגילוי, גבול ובל"ג (line 7210)

**Atzilus, the two Names, Yaakov and Yisrael.** In Atzilus the lights are "in essential unity". Atzilus is "Adam", a revealing of what is hidden and not a creation from nothing (7173). The higher unity, "all before Him is as nothing", belongs to Atzilus. Below, it shows only as the hidden love in each Jew (F02): > שבאורות נרגש האין האמיתי, והם בביטול והעדר המציאות לגמרי ובבחי' יחוד עצמי (line 7213)

> והגילוי באצילות הוא בחי' יחו"ע וד"ע, ביטול אמיתי דכולא קמי' כל"ח (line 7226)

The higher knowledge goes "from something to nothing, from the true Being" (F02): > ד"ע - מיש לאין - מיש האמיתי (line 7250)

Yaakov repairs the hiding by bitul of the "something". Yisrael reveals Havayah with the world not hiding at all (F02, F07): > שאין העולם מסתיר כלל, אלא הוא בטל בתכלית (line 7260)

The souls are rooted higher than Torah, yet Atik is revealed in them through Torah (F10): > כי אורייתא וקב"ה כולא חד, משא"כ נש"י נתהוו להיות במהות בפ"ע (line 7268)

Prayer is "the back": a son follows his father even when no face is shown, from essential bond (F08): > מצד הביטול וההתקשרות בעצם (line 7270)

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The top of the ladder is the Essence above both filling and surrounding light.** Tanya ch. 48 and Shaar HaYichud work with filling and surrounding light and with the letters of speech. Here the unity that matters is the Essence above both. It is reached by a union of the two (6402), and it is "included" in both without being either (6439, 7055).
- **A calendar of the unity.** The Tanya has no teaching tying the levels of the unity to Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Shabbat and its three meals. Here each day draws one level. Yom Kippur alone draws His Name "that is not called light" (6417).
- **Creation from surrounding light, life from filling light.** Shaar HaYichud 1-2 locates the ongoing creation in the divine speech (filling light). The Rayatz's notes say creation needs distance and so is from the surrounding light. Filling light enlivens; it also creates, but only because each light includes the other (6603, 6610). The summary agrees (7022-7023).
- **No "other" in Godliness.** Tanya ch. 21-22 explain why the world *seems* separate. The Rayatz states the principle directly: hiding needs an other, there is none, and His intent that the worlds exist makes something like an other (6611).
- **The will of creation is only outer will.** Tanya ch. 4 and 23 call Torah and mitzvot His will and wisdom. Here there are two wills. The mitzvot are the inner will, with no reason. The will that made the worlds is outer will, "for the sake of Israel" (7043, 7053). That is why the mitzvot draw the light "below as above", past all measure (7074).
- **The parable of sunlight is corrected.** Tanya ch. 33 and Shaar HaYichud 3 use the sun's ray in the sun. The summary says the parable holds for dependence and fails for kind: sunlight is of the sun's kind, and creatures are not of their source's kind (7152). This is G06 (explain by analogy and mark where it fails) stated in the text itself.
- **The unity of the souls as a condition.** In the Tanya (ch. 32) the unity of Israel follows from the souls' root. Here it is a condition: the shofar drew inner light only when Israel was "one band" (6454).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **Most of the range is apparatus.** 308 lines are prefaces, index and facsimile noise, and 101 more are pure references. None is forced.
- **Four voices.** Only lines 6400-6497 are the Rashab's own words, and of their 98 entries 55 are editor's footnotes and notes, leaving 43 lines of his text. The Rayatz's notes are his explanations of the Rashab, and the summary is the Rebbe's condensation. A tie "stated" in the summary is stated by the Rebbe as the Rashab's teaching. It is not a new line of the Rashab. `where` marks every entry's part.
- **The love notes.** The Rayatz's long analysis of the grades of love (6500-6583) ties to the unity only through F08 (bitul, the wish "to be included", the handing over of self). Most of it is structural.
- **Two readings.** At 6518 the union of opposites (comprehension and wonder) is in the worshipper's faculties; tying it to God is ours. At 6534 "even the smallest part is part of the essence itself" speaks of the essence of a love; reading it as the part-is-the-whole logic of F05/F06 is ours.
- **Section numbers in the summary.** The OCR drops some section letters (6, 17, 34, 44, 47-48, 59). The `§` numbers in `where` are the section in force at the start of each line, filled in where a letter is missing.
- **OCR damage.** The text has "מהאוד" for "מהאור" (7188), "כת לאש" for "כח לאש" (7203), "הול" for "הוי'" (7234) and "אץ" for "אין" (6627). Stray letters and numerals are fused onto words ("עולםד" at 7050, "מאליטי" at 7139), and "בחי'" is clipped to "בח" at 6417. Yiddish in the Rayatz's notes is mis-voweled. Quotes keep all of these.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **What alone reaches the unfelt Essence?** The range gives five answers and does not reconcile them: - the Shofros verses and Yom Kippur (6405, 6417); - refining in practical mitzvot with acceptance of the yoke (6478, 6494); - mitzvot done in deed, not their reasons (7036, 7114); - teshuvah, which is "above Torah and mitzvot in their first root" (7124); - toil and the clearing of dross in Torah study (7111, 7117, and 7124 section 26). The summary itself asks how Torah study can reach Eden when the unlearned reach it through deed (7113-7117). It gives two answers and leaves both standing.
- **Is "to be known" the purpose?** The Rayatz calls "so that they may know Him" the intended essence of the worlds, the filling light (6626). The summary rejects both "to reveal His powers" and "to be known" as the ultimate purpose. The purpose is the desire, with no reason, for a dwelling in this world, where Godliness is not felt (7023-7024).
- **Where does the power to create sit?** The spine records Igeres HaKodesh 20 and the Rebbe: the power to make "something" is the Essence's alone (F05a, F05b). Here the summary gives the Rashab's view that creation is from the *light*, "naught before the Essence". The light has the power only "because it is of the luminary's kind", since creation "from nothing" cannot be from the Essence, the true Being (7187-7188, 7195). The Ramak's view that "Ein Sof" names the Essence alone is stated and rejected (7185-7186).
- **Filling light: life or creation?** The Rayatz says the Godly life "is itself the creating" (6603). In the same line he says life and creation are opposites and that creation belongs to the surrounding light. He holds both by the rule that each light includes the other.
- **Servant or son?** In the conclusion, "a son who becomes a servant" draws the essential concealment (6405). In the summary, the bitul of acceptance of the yoke is "above even 'with all your might', as a servant is above a son" (7082). Yet the souls of Atzilus are "sons" who become one with the lights (7248), and Yisrael the son ranks above Yaakov the servant (7259-7260).
- **The shofar on Shabbat.** The Rashab gives a reason from the unity: in exile the souls are scattered, so the inner light cannot be drawn (6454, 6460). The Rayatz records the question in its halachic form: why did the rabbis uproot a Torah mitzvah over a fear about the simple (6600)? The two stand side by side.
