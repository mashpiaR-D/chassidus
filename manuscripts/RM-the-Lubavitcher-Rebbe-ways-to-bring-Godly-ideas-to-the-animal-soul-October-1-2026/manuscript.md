## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

- **A full model, not just a principle.** The Rebbe gives the whole chain. The G-dly soul dresses in the *thinking soul* (human reason), which explains to the animal soul until the idea "settles" (RM01). The explanation is pitched at the animal soul's own price, starting "not for its own sake" (RM02), and shows it "that this is good for him" (RM03, RM04).
- **Its own subject matter.** Contemplate G-d through physical things, the animal soul's home ground (RM05). Torah reaches it because Torah also speaks of the world (RM06).
- **Worked, step-by-step examples from the letters.** The business-rival letter shows atkafya then is'hapcha in deed, speech, thought, then love (RM07). There is a regimen for a scattered, anxious mind (RM21), and memory of one's own rescue used as proof (RM17).
- **A strong warning about debate.** Arguing with the animal soul gives it a seat and a win is only for now. Go with will and delight, and teach it, do not argue (RM12). This sits beside the Rebbe's equally strong demand to explain, even to a person of pure yoke (RM11).
- **The animal soul's own reasons for the yoke** (relief from deciding, RM13) and for joy (meat and wine, habit, its own experience: RM14-RM17).
- **A diagnosis step.** Some animal souls can be "caught" by reason; the "dog" cannot, and needs the stick (RM23).
- **Bodily practice in place of self-punishment.** Afflict the animal soul, not the body (RM19). Speak the prayer (RM20). Sing (RM29). Pray before eating (RM30).

## RM01. Translate through the thinking soul

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** The G-dly soul does not speak to the animal soul directly. It dresses itself in the 'intellectual soul' (נפש השכלית), plain human reason, which sits between the two. Through it, the G-dly soul explains a Godly matter until the matter 'begins to settle' in the animal soul.
- **How to do it:** 1. Take the Godly idea. 2. Put it into plain human reasoning, the kind any thinking person follows. 3. Keep explaining until it 'lies' in the animal soul (Yiddish: 'it will begin to settle with her'). 4. Expect conduct to follow: 'and so also behaviour by the Torah and keeping the mitzvos'.
- **Why it works:** The intellectual soul is called the 'image' (צלם) in man. It fell, like the sparks of Tohu, to the level of kelipas nogah. So it can go either way: joined to holiness it becomes good. Because it is reason, its nature is to be drawn to what is above it. The intellectual soul's job, in a letter: to make the animal soul understand, by reason, how to conduct its traits.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For everyone. This is the Rebbe's main frame for the whole relationship of the two souls. **Not when:** Even then the thinking soul 'only agrees' with what the G-dly soul grasps. It stays human reason in its essence (MM 1946, not quoted here).
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul grasps Godliness as its own; the thinking soul only translates and agrees; the animal soul is persuaded. Three listeners, not two.
- **Quotes:** - «שלכן יכולה להיות ממוצע בין נפש האלקית העולה למעלה לנפש הבהמית שיורדת למטה» — *so it can be a middle between the G-dly soul, which rises, and the animal soul, which goes down.* Toras Menachem, 11 Nissan 5732. `TM` line 108250. OCR: no. - «שעל ידה תוכל נפש האלקית להסביר לנפש הבהמית עד שיתחיל להיות מונח אצלה» — *through it the G-dly soul can explain to the animal soul until [a Godly matter] begins to settle in her.* Toras Menachem, 11 Nissan 5732. `TM` line 108250. OCR: no. - «נפש השכלית ענינה להבין את הנה"ב ע"י שכל ומוחין איך להתנהג במדותי'» — *the intellectual soul's task is to make the animal soul understand, by reason and mind, how to conduct itself in its traits.* Igros Kodesh vol. 2, letter 170. `IK` line 3795. ## RM02. Explain at its own price

- **Family:** self-interest and good
- **What it is:** The explanation is pitched at the animal soul's level. The G-dly soul explains 'the preciousness of Torah and mitzvos' in terms the animal soul values, and gets it to keep them 'not for their own sake' (שלא לשמה). Step by step this rises to 'for its own sake', and in the end even to doing it for the Creator's pleasure.
- **How to do it:** 1. Explain the worth of Torah and mitzvos in the animal soul's currency (reward, benefit, its own good). 2. Get the deed done on that basis. 3. Keep raising the reason, as the Rambam lays out the ladder from lo lishmah to lishmah. 4. Last rung: explain to it even the Creator's pleasure, not only the creature's.
- **Why it works:** The halachah: 'a person should always busy himself with Torah and mitzvos even not for their own sake', because from not-for-its-sake one comes to for-its-sake. The Rebbe applies this ladder to the animal soul.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** From the start, with any animal soul that can be reasoned with. **Not when:** The Rebbe notes that reaching true lishmah is 'very hard, belonging to the wise'.
- **Quotes:** - «ואופן ההסברה הוא לפי ערכה של נפש הבהמית» — *and the way of explaining is according to the animal soul's measure.* Toras Menachem, 11 Nissan 5732. `TM` line 108251. OCR: no. - «להסביר לנפש הבהמית ולפעול עלי' קיום התומ"צ שלא לשמה, וסוכ"ס באה ללימוד התורה וקיום המצוות לשמה» — *to explain to the animal soul and get it to keep Torah and mitzvos not for their own sake; and in the end it comes to learning Torah and keeping mitzvos for their own sake.* Toras Menachem, 11 Nissan 5732. `TM` line 108251. OCR: no.

## RM03. Show it that it is good for it

- **Family:** self-interest and good
- **What it is:** Explain to the animal soul, in its own mind, that closeness to G-d is good for it. The Rebbe glosses in Yiddish: 'that this is good for him'. The refiner must dress in the clothes of the thing refined.
- **How to do it:** 1. Go close: look 'from near and with intent', not from far. 2. Dress the point in the animal soul's terms. 3. Show it the benefit to itself, until it understands with its own mind.
- **Why it works:** 'The refiner must dress in the garments of the one refined.' To refine the body and animal soul one must be clothed in them 'inwardly'. The soul came down not for itself but to fix the body, animal soul and its share of the world.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For the work of the whole day, not only prayer. Also used in the letters: even the animal soul's mind sees that rivalry harms both rivals (IK 57896, not quoted). **Not when:** See RM12: if this turns into a debate, the animal soul gets a seat at the table.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul loves because it sees; the animal soul agrees because it is shown it gains.
- **Quotes:** - «להסביר לנפש הבהמית עד שיבין בשכלו איך שקרבת אלקים לו טוב» — *to explain to the animal soul until it understands with its own mind how 'closeness to G-d is good for it'.* Toras Menachem, 13 Tammuz 5722. `TM` line 60175. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RM04. Compare the pleasures

- **Family:** self-interest and good
- **What it is:** Answer the animal soul's complaint ('they tied me to a body and to this world; how can you ask me to give up its pleasures?') with a comparison of pleasures. All this world's pleasures, even the true ones, are only waste left over from the pleasures of Gan Eden. Why give up the main pleasure to get the waste?
- **How to do it:** 1. Hear the complaint as the animal soul's. 2. Contemplate deeply what is to come and Gan Eden, until it is almost felt now. 3. Put the question to it: why trade the main pleasure for its leftovers? 4. Then put all thought into the main thing. The way to do that is bitul, which in practice is accepting the yoke.
- **Why it works:** The animal soul runs on pleasure. Shown a bigger pleasure of the same kind, it can let the smaller go. The Rebbe ties the power to 'the G-d of Yitzchak' (laughter, delight).
- **When / for whom, and when not:** When asked to hold back from permitted worldly pleasures. **Not when:** The comparison alone does not finish the job: the text adds that the way to it is bitul and accepting the yoke.
- **Quotes:** - «טוען הוא, שקשרו אותו דוקא עם הגוף ונפש הבהמית ודוקא בעוה"ז» — *he argues: they tied him specifically to a body and animal soul, and in this world.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Toldos 5714. `TM` line 16521. OCR: no. - «שכל תענוגי עוה"ז, אפילו התענוגים האמיתיים, אינם אלא פסולת מהתענוגים דגן עדן, וא"כ, כיצד יניח את עיקר התענוג בשביל לקבל את הפסולת» — *all this world's pleasures, even the true ones, are only waste from the pleasures of Gan Eden; so how will he leave the main pleasure to receive the waste?.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Toldos 5714. `TM` line 16522. ## RM05. Contemplate G-d through physical things

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** The G-dly soul contemplates Godliness through the animal soul's own subject matter: physical things. 'Lift your eyes and see who created these.' This makes the G-dly soul's grasp almost like sight, and it makes it able to explain the matter to the animal soul.
- **How to do it:** 1. Take a physical, concrete thing. 2. Contemplate how it comes to be from nothing, and is still nothing now. 3. Then explain it to the animal soul, until it too relates to 'understanding the Godly good', and from the mind it flows into its feelings.
- **Why it works:** The animal soul's mind deals only with the 'something' after it exists, its rules and limits. Holy intellect asks how the something came from nothing. Meeting the animal soul on its own ground, physical things, lets the G-dly soul speak its language.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Daily contemplation; prayer.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's own subject is spiritual things. When it works only with them, it does not reach the animal soul.
- **Quotes:** - «ועי"ז שההתבוננות דנפש האלקית היא בענינים גשמיים, ביכלתה לבאר ולהסביר גם לנפש הבהמית» — *because the G-dly soul's contemplation is in physical things, it is able to explain and clarify it to the animal soul too.* Toras Menachem, 19 Kislev 5716. `TM` line 25260. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RM06. Torah, which speaks of the world

- **Family:** speech
- **What it is:** Torah contains not only the Ten Commandments but also the Ten Utterances of creation, things that belong to the world. By that power it can reach the animal soul and give it love of G-d. The 'toil of the mouth' (עמל פה) is Torah toil. Torah is 'strength' to the G-dly soul and 'resourcefulness' (תושיה) to the animal soul. Only the Essence, which Torah carries, can turn evil to good.
- **How to do it:** 1. Study Torah, with the mouth (toil of speech). 2. Let it work on both: revealing the G-dly soul and weakening and refining the animal soul. 3. Rely on Torah, not on lights and revelations, to turn the bad.
- **Why it works:** Lights and revelations, however high, are opposed by evil, so they can only fight it. Only G-d's Essence, which has no opposite, can turn it. Torah is one with the Essence: 'I created the evil inclination; I created Torah as its spice.'
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always; especially when the animal soul is too far from Godliness for contemplation to grip.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Revelations reach the G-dly soul; only the Essence turns the animal soul.
- **Quotes:** - «ועי"ז שיש בתורה ענינים השייכים לעולם, הנה בכח זה אפשר לפעול גם בעולם שגם הנה"ב יהי' לו אהבה לאלקות» — *because Torah holds matters that belong to the world, by that power one can act on the world too, so that the animal soul too will have love of G-d.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Mishpatim 5713. `TM` line 13327. OCR: no. - «שהכח לתבל את יצר הרע ולהפכו לטוב , הוא מצד עצמותו ית' דוקא» — *the power to spice the evil inclination and turn it to good comes only from His Essence.* Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus §19. `IS` line 110. OCR: no. - «הרי מציאות הרע – היפך האור – מנגד אליהם, ובמילא, אין ביכלתם להפכו לטוב» — *the existence of evil, opposite of light, opposes them; so they cannot turn it to good.* Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus §20. `IS` line 111. OCR: no.

## RM07. Argue it down step by step, then turn it

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** A worked example from a letter. A man hates his business rival. The G-dly soul's mind argues with him: 'either way' (ממה נפשך). If G-d decreed you a living, the rival cannot take it; if He decreed less, He would find another way without the rival. So why hate him? With more arguments the hatred stops acting in deed, then in speech, at last even in thought. That is atkafya. Then the G-dly soul goes further: 'love your fellow'. You know the trade; help him with advice or a loan. In the end the hatred turns into love. That is atkafya then is'hapcha.
- **How to do it:** 1. Name the trait and the trigger. 2. Bring a simple faith-based 'either way' argument. 3. Watch the trait stop in deed, then speech, then thought. 4. Do not stop there: give the trait a good outlet in action (help the rival). 5. Let the feeling turn.
- **Why it works:** The first stage makes the traits obey the mind. The second remakes the traits. The Rebbe says only the second is truly 'working on the traits', the Chassidic trait, though the law alone could be met with the first.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Everyday emotional reactions: rivalry, hatred, anger.
- **Quotes:** - «ערשטענס אז די מדות זאלען פאלגען דעם שכל אתכפיא, און דערנאך איבערמאכען די מדות אתהפכא» — *first, that the traits obey the mind, atkafya; and then to remake the traits, is'hapcha.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 535 (Yiddish). `IK` line 10858. OCR: no. - «פיעלט ער אז די שנאה הערט אויף ווירקען במעשה דאן אויך ניט בדבור» — *he feels the hatred stop acting in deed, then also not in speech.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 535 (Yiddish). `IK` line 10858. OCR: no. - «און סוף סוף ווערט די שנאה נתהפך לאהבה» — *and in the end the hatred is turned to love.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 535 (Yiddish). `IK` line 10859. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RM08. Separate first, then redirect the craving

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** The two souls are stuck back to back, each pulling its own way. First they must be 'sawn apart' (נסירה): the animal soul's traits separated from the G-dly soul. Then they can face each other and unite. What is cancelled is only the bad 'form' the craving wears. The craving power itself is not killed; it has value ('much produce by the strength of the ox'). It is turned to love G-d. Elsewhere: work never changes a natural trait's essence, only its form (ציור), even in teshuvah's full reversal.
- **How to do it:** 1. Distinguish clearly what is the animal soul's pull and what is the G-dly soul's. 2. Cancel the bad picture the desire is wearing. 3. Keep the energy of the desire. 4. Give it a Godly object. 5. Expect the same trait, now in a good form (anger at evil, then no anger, but the same gevurah line).
- **Why it works:** The animal soul's strength is a resource; the aim is its love, not its death.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For strong-natured people; for builders, a reason never to aim at killing desire.
- **Quotes:** - «ולזאת צריך להיות ענין הנסירה, להפריד ולהבדיל המדות דנה"ב מנה"א» — *so there must be the 'sawing', to separate and divide the animal soul's traits from the G-dly soul.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Mishpatim 5713. `TM` line 13326. OCR: no. - «דציור הרע שבו מתלבש כח המתאוה דנה"ב, צריכים לבטלו, אבל הנה"ב עצמה, שהוא הכח המתאוה, אין צריך לבטלו» — *the evil form in which the animal soul's power of craving is dressed must be cancelled; but the animal soul itself, the power of craving, need not be cancelled.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Mishpatim 5713. `TM` line 13326. OCR: no. - «מה שמשנה מדותיו הטבעיים אינו אלא שבתחילה היו בציור הלא טוב, וע"י עבודתו משנה אותם שיהיו בציור הטוב» — *changing his natural traits is only that at first they were in a bad form, and by his work he changes them to be in a good form.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Shelach 5715. `TM` line 22893. OCR: no.

## RM09. Break it in general before explaining

- **Family:** above reason (yoke, faith, mesirus nefesh, yechidah)
- **What it is:** The animal soul has a mind, so the G-dly soul's mind can explain to it and through that act on its feelings. But all that comes after a general nullifying and breaking of the animal soul. Its 'building' as it stands is not wanted.
- **How to do it:** 1. First a general bitul and breaking (in the season: Pesach, matzah, atkafya). 2. Only then the work of explaining and refining. 3. Then 'with all your heart, both your inclinations'.
- **Why it works:** 'Tyre was filled only from the ruin of Jerusalem': the animal soul's structure is built on the other side's ruin, so it must be broken before it is built anew.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** At the start of any period of work; the order of Pesach to Shavuos (also TM 89632: love in the animal soul 'comes after the prior bitul and atkafya of matzah').
- **Quotes:** - «לכל לראש יש לפעולבנה"ב ביטול ושבירה כללית, ולאח"ז יכולה להיות העבודה דבירור נה"ב» — *first of all one must bring about in the animal soul a general bitul and breaking, and after that there can be the work of refining it.* Toras Menachem, Shabbos Bamidbar 5719. `TM` line 42846. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated (adds to SOURCES P15)

## RM10. Two roads: tricks toward the yoke, or explanation

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** A letter names two ways of acting on another person, 'and also on one's own animal soul'. One: look for 'stratagems' (תחבולות) to get him to do what is required, even only by accepting the yoke, before he understands or loves. Two: start by explaining the Creator's greatness, love and fear, until he comes to it by himself. The first suits one who himself lives by the yoke: he senses the words and deeds that will move the other. The second suits people of mind.
- **How to do it:** 1. Decide which kind of listener this is (also inside yourself). 2. For the yoke road: find the right 'letters' in speech and deed that move him to act now. 3. For the explanation road: build understanding until he comes on his own.
- **Why it works:** The Rambam: children are trained to serve from fear until their knowledge grows and they serve from love.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Choose by the person's type. Both are legitimate.
- **Quotes:** - «אופן הפעולה עם זולתו - וגם עם נה"ב שלו עצמו - יכול להיות בשנים» — *the way of working with another, and also with one's own animal soul, can be in two ways.* Igros Kodesh vol. 1, letter 48. `IK` line 1019. OCR: no. - «קודם כל מחפש תחבולות שיעשה חברו כל המוטל עליו» — *first of all he looks for stratagems so that his fellow does all that is placed on him.* Igros Kodesh vol. 1, letter 48. `IK` line 1020. ## RM11. Do first, then make it understand

- **Family:** deed and body
- **What it is:** Begin with the deed and the yoke (na'aseh before nishma; tefillin of the hand before the head). But do not stop at doing. Afterwards comes hearing and explanation, until it settles in the mind. Even a person of pure yoke, who will obey anyway, 'needs an explanation so the animal soul understands', because the aim is 'with all your heart, both inclinations'. And it should be an explanation 'that even a child understands'. In a letter: mitzvos are not conditional on understanding; doing them is what makes the mind able, over time, to understand.
- **How to do it:** 1. Do the act now, on the yoke. 2. Then explain, in plain words a child could follow. 3. Do not argue with the animal soul; teach it Torah, and work to make it understand, not as a bare decree. 4. Keep going until it 'lies in the mind'.
- **Why it works:** Running with the feet and doing with the hands is not enough; G-d wants the heart, both inclinations. The deed opens the mind.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always. The Rebbe also applies it to teaching others: first the doing, then the explaining. **Not when:** Do not wait for understanding before acting (TM 65186: 'first do the deed... then one can contemplate'; if he also understands, fine; if not, it does not affect the deed).
- **Quotes:** - «איז אט דעמאלט דארף זיין טאקע די התהלה דער ענין פון נעשה, קודם לנשמע» — *then the beginning must indeed be 'we will do' before 'we will hear'.* Sichos Kodesh (OCR). `SK` line 297165. OCR: yes. - «ער ד;רף זיך משתדל זיין אז דער נפש הבהמית זאל פארטטיין א דבר תורה» — *he must make the effort that the animal soul understand a word of Torah.* Sichos Kodesh (OCR). `SK` line 297166. ## RM12. Don't debate it; go with firm will

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** There are two ways to refine the animal soul. 'War': enter give-and-take, explaining to its mind how good Godliness is. But reason leans both ways, so this gives the other side room to argue. Now each side states its case, and even a win may be only for the moment. 'Rest': do good, add light, give the bad no standing at all. Stay close to the body and animal soul, but do not enter arguments with them; go with firmness, the strength of will and delight, 'that it cannot be otherwise'. The opponent then has no hold and fades by itself. A sicha says the same: 'the first thing is, he does not enter into claims and counter-claims'.
- **How to do it:** 1. Do not open the case for the animal soul to answer. 2. Fill the time with doing good and with light (Torah, mitzvah). 3. Act from will and delight, as a settled fact. 4. Teach it rather than debate it.
- **Why it works:** Will and delight are above reason. In them there is no 'maybe': either one delights in a thing or not. So there is no room for argument (TM 5904, the yechidah).
- **When / for whom, and when not:** When the animal soul is clever and argumentative. Note the tension with RM03: explaining good is fine; debating is not.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's own work can ignore the body; the 'war' way engages the body by reason; the 'rest' way engages it by will.
- **Quotes:** - «הרי זה נותן מקום לטענות של הצד שכנגד» — *this gives room to the claims of the opposing side.* Toras Menachem, 19 Kislev 5712. `TM` line 8099. - **What it is:** A letter: the kind of Torah that holds something for the intellectual soul, the animal soul and the body is the Rebbeim's talks (sichos). They contain deep ideas, guidance in good traits and feeling of the heart, and stories and conduct in practice.
- **How to do it:** In any teaching, give three parts: a deep idea (for the thinking soul), guidance for traits and heart (for the animal soul), and a story with a practical act (for the body).
- **Why it works:** Each listener needs its own food.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Teaching, farbrengens, content design.
- **Quotes:** - «שיהי' כולל בתוכו ענין בשביל נפש השכלית נפה"ב וגם הגוף» — *that it hold within it something for the intellectual soul, the animal soul and the body too.* Igros Kodesh vol. 2, letter 215. `IK` line 4933. ## RM28. That was not you

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** Separate the person from the animal soul's acts. Bringing an offering means contemplating that the sin came from the animal soul and evil inclination, 'which is not the true being of a Jew'. A letter: everyone is somewhat 'forced by his evil inclination', but 'truly he wants' to do all the mitzvos (Rambam); an educator must free the student from outer and even inner coercion. Bad deeds come from 'the animal in a person'; they are limited in essence and can be wiped out entirely.
- **How to do it:** 1. Name the act as the animal soul's. 2. Affirm the true will underneath. 3. Do not coerce; bring the inside and outside into one. 4. Know the bad is limited and erasable.
- **Why it works:** The true will is good; addressing it frees the person from the war of inclination against inclination.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Guilt, shame, education.
- **Quotes:** - «שזה שחטא ופגם והעביר את הדרך הוא בסיבת הנפש הבהמית ויצרו הרע, שאין זה מציאותו האמיתית של יהודי» — *that he sinned and damaged and strayed was because of the animal soul and his evil inclination, which is not the true being of a Jew.* Likkutei Sichos vol. 32, Vayikra 3. `LS` line 7970. OCR: no. - «והרי מתפקידו של כל מחנך הוא לשחרר את המחונך והמושפע ממנו מכל ענין של כפי' חיצונית, ועל אחת כמה וכמה מכפי' פנימית» — *every educator's task is to free the one he educates from all outer coercion, and how much more from inner coercion.* Igros Kodesh vol. 10, letter 3060. `IK` line 39257. OCR: no. - «זיי זיינען בעצם באגרענעצט, און קענען אינגאנצען בטל און אפגעווישט ווערען» — *they are limited in essence and can be entirely nullified and wiped away.* Igros Kodesh vol. 13, letter 4739 (Yiddish). `IK` line 57946. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RM29. Sing your way out

- **Family:** feeling and the senses
- **What it is:** At farbrengens the Rebbe asks for a niggun ('From Egypt You redeemed us') as the start of leaving the straits of the animal soul. Hayom Yom: a Chassidic niggun strengthens hope and trust and brings joy. In koshering the 'meat': soaking is steeping in the Rebbe's words, salting is yechidus, rinsing is niggun.
- **How to do it:** 1. Sing a niggun with others, with intent. 2. Use it to mark a step out of a limit. 3. Use it as the 'rinse' after hard inner work.
- **Why it works:** Melody brings hope, trust and joy, which the animal soul feels.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Farbrengens; after self-reckoning.
- **Quotes:** - «ינגנו עתה הניגון "ממצרים גאלתנו",ויהי' זה התחלה לצאת מהמיצרים וגבולים של נפש הבהמית» — *let them now sing the niggun 'From Egypt You redeemed us', and let it be a beginning of leaving the straits and limits of the animal soul.* Toras Menachem, Sukkos 5715. `TM` line 20454. OCR: no. - «א חסידעשער ניגון שטארקט די תקוה ובטחון» — *a Chassidic niggun strengthens hope and trust.* Hayom Yom, 22 Tammuz (the Rashab). `HY` line 380. ## RM30. Pray so it wants G-d, then eat

- **Family:** deed and body
- **What it is:** To make one's eating truly for Heaven, first pray with contemplation that the animal soul too understands: that it must want Godliness and not the world's materiality. Then eating refines the food.
- **How to do it:** 1. Pray before eating with contemplation pitched to the animal soul. 2. Eat for strength to learn and pray. 3. Do not be drawn after the food's physical pleasure.
- **Why it works:** Without the animal soul's agreement, the intent in eating stays the G-dly soul's wish, not the eater's reality.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Daily meals.
- **Quotes:** - «צ"ל תחלה העבודה דתפלה בהתבוננות כזו שמובנת גם לנה"ב, שגם נה"ב תבין ותשיג שצריך לרצות באלקות ולא בחומריות העולם» — *first there must be the work of prayer with a contemplation that is understood by the animal soul too, so that the animal soul too understands that one must want Godliness and not the world's materiality.* Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, 10 Shevat 5729. `MM` line 1340. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RM31. Read to it until it repeats

- **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering)
- **What it is:** A letter gives three stages. a) At first the two are still apart; the G-dly soul draws its light into the animal soul, and the animal soul is silent. b) The animal soul prays and learns after the G-dly soul, which 'reads it out' to it, like a child repeating after a teacher; but it does not yet bring 'much produce by the ox's strength'. c) The goal: order inside the chaos, the animal soul's great energy in holy vessels.
- **How to do it:** 1. Accept a silent animal soul at first. 2. Lead it, word by word, in prayer and learning. 3. Aim at its full strength joining.
- **Why it works:** Repetition after a reader is how one teaches a beginner.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Stages of growth.
- **Quotes:** - «שממשיך גילוי נה"א בנה"ב ונה"ב שותק» — *he draws the G-dly soul's revelation into the animal soul, and the animal soul is silent.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 548. `IK` line 11101. OCR: no. - «נה"ב מתפללת ולומדת אחר נפה"א המקריא אותה» — *the animal soul prays and learns after the G-dly soul, which reads it out to her.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter 548. `IK` line 11101. OCR: no.

## RM32. In the test: joy, not cold accounting

- **Family:** feeling and the senses
- **What it is:** In a trial a person must take courage: G-d asks only according to one's strength, and if the test grew, inner powers were added first. As in a physical fight, one who is heavy, doubts his strength and is down in spirit can lose even to a weaker foe. So serve with joy and strong trust in the victory of the good. 'Not always is it the time for a cold, dry reckoning', especially a reckoning by the animal soul's instructions.
- **How to do it:** 1. In the fight, do not stop to audit yourself. 2. Recall that the strength was given with the test. 3. Act with joy and confidence.
- **Why it works:** Low spirit loses battles; joy and trust win them.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Times of struggle, temptation, hardship. **Not when:** Self-reckoning at the wrong time.
- **Quotes:** - «אם האחד הוא בכבדות, מטיל ספק בכחות שלו ובנפילת רוח, עלול שינצחוהו ויפול גם אם הוא גבור יותר מהמנגד שלו» — *if one is heavy, doubts his strength and is fallen in spirit, he may be beaten and fall even if he is stronger than his opponent.* Igros Kodesh vol. 22, letter 8549. `IK` line 103168. OCR: no. - «ולא תמיד הוא הזמן לעשיית חשבון קר ויבש [ובפרט חשבון ע"פ הוראת נפש הבהמית]» — *not always is it the time for a cold, dry reckoning [especially a reckoning by the animal soul's instructions].* Igros Kodesh vol. 22, letter 8549. `IK` line 103168. OCR: no.
- **Stated / ours:** stated ## 3. What the sources in this slice say does NOT work, or warn against

1. **Debating it.** Give-and-take with the animal soul's mind "gives room to the claims of the opposing side"; each side gets to judge, and "even when he defeats it, it may be only for the moment" (TM 8099). See RM12.
2. **Explaining to the "dog".** "For this kind of animal soul explanation is of no use" (TM 22387). Contemplation also fails against an inner "iron wall" built by sins; then strength above reason is needed (TM 47335: «ולכן יש צורך לעורר את התוקף שלמעלה מהשכל»).
3. **Prayer from the soul alone.** «משא"כ כשהתפלה היא מצד הנשמה עצמה, אינה פועלת בנפש הבהמית» — *when prayer comes from the soul itself, it does not act on the animal soul* (TM 55406). The G-dly soul must dress in the animal soul and contemplate things it can grasp (same line).
4. **Fasts and self-degrading mussar.** Weaken health and in this generation can bring illness, despair, or the body and animal soul "rebelling against the humiliation" (IK 33135; IK 51731). See RM19.
5. **Outward joy.** "Pinching the cheeks" to look happy does not count; joy must reach the animal soul (TM 110285: «לא רק שמחה באופן חיצוני, לצבוט את לחייו»).
6. **Leaving it alone.** "When one leaves the animal soul to itself... it will have no connection to the festival joy" (TM 110285).
7. **Trusting the animal soul's mind.** Its intellect serves its feelings: it uses reason "to find ways to fill the desire" or to justify it (TM 25260: «שמשתמש בשכלו למצוא דרכים כיצד למלאות את התאווה שבאה מצד מדותיו שבלבו»). A letter: the will "misleads even the mind" (IK 45784: «שמפני הטית רצונו מטעה זה גם את השכל עד ש»). A ruling must follow "the intellect of Torah, not the intellect of the animal soul" (IK 116034, Yiddish: «על פי שכל פון תורה (ניט דער שכל פון דער נפש הבהמית)»).
8. **Lofty reasons that stop the deed.** The pious, even Chassidic, animal soul (IK 40836; TM 29902). See RM22.
9. **Learning so absorbed that action is forgotten.** «שמרוב הדביקות בהלימוד, עלול לשכוח אודות המעשה בפועל, ובינתיים, יפרחו להם הגוף ונפש הבהמית» — *from too much cleaving in learning one may forget the deed, and meanwhile the body and animal soul fly off* (TM 11745).
10. **Cold self-accounting in the middle of a test** (IK 103168). See RM32.
11. **No taste is a symptom, not proof.** People who find no "geshmak" in inner Torah, even no understanding, show the animal soul's dominance, not that the matter is unclear (TM 21802: «שכאשר מסבירים להם ענין בפנימיות התורה, אין להם בזה שום געשמאַק» … «שזהו מצד התגברות נה"ב»).
12. **Delight that sinks down.** A Godly delight "to the point of expiry" can drift down into the animal soul's delight in buckwheat kasha (TM 35544: «התענוג שלו בעניני אלקות עד לכלות הנפש, יורד ומתלבש»). Guard the channel.
13. **Even a won animal soul still hides opposition** (MM 683 / TM 55421-55422; already SOURCES P19).
