The last part of the Zohar on Bereshit. It holds the Zohar's Shabbat teaching (the "sukkah of peace" spread over the world), a long passage on man and woman read upward, Rabbi Shimon's walk to Tiberias with Rabbi Pinchas, **the contemplation of the flame** (lines 989-1010), the serpent and the undoing of Sinai, Cain and Abel, Adam's book, Enoch taken early, God's grief over the wicked, and the generation of Enosh and the flood. All 259 lines were read in full; lines 1191-1223 (the opening of Noach) were read with them and go in the Noach report. ## 2. The unity of God

**The flame: the Zohar's contemplation of the unity.** This is the passage the guide most needs from the range, because it is a plain instruction to look at something in order to know the unity: «מאן דבעי למנדע חכמתא דיחודא קדישא. יסתכל בשלהובא דסלקא מגו גחלתא. או מגו בוצינא דדליק» (Bereshit 86:3; line 990): "Whoever wishes to know the wisdom of the holy unity, let him look at the flame that rises from a coal or from a burning lamp." In the flame are two lights: a white light above that rises straight and never changes, and a blue or black light below it, its throne, which is sometimes blue-black and sometimes red. The white rests on the blue: «ואתאחידו דא בדא למהוי כלא חד» (Bereshit 86:5; line 992): "and they are held to each other, so that all is one." The blue light holds two ways, up to the white and down to the wick it burns, and it consumes whatever it rests on below (lines 995-996). The white light never consumes. And the blue light is woken to join the white only by Israel cleaving beneath it (line 998). Then comes the wonder: «בההוא נהורא תכלא אוכמא דאכיל ושצי כל מה דאתדבק ביה תחותיה. ואתון מתדבקן ביה וקיימי» (Bereshit 86:12; line 999): "that blue-black light, which consumes and destroys whatever cleaves to it below, and you cleave to it and live." Over the white light there is yet "a hidden light that surrounds it" (line 1000), and the whole secret is in the rising flame: «וכלא תשכח בשלהובא דסליק וחכמתין דעליונין ביה» (Bereshit 86:13; line 1000): "and you will find everything in the rising flame, and the wisdom of the higher ones is in it."

Ours: this is the source of the Tanya's image of the soul as a candle flame that "strains upward" to be nullified in its root (Tanya ch. 19), and of the Chabad sense that cleaving to God would consume a person were it not that He wants us to live. The Zohar holds both: the blue light eats what it touches, and those who cleave live.

**The unity needs the one below.** When no one below cleaves, the blue light is a Dalet (poor, apart); when Israel below wakes it to join the white, it is a Heh: «וכדין כלא חד» (Bereshit 86:17; line 1004): "and then all is one." And «בכל אתר דלא אשתכחו דכר ונוקבא כחדא קודשא בריך הוא לא שוי מדוריה בההוא אתר» (Bereshit 95:4; line 1111): "in any place where male and female are not found together, the Holy One does not set His dwelling in that place." Unity above waits on union below.

**The two hidden, the two heard.** Torah stands between two houses. The inner voice is not heard: «והאי קול פנימאה איהו דלא אשתמע ולא אתגליא … ונביע תדיר ולא פסק» (Bereshit 84:2; line 979): "and this inner voice is not heard and not revealed; … it flows always and does not stop." From it comes the voice of Jacob (Torah) and then speech (lines 980-982). Ours: the Zohar's picture of the silent source under every heard word, the same shape as the Tanya's "letters of thought" below the "letters of speech".

**He grieves over His own works.** Even judging the wicked, God does not want to destroy them, «על דאנון עובדי ידוי» (Bereshit 98:2; line 1142): "because they are the work of His hands." When the angels wanted to sing as the Egyptians drowned, God said: «ומה עובדי ידי טבעין בימא ואתון אמרין שירה» (Bereshit 98:11; line 1151): "My handiwork is drowning in the sea, and you sing?"

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **Under the sukkah of peace.** On Shabbat no prayer for guarding is needed, for peace is spread over the world, «כאמא דשרייא על בנין» (Bereshit 78:3; line 936): "like a mother resting over her children." With the extra soul, «ויתנשי מנייהו כל מלין דחול וכל צערין וכל עאקין» (Bereshit 78:5; line 938): "and all weekday matters, all sorrows and all troubles are forgotten." From inside: covered, held, rested, sorrow set down. Ties: S76 Shabbos: the soul returned; S84 Trust. Even those in Gehinnom are guarded (line 935).
2. **Cleaving to the fire that consumes, and living.** Line 999. Ties: S57, S60, S62. The Zohar sets the state inside a guard: the one who cleaves lives because he cleaves to the blue light from below, as the one who wakes it, not as its fuel.
3. **Walking with the Shechinah.** A person on the road is to pray before he leaves, «בגין לאמשכא עליה שכינתא דמריה» (Bereshit 83:3; line 966): "in order to draw upon him the Shechinah of his Master"; then the Shechinah goes with him and «זווגא עלאה נטיר ליה בארחא ולא מתפרשא מניה עד דיתוב לביתיה» (Bereshit 83:4; line 967): "the supernal union guards him on the way and does not leave him until he returns home." Ties: S84 Trust, S67 Surrounded.
4. **The desire to go down.** Before the sin Adam stood in the wisdom of the upper light; when his desire to know and to go down below grew, he was drawn after it until he was parted from the Tree of Life (line 1030). Ours: the Zohar names a real pull, curiosity about the lower world, as the first separation. Ties: S07 Restless wanting, S09 The pull that keeps winning.
5. **The way a person walks draws its own spirit.** «במה דאזיל בר נש ובההוא ארחא דאתדבק ביה, הכי משיך עליה חילא ממנא דאזיל לקבליה» (Bereshit 91:7; line 1060): "in the way a person walks, and the path he cleaves to, so he draws on himself the appointed power that goes before him." Walking in truth draws a holy spirit that rises to cleave above (line 1068). Ties: S29, C3.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **Look at the flame.** Line 990: a named contemplation. How, as the text gives it: look at a flame from a coal or a lamp; see the white light above, straight and unchanging; see the blue-black light below it, sometimes red; see it hold up to the white and down to the wick, and eat the wick; know that it joins the white only because something below holds to it; know that over the white there is a hidden light that surrounds it. What it is for: "to know the wisdom of the holy unity." Ties: technique 9 (the picture, and where it breaks) and 11 (seeing the garment); it is also a real object for the eyes, which the guide's techniques rarely use.
- **Pray before a journey.** Lines 966 and 1181: before going out, pray and "take counsel" with Him; «ומאן דאיהו לא מהימנא במריה … לבו חסר» (Bereshit 102:9; line 1182): the one who does not trust his Master and does not ask His help before the road "lacks his heart", for God does not go with him. On the road, speak words of Torah (line 1183).
- **Make your wife glad.** When a man comes home he must gladden his wife, for she brings the union above to him; this is «חדוותא דמצוה … חדוותא דשכינתא» (Bereshit 83:5; line 968): "the joy of a mitzvah, … the joy of the Shechinah." And he must win her with words: «בעי למפגע לה ולבסמא לה במלין … בגין דיהא רעותא דלהון כחדא בדלא אניסו» (Bereshit 82:3; line 957): "he must entreat her and sweeten her with words, … so that their will be one, without compulsion." Adam's "bone of my bones" was said «בגין לאחזאה לה דאנון חד ולא אית פירודא בינייהו בכלא» (Bereshit 82:5; line 959): "to show her that they are one and there is no separation between them at all."
- **The Shabbat candles.** The woman must light them «בחדוה דלבא ורעותא» (Bereshit 78:8; line 941): "with joy of heart and will."
- **Washing the hands on waking.** At night all taste death and an impure spirit rests on the hands; on waking, wash before touching anything (lines 1063-1065).
- **Confession.** «כל מאן דאודי על חטאיה, קודשא בריך הוא שביק ליה ומחיל על חוביה» (Bereshit 95:9; line 1116): "whoever confesses his sin, the Holy One leaves it and forgives his sin." Adam learns from Cain the power of return and begins to praise: "it is good to give thanks" (line 1087).
- **Guard: sin in the open.** «כל חובא דאתעביד באתגליא דחי לה לשכינתא מארעא» (Bereshit 98:20; line 1160): "every sin done openly pushes the Shechinah from the earth."
- **Ask for the way and a united heart.** David asks "teach me Your way; unite my heart to fear Your name": show me the straight way to open my eyes, then I will walk in Your truth and not turn right or left (line 1186). «כל בר נש דדחיל ליה לקודשא בריך הוא שריא עמיה מהימנותא כדקא יאות» (Bereshit 102:14; line 1187): "every person who fears the Holy One, faith rests with him as it should." Ties: S83.

## 5. The big human questions

- **Why do good people die young?** When God sees that a righteous man will spoil later, «אקדים ולקיט ריחיה טב מניה וסליק ליה מעלמא» (Bereshit 96:1; line 1130): "He goes first and gathers his good scent from him and takes him up from the world." So it was with Enoch. And the wicked are given long days so they may return, or so good children may come from them (line 1132). Ours: the Zohar's answer is love that gathers before the fall; the guide should hold it gently and never offer it to a mourner as an explanation.
- **Death.** Every person, before leaving the world, sees Adam, who tells him: I sinned in one thing; see how many you have (line 1155). "There is no death without sin" (line 1157), except four who died only by the serpent's counsel (line 1158).
- **Suffering and grief in God.** God's grief (lines 1142-1151) is set against the angels' song: even judgement is not joy for Him.
- **The point of it all.** «זכאין אנון צדיקיא … וארח צדיקים כאור נוגה הולך ואור» (Bereshit 102:15; line 1188) is read as the first hidden light, "which rises in its light always and does not lessen": a life that keeps getting brighter.
- **The world is full.** «ווי לון לבני נשא … דלא ידעין כמה מלייא עלמא מבריין משניין דלא אתחזון» (Bereshit 94:16; line 1098): "woe to people who do not know how full the world is of strange creatures that are not seen"; if the eye were allowed to see, people would wonder how they can live in it. Ours: the Zohar's world is thick with presences; the guide keeps this as the Zohar's own view and does not build on it.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **The flame as a contemplation of the unity (lines 990-1004).** A real, simple, safe practice with a physical object, which Chassidus later takes up (Tanya ch. 19).
2. **The sukkah of peace (lines 934-938)**: Shabbat as being covered like a child, with sorrows set down.
3. **Praying before a journey and not traveling alone (lines 966-967, 1181-1183).**
4. **Gladness at home as holiness (lines 957-959, 968).** A marriage teaching in the language of unity: "to show her they are one."
5. **Grief in God over His works (lines 1142, 1151)**: a picture of God who does not rejoice in the fall of anyone.

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "The flame."** Sit before a candle. See the white light and the blue light, the white resting on the blue, the blue holding to the wick and consuming it, the white never consuming. Know that the blue joins the white because something below holds to it, and that you are that. Know that over it all is a hidden light that surrounds it. Close: "you who cleave to Havayah your God are alive, all of you, today" (lines 990-1008). Guard: the aim is knowing the unity, not staring; stop if it turns into a trance.
- **State, new: "Under the sukkah of peace."** Inside: covered like a child under a mother's wings; weekday sorrows are forgotten for now. Enter: by welcoming the Shabbat and blessing "who spreads the sukkah of peace" (lines 936-938). Near S76.
- **Technique, new: "Before the road."** Before any going out, pray briefly to draw the Shechinah with you, and on the road carry a word of Torah (lines 966, 1181-1183). Ties technique 22 (carrying a line through the day).
