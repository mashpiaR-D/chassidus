## How the range builds the idea

The whole range is Iggeret HaKodesh, from the end of letter 8 to the middle of letter 25, and three kinds of writing alternate in it. The charity letters (8-10, 12, 14, 16-17, 21) are appeals, but most of them argue from the unity: a mitzvah draws Or Ein Sof into action, and boundless giving draws the boundless light that surrounds the worlds. The doctrinal letters (11, 13, 15, 18-20, 25) hold some of the book's most systematic statements. They give faith in creation at every moment as the answer to suffering (11), the soul analogy and where it fails (15), and the two knowledges with the Essence's sole power to make something from nothing, shown most in the lowest earth (20). Letter 25 applies the Besht's "Your word stands in the heavens" to anger. The communal letters (16, 21-24) carry rebukes and decrees. Much of them is "none", but the Shechinah resting on ten (23) and the "separation" shown by talk in prayer (24) are argued from the unity.

**IH 8, end (1121-1124).** Charity rises as "feminine waters" to the Shechinah and rouses "God's chesed", His light descending to the souls in prayer. One sentence holds both sides: all is as naught before Him, yet "במקום שאתה מוצא גדולתו, שם אתה מוצא ענותנותו" (1121). The soul is clothed in a body that stands in darkness, and salvation is "כד אתהפכא חשוכא לנהורא" (1122, F11). The Holy Land is the place where no screen intervenes: "מאחר שאין שום דבר חוצץ ומפסיק כלל בין ״ארצות החיים״" (1124).

**IH 9 (1125-1127).** The letter makes the Shema a test of business. Israel is "one nation in the earth" because "שגם בעניני ארץ לא יפרידו … מאחד האמת" (1125). To say "Havayah is one" with closed eyes and then see a world without Him is false testimony. Worldly dealings are fitting only when they are not "for themselves" but "להחיות נפשות חלקי אלקות" (1126), in imitation of the One whose kindness gives life every moment. Then the letter ranks the services. In the Sages' days Torah study was the main service. In the footsteps of Moshiach the Shechinah has fallen to its "heels", and one cleaves to it only by action, "שבחינת עשיה באלקות – היא בחינת השפעת והמשכת החיות למטה מטה למאן דלית ליה מגרמיה כלום" (1127). Giving that overcomes the yetzer is "אתכפיא סטרא אחרא ומהפך חשוכא לאור השם יתברך" (1127). F07 and F11 are stated together: the lowest place and the last times set the form of the service.

**IH 10 (1128-1142).** There are two kinds of chesed. The world's chesed is bounded, and so are the mitzvot. The light was first clothed in gevurah so it could enter physical acts (1130). Even love and fear are bounded, since boundless love would not let the soul stay in the body, as at Sinai (1131). The text itself ties the halachic measures to the tzimtzum: "לכן רוב המצות יש להן שיעור מצומצם" (1132). The supernal chesed, "rav chesed", is the light above the worlds, "אלא בבחינת מקיף עליהן מלמעלה, מריש כל דרגין עד סוף" (1135). That is F04 in the letter's own words. Only this light repairs a blemish, and it sets the penitent above the righteous. It is drawn by giving without limit to the "dal", the poor man, and every world is that poor man before Him: "שכולם הם בבחינת דלי״ת אצלו יתברך, דלית להון מגרמיהון כלום" (1137, F02 and F08). The halachic cap of a fifth (1139) and the verse on sacrifice (1138) are read through the argument and carry no unity claim of their own.

**IH 11 (1143-1151).** This is the range's plainest account of suffering in terms of the unity. It starts from bitul of the will (1143). Then comes F01: "הבריאה הזאת, היא בכל עת ורגע" (1144), from His wisdom, the "nothing" that is the source of life and delight. One who pictures his own coming into being from nothing at every moment cannot think that he suffers, "אבל באמת אין רע יורד מלמעלה, והכל טוב" (1144). This faith is man's purpose: "וזהו עיקר האמונה שבשבילה נברא האדם" (1145), that no place is empty of Him. A mood is judged as a position. Sadness "והרי זה ככופר חס ושלום" (1146), while for the believer "״הן״ ו״לאו״ שוין אצלו בהשוואה אמיתית" (1147). Self-love marks the "erev rav" (1148), bodily desire is "other gods" (1149), and providence reaches "the minutest particulars" (1150). The letter ends on transformation, stated outright: through this faith "נכלל ומתעלה באמת הרע המדומה ב״טוב״ העליון הגנוז" (1151). One detail matters for F12. The same content is called contemplation "in the depth of his understanding" (1144) and also "faith" (1145). The letter joins knowing and believing without ranking them.

**IH 12 (1152-1170).** Fire and water, Michael and Gabriel, are opposites as attributes (1153). They make peace when Or Ein Sof, "not an attribute at all", is revealed in them: "והיו לאחדים ממש, ובטלים באורו יתברך המאיר להם בבחינת גילוי" (1154). That is F09 stated with its reason, the nothing that joins. Charity rouses that revelation (1157). The world stays mixed good and evil until the End (1158-1161), and the individual gets a foretaste at prayer, through charity and a contemplation that bears love and fear (1162-1163). The letter then separates "deed" from "service". Israel's mercy is natural, since the soul was breathed from His attributes (1164-1165). "Service" is "רק שמבטל טבעו ורצונו מפני רצון העליון" (1166). The letter is sober about the foretaste: "אך אחר התפלה, יוכל להיות הרע חוזר וניעור בקל" (1168). Only service against one's nature subdues the sitra achra so that evil does not return by itself (1169-1170). So F11 comes here with a stated condition.

**IH 13 (1171-1184).** Souls divide into right and left, expansion and contraction (1172-1175), and every Jew must include both (1176). Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel are read as soul-roots that include each other, because above "ד״לית תמן קיצוץ ופירוד״" (1178). Halachic stringency and leniency thus become a case of F09. The hidden good is no smaller than the revealed (1180). What is hidden in a soul "אלא הוא בבחינת מקיף מלמעלה" (1182): the soul has its own surrounding light, and it shines down "to come to actual deed". The supernal chesed "אין כנגדו מדת הדין", "כי הוא נמשך מבחינת ״סובב כל עלמין״ ו״טמירא דכל טמירין״" (1184). What has no opposite comes from what surrounds all. It is our reading to set this beside the Rebbe's claim that only what has no opposite can transform evil (SPINE F09e).

**IH 14 (1185-1195).** The holiness that shines on the Land is renewed every year (1185). The upper earth is "memale kol almin" (1187). The light rises without end, "כי הוא יתברך וחכמתו אחד בתכלית היחוד" (1188), the Rambam's formula of the unity. The year's light withdraws on the eve of Rosh Hashanah, and a higher one is drawn by the shofar and prayer (1189), and daily in the morning prayer (1190). It stays hidden in the "land of life": "אך גילויו מההסתר הזה, תלוי במעשה התחתונים" (1195). F03 is stated here: the revelation waits on deeds below.

**IH 15 (1196-1229).** The text itself does both halves of G06. First the license: "From my flesh I see God", because "מאן דנפח, מתוכיה נפח" (1197), and even the nefesh of Asiyah comes from vessels of Atzilut that are Godliness. Then the failure: the Maggid's "dust and ashes" shows that Avraham's love bears no proportion to supernal love, "להבדיל באלפים הבדלות, רק ש״דברה תורה כלשון בני אדם״" (1203). The long analogy of a father teaching his son (1204-1215) is structural; netzach and hod are contraction for the receiver's sake, F03 inside the analogy. Then the analogy is withdrawn. It describes the soul from kelipat nogah, whereas in the divine soul "כל המדות, פנימיות וחיצוניות, הן לה׳ לבדו" (1216). Each trait is turned toward the unity. Netzach fights whatever keeps God's glory from filling the earth (1220, F07). Hod acknowledges that all is nullified before Him: "ואף שאין אנו משיגים איך הוא הכל אפס ממש קמיה", "מודים אנחנו בהודאה אמיתית שכן הוא באמת לאמיתו" (1221). This is the Alter Rebbe's side of F12, acknowledgment where comprehension ends. The letter closes on method. ChaBaD contemplates God's greatness and bears love and fear (1225), and da'at must stay fixed, "כי בהיסח הדעת כרגע מהשגה זו – מסתלקת גם כן המדה הנולדה ממנה" (1227).

**IH 16 (1230-1239): almost all "none".** A pastoral charity letter: hard times, "your life comes first" only for equal needs (Nedarim 80), going beyond the letter of the law, reward in this world. Only 1235, mercy below rousing mercy above, is kept, and as reading.

**IH 17 (1240-1250).** The "fruits" are life from the Life of life, clothed in "children, life and sustenance" (1241). The "principal" is charity as God's own mitzvah, "מה שהקדושברוךהוא בכבודו ובעצמו עושה תמיד להחיות העולמות" (1243), the revelation of sovev drawn down to this world. Gan Eden is memale, a bounded comprehension of Torah's secrets (1244). The resurrection will be sovev, and the letter repeats Likkutei Amarim 48 that sovev is "שאינו כמשמעו כמו עיגול, חס ושלום" (1245). Keter, "ayin", beyond comprehension, is revealed "הוא דוקא כשהנשמה תתלבש בגוף זך וצח אחר התחיה" (1247), "their beginning embedded in their end". G05 and F07 are stated: the highest shows in the body.

**IH 18 (1251-1261).** There are two loves. The "love in delights" is a gift, "כי אין דרך להשיגה עלידי יגיעת בשר" (1253), whereas fear needs toil. It comes only after the toil of awe, "לשכון ולהתייחד עם היראה" (1254): gift and toil joined, which is F09, and a kept strand of F12. The second love is longing: "ואהבה זו היא מוסתרת בלב כלל ישראל, אפילו ברשעים" (1256, F06). It is in exile in the body (1257). It is revealed by contemplating "איך שהוא חיי החיים בכלל וחיי נשמתו בפרט" (1258). The longing, natural "as fire to its source", reaches God Himself through Torah and mitzvot, "Torah and the Holy One are entirely one" (1259). Worldly desire is not abolished but made a servant for necessities, "in all your ways know Him" (1261).

**IH 19 (1262-1284).** Moshe saw the "back" of chochmah, and the Arizal grasped higher by wisdom: "a sage is greater than a prophet" (1262-1267, structural). The F10 statements follow. The reasons of the mitzvot are above understanding (1268). Even a stated reason "אין זה – הטעם המובן לנו לבדו – תכלית הטעם וגבולו" (1269). Every word of Scripture, the stories included, clothes Godly wisdom above understanding (1270). The face and back of a sefirah are the two unities in small. What descends into creatures is the back; the face is united with Ein Sof (1272-1273), and all that descends is as nothing before the face, "ד״כולא קמיה כלא חשיב״" (1275). The Torah that descended exists for deed: "וכל אדם מוכרח להתגלגל עד שיקיים כל התרי״ג מצות בפועל ממש" (1276). The piece on the letters of action, speech and thought (1277-1284) is structural. Speech lives from thought, and the outer part of each level is the life of the one below.

**IH 20 (1285-1321): the Essence's power to make something.** This letter is the range's doctrinal center, and the SPINE already anchors it (G02; F05b). - The vessels of Atzilut are Godliness, "שהן אלקות לברוא יש מאין כמו ה״איןסוף״" (1286), not links in a chain of causes. In such a chain the effect is nullified in its cause like the ray in the sun (1287), and no number of contractions yields matter (1288). - The two knowledges are kept side by side: "היינו ״קמיה״ דוקא, שהיא ידיעתו יתברך מלמעלה למטה" (1289); "אבל בידיעה שממטה למעלה – היש הנברא הוא דבר נפרד לגמרי" (1290). - F05 is stated: "ולכן הוא לבדו בכחו ויכלתו לברוא יש מאין ואפס המוחלט ממש" (1295), because His existence is from Himself. - F03 is stated as the reason for separateness. Yesh comes from Malchut, "כי ״אין מלך בלא עם וכו׳״" (1297). "Their beginning embedded in their end" is the power to make a separate yesh, "בכדי שיהיה המאציל ברוךהוא מלך על כל הנפרדים, עלידי שיקיימו מצותיו" (1302). Separateness exists for kingship through the mitzvot, which joins F03, F05 and F10 in one clause. - F04 is stated. The life in creatures is "והארה דהארה דהארה, הוא בכל הנבראים ונוצרים ונעשים" (1305). Or Ein Sof itself fills heaven and earth equally, "ו״לית אתר פנוי מיניה״ אף בארץ הלזו הגשמית" (1306), as surrounding light. And it is sovev that gives the vessels the power to create: "ובהארתו תוך הכלים נותן בהם כח ועוז לברוא יש מאין" (1307). This is the ground of the Tzemach Tzedek's claim that the somethingness of matter comes from sovev (SPINE F04d; see below). - G05 is stated. The power shows most in the lowest, "מראה כחה ויכלתה ביסוד העפר הגשמי בגילוי עצום" (1309), because beneath the feet of Adam Kadmon "ותחת רגליו מאיר אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא הסובב כל עלמין" (1310). "Let the earth sprout" acts forever (1313-1315), F01 in the plant world. Sown fruits surpass wild ones, as man's work draws higher lights (1317). The higher lives from the lower (1318). The conclusion is that the practical mitzvot are "אשר הן תכלית ירידת הנשמות לעולם הזה הגשמי" (1320).

**IH 21 (1322-1329).** The letter asks for charity to the Holy Land, given often. Its unity point is 1327: charity is "להמשיך חיים עליונים מחיי החיים איןסוף ברוךהוא לארץ החיים", to the Shechinah fallen to the dust. Many gifts effect "the supernal union many times" (1328). The Rambam's "refining the soul" is read as raising the Shechinah, called "soul" (1329). The lines on zeal (1323, 1325) are "none"; Avraham's joyful zeal at the Akeidah (1324) is kept as reading.

**IH 22 (1330-1351).** The first part argues against seeking material counsel (1331-1333: none, with 1332 as reading). Suffering is love's rebuke, to be received "face to face" (1334-1335). "Face" above is will, given through the Torah of life (1336). The "other gods" still live from Him, from behind: "לכך נקראים ״אלהים אחרים״, שיונקים מבחינת ״אחוריים״" (1337). Love is roused because "כי החסד שהוא בחינת אהבה, הוא חיי החיים שבכל העולמות" (1340), and then the hidden love is revealed and the gevurot are sweetened at their root (1341, F03). The second part asks for prayer with a will above reason, which is answered by forgiveness, "כי הסליחה היא גם כן למעלה מן החכמה" (1345). Israel is one body, "אם כן אנחנו היות כולנו כאיש אחד ממש" (1348), and an evil thought of one's fellow is to be driven off "וכמו מחשבת עבודהזרה ממש" (1349, F06 and F08). That the reason is separation from the one body is our reading.

**IH 23 (1352-1365).** "Ten who study, the Shechinah rests among them." The soul's whole descent was for this: "ואף גם זאת היתה כל ירידתו בעולם הזה לצורך עליה זו" (1352, F07 stated). The letter draws its own F04 distinction. Reward is bounded light clothed in the soul, which is why seekers are moved in prayer, "ועל כן יתפעל לב מבקשי ה׳ בשעת התפלה" (1355). Resting is boundless light, "ואינו יכול להתלבש בנפש גבולית, כי אם מקיף עליה מלמעלה" (1356). So felt arousal belongs to the bounded light, and the higher resting cannot be felt in this world (1357). That this bears on the hisbonenus disagreement about felt arousal is our reading. An angel among ten Jews would be nullified "עד שהיה מתבטל ממציאותו לגמרי" (1358): the Shechinah below outranks the angels above (G05). Idle talk insults the Shechinah, who stays even so, "אלא ש״מלך אסור ברהטים״" (1359). The study program (1361-1363) is reading or none. Line 1364 grounds a communal rule in F10: there is no holiness like Torah's, since Torah and God are one.

**IH 24 (1366-1374).** Prayer is the hour He wills to be revealed, a visit to the Shechinah "״השוכן אתם בתוך טומאותם״" (1367). His glory is clothed in the words of the fixed prayer (1368). Talking in prayer shows what the Zohar calls "ואחזי פרודא" (1371), a separation that the unity does not allow (F02, F08). The decree and its release (1372-1374) are none.

**IH 25 to line 1400 (1375-1400).** The letter explains a saying in "Tzava'at HaRivash", whose compilers "ולא ידעו לכוין הלשון על מתכונתו, אך המכוון – הוא אמת לאמיתו" (1375). Anger is like idolatry "כי אילו היה מאמין שמאת ה׳ היתה זאת לו – לא היה בכעס כלל" (1376). An emotion is judged as a stance toward the unity, as sadness was in IH 11 (1146). The striker is liable for his choice, yet the harm was decreed (1377); reading these as the two knowledges in human terms is our framing. Even the curser lives by His breath: "כי אילו נסתלק רוח פיו יתברך רגע אחד מרוחו של שמעי – לא יכול לדבר מאומה" (1378). Then the Besht's "Your word stands in the heavens" (1380) is set against the philosophers' craftsman (1381), as in SH 1-2: "אלא, צריך להיות כח הפועל בנפעל תמיד" (1382), and that holds even for stones (1383-1384). The word of God is the Shechinah dwelling in creatures (1385), graded as the Malchut of each world (1386-1390). Outside the Land the nations' princes live from it as surrounding light (1391-1393). Even the "other gods" live from a spark in exile, "שגם הם, הן בחינת אלקות" (1394). That is the exile of the Shechinah (1395), made complete when Israel went among the nations (1396). His word is one and divides into four worlds only by screens, "אבל אין שום שינוי חס ושלום בעצמות השכינה" (1398). So "ולכן, אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא שבאצילות, הוא גם כן בעשיה ובעולם הזה החומרי" (1399). The range ends with the soul's speech mirroring the Shechinah, "כשמדבר דברי תורה – מעורר דבור העליון ליחד השכינה" (1400). From that follows the halacha that Shema and Grace need speech.

## Where the tie is thin, and what is kept apart

- **Thin.** 28 lines are "none": IH 16 (nine of its ten lines), the salutations and blessings, the decrees and study instructions of IH 21-24, and the IH 22 rebuke about material counsel with the exegesis of "counsel". Eight lines are "reading", where the tie is ours: 1142, 1235, 1324, 1332, 1344, 1346, 1350, 1361. The analogies of IH 15 (1201-1202, 1204-1215) and the definitions of letters in IH 19 (1277-1281) are structural and carry no unity claim of their own.
- **Halacha with a stated reason.** Three rules here get a reason from the unity in the text itself: the measures of the mitzvot come from the contraction (1132); one may not leave the ten, because Torah and God are one (1364); and Shema, Grace and Torah study need speech, because speech unites the Shechinah (1400). This does not change the SPINE's point that the halachic works themselves do not state the tie. It shows that the Tanya does, for these rules.
- **A ranking kept.** IH 9 makes charity "the main service" in the footsteps of Moshiach and limits "Torah study equals kindness" to the Sages' days (1127). But IH 17 requires Gan Eden's comprehension before the light of the resurrection (1248), IH 19 sets the sage above the prophet (1267), and IH 23 says there is no holiness like Torah's (1364). The range does not reconcile them. On the SPINE's question of where the unity is brought down, IH 9 answers with action, while IH 12 and IH 23 answer with prayer and with Torah in ten.
- **F12 kept.** Four positions sit in the range. IH 11 calls the same content contemplation and faith (1144-1145). IH 15 acknowledges what is not grasped (1221). IH 18 says the love in delights cannot be reached by toil, while fear must be toiled for (1253). IH 23 says the boundless light cannot be grasped in this world (1357). They sit with the previous range's IH 4 and IH 6, where the highest love is a gift beyond contemplation.
- **F05 kept.** IH 20 holds in one letter that only He, whose existence is from Himself, makes something from nothing (1295), and that creatures live by "a radiance of a radiance of a radiance" (1305), with the surrounding light giving the vessels the power to create (1307). The letter divides the work: life comes through radiance, and the power to make yesh comes from the essence of Or Ein Sof, which surrounds. The Maharash's "creation from nothing is only a mere radiance" (SPINE F05j) is a different formulation. Whether IH 20's division reconciles them is our reading.
- **The source of F04d.** The SPINE cites the Tzemach Tzedek that the somethingness of matter comes only from sovev. Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem 2 (line 120), read in context, follows IH 20 step by step: "והארה דהארה דהארה מהקו מתלבש בכל נברא פרטי להחיותו"; "אפי' רבוא רבבות צמצומים והשתלשלות עילה ועלול לא יועילו להיות גוף גשמי"; "רק שבאמת מ״מ עיקר החיות הוא מהסובב"; "ובזה מובן מעלת מצות מעשיות שהן תכלית ירידת האדם בעוה״ז". That passage does not name the letter. That it depends on IH 20 (1288, 1305-1307, 1320) is our reading, though the parallels are close.
- **Tzava'at HaRivash.** The saying IH 25 sets out to explain is Tzava'at HaRivash 120:4 (line 245): "הלא כל זה בהשגחה פרטיית. אך הדיבור הוא השכינה ושרתה השכינה בפיו של זה האדם". The letter corrects the compiler's word just past this range, "ומה שכתב המלקט ״שרתה״" (1408): the Besht taught in Yiddish and meant "clothed itself", in exile. Its opening caveat about the compilers' wording (1375) may bear on the SPINE's kept tzimtzum dispute, Tzava'at HaRivash 137:3's spatial wording (SPINE F03l) against SH 7 (F03k). That link is our reading; this letter says nothing about tzimtzum.
- **The soul analogy** is marked where it fails at 1203 and 1216, and with "as it were" at 1275 (G06). The index does not treat it as an identity.
- **The unity reaches what opposes it,** and the text says so. The "other gods" live from His hidden spark (1337, 1394), the Shechinah dwells amid impurity (1367), and the curser lives by His breath (1378). None of this makes them good in conduct: the princes remain "other gods", and the curser remains liable (1377, 1395).
