## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

1. **For the Mitteler Rebbe, contemplation is mostly the natural soul's work.** All five rungs of arousal from contemplation are "serving G-d with the body" (KH 312). The G-dly soul is moved by its own nature. So almost every method below is a method for the animal soul.
2. **It works by toil, habit and the heart of flesh.** The natural soul is moved by Godliness "only through great toil" (KH 426) and "acquired by habit" (ST 347). The levers are: make it touch yourself (business, life, "good for me"); many analogies; detail; eyes of flesh on physical creation; long repetition, aloud, with friends.
3. **Prayer is a sacrifice of the animal soul, in a set order.** First the animal is fed (Pesukei deZimra and Yotzer, the angels' fire, natural love and fear: IB 324). Then "fire from below", the natural heat of the heart, lifts it (MO 50064). Then Shema and Ve'ahavta, where the natural soul too gives itself (SO 548). Then the arousal must become a resolution and a deed (DC 514; KH 772), and halachah study after prayer completes the refining (ST 427; AR 183).
4. **New levers not in SOURCES.md:** the rod (fear from above, and suffering) for the "Canaanite slave" (DC 495-501); hope of reward as a valid start (DC 501-502); serve through your own temperament (DC 533); the cooked vs raw heart (KH 781; DC 527); the broken heart as vessel (KH 788); simple teshuvah without contemplation (KH 654); plowing the hard earth (AR 194; ST 356); praying aloud (IG 874-960); repeating a hundred times with a friend (KH 97); private talk with the mashpia (KH 74); halachah as the natural mind's food (AR 183); food, Shabbos pleasure and joy (MO 59113, 40663); wrestling at close quarters in prayer (ST 286); simple wholeheartedness of the "beast below knowledge" (KH 871); keeping the heart out of business (DC 1317).
5. **What the slice is firm about:** prayer's arousal does not change the natural soul's nature (DC 512-514). It is "for the hour", like a holy man impressing a stranger. Its lasting fruit is a decision not to turn the heart, kept in deed.
6. **R. Aharon of Strashelye** adds the counter-voice: arouse the love first "from the yesh", the animal soul's own felt love, and refine it after with knowledge. The heart is the main thing (SA 7-10).

## 2. The methods: the Mitteler Rebbe

### MR-01 · Make it touch yourself - **Family:** self-interest and good - **What it is:** Hold the Godly idea the way you hold news of a deal that is yours. A cold thought about someone else's fortune does not move. A thought about your own profit grips the whole mind. - **How to do it:** Take the contemplation and ask what it means for you, now. Stay with it until it clings like your own business. Love is then like eagerness for profit; fear is like care to avoid a loss. - **Why it works:** The natural soul is moved by what touches itself. A 'good thought' (machshavah tovah) clings by itself, as worldly thoughts do. - **When / for whom:** The second rung of the natural soul's ladder (ruach). For anyone whose agreement stays cold. - **When not:** Not a goal in itself: wanting the excitement for its own sake is 'level zero' (see the not-work list, item 1). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul is moved by Godliness by its own nature and needs no such bridge (KH 424). - **Quotes:** - «והמחשבה הב׳ היא מחשבה שחושב בענין טוב הנוגע לעצמו, כמו שמרויח במשא ומתן טוב» — *The second kind is a thought about a good thing that touches oneself, as one who profits in a good deal.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 248. - «וכך יובן בהתבוננות אלהות במחשבה כזאת שנדבק היטב בה כענין הנוגע לעצמו» — *So too in contemplating Godliness with such a thought, that clings well to it as to a matter touching oneself.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 252. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated (already in SOURCES P9; repeated here as the base of the slice).

### MR-02 · Climb the natural soul's ladder - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** Contemplation produces five rungs in the natural soul: agreement, the good thought, the heart moved, kavanah (intellectual love and fear), simple will. All five are 'serving G-d with the body'. - **How to do it:** Start from agreement (hoda'ah). Work until the thought clings (MR-01). Then work until the heart moves. Then keep the whole length of the contemplation in mind and heart. The top rung is above reason. - **Why it works:** The 'intellectual soul called the natural, living soul' can itself agree, acknowledge and be moved in mind and heart. - **When / for whom:** Everyone. Each person should find his own rung and not pretend to a higher one (KH 890). - **When not:** Agreement alone 'from far away' is cold and passes in an hour (KH 643). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** All five rungs belong to the natural soul. The G-dly soul's own arousal is 'Godly arousal' of a different order (KH 313-314). - **Quotes:** - «ולא נולד מזה רק בחינת ההסכם להיות סור מרע» — *All that is born from it is agreement to turn from evil.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 257. - «הכל הוא בנפש השכלית שנקרא נפש הטבעית החיונית, שגם היא תסכים ותודה בהודאה והתפעלות במוח והתפעלות בלב» — *It is all in the intellectual soul, called the natural living soul, which also agrees and acknowledges, with arousal in mind and heart.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 312. - «וכל זה נקרא עובד ה׳ בגופו ולא נקרא עובד ה׳ בנשמתו» — *All this is called serving G-d with his body, not serving G-d with his soul.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 312. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated (SOURCES P10 has the full ladder).

### MR-03 · Toil until the heart answers, then let the heart deepen the mind - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** The natural soul is moved by Godliness only through great toil. The heart does not follow a moved thought easily. When it does, the heart's excitement must go back up and widen the contemplation, not cut it short. - **How to do it:** Toil in thought until the heart is moved. When the heart is moved, do not make a 'something' of it. Use its force to widen and deepen the contemplation. The heart should be 'in the mind', like a merchant whose excitement over a deal rises into his calculation. - **Why it works:** Bodily things move the natural soul by nature; Godly things only by toil, because the Godliness in them is hidden. Excitement with no mind under it has nothing to stand on and is lost. - **When / for whom:** For the 'quick-hearted', whose heart jumps right away. For the slow, the heart comes after a while and the mind rests; both are valid. - **When not:** Do not forbid the heart's arousal (that is an error, KH 743). Do not let it shorten the depth. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul is moved 'of itself'; the natural soul 'only through great toil' (KH 426). - **Quotes:** - «וכמו כן רחוק מנפש הטבעית הגופנית להתפעל מעניני אלהות רק על ידי יגיעה רבה» — *Likewise the bodily natural soul is far from being moved by Godly matters, except through great toil.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 426. - «אדרבה התפעלות זו תוסיף תת כחה בכח בינתו להרחיב ולהעמיק לתכליתו יותר» — *On the contrary, this arousal should add its strength to his understanding, to widen and deepen it further.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 742. - «כאדם שמתפעל בלבו במשא ומתן טוב וכהאי גוונא שלבו עולה במוחו ואינו מרגיש אותה» — *Like a man moved in his heart by a good deal, whose heart rises into his mind and he does not feel it.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 746. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated.

### MR-04 · Many analogies and explanations - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** To bring a Godly matter close to the human, natural intellect, use many analogies (meshalim) and explanations. Through 'human pictures' the natural mind grasps what the G-dly mind grasps above. - **How to do it:** Pick analogies from nature and the soul (the soul's life in the body, light from the sun, speech from the soul). Explain at length and in width. Only after the length and width of analogies does one reach the 'depth' that comes out of them. - **Why it works:** Ve'ahavta asks for love in the heart of flesh. That needs 'toil of the soul and toil of the flesh': bringing the matter near the intellect. The animal soul's human intellect can be reached 'in every detail' by natural pictures. - **When / for whom:** Before and during prayer, for Ve'ahavta. Pleasure 'clothed in analogies and human intellect' is the infant stage (MA 1501). - **When not:** If one is moved only by the explanation and keeps only what he grasped, it shrinks and dies (KH 393-396). The aim is the 'essence' inside the explanation. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul is moved by the Godliness in the grasp itself; the natural soul by the explanation (KH 381-386). - **Quotes:** - «לקרב ענין אלקי אל השכל עי ריבוי משלים והסברים» — *To bring a Godly matter near the intellect through many analogies and explanations.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 63608 (OCR). - «כי הרי יוכל לקרב הדבר השגה אלקיי בכל פרט ופרט גם בשכל האנושי שבמוח הגשמי דנה"ט» — *For one can bring the Godly grasp, in every detail, near even the human intellect in the physical brain of the natural soul.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 3534 (OCR). - «ודווקא אחר האריכות והרוחב במשלים בעיון רב יבא לבחינת העומק שמובן מהם» — *Only after the length and width of analogies, with much study, does one reach the depth understood from them.* The Gate of Unity (incl. Kuntres HaHisbonenus), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820).txt`, line 101. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated (MO 63608 is in the 'Kuntreisim' volume, OCR).

### MR-05 · Truth persuades by itself - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** When a Godly grasp comes from above, even the animal soul's coarse human intellect and heart of flesh will agree and admit the truth. 'Truth shows its own way.' - **How to do it:** Let the G-dly soul grasp the matter clearly and truly. Do not argue with the animal soul's opinions; let the clear truth stand in the human mind until it 'requires' the truth. - **Why it works:** Light of truth in the G-dly soul's mind is caught as plain truth in the human intellect too. - **When / for whom:** After real grasp, not before. - **When not:** Agreement is the floor, not the goal (MR-02). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul sees; the animal soul only agrees and admits. - **Quotes:** - «אזי גם חושך הגס הטבעי דנה'ב שהוא שכל אנושי ולב בשר הטבעי יסכים גם הוא ויודה על האמת» — *Then even the coarse natural darkness of the animal soul, the human intellect and natural heart of flesh, will also agree and admit the truth.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 3682 (OCR). - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated (OCR).

### MR-06 · Detail, not generality - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** Contemplate in detail (derech prat): this world, this sphere, this level, step by step. General contemplation fools a person into thinking G-d is close, when 'from very far G-d appears to him'. - **How to do it:** Go from one particular to the one above it, until you join them into the whole. Beginners may start general and slowly get used to detail. Make the long chain short by habit, until one glance at physical things holds it all (GU 328-331). - **Why it works:** A particular grasp is fixed in the soul 'as closeness'. - **When / for whom:** For anyone; beginners start general. - **When not:** General-only contemplation leads to false imaginings (GU 137). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Not stated as an animal-soul rule. Ours: the animal soul's mind works in particulars; detail is its door. - **Quotes:** - «ובאמת מרחוק מאד יהו״ה נראה לו בהיותו דרך כלל לבד» — *In truth, G-d appears to him from very far, while it is only general.* The Gate of Unity (incl. Kuntres HaHisbonenus), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820).txt`, line 124. - «שכל פרטי בהשגה בדבר פרטי, הרי יוקבע בנפשו בבחינת הקירוב ביותר» — *Every particular grasped in a particular thing is fixed in his soul as greatest closeness.* The Gate of Unity (incl. Kuntres HaHisbonenus), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/The Gate of Unity (Lubavitch, pub. 1820).txt`, line 125. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated as a rule of contemplation; filing under the animal soul is ours.

### MR-07 · See G-d in physical works with eyes of flesh - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** The natural soul must toil to know G-d 'in mind and heart of flesh' through His physical works: 'lift your eyes and see who created these'. Psalms praises G-d only through physical heaven and earth. - **How to do it:** Look with physical sight at the heavens, the elements, this world. Ask who created these. Use the praises of Psalms about His wonders in physical works. Contemplate creation from nothing until it is 'as if seen with the senses'. - **Why it works:** Seeing is stronger than hearing. The 'eye below' (the natural soul's sight, once refined) is rooted higher than the G-dly soul's eye of the intellect. Without this toil in the natural soul, there is no share in seeing the Essence later. - **When / for whom:** Daily, in prayer (Pesukei deZimra are praises of physical works). - **When not:** Not a substitute for mesirus nefesh and refinement, which this full sight needs (NM 148). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul sees with 'the eye of the intellect'; the natural soul with 'eyes of flesh'. - **Quotes:** - «אם לא ישתדל בכל כח יגיעתו בנפש הטבעית להכיר במוח ולב בשר למינדע ליה לקוב"ה בהאי עלמא» — *If he does not strive with all his toil in the natural soul to recognize, in mind and heart of flesh, to know G-d in this world.* Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt`, line 148. - «בסיפורי השבחים שבתהלים בכל נפלאות ה' במעשיו הגשמיים דוקא» — *Through the praises in Psalms, in all G-d's wonders in His physical works specifically.* Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (Lubavitch, c. 1820).txt`, line 148. - «ע"י התלבשות של נה"א יוכל גם השכל האנושי להתפעל ממש מהתהוות הבריאה יש מאין ויוקלט הדבר במוחו ויתאמת כאילו רואה בראיה חושיית ממש» — *Through the G-dly soul's clothing, the human intellect too can be truly moved by creation from nothing, and it is taken into his mind and made true as if he saw it with his senses.* Derech Chaim, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Derech Chaim (scans, OCR).txt`, line 511 (OCR). - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated.

### MR-08 · He is your life: good for me - **Family:** self-interest and good - **What it is:** Love G-d 'for He is your life'. 'Nearness to G-d is good for me', literally. This is self-pleasure, and it is allowed when the aim is Godliness itself. - **How to do it:** Contemplate that G-d is the life of your life, the way a man knows he wants to live and not die of hunger. Let the soul feel real vitality from Godliness. Keep the aim on G-d, not on the feeling. - **Why it works:** The natural soul's deepest will is to live (TC 2825). 'Because He is your life' is the verse's own reason for love (KH 141). - **When / for whom:** The love of 'with all your heart', both inclinations. - **When not:** Even full vitality from G-d is still 'ratzo' and not completeness (TC 2425). It fails if the aim becomes self-filling ('lifting the soul in vain', TC 2425). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Dveikus (the G-dly soul's bond) needs no reason; love needs 'because He is your life' (KH 143-146). - **Quotes:** - «לאהבה את הוי' אלקיך כי הוא חייך ממש וכמאמר קרבת אלקים לי טוב לי ממש» — *To love G-d, for He is literally your life, as it says: nearness to G-d is good for me, literally.* Toras Chaim, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Toras Chaim (Lubavitch, c. 1820-1827).txt`, line 2424. - «דכי הוא חייך נתינת טעם הוא, למה תאהוב לפי שהוא חייך» — *'For He is your life' gives the reason: why love? Because He is your life.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 141. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated.

### MR-09 · Fire from below first - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Prayer is the sacrifice. The G-dly soul ('Adam') offers the animal soul ('the animal'). As on the altar, 'fire from the ordinary' (aish shel hedyot) must come first: the natural heat of the heart of flesh, set alight by contemplation. Then fire from above descends. - **How to do it:** Contemplate until the heart of flesh burns with longing (reshpei aish). Let that natural heat carry the animal soul up, to give itself over at Ve'ahavta. Then love from above (Shemoneh Esreh) binds the good and pushes off the waste. - **Why it works:** Below rises first, then above descends. The natural soul's heat is the fuel. - **When / for whom:** Weekday prayer (refining). Shabbos prayer is different: delight without the heart of flesh (DC 525). - **When not:** This alone does not change the animal soul's nature (MR-15). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Shalhevet Kah (the G-dly flame) is 'not mixed with the heat of the material heart' (MO 33690). - **Quotes:** - «וע"י ההתלהבות שעל"י ההתבוננות הוא כמו אש של הדיוט שע"ג המזבח ושורף ומעלה לנפש הבהמית» — *Through the burning that comes from contemplation, like the ordinary fire on the altar, it burns and raises the animal soul.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 50064 (OCR). - «כמו התשוקה ברשפי אש בחום הטבעי בלב בשר לעלות ולידבק באלקיי» — *Like the longing in flames, in the natural heat of the heart of flesh, to rise and cleave to Godliness.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans), `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 33675 (OCR). - «כשמתפעל באה"ר העולה ברשפי אש שלהבת יה מחום הטבעי שבלב בשר דוקא» — *When he is moved with love and awe rising in flames, from the natural heat of the heart of flesh specifically.* Toras Chaim, `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Toras Chaim (Lubavitch, c. 1820-1827).txt`, line 1464. - **Whose / stated or ours:** Mitteler Rebbe; stated (MO is OCR).

### MR-10 · Feed your animal first - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** 'One may not eat before feeding his animal.' In prayer, first give the animal soul its food: Pesukei deZimra and Yotzer, with the angels' fervor and natural love and fear. Only then comes 'human food', the G-dly soul's Shema. - **How to do it:** Before Baruch She'amar: wake up and accept the yoke in deed (Canaanite slave). Pesukei deZimra to Yotzer: let the natural mind and heart be moved (Hebrew slave). Hear in Yotzer how the angels burn and long, as a model for your own heat. Then Shema. - **Why it works:** The animal soul and the 'smallness' of the G-dly soul are fed by the same level as the Ofanim and Chayos. ## 2b. The methods: R. Aharon of Strashelye (his, not the Mitteler Rebbe's)

R. Aharon was the Alter Rebbe's student and the Mitteler Rebbe's rival. Their argument was about the natural soul's arousal. The Mitteler Rebbe warned against excitement that is self-feeling (KH level zero, 'strange fire'). R. Aharon's introduction to Sha'arei Avodah attacks those who 'push away the heart's arousal' and says the first arousal must come from the animal soul's 'yesh'. Note that the Mitteler Rebbe, in KH 28-47, *also* attacks those who forbid feeling. The real line between them is narrower than the slogans (see §4).

### MR-RA-01 · Arouse the love from the 'yesh' first, then refine it - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** R. Aharon: the G-dly soul shows itself only through the animal soul's traits. Afterward, knowledge (da'at) separates the self-feeling until the love is pure. - **How to do it:** Contemplate in order to be moved in the heart with love and fear. Let it come, even mixed. ## 3. What does NOT work (as the slice says)

1. **Wanting the arousal itself.** "Level zero": his whole wish is to be moved and get vitality from it (KH 226). «הא׳ הגרוע שבכולם והוא שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» KH 226.
2. **Shouting that is not to G-d.** A cry "only to make his voice heard or to push off a foreign thought" (KH 55). Heat "from boiling blood alone", strange fire (KH 175).
3. **Cold agreement from afar.** It lasts "an hour or two" and is gone (KH 643). «והנולד מזה שהוא ההסכם הוא בקרירות עצומה ולא מספיק רק לפי שעה» KH 643.
4. **Fluency without life.** Learning and repeating until one can answer questions, "from the lip outward" (KH 724-725). Remembering the general idea by repetition, with no Godly light, "like one who remembers worldly matters" (KH 395-396).
5. **Shortening into a feeling.** As soon as the heart is moved he cuts the length and depth; "this sick evil loses everything" (KH 735-737). Heart without mind does not last (KH 740).
6. **Superficial hearing.** Hearing "quickly and lightly, from on top" (KH 685), like hearing a song in passing (KH 683).
7. **General contemplation only.** "From very far G-d appears to him" (GU 124).
8. **Arousal with no deed.** "An empty vessel holds nothing"; a flame in the air; a straw fire (KH 777-779).
9. **No broken heart.** The "whole" person takes nothing in (KH 691-692); the new generation that began with great ideas (KH 713-722).
10. **Understanding the truth and staying put.** The stone heart understands sovev and memale and still "stubbornly insists" (TC 4646); Amalek in the natural soul fights the light "with many kinds of tricks, many foreign thoughts" (TC 4646).
11. **Expecting prayer to change the nature.** After prayer he is "in all his natural traits as he was" (DC 512). Thinking otherwise is imagination (DC 512).
12. **Learning without the rise of teshuvah.** "Even if he learns constantly, it does no good or repair to his soul" (AR 188).
13. **One-time teshuvah.** A big cry once and back to business; the heart returns to its stubbornness (PI 20).
14. **Self-deception and fake nullification.** "Do not fool yourself" (KH 633). The test: if insulted he is upset to the point of illness, his 'bitul' was pride (KH 813).
15. **Too much worldly cleverness / heart sunk in business.** The natural soul's "yechidah" is spent on business (KH 644; DC 1317; KH 898).
16. **Overeating and lust in eating.** It strengthens the evil and makes horse-like pride (DC 445, 607; PI 50-52).
17. **R. Aharon's list:** knowledge without the heart's arousal "does nothing" (SA 10); avoiding the heart's arousal leaves the animal soul's traits "in full force" (SA 8); heart without knowledge is mixed with self (SA 5).

## 4. Gaps and unverified leads

1. **The MR / Strashelye dispute is narrower than its reputation.** KH 28-47 attacks those who "forbid the heart's arousal like a carcass" and cry out against them; that is R. Aharon's complaint too (SA 8). Their difference is about *which* arousal counts (KH: arousal from the thing itself, not self-feeling; SA: start with self-mixed feeling and refine). A builder should not present them as "mind vs heart". The polemical letters on this (Ma'aneh Lashon Chabad, the Igros) were not read in full.
2. **Avodas HaLevi is unusable.** The OCR file (`Chassidus-txt/R. Aharon HaLevi of Strashelye (1766-1828)/Avodas HaLevi (scans, OCR).txt`) is a letter-substitution garble (e.g. ה→ס, א→6) for almost all of its 5,150 lines; only a few pages in vol. 2 (~lines 4600-4650) read normally. No quote taken. Needs a clean text.
3. **Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah** (R. Aharon) is in the folder but was not in the brief's list; not searched.
4. **Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR)** is 72,000 lines. Only targeted hits were read (Kuntreisim, Bamidbar 5, Drushei Chasunah, Devarim). The "fire of hedyot" theme recurs (lines 33279-33690, 50051-50064) and likely has more practical detail. Biurei HaZohar (OCR) was not in the brief and not read.
5. **Toras Chaim** (10.7 MB typed) was searched by terms only. TC 78-96 (natural traits born from contemplation, then growing beyond their reason) and TC 2118 (the animal soul's powers move all at once, "like a circle") are promising for how the natural soul's love behaves; not developed here.
6. **Derech Chaim** gives a full "three servants" map of the morning prayer (Canaanite slave → Hebrew slave → Hebrew maidservant, DC 494-560). Only the first two were read closely.
7. **Poke'ach Ivrim** is Yiddish OCR for simple penitents; it holds many body-level practices (fasting substitutes, charity, eyes, sleep). Only lines 8-58 were read.
