## Why a meditation

Every concept in Chassidus points to the unity of God, and that unity cannot be understood by definition. It can only be glimpsed, in contemplation (*hisbonenus*). So behind each subject below is a sitting, and the sitting, not the idea, is what matters.

## How to sit with a section

This is *hisbonenus*, the Chabad way of contemplation, not mindfulness. There is nothing here to watch in the body and nothing to empty out.

1. **Hold** the one line of teaching. It is the object for the whole sitting.
2. **The move.** Think it through slowly: open it detail by detail, bring it down into your own day, take the source's own picture and remember that it is only a picture, and make it particular: you, here, now.
3. **Stay.** Bind the mind to it, the Tanya's *da'at* (Tanya 3): say the line again slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought. The pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.
4. **From inside** describes the glimpse. It is never asked for. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts: what you held in your mind carries the act that follows.
5. **Back into the day.** Say it with your lips in words of Torah or prayer, do the one mitzvah or act of Torah in your own measure, and carry the return line through the day.

**Tie** says whether the move rests on derivation steps the entry marks *stated*, or on our own *reading*; the steps are listed. **Thin** marks subjects whose tie to the unity the entry calls partly thin (4 of 12 here); their sittings are kept modest and say why. **Kept** records a disagreement the entry keeps between the sources; it is left standing, not smoothed. **Before you start** is a safety line where a subject touches health, fasting or sleep.

## S174 · Exile · גלות

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F07 The home below, F11 Darkness turned to light, F06 The soul as part of the One. Tie: stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The descent is real, and it is for something: your soul came down into this world to join its lowest part to Him. In exile He is nearer than it looks.

**Hold.** 'Though it is a great descent and a real exile … it is to bind and unite them with Or Ein Sof.' (Tanya 37)

**The move.** Open it. Each soul's coming down into a body and an animal soul is a real exile, and it happens only to refine them and bind them to the infinite light; the revelation of that light in this physical world depends on 'our deeds and service throughout the exile'; and the Sfas Emes: no evil comes down from above, exile is only a hiding, caused by the receivers' lack, and so it has an end, for 'falsehood has no legs'. Bring it down: the place you would not have chosen, this job, this city, this century. The Maggid's picture: in exile the King is on the road, and even a villager can reach him at the inn; only a picture, since He is no king of flesh and has no road. You, here, on the road: whoever thinks of Him in exile finds Him dwelling with him at once.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'to bind and unite them with Or Ein Sof.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The relief of finding what was never gone: the King met at the inn, far from the palace and near. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'There is nothing besides Him.' Do one mitzvah today in the place you would least choose to be. Through the day, return to: 'the King at the inn'.

**Kept.** Why exile? The Alter Rebbe places its purpose in building the dwelling; Breslov places its cause in failed faith, prayer and the Land; the Sfas Emes calls it a hiding that rules only over one who does not know.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:17 (line 511): אף שהיא ירידה גדולה ובחינת גלות ממש … ולקשרם ולייחדם באוראיןסוף ברוךהוא Tanya 37: each soul-spark's descent into this world is 'a real exile', and it is only in order to refine the animal soul and its share of the world and bind and unite them with Or Ein Sof. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:1 (line 495): תכלית השלימות הזה של ימות המשיח ותחיית המתים, שהוא גילוי אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא בעולם הזה הגשמי – תלוי במעשינו ועבודתנו כל זמן משך הגלות Tanya 37: the revelation of Or Ein Sof in the physical world (the dwelling below) depends on our deeds precisely during the exile. - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 20:1 (line 69): היום בזמן הגלות הוא בקל להשיג רוח הקודש יותר מבזמן הבית … כך היום בגלות כשאדם מחשב בדביקות השיתב"ה אזי מיד שורה עמו ושוכן אצלו The Maggid: in exile the King is 'on the road', so whoever thinks of Him in devekus finds Him dwelling with him at once; exile makes the Presence nearer, not farther.

## S175 · The exile of the Shechinah · גלות השכינה

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F06 The soul as part of the One, F04 Filling and surrounding, F05 The Essence beyond both. Tie: stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 6). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The Presence that dwells with you is wrapped in what hides it, even in you. The wrapping is real, and nothing in her essence changes.

**Hold.** 'That is the secret of the exile of the Shechinah: to arouse great mercy for the Name of God dwelling with us.' (Tanya 45)

**The move.** Open it. The spark of Godliness that gives your soul life comes down from Ein Sof, who fills and surrounds all worlds, and is clothed in a coarse body; among the husks and the nations her light is in exile too, yet 'there is no change at all in the essence of the Shechinah'; and the Maggid: pray always for her redemption, not for your own needs. Bring it down: your own day, its habits and distractions wrapped around the spark that gives you life. The Tanya's picture is Yaakov who kissed Rachel and wept, mercy for what is hidden; only a picture, since the Shechinah is not a person and is not diminished. You, now: the Name of God dwells with you, in this day, wrapped and whole.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'the Name of God dwelling with us.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Mercy rather than fear, as Yaakov kissed Rachel and wept: close to the Presence that is hidden, for her sake and not your own. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips from the Amidah: 'May our eyes see Your return to Zion in mercy.' Then, for a minute, pour out your heart to Him in your own words, asking for her and not for yourself. Through the day, return to: 'the Name dwelling with us'.

**Kept.** Is the exile of the Shechinah real, and does it reach her essence? Iggeret HaKodesh 25: no change at all in her essence. The Rashab: exile is when the contraction becomes 'as if compelled'. Radomsk: a widow whose pain peaks on the festivals, to be prayed for. Sfas Emes: only a hiding. The Mitteler Rebbe: in exile He withdraws into the Essence, and just then He looks at the bare deed. The entry reads these as two kinds of knowing; no text says so.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 45:2 (line 654): והוא סוד גלות השכינה. ועל זה נאמר: ״וישוב אל ה׳ וירחמהו״, לעורר רחמים רבים על שם ה׳ השוכן אתנו Tanya 45: the spark of Godliness that gives one's soul life, drawn from the Ein Sof that fills, surrounds and before whom all is nothing, is bound in a coarse body: that is the exile of the Shechinah, and mercy is aroused for the Name dwelling with us. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 25:24 (line 1364): אבל אין שום שינוי חס ושלום בעצמות השכינה, שהיא ״דבר ה׳״ ו״רוח פיו״ IH 25: the Shechinah's light is in exile among the husks and the nations, yet there is no change in the Shechinah's essence; the exile is in the radiance and its screens, not in Him. - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 6:3 (line 19): רק יתפלל תמיד בשביל השכינה שתגאל מגלות … מפני כי אוריתא וקב"ה כולא חד The Maggid: pray always for the Shechinah's redemption, not one's own needs; a mitzvah done with an ulterior motive makes separation, since Torah and God are one.

## S176 · Redemption · גאולה

*Face F07 The home below. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart', F11 Darkness turned to light. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The redemption is 'there is none besides Him' seen with open eyes in this physical world, and it is being built now by plain deeds.

**Hold.** 'Israel will see eye to eye, as at the giving of the Torah: "you have been shown to know that Havayah is God; there is none besides Him".' (Tanya 37)

**The move.** Open it. The purpose of creation is this lowest world as His dwelling, and its completion is the revelation of the infinite light in this physical world; it is built by our deeds throughout the exile; and the last redemption completes the purpose of all four exiles, 'a redemption after which there is no exile'. Bring it down: your own street and kitchen, the same brick and bread, where 'there is none besides Him' is to be seen. Seeing 'eye to eye' is the Tanya's picture; only a picture, since He is not seen as a thing is seen. You, now, doing one plain thing: that is a brick of it.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'there is none besides Him.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The joy of the plain man whose king lives with him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'You have been shown to know that Havayah is God; there is none besides Him.' Do one plain mitzvah today in your own kitchen or street. Through the day, return to: 'eye to eye'.

**Kept.** For R. Nachman the redemption is the defeat of 'nature'; in Chabad, nature itself is revealed as Elokim, one with Havayah. The Rayatz's 'Israel is redeemed only through teshuvah' stands in the text without a tie to the unity.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:12 (line 506): וישראל יראו עין בעין כבמתן תורה, דכתיב: ״אתה הראת לדעת כי ה׳ הוא האלהים אין עוד מלבדו״ Tanya 37: redemption is when the animal soul and the whole world's nogah become a chariot, and 'there is none besides Him' is seen eye to eye. - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 27:2 (line 142): שבגמר העבודה והבירורים של גלות הלזו, יוגמר ויסתיים גם ענין ותכלית כל הגליות (שהרי הגאולה מגלות זו היא גאולה שאין אחרי' גלות) The Rebbe: the final redemption completes the purpose of all four exiles; it is the one redemption with no exile after it, and it depends on our deeds.

## S177 · Moshiach · משיח

*Face F06 The soul as part of the One. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F07 The home below, F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The single point of the soul, one with His Essence, is what Moshiach is. It is in every soul and in the whole world, and it will be revealed.

**Hold.** 'The main point of Moshiach is yechidah.' (the Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus)

**The move.** Open it. Of the soul's five levels the yechidah is its single point, above all parts, 'yachid' and not one of many; David reached nefesh, Eliyahu ruach, Moshe neshamah, Adam chayah, and Moshiach will reach yechidah; and the Besht asked Moshiach when he would come and was told: when your teaching is revealed and your wellsprings spread outward. Bring it down: the simplest 'I' under all your parts, the one that is the same on a good day and a bad one. The Tanya's picture is the candle flame, always reaching up to its source; only a picture, since a flame is fire and the soul is not. You, now: the yechidah in you is that same single point.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'yechidah, the single point.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** A core no one has ever touched, single and whole. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'I believe with perfect faith in the coming of Moshiach.' Say one line of prayer today with your thought on the very words you say; the Chernobyler says such a prayer builds your own part of Moshiach. Through the day, return to: 'the single point'.

**Kept.** A single point or an assembled stature? Inyanah: Moshiach is yechidah, single beyond parts. The Chernobyler: a stature built from all souls, each united prayer building its limb. The entry sets them side by side; neither text addresses the other.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 5:4 (line 27): ענינו העיקרי של משיח הוא – יחידה. כידוע, שדוד זכה לבחי' נפש, אלי' – לרוח, משה – לנשמה, אדם הראשון – לחי' ומשיח יזכה ליחידה The Rebbe: Moshiach's essence is yechidah, the soul's single point beyond parts ('yachid' not 'echad'), the yechidah of the whole chain of worlds. - Ba'al Shem Tov, Kuntres Meirat Einayim 86:1 (line 114): ששאל למשיח אימתי אתי מר, והשיב כשיתגלה תורתך ויפוצו מעיינותיך חוצה The Besht's letter (via the Degel): Moshiach comes when the Besht's teaching spreads outward; the link of Moshiach to spreading the wellsprings is the Besht's own. - Me'or Einayim, Pinchas 13 (line 518): שצריך כל א׳ מישראל לתקן ולהכין חלק קומת משיח השייך לנשמתו … ולכך נקרא משיח כי משיח הוא לשון דבור The Chernobyler (Pinchas 13): every prayer in which thought and speech unite builds the piece of Moshiach's stature that belongs to one's soul.

## S178 · The heels of Moshiach · עקבתא דמשיחא

*Face F11 Darkness turned to light. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F07 The home below, F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** In the lowest generation, where the dark is doubled, the highest light reaches down, and the plain deed is what holds.

**Hold.** 'This is a great rule: whatever is higher can extend lower.' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Sha'arei Orah)

**The move.** Open it. In the heels of Moshiach the Shechinah has fallen to the feet, the level of deed, so there is no way to cleave to her and turn her darkness to light except through action, through charity; the highest reaches lowest, so the last generation refines even Esau's heel; and the Rebbe: the task of this generation is to turn doubled darkness into doubled light. Bring it down: this generation, its noise and its coarseness, and one plain deed you can do in it today. The Rayatz's picture is the heels that hold up the whole body; only a picture, and it only settles the ear. You, now, in this generation: your plain deed is what holds the body up.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'whatever is higher can extend lower.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The dignity of the struggle, and the sweetness of light that came out of darkness. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'There is nothing besides Him.' Give one coin to charity today, the deed Iggeret HaKodesh 9 names for this generation. Through the day, return to: 'the heel holds the body'.

**Kept.** What is the main service in the heels? Iggeret HaKodesh 9: charity. Kuntres Acharon 8: prayer. The Mitteler Rebbe: the bare yoke of practical mitzvot. The Rayatz: 'a heel must know it is a heel; the main thing is action'. The texts leave these side by side.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 9:3 (line 1093): מה שאין כן בעקבות משיחא, שנפלה ״סוכת דוד״ עד בחינת רגלים ועקביים, שהיא בחינת עשיה, אין דרך לדבקה בה באמת ולהפכא חשוכא לנהורא דילה IH 9: in the heels the fallen Shechinah is at the level of feet and heels (Asiyah), so the only way to cleave to her and turn her darkness to light is by action, charity. - Sha'arei Orah, שער החנוכה / ה,א:2 (line 16): שזהו כלל גדול שכל הגבוה גבוה ביותר יוכל להתפשט למטה מטה ביותר The Mitteler Rebbe (Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah): the last generation in the heels of Moshiach refines even Esau's heel, because the higher a thing, the lower it can reach. - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 27:3 (line 143): כי הלא עבודתנו זו היא הכנה אחרונה לגאולה השלימה: להפך את החשך כפול ומכופל – לאור כפול ומכופל The Rebbe: because the last generations are the heels, their task is greater: to turn doubled darkness into doubled light.

## S179 · The future: the days of Moshiach and resurrection · תחיית המתים / לעתיד לבוא

*Face F07 The home below. Also touches F04 Filling and surrounding, F05 The Essence beyond both, F01 The world said into being now. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 4). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The whole point of this world is fulfilled in the body: the unlimited light, and the power only He has to make a physical thing, shown openly here.

**Hold.** 'The days of Moshiach, and especially when the dead live, are the purpose and completion of the creation of this world.' (Tanya 36)

**The move.** Open it. Gan Eden receives the measured light that fills; the resurrection will receive the light that surrounds, 'not by way of contraction, measure and limit', and in this world; and the Rebbe: only the Holy One Himself can create a physical thing from nothing, and that power, found in physicality, will be revealed at the resurrection, which is why the highest reward is in the body. Bring it down: your own hands at work today, the plainest physical thing you have. The Tanya's picture is a dwelling, a home made in the lowest place; only a picture, since He needs no house. You, now, in this body: the purpose lands here.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'the purpose and completion of this world.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The joy of the plain man whose king lives with him: the body is where the purpose lands. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips the blessing of the Amidah: 'Blessed are You, Havayah, who gives life to the dead.' Do one mitzvah today with your hands, slowly. Through the day, return to: 'the purpose is here'.

**Kept.** Nature in the future: R. Nachman says nature will be nullified entirely and only providence remain; Tanya 36 says Torah gives strength 'not to be nullified' in the revealed light, and the Rebbe speaks of the physical itself revealed. The contrast is the entry's own and loose.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:7 (line 490): ונודע, שימות המשיח, ובפרט כשיחיו המתים, הם תכלית ושלימות בריאת עולם הזה, שלכך נברא מתחילתו Tanya 36: the days of Moshiach, and especially the resurrection, are the purpose and completion of this world's creation. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 17:6 (line 1211): אבל גילוי ההארה שבתחיית המתים, יהיה מבחינת ״סובב כל עלמין״, שאינה בבחינת צמצום ושיעור וגבול IH 17: Gan Eden receives memale; the resurrection will receive sovev, the unlimited light, in this world. - Likkutei Sichos, כרך לט / מכתבים כלליים לראש השנה / מכתבים כלליים מכ"ק אדמו"ר שליט"א / 441:4 (line 38439): שכן רק הקב"ה בעצמו ("הוא לבדו, יתברך") יש לו הכוח והיכולת לברוא דבר גשמי, "יש מאין", ומעלה זו, הנמצאת דווקא בגשמיות, תתגלה בזמן תחיית המתים The Rebbe (general letter): the highest reward is in the body (Ramban), because only He Himself can create physical yesh from nothing, and that power hidden in physicality is revealed at the resurrection.

## S180 · The nations of the world · אומות העולם

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity, F02 The higher and the lower unity, F11 Darkness turned to light. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 5). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** Even the powers that seem to stand on their own live from a spark of His word held in exile within them, and at the end all will be turned to call on His Name.

**Hold.** '"Then I will turn to the peoples … to call all of them in the Name of God."' (Iggeret HaKodesh 25)

**The move.** Open it. All life, the nations' included, comes from the spark of 'the word of God', reaching them through their princes and many screens, and there it is 'in exile within them'; the Maharash: they draw life from His exaltation, before which all worlds are as nothing; and at the end Israel's light 'will illuminate the darkness of the nations too'. Bring it down: the world's powers in today's news, governments and markets that seem to run on their own strength. The Iggeret HaKodesh's picture is life passing down through many screens; only a picture, since His word is never cut off from Him. You, now, reading the news: the word of God is in exile there, not absent.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'to call all of them in the Name of God.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The relief of finding what was never gone: the word of God in exile there, not absent. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Then I will turn to the peoples, to call all of them in the Name of God.' Do one kindness today for someone outside your own circle. Through the day, return to: 'the word of God, in exile there'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the tie is stated in Iggeret HaKodesh 25 and the Maharash, but Tanya 1:18 is a classification of souls that does not mention the unity, and reading the nations as the unity's negative image is the entry's own. Keep this sitting modest.

**Kept.** How are the nations held? Tanya 1:18 and 19:8: their souls are from the impure husks, 'things in their own right'. Iggeret HaKodesh 25 and Tanya 36:11: the word of God is in exile within them, and at the end they will call on His Name and be lit by Israel's light. The Rebbe: the Noachide laws kept because God commanded them bring the Torah's peace. Harsh and universal texts are kept side by side.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 25:21 (line 1361): הם עובדי עבודהזרה ממש, עד עת קץ, שיבולע המות והסטרא אחרא, ו״אז אהפוך אל עמים כו׳ לקרוא כולם בשם ה׳״ IH 25: the nations' life is a spark of 'the word of God' in exile within their princes; at the end all will be turned to call in the Name of God. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:11 (line 494): ומיתרון ההארה לישראל – יגיה חשך האומות גם כן Tanya 36: in the messianic revelation the light of Israel will illuminate the nations' darkness too: 'all flesh shall see together'. - Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ט / בכה תבכה כו' / רצב:1 (line 2317): כי הנה כתיב רם על כל גוים ה' שהעכו"ם מקבלים חיות מצד רוממות ה' והתנשאותו בעצמותו כביכול The Maharash (5629): the nations draw life from His exaltation in His Essence, before which 'darkness is as light' and all is as nothing; from this comes their rule over Israel in exile.

## S181 · The Land of Israel · ארץ ישראל

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F07 The home below, F01 The world said into being now, F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 6). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** There is a place where the hiding is thinnest, where His eyes always rest; and the task is to make wherever you are into that place.

**Hold.** '"The eyes of Havayah your God are on it always."' (Iggeret HaKodesh 14)

**The move.** Open it. Outside the Land the flow from above is clothed in nature, 'the utmost concealment of Godliness', where one can say 'my strength made me this wealth'; in the Land it passes through nature 'by way of passage, not true clothing'; the 'eyes' always on it are the light of wisdom in which the infinite light is clothed; and the Tzemach Tzedek said: 'make here the Land of Israel'. Bring it down: the part of the world providence gave you, your home, your street and your work, where, the Rebbe writes, the Land's holiness is to take in the physical. Likkutei Torah's picture is sunlight through clear glass; only a picture, and it only settles the ear. You, here, in this corner: make it the Land.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'His eyes are on it always.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like sunlight through clear glass: the light passes, and the glass is still there. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'The eyes of Havayah your God are on it always.' Touch the mezuzah as you go out today, and do one mitzvah in the place you are going. Through the day, return to: 'make here the Land of Israel'.

**Kept.** Does the Land's flow pass through an intermediary? Likkutei Torah: it passes through the prince of Asiyah 'by way of passage'. Peri HaAretz: its food is from the Holy One Himself 'and not through any prince'. A difference of emphasis about the same point.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Masei 3:1 (line 2710): המכוון מזה שיהיה כל העולם כולו אז במדרגת ארץ ישראל דעכשיו … אבל בא"י נמשך ההשפעה ממל' דאצילות ע"י התלבשות במל' דבריאה ויצירה לבדן מבלי שיצטרך להתלבש ג"כ במל' דעשיה LT Masei 3: in the Land the flow from Malchus of Atzilus passes through Asiyah without being clothed in it, so the concealment of nature is least; in the future the whole world will be at this level. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 14:8 (line 1158): וזהו שכתוב ״תמיד עיני ה׳ אלהיך בה״, שה״עינים״ הם כינוים להמשכת והארת אור החכמה … ו״אוירא דארץ ישראל מחכים״ IH 14: the 'eyes of God' always on the Land are the drawing of the light of Chochmah, in which Or Ein Sof is clothed, renewed each year. - Likkutei Sichos, כרך לט / מכתבים כלליים לראש השנה / מכתבים כלליים מכ"ק אדמו"ר שליט"א / 442:1 (line 38442): שהקדושה של האלקות תחדור ותקיף גם את הגשמיות של חלק העולם שההשגחה העליונה הועידה עבורם The Rebbe (general letter): the Land's virtue is that its holiness takes in its physicality; Jews abroad must bring that virtue into the physical part of the world providence assigned them.

## S182 · Jerusalem · ירושלים

*Face F02 The higher and the lower unity. Also touches F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart', F05 The Essence beyond both, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 5). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** Jerusalem is complete awe, the lower and the higher together. And even the holiest place is a limit before Him.

**Hold.** 'Jerusalem means complete awe (yirah shalem); there is a lower awe and a higher awe.' (Likkutei Torah, Pekudei)

**The move.** Open it. There are two awes and two unifications, one drawn by the commandments of refraining and one by the commandments of doing; the divine soul is itself 'Jerusalem, complete awe', and before birth the souls are 'daughters of Jerusalem'; yet even upper Jerusalem, 'the gate of heaven', is a border with no comparison to the infinite light, and the journeys go out of every limit, even the holiest. Bring it down: one 'do not' you keep today and one 'do' you do; together they are complete awe. Likkutei Torah's picture is two cities, an upper Jerusalem and a lower one; only a picture, since awe is not a place. You, now: whole awe, and before Him even the gate of heaven is only a gate.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'complete awe.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Like a ray inside the sun: before Him even the gate of heaven has nothing of its own; then back to the day, with the awe whole. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'If I do not set Jerusalem above my chief joy.' Keep one 'do not' and do one 'do' today, knowing that the two together are complete awe. Through the day, return to: 'complete awe'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the tie runs through a wordplay (Yeru-shalem, yirah shalem). Likkutei Torah builds a doctrine of two unifications on it, but outside Chabad the tie is to the Shechinah's place, and mapping the two unifications onto the higher and lower unity is the entry's reading.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Pekudei 1:22 (line 67): והיינו כי ירושלים פי׳ יראה שלם ונודע שיש יראה תתאה ויראה עילאה LT Pekudei 1: Jerusalem means complete awe; lower Jerusalem is the union of vav-heh (positive mitzvot), upper Jerusalem the union of yud-heh (the higher awe). - Or HaTorah, במדבר כרך ד / פרשת מסעי / אלה מסעי / א'שנט:2 (line 4961): כי הנה"א נק' בחי' ירושלים יראה שלם, ולכן הנשמות קודם בואן לגוף נקראו בנות ירושלים The Tzemach Tzedek: Jerusalem and the desert exist in the soul too: the divine soul is Jerusalem, the animal soul is the desert of the nations to be subdued. - Or HaTorah, במדבר כרך ד / פרשת מסעי / אלה מסעי / א'שסד:2 (line 4970): ואפי' בחי' ירושלים שלמעלה יראה שלם ששם שער השמים הוא ג"כ בבחי' גבול ותכלית ואינו ערוך לגבי אוא"ס ב"ה The Tzemach Tzedek: even upper Jerusalem, the gate of heaven, is a limit with no comparison to Or Ein Sof; the journeys are an exodus from every limit, even the holiest.

## S183 · The choosing of Israel · ובנו בחרת

*Face F05 The Essence beyond both. Also touches F06 The soul as part of the One, F02 The higher and the lower unity, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** He chose not a quality anyone could see but the plain body, which looks like every other body. The choice is His own, bound by no reason, and its aim is to include you in His unity.

**Hold.** '"And You chose us from every people and tongue": this is the physical body, which in its physicality resembles the bodies of the nations.' (Tanya 49)

**The move.** Open it. He contracts His infinite light into 'world' out of love for His people, to bring them near and include them in His unity and oneness; the Rebbe: true choice belongs to the Essence alone and is 'bound by no limit', so the chosen body has an advantage even over the soul; and 'one nation on earth' means not separating even earthly business from the true One. Bring it down: your own body at its plainest, eating, walking, working, no different to look at from anyone's. The Sages' picture is the Holy One's own tefillin, which say 'who is like Your people Israel'; only a picture, since He has no head and no arm. You, now, in this plain body: chosen, not for anything you reached.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'You chose us.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Being chosen, not for anything you reached; He is in the plainest place. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips, in the morning prayer: 'And You chose us from every people and tongue.' Do one mitzvah with the plain body today, tefillin or a hand that gives. Through the day, return to: 'You chose us'.

**Kept.** Is Israel's holiness in the chosen body itself, or conferred by deeds? Tanya 49 and the Rebbe: the choice falls on the physical body, and true choice is the Essence's. Radomsk: like a Torah scroll's parchment, a Jew's body is holy only through Torah and mitzvot done for their sake. Kept apart, as the harsh and the universal texts on the nations are.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:11 (line 698): ״ובנו בחרת מכל עם ולשון״ – הוא הגוף החומרי הנדמה בחומריותו לגופי אומות העולם Tanya 49: 'You chose us' refers to the physical body, which looks like any other body; the choice falls on the body, not only the soul. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:10 (line 697): בעבור אהבת עמו ישראל, כדי לקרבם אליו, ליכלל ביחודו ואחדותו יתברך Tanya 49: He contracts His infinite light into 'world' out of love, to bring Israel near and include them in His unity; the purpose of the choice is the unity. - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד. ש"ק ומוצאי ש"ק פ' בשלח, י"א שבט, ה'תשל"א:16 (line 1390): וע"פ הידוע שאמיתית ענין הבחירה הוא בהעצמות,מובן, שבהגוף דישראל יש יתרון גם לגבי הנשמה The Rebbe (Basi LeGani 5731): true choice belongs to the Essence alone, so the chosen body of a Jew has an advantage even over the soul.

## S184 · The descent of the generations · ירידת הדורות

*Face F11 Darkness turned to light. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** As the generations grow smaller, the service moves down to the plain deed, and precisely there more of the inner light is given.

**Hold.** 'In each generation that comes nearer to Moshiach, as the responsibility grows, so the revelation of the lofty matters of Torah grows.' (the Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus)

**The move.** Open it. In earlier generations great souls refined everything in a moment, in the Shema alone; generation after generation the light of souls darkens until even prayer falls, and what remains is the bare yoke of deed, which rises above even the beginning of thought and alone brings Moshiach; and the Rebbe states the paradox: the heel generations, with doubled darkness, receive the inner Torah the earlier ones did not. Bring it down: your own smallness beside the giants you read about, and one practical mitzvah you can still do today. The Rayatz's picture is the heel that carries the whole body; only a picture, and it only settles the ear. You, now, small as you are: the plain deed is where the higher light lands.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'as the responsibility grows, the revelation grows.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The dignity of the struggle, and the sweetness of light that came out of darkness. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Hear, Israel, Havayah is our God, Havayah is One.' Do one practical mitzvah today with no feeling asked of it. Through the day, return to: 'the plain deed'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: as history the descent is mostly lament or deference, with no tie to the unity; the tie is stated only in Chabad, through the heels.

**Kept.** Do the generations go down or up? Kuntres Acharon 8, the Mitteler Rebbe and the Rashab: souls and bodies descend. Inyanah 26-28: each generation nearer Moshiach receives more. Radzin: deference to 'the earlier ones like angels'. The Rebbe's resolution, making the descent the reason for the revelation, is his reading.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 28:2 (line 147): שבכל דור ודור שמתקרב יותר לביאת המשיח, כמו שהאחריות הולכת וגדלה – כן הולכת ורבה התגלות הענינים הנעלים דתורה The Rebbe: as the generations descend, responsibility and revelation grow together; the descent runs the other way in revelation. - Tanya, Part V; Kuntres Acharon 8:8 (line 1611): מהשאיןכן בדורות הראשונים, שהיו נשמות האלקית גדולי הערך, היה הבירור נעשה כרגע בקריאתשמע לבד Kuntres Acharon 8: in earlier generations great souls refined in a moment of Shema; now it takes long prayer. - Sha'arei Orah, שער החנוכה / נא,ב:1 (line 225): ועתה בנפילת מדרגות דנש"י דור אחר דור יחשך אור נשמתם … וזהו ענין ביאת משיח ב"ד שאינו בא רק מכח של המעשה בעול תו"מ בפ"מ לבד The Mitteler Rebbe: generation after generation the souls darken until even prayer falls, yet Moshiach comes only through the bare yoke of practical deed, which 'rises above even the first thought'.

## S185 · Converts · גרים

*Face F11 Darkness turned to light. Also touches F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** What fell far from holiness comes home by being taken into a greater light, the way a candle is lost in a torch.

**Hold.** 'They are nullified and included, like a candle before a torch.' (Torah Ohr, Lech Lecha)

**The move.** Open it. 'Israel were exiled only so that converts be added' is read as the sparks in food and drink, which rise into holiness because of the revelation of the Shechinah, through which they are nullified and included; the Alter Rebbe says 'He guards the converts' 'does not necessarily mean actual converts' but the fallen sparks, which, once raised, need extra guarding, like one who has returned; and the Tzemach Tzedek applies it to actual converts: sparks fallen into the husks, drawn near by Avraham's light. Bring it down: the bread on your table, the drink in your cup, the newcomer at the back of your shul. The candle before a torch is Torah Ohr's own picture; only a picture, and it only settles the ear. You, now: what was far is being taken in, by bitul and not by force.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'like a candle before a torch.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Lightness: like a candle before a torch, nothing to defend, taken into the greater light. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the blessing at your next meal slowly with your lips. This week, welcome one newcomer to your table or your shul. Through the day, return to: 'a candle before a torch'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the tie is stated for converts as sparks, but the Alter Rebbe warns that these texts need not mean actual converts; for the individual convert under halachah, the tie to the unity is the entry's reading.

**Kept.** Are the 'converts' of these texts actual converts? Likkutei Torah: not necessarily; the sparks. The Tzemach Tzedek and the Sfas Emes: actual converts, who are sparks. Radomsk: the 'ger' is the Holy One Himself, now in exile like a stranger in the land.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Torah Ohr, Lech Lecha 1:5 (line 260): כי לא גלו אלא כדי שיתוסף עליהם גרים והן הניצוצות שבק"נ שבמאכלים ומשקים שמתעלים להתכלל בקדושה מפני גילוי שכינה שעי"ז הם בטלים ונכללים כנר בפני אבוקה Torah Ohr (Lech Lecha): 'Israel were exiled only so that converts be added' means sparks rising into holiness; they are included by bitul, 'as a candle before a torch'. - Likkutei Torah, Vaetchanan 8:13 (line 2970): וזהו שנאמר שומר את גרים דאין ר״ל גרים ממש דוקא … שהם הניצוצים שנפלו בשבה״כ או ע״י חטא אדה״ר LT Vaetchanan 8: 'He guards the converts' does not necessarily mean literal converts; it means the sparks, which need extra guarding once refined, like a penitent. - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of circumcision 10 (line 16): עד שיקרא אב המון גוים שיתגיירו גרים הרבה הם ניצוצי קדושה שנפלו בקליפות ויתקרבו לקדושה ע"י אורו שיזרח עליהם The Tzemach Tzedek: circumcision removes the screen so Avraham's light shines on the nations, and many converts come, who are sparks fallen into husks drawn near by that light.
