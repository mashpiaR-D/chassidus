## What this cluster shows

What separates them is which side they say it from: the name of the unity (S001-S002), its bearer (S003-S004), its exclusivity (S005), its two directions (S006-S007), its creative power (S008-S009, S012), its knowledge and care (S010-S011), and its purpose (S013). ## S001 · The unity of God · אחדות ה'

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F05 The Essence beyond both · F12 Knowing the unity **Search terms:** אחדות ה' · יחודו ואחדותו · אחדותו · אחדות פשוטה · יחוד ה'

> The unity of God is a simple singularity that creation leaves unchanged and that every detail of creation shows.

## Derivation

1. Before explaining idolatry and the whole Torah (Anochi and Lo yihyeh), the Alter Rebbe says one must first grasp 'the essence of His unity', called yachid u-meyuchad: the worlds, made from nothing, cause no change in His unity (Tanya 20:1-3). (*stated*)
2. The reason is that He is 'simple oneness without any composition'. His essence and His knowledge are one, as the Rambam says, so the unity is not a sum of parts (SH 7:11-12). (*stated*)
3. So the commandment is more than 'one God, not two'. The Tzemach Tzedek defines it as 'to know the truth that He is one and there is nothing besides Him', and sets it against shituf, honoring His servants beside Him (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem 1). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab reads the same commandment as 'Havayah is Elokim': the concealing Name does not truly conceal, so all creatures are nullified in their source (Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 32). (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe makes this unity the subject of the book the Rayatz calls the key to Chassidus: 'the unity of Godliness in every single detail' of the chain of worlds (Shaar HaYichud, Introduction). (*stated*)
6. The Besht's circle gives the same point a practical form: whoever grasps part of the unity grasps all of it, because Torah and mitzvot come from His Essence (Keter Shem Tov 1:78). That this is the same thing as the Mitteler Rebbe's 'unity in every detail' is our reading. (**reading**)
7. The Rayatz requires that this unity in worlds and creatures be known by real comprehension (Limud HaChassidus p. 26). The Rebbe names the joining of the particulars with the Essence as one of Chassidus's main emphases (Inyanah 18:4). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 20: the unity as the ground of the whole Torah; creation effects no change. SH 7:10-12: the Rambam's simple unity, beyond comprehension and held by faith. Tanya 24 (seen in search, not quoted here) measures every transgression as 'utter separation from His unity', so the unity is also the measure of sin.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud, Introduction and 53:12: the goal of every contemplation is that each detail, from before the first tzimtzum to the end of Asiyah, be included in the simple unity. He treats the unity as something reached detail by detail, not in general.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem: the commandment is to know that He is one and there is nothing besides. He contrasts it with shituf, which he says is permitted to the nations but forbidden to Israel.
- **Maharash.** Not treated separately under this subject in what was read. His two knowledges belong to S006 and his Anochi to S003.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 32 (OCR): the mitzvah of unity is that Havayah and Elokim are one, so creatures are nullified in existence.
- **Rayatz.** Limud HaChassidus p. 26 (OCR): the unity must be known 'by way of knowledge and real comprehension'. On the meaning of 'echad', see S002.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 18:4: one of the main emphases of Chassidus is the union of the particulars with the Essence. He adds that the unity is 'yachid', not 'echad' (S002).
- **Other schools.** Baal Shem Tov (Keter Shem Tov 1:78): grasping part of the unity is grasping all of it, applied to a single mitzvah done with love. Keter Shem Tov 1:41 (spine F08) turns the unity into conduct: praise God and fault yourself, since one who disparages others 'separates from the unity'. The Radzin line hears 'Hashem echad' so strongly that choice disappears (S005). Breslov was not checked for this subject in this pass.

## S002 · Echad and Yachid: one and only · אחד ויחיד

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** אחד ויחיד · יחיד ומיוחד · ה' אחד · אחד האמיתי

> Echad and yachid is the unity of God as singularity: 'yachid' names Him alone, beyond even being the one that gathers parts, and 'echad' names the many nullified in that one.

## Derivation

1. The Alter Rebbe: He is 'yachid u-meyuchad' after creation exactly as before, because everything is as nothing before Him (Tanya 20:3). (*stated*)
2. The Mitteler Rebbe separates the words. 'Yachid means literally alone', above the union of separate things, which is called 'echad' (SH 10:12-13). At that level one cannot even speak of 'union' (SH 10:22). (*stated*)
3. The Rayatz: 'Havayah echad' means 'the essential One, whose meaning is yachid'. Scripture says 'echad' rather than 'yachid' to teach the unity of all created levels, the seven heavens, the earth and the four directions, nullified in the aleph (Limud HaChassidus p. 3). (*stated*)
4. The Tzemach Tzedek glosses 'echad' as 'I have not changed': He is one now as before creation, because creation comes only from a radiance. Pharaoh's error was to deny this and picture creation as a chain of cause and effect (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Supplements 3:4). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe uses 'the known difference between yachid and echad' to define the yechidah: a simple unity with no relation to parts, not even as their source (Inyanah 5:5). He states it of the soul. Carrying it over to the one idea of Chassidus, as the spine does, is our reading. (**reading**)
6. Radzin (Tiferet Yosef) makes a comparable cut from the Tikkunei Zohar. He is 'chad ve-lo be-chushban', one that does not require a second and is above the whole chain of worlds, as against 'chad be-chushban', 'first', which requires a second and is the chain of worlds. The source is stated. His cut, one against first, is the Rayatz's own in Limud HaChassidus p. 3 ('yom echad' means 'first'; 'Havayah echad' is the essential One), and both use 'echad' for the higher term, where the Mitteler Rebbe uses 'echad' for the lower union of separate things (SH 10:13). So the doctrine lines up and the vocabulary does not, even inside Chabad. That Radzin's 'one, not by count' is the same as Chabad's 'yachid' is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 20 and 36:3: 'yachid u-meyuchad' before and after creation. Iggeret HaKodesh 20 (read in context) speaks of creatures made 'by the power of Ein Sof, yachid u-meyuchad in the utmost'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 10:11-23: yachid means alone, above echad. It is 'Ant hu chad ve-lo be-chushban' ('You are one, but not in number'). SH 7:31-32 calls the same pre-tzimtzum level 'simple oneness' and 'chad ve-lo be-chushban', so his own vocabulary moves between the two words.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Supplements 3:4: echad means changelessness ('I have not changed'), grounded in creation's being only a radiance.
- **Maharash.** Not found for this subject in what was read.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres HaTefillah (S012): the Essence bears all opposites 'all in true singularity (yachid mamash)'. His echad/yachid terminology was not searched separately.
- **Rayatz.** Limud HaChassidus p. 3 (OCR): 'Havayah echad' means the essential One, 'yachid'. 'Echad' teaches the nullification of all created levels in the aleph.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 5:5: yachid against echad, stated of the yechidah and of Moshiach's point.
- **Other schools.** Radzin (Tiferet Yosef, Pekudei 7): 'chad ve-lo be-chushban', one that requires no second, above the chain of worlds. He joins it at once to the dwelling below: 'from when the Holy One was yachid in His world He desired a dwelling below' (S013). The Tikkunei Zohar phrase is common to both schools.

## Thin or forced?

Not thin. The terminology is unstable, though. The Mitteler Rebbe himself uses 'echad' and 'simple oneness' for the pre-tzimtzum level (SH 7:31) and reserves 'echad' for the union of separate things in SH 10:13. The Rayatz (Limud HaChassidus p. 3) and Radzin both use 'echad' for the higher term, against 'first'. The doctrine agrees and the words do not.

## S003 · The Essence · עצמות ומהות

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below **Search terms:** עצמות ומהות · העצמות · מהותו ועצמותו · עצמותו ית'

> The Essence is the unity of God as what He is in Himself: not light and not radiance, beyond concealment and revelation, and for that reason found in the lowest physical thing.

## Derivation

1. The Maharash: 'Anochi' is 'Anochi mi she-Anochi', His Essence, not a mere radiance. Only from it comes the exodus from limits, and the dwelling below 'below as above' (Toras Shmuel 5627). (*stated*)
2. The Rebbe: Chassidus draws down Ein Sof, and 'Ein Sof is the essential'. Everything else is caused by it (Inyanah 2:3). (*stated*)
3. The Mitteler Rebbe: even the all-encompassing circle is 'one detail' next to the Essence of the light before the tzimtzum, which is beyond being a whole to parts (SH 7:37-38). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab: the Essence itself is above concealment and revelation. Light reveals only that the Essence exists, not what it is (Hemshech Ayin Beis, Vayera 5670). (*stated*)
5. The Alter Rebbe's contemplation in prayer, as the Rayatz transmits it from the Tzemach Tzedek through the Maharash and the Rashab: asked by the Mitteler Rebbe, who had prayed with the bitul of Adam Kadmon ('every stature shall bow before You'), he answered, 'I prayed with the lectern: the physical comes into being from the Essence' (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 7, OCR). (*stated*)
6. So the Essence is not reached by climbing the ladder of lights: the highest object of contemplation is answered with the lowest. The story sets the two contemplations side by side and does not draw this conclusion itself. The conclusion is our reading. (**reading**)
7. The Rebbe states the consequence for the dwelling: it is 'a dwelling for His Essence', since 'a dwelling is where the self is found' (the Rebbe, Rosh Hashanah 5741). (*stated*)
8. The word also occurs in the Besht's circle: Torah and mitzvot 'came forth from His Essence, the true unity' (Keter Shem Tov 1:78). The systematic contrast of Essence and light, however, belongs to Chabad. That the Besht's usage carries the Chabad sense is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** His prayer 'with the lectern', that the physical comes from the Essence (told by the Rayatz, OCR). Iggeret HaKodesh 20: only He whose existence is from Himself creates yesh (S008).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:37-38 and 10:11-22: the Essence is beyond whole and part. Every contemplation of detail is meant 'to come to His Essence and being' (SH 4:42, seen in search).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha: renewal of heaven and earth 'by drawing the vitality from the very Essence of the Infinite Light' (Shoresh Mitzvat HaTefillah 12:1, seen in search). The Essence is above the category of intellect (S011).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: Anochi is the Essence, not a radiance. Faith is the relation to what is above the chain of worlds.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis: light reveals only the Essence's existence, not its being. In the Essence there is no division and opposites are one (S012).
- **Rayatz.** Transmits the Alter Rebbe's 'the physical from the Essence' (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, OCR).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 2:3 ('Ein Sof is the essential'); Rosh Hashanah 5741 ('a dwelling for His Essence'); Basi LeGani 5719 (the yesh feels uncaused because it comes from the Essence, S008).
- **Other schools.** Keter Shem Tov 1:78 uses 'His Essence' for the source of Torah and mitzvot. The Maggid (4:1) says 'the honor of His Essence we do not grasp, only His honor in the worlds' (S004). Outside Chabad the word is rare as a doctrinal term. The spine notes that coverage counts are inflated by the ordinary sense 'bones'.

## S004 · Ein Sof and the Infinite Light · אין סוף / אור אין סוף

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** אור אין סוף · אוא"ס · אין סוף ב"ה · אין לו סוף

> Ein Sof is the unity of God as the unlimited: the name for Him as bound by nothing, and the light through which that unlimitedness is present in limited worlds.

## Derivation

1. Keter Shem Tov argues from infinity to unity. Were the Creator bounded He would be composite. If there were two, there would be a difference between them, and whatever is distinct is bounded. So, being Ein Sof, 'there is nothing else' (Keter Shem Tov 1:55). (*stated*)
2. Chabad separates the name from its bearer. 'Ein Sof' names His name, not His Essence (Pelach HaRimon, cited by the Rashab in Hemshech Ayin Beis). The Mitteler Rebbe: as the Essence of Ein Sof is simple light, so is its radiance, called Or Ein Sof: 'He and His name alone' (SH 10:18-19). (*stated*)
3. The Infinite Light is above intellect altogether, even above supernal Chochmah (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Tefillin 4). In the same passage he says the Maharal was right that wisdom is only His instrument (S011). (*stated*)
4. The Maggid: we do not grasp the honor of His Essence, only His honor in the worlds; in Ein Sof, will does not apply (Maggid 4). The light of Ein Sof is always spreading, and 'enough!' makes the world a vessel that can hold it (Maggid 18). That this is the Maggid's form of tzimtzum for the receiver's sake is our reading. (**reading**)
5. The Rebbe: the essential point of Chassidus is the drawing down of inner Atik, 'Ein Sof present in RaDLA', and 'Ein Sof is the essential' (Inyanah 2:2-3). (*stated*)
6. And the Infinite comes down into the finite. A mitzvah draws the revelation of Or Ein Sof into the physical things of this world (Tanya 37:2). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 37:2: a mitzvah draws Or Ein Sof into physicality. Tanya 48: Or Ein Sof is called sovev because it is not revealed within the worlds (S007).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 10:18-22: the Essence of Ein Sof and Or Ein Sof are both simple, 'He and His name alone'. The Mitteler Rebbe's Essence of Ein Sof before the tzimtzum (7:37) is the whole's measure.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Tefillin 4: Or Ein Sof is above wisdom 'by myriads of degrees' and is not of the nature of intellect.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627 (read in context): faith in 'Or Ein Sof above the chain of worlds'.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, part 3, citing Pelach HaRimon: 'Ein Sof' refers to His name, not His Essence. The bearer of the name is obviously infinite.
- **Rayatz.** Limud HaChassidus p. 3: the aleph of 'echad' signifies 'Ein Sof, Master of the world' (S002).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 2:2-3: the point of Chassidus is the drawing of Ein Sof present in RaDLA. 'Ein Sof is the essential.'
- **Other schools.** Keter Shem Tov 1:55: infinity implies 'ein od'. The Maggid (4, 18): His brightness is beyond all worlds, which cannot bear it, and is always spreading; in Ein Sof will does not apply. Pillar 08 notes that the early masters do not draw Chabad's distinction between the light and the Essence. The Maggid moves freely between 'Ein Sof' and 'Or Ein Sof'.

## Thin or forced?

Not thin. The Rebbe says 'Ein Sof is the essential' (Inyanah 2:3), while Ayin Beis, after Pelach HaRimon, says 'Ein Sof' names His name and not His Essence. These are two levels of speech, the Ein Sof in RaDLA relative to everything below it versus the name relative to its bearer. That this reconciles them is our reading. The sources state both.

## S005 · Nothing besides Him · אין עוד מלבדו

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F07 The home below · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** אין עוד מלבדו · לית אתר פנוי · כולא קמיה כלא · אין עוד

> Nothing besides Him is the unity of God as exclusivity: there is no other god, and beyond that nothing at all that stands on its own before Him, even in the lowest earth.

## Derivation

1. SH 1:2 asks why 'know today… there is nothing else' must be 'returned to the heart'. Nobody thinks there is a god in the waters under the earth. So the verse speaks of the standing of the world itself, not of idols. The answer comes in SH 6:6: do not think that heaven and earth are a separate thing which He fills as a soul fills a body. (*stated*)
2. SH 6:5: since the Name Elokim conceals only toward the lower beings, even the physical earth, which looks like full 'yesh', is literally nothing before Him. It is not called by any name, not even 'another' (od). (*stated*)
3. Tanya 33:6: this is 'the true unity of God': even in the earth below there is nothing besides Him, and that is His dwelling below. (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe: once one recognizes that creation comes from the Essence, one recognizes the unity as 'ein od milvado'. The world is not even 'od', a subordinate thing (Inyanah 18:2). (*stated*)
5. This is not illusion. The Rebbe cites the Maharash: the world 'is not imagination and sleight of hand but true existence', proven from a halachic ruling, because all existents come 'from the truth of His being' (Rosh Hashanah 5741). (*stated*)
6. That this is Chabad's guard against acosmism, as the Ropshitz 'garment' is in pillar 01, is our reading. The Rebbe's passage does not use that frame. (**reading**)
7. Grades. The Yismach Moshe reads 'ein od milvado' plainly: no other ruler or power, nothing against His will, and this grasp is obligatory for every Jew. Moshe for himself said 'and what are we', the nullity of everything, but to Israel, who lacked that revelation, he said 'ein od milvado'. Shlomo, in the generation of the full moon, said the bare 'ein od', that nothing yet exists (Vaetchanan 17:1). Magen Avos (in Mekor Mayim Chayim) contrasts Moshe, who put Him 'even in the void of the world', with Rachav, who left the void. (*stated*)
8. The edge. Radzin: when a person truly hears 'Hashem echad', 'the power of choice ceases', because he sees there is no power besides Him. That is why the kingship verses need concealment, so that choice can exist (Sod Yesharim, Rosh Hashanah 53). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 1-7 and Tanya 33:6. The earth is 'literally nothing before Him'. This is the true unity and His dwelling.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 10:22: at the level of yachid 'there is nothing besides Him' to speak of union with (S002).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem: 'He is one and besides Him there is nothing else' (S001).
- **Maharash.** As cited by the Rebbe (Rosh Hashanah 5741): the world is true existence, not illusion, proven from halacha. His own maamar was not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 32 (OCR): Elokim does not truly conceal Havayah, so all is nullified in existence (S001, S006).
- **Rayatz.** Not quoted for this subject; see S010 on providence.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 18:2: from creation-from-the-Essence comes the recognition of 'ein od milvado', even as a subordinate. Rosh Hashanah 5741: yet the world is truly real.
- **Other schools.** Yismach Moshe (Vaetchanan 17:1): two grades, 'no other ruler' for all and 'nothing exists' for the full-moon generation. Radzin (Sod Yesharim): the full hearing of 'Hashem echad' leaves no room for choice. Pillar 01 records the tension with Dover Tzedek, where hiding exists so that choice can (not re-verified here). Mekor Mayim Chayim (Magen Avos): Moshe versus Rachav. This is a late anthology that cites Tanya, so its reading of the Besht is partly a Chabad reading.

## S006 · Higher and lower unity · יחודא עילאה ויחודא תתאה

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** יחודא עילאה · יחודא תתאה · יחוד עליון · ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו

> Higher and lower unity is the unity of God said twice: once from His side, where the world is nothing, and once from below, where He fills a real world in time and place.

## Derivation

1. SH 1:1 opens with the Zohar: 'Shema' is the higher unity and 'Baruch Shem' is the lower unity. (*stated*)
2. Higher: from His side even the earth is nothing (SH 6:5). Lower: His Essence, called Ein Sof, fills the earth in time and place through Malchus. Malchus is the attribute of contraction that keeps time and place from being dissolved (SH 7:8-9). (*stated*)
3. The Mitteler Rebbe: the first verse of Shema is the simple unity before the first tzimtzum, 'one, not by count'. Baruch Shem is Malchus of Ein Sof after the tzimtzum, down to Malchus of Asiyah (SH 7:30-34). (*stated*)
4. The Maharash turns the pair into two knowledges. The lower: below is 'something' and above is 'nothing'. The higher: all is as nothing before Him. The higher is rooted in Anochi and held by faith (Toras Shmuel 5627). (*stated*)
5. The Rashab: most souls today serve with the bitul of the lower unity, and very few truly belong to the higher. Yet everyone must touch it, because the lower unity alone easily falls into complete yesh (Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 34). (*stated*)
6. Strashelye maps the pair onto the lights. The higher unity is the worlds as they are within His power, called Kudsha Brich Hu and sovev. The lower is His revelation to us through divided powers, called Shechinah and memale (Sha'arei Avodah III 1:6). That this makes S006 and S007 one structure is our reading. (**reading**)
7. Radzin: in truth there is only the higher unity. He set up a place for the lower unity for the sake of service. One who truly knows the higher unity becomes himself 'the glory', the garment of His will, and that is why Baruch Shem is said quietly (Sod Yesharim, Yom Kippur 13). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 1:1, 6:5, 7:8-9. Iggeret HaKodesh 20 (spine F02d-e): 'nothing before Him' is His knowledge from above downward, while from below upward the creature is a truly separate thing.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:29-35: contemplating every detail from Malchus of Ein Sof to Malchus of Asiyah draws everything to the higher unity. Even so, the whole joined chain is only 'one detail' (S007).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** The pillar-01 architecture credits him with the two knowledges (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Tefillah 23). That passage was not re-verified in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: the lower and higher knowledges. The higher is held by faith.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim ch. 7: most serve in the lower unity, yet everyone must touch the higher.
- **Rayatz.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 14:5-15:1: the four lower levels of the soul feel the worlds' existence, and only the yechidah, above the worlds, feels simply that all the worlds are absolute nothing, renewed every moment. He says this of the revealed yechidah (gilui ha-yechidah). Whether this stands against the Rashab's 'very few' who belong to the higher unity is unclear: the Rashab speaks of actual avodah, the Rebbe of what the yechidah feels. Setting them against each other is our reading.
- **Other schools.** Strashelye: higher = sovev = Kudsha Brich Hu; lower = memale = Shechinah. Radzin: only the higher is true, and the lower is a place made for service. Pillar 01 adds R. Tzadok's reading (Dover Tzedek, Ner HaMitzvot 2:38), not re-verified here. The real difference is this. For SH 7 the lower unity is itself a unity of the Essence in time and place. For the Rashab it is the normal station of avodah, and a danger if held alone. For Radzin it is a concession for service.

## S007 · Filling and encompassing all worlds · ממלא כל עלמין וסובב כל עלמין

**Faces:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** ממלא כל עלמין · סובב כל עלמין · ממכ"ע · סוכ"ע

> Filling and encompassing is the unity of God as a double presence: inside each creature by its measure and around all creatures equally, and neither of the two is a place.

## Derivation

1. SH 7:17 cites the Tikkunim, 'no place is empty of Him', and Ra'aya Meheimna, 'He surrounds all worlds… He fills all worlds'. (*stated*)
2. Filling is the vitality clothed inside the creature, contracted to its measure (SH 7:20). (*stated*)
3. Surrounding is not spatially above. It is the influence not revealed within the worlds (Tanya 48:5). His knowledge surrounds each creature 'in actuality', and that knowledge is its vitality and its coming into being (SH 7:19). (*stated*)
4. The Tzemach Tzedek: filling light explains why the worlds have ranks. But 'the main vitality is from sovev', because matter and yesh cannot come about through cause and effect, only through sovev, the omnipotent (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2). (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe: the encompassing circle around the line is still 'one detail' next to the Essence (SH 7:36-37). So neither filling nor surrounding is the Essence. That this links S007 to S003 is stated there. (*stated*)
6. The Rashab grades the two by who can grasp them. Filling light can be understood even by the animal soul's natural mind. Surrounding light needs the divine soul (Kuntres HaTefillah 1). (*stated*)
7. Outside Chabad: Strashelye identifies sovev with Kudsha Brich Hu and memale with Shechinah. Radzin defends the chassidim against opponents who said a servant of God should think only that He surrounds all worlds, not that He fills them. He calls that view 'the reverse of our holy Torah' (Sha'ar HaEmunah, Introduction 25:5). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 7:17-22 and Tanya 48: memale is contracted vitality, sovev is unrevealed and not spatial. Surrounding is His knowledge of each creature 'in actuality'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:36-37: the encompassing circle is one detail before the Essence. SH 3:2 (seen in search): grasping memale as 'the power of the maker in the made'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2: the main vitality, and the very yesh of matter, is from sovev. This runs against the natural picture in which the lowest comes from the most contracted light.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627 (read in context): matzah strengthens faith in Anochi, 'sovev, above the chain of worlds'.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres HaTefillah 1: the natural mind can grasp memale; sovev and what is above it need the divine soul.
- **Rayatz.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located for this subject in this pass. His Inyanah 17-18 puts the power to create above both, in the Essence (S008).
- **Other schools.** Strashelye (Sha'arei Avodah III 1:6): sovev = Kudsha Brich Hu = higher unity; memale = Shechinah = revelation divided by vessels. Radzin (Sha'ar HaEmunah, Introduction 25:5): the opponents who allowed only 'sovev' contradict the Torah. The opponents also misread chassidic immanence as Kalam occasionalism (S009). Pillar 01 adds the Piaseczno Rebbe (the world is held by filling light, prayer for Heaven's need reaches surrounding light), not re-verified here.

## Passages

## S008 · Creation something from nothing · יש מאין

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F01 The world said into being now · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** יש מאין · מאין ליש · בריאה יש מאין · התהוות היש

> Creation something from nothing is the unity of God as the power only the uncaused has: to make a thing that feels it has no cause.

## Derivation

1. Iggeret HaKodesh 20: the light is like its source, His very Essence, 'whose existence is from Himself' and is caused by nothing. Therefore He alone can create yesh from absolute nothing. (*stated*)
2. The Tzemach Tzedek: an effect naturally dissolves into its cause, so a yesh, and matter above all, cannot come through cause and effect. It comes only through sovev, the omnipotent. For Ein Sof there is no 'impossible' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2). (*stated*)
3. The Rebbe: in practice creation comes through Malchus, but the power in Malchus to renew yesh from ayin is from the Essence, which is defined neither as 'ayin' nor as 'yesh' (Inyanah 17:2). The created yesh feels uncaused precisely because it comes from the Essence (Basi LeGani 5719). (*stated*)
4. The kept tension. The Maharash: God did not say 'who created you yesh me-ayin', because 'creation yesh me-ayin is only from a mere radiance'. Even the righteous of the nations admit it. What is from the Essence is the exodus from limits (Toras Shmuel 5627). Reading these as two levels, the act of creation through speech against the power behind it, is our reading. (**reading**)
5. Purpose. The Rashab: the purpose of creating yesh from ayin is that the yesh be nullified to ayin (Eitz HaChaim). The Maggid: heaven and earth were made yesh from ayin, and tzaddikim make ayin from yesh, even in eating (Maggid 6). (*stated*)
6. The analogy from the soul. Keter Shem Tov: one who innovates in Torah makes something from nothing, and this proves the Etz Chaim's 'simple will'. Where the analogy fails, SH 2:2 says human making is 'yesh from yesh'. That the Torah-innovation analogy is one the Alter Rebbe would limit the same way is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Iggeret HaKodesh 20:11-14: only the uncaused creates yesh; the separate yesh comes through Malchus of Atzilus. SH 2:2: human making is yesh from yesh.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:44 (spine T-anchors) aims contemplation at 'the coming-into-being of the physical yesh from nothing'. SH 18:5 (read in context) calls Keter of Adam Kadmon 'the true creation yesh from ayin'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2: yesh only from sovev, the omnipotent. This, he says, answers the philosophers' difficulty of how the physical came from the spiritual.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: yesh me-ayin is 'only from a mere radiance'. The exodus is from the Essence.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 19: yesh is revealed through the word of Malchus, and its purpose is bitul and dwelling.
- **Rayatz.** The Alter Rebbe's 'the physical comes into being from the Essence' (S003).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 17:2-3 and Basi LeGani 5719: the power to make yesh is from the Essence; recognizing this, 'recognize and not only understand', belongs to the yechidah.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (6:2): yesh from ayin and ayin from yesh. Keter Shem Tov 1:6: Torah innovation as yesh me-ayin. The Maggid and the Besht treat yesh me-ayin chiefly as a model for the work of tzaddikim. The Chabad question of which level has the power to create is not theirs.

## S009 · Continuous re-creation · התהוות תמידית / מחדש בכל יום

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** מחדש בטובו · מחיה ומהוה · בכל רגע · התהוות

> Continuous re-creation is the unity of God as dependence without pause: the world is only while the creating word stands in it.

## Derivation

1. The Besht, in SH 1:3: 'Your word stands in the heavens'. The letters of 'let there be a firmament' stand in the heavens now, and if they left for an instant the heavens would be nothing. (*stated*)
2. The same holds of stone and dust (SH 1:4-5), and a thing's Hebrew name is the letters that keep it in being (SH 1:6-9). (*stated*)
3. Havayah means present and continuous action. It is not enough for creatures that they were created in the six days (SH 4:2). (*stated*)
4. The error SH 2 corrects is the craftsman model, in which the vessel stands after the smith leaves. The same error is the root of denying providence (S010). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe: were creation from Malchus in itself, the worlds would have some standing of their own, as a body has some existence without the soul. Because it is from the Essence, before which the worlds take up no room, their entire existence is renewed (Inyanah 17:5-18:1). (*stated*)
6. The Maggid: He created the worlds by speech, and 'the power of the maker in the made' means the power of speech is in the heavens (Maggid 17). (*stated*)
7. Differences. Me'or Einayim: vitality must flow at every moment 'through the chain, from cause to effect'. He asks how the finite can receive from Ein Sof with no intermediary, and answers with the tzaddik, the channel through which it descends (Yitro 28-29). SH also has the creating letters descend 'from level to level' by substitutions until they reach the creature (SH 1:7-8), so the chain itself is not the difference. The tzaddik as channel is, and SH 1-4 does not raise it. The Tzemach Tzedek's denial that yesh can come through cause and effect (S008) concerns the coming-into-being of matter, not the flow of vitality, so setting it against Me'or Einayim would be forced. Sfas Emes: He renews creation daily 'so that nature not prevail', and a person must seek the renewal. (**reading**)
8. Radzin rejects the opponents' charge that chassidic immanence is the Kalam doctrine the Rambam refuted, that existence and non-existence are accidents created every moment, a view from which no worship follows (Sha'ar HaEmunah, Introduction 25:5). SH itself speaks of renewal 'every moment'. What exactly Radzin denies, the Kalam's atomism or renewal as such, is left open. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 1-4: the Besht's teaching, the stone's name, Havayah as present-continuous, the craftsman error.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not quoted for this subject; his contemplation of 'yesh from ayin' in every detail (S008) presupposes it.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Shoresh Mitzvat HaTefillah 12:1 (seen in search): heaven and earth 'come into being every moment from absolute nothing', by vitality drawn from the Essence. Supplements 3:4: Havayah is 'what brings into being from nothing every moment'.
- **Maharash.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not quoted here; Eitz HaChaim ties the Word of Malchus to the revelation of yesh (S008).
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 29 Sivan (S010): the avodah of Chassidus is to see renewal every moment as providence.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 14:4 (spine) and 17:5-18:1: renewal every moment 'as at the six days of creation'. Only if creation is from the Essence is it true renewal. Only the yechidah feels it simply (15:1).
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (17): the power of speech in the heavens. Me'or Einayim (Yitro 28-29): renewal every moment through the chain and through the tzaddik as intermediary. Sfas Emes (Lech Lecha 31:3): daily renewal against the tyranny of habit and nature. Radzin: not the Kalam's occasionalism. Pillar 01/10 cites Amdur's 'the renewal is for us, since He is above time'; not re-verified here.

## S010 · Particular providence · השגחה פרטית

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F12 Knowing the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** השגחה פרטית · השגחתו · בהשגחה פרטית · מאת ה' היתה זאת

> Particular providence is the unity of God as continuous creation seen in each particular: every movement is His doing, and each one bears on the whole purpose.

## Derivation

1. Hayom Yom 29 Sivan: the avodah of Chassidus is to train oneself to see providence as His renewing the world and its creatures at every moment. This alone is their existence. Providence and continuous creation are one fact (S009). (*stated*)
2. SH 2:1: those who deny providence imagine creation as craftsmanship. (*stated*)
3. SH 7:15-16: those who took the tzimtzum literally still believed He watches over every creature from above. They contradict themselves, because to know everything without change He must know it by knowing Himself (S011). (*stated*)
4. The Mitteler Rebbe: the aim of all contemplation is that everything be included in the simple unity 'until divine providence in detail, down to the very last particular, is fixed in the soul' (SH 53:12). (*stated*)
5. Hayom Yom 28 Cheshvan: a particular movement of a creature relates to the whole purpose of creation. The Rayatz, from the Besht: a single straw rolling in the marketplace is moved by providence and is a ring in the whole chain of His will. (*stated*)
6. The Besht: everything is by providence, and one who does not believe it is subject to the kelipah that hides it. In the days of Moshiach His providence will be revealed in detail (Keter Shem Tov 2:60). The Toldot: one who believes knows every event is from Him, so even a small loss atones. (*stated*)
7. Tagging this subject with F03 (the hiding for the sake of revelation) is our reading of the Besht's passage: providence is hidden by the kelipah and revealed in the days of Moshiach. The earlier tag F11 (darkness turned to light) was not supported by any passage here and has been dropped. (**reading**)
8. Reach. Radzin reconciles the Rambam's restriction (the falling leaf, Guide III 17): from God's side all is providence, and from creation's side it is received according to the vessel (Sha'ar HaEmunah, Introduction 16:3). This is the two knowledges of S006 applied to providence, which is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 2 and 7:15-16: providence follows from continuous creation and from His self-knowledge. Literal tzimtzum plus providence is incoherent.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 53:12-14: providence in detail, even of the stars 'by His simple will in His very Essence'. Hayom Yom 7 Tishrei (spine) credits him with the advantage of businessmen in 'seeing Godliness palpably'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Supplements 3:4 (read in context): all is revealed before Him and there is 'particular providence', yet His thought is not clothed in things and He does not change.
- **Maharash.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rashab.** As reported by the Rayatz (Sefer HaSichos 5710, 374:9): in 5654 he explained that by Chassidus providence is 'not only in matters of understanding but even in simple things'.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5687 (134:10): the straw in the marketplace. Sefer HaSichos 5687 (126:2): 'the Besht revealed, the Maggid explained and the Rebbe [the Alter Rebbe] brought down into comprehension' particular providence.
- **The Rebbe.** Hayom Yom 29 Sivan and 28 Cheshvan, as compiler. Igros Kodesh, Likkutei Sichos and Toras Menachem carry hundreds of uses (term counts), not read in this pass.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 2:60), the Toldot (Vayishlach 73; also Pekudei 62, where believing in providence is the root of love of one's fellow). Radzin: universal from His side, graded from ours. Pillar 01 notes Tiferet Yosef's 'in the measure of belief', not re-verified here.

## S011 · He is the knower and the known · הוא היודע והוא הידוע

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F12 Knowing the unity · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** הוא היודע · והוא הידוע · הוא המדע · ידיעתו

> He is the knower and the known is the unity of God as knowledge that adds nothing: He knows all creatures by knowing Himself, so their many-ness makes no many-ness in Him.

## Derivation

1. The Rambam, adopted in Tanya 2 and 48: He is the knowledge, the knower and the known. He knows all creatures by knowing Himself, not by a knowledge outside Him, and this is beyond human comprehension. (*stated*)
2. SH 7:10-12: this is why 'I have not changed'. Knowing the creatures adds no composition, because His essence and knowledge are one. It is to be believed, with a faith above intellect. (*stated*)
3. Consequence: providence without change in Him (SH 7:16; S010). (*stated*)
4. A limit. SH 9, gloss: the Rambam's formula applies only after the Infinite Light is clothed in the vessels of Chochmah, Binah and Da'at of Atzilus. Without that, knowledge and knowing do not apply to Him at all. Tanya 2 has a similar gloss (read in context). (*stated*)
5. A kept dispute. The Tzemach Tzedek reports that the Maharal (Gevuros Hashem) and the Tosafos Yom Tov 'differ with the Rambam': knowledge is one of His acts, not His Essence. He reconciles them. The Essence is above the category of knowledge, and the Rambam's formula holds for the light united with the vessels (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Emunah 4:1-4). In Mitzvat Tefillin 4 he says of the Maharal 'how right his words are', and adds that the Rambam is also true once the vessel of wisdom is emanated. (*stated*)
6. Strashelye draws the same limit. The description 'knower and known' fits only Atzilus, from the creatures' side. He cites the SH 9 gloss (Sha'arei HaYichud I 25:5). The Mitteler Rebbe: 'wise, but not with a known wisdom' (SH 10:17). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2, 48; SH 7:10-16; SH 9 gloss. He accepts the Rambam and confines the formula to Atzilus.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 10:17: 'wise but not with a known wisdom', said of the Essence of Ein Sof.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Emunah 4 and Mitzvat Tefillin 4: sets out the Rambam against the Maharal and the Tosafos Yom Tov, and reconciles them through the Essence above knowledge and the light in vessels.
- **Maharash.** Term counts show uses in Toras Shmuel. Not read in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located for this subject in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Term counts show uses in Toras Menachem and Sichos Kodesh. Not read in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Strashelye (Sha'arei HaYichud I 3:4, 10:5, 25:3-5) develops it at length, in the Alter Rebbe's line. The Maharal, cited by the Tzemach Tzedek, is the dissenting voice. A plain-substring count finds the formula outside Chabad and Strashelye only in Derekh HaMelekh (Piaseczno, 2), Yosher Divrei Emet (Zbarazh, 1) and Be'er Mayim Chaim (Chernovitz, 1). None of these was read in this pass.

## S012 · Omnipotence and the joining of opposites · כל יכול / נושא הפכים

**Faces:** F09 The unity of opposites · F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** כל יכול · נמנע הנמנעות · נושא הפכים · בכחו ויכלתו

> Omnipotence and the joining of opposites is the unity of God as a power that knows no 'impossible': limit and limitless, place and no-place, hiding and being, all borne together by the Essence.

## Derivation

1. SH 4:6: His gevurot, 'who is omnipotent', contract and hide the vitality so that the creature is not nullified. Hiding is itself His power. (*stated*)
2. The Tzemach Tzedek: 'impossibility applies only to the bounded; for Ein Sof there is no impediment at all'. So omnipotence is not a large quantity of power but the absence of limitation (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2). (*stated*)
3. Omnipotence includes limitation. After Avodat HaKodesh, the Rebbe: 'as He has power in the limitless, so He has power in limitation'. To deny it would diminish His perfection (Basi LeGani 5737). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab: in the Essence there is no division, and 'two opposites are in one subject… like the impossible impossibilities in the Creator's bosom' (Hemshech Ayin Beis). 'The perfection of His Essence bears all opposites, all in true singularity' (Kuntres HaTefillah). (*stated*)
5. The example. The ark in the Holy of Holies took no space, 'place and above place at once'. Strashelye uses it to show that He is omnipotent to join two opposites in one subject: His attributes are vessels and yet one with Him. For the Rebbe only the revelation of the Infinite Light above even the light above worlds makes it, 'the impossible impossibility' (Basi LeGani 5730). Both state it. That they are one argument is our reading. (**reading**)
6. The Maggid: in God, love and fear exist together, because He joins them, and the joiner is ayin (Maggid 3). (*stated*)
7. In conduct the opposites stay opposites. Hayom Yom 30 Sivan (spine F09h) has the Alter Rebbe tell a young prodigy that spirituality and physicality are opposites in their essence. (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 4:6: hiding as omnipotence. Iggeret HaKodesh 20:11: 'He alone, by His power and ability' creates yesh. Kuntres Acharon 4 (seen in search): prayer draws vitality from Ein Sof, 'who alone is omnipotent'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Term counts show 'nimna ha-nimna'ot' twice in Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (OCR). Not read in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Achdut HaShem 2: 'for Ein Sof there is no impediment'. This answers the philosophers on how the physical came from the spiritual.
- **Maharash.** Term counts show 'nimna ha-nimna'ot' in Toras Shmuel (OCR). Not read in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis (Vayera 5670): two opposites in one subject in the Essence. Kuntres HaTefillah 1: 'bears all opposites'.
- **Rayatz.** Term counts show 'nosei hafachim' twice in Sefer HaMaamarim (OCR). Not read in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5730 (the ark, 'nimna ha-nimna'ot'); 5737 (power in limitation); Tu BiShvat 5734 (the Essence, 'bearer of opposites', lets Torah above the world act below, read in context).
- **Other schools.** Strashelye (Sha'arei HaYichud I 10:3-5): 'two opposites in one subject', the ark, 'He is omnipotent… by His wondrous power'. The Maggid (3:1): ayin joins fire and water, love and fear. Keter Shem Tov 1:17 (spine F09a): the Shechinah holds good and evil 'in one subject'.

## Thin or forced?

Stated in Chabad and Strashelye. Outside them the formal argument ('bearer of opposites', 'impossible impossibility') is thin. The nearest are the Maggid's ayin that joins and the Besht's 'one subject', which concern joining rather than omnipotence. Treating these as the same doctrine is our reading.

## S013 · A dwelling in the lowest realm · דירה בתחתונים

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** דירה בתחתונים · נתאוה הקב"ה · דירה לו ית' · תחתון שאין תחתון

> A dwelling in the lowest realm is the unity of God as purpose: His Essence at home in the lowest place, where there is nothing besides Him even in the earth below.

## Derivation

1. Tanya 36:1: the purpose of creating this world is that the Holy One desired a dwelling in the lowest realms. (*stated*)
2. Nothing changes in Him. Before creation He alone filled the place, and so He does now; the change is only in the receivers (Tanya 36:2-3). (*stated*)
3. The dwelling is the unity itself. 'The true unity: even in the earth below there is nothing besides Him, and that is His dwelling below' (Tanya 33:6; S005). (*stated*)
4. The Maharash: a dwelling below means Godliness revealed 'below as above'. Only Anochi, the Essence, which is beyond limit, can do this (Toras Shmuel 5627). (*stated*)
5. The Rashab: the purpose of creating yesh is that it become nullified and so become a vessel and a dwelling for Him (Eitz HaChaim). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: the physical yesh, 'with nothing lower than it', is specifically a dwelling for His Essence, revealed in the future and true already now (Basi LeGani 5719; Rosh Hashanah 5741). The Rayatz: man's mission is to light the world's darkness with Torah and mitzvot and make it a place for His dwelling. (*stated*)
7. Outside Chabad. The Besht: God created the world and man in His image to dwell in him, and the condition is that a person grasp there is nothing in him but the divinity that gives him life. Radzin: 'from when the Holy One was yachid in His world He desired a dwelling below', so service has a place even where He is 'one, not by count'. Both are stated. That they match the Chabad sense is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 33, 36-37: the dwelling is the purpose; nothing changes in Him; completion depends on our deeds in exile (37:1, spine).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** As cited by the Rebbe (Basi LeGani 5719:6, read in context): his three parables of 'the higher a thing, the lower it descends'. His own text was not located for this subject.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Term counts show over 350 uses in Or HaTorah (OCR). Not read in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: the dwelling is 'below as above', possible only from the Essence.
- **Rashab.** Eitz HaChaim, scan p. 19: yesh created so that it be nullified and become a dwelling.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 10 (OCR): to make the lower world a place for His dwelling. Only man, not the angels, was given this mission.
- **The Rebbe.** Basi LeGani 5719 (the physical yesh as the dwelling of the Essence, now in truth); Rosh Hashanah 5741 ('a dwelling is where the self is'; made by each Jew's service); Inyanah 28 (spine): the last generations complete it.
- **Other schools.** Keter Shem Tov 2:53:15: God has more delight from physical man than from angels, like a king from a talking bird; the dwelling rests on one who knows he is nothing but the divinity in him. Tiferet Yosef (Pekudei 7): yachid and dwelling joined. Peri Tzadik, Sfas Emes and Arvei Nachal (grep hits, read in the grep context) cite the midrash mainly of the Mishkan and the Shechinah resting below. The real difference: outside Chabad the dwelling is the Shechinah resting on man or on the Sanctuary; in Chabad the Maharash already ties the dwelling to Anochi, the Essence (Toras Shmuel 5627), the Rashab ties it to the bitul of the yesh, and the Rebbe names it 'a dwelling for His Essence' in the physical yesh itself.
