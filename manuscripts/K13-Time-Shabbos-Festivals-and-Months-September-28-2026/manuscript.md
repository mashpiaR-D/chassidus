## What this cluster shows

These thirteen subjects are time itself seen from the one idea. Shaar HaYichud 7 puts time on the created side: a 'world' is space and time, both brought from nothing at every moment by His Malchus, both nullified before the Essence, and yet 'though above space and time He is found below in space and time' (SH 7:4-9). The calendar is then a pattern in which that unity is lived. Day and night are 'one day', the model for 'know that Havayah is Elokim' (LT Vaetchanan 7). Each month is vitality renewed from nothing, and the moon's shrinking to nothing and refilling shows that the tzimtzum lasts an instant (LT Rosh Hashanah 1; R. Nosson). Rosh Hashanah is creation renewed each year: the year's light withdraws, the kingship is drawn anew by the shofar's simple cry, and a light never seen before waits for the deeds of those below (IH 14). Yom Kippur reveals the bond of the soul's essence with the Essence, 'Israel and the King alone' (the Rebbe, 5723). Sukkos is the hidden sought and found, the encompassing light, and the many made truly one; Shemini Atzeres takes it inward (LT; the Rebbe, 5743). Pesach is the exodus from limits by 'Anochi', the Essence, not a radiance (the Maharash), and chametz and matzah are selfhood and bitul. The Omer refines the animal soul so it too can give itself up 'in One' (the Tzemach Tzedek). Shavuos is 'none besides Him' seen with physical sight, and the Torah as His giving of Himself (Tanya 36, 47). Chanukah carries light from above wisdom out to the public domain and below ten handbreadths (Torah Ohr; R. Nosson). Purim is the hidden face, acceptance from below, and the revealed radiance of the Essence, in which Haman and Mordechai are equal (Torah Ohr). The Three Weeks are revealed light withdrawn, descent for a higher ascent, with the soul itself never in exile (LT; the Rebbe, 5741). Shabbos is the return of all things to their source and to the Essence (LT, Torah Ohr, the Mitteler Rebbe), and R. Tzadok names it outright as the higher and lower unity. Four corrections to SUBJECTS follow from the sources: day and night, the new moon, and Sukkos, all marked 'reading', are stated; the Omer, marked 'reading', is stated by one text (Derekh Mitzvotekha) and otherwise one step removed. The tie is thin for the fast as such, which Tanya links to sacrifice and teshuvah, not to the unity. Most of this cluster is the Alter Rebbe's Likkutei Torah and Torah Ohr, which are organized by the calendar; the later Rebbeim appear where they were found, and many cells are honestly empty.

## S150 · Time · זמן

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F05 The Essence beyond both · F01 The world said into being now · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** זמן · למעלה מהזמן · היה הוה ויהיה · סדר זמנים

> Time is the unity of God as a creature: brought from nothing at every moment through His Malchus, while His Name Havayah is 'was, is and will be in one instant', above it and yet present within it.

## Derivation

1. Shaar HaYichud 7 defines a 'world' as space and time: east and west, past, present and future. Time is therefore on the created side of the line (SH 7:4). (*stated*)
2. The life of time, its coming to be from nothing and its whole duration, flows from His Malchus, 'Havayah reigned, reigns and will reign'. Time is a creature re-created like any other (SH 7:5; F01). (*stated*)
3. Because Malchus is one with His Essence, time is 'nullified in existence' before Him like the sun's ray in the sun. The Name Havayah means He is above time, 'was, is and will be in one instant' (SH 7:6-7). This is the higher unity (F02). (*stated*)
4. Yet 'though above space and time He is found below in space and time': this is the lower unity, the Essence filling the earth within time. Malchus is a power of concealment so that time is not annulled (SH 7:8-9; F03). (*stated*)
5. Across the changes of time He does not change: as He was alone before the six days, so He is now (IH 6:6). The Rashab traces how time arises: Ein Sof is wholly beyond time; Adam Kadmon holds time and no-time together; through it the 'order of times' comes to be (Hemshech Ayin Beis, Devarim 5673) (F05, G04). (*stated*)
6. For the person: whatever is above time can enter time. The Rebbe: teshuvah is above time, so it draws the limitless into a moment and changes the past (5745). R. Tzadok: Torah is above time and time is drawn from it (Peri Tzadik 7:16); he says this of Torah and of labour, not of the unity, so placing it in this derivation is our reading. (*stated*)
7. That the calendar is therefore not a line but a recurrence, each appointed time 'happening again as the first time' (Me'or Einayim, Tzav 1), follows from this picture, but joining the Chernobyler's rule to SH 7 is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 7:4-9 is the systematic text: time is a creature of Malchus, nullified before the Essence, and the Essence is nonetheless present in it (the lower unity). IH 6: 'I have not changed' across time. Likkutei Torah, Rosh Hashanah 1:2 adds the 'soul of time': the year has a head and limbs (see S153-S154).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass as a separate treatment of time; his Shaar HaEmunah on Shabbos rest (S152) is the nearest.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass beyond the calendar mitzvot in Derekh Mitzvotekha (S153).
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis (Devarim 5673, sections 341-342): Ein Sof is wholly above the category of time; in Atzilus there is only an 'order of times' of before and after; Adam Kadmon, 'time and no-time', is the middle term from which the order of time arises. Also (sec. 342:3) mitzvot are 'under time' (matzah on Pesach, Shabbos) while Torah is above it.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass as a doctrine of time.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Chayei Sarah 5745: teshuvah is above time, so what is above time enters a moment and even changes the past. Yitro 5734 (S159): the moment of Sinai was above time and included all time.
- **Other schools.** R. Tzadok (Peri Tzadik, Kedushat Shabbat 7:16): the six days of creation are time, the root of all creatures; Torah is above time and time is drawn from it; the 'day that is all Shabbos' (the future rest) is 'the completion of all time'. The Chernobyler (Me'or Einayim, Tzav 1): every appointed time, when it comes round, happens again as the first time. Kedushat Levi (Kedushot for Purim, Kedusha Shlishit 9, read but not cited as a passage) states continuous renewal 'every day, every moment and every hour'. SUBJECTS names Ropshitz's calendar as a ladder of revelations; that was not re-verified here. Difference kept: the Chernobyler stresses recurrence, while IH 14 (S154) insists each year's light is new and higher than any before.

## S151 · Day and night · יום ולילה

**Faces:** F12 Knowing the unity · F09 The unity of opposites · F11 Darkness turned to light · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** יום ולילה · בלילה · בבוקר · חשך ואור

> Day and night are the unity of God as 'one day': darkness and light joined, the order of revelation in which light comes out of darkness, and the daily pattern of the Shechinah's exile and rising.

## Derivation

1. 'Know today (hayom)': just as the day includes the night and both are one, 'there was evening and there was morning, one day', so the two hearts are to be one and Havayah is Elokim (LT Vaetchanan 7:1; F12). (*stated*)
2. The order evening-then-morning is the order of revelation: 'first darkness, then light'. Ein Sof's light comes out of the higher darkness through contractions, and the 'awesome God' joins the two opposites (LT Vaetchanan 7:11-13; F09, F03). (*stated*)
3. The nightly darkening until midnight and the lightening after it is the fall and rising of the Shechinah in exile, raised by those who rise at midnight and by morning prayer (LT Shir HaShirim 24:3; F11). (*stated*)
4. Each day is its own renewal: each morning prayer draws higher mochin than yesterday's (IH 14:6). R. Nosson: each day is a new creation, so at every day's end the tzimtzum of the empty space is renewed (Likutei Halakhot, Mincha 4:1) (F01). (*stated*)
5. Night as exile: the Rashab reads 'why is this night different' as the last exile (Hayom Yom 19 Nisan); the Rayatz: 'darkness is night, the time of exile, and the work is to turn darkness into light' (Purim 5692). (*stated*)
6. How night is held: the Alter Rebbe's 'know today' asks for knowledge that day and night are one; R. Nosson says night is the withdrawal of daat, held by faith alone. The Rayatz (Purim 5692, the passage cited here) stands between them: he too cites 'and Your faithfulness at night' and says that in exile the main thing is faith, which is essential but only encompassing, and must be brought inward into conduct. Setting these against each other as the F12 disagreement is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Likkutei Torah, Vaetchanan 7 (Vayedata hayom): day and night as 'one day', the model for the two hearts and for Havayah-Elokim. LT Shir HaShirim 24: midnight and the Shechinah's exile. IH 14:6: daily renewal in the morning prayer.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (grep only in this pass) discusses tikkun chatzot with weeping; not read in context.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Via the Rayatz, Hayom Yom 19 Nisan: 'this night' is the last exile.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim, Purim 5692: tzimtzum precedes revelation, night is exile, the work is to turn darkness into light, and souls are 'lamps' for the night.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located in this pass beyond Hayom Yom, which he compiled.
- **Other schools.** R. Nosson (Likutei Halakhot, Mincha 4:1-3): each day re-enacts the tzimtzum of the empty space at Mincha, which is why judgment is strongest then; the tzimtzum lasts 'only an instant' and fills at once. Morning Conduct 3:2: at night daat withdraws and one holds on by faith alone ('and Your faithfulness at night'); the questions that come from the empty space cannot be answered, only believed. That is a real difference of emphasis from the Alter Rebbe's 'know today'.

## S152 · Shabbos · שבת

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F01 The world said into being now · F07 The home below **Search terms:** שבת קודש · שבת · מנוחה · ששת ימי המעשה

> Shabbos is the unity of God as return: the worlds and the powers clothed in speech rising back to their source and to the Essence, while He goes on creating, and the higher and lower unity joined.

## Derivation

1. Even on Shabbos He renews creation; were He to withdraw for a moment the world would return to nothing (LT Shabbat Shuvah 3:6). His 'rest' is not an absence (F01). (*stated*)
2. What rests is the mode of creation: on Shabbos He 'rests from speech', the Ten Utterances, and vitality comes through 'thought' alone; hence the additional soul (LT Shabbat Shuvah 3:6). (*stated*)
3. Like a person who stops working and whose mind returns to itself, on Shabbos the vitality spread in the six days rises to its source; 'Shabbos' is the letters of 'tashev', return, and Shabbos and teshuvah are one (LT Shabbat Shuvah 3:8). The Mitteler Rebbe: at rest one comes to the very essence of the soul, and Shabbos is the returning light included in the Essence of the Emanator (Shaar HaEmunah 29) (F05). (*stated*)
4. In the six days only Elokim is named; after 'vayechulu' Havayah appears. Shabbos is Havayah shining through its shield, Elokim (Torah Ohr, Yitro 6:3-5; F03, G14). (*stated*)
5. R. Tzadok: the 'secret of Shabbos united in the secret of One' is the higher and lower unity of Shema and Baruch Shem; even after creation nothing is separate from Him (Peri Tzadik, Kedushat Shabbat 6:1) (F02). (*stated*)
6. Yet the ascent is not the goal. Kuntres Acharon 4: the ascent of Shabbos and Yom Kippur is 'only for the hour'; the purpose of the chain is revelation below, drawn down through practical mitzvot (Kuntres Acharon 4:36-38; F07). Torah Ohr, Yitro 6:6: Israel 'make' the Shabbos by their weekday work. (*stated*)

## S153 · The new moon and the months · ראש חודש ולבנה

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F11 Darkness turned to light · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** ראש חודש · מולד · לבנה · קידוש החודש

> The month is the unity of God as renewal: vitality drawn again from nothing, the moon's shrinking to nothing and refilling showing that the tzimtzum is only for an instant, and renewal given to Israel because 'everything is His vitality'.

## Derivation

1. The year is like a soul with a head and limbs: Rosh Hashanah is the general vitality and 'every month is a renewal of vitality from nothing to something' (LT Rosh Hashanah 1:2; F01). (*stated*)
2. Rosh Chodesh is between Shabbos and weekday: work is allowed because it is still 'speech', but it is not a 'day of work', because on it speech receives from 'before the work of creation' (LT V'Zot HaBerachah 4:7-9). (*stated*)
3. The Tzemach Tzedek: sanctifying the month is Malchus, the moon, rising to receive directly from Chochmah, 'the higher holiness', in which Or Ein Sof is clothed and set apart from all beings (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Kiddush HaChodesh 2). (*stated*)
4. R. Nosson: the moon's waning to nothing is the tzimtzum of the empty space, and its refilling as soon as it reaches nothing shows that the tzimtzum is 'only for an instant'. That is why Moshe found the new moon hard (Likutei Halakhot, Mincha 4:4) (F03). (*stated*)
5. Sfas Emes: 'this month is for you' belongs to the redemption: renewal comes when it is made clear that all is His vitality; one sunk in nature says 'there is nothing new', and each Jew can awaken renewal by faith that all is from Him (Bo 1:6). (*stated*)
6. That Israel's counting by the moon is itself the lower unity (Malchus) receiving from the higher is our joining of the Tzemach Tzedek's Malchus-Chochmah reading with SH 7; he does not use the two-unity terms here. Sfas Emes comes nearer: in the same line as the passage cited, the new moon was shown to Moshe at dusk 'to inform him that Hashem and His Name are one', and the month is renewal 'by way of faith', against Shabbos's clear sight (Bo 1:6). Reading 'Hashem and His Name are one' as the two unities follows R. Tzadok (S152), not Sfas Emes. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** LT Rosh Hashanah 1:2 (each month a renewal from nothing); LT V'Zot HaBerachah 4:7-9 (Rosh Chodesh receives speech from before creation); the maamar 'Vehaya midei chodesh' (in the Siddur) is cited there and not read here.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Kiddush HaChodesh: the rising of Malchus into Chochmah; witnesses 'see' because sight comes from Chochmah.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** R. Nosson (Likutei Halakhot, Mincha 4:4) as above: the moon as the empty space. Sfas Emes (Bo 1:6): renewal as the recognition that all is His vitality. The Degel (grep only) ties Nisan, 'head of the months', to Malchus. SUBJECTS cites Sfas Emes that Israel count by the moon so as not to sink into nature; the Bo passage read here says the same in substance.

## S154 · Rosh Hashanah and Elul · ראש השנה ואלול

> Rosh Hashanah is the unity of God as creation renewed each year: the year's light withdraws to its root, His will to reign is drawn anew by the simple cry of the shofar, and the new, higher light waits to be revealed by the deeds of those below.

## Derivation

1. Tishrei is 'the month' as such, the general renewal of all worlds 'from nothing to something', because Rosh Hashanah is the head of the year (LT Rosh Hashanah 1:2; F01). (*stated*)
2. The year's light withdraws to its root on the eve of Rosh Hashanah; through the shofar and prayer a new light comes down, higher than any that has shone before (IH 14:4-5, 14:10). ## S155 · Yom Kippur · יום הכפורים

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** יום הכפורים · יוה"כ · כפרה · לפני ה' תטהרו

> Yom Kippur is the unity of God as the bond of the soul's essence with His Essence revealed: 'before Havayah' specifically, beyond the angels and beyond wisdom, where every flaw is filled because the yechidah was never apart.

## Derivation

1. For transgressions there is no atonement 'above' until Yom Kippur, 'before Havayah' specifically (Iggeret HaTeshuvah 1:4). (*stated*)
2. In the Ten Days Or Ein Sof itself is drawn, not through the chain of worlds; on Yom Kippur the prayers need no angels to raise them, 'the Holy One Himself receives them', hence no shoes (LT Shabbat Shuvah 3:34). (*stated*)
3. Every flaw is filled on Yom Kippur, the 'Shabbat Shabbaton', because it is the Holy of Holies, above even Chochmah (LT Yom Kippur 2:43; F05). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe: the atonement of Yom Kippur comes from 'the essence of the day' itself, above what teshuvah alone reaches. He distinguishes: teshuvah draws from the 'Master of the will', which is only the hafla'ah of Or Ein Sof and 'not the Essence itself'; the essence of the day reveals the bond of the soul's essence with the Essence above the Master of the will, where no flaw applies at all; teshuvah then draws this into the revealed powers (Shabbat Shuvah 5723, 12-13). (*stated*)
5. In the Holy of Holies the essential bond of the soul's essence with the Essence is revealed: 'Israel and the King alone', no room even for angels. The five prayers match the five soul-levels, and Neilah reveals the yechidah (Shabbat Shuvah 5723, 15-16) (F06). (*stated*)
6. R. Tzadok: 'who dwells with them in their impurity' means that Israel's inner holiness never moved from the root of holiness; the purification reveals this (Peri Tzadik, Motzei Yom Kippur 7). That both hold the same yechidah-level claim is our joining. (**reading**)

- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627, Mi El Kamocha (see K11): forgiveness from above the measuring line; not re-read.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Shabbat Shuvah 5723: the essence of the day, the yechidah at Neilah, the essential bond of the soul with the Essence. K11 cites his Shabbat Shuvah 5737: inwardly there was no blemish.
- **Other schools.** R. Tzadok (Peri Tzadik, Motzei Yom Kippur 4, 7): the purification is from the 'leaven in the dough'; Israel's inner holiness never left its root, so 'who purifies you? your Father in heaven'. ## S156 · Sukkos, Shemini Atzeres and Simchas Torah · סוכות ושמיני עצרת

**Faces:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** סוכה · ד' מינים · ארבעה מינים · לולב · שמיני עצרת · שמחת תורה

> Sukkos is the unity of God as the hidden made joyfully manifest: the encompassing light that surrounds all equally (the sukkah), the many made truly one (the four species), and on Shemini Atzeres the Essence taken into the inwardness of the soul, 'yours alone'.

## Derivation

1. The wise son knows that the father hides only to be sought; the hiding of Rosh Hashanah is 'for the day of our festival', the revelation of Sukkos (LT Sukkot 4:11; F03; the parable is familiar from the Besht, but LT does not name him here). (*stated*)
2. On Sukkos the joy is revealed from above as 'sukkah and makif', encompassing light; on Shemini Atzeres it leaves the encompassing mode and is taken into the inwardness of souls (LT Shemini Atzeret 5:19; F04). (*stated*)
3. The water libation is the water of bitul: 'ein od milvado' seen as if by sight, which cools the fire of longing into the silent nearness of one who stands before the King (LT Sukkot 1:9-10; F08). (*stated*)
4. The four species, one mitzvah from four kinds of Jew, are 'a true unity above division', not a joining of parts. For the sukkah he distinguishes: seven days joined in one sukkah is only the joining of separate things; the true unity is 'every citizen shall dwell', with no count of days (the Rebbe, 13 Tishrei 5743). He does not use the words 'yachid' and 'echad' here; matching this to the spine's yachid-echad precision (Inyanah 5:5) is our reading. (*stated*)
5. Shemini Atzeres is Simchas Torah, the crown of Torah above intellect, the minds gathered under it in bitul (LT Sukkot 4:14). The Rebbe: it reveals the essential bond of the Essence with the soul, all of Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur 'in the line of joy', 'yours alone' (Shabbat Shuvah 5723, 17) (F06). (*stated*)
6. R. Nosson: after Yom Kippur's search for 'where is the place of His glory', the boundary of holiness widens; the sukkah's many walls outdoors bring even the outside in (Likutei Halakhot, Meat and Milk 3:3-5). Reading this as the lower unity reaching outward is ours. (**reading**)

Shabbat Shuvah 5723, 17: Shemini Atzeres as the revelation of the yechidah. Simchas Torah 5723 (grep only): 'Yeshurun', the essence of the soul in which all Jews are equal.
- **Other schools.** R. Nosson (Likutei Halakhot, Meat and Milk 3:3-5): the sukkah widens holiness outward, even to the seventy nations. Ropshitz (pillar 10, not re-verified): the sukkah's hidden heat all but consumes those who cannot receive it. Difference of emphasis kept: the Alter Rebbe's sukkah is light from above (makif), R. Nosson's is holiness extended outward from below after teshuvah.

## S157 · Pesach and the Exodus · פסח ויציאת מצרים

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F07 The home below · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** פסח · יציאת מצרים · מצה · חמץ

> Pesach is the unity of God as the exodus from limits, done by 'Anochi', His Essence, not by a radiance, and lived as the divine soul's leaving its prison to be included in His unity, chametz being the swelling 'I' and matzah its nullification.

## Derivation

1. Every day one must see oneself as leaving Egypt: the divine soul leaving the body's prison 'to be included in the unity of Or Ein Sof', above all through the Shema (Tanya 47:1; F06). (*stated*)
2. The Maharash: the first commandment says 'who took you out of Egypt' and not 'who created', because creation from nothing is 'only from a mere radiance', while the Exodus comes from 'Anochi', His Essence, which alone can take one out of the limits so that there be a dwelling below, 'below as above' (Toras Shmuel 5627, Matzos Yei'achel) (F05, F07). (*stated*)
3. Sfas Emes: the ten sayings were light work; making His vitality clear inside nature, the Exodus, was 'heavier', because choice and power were given to the other side (Bo 2:2). (*stated*)
4. The person's side: chametz rises and swells, the selfhood of 'something', even the pride of great comprehension; matzah is flat, the bitul of faith (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; the Rashab via the Rayatz, Hayom Yom 19 Nisan; LT Pekudei 1:54) (F08). (*stated*)
5. The Chernobyler: matzah and chametz differ only by the stroke between chet and heh; the inclination dresses itself as a mitzvah (Me'or Einayim, Tzav 3). Its bearing on bitul is our reading. (**reading**)
6. The Maharash's 'creation is only a radiance' stands against IH 20 and the Rebbe, for whom only the Essence creates yesh (spine F05). Torah Ohr, Megillat Esther 12:23 agrees with the Maharash on this point. The tension is kept. (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 47 (daily exodus into the unity), Tanya 31 (grep only: the people 'fled' because evil was still strong), LT Pekudei 1:52-54 (matzah as bitul, preparation for Sinai), LT Sukkot 3:8 (the exodus as leaving the limits of love). Via the Rayatz (spine F08): chametz is selfhood, matzah bitul.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, chametz and matzah: matzah is 'the food of faith'; faith is not the knowledge that He gives life to the worlds, which the eye can almost see, but the world's nullity like the ray in the sun; chametz is the swelling of comprehension as a 'something'.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627, Matzos Yei'achel: the Exodus is from Anochi, the Essence, 'not a mere radiance'; the two knowledges; matzah strengthens faith in what is above the chain of worlds.
- **Rashab.** Hayom Yom 19 Nisan (his reading of Mah Nishtanah, via the Rayatz): after the last exile 'all of it matzah'. Hemshech Ayin Beis 342:3: the mitzvah of matzah is 'under time'.
- **Rayatz.** Transmits the Alter Rebbe's and the Rashab's readings (Hayom Yom).
- **The Rebbe.** The Haggadah volumes on the shelf were not searched in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes (Bo 1:4-2:2): the hardening of Pharaoh's heart is itself help, 'the hiding itself is for good'; the Exodus clarifies His vitality inside nature. The Chernobyler (Me'or Einayim, Tzav 1-3): each Nisan the redemption happens again as the first time; the exile is both general and personal, the soul in exile with the inclination; chametz and matzah differ by a stroke.

## S158 · Counting the Omer · ספירת העומר

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F09 The unity of opposites · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** ספירת העומר · העומר · מ"ט ימים · שבע שבתות

> The Omer is the unity of God as the animal soul refined, trait by trait, until it too can give itself up 'in One' at Sinai.

## Derivation

1. On the first night of Pesach the revelation comes 'by leaping', from above, and then withdraws; the counting is the effort from below to draw it again (LT Pekudei 1:53). (*stated*)
2. The omer is barley, animal food; the counting refines the seven traits of the animal soul, each containing seven, 'so that all be for God' (LT Vayikra 2:26; Pekudei 1:53; F11). (*stated*)
3. The Supreme Will revealed at Sinai could come only after a will prepared below; that is the counting (LT Emor 4:14; F10). (*stated*)
4. The Tzemach Tzedek: the counting draws the hidden light into the omer step by step so that 'even the animal soul can give itself up in One (echad)' when 'Anochi' is revealed (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Omer 5). This is the explicit tie to the unity. (*stated*)
5. Ropshitz: in the counting days each trait is to be included in the others at their root, drawn from Shabbos, 'the root of all traits'; the Omer means joining, like 'love your fellow' (Zera Kodesh, Kedoshim 1). He speaks of the traits joined in their root and in love of one's fellow, not of the unity of God; filing this under F09 is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** LT Vayikra 2:25-26, Pekudei 1:53, Emor 4:12-15, Tzav 9:3: the Omer as the refinement of the animal soul, the effort from below after the gift from above.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat HaOmer 1-5: barley as 'shiur Hei', the measured vitality of angels and of the animal soul; the counting so that the animal soul too gives itself up in 'echad'; the fiftieth gate as the source of the forty-nine above division.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Ropshitz (Zera Kodesh, Kedoshim 1; Achrei Mot 2 grep only): the Omer is repairing and joining the traits in their root, and the gematria of 'omer' with 'lere'acha'. Pillar 21 adds his forty-nine including their dark twin (Chanukah 29, not re-verified). Difference of emphasis: Chabad frames the counting as refining the animal soul toward Sinai; Ropshitz as the inclusion of the traits in one another and in the love of one's fellow.

## S159 · Shavuos · שבועות

**Faces:** F12 Knowing the unity · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below **Search terms:** שבועות · חג השבועות · עצרת · זמן מתן תורתנו

2. Because His inner will was revealed without concealment, they were nullified in existence and their souls flew out (Tanya 36:10; F08). (*stated*)
3. The Torah clothes His will and wisdom, one with His Essence, so giving it is 'as if He gave us Himself' (Tanya 47:3; F10). (*stated*)
4. The Maharash: 'Anochi' is His Essence, not a radiance (S157), and matzah prepares for it; the Tzemach Tzedek: at 'Anochi' even the animal soul gives itself up in 'echad' (S158) (F05). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe: at Sinai the decree separating upper and lower was annulled; Torah is above time and place, and the moment of the giving was above time and included all of it (Yitro 5734, 4-5; the point about time he offers as 'it may be said'). Sfas Emes: as the Ten Sayings made the heavens, so at 'Anochi' Godliness rested on the souls of Israel; Torah is above time and renews (Yitro 5647) (F07). (*stated*)

## S160 · Chanukah · חנוכה

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** חנוכה · נר חנוכה · נרות חנוכה · נס חנוכה

> Chanukah is the unity of God as light carried outward: a light from above wisdom set at the door facing the public domain and below ten handbreadths, turning the darkness of 'the wisdom of the husk' into light and carrying the higher unity into the lower (in R. Nosson's sense of those terms).

## Derivation

1. The Greek decree was not an exile of bodies but an exile of Torah, 'to make them forget Your Torah', so the miracle is fixed in lights: 'a mitzvah is a lamp and Torah is light' (Torah Ohr, Miketz 3:1). (*stated*)
2. Against 'the wisdom of the husk' a light from above holy wisdom had to be drawn, encompassing light; therefore it reaches down even now, without a Temple (Torah Ohr, Miketz 4:42; F04). The Tzemach Tzedek: the lamp itself draws the 'hidden wisdom' above the chain of worlds, so one may not use its light (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ner Chanukah 6). (*stated*)
3. It is lit outside at the door facing the public domain, 'the place of the husks', to turn darkness into light, so that Or Ein Sof reaches even the one standing outside (Torah Ohr, Miketz 8:20; F11). (*stated*)
4. R. Nosson: it is placed below ten handbreadths, where the Shechinah never descended, so the light reaches the lowest levels (Likutei Halakhot, Kilayim 2:2); the twenty-fifth of Kislev answers to the twenty-five letters of the Shema, and Chanukah 'includes the higher and the lower unity' (Kilayim 2:4). In R. Nosson's usage here the two unities are two modes of guarding the covenant, the tzaddikim's (Shabbos to Shabbos) and the many's (the weekdays), and Chanukah draws from the great to the small (Kilayim 2:1, 2:4); they are not the SH 7 pair of 'nothing before Him' and 'a real thing below'. Filing this under F02 is our reading. (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe: Chanukah (like Esther) is a complete miracle clothed in nature, by war, like the hidden miracles of every exile (Shaar HaEmunah 30) (F03). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Torah Ohr, Vayeshev 4 and Miketz 3-14: exile of the Torah, encompassing light above wisdom, the lamp at the public domain, Chanukah (drawing down) against Shabbos (raising up), mezuzah on the right and lamp on the left.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or, Shaar HaEmunah 30: the miracles of Chanukah and Esther clothed in nature.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ner Chanukah 1-8: why the lights and not the victory; the lamp draws the hidden wisdom above the chain; the details of the halacha (the door, the left side, the adding each night) read as orders of divine drawing.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Via the Rayatz, Hayom Yom (Kislev entries, read only for customs): his practice of lighting; no doctrinal statement found.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass beyond customs in Hayom Yom.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** R. Nosson (Likutei Halakhot, Kilayim 2:2-5): the lamp as 'speech that shines', below ten handbreadths, the Shema's twenty-five letters, and 'higher and lower unity' in his own sense (the tzaddikim and the many; Shabbos and weekday). Sfas Emes (Miketz 5646, grep only): when Greek rule fell, the renewal was revealed. Ropshitz (pillar 21): Chanukah as repair of the seven traits; not re-verified.

> — Torah Ohr (Miketz 8): the Chanukah lamp is lit outside, by the door facing the public domain, the place of the husks, to turn darkness into light; Or Ein Sof reaches even the one standing outside.

> — Torah Ohr (Miketz 3): the Greek decree was an exile of the Torah, not of the body, so the miracle is fixed in lights ('a mitzvah is a lamp and Torah is light').

> וע"כ הוצרך להמשיך מבחי' שלמעלה מהחכמה דקדושה והוא מבחי' מקיפים עליונים
> — Torah Ohr (Miketz 4): against 'the wisdom of the husk' a light from above holy wisdom had to be drawn, encompassing light, which is why it reaches down to us without a Temple.

## S161 · Purim · פורים

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F05 The Essence beyond both · F09 The unity of opposites · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** פורים · מגילת אסתר · ימי הפורים · עד דלא ידע

> Purim is the unity of God as the hidden face: a salvation clothed in nature, an acceptance from below that outranks Sinai, and a joy 'until one does not know', because when the radiance of the Essence shows, cursed Haman and blessed Mordechai are equal.

## Derivation

1. The salvation of Esther is a complete miracle clothed in the ways of nature, like the hidden miracles of every exile (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaEmunah 30; F03). Sfas Emes: 'Where is Esther in the Torah? I will surely hide My face'; the 'Anochi' of Sinai remains in hiding in Israel (Purim 5655). (*stated*)
2. The Rayatz: tzimtzum precedes revelation and the inclination precedes the good, so that light gains from darkness (Purim 5692). (*stated*)
3. Their self-sacrifice came from not wanting to be separated from His very Essence, above even 'the life of all life' (Torah Ohr, Megillat Esther 12:23; F05, F06). (*stated*)
4. At Sinai the bitul was forced from above; in the days of Achashverosh it came from below, so the light drawn on Purim is higher and Sinai was only 'what they began' (Torah Ohr, Megillat Esther 12:24; F08). Sfas Emes says the same: Sinai from above, Purim from below. (*stated*)
5. Hence 'until one does not know': festival joy is measured by the chain of worlds; Purim's joy is above knowledge, because when 'the radiance of His Essence' (he'aras atzmuso) is revealed 'all are truly equal, darkness like light' (Torah Ohr, Megillat Esther 12:25-26; F09). (*stated*)
6. Torah Ohr draws a halachic consequence, but not the one G10 would want: because Purim's revelation is above the order of the worlds, the festival ban on work, which belongs to the ascent within that order, does not apply to Purim (12:27-28). The text does not say that 'until one does not know' leaves good and evil distinct in conduct; applying G10 here is wholly our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Torah Ohr, Megillat Esther 1-12 and Parashat Zachor: acceptance from below, joy above knowledge, the radiance of the Essence where Haman and Mordechai are equal; also that creation of yesh is 'only a radiance' (12:23), which bears on the S157 tension.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaEmunah 30: the miracle of Esther clothed in nature. In the same passage he frames miracles by the two unities: now the lower unity is not equal to the higher even in miracles; in the future 'Havayah will be one and His Name one', the lower equal to the higher (Shaar HaEmunah 30:1). His Sha'arei Orah (on Chanukah and Purim) was not read in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Ner Chanukah 1 (grep and skim): Purim fixed on the day after the war, like Chanukah on the day after the victory.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim, Purim 5692: tzimtzum before revelation; darkness is night, exile, and the work is to turn it into light; the soul as a lamp for the night.
- **The Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes (Purim 5655, 5657): Esther as hiding; the salvation 'from within the strait itself'; the reading of the Megillah makes the miracles happen again. Kedushat Levi (Purim, pillar 06, not re-verified): joy in His joy. SUBJECTS names 'the hidden face' and Torah Or's 'acceptance from below outranks Sinai', both confirmed here.

## S162 · Fasts and the Three Weeks · בין המצרים ותענית

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F11 Darkness turned to light · F06 The soul as part of the One · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** בין המצרים · תשעה באב · חורבן · תענית

> The Three Weeks are the unity of God as concealment: the destruction is revealed light withdrawn into hiding, not His absence, the soul itself never in exile, and the descent made for a higher ascent; the fast draws from above what eating raises from below.

## Derivation

1. The destruction is the withdrawal of revealed light ('eleh') back to its source in concealment; the weeping is over the hiding (LT Pekudei 1:42; F03). (*stated*)
2. Exile does not reach the soul: 'only the body is in exile, not the soul', which is a part of God, and one who holds part of the Essence holds all of it (the Rebbe, Shabbat Chazon 5741, citing the Rayatz and the Besht; F06). (*stated*)
3. The descent of exile is for an ascent higher than the Temple period; the lower the descent, the higher the ascent, 'the advantage of light from darkness'. Turning the darkness itself into light is 'by the power of the Essence' and completes the dwelling below (Shabbat Chazon 5741, 7; F11, F07). (*stated*)
4. R. Nosson: in 'between the straits' the repair is only the cry 'Eichah', read 'Ayeh', the search for where His glory is, and this is attained only through Shabbos, when all worlds rise to the 'hidden saying'; so even one fallen to the lowest places is to search from there 'where is the place of His glory' (Likutei Halakhot, Shabbat 7:23). (*stated*)
5. The fast: a fast is a 'day of will' because not eating reverses the flow, drawing life from above, from the thirteen attributes, instead of raising it from below (LT Yom Kippur 2:24). Tanya: now that there is no sacrifice, a fast stands in its place (IT 2:4). (*stated*)
6. That the fast as such expresses the unity is our reading: Tanya ties fasts to sacrifice and teshuvah, and warns that teshuvah is not fasting (IT 1:9-10, see K11). (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** LT Pekudei 1:41-42 (the destruction as light withdrawn), LT Shir HaShirim 24 (midnight lament and the Shechinah's exile, S151), LT Yom Kippur 2:24 (fasting as drawing from above), Iggeret HaTeshuvah 1-3 (fasts in place of sacrifices, not the essence of teshuvah).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Ner Mitzvah VeTorah Or (grep only): the benefit of weeping at tikkun chatzot for the self-delight revealed in prayer.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Cited by the Rebbe (his Reshimot on 'Zion shall be redeemed with justice'; grep only); not read directly.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** His maamar 'Tzion bemishpat tipadeh' (5674, grep only) is the basis of the Rebbe's 5741 maamar; not read directly.
- **Rayatz.** 'Only the body is in exile, not the soul', as quoted by the Rebbe.
- **The Rebbe.** Shabbat Chazon 5741: exile belongs to vessels, not light; the soul is not in exile; descent for a greater ascent, greatest in the body.
- **Other schools.** R. Nosson (Likutei Halakhot, Shabbat 7:22-23): the three Shabbatot of rebuke; the breaking of the tablets as the way holiness is received in hiding; 'Eichah' as 'Ayeh'. Kedushat Levi (Purim 3:8, grep only): when Israel does not do His will the Shechinah is 'in heaven'. Difference kept: the Rebbe says exile does not touch the soul; LT and R. Nosson speak of the Shechinah itself fallen in exile. We read these as the two knowledges (G02); no text says so.
