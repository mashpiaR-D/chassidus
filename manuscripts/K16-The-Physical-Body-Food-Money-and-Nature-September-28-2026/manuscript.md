## What this cluster shows

These twelve subjects are the one idea at its lowest point, where the spine says it shows most fully (Inyanah 19:4; Tanya 36). Each is read the same way. First, the thing exists and lives now by the word of God within it: food, stone, body (F01; SH 1:4-5, 2:5; Keter Shem Tov 1:159); livelihood comes down as creation from nothing, its substance from the inwardness of Ein Sof (the Rashab, Umaayan 22, 24). Second, its apparent independence is a concealment. Nature is Elokim (SH 6), the stars that bring food are only messengers (the Rashab), and the doctor is the Healer's messenger (the Rebbe) (F03, G14); for Radzin 'nature' is only the habit of a creation that has forgotten its beginning. Third, it is rooted higher than it looks. Bread feeds man because its spark fell from Tohu. The mineral is rooted above the plant. The body is the Essence's free choice. Begetting is like creation from nothing. The ark took up no space (F05, G05). Fourth, the human work is to join it back: 'know Him in all your ways', where knowing is joining (TzH 94), eating that rises 'like an offering' (Tanya 7), business that is not 'for themselves' (IH 9), charity that turns the working day into His dwelling (Tanya 34) (F07, F08, F11). The strongest stated ties are IH 9 (whoever separates earthly business from the One bears false witness in the Shema), Keter Shem Tov 1:159 (the utterance in the fruit is the soul's food), Torah Ohr Vayeshev 3 (sleep as the mind's withdrawal into the soul's essence, and the dream joining opposites from where all is one), the Rebbe on the body (the Essence's free choice), on procreation (the infinite power, like creation from nothing) and on nature and miracle (Elokim and Havayah joined by the Essence), the Rashab on waking as a renewal like creation, and SH 7 and Keter Shem Tov 1:55 on place ('He is the place of the world, being infinite; so there is none else'). Corrections to SUBJECTS.md: sleep (marked 'thin') is stated in Chabad through Torah Ohr and the Rashab; money, work, the four kingdoms and procreation (marked 'reading') are stated; health (marked 'thin') is partly thin. Real differences are kept: how much of the physical can be raised and by whom (Tanya 7's rule, which binds everyone, against Ruzhin's 'path' for the chosen few; within Chabad, the earlier Chassidim who broke desire by refusing permitted things they craved), the future of physicality (Ropshitz and R. Nachman: nullified; the Rebbe, Basi LeGani 5719: the created yesh will reveal its true root), breaking or refining the body (Amdur against the Besht, with R. Nachman asking both mercy for the body and the breaking of its brazenness), and what sleep and vitality are (Torah Ohr, SH and the Rashab against R. Nachman, who uses the words in another sense). The Alter Rebbe, the Besht's circle and the Rebbe carry most of the weight. The Tzemach Tzedek appears through Derekh Mitzvotekha, the Rashab through Kuntres Umaayan and Ayin Beis, the Maharash through Toras Shmuel. The Mitteler Rebbe appears only for place, and the Rayatz only through Hayom Yom and the spine.

## S186 · Serving God in the physical · עבודה בגשמיות

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F11 Darkness turned to light · F09 The unity of opposites · F01 The world said into being now **Search terms:** עבודה בגשמיות · בכל דרכיך דעהו · גשמיות ורוחניות · גשמיות

> Serving God in the physical is the unity of God as a joining: the physical act is bound to the Name ('know Him' means 'join') so that the lowest thing, which holds His vitality, becomes part of His dwelling below.

## Derivation

1. 'The whole earth is full of His glory, and in everything is His vitality', so that all love and fear, even those found in bad things, are from Him (Tzava'at HaRivash 127:1; spine F01). So a physical act is not outside the domain of the One. (*stated*)
2. The Besht turns this into a rule: 'in all your ways know Him', where 'know' means 'join', the Vav to the Heh of the Name, 'even in physical things one does' (TzH 94; Keter Shem Tov 2:16). Keter Shem Tov 1:69 says da'at must be put into every physical thing 'to raise it, bind it, and join the tent to be one'. (*stated*)
3. The Alter Rebbe sets the measure. Permitted things done for the body's wish, even its real need, without intent for Heaven, draw their life from kelipat nogah. Eaten to widen the mind for God, the same meat and wine are refined and 'rise to God like an offering' (Tanya 7:1, 7:4). (*stated*)
4. Why the physical is the place: the purpose of the worlds is the lowest one, a dwelling below (Tanya 36; spine F07). The Rashab: the inner intent is that souls refine the spark of good in the physical thing and raise it to its root, 'the purpose of all the worlds', which is why most of the Torah and mitzvot are clothed in physical things (Kuntres Umaayan 24:1:9). The Rebbe adds that within the work of refinement itself, refining optional, permitted things for Heaven completes the dwelling 'more' than refining the physical objects of mitzvot, because they remain 'your deeds', the lowest level (SMM, 9 Kislev 5738). He does not rank them above the mitzvot as such. (*stated*)
5. Ropshitz gives the same reason in other words: this world of action, 'under time', was made so that lowly things are raised, 'and He has delight that even through lowly things He is served' (Zera Kodesh, Bechukotai 2). (*stated*)
6. The opposites stay opposites in conduct. The Alter Rebbe tells a young prodigy that spirituality and physicality are 'opposites in their essence'; his example is contentment, the greatest virtue in the physical and the greatest flaw in the spiritual (Hayom Yom 30 Sivan). The joining is done by the person's intent and deed. It is not a claim that the two are the same (G10). (*stated*)
7. That this is F11 (the physical 'public domain' turned into the 'domain of the Single One') and that the whole doctrine is the human side of F07, is our reading of how the pieces fit. Each piece is stated separately. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 7:1-7:6: the permitted is nogah, raised only when done for Heaven; eating in gluttony drags it down 'for the time being' but, being permitted, it can rise again when he returns. Tanya 34:6-7: the business day becomes 'a place for His dwelling' through charity, and whatever he ate for health rises in Torah and prayer. Hayom Yom 30 Sivan: spirituality and physicality are opposites in essence (said of contentment).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass for this subject.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achilat Shirei Menachot 6-7 (lines 169-170, read; cited in S187): weekday eating refines the fallen spark of Tohu out of nogah, while eating on Shabbat is a drawing down 'from above downward', like the priests' eating, not a refinement, and must be preceded by prayer as the priests' eating was by a blessing.
- **Maharash.** See S187 (food's spark higher than man's).
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Umaayan 24:1:7-9 (cited): the kindness of livelihood is clothed down to the stars, as a father plays with his child in nutshells, and its inner intent is that souls refine the spark of good in the physical thing, 'the purpose of all the worlds'. Hayom Yom 25 Teves (carried from the spine, F11): 'the world itself is truly good, because it is His will'; Chassidus's exodus is from the world's limits within the world.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (carried from the spine, OCR): Chassidus is 'the wisdom of joining form and matter', through which 'the physical becomes a vessel for Godliness'.
- **The Rebbe.** SMM, 9 Kislev 5738 (Vayetze): within the refinements, refining optional, permitted things for Heaven completes the intent of the dwelling below more than refining the physical objects of mitzvot, since they stay 'your deeds'. The desire for a dwelling below is 'in His Essence' ('about a desire there is no question').
- **Baal Shem Tov.** TzH 94 and Keter Shem Tov 1:69, 2:16: 'know Him' is joining, even in physical deeds. TzH 22: even in the privy one thinks 'I separate evil from good'. Keter Shem Tov 1:70: 'see life with the woman', even in talk and worldly business, to raise the words.
- **Other schools.** Ruzhin (Irin Kadishin, Bechukotai 1): the 'road' of Torah and mitzvot is for everyone; the 'path' of serving God in eating, drinking and every pleasure, seeing His vitality there, is for 'chosen individuals' and opens only to one who keeps the road. Ropshitz (Zera Kodesh, Bechukotai 2): God delights in being served by lowly things now, but 'in the future, when physicality is nullified, we will truly keep the mitzvot in thought'. Pillar 04 adds Berditchev (desire is love 'in a vessel within a vessel'), Bat Ayin ('two dwellings') and R. Nosson (the imagination is the arena); not re-verified here.

## S187 · Eating · אכילה

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F11 Darkness turned to light · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below **Search terms:** אכילה · סעודה · מאכל · לא על הלחם

> Eating is the unity of God as nourishment: what feeds the soul is the word of God that stands in the bread and brings it into being, so that eating joins the word in the food to the word in the eater and raises it.

## Derivation

1. Every creature exists by the utterance that stands in it now (F01). The Ari, in Keter Shem Tov 1:159, applies this to food: the utterance 'let the earth bring forth' entered the fruit 'as its inner life', 'as it says, Forever, O God, Your word stands in the heavens'. (*stated*)
2. Hence the verse 'not by bread alone does man live, but by all that comes from the mouth of God': the soul is fed by the divine word clothed in the bread, not by the bread 'in itself' (Keter Shem Tov 1:159; Likkutei Torah, Tzav 7:8; Tzofnat Paneach, Shemot 2:5). (*stated*)
3. Why a lower thing (plant) can feed a higher one (speaker): its spark fell from Tohu, which is higher than Tikkun, where man is rooted (LT Tzav 7:9-11; the Maharash, Toras Shmuel 5627; the Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha, Shirei Menachot 6). The rule 'whatever fell lower is higher in its root' is put in so many words by the Tzemach Tzedek, of 'all foods and the body's needs' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Procreation 2), and by the Rebbe (13 Tishrei 5741; 9 Kislev 5738). Likkutei Torah and the Maharash give the Tohu root without the formula. This is G05 stated for food. (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe goes further. The 'source' (motza) of God's mouth is not only the filling light that makes the bread exist; it is sovev and, more generally, 'what is above memale and sovev, above the whole order of the chain' (SMM, 15 Shevat 5737). He learns it from the manna, which made this known. He does not name the Essence here; filing it under F05 is ours. (*stated*)
5. The human work is to eat so that the vitality rises: 'bring out the vitality in it and raise it' (TzH 127:3); eaten for God, the food rises 'like an offering' (Tanya 7:4), and whatever one ate for health rises in Torah and prayer (Tanya 34:7). The blessing wakes the word in the food (Keter Shem Tov 1:159). (*stated*)
6. The limit: forbidden food cannot rise even if eaten for Heaven (Tanya 8:1), save through a teshuvah so great that sins become merits (Tanya 7:9), and gluttony drags the permitted down 'for the time being' (Tanya 7:6). (*stated*)
7. That the purpose of creation itself is 'to refine the holy sparks through Israel from permitted foods' (Keter Shem Tov 1:160) links eating to F07. The Keter Shem Tov states the purpose; calling it the dwelling below is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Likkutei Torah, Tzav 7:8-10 (cited); Tanya 7:4, 7:6, 8:1 and 34:7 (7:4 cited in S186; 34:7 cited here).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Grep only: 'not by bread alone' appears in Toras Chaim, Imrei Binah, Ner Mitzvah and Pirush HaMilos; not read in context in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Shirei Menachot 6-7 (cited; lines 169-170, read): a Jew who eats refines the Tohu spark out of nogah, and the food gives him life 'because its root is higher than man'; Shabbat eating is instead a drawing down from above, like the priests' eating. Procreation 2 (cited): 'whatever fell lower is higher in its root', said of the evil inclination, the animal soul, 'all foods and the body's needs'.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627, 'VeHaBriach HaTichon' (cited): food feeds man because its spark is higher, from Tohu. The same image recurs at line 7303 (grep only): bread joins soul and body, and 'a child cannot say Abba until he tastes grain'.
- **Rashab.** Not located for eating in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** SMM 15 Shevat 5737 (cited): the manna teaches that the 'source' of God's mouth is above memale and sovev, and it 'became blood and flesh like his flesh'. SMM 13 Tishrei 5741 (line 298, read, not cited): food shows 'the higher, the lower'; bread includes 'all the needs of man'.
- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:159-160 (from the Ari): the utterance in the fruit is the soul's food; the parable of the king's lost jewel, which only his son may find; creation is for refining the sparks through permitted food. TzH 127:3 (cited).
- **Other schools.** Polonoye (Tzofnat Paneach, Shemot 2:5-6): the spiritual in food is called 'bread', and 'there is nothing physical in the world without spiritual life in it'. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 60:6): eating in holiness keeps the mind as 'the face'; unrefined food casts the mind into 'sleep'. The Breslov text ties eating to the mind, not to the unity; that link is our reading. Pillar 04 notes that most of the pillar's volume is R. Nosson on eating (Likutei Halakhot); not re-verified here.

## S188 · Sleep · שינה

**Faces:** F09 The unity of opposites · F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F06 The soul as part of the One · F01 The world said into being now **Search terms:** שינה · בשעת השינה · בשנתו · ישנים

> Sleep is the unity of God as withdrawal into the essence: the dividing mind leaves, the soul's life goes back to its root where all opposites are one, and exile itself is this sleep whose life will be revealed; waking is the soul's daily renewal, like creation.

## Derivation

1. In sleep the discriminating mind withdraws and only imagination remains, which can join two opposites in one subject; awake, the mind sees they are separate (Torah Ohr, Vayeshev 3:1-2). (*stated*)
2. The dream's power to join opposites has a root above. In the 'circles' (igulim) there is no above and below, and all things divided below are 'included and united without any separation' (TO Vayeshev 3:8-9). The dream is a trace of the unity of opposites (F09). (*stated*)
3. Sleep is 'the withdrawal of the mind into the essence of the soul'. Above, the corresponding event is the light withdrawing from the vessels 'into the Essence of Or Ein Sof', which below is called 'sleep' (TO Miketz 6:16-17). That upper sleep is no gain below: it is what gave Egypt and Babylon dominion, unlike the light clothed in the sefirot, 'the Holy One puts on tefillin' (TO Miketz 6:18). Filing this under F05 (withdrawal into the Essence) and F03 (concealment) is ours. (*stated*)
4. Exile is this sleep. In exile the divine spark in a person is 'in sleep and withdrawal of the mind', and just then the upper light of the circles glints through, hidden in 'darkness'. At the redemption 'we were like dreamers' will be revealed, when all will know 'the life drawn to them in exile from the dream' (TO Vayeshev 3:3, 3:10-11). F03: the hiding is itself the channel. (*stated*)
5. The soul's part of God also 'sleeps': in sinners the chochmah of the divine soul is 'in sleep' and wakes in a trial of faith, 'He awoke like a sleeper' (Tanya 19:10-11). F06. (*stated*)
6. In practice Tzava'at HaRivash teaches going to sleep with the thought that one's mind goes up to the Holy One to be strengthened for His service, and rising briskly because one is 'renewed, another person', fit to beget, like God who begot worlds (TzH 20, 23). Keter Shem Tov 1:71: a sleeper wakes when called by name, because the soul is grasped by its name. That this is G01 (the name as the thing's life) is our reading: the text speaks of a person's name, not of divine speech. (*stated*)
7. The Rashab makes sleep and waking a case of F01 in so many words. Night is 'the time of darkness and concealment, when materiality prevails'; rising from sleep, a person 'is renewed in the light and life of the soul', for 'as in the creation of the worlds He renews in His goodness every day the work of creation', so a person is every day 'like a new creature' (Hemshech Ayin Beis 5676, Vayechi). Tzava'at HaRivash says only 'renewed'. (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Torah Ohr, Vayeshev 3 and Miketz 6 (cited); Tanya 19:10-11 (cited). Likkutei Torah (line 4836, grep only): exile 'is like sleep': all the life is whole, only the eyes are closed. Line 4597 (grep only): 'in exile the Holy One rises higher and higher, and this is called sleep'. IH 9:1 uses sleep as rebuke: 'I come as one who wakes sleepers in the slumber of vanities' (read; cited in S190).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Carried from K15, not re-verified: Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah, 'in exile the Holy One withdraws higher and higher' into the Essence. This is the Torah Ohr image of sleep, without the word.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 5676, Vayechi (cited; lines 3198-3199, read): night is the time of darkness and concealment when materiality prevails, and a spirit of impurity rests on the animal soul; on waking the soul is still 'in his nostrils', so one says Modeh Ani, a general thanks, and prayer then makes the animal soul a vessel; rising, a person is 'like a new creature', as God renews creation daily.
- **Rayatz.** Grep only: the phrase 'one sixtieth of death' appears in his Sefer HaMaamarim (OCR); not read.
- **The Rebbe.** Grep only: 'one sixtieth of death' in Toras Menachem; not read.
- **Baal Shem Tov.** TzH 20:1 and 23:1 (cited); TzH 26-27 on rising at midnight and sleeping by day to shorten the night; Keter Shem Tov 1:71 (read, not cited): a sleeper wakes when called by name.
- **Other schools.** R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 60:6): 'sleep' is the mind fallen into smallness, through lusts or unrefined food; people can 'sleep away their days' while seeming to serve God, and must be woken, by the sage's stories (60:6-8). This is a real difference: in Torah Ohr sleep is the soul's withdrawal to its root and the dream carries the unity of opposites, while for R. Nachman sleep is the absence of mind, to be woken from. Neither text addresses the other. The Rashab holds both sides within Chabad: night is concealment, waking is renewal.

## S189 · Money and livelihood · פרנסה וממון

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** פרנסה · ממון · עשירות · ממונם

> Money is the unity of God as sustenance: it holds a person's very life, comes through natural messengers that have no power of their own, and becomes His dwelling when it is given back; to worship it is to set up another god.

## Derivation

1. All sustenance is His, and the stars and nature through which it comes are 'only messengers of the flow', like an axe in the builder's hand. Idolatry began when people bowed to sun and moon because food comes 'from them' (Kuntres Umaayan 23:4-5). This is F03 stated about livelihood. That the idolater's error is the one F08 names, taking a thing for a power in its own right, is our filing: the Rashab does not speak of bitul here. (*stated*)
2. Livelihood comes down as creation from nothing, through the vessels of the ten sefirot of Asiyah, and is judged every day (Umaayan 22:6, 23:2). Existence as such comes about only through concealment, Elokim being the shield that covers the light of Havayah that creates (Umaayan 23:2:3-4), yet 'the body and essence of the flow is from Atika Kadisha, the inwardness and essentiality of Ein Sof' (Umaayan 24:1:2). So a vessel of natural effort is needed. It is not the cause (F05; G14). (*stated*)
3. Hence the person's side: 'one only has to make a vessel for his livelihood', kept pure of fraud by Torah law (Hayom Yom 27 Sivan). The Alter Rebbe to a ruined Chassid: 'you must light up your surroundings with Torah and prayer; livelihood is what God must give you' (Hayom Yom 5 Tammuz). (*stated*)
4. Money holds a person's life. Earned by toil, it contains his whole vital soul, so charity raises the whole vital soul to God (Tanya 37:19-20; IH 4:11 'his money, which is his life'). (*stated*)
5. Lust for money is idolatry. R. Nachman: those who fall into it 'do not believe the Holy One can sustain a person by a slight cause', and 'all idolatries are lodged in money' (Likutei Moharan 23:1). The root of idolatry, Tanya 22 says, is to regard something as separate from Him (F08). R. Nachman does not cite Tanya 22; joining the two is our reading. (**reading**)
6. Sfas Emes: set apart a share for God from everything; 'the whole of prayer is to testify that everything is from God's vitality' (Terumah 5633). (*stated*)
7. That the charity given from earnings makes the business day His dwelling is stated in Tanya 34:6 (S190). The Rashab states the purpose of physical livelihood itself: the kindness is clothed down to the stars, as a father plays with his child in nutshells, so that souls may refine the spark of good in the physical thing, 'the purpose of all the worlds' (Umaayan 24:1:8-9). F07. (*stated*)

- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Grep only: 'worry over livelihood' appears in Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai; not read.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Hayom Yom 28 Sivan (read, not cited): helping a Jew with livelihood, even to earn seventy kopecks on a calf, opens all the gates of the upper palaces.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.

## S190 · Work and commerce · משא ומתן / עסק

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F07 The home below · F12 Knowing the unity · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** משא ומתן · יגיע כפיך · עסקים · מלאכה

> Work and commerce are the unity of God as testimony: whoever separates his earthly business from the One bears false witness to the Shema, while business done to give life to others makes the form like its Maker and turns the working day into His dwelling.

## Derivation

1. Morning and evening a Jew testifies 'Havayah is one' in the four directions, in heaven above and on earth below. To run one's business 'for themselves', like the nations, is to separate earthly affairs 'from the true One' and so to 'testify falsely' in the Shema (IH 9:1). F02 and F12 stated about commerce. (*stated*)
2. The remedy is to do business in order to give life to souls that are parts of God. In this 'we make the form like its Maker, Havayah is one', who does kindness to give life to the world every moment (IH 9:2). F01 as the pattern for trade. (*stated*)
3. The business day becomes 'a place for His dwelling' through the charity given from its earnings; even a fifth raises the other four-fifths (Tanya 34:6-7). F07. (*stated*)
4. The Alter Rebbe exposes the counterfeit. A love of God in prayer followed by a day 'absorbed in business, each for his gain' is joined only as a dream joins opposites. Most people make the body's needs the main thing, the reverse of prayer (TO Vayeshev 3:3-5). He adds at once that one should not despair and call the prayer vain imagination: this dream-level 'has its root above', where all that is divided below is joined (TO Vayeshev 3:6-9). So the divided day is exposed, but the arousal in prayer is not dismissed. (*stated*)
5. The Besht: 'even when he goes to his business he keeps God before him always', clinging at least by a small thought, like a spark from which a fire can be rekindled (Keter Shem Tov 1:185:2). Polonoye: the higher level is to live not only by the 'bread' of Torah but in commerce too, understanding that 'whatever he meets is God's mouth' (Tzofnat Paneach, Vaera 1:182). G12. (*stated*)
6. Why effort at all: blessing reaches this world only through the vessels and garments of Asiyah, hence 'in all that you do' (Rashab, Umaayan 23:2). Karlin: the thirty-nine labours come from the Mishkan, and the six days of work are for making oneself a sanctuary where God dwells (R. Asher of Stolin). (*stated*)
7. R. Nachman: those whose trade is 'in faith' cleave to the light of the holy face (Likutei Moharan 23:1). That honest trade is itself a form of the Shema's testimony, as IH 9 says, is our reading of the Breslov text. (**reading**)

## S191 · The body · גוף

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below · F09 The unity of opposites · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** הגוף · גוף ונפש · חומר הגוף · גוף הגשמי

> The body is the unity of God as choice: the Essence freely chose this physical body, which on its own is no vessel for Godliness, and only 'the One who does wonders', binding spiritual to physical, makes it a chariot for His will.

## Derivation

1. Body and divine soul are utterly apart: the body and vital soul are from kelipat nogah, 'the serpent's skin', and the divine soul from the heights of holiness. Only 'the power of the One who does wonders, who binds spiritual to physical' joins them (Rashab, Ayin Beis 5676). The Chernobyler: form and matter in every thing are 'two opposites in one subject', and the Holy One binds them (Me'or Einayim, Additions 29). F09. (*stated*)
2. So a person's powers are not his own: 'all his powers and vitality are the Creator... when you move a limb, who moves it but the Creator?' To forget this is already 'other gods', the Besht's reading of 'other powers' (Me'or Einayim, Vayigash 1). F01 and F08 stated for the body. (*stated*)
3. The body was chosen. 'You chose us from every people' refers to 'the physical body, which resembles in its physicality the bodies of the nations' (Tanya 49:11). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe makes the choice the Essence's own: God's bond with Israel through the body is not from any virtue of the body but 'because the Holy One chose the body of Israel with His free choice, and this choice is from His very Essence'. Body and animal soul are not vessels for Godliness by their own definition; they become vessels 'by the revelation of the power of the Essence' (SMM, Shemini 5728). F05. (*stated*)
5. The body's work: in a mitzvah the limbs 'become a literal chariot for the Supreme Will' (Tanya 23:6). The Besht: refine the body, do not break it with mortifications (Hayom Yom 28 Shevat). R. Nachman: have mercy on the body and refine it, so the soul can show it what it sees; 'from my flesh I see God' (Likutei Moharan 22:5). In the same teaching he asks to break the body's brazenness with holy boldness, 'for a sigh breaks a person's body' (22:5:6). Within Chabad, Hayom Yom 27 Shevat (the Rashab) reports that the earlier Chassidim refused any permitted thing they craved, and so broke desire. (*stated*)
6. The purpose is the dwelling below (F07): the soul descends not for its own sake but 'to refine and purify the body, the animal soul and its share in the world', and through this specifically the intent of a dwelling below is revealed (SMM, 9 Kislev 5738; so Tanya 37:17, 'to bind and unite them with Or Ein Sof'). In the Shemini 5728 maamar the Rebbe adds that through the soul's work in the body, the Essence's choice, hidden in the soul, is revealed (SMM, Shemini 5728:16); that maamar does not name the dwelling. (*stated*)
7. That R. Nachman's 'from my flesh I see God' is the same move as the Rebbe's use of the verse for Chassidus (G06) is our reading. The two use the verse differently: the Rebbe uses it for knowing God by analogy from the soul, R. Nachman for the refined body receiving the soul's vision. (**reading**)

- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 5676 (cited); also lines 1553 and 2996-2997 (grep only): only by 'the One who does wonders' can the divine soul grasp Godliness in natural things, and the soul's clothing in the body is 'not gradual'. Hayom Yom 27 Shevat (cited): the earlier Chassidim's pact to refuse permitted things they craved, to break desire.
- **Rayatz.** Through Hayom Yom 28 Shevat.

## S192 · Health and healing · רפואה

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F01 The world said into being now · F07 The home below · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** רפואה · חולי · בריאות · רופא

> Health and healing are the unity of God as nature serving its Maker: the doctor is the messenger of 'the Healer of all flesh who does wonders', nature is His garment and is repaired through Torah, health serves the soul's mission below, and the life of the sick is the Shechinah's.

## Derivation

1. Health belongs to nature. Elokim equals 'nature'; the elements are nature, 'all bodily health depends on the elements being in balance', and 'all medicines only strengthen nature'. Nature was created by wisdom, and wisdom is Torah, so 'the repair and strengthening of nature is also through the Torah', which contains matters of healing, and tzaddikim who cleave to Torah can change natures, physical and spiritual (Degel, Bereshit 2). (*stated*)
2. So one acts through nature, yet knows the Healer. The Rebbe: see a specialist, 'that is the natural way'; inwardly, 'every moment He creates the world and guides it'; and a Chassid must be healthy in body too, so that the mission for which his soul came down 'to this lowest, physical world' is fulfilled (letter, vol. 4). The doctor who knows he is 'the messenger of the Healer of all flesh who does wonders, who is Havayah our God' removes the fear that people will forget their Maker (letter, vol. 7). In every matter there must be 'a hold in the ways of nature', as 'in all that you do' (citing Kuntres Umaayan), so follow the doctors, but without a divided heart; faith in providence brings joy, and joy health (letter, vol. 9). F01, F07 and G14. (*stated*)
3. The Rema's 'who does wonders' reads the blessing as God binding spiritual to physical (cited in Tzofnat Paneach, Shemot 2:5, and Me'or Einayim, Additions 29; see S191). That health is this bond kept whole is our reading. No text in this pass says it about illness. (**reading**)
4. The Alter Rebbe warns against the reverse. His letter protests the custom of asking sages for counsel in material matters: those who love bodily life 'for Heaven' so much that they cannot bear its suffering 'run from town to town seeking counsel from afar, and did not turn to God'. Suffering is the blow of a loving father, to be borne 'face to face'. Life of soul and body flows from His 'face', His will (IH 22). The concealment is love (F03). (*stated*)
5. The Besht (from the Degel, in his grandfather's name): the 'donkey lying under its load' is the body in illness or loss of livelihood; help it with prayer, and it must pray and return too (Sefer Baal Shem Tov, Mishpatim 8). (*stated*)
6. R. Nachman: the sick person 'has no life, only the Shechinah gives him life'; sadness breeds illness and 'joy is a great healing' (Likutei Moharan II 24). (*stated*)
7. That the Degel's 'nature' is the same Elokim that SH 6 calls the shield of Havayah, so that healing through nature is the concealment that serves revelation (F03), is our joining of the two texts. The Degel's own tie runs through wisdom and Torah. (**reading**)

## S193 · Nature and miracle · טבע ונס

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** הטבע · אלקים בגימטריא הטבע · למעלה מן הטבע · נסים

> Nature and miracle are the unity of God as the two Names joined: nature is Elokim, the filling light hidden so the world seems to run by itself, miracle is Havayah, the surrounding light, and the Essence above both unites them so that nature too is a wonder.

## Derivation

1. Elokim, the name of contraction, equals 'hateva', nature, 'because it hides the light that brings the world into being and gives it life, and it seems as if the world stands and runs by nature'. It is a shield for Havayah, so creatures are not nullified, and it is gevurah included in chesed (SH 6:1-2). F03. (*stated*)
2. Sfas Emes: 'olam' is from 'he'elem': nature covers the inner divine vitality, yet God can be recognized only through the contraction of nature, 'and through the hiding itself the recognition of His Godliness is found' (Bereshit 5662). 'All creation is a wonder'; the 'great wonders' above nature are revealed on Shabbat (ibid. 27:4). (*stated*)
3. The Rebbe maps it onto F04: 'the root of nature is the Name Elokim, memale, and the root of miracles is the Name Havayah, sovev'. In the redemption of 12 Tammuz, the Essence above both 'joins them', so miracle and nature were joined, 'the union of Havayah and Elokim' (SMM, 12 Tammuz 5729). G14 stated. (*stated*)
4. He gives three grades: a miracle wholly above nature, a miracle clothed in nature where the miracle shows, and one clothed so that nature hides it (SMM, 12 Tammuz 5738). 'Nes' as 'raising' belongs chiefly to the miracles clothed in nature, where the light of Havayah lifts nature from within; even a miracle above nature changes only one detail and so lifts the rest (ibid.). (*stated*)
5. The Degel: 'In the beginning Elokim created': God created nature by wisdom. Man, by wisdom, can create for himself 'a new nature' and change his temperament, 'for the form should resemble its Maker' (Bereshit 2). (*stated*)
6. The schools differ on nature. Amdur speaks of a person's own nature and habit: it is bitter to break at first, and he reads 'Bereshit bara Elokim' (Elokim = hateva) as God's first intent that it be broken (Chayyim VaChesed 155). Radzin: all that seems to be 'the habit of nature' is so only because creation has forgotten its beginning (Sod Yesharim, Rosh Hashanah 58), and to a refined grasp 'there is no nature at all', since God renews the work of creation every day (Tiferet Yosef, citing his grandfather). R. Nachman: prayer is providence above nature and changes it, and at the end 'nature will be nullified entirely, and there will be only providence' (Likutei Moharan 250). The Rebbe: miracle clothed in nature, with nature itself no longer hiding it, from the Essence that joins both (said of the 12 Tammuz redemption). The texts do not answer each other. (*stated*)
7. That the Rebbe's 'joining' is the higher and lower unity (F02) applied to history is our reading. He names Havayah and Elokim, not the two unities. (**reading**)

## S194 · Vitality in all things · חיות

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F04 Filling and surrounding · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** חיות · חיות אלקי · החיות שבו · מחיה את כולם

> Vitality is the unity of God as continuous creation: the life in every thing, stones included, is the divine word bringing it from nothing now, so that 'You give life to all' means 'You bring all into being'.

## Derivation

1. 'You give life to all': read not 'give life' but 'bring into being', from nothing (SH 2:5). Vitality in the Chabad sense is not biological life but existence given every moment. F01. (*stated*)
2. Therefore even stone, dust and water have 'a soul and spiritual vitality', the letters of the Ten Utterances clothed in them (SH 1:5, from the Ari). Light and vitality in stones keep them from 'returning to nothing' (Tanya 38:3). (*stated*)
3. Strashelye, the Alter Rebbe's disciple expounding his Shaar HaYichud, states it in his own terms: every kingdom of creation has body and soul, 'even the inanimate', whose soul is 'its being and existence' (Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah 1:4). There the point is composition; the tie to the Creator comes later in the gate: all creatures exist from the truth of His existence, and His drawing-forth is the same in all, differing only in how it is revealed (2:1, 2:7). (*stated*)
4. The same light is hidden equally in body and soul, since the whole of this world is under the hidden face (Tanya 38:4). F03. The illumination that shines through the hiding is not equal: between body and soul there is 'no comparison' (Tanya 38:3, 38:5). (*stated*)
5. Where the vitality comes from: the Tzemach Tzedek says that the filling light alone cannot make matter exist, and 'the main vitality is from the surrounding light' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem 2). F04. (*stated*)
6. Tzava'at HaRivash: one must believe that 'the whole earth is full of His glory, and in everything is His vitality'; all love and fear, even those found in bad things, are from Him, and so one should fear and love nothing but Him (TzH 127:1). (*stated*)
7. Chassidus itself is named from this: 'Chassidus is vitality, bringing life and light into everything, even the not-good' (the Tzemach Tzedek, Hayom Yom 24 Teves). That this definition and SH 2's 'vitality = creation' name the same thing is our reading. Hayom Yom speaks of the vitality a person brings, SH of the vitality God gives. (**reading**)

## S195 · Mineral, plant, animal, speaker · דומם צומח חי מדבר

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F11 Darkness turned to light · F07 The home below **Search terms:** דומם צומח חי מדבר · דצח"מ · דומם · צומח

> Mineral, plant, animal and speaker are the unity of God as a ladder with its top at the bottom: each kingdom lives by the word within it, the sparks fell into all four for man to raise, and the lowest kingdom is rooted highest.

## Derivation

1. Every kingdom, even 'the physical earth and the level of the inanimate itself', would return to nothing if the letters of the Ten Utterances left it for an instant (SH 1:4). The mineral has 'a soul and spiritual vitality' (SH 1:5). F01. (*stated*)
2. Strashelye: all four kingdoms are compounds of the four elements, and each has body and soul (Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah 1:3-4). The tie to the One comes later in the gate (2:1, 2:7; see S194). (*stated*)
3. The Besht's circle (from the Ari): in the shattering 'holy sparks fell into four parts, mineral, plant, animal, speaker, for man to raise', and the purpose of creating the worlds was to refine them through Israel (Keter Shem Tov 1:159-160). F11 and, by our reading, F07. (*stated*)
4. The lower kingdoms are rooted higher. Mineral, plant and animal are from Tohu, which preceded Tikkun, where man is rooted; therefore bread (plant) can feed the speaker (LT Tzav 7:9-10). The Rashab, citing Torah Ohr: animal bodies are 'plant', the human body is 'mineral', and its soul is 'speaker' (Ayin Beis 5676). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe: letters are 'the mineral in man's powers', and, by 'the higher, the lower', their root is higher even than the plant. So the service of letters reaches higher than the service of the mind ('he took of the stones of the place') (SMM, 9 Kislev 5738). G05 stated. (*stated*)
6. In the Temple each kingdom rose through one representative: all animal life through one beast, all plant life through a tenth of flour (Tanya 34:7). Tanya gives it to show that a part raises the whole, as a fifth given to charity raises the other four-fifths. (*stated*)

## S196 · Procreation · פריה ורביה

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding · F07 The home below · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** פריה ורביה · פרו ורבו · לשבת יצרה · הולדה

> Procreation is the unity of God as a likeness of creation from nothing, done by man: begetting reveals the infinite power, like creating yesh from ayin, which belongs to the Essence alone, and it brings a soul down to draw the surrounding light into the world.

## Derivation

1. Derekh Mitzvotekha opens its whole account of the mitzvot with procreation: three partners in a person, father, mother and the Holy One who gives the soul, which is 'a part of Havayah' (Procreation 1). F06. (*stated*)
2. The purpose of man's descent is 'to draw the revelation of Or Ein Sof, the surrounding light, from concealment into inward revelation' (Procreation 2). Begetting brings a soul into this world for that purpose, moving it 'from limited grasp to the revelation of Or Ein Sof' (Procreation 3). F04 and F07. (*stated*)
3. The Rebbe: 'in begetting, generation after generation, the infinite power is revealed, and especially since begetting resembles creation from nothing, which is by the power of the Essence' (SMM, Ki Teitzei 5741). Above, only the union of Zeir Anpin and Malchus (ZuN) 'draws the essence', and so only it makes 'a new existence', the birth of souls. F05, G05. (*stated*)
4. The dwelling below begins 'from the first mitzvah, be fruitful and multiply, fill the earth and subdue it' (SMM, Simchat Torah 5742). F07. (*stated*)
5. The physical is not incidental. Krakow: it is impossible to draw a pure soul into this low world 'except that a little physicality be entangled in the union', since the body is made by it (Maor VaShemesh, Vayera 6). Tzava'at HaRivash: rising from sleep, one is renewed and 'fit to beget, like the Holy One who begot worlds' (TzH 20). (*stated*)
6. The limit: the vitality in seed emitted in animal lust, without self-sanctification, goes down for the time being into the three impure kelipot, as with gluttony, though being permitted it can rise again when he returns (Tanya 7:6-8). The act unites only when sanctified. (*stated*)
7. That the Rebbe's 'creation from nothing' in begetting (F05) and Krakow's 'a little physicality' (F07, the lowest as the vessel) are two sides of one claim is our reading. Krakow does not name the unity. (**reading**)

## S197 · Place and space · מקום

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** הוא מקומו של עולם · בלי מקום · מקום ארון · אינו מן המדה

> Place is the unity of God as containment reversed: He is the place of the world and the world is not His place; space is brought into being by Malchus and is nothing before Him, yet He is found within it, and where He is revealed the place itself is no measure.

## Derivation

1. Space and time come into being from His Malchus, and because Malchus is one with His Essence, 'space and time are nullified in existence before His Essence', like sunlight in the sun (SH 7:6). F02 (higher unity). (*stated*)
2. 'Though He is above space and time, He is found below within space and time', filling the earth 'in time and space' equally in heaven and on earth. This is the lower unity. Malchus hides the light only so that space and time do not cease to exist (SH 7:8-9). (*stated*)
3. 'He is the place of the world and the world is not His place' (the Sages). The Mitteler Rebbe reads it as 'the essential Place' (Shaar HaYichud 8:62). The Rebbe: 'place of the world' is the filling light that gives it life; 'the world is not His place' means He is not caught by it (SMM, Devarim 5736). F04. (*stated*)
4. The surrounding light is not 'above' in place (Tanya 48; spine F04). The Tzemach Tzedek draws a consequence: the doubts of the later kabbalists about the tzimtzum fall away, and 'there is no difference whether the place is clean or soiled' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Procreation 2). Space counts as nothing, 'to the point that He is the place of the world' (DM, Emunah 12:2). (*stated*)
5. Where the Essence shines in space, space stops measuring: 'the place of the ark was not of the measure' because Ein Sof, above space and time, shone within space and time (Toras Shmuel 5639, the Rashab's notes). The Rebbe: the Temple site is 'no ordinary place' because 'the revelation of Havayah is in the place itself' (SMM, Matos-Masei 5722). F05, G05. (*stated*)
6. Keter Shem Tov ties place to the unity outright: He is 'the place of the world, being infinite; if so, there must be none else' ('ein od'), since a second would need a difference and whatever is set apart is limited (Keter Shem Tov 1:55:5). It then turns it inward with the Rema's analogy (Shulchan Aruch OC 1): as God is the place of the world, so a person is his own 'place', standing over his deeds and seeing them (1:55:8). That this inward turn is G06 is our label; here the analogy runs from God to man. (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe states that since the tzimtzum is not literal, it does not conceal relative to Or Ein Sof, and so the revelation of Havayah shines 'in the existence itself' made by Elokim's concealment; in the Temple this was revealed 'from the side of the place itself' (SMM, Matos-Masei 5722). That the Temple's 'no measure' can come to show in other places, through avodah, is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 7:6-9 (cited); Tanya 36:3 (spine): before creation He alone filled 'all this place where He created the world, and so it is now before Him'. Torah Ohr, Bereshit 1:1 (read): He 'is not within the category of place'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Shaar HaYichud 8:60-63 (cited): 'see a place with Me' is the nullification of the six directions; 'He grasps all worlds and none grasps Him'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Emunah 12:2 and Procreation 2 (cited and read).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel (cited, from the Rashab's notes); also lines 464, 1053 and 1988 (grep only): place is nullified to Godliness; 'near' and 'far' above mean rank, not space.
- **Rashab.** Notes in Toras Shmuel (cited). Hemshech Ayin Beis (line 3627, read in part): since He is the place of the world, the world is not within space and time at all.
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** SMM Devarim 5736 and Matos-Masei 5722 (cited). SMM Vayetze 5738 (lines 720 and 723; 723 read): 'place' is an existence in which the divine light is hidden, and Havayah squared equals 'makom' (186), Havayah present in it only 'by way of gematria'.
- **Baal Shem Tov.** Keter Shem Tov 1:55:5 and 1:55:8 (cited): He is the place of the world, being infinite, so there is none else; and, with the Rema, as the Holy One is the place of the world, so man.
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes, Terumah 5632 (cited), in the name of his grandfather the Chiddushei HaRim: in everything there is a point of vitality from Him; He is called the place of the world, and as far as one knows that nothing has a place except from Him, the inward is revealed.
