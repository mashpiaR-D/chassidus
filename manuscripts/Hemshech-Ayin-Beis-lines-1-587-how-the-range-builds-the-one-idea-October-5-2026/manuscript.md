## The shape of the range

Samach Vav asks why there are worlds at all. Ayin Beis asks something narrower: how, exactly, does light come from the Essence? The Rashab takes nothing about "light" for granted. The first 78 chapters are one long analysis of inner light (or pnimi) and surrounding light (or makif). He looks at their roots before the tzimtzum, at the lights and the vessels of Atzilut, at the ten sefirot hidden in Ein Sof, at the will, and at the Essence above even the will. That is why so much of the range is *structural* (366 lines). Most lines build definitions and parables. The soul's powers in its limbs, Hillel and Shammai, the letter bet cut out of "baruch", the thrown stone, sunlight and the soul's life: each is set up at length. The unity is stated at the joints of the argument. It comes when the Rashab says what the analysis means: what "one" means, what "nothing else" means, and where the Essence itself is reached.

F05 (the Essence beyond both) leads by far (117). F04 (filling and surrounding) is second (70), because the whole series is about those two lights. F03 (the tzimtzum) and F08 (bitul) tie for third. Bitul here is not mainly the soul before its source. It is the bitul of vessels, of the body, of the one who "sets himself aside", and above all the bitul above reason. Each maamar ends in practice, in prayer, teshuvah, the Elul-Rosh Hashanah cycle and the mitzvot, so F10, F11 and F06 stay steady throughout. F12 (knowing) is last (27). That is not because knowing is unimportant. The Rashab keeps saying the Essence is reached above knowing, by re'uta d'liba and by the acceptance of the yoke, even while he insists that contemplation must come first.

## Bsha'ah shehikdimu, Oteh or, Vayichan (1-73): will, lights and vessels, and the one who can create

The series opens with Keter as will. Before the will arises, Or Ein Sof is utterly separate from the worlds, not even revealed to itself (line 7, F05): > הרי האוא"ס בבחי' הבדלה לגמרי מן העולמות (line 7)

Mitzvot are equal in the will, though not in their reasons, because "I am Havayah" is the Essence joining itself to the name (line 29, F05, F10). Then comes the first great statement. The power to create is the Essence's alone. "Havayah is one" means the worlds, even after they came to be, remain wholly nullified. A "something" exists only through the hiding name Elokim, the vessels (line 39, F05, F01, F02, F03): > דכח ההתהוות הוא בעצמות דוקא ובא ע"י האור (line 39.1) > וז"ע הוי' אחד דמצד שם הוי' הרי העולמות גם לאחר שנתהוו הם בבחי' ביטול בתכלית (line 39.2)

Chapter 7 takes the Torah Or distinction and makes it exact. A lower world is nullified before a higher one only by rank, and cut off it would be absorbed into the higher world. Before Ein Sof nothing is "included" at all. He is outside every category of place, and without His flow a world would be as if it never were (lines 44-46, F01, F05): > ואין ערוך הוא שאינו נמצא כלול במקורו (line 46)

And it is precisely this physical world that shows it. A physical thing cannot come from anything by degrees (line 47, F01, F12): > רואין דבר גשמי בהכרח לומר שהתהוותו הוא מאין בבחי' אין ערוך (line 47)

Torah is an intermediary that "does not interrupt": the Essence itself is drawn through it, even in its garment (lines 54-55, F10, F05). The vessels are "Godliness itself" emanated from the power of limit in the Essence (line 65, F09). They have an advantage over the lights, because their root, the reshimu, was never touched by the tzimtzum. So the bitul of the vessels reaches higher than the bitul of the lights: "we will do" before "we will hear" (line 71, F08, F11). Israel at Sinai were "one man" because division belongs to the vessels, while the souls are one in essence. That bitul of the vessels was what the drawing of the Essence at Sinai needed (line 72): > דהמשכה זו הוא ע"י ביטול הכלים דוקא (line 72)

## Naso, Shelach, Korach, Chukat (74-188): the ten hidden in the One

The middle maamarim study the ten hidden sefirot in Ein Sof before the tzimtzum. The Rashab asks how ten can be in the One without making it many. They add no multiplicity, as the soul holds all its powers and is one (line 118, F02, F09): > ואינם מוסיפים שום ריבוי ח"ו באוא"ס ב"ה (line 118)

Opposites like fire and water cannot mix, yet here they are one. That is because opposition belongs only to a thing spread out into a limited form (line 119, F09): > דענין ההפכיות הוא רק בהתפשטות הדבר שבא בבחי' ציור מוגבל (line 119)

Their cleaving to the source demands that they be simple like it (line 136). Their inclusion demands that they have no existence of their own (line 137). With the infinite light shining, they were nullified "like the sun's radiance in the sun" (lines 114, 175, F02). Before the tzimtzum the unlimited light held the letters of the reshimu openly. That was because the power of limit in Ein Sof is Ein Sof itself (line 79, F09): > והכח הגבול הוא א"ס ממש כמו כח הבל"ג (line 79)

Line 80 states the omnipresence and its hiding together: > הגם דאוא"ס נמצא בכל מקום ממש בכל החלל עד למטה מטה דלית אתר פנוי מיני' (line 80)

Two names of Havayah are distinguished. The first is the will itself, the root of sovev. The second is the rising of the will, the root of memale. "In truth all is one" (line 102). The Idra's lamp is the key image for how spreading remains one (line 107, F02): > וממילא כל ההתפשטות הוא בבחי' חד (line 107)

At Sinai they "saw the Godly nothing that brings into being, the truth of the thing". The physical was only "heard", because it is not a true existence (line 124, F02, F01, F12). Shelach adds a practical point that runs through the whole series. Knowledge and delight are only revelations. The Essence is drawn by the physical act done only because He wills it (line 128, F10, F05, F07): > אבל המשכת העצמות הוא ע"י עשי' בפו"מ דוקא (line 128)

The red heifer (lines 186-187, F11) turns the evil inclination to ash. What remains is the bare force of desire, "which can be turned to holiness as powerful will for Godliness". Through it the Essence above the hidden sefirot is drawn.

## Balak, Pinchas, Matot (189-283): echad and yachid; the true Being

Chapter 29 contains the range's most exact statement of what "one" means. It is the SPINE's own precision, in the Rashab's words (lines 192-194, F02, F05): > ההפרש בין אחד ליחיד דאחד הוא התאחדות הפרטים (line 193.1) > אבל יחיד הוא שמיוחד בעצם שאין כאן פרט כלל (line 193.2) > וזהו אנת הוא חד בבחי' יחיד בעצם (line 194)

The Rashab gives "yachid" to the hidden sefirot, "You are One and not in number". Echad is the uniting of particulars that were once separate. He cites Shaar HaYichud 11 (yachid, echad, kadmon) for the same ladder. After the tzimtzum, the lights that are clothed in vessels become real Chesed and Gevurah. They are united only by a light from above both: "You are the one who binds them and unites them" (lines 199-200, F02, F08). Teshuvah draws from the "One and not in number" level itself (line 213). The will grasps the Essence, while love grasps only revelation (line 224, F05, F08): > אבל הרצון דבחי' רעו"ד תופס בעצמות אוא"ס ב"ה (line 224)

Chapter 38 limits all revelation. Even the light that reveals the Essence shows only that He exists, not what He is (line 263, F05): > והיינו שהאור מגלה רק מציאות המאור לא עצם מהותו (line 263)

Chapter 40 gives Shabbat as the mochin of Abba, the "essence" of Godliness, whose service is ithapcha: "everything is Godliness, even the physical" (line 279, F11, F07, F02).

## Devarim to Shoftim (284-414): Chochmah as the true Being, and the will that is "nothing"

Chapter 42 says something striking about Chochmah. Or Ein Sof shines in it as "the true Being", and everything that comes down into grasp is "nothing" (line 294, F02, F05). The Rashab notes openly that this differs from his own explanation elsewhere: > שבחכ' מאיר האוא"ס יש האמיתי דהיינו בחי' העצמות שזהו בחי' יש האמיתי (line 294)

Every grasp of ours is pictured in terms of place, though we know Godliness is not in place. In Atzilut "everything is Godliness itself" (lines 317-318, F12, F02). Surrounding light is a revelation of the Essence and is not meant to light anything. It is like the colour of a thing, and it is not necessary but willed (lines 340-341, F05). Chapter 49 then brings the range's deepest statement of "ein od". Creation comes from the will. The will is "nothing" and leaves nothing behind when it withdraws. So even while a thing exists, its existence is truly no existence (lines 355-356, F02, F01, F05): > הנה אם הי' הרצון מסתלק ח"ו הי' אין ואפס לגמרי (line 355) > ובזה יובן איך שענין אין עוד הוא במציאות היש דוקא (line 356)

Sovev is not "above" the worlds. It is within every creature, their main life (line 352, F04): > איהו תפיס בכולהו עלמין שנמצא בתוכם ופנימיותם והוא עיקר החיות שלהם (line 352)

"Or Ein Sof" means "infinite light". The Essence itself should be called "without beginning", not "Ein Sof" (line 383, F05). The Essence is drawn only through "the absence of a vessel", the bitul above reason of a small child before a great man. That is the acceptance of the yoke on Rosh Hashanah (lines 409-411, F08, F05): > דהעדר הכלי זהו כלי לעצמות אוא"ס (line 409) > שהו"ע המשכת עצמות אוא"ס, שזהו דוקא ע"י ביטול הנ"ל (line 411)

It is in every Jew because they are rooted in the Essence (line 412, F06).

## Ki Tetzei, Ki Tavo, Nitzavim (415-509): sovev as the source of the "something"

Chapter 58 reverses the usual picture. Surrounding light is not absent from the worlds. It is their whole source (line 426). The "something" comes only from the surrounding light, which gives the vessels "power to create something from nothing", as Iggeret HaKodesh 20 says (lines 427-429, F04, F01): > ואדרבה כל עיקר מקור ההתהוות דכל העולמות הוא מבחי' אור המקיף (line 426) > ובכדי שיתהווה מציאות יש זהו מהאור מקיף דוקא (line 428)

"No place is empty of Him" (line 432). The two "nothings" are distinguished. One is the nothing of the true Being, which is truly nothing. The other is the nothing of the created something, called nothing only by borrowing (line 421, F02, F04). In the form of Adam Kadmon every creature is truly nullified to Or Ein Sof, yet this is hidden. Knowledge does not force choice, and a creature can choose to be wholly separate (lines 449-453, F02, F08, F09, F11): > הרי בהביטול דציור קומה דא"ק כל הנבראים מתבטלים באמת לגבי אוא"ס ב"ה (line 449) > וכנודע דהכלי לקבלת החיות מאלקות הוא הביטול דוקא (line 453)

The vessels' power to be nullified comes from the great circle before the line. Giving up the body reaches higher than giving up the soul, and in the future "the physical body will be Godliness" (lines 466-468, F07, F08): > דהנה ידוע דמסירת הגוף מגיע למעלה יותר ממסירת הנפש (line 466) > וע"כ יהי' היתרון דלעתיד מה שהגוף יהי' אלקות (line 468)

In Israel, "the Holy One desires them" for their own sake, rooted in His Essence (line 483). The essence of the soul wants the Essence with no request at all, "like a flame rising of itself" (line 485, F06, F05). The simple will is "the main thing of Israel" (line 500).

## Rosh Hashanah and Shabbat Shuvah 5673 (510-587): the purpose, and "Creator" as His only name

The will to create is "that there be something separate from His Essence". Its inner intention is the joy of refinement below, "a dwelling below" (lines 519-521, F07): > שעי"ז נשלמה הכוונה דנתאווה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 521)

The purpose is carried out by the soul, "the lamp of Havayah", which turns the world's darkness to light through contemplation that reaches the essential point of the soul (lines 534-537, F07, F11, F12): > דתכלית הכוונה בבריאת והתהוות העולמות הוא להמשיך גילוי אור אין א"ס למטה (line 537)

Chapter 72 ends the range's metaphysics. The Essence cannot be named, not even "Luminary". The only name that fits is "Creator". Creation shows that His being is from Himself, that He "exists as unexisting existence". The something He makes is also truly no existence, made "in the likeness of the Essence" (lines 542-544, F05, F01, F02): > ומה שאנו יכולים לכנות בעצמות הוא רק בשם בורא (line 543) > וזה מפלאי תמים דעים כל יכול ית' להוות דבר בדוגמת העצמות (line 544)

"Their beginning is wedged in their end". Malchus alone holds the power of the Essence to create, and on Rosh Hashanah that will to reign is drawn from the Essence by Israel giving themselves over (lines 556-557, F05, F07, F09): > וכוונת נעיצת התחלה הוא בסוף דוקא דבבחי' מל' דוקא יש כח העצמות (line 556)

The last maamar returns to teshuvah. The bitter cry of the embodied soul turns the animal soul's stone heart, and it reaches higher than the will of the soul's own root (lines 578-580, F11). The range ends inside ch. 78. Only love born of contemplation engraves an inner "form of a man" in the animal soul, because there even the natural mind understands the Godly matter (line 587, F11, F12).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **Echad and yachid defined** (line 193). The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud does not draw this line. The SPINE's opening precision is here, in the Rashab's own words.
- **"Ein od" inside the existence of the something** (line 356), and **creation as a likeness of the Essence's "unexisting existence"** (lines 543-544). In the Tanya the world is nothing before its source, like sunlight in the sun. The Rashab adds that the very way the world exists, a being that is not a being, is what shows the Essence. He also says the sun-and-ray parable does not fit Or Ein Sof (line 542).
- **Sovev as the source of every "something"** (lines 426-429). The Tanya (ch. 48; Iggeret HaKodesh 20) gives the materials. Here the thesis is made central: inner light alone would produce only spiritual worlds.
- **Bitul of vessels and body above bitul of lights and soul** (lines 71, 466-468). The Tanya's bitul is the soul's. Here the vessel, the body and the physical act reach highest, and the future body "will be Godliness".
- **The absence of a vessel as the vessel for the Essence** (line 409), and re'uta d'liba as easier to reach than contemplative love (lines 377, 412, 501).
- **The Essence cannot be called "Ein Sof" or "Luminary"** (lines 383, 542). Only "Creator" fits it.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 20 lines are maamar headings, and line 2 is the opening question. These 21 are *none*. - The long parables (soul's powers in limbs, Hillel and Shammai, teacher and student, the letter bet, the thrown stone, will and movement) are *structural*. A face is given only where it pictures the unity directly. Soul-parables are not tagged F06 unless the text speaks of the soul's root or spark (for example 238, 266, 377, 412, 483-485, 500). - The Kabbalistic mapping (Adam Kadmon, Akudim and Nekudim, the partzufim, the five surroundings of the Yotzer blessing) is *structural* unless the text itself draws the unity from it. - Only one line is *reading*: line 299, the oil that "permeates everything and does not mix". The Rashab uses it for Chochmah and "none is holy like Havayah". Hearing in it the Rebbe's later image for the Essence present in all is our tie, not his. - F09 is given where the text holds limit and unlimited, or opposites, as one (the vessels as "Godliness becoming a something", the power of limit as Ein Sof itself). Some of these could be read as plain structure. - The chapter labels were read automatically from the line-initial markers. The ch. 66 marker (line 493) is in the text, but my note at that line did not name the chapter.

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **Itkafia and ithapcha.** Line 71 says the advantage of itkafia over ithapcha, rooted in the vessels, reaches higher. The Tanya sets the tzaddik, who has turned evil to good (ch. 10), above the beinoni, whose service is itkafia (ch. 12), though ch. 27 gives itkafia a delight of its own. The Rashab's own lines 443 and 579 treat ithapcha as the higher gift, given from above and beyond one's own power. We keep both sides and do not reconcile them.
- **Who holds the "true Being"?** In Shaar HaYichud (and in the Rashab's own "elsewhere", which he cites at line 294), higher knowledge (da'at elyon) means that from above, Ein Sof is the true Being and the worlds are nothing. Here Chochmah itself is where Ein Sof shines as the true Being. The Rashab flags the difference himself.
- **Names of the Essence.** The Tanya freely calls the Essence "Ein Sof, blessed be He" and uses the sunlight parable for creation's nullity. The Rashab says both are strictly inexact (lines 383, 542). Only "Creator" names the Essence. The substance agrees, but the language pulls apart.
- **The route to the Essence.** The Tanya (chs. 41-44) builds love and awe through contemplation. The Rashab agrees that contemplation must come first and alone engraves the animal soul (lines 228, 587). Yet he places the reach of the Essence in bitul and will above reason, which every Jew has and which is easier to reach (lines 377, 409-412, 501). This is the same tension as the SPINE's F12 note. The Rashab holds both sides, and so do we.
