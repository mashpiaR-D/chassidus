## The short answer

Each book says the one idea: there is nothing but God. Each also sees something the others do not.

- **The Tanya** says it from the person's side. The soul is a part of Him, the struggle is where He is hidden, and the deed is where He is held. Its test is a life.
- **The two Gates** say it from His side. The Essence is alone after creation as before, the hiding is His own power and only toward us, and opposites meet in Him. Their test is a mind that can hold that without flinching.
- **Sefer HaArachim**, the map made in the Rebbe's generation, lays the whole teaching out in order. What it adds is the ladder: the unity has rungs, and a teacher who names one rung as the whole teaches it wrongly.

Together they say what none says alone. **The unity is not one statement but a ladder, and its top is not the world erased. The world is real. It stands, and it is still one with Him, because only He, who holds opposites together, can let something be and still be alone.** The Tanya gives the bottom rung and the purpose: this world made His home. The Gates give the upper rungs and their words. Sefer HaArachim gives the order, and the warning that the truest unity is not the one that denies the world but the one that holds it.

The order of the faces tells the story. In the Tanya the practical faces lead and "opposites joined" comes last. In both Gates the Essence leads by far and the soul comes last, since there the soul is mostly an analogy. Sefer HaArachim is not a book of lines but of teachings (about fifty in the two reports). It spends most of its length on the higher and lower unity, and on the question the other three leave loose: which unity is meant, and against what.

## The three rungs

All four know a lower and a higher unity. Sefer HaArachim adds a third rung above both and names all three:

1. **The lower unity.** The world is real, but it is not a thing on its own. It exists only because God is giving it being this moment. In the Shema this is "Baruch Shem... *va'ed*", and *va'ed* is *echad* with its letters changed. The Tanya says so: כי ״ועד״ הוא ״אחד״ בחלופי אתוון (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud 7:1, line 823).
2. **The higher unity.** Before Him the world counts for nothing. It is there, but it has no weight and takes up no room: כל העולמות אין להם שום ערך ותפיסת מקום (Sefer HaArachim, scan p. 137, line 2273). The Tanya's own line: משום דכולא קמיה כלא חשיב, וכאין ואפס ממש (Tanya ch. 20, line 301).
3. **Yachid, "alone".** Before God as He is in Himself, nothing was made at all: למעלה מ,אחד" הוא ,יחיד" (Sefer HaArachim, scan p. 75, line 1202).

One more point decides how the index must speak. Sefer HaArachim says outright that continuous creation, the teaching that the world is spoken into being every moment, is only the first rung. It is Chassidus's own teaching, and yet within Chassidus it is the lower unity: ענין זה הוא יחודא תתאה (scan p. 137, line 2276). The Tanya's first chapter of the Gate of Unity starts there. It is the door, not the house.

## Echad and yachid, said correctly

The index's first precision was "yachid, not echad". Here is what each source actually says.

**The Gate of Unity** puts yachid above echad plainly. Yachid means "truly alone", above the joining of separate things that is called echad: בחינת יחיד משמעו לבדו ממש / והוא למעלה מהתאחדות נפרדים שנקרא אחד (Gate of Unity 10:12-13, lines 493-494). The Mitteler Rebbe goes further. Of Him not even the word "unity" fits, because there is nothing besides Him to be united: והוא הנקרא יחיד, שאין עוד מלבדו לומר בו לשון התאחדות או לשון יחוד (10:22, line 503).

**Strashelye** draws the same line. Yachid is alone, with nothing besides it, and echad is two things joined: כנודע החילוק שבין יחיד לאחד כי תואר יחיד הוא דבר שהוא יחיד לבד ואין זולתו (First Gate 16:2, line 223). He calls the union of light and vessel "echad, not yachid" (Fifth Gate 2:2, line 942). Then he goes one step further than anyone. Even yachid is said of Him only from our side, from the levels that show toward us: ואפילו בחינת יחיד אין לתארו כי אם מצד הספירות והדרגין הנגלה לגבי דידן (Second Gate 27:7, line 447). Of the Essence itself only denials may be said.

**Sefer HaArachim** keeps the ranking and adds a turn. On the ladder of how far the world is denied, yachid is highest, and the Tzemach Tzedek says the Essence can be called only yachid: עצם מהות הא"ס ב"ה אין שייך לקרותו אחד רק יחיד (scan p. 239, line 3912). But the Shema says "echad" on purpose: ולכן נאמר ,הוי' אחד" (ולא ,הוי' יחיד") (scan p. 48, line 749). Its news is that the heavens, the earth and every direction, many and even opposed, are one in Him. In one respect echad is higher: שישנה מעלה בהאחדות דבחינת ,אחד" על האחדות דבחינת ,יחיד" (scan p. 27, line 356). Under yachid nothing else exists, but the world's existence stays apart from His unity. Under echad the world's existence is itself one with Him. The entry's highest unity is exactly this: בזה שגם המציאות דהעולם מיוחד עמו ית (scan p. 75, line 1205). A unity that works by denying the world is, it says, a limited one, and the world's own condition is mixed into it. A unity that holds the world's existence comes from the Essence alone, because the Essence can do anything and bears opposites: הוא מפני שהעצמות הוא כל יכול ונושא הפכים (scan p. 115, line 1884).

**The Tanya** names the lower and higher unity (Echad and Va'ed, line 823) and calls God "yachid u'meyuchad" throughout, but it never ranks yachid over echad. Its highest line is not a word for God but a place: even on the earth below there is nothing else, "and this is His home below" (ch. 33, line 477).

## Where they agree and where they differ, on this point

- **Agree:** "echad" in its plain sense is a joining of parts, and that is not what God's unity is. The many added up into one is the unity of creatures, not His. All four hold this.
- **Agree:** the higher unity of the Shema is above the lower unity of Baruch Shem (Tanya line 823; Sefer HaArachim line 2285: האחדות ד,אחד" היא יחודא עילאה, והאחדות ד,ועד" - יחודא תתאה).
- **Differ, on what "yachid" names.** The Gate of Unity gives it to the Essence of the Infinite Light before the contraction (line 587). Strashelye gives it to the will, the highest sefirah (Keter), and denies even it to the Essence (lines 223, 447). The Tzemach Tzedek, in Sefer HaArachim, gives it to the Essence, and to the Essence only (line 3912). These are kept as three positions.
- **Differ, on whether yachid is simply highest.** Both Gates rank it above echad without qualification. Sefer HaArachim ranks it highest on the ladder of denial, then says the Shema's echad has an advantage yachid lacks, and that yachid "is not enough" unless it is drawn into the details: וצריך לקשר ולהמשיך בחינה זו גם בפרטים (scan p. 28, line 371).
- **A paradox only Sefer HaArachim states.** The lowest rung reaches the highest. *Va'ed* is the word for eternity, and it belongs to what is above echad: שייכת לבחינת יחיד שלמעלה גם מבחינת אחד (scan p. 152, line 2537). Refining the physical world, below even the lower unity, is what touches yachid. Strashelye comes close: the deepest self-nullification shows inside the solid thing (Third Gate 27:1, line 715).

## How the index now states it

> God's unity is the unity of the Essence. It has rungs. In the lower unity the world is real and is
> given being by Him each moment. In the higher unity the world counts for nothing before Him. Before
> Him as He is in Himself, nothing was made at all. For Him as He is, the truest word is "yachid",
> alone, not a whole made of parts. Even that word is ours, said from our side. What the Shema calls
> Him is "echad", one, and on purpose: the many, and the world's own existence, are one with Him while
> they stand. Only the Essence, which bears opposites, can do that. So the unity is never the many
> added up into one, and never the world erased so that He is left alone.

## Where the four agree

Four points on which every source speaks, each with one verified line.

**1. Nothing changed in Him. He is alone now as He was before.** - Tanya (ch. 20, line 301): משום דכולא קמיה כלא חשיב, וכאין ואפס ממש, "because before Him everything counts as nothing". - Gate of Unity (9:19, line 446): על כן אין בעצמות אלהו״ת שום שינוי כלל, "so there is no change at all in God's Essence". - Strashelye (Preface 9, line 9): וכמו שהיה לבדו מקודם שנבראו העולמות כן הוא יתברך לבדו אחר בריאת העולמות, "as He was alone before the worlds were made, so He is alone after". - Sefer HaArachim (scan p. 111, line 1822): אבל לגבי עצמות אוא"ס כפי שהוא מצד עצמו, "but before the Essence as it is in itself" there is no existence at all.

**2. Before Him, the highest world and ours are equal.** - Tanya (Holy Letter 6, line 1090): שכמו שהוא יתברך מצוי בעליונים – כך הוא ממש בשוה בתחתונים, "as He is found above, so He is truly, equally, below". - Gate of Unity (53:8, line 2653): כי שרש האצילות ושרש העשיה שוין ממש לגבי עצמותו, "the root of the highest world and of ours are exactly equal before His Essence". - Strashelye (Fourth Gate 19:3, line 875): שאין עוד שאפילו בבחינת הצמצומים הכל הוא עצמותו ברוך הוא בבחינת השוואה גמורה, "even in the contractions, all is His Essence, in complete equality". - Sefer HaArachim (scan p. 74, line 1188): באופן שאצילות ועשי' הם בשוה ממש, "so that the highest world and ours are exactly equal".

**3. The hiding is real toward us and not in Him. The world is not an illusion.** - Tanya (Holy Letter 20, line 1290): אבל בידיעה שממטה למעלה – היש הנברא הוא דבר נפרד לגמרי, "from below looking up, the created thing is a completely separate thing". And reading the contraction literally is an error (Shaar HaYichud 7:15, line 837). - Gate of Unity (12:19, line 662): ולא שנסתלק ונתעלם לגמרי, "and not that He withdrew and hid completely". - Strashelye (First Gate 21:6, line 251): ולהבין הצמצום כפשוטו שכביכול צמצם עצמו וזה עון פלילי, "to take the contraction literally... is a grave sin". - Sefer HaArachim (scan p. 162, line 2696): גם זה שהעולם הוא מציאות יש (דעת תחתון דהנבראים) הוא אמת, "that the world is a real something is also true".

**4. The aim is the lowest place.** - Tanya (ch. 33, line 477): אשר אפילו בארץ מתחת – אין עוד מלבדו, וזו היא דירתו בתחתונים, "even on the earth below there is nothing else, and this is His home below". - Gate of Unity (7:46, line 332): שבזה ממש שורה בבחינת אין סוף דוקא מבחינת תחלת הכל, "right here rests the Infinite itself". - Strashelye (Third Gate 27:1, line 715): אבל בבחינת נוקבא הוא דייקא הכוונה שיהיה נגלה הביטול בבחינת היש דייקא, "the intent is that self-nullification show precisely in the solid thing". - Sefer HaArachim (scan p. 75, line 1205): בזה שגם המציאות דהעולם מיוחד עמו ית, "in this: that even the world's existence is one with Him".

## Where they differ (kept, not smoothed)

- **Is "created every moment" the ground of the unity or its first rung?** The Tanya's Gate of Unity opens with it, from the Baal Shem Tov, and builds everything on it. Sefer HaArachim calls it the lower unity (line 2276) and says the creating power is a limited power, so it gives only "the nullification of selfhood" (line 2116, in the range report). Both stand. The index keeps the teaching and stops calling it the summit.
- **May God and the world be pictured as soul and body?** The Tanya refuses at the root. The analogy fails because soul and body really are separate at their root: אך באמת, אין המשל דומה לנמשל כלל, כי הנשמה והגוף הם באמת נפרדים זה מזה בשרשם (Shaar HaYichud 6:7, line 821). Strashelye uses the soul-body picture for the world of Atzilut and refuses it only for the Essence (Second Gate 30:4, line 469). Sefer HaArachim names it the nations' error: חושבים שהקב"ה והעולם הוא כמו נשמה וגוף (scan p. 170, line 2839).
- **Is knowing the way in, or the last obstacle?** The Tanya puts what is beyond the mind in faith. The Gate of Unity says everyone must know it, in detail, and tests the knowing: joy and self-nullification in exactly equal measure (5:39, line 199). Strashelye says understanding still leaves someone who understands: כי היחוד על ידי השכלה הרי עדיין יש מי שמשכיל ביטולו (Second Gate 32:6, line 491). The last step is to be nullified above understanding (40:6, line 558). Sefer HaArachim says the higher self-nullification is given from above, and that the self-nullification a person makes on his own is still "something". Most people hold the higher truth by acknowledging it, not by seeing it.
- **Which unity is the main one, lower or higher?** Sefer HaArachim holds both directions. Most souls today serve in the lower (line 3199), and the dwelling below is completed there. Yet without a taste of the higher the lower "is not true" (line 3208). On Shabbos neither outranks the other: אין יתרון ביחו"ע על יחו"ת, ושניהם שוים ממש (scan p. 210, line 3572). Strashelye ranks the self-nullification above the worlds higher in service, and the one inside the solid thing higher in purpose. The Tanya makes the lower world the purpose (ch. 36). These are kept as emphases, not reconciled.
- **Who can reach it?** The Gate of Unity sets a high bar and says so. Strashelye says people differ in how much they grasp, but not in how nullified they can be (line 510). Sefer HaArachim says the higher unity belongs to every Jew by his soul, and as experience is a gift, fully so only for lofty souls.
- **Is the contraction a full withdrawal?** All four deny the literal reading. The Gate of Unity also says its own best analogy fails, since the trace "shows that all withdrew". Sefer HaArachim says the contraction is real above: the contracted light truly gives creatures room.

## What it means for a person

- **Start where you are standing.** The lower unity is not a lesser truth. The world in front of you is real, and it is being given to you this moment. Most of the work of a life happens here, and here is where the home is built.
- **Then look up one rung.** Now and then, even for a moment, even in imagination, let it count for nothing before Him. Without that, the sources say, the lower slides back into self.
- **Don't try to hold the top.** You do not need to feel "nothing was ever made". The sources say no word reaches Him, not even "alone". What you can hold is the Shema's "one": the world stays, and it is His.
- **Look for Him in the hard place.** All four put the aim at the bottom: the solid, stubborn, ordinary thing, and the deed done with it.
- **When you understand, let go of the one who understands.** Knowing is a door. The next room is quiet.

## Warnings for anyone teaching this

- **Not acosmism.** "Nothing besides Him" never means the world is a dream. If it were, the mitzvot done in it would be a dream too: גם עשיית המצוות היא רק דמיון ח"ו (Sefer HaArachim, scan p. 72, line 1167). The Tanya says that from below the creature really is a separate thing (line 1290).
- **Not erasing distinctions.** Unity does not cancel the lines God drew between things: ענין האחדות לא בא לשלול את החילוק שקבע הקב"ה בין דבר אחד לחבירו (Sefer HaArachim, scan p. 58, line 919). Korach saw a real unity above all boundaries and applied it now. That was his error. Opposites are one in Him and stay opposites in conduct.
- **The world is real.** Its "somethingness" is true on its own level (line 2696). The contraction is not literal, and it is also not nothing.
- **No soul-and-body picture of God and the world.** The Tanya refuses it (line 821), and Sefer HaArachim calls it an error (line 2839). He is not the world's soul inside its body.
- **Balance the two unities.** Heart above, eyes below: שבתפלה צריך להיות לבו למעלה ועיניו למטה (Sefer HaArachim, scan p. 195, line 3293). The higher alone falls after prayer and does not refine the world. The lower alone is not true.
- **"Created every moment" is only the first rung.** Teach it as the door. Do not teach it as the summit, and do not stop there.
- **Do not take your own self-nullification for the higher unity.** Self-nullification that comes from your own understanding or effort is still "something". The higher is given.
- **No word holds Him.** Even "alone" is said from our side (Strashelye, line 447). Say less, and say it as a door.

## The short answer, now with eight

The first four sources said the unity from two sides: the Tanya from the person's, the two Gates from His, and Sefer HaArachim laid out the rungs. The new four add something none of those did at length. **They say what the unity is like from the inside when it is real, and what it is like when it is not.**

- **Imrei Binah** is a book of doctrine like the Gates, but its subject is the Shema, the tzitzit and the tefillin, the unity as something a person does. It argues yachid and echad more fully than any other source here, and it argues that the lower unity reaches higher than the higher one.
- **Kuntres HaHitpa'alut** is a field guide to arousal in prayer. Its test is whether self is felt in it: the deeper the arousal, the less it is felt.
- **Kuntres HaAvodah** and **Kuntres HaTefillah** put the unity in prayer. Knowing must be particular and felt in the heart, and knowledge that never reaches prayer turns into selfhood.

On one point both authors of the new books, the Mitteler Rebbe and the Rashab, speak together, and they go further than the Tanya does. **What reaches Him as He is, is not love or excitement, which are a "something", but bitul, becoming nothing; and the sign of real bitul is that you do not feel it as yours.**

## Yachid and echad: what the new sources add

Part one found three positions on what "yachid" names (the Essence before the tzimtzum, in the Gate of Unity; the will, with even yachid denied to the Essence, in Strashelye; the Essence alone, in the Tzemach Tzedek), and Sefer HaArachim's turn: the Shema says "echad" on purpose, and the world's existence one with Him is the highest unity. The new sources add two things.

**Imrei Binah argues it both ways, in the Mitteler Rebbe's own words.** Yachid is above echad: the level before the tzimtzum עד לפני הצמצום דקו"ח שנק' יחיד שלמעלה מאחד (Imrei Binah, Petach HaShaar 9, line 32). Echad is a counted one; yachid has no partner and no number: ולזה יקראוהו בל' יחיד זאין לו שיתוף וחיבור במספר כלל וכלל (line 120). The first Havayah of the Shema is yachid, the Essence before the tzimtzum, "with nothing besides Him at all" (line 124). The kabbalists' unions of the Shema all belong after the tzimtzum, ובכ"ז אין יוצאי' י"ח היחוד האמיתי שהוא במוה"ע ממש (line 43). Then the other end: Israel is the intermediary between them, מפני שישראל דוקא הוא בחי' הממוצע בין יחיד לאחד (line 129), and so ונמצ' מובן מכל זה דאין הפרש כלל בין בחי' יחיד לבחי' אחד (line 150). One who gives his whole soul into the echad is thereby given into the yachid above it (line 126).

So Imrei Binah holds what Sefer HaArachim holds, by another road. Sefer HaArachim says the Shema's echad, the world's existence one with Him, is the truest unity (Sefer HaArachim lines 749, 1205). Imrei Binah says the echad of the worlds is made equal to the Essence's yachid, and that Israel, the soul that gives itself, is what makes them equal. That the two books say one thing is our reading; the words differ.

**The Rashab uses "yachid" for the lower unity.** In Kuntres HaTefillah the Shema teaches איך שהוא אחד בז' רקיעים וארץ וד' רוחות העולם בבחינת יחיד ממש (line 85): His oneness in the heavens, the earth and the four directions is "truly yachid". And at the top of the ladder, עד מה ששלימות עצמותו נושא כל ההפכים והכל בבחי' יחיד ממש (line 46). For the Rashab, yachid is not reserved for a level above the world. It is what the world's echad is, seen truly. This is a fourth position, and it is kept beside the other three.

**Proposed addition to how the index states it** (Part one, "How the index now states it"): after "Only the Essence, which bears opposites, can do that", add: *The Mitteler Rebbe says the soul that gives itself is what makes the world's echad equal to His yachid, so that "there is no difference at all" between them (Imrei Binah lines 129, 150); and the Rashab calls His oneness in the heavens and earth itself "truly yachid" (Kuntres HaTefillah line 85).* This refines the statement; it does not change it.

## The lower unity higher: the end holds the beginning

Part one kept a disagreement over which unity is the main one. Imrei Binah takes the strongest side of it of any source here, and says so outright.

- The lower unity's root is higher: ונמצא דיח"ת דאיהו וגרמוהי חד שבמל' שרשו יותר עליון מיח"ע (Imrei Binah line 70). "The main unity is in Baruch Shem" (line 157). Baruch Shem has an extra advantage over the first verse: מעלה יתירה שיש ביח"ת דבשכמל"ו על יח"ע דפסוק ראשון (line 183), more so in exile than in the Temple (line 186), and in the future it will be above the higher unity (line 231). - The reason is the end that holds the beginning. The earth, lowest of all, has the power to create from nothing: הוא מבחי' כחו של אא"ס שבתחלה כי הארץ סוף הכל שאין עוד למטה הימנה לכך נמצא בה כח התחלה (line 179). A limit that does not limit can only be the Essence: הרי הגבול עצמו נעשה בלתי בע"ג שזהו א"א להיות כ"א מצד העצמות ממש (line 245). - The hiding is why. The Tanya calls His power to hide "His mighty acts", הן הן גבורותיו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, אשר כל יכול, לצמצם החיות והרוחניות (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud 4, line 809). Imrei Binah takes the same words further: the complete hiding of the second day, והן הן גבורותיו שיוכל לצמצם ולהסתיר לגמרי כדי שיהי' בריאה יש (line 162), is higher than the first kindness.

- **Imrei Binah:** the lower is higher, now and more so later. It also keeps the opposite on the page: "as they are united above, so below, and not more" (line 162).
- **The Gate of Unity:** the roots of the highest world and ours are "exactly equal" before the Essence (Gate line 2653).
- **Sefer HaArachim:** both directions. The lower is where the home is completed and where most souls serve, yet without the higher it is not true (lines 3199, 3208); on Shabbat neither is above the other (line 3572); and *va'ed*, the lowest word, belongs to yachid (line 2537).
- **Strashelye:** the bitul above the worlds is higher in service; the bitul inside the solid thing is the main intent (lines 715, 753).
- **The Tanya:** the lower world is the purpose, שתכלית בריאת עולם הזה הוא – שנתאוה הקדוש ברוך הוא להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (Tanya ch. 36, line 506), while the higher unity is the deeper view, the worlds nullified before their source "like the light of the sun" (Tanya line 802).

These are kept as positions. Imrei Binah's is the most daring, and it is the one closest to Sefer HaArachim's paradox that the lowest rung touches the highest.

## What reaches the Essence: bitul, not love

This is where the new sources agree most, across two authors and four books.

- **Imrei Binah:** "Spirit brings spirit, just like it" (line 571). Love from below is a raised seal, a "something", and it draws down light clothed in vessels: וכל זה שבא האורו' בכלים ע"י בחי' הכלים דמ"ע למטה זהו ע"י בחי' אהבה בחי' רצוא הנ"ל (line 574). Bitul, becoming בטל כאבן דומם בהעדר הרגשת עצמו (line 572), draws what is נמשך מבחי' העצמות שבאא"ס שאינו מתיישב בכלי ויש כלל (line 572), "the light of the Essence as it is" (line 574). The servant's bitul from awe reaches the Essence more than the most wondrous love (line 424). Bowing the body in the Amidah is higher than the bitul of mind and heart (line 562).
- **Kuntres HaHitpa'alut:** the real arousal is felt, and yet "not called felt in itself; on the contrary, there is no self-feeling in this arousal" (line 555). And the rule: וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר כמו באהבה ורצון ותענוג היותר פנימית, יותר לא ירגיש בה (line 556).
- **Kuntres HaAvodah:** awe rests on a hidden feeling of the Essence, והוא באמת הרגש בחי' עצמות אוא"ס ב"ה שלמעלה ממדריגות פרטיות (line 107); love is in the lights. So awe, as bitul, is above love.
- **Kuntres HaTefillah:** before the Amidah one contemplates that ועצמות המאור הוא בהתגלות שנמצא בכל מקום ומקום ממש (line 91), and stands before it in inner bitul, "the main point of the Amidah".

- **The Tanya** builds the service on love and awe, and puts love in delights above: it comes from above as a gift ובאה מלמעלה בבחינת מתנה למי שהוא שלם ביראה (Tanya ch. 43, line 651). The Tanya does say that holiness rests only on what is nullified. But it does not rank love below bitul as a way to the Essence. The new sources do.
- **The Gate of Unity** asked for joy and bitul in exactly equal measure (Gate line 199). Imrei Binah, by the same author, puts bitul alone at the top.
- **Strashelye** is closest: understanding still leaves "someone who understands his bitul" (line 491), and the main bitul is above Da'at (line 558).
- **Sefer HaArachim** adds the caution the new sources also give: the bitul a person makes on his own is still "something", and the higher is given.

This is kept as a real difference of emphasis between the Tanya and the later Rebbeim. It is not a contradiction: the Rashab still makes love the work of prayer, because only love binds and refines (Kuntres HaAvodah lines 122-125), and Imrei Binah still calls love "the main service" of the Shema (lines 261-262).

## The counterfeit: Godly arousal against self-arousal

Kuntres HaHitpa'alut turns the question round. It asks not what the unity is but how to tell when the feeling of it is false. Its rule: שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר (line 135). The counterfeit has a name: וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו (line 167). The error is to make the arousal the main thing, עד שיעשה עיקר מן ההתפעלות ולא מן האלהות כלל (line 548). The sign of false bitul is the aftermath: בא לכלל הרגשת העצמיות מזה עצמו דוקא (line 808). And bitul sought as a prize is its own opposite: הרי יצא דבר והיפוכו, כי גם באותה השעה מוטעה היה (line 814).

The other sources say the same thing from their own sides:

- **The Rashab:** general contemplation, however excited, ה"ז בבחי' מקיף לבד ואין זה התפעלות אמיתי כי אם דמיון כוזב (Kuntres HaAvodah line 204). Learning without prayer ובהעדר העבודה בתפלה גם מה שלמר וידץ נהפך לו לפה"מ בקרבו ר"ל שנעשה עי"ז יש וגסה"ר ביותר (Kuntres HaTefillah line 81, OCR for שלמד וידע).
- **Imrei Binah:** the unity dwells only בבחי' הבטול והעדר הרגשת עצמו מכל וכל (line 6); and people take natural heat for the flame of God, ורבים טועים בזה (line 517).
- **The Gate of Unity:** the first step toward evil is a good trait that feels itself, and its example is excitement in prayer (Gate lines 2699-2703).
- **Strashelye:** understanding that still has someone who understands (line 491).

So six of the eight sources name the same danger: the self slipping back in through the very experience of the unity. Sefer HaArachim says it another way (the bitul a person makes on his own is still "something"). The Tanya says a related thing in its own terms: pride is like idolatry, because it makes oneself a thing apart (Tanya ch. 22, line 323).

## Crying and joy as one

Kuntres HaHitpa'alut defends the man who cannot contemplate and only cries out. His bitter cry and the joyful song of the one who is close are one arousal of the same Godly soul, ונמצא אחת היא רק שזה מצד המנגד (line 662). The ground is the Zohar that the Tanya quotes, בכיה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא, וחדוה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא (Tanya ch. 34, line 492), which the Kuntres cites "as in Likkutei Amarim" (line 664): at the very level at which the Godly soul rejoices, ובאותה מדריגה עצמה תתמרמר הנפש האלהית ותבכה מצד ריחוקה מאחד (line 666).

- **Imrei Binah:** no light without the darkness before it, and no love and joy without bitterness first, וזהו כמו שא"א לאור בלא חשך הקדום כך א"א לאהבה ושמחה בלא מרירות והכנעה קודם (line 33). The soul's weeping in the body reaches deeper than the pleasure it had at its source (line 185).
- **The Gate of Unity:** joy and bitul in equal measure (Gate line 199).
- **Kuntres HaHitpa'alut** again, on the natural melancholy: when God dwells with the crushed (line 704), ואז נהפך האנחה שלו והמרה שחורה הטבעיות הנזכר לעיל לשמחה ועונג רק מצד אלהות ששורה על נפשו ממש (line 708).
- **Kuntres HaTefillah:** "the true vessel for inner Godliness is the broken heart" (line 100).

What the new sources add is that the cry is not a lower stage to be got past. It is the same nearness felt from the far side.

## Knowing: particular, in prayer, and with a limit

- **The Tanya** puts what is above grasp in faith, רק להאמין באמונה, שהיא למעלה מהשכל ומהשגה (Tanya line 834), and asks for a strong and firm bond of the mind (Tanya line 629).
- **Imrei Binah** says the Shema's whole purpose is that faith above knowing come into knowing, שיבא כח האמונה זו שלמעלה מן הדעת לידי גלוי בדעת והבנה (line 4), and that contemplating how heaven and earth come from nothing ה"ז כמו שמתבונן ממש בעצמות אא"ס שלפני האצילות בלי שינוי כלל (line 191).
- **Kuntres HaAvodah** says only particular knowing is knowing (lines 204, 211), and also that "in truth he cannot grasp a divine matter with no garment at all" (line 46).
- **Kuntres HaTefillah** says study is preparation and prayer the thing (line 49); the main point of "know today" is the heart (line 62); and "the main service now is in these matters most of all", memale, sovev and creation, not the Essence before the tzimtzum (line 71). Those who reach higher with the mind coarsen it (line 72).

All four new books stand on the side of knowing in the SPINE's knowledge-or-faith dispute. Each also keeps a limit: Imrei Binah's sealed secret, שזהו עיקר כבודו להיות בהעל' דווקא (line 302); the Rashab's garment that never falls away, and his warning against reaching for the Essence with the mind. The limit is not faith in place of knowing. It is where knowing turns into bitul.

## The animal soul: subdued, or turned?

The Tanya's beinoni is to regard his evil as still "in its strength and might" in its place (Tanya ch. 13, line 222). The new sources go further, each with a limit:

- **Kuntres HaAvodah:** the natural mind itself comes to see that the physical is "not worthy of the name of a thing" (line 142); the fire of holiness burns the other fire (line 149); the revealed yechidah turns the animal soul "from the opposite to the opposite" (line 180). But the Rashab also says the animal soul never wants Godliness by its own nature, and leaves the question open (line 180).
- **Kuntres HaTefillah:** contemplating what the natural mind can hold lets the animal soul itself feel and love (line 77).
- **Kuntres HaHitpa'alut:** the lowest point of the heart, the "beast" below knowledge, is rooted above knowledge (line 871), and its fleshly joy can reach the essential delight of the yechidah (line 882).
- **Imrei Binah:** true teshuvah makes good out of the evil itself, וזה כח התשוב' אמיתי' שעוש' מן הרע עצמו טוב (line 514), and "the foundation of all Torah and mitzvot" is to turn darkness to light (line 513).

## A correction to the SPINE

SPINE §5 listed Kuntres HaHitpa'alut among the places where the tie is thin: "most passages are about the psychology of arousal. The link of each passage to the unity is our reading." The line-by-line reading found the first half true (605 of 912 lines are structural) and the second half wrong: 222 lines state the tie in the text's own words, and only 9 ties are ours. What is true is narrower. The book states the unity from the soul's side (Godly arousal, bitul, the part of God, the bond to the Essence, separation from the One) and almost never from the side of the worlds. SPINE §5 (and its twin in `spine.json`) now says this. The same is true, less strongly, of the two Rashab tracts: their purpose is stated, most lines are structural, and the ties that are stated are stated from the soul's side.

## Where the four new works add to the four agreements

Each of Part one's four agreements has a line in Imrei Binah:

1. **Nothing changed in Him.** ע"כ ודאי מובן שאין שינוי כלל בין יחיד בעצמותו לאחדות ה' באבי"ע (Imrei Binah line 132).
2. **Before Him, the highest and the lowest are equal.** אבל קמי' עצמותו ממש שניהם כא' שוין (line 418); and the Rashab: the Essence is found in every place (Kuntres HaTefillah line 91).
3. **The hiding is real toward us and not in Him.** שכל השנוי הזה בין העלם וגלוי לא שייך רק לגבי המקבלים למטה ולא לגבי עצמותו (line 273); the tzimtzum is שאין זה הפסק גמור רק העדר גילוי אור העצמי (line 136).
4. **The aim is the lowest place.** The future revelation אין לו כלי רק דוקא גופה של מעשה המצוה שלמטה בפ"מ (line 564); Godliness will be revealed מפני שיהי' גלוי אלקו' למטה יותר מלמעלה (line 439); and the Rashab: the soul was bound to the body והו"ע קישור הנפש עם הגוף בכדי לעשות הכל בבחי' אחד (Kuntres HaAvodah line 230).

## Where they differ (kept, not smoothed)

- **Is the lower unity higher?** Imrei Binah: yes (lines 70, 157, 183). The Gate of Unity: equal (line 2653). Sefer HaArachim: both, and equal on Shabbat. The Tanya: the lower is the purpose, the higher the deeper view. Kept as four positions.
- **Love or bitul?** The Tanya puts love in delights above, as a gift to one complete in awe (line 651). Imrei Binah and the Rashab put bitul, and awe as bitul, above love as the way to the Essence (Imrei Binah lines 424, 574; Kuntres HaAvodah line 107). Both Rashab tracts and Imrei Binah still make love the work of prayer.
- **How much does feeling matter?** The Tanya consoles the one who cannot feel: understanding clothed in the deed already gives the mitzvot wings, הרי תבונה זו מתלבשת במעשה דבור ומחשבת התורה ומצותיה (Tanya ch. 16, line 257). Kuntres HaHitpa'alut ranks that level third of five and says "the light of God never reached" those who pray dry (line 34). Kuntres HaTefillah says knowledge that is not felt in prayer turns to selfhood (line 81). Yet Kuntres HaHitpa'alut also says the truest feeling is not felt as one's own (line 556).
- **Mind and heart.** The Tanya: Chochmah, Binah and Da'at are "the mothers and source" of the middot, וחכמה בינה דעת נקראו אמות ומקור למדות (Tanya ch. 3, line 103). Imrei Binah: the middot have their being above the intellect, שיש למציא' מדו' בעצם תחלת התהוותם למעלה מעלה מן השכל (line 593), and the mind is a passage. Kuntres HaTefillah, from the other side, makes the mind's garments "a passage" to the heart (line 66).
- **Simplicity or comprehension.** Kuntres HaHitpa'alut demands toil in contemplation, and also says the simple reach the yechidah more than the wise (lines 623, 887). Kuntres HaAvodah demands particular knowing (line 204). The Tanya makes the hidden love every Jew's. Kept as the SPINE keeps it.
- **Hidden things.** The Gate of Unity asks everyone to contemplate the Essence before the tzimtzum in detail (Gate line 489). Imrei Binah, by the same author, keeps a secret sealed "for His glory is to be hidden" (line 302), and the Rashab tells those who reach for it that the main service now is lower down (Kuntres HaTefillah line 71).
- **The advantage of light from darkness.** The Gate of Unity: only to the eye (line 2414). Imrei Binah: "a real advantage over the light of the Essence" (line 160). Same author; neither retracts.

## What it means for a person (added)

- **What moved you?** If the thing itself moved you, it is His. If the wish to be moved did, it is you.
- **The deeper, the quieter.** The truest arousal is felt, and you are not felt in it.
- **Love is a door; becoming nothing is the room.** Love and excitement are good, and they bring Him down clothed. What brings Him as He is, is bitul, and bitul does not feel like an achievement.
- **Your cry counts.** Weeping at your distance and joy at His nearness are one movement of the same soul.
- **Take one thing, in detail, into prayer.** Not "He fills all worlds" in general, but this creature and the life in it, held until it reaches the heart.
- **The low place is the high place.** The ordinary, bounded day, the yoke taken on without understanding, the deed done with the body: that is where the end holds the beginning.

## Warnings for anyone teaching this (added)

- **Do not take the feeling for the thing.** Arousal that makes itself the main thing is "serving oneself" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut lines 167, 548).
- **Do not hand out bitul as a prize.** Bitul sought as the one thing lacking is its own opposite (line 814). Its sign is the afterward: lowliness, and indifference to insult (line 829).
- **General is not contemplation.** "He fills and surrounds all worlds" said in general only hovers over the mind (Kuntres HaAvodah line 204; Kuntres HaTefillah line 49).
- **Knowledge without prayer turns to self.** Do not let the teaching become a possession (Kuntres HaTefillah lines 80-81).
- **Do not reach for the Essence with the mind.** The working matters are memale, sovev and creation from nothing (Kuntres HaTefillah lines 71-72).
- **Do not despise the simple cry.** "Many err" in looking down on it (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut lines 663-664).

## The short answer, now with nine

The first eight sources said what the unity is (Part one) and what it is like from inside when it is real (Part two). Samach Vav asks why there is anything for it to be the unity of. Its answer is a desire with no reason, ואין אנו יודעים טעם שכלי מפני מה נשתוקק (line 208), that the Essence, which has no above and below, be revealed below (line 213). From that it draws the one thing none of the eight says in so many words: **God's will has two layers. The will that made the world has reasons and is the root of space and time. The will in the mitzvot has none: ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא (line 596). So only the mitzvah brings the Essence itself into space and time,** דהיינו שגם בבחי' מקום וזמן לא יהי' ההגבלה דמקום וזמן (line 740).

## What it changes in Parts one and two

- **The rungs get a top above the Tanya's.** The Tanya's gloss, שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו, וזו היא מדרגת החכמה (Tanya line 499), is "there is none besides You", the bitul of the light. Above it the Rashab puts "nothing apart from You": אבל אפס בלתך (הוא) היינו שגם עמך ית' לא יש שום מציאות כלל (line 4538). And "there is none else" is found, he says, precisely in physical "something": דבחי' אין עוד זהו דוקא בהתהוות היש הגשמי (line 5251). So the highest rung is not away from the world.
- **A fifth position on which unity is higher.** Part two kept four: the lower (Imrei Binah), equal (the Gate of Unity), both (Sefer HaArachim), the lower as purpose and the higher as the deeper view (the Tanya). The Rashab puts the union of the two above the higher alone: אבל ענין יחוד יחו"ע ויחו"ת הוא, שגם כמו שהעולם הוא במציאות, הוא בבחי' ביטול ויחוד בתכלית (line 2019). And of the two contemplations of the higher unity, "they do not exist" and "their existence is Godliness", he calls the second not lower: והיינו שהן בבחי' מציאות, אלא שמציאותם הוא אלקות (line 3173). This stands nearest Sefer HaArachim's "the world's existence one with Him" (Sefer HaArachim line 1205). That they say one thing is our reading.
- **Yachid.** Part two found the Rashab calling His oneness in the world "truly yachid" (Kuntres HaTefillah line 85). Samach Vav says it again, שגם מצד העולמות הוא בבחי' יחיד ממש (line 5147), and defines the word: אבל בחי' יחיד היינו יחוד בעצם (line 5987). The fourth position of Part two is the Rashab's settled one.
- **What reaches the Essence.** Part two: bitul, not love. Samach Vav names its forms: the physical deed, ולכן המשכת בחי' העצמות הוא במצות מעשיות דוקא, שאינו בגדר כלי (line 2005); the prohibition (line 3373); the simple servant with no self of his own (line 3999); toil over a question in the Oral Torah (line 4718); and the return, דבחי' השוב באמת נטוע ומושרש בעצמות א"ס ב"ה (line 2008). Bitul is the vessel, דהכלי לאוא"ס הוא מה שבטל דוקא (line 5253), and bitul reached only by thinking fades when the thinking stops (line 4486).
- **The hiding.** Part one kept the Gate of Unity's "light from darkness only looks brighter" (Gate line 2414) against Imrei Binah. Samach Vav stands with Imrei Binah and gives a reason: ועצם אינו מסתיר על עצם (line 2891), the power to hide is the chief power (line 2503), and שההעלם בעצמו יאיר (line 2947).
- **Knowing.** Samach Vav stands with the four books of Part two on the side of knowing (line 368), and sets the tightest limit of all: no grasp of the Essence, not even by negation (line 876), and all contemplation, "all before Him is as nothing" included, is seeing Him "from behind" (line 3735). What does grasp Him is the heart's simple will: אבל ברעו"ד נתפס בחי' העצמות (line 897).
- **"Ein Sof" is not the Essence.** None of the eight draws this line. The Rashab does, and argues that the word names the light (lines 2253, 2302).

## Where it differs (kept, not smoothed)

- **From the Tanya on faith and knowing** (Shaar HaYichud 7, Tanya line 834; Samach Vav lines 368, 1996), **on the mind's eye and prophecy** (Iggeret HaKodesh 19, Tanya line 1267; the Rebbe's footnote at line 1367 says the Hemshech "writes the opposite" and leaves it "needing study"), **and on where the power to create sits** (Iggeret HaKodesh 20, Tanya line 1295; Samach Vav reads it as creation from the light, lines 2273, 2302). See `SAMACH-VAV.md` for each.
- **Will or delight.** Held one in essence (line 959); the Rebbe's footnote reads the conclusion as delight higher (line 1024) and then qualifies it.
- **Servant or son, the "no" or the deed, study or deed.** The series holds each pair and does not reduce it.

## What it means for a person (added)

- **The mitzvah is not small.** It carries the will with no reason, and the part is the whole: שכשאתה תופס במקצת מן העצם אתה תופס בכולו (line 6189).
- **Your simple will is the bond.** Not the feeling your thinking produces, but what you will not give up.
- **Hold the world, don't erase it.** It is easier to long to expire than to live below keeping the Torah (line 2016), and the second is higher.
- **The hiding is His.** A hiding He chooses is no hiding, והסתר רצוני אינו הסתר (line 5684). Do not use this to explain anyone's suffering.
- **Let contemplation make you thirsty, not full.** It sees Him from behind, and that is what it is for (lines 3735, 3829).

## Part four: Hemshech Ayin Beis

*Added 2026-09-29. The Rebbe Rashab's Hemshech Ayin Beis (the series of 5672, 1912-1915, with a written continuation that was not delivered) was read line by line in ten ranges (`AYIN-BEIS.md`, `ayin-beis/`). The text is a clean edition, not OCR. Line numbers in this part are Ayin Beis lines unless another book is named. Every Hebrew phrase in this part was checked with `find_he.py verify` at the line given and confirmed to sit on that line.*

## The short answer, now with ten

Part one said what the unity is, Part two what it is like from inside, and Part three why there is anything for it to be the unity of. Ayin Beis asks how anything comes from the Essence and still shows it, and answers that **the hiding is how revelation happens.** The Essence is not light, דבחי' עצמות המאציל הרי אינו בגדר אור כלל (line 719); the aim is not that the tzimtzum be undone but ולא שיתבטל הצמצום לגמרי כ"א שהצמצום גופא לא יסתיר ויעלים (line 3657); and precisely the created "something" proves the Essence, ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי (line 4454). It also says, more plainly than any of the nine, that knowing the unity and contemplating it are one process: the higher unity understood is זהו רק ההשגה לבד (line 1551), and the knowing that counts, settled, felt and nullifying, is given from above through the Torah (lines 1552-1556).

## What it changes in Parts one to three

- **Echad and yachid, in the Rashab's own definition.** ההפרש בין אחד ליחיד דאחד הוא התאחדות הפרטים (line 193), אבל יחיד הוא שמיוחד בעצם שאין כאן פרט כלל (line 193). "One" is said of the light related to worlds; of the Essence one cannot say even "one" (line 3319). And the last line of the book joins them: שגם יחו"ת הוא בבחי' יחוד כמו יחו"ע ממש (line 5750). This is Part three's settled Rashab position, now defined and then closed.
- **Both knowledges true, from His side.** Part one kept the two knowledges as two views. Ayin Beis: הנה באמת מצד האוא"ס ב"ה שניהם אמת (line 794), both are "in Godliness itself" (line 3871), and in the Essence they are one (line 5738).
- **Only "Creator" names Him.** Part three: "Ein Sof" names the light. Ayin Beis adds that not even "Luminary" fits, and ומה שאנו יכולים לכנות בעצמות הוא רק בשם בורא (line 543).
- **What reaches the Essence: having no vessel.** Part two found bitul, Part three the deed "outside the category of a vessel". Ayin Beis puts it in the person: דהעדר הכלי זהו כלי לעצמות אוא"ס (line 409).
- **Knowing, with a new kind of limit.** Part three's limit was "no grasp, not even by negation". Ayin Beis's is that understanding is open to every mind and holding is a gift (lines 1551-1552); and it maps the road to that holding: details, extract, recognition, confirmation, והתאמתות היא הנחה בשכל מה שלמעלה מן השכל לגמרי (line 4665).
- **The hiding.** Part three: the hiding itself will shine. Ayin Beis gives the how, level by level (lines 719-720, 2578, 3657), ends with דבחי' ההסתר הוא כח הא"ס כמו כח הגילוי (line 5739), and says the night itself will shine, שהלילה עצמה תאיר (line 4688).
- **Which unity is higher.** A sixth voice beside Part two's four and Part three's fifth: the lower unity is first "only a likeness" (line 944) and at the end "a union just like the higher" (line 5750). Its last word stands with Samach Vav and Sefer HaArachim.

## Where it differs (kept, not smoothed)

- **From Samach Vav, on Torah and the deed.** Samach Vav: the Essence is drawn through the practical mitzvot, and the heart's will grasps it. Ayin Beis: דבאמת התו' גופא הוא בחי' עצמות א"ס (line 1432), and of Torah and the heart's will, Torah grasps the Essence's inwardness and the heart's will only its outside (line 3042). Ayin Beis also keeps Samach Vav's side (the deed reaches the Essence, line 4457; souls are above Torah, line 4857).
- **From Samach Vav, on itkafia and ithapcha.** Samach Vav prizes the servant who subdues without changing his nature. Ayin Beis ranks itkafia the lowest of three sweetenings, which changes nothing in essence (line 5116), and only severity sweetened in its root completes the dwelling (line 5160), though it opens by praising the bitul of the vessels (line 71).
- **From Samach Vav, on Iggeret HaKodesh 20.** Samach Vav reads it as creation from the light. Ayin Beis cites it for creation "only by the power of the Essence", ובאמת ענין ההתהוות זהו רק בכח העצמות כידוע ומבו' באגה"ק סי' ך' (line 2619). - Inside the series and against the Tanya: see `AYIN-BEIS.md`.

## What it means for a person (added)

- **Understanding it is not yet knowing it.** Say "I have heard it" (line 2832), and learn: the holding comes through Torah (lines 1552-1556).
- **Having no vessel is the vessel.** The small child's bitul, the yoke, the broken heart (lines 409-411, 3942).
- **You are given a place; you need not defend it.** The world takes up no room of its own (line 1916).
- **The hiding is how He shows Himself,** and the aim is a hiding that no longer hides (line 3657). Do not use this to explain anyone's suffering.
- **The world held is a union like the world erased** (line 5750).
