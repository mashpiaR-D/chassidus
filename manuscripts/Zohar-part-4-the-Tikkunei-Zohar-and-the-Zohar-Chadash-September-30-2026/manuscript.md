## The short answer

The Tikkunei Zohar and the Zohar Chadash say the same thing about God from two sides. From His side: He is one, but not in number; He has no image, no measure and no partner; He is inside every world as He is outside it and does not change; there is no place empty of Him; and all before Him is as nothing. From our side: the Shechinah, His Presence, is in exile with us and in us, and the work of a person is to make a home for Her and to join Her to Him, in thought, in the mouth and in every limb, by prayer, Torah, mitzvot and teshuvah. Both books insist that a person can always come back, that God waits for it, and that the gates of tears are never locked.

## The core teaching on unity

**Patach Eliyahu and the Tikkunim.** The Tikkunei Zohar opens with Elijah's prayer, the most quoted unity text in Chassidus:

> לית מחשבה תפיסא בך כלל (TZ 17a:10, line 293)
> *No thought can grasp You at all.*

> ואנת הוא דקשיר לון ומיחד לון (TZ 17a:12, line 295)
> *And You are the One who binds them and unifies them* (the sefirot; so whoever separates one from another is as if he separated You).

The famous "no place empty of Him" appears in the Tikkunim twice. Once for the upper and lower worlds:

> דלית אתר פנוי מניה לא בעלאין ולא בתתאין (TZ 91b:6, line 2658)
> *For there is no place empty of Him, neither above nor below.*

> לית אתר פנוי מניה כנשמתא דאשתכחת בכל אבר ואבר דגופא (TZ 122b:5, line 3043)
> *There is no place empty of Him, like the soul that is found in every single limb of the body.*

**The Zohar Chadash's own form.** In Yitro, in a passage that reads like Patach Eliyahu:

> לית אתר דלאו איהו תמן, לעילא עד אין סוף, ולתתא עד אין תכלית (ZC Yitro 179, line 1521)
> *There is no place where He is not: above without end, below without limit.*

And in the additions, the whole of "He fills and surrounds" in one line:

> הכי איהו מלגאו דכל עלמין כמו מלבר דכל עלמין לא אשתני (ZC additions, Shelach 18, line 4410)
> *He is within all worlds as He is outside all worlds; He does not change.*

The Tikuna Kadma'ah gives the negative side:

> חד בלא חשבן ובלא שתוף (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 91, line 4380)
> *One, without number and without partnership.*

The Midrash HaNe'elam gives the nullification:

> וכלם לנגדו כאין וכלא חשיבין (ZC Bereshit 265, line 265)
> *All of them are as nothing before Him, counted as nothing.*

And the Midrash on Ruth brings the unity into the person:

> כמו שהקדוש ברוך הוא נסתר בעולמו, כך הוא זה השם נסתר באדם (ZC Midrash Rut 424, line 3741)
> *As the Holy One is hidden in His world, so this Name is hidden in a person.*

**The shape of it (ours).** Put together, the two books teach unity in five layers. (1) He is beyond all: no image, no measure, no number, no thought grasps Him. (2) He is in all: no place empty, in every sefirah and every limb, unchanged. (3) All is as nothing before Him. (4) The sefirot and names exist so that we can know and call Him; the changes are in them, not in Him. (5) Unity is also a union to be made: "when the Community of Israel is in exile He is not called One" (ZC Ki Tisa 3, report zc-4), and a person's prayer, Torah and deeds join the Holy One and His Shechinah. Chassidus takes all five and lives mostly in the second, third and fifth.

## The Shechinah and the soul

The Tikkunim and the Zohar Chadash spend more pages on the Shechinah than on anything else. She is a mother bird driven from her nest, a bride sick with longing, a dove with nowhere to rest, a poor man's pledged cloak, a mother who took the lashes for her children and now cannot be found. She is also the soul in the body, the lamp, the point that holds the letters, the mirror in which each prophet sees according to his soul. Three teachings recur in both books:

- **She is with us and in us.** In exile She goes down with Her children; She is "the Name hidden in a person"; She rests on whoever does the mitzvot.
- **She needs us.** "There is no one who labors in Torah to raise the Shechinah from exile" (ZC Sifra Tanina 58). The masters of Torah "make her a dwelling" (TZ, report 1). Every sin strips Her; every mitzvah clothes Her.
- **The soul is Hers.** The soul's powers are the letters of the Name: fear, teshuvah, Torah, good deeds. Each night the soul is given to God as a pledge and returned in the morning. It can always rise again by teshuvah.

## States and meditations added

Twenty-two states and twenty-three techniques are proposed across the twelve reports (the full list is in the JSON below). The ones we would put first:

- **Waited for (Z-S13).** "He waits always: when will they return?" (ZC Noach 146, line 1007). For someone who believes they are finished.
- **The tear that goes up (Z-S20).** Tears of prayer and teshuvah, in which the heart, the will and the whole body go to God; the gates of tears are never locked.
- **The heart that whispers (Z-S16).** Prayer that has gone below the lips.
- **Held by His imprint (Z-S03)** and **never left (Z-S10).** For the one who feels abandoned.
- **The whole day held (Z-T12).** From "may my heart be steady, that I not forget You" in the morning to teshuvah before sleep.
- **Three steps of thought (Z-T16).** From a bad thought to a pure thought to the hidden thought.
- **Handing over the soul (Z-T15).** At nefilat apayim, giving the soul in love and receiving it back.
- **The stone and the sling (Z-T22).** Throwing the words of prayer past every name to the Hidden One.
- **The King's table (Z-T21).** Eating as before the King and giving the best to the poor.
- **Crown Him limb by limb (Z-T09)** with **Shema through the body (Z-T17)** and **the limb as gift (Z-T23).**

## Agreement and disagreement with Chabad

**Agreement.** Chabad Chassidus is built on these books, and our reading confirms how closely. Patach Eliyahu is quoted in the Tanya and throughout the Alter Rebbe's discourses. "No place empty of Him" is the Tikkunim's line. "He binds and unifies them", "wise but not with a known wisdom", "one but not in number", "the changes are in the names, not in Him", "within as without, unchanged", "as nothing before Him" all appear in Chabad teaching as the backbone of Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah. The Shechinah in exile and the service "for the sake of unifying the Holy One and His Shechinah" is the frame of Chassidic prayer. "The Holy One wants the heart" (ZC Midrash Rut 221) is a founding saying of the Baal Shem Tov's movement. The dwelling below like the dwelling above (ZC Terumah 36) is Tanya chapter 36.

**Where Chabad goes further.** (1) The Tikkunim say the world and the sefirot are for us, to know and call Him. Chabad adds that, from His side, the world's existence is itself a concealment and is truly nothing; the Zohar books say "as nothing" and stop there. (2) The Tikkunim place the work of raising the Shechinah mostly in Torah secrets, prayer with intention and mitzvot. Chabad, following the Baal Shem Tov, extends it to every plain act done with love; the Zohar Chadash hints at this ("He delights in his way, even in worldly matters", ZC Shir HaShirim 629) but does not build on it. (3) The Kav HaMiddah names a line drawn from Ein Sof and "the highest of high that has no measure"; Chabad, through the Arizal and the Rebbeim, makes this the doctrine of tzimtzum and of the Essence beyond both line and light.

**Where there is tension.** (1) The Zohar books sometimes make the value of a soul depend on descent, and say hard things about the nations, converts and women. Chabad's own teaching softened some of this and the guide cannot use it (D28). (2) The books include magic of names, amulets, incense as protection, calculations of the end, and demons. Chabad keeps the practical Kabbalah closed, and the guide keeps it out. (3) The Midrash on Eichah lets the Shechinah say She "cannot take the troubles from us". Chabad insists on bitachon, that the hidden good will be seen. Both are true, and the guide should hold both: honesty about pain, and trust.

## What the guide should take (ours)

1. **Unity lines for the four kinds of seekers.** For the thinker: "one, but not in number" and "no thought grasps You". For the one in the world: "no place empty of Him, like the soul in every limb". For the one in pain: "He is inside all worlds as outside, and does not change". For the one ashamed of himself: "as God is hidden in the world, so the Name is hidden in you".
2. **Teshuvah as the steady note.** Every reader who has fallen should meet: He waits (ZC 1007); a needle's eye and He opens great gates (ZC 1020); the accuser comes exactly when you want to return (ZC 857); the returner's return is private (ZC 1734); no love like His love for one who returns (ZC 2368); the gates of tears are open (ZC 3550).
3. **Words spoken in pain are not held against you.** Three separate places say it (ZC 967, 2147, 3337, 3342). This is precious for grief and anger at God.
4. **Practices for the body and the day,** not only the mind: the whole day held, the Shema through the limbs, the King's table, the night deposit, the midnight verses, lifting the eyes to the sky.
5. **Never drink.** The Zohar Chadash gives its own reason: "wine and the service of Heaven do not sit together" (ZC Noach 94).
6. **What to leave out:** letter permutations and names used for power, amulets and incense as protection, astrology beyond the teaching that Israel has no star, demons, end-time dates, and every claim that makes a soul less by birth, people, or sex.
