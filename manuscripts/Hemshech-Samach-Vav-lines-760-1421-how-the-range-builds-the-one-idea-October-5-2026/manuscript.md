## The shape of the range

The range covers the maamarim for the Shabbatot of Noach to Vayishlach 5666 (autumn 1905): the end of the opening of *Eleh Toldos Noach* (760-864), *Vayomer... Lech Lecha* (865-979), *Ki Yedativ* (980-1086), *Ve'Avraham Zaken* (1087-1156), *Vayiten Lecha* (1157-1282), *Vayetze* (1283-1401) and the first page of *Vayishlach* (1402-1421).

The Tanya shows the unity mostly as creation and nullification (F01, F02, F03). This range works on a different axis. Almost every step asks *what reaches the Essence* (F05) and answers: the mitzvah done as a bare act (F10), the essential will of the soul (F06, F08), and toil in Torah (F10, F11). The Essence is then revealed *below* in the future (F07). That is why F05 and F10 lead the count and F01 and F04 are nearly absent. Creation is set up only to be put in its place: it is the outer will, measured, "a mere radiance that does not touch Him". The unity here is not "the world is nothing before Him". It is that the Essence itself, which no thought grasps, is present where it is least expected: in a strap of leather, in the stubborn will of the simplest Jew, in the darkness of a Babylonian question.

## Eleh Toldos Noach (760-864): two wills, and why the mitzvah carries the Essence

The range opens inside the argument about the kav. If Ein Sof shone without the kav, the worlds would be wholly nullified, and they truly are, even now, because to Ein Sof the tzimtzum hides nothing: > כי לגבי אוא"ס אין הצמצום מעלים ומסתיר, ומאיר בגילוי לאחר הצמצום כמו קודם הצמצום (line 762)

Their being "something" at all is made by the kav, the line of light: > אבל כאשר מאיר האור ע"י הקו, לא האוא"ס עצמו כמו שהוא, ה"ה נעשים בבחי' מציאות (line 762)

This is the higher and lower unity (F02) put in the language of the kav. The world's somethingness is a product of the measured channel, not a fact before Him. The screens (773, 782) hide the light, and all hiding follows the "measuring line", so that a world can exist (F03 stated): > כמו שהוא, לא הי' אפשר להיות התהוות העולמות (line 786)

The measure is rooted in the Essence's own will. Above the line stands the one who drew it and "can change it entirely" (788, the scribe and the cubit): > והג', הוא בחי' העצמי שלמעלה מקו המדה, שממנו נמשך קו המדידה שיהי' באופן זה, ובכחו לשנות שיהי' באופן אחר לגמרי (line 788)

Then the maamar's thesis (794-800). The will to create, though it is in the Essence before the tzimtzum, is the Essence's *outer* will, measured in potential. It is the root of above and below, of space and time, and of concealment. The will of the mitzvot is its inner will: > בחי' הרצון דמצות הוא בחי' פנימיות ועצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מהרצון דעולמות (line 795)

So a mitzvah reveals the light "below just as above" (800). A mitzvah has exact measures (four parshiyot, seven handbreadths). These do not limit the light. They only fix *when* the essential light comes, and that is a will with no reason (802, 816-825): > אבל מה שנמשך בזה הוא בחי' אור העצמי כמו שהוא בלי מדה וגבול (line 825)

The tefillin draw "new mochin", and the Havayah of the mitzvot is "the name of the essence" (827). The yud of that name is a *tzimtzum* of a new kind: not a veil over the light, but the Essence, which heaven cannot hold, contracting itself to be present below as it is: > שבחי' העצמות, מה שהשמים ושמי השמים לא יכלכלוך, צמצם א"ע כבי' להיות בקדה"ק כמו שהוא ממש (line 827)

This is why one says "for the sake of the union" on mitzvot (836). In creation sovev gave power only in hiding. Through mitzvot sovev is revealed within memale, the union of "blessing and holiness" (F04 + F09 + F10). The Essence is drawn only by the physical act (838, 847). Spiritual mitzvot carry reason. The Patriarchs kept the Torah spiritually and still did not know "My name Havayah". Hence the yoke of heaven, and the love "with all your might" that comes "from the essence of the soul drawn in essence to the Essence" (859), are what bring the revelation *below*; intent-laden service reveals it mainly above: > ונמשך למטה ממש כמו שהוא (line 861)

## Lech Lecha (865-979): what thought cannot grasp, the heart's will does

"No thought grasps You at all, but He is grasped by the will of the heart" (867). The Rashab sharpens the via negativa. Positive knowledge applies to the radiance in worlds. Negative knowledge applies to the Ein Sof that relates to worlds. Of the Essence, not even negation says anything: > דשום השגה, אפי' השגת השלילה, שזהו המדרי' היותר נעלית שבהשגה, אינו שייך באוא"ס (line 876)

The whole order of prayer (Pesukei deZimra, the Shema blessings, Shema, with its higher unity 'all before Him is as nothing') is still the outer heart (884, 897). What grasps the Essence is the inner heart, the bond that comes from the yechidah, as a son's bond with his father comes from being drawn from the father's essence (898-906): > שהן בבחי' התקשרות עצמיות לאלקות (line 906)

The heart can hold this, though it stands lower than the brain, because the middot are rooted in Atik (907-915). After re'uta deliba, mitzvot are pure yoke, and the yoke is a greater bitul than even the love of "with all your might": the servant's advantage over the son (917, F08). Torah is the vessel, "the first garment of the Emanator's Essence" (935), and the act is set apart even from beriah: > להורות על הפלאת ערך העשיה שמובדל בערך למעלה מבחי' בראתיו (line 935)

The second half turns to will and delight (936-969). Each seems innermost; each can clothe the other. The resolution is an F09 statement in the soul: > דשניהם אמת, כי התענוג והרצון א' הם, דשניהם בחי' עצם (line 959)

In their essence, simple will and simple delight do not clothe one another (960). Hence there is delight without will ('only in your fathers did Hashem delight', before the Torah) and will without delight (the chukim). The editor's note (972) records the Rebbe's view that God's choice of Israel is above both.

## Ki Yedativ (980-1086): the essential will of the Jew, and the hidden delight in the mitzvah

If both are essence, how can there be two things in the essence at all (989)? The answer takes the whole maamar. Will is stronger: it turns pain to delight, and nothing stands against it. The essential will of a Jew never changes: > וכמו הרצון העצמי שיש בכאו"א מישראל, לא ישונה כלל (line 996)

Even those "lost in the land of Ashur", sunk in pleasures, will come at the great shofar. Martyrdom does not come from the love of delight, which Rabbi Akiva had and still said "when will it come to my hand". It comes from the stubborn simple will: > שזהו בחי' רצון פשוט ועצמי שיש בכאו"א מישראל שאינו יכול להיות נפרד מאלקות, מפני שהן עצם א' (line 1005)

Yet delight is more inward (1019). Within martyrdom there is a hidden delight: "for the sake of the unity of Hashem it is worth it" (1026). Will grasps the essence of what it wants; delight grasps the outside. So too above: > ובדוגמא כזאת הוא ג"כ למעלה באהבת הקב"ה לכנס"י, שרוצים בנש"י כבי' בעצם מהותם דוקא (line 1040)

> דבישראל מה שנמצא רע ח"ו ה"ז דבר נוסף על העצם, אבל העצם הוא טוב (line 1046)

In prayer, will from depth after depth reaches Him and not His attributes (1048). "Whom have I in heaven" excludes everything besides Hashem alone, Gan Eden included (1056). The mitzvah's will is the essential will, compounded with nothing. Hidden in it is the essential delight, "pleasure before Me", which will be revealed in the future: > ולעתיד יתגלה בחי' התענוג העצמי שברצון דמעהמ"צ (line 1073)

The Targum ("known") and Rashi ("cherished") name the two: essential will, revealed now, and essential delight, drawn now and revealed later (1085).

## Ve'Avraham Zaken (1087-1156): toil reaches what is hidden even to itself

"Blessed him with everything" names no particular blessing, because Avraham reached a level with no particulars. The soul-model is the core of this maamar and is mostly structural (1101-1149). Wisdom that comes as direct light is limited by its source. The intellective power is already "something". The essential hyle of intellect is not even a source: things are *found* from it, as springs from the deep. The flint (in Yiddish, "kremen") holds no fire at all and yields fire without end, but only when struck: > והוא בבחי' העלם גם לעצמו (line 1125)

> אבל לא שכח השכל העצמי הוא מקור לכח המשכיל, מאחר שאינו בבחי' המשכה כלל (line 1148)

Toil in Torah is the striking. It reaches wisdom as it is in the Essence (1151-1152, "returns to its origin"). Avraham reached it through his trials. The line then turns to the last exile: > ועי"ז דוקא יהי' גילוי העצמות לעתיד (line 1155)

## Vayiten Lecha (1157-1282): the question from the other side, and the darkness of Babylon

Creation itself was for the sake of Torah (1158). Why then bless Yaakov with Torah? Torah as the Tree of Life needs no toil (1168). Toil came with Moshe's striking the rock and with the golden calf, as a punishment (the second tablets, 1179). The contemplation of "I have not changed" should make one expire, since the whole chain is "a mere radiance that does not touch Him" (1192, F05 + F02): > והיינו לפי שכלליות ההשתל' הוא מבחי' הארה בעלמא שאינו נוגע כלל (line 1192)

We do not expire, because "your iniquities separate". So Torah, "strength", gives the divine soul peace above (Or Ein Sof in it, "bound and united in real bitul") and peace below (1200). The added Torah after the sin (particulars, halachot, rabbinic fences) comes from hidden wisdom and from ahavah rabbah (1214-1216). By refinement one rises higher (1229). The Babylonian Talmud, "He sat me in darkness", hides every understanding under questions. Every question "comes from the side of kelipah" (1247), and working through them reaches past the essential hiddenness of wisdom to Torah as it is in His Essence: > והיינו בח" התורה כמו שהיא בבחי' אמיתית עצמותו ית' (line 1245)

> ובחי' אלקים הוא בחי" העלם העצמי ממש (line 1245)

The Jerusalem Talmud has more revealed light; the Babylonian reaches higher (1254). The one who refines must clothe himself in what he refines, hence Rabbi Zeira's hundred fasts (1257). Yaakov's blessing, "may Elokim give you", is the essential hiddenness as the power to refine Esav (1278). The Machar Chodesh note (1282) adds that this power reaches even the sparks in the three impure kelipot.

## Vayetze and Vayishlach (1283-1421): simple delight, seeing the Essence, and the negative commandments

If Eden comes through physical mitzvot, which every Jew has, how does exactness in wisdom reach it (1286-1288)? Because "the inwardness of Abba is the inwardness of Atik", not a receiving from it (1295, 1303). Hidden wisdom is the one intermediary that holds both sides (1303). So Israel, rooted in Chochmah, are rooted in the Essence, and Torah and the Holy One are "truly one": > דאורייתא וקוב'"ה כולא חד'*, חד ממש (line 1313)

The future delight is "simple song" (1335-1365). Delight compounded with understanding, even the wordless song of the seeing of Chochmah, is contracted, "not the essence itself" (1352). At the Sea the Essence was revealed to the yechidah and each pointed, "this is my God" (1354). In the future: > שבא מן בחי' ההעלם דתענוג העצמי הפשוט לגילוי גמור במורגש בלי שינוי המהות כלל (line 1382)

Above even that is the second level: the essential sha'ashu'im that is never felt, reached only by refinement (1391). Yaakov went out "from Be'er Sheva", the depth of the oath, which comes from the Essence and is the source of the power to refine (1393). The range ends early in Vayishlach, on a quiet point. The ayin of inner Keter is grasped by no knowing. As it comes into Chochmah, it is held at least through the *negative* commandments, whose root is higher: > דשרש מליית למעלה משרש מ"ע (line 1413)

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **Two wills, not one creation.** In the Tanya, creation from nothing is itself the unity (SH 1-7). Here the will to create is the Essence's *outer* will. Even before the tzimtzum it is the root of measure, of space and time, and of hiding (795, 836). Only the will of the mitzvot is the inner Essence. The Tanya grounds the mitzvah in the unity as well (ch. 35-41, 'Torah and the Holy One are one'), but it does not set the two wills against each other.
- **The world's "somethingness" is made by the kav** (762). The Tanya puts it in Elokim's concealment (SH 4-6). The Rashab puts it in the channel: had Ein Sof shone without the kav, the worlds would be nullified. He adds that they truly are, even now.
- **A second tzimtzum** (827): the Essence contracting *itself* into the Holy of Holies, which is presence, not veil. In the Tanya, tzimtzum is concealment from the lower ones (SH 7) or an act of love (ch. 49), not the Essence's own descent.
- **Grasping the Essence by the heart's will.** The Tanya says the Essence is known "only by faith, above intellect" (SH 7; line 834 in the Tanya index). The Rashab says no knowing, not even negation, reaches it (876), and names re'uta deliba, the essential will of the yechidah, as what does (897-915). The two are close, but the faculty is named differently, and the Rashab makes the will active and "running" (1037).
- **The yoke above love.** Tanya ch. 41 makes the yoke the foundation. Here the yoke is a *higher* bitul than the love "with all your might", the servant above the son (917), and it is what draws the Essence *below* (859).
- **Darkness turned to light inside the intellect.** F11 in the Tanya is about the animal soul and the kelipot. Here it is about questions in study (1242-1257): the question "comes from kelipah", and refining it reaches higher than the Torah of the Land of Israel. The Tanya's IH 26 treats the Tree of Knowledge and the need to refine; the claim that toil, a punishment, is the higher path is the Rashab's.
- **Israel's essence is good** (1046): evil is "an addition to the essence". The Tanya says the same in the image of the hidden love (ch. 18-19). The Rashab extends it: the nations will turn to Him, yet the essential point of will "is in Israel alone" (996).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **The footnotes.** 383 of 662 lines are the editor's apparatus. 356 of them are pure references and are marked *none*. Twenty-seven that carry substance (parallel wording, the Rebbe's notes) are marked structural or, twice, stated (972 on the choice of Israel, 1282 on refining the three kelipot).
- **The soul-psychology runs.** Much of Ki Yedativ (943-958, 983-991) and Ve'Avraham Zaken (1101-1149) analyzes will, delight, invention, analysis and the hyle of intellect. These lines are structural, usually with no face. The tie comes when the analysis is applied (959, 1026, 1148, 1151), and those lines are marked stated.
- **Two readings only.** The flint as a model of creation from nothing (1119.2) and pilpul's "hiding for revelation" as F03 (1242.1). The text uses these for the soul and for study; the cosmic link is ours.
- **F01 and F04 are nearly absent** (seven lines each). Continuous creation appears only in contemplation (878, 884), and sovev/memale only in the "blessing and holiness" union (836). This range is not about how the world stands. It is about what in the world reaches the Essence.
- **OCR damage.** Several running sentences are broken across page lines and footnotes (e.g. 793-800, 1303-1313). Where a line is a fragment, it is indexed with the sentence it completes. Quotes keep the OCR's errors (e.g. the stray "*" and "י" markers).

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Will or delight: which is higher?** The maamar goes both ways (944 against 951; 1007 against 1019). It concludes that delight is more inward and will more forceful. The editor's footnote (1024-1025) records the Rebbe saying that the conclusion is that delight is highest. In a handwritten reply he qualified that this is "not as written here", and that Sefer Yetzirah means the King's delight in Himself, which will does not reach: > הרי המסקנא היא שכח התענוג הוא הכי נעלה (line 1024)
- **Prophecy or the mind's eye?** Line 1362 says Moshe saw the Essence with the eye and that "you will see My back" is only the mind's seeing. The Rebbe's footnote (1367) says this needs study. It sets the line against the Tanya (Iggeret HaKodesh 19), which says the mind's eye sees higher than prophecy: > דבעין השכל רואין במדרי' גבוהות יותר מבעין הנבואה, וכמו שהאריך באגה"ק סי"ט, וכאן כ' להיפך (line 1367)

His suggested reconciliation (1368) is that the sage sees higher *levels* while the prophet sees *the essence* of his level. The note still ends "needs study". - **The inwardness of Abba.** Line 1295 says the inwardness of Abba *is* the inwardness of Atik, essential delight. The Rebbe's note (1298-1299) cites places that call it Atik's outwardness. Following other maamarim, he suggests it holds only felt delight and only in the future. Kept as a live qualification. - **Is toil a punishment or the higher path?** Both are stated (1168, 1254). The Rashab does not deny that toil began as a punishment. He argues that it is still the higher path. The tension is kept.
