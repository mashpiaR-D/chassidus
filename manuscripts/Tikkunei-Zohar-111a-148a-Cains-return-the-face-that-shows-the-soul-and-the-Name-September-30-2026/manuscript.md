## What this range is

The last third of the book. Tikkun 69 ends on Cain: his offering of leftovers, his late teshuvah, and why God would still have taken him back. From there it turns to the breaths (hevel) of prayer, Moshe spread through every generation, and the most careful lines in the whole book on the Cause of causes who has no image and no partner. Tikkun 70 is the physiognomy tikkun. It reads the hair, forehead, eyes, nose, lips, hands and lines of a face as letters of the Name, and it keeps saying the same thing underneath: the Name is in every limb, He does not change, and a face is made by deeds. It closes with the soul after death, a story of a mother and a dove, and a newborn who keeps its eyes shut because it has just seen too much. The printer's additions read creation (בראשית) again for sacrifice, the organs of the body, the two kinds of fear and love, the Shechinah lying in exile with nowhere to rest, and the bride adorned for her wedding.

## The unity of G-d

**The second "no place empty of Him."** Tikkun 57 gave the famous line (part 4, report 4, line 2658). Tikkun 70 gives it again, now inside the body, when it reaches the hair and limbs of the upper form:

> לית אתר פנוי מניה כנשמתא דאשתכחת בכל אבר ואבר דגופא (TZ 122b:5, line 3043)
> *There is no place empty of Him, like the soul that is found in every single limb of the body.*

The same line says He is YHVH in every limb, "and He is between every limb and limb". The next line gives the reason He is shown in limbs at all:

> וינדע למקרי ליה בכל אבר כדקא יאות ליה (TZ 122b:6, line 3044)
> *So that a person will know how to call Him in every limb as is fitting.*

So the body picture is not a claim about His shape. It is a map for the one who prays.

**The changes are in the names, not in Him.** This is said four times in the range, each time in plain words:

> דשנויין בשמהן אינון ולאו ביה (TZ 122b:7, line 3045)
> *The changes are in the names, not in Him.*

> אבל ביה לית שנויא כלל (TZ 130b:1, line 3142)
> *But in Him there is no change at all.* (The changes are "in the vessels of the body".)

And with the sefirot, in the line Chassidus lives by:

> ואיהו מלבר דכל ספירה וספירה, ואיהו בין כל ספירה וספירה, לא אשתני בכל אתר (TZ 127a:6, line 3104)
> *He is outside every sefirah, and He is between every sefirah and sefirah; He does not change in any place.* (The same line says first that He is inside each one.)

Inside, outside, between, unchanged. This is the Tikkunim's own form of "He fills all worlds and surrounds all worlds" (F02).

**No image, no partner, no number.** Tikkun 69 has the book's sharpest negative theology:

> דבעלת העלות לית ביה גוון ולא צורה ולא דיוקנא ולא שותפו (TZ 115b:2, line 2950)
> *In the Cause of causes there is no color, no form, no likeness and no partner.*

> ובאתר דעין לא שליט מאן יכיל למעבד דמיון (same line)
> *And where the eye has no reach, who can make a likeness?*

He is the life of life, "eye cannot see Him and thought cannot hold Him" (עין לא תשורהו ומחשבה לא תכילהו, line 2947), and "where He dwells there is no death" (ובאתר דאיהו שריא לית מיתה תמן, same line). The sefirot create in partnership with each other, but He creates alone:

> הוא ברא כלא ולית בורא עליה (TZ 116a:3, line 2955)
> *He created everything, and there is no creator over Him.*

> וכי אית דיכיל למברי אפילו יתוש זעיר אלא הוא (same line)
> *Is there anyone who can create even a tiny gnat, except Him?*

And the warning after it: דלאו כל מוחא יכיל למסבל האי, "not every mind can bear this" (line 2957). The three yuds of one of His names "testify that there is nothing above Him, nothing below Him and not in the middle"

> דלית לעילא מניה ולית לתתא מניה ולאו באמצעיתא (TZ 120a:7, line 3013)

"and by this name He is known without number" (בהאי שמא אשתמודע הוא בלא חושבן, same line).

**The printer's guard.** A Hebrew gloss in Tikkun 70 (lines 3027-3028) stops the reader before the face-reading starts: none of this gives God a body. "He has no known name, but He fills all names":

> ולית ליה שם ידיע, אבל הוא ממלא כל שמהן (TZ 121b:1, line 3028)

**All lights go dark before Him.** Even Keter, the first light, is black before the Cause of causes:

> דכלהו נהורין אתחשכאן קמיה (TZ 135b:1, line 3209)
> *All the lights grow dark before Him.*

> וכלהו שמהן אינון מרכבה דיליה, ולית דרכיב עליה (TZ 135b:3, line 3211)
> *All the names are His chariot, and nothing rides on Him.*

He needs no blessing, since "there is none above Him to give to Him" (ולא צריך איהו ברכאן מאחרא, דלית עליה מאן דאשפע ליה, line 3158).

**One, with nothing cut.** Moshe's Shema: דהא כלא ביחודא חדא לית תמן קצוץ ופרוד, "for all is in one unity, there is no cutting and no separation there" (line 3008). The warning from the other side: "whoever takes Malchut without the nine sefirot cuts the shoots, and whoever takes the nine without Malchut denies the root" (וכל מאן דנטיל תשע ספירן בלא מלכות איהו כופר בעיקר, line 2980). The world below cannot be left out of the One.

**He does not live in division.** The additions put it in one line: when the four elements of a person are torn apart by sin, the Name leaves, "for YHVH does not dwell in separation" (דיהו"ה לא שריא בפרודא, line 3251). An offering brings the parts close again, and at once He comes down.

## States of consciousness

**Called, and answered, in every place (ties S62 Only Him, S63 The world held, S33 Before Whom).**

> בכל אתר דקרי ליה בר נש עני ליה (TZ 130b:4, line 3145)
> *In every place a person calls Him, He answers him.*

The condition that follows: unless that place in him "is flawed by sin". From inside: there is no room where calling is useless, only rooms I have to clean first. Entry is the practice in the next section, Z-T09.

**The one who returns is never left (proposed Z-S10, "never left: the returner's Presence").**

> זכאין אינון מארי דתיובתא (TZ 116a:1, line 2953)
> *Fortunate are the masters of return.*

The upper Shechinah comes down on them "every day, every hour, every moment", and does not leave them anywhere. The line contrasts this with the extra soul that others receive only on Shabbat. After Adam's first thought of teshuvah, it "stood with him to shield him and cover him, for he was naked" (line 2951). From inside: the one who has come back does not wait for Shabbat or a holy hour to be held; the holding is constant. Ties to S77 The lower return and S84 Trust. It adds a steady state, not a peak: the returner is covered all the time.

**Seeing without being seen (ours, from line 2961; ties F06, the soul as part of the One).** "In His image" is read as: as He sees and is not seen, so the soul; as He has no form, so the soul; as He is one with no partner and no number, so the soul (כגוונא דאיהו רואה ואינו נראה דיהא איהו הכי, line 2961). From inside: the one who is looking cannot be found among the things looked at. We do not make this a new state. It is the Tikkunim's root for S62 (Only Him) turned inward, and the guide can use it as a pointer.

**Light known out of darkness (ties S68 The hiding seen as His).** מגו חשוכא אשתמודע נהורא, "from within the darkness the light is known" (line 3055). And: "He knows what is in the dark, because darkness is a garment of light" (בגין דחשך איהו לבושא דאור, line 3106).

**The heart that waits for the right voice, again (Z-S09 confirmed).** In the additions the Shechinah is a king's daughter "sick from love of her husband" (line 3306). She falls into bed when he goes, and she asks the Shabbat's extra soul to hold her up: "sustain me with raisin cakes". The extra soul comes down each Friday night "to support the lower Shechinah in exile" (בלילי שבת נשמה יתירה קא נחתא לסמכא שכינתא תתאה בגלותא, line 3305). At the end of Shabbat it leaves, and she says "I looked for him and did not find him". Then comes the vow: at the last redemption "I will hold him and not let go" (lines 3308-3309). Ties to S76 Shabbos and S13 The unnamed longing.

**Master of yourself, master of your enemies (ties S30 Holding back).** "Fortunate the soul that rules the body and rides it" (line 3181), דכד בר נש איהו שליט על יצריה, הכי איהו שליט על כל שנאוי, "when a person rules his inclination, so he rules all his enemies" (same line).

## Meditation and practice

**1. Crown Him in every limb, and search the rooms (proposed technique Z-T09, "crown Him limb by limb").** This is the practical center of Tikkun 70. In every limb a person must make Him king, "and prepare a clean and pure place for Him to dwell there":

> ולתקנא ליה אתר דכיא ונקייא לשריא ליה תמן (TZ 130b:2, line 3143)

The same line says how: clear out of each limb every bad thought and fancy, and "burn them" with the mitzvot, for each mitzvah is a lamp, searching all the chambers of the belly (Proverbs 20:27). Then the reward line:

> כאלו אמליך ליה על כל עלמא (TZ 130b:5, line 3146)
> *(One who makes Him king over every limb) it is as if he made Him king over the whole world.*

It must not be done for a reward (same line). Two folios later the goal is stated in the Tikkun 57 words, now as a rule for the body:

> דלא יהא ביה אבר פנוי מניה (TZ 132a:4, line 3165)
> *So that there be no limb in him empty of Him.*

The line adds that a limb left out brings the soul back in gilgul until it is whole. **How (ours):** sit, and go through the body slowly: eyes, ears, mouth, hands, heart, feet. At each one say inwardly "You are King here." Notice any thought that lives there and does not belong with Him. Name the act of this day that lights that limb: a kind look, a true word, a coin given. End: "no limb empty of You." Ties to technique 7 (detail by detail), 21 (the act that closes) and 24 (the evening look-back). It is the bodily form of S62.

**2. Awe and love live in the brain and heart.** The mitzvot are sorted by limb (feet walk to the synagogue, hands give charity, mouth learns and prays), "but the mitzvah of awe and love hangs on the brain and heart" (אלא פקודא דיראה ואהבה אלו תליין במוחא ולבא, line 3161). Speech that goes out without love and awe has a "crooked mouth", and the Name is not in it (line 3166). Ties to technique 16.

**3. Love Him whether He pays or not.** The additions set two fears and two loves side by side. One fears Him so that his children live or so that he will not go poor, and "tests" Him: I will do this mitzvah and see "is the Lord among us or not" (line 3272). That is the mixed multitude's fear. The real one:

> בין דיהיב ליה אגרא בטיבו בין דלא יהיב ליה (TZ 141a:4, line 3273)
> *(Love and awe of the Holy One whole in every mitzvah he does) whether He gives him reward in good or does not.*

And love Him with what you hold dearest: if money is dearer than life, "with all your might"; if life is dearer, "with all your soul" (line 3274). Ties to technique 16, S43, and the Tanya's love that depends on nothing (ch. 41-44). Tikkun 70 draws the same line: mitzvot done for reward are "handmaids" and cannot shield the soul (line 3297).

**4. Mouth and heart equal.** The one who is "one thing in the heart and another in the mouth" is of the tree of knowledge; one from the tree of life is the same inside as outside, פימא ולבא שוים, "mouth and heart equal" (line 3120).

**5. Keep your ears open below.** If a person does not hear the poor or the Torah, "so are the gates of hearing closed above to them" (הכי אינון אטימין תרעין דשמיעה לעילא לגביהו, line 3139). Ties to technique 21; S54 Compassion.

**6. Know how to pray in each of His ways.**

> זכאה איהו מאן דידע לצלאה לקודשא בריך הוא בכל תקונין דיליה כדקא יאות (TZ 123b:1, line 3052)
> *Fortunate is one who knows how to pray to the Holy One in all His tikkunim as is fitting,* "and to ask of Him in each rung".

The next line gives each kind of person a sefirah, the wise in Wisdom and the kind in Kindness, and then:

> ומארי יחודא כלהו רשימין בכתר עלאה דאיהו מחשבה (TZ 123b:2, line 3053)
> *And the masters of unity are all engraved in the supreme Crown, which is thought.*

There, "one must raise the unity in hidden thought". In every one of these, one must praise the Cause of all causes, דאיהו מלגאו כנשמתא בגופא, "who is within, like a soul in a body" (same line). The breaths of prayer also need thought: ואם לא סלקין במחשבה, "if they are not raised in thought", they are not looked at (line 2979). Ties to technique 3 and Z-T07.

**7. Pray for straight words.** Elijah, before speaking: יהא רעוא דילך דאימא מלין בארח מישור, "may it be Your will that I say words in a straight way" (line 3195). Rabbi Shimon lifts his hands: דלא איעול בכסופא קדמך, "that I not come in shame before You" (line 3025). This confirms Z-T08.

**8. Light a lamp for Her at night (proposed technique Z-T10, "a lamp for the Shechinah in the dark").** The Shechinah in exile is the poor man's pledged cloak. "With what shall she lie down" in the night of exile, when the sun does not shine for her (lines 3288-3289)?

> צריכין ישראל לאנהרא לה בגלותא באור ונר, דאיהו תורה ומצוה (TZ 142b:1, line 3289)
> *Israel must light her way in exile with light and lamp, that is, Torah and mitzvah.*

The tefillin boxes are her bed and the tzitzit is her cover (same line); ובגין פקודין אלין שריא שכינתא על בר נש, "because of these mitzvot the Shechinah rests on a person" (line 3290). **How (ours):** in a dark hour, instead of asking for light for yourself, do one act of Torah or kindness "for Her," so the Presence has somewhere to rest tonight. Ties to Z-T03 (prayer of the Shechinah) and S73 Exile inside. Honoring father and mother is read the same way: "whoever does a mitzvah to lift her from exile, it is as if he honored the Holy One" (כל מאן דעביד מצוה לאקמא לה מן גלותא כאלו אוקיר לקודשא בריך הוא, line 3340).

**9. The best, not the leftovers.** Cain brought what was left; Abel brought the best (line 2920). The Tikkunim make it a rule for charity: whoever eats the good and gives the poor what is left over gives it "to the dog" (line 2934). If you raise the poor, "I will carry your burden" (line 2989).

**10. Read the inside, not the straw.** Fools look only at the straw of Torah; the wise "throw away the straw and chaff outside and eat the wheat of Torah that is inside" (זרקין תבנא ומוץ דלבר ואכלין חטה דאורייתא דאיהי מלגאו, line 2936). And: אל תסתכל בקנקן אלא במה דאית ביה, "do not look at the jug but at what is in it" (line 2976). Ties to technique 11 (seeing the garment).

## The big questions

**Will He take back the one who fell late?** Cain came to teshuvah only at the end, when he had no strength left (line 2920). Still, God's mercy is on all His works and He "does not want the death of the dead" (line 2923). Cain did repent: וכי לחייבא קביל קודשא בריך הוא בתשובה, על אחת כמה וכמה לצדיקייא, "if the Holy One accepts the wicked in teshuvah, how much more the righteous" (line 2993). A sage who sinned by day surely repented by night (line 3280). The door does not close.

**Does my face tell my fate?** Tikkun 70 reads faces for gilgul, sin and temperament, and it says marks of past sin can move once a person returns (ואם חזר בתיובתא, line 3084). Then it gives the key to the whole tikkun:

> עקרא דשרטוטין וציורין וגוונין איהו עובדא (TZ 133b:5, line 3187)
> *The essence of the lines, forms and colors is the deed.*

Our note: the guide keeps the conclusion (a face is made by deeds and can change) and leaves the reading of faces out. The Tikkunim themselves forbid seeking omens and allow only taking a hint toward return (lines 3068-3069).

**Why be human and not an angel?** Angels "serve by compulsion; they have no mixing of flesh and evil inclination" (דאינון פלחין בהכרח ולית לון ערובא דבשרא ויצרא בישא, line 3228). So "let us make man": one who serves while he could do otherwise. Our reading: the struggle is the point, not a flaw. It ties to Tanya ch. 27 on the delight above when the other side is pushed back.

**What happens to the soul after death, and in sleep?** Torah is the light and a mitzvah the lamp that walk before the soul there (line 3151). In sleep the soul goes up and is held as a pledge until the day's debts are paid, then returned before sunrise (lines 3292-3293). The Shechinah testifies to hidden sins "for the Shechinah is over his head" (בגין דשכינתא על רישיה, line 3294). The soul is sold into the body like a handmaid; one who toiled in Torah leaves it "free of the angel of death" (תיפוק בר חורין ממלאך המות, line 3344). A newborn keeps its eyes shut because in the womb it saw lights and learned Torah (lines 3221-3223).

**Why do I have to learn from someone else?** Had Israel merited, Torah would have come down from heaven like manna, ולא הוו צריכין לאלפא חד לחבריה, "and they would not need to teach one another" (line 3267). Our reading: the toil of learning from another person is the form Torah takes in exile, and it promises an end to it.

**What does a judge do?** One who judges a true judgment "is as if he brought the Shechinah and Israel out of exile" (כאלו הוא אפיק לשכינתא ולישראל מגלותא, line 3298). Justice is itself a redemption.

**Is poverty in Torah the real poverty?** ולית עניותא קמי קודשא בריך הוא כעניותא דאורייתא, "there is no poverty before the Holy One like poverty in Torah" (line 2996). The same line: "song in the house, ruin in the house", unless it is "rejoice before the Lord", like David's melody. Ties to technique 19.

**Is Moshe gone?** ואתפשטותיה דמשה בכל דרא ודרא ובכל צדיק וצדיק, "Moshe's extension is in every generation and in every righteous person" (line 2918), and "in every righteous and wise person who toils in Torah, up to six hundred thousand" (line 2906). The line says he is found only where there is Torah.

## Chassidus and the Tikkunim here (ours)

- **The second leit atar panui (3043) and "the Name in every limb" (3044, 3143, 3165)** are the body form of Chabad's unity. Chassidus turns "no place empty" into a meditation on space. Here it is a meditation on the body, and a work: to make no limb empty of Him. The guide should keep both.
- **"Inside, outside, between, unchanged" (3104) and "changes in the names, not in Him" (3045, 3142)** are the Tikkunim's form of memale and sovev and of "I the Lord have not changed" (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; Tanya ch. 20-21). They agree fully.
- **"Moshe's extension in every generation" (2918)** is the source the Alter Rebbe cites (Tanya ch. 42, 44) for the rebbe's role in the soul. This is agreement.
- **Where they differ:** the physiognomy and the planet readings (Saturn-born are sad, lines 3062-3071) belong to a world Chassidus did not build its practice on. Chabad keeps the Tikkunim's conclusion, that deeds make the person (3187), and its trust in providence, not the reading of lines on the hand.
- **The new ideas for the guide:** Z-S10 (never left: the returner's Presence), Z-T09 (crown Him limb by limb), Z-T10 (a lamp for the Shechinah in the dark); Z-S09 and Z-T08 confirmed.
