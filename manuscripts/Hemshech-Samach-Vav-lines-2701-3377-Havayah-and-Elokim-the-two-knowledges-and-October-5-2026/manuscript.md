## The shape of the range

Lines 1422-2062 asked how a person reaches the Essence. This range asks what stands between a person and the Essence, and whether it truly stands between. Its answer is built on two Names. Havayah is the source of all being. Elokim is the "shield and sheath" that hides Havayah so that a separate world can exist. The range then asks what the hiding really is, and gives three answers that deepen one another:

1. From the giver's side the hiding is real only toward us. Elokim, the limiting power, is itself the Essence, and "essence does not hide essence" (2891).
2. From our side the hiding is a task. The soul came down "to serve Elokim", to repair the hiding, until the world itself shows Godliness more plainly than its absence would (2938).
3. At the deepest level the hiding is higher than the revealing. The root of the "something" and of every concealment is the Essence's own hiddenness, and in the future "the hiding itself will shine" (2947).

So F03 (the hiding) comes back in force. It was rare in the last range, with 5 tags. Here it has 55. F10 (Torah and mitzvah) leads the count, because the second half of the range asks which service reaches which level: Torah, prayer, charity, the positive commandments, the prohibitions. F06 (the soul) is high because the range keeps asking where souls are rooted: in Atzilus, in the thought of Adam Kadmon, above the Torah.

The range's plainest statement of the unity is a single clause:

## Kedoshim Tihyu, second half (2701-2741): Havayah, the Name of bringing into being

The maamar reads the four letters of Havayah as the order in which light reaches a receiver. It uses the parable of a great teacher and a small student. The yud is the first and total contraction of the Essence's light down to a point (2701, 2709):

That point is "Bereishis is also a saying", one general saying that holds everything to be drawn down over six thousand years (2709.2). The heh spreads the details, and the vav draws them down in brief. Only the final heh makes a real something from nothing, because it is (2710):

> שהוא בחי' כח הפועל בנפעל ממש (line 2710)

"the power of the Maker in the made itself." That is F01 in the Alter Rebbe's own words (Shaar HaYichud 2).

Two conclusions follow. First, all creatures can praise Havayah. The life clothed in them is Elokim, but the source of their being is Havayah (2720, 2725). Second, above the Havayah of Atzilus there is a Havayah in the will of Adam Kadmon, and above that a "decided will" hidden in Ein Sof before the tzimtzum:

> דאוא"ס כמו שהוא לפני הצמצום אינו בגדר העולמות כלל (line 2727)

"Be holy, for I am holy" then gets a limit. Souls rise through the mitzvot, above all through the prohibitions, to the holiness of Atzilus, which is "like the Above". But "My holiness is above yours" (2736-2737, F05).

## Usfartem Lachem (2742-2851): hidden sefiros, souls of Atzilus, the Omer

The maamar asks what the ten sefiros of Adam Kadmon are. They are not distinct existences, so all stand "in one glance" (2747). The Rashab explains this from the soul. The hidden powers of wisdom and understanding are not intellect but a feeling of the divine, and they are the two unities of Shema (2756):

> כח החכמה הוא שרואה ביטול כל דבר והתכללותו בבחי' אין (line 2756)

"The power of wisdom sees the nullity of each thing and its inclusion in the 'nothing'." Hidden understanding feels how the "nothing" is in each thing. These are "the Lord is One" from above down and from below up (F02). Even the soul's seeing of "all before Him is as nothing" is still a defined seeing (2761). The Rashbi's seeing was not his students'.

Then comes the distinction that runs through the whole range. The revealed world is where "the creature appears as a separate something". The hidden world, Atzilus, is the higher unity (2774.2):

> דשם הכל בבחי' ביטול כנוני ימאי שא"א להיות נפרד (line 2774)

"There all is nullified like fish of the sea, which cannot be separated." Thought letters are one with the soul, and speech letters are separate from it. That is Atzilus against the lower worlds (2785). Atzilus, even its vessels, is "Godliness itself", in utter union (2805):

> אבל אצילות הוא בחי' אלקות ממש, גם בחי' הכלים, והן בבחי' יחוד בתכלית (line 2805)

Souls of Atzilus carry this down with them. The world does not hide from them at all, and so Yosef could work over his accounts and stay "a real chariot" in the very act (2807, 2810, F06 + F08 + F07). For such souls "all their physical matters are Godliness itself" (2812). Counting the Omer draws this light to souls of the lower worlds. It refines the animal soul's traits (2823.1, F11), and the "seven complete weeks" draw the Ein Sof above Atzilus (2823.2). Lag BaOmer, as the giving of the hidden Torah, is the thinnest stretch here (2834, marked reading).

## Ki Tavo'u el HaAretz (2852-2972): the two knowledges, three contractions, servant and son

This is the core of the range. "Give truth to Yaakov" leads to "the truth of Havayah forever". That leads to the two knowledges, higher and lower, which are "real opposites" (2860):

> ד"ע הוא מלמעלמ"ט, מצד המשפיע, וד"ת היא דעה שמלמטלמ"ע (line 2860)

From the giver's side, creation is "from something to nothing": from the true Something, the Essence, to a glow that is naught (2868.1). From the receivers' side the same power is called "from nothing to something". It is "nothing" because they grasp that it is not a thing, and because it is hidden in them (2868.2). The Rashab puts the two sides into one line (2874):

> אין כאן רק ג' מדרי, יש ואין ויש (line 2874)

"There are only three levels: Something, nothing, something."

He then refines this into four levels and three contractions (2886-2889). The "nothing" of the true Something is light still cleaving to its source. Such light cannot source a world, and he proves it from Tanya 35 (2887):

> דהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו וזו היא מדרי' החכמה (line 2887)

Only a "glow of a glow", separated from its source, can become the source of a separate something. That is the Name Elokim. Creation is not from Elokim itself but from Havayah drawn through Elokim, like sunlight through a sheath (2885, 2929-2931, 2938):

> עיקר סיבת [ה]התהוות מאין ליש נפרד הוא ע"י שם אלקים דוקא (line 2938)

But the maamar does not let the hiding stand as final. Its first answer (2891.1) is that the hiding is only toward us:

So "the truth of Havayah" means revealing the Essence's light as it was before it contracted into the four letters (2891.2, 2900). The higher knowledge is the revelation of "the true nothing". In it there is no room for a something, and the soul feels "all before Him is as nothing" (2902.1). Souls of Atzilus reach even the hidden lights above Atzilus (2913).

Its second answer is the service of the servant. Yaakov "serves Elokim": he repairs the hiding (2923). The soul came down for this (2938.2). A person clothed in body and animal soul refuses, in each detail, to want physical things for themselves, and works to feel the divine life in them. Then the world stops hiding. "Through the world he sees Godliness more palpably", and that is because Havayah and Elokim are one (2938.3, F11 + F12 + F09).

Its third answer is the highest (2947.2). Even the servant who knows Havayah from within the hiding does not know Havayah as it is. The true height is when the hiding itself shines:

The root of the something and of all concealments is the Essence's own hiddenness, higher beyond relation than revealed Havayah. That is the future revelation, made by the present work of refining. Yisrael, the son, is Havayah revealed with no hiding (2947.3). In the Temple the Ark took up no room, because what is above place shone there. In the same way time did not limit the Baal Shem Tov, and in the Torah past and future are one (2956). Space and time remain, but they do not hide. This is "the union of Havayah and Elokim from above down" (2956.3). The land's Sabbath follows. For Yisrael rest comes "at once", bitul without toil (2969.1). For Yaakov there are six years of sowing first, the work of refinement (2969.2).

## BeMidbar Sinai (2973-3099): the soul above the Torah, revealed only through the Torah

Yaakov and Yisrael, servant and son, become prayer and Torah (2976-2980). The maamar then asks what "the likeness of a man" on the throne is. Its answer: "the thought of Israel preceded everything" (2991). Speech makes an "other", but thought makes creatures that are "not other but united" (3025.2, F02):

> לזאת נתהווה מזה נבראים שאינם בבחי' הזולת, כ"א מיוחדים (line 3025)

Souls rose in the sublime thought of Adam Kadmon. Its light must shine in every Jew. It is the natural longing "to be included and cleave to the One God" (3000.1), and the power of self-sacrifice "even in the emptiest, without reason" (3000.2). Above the yechidah is a spark of Godliness rooted in inner Adam Kadmon. So:

> וזהו דישראל עלו במחשבה למעלה גם מבחי' התורה (line 3010)

Yet the revelation of that root in souls comes only through the Torah. The reason is a distinction within the unity itself (3080):

> דבתורה הרי אורייתא וקוב"ה כולא חד*י, אבל נש"י הרי נתהוו להיות בבחי' מהות בפ"ע (line 3080)

Torah is simply one with Him. Souls were made to be entities of their own, like a son who is of his father's essence yet a being of his own. So the Essence reaches the soul through the Torah (3086-3088). But first there must be prayer, the joining of the soul in the body with its root (3091-3092). Ein Sof itself cannot be "like a man", since man means divided attributes and He is above attributes (3034). "The likeness of a man" is only what He clothed for the souls. The purpose of creation is man (3045.2).

## VeAsita Chag Shavuot (3100-3251): face-light, the inner heart, forgetting, and the point that never forgets

Torah below is a parable (mashal) for what is above. A parable hides its meaning, and yet only through it is the meaning grasped (3104). So through the "ancient parable" of Torah the Ancient One, Ein Sof, reaches even the man below (3118). Charity draws "the light of the King's face", the essence of Atik (3131-3132).

The maamar maps three hearts, and this is its main contribution to F12 (3146). The outer heart contemplates the light that makes and fills worlds, and it needs effort. The inner heart contemplates the Essence of Or Ein Sof as it is in itself. The "hidden heart" is not grasp at all but seeing:

> והוא בחי' ראיית המהות דאלקות, שאינו בבחי' השגה כלל (line 3146)

When the feeling is in the Godliness itself, and not only in one's grasp of it, the heart is moved without effort (3153):

> הרי בזה נרגש בחי' העצמות דאלקות, ולכן מאיר ממילא בלב ג'כ (line 3153)

Prayer is "back" (the scroll's reverse side), walking after God like a son who follows his father even when shown no face (3166). Here the maamar gives the practical side of the two unities (3173). It sets out two contemplations of the higher unity. In the first, physical things lack existence and are now exactly as before they were created:

> וה'יה במציאותם עתה ממש כמו שהיו במציאותם קודם שנתהוו (line 3173)

In the second they exist, but their existence is Godliness:

> והיינו שהן בבחי' מציאות, אלא שמציאותם הוא אלקות (line 3173)

The second, the Rashab says, is not lower but higher, "God is One from above down". From it a person is "not separate from Godliness" even while busy with physical things.

Then comes forgetting. "Havayah left me, and Adnut forgot me." Forgetting is not from Godliness but from its clothing in concealments (3184), and souls' forgetting below causes forgetting above (3205). It belongs to the filling light, which is clothed. The surrounding light is not clothed, so no forgetting reaches it (3219). A glow of it always reaches every soul, even in exile, as the point of the heart (3223):

> ולבי ערייי, מהארת בחי' סוכ"ע, שמאיר בכל נפש מישראל גם בזה"ג בבחיי נקודת הלב שלמעלה מהדעת (line 3223)

Its root is Keter of Malchus, which never goes down. The maamar calls it "Forever, O God, Your word stands in the heavens" (3223.2). This takes F01's own verse and uses it for the soul's untouched point. Shavuot draws inner Keter even into outer Malchus, "so that even in Malchus there is no forgetting" (3239).

## HaShamayim Mesaprim (3252-3332): the father who hides, and the far surrounding

The Baal Shem Tov's parable of the father who hides to test his son is told in full (3255, 3261). The foolish child weeps and forgets. The wise child runs after his father on his paths. The father's love doubles, and "is revealed from darkness to light" (F03 + F11). In service this means the following. When nothing shines in prayer, a person still contemplates that "all before Him is as nothing" and wants only to be included in the Essence (3263.2). He holds all day to Torah, where "the Essence is drawn only" (3265.1). He does this "only because he knows the truth", though he feels nothing (3278.1). That is how Egypt led to Sinai (3274.2). At Sinai the Anochi, whom "none knows but Himself", was revealed in "your God", in the souls (3274.1). And that is how the last exile, "I will surely hide My face", leads to "all will know Me" (3276, 3278.2).

The maamar then separates two surroundings (3302-3327). The near surrounding belongs to the chain of worlds. Its rule is that the upper's inner side becomes surrounding to the lower. The far surrounding is wholly cut off:

> אמנם בחי' מקיף הרחוק הוא שמובדל בערך לגמרי, ואינו שייך אל ההשתל' כלל (line 3316)

Its root is "the essence of the light", which is no drawing or spreading at all (3320, 3327). The mitzvot are garments, the near surrounding. The canopy of Shavuot is the far one (3329).

## Naso (3333-3377), opening: why the prohibitions reach the Essence

Positive commands make and enlarge vessels, and so their light, even their surrounding light, is the near surrounding (3336, 3346.1). Prohibitions are the absence of vessel, a restraint. That is might (gevurah), and might in its root is above kindness, "His essential hiddenness" (3346.2-3):

> אבל ע"י שסייה לית שהן בחי' ה'ג, נמשך בחי' מהות ועצמות אוא"ס שהוא בחי' העלם העצמי (line 3346)

The whole chain of worlds comes from one "thought", split out of the hidden light by the overflow of His kindness: "Or Ein Sof is higher without end and lower without limit" (3372). So the positive commands, rooted in kindness, cannot reach the Essence itself. The prohibitions can (3373).

> ומשו"ז המלת מגיעים בבחי' העצמות (line 3373)

The range ends mid-argument, at "the 'no' comes before the 'yes'" (3374).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **Elokim is the Essence too.** Shaar HaYichud 4-6 says Elokim hides Havayah only toward the creatures. The Rashab gives the reason in terms of the Essence: the limiting power is itself the Essence, and "essence does not hide essence" (2891). This ties F03 to F05 directly.
- **The hiding itself will shine.** The Tanya's F03 says concealment serves revelation. The Rashab says the concealment, at its root, is higher than the revelation it serves. It is the Essence's own hiddenness, and in the future it will itself shine (2947.2). The prohibitions reach the Essence for the same reason: might, restraint and hiddenness are higher in their root than kindness and drawing (3346, 3373).
- **Three contractions and four levels.** Tanya 35's gloss ("He alone is, and this is the level of Chochmah") becomes a proof. Light that feels its source cannot source a world, so a "glow of a glow" is needed (2887). The Tanya does not draw this out.
- **Two forms of the higher unity, ranked.** "They do not exist" and "their existence is Godliness", with the second called higher (3173). Shaar HaYichud 3 has only the first model, the ray nullified in the sun.
- **A map of the heart.** Outer heart, inner heart and hidden heart, with the rule that the heart is moved without effort when the feeling is in the Godliness and not in one's own grasp (3146, 3153).
- **The point of the heart that never forgets.** It is placed in Keter of Malchus, the surrounding within the filling, under F01's verse "Your word stands in the heavens" (3223). The Tanya (18-19) speaks of hidden love and the soul's inborn faith. It does not give this cosmology of why it cannot be forgotten.
- **Souls for whom the world does not hide at all** (souls of Atzilus, Yosef at his accounts, 2807-2812). The Tanya's tzaddik has turned evil to good. These souls never had the screen.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 385 lines are *none*: editor's footnotes and seven bare running heads. Some footnotes carry real content: the Rashab's later note on the beauty parable (2766), the Rebbe's letter on the Baal Shem Tov passage (2961), and the Rashab's gloss on "splitting" (3358, 3359, which are marked structural because they are his words). - Kabbalistic mapping is structural unless the text draws the unity from it. This covers the Omer's barley and the 49 days (2823 is stated only for the refining and for the Ein Sof above Atzilus), the beards of Arich and Z"A (3050-3051), the families and fathers' houses of the census (3099 is tagged only for its last clause), and the anatomy of Tevunah, netzach-hod-yesod and the Keter of the Nukva (3305).
- *Reading* is used four times. At 2747 the undivided equality of Adam Kadmon's sefiros is read as F09. At 2834 Lag BaOmer is given a unity tie that the text does not make. At 2956.1 the Ark taking no space is read as F09 one sentence before the text itself names the union of Havayah and Elokim. At 3112.2 the snow parable of Torah is read as F10. - F07 (the home below) is thin, with 8 line tags. The range speaks of the physical made Godliness only for souls of Atzilus (2810-2812), for the second contemplation (3173.4), and for the purpose of creation being man (3045.2). It never uses the word "dwelling". - F09 is tagged only where opposites are explicitly held together: the two knowledges called "real opposites" (2860), fire and water not quenching each other, and past and future as one in Torah (2956), and the black and the white fire (3352-3353 are structural for F10 only).

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **Which unity-contemplation is higher?** Shaar HaYichud 3 and Tanya 33 teach the higher unity through the ray nullified in the sun: things have no existence. At 3173 the Rashab says the contemplation "their existence is Godliness" is not lower, and may be higher. This fits the last range's finding (the union of both unities ranked above the higher unity, 2019). The Tanya does not rank them this way.
- **Soul above Torah, or Torah above soul?** Tanya 4-5 and 23 make Torah the supreme union: "a union like no other". At 3010 and 3080 the Rashab places the soul's root (the essence of delight, inner Atik) above the Torah, even the inner Torah. He agrees that the soul's root is revealed only through Torah. The ranking still differs, and we keep it.
- **Positive commands and prohibitions.** Tanya 4 and 35-37 put the drawing of Or Ein Sof in the positive mitzvah, and treat the prohibitions mainly as keeping life from the kelipah (Tanya 7-8). At 3346 and 3373 the Rashab says the prohibitions reach higher, to the Essence itself, because restraint is might and might in its root is above kindness. He is following Likkutei Torah (Va'etchanan), so this is a Chabad line. It is still a different emphasis from the Tanya's, and we record it as one.
- **Does all service need toil?** Tanya 27-30 frames service as the unending toil of the beinoni. At 2969 the Rashab says the Yisrael-souls need no toil of repair, only bitul and drawing down. The Tanya's category of the complete tzaddik comes close. But the Rashab ties this to a Name (Havayah without Elokim), not to having defeated evil.
