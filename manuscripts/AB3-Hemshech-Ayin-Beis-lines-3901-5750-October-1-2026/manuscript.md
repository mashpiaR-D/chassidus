## Summary

**What this slice is.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, lines 3901-5750: the second part (the maamarim of Shavuos 5672 through Vayera 5673, said aloud) and the long written continuation (Ein od; three sweetenings; truth and the Talmud; letters of the soul; souls and angels; "black am I and beautiful"; the lamp; Kel De'os). The Rebbe Rashab keeps one question in front of him: how does the hidden Essence itself, not just its light, come down into a person, a body and a dark world?

**What it adds to the full depth (stated by the text).** 1. *The made thing proves the Essence.* Light only shows there is a source. A solid "something" made from nothing could only come from the One whose being is from Himself, so His own power is "carried down" into the plain thing (4446-4455; 5612-5617). The descent's "world given back as His" (D10) gets its reason. 2. *Love is still "someone who loves."* Even "He is your life" love is felt as mine. Higher are the yechida's bond and "complete fear" from seeing. True servants feel like sinners at Rosh Hashanah because their love was felt (4124-4128; 5664). This sharpens D2. 3. *Nearness feels like sin.* "Forgive us" comes right after the Shema of all your heart; in Moshiach's days the righteous will feel it more (5219-5225). New. 4. *Hoda'ah.* Three contemplations: the life in me; the endless power (lovesick, the deeper the sicker); the Essence, where the heart is too small and the mind cannot bear it, and the whole self bows (5424-5430). This is D5 made exact: not blankness, but a mind that touches and bows. 5. *Darkness that turns itself.* Beyond the sun pushing darkness away is the black flame that eats the wick, the animal soul's own cry, incense from bitter spices. It reaches higher than the Thirteen Attributes (5115-5218; 5229-5235). Deepens D9. 6. *The desert.* Only below does the soul become a "kallah", consumed by thirst. Starting from this world's dryness it sees all worlds as desert before Him, and only He alive (5662-5693). Gives D8 its engine. 7. *Kel De'os.* "Above real, below nothing" and "below real, bowing by choice" are both true and one in the Essence, because the hiding is His power. Shema makes them "one in one" (5729-5750). Sharpens D4.

**What to do (stated).** Pray as you are on a broken morning; don't wrestle stray thoughts, outshine them; say the Kedusha of Yotzer so the animal soul stops interfering; explain G-dliness to your own common sense until it agrees; contemplate until it is alive and sweet (cold understanding doesn't move the heart); guard against sadness ("the lower world receives as it stands"); answer Amen yehei shmei rabbah with all your strength.

## AB3-C01 (lines 4441-4456 (Teitzei/Tavo maamarim: yesh, ayin, yesh))

**Start:** Is the world around me real, or is only G-d real?

1. **A rooster in the yard is not just a far-off 'echo' of an angel. No number of steps down from heaven could ever produce one solid thing; it has to be made from nothing.** [M2] How: The text follows the chain from the angel Gavriel down to the physical rooster and says the chain alone can never make it; only creation from nothing can. > ובכדי שיתהווה התרנגול הגשמי באמת לא יועיל גם ריבוא רבבות השתל' רק צ"ל בדרך בריאה יש מאין (line 4446) > "For the physical rooster really to come into being, even countless steps down will not help; it must be by creation, something from nothing."

2. **Something that can stop existing is not truly real, even while it is here.** [M10] How: A river that dries up once in seven years is unfit for the red-heifer water even in the years it flows. So too everything that can be undone is 'not real' even now. > והראי' לזה מנהרות המכזבין שמכזבין אחת לשבוע היינו לשבע שנים ה"ה פסולים לקידוש מי חטאת הרי גם בשעה שהולכים בטוב פסולים (line 4447) > "The proof: rivers that fail once in seven years are unfit for the purifying water, even when they are flowing well."

3. **Only One is real in a way that no one made: He simply is what He is.** [M5] How: Everything else was made. What was never made has no 'cause'; its being is only 'that He is what He is'. The Rebbe says it in Yiddish. > וענין מציאותו היינו רק אמיתת היותו מה שהוא (וואס ער איז) (line 4450) > "His existence means only the truth of His being what He is."

4. **That is why the only true name we can give Him is 'Creator'. It does not describe Him; it points at the One whose being is from Himself.** [M14] How: Only a Being whose existence is from Himself can make something from nothing. So the word 'Creator' is not a quality, it points to the Essence. > שאנו יכולים לכנות אותו ית' רק בשם בורא (line 4454) > "We can call Him only by the name 'Creator'."

5. **So the solid world does not hide Him. It is the proof of Him: the made thing forces you to the True Being.** [M15] How: Light only shows that there is a source. A real 'something' out of nothing could only come from the Essence itself. > ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי (line 4454) > "So it is precisely the created thing that proves the True Being."

6. **The Essence's own power is carried into the ordinary thing in front of you, more than into any spiritual light.** [M16] How: Light is only a go-between. Through it the power of the Essence 'is carried down' to make the world. > ולפ"ז יובן דכח העצמות הוא בהתהוות מציאות היש יותר מבבחי' האור (line 4455) > "So the Essence's power is in the coming-to-be of the physical thing more than in the light."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the plain, solid thing is where the Essence's own power is most present (stated). **Return:** Return: look at one ordinary object and say 'Creator' over it; its being solid is His being, carried down (stated: 'carried down'; the practice is ours).

## AB3-C02 (lines 3917-3960 (Kol ha'am ro'im / Al shlosha devarim))

**Start:** Some mornings I wake up broken and dull for no reason. Is my prayer wasted?

1. **Sometimes a person wakes up broken-hearted and low for no reason at all, and can only pray with bitterness.** [M9] How: The text describes the state plainly, without blame. > ולפעמים יקום בבקר בלב נשבר ושפלות ועוצב והכנעה בלי שום טעם כלל (line 3917) > "Sometimes he gets up in the morning with a broken heart, low and sad and humbled, for no reason at all."

2. **Absence always comes before being. Every new thing starts with something missing.** [M3] How: This is a rule of creation: the hiding (tzimtzum) comes before the showing. > כי העדר הוא קודם להוי' וכל הוי' צ"ל תחלה העדר (line 3939) > "Absence comes before being; all being must first have absence."

3. **A seed only grows once it rots in the ground.** [M2] How: The mashal: the seed must fall apart, and then the power of the earth makes it grow. > דוקא ע"י שנרקב הגרעין עי"ז הוא הצמיחה (line 3939) > "Only through the seed rotting does the growth come."

4. **The breaking and the joy hang on each other: the more the heart is broken over being far, the greater the joy that comes.** [M12] How: The text says the two are measured against each other. > והא בהא תלי' לפ"ע דוקא דכל מה שלבו נשבר ונדכה (line 3942) > "The one depends on the other, in exact measure: the more his heart is broken and crushed..."

5. **Being pushed away lifts you higher than being drawn close.** [M4] How: 'His left hand under my head': the left hand that pushes away brings a higher level than the right hand that draws near. > שע"י בחי' שמאל דוחה בא למדרי' גבוה יותר מע"י ימין מקרבת (line 3943) > "Through the left that pushes away he comes to a higher level than through the right that draws near."

6. **This is what makes a person a 'walker'. Souls above only stand; down here, through distance, you move.** [M15] How: A soul in heaven is never far, so it cannot climb. Only distance makes walking. > ועי"ז דוקא נעשה האדם בבחינת מהלך (line 3943) > "Only through this does a person become one who walks."

7. **An empty vessel holds. The broken heart is the vessel G-dliness fills.** [M7] How: Quoting the Mitteler Rebbe: an empty vessel holds; a broken heart becomes a vessel for G-dliness. > דכלי ריקן מחזיק שעי"ז נעשה כלי לאלקו' (line 3960) > "An empty vessel holds; through it he becomes a vessel for G-dliness."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the empty, broken morning is itself the vessel (stated). **Return:** Return: pray that morning as it is, in bitterness, without forcing joy; joy comes after, measured by the breaking (stated).

## AB3-C03 (lines 4121-4128 (Nitzavim/RH: two circumcisions))

**Start:** I love G-d. Isn't that the goal?

1. **Even great love, 'with all your heart and soul', is still 'someone who loves'. You love Him because He is your life.** [M8] How: The verse says 'love G-d, for He is your life'. Loving Him as your life is like loving your own life. > ה"ה בבחי' יש מי שאוהב, שעז"נ לאה' את ה"א כי הוא חייך (line 4124) > "It is still 'someone who loves', as it says: love G-d, for He is your life."

2. **So inside the love there is a feeling of yourself: your own soul is precious to you and you want what is good for you.** [M11] How: The love comes from feeling the good of G-dliness for me. > מ"מ ה"ז בבחי' הרגשה שמרגיש א"ע דנפשו יקרה בעיניו (line 4125) > "Still, it is a feeling in which he feels himself: his soul is dear in his own eyes."

3. **That is why, after a warm prayer, a coarse feeling can come.** [M11] How: Feeling yourself in love opens the door to feeling yourself in other ways. > ולכן מהרגשת האה' יכול להיות אחר התפלה הרגשה חומרי' (line 4125) > "So from feeling the love, a physical feeling can come after prayer."

4. **Someone with real bittul — 'a person who is put away' — does not feel even his own excitement.** [M4] How: The Yiddish phrase: 'an aveckgeleigtkeit', one who has set himself aside. > במי שיש בו ביטול בעצם (א אוועק גילייגטקייט) (line 4126) > "In someone who has bittul in essence (a set-aside-ness)."

5. **Higher is the love of yechida, 'with all your might': a bond with the Infinite itself, like a son's.** [M5] How: This love does not come from feeling the good; it is the soul's own bond. > אבל בחי' אה' דבכל מאדך הוא האה' דבחי' יחידה שבנפש (line 4126) > "But 'with all your might' is the love of the yechida in the soul."

6. **So on Rosh Hashanah even true servants feel like sinners, because their service was felt.** [M15] How: Their only 'sin' is that the love was felt as theirs. Bitterness over that opens the inner heart. > שגם עובדי ה' בכל השנה משפילים א"ע וה"ה כחוטאים בעיני עצמם (line 4127) > "Even those who served G-d all year lower themselves and are like sinners in their own eyes."

7. **Even the kernel is only a shell to what is higher.** [M5] How: Each level, even good, must be removed to reveal the higher love. > שגם מה שהוא בחי' מוחא באמת ה"ז בבחי' קליפה לגבי מדרי' עליונה (line 4128) > "Even what is truly 'brain' is a 'shell' compared to a higher level."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the love that has no 'I' in it, the soul's own bond (stated). **Return:** Return: feel bitter not over sins but over how much of yourself was in your love (stated, RH).

## AB3-C04 (lines 3952; 5276; 5331-5332; 5536-5538)

**Start:** If G-d hides, is He really hidden?

1. **A teacher who wants to teach must first hide his own light, as if he never understood.** [M3] How: Only one who can limit himself can find the 'outside' of his idea that fits the student. > ע"י שמעלים אור שכלו בעצמו כאלו לא השכיל כלל (line 3952) > "By hiding the light of his mind in himself as if he had not understood at all."

2. **He must cut the giving short to fit the student's vessel.** [M2] How: This is the meaning of the sefiros Netzach and Hod: holding back. > לכן צריך המשפיע לקצר ההשפעה לפי אופן כלי המקבל (line 5331) > "So the giver must shorten what he gives to fit the receiver's vessel."

3. **He even holds back his own love and insight at their source, so they do not pour out.** [M7] How: If the love were fully revealed, the giving would flood. > וע"כ צריך לעכב מקור ההשפעה בעצמו שלא יהי' בהתגלות (line 5332) > "So he must hold back the source of the giving in himself so it is not revealed."

4. **Sometimes the opposite: the teacher pours out light too deep, and the student falls in his own eyes, and that makes him a vessel.** [M4] How: Too much light humbles the student; the Yiddish: he falls very low within himself. > ועי"ז המקבל נופל בעצמותו (line 5276) > "Through this the receiver falls in himself."

5. **But the hiding is only for the student. The teacher sees the whole depth in every simple word.** [M10] How: The mashal breaks open: the tzimtzum hides only toward the receiver. > והרי הצמצום הזה הוא רק לגבי המקבל שאינו יודע העמקות והפנימיות שבזה (line 5537) > "This hiding is only toward the receiver, who does not know the depth and inner part in it."

6. **For the giver nothing is hidden at all.** [M6] How: So for the Infinite, after the tzimtzum He shines as before. > אבל לגבי המשפיע אינו מסתיר כלל (line 5538) > "But for the giver it does not hide at all."

7. **The hiding is His own power. An essence does not hide an essence.** [M13] How: The tzimtzum is not a thing apart from Him, so it cannot cover Him. > הרי עצם אינו מסתיר על עצם (line 5536) > "An essence does not hide an essence."

**Bottom:** Bottom: there is no place empty of Him; the hiding is Him (stated). **Return:** Return: the same as the burning bush: in the lowest thorn bush, He is there (stated, 5538).

## AB3-C05 (lines 5115-5160 (the three sweetenings, ketores))

**Start:** How can the bad in me become good, not just pushed down?

1. **When a stray thought falls on you suddenly, do not lose heart; strengthen your love for G-d.** [M9] How: Quoting the Maggid: when a thought rises in prayer, overcoming it brings more light. > שאם נפלה פתאום אל יפול לבו מזה אלא יגביר עלי' האהבה לה' (line 5118) > "If it fell suddenly, let him not lose heart, but strengthen his love for G-d over it."

2. **But wrestling with it is like wrestling with a filthy man: you get dirty too. That is the first, weak sweetening.** [M10] How: So not everyone should try to 'raise' such thoughts. > ה"ז כמתאבק עם המנוול שיתנוול גם הוא בהאבקו עמו (line 5118) > "It is like wrestling with a filthy man; he gets dirty too in wrestling."

3. **A second sweetening: a much higher light shines, and the accuser simply becomes a defender.** [M5] How: When the king's face shines with kindness, the prosecutor himself turns to defend. > אז גם הקטיגור נעשה סניגור (line 5134) > "Then even the accuser becomes a defender."

4. **Like those sentenced to death who meet the king: they are pardoned without any trial.** [M2] How: The king does not even look at the case. > כשפוגעים בהמלך נמחל להם העון לגמרי (line 5135) > "When they meet the king, the sin is fully forgiven."

5. **The third sweetening is deepest: the animal soul itself wakes up, finds no comfort, and is disgusted with its life.** [M15] How: When the G-dly soul does teshuvah it still has some comfort; when the animal soul itself regrets, the pain is total. > ההתעוררות שלו הוא שאינו מוצא נוחם לנפשו כלל ומואס בחייו ממש (line 5152) > "Its awakening is that it finds no comfort at all and is truly disgusted with its life."

6. **That is the incense: the sharpest, bitterest spices become the strongest, sweetest smell.** [M2] How: Bitter spices in the ketores revive the soul from its essence. > וזהו ענין הקטרת שהסמים החריפים וקשים ביותר נעשים לריח וחזק וערב ביותר (line 5153) > "This is the incense: the sharpest, hardest spices become the strongest, sweetest scent."

7. **Like poison that becomes medicine for a dying man: the bad itself turns into life.** [M7] How: Sharp drugs that kill a healthy man heal a sick one. > ונהפכו ונעשו הן עצמן לסם חיים המרפא (line 5153) > "And they themselves turned into a healing medicine of life."

8. **The groan of the body from below reaches the Essence: 'the lower waters weep'.** [M15] How: The cry of the slain in Egypt; the lower waters cry to be close. > והו"ע מים תחתונים בוכין (line 5157) > "This is 'the lower waters weep'."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the animal soul's own cry, from its own darkness (stated). **Return:** Return: do not run from the crude pain of the body-self; let it cry; that crying reaches higher than a polite teshuvah (stated in principle; phrasing ours).

## AB3-C06 (lines 5164-5180; 5217-5218; 5229-5235)

**Start:** Is light chasing darkness away the deepest thing?

1. **The sun only pushes darkness away. When it sets, the dark is back.** [M10] How: The text sets the sun against the future 'eternal light'. > דאור השמש הוא רק שדוחה את החשך לא שהחשך נעשה אור ולכן כשנפסק אור השמש נעשה חשך כמו קודם (line 5229) > "Sunlight only pushes darkness away; the darkness does not become light. So when the sun stops, it is dark as before."

2. **A black flame in a candle does not light the room. It eats the wick. The darkness itself is used up.** [M4] How: The blue-black part of the flame 'consumes' rather than 'shines'. > נהורא אוכמא אינו מאיר רק ששורף ומכלה את הפתילה (line 5180) > "The black flame does not shine; it only burns and consumes the wick."

3. **Rabbi Elazar ben Durdaya, seeing that it depended only on him, cried out and his soul left him. That is darkness turning itself.** [M9] How: His crying came from the very tightness of the dark. > וכמו תשובת ראב"ד כשראה שאין הדבר תלוי אלא בו כו' געה בבכי' (line 5217) > "Like the teshuvah of R. Elazar b. Durdaya: when he saw it depended only on him, he burst into tears."

4. **Then the harsh powers are sweetened in their root, by themselves, not by kindness from outside.** [M6] How: This is the third sweetening, from the hidden Essence. > שהגבורות נמתקין בשרשן לא ע"י בחי' החסד כ"א שנמתקין בעצמן (line 5218) > "The severities are sweetened in their root, not by kindness, but sweetened in themselves."

5. **In the future the night itself will shine, not by any sun.** [M5] How: 'Night will shine like day.' > שהלילה בעצמה ולא מצד אור השמש כלל רק חשך הלילה יאיר מצ"ע (line 5229) > "The night itself, not from sunlight at all; the dark of night will shine on its own."

6. **Like time itself without time, as space under the Ark took no space.** [M10] How: The Ark took no room; the Baal Shem saw the future as past. > שהזמן עצמו הוא בלי זמן כמו שהמקום הוא בלי מקום (line 5234) > "Time itself is without time, as the place is without place."

7. **'Eternal light' means not light on the world, but the world itself is light.** [M13] How: Like 'G-d of the world': the world is G-dliness. > אור עולם הוא לא רק שמאיר את העולם כ"א שהעולם הוא בבחי' אור (line 5235) > "'Light of the world' is not only that it lights the world, but that the world is light."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the dark itself as light; time itself as timeless (stated). **Return:** Return: right now, by a turn from the bottom of the dark, not by waiting for a 'sun' (stated through R. Elazar).

## AB3-C07 (lines 5185-5208 (gevurah as strength of life))

**Start:** Why does G-d's anger, or my own intensity, matter?

1. **Kindness is like water: cool. A cow is gentle, but slow and cold.** [M3] How: The ox comes from chesed; its life is held in, not flowing. > והוא להיותה מבחי' החסדים וכטבע המים שהן קרים בטבעם (line 5186) > "Because it comes from kindness, like water, which is cold by nature."

2. **Teaching from the cold mind does not move anyone.** [M9] How: Wisdom is cool; spoken coldly, it gives no life. > וכשמשפיע השכל אל הזולת בקרירות כמו שהוא מצד השכל אינו עושה התפעלות וחיות בהזולת (line 5187) > "When he gives the idea to another coldly, as it is in the mind, it does not move or enliven the other."

3. **Words from the fire of the heart go into the other's heart.** [M7] How: Not because the idea is new, but because it comes from the heart. > רק כאשר בא בדבור בדברים היוצאים מן הלב שהוא יסוד האש ה"ה נכנסים אל לב הזולת (line 5187) > "Only when words come out from the heart, the element of fire, do they enter the other's heart."

4. **And then the speaker himself hears new things he never knew.** [M5] How: With real inner warmth (not false over-excitement) new light comes from above the mind. > מתוסף התחדשות באור השכל מה שלא ידע מקודם כלל (line 5188) > "New light is added to the idea that he did not know before at all."

5. **Strength breaks into drops, like rain; each drop is full of life.** [M13] How: Intensity divides; each small insight is full. > וכמו בירידת גשמים הרי כל טפה וטפה היא בבחי' חיות ואין בה שום מיעוט כלל (line 5191) > "Like falling rain: every drop is full of life and is not less at all."

6. **A king's anger is more life, not less: the more life in the blood, the more anger.** [M4] How: A wild animal angers more than a cow because it has more life. > וכל מה שיש יותר חיות בדמים שבאדם יהי' כעסו יותר כשאינו משיג חפצו (line 5201) > "The more life in a man's blood, the more angry he is when he does not get what he wants."

7. **So when he is appeased, more life flows than before.** [M6] How: The 'loss' from sin is not real loss. > וכשמתרצה מכעסו הרי נמשך בזה תוס' אור וחיות יותר (line 5202) > "When he is appeased from his anger, more light and life flow."

8. **That is why teshuvah from distress draws a light higher than the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy.** [M15] How: Mercy erases sin; appeased anger adds life. > וע"כ ע"י התשובה נמשך אור עליון יותר שלמעלה מבחי' יגמה"ר (line 5208) > "So through teshuvah a higher light flows, above the Thirteen Attributes."

**Bottom:** Bottom: even His anger is His life; the far place holds more (stated). **Return:** Return: speak and pray from the heart's warmth, not cold accuracy (stated).

## AB3-C08 (lines 5209-5215 (essential mercy))

**Start:** What kind of mercy can reach me, if G-d is so far above?

## AB3-C19 (lines 5662-5683 ('black am I and beautiful'; the desert))

## AB3-C20 (lines 5685-5693 (only the Essence lives))

5. **'He is your life' means: you have no life but Him.** [M9] How: Quoting Torah Or: there is no other life and no other pleasure; that is why the soul 'faints' to cling to Him. > לאהבה את ה' אלקיך כי הוא חייך פי' שאין חייך אלא הוא (line 5688) > "Love G-d for He is your life — your life is only Him."

6. **The thirst is quenched in Torah and mitzvos, which are His essence.** [M15] How: The thirst ('when will I come and see') is satisfied below, in Torah and mitzvos, where He 'sold Himself' with the Torah. > מרווה צמאונו בתו' ומצות שהן בחי' עצמות (line 5693) > "He quenches his thirst in Torah and mitzvos, which are the Essence."

**Bottom:** Bottom: only the Essence lives by itself (stated). **Return:** Return: mitzvos (stated).

## AB3-C21 (lines 5694-5703 (lamp: wick, oil, flame))

**Start:** Why doesn't my animal side change when I pray?

1. **The animal soul has a mind, but only for physical things.** [M3] How: It knows how to find money, honor and the science of nature, but only the 'something', never the nothing that makes it. > רק שהשכל והדעת שלו עיקרו הוא בענינים גשמיים (line 5697) > "Its mind is mainly in physical things."

2. **Opposites joined.** [M12] How: The G-dly soul and the animal soul are total opposites, yet each power of one is dressed in a power of the other. > וזה מנפלאות ה' שמקשר ומחבר דבר בהפוכו ממש (line 5699) > "It is a wonder of G-d that He joins a thing with its opposite."

3. **Work so the natural mind itself understands.** [M9] How: Not only that the G-dly soul understands through the natural mind, but that it explains it until the natural mind agrees. > כ"א שמשתדל ומתעסק עם השכל הטבעי דנה"ב שיבין ג"כ את הענין ויונח אצלו בטוב (line 5701) > "He works with the animal soul's natural mind so it too understands and it settles well."

4. **Otherwise it does not move.** [M10] How: If the natural mind does not understand, even deep G-dly love does not touch the animal soul. > לא יפעול מאומה בהנה"ב שאינו ניזוז ממקומו (line 5702) > "It will do nothing to the animal soul; it does not budge."

5. **At most it is weakened.** [M11] How: Strong light can only weaken it for a while; that is not a change. > ויכול להיות רק בדרך חלישות שנחלש כחו (line 5702) > "It can only be weakened."

6. **When it understands, it loses its taste for physical things.** [M15] How: Since the animal mind serves its emotions, once it agrees, its pull toward the physical loses its flavor. > עס נעמט בא עם צוא דעם גישמאק פון דיא ענינים החומריים (line 5703) > "It takes away its taste for physical things."

7. **Each power refined adds much light.** [M13] How: The animal soul is 'oil', a concentrated essence; like a drop of essence that flavors a whole drink. > כמו"כ בכל בירור כח מהנה"ב מתוסף הרבה אור בנה"א (line 5703) > "In every refining of a power, much light is added."

**Bottom:** Bottom: the oil burns into the flame (stated). **Return:** Return: explain G-dliness to your own common sense (stated).

## AB3-C22 (lines 5729-5750 (Kel De'os))

**Start:** Is the world nothing, or is it something that bows to Him?

## AB3-D01 The made thing proves the Essence (extends D10)

**Teaching:** The solid, ordinary world is not further from G-d than spiritual light. A real 'something' from nothing could only come from the One whose being is from Himself, so His very power is 'carried down' into it. Light only shows that there is a source; the thing shows the Essence. (stated)

**Hisbonenus:** 1. Look at a rooster, a table, your hand. No chain of spiritual causes could make it solid; it is made from nothing (4446). 2. What can stop being is not truly real even now, like a river that fails once in seven years (4447). 3. Only One was not made; He is 'what He is' (4450). The only name for Him is 'Creator' (4454). 4. So the thing in front of you proves the True Being; His own power is in it more than in light (4454-4455). 5. In the future the body itself will be G-dliness; the soul may draw life from the body (5614, 5617).

**From inside:** The rooster that would vanish if its root showed; the river that fails; the tree whose being is the power joining its elements (5612). **Lands:** In the plainest object, now. (Ours: say 'Creator' over one ordinary thing.)

**Quotes:** - ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי (line 4454): "It is precisely the created thing that proves the True Being." - בל"א ווערט ארפגיטראגין דער כח העצמות (line 4455): "In Yiddish: the power of the Essence is carried down." - וזהו שלעתיד יהי' הגוף אלקות (line 5614): "In the future the body will be G-dliness."

## AB3-D03 Nearness feels like sin (extends new)

## AB3-D04 Hoda'ah: the mind touches and bows (extends D5)

**Teaching:** There are three contemplations. Seeing G-d as the life within (heart fills). Seeing the endless power in worlds (lovesick, the deeper the sicker). This is not 'not knowing' but knowing that touches and bows, and it nullifies the whole self. (stated)

**Hisbonenus:** 1. Feel the life in your own body; then the life in all things (5424). 2. Look at the endless power keeping worlds; feel it cannot be held (5425-5426). 3. Because mind and heart cannot bear it, let the whole self bow (5430).

**From inside:** Lovesick; 'the heart too small, the mind cannot bear'; the spirit in the soul that is never filled (4655). **Lands:** In the Amida (Yehuda = admission) after the Shema (5428-5429).

**Quotes:** - אדרבה שם הוא ביטול ההשגה שמתבטל בהשגתו והוא רק בבחי' הודאה לבד (line 5427): "There understanding cancels itself; only admission." - והוא משום שלא תוכל נפשו שאת גם במוחו וכ"ש בלבו דמשו"ז הוא מתבטל לגמרי מכל מציאותו (line 5430): "Because his soul cannot bear it in mind, much less heart, he is wholly nullified." - במדרי' כזו בנפש שאינו מתמלא ומתרווה (וואס בינוגענט זיך ניט) בשום גילוי (line 4655): "A level in the soul never filled by any revelation."

## Avodah

- **On a broken morning, pray as you are, in bitterness, without despair; joy comes after, measured by the breaking.** ולפעמים יקום בבקר בלב נשבר ושפלות ועוצב והכנעה בלי שום טעם כלל (line 3917): "Sometimes he gets up broken-hearted for no reason."
- **When a stray thought falls suddenly in prayer, don't lose heart; strengthen love for G-d over it. Don't wrestle with it.** שאם נפלה פתאום אל יפול לבו מזה אלא יגביר עלי' האהבה לה' (line 5118): "If it falls suddenly, don't lose heart; strengthen love."
- **Say the Kedusha of Yotzer before Shema, to stir the animal soul from its own root so it won't interfere.** לכך עצה היעוצה מאנשי כנה"ג לומר שתים לפני' להזכיר קדושת המלאכים (line 5621): "The Men of the Great Assembly advised saying two blessings before it, mentioning the angels' holiness."
- **Yotzer only moves the animal soul so it agrees and doesn't disturb; the real change is in Shema.** וענין ההזזה היא שיסכים על הביטול דנה"א שלא ינגד אליו ולא יבלבלו בעבודתו (line 5644): "The 'moving' is that it agrees and doesn't oppose or disturb."
- **Explain the G-dly idea to your own natural mind until it agrees.** כ"א שמשתדל ומתעסק עם השכל הטבעי דנה"ב שיבין ג"כ את הענין ויונח אצלו בטוב (line 5701): "Work with the natural mind until it understands."
- **Contemplate until there is life and pleasure in it; serve with joy.** וכתי' עבדו את ה' בשמחה שיהי' לו חיות ועונג גדול בהאלקות המושג (line 5650): "Serve G-d with joy: have life and great pleasure in the G-dliness grasped."
- **Guard against sadness: the lower world receives as it stands.** וכאן מובן כמה גרוע ענין העצבות (line 5574): "Here you understand how bad sadness is."
- **Answer 'Amen yehei shmei rabbah' with all your strength.** כל העונה איש"ר בכל כחו קורעין לו גז"ד (line 5512): "Whoever answers Amen yehei shmei rabbah with all his strength, a harsh decree is torn up."
- **'With all your soul' in the day: shut your eyes from evil, guard thoughts.** בקיום עוצם עיניו מראות ברע ובשמירתן ממח' והרהורים (line 5640): "Shutting his eyes from evil and guarding from thoughts."
- **Eat permitted food so it strengthens heart and mind for prayer.** שבכח המאכל הכשר ישבע לבו ויחזק דעתו ושכלו בעבודה (line 5363): "By kosher food his heart is satisfied and his mind strengthened for service."
- **On Shabbos, let eating be G-dly pleasure.** וכמו התענוג דשבת דצדיק אוכל לשובע נפשו (line 5655): "Like Shabbos pleasure: the righteous eats to satisfy his soul."
- **Learn Torah first even for yourself (to bind your soul), then for its own sake.** מעיקרא כי עביד אינש אדעתי' דנפשי' עביד (line 5467): "At first a person acts for himself."
- **On Rosh Hashanah, feel like a sinner for how much self was in your service.** שגם עובדי ה' בכל השנה משפילים א"ע וה"ה כחוטאים בעיני עצמם (line 4127): "Even those who served all year lower themselves like sinners."
- **Say 'forgive us' in the Amida as nearness.** וזהו שאומרים בש"ע סלח לנו אבינו כי חטאנו (line 5222): "That is why we say in the Amida: forgive us."
- **Know that the world now is One as on the first day.** וז"ע ה' אחד לידע ולהאמין שגם עכשיו שהעולם נראה במציאות הוא באמת ה' אחד (line 3910): "To know and believe that even now, when the world seems real, it is truly One."
