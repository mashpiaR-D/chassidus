## What this slice adds (one page)

This slice is the end of Samach Vav and a key to the whole of it. It holds the close of the last maamar (Vaera 5668) and its completion on Rosh Hashana, Yom Kippur and Shabbos; the Rashab's own notes; the Rebbe Rayatz's explanations of the opening; and a printed summary of all 61 maamarim (the publishers' 'content, chapter heads and summary'; its author is not named in the slice). So it shows the Hemshech's whole climb in small: why there is a world at all (a longing with no reason), why no rise ever reaches Him ('up to, and not including'), and where He is reached instead: the plain deed, the yoke of Heaven, the simple servant, teshuva and the toil of Torah. It also shows how this is revealed (through Torah, through the 'sons'), and that the goal is His home down here. (stated)

What it adds to the full depth: (1) The deepest delight cannot be felt, and the texts place the touch of it in what feels like the opposite of pleasure. That means the contracted fear of missing His will, the bitterness before prayer, His hidden face, the dry walk, the craftsman bent into his work. DESCENT's bottom (D8, the bond) is right. SV3 adds that the way into it often feels tight or dark, and that this is a door, not a failure. (2) The lowest-looking service reaches highest. Son, faithful servant and simple servant are ranked, and the simple servant 'whose whole existence is the master's existence' reaches the 'truth of truth': 'there is nothing else', which is beyond even 'all before Him is as nothing'. (3) The return is deeper than the ascent. The 'return' is more your own than the 'run', and it is rooted in the Essence. The end of a deed holds more of the beginning than the head does. In the future the body comes first. (4) The Rayatz's three knowings: knowing what it is, knowing it is beyond that, and grasping it, where 'he loses his whole existence', broken and lifted at once. The worker who feels it in a deed knows it better than the greatest thinker. (stated, each point; the arrangement is ours)

For the guide (ours): this slice gives taps for people DESCENT serves less well. For the dry and dark there is SV3-D4 (keep walking; held from behind). For the scrupulous scholar there is SV3-D1 (the tightness of caring is a door). For the returner there is SV3-D3 (a run with no goal). For the simple doer there is SV3-D5 and SV3-D6 (the deed, the low job, done as His). For the one who fears not being enough there is SV3-D7 (wanted 'even if he is not as he should be'). Guards are written into each depth. Never use contraction or bitterness with someone anxious, depressed or self-hating. Never use 'nothing for yourself' as a reason for anyone to accept mistreatment.

## SV3-C01 · Last maamar of the Hemshech, Vaera / Rosh Chodesh Shevat 5668, lines 6201-6214 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M3** · Every limit you run into, every 'this far and no further', starts at one place: G-d's first holding-back, when He said to His world 'enough'. *How:* The text reads Egypt (Mitzrayim) as 'narrow places'. It traces all narrowness, even the limits of the high worlds, back to the name Shakai, 'who said to His world: enough', and from there to the first tzimtzum. «ושרש וסיבת כל הגבלות הנ"ל הן משם שדי, שאמר לעולמו די» · *and the root and cause of all these limits is from the name Shakai, who said to His world 'enough'* · line 6201 (OCR)

2. **M6** · The Torah comes from before that holding-back. It is not a message about Him from far away; it is one with Him. *How:* To receive Torah they first had to leave Egypt, because Torah is from the light that has no limit, and at its root it is one with His essence ('the Torah and the Holy One are all one'). Its whole purpose is to bring that limitless light down here. «וכל הכוונה בנתינת התורה להמשיך בחי' הגילוי דאוא"ס הבבע"ג למטה» · *the whole intent in giving the Torah is to draw the revelation of the limitless Infinite light down below* · line 6201 (OCR)

3. **M1** · So here is the puzzle: something that high came down into a voice you could hear with your ears, and into deeds done with your hands. That is not a step down; it is a jump. *How:* The text stops on the gap: G-d came down on Sinai and spoke in a real voice; mitzvos were put into physical things that are 'not vessels at all'. That is a huge leap, with no ladder in between. «ה"ז דילוג גדול מאד, מבחי' רוממות העצמות, להיות נראה ונשמע בקול ממש» · *this is a very great leap, from the loftiness of the Essence, to be seen and heard in an actual voice* · line 6203 (OCR)

4. **M2** · A leap never happens by steps. It happens only by a hidden strength, the kind that is not used up in ordinary showing. *How:* The general rule the Rashab gives: any coming-down that skips levels comes from a hidden power, not from the normal flow of light. «דכל המשכה שבבחי' דילוג הוא בבחי' כח נעלם דוקא» · *every drawing-down that is by a leap is only through a hidden power* · line 6211 (OCR)

5. **M5** · That hidden strength is His own delight. Torah is called His 'plaything'; the deepest thing in Him, His pleasure, is what reaches all the way down. *How:* 'I was His delight' (Proverbs) is read as: Torah is the essential pleasure of the Infinite, higher even than His will in the mitzvos; and in the mitzvos too that pleasure is hidden. «שהתורה היא בבחי' תענוג העצמי» · *the Torah is His essential delight* · line 6212 (OCR)

6. **M15** · Because it is His delight, it does not stop halfway. It comes 'from the highest of high to the lowest of low, even to us, souls in bodies'. When you say 'asher kideshanu' over a mitzvah, you are naming that delight. *How:* 'Asher' (in the blessing) means the most praised good, the delight with nothing above it. That delight gives the power for holiness to come down to the bottom, to us in bodies; 'the voice of G-d with power'. «מלמעלה מעלה עד למטה מטה, גם אלינו, נשמות שבגופים» · *from the highest above to the lowest below, even to us, souls in bodies* · line 6213 (OCR)

**Bottom.** His essential delight: the one thing strong enough to leap all the way down. **Return.** So by Torah and mitzvos, done here in a body, a person is lifted into His inner essence (6214). The return is the blessing over the next mitzvah: 'who made us holy' is said by a body, about a deed.

## SV3-C02 · Vaera 5668, lines 6216-6234 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M2** · There are two kinds of simple joy: the kind you can feel, and the kind that is too simple to be felt at all. *How:* The text names two levels of 'simple delight': one that comes into feeling at least a little, and one that does not come into feeling at all. «בחי' תענוג הפשוט הבא במורגש עכ"פ, והב', בחי' התענוג הפשוט ממש שאינו בא במורגש כלל» · *simple delight that comes into feeling at least somewhat; and the second, truly simple delight that does not come into feeling at all* · line 6217 (OCR)

2. **M7** · Joy that sits in a feeling is mixed with the feeler. The simplest joy is the light that has no hold on any vessel. *How:* Light inside vessels (feelings, powers) is 'compound' pleasure. Even light above the vessels still has a hold on them. The main simple delight is the light 'set apart beyond comparison' from vessels. «ועיקר בחי' עונג הפשוט הוא בהאור המובדל בערך מבחי' הכלים» · *the main simple delight is in the light that is separate beyond comparison from the vessels* · line 6222 (OCR)

3. **M5** · Even the very core of the light is still 'light', something given off. Deeper is His own name held inside Him, the hidden Eden no eye has seen. *How:* Higher than any drawing-down is the light held within His essence, 'not light at all': His essential name, the essential hiddenness, 'Eden that is hidden'. «והוא בחי' שמו העצמי, בחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס כו', והוא בחי' עדן סתימאה» · *it is His essential name, the essential hiddenness of the Infinite, the hidden Eden* · line 6232 (OCR)

4. **M4** · And the surprise: you do not reach that unfelt joy by understanding. You reach it by doing a mitzvah that was 'not given for enjoyment'. *How:* Torah is reason and knowing, so its pleasure is felt. Mitzvos are done mainly as the yoke of Heaven; 'mitzvos were not given for pleasure'; so the delight in them is His essential delight as it is in Himself, which does not come into feeling. «אבל מצות לאו ליהנות ניתנו» · *but mitzvos were not given for enjoyment* · line 6234 (OCR)

5. **M15** · The same is true of the plain work of the day: making your own animal side and the low things around you into vessels for G-d. That is His home below. *How:* The second, higher rise of the future (the world to come with no eating or drinking) comes through deeds done as kabbalas ol, and through refining from below upward: making the animal soul and low things into vessels. «לעשות את הנה"ב ואת הדברים תחתונים שיהיו כלים לאלקות» · *to make the animal soul and the low things into vessels for G-dliness* · line 6234 (OCR)

**Bottom.** The unfelt delight: His name inside Him, hidden Eden. **Return.** Back to a deed whose pleasure you do not feel, done because He asked. The text says this is exactly where the deepest delight lives.

## SV3-C03 · Vaera 5668, lines 6237-6249 and 6274 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English, the move labels, and calling step 3 'the turn'.*

1. **M17** · Torah also has two layers: clear light, and truth buried under questions that you have to dig out. *How:* Torah as clear wisdom (the first tablets, the Written Torah) needs no sorting. Torah that comes through 'concealments' (Talmud Bavli, 'He set me in dark places') needs you to toil, answer the questions, sort and clarify. «וצריכים לייגעץ א"ע להסיר ההעלמות וההסתרים» · *and one must toil to remove the hidings and concealments* · line 6237 (OCR)

2. **M5** · If you dig for the reason, you reach the hidden root of wisdom. If you dig because you must know what He truly wants, you reach Him beyond wisdom. *How:* Sorting a law by its reason reaches the 'essential hiddenness of wisdom'. Sorting it as kabbalas ol, wanting only His true will, reaches the essential hiddenness of the Infinite, His essential name. «ה"ז מגיע לבחי' עונג הבלתי מורגש» · *it reaches the delight that is not felt* · line 6241 (OCR)

3. **M12** · Here is the turn: the one who studies this way often feels no pleasure at all. His heart tightens with fear of getting it wrong. That tightness, the opposite of pleasure, is what touches the pleasure that cannot be felt. *How:* The text says it outright: since he does not aim at the reason but at the truth of His will, he is 'in fear and contraction of soul, the opposite of pleasure', and because of that he reaches the unfelt delight. «ואדרבה, הוא בבחי' יראה וכיווץ בנפשו, היפך העונג» · *on the contrary, he is in fear and contraction of soul, the opposite of pleasure* · line 6241 (OCR)

4. **M15** · This only happens down here. Torah up above is clear, with nothing to dig. Only Torah in a world of doubt lets you reach the root. *How:* Torah as it is above is only 'direct light', compound pleasure. Only as it came down is there sorting of law, and only then is its root, the hidden essential delight, drawn into it. So the Torah was given below. «ולכן ניתנה התורה למטה דוקא» · *therefore the Torah was given specifically below* · line 6249 (OCR)

5. **M6** · Why you and not an angel? Angels are made of His 'speech', things that stand on their own. Your soul is cut into Him like letters engraved in stone, not ink laid on top. *How:* Angels are 'standing' because they come from 'the word of G-d', letters that are a thing in themselves. Souls in bodies are rooted in 'engraved letters' that are not a separate thing from the light, so they can rise into the essence of light. «שרשם בבחי' אותיות החקיקה, שאינם בבחי' מהות ומציאות בפ"ע מן האור» · *their root is in engraved letters, which are not a separate being apart from the light* · line 6249 (OCR)

**Bottom.** The tight, unpleasant fear of missing His truth is a touch of the delight no one can feel. **Return.** Open the page you are stuck on. The struggle with the question is the contact, not the obstacle to it.

## SV3-C04 · Vaera 5668, lines 6254-6272 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M2** · The lowest level in G-d's order, Malchus, the receiver, has the highest root of all. *How:* In the future Malchus will be 'the crown of her husband' and will give to all the others, because 'in truth the root of Malchus is above them all', rooted in the essence of the Infinite. «כי באמת, שרש המלכות הוא למעלה מכולם» · *for in truth the root of Malchus is above them all* · line 6254 (OCR)

2. **M9** · Look at birth. The father's part is visible and stirs things up. But the child's very body comes from the mother, from the hidden side. *How:* In human birth, the power to make new being is in the woman; the male seed is open and only awakens the power hidden in her, 'but the body of the child comes from the female seed'. «אבל גוף התהוות הולד הוא מטפת המ"ן דוקא» · *but the actual coming-into-being of the child is from the female seed* · line 6256 (OCR)

3. **M6** · Making something from nothing belongs only to the Essence. Nothing else can do it. *How:* The real power to make being from nothing is in the essence of the Infinite alone; light carries it only because it is 'like its source'. «דאמיתת כח ההתהוות הוא בבחי' עצמות א"ס» · *the true power of bringing into being is in the Essence of the Infinite* · line 6256 (OCR)

4. **M9** · Notice this in yourself: what you feel of yourself is your powers, your thinking and feeling. Not the plain fact that you exist. That fact is the hidden part, and it is the deepest. *How:* Now, what shines openly is revelation. In a person what shines is his soul-powers, 'not his very coming-into-being'. The same is true above. «מה שמאיר בו עתה בגילוי הוא בחי' הכחות הנפשיים שבו, לא גוף ההתהוות שלו» · *what shines in him openly now is his soul-powers, not his very coming-into-being* · line 6263 (OCR)

5. **M12** · So the deed and the study need each other. The deed wakes the Essence. The study lets it be seen. *How:* Rabbi Tarfon: deed is greater, because doing as kabbalas ol arouses the Essence. Rabbi Akiva: study is greater, because Torah is the vessel for revealing. Both are true; the sages ruled 'study is great because it brings to deed'. «ועי"ז דוקא מעוררים וממשיכים בחי' עצמות א"ס» · *and through this specifically one arouses and draws the Essence of the Infinite* · line 6266 (OCR)

6. **M15** · In the end the body comes first. The text says that in the future the soul will receive its life from the body. *How:* In the future the hidden power (Malchus) will be the main thing revealed, and 'below too the body will be the main thing'. «לעתיד, גם למטה יהי' העיקר הגוף» · *in the future, below too, the body will be the main thing* · line 6263 (OCR)

**Bottom.** Your plain being, not your powers, is the place where only the Essence works. **Return.** Do one plain deed with the body. It wakes what study will later light up.

## SV3-C05 · Vaera 5668, lines 6276-6289 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M2** · 'We will hear' does not mean hearing with the ears. It means the heart accepts it and finds it sweet. *How:* You cannot do before you hear, so 'nishma' must be the heart's hearing, as in 'they did not listen to Moshe': they heard, but it did not sit with them. «אלא רל נשמע הוא שמיעת הלב, שהדבר מקובל אצלו» · *rather, 'we will hear' means hearing of the heart, that the thing is accepted by him* · line 6276 (OCR)

2. **M7** · 'We will do' is the opposite: doing it when you have no wish for it at all, the way a forced document is still binding. *How:* 'Na'aseh' is iskafya, compulsion, like a get given under compulsion: 'even though in himself he has no will or desire' for Torah, mitzvos or prayer, he cancels his nature and forces himself in each detail. «ואע"פ שבעצם אין לו רצון וחשק בעסק התורה ומצות וכן בתפלה» · *even though in himself he has no will or desire for Torah, mitzvos and prayer* · line 6282 (OCR)

3. **M5** · Putting 'do' before 'hear' was not just obedience. It was laying down their very selves. *How:* Saying na'aseh first meant the bittul of kabbalas ol 'in the sense of setting down their very selfhood completely', as servants, which is beyond any bittul based on understanding. «שהי' בהם הביטול דקבעומ"ש בבחי' הנחת עצמותם לגמרי» · *they had the bittul of the yoke of Heaven, setting down their very selves completely* · line 6282 (OCR)

4. **M6** · And that is why it reached so high: a self laid down meets His own hiddenness. *How:* Through this they drew the 'essential nothing' of Keser, rooted in the hidden Essence of the Infinite. «שזהו בשרשו בחי ההעלם דעצמות א"ס» · *which at its root is the hiddenness of the Essence of the Infinite* · line 6282 (OCR)

**Bottom.** A self laid down, doing before feeling, meets His hidden Essence. **Return.** Do the next mitzvah before the heart agrees. The heart's 'yes' may come after; it is the second crown, not the first.

## SV3-C06 · Vaera 5668, lines 6318-6351 (the Rashab), with 7594-7597 (editors' summary)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M5** · The very hidden Essence is reached only by the plain servant who serves because he was told. *How:* Souls of 'Atzilus', sons, draw light. But 'to reach the very essence', it is only through the work of the 'servant', 'the simple servant, serving with the yoke of Heaven'. «בבחי' עבודת עבד דוקא, בחי' עבד פשוט, בעבודה בקבלת עול מלכות שמים» · *specifically the service of a servant, a simple servant, in service with the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven* · line 6318 (OCR)

2. **M12** · But what he reaches, he cannot show. The returner draws the Essence up; the steady righteous one lets it shine down. Each needs the other. *How:* Baalei teshuva draw with 'greater force', from the Essence; but revelation below comes through tzaddikim. So both are needed: 'who will make you like a brother to me... I will find you outside'. «דבע"ת מעוררים וממשיכים בחי' עצמות א"ס, והגילוי הוא ע"י הצדיקים» · *the returners arouse and draw the Essence, and the revealing is through the tzaddikim* · line 6318 (OCR)

3. **M4** · So the one who looks lower is truly higher. Yehuda, the shepherd who works from below, is above Yosef the ruler, even if Yosef shines now. *How:* Yosef was a soul of Atzilus, a 'chariot' to G-d even in Egypt; the brothers were shepherds, working from below. 'In truth Yehuda's level is above Yosef's', though now revelation leads, so Yosef rules. «ובאמת, בחי' ומדרי' יהודה למעלה ממדרי' יוסף» · *and in truth the level of Yehuda is above the level of Yosef* · line 6327 (OCR)

4. **M8** · Higher still: a rich man who calls out like a poor man. A scholar who sets his whole mind aside to learn only what G-d wants. *How:* The 'son who becomes a servant': when a learned person sets down his intellect entirely, not judging by his mind but trembling to find His true will, this is more self-bittul than a servant who never had a mind to set down. «וכאשר עשיר קורא כעני, ה"ז מגיע למעלה יותר, מפני שיש בזה ביטול עצמי הרבה יותר» · *when a rich man calls out as a poor man, it reaches higher, because there is far more self-nullification in it* · line 6351 (OCR)

5. **M12** · But the text holds back: it is hard to say scholars get more, since 'all Israel have a share'. The son-made-servant is higher only because he has both: he wakes the Essence and also reveals it. *How:* The Rashab raises the difficulty himself ('this is hard to say'): simple people who keep mitzvos in deed must be equal; so the advantage is only having both levels, like tzaddik and baal teshuva in one. «כל ישראל יש להם חלק לעוה"ב» · *all Israel have a share in the world to come* · line 6354 (OCR)

**Bottom.** The simple servant reaches what the bright son cannot; the bright son reveals what the servant reached. **Return.** Whatever your level, add the servant's part: one thing today done only because He asked, without judging it.

## SV3-C07 · Completion of the maamar (השלמת הדרוש), Rosh Hashana, lines 6363-6405 (the Rashab)

*stated: each step. ours: the English and the move labels.*

1. **M2** · G-d's life is inside every single thing, to the last blade of grass. Each one has a force over it saying: grow. *How:* Kingship (malchiyos) is the life that fills worlds, each by its measure, 'so that even in the physical there is no blade of grass without a mazal that strikes it and says: grow'. «עד שגם בגשמיות אין לך עשב שאין לו מזל המכה בו ואומר לו גדל» · *so that even in the physical there is no blade of grass without a force that strikes it and says to it 'grow'* · line 6365 (OCR)

2. **M1** · Then how can He say 'I have not changed'? If He is inside all of it, giving it life, surely something in Him moved. *How:* The kasha in the text: the worlds live and stand from Him, His power is clothed in them, so how 'I have not changed'? The answer: the glow that gives them life is only an outer glow from His Essence and does not touch Him. «אך הענין הוא, מפני שההארה המחי' עולמות ה"ה הארה חיצוניות לבד מעצמותו ית'» · *but the matter is: the glow that gives life to worlds is only an external glow from His Essence* · line 6372 (OCR)

3. **M10** · Like a king whose name is called over many lands. His name rules there; he himself is not spread out in them. *How:* The mashal of a king of flesh and blood: being called king over the lands is not a spreading of his self, 'only his name is called over them'. Before creation 'there was He and His name alone'. «רק ששמו נקרא עליהם» · *only that his name is called over them* · line 6372 (OCR)

4. **M4** · That is why Rosh Hashana says 'remembrance'. You only need to remember something far and of no account. To Him, the whole world's life is that small. *How:* 'Remembering applies only to something far and not counted.' The glow that is source to the worlds takes up no place before Him, so it can, as it were, be forgotten, and must be 'remembered'. «דהנה, זכירה אינו שייך אלא על דבר הרחוק ושאינו נחשב» · *for remembering applies only to something far away and not counted* · line 6381 (OCR)

5. **M12** · This does not mean He looks away. His care for each detail never stops for a moment. Remembrance asks for more: that what surrounds the world should shine inside it. *How:* The text heads off the mistake: 'individual providence is never withheld for a moment'. Remembrance is the union of the light that surrounds with the light that fills, openly. «וכללות ענין הזכרונות אין זה סותר לענין ההשגחה, דבאמת, ההשגחה פרטיות אינה נמנעת בכל רגע» · *remembrance does not contradict providence; in truth individual providence is not withheld for a single moment* · line 6381 (OCR)

6. **M13** · Two opposites, the G-d beyond the world and the G-d in it, can be one only where neither takes up any place: in Him Himself. *How:* Surrounding and filling are 'opposites'. ## SV3-D8 · Rest in itself

*Extends:* Extends D13 (plainness is the deepest). *stated: completion of the maamar (6428-6439) and the editors' summary (7607). ours: the steps.*

**Teaching.** The craftsman feels no joy while working. When he finishes, he is glad at the good work; then glad in his very self; then glad with a gladness that no longer hangs on anything. Shabbos climbs the same way, to a delight 'that has no hold in anything', and at the root it is 'rest in itself', not rest from something.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Finish one piece of work well. 2. Look at it and let yourself be glad it came out right. 3. Then let the gladness move from the thing to yourself, to the whole of it. 4. Then let it be gladness with no object. Just rest.

**From inside.** The craftsman; Friday night, Shabbos day, the third meal; 'rest after toil, and rest in itself'. **Lands.** After a finished task; on Shabbos afternoon.

## SV3-D9 · There is nothing else, beyond 'as nothing'

*Extends:* Extends D3 and D4. *stated: editors' summary, lines 7415-7416, 7480, 7493-7497. ours: the steps.*

**Teaching.** There is 'none besides You' (apart from Him nothing, yet with Him there is light) and 'nothing besides You' (less even than 'none'). There is the higher unity, 'all before Him is as nothing', and above it 'truth of truth: there is nothing else', which is beyond even that bittul. It is not reached by thinking but by stillness: 'I composed and quieted myself', the silence of the yechida; and the humble, not the sharp, reach it.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Begin with 'all before Him is as nothing'. 2. See that this still leaves a 'before Him', a light, a 'something' that is nothing. 3. Go quiet. Not thinking 'nothing', just not speaking inside. 4. Let 'there is nothing else' be not a thought but the stillness.

## SV3-D10 · The return is the deeper half

*Extends:* Sharpens D9 (the bottom sends you back) and D14. *stated: editors' summary, lines 7130-7168, 7703. ours: the steps.*

**Teaching.** The pull upward comes as a gift each morning and passes fast. The coming back down into the day is more your own than the rising, and it is rooted in His Essence, His wish for a home below. Going up is like taking off a coat; coming back is putting it on, and that is harder. Even one who never had great moments has this: he finds no rest without Torah and mitzvos all day. And through prayer as 'behind', especially the return, the essential hiddenness is drawn.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Remember a moment when you were lifted. 2. Notice it faded. Do not chase it. 3. Choose to come back into the day on purpose: put the coat back on. 4. Know that this coming back is rooted deeper than the rising.

**From inside.** A flame that needs air to stay on the wick; Aaron's sons who rose and did not return; taking off is easier than putting on. **Lands.** The first ordinary task after prayer.

## SV3-D11 · Your being is where only He works

*Extends:* Extends D1 and D10. *stated: Vaera 5668 (6256-6263) and the Rayatz (6603). ours: the steps.*

**Teaching.** What you feel of yourself is your powers: thinking, feeling, wanting. The plain fact that you exist is hidden, and making something from nothing belongs to the Essence alone. G-d's life in a thing is not like sight in an eye (a blind eye is still an eye); without that life the thing would not be there at all, not even its 'vessel'. In the future the body will come first.

**Hisbonenus.** 1. Notice your thoughts and feelings: these are your powers. 2. Now notice the plain fact underneath: you are here. 3. Know the text says this plain being is from the Essence alone, and is made now. 4. Feel it in the body: weight, breath.

**From inside.** The mother's hidden part from which the child's body comes; the eye that can be blind and still an eye, unlike a creature without G-d's life. **Lands.** The body, now.

**Quotes.** - «מה שמאיר בו עתה בגילוי הוא בחי' הכחות הנפשיים שבו, לא גוף ההתהוות שלו» · *what shines in him openly now is his soul-powers, not his very coming-into-being* · line 6263 (OCR) - «דאמיתת כח ההתהוות הוא בבחי' עצמות א"ס» · *the true power of bringing into being is in the Essence of the Infinite* · line 6256 (OCR) - «דבהעדר החיות ח"ו יבטל הדבר לגמרי מכל וכל מבלי שישאר אף הכלי» · *without the life, the thing would cease completely, without even the vessel remaining* · line 6603 (OCR) - «לעתיד, גם למטה יהי' העיקר הגוף» · *in the future, below too, the body will be the main thing* · line 6263 (OCR)

## SV3-D12 · The soundless cry

*Extends:* Extends D6 and D8; the shofar route. *stated: completion of the maamar (6400) and the Rayatz (6577, 6583). ours: the steps.*

**Teaching.** What reaches the Essence on Rosh Hashana is not a word but a cry of the heart with no sound, which 'moves the very essence of the soul', together with giving oneself over not by reason, clever as a person and following like an animal. ## Limits and notes

- The slice is mostly the Hemshech's ending, additions and a summary, not 3,000 lines of new maamarim. Lines 7020-7706 restate the whole Hemshech in short form. Where a chain rests on the summary, the full maamar earlier in the Hemshech is the place to deepen it (other slices). (ours) - The Rashab himself hesitates on one point and we kept it. He asks whether the 'son who becomes a servant' (the scholar studying with kabbalas ol) is above the simple doer. He settles it: 'all Israel have a share', and the scholar's edge is only that he has both levels (6351-6354; 7597). (stated) - The Rayatz notes that the hidden help of exile is 'not like being carried on the shoulder' as at the Exodus (7441). The guide should not promise felt help. It may promise held help. (stated; the guide rule is ours) - Not used: the Rayatz's comparison of simple Jews and sages of the nations (6614), and the long Kabbalistic mechanics of A"K, Atzilus and the letters (7173-7260, 7532-7545). Only the parts that turn into a lived step are used. (ours)
