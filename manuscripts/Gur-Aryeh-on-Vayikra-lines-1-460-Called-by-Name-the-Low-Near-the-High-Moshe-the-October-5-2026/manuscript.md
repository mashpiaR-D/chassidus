This is the first half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Vayikra: the offerings, the eight days of the Mishkan's start, the death of Nadav and Avihu, the laws of kosher and impure animals, and the laws of skin disease. It is the most legal part of Gur Aryeh so far. Most lines weigh one reading of a verse against another. But a few lines open onto the Maharal's larger thought.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **Called by name is love.** Why does Vayikra begin "He called to Moshe"? "כאשר יקרא אותו בשמו הוא הוראה על שחפץ בנקרא וידוע אליו בשמו" (4): "when He calls a person by his name, it shows that He wants the one called and knows him by his name." "ולפיכך הקריאה בשמו הוא חיבה" (4): "so calling by name is love."
- **The low is near the high.** The lower waters were promised a place on the altar. "לפי שכל הדברים תמיד הם הולכים להעלות" (97): "all things always go upward." "כלל הדבר, כי מיעוט המציאות של עולם הזה מורה על שהוא קרוב אל הנבדל" (97): "the rule is: having little of this world's being shows that one is near to what is set apart." And "כי הדברים אשר נדחים למטה הם מבקשים להתעלות יותר" (97): "things pushed down below seek all the more to rise."
- **Moshe the simple.** Moshe served in a plain white robe without a hem. "כי היה לו מעלה שהוא כמו שכל פשוט בתכלית הפשיטות" (243): "he had the level of a simple mind, simple to the end." "כי הלבנות מורה על הפשיטות, כי כל הגוונים הם צבע, חוץ מן הלבנות שאינו צבע" (243): "whiteness shows simplicity; every other color is dye, but white is no dye."
- **The menorah is from the world of oneness.** Moshe found the menorah hard to grasp. Why? "כי משה ראה במראה הנבואה מציאות העולם וצורתו והקשרו" (295): "Moshe saw in prophecy the world's being, its form and how it is tied together." But the menorah is above the world: "לפי שהיא מעולם האחדות, לכך היה מקשה שכל גופה אחת" (295): "it is from the world of oneness, so it was beaten from one piece, its whole body one."
- **The mind is wholly holy.** The olah atones for thought. "כי אשר חטא במזיד הוא חוטא כשהוא בדעתו ושכלו, שהוא כולו קודש לה'" (33): "one who sins knowingly sins with his mind, which is wholly holy to G-d."
- **Say "I sinned."** Happy the generation whose leader regrets his error: "שהוא אינו מתבייש לומר 'חטאתי'" (128): "he is not ashamed to say 'I sinned.'" And Moshe himself "הודה ולא בוש לומר" (292): "admitted and was not ashamed to say" he had forgotten.

## 2.1 The menorah and the world of oneness

This is the clearest line on oneness in the range. Moshe found four things hard: the menorah, the creeping things, the new moon, and slaughter. The Maharal's rule: "כי משה ראה במראה הנבואה מציאות העולם וצורתו והקשרו" (295). "כך נמצא בהשגת הנביא גם כן" (295): being as it is, "so it is found in the prophet's grasp." What is far from being, on either side, is hard to see. The creeping things are too far below. But the menorah is too far above: "ומזה תדע כי המנורה היא עליונה במעלה על העולם, כי מעולם האחדות הוא" (295): "the menorah is higher than the world, for it is from the world of oneness." That is why it was made from one piece of gold. It has no joins, because it belongs to the One.

## 2.2 The low is near the set apart

The waters below wept to be far from G-d, and were promised the altar (the salt and the water libation). The Maharal draws a rule. G-d chose for the altar the birds and animals that are chased, not the ones that rule. "כי העולם הזה הוא עולם הגשמי" (97): "this world is the world of the body." So whoever is great in it is tied to the body. But "כלל הדבר, כי מיעוט המציאות של עולם הזה מורה על שהוא קרוב אל הנבדל" (97). And "ולפיכך אל כל הנמצאים תחת שפלות יותר מדאי" (97), to all who are very low, a high level comes. "לפי שכל הדברים תמיד הם הולכים להעלות" (97): everything is always going up toward its root.

## 2.3 Moshe, a simple mind

"ומדתו של משה שהוא פשוט" (243): "Moshe's trait is that he is simple." The Maharal ties this to his teaching elsewhere that the One is simple, with no parts (Gur Aryeh Shemot 446, "what is set apart has no parts"). The nearer a person is to the One, the simpler he becomes. White is not one more color; it is the lack of dye.

## 2.4 G-d is the third

One who denies a deposit that only G-d saw: "הקב"ה הוא השלישי שבניהם" (162): "the Holy One is the third between them." "הוא כופר בו יתברך" (162): if he denies it, "he denies Him." G-d is present in every private deal between two people.

## 2.5 The Torah finishes creation

Why does the law of animals come before the law of people? Because the Torah follows the order of creation. "כמו שהבריאה הוא תיקונם, הכי נמי תורתם היא תקון הוויתם" (340): "as creation set them in order, so their Torah sets their being in order." "וזהו מפני שאין גמר בריאתן רק על ידי התורה, שהיא מתקן העולם" (340): "their creation is finished only through the Torah, which sets the world in order."

## 2.6 Where the cleaving is complete

In the Holy of Holies the high priest wears no gold, since the calf was gold. "לפי שההפכים לא יסבלו בענין אחד" (457): "opposites cannot bear each other in one thing." And there "ששם הדבוק לגמרי" (457): "the cleaving is complete." Outside, where the cleaving is partial, gold may be worn.

- **MH-S55 "Called by name" (4).** "כאשר יקרא אותו בשמו הוא הוראה על שחפץ בנקרא וידוע אליו בשמו" (4). Inside: I am not one of a crowd before Him. He knows my name and wants me. How to enter: before prayer, hear your own name in your mind as if He is calling it, the way He called "Moshe, Moshe." Then answer "Hineni," here I am. It differs from S60 The essential bond, which is the bond at the root. Here the bond is personal: He knows me by name.
- **MH-S56 "Low, so near" (97).** "כלל הדבר, כי מיעוט המציאות של עולם הזה מורה על שהוא קרוב אל הנבדל" (97). Inside: when I have little of this world, less power, less show, I am not far from Him. I am nearer. How to enter: when you feel small or pushed aside, remember the lower waters. Say: what is pushed down seeks to rise, and He lifts the low. It differs from MH-S48 Close in weakness, which is His nearness in illness. This is nearness in having little.
- **MH-S57 "Simple as white" (243).** "כי הלבנות מורה על הפשיטות, כי כל הגוונים הם צבע, חוץ מן הלבנות שאינו צבע" (243). Inside: a mind without dye. No show, no extra folds. How to enter: before a mitzvah, drop the extra thoughts about how it looks. Do it plainly, like a white robe without a hem. It differs from S56 The self quieted, which is a stilling of the self. This is a plainness of mind that has nothing added.

## 4.1 Say "I sinned" (`mh_say_i_sinned`, new)

"שהוא אינו מתבייש לומר 'חטאתי', ואינו אומר גדול אני וחשוב אני איך אומר 'חטאתי'" (128). And Moshe "שאמר 'שמעתי שאסור בקדשי דורות ושכחתי', וזה פשוט" (292): he said "I heard it, and I forgot." Practice: when you are wrong, say it plainly, even to those below you. Do not think "who am I to admit this." The more honored you are, the more your plain "I was wrong" is worth.

## 4.2 Return through the same door (`mh_return_with_same`, new)

"יותר טוב אם ישוב בתשובה באותו דבר בעצמו" (254): "it is better to return through that very thing." "כי בדבר שחטא בו יפוייס המלך" (254). Like one who stole the king's cup and then brings back a cup just like it. Practice: if you sinned with speech, return with good speech. If with money, return with giving. If with time, give time. Let the tool of the sin become the tool of the return.

## 4.3 First the worm, then the hyssop (`mh_worm_then_hyssop`, new)

The healing of the one with skin disease used a red worm and hyssop. For pride: "דודאי מתחלה יש להשפיל עצמו כתולעת, שהוא שפל מאד, שיתרחק מן העבירה" (389): "at first he must lower himself like the worm, very low, to get far from the sin." "ואחר כך יעמוד בענוה" (389): "then he will stand in humility." For "כי השפלות יותר מדאי אין ראוי, כי כל מדה טובה בעולם יש לו מצוע, ושני הקצות אינם טובות" (389): "too much lowliness is not right; every good trait has a middle." Practice: when you work on a bad trait, lean hard to the other side for a while. Then, once the trait is gone, come back to the middle. Do not stay in the extreme.

## 4.4 Find the true will (`mh_true_will`, new)

A person who must bring an offering may be pressed "until he says 'I want.'" Is that forced? The Maharal cites the Rambam: "יצרו הוא שמתגרה בו" (31): "it is his urge that presses on him." When it is removed, he comes back "עד שחזר אל הדעת הראוי לו" (31): "to the mind that is right for him." Practice: when you resist a mitzvah, ask: who is saying no, me or my urge? Say aloud: "I want to do His will." That is the true you speaking.

## 4.5 Pause between sections (`mh_pause_between`, new)

Even Moshe, learning from G-d, had pauses "כדי להתבונן בהבדלת הפרשיות" (49): "to reflect on the difference between the sections." All the more one learning from a person. Practice: when you learn, stop after each section. Ask: what did this one teach that the last did not? Then go on.

## 4.6 Do it with joy, as if He said it (`mh_joy_as_if_heard`, new)

Aharon and his sons did what Moshe told them "בשמחה לקיים מה שאמר להם משה כאילו שמעו מפי הגבורה" (247): "with joy, as if they heard it from the mouth of the Almighty." A great man told what to do by another does not usually rejoice. "אבל אהרן היה שמח כאילו בעצמו שמע" (247). Practice: when a teacher, parent or rabbi asks you to do a mitzvah, do not do it grudgingly. Do it as if G-d Himself had asked you.

## 4.7 Called by name (`mh_called_by_name`, new)

This is the practice for MH-S55 (4). Once a day, in a quiet moment, hear your name as He would say it. Answer "Hineni." Then ask: what does He want from me today?

## 4.8 Never drink (`mh_silence_for_mind`, extended as a guard)

"כי אין המקדש סובל השכרות, לפי שהוא הפך הקדושה" (268). Add to the guard: drink is the opposite of holiness, and the Sanctuary, where the Shechina dwells, cannot bear it. The guide never suggests drinking.

## 5. The big human questions

**Does G-d know me?** He knows you by your own name and wants you. Calling by name is love (4).

**Why am I so small in this world?** Having little of this world is nearness to what is set apart (97). What is pushed down seeks to rise.

**Is my urge to do wrong the real me?** No. The true will is to do good. The urge presses from outside (31).

**How do I fix a sin?** Return through the very thing you sinned with (254). Say "I sinned" without shame (128, 292).

**How do I fix pride?** First go very low, like the worm, then stand in the middle, like the hyssop (389). Every good trait has a middle.

**Is G-d present in my private dealings?** He is the third between any two people (162).

**Is the Torah just laws?** It finishes creation and sets the world in order (340).

**Why can't I grasp the highest things?** They are above the world's being, from the world of oneness (295). Even Moshe had to be shown.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **The menorah from the world of oneness** (295) is close to the Chabad teaching that the menorah stands for the seven emotional powers of the soul, all drawn from one source and made one (Likkutei Torah, Behaalotecha). The Maharal stresses that its oneness puts it above prophecy's grasp. Chabad stresses that the soul's powers can become one like it.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not named. But "this world is the world of the body" (97), and the low is near what is set apart, is the Maharal's way of saying that the hiding is strongest where the world is greatest. Tanya ch. 36 says the lowest world was made for the highest revelation. The two meet: the low is near the high. They differ: the Maharal speaks of the low as near the set apart; Tanya speaks of the low as the place where the Essence will be revealed.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** Moshe as "a simple mind, simple to the end" (243) uses the Rambam's language for the separate intellects, and the Rambam's Moshe (Guide 2:36; Yesodei HaTorah 7:6). The Maharal adds the white robe: simplicity is not something added but the lack of dye. This fits the negative way: we speak of Him by taking away. Chabad (Tanya ch. 2 and 18) says the soul is simple at its root, a part of G-d above.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "G-d is the third between them" (162) is the first half: He is in every deal. The menorah above prophecy (295) is the second: the world's being, as prophecy sees it, is not the world of oneness.
- **The true will** (31) is Chabad's teaching that every Jew wants, at the root, to be one with G-d (Tanya ch. 18-19, the hidden love). The Maharal and the Rambam say the urge is what presses; Tanya says the soul's love is hidden and wakes up.
- **Return through the same thing** (254) fits Tanya's teaching on return: the one who returns turns the sins into merits (Iggeret HaTeshuva ch. 8-9, based on Yoma 86b).
- **The middle way** (389) is the Rambam's (Eight Chapters ch. 4), which the Maharal cites. Chabad keeps it and adds that the soul's work is not only balance but turning the animal soul's force into holiness (Tanya ch. 12-13).
