## 1. What the range is

**Vaera (lines 397-602)** turns on God's words to Moses, "I appeared to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob as El Shaddai, but by My name YHVH I was not known to them." The Zohar reads it as a teaching about two kinds of seeing (lines 417-425), about Moses as a "voice" whose "word" is in exile (lines 462-468), and about the unity of opposites in the four elements (lines 428-446). A Raya Mehemna ("Faithful Shepherd") passage sets out the first commandment, to know God "in general and in detail" (lines 451-460). R. Elazar reads "know today and return it to your heart" (lines 485-495). The plagues follow, read as God's "hand" breaking the powers of Egypt level by level (lines 513-571), with travelers' tales and a long vision of the wars at the end of days (lines 594-602).

**Bo (lines 603-848)** opens with Job: why the accuser was given power over him (lines 607-637). Then comes the "great dragon" of the Nile, a dense myth of creation from the dark side (lines 638-675); the blood on the doorposts (lines 676-694); midnight and the death of the firstborn, told as a night journey of R. Chiya and R. Yosi to R. Shimon (lines 695-737); Solomon's "days of my breath" (lines 738-749); and a long Raya Mehemna on the commandments of Pesach, the firstborn, and tefillin (lines 780-848), which holds the range's most important statement on the unity (lines 816-828).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**Before the form, only He; the form is for us.** The central passage (Bo 15, lines 816-828) is one of the Zohar's clearest statements of how the One relates to the sefirot (His attributes). Before He made any form, הוה הוא יחידאי בלא צורה ודמיון, "He was alone, without form or likeness" (line 819). It is forbidden to make Him any image, "not with the letter heh, not with the letter yud, not even with the holy Name" (line 819). Then He made the form of the "supernal Adam" and came down into it, בגין דישתמודעון ליה במדות דיליה, "so that they would know Him through His attributes" (line 820), for דאי לא יתפשט נהוריה על כל בריין, איך ישתמודעון ליה, "if His light did not spread over all creatures, how would they know Him?" (line 820). But the form is not He:

> וכד אסתליק מינה, לית ליה מדה, ולא דמיון, ולא צורה (line 821)

"And when He withdraws from it, He has no measure, no likeness, no form."

Then the parable of the sea (lines 822-827). Water has no shape until it is poured into a vessel. The source is one; the spring from it is two; the great basin is three; it splits into seven streams: ten. And

> ואי יתבר אומנא אלין מאנין דתקין, יהדרון מיא למקור, וישתארו מאנין תבירין יבשין בלא מיא (line 823)

"If the craftsman breaks these vessels he made, the waters go back to the source, and the vessels are left broken and dry, without water." So the Cause of Causes made the ten sefirot. The first is the source, "and in it there is no end to the flowing of His light; therefore He called Himself Ein Sof (without end), and He has no likeness or form, ותמן לית מאנא למתפס ליה, and there is no vessel there to grasp Him" (line 824). Wisdom is not wise of itself but from the Wise who fills it, דאי הוה מסתלק מנה, אשתארת יבשה, "for if He withdrew from it, it would be left dry" (line 826). And ולית עליה אלהא, דיוסיף ביה, או יגרע ביה, "there is no God over Him who could add to Him or take from Him" (line 828).

**What this gives Chassidus (ours).** This passage is a source of the Tanya's claim (and the Gate of Unity's) that the attributes are nothing in themselves: Wisdom is wise only while He fills it. The same logic, pushed one step further, becomes the Tanya's "all is nullified before Him": if the highest vessel is dry without Him, how much more the world. The Zohar stops at the sefirot. Chassidus carries the "it would be left dry" all the way down to every creature (the Alter Rebbe's "if the power of the Maker left the made, it would return to nothing", which is F01).

**Two kinds of seeing.** God "appeared" (וארא) to the fathers through colors that are seen; Moses "knew" the hidden colors that shine (lines 418-425). The image is the eye itself: the eye shows three colors that do not shine. Then:

> סתים עינך, ואסחר גלגלך, ויתגליין אינון גוונין דנהרין (line 423)

"Close your eye, and turn your eyeball, and the colors that shine are revealed." These may be seen "only with closed eyes". So: סתים, חמי אספקלריא דנהרא, אתגלייא, חמי אספקלריא דלא נהרא, "closed, it sees the mirror that shines; open, it sees the mirror that does not shine" (line 425). Open seeing is called "seeing"; closed seeing is called "knowing".

**The three names are one, as a voice is one.** In the daily Shema: "YHVH, our God, YHVH: three names, how are they one?" (line 842). It is known "in the vision of the holy spirit", בחיזו דעינא סתימא, "in the seeing of the closed eye, to know that these three are one" (line 843). The picture: קול איהו חד, ואיהו תלתא גוונין, אשא ורוחא ומיא, וכלהו חד, "a voice is one, and it has three colors, fire and wind and water, and they are all one" (line 843). The voice a person makes in the Shema is the unity, "setting his will on the unity of all, מאין סוף עד סופא דכלא, from Ein Sof to the end of all" (line 844). And, generously: וכמה גוונין דיחודא אתערו, וכלהו קשוט, "many ways of the unity have been taught, and all of them are true" (line 845).

**The elements lend to each other.** Fire, wind, water and earth are opposites (hot and dry against cold and wet). God mixes them so that וכל חד וחד אוזיף לחבריה מדיליה, "each one lends its fellow from its own" (line 433), and wind stands between fire and water "and takes away the quarrel" (lines 434-435). אש רוח מים ועפר, כלהו אחידן דא בדא... ולא הוי בהו פרודא, "fire, wind, water, earth are all held one in the other, and there is no separation in them" (line 442). F09, the unity of opposites, in the plainest physical form.

**Know Him in general, then in detail (F12).** The Raya Mehemna's "first of all commandments" is למנדע ליה לקודשא בריך הוא בכללא, "to know the Holy One in general" (line 451): that there is a Ruler who made all. The end is to know Him "in detail" (line 451), which Moses taught only after forty years: "know today and return it to your heart that YHVH is Elokim" (line 454). אני ראשון בכלל, ואני אחרון בפרט. וכלא בכללא חדא, "I am first in the general and last in the detail, and all is one whole" (line 456).

**Both hearts in the one.** R. Elazar asks why the verse says "return it to your heart" (לבבך, with two bets) and why the knowing comes after. His answer (line 486):

> לבבך: יצר טוב ויצר רע, דאתכליל דא בדא, ואיהו חד, כדין תשכח כי ה' הוא האלהים

"'Your heart': the good inclination and the bad, included in each other, and it is one; then you will find that YHVH is Elokim." The unity above is found by making the unity in one's own heart first. The wicked do the opposite: מפרישין יצר רע מיצר טוב, ומתדבקין ביצר רע, "they separate the bad inclination from the good and cleave to the bad" (line 488). **(ours)** This is the root of a central Chabad move: the Tanya's "with all your heart, with both your inclinations" and the service of the animal soul. Here it is stated as a law of knowing: you cannot know the One while you are two.

**The world stands by His Name.** "The world is sustained by His holy Name" (line 404), and one who puts his trust in Him properly cannot be harmed "by all the people of the world" (line 403).

## 3. States of consciousness

**Knowing with the eyes closed (proposed new state ZS2-04).** Lines 423-425 describe a state, not only an idea: the outer eye closed, the inner light seen. "Seeing" is of what shows; "knowing" is of what hides and shines. Inside: the world goes dark and something clearer is there. It differs from S37 Depth (which is a point of understanding) and S36 Faith above knowing (which does not see). It is the Zohar's "closed eye" used again in the Shema (line 843). Entry: in the Shema, eyes covered, hold the three names as one voice.

**The voice without its word (proposed new state ZS2-05; ties to S73 Exile inside, S16 Knowing it, not feeling it).** Moses says "I am of uncircumcised lips". The Zohar: Moses is the voice, and the word (the Shekhinah, speech) is in exile. כל זמנא דדבור הוה בגלותא, קלא אסתלק מניה, ומלה הוה אטים בלא קול, "as long as speech was in exile, voice left it, and the word was blocked, without a voice" (line 464). At Sinai voice and speech joined, and אשתלים משה ואתסי, "Moses was made whole and was healed" (line 466). Of the community in exile: קלא אסתלק מינה, ומלה אשתכך מינה, "the voice left her and the word fell silent", "I was mute with silence" (line 468); Rachel "is dumb before her shearers" because "the voice was cut off from her" (line 546). Inside: I have a voice, a force in me that wants to speak, and no words come; or I have words and no force behind them. **(ours)** This is a precise map of a common inner exile: feeling without words, or words without feeling. The healing is their joining.

**Trust that holds (S84 Trust).** בטחו ביי' עדי עד: a person should "be strong in Him all his days" (line 403), and trust up to the place called עד, which "holds all sides"; past that is hidden, "up to here a person may look; from here on he may not" (line 401). Trust has a place to rest and a place it does not pry.

**Glad in bread and wine (S53 Glad).** "Go eat your bread in joy": when a person walks in His ways, "the Holy One brings him near and gives him quiet and rest, and then the bread and wine he eats and drinks are in the joy of the heart" (line 535). The joy is a sign of being accepted, not a goal.

**Guarded at the door (ties to S84 and to being held; proposed new state ZS2-06).** On the blood on the doorposts and the mezuzah, R. Abba: God says, "write My Name and put it on your door; ואת שרי לגו ביתא, ואנא אותיב לבר בפתחך לנטרא לך, you sit inside the house, and I will sit outside at your door to guard you" (line 683). Inside: safe at home with Someone keeping watch outside. It is S84 in its simplest picture, and it belongs to night: Israel were told not to go out until morning (lines 683, 686).

**Awake at midnight (ties to S47 Running, S52).** "Those whose desire is to remember the Holy One always do not give sleep to their heart" (line 557); at midnight a voice calls, מתערין קומו. ניימין אתערו, "wakers, rise; sleepers, wake" (line 700). David by day worked on judgments, by night on songs and praises until morning (line 498).

**Rejoicing in the telling (S53, S85 Seeing Him in what happens).** "Whoever tells of the going out of Egypt and rejoices in the telling will rejoice with the Shekhinah in the world to come" (line 781); דהאי איהו בר נש דחדי במריה, וקודשא בריך הוא חדי בההוא ספור, "this is a person who rejoices in his Master, and the Holy One rejoices in that telling" (line 781).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**The closed-eye unity of the Shema (proposed technique ZT2-02; ties to technique 8, Depth, and technique 14, Stopping at the edge).** How: in the Shema, with the eyes closed (line 843), hold the three names "YHVH, our God, YHVH" as three colors of one voice, fire, wind and water (line 843); make the voice and set the will "on the unity of all, from Ein Sof to the end of all" (line 844). What it is for: to know, not only to say, that they are one. Guard: the Zohar itself says the ways of unity are many and all true (line 845); this one is offered, not required. **(ours)** The Chabad practice of covering the eyes at the Shema has one of its roots here.

**Make the two hearts one before you look for the One (proposed technique ZT2-03; ties to technique 16, Love and awe).** How: before contemplating that "YHVH is Elokim", bring the two inclinations together in one aim (line 486); then love "with all your heart", both of them; "with all your soul", the soul set between them; "with all your might", taking them both up "to the place that has no measure", which is "the complete union, to love the Holy One as is fitting" (line 494). What it is for: love that leaves no part of the person outside. Guard: the Zohar blames not the bad inclination but its separation (line 488).

**Awakening from below.** "By an awakening below there is an awakening above; until there is an awakening below, there is none above" (line 592). All, good and bad, "hangs on the deed" (line 677): providence is stirred by what is done. And "you do a deed below, and I will break their power above" (line 764).

**Tell His wonders aloud.** One must tell His wonders before Him, not because He does not know, but because the words rise and the hosts above gather and praise (lines 783-784). **(ours)** A practice for any evening: tell over, out loud, what He did for you today.

**Confess first.** "When a person goes first and lists his sins, each one, he leaves the accuser no opening" (line 785). The accuser's way is to demand judgment first and then tell the sins; telling them yourself takes that away.

**Afternoon prayer with care.** "A person should always take care with the afternoon prayer, for it is the hour when judgment hangs, and one must direct his mind" (line 695).

**Walk with the good.** Rav Chisda moved from a place of trouble to live among people "the Holy One watches over for good", and prospered (line 741). Companions on the road "must walk with one heart" (line 572).

**Tefillin.** The hand tefillin and the head tefillin must be one, "with no separation between them" (line 835); one who wears them stands "in the secret of the upper color" (line 836), "to be in the supernal image" (line 848).

**Each year, wear the clothes of freedom.** The seven days of matzah are like a nobleman who every year, on the days he was raised up, puts on his robes again (line 770).

## 5. The big human questions

**Why are we here?** R. Chizkiyah: כמה אינון בני נשא אטימין, דלא ידעין, ולא מסתכלן, על מה קיימין בעלמא, "how sealed people are, who do not know and do not look at what they stand in the world for" (line 428). Man was made in His image, joined of two worlds, lower and upper (line 429), "so that he would study Torah and walk in His ways" (line 428).

**Suffering, and Job.** The range gives a hard answer and a deep one. The hard one: Job feared God for his wealth and children, which "is not fitting fear" (line 627); he was judged measure for measure for his advice to Pharaoh (line 617). The deep one: Job kept himself apart and gave nothing to the other side; he did not "include good and evil", so he was judged the same way: good, then bad, then good again (line 637):

> דהכי אתחזי לבר נש, למנדע טוב, ולמנדע רע, ולאהדרא גרמיה לטוב, ודא איהו רזא דמהימנותא (line 637)

"For this is fitting for a person: to know good, and to know evil, and to turn himself back to good; and this is the secret of faith." **(ours)** The guide should take the deep answer and leave the hard one: suffering as part of a whole life that knows both and comes back.

**Do not stand out alone.** When Job was singled out above, the accuser seized him. So the Shunammite said "I dwell among my people": לא בעינא לאפקא גרמי מכללא דסגיאין, "I do not want to take myself out of the whole of the many" (line 626). Being part of a people is a shelter.

**Our deeds affect Him.** When Israel's deeds are not right, "as it were, they weaken the strength of the Holy One; and when they do fitting deeds they give strength and power to the Holy One" (line 609), "give strength to God" (Psalms 68:35).

**Judgment and mercy in one hour (F09).** "As judgment was found, mercy was found, all in one hour" (line 687); דא מחי, ודא מסי, וכלא בשעתא חדא, "this one strikes and this one heals, all in one hour" (line 689).

**Breath.** Solomon's "days of my hevel" means breath, not vanity: "the world stands only on the breath of the mouths of schoolchildren who have not sinned" (line 745), and "everything made in the world is made with breath" (line 745).

**The whole world hangs on you.** A person should always see himself "as if the whole world hangs on him" (line 813).

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **The sea and its vessels (lines 822-828)** is the Zohar's own statement of the unity the Tanya builds on: no form but what He pours; without Him the vessel is dry.
2. **The closed eye (lines 423-425, 843)** gives a real practice for the Shema and names a real state.
3. **Voice without word (lines 464-468)** gives the guide language for feeling without words and words without feeling, and a picture of healing as their joining.
4. **"Your heart: both inclinations, and it is one" (line 486)** makes inner unity the door to knowing the One.
5. **"I sit outside at your door to guard you" (line 683)** is the simplest picture of being held at night.

Proposed for the map (details in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`): ZS2-04 *Knowing with the eyes closed*, ZS2-05 *The voice without its word*, ZS2-06 *Guarded at the door*; techniques ZT2-02 *The closed-eye Shema*, ZT2-03 *Two hearts made one*.
