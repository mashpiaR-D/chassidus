## What this cluster shows

These thirteen subjects are the Torah and the study of it. Here the one idea turns from things to words. If nothing exists besides Him, then what He says is not separate from Him. The Torah is 'His wisdom and will', and He and His wisdom are one (Tanya 4:5), so a person who knows a halachah holds His will in his mind in 'a wondrous union with no parallel' (Tanya 5:6). The same speech, as letters, is the substance of every creature: a stone's name is the combination of letters that is its life (SH 1:6). At Sinai the decree separating above and below was annulled, and 'Anochi', the Essence and not a radiance, spoke into the lowest world (Toras Shmuel 5627; Purim 5711). From there the other subjects follow. The Oral Torah births the hidden will into detailed law and is Malchus of Atzilus, 'all one' (Iggeret HaKodesh 26, 29). Each halachah is a spark of the Shechinah raised by clarifying it. A chiddush frees wisdom from exile, and the upper worlds come to hear it (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:26). Lishmah is study with no self in it (Tanya 5:12; the Rebbe 5711). The inner Torah is the soul of the revealed (the Rashab), and Chassidus is the yechidah of Torah, whose forms do not truly conceal the Ein Sof (Inyanah 3:6, 9:1). Parable is the method by which this is known from below, and its limit is where the unity shows (SH 6:7). Outside wisdom is judged by bitul (the Tzemach Tzedek on the Rambam and Aristotle). The tie is stated for most of the cluster. It is partly our reading for Written and Oral Torah, lishmah, new insight, parable and philosophy, and those entries mark where.

## S076 · Torah as His wisdom and will · אורייתא וקודשא בריך הוא כולא חד

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding **Search terms:** אורייתא וקוב"ה · חכמתו ורצונו · אורייתא מחכמה · תורה שהיא חכמתו

> Torah as His wisdom and will is the unity of God as His own mind given into ours: the Essence contracted, without ceasing to be one with Him, into laws, letters and parchment that a human mind and hand can hold.

## Derivation

1. No thought can grasp Ein Sof, and the same is true of His will and wisdom (Tanya 4:6). (*stated*)
2. Torah is His wisdom and will. By the Rambam's unity, He is the knower and the known, so 'the Torah and the Holy One are all one' (Tanya 4:5). (*stated*)
3. Therefore He contracted His will and wisdom into the 613 commandments, their laws and the letters of Tanach. Like water, it came down through the chain of worlds until it was clothed in physical things (Tanya 4:7-10). (*stated*)
4. To grasp the Torah is to grasp Him: 'when it is grasped and clothed in Torah and its commandments, it grasps the Holy One Himself', as one who embraces a king through his garments (Tanya 4:12-13). (*stated*)
5. The Torah is above the worlds, which draw their life from it. The supernal will is the Torah itself, called sovev (Tanya 23:11; F04). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: sustaining the worlds is not the Torah's essence, since they are as nothing before it. Its essence is being wholly one with the Ein Sof clothed in it (Inyanah 3:4). That oneness with the Essence shows in its power to 'spice' the evil inclination, a power only the Essence has (Inyanah 20:1-2; F11). (*stated*)
7. The Maggid draws a structural consequence: He has no parts, so the Torah has no parts, and each mitzvah contains all 613. The Sfas Emes puts a point of life from Torah in everything, hidden, reached by bitul. Setting these next to Chabad's union-in-comprehension as three sides of one claim is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 4:5-13 (Torah is His wisdom and will, one with Him; contracted into commandments, laws and letters; the king embraced through his garments). Tanya 23 (the supernal will is the halachah itself; Torah is sovev). Iggeret HaKodesh 26:19 (the Oral Torah is Malchus of Atzilus, where 'He, His will and His wisdom are all one'; S079).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located on the phrase in this pass. His account of letters rooted in the Essence (Gate of Unity 38, S077) gives the same structure.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located on the phrase in this pass (Derekh Mitzvotekha has no hit on אורייתא וקוב"ה; Or HaTorah not searched).
- **Maharash.** Not quoted on the phrase. Toras Shmuel 5627 ties Torah and mitzvot to 'Anochi', the Essence, which alone brings 'below as above' (S084).
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Eitz HaChaim 11 (OCR): a halachah is 'complete holiness, His wisdom and will', yet because it is clothed in physical cases one can learn it and forget the Giver (S082).
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (OCR): God, Torah and Israel are three knots, each with a revealed and a hidden side (S085).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 3:4-6 (Torah's essence is its union with the Ein Sof; every part of Torah is one with it, but the point shows most in Chassidus). Inyanah 20:1 (the Torah's union with the Essence shows as the 'spice' for the yetzer). Purim 5711: the Torah is one not only with 'the Holy One' but with the Essence, 'Anochi = I wrote and gave Myself' (S081).
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (63:1): 'Torah and the Holy One are one', and since He has no parts neither does the Torah; each mitzvah includes all 613. The Besht (Tzava'at HaRivash 111): the Torah is His garment (S080). The Sfas Emes (Noach 5632, 2:4): in everything a point of life from the Torah, hidden at times, returned to by true bitul. These are real differences of emphasis. Chabad puts the union in comprehension (knowing a law). The Sfas Emes puts it in the life-point inside things, reached by bitul. The Maggid puts it in the partlessness that makes each mitzvah whole.

## S077 · The letters · אותיות

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F05 The Essence beyond both · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** אותיות · צירופי אותיות · אותיות התורה · כ"ב אותיות

> The letters are the unity of God as the substance of every created thing: His speech contracted into combinations that are each thing's name and life, and rooted in 'essential letters' within the Ein Sof itself.

## Derivation

1. Creation is by speech, and speech is letters. The letters of the Ten Utterances stand in each creature and give it life; if they withdrew it would return to nothing (SH 1:3-5). (*stated*)
2. A thing not named in the Utterances, like a stone, receives life through permutations and substitutions of letters through the 231 gates. Its Hebrew name is those letters, 'and that is the life of the stone' (SH 1:6-8; G01). (*stated*)
3. The name is a vessel for contracted life, and it has the power to create yesh from nothing 'because the Torah and the Holy One are all one' (SH 1:9). (*stated*)
4. A letter is not a code laid on a thing. Likkutei Torah distinguishes ink letters, which clothe the parchment and remain separate from it, from engraved letters, which are of the stone itself (Bechukotai 2:3-5). The Mitteler Rebbe: the root of letters is in the very essence of the soul, and through them a chain runs from the essence to the last act. Above, the first 'engraving' is of essential letters within the Or Ein Sof itself (Gate of Unity 38:4-47). (*stated*)
5. So the letter is both the most particular thing there is (one combination) and continuous with the Essence. This is G04 inside a single letter. Each half is stated; putting them together is ours. (**reading**)
6. The human side. The Besht: every letter holds worlds, souls and Godliness, and letters join into words that make true unions (Tzava'at HaRivash 75). One can always cleave to the letters of Torah, His garment (111). The Sfas Emes: all things are vessels for the letters hidden inside them. (*stated*)
7. The task below: 'wherever our foot steps', purify the earth with the letters of Torah and prayer (Hayom Yom 5 Adar I). Tying that instruction to the dwelling below (F07) is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 1:3-9 (the letters of the Utterances in every creature; the stone's name is its life). SH 7:22-23 (every substitution of letters marks a further descent of the light). Likkutei Torah, Bechukotai 2 (ink against engraving; the letters of the soul's speech are garments, while those contained in the intellect are 'engraved').
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Gate of Unity 38: the soul is 'full of letters' from its very coming-to-be. Letters of thought and speech are rooted in its essence, and the 'engraved' letters in Tehiru Ila'ah are essential letters in the Or Ein Sof.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located on letters in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located on letters in this pass. He places creation by speech ('letters') as a mere radiance below 'Anochi' (S078).
- **Rashab.** Not located on letters in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 5 Adar I (purify the earth with the letters of Torah and prayer). Hayom Yom 9 Sivan (read, not quoted): the air is purified only by the letters of Torah, and 'Mishnah is the letters of neshamah'.
- **The Rebbe.** Compiled the Hayom Yom entries above. Not separately quoted on letters here.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Tzava'at HaRivash 75:2-3, 111:3): worlds, souls and Godliness in every letter; hear the Shechinah, the world of speech, speaking in each word. The Maggid (63:1): at the primordial will all 613 commandments and all the letters are a simple unity. The Sfas Emes (Noach 5632): letters are lights above comprehension, and all things are vessels for the letters hidden in them. Pillar 12 records Ropshitz (sparks are letters) and Zhitomir (the world is the trace of Torah); these were not re-verified here. There is a real difference. Chabad's letters are mainly cosmological, the life-force of each creature. The Besht's are devotional, a body to cleave to in prayer and study. Ropshitz makes them sparks to be freed.

## S078 · The ten utterances · עשרה מאמרות / דבר ה'

**Faces:** F01 The world said into being now · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** עשרה מאמרות · דבר ה' · לעולם ה' דברך · מאמרות

> The Ten Utterances are the unity of God as creation spoken now: His speech, one with Him, standing in each thing and bringing it from nothing at every moment.

## Derivation

1. 'Forever, O God, Your word stands in the heavens.' The Besht's reading, via SH 1: the words stand in the firmament now, and if they left for an instant heaven and earth would return to nothing (SH 1:3-4). (*stated*)
2. His 'speech' is a name given by parable to the going-out of light and life from concealment into revelation. These are the Ten Utterances, and also all of Tanach that the prophets grasped (Tanya 21:3-4). (*stated*)
3. Unlike a person's speech, His speech remains united with His Essence after it has gone out into creation. The change is only toward the creatures, through contractions that are 'hiding of the face' (Tanya 21:5-7; F02, F03). (*stated*)
4. The Maggid: behind the utterances is the primordial will, 'not even called a saying', in which all worlds are held together. The utterances unfold it into particulars (Maggid 63). (*stated*)
5. The Maharash puts the Utterances in their place: creation from nothing is 'only a mere radiance', speech that exists only for another. 'Anochi', the Essence, is beyond it (Toras Shmuel 5627). This is the kept F05 tension, since IH 20 and the Rebbe say creation is the Essence's own power. (*stated*)
6. The Tzemach Tzedek argues it philosophically: the endless motion of the spheres can come only from infinite power, 'His command and speech', which renews creation every day (Sefer HaChakirah). (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe (5738): the Ten Utterances correspond to the Ten Commandments. The wise draw the Commandments into the Utterances, and so the world made by speech becomes a dwelling (F07). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** SH 1 (the Besht's reading; the Utterances stand in every creature, even the inanimate). Tanya 20-21 (speech as parable; His speech one with His Essence after creation; concealment only toward the creatures).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** The Gate of Unity has no hit on עשרה מאמרות. Its treatment of speech-letters (S077) carries the idea.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Sefer HaChakirah (Derech Emunah, ch. 3): the infinite motion of the spheres is His speech, renewing creation daily. He accepts the philosophers' premises and draws continuous creation from them.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627 (Matzos Yei'achel, summary): creation by speech is a radiance; 'Anochi' is the Essence, from which alone comes 'below as above'.
- **Rashab.** Not located on the Utterances in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 29 Sivan (spine F01): the world renewed every moment by particular providence. Not re-quoted here.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Vayeshev 5738: the Utterances correspond to the Commandments; the Torah study of the wise draws from the Essence and 'increases peace'.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (17:1): 'the power of the maker in the made', the power of speech in the heavens. The Maggid (63:1): the primordial will before any saying. Ohr HaMeir (RH 15, read, not quoted): renewal of creation every day, so that each day could revert. Radomsk (S083): the daily renewal is carried by the tzaddik's chiddushei Torah. The Maharash's 'mere radiance' is a real difference from the Rebbe's 'the physical comes from the Essence'.

## S079 · Written and Oral Torah · תורה שבכתב ושבעל פה

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** תורה שבכתב · תורה שבעל פה · תושב"כ · תושבע"פ

> The Written and Oral Torah are the unity of God as concealment and birth: the will held whole and hidden in Chochmah (the 'father'), and brought out into revealed, detailed limbs by Malchus (the 'mother'), where He and His word are one.

## Derivation

1. The will clothed in the 613 commandments of the Written Torah is hidden and sealed. 'Bind them for a sign on your hand' does not say how, what or where (Iggeret HaKodesh 29:22-24). (*stated*)
2. The Oral Torah reveals it, as a mother brings the father's seed, where all the limbs are concealed, into a whole child. The Written Torah comes from supernal Chochmah, the 'father'. The Oral Torah is 'your mother's teaching' (29:25). (*stated*)
3. The Oral Torah, even laws of forbidden and permitted, is Malchus of Atzilus, and in Atzilus 'He, His will and His wisdom, clothed in His speech called Malchus, are all one' (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:19). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab adds the danger. In the Written Torah the divine light is felt ('God spoke', 'I am Havayah'). The Oral Torah is clothed in physical cases, false claims and the forbidden and permitted. Its task is refinement, and one can learn it and forget the Giver, so it needs love and awe (Eitz HaChaim 11-12). (*stated*)
5. The pair therefore maps onto the two unities. In the Written Torah the Source is heard. In the Oral Torah it is hidden in the details of the lower world and must be found there (F02, F03). (**reading**)
6. R. Tzadok: prophecy is His speech in the prophet's throat and cannot hold two opposites. The sage of the Oral Torah cleaves to His wisdom 'in thought', where two opposites can stand in one subject. That is why 'these and these are the words of the living God' (Resisei Layla 16; F09). (*stated*)
7. Likkutei Torah contrasts the Written Torah, in ink on parchment, with the Tablets' engraving: two degrees of union between a letter and its source (Bechukotai 2:2). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Iggeret HaKodesh 29:22-26 (father and mother; the Oral Torah births the hidden will into limbs, 'halachos without number'). Iggeret HaKodesh 26:19 (the Oral Torah is Malchus of Atzilus, all one). Iggeret HaKodesh 1 (read, not quoted: the Oral Torah is the revelation of the supernal will). Likkutei Torah, Bechukotai 2 (ink and engraving).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Eitz HaChaim 11-12 (OCR): the Oral Torah is called a 'tree of good and evil' only because of its garments. The Written Torah shows the light more. Study without love and awe falls into selfhood.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 9 Sivan (read, not quoted): reviewing Mishnah by heart, 'Mishnah is the letters of neshamah'.
- **The Rebbe.** Not quoted here on the pair.
- **Other schools.** R. Tzadok (Resisei Layla 16): the Oral Torah's disputes are 'words of the living God', because the sage cleaves to divine thought, which bears opposites. Pillar notes (not re-verified): R. Tzadok elsewhere calls the Oral Torah the light seen from within the dark. Torah Or on seed and birth was not re-verified; Iggeret HaKodesh 29 gives the same image.

## S080 · Torah study · תלמוד תורה

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F07 The home below · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** תלמוד תורה · לימוד התורה · עסק התורה · לימוד תורה

> Torah study is the unity of God as a union of minds: in knowing a law the knower's intellect and His wisdom enclose each other, 'a wondrous union with no parallel'.

## Derivation

1. The law in any case is His wisdom and will, even if the case never arises (Tanya 5:4-5). (*stated*)
2. In knowing it, the mind encloses the law and is clothed in it. So the mind grasps His will, and His wisdom is inside the knower (Tanya 5:5-8). (*stated*)
3. Hence 'a wondrous union with no parallel in physical things', higher than commandments of deed and even than spoken Torah. Torah is 'food': it becomes one with the soul, as bread becomes flesh (Tanya 5:6-10). (*stated*)
4. Study draws the Or Ein Sof 'infinitely more' than the practical commandments, because it is His Chochmah-Binah-Daas (Tanya 37:24, 27). (*stated*)
5. Yet one interrupts study for a deed no one else can do, because the purpose is the dwelling below, 'the physical earth precisely' (Tanya 37:26). The tension between the higher union (knowing) and the purpose below (the deed) is kept (F07). (*stated*)
6. The Maggid and the Besht move the union into the letters. Study in love and awe draws life to all creatures through the letters (Maggid 34). One cannot always cleave to Him in fervor, but one can always cleave to the letters of Torah, His garment (Tzava'at HaRivash 111). (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe (5738): through study the wise draw from the Essence above the root of the worlds and so complete the dwelling. The Rayatz: revealed Torah, Chassidus and practice are all needed, and the end of study is practice (G11). (*stated*)

## S081 · Lishmah · תורה לשמה

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F07 The home below **Search terms:** לשמה · שלא לשמה · תורה לשמה · לשם שמים

> Lishmah is the unity of God as the learner's own absence: study with no self in it, so that nothing stands between the knower and the Torah that is one with Him.

## Derivation

1. The Alter Rebbe defines lishmah by the unity itself: 'to bind his soul to God through grasping the Torah, each according to his mind' (Tanya 5:12). (*stated*)
2. Its opposite is self-interest. Study for one's own honor clothes the Torah in kelipas nogah, and the Torah is 'in exile within the kelipah' until the person returns (Tanya 39:19-20). (*stated*)
3. Linking that exile to F08, where separateness, being something in one's own right, is the root of kelipah (Tanya 22), is our reading. (**reading**)
4. The Besht (via the Toldos): lishmah itself has two levels. The first adorns the bride with garments (study in 'garments', which the passage also links to reward or some benefit; and pilpul that breaks the husks), and it is called 'not lishmah' only relative to the second. The second strips the garments and cleaves to the inwardness of the letters, 'kisses'. A complete person does both every day, as the Ari did. (*stated*)
5. The Degel: lishmah is the mistress, and through it God and His Shechinah are enthroned in all worlds and His kingship is revealed. Not-lishmah is the handmaid, allowed 'perhaps I shall be built through her'. (*stated*)
6. Why not-lishmah leads to lishmah. R. Tzadok: the act of the commandment itself puts holiness into the heart, until it opens the gates of holiness to full lishmah, if not in this incarnation then in another (Takanat HaShavin 10:7). Tanya: 'certainly', in this incarnation or another (39:20). (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe's highest mode: the Torah is one with the Essence ('Anochi: I wrote and gave Myself'). As the Holy One is not a means to anything else, the purpose of study is the Torah itself, without even the calculation of becoming attached, 'in complete bitul' (Purim 5711). (*stated*)
8. The Rashab's warning: Oral Torah learned without love and awe makes one a yesh and ends in study not-lishmah outright (Eitz HaChaim 12). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 5:12 (definition), Tanya 39:17-40:7 (plain study, not-lishmah and its exile, 'certainly comes to lishmah', what rises to which world), Kuntres Acharon 3 (read, not quoted: flawed prayer above Torah not-lishmah).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Eitz HaChaim 12-14 (OCR): lishmah requires love and awe, which come from the inner Torah; 'zachah' means joining the tree of knowledge to the tree of life.
- **Rayatz.** Not located on lishmah in this pass.
- **The Rebbe.** Purim 5711: three modes of study. From the body's love of wisdom; from the soul, as the connector between Israel and God; from the Torah itself, one with the Essence, in complete bitul.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Vaetchanan 42): two studies, both daily. The Degel (Lech Lecha 14): mistress and handmaid. The not-lishmah the Shechinah herself allows can despise the mistress. R. Tzadok (Takanat HaShavin 10): the act inserts holiness; not-lishmah is still a rabbinic transgression that is permitted for its end. Pillar 12 (not re-verified) adds R. Nosson (a touch of not-lishmah is needed), Kometz HaMinchah (never for awe), Sochatchov and Krakow.

## S082 · Halachah · הלכה

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F11 Darkness turned to light · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** הלכה · הלכות · הלכה למשה מסיני · פסק דין

> Halachah is the unity of God as His will in the particular case: every 'permitted, kosher, exempt, liable' is a drawing-down of His inner will and a spark of the Shechinah.

## Derivation

1. A law for a case that may never arise is still His wisdom and will (Tanya 5:4-5). (*stated*)
2. Every law, whether 'permitted, kosher, exempt or liable', is a particular drawing-down of the inner supernal will itself. So thinking and speaking a law unites the soul with Him, not merely as a chariot (Tanya 23:8-10). (*stated*)
3. 'The word of God is halachah.' Each halachah is a spark of the Shechinah that fell into kelipas nogah at the breaking of the vessels. Clarifying a law raises it from the husks that hid it (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:25-28). (*stated*)
4. Halachah is therefore the lowest and most particular form of Torah: cows, donkeys, two people holding a garment. That makes it the place of refinement (Rashab, Eitz HaChaim 11; F11, G05). (*stated*)
5. The same lowliness is a risk: one can learn the laws and forget the Giver (Rashab). (*stated*)
6. The task of Chassidus is to understand each law in its root and in avodah (Hayom Yom 11 Adar II). Every matter of the inner Torah shows in the revealed Torah, 'down to practical law' (Inyanah 7:4; the four cubits, Inyanah 20-22). (*stated*)
7. Outside Chabad, R. Nachman: a ruling is the peace that settles the holy dispute. Learning the codes separates good from evil and unites the two inclinations (Likutei Moharan 8, 62). ## S083 · New insight in Torah · חידושי תורה

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F01 The world said into being now · F07 The home below **Search terms:** חידושי תורה · חידוש · שמים חדשים · מחדש

> A new insight in Torah is the unity of God as wisdom freed from exile: a new combination that reveals what was hidden in the husks and makes new heavens.

## Derivation

1. The reason behind a halachah comes from supernal Chochmah, whose sparks fell into the kelipos. Clarifying a law lifts it out of the husks that hid it (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:25). (*stated*)

## S084 · The giving of the Torah · מתן תורה

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below · F09 The unity of opposites **Search terms:** מתן תורה · קבלת התורה · הר סיני · אנכי

> The giving of the Torah is the unity of God as the Essence saying 'Anochi' into the lowest world: the decree separating above and below annulled, so that physical things can become holy.

## Derivation

1. Before Sinai, 'the heavens are the heavens of God and the earth He gave to men'. At Sinai He annulled the decree (Midrash Shemos Rabbah 12, cited by the Maharash, the Rashab and the Rebbe). (*stated*)
2. The Maharash: creation from nothing is a radiance. 'Anochi' is His Essence, not a radiance, and only from it comes leaving the world's limits and 'below as above' (Toras Shmuel 5627). (*stated*)
3. The Rashab: before Sinai Atzilus did not join Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah. Sinai made the revelation of Atzilus in the lower worlds possible, and this comes from the essence of Atzilus (Hemshech Ayin Beis, Shavuos 5673). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe: after Sinai the physical object becomes the object of the mitzvah. The joining of upper and lower, which are opposites, is done by the Ein Sof that is above both (Purim 5711; F09). (*stated*)
5. Tanya 36: at Sinai 'you were shown to know that Havayah is Elokim, there is none besides Him', with physical sight, because His will was revealed with no concealment. The souls expired at each word and were revived by the 'dew of Torah'. It was a foretaste of the messianic dwelling (F07). (*stated*)
6. 'Anochi' contains all 248 positive commandments and 'You shall have no other' all 365 negative ones; they are the commandment and warning against idolatry, and the Alter Rebbe explains this at once through His unity, 'yachid u'meyuchad' before and after creation (Tanya 20:1-3). (*stated*)
7. The Maggid: all the commandments were said in one utterance and then unfolded. R. Nachman: Israel received the Torah only by casting away their 'wisdoms'. There is a real difference here. In Chabad, Sinai is seeing and knowing ('you were shown to know'). In Breslov, receiving is faith beyond wisdom. (*stated*)

## S085 · Revealed and hidden Torah · נגלה ונסתר / פנימיות התורה

**Faces:** F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F05 The Essence beyond both · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** פנימיות התורה · נגלה · נסתר · רזין דאורייתא

> Revealed and hidden Torah are the unity of God as the body and soul of one Torah. The inner Torah, the least clothed, is where the Ein Sof present in all of Torah shows, and it gives the revealed Torah its life.

## Derivation

1. God, Torah and Israel each have a revealed and a hidden side. The revealed Torah is in the Talmud and codes; the hidden is in Midrash, the Zohar and Kabbalah. Chassidus joins the hidden and the revealed (Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus). (*stated*)
2. The inner Torah is the tree of life. It is not clothed in physical garments; it speaks of Godliness, and its whole point is to know God and come to love and awe (Rashab, Eitz HaChaim 13). (*stated*)
3. It is the soul of the revealed Torah and gives it life. Without it, the revealed Torah one learns is 'a body without a soul' (Eitz HaChaim 15, from the Zohar on PaRDeS). (*stated*)
4. Every part of Torah (pshat, remez, drush, sod) clothes the Ein Sof in a form that defines it. Chassidus is defined by no form, so its forms do not truly conceal the Ein Sof. Chassidus is the yechidah of the Torah and raises the other four (Inyanah 3:6, 9:1). (*stated*)
5. Why it is revealed now: 'only in these last generations is it permitted and a mitzvah to reveal this wisdom' (the Ari via Iggeret HaKodesh 26:5). The Rashab gives the reason: souls have descended and concealment has grown, so knowledge of God must be given explicitly (F11). (*stated*)
6. Both kinds of study are needed. The Besht's two studies, every day. The Rayatz (Hayom Yom 5 Adar II). The Rashab: the main study is halachah, for refinement, but one who does not learn the inner Torah 'is liable for his soul'. (*stated*)
7. The kept dispute: the Zohar (Ra'aya Mehemna) calls forbidden and permitted the 'tree of good and evil', which would make the revealed Torah lower. The Alter Rebbe rejects that reading: all of the revealed Torah is a 'tree of life' (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:4). (*stated*)

- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (OCR): revealed and hidden in God, Torah and Israel; Chassidus joins them. Hayom Yom 5 Adar II.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 3:5-6, 8:5-9:1 (Chassidus as the yechidah of Torah, enlivening all of PaRDeS), 25-29 (why the inner Torah is revealed now).
- **Other schools.** The Besht (Vaetchanan 42): one study breaks husks, the other cleaves to the soul of the letters; both daily. The Rebbe and the Rashab rank the inner Torah as soul to body. The Besht instead gives the two studies two tasks, breaking and cleaving, without calling one the soul of the other.

## S086 · Parable and meaning · משל ונמשל

**Faces:** F12 Knowing the unity · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** משל ונמשל · והנמשל · עד"מ · המשל

> Parable and meaning are the unity of God as known from below: the soul's workings make His unity graspable, and the point where every parable fails ('not like it at all') is the unity itself.

## Derivation

1. 'From my flesh I see God.' Chassidus brought the Torah's sod close to the mind through analogies from the soul's powers, so that anyone can grasp Godliness (Inyanah 1:8; the Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus p. 5). (*stated*)
2. Even the basic terms are parables: His speech is called 'speech' only by analogy (Tanya 21:3). (*stated*)
3. Every analogy fails at exactly the point of unity. Soul and body are separate in their root, but the world is not separate from His word: it is like the sun's light within the sun (SH 6:7; G06). (*stated*)
4. The Maharash states that faith belongs to sovev, because for memale 'from my flesh I see' gives knowledge felt like sight (Toras Shmuel 5627). He is not discussing parable. Reading this as the range of the soul-analogy (it reaches memale, not sovev) is ours. (**reading**)
5. Strashelye: the Ari hid the wisdom in parables and riddles, and later kabbalists took them almost literally, 'with great corporealization'. The Besht, and after him the Maggid, made new parables from the bond of soul and body to bring it close to the mind. The parable both conceals and reveals (F03); that link to F03 is ours. (**reading**)
6. The Maggid tells the tzimtzum itself as a parable: a father narrowing his mind for his child's delight (Maggid 100). (*stated*)
7. That the parable is itself an instance of the unity, an image that the meaning fills as His word fills a thing, is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 20-21 (the soul's speech as parable, and where it fails). SH 6:7 ('not like the thing at all'). Likkutei Torah, Bechukotai 2:6 (from the physical parable the meaning above is understood). 'From my flesh I see' occurs on about 40 lines of Likkutei Torah.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** The Gate of Unity uses משל about 113 times and grades analogies (19:10: 'the most true likeness' for revealing the hidden is movement from the living, and letters of thought and speech). Read, not quoted.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not quoted here. 'From my flesh I see' occurs on 16 lines of the non-OCR Or HaTorah.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: 'from my flesh I see' applies to memale, as knowing; sovev is faith. Other places (lines 1454, 5818, read in the grep) use the same formula for Atzilus against BYA and for one utterance being as nothing before the soul.
- **Rashab.** Inyanah 28:3-29:1 records that his maamarim bring Chassidus 'with clarifying parables', which is why he is called 'the Rambam of Chassidus'.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus p. 5 (OCR): Chassidus, though deep divine inquiry, explains everything 'with examples and parables close to comprehension' (S087).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 1:8.
- **Other schools.** Strashelye (Sha'arei HaYichud VeEmunah, preface 21-25): the parable of the city-dweller and the villager who see the king (read, not quoted); later kabbalists took the Ari literally; the Besht and the Maggid repaired it with soul-body parables. The Maggid (100:1): the father parable for tzimtzum. The Besht (Keter Shem Tov 1:39, spine F03): the king's illusory walls.

## Thin or forced?

Partly. The method is stated (Inyanah 1:8) and so is its limit (SH 6:7). Applying the Maharash's memale/sovev line to parable (step 4) and calling the parable itself a face of the unity (step 7) are ours. This subject is better read as the grammar G06 than as a face.

## S087 · Chassidus and Kabbalah · חסידות / קבלה

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F12 Knowing the unity · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** חסידות · תורת החסידות · דברי אלקים חיים · קבלת האריז"ל

> Chassidus and Kabbalah are the unity of God as one hidden wisdom made to live in the mind: Kabbalah maps the worlds, and Chassidus shows the Ein Sof present in them and brings it into understanding, feeling and deed.

## Derivation

1. The wisdom was hidden and given only to individuals. The Ari said that in the last generations it is 'permitted and a mitzvah' to reveal it (Iggeret HaKodesh 26:5; Inyanah 25:5). (*stated*)
2. Strashelye: the Ari still hid it in parables, and later kabbalists turned it into corporealism. The Besht and the Maggid explained it through soul-body parables, 'to bring it close to the mind', as a path of service and of cleaving to His unity. (*stated*)
3. The Rebbe: the matters of Kabbalah are explained to the intellect only through Chassidus, and through it their inner content comes alive (Inyanah 16:5). Chassidus draws down the Ein Sof, the Essence, so it is not one more layer on top of Kabbalah (Inyanah 2-3). (*stated*)
4. The Rayatz: Chabad Chassidus is a divine inquiry that opens Chochmah and Binah, to know the One who spoke and the world came to be (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus). The Rashab: love and awe depend on knowing Godliness (Eitz HaChaim 13). (*stated*)
5. Limits are kept. Strashelye forbids speaking of the inwardness of Adam Kadmon, allowing only what is revealed of it in the worlds (Sha'arei Avodah 1:25). The Alter Rebbe: 'Chassidus is Shema Yisrael', to reach above the intellect, and to understand that too with the intellect (Hayom Yom 19 Cheshvan). (*stated*)
6. Each Rebbe names its point from a different side (spine). The Tzemach Tzedek: 'Chassidus is vitality', bringing light even into the not-good (F11). (*stated*)
7. That Chassidus stands to Kabbalah as soul to body, the yechidah of Torah, is the Rebbe's claim. Other schools use the Kabbalah in devotion without making it. Setting them side by side is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Iggeret HaKodesh 26:4-5 (the Ari's permission). Hayom Yom 19 Cheshvan ('Chassidus is Shema Yisrael'). Hayom Yom 21 Adar II (read, not quoted): the Tzemach Tzedek calls him 'the Moshe Rabbeinu of Chabad Chassidus', who gave the Maggid's fire to all.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Spine: the Rayatz, citing the Maharash, calls his Shaar HaYichud 'the key to Chassidus' (Hayom Yom 15 Adar II).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Hayom Yom 24 Teves: 'Chassidus is chayus'.
- **Maharash.** Transmits the 'key' statement (spine). Not otherwise located here.
- **Rashab.** Kuntres Eitz HaChaim 13 (OCR): the Besht's teaching reveals God's greatness and brings love and awe; each generation needs more of it.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus pp. 5-17 (OCR): Chabad Chassidus as divine inquiry, with parables close to understanding; mussar, chakirah and Chassidus (S088).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 2-3, 16:5, 25-29.
- **Other schools.** Strashelye (preface 23-25): the Ari concealed, later kabbalists corporealized, the Besht and the Maggid explained. Strashelye (Sha'arei Avodah 1:25:6): the inwardness of Adam Kadmon is not to be spoken of. Outside Chabad, the relation between Chassidus and Kabbalah is rarely theorized in the texts read here.

## S088 · Outside wisdom and philosophy · חכמות חיצוניות / חקירה

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F12 Knowing the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Search terms:** חכמות חיצוניות · חקירה · פילוסופים · מחקר

> Outside wisdom and philosophy are the unity of God as a test of bitul: the same knowledge of the One can humble or inflate, and wisdom that stands on its own as 'something' conceals Him.

## Derivation

1. Torah is the highest wisdom and contains the good in every wisdom (Inyanah 3:1-2, read, not quoted). (*stated*)
2. The wisdom of the nations fell from the 'back' of holy Chochmah into kelipas nogah. Studying it defiles the divine soul's intellect, which is worse than idle talk, unless one uses it 'as a spade to dig with', for livelihood or for divine service, as the Rambam and the Ramban did (Tanya 8:8-11). (*stated*)
3. The Tzemach Tzedek: the difference between the Rambam and Aristotle in philosophy was bitul. The more the Rambam grasped, the more he recoiled and grew small in his own eyes; the more Aristotle grasped, the more he became a yesh. Bitul is the whole difference between holy and profane (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Milah 7; F08). (*stated*)
4. The Tzemach Tzedek also enlists philosophy and corrects it. Infinite motion proves that His speech renews creation (S078). Against the philosophers' providence over species only, providence reaches each particular creature (Sefer HaChakirah; F01). (*stated*)
5. The Rayatz: chakirah, the second of mussar, chakirah and Chassidus, is needed, but on its own it coarsens those who pursue it, and Chassidus includes and transforms it. The Rambam wrote 'to know', not 'to believe'. Yet the Rayatz himself sets the limit: one must first toil to understand as far as one can, 'and what he does not understand he believes with simple faith' (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 14; G13). (*stated*)
6. R. Nachman: the heresies that come from the 'vacated space' have no answer by inquiry, because God is hidden there. Israel passes over them by faith, and at Sinai Israel received the Torah by casting away 'wisdoms' (Likutei Moharan 64, 123). This is a real disagreement with Chabad's 'know as far as the mind can go'. (*stated*)
7. The Maggid freely borrows a principle 'known from the philosophers' to explain how deeds rise to their root. Reading that ease as a sign of the founding circle's openness is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 8:8-11 (outside wisdom defiles the intellect; the exception is the 'spade'). Note that SH 7 and 9 tell one to believe and not to probe hidden things (spine F12).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Milah 7 (the Rambam and Aristotle). Sefer HaChakirah throughout (philosophical proofs of creation, time and providence, engaging Saadia, the Kuzari, the Rambam and the Ikkarim).
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Spine: those who dwell only on the deepest matters coarsen them with the selfhood of comprehension (Kuntres HaTefillah). Not re-quoted here.
- **Rayatz.** Kuntres Toras HaChassidus pp. 13-18 (OCR): mussar, chakirah and Chassidus as force, light and life; chakirah alone breeds conceit; 'to know', not 'to believe', and what the mind cannot reach is held by simple faith.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 2:6-3:2 (read, not quoted): all good in any system of ethics or wisdom has its source in Torah.
- **Other schools.** R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan 64, 123; Part II 19): inquiry sinks one into the vacated space; Israel passes over by faith; the Torah was received by casting away wisdoms. The Maggid (63:1) cites 'the philosophers' approvingly for a principle. The disagreement is real. Chabad: know by the intellect as far as it goes, and believe beyond it. Breslov: faith without inquiry. The Alter Rebbe's own SH 7 and 9 stand closer to faith on hidden matters.
