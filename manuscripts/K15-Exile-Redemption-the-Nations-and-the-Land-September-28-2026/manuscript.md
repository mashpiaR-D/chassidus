## What this cluster shows

These twelve subjects are the one idea read along history and geography. The spine's F07 says the dwelling below is made 'by our deeds throughout the exile' (Tanya 37:1). This cluster is that clause opened out. Exile is the soul's and the Shechinah's descent into real concealment. Tanya 37:17 calls the soul's descent 'a real exile' whose only purpose is to 'bind and unite' the animal soul and its share of the world 'with Or Ein Sof', and IH 25 says that the Shechinah's life is in exile among the husks and the nations while 'there is no change at all in the essence of the Shechinah'. The Rashab sharpens it: a tzimtzum He wills is no tzimtzum, and exile is when sin makes it 'as if compelled'. The Mitteler Rebbe adds that in exile He withdraws into the Essence, and precisely then He regards the bare deed. Redemption is the unity made visible: 'Israel will see eye to eye… there is none besides Him' (Tanya 37:12). The Maharash: there can be an exodus even while speech is still in exile, because 'Anochi Havayah' is a drawing from the Essence. The Rebbe hints that the Aleph in golah makes geulah. Moshiach is the yechidah, the single point (Inyanah 5). The heels are the Shechinah fallen to the feet, where only deed can cleave to her and 'the higher a thing, the lower it reaches' (IH 9; Sha'arei Orah). The resurrection is sovev and the Essence's power to create the physical, revealed in the body (IH 17; the Rebbe). The nations are concealment with a life of its own: the word of God in exile within them, measured by yeshus, destined to call on His Name. The Land is the place of least concealment, where the flow passes through nature without being clothed in it (LT Masei 3; R. Nachman: providence). Jerusalem is complete awe and two unifications, yet a limit before Ein Sof. The choosing of Israel is the Essence's free choice of the body (Tanya 49; the Rebbe). The descent of the generations is inverted by the Rebbe into rising revelation. Converts are sparks come home through bitul. Corrections to SUBJECTS: the heels (marked 'reading') and the Land (marked 'reading') are stated. Jerusalem (marked 'thin') is only partly thin, since LT Pekudei builds a doctrine of unifications on the wordplay. The descent of the generations (marked 'thin') is thin outside Chabad but stated in Chabad through the heels. The nations and converts stay 'partly', with the harsh and universal texts, and the literal and figurative converts, kept side by side. The Alter Rebbe, the Rebbe and Inyanah carry most of the weight. The Maharash, the Rashab and the Mitteler Rebbe appear where found. The Rayatz appears chiefly through his sichos and Hayom Yom. Among the other schools the Maggid, the Chernobyler, Radomsk, the Sfas Emes, R. Nachman, R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk and the Piaseczno Rebbe each state a tie in their own terms.

## S174 · Exile · גלות

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F07 The home below · F11 Darkness turned to light · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** גלות · בגלות · גלות מצרים · ד' גליות

> Exile is the unity of God as a descent: the soul and the Shechinah go down into a real concealment so that the lowest place can become His dwelling, and, on our reading of the Rebbe's golah/geulah hint, the redemption is that same exile with the One (the Aleph) revealed in it.

## Derivation

1. There is nothing besides Him, yet He is hidden in garments that go down level by level to this world, the lowest, with 'doubled darkness' and husks that say 'I and nothing else' (Tanya 36:3-4). The hiding is His own act (F03). (*stated*)
2. Each soul-spark's coming down into a body and animal soul is 'a great descent and a real exile'. It happens only to refine them and 'bind and unite them with Or Ein Sof', and the Alter Rebbe calls it 'exactly the pattern of the exile of the Shechinah' (Tanya 37:17-18). (*stated*)
3. The revelation of Or Ein Sof in the physical world, which is the dwelling below, 'depends on our deeds and service throughout the exile' (Tanya 37:1). Exile is therefore when the dwelling is built (F07), not a pause from it. (*stated*)
4. Exile is a hiding, not a rival power. The Sfas Emes: 'no evil comes down from above'. Exile is 'only a hiding', caused by the receivers' lack, so it has an end, because 'falsehood has no legs'. Its root is redemption, and it rules only over those who do not know this (Vayechi 5635). (*stated*)
5. The Maggid: in exile the King is 'on the road', so even a villager can reach Him at the inn. Whoever thinks of Him in devekus in exile finds Him dwelling with him at once (Maggid 20:1). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe's hint: the Aleph of 'the Master of the world' drawn into golah makes geulah (Bechukosai 5746). The hint is stated. In the line just before, he speaks of God taking Israel out of the exile at once, so the text does not say that redemption leaves exile in place. That geulah is golah with the One revealed in it, rather than exile removed, is our reading of the hint. (**reading**)
7. That the Besht's 'if one knows He hides there, it is no hiding' (spine F03) and the Sfas Emes's 'exile rules only over one who does not know' are one teaching, and that exile is the chief case of F11 (transforming the dark rather than escaping it), is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 36-37: the chain of worlds descends to doubled darkness, the soul's descent is 'a real exile' for the sake of refinement, and the messianic revelation depends on deeds 'throughout the exile'. IH 25:22: when Israel went into exile among the nations it was 'a complete exile', and 'they went to Edom and the Shechinah with them'. Torah Ohr, Lech Lecha 1:5 (S185): Israel were exiled only so that 'converts', meaning sparks, be added.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah (line 225, S175/S184): in exile the Holy One 'withdraws higher and higher' into the Essence, and precisely then He 'lowers Himself to see' the bare deed of the lowest generations.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Discourses 13:3 (read, not cited): the Shechinah wears 'shoes' only when she is lowered into Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah in exile to give life to the lowly. 'Make here the Land of Israel' (via the Rebbe, S181) turns exile into a task.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627 (S176): speech can be in exile and there can still be an exodus, because 'Anochi Havayah' is drawn from the Essence. Toras Shmuel 5629 (S180): the nations' rule over Israel in exile draws on His exaltation, before which all is as nothing.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, Nitzavim 5680 (read, not cited here; see S175): in exile the Shechinah is clothed 'like one bound in sackcloth', the garment ruling the wearer, against the supreme will. She went into exile with Israel for Israel's sake, and that is what keeps Israel alive among the nations.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 3 Menachem Av (read, not cited): 'when Moshiach comes we will long for the days of exile', because now is the time of avodah.
- **The Rebbe.** Likkutei Sichos 32 (Bechukosai 5746): the cause of exile is baseless hatred, removed by baseless love; the Aleph in golah makes geulah. Inyanah 27:2: the work of the last exile completes the purpose of all four.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (20:1): in exile the Presence is nearer, the King at the inn. Sfas Emes (Vayechi 5635; Shemot 10:2): exile is only a hiding; all exiles are for raising holy sparks. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan I 7): exile comes from a blemish in faith, prayer and the Land; Egypt is the place where there are no miracles. Real difference: Breslov places the cause of exile in failed faith in the One who renews above nature. The Alter Rebbe places its purpose in building the dwelling. The Sfas Emes makes it a hiding that has power only over one who does not know.

## Thin or forced?

No. Tanya 37 states the tie outright: exile is the soul's descent for the sake of uniting the world with Or Ein Sof. The Sfas Emes and the Rebbe state it in their own terms.

## S175 · The exile of the Shechinah · גלות השכינה

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F06 The soul as part of the One · F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both **Search terms:** גלות השכינה · שכינתא בגלותא · שכינה בגלות · צער השכינה

> The exile of the Shechinah is the unity of God as the indwelling Presence clothed in what hides it (the individual soul, the husks, the nations) while the Shechinah's own essence does not change, and He withdraws 'higher and higher' into the Essence.

## Derivation

1. 'Shechinah' is His speech, Malchus, 'so called because it dwells and is clothed within all worlds' (Inyanah 29:5; IH 25:23-25). It is the lower unity, His presence filling the world (F04 memale). (*stated*)
2. That life reaches the husks and the nations only from 'the back side' and through many screens. There it is 'in exile within them', and 'that is also called the exile of the Shechinah'. Yet 'there is no change at all in the essence of the Shechinah' (IH 25:21, 24). The exile is in the radiance and its garments, not in Him (F03, F05). (*stated*)
3. In each person: the spark of Godliness that gives one's soul life comes down from Ein Sof, who fills and surrounds all worlds and before whom all is as nothing, and is clothed in 'the snake's skin'. One's sins bind 'the King in the tresses': 'that is the secret of the exile of the Shechinah', and mercy is aroused for 'the Name of God dwelling with us' (Tanya 45:1-2; F06). (*stated*)
4. The Rashab sharpens the concealment. A tzimtzum He chooses is no tzimtzum, since 'a limit one wills is no limit'. The exile of the Shechinah begins when sin makes the contraction 'as if compelled'. Bitter reflection and then prayer bring the divine light back out (Hemshech Ayin Beis, Vaetchanan 5673). (*stated*)
5. The Mitteler Rebbe: in exile 'the Holy One withdraws higher and higher', as if seated in the exaltation of the Essence. The same passage says that precisely then He 'lowers Himself to see' the bare deed (Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah; G05). (*stated*)
6. The human response is to pray for her and not for oneself. The Maggid grounds this in 'Torah and the Holy One are one': a mitzvah done for an ulterior motive makes separation (Maggid 6:3). Radomsk: 'My prayer', hers, not 'their prayer' about their own exile (Tiferet Shlomo, Shavuot 45). (*stated*)
7. Whether the Shechinah's pain is real (Radomsk's widow) or only a hiding (Sfas Emes), and how this fits the Rebbe's report of the Rayatz that only the body is in exile (K13, S162), is read here through the two knowledges (G02). No text in this pass makes that reconciliation. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 45 (the individual spark in exile, 'Yaakov kissed Rachel and wept' to raise the souls from their exile); Tanya 19:8 (the soul's wisdom 'in exile in the body… in the secret of the exile of the Shechinah'); Tanya 17:7 (grep only: the lower teshuvah raises the lower heh fallen to the externals); Iggeret HaTeshuvah 6 (sin lowered the lower heh into nogah, the exile of the Shechinah); IH 25 (the Shechinah in exile among the husks and nations, her essence unchanged). Tanya 37:18 ties it to the purpose: to refine sparks.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah (line 225): in exile He withdraws into the Essence and so regards the bare yoke of deed. Toras Chaim has 43 hits for the term (grep only).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Discourses 13:2-3: the Shechinah as a stature with feet, 'shoes' in exile. Derekh Mitzvotekha, discourse section at line 358 (grep only): until 'I will remove the spirit of impurity', the husks prevail, 'the king bound in the tresses', the exile of the Shechinah.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627, 'Mi sam peh' (line 293, read): speech in its defect, 'like a sheep before its shearers she was dumb', is the exile of the Shechinah. There is also a muteness that is a virtue, the bitul of raising sparks.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis 5673 (the tzimtzum made 'as if compelled'); 5680 (in exile the garment rules the one it clothes, against the supreme will; the Shechinah went down to Egypt and Babylon for Israel's sake, which keeps them alive).
- **Rayatz.** Not located in this pass as a separate treatment. The Rebbe reports the Rayatz's view that the soul is never in exile (K13, S162).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 29:5: 'they were exiled to Edom and the Shechinah with them', so even at the lowest He dwells with us and gives the power to finish the exile. Inyanah 4:2: the redemption is of Israel 'and of the Shechinah'.
- **Other schools.** The Maggid (Maggid Devarav 6:3; the same teaching in Keter Shem Tov 2:18): pray always for the Shechinah; whoever prays for himself is a 'dog crying give, give'. Ohr HaMeir (Balak 3, read, not cited) passes on the Maggid's teaching in his name. Radomsk (Shavuot 45-47): every soul swore before birth to raise the Shechinah from the dust; she is a widow whose pain peaks on the festivals. Sfas Emes (pillar 17): it is only a hiding. Real difference: Radomsk and the Maggid make her suffering the object of prayer. The Alter Rebbe and the Rashab explain it as the concealment of a radiance whose essence is unchanged.

## S176 · Redemption · גאולה

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both · F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart' · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** גאולה · הגאולה · גאולה העתידה · קץ

> Redemption is the unity of God made visible: 'there is none besides Him' seen eye to eye in this physical world, drawn from the Essence ('Anochi') and completed by the deeds of the exile.

## Derivation

1. The purpose of creation is this lowest world as His dwelling. Its completion, the days of Moshiach and the resurrection, is 'the revelation of Or Ein Sof in this physical world' (Tanya 36:7, 37:1; F07). (*stated*)
2. What is revealed is the unity. When the animal soul of all Israel is a chariot, the nogah of the whole world rises and 'Israel will see eye to eye as at Sinai: you have been shown to know that Havayah is Elokim, there is none besides Him' (Tanya 37:12; F12). Radomsk: 'now He is our God, in the future Havayah will be One and His Name One', when all dwellers of the world know His Godliness (Shavuot 45). (*stated*)
3. The Maharash: all the states of speech and hearing, the defective and the elevated alike, are made 'through Anochi Havayah, which is a drawing from His Essence', and therefore there can be an exodus while speech is still in exile (Toras Shmuel 5627; F05). He says this of Moshe's speech at the Exodus. Generalizing it to 'only the Essence redeems' is our reading. (*stated*)
4. Redemption reveals the root of the exile: 'know that there is Godliness in the root of the exile' (Sfas Emes, Vaera 5641). The Rebbe: the Aleph in golah makes geulah (S174). (*stated*)
5. It is built by the deeds of the exile, and the final redemption completes the purpose of all four exiles, 'a redemption after which there is no exile' (Inyanah 27:2). (*stated*)
6. The Rayatz's call, 'Israel is redeemed only through teshuvah', with the sufferings as birth-pangs (Hayom Yom 15 Teves), stands in the text without a tie to the unity. Reading teshuvah as the separate self's return (F08) is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 36-37: the purpose is completed in the body and the physical world; the revelation seen as at Sinai; 'all flesh shall see together'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah (line 225, S184): Moshiach comes only through the bare yoke of deed. Toras Chaim (line 35, grep only): the refinement of the 288 sparks is completed at the end of the sixth millennium, in the days of Moshiach.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Milah 10 (S180/S185): in the future the separating screen is removed entirely, 'all flesh shall see together'.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5627: the Exodus could come while speech was still in exile because 'Anochi Havayah' is a drawing from His Essence (this passage does not set it against 'a mere radiance'); 'the voice of my beloved leaping over the mountains', since the voice had left exile.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, Vaetchanan 5672 (lines 344-345, grep only): a higher comfort 'from the Essence of the infinite Ein Sof', 'Anochi anachemchem'. Not read in context here.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 15 Teves (his 1941 proclamation): now is the time of redemption, the sufferings are Moshiach's birth-pangs, redemption comes only through teshuvah.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 4:2, 27:2-3, 29:5-30:1; Likkutei Sichos 32 (golah and geulah).
- **Other schools.** Radomsk: the redemption is the Shema's 'Havayah is One' fulfilled. Sfas Emes: redemption begins when bodily pain turns into the soul's pain, and its point is to know Godliness in the root of exile. The Chernobyler (S177): Moshiach built from prayers. Esh Kodesh (S178): redemption is divine revelation through Israel. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan I 7): Moshiach comes when the heretics who cover miracles with nature are gone. Difference: for R. Nachman the redemption is the defeat of 'nature'; for Chabad, nature itself is revealed as Elokim, one with Havayah.

## S177 · Moshiach · משיח

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** משיח · מלך המשיח · ביאת המשיח · משיח צדקנו

> Moshiach is the unity of God as the yechidah: the single point of the soul, of Israel and of all the worlds, one with the Essence and revealed throughout the world.

## Derivation

1. Of the soul's five levels, the yechidah is its essential point, above the category of parts. It is 'yachid', not 'echad': not even the source of the parts (Inyanah 5:5; the spine's precision). (*stated*)
2. 'Moshiach's main point is yechidah': David reached nefesh, Eliyahu ruach, Moshe neshamah, Adam chayah, and Moshiach will reach yechidah (Inyanah 5:4). He is the yechidah of the whole chain of worlds, 'the very point of life above all form' (6:1). (*stated*)
3. Every messianic novelty (the redemption, knowledge of God, change in creation) branches from that point (5:3). Chassidus is that same point in Torah, so 'from Moshiach, its reward, we know its essence' (4:1). The Besht's letter ties Moshiach's coming to the spreading of the wellsprings (via the Degel). (*stated*)
4. The Chernobyler: Moshiach is a stature made of all the souls of Israel. Each prayer in which thought and speech are united builds the part of that stature that belongs to one's own soul, and he is named 'Moshiach' from speech (Me'or Einayim, Pinchas 13). (*stated*)
5. Moshiach's greatness is bitul: he will learn with the Patriarchs and with simple people (Hayom Yom 1 Av). Radomsk: he is the servant who owns nothing of his own. That this bitul is how the yechidah shows (F08) is our reading. (**reading**)
6. The Chernobyler's stature, assembled from souls, is an 'echad' model. Inyanah's single point is a 'yachid' model. Setting them side by side is ours; they are not in conflict, but they are not the same claim. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 36-37: the days of Moshiach as the purpose of creation; IH 26:32 (grep only): 'mitzvot are annulled in the future' refers to the resurrection, not the days of Moshiach.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah (line 225): 'the coming of Moshiach ben David comes only from the power of deed, in the yoke of Torah and mitzvot in practice alone'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass as a separate teaching on Moshiach.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Hayom Yom 22 Nisan (read): in 5666 he instituted the Acharon shel Pesach meal of the Tomchei Temimim students and called it 'the meal of Moshiach' (the Besht's name for it). Inyanah 29:4: he linked Hemshech 5666 to the 'end' named for that year.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 23 Nisan (read; unattributed entry): Acharon shel Pesach is 'the revelation of Moshiach', the strength to go out into the world after Pesach's leaping; Hayom Yom 15 Teves (S176).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 4:1-6:5 is the systematic text: Moshiach is yechidah, Chassidus is the yechidah of Torah, and its spreading is the vessel for his coming.
- **Other schools.** The Besht (letter, via the Degel, Beshalach): 'when your wellsprings spread outward'. The Chernobyler: every united prayer builds a limb of Moshiach's stature. Radomsk: Moshiach as 'yismach', the servant's joy in his portion. SUBJECTS notes, and this pass agrees, that Chabad's messianism is the sharpest on this; the other schools treat Moshiach through yichud and prayer, not as the soul's single point.

## S178 · The heels of Moshiach · עקבתא דמשיחא

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** עקבתא דמשיחא · עקבות משיחא · חבלי משיח · דור האחרון

> The heels of Moshiach are the unity of God at its lowest reach: the generation in which the Shechinah has fallen to the feet, where only action can cleave to her, and where the highest light is needed, and given, to turn doubled darkness into light.

## Derivation

1. The Shechinah is pictured as a body. In the heels, 'the fallen sukkah of David' has come down to the feet and heels, the level of Asiyah (IH 9:3). The Tzemach Tzedek: the heel-souls come from 'the feet of the Shechinah', yet the foot too has the soul's life, His filling light (memale), in it (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Discourses 13). (*stated*)
2. So the service must be at that level: 'there is no way to cleave to her truly and turn her darkness to light except through action as well', which is charity (IH 9). Kuntres Acharon 8 names prayer, refining sparks by subduing or transforming the animal soul. The disagreement is kept below. (*stated*)
3. The concealment is greatest there: 'the sons of Jacob clothed in heels, above all in the heels of Moshiach, where the hiding is very great' (the Maharash 5632). Even when one contemplates, one does not move 'because of the strength of the darkness and the yesh' (the summaries printed with Sha'arei Orah, line 743). (*stated*)
4. Yet 'this is a great rule: whatever is higher can extend lower', so the Wisdom of Jacob reaches even Esau's heel in the last generation (Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah 5:1-2; G05). Sha'arei Orah credits this reach to the power of Chochmah (the yud of Yaakov) and to charity, not to the Essence by name. That the transforming power is the Essence's alone is carried from Inyanah 20:1 (spine F11); joining it to this passage is our reading. (*stated*)
5. Therefore the heel generation is 'the main thing', since the heels hold the body up. Overcoming the evil inclination, which the angels were not asked to do, is 'the main intention' (the Rayatz, Simchas Torah 5689). Their task is to turn doubled darkness into doubled light (Inyanah 27:3). (*stated*)
6. Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 5701): the redemption is a revelation of Godliness through Israel. To bear so much light their powers must be nullified, and 'those are the birth-pangs of Moshiach'. The text states this; reading it as F08 bitul is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 9: in the heels the main service is charity, because the Shechinah has fallen to the level of deed. Kuntres Acharon 8: in the heels the main service is prayer, since Torah is no longer 'fixed'.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah: 'the higher, the lower it extends'; the last generation refines Esau's heel. Chanukah's light shines 'even to the last generation in the heels of Moshiach' (grep only, line 77).
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Discourses 13 ('How beautiful are your steps in shoes'): heel-souls are the feet of the Shechinah, and the feet too carry the soul's life.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5632 (line 4723): the concealment is greatest in the heels, which is why contemplating 'I have not changed' does not produce expiry of the soul; also 5632-5634 (lines 10577, 10865, grep only): souls of the heels have 'become coarse and distant from Godliness' and need more frequent arousal.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674 (S184): the heel-souls are at 'the very last diminution', the bodies further from bitul; Tzav 5673 (line 2220, grep only): before dawn the darkness thickens, 'in the heels of Moshiach chutzpah will increase'.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5686 ('we are heels of the heels; we must do what a heel does'), 5688 ('a heel must know it is a heel; the main thing is action'), 5689 (the heel generation is the main one).
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 26:3-28:1: the heels are an 'orphan generation' with doubled darkness, and that is why they receive the inner Torah and special powers. Hayom Yom 18 Sivan (read): 'in this time, truly the heels of Moshiach', each must seek the good of the other and arouse him to teshuvah.
- **Other schools.** Esh Kodesh (Masei 5701): birth-pangs as the nullifying of Israel's powers so that the light of Moshiach can be born, and the more one suffers the greater the part of the light that comes through. The Piaseczno Rebbe adds that Ula's 'let him come, and may I not see him' was love of Israel. Hits in R. Tzadok and Radomsk were not read in this pass.

## S179 · The future: the days of Moshiach and resurrection · תחיית המתים / לעתיד לבוא

**Faces:** F07 The home below · F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both · F01 The world said into being now **Search terms:** תחיית המתים · לעתיד לבא · לעתיד לבוא · ימות המשיח

> The days of Moshiach and the resurrection are the unity of God as the purpose fulfilled in the body: the unlimited light (sovev), and the Essence's power to create physical being, revealed without a garment in this world.

## Derivation

1. 'The days of Moshiach, and especially when the dead live, are the purpose and completion of the creation of this world, for which it was created from the start' (Tanya 36:7; F07). (*stated*)
2. Then the light of God will be revealed 'without any garment', and Torah is the strength by which the righteous will 'not be nullified out of existence'. The righteous remain and see (Tanya 36:6). That this holds F02's two sides together is our reading. (*stated*)
3. Gan Eden receives memale, measured and contracted. The resurrection will receive sovev, 'not by way of contraction, measure and limit', in this world (IH 17:5-6; F04). (*stated*)
4. Why in the body: 'only the Holy One Himself has the power to create a physical thing, yesh from nothing', and that power, found in physicality, will be revealed at the resurrection. That is why the Ramban, against the Rambam, puts the highest reward in the body (the Rebbe, general letter; F05). (*stated*)
5. Life will be eternal because the essence of life (yechidah) will be drawn, and 'whatever is essential does not change'. Now only its spreading-out reaches the world, and that can end in death (Inyanah 6:3). (*stated*)
6. R. Nachman: the world will be renewed at the level of the Land of Israel, when it is revealed that He created everything. Nature will be nullified entirely and the world run by providence alone (Likutei Moharan II 8; F01). That this differs from the Chabad texts, where the physical yesh itself becomes the vessel, is our comparison, and a loose one: R. Nachman speaks of nature as the way the world is run, Tanya 36:6 of the existence of the righteous in the revealed light. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 36-37; IH 17 (sovev at the resurrection); IH 26:32 (grep only): 'mitzvot are annulled in the future' refers to the resurrection.

## S180 · The nations of the world · אומות העולם

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** אומות העולם · אוה"ע · שבעים אומות · שרי האומות

> The nations of the world are the unity of God as concealment given a life of its own: vitality from His word held 'in exile' within them, defined by being 'a thing in its own right', drawn from His exaltation above all, and destined to be turned to call on His Name.

## Derivation

1. All vitality, the nations' included, comes from the spark of 'the word of God'. It reaches them through their princes and many screens and is 'in exile within them', which is why the princes are called 'other gods' (IH 25:20-21; F03). (*stated*)
2. The husk is defined as what makes itself 'a thing in its own right' ('give, give'). Tanya names the nations' souls from it and calls them 'dead', because life is wisdom and bitul (Tanya 19:8, 1:18). The measure is yeshus, the opposite of bitul (F08). The harsh texts are kept as they stand. (*stated*)
3. The Maharash: the nations draw life from 'His exaltation in His Essence', before which darkness is as light and all worlds are as nothing. From this, in exile, comes their rule over Israel (Toras Shmuel 5629; F02, the higher knowledge). (*stated*)
4. The Sfas Emes: the concealments of the seventy nations are set in proportion to Israel's power to refine (Noach). That this is F03, concealment for the sake of revelation, is our reading. (**reading**)
5. The end: 'then I will turn to the peoples to call all of them in the Name of God' (IH 25:21). Israel's light 'will illuminate the darkness of the nations too' (Tanya 36:11). The separating screen will be removed entirely (the Tzemach Tzedek, Milah 10; F11). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: a Noachide who keeps the seven laws 'because the Holy One commanded them in the Torah' receives Torah's effect, peace (Tetze 5751). The Rebbe introduces this with 'lechora', as a first way of explaining the Rambam's 'to make peace in the world'. SUBJECTS' 'the nations' seven laws as a dwelling for Him' was not found in those words in this pass; that link is our reading. (**reading**)

## S181 · The Land of Israel · ארץ ישראל

**Faces:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F07 The home below · F01 The world said into being now · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual **Search terms:** ארץ ישראל · ארץ הקודש · אוירא דארץ ישראל · ישיבת ארץ ישראל

> The Land of Israel is the unity of God as the place of least concealment: where His eyes always rest, where the flow from Atzilus passes through nature without being clothed in it, and whose holiness takes in its very soil. It is the pattern the whole world is to become.

## Derivation

1. Outside the Land, the flow from Malchus of Atzilus is clothed in Malchus of Asiyah, the garment of nature, 'the utmost concealment of Godliness', where one can say 'my strength made me this wealth'. That is why impurity was decreed on its air (LT Masei 3; F03). (*stated*)
2. In the Land the flow passes through Asiyah only 'by way of passage, not true clothing', 'like sunlight through clear glass'. 'The eyes of Havayah your God are on it always': the light of Chochmah, in which Or Ein Sof is clothed, renewed each year; 'the air of the Land makes wise' (LT Masei 3; IH 14:8). (*stated*)
3. Outside Chabad the same point: 'the essence of the Land's holiness is that His providence is there always' (R. Nachman). Its food is 'from the Holy One Himself and not through any prince', like the manna (R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk; F01). (*stated*)
4. Its mitzvot are in deed. The spies wanted to keep the mitzvot in thought, but 'the main thing is to do them in the Land', because 'whatever is higher is clothed precisely in the lowest' (LT Shlach 2:21-24; F10, G05). (*stated*)
5. In the future 'the Land of Israel will spread over the whole world': the whole world will be at the Land's present level (LT Masei 3; Likutei Moharan II 8). (*stated*)
6. The work now: 'Make here the Land of Israel' (the Tzemach Tzedek, via the Rebbe). The Land's virtue is that its holiness takes in its physicality, and a Jew anywhere must bring that into the physical part of the world providence gave him (Likkutei Sichos 33, 39; F07). (*stated*)

## S182 · Jerusalem · ירושלים

**Faces:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart' · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below **Search terms:** ירושלים · ירושלים של מעלה · ציון · יראה שלם

> Jerusalem is the unity of God as complete awe: the lower and the higher awe, two unifications, joined in one place the whole Land will become. Yet before Ein Sof even upper Jerusalem is a limit to be left behind.

## Derivation

1. 'Jerusalem means complete awe (yirah shalem)'. There is a lower and a higher awe, and two unifications: yud-heh, drawn by the negative commandments, and vav-heh, drawn by the positive ones (LT Pekudei 1:22). The text states the unifications. Mapping them onto the higher and lower unity (F02) is our reading. (*stated*)
2. Lower Jerusalem is the Name Adnut, 'a palace for the Name Havayah', the union of vav-heh. Going up to the lower Jerusalem, the tribes went up to both (LT Pekudei 1:21-23; G14). (*stated*)
3. In the soul: the divine soul is 'Jerusalem, complete awe'; before birth the souls are 'daughters of Jerusalem'. The animal soul is the desert of the nations, to be subdued (Or HaTorah, Masei). (*stated*)
4. In the future the Land will be at Jerusalem's present level, and Jerusalem will rise higher still (LT Masei 3). (*stated*)
5. Even 'upper Jerusalem, complete awe, the gate of heaven' is a border and limit with no comparison to Or Ein Sof. The journeys are an exodus from every limit, even the holiest (Or HaTorah, Masei; F05). (*stated*)
6. Radomsk: 'if I do not set Jerusalem above my chief joy' is prayer for the Shechinah to return to 'the place He will choose', where the unification is complete. The tie there is to the Shechinah's place, not to the unity as such. (**reading**)

So in Chabad it is more than homiletic. Outside Chabad, in this pass, it is about the Shechinah's place, and the tie to the unity is thin.

## S183 · The choosing of Israel · ובנו בחרת

**Faces:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F06 The soul as part of the One · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F07 The home below **Search terms:** ובנו בחרת · עם סגולה · בחירת ישראל · ישראל עלו במחשבה

> The choosing of Israel is the unity of God as the Essence's free choice. It falls on the physical body, which looks like any other, so that Israel be included in His unity and bear witness that He is One even in earthly things.

## Derivation

1. He contracts His infinite light into 'world' 'for the love of His people Israel, to bring them near to Him, to be included in His unity and oneness' (Tanya 49:10). The purpose of the choice is the unity. (*stated*)
2. 'You chose us from every people and tongue': 'this is the physical body, which in its physicality resembles the bodies of the nations' (Tanya 49:11). The choice does not fall on a visible quality. (*stated*)
3. 'True choice belongs to the Essence', so the chosen body of a Jew has an advantage even over the soul (the Rebbe, Basi LeGani 5731). 'True choice is only where nothing is bound by any limit' (Likkutei Sichos 35; F05). (*stated*)
4. Both sides are held: Israel's souls 'arose in thought', yet 'even the thought is as nothing before Him' (LT Behar 2:3; F02). Israel arose in thought, and the Holy One's tefillin say 'who is like Your people Israel' (Torah Ohr, Yitro 2:14). (*stated*)
5. The chosen people is 'one nation on earth' so as not to separate earthly matters 'from the true One' (IH 9:1). Its light is to light up the nations too (Tanya 36:11; S180). (*stated*)
6. Radomsk: 'Torah and Israel are one'; Israel's holiness, like a Torah scroll's, comes only through Torah and mitzvot done for their sake, when the Name Havayah rests on the one who learns and does them (Shavuot 44). This is not the Rebbe's claim: for Radomsk the body, like the parchment, is holy only through deeds done for His sake; for the Rebbe the physical body itself is what the Essence chose. The difference is kept. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 49 (the choosing of the body), IH 9 ('one nation on earth'), LT Behar 2 (arose in thought, yet as nothing before Him), Torah Ohr, Yitro 2 (God's tefillin). The harsh counterpart is Tanya 1 (S180).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5629 (line 2317, S180): 'His face', the inner light, shines only on Israel, because they make vessels for Or Ein Sof.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom (line 455, grep only): each soul stands in the primordial thought of Adam Kadmon. Not read in context.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat (Beshalach 5731): true choice is in the Essence, so the body has an advantage. Likkutei Sichos 35 (Teves letter 5734): the Essence takes pleasure from the Jewish body; indexes of Likkutei Sichos cite Tanya 49 repeatedly.
- **Other schools.** Radomsk (Shavuot 44): Torah and Israel one, 600,000 letters and souls. Sfas Emes (Bo 14, S185): Israel were mixed among the nations and then chosen, so that sparks rise. SUBJECTS: 'its universalist and particularist sides both stay'. Kept: Tanya 1 and 19 on one side, Tanya 36:11 and IH 25 on the other.

## Thin or forced?

## S184 · The descent of the generations · ירידת הדורות

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F05 The Essence beyond both · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** ירידת הדורות · דורות הראשונים · ראשונים כמלאכים · נתמעטו הלבבות

> The descent of the generations is the unity of God as descent for the sake of the lowest: as souls weaken, the service moves from study to prayer to bare deed, and precisely there more of the Essence and the inner Torah is revealed.

## Derivation

1. In earlier generations, great souls: refinement happened 'in a moment, in the Shema alone' (Kuntres Acharon 8:8). For the Tannaim and Amoraim Torah study was the main service (IH 9:3). (*stated*)
2. Then descent: 'generation after generation the light of their souls darkens' until even prayer falls (the Mitteler Rebbe, Sha'arei Orah). The souls of the heels are at their lowest and 'the bodies further from bitul than earlier generations' (the Rashab, Re'eh 5674). (*stated*)
3. The service moves down with them: to charity, the level of deed (IH 9), to prayer (Kuntres Acharon 8), and to 'the bare yoke of deed', which 'rises above even the beginning of thought', the only thing that brings Moshiach (Sha'arei Orah). (*stated*)
4. The Rebbe reverses the direction for revelation. The heel generations, with doubled darkness, receive the inner Torah that the Tannaim did not, because their task is greater, and 'each generation closer to Moshiach' receives more (Inyanah 26:3-28:2). (*stated*)
5. That this is the grammar of G05 ('the higher a thing, the lower it descends') applied to history is our reading, though Sha'arei Orah states the rule in the heel context (S178). (**reading**)
6. Outside Chabad, 'the earlier ones like angels' is a rule of deference: 'where we do not understand the earlier ones, the lack is ours' (Radzin). It has no tie to the unity. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 9:3 (study then, charity now); Kuntres Acharon 8:3, 8:8 (earlier generations refined in a moment; now prayer is the main service).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Sha'arei Orah, Chanukah (line 225): generations darken, prayer falls, only the bare deed remains, and that deed reaches higher than thought.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass (Derekh Mitzvotekha has 2 hits, not read).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5632 (S178), and lines 10577, 10865 (grep only): earlier tzaddikim needed arousal once in thirty days; 'we who have become coarse', souls of the heels, need more.
- **Rashab.** Hemshech Ayin Beis, Re'eh 5674: earlier generations returned in teshuvah at a small arousal from above, now 'like those who sleep in the dust'. The remedy is bitul from the side of soul and body.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5688 (read): a hundred years ago one could not have imagined such comprehension of Chassidus as now.
- **The Rebbe.** Inyanah 26-28: the paradox and its resolution.
- **Other schools.** Radzin (Sha'ar HaEmunah, intro. 28): the earlier ones are 'like angels', and the lack of understanding is ours. Ohr HaMeir (Balak 3; Ki Tavo 2, read, not cited): 'now, in our generations when hearts have diminished', as a lament about prayer. Neither ties the descent to the unity.

## S185 · Converts · גרים

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F07 The home below **Search terms:** גרים · גר צדק · גירות · גר שנתגייר

> Converts are the unity of God as sparks coming home: holiness fallen among the nations, raised by the exile and by Israel's light, and taken into holiness through bitul, 'like a candle before a torch'.

## Derivation

1. 'Israel were exiled only so that converts be added' (Pesachim 87b) is read as the sparks in nogah, in food and drink, which rise and are included in holiness 'because of the revelation of the Shechinah, through which they are nullified and included like a candle before a torch' (Torah Ohr, Lech Lecha 1:5; F11, F08). (*stated*)
2. The Alter Rebbe says outright that 'He guards the converts' 'does not necessarily mean actual converts' but the sparks fallen in the breaking of the vessels and Adam's sin. Once raised, they need extra guarding, like a penitent (LT Vaetchanan 8:13-14). (*stated*)
3. The Tzemach Tzedek applies it to actual converts: circumcision removes the screen, Avraham's light shines on the nations, 'many converts come, who are sparks of holiness fallen into the husks' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, Milah 10; F03). (*stated*)
4. The Sfas Emes: Israel were mixed among the nations first and chosen after, so that sparks always rise from them. 'That was the purpose of the exile, to raise the converts' (Bo 14; F07). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe, citing the Rayatz: how can 'your convert within your camp' stand 'before Havayah'? The convert, 'drawn from the nations', corresponds to the lowest levels, the animal soul. 'Nitzavim' means being raised and set upright, as Yaakov set up the stone (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Vayetze 5732). (*stated*)
6. Radomsk turns the image around: the 'ger' of 'the ger, the orphan and the widow' is the Holy One Himself, 'now in exile like a stranger in the land' (Shavuot 47). R. Nachman: prayer makes converts, and through them the Glory is revealed (Likutei Moharan II 8). The tie is stated in each; that these are one idea is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Torah Ohr, Lech Lecha 1 (exile for converts = sparks; Bereshit 6:12 has the same teaching, grep only); LT Vaetchanan 8 ('not necessarily actual converts'). Shulchan Aruch HaRav has the halachah (not examined).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Toras Chaim (32 hits for 'converts', 5 for 'righteous convert', grep only). Not read in context.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Milah 10 (converts as sparks drawn by Avraham's light); Milah 4 (grep only): Avraham and Sarah 'begot the souls of converts'.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass (6 hits in Hemshech Ayin Beis, not read).
- **Rayatz.** His maamar 'Atem nitzavim' as cited by the Rebbe: the convert stands before Havayah by being raised.
- **The Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat (Vayetze 5732; also lines 2842, 3126, grep only: 'a convert is like a newborn' applied to the Exodus as the service of tzaddikim). Toras Menachem has 79 hits, not examined.
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes (Shemot 10, Bo 14): exile and the mixed multitude are for raising converts and sparks. Radomsk (Shavuot 47; Purim 97-100): God as 'ger'; sparks among the nations. R. Nachman (Likutei Moharan II 8): prayer makes converts and reveals the Glory. R. Tzadok (Yisrael Kedoshim 9:16, read, not cited): Avraham is 'the first of converts' and 'father of a multitude of nations'. SUBJECTS' claim that for R. Tzadok love of Israel is rooted in the convert was not confirmed in this pass.
