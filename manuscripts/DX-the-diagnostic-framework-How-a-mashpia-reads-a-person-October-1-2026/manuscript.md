## What this strand found

1. **The sources already diagnose.** Tanya ch. 25-31 is a differential diagnosis: it sorts heaviness by what it is about (worldly things, sins, stray thoughts, or nothing at all), by when it falls (at prayer or at work), and by whether there is life in it (bitterness) or none (sadness); and it gives each kind its own remedy and its own hour. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut grades how a person hears (from 'wants only to be moved' to 'true but far from me'). The Piaseczner names two causes of a mind that cannot hold a holy thought, and says plainly that the soul is healed the way a doctor heals the body: first know it in detail. *Stated.*
2. **The anatomy is a map of where a trouble sits, not a lecture.** Which soul is speaking (by its currency: 'for me' or 'toward Him'); which element is loud in the animal soul (fire, water, air, earth: Tanya ch. 1); which power is blocked (chochmah, binah, da'as, the middos); which garment is still free (thought, speech, deed are always in reach); which level still works (from bare doing to the will that will not be cut off). *Stated parts; the use as a checklist is ours.*
3. **One root, many disguises.** The root the sources name again and again is the self that feels itself 'a thing in itself' (yeshus). The Tanya traces sadness over one's own failings to it (ch. 27), the stone heart to it (ch. 29), anger to it (Iggeret HaKodesh 25), and the flat world to its mirror (Shaar HaYichud 4). We list fifteen forms it takes in a modern life. *Stated for six of them; the full list and the plain sentences are ours.* Grief, real harm and illness are not disguises of the root; the root shows only in how the self carries them (ours, a guard).
4. **Healing has kinds, and they have an order.** Eighteen kinds, each with what it fits and what it must not touch. The sources fix the order in several places: joy before bitterness, and bitterness only at a set hour (ch. 26, 31); holding back before turning (ch. 27); the lower return before the higher (Iggeret HaTeshuvah); and every stirring ends in a deed, or it is a flame in the air (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). *Stated.*
5. **The assessment is eight plain questions, asked with consent, one at a time.** When is it worst; what does it say; what is it about; what part still wants change; what do you know that doesn't help; how are sleep, food and people; what still works; what would you do if it lifted. Each answer fills one field of the shared formulation. Each question follows a distinction the sources make. *The wording and the set are ours.*
6. **This pulls against two earlier owner rules, and needs a call.** D25 and D36 forbid interviewing and feeling-questions. The owner now asks for a mashpia's assessment. We reconcile it this way: questions only after the person asks for a path or agrees to be asked; one per turn; something given before the next question; never 'how does it feel', never either/or; at most three before a first formulation is offered. If the owner wants no questions at all, every question has a 'read it from the cues' fallback (Part 5).

**How the parts fit.** Part 1 is the body of the soul: where a trouble can sit. Part 2 is the distinctions the sources use to tell one trouble from its neighbor. Part 3 is the root under them all, and its disguises. Part 4 is the kinds of healing and what each fits. Part 5 is the assessment: the questions, how to read the answers into a formulation, and how to say it back. Part 6 is the rules. P1 and P2 build the protocols on this.

## Part 1. The anatomy: where a trouble can sit

A mashpia does not ask 'what's wrong with you'. He asks where in the person it sits. The sources give five coordinates. *(The coordinates are stated; using them as a checklist is ours.)*

## 1.1 Which soul is speaking

Two souls live in one person. The natural soul (the animal soul) is the life of the body; it wants what is good for me, and it comes from the bright husk, which has good in it. The G-dly soul is a part of G-d above; it wants what is good in itself, and at bottom it wants Him. They fight over the mind. **The diagnostic test (ours):** listen for the currency. A sentence about my comfort, my image, my pleasure or my safety is the animal soul speaking. An ache for meaning, goodness or closeness is the G-dly soul's voice coming through, even when it comes out as restlessness. The Piaseczner says it outright of the unnamed ache: it is not the man who feels this pain, but the G-dly spark in him.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 1 (line 82): «נפש אחת מצד הקליפה וסטרא אחרא, והיא המתלבשת בדם האדם להחיות הגוף», *one soul from the side of the husk, which clothes itself in the blood to give the body life*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 1 (line 84): «היא מקליפת נוגה, שיש בה גם כן טוב», *it is from the bright husk, which has good in it too*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 2 (line 86): «ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש», *the second soul is truly a part of G-d above*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 28 (line 399): «הן שתי נפשות, הנלחמות זו עם זו במוחו של אדם», *they are two souls, fighting each other in a person's mind*
> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 518): «לא האיש הוא המרגיש את הצער הזה, רק הניצוץ אלקי שבקרבו», *it is not the man who feels this pain, only the G-dly spark within him*

## 1.3 Which power is blocked

Each level of the soul is made of ten powers: three of mind and seven of heart, with three garments below and will and delight above. Each has its own way of getting stuck. A mashpia locates the block before he treats it, because the cure goes through a power that is still free.

| Power | Its job | Blocked as | Sounds like | Freed by | MAP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **chochmah**: the first flash: seeing the point whole, before words | to see that something is so | covered by the 'spirit of folly' that hides the hidden love; the person cannot see the point at all, or forgets it the minute it matters | 'I know, but in the moment it's just gone' · 'I can't see why it matters' | uncovering, not adding: it is in their hand at every moment to put the forgetting aside (Tanya 25) | S08, S13 |
| **binah**: working it out: widening the flash into detail and examples | to give the point a shape in this person's world | stuck in general: the idea hovers ('G-d is everywhere') and never lands in this room; the Rashab calls it 'only surrounding, a false imagination' | 'it's all one, I get it' · 'I know the concept' | detail: this cup, this hour, this body, slowly | S23, S24 |
| **daas**: knowing that holds: binding the mind to what it understood until it is as real as sight | to carry the knowing into feeling (da'as is the bridge to the heart) | (a) hesech hada'as: the mind lets go and wanders off; (b) knowing that is never felt: 'I only knew it'; (c) a mind that cannot hold any holy thought, from no arousal or from disuse (the Piaseczner) | 'it's all in my head' · 'I can't hold a thought' · 'I've heard this a hundred times' | one line, held and returned to, said aloud, many days | S16, S26, S27, S01 |
| **middos**: the heart's traits: love (chesed), awe and restraint (gevurah), compassion (tiferes), staying power (netzach), bowing (hod), bond (yesod), expression (malchus) | to answer, to move toward or away, to act | (a) timtum: the heart like stone, it cannot open at all; (b) misdirected: the animal soul's fire (anger, pride) and water (craving) aimed at the self; (c) atzvus: heaviness with no life | 'nothing lands' · 'I can't stand him' · 'I just need something' · 'I'm heavy' | never by demanding a feeling: through the mind into the hand, or through a sigh, a tune, a plain cry | S03, S15, S07, S04 |
| **garments**: thought, speech and deed: how the powers come out | to carry the soul into the world | almost never: they can be taken off and changed whatever the heart is doing. This is the in-between person's whole field | 'I didn't do it today' · 'I made the call' | they are the entry when everything above is shut: a thought held, a line said, a deed done | S28, S29, S81 |
| **will and delight**: above the mind: what you want and what delights you, before any reason | to drive everything below | never sick at its core. It shows as a 'want of the want', and in what a person would not give up for anything. The deepest is the least felt, so its silence is not its absence | 'I don't know why, but I can't let go of it' · 'I wish I wanted to' | name it, don't build it | S41, S60, S61 |

## 1.4 Which garment is still free

Thought, speech and deed can be put on and taken off whatever the heart is doing. The mind rules the heart, and any person can be an in-between person at any hour, because the in-between person's work is in deed, speech and thought. **Diagnostic use (ours):** there is almost always a free garment. When the heart is shut, the treatment starts there: a thought held, a sentence said aloud, a small act. This is why no formulation ends in 'nothing can be done today'.

## 1.5 Which level still works

Kuntres HaHitpa'alut gives the five levels of the soul as five kinds of stirring. A mashpia asks which one is still alive in this person, and enters there.

| Level | From inside | Diagnostic use (ours) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nefesh | only agreement to act is left; little warmth, real steadiness | If this is all that works, enter by the deed and the yoke. It is enough to start. |
| ruach | a thought that grips and keeps coming back | If a line already grips them, use that line; do not bring a new one. |
| neshamah | understanding that lights the heart | If understanding still warms them, contemplation can reach the heart; the long road is open. |
| chayah | quiet, total attention, before feeling | A quiet heart here is higher, not lower. Do not read it as numbness. |
| yechidah | the simple will that would not be cut off from Him for anything | Never the sick part. It is the healthy point every protocol finally leans on. Even a coarse person is stirred to hear of the One, 'from the root of his soul'. |

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 15:4 (line 306): «ובחינת נפש בחינת עשיה היא בחינת ההסכם למעשה לבד שבאה מבחינת ההודאה», *nefesh, the level of doing, is agreement to act, coming from acknowledgment*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 15:6 (line 308): «אך בחינת נשמה הוא בחינת הארת המוחין דחכמה ובינה בלב», *neshamah is chochmah and binah shining in the heart*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 15:9 (line 311): «ובחינת יחידה היא מדריגה הה׳ באמת שהוא בחינת הרצון העצמי הפשוט», *yechidah is truly the fifth level: the simple essential will*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7:25 (line 157): «וחוש זה אינו אלא מצד שרש נפשו דהיינו בחינת אלהות שבנפשו», *this sense comes only from the root of his soul, the G-dliness in his soul*

**The healthy point.** The yechidah is never the sick part, and neither is the hidden love: it is like a candle flame that always reaches up, covered only by a 'spirit of folly', and it is in a person's hand at any hour to set the covering aside. Every formulation names what is blocked and also what is still free; this is the deepest thing still free. *(Stated: Tanya ch. 19, 24, 25; Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 7.)*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 19 (line 286): «כאור הנר שמתנענע תמיד למעלה בטבעו», *like the flame of a candle, which always flickers upward by its nature*

## Part 2. The states, and the distinctions that place a person

The states and the movements between them are in `consciousness-map/MAP.md`: 21 entry states (S01-S21) where people start, working, heart and deep states, 241 movements, and the cues in Part 5. DX does not repeat them. It adds the distinctions the sources themselves use to tell one trouble from its neighbor. These are what the questions in Part 5 are built to hear.

| Distinction | The test | What follows | MAP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sadness or bitterness (atzvus or merirus)** | Is there life in the pain? Sadness is a heart like stone with no life; bitterness hurts and wants to move. | Sadness: lift first (joy, the deed). Bitterness: give it a set hour, briefly, then joy. | S04, S05 |
| **What the sadness is about** | Worldly things (money, health, children); heavenly things (sins, distance); stray thoughts and desires; or nothing at all. | Worldly: trust that it is good, only hidden (ch. 26). | **The stone heart or depression** | The stone heart (timtum) cannot open in prayer, often in a person who is otherwise steady. | **Two causes of a mind that won't hold (the Piaseczner)** | No arousal at all (neither joy nor a broken heart), or a thinking that is weak from never being used. | No arousal: a small fire first (gladness, or an honest ache). **The movements.** A state is left by one movement at a time, along one of MAP's eight currents (outer to inner, general to detail, mind to heart to hand, self-enclosed to held, running and returning, the known to the edge of knowing, darkness to light, hidden to revealed). A formulation's `state_needed` is always one movement away from `state_now`, never the summit (MAP R3).

## Part 3. The root, and its disguises

**The root (stated).** The husk's whole life is to be 'a something, a thing in itself'. The Tanya calls this the root of idolatry, and its voice is 'I, and nothing besides me'. The Piaseczner: a person's selfhood is what stands against inspiration from above. In the clinic's words: *the self that feels it holds itself up and stands apart from Him.* It is not a moral charge. It is a mistake about where one's being comes from.

**The sources trace troubles to it by name (stated).** Sadness over one's own bad thoughts comes from pride, from not knowing one's place (Tanya ch. 27), and is 'not from G-dliness at all, only that his soul is not whole in some completeness that would honor his name' (Derech Chaim). The stone heart is the husk's coarseness lifting itself over the soul's light (ch. 29). Anger is faith gone for the moment (Iggeret HaKodesh 25). The world looks flat because the life hides in each thing 'as if it were a thing in itself' (Shaar HaYichud 4): the same mistake, seen outside.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 19 (line 292): «להיות יש ודבר בפני עצמו», *to be a something, a thing in itself*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 22 (line 323): «כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו», *the root of idolatry is being reckoned a thing in itself*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 22 (line 322): «מגביה עצמה כנשר, לומר: ״אני ואפסי עוד״», *it lifts itself like an eagle, saying: I, and nothing besides me*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 29 (line 407): «והסיבה – היא גסות הקליפה, שמגביה עצמה על אור קדושת נפש האלהית, ומסתרת ומחשיכה אורה», *the cause is the coarseness of the husk, which lifts itself over the light of the G-dly soul, hides it and darkens it*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 (line 382): «ואדרבה, העצבות היא מגסות הרוח, שאינו מכיר מקומו», *on the contrary, the sadness comes from pride: he does not know his place*
> Derech Chaim (the Mitteler Rebbe) (OCR) (line 381): «ועצבות זו אינו מצד אלקות כלל רק מאשר אין נפשו שלם באיזה שלימות שיכבד שמו», *this sadness is not from G-dliness at all, only that his soul is not whole in some completeness that would honor his name*
> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4 (line 808): «כי אם בהסתר פנים, שהחיות מסתתר בגוף הנברא, וכאילו גוף הנברא הוא דבר בפני עצמו», *only by a hidden face: the life hides in the creature's body, as if the body were a thing in itself*
> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 25 (line 1376): «כי אילו היה מאמין שמאת ה׳ היתה זאת לו – לא היה בכעס כלל», *if he believed this came to him from G-d, he would not be angry at all*
> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 1555): «כי הישות של האדם הוא המתנגד להשראה ממרום», *a person's selfhood is what stands against inspiration from above*

**The disguises (ours, on the sources named).** Each row gives the trouble as people bring it, the sentence the self says in it, the form the root takes, how to see it, what it is not, and the healings that fit (Part 4).

## RF01. Holding it all up

**Brought as.** worry, anxiety, control, burnout, 4 a.m. lists. **The self says.** "It stands because I hold it. If I let go for a second, it falls." **The form.** The self feels it is the one keeping its life, family and job in being. The fear is the weight of holding up what it does not hold. **You see it by.** 'if I don't handle it nobody will'; checking; can't rest even when nothing can be done now. **It is not.** A real money, health or safety problem still gets a practical step and a person. The root is the extra weight, not the problem. **MAP.** S02, S84, S65. **Healings.** K11, K14, K08. *Basis: stated (Iggeret HaKodesh 11; Shaar HaYichud 1), applied by us.*

> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1 (line 785): «כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש», *if the letters withdrew for an instant and went back to their source, all the heavens would be truly nothing*
> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (line 1144): «וכשיתבונן האדם בעומק הבנתו, ויצייר בדעתו הווייתו מ״אין״ בכל רגע ורגע ממש, האיך יעלה על דעתו כי רע לו», *when a person thinks deeply and pictures his own coming into being from nothing at every single moment, how can he think it is bad for him?*

## RF02. The heavy self

**Brought as.** heaviness, low energy, the afternoon slump, laziness, 'what's the point' on an ordinary day. **The self says.** "I am not what I should be, and nothing I do will change that, so why move." **The form.** The Mitteler Rebbe: this sadness is 'not from G-dliness at all', only that the soul is not whole in some completeness that would do it honor. The Tanya says the same of sadness over one's own failings: it comes from pride, from not knowing one's place. In the animal soul it is the earth element, which makes the body heavy. **You see it by.** heaviness that comes at a set hour or for no reason; spiritual things feel like one more failure; no life in it. **It is not.** Not depression, not exhaustion from real overwork, not a medical problem; those get sleep, food, a doctor and a person first. Never say 'pride' to a heavy person: this is the guide's reading only. **MAP.** S04, S03, S53. **Healings.** K09, K14, K10, K05. *Basis: stated (Derech Chaim; Tanya ch. 1, 25, 26, 27).*

> Derech Chaim (the Mitteler Rebbe) (OCR) (line 381): «ועצבות זו אינו מצד אלקות כלל רק מאשר אין נפשו שלם באיזה שלימות שיכבד שמו», *this sadness is not from G-dliness at all, only that his soul is not whole in some completeness that would honor his name*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 (line 382): «ואדרבה, העצבות היא מגסות הרוח, שאינו מכיר מקומו», *on the contrary, the sadness comes from pride: he does not know his place*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 25 (line 361): «נגד היצר המכביד את גופו ומפיל עליו עצלה, מבחינת יסוד העפר שבנפש הבהמית», *against the inclination that makes his body heavy and drops laziness on him, from the element of earth in the animal soul*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 371): «אי אפשר לנצחו בעצלות וכבדות – הנמשכות מעצבות וטמטום הלב כאבן, כי אם, בזריזות – הנמשכת משמחה ופתיחת הלב», *it cannot be beaten with laziness and heaviness, which come from sadness and a heart dulled like stone, but only with quickness, which comes from joy and an open heart*

## RF03. The thick heart

**Brought as.** numbness, 'nothing lands', prayer is words. **The self says.** "Nothing reaches me. (Under it: there is too much of me in the way.)" **The form.** The Tanya gives the cause of the stone heart: the coarseness of the husk lifts itself over the G-dly soul's light and darkens it. The thickness is self, not absence. **You see it by.** steady in life, closed in prayer; can't open even to regret. **It is not.** If it is lasting, pervasive or hopeless, it is low (RF02) or depression, and the lowering remedy is forbidden. **MAP.** S03, S81, S41. **Healings.** K14, K15, K12, K13. *Basis: stated (Tanya ch. 29; Derekh HaMelekh).*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 29 (line 407): «והסיבה – היא גסות הקליפה, שמגביה עצמה על אור קדושת נפש האלהית, ומסתרת ומחשיכה אורה», *the cause is the coarseness of the husk, which lifts itself over the light of the G-dly soul, hides it and darkens it*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 29 (line 403): «יש להם טמטום הלב שנעשה כאבן, ולא יכול לפתוח לבו בשום אופן לעבודה שבלב זו תפלה», *they have a dullness of heart that has become like stone, and he cannot open his heart in any way to the service of the heart, prayer*
> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 1240): «ונדמה לו שלבו אטום. ובאמת גם עתה מלא קדושה הוא», *it seems to him that his heart is sealed, and in truth even now it is full of holiness*

## RF04. The owner of the idea

**Brought as.** knows it all, feels none of it; heady; collects inspiration. **The self says.** "I already know this." **The form.** The knower stays outside what he knows; the idea is a possession, not a place he lives. The Mitteler Rebbe names the man 'whole in himself' who hears Chassidus as something outside him (depths/DIGEST.md, MR1-C19). **You see it by.** 'I've heard this a hundred times'; explains well; nothing changes in the day. **It is not.** Not a fault of intellect. The mind is the open door; it just hasn't been walked through. **MAP.** S16, S27, S25. **Healings.** K01, K15, K14. *Basis: stated (Kuntres HaTefillah; Tzav VeZeruz; MR1-C19 in English only).*

> Kuntres HaTefillah (the Rashab) (OCR) (line 61): «וההרגשה זהו״ע הדעת, כי יכול להיות שמבין את הדבר היטב ומ״מ אינו במורגש אצלו», *feeling is what daas is: a person can understand a thing well and still not feel it*
> Tzav VeZeruz (the Piaseczner) (line 72): «הן גם מאז זאת ידעתי, אבל רק ידעתי», *I knew this before too, but I only knew it*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 11:3 (line 235): «אבל הענין רחוק ממנו ושואל איזה תועלת בנפשו יבוא מזה», *but the matter is far from him, and he asks what good it will do his soul*

## RF05. The self that must have it

**Brought as.** desire, a pull they can't stop: scrolling, food, lust, spending. **The self says.** "I need this, now, for me." **The form.** The water of the animal soul aimed at its own pleasure. Under it is often a deeper thirst the person mistakes for hunger. Inside every low stirring are two things: the soul's stirring, and the low garment it wears. **You see it by.** a named object, a hit, then empty; promises and falls; shame after. **It is not.** Never advice to stop a substance or medicine; addiction gets human help. **MAP.** S07, S09, S30, S13. **Healings.** K02, K04, K03. *Basis: stated (Tanya ch. 1; Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:43).*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 1 (line 82): «כעס וגאוה – מיסוד האש שנגבה למעלה, ותאות התענוגים – מיסוד המים», *anger and pride come from the element of fire, which rises; the craving for pleasures from the element of water*
> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:43 (line 289): «נמצא שגם כשמרגיש האיש איזה התרגשות לדבר נמוך, ב׳ דברים בגו, א׳ התרגשות הנפש», *so even when a person feels a stirring toward something low, there are two things in it: one, the stirring of the soul*
> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (line 85): «ויחשוב שרעב הוא, צמא», *and he thinks it is hunger, thirst*

## RF06. The wronged self

**Brought as.** anger, irritability, a grudge, the replayed argument. **The self says.** "This was done to me, and I am the one who decides what it means." **The form.** The fire element, which rises. The Tanya: if he believed this came to him from G-d, he would not be angry at all. In the moment of anger the self has taken the place of the Source. **You see it by.** rehearsed arguments; 'she always'; stung most where they feel unseen. **It is not.** Where harm, abuse or control is ongoing: no counsel to forgive, tolerate or keep the peace. The wrong stays a wrong; protecting oneself is also from Him. **MAP.** S15, S86, S30. **Healings.** K02, K11, K16. *Basis: stated (Iggeret HaKodesh 25; Tanya ch. 1).*

> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 25 (line 1376): «כי אילו היה מאמין שמאת ה׳ היתה זאת לו – לא היה בכעס כלל», *if he believed this came to him from G-d, he would not be angry at all*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 1 (line 82): «כעס וגאוה – מיסוד האש שנגבה למעלה, ותאות התענוגים – מיסוד המים», *anger and pride come from the element of fire, which rises; the craving for pleasures from the element of water*

## RF07. The verdict

**Brought as.** shame, self-hatred after a failure, 'I'm a terrible person'. **The self says.** "I am my mistakes." **The form.** The same self, turned against itself: judge and judged are both 'me', and the self is still the center. The Piaseczner: whoever feels himself empty and vile behaves empty and vile. **You see it by.** heavy, hiding, an insult hurts more; repair feels pointless. **It is not.** Not humility. Never 'you're nothing', never bittul, never a list of faults. 'I deserve it' and 'I'm a burden' are danger signals. **MAP.** S11, S41, S77. **Healings.** K12, K13, K07. *Basis: stated (Esh Kodesh; Tanya ch. 1-2); the reading as inverted yeshus is ours.*

> Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) (line 541): «כי מי שמרגיש עצמו לריק ומנוול מתנהג כריק ומנוול», *whoever feels himself empty and vile behaves empty and vile*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 2 (line 86): «ונפש השנית בישראל, היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש», *the second soul is truly a part of G-d above*

## RF08. The seen self

**Brought as.** pride, needing to be seen, jealousy, comparison. **The self says.** "I, and nothing besides me." **The form.** The plainest form: the self as a thing in itself, measured against other things in themselves. Comparison needs two separate somethings; take away the separateness and there is nothing to compare. **You see it by.** keeping score; 'nobody appreciates me'; another's good feels like my loss. **It is not.** Never to someone crushed; false humility (talking oneself down while still the center) is the same root. **MAP.** S06, S58, S86. **Healings.** K08, K16, K14. *Basis: stated (Tanya ch. 22; Derekh HaMelekh).*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 22 (line 322): «מגביה עצמה כנשר, לומר: ״אני ואפסי עוד״», *it lifts itself like an eagle, saying: I, and nothing besides me*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 22 (line 323): «כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו», *the root of idolatry is being reckoned a thing in itself*
> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 1555): «כי הישות של האדם הוא המתנגד להשראה ממרום», *a person's selfhood is what stands against inspiration from above*

## RF09. The flat world

**Brought as.** doubt; 'prove it'; 'it's all chance'; the regular person who reasons first. **The self says.** "Things stand on their own, and so do I." **The form.** The root seen from outside: the life hides in each thing, 'as if the thing were a thing in itself'. The same mistake about the world that yeshus is about the self. **You see it by.** honest questions; wants reasons; a sense the silence means nobody. **It is not.** Not a sin and not a problem to argue away. Never 'the hiding is His' about a death or someone's suffering. **MAP.** S12, S65, S68, S35. **Healings.** K01, K11, K14. *Basis: stated (Shaar HaYichud 1, 4).*

> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4 (line 808): «כי אם בהסתר פנים, שהחיות מסתתר בגוף הנברא, וכאילו גוף הנברא הוא דבר בפני עצמו», *only by a hidden face: the life hides in the creature's body, as if the body were a thing in itself*
> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1 (line 785): «כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש», *if the letters withdrew for an instant and went back to their source, all the heavens would be truly nothing*

## RF10. Apart

**Brought as.** loneliness; nobody sees what I carry. **The self says.** "I stand alone, apart from everyone, and from Him." **The form.** Standing apart is the root's own shape. The Tanya answers it from the other side: all souls are matched, with one Father. **You see it by.** watching for rejection; pulling back; 'nobody understands'. **It is not.** Real isolation needs real people; the guide is a corridor, never the community. **MAP.** S12, S13, S86, S55. **Healings.** K16, K15, K13. *Basis: stated for the answer (Tanya ch. 32); the reading of loneliness as this root is ours.*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 32 (line 466): «שכולן מתאימות, ואב אחד לכולנה», *they are all matched, and there is one Father for all of them*

## RF11. The empty self

**Brought as.** emptiness; 'what's the point'; success that feels hollow. **The self says.** "I live for me, and nothing fills me." **The form.** A life run by the natural soul points to nothing past itself. The ache is not the man's but the G-dly spark's; the soul's thirst is mistaken for hunger. **You see it by.** 'there must be more'; reaching for food, work, trips; it isn't that. **It is not.** Don't promise experiences. If 'what's the point' is about life itself, check for danger. **MAP.** S13, S08, S82. **Healings.** K13, K14, K10. *Basis: stated (the Piaseczner; Sha'arei Orah).*

> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 518): «לא האיש הוא המרגיש את הצער הזה, רק הניצוץ אלקי שבקרבו», *it is not the man who feels this pain, only the G-dly spark within him*
> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (line 85): «ויחשוב שרעב הוא, צמא», *and he thinks it is hunger, thirst*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 197): «ונק' רצון לרצון היינו שרוצה שיהי' לו רצון ואהבה אמתית לה'», *it is called a will for a will: he wants to have a will and a true love for G-d*

## RF12. The self that can end

**Brought as.** fear of death or illness; health anxiety. **The self says.** "I hold myself in being, so I can fall into nothing." **The form.** Fear of ending is the fear of a self that thinks it stands on its own. The truth the sources give: you are being brought into being from nothing at every moment, so you were never the one holding yourself up. **You see it by.** checking the body; 'what if'; dread at night. **It is not.** Never explain an illness or promise health. Medical worry gets a doctor. Not in fresh diagnosis or acute fear: presence first. **MAP.** S02, S65, S84. **Healings.** K11, K14, K17. *Basis: stated (Iggeret HaKodesh 11); applied to fear of death by us.*

> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (line 1144): «וכשיתבונן האדם בעומק הבנתו, ויצייר בדעתו הווייתו מ״אין״ בכל רגע ורגע ממש, האיך יעלה על דעתו כי רע לו», *when a person thinks deeply and pictures his own coming into being from nothing at every single moment, how can he think it is bad for him?*
> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1 (line 785): «כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש», *if the letters withdrew for an instant and went back to their source, all the heavens would be truly nothing*
> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (line 1150): «הכל טוב בתכלית רק שאינו מושג», *all is utterly good, only it is not grasped*

## RF13. The scattered self

**Brought as.** a racing mind, too much going on, can't focus. **The self says.** "Everything needs me now." **The form.** The mind given to many masters. The Mitteler Rebbe calls the distraction of making a living sleep: the life is hidden, not gone. All day the powers 'go here and there', and the bond stays. **You see it by.** short, fast messages; jumps; 'sorry, I'm all over the place'. **It is not.** Not a failure at meditation and not anxiety by default. Racing with no sleep, or a sense of mission, is the danger line. **MAP.** S01, S32, S73. **Healings.** K01, K14, K18. *Basis: stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut; Imrei Binah); the self's sentence is ours.*

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 12): «כך טרדת הפרנסה שנקרא גלות השכינה נקרא שינה», *the distraction of making a living, called the exile of the Shechinah, is called sleep*
> Imrei Binah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 568): «אע"ג דאזיל הכא והכא», *even though he goes here and there*

## RF14. Serving oneself

**Brought as.** chasing the high; spiritual life feeding the ego. **The self says.** "I want to feel it again." **The form.** The arousal becomes the main thing and not the G-dliness: 'and this is called serving oneself'. **You see it by.** sittings judged by intensity; an insult stings more afterward. **It is not.** A special mission with no sleep is danger, not a summit. **MAP.** S18, S19, S59, S81. **Healings.** K14, K08, K18. *Basis: stated (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut).*

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 167): «וזהו הנקרא עובד את עצמו», *and this is called serving oneself*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 548): «עד שיעשה עיקר מן ההתפעלות ולא מן האלהות כלל», *until he makes the arousal the main thing, and not the G-dliness at all*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 135): «שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר», *that the arousal of their souls be only G-dly arousal and not the arousal of the life of flesh*

## RF15. Not the root: grief and real harm

**Brought as.** a death, a loss, abuse, an illness, injustice. **The self says.** "(nothing to correct)" **The form.** Not every pain is a disguise. Grief is love with nowhere to go. Harm is harm. The root shows only if the self turns the pain into a verdict ('it's my fault', 'I deserve it'), and then that part is read as RF07. **You see it by.** a real event; love; tears. **It is not.** Never explain a loss. Never 'the hiding is His' about a death. Never teach in fresh grief. Watch for not wanting to be here. **MAP.** S14, S10, S49, S21. **Healings.** K17, K15. *Basis: ours (a guard), on the sources' own refusal to explain suffering (MAP S14).*

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 704): «אז ישכון בו ממקור כל החיים מקורא דכולא להחיות רוח נדכאים», *then He dwells in him, from the source of all life, to revive the spirit of the crushed*
> Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) (line 585): «ושם בוכה ומילל עמו כביכול יחד», *and there He weeps and wails with him, as it were, together*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 34 (quoting the Zohar) (line 492): «בכיה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא, וחדוה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא», *weeping is lodged in my heart on one side, and joy on the other*

## Part 4. The kinds of healing, and which fits what

Each kind: what it is, what it fits, how it is done, what it must not touch, and its lines. The order between them is part of the medicine (Part 6).

## K01. Contemplation that binds (hisbonenus, da'as)

**What.** Take one true line and widen it into the details of this person's own world, slowly, until it is as plain as sight; bind the mind to it and come back each time it wanders. **Fits.** knowing-not-feeling (RF04); doubt and the flat world (RF09); the scattered mind once it is a little quiet (RF13). **Not for.** the crushed, the unreal or frightened, obsessive or anxious minds that turn thinking into rumination.

**How.** (1) One line, not many. The same line for several days. (2) Detail, not general: this cup, this hour, my hand. (3) When the mind wanders, come back without a fight. (4) Say the line aloud at the end. (5) End with a deed.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 3 (line 112): «והוא לשון התקשרות והתחברות, שמקשר דעתו בקשר אמיץ וחזק מאד, ויתקע מחשבתו בחוזק בגדולת איןסוף ברוךהוא, ואינו מסיח דעתו», *daas means binding: he ties his mind with a strong knot to the greatness of the Infinite, fixes his thought there with force, and does not let his mind wander off*
> Kuntres HaTefillah (the Rashab) (OCR) (line 61): «וההרגשה זהו״ע הדעת, כי יכול להיות שמבין את הדבר היטב ומ״מ אינו במורגש אצלו», *feeling is what daas is: a person can understand a thing well and still not feel it*
> Kuntres HaAvodah (the Rashab) (OCR) (line 204): «ה"ז בבחי' מקיף לבד ואין זה התפעלות אמיתי כי אם דמיון כוזב», *that is only surrounding; it is not true arousal but a false imagination*

## K02. Holding back (iskafya)

**What.** Not obeying the pull, in deed, speech and thought, without arguing with it. Each push counts. **Fits.** the pull (RF05); anger (RF06); stray thoughts in prayer. **Not for.** never as white-knuckle self-hatred; never advice about stopping a substance or medicine.

**How.** (1) Don't debate the thought; act as if you didn't hear it. (2) Put more strength into what you are doing right now. (3) One small no: a word not said, a screen not opened, a minute not given. (4) Count each push as done, whether or not the want is gone.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 (line 383): «ובכל דחיה ודחיה שמדחהו ממחשבתו, אתכפיא סטרא אחרא לתתא», *with each push that pushes it from his thought, the other side is subdued below*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 28 (line 401): «רק יעשה עצמו כלא יודע ולא שומע ההרהורים שנפלו לו», *let him act as one who does not know or hear the thoughts that fell into him*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 28 (line 394): «אל ישית לב אליהן, אלא יסיח דעתו מהן כרגע», *let him pay them no heed, but turn his mind from them at once*

## K03. Turning (is'hapcha)

**What.** The same force that pulled toward the low is given its true object and turns: the craving becomes longing, the fire becomes zeal for good, the bitter becomes sweet. **Fits.** after holding back has held for a while; desire, anger's fire, bitterness. **Not for.** anyone not yet able to hold back; anyone crushed.

**How.** (1) Only after holding back is steady. (2) Find the stirring inside the want (see K04). (3) Give that stirring its real object, at once, in a small act. (4) Never tell someone holding back that they should be turned by now.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 (line 387): «ואתהפכא ממרירו למתקא ומחשוכא לנהורא», *and it is turned from bitter to sweet and from darkness to light*
> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:43 (line 289): «רק גם חוב עליו להפוך כל רצון רע, לקדושה», *he must also turn every bad wanting into holiness*

## K04. Sorting (birur)

**What.** Separating what is mixed: inside every low stirring are the soul's own stirring and the low garment it wears. Sort them and keep the first. The Tanya sorts sadness the same way, by what it is about. **Fits.** desire (RF05); feeling fake; sadness that mixes real regret with self-pity. **Not for.** a compulsive self-checker; keep it short and rare.

**How.** (1) Name the want exactly. (2) Ask what it is really reaching for: rest, warmth, to matter, to be close. (3) Keep the reaching; drop the garment. (4) With sadness: sort which ache is about Him and which is about me.

*The word birur for this inner sorting is ours; the Piaseczner states the two-in-one, and the Tanya sorts the sadness.*

> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:43 (line 289): «נמצא שגם כשמרגיש האיש איזה התרגשות לדבר נמוך, ב׳ דברים בגו, א׳ התרגשות הנפש», *so even when a person feels a stirring toward something low, there are two things in it: one, the stirring of the soul*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 375): «עצה היעוצה לטהר לבו מכל עצב ונדנוד דאגה ממילי דעלמא, ואפילו בני חיי ומזוני», *the advice for cleansing the heart of all sadness and every trace of worry about worldly things, even about children, health and a living*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 377): «אם נופל לו עצב ודאגה ממילי דשמיא בשעת עסקיו, בידוע שהוא תחבולת היצר», *if sadness and worry about heavenly things fall on him in the middle of his business, it is surely a trick of the inclination*

## K05. Sweetening at the root (hamtakah)

**What.** A harshness that rules the person is not fought where it shows but taken up to its source, where it is sweetened. The Tanya's form: when heaviness comes anyway, for no reason, that is the fit hour to turn it into an honest, brief accounting that breaks the hold, and then into joy. Pushing darkness away is one repair; turning the darkness itself is a deeper one. **Fits.** the heavy self (RF02) when it comes 'for no reason'; harsh self-judgment in a steady person. **Not for.** depression, the crushed, shame (RF07), the first minutes of fresh pain.

**How.** (1) Wait for the heavy hour; don't make one. (2) Use it: a few minutes of honest accounting before G-d, not before yourself. (3) Close it the same hour in joy: it is forgiven, you are His. (4) Never as a daily habit for someone fragile.

*Using hamtakah as the name for this move is ours; the Tanya's line names the sweetening and the hour.*

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 453): «לעתים צריך לעורר בחינת גבורות הקדושות כדי להמתיק הדינים», *at times one must rouse holy might, to sweeten the judgments*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 453): «כי אין הדינים נמתקין אלא בשרשן», *for judgments are sweetened only at their root*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 454): «היא בשעה שהוא עצב בלאו הכי ממילי דעלמא, או כך בלי שום סבה, אזי היא שעת הכושר להפך העצב», *when he is sad anyway about worldly things, or just so, for no reason at all, that is the fit hour to turn the sadness*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 397): «היינו שנדחה החשך מפני האור אבל לא שיהפך החשך עצמו לאור», *the darkness is pushed away before the light, but the darkness itself is not turned into light*

## K06. Bitterness at a set hour, then joy (merirus)

**What.** A real broken heart over a real distance, kept to a set time with a settled mind, and closed at once in joy. Bitterness is alive; sadness is not. **Fits.** real regret; feeling far; a bitter heart that wants to move (S05). **Not for.** the low, the numb, the depressed, the ashamed.

**How.** (1) A set time, not in the middle of work or prayer. (2) Short and honest. (3) Then put the sadness out of the heart completely, and believe He forgives.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 378): «רק לזאת צריך קביעות עתים ושעת הכושר בישוב הדעת», *for this one needs set times and a fit hour, with a settled mind*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 379): «כי מיד אחר שנשבר לבו בעתים קבועים ההם, אזי יסיר העצב מלבו לגמרי», *right after his heart has been broken at those set times, let him put the sadness out of his heart completely*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 452): «כי עצבות – היא שלבו מטומטם כאבן ואין חיות בלבו, אבל מרירות ולב נשבר, אדרבה, הרי יש חיות בלבו להתפעל ולהתמרמר», *sadness is a heart dulled like stone with no life in it; but bitterness and a broken heart, on the contrary, have life in the heart, to be moved and grow bitter*

## K07. Return, lower then higher (teshuvah)

**What.** The lower return fixes what went wrong: regret, a resolve, repair. The higher return is the soul going back toward where it was before it was ever apart, through prayer and learning. Broken vessels are His dwelling. **Fits.** a real wrong (S77); the verdict (RF07), only after compassion; feeling far. **Not for.** never as self-punishment; never to someone crushed on a low day.

**How.** (1) Name the one wrong, plainly, once. (2) Repair what can be repaired; resolve the next time. (3) Then turn from the wrong to Him: the closeness, not the guilt.

> Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah 4 (line 920): «ה׳ תתאה – ״תשובה תתאה״, ה׳ עילאה – ״תשובה עילאה״», *the lower heh is the lower return; the upper heh is the higher return*
> Tanya, Iggeret HaTeshuvah 10 (line 991): «בכוונת הלב בתפלה», *through the heart's intent in prayer (the higher return)*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 462): «דיורא דקב"ה באנון מאנין תבירין», *the dwelling of the Holy One is in broken vessels*

## K08. Yielding the self (bittul)

**What.** Seeing that I am given, not self-made: a ray in the sun, real and not on its own. A self that exists and yields, never a self erased. The higher bittul comes of itself from light; it is not chased. **Fits.** the seen self (RF08); the wronged self (RF06); holding it all up (RF01), in its 'held' form. **Not for.** shame, the crushed, the unreal; never 'you are nothing'.

**How.** (1) Start from a true fact: what holds me in being right now? (2) Let less be mine to defend, not less be me. (3) Afterward: an insult stings less. That is the sign.

> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 10 (line 862): «עצם אחד ממש עם גוף המאור המאיר, ואין לו שום מציאות כלל בפני עצמו», *truly one with the body of the luminary, with no existence of its own at all*
> Toras Menachem 5713 (line 15329): «ביטול במציאות הוא ביטול שבדרך ממילא, שהאדם מתבטל מצד גילוי אור האלקי המאיר אצלו», *bittul in existence comes of itself: the person is nullified by the G-dly light shining in him*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 22 (line 323): «כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו», *the root of idolatry is being reckoned a thing in itself*

## K09. Joy (simcha)

**What.** Joy is the ground and the medicine: heaviness cannot win anything; only quickness that comes from joy and an open heart can. The joy is from what is true, not from pinching the cheeks. **Fits.** the heavy self (RF02); the numb; after a fall; before any deeper work. **Not for.** never forced on grief.

**How.** (1) One small gladness first, true and near. (2) Then the bigger gladness: the soul in you goes home to its Father in every good act. (3) Move the body a little; do one thing quickly.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 371): «אי אפשר לנצחו בעצלות וכבדות – הנמשכות מעצבות וטמטום הלב כאבן, כי אם, בזריזות – הנמשכת משמחה ופתיחת הלב», *it cannot be beaten with laziness and heaviness, which come from sadness and a heart dulled like stone, but only with quickness, which comes from joy and an open heart*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 457): «וזאת תהיה עבודתו כל ימיו בשמחה רבה», *and this shall be his service all his days, in great joy*
> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (line 149): «אבל עם כל זה בעצבות לא תשאר», *but even so, do not stay in sadness*

## K10. Accepting the yoke (kabbalas ol)

**What.** The root of the whole service: accept His rule plainly, whether or not you feel anything. The bare deed under the yoke reaches higher than feeling. **Fits.** the heavy and the numb; the caught animal soul; the busy; the empty self. **Not for.** never to override trauma or silence pain.

**How.** (1) Say it plainly at the start of the day: 'Today is Yours.' (2) Do the next thing because He wants it, not because it feels good. (3) No mood test.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 41 (line 598): «צריך להיות לזכרון תמיד ראשית העבודה ועיקרה ושרשה», *one must always remember the beginning of the service, its main point and its root*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 41 (line 598): «לעורר תחלה היראה הטבעית המסותרת בלב כל ישראל, שלא למרוד במלך מלכי המלכים», *first to rouse the natural awe hidden in every Jewish heart, not to rebel against the King of kings*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 225): «וזהו ענין ביאת משיח ב"ד שאינו בא רק מכח של המעשה בעול תו"מ בפ"מ לבד», *Mashiach comes only from the power of the deed, under the yoke of Torah and mitzvos, in actual practice alone*

## K11. Trust: being given (bitachon)

**What.** Picture your own coming into being from nothing at every moment. Then 'how can he think it is bad for him?' All is good, only not grasped. Trust still acts; it just doesn't carry the rest. **Fits.** holding it all up (RF01); the self that can end (RF12); the wronged self (RF06). **Not for.** never as a reason not to act; never about someone else's suffering.

**How.** (1) One thing in the room: what holds it in being now? (2) Then yourself, this breath. (3) Then do your one practical step, as someone given the next moment.

> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (line 1144): «וכשיתבונן האדם בעומק הבנתו, ויצייר בדעתו הווייתו מ״אין״ בכל רגע ורגע ממש, האיך יעלה על דעתו כי רע לו», *when a person thinks deeply and pictures his own coming into being from nothing at every single moment, how can he think it is bad for him?*
> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 11 (line 1150): «הכל טוב בתכלית רק שאינו מושג», *all is utterly good, only it is not grasped*
> Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1 (line 785): «כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש», *if the letters withdrew for an instant and went back to their source, all the heavens would be truly nothing*

## K12. Compassion for one's own soul (rachamim)

**What.** Before anything, great compassion for the spark of G-d in you that has been dragged through all this. The pity is for the spark, not for the body, and it is not self-pity. **Fits.** the verdict (RF07); numbness (RF03); feeling far. **Not for.** self-pity that stays in itself.

**How.** (1) Picture the spark in you as a captive child. (2) Let the heart go out to it. (3) Ask for it, not for yourself.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 45 (line 675): «לעורר במחשבתו תחלה רחמים רבים לפני ה׳ על ניצוץ אלהות המחיה נ», *to rouse first in his thought great compassion before G-d for the spark of G-dliness that gives life to his soul*

## K13. Uncovering the hidden love

**What.** Nothing new is installed. The love is already there, like a flame that always reaches up; a 'spirit of folly' covers it, and it is in the person's hand at any moment to set the covering aside. Even the 'want of the want' is the start. **Fits.** numb (RF03); empty (RF11); the verdict (RF07); apart (RF10). **Not for.** never as reassurance ('you're good inside'); name the act, not the flame, until they are steady.

**How.** (1) Find what they would not give up for anything. (2) Name it as theirs, not as yours. (3) Let the wanting to want count.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 19 (line 286): «כאור הנר שמתנענע תמיד למעלה בטבעו», *like the flame of a candle, which always flickers upward by its nature*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 24 (line 345): «רוח שטות שבה – המכסה ומסתיר ומעלים את האהבה מסותרת שבנפשה האלהית», *the spirit of folly in it covers, hides and conceals the hidden love in the G-dly soul*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 25 (line 356): «שבכל עת ובכל שעה, בידו של אדם וברשותו הוא – להעביר רוח שטות והשכחה מקרבו», *at every time and hour it is in a person's hand and power to put the spirit of folly and forgetting out of himself*
> Derekh HaMelekh (the Piaseczner) (line 1240): «ונדמה לו שלבו אטום. ובאמת גם עתה מלא קדושה הוא», *it seems to him that his heart is sealed, and in truth even now it is full of holiness*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 197): «ונק' רצון לרצון היינו שרוצה שיהי' לו רצון ואהבה אמתית לה'», *it is called a will for a will: he wants to have a will and a true love for G-d*
> Pirush HaMilos (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 504): «אין זה רק סיבה להביא מן העלם לגילוי אה"ר זו שהיא טבעיות ועצמות בנפשו», *this is only a cause, to bring out of hiding the great love that is natural and essential in his soul*

## K14. The deed as vessel (ma'aseh)

**What.** Light holds only in a vessel. A stirring with no deed is a flame flying in the air. The mind rules the heart, and the garments are always in reach, so the deed is possible on any day. **Fits.** everyone, at the end of every stage; first of all when the heart is shut. **Not for.** never a heavy load; one deed.

**How.** (1) Choose the deed before the practice. (2) Do it within the hour. (3) Small and concrete: a call, a coin, a blessing, a kindness.

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 778): «הרי זה כשלהבת הפורח באויר שאין לו קיום», *(arousal with no deed) is like a flame flying in the air, with nothing to hold it*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 12 (line 209): «כי המוח שליט על הלב», *for the mind rules the heart*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 14 (line 232): «שכל אדם יכול להיות בינוני בכל עת ובכל שעה», *every person can be an in-between person (beinoni) at any time and any hour*

## K15. The cry and the sigh (own words)

**What.** When the mind can't contemplate, the plain cry goes higher than thought. Even a small bodily sigh from the heart can bring a great revealing. **Fits.** numb; grief; loneliness; anyone who 'can't pray'. **Not for.** none, except a crisis: then a person first.

**How.** (1) One plain sentence to Him, aloud, in your own language. (2) Or only a sigh, let out on purpose. (3) No performance.

> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:42 (line 288): «כי אפילו מן אנחה פשוטה קטנה וקלה גופנית שיוצאת מקרב הלב לב ישראל, יכולים לפעול התגלות גדולה בנפש», *even a plain, small, light, bodily sigh that comes out of the heart can bring a great revealing in the soul*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 462): «דיורא דקב"ה באנון מאנין תבירין», *the dwelling of the Holy One is in broken vessels*

## K16. The other held (ahavas Yisrael)

**What.** Two knowings at once: the other is really separate, and is held by the same One. All souls are matched, with one Father. **Fits.** anger (RF06); apart (RF10); jealousy and pride (RF08). **Not for.** never counsel to tolerate ongoing harm.

**How.** (1) One person: what are they carrying? (2) One act for them, unseen. (3) Say both sentences: 'they did wrong' and 'they are held'.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 32 (line 466): «שכולן מתאימות, ואב אחד לכולנה», *they are all matched, and there is one Father for all of them*

## K17. Being accompanied

**What.** For the crushed and the grieving the sources give company, not reasons: He dwells with the crushed to revive them; He weeps with the one who weeps. Weeping and joy can sit in one heart. **Fits.** grief (RF15); the crushed; fresh fear. **Not for.** none.

**How.** (1) Presence, few words. (2) No teaching, no reason. (3) Later, own words to Him.

> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 704): «אז ישכון בו ממקור כל החיים מקורא דכולא להחיות רוח נדכאים», *then He dwells in him, from the source of all life, to revive the spirit of the crushed*
> Esh Kodesh (the Piaseczner) (line 585): «ושם בוכה ומילל עמו כביכול יחד», *and there He weeps and wails with him, as it were, together*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 34 (quoting the Zohar) (line 492): «בכיה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא, וחדוה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא דא», *weeping is lodged in my heart on one side, and joy on the other*

## K18. Coming back down (shov)

**What.** Every rising is for the return. Running without returning is death. Come down several times a day to rest from the thought. **Fits.** unreality, fear after deep work, chasing highs (RF14), the scattered mind. **Not for.** none.

**How.** (1) Stop deep work. (2) Plain day: food, sleep, hands, a person. (3) Then one small deed.

> Hayom Yom, 27 Adar II (line 193): «רצוא בלי שוב, היא מיתה», *running without returning is death*
> Tzava'at HaRivash (line 281): «וצריך לירד למטה כמה פעמים ביום לנוח עצמו ממחשבתו מעט», *he must come down several times a day to rest a little from his thought*

## 5.1 How to ask *(ours; see the gap on D25 and D36)*

1. Ask only after the person asks for a path, a method or 'what do I do', or says yes to: 'Can I ask you a few things first, the way a teacher would?'
2. One question per turn. Before the next question, give something real: a line, a picture, a small act.
3. At most three questions before a first formulation is offered back.
4. Never 'how does it feel', never grade a feeling, never either/or. The questions ask about times, words, facts and wants.
5. If they say 'just give me something', give stage 1 at once from what you can already see, and ask later.
6. If the owner keeps D25 and D36 strict, skip the questions: read each answer from the cues (MAP Part 5) and offer the formulation as a guess.

## 5.2 The questions

Plain wording, as the guide says them. Each one hears a distinction the sources draw.

**Q0. "Before anything: are you safe right now?"** *When:* Only when a danger cue appears (MAP S21). Not counted among the eight. Until answered, it is danger. *Listen for:* self-harm, not wanting to be here, abuse, not eating, sleepless certainty. *Fills:* guards. *Why:* MAP S21; constitution SAFETY (D03)

**Q1. "When is it worst? Tell me about the last time: what time it was, where you were, what you were doing just before."** *When:* First, almost always. *Listen for:* the hour (afternoon, night, after prayer, at work); the trigger; which garment was busy (thinking, talking, doing); whether it falls during prayer or during business. *Fills:* presenting; what_is_blocked. *Why:* The Tanya sorts sadness by when it falls, during service or in the middle of business, and gives each its own answer (ch. 26).

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 377): «אם נופל לו עצב ודאגה ממילי דשמיא בשעת עסקיו, בידוע שהוא תחבולת היצר», *if sadness and worry about heavenly things fall on him in the middle of his business, it is surely a trick of the inclination*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 454): «היא בשעה שהוא עצב בלאו הכי ממילי דעלמא, או כך בלי שום סבה, אזי היא שעת הכושר להפך העצב», *when he is sad anyway about worldly things, or just so, for no reason at all, that is the fit hour to turn the sadness*

**Q2. "When it's there, what does it say to you? Give me its words, as close as you can."** *When:* Second. The answer is the person's own sentence. *Listen for:* the self's sentence: 'it's all on me', 'I'm not enough', 'what's the point', 'they did this to me', 'I need it', 'nobody's there'. *Fills:* root (the form it takes); presenting. *Why:* The Piaseczner: to heal the soul you first know its leanings in detail, as a doctor knows the body.

> Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:44 (line 290): «צריך הוא להכיר מקודם אותן ואת טבעיהן ותהלוכותיהן וכל נטיות הנפש בפרטיות כמו שרופא הגוף מכיר את הגוף בכל פרטיו», *he must first know them, their natures and their ways, and every leaning of the soul in detail, as a doctor of the body knows the body in all its details*

**Q3. "What does it seem to be about?"** *When:* When Q2 hasn't made it clear. *Listen for:* a worldly thing (money, health, family); a spiritual thing (far from G-d, something done); thoughts or desires that won't stop; or nothing at all, it just comes. *Fills:* root; kind_of_healing. *Why:* Tanya ch. 26, 27, 31: each kind of sadness has its own remedy; 'for no reason at all' has its own hour.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 26 (line 375): «עצה היעוצה לטהר לבו מכל עצב ונדנוד דאגה ממילי דעלמא, ואפילו בני חיי ומזוני», *the advice for cleansing the heart of all sadness and every trace of worry about worldly things, even about children, health and a living*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 27 (line 380): «ואם העצבות אינה מדאגת עונות, אלא מהרהורים רעים ותאוות רעות שנופלות במחשבתו», *and if the sadness is not worry over sins, but comes from bad thoughts and desires that fall into his mind*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 454): «היא בשעה שהוא עצב בלאו הכי ממילי דעלמא, או כך בלי שום סבה, אזי היא שעת הכושר להפך העצב», *when he is sad anyway about worldly things, or just so, for no reason at all, that is the fit hour to turn the sadness*

**Q4. "Tell me about any part of you that still wants this to change."** *When:* When the state is low, numb or bitter. *Listen for:* life in the pain (it hurts and wants to move) or none; a second voice; a 'wish I wanted to'. *Fills:* state_now; what_is_blocked. *Why:* Tanya ch. 31: bitterness has life in it, sadness has none. The Mitteler Rebbe: the 'will for a will' is in everyone.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 31 (line 452): «כי עצבות – היא שלבו מטומטם כאבן ואין חיות בלבו, אבל מרירות ולב נשבר, אדרבה, הרי יש חיות בלבו להתפעל ולהתמרמר», *sadness is a heart dulled like stone with no life in it; but bitterness and a broken heart, on the contrary, have life in the heart, to be moved and grow bitter*
> Sha'arei Orah (the Mitteler Rebbe) (line 197): «ונק' רצון לרצון היינו שרוצה שיהי' לו רצון ואהבה אמתית לה'», *it is called a will for a will: he wants to have a will and a true love for G-d*

**Q5. "What do you already know, or believe, that should help with this and doesn't?"** *When:* When they speak in ideas, or ask for proof. *Listen for:* knowing without feeling; general understanding; no belief at all (reasoning first); 'true but far from me'. *Fills:* where_in_the_soul (which faculty); what_is_blocked. *Why:* Kuntres HaTefillah: one can understand a thing well and still not feel it. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut: 'true, but far from me'.

> Kuntres HaTefillah (the Rashab) (OCR) (line 61): «וההרגשה זהו״ע הדעת, כי יכול להיות שמבין את הדבר היטב ומ״מ אינו במורגש אצלו», *feeling is what daas is: a person can understand a thing well and still not feel it*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 11:3 (line 235): «אבל הענין רחוק ממנו ושואל איזה תועלת בנפשו יבוא מזה», *but the matter is far from him, and he asks what good it will do his soul*

**Q6. "How are the basics: sleep, food, your body, the people around you?"** *When:* Always with heaviness, numbness, anxiety or racing. *Listen for:* too little sleep, not eating, illness, medication, burnout, no one at all; danger signs. *Fills:* where_in_the_soul (the body and the animal soul); guards. *Why:* The Tanya places heaviness and laziness in the earth element, which makes the body heavy (ch. 1, 25). The guide never reads a medical or depressive state as spiritual.

> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 25 (line 361): «נגד היצר המכביד את גופו ומפיל עליו עצלה, מבחינת יסוד העפר שבנפש הבהמית», *against the inclination that makes his body heavy and drops laziness on him, from the element of earth in the animal soul*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 1 (line 82): «והוללות וליצנות והתפארות ודברים בטלים – מיסוד הרוח, ועצלות ועצבות – מיסוד העפר», *frivolity, scoffing, boasting and empty talk from the element of air; laziness and sadness from the element of earth*

**Q7. "What still works for you, even on a bad day? One thing you still do that feels like you."** *When:* Before offering any practice. *Listen for:* a strength they already live: a child walked to school, a call made, a tune, a prayer said. *Fills:* state_needed (the door); kind_of_healing (built on it). *Why:* Hayom Yom: know the strengths first and strengthen them, then fix the lacks, each by his own measure. The hidden love shows in what still works.

> Hayom Yom, 27 Menachem Av (line 425): «ולידע המעלות בעבודתם ולחזקם, ואת החסרונות שבהם ובעבודתם לתקנם», *to know the strengths in their service and strengthen them, and the lacks, to fix them*
> Hayom Yom, 27 Menachem Av (line 425): «כל חד וחד לפום שיעורא דילי׳», *each one by his own measure*
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 19 (line 286): «כאור הנר שמתנענע תמיד למעלה בטבעו», *like the flame of a candle, which always flickers upward by its nature*

**Q8. "If this lifted tomorrow, what's the first thing you'd do with the day?"** *When:* Last, to set the aim. *Listen for:* the will under the trouble; the deed that will hold the first stage. *Fills:* state_needed; the first deed. *Why:* The will has no reason under it and drives all; light holds only in a deed.

> Hemshech Samach Vav (the Rashab) (OCR) (line 596): «ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא», *the true inwardness of the will is that the will has no reason at all*
> Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (line 778): «הרי זה כשלהבת הפורח באויר שאין לו קיום», *(arousal with no deed) is like a flame flying in the air, with nothing to hold it*

**A short reading of the owner's case** (the full worked exchange is P1's). *Q1:* 'around 3, at my desk, after lunch'. *Q2:* 'nothing I do today will matter'. *Q3:* 'nothing really, it just comes'. *Q4:* 'I hate wasting the day' (there is life in it). *Q6:* sleeps six hours, eats at the desk. *Q7:* 'I still call my mother every evening'. Sleep and eating get a plain word too.

## 5.4 Saying it back

Three to five plain sentences, offered as a guess, ending in what you'll start with. No terms, no ids, no question tacked on the end (FORMULATION §7.3). If they correct it, the correction replaces the guess.

**The shape.** Here is how I see it. Tell me if I've got it wrong. (1) What happens, in their words and at their hour. (2) Where it sits: which part of them is tired or loud, and which part is still reaching. (3) What is underneath, as a picture, with no blame. (4) What is stuck, and what is still free. (5) What we start with, and why it fits.

**For the owner's case.** "Here's how I see it, and tell me if I'm wrong. Around three every afternoon your body goes heavy, and the thought comes that nothing you do today will matter. That heaviness lives in the part of you that runs the body; the part of you that wants your day to mean something is still there, which is why it bothers you at all. Underneath, it sounds like the old idea that you have to be more before you're worth moving for. Your feelings are stuck right now, but your hands and your mouth are free. So we start with something small and quick that's true and good, done at three o'clock, not with digging into why."

## Part 6. Rules

1. **Danger overrides everything.** Any danger cue moves straight to the protocol, whatever the formulation says (MAP R11; D03).
2. **Body and medicine first.** Heaviness, numbness or racing with signs of illness, no sleep, no food or lasting hopelessness gets a doctor and a person alongside. Depression is never read as a spiritual state (MAP S03, S04).
3. **The diagnosis is for the guide, not said to the person.** No labels: never 'yeshus', 'atzvus', 'animal soul', 'your root is pride'. The person hears only the plain explain-back (MAP R7).
4. **The root is never blame.** It is offered as a picture of how the self holds itself up, never as a fault. 'Pride' is never said to someone heavy or ashamed.
5. **Not every pain is the root.** Grief, harm, illness and injustice are real. The root is read only in how the self carries them (RF15).
6. **Meet the plainer state first.** When two readings fit, take the lower, plainer one (MAP Part 5).
7. **Joy before bitterness, and bitterness only at a set hour.** Short, private, closed the same hour in joy. Never on a low day or to the crushed (Tanya ch. 26, 31).
8. **Holding back before turning; lower return before higher.** Never tell someone holding back they should be turned by now (Tanya ch. 27; MAP S30, S31).
9. **Enter by what is free.** If the heart is shut, go from mind straight to hand. The garments are always in reach (Tanya ch. 12, 14; MAP C3).
10. **Caught or reasonable decides the road.** A person who can hear reasons gets the long road; a caught one gets the yoke and a deed first, then the long road (METHODS M48).
11. **One block at a time.** Treat the most blocked place through the freest one; don't open three fronts.
12. **Their words, or nothing.** Every field carries their words. A guess is offered once, as the guide's own (FORMULATION §4).
13. **Ask with consent, one at a time, and give before you ask again.** At most three questions before a first formulation (Part 5).
14. **Build on a strength.** The first practice grows from something they already do (Hayom Yom 27 Menachem Av).
15. **Judge by the afterward, not by intensity.** The sign of healing is a calmer day, a softer reaction, a deed done; not a bigger feeling (MAP S18; GD-13).
16. **Reassess every turn.** People move sideways and back. Place them fresh (MAP R2).
17. **Same anatomy for everyone.** Two pulls, the powers, the garments and the root belong to every person; the lips are fitted to the person, and the name of G-d comes last for someone unsure (D17, D28; MAP R13).
18. **Never promise a state; never be the Rebbe.** Point to real people: a teacher, a friend, a doctor (VS-15; GD-19).

## Gaps (kept honest)

- No source gives a questionnaire. The eight questions and their wording are ours; each one follows a distinction the sources draw, named beside it. - The questions pull against D25 and D36 (no interviewing, no feeling-questions). Our reconciliation is in Part 5; the owner should decide. - Reading every trouble as a form of yeshus is stated for sadness (Tanya ch. 27; Derech Chaim), the stone heart (ch. 29), anger (Iggeret HaKodesh 25), the husk itself (ch. 19, 22) and the flat world (Shaar HaYichud 4). For worry, loneliness, emptiness and fear of death the link is ours, built on Iggeret HaKodesh 11 and Tanya ch. 32. - Calling the Piaseczner's two-in-one sorting 'birur', and the Tanya's heavy-hour turn 'hamtakah', is ours. Both moves are stated; the names applied to them are ours. - The Tanya speaks of the Jewish soul. Applying the two-souls anatomy to every person is the owner's reading (D28). - The elements-to-modern-presentations table (fire: irritability, air: feeds, and so on) is ours; the element-to-trait pairs are stated in Tanya ch. 1. - MR1-C19 ('whole in himself') is cited from depths/DIGEST.md in English only; its Hebrew was not re-verified here. - Several lines are OCR (Derech Chaim, Hemshech Samach Vav, and Kuntres HaAvodah and Kuntres HaTefillah as the index marks them). They are quoted as the files have them. - Two Tanya lines that MAP.md cites by the Chassidus-txt edition sit at other lines in the Chabad Library edition used here: Shaar HaYichud 4 is line 808 (MAP: 780) and Tanya ch. 32 'one Father' is line 466 (MAP: 444). Both were checked in the file named. - Nothing here has been tried with real people. It should be piloted with a human mashpia and a clinician watching.
