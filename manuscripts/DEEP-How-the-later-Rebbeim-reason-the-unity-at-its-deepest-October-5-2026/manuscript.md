## Summary (one page)

The deep layer of Chabad, as the Tzemach Tzedek, the Rebbe Rashab and the Rebbe reason it, does not get its depth by climbing to a higher light. It gets it by changing the subject of the sentence. Almost every move begins by asking whether a claim is about G-d's light (a shining that shows Him without being Him, changes nothing in Him, and pushes out what is not itself) or about His Essence (which nothing hides, which negates nothing, and which is the self of each thing). Once the subject is right, the old paradoxes come apart. The tzimtzum hides only toward the receiver. The world's solid 'I am' is not a wall but the Essence's signature, since only what exists from itself can make something that feels that way. The finite is not a falling-away, because a G-d without power in limit would be lacking. Concealment is His own power, since only the infinite can hide itself.

Three habits mark the reasoning. First, it indexes everything: hidden toward whom, nothing relative to what. Second, it argues from capacity: what a limited power cannot do (make matter, hide itself, turn evil) shows where a thing comes from. Third, it ranks the two unities and puts the lower one higher: the world as it exists, bounded in time and place, is one with the Essence, and the dwelling He desired is here, in the plain physical deed.

Its guardrails are equally sharp. It is not pantheism: creation changes nothing in Him and does not flow from Him by necessity. It is not acosmism: removing the tzimtzum would not erase the creatures. It does not erase the self: the yechidah is the self at its truest, always whole, and the bittul a person reaches by effort (bittul hayesh) is exactly the vessel. The result, for a guide, is a procedure: sort light from Essence, index the claim, find what stands above both poles, and land it below.

## The procedure in one look (ours, built from the moves)

1. **Hear the question.** Restate the real question in one plain line. Note who is asking (beginner, advanced, doubter) and whether they are in pain. (ours)
2. **Sort the subject (DM1).** Is the claim about the light or the Essence? Apply the right rules. (stated rules; ours as a step)
3. **Index it (DM2).** Hidden toward whom? Nothing relative to what? Say both sides. (stated)
4. **Locate it (DM3).** Memale, sovev, or the Essence above both? (stated)
5. **Find the poles and what is above them (DM10).** Name the two things that seem to clash; show each is a power or name of His; find the Essence that holds both. Use DM4 (capacity), DM5 (sign backward) or DM6 (wholeness) to show why. (stated)
6. **Picture it, then break the picture (DM12).** One mashal; say exactly where it fails and what the failure shows. (stated)
7. **Choose the unity and the bittul for this person (DM7, DM8, DM9).** Upper unity or lower; which bittul is real here; make sure the self is revealed, not erased. (stated ranking; ours for the choosing)
8. **Ask why and land below (DM11).** Rest on the desire for a dwelling; end with one plain act. (stated)
9. **Run the guardrails (G1-G11).** Read each self-check line against your answer before giving it. (ours)
10. **Speak plainly.** 120-180 words, Hebrew terms once with meaning, mark what the text says and what is ours. (ours)

## Terms

## T01 · The Essence (Atzmus) (עצמות ומהות)

**Definition.** G-d Himself, as He is: not a light, not a quality, not a level, and not limited by any definition, even 'infinite'.

**Relations.** is the source of T02; is beyond comparison with T02; is above both T05, T06; is beyond T10 (both 'something' and 'nothing'); is shown precisely in T08; is not changed by creation (T02 does the creating work); is the root of T18.

**Common misreadings.** - Treating the Essence as the biggest light, or the highest 'level' you can reach in meditation. - Thinking 'Infinite' (Ein Sof) names the Essence; the Rashab says 'infinite' properly describes the light, since an essence does not spread (Q91, Q92). - Saying anything positive about it as a description. Even the surrounding light is known only by negation (Q87).

**Sources.** - **Q01** AB 398: אמנם ענין האור הוא שזהו רק הארה לבד שאין בו מן העצם — *The light is only a shining; it has nothing of the essence in it* - **Q29** AB 4455: דעצם העצמות נבדל בערך מן האור — *the very Essence is beyond comparison with the light* - **Q85** CH 112: ולכן אין שינוי אצלו ית' בין קודם בריאת העולם לאחר הבראו כיון שמקור ההתהוות רק מהארה בעלמא שאינו נוגע להעצמות — *so there is no change in Him between before and after the world was created, since creation comes only from a mere shining that does not touch the Essence* - **Q60** SV 5948 (OCR): כי אין שום דבר מעלים על עצמות אוא"ס — *for nothing at all hides the Essence of the Infinite Light* - **Q87** ML 1914: ואור הסובב, אין שייך בו השגת החיוב ורק ידיעת השלילה — *the surrounding light cannot be grasped positively, only known by negation* - **Q91** AB 385: והרי עצמות אינו בבחי' התפשטות דכל עצמי אינו מתפשט — *for the Essence is not a spreading-out; nothing essential spreads* - **Q92** AB 385: אלא הכוונה באוא"ס על האור — *rather, "Infinite Light" refers to the light*

## T02 · The light (Or), Infinite Light (Or Ein Sof) (אור, אור אין סוף)

**Definition.** A shining that shows that the Essence is there, without being the Essence and without changing it, the way sunlight shows the sun.

**Relations.** reveals only the existence of T01, not what it is (Q03); clings to and is 'like' T01 (Q05); is the go-between through which T01 creates T09 (AB 4455); is contracted by T04; divides into T05 and T06; negates what is not itself while T01 negates nothing (Q07-Q08).

**Common misreadings.** - Taking a spiritual experience, a revelation or a feeling of G-d's presence as contact with the Essence itself (Q03, Q81). - Thinking creation adds to or takes from G-d (Q02, Q06). - Thinking the light streams out by necessity, like sunlight. The Tzemach Tzedek: it comes by His simple will (Q84, Q86).

**Sources.** - **Q01** AB 398: אמנם ענין האור הוא שזהו רק הארה לבד שאין בו מן העצם — *The light is only a shining; it has nothing of the essence in it* - **Q02** AB 398: וע"כ אין האור פועל שום שינוי בהעצם — *so the light makes no change at all in the essence (the sun is not added to when it shines, nor lessened when clouds block it)* - **Q03** AB 263: שהאור מגלה רק מציאות המאור לא עצם מהותו — *the light reveals only that the source exists, not what the source is* - **Q04** SV 2403 (OCR): דאור א"ס הוא בחי' הארה לבד, ולא עצם — *the Infinite Light is only a shining, not the essence* - **Q05** SV 2403 (OCR): שהאור הוא כמו העצם, כי הוא דבוק ממש במקורו — *the light is like the essence, because it clings to its source* - **Q06** ML 1983: אין זה פועל שינוי בו ית', כי החיות דכל העולמות הוא הארה בלבד שבאין ערוך אליו כלל — *His making and enlivening all worlds makes no change in Him, for the life of all worlds is only a shining, beyond comparison to Him* - **Q84** CH 112: אך כיון שאור זה נמשך ממנו ית' ברצונו הפשוט — *but since this light comes from Him by His simple will* - **Q86** CH 112: כפי השקר של מאמיני הקדמות שהעולם נמשך מהש"י על צד החיוב — *according to the falsehood of those who believe in an eternal world, that the world flows from G-d by necessity*

## T03 · Infinite Light before the tzimtzum (אוא"ס שלפני הצמצום)

**Definition.** The light as it is included in the Essence, before there is any 'room' for worlds: everything there would be 'nothing', not even a thing that bows.

**Relations.** is the root of T05 and T06 (AB 1678); shines after the tzimtzum exactly as before (Q11, Q13); leaves no room for a separate 'something' (Q14).

**Common misreadings.** - Picturing a time 'before', and a place that was full and then emptied. - Thinking it is gone now. Its shining 'after as before' is the whole point (Q13).

**Sources.** - **Q11** ML 2739: שאחרי הצמצום מאיר אוא"ס בגילוי ממש במקום החלל כמו קודם הצמצום — *after the tzimtzum the Infinite Light shines openly in the "empty space" just as before* - **Q13** SV 762 (OCR): כי לגבי אוא"ס אין הצמצום מעלים ומסתיר, ומאיר בגילוי לאחר הצמצום כמו קודם הצמצום — *for to the Infinite Light the tzimtzum does not hide; it shines after the tzimtzum as before* - **Q14** AB 5538: וז"ע לית אתר פנוי מני' דהיינו מבחי' עצמות אוא"ס — *this is "there is no place empty of Him", meaning of the Essence of the Infinite Light*

## T04 · Contraction (tzimtzum) (צמצום)

**Definition.** G-d's hiding of His light so that a world can feel like a separate something; it hides only toward the creatures, not toward Him.

**Relations.** hides T02 from the creature, never T01 (Q16, Q60); is not literal (Q10); is the work of T18 'hen hen gevurosav' (Q70); is itself for the sake of revealing (Q72); is one with Havayah, as Elokim (T07).

**Common misreadings.** - Literal: G-d withdrew and the world is a space He left (ruled out: Q10, Q11, Q13). - The opposite mistake: 'nothing happened, so the world is an illusion'. The hiding is real for the receiver and is what lets creatures be (Q15, Q79). - Thinking it was forced on Him as a technical need. The Rebbe: the Essence 'is not compelled' to reveal and can also be hidden (Q74).

**Sources.** - **Q10** ML 2739: שהצמצום אינו כפשוטו (היינו שהצמצום אינו מעלים לגבי אוא"ס) — *the tzimtzum is not literal (that is, it does not hide anything from the Infinite Light itself)* - **Q11** ML 2739: שאחרי הצמצום מאיר אוא"ס בגילוי ממש במקום החלל כמו קודם הצמצום — *after the tzimtzum the Infinite Light shines openly in the "empty space" just as before* - **Q12** ML 2738: שהצמצום וההעלם הוא רק לגבי התלמיד ולא לגבי הרב — *the contraction and hiding are only toward the student, not toward the teacher* - **Q13** SV 762 (OCR): כי לגבי אוא"ס אין הצמצום מעלים ומסתיר, ומאיר בגילוי לאחר הצמצום כמו קודם הצמצום — *for to the Infinite Light the tzimtzum does not hide; it shines after the tzimtzum as before* - **Q15** AB 5537: והרי הצמצום הזה הוא רק לגבי המקבל — *this contraction is only toward the receiver* - **Q16** SV 802 (OCR): אבל בבחיי עצמות האור, אינו שייך מדידה זו כלל, ואין הצמצום פועל בו — *but in the essence of the light this measuring does not apply at all, and the tzimtzum does not act on it* - **Q70** AB 3544: ורק הן הן גבורותיו שאינו נרגש בנבראים — *only "these are His mighty acts": that He is not felt in the creatures* - **Q74** ML 1391: שהעצמות אינו מוכרח ח"ו בענין הגילוי וביכלתו להיות (גם) בהעלם — *the Essence is not compelled, heaven forbid, to reveal; it is able to be (also) hidden* - **Q79** ML 2465: אין הכוונה שבהסיר הצמצום יהי' העדר מציאות הנבראים — *it does not mean that removing the contraction would make the creatures cease to exist* - **Q88** ML 2739: כי בהמשל דרב ותלמיד, הצמצום עצמו הוא העלם גם לגבי הרב — *for in the teacher-student parable, the contraction itself is a hiding even for the teacher* - **Q89** ML 2739: משא"כ למעלה, גם הצמצום עצמו אינו מעלים לגבי אוא"ס — *whereas above, even the contraction itself does not hide from the Infinite Light*

## T05 · Filling all worlds (memale kol almin) (ממלא כל עלמין)

**Definition.** The light that enters each creature from inside and gives it life by its measure, differently for each thing.

**Relations.** is light measured by T04; cannot by itself make physical 'something' (Q23); is G-dliness, not a separate thing (Q21); is lit up from within by T06 (Q21); matches the love 'with all your heart' (ML 2574).

**Common misreadings.** - Thinking memale is G-d 'inside' things like water in a jar; it is the life of the thing by its measure, not a substance in it. - Thinking memale is less G-dly than sovev (Q21).

**Sources.** - **Q19** ML 2592: ממכ"ע הוא האור המתלבש בעולמות ובהנבראים להחיות אותם לפי ערכם — *memale is the light that clothes itself in the worlds and creatures to enliven each according to its measure* - **Q21** DM 119: אינה בבחי' נפרד בפ"ע ח"ו אלא הוא אלקות והסובב הוא הממלא — *(the life shining through contractions) is not something separate on its own, heaven forbid; it is G-dliness, and the surrounding is the filling* - **Q23** SV 2261 (OCR): שא"א להיות התהוות היש מבחי' ממכ"ע, לפי שממכ"ע הוא בחי' גבול — *the "something" cannot come into being from memale, because memale is a level of limit*

## T06 · Surrounding all worlds (sovev kol almin) (סובב כל עלמין)

**Definition.** The light that does not enter things as their measured life but holds them all equally, from 'above', meaning beyond grasp, not beyond in space.

**Relations.** is equal for all worlds, the highest and the physical (Q22); is not spatially above (Q20); is known only by negation (Q87); gives the power of 'something' through T05 (DM 120); is below T01 (Q18).

**Common misreadings.** - A circle around the universe (Q20). - The top of the ladder. The Rebbe places the Essence 'above filling and surrounding' (Q18).

**Sources.** - **Q18** ML 2592: ממלא כל עלמין, סובב כל עלמין ועצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מממלא וסובב — *"filling all worlds", "surrounding all worlds", and the Essence of the Infinite Light that is above filling and surrounding* - **Q20** DM 119: שאין פי' שסובב מלמעלה כעיגול כי אינו בגדר מקום — *sovev does not mean surrounding from above like a circle, for He is not in the category of place* - **Q22** DM 119: אבל אור הסובב הוא בשוה לכולן לאצי' ועשי' כאחד ממש — *but the surrounding light is equal to all, to Atzilus and Asiyah alike* - **Q87** ML 1914: ואור הסובב, אין שייך בו השגת החיוב ורק ידיעת השלילה — *the surrounding light cannot be grasped positively, only known by negation*

## T07 · Havayah and Elokim (הוי' ואלקים)

**Definition.** Two names of the one G-d: Havayah, the power that brings into being and reveals; Elokim, the power that hides and limits ('nature').

**Relations.** are one 'Havayah is Elokim' (DM 119); are both names of the Holy One above both (Q71); Elokim is T04 and is also for revealing (Q72).

**Common misreadings.** - A 'good' name and a 'bad' name; or nature as a second power beside G-d (Q77).

**Sources.** - **Q71** ML 2740: דשני השמות הוי' ואלקים הם שמותיו של הקב"ה שלמעלה משניהם — *the two names Havayah and Elokim are names of the Holy One who is above both* - **Q72** ML 2740: שגם שם אלקים (צמצום) ענינו הוא לגלות — *the name Elokim (contraction) too is for the sake of revealing* - **Q77** DM 119: כולם ממונים על פעולותיהם אין להם שלטון בעצמם — *all are appointed over their work; they have no rule of their own*

## T08 · The created something (yesh hanivra) (יש הנברא)

**Definition.** A created thing, which feels that it exists by itself and does not feel its source.

**Relations.** comes into being only by T01 (Q26, Q31); proves T09 (Q27); feels self-standing because its root is T01 (Q30); has no standing of its own (Q35); is one with T01 as it is, bounded (Q36).

**Common misreadings.** - That its solidity is a mistake to be got rid of; the Rebbe says its self-standing is a sign of the Essence (Q30). - That it exists on its own once made (Q35).

**Sources.** - **Q26** DM 120: כי באמת התהוות הגשמית הוא בכח הא"ס לבד ולא ע"י השתלשלות עו"ע — *in truth the physical comes into being by the power of the Infinite alone, not by a chain of cause and effect* - **Q27** AB 4454: ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי — *so it is precisely the created "something" that proves the True Being* - **Q30** ML 1252: מ"מ הנה זה גופא שיהי' נדמה עכ"פ שמציאותו מעצמותו זהו מפני ששרשו מהעצמות שמציאותו מעצמותו — *still, the very fact that it seems its existence is from itself is because its root is from the Essence, Whose existence is from Itself* - **Q31** ML 1252: דהנה ידוע דהבריאה הוא רק בכח העצמות — *it is known that creation is only by the power of the Essence* - **Q35** IN 93: שיכיר (ולא רק שיבין) שאין שום נתינת מקום להיש מצד עצמו — *that he recognize, not only understand, that the "something" has no standing of its own* - **Q36** IN 100: שגם העולמות עצמם כמו שהם נמצאים במציאותם (ומוגדרים בגדר זמן ומקום) מיוחדים הם בתכלית היחוד בעצמות אוא"ס ב"ה — *the worlds themselves, as they exist (bounded by time and place), are utterly one with the Essence of the Infinite Light*

## T09 · The True Being (yesh ha'amiti) (יש האמיתי)

**Definition.** G-d as the One whose existence is from Himself, with no cause before Him: the only 'is' that is not borrowed.

**Relations.** is shown by T08 (Q27); is the truth of the physical thing (Q32); is found specifically in the physical, not the spiritual (Q33).

**Common misreadings.** - A 'something' alongside other somethings; it is the truth inside every something.

**Sources.** - **Q27** AB 4454: ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי — *so it is precisely the created "something" that proves the True Being* - **Q32** ML 1290: יתגלה בהיש הנברא שרשו האמיתי, והוא יש האמיתי — *in the created "something" its true root will be revealed: the True Being* - **Q33** ML 1291: מפני מה ביש הנברא דוקא הוא יש האמיתי ואינו נמצא ברוחניות — *why is the True Being found specifically in the created thing and not in the spiritual?*

## T10 · Nothing (ayin), creation from nothing (yesh me'ayin) (אין, יש מאין)

**Definition.** 'Nothing' names what is beyond grasp and the 'absolute nothing' things come from; the Essence is above both 'something' and 'nothing', so it can make 'is not' into 'is'.

**Relations.** T01 is above both yesh and ayin (Q34); creation is renewed every moment from it (IN 95); the self is to become 'ayin' (Q51).

**Common misreadings.** - Nothing = emptiness or zero. - That being 'ayin' means having no self (see T12-T14).

**Sources.** - **Q34** IN 92: ובמילא אינו בהגדרים ד"אין" ו"יש", ולכן ביכלתו לעשות את ה"אינו" "ישנו" — *so He is not within the definitions of "nothing" and "something", and therefore He can make the "is not" into "is"* - **Q51** DM 120: ולא לומר אני אלא לעשות מאני אין — *not to say "I", but to make the "I" into nothing*

## T11 · A dwelling below (dirah betachtonim) (דירה בתחתונים)

**Definition.** The purpose of creation: that the Essence itself be at home in this lowest physical world, as a man is himself in his own home.

**Relations.** rests on a desire with no reason (Q38); means T01 revealed below as above (Q39); is made by Torah and mitzvos in physical deeds (ML 77).

**Common misreadings.** - That the world is a waiting room for heaven; or that the goal is to escape the physical.

**Sources.** - **Q37** ML 77: וכידוע דענין הדירה הוא מה שהעצם נמצא בהדירה — *what makes a home a home is that the essence (of its owner) is found in it* - **Q38** SV 219 (OCR): נתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים, ומאחר שנתאוה הנה על תאוה אין שואלים קושיא — *the Holy One desired a dwelling below; and since He desired, about a desire one asks no question* - **Q39** SV 3680 (OCR): אבל ענין הדירה בתחתונים הוא להיות גילוי בחי' עצמות אוא"ס למטה כמו למעלה — *a dwelling below means that the Essence of the Infinite Light be revealed below as above*

## T12 · Nullifying the self-feeling (bittul hayesh) (ביטול היש)

**Definition.** Remaining a something, but no longer a something for yourself: the ego set down, the person still there and working.

**Relations.** is the only kind of vessel that can receive (Q43); is, in truth, the most a creature reaches by its own effort (Q41); matches accepting the yoke, where the thing stays in its existence (Q45).

**Common misreadings.** - That it is a low or 'beginner' bittul to outgrow; it is the vessel.

**Sources.** - **Q41** AB 2501: שגם המדרי' הגבוהות והנעלות בענין הביטול שנק' ביטול במציאות הנה לפי האמת הוא בחי' ביטול היש לבד — *even the highest levels of bittul called "bittul of existence" are, in truth, only bittul hayesh* - **Q43** AB 3509: והכלי לקבל הוא דוקא בחי' ביטול היש, שהרי מה שבבחי' ביטול במציאות לגמרי שאינו בבחי' יש כלל הרי אינו בבחי' כלי — *the vessel to receive is specifically bittul hayesh; what is wholly nullified, not a "something" at all, is no vessel* - **Q45** ML 1964: הוא ע"ד הביטול דקבלת עול, דהגם שנשאר במציאותו — *like the bittul of accepting the yoke: though it remains in its existence*

## T13 · Nullification of existence (bittul bimetzius) (ביטול במציאות)

**Definition.** Not counting as anything at all before the source: 'all before Him is as nothing'; the bittul of the higher unity.

**Relations.** is the bittul of T15 (Q57); cannot be reached by the creature's own power (Q40); what is wholly nullified is no vessel (Q43).

**Common misreadings.** - That a person should try to vanish; or that a feeling of dissolving proves you are there.

**Sources.** - **Q40** AB 2501: הרי הנברא א"א לו להיות בבחי' ביטול במציאות ממש — *a creature (by its own power) cannot be in true bittul bimetzius* - **Q43** AB 3509: והכלי לקבל הוא דוקא בחי' ביטול היש, שהרי מה שבבחי' ביטול במציאות לגמרי שאינו בבחי' יש כלל הרי אינו בבחי' כלי — *the vessel to receive is specifically bittul hayesh; what is wholly nullified, not a "something" at all, is no vessel* - **Q57** ML 1964: הוא ע"ד הביטול דיחודא עילאה (ביטול במציאות) הבא ע"י ההתבוננות דכולא קמי' כלאחשיב — *like the bittul of the higher unity (bittul of existence), which comes from contemplating "all before Him is as nothing"*

## T14 · Essential bittul; the yechidah (ביטול עצמי; יחידה)

**Definition.** The soul's innermost point, one with the Essence; its bittul is not an achievement but what it is, so it 'cannot be separated' from Him.

**Relations.** is one with T01 (Q90, Q48); is beyond flaw and impurity (Q48); has no opposing counterpart (Q50); shows in self-sacrifice not from one's own will (Q46-Q47); is given from above through Torah (Q42).

**Common misreadings.** - That the self is erased; in fact the yechidah is the self at its truest (Q49). - That only great souls have it (IN 63: every Jew's Modeh Ani).

**Sources.** - **Q42** AB 2501: וע"כ הביטול בזה הוא בבחי' ביטול עצמי — *therefore the bittul here (given from above, through Torah) is essential bittul* - **Q46** ML 751: הוא לא מצד הרצון שלו — *(his self-sacrifice) is not from his own will* - **Q47** ML 751: אלא מפני שאינו יכול להפרד ח"ו מאחדותו ית' — *but because he cannot be separated, heaven forbid, from His Oneness* - **Q48** IN 64: אבל מצד היחידה שבנשמה שמיוחדת תמיד בעצמותו ית', אין שייך כל פגם וטומאה ח"ו ונשאר תמיד בשלמותו — *but the yechidah of the soul, always one with His Essence, can have no flaw or impurity and always stays whole* - **Q49** IN 28: ובחי' יחידה היא עצם הנשמה שלמעלה מגדר פרטים, כשמה — *yechidah is the essence of the soul, above the category of particulars, as its name says* - **Q50** ML 750: כי כיון שבחינת היחידה אין כנגדה לעומת זה — *since the yechidah has no opposing counterpart* - **Q90** ML 147: אחת למעלה היא גילוי היחידה, שהיא מיוחדת עם העצמות — *the one (sprinkling) above is the revealing of the yechidah, which is one with the Essence*

## T15 · The higher unity (yichuda ila'ah) (יחודא עילאה)

**Definition.** Seeing from 'above': the worlds do not count as existing at all; all is included in the Nothing.

**Relations.** its bittul is T13 (Q57); is felt as 'they are not there' (Q53); is not higher than T16 (Q55, Q58).

**Common misreadings.** - That this is the final truth and the world is an illusion.

**Sources.** - **Q53** SV 3173 (OCR): דבאופן הא' ההרגש הוא שאינם במציאות (והיינו שהכל נכלל באין) — *in the first way, the feeling is that they do not exist (all is included in the Nothing)* - **Q57** ML 1964: הוא ע"ד הביטול דיחודא עילאה (ביטול במציאות) הבא ע"י ההתבוננות דכולא קמי' כלאחשיב — *like the bittul of the higher unity (bittul of existence), which comes from contemplating "all before Him is as nothing"*

## T16 · The lower unity (yichuda tata'ah) (יחודא תתאה)

**Definition.** Seeing from 'below': things do exist, and their existence is G-dliness.

**Relations.** is felt as 'they exist and are G-dliness' (Q54); is higher than or equal to T15 (Q55, Q58, Q59); matches the Rebbe's 'the worlds as they are are one with the Essence' (Q36).

**Common misreadings.** - That 'existence is G-dliness' means the things are the Essence (see G1).

**Sources.** - **Q54** SV 3173 (OCR): ובאופן הב' ההרגש הוא שהן בבחי' מציאות והן אלקות — *in the second way, the feeling is that they exist and they are G-dliness* - **Q55** SV 3173 (OCR): האופן הב' הוא במדרי' נעלית יותר — *the second way is a higher level* - **Q56** AB 2342: אם שהעולמות לאחר שנתהוו ה"ה ממש כמו קודם שנתהוו כו' או שהמציאות גופא הוא אלקות שהן ב' אופני היחוד — *whether the worlds after they came to be are exactly as before, or that existence itself is G-dliness: these are the two modes of unity* - **Q58** ML 2716: ולכן העבודה דיחו"ת מגעת למעלה יותר מהעבודה דיחו"ע — *therefore the service of the lower unity reaches higher than the service of the higher unity* - **Q59** ML 1085: יחודא עילאה ויחודא תתאה (ששניהם הם בשוה) — *the higher unity and the lower unity, both equal*

## T17 · The power of limit and the power of the unlimited (כח הגבול וכח הבלי גבול)

**Definition.** The Essence, being whole, has power in limit just as in the unlimited; limit is not less G-dly than infinity.

**Relations.** both belong to T01 (Q65); limit is truly infinite power (Q67); multiplicity is required by the Essence's simplicity (Q66).

**Common misreadings.** - That the finite is a falling away from G-d; or that a bigger number is nearer to the infinite (Q69).

**Sources.** - **Q65** AB 3691: כשם שיש לו כח בבלתי גבול כן יש לו כח בגבול — *just as He has power in the unlimited, so He has power in limit* - **Q66** AB 3691: דהעצמות גופא מכריח ענין הריבוי וההתחלקות — *the Essence itself requires multiplicity and division* - **Q67** AB 79: והכח הגבול הוא א"ס ממש כמו כח הבל"ג — *the power of limit is truly infinite, just like the power of the unlimited* - **Q68** CH 604: שאף למטה בגבול מלובש בחי' א"ס ואעפ"כ הם בחי' גבול ומספר ממש, והן הן גבורותיו — *even below, in limit, the Infinite is clothed, and yet they are truly limit and number; these are His mighty acts* - **Q69** CH 211: ואין במילייאן אחד שום קירוב לבלי גבול יותר מבאחד ממש — *in one million there is no more nearness to the infinite than in one*

## T18 · Essential hiddenness; 'these are His mighty acts' (העלם העצמי; הן הן גבורותיו)

**Definition.** The hiding of G-d in the world is His own act and power, rooted in the Essence; only the Infinite can hide itself.

**Relations.** is the root of the 'something' and all hidings (Q63); hides only toward the worlds (Q62); will in the end shine itself (Q64).

**Common misreadings.** - That something else hides G-d (Q60). - That hiddenness is distance or rejection.

**Sources.** - **Q60** SV 5948 (OCR): כי אין שום דבר מעלים על עצמות אוא"ס — *for nothing at all hides the Essence of the Infinite Light* - **Q61** SV 5948 (OCR): ורק האוא"ס הבלי גבול בכחו להעלים א"ע — *only the boundless Infinite Light has the power to hide itself* - **Q62** SV 5948 (OCR): הרי ההעלם הוא רק לגבי עולמות, לא לגבי עצמו — *the hiding is only toward the worlds, not toward Himself* - **Q63** SV 2947 (OCR): וכידוע דשרש התהוות היש ושרש כל ההעלמות הן מבחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס — *the root of the "something" and the root of all hidings is the essential hiddenness of the Infinite* - **Q64** SV 2947 (OCR): שההעלם בעצמו יאיר — *that the hiding itself will shine* - **Q70** AB 3544: ורק הן הן גבורותיו שאינו נרגש בנבראים — *only "these are His mighty acts": that He is not felt in the creatures*

## Moves

*The moves are the texts' own reasoning; the names, numbering and step lists are ours. Each worked example is from the text, with the verified lines.*

## DM1 · Light or Essence? (the sorting question)

**What it does.** Before saying anything about G-d and the world, it asks whether the claim is about the light (a revelation, a level, a life-force) or about the Essence. Most apparent contradictions come from mixing the two.

**When it is used.** Whenever a statement says G-d 'fills', 'surrounds', 'withdraws', 'is revealed', 'is felt', or 'changes'. Also whenever a person reports a spiritual experience.

**Steps.** 1. Name the claim in one plain sentence. 2. Ask: is this about something that shines, spreads, is measured or felt? Then it is about the light (T02). 3. Apply the light rules: it shows only that the source exists (Q03); it changes nothing in the source (Q02, Q06); it pushes aside what is not itself (Q07). 4. Ask: does the claim need the Essence itself? (Bringing a 'something' into being, being found equally below, transforming evil.) Then apply the Essence rules: nothing hides it (Q60), it negates nothing (Q08), it is in everything (Q09). 5. Re-state the claim with the right subject, and see if the contradiction is gone.

**Worked example from the text.** The Rashab, Ayin Beis: the sun's light is only a shining with nothing of the sun's essence in it; when clouds block it, the sun loses nothing. - **Q01** AB 398: אמנם ענין האור הוא שזהו רק הארה לבד שאין בו מן העצם — *The light is only a shining; it has nothing of the essence in it* - **Q02** AB 398: וע"כ אין האור פועל שום שינוי בהעצם — *so the light makes no change at all in the essence (the sun is not added to when it shines, nor lessened when clouds block it)* - **Q03** AB 263: שהאור מגלה רק מציאות המאור לא עצם מהותו — *the light reveals only that the source exists, not what the source is*

**Done badly.** Stopping at the light and calling it G-d (a strong experience becomes 'I touched the Essence'); or using Essence-language for the light, so G-d seems to change, grow, or leave. Also: making the distinction a reason to feel far, when the point is that the Essence is nearer than the light (Q24).

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M6 (From the light to its source); adds the explicit test of which rules apply. Tag: stated (light/essence rules); ours (the sorting procedure).*

## DM2 · From whose side? (relative to whom)

**What it does.** Every statement of hiding, distance or nothingness is indexed: hidden toward whom? nothing relative to what? The texts write this as 'legabei' (relative to).

**When it is used.** Tzimtzum, concealment, 'the world is nothing', 'G-d is far', suffering.

**Steps.** 1. Find the hidden or 'nothing' thing and ask: toward whom is it hidden or nothing? 2. Check His side: to the Infinite Light the tzimtzum does not hide (Q13); the hiding is only toward the worlds (Q62). 3. Check the creature's side: there the hiding is real, and it is what lets the creature exist (Q15, Q79). 4. Say both truths together, each with its index. Do not let one cancel the other.

**Worked example from the text.** The Rebbe's teacher-and-student mashal: a teacher hides his deep idea to teach a weak student; the hiding is only toward the student. Then he shows where the mashal breaks: for a human teacher, the act of contracting does briefly hide the idea from himself too; above, even the contraction itself does not hide. - **Q12** ML 2738: שהצמצום וההעלם הוא רק לגבי התלמיד ולא לגבי הרב — *the contraction and hiding are only toward the student, not toward the teacher* - **Q88** ML 2739: כי בהמשל דרב ותלמיד, הצמצום עצמו הוא העלם גם לגבי הרב — *for in the teacher-student parable, the contraction itself is a hiding even for the teacher* - **Q89** ML 2739: משא"כ למעלה, גם הצמצום עצמו אינו מעלים לגבי אוא"ס — *whereas above, even the contraction itself does not hide from the Infinite Light*

**Done badly.** Dropping the index: 'the world is nothing' said flat, to a person, as if it were true from their side. Or the reverse: 'G-d is hidden' said flat, as if He were hidden from Himself.

*Builds on: Shaar HaYichud 7 (two knowings, from above and below), and DEPTH-ENGINE M12 (Hold both sides). Tag: stated.*

## DM3 · Place it on the three tiers (memale, sovev, Essence)

**What it does.** Locates any G-dly presence on three tiers: the light that fills each thing by its measure, the light that holds all equally beyond grasp, and the Essence above both.

**When it is used.** When asked 'where is G-d in this?' or when two teachings seem to disagree because one speaks of memale and the other of sovev.

**Steps.** 1. Memale: what in this thing is its specific life and order? (Q19) 2. Sovev: what holds it that is equal for the highest and the lowest, and that it cannot grasp? (Q22, Q87) 3. Essence: what is here that neither of those can explain, such as the thing's very existence (Q23) or a bond with no reason? 4. Name which tier the person is speaking from, and which tier the answer needs.

**Worked example from the text.** The Rebbe lists the three in order and above both 'filling and surrounding' puts the Essence; the Tzemach Tzedek insists that sovev is not a circle in space and that 'the surrounding is the filling'. - **Q18** ML 2592: ממלא כל עלמין, סובב כל עלמין ועצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מממלא וסובב — *"filling all worlds", "surrounding all worlds", and the Essence of the Infinite Light that is above filling and surrounding* - **Q20** DM 119: שאין פי' שסובב מלמעלה כעיגול כי אינו בגדר מקום — *sovev does not mean surrounding from above like a circle, for He is not in the category of place* - **Q21** DM 119: אינה בבחי' נפרד בפ"ע ח"ו אלא הוא אלקות והסובב הוא הממלא — *(the life shining through contractions) is not something separate on its own, heaven forbid; it is G-dliness, and the surrounding is the filling*

**Done badly.** Picturing the tiers as layers in space; or treating sovev as the top. Also using 'filling' to mean G-d is a substance inside things.

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M5 (Higher still), given a fixed map with a top that is not a level. Tag: stated.*

## DM4 · Only the unlimited can (the argument from capacity)

**What it does.** Reasons from what a thing can do to where it must come from: a limited power cannot do X, so X shows the unlimited. It turns a fact about the world into evidence of the Essence.

**When it is used.** Questions about how matter, self-standing, hiddenness or the turning of evil can come from G-d.

**Steps.** 1. State the fact plainly (matter exists; the world hides Him; evil can become good). 2. Ask what kind of power it needs. Show that a measured, limited power cannot do it: no chain of spiritual causes makes matter (Q26); a limited light cannot hide itself (Q61); a light cannot turn its opposite (Q73). 3. Conclude: this fact comes from the unlimited, from the Essence itself (Q25, Q31). 4. Turn it around: so the very fact that seemed to stand against G-d is His signature.

**Worked example from the text.** The Tzemach Tzedek: 'impossibility applies only to what has limit'; the physical comes from the power of the Infinite alone. The Rashab: only the boundless light can hide itself. - **Q25** DM 120: כי המניעה לא יתכן אלא לגבי בע"ג אבל לגבי הא"ס אין שום מניעה נמצאת — *impossibility applies only to what has limit; for the Infinite there is no impossibility* - **Q26** DM 120: כי באמת התהוות הגשמית הוא בכח הא"ס לבד ולא ע"י השתלשלות עו"ע — *in truth the physical comes into being by the power of the Infinite alone, not by a chain of cause and effect* - **Q61** SV 5948 (OCR): ורק האוא"ס הבלי גבול בכחו להעלים א"ע — *only the boundless Infinite Light has the power to hide itself*

**Done badly.** Turning it into a proof that G-d 'must' create (necessity) (Q84, Q86); or claiming to know how He does it. The argument shows from where, not how.

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M11 (Find the root of what blocks), made into a general rule. Tag: stated.*

## DM5 · The lowest proves the highest (read the sign backward)

**What it does.** Takes the feature that most seems to separate the world from G-d (its solid 'I am') and shows it is a likeness of the Essence, which alone exists from itself.

**When it is used.** 'Why does the world feel so real and separate?' 'Why does my ego feel so solid?' 'Why the physical?'

**Steps.** 1. Name the feature: the created thing feels it exists from itself. 2. Ask: what is the only thing that truly exists from itself? The Essence (T09). 3. Conclude: only what comes from the Essence can feel that way (Q30); the created 'something' proves the True Being (Q27). 4. Add the mashal of the great torch: its greatness shows far away, where its light is faintest (ML 1291). 5. Land it: so the physical is where He wants His home (Q33, Q37).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rebbe: the very fact that a created thing seems to exist from itself is because its root is from the Essence, whose existence is from Itself. - **Q30** ML 1252: מ"מ הנה זה גופא שיהי' נדמה עכ"פ שמציאותו מעצמותו זהו מפני ששרשו מהעצמות שמציאותו מעצמותו — *still, the very fact that it seems its existence is from itself is because its root is from the Essence, Whose existence is from Itself* - **Q27** AB 4454: ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי — *so it is precisely the created "something" that proves the True Being* - **Q33** ML 1291: מפני מה ביש הנברא דוקא הוא יש האמיתי ואינו נמצא ברוחניות — *why is the True Being found specifically in the created thing and not in the spiritual?*

**Done badly.** Saying the ego is holy as it is. The sign points to its root; the feeling 'I exist from myself' is still a hiding (Q35) that must be recognized for what it is.

*Builds on: ON-14 (The world's 'I am' is His signature), turned into a reusable step. Tag: stated.*

## DM6 · The wholeness argument (He lacks nothing, so limit is His)

**What it does.** Shows that limit, number and multiplicity are not a falling-away from G-d: a G-d with power only in the unlimited would be lacking.

**When it is used.** 'How can the infinite be in something small?' 'Isn't the finite less G-dly?' 'Does a big deed matter more?'

**Steps.** 1. State the objection: the infinite cannot be in the finite. 2. Reply: the Essence is whole without any lack, so it has power in limit as in the unlimited (Q65). 3. Strengthen: true simplicity requires that many can come from it (Q66); limit is itself infinite power (Q67). 4. Guard: before the infinite, a million is no closer than one (Q69), so size is not nearness. 5. Keep both: the finite stays truly finite while the infinite is in it (Q68).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rashab, citing the Avodas HaKodesh and the Maharal: just as He has power in the unlimited, so He has power in limit; the Essence itself requires multiplicity. - **Q65** AB 3691: כשם שיש לו כח בבלתי גבול כן יש לו כח בגבול — *just as He has power in the unlimited, so He has power in limit* - **Q66** AB 3691: דהעצמות גופא מכריח ענין הריבוי וההתחלקות — *the Essence itself requires multiplicity and division* - **Q69** CH 211: ואין במילייאן אחד שום קירוב לבלי גבול יותר מבאחד ממש — *in one million there is no more nearness to the infinite than in one*

**Done badly.** Concluding that the finite is the infinite, or that limits do not matter. The text keeps the limit real: 'they are truly limit and number' (Q68).

*Builds on: ON-21 (One and many are not opposites). Tag: stated.*

## DM7 · Two unities, in order (upper, then lower, and the lower is higher)

**What it does.** Gives two true ways to see the unity and ranks them: from above, the world is nothing; from below, the world exists and its existence is G-dliness. The second is not a step down.

**When it is used.** Whenever 'nothing but Him' is in play; whenever someone fears the unity erases the world or themselves.

**Steps.** 1. Upper unity (T15): contemplate 'all before Him is as nothing'; things are included in the Nothing (Q53, Q57). 2. Lower unity (T16): things exist, and what they are is G-dliness (Q54). 3. Rank them: the second is 'a higher level' (Q55); the service of the lower unity 'reaches higher' (Q58); at their root both are equal (Q59). 4. Choose the one this person needs now (see guardrail G9).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rashab in Samach Vav, on Shema: in the first way, they do not exist; in the second, they exist and are G-dliness; and 'the second way is a higher level'. - **Q53** SV 3173 (OCR): דבאופן הא' ההרגש הוא שאינם במציאות (והיינו שהכל נכלל באין) — *in the first way, the feeling is that they do not exist (all is included in the Nothing)* - **Q54** SV 3173 (OCR): ובאופן הב' ההרגש הוא שהן בבחי' מציאות והן אלקות — *in the second way, the feeling is that they exist and they are G-dliness* - **Q55** SV 3173 (OCR): האופן הב' הוא במדרי' נעלית יותר — *the second way is a higher level*

**Done badly.** Stopping at the upper unity and teaching that the world is unreal; or skipping it and calling everything G-dly without the bittul that the upper unity teaches.

*Builds on: ON ladder rungs 6-12; DEPTH-ENGINE M15 (The deepest lands lowest). Tag: stated.*

## DM8 · Essence does not push out (revelation versus essence)

**What it does.** When a unity claim seems to erase something (the world, the self, the details), it moves the claim from the light, which negates what is not itself, to the Essence, which is the self of each thing and negates nothing.

**When it is used.** Fear of being erased; 'if He is everything, am I nothing?'; any acosmic conclusion.

**Steps.** 1. Notice the erasing claim ('there is no world', 'there is no me'). 2. Ask whether it comes from a revelation: a revelation is particular and pushes aside what is not it (Q07). 3. Move to the Essence: the essence is the very self of each and every thing (Q08) and is found in everything (Q09). 4. Conclude with the Rebbe: 'nothing but Him' does not mean the worlds do not exist; the worlds as they are, bounded, are one with the Essence (Q36, Q79).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus 17: the yechidah, being essential, does not negate the soul's other levels; it is their essence. So 'nothing but Him' does not mean the worlds do not exist. - **Q07** IN 99: "גילוי" הו"ע פרטי, ולכן הענינים שאינם גילוי — *a revelation is a particular thing, and so (it negates) what is not it* - **Q08** IN 99: משא"כ "עצם" הוא העצמיות של כל ענין וענין — *whereas the essence is the very self of each and every thing* - **Q36** IN 100: שגם העולמות עצמם כמו שהם נמצאים במציאותם (ומוגדרים בגדר זמן ומקום) מיוחדים הם בתכלית היחוד בעצמות אוא"ס ב"ה — *the worlds themselves, as they exist (bounded by time and place), are utterly one with the Essence of the Infinite Light*

**Done badly.** Using this to skip bittul ('so I don't need to change'). The same passage says the person then feels that mere animal life is 'not existence at all' and existence is connection with G-dliness (Q78).

*Builds on: ON-13 (Light pushes out; essence holds). Tag: stated.*

## DM9 · Grade the bittul honestly

**What it does.** Names which bittul is present, refuses to inflate it, and shows that the 'lower' one is the vessel while the deepest is given, not made.

**When it is used.** Prayer, humility, ego, a person who says they 'dissolved' or 'felt nothing'.

**Steps.** 1. Bittul hayesh (T12): ego set down, person still there. It is the vessel (Q43). 2. Bittul bimetzius (T13): not counting at all, as in the upper unity (Q57). By its own effort a creature does not truly reach it; even what is called by that name is really bittul hayesh (Q40, Q41). 3. Essential bittul (T14): from the yechidah, given from above (Q42); it is not from one's will but because one cannot be separated (Q46, Q47). 4. Say which one this is, and what the next honest step is.

**Worked example from the text.** The Rashab, Ayin Beis: prayer climbs from below, so its bittul, even when called 'bittul of existence', is really bittul hayesh; the bittul that comes through Torah from above is essential. - **Q40** AB 2501: הרי הנברא א"א לו להיות בבחי' ביטול במציאות ממש — *a creature (by its own power) cannot be in true bittul bimetzius* - **Q41** AB 2501: שגם המדרי' הגבוהות והנעלות בענין הביטול שנק' ביטול במציאות הנה לפי האמת הוא בחי' ביטול היש לבד — *even the highest levels of bittul called "bittul of existence" are, in truth, only bittul hayesh* - **Q42** AB 2501: וע"כ הביטול בזה הוא בבחי' ביטול עצמי — *therefore the bittul here (given from above, through Torah) is essential bittul*

**Done badly.** Flattering a person that they reached bittul bimetzius; or shaming bittul hayesh as low. Or teaching self-erasure as the goal, which leaves no vessel (Q43).

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M8 (Up the inner ladder of a person). Tag: stated.*

## DM10 · Find what stands above both poles

**What it does.** When two opposites both seem true (something and nothing, revealing and hiding, limit and unlimited), it finds the one above both, who is not defined by either and so can hold both.

**When it is used.** Any paradox: 'G-d is everything yet the world exists'; 'He is hidden yet present'; 'finite and infinite together'.

**Steps.** 1. Name the two poles. 2. Show each is a name or power of His, not Him: Havayah and Elokim are names of the One above both (Q71). 3. Show the Essence is not inside the definitions of either pole (Q34). 4. Conclude that the Essence can hold both at once, because it is limited by neither. 5. Re-read each pole in this light: even the hiding pole is for revealing (Q72).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rebbe in Inyanah: the power to make something from nothing comes from the Essence, which is not within the definitions of 'nothing' and 'something', so it can make 'is not' into 'is'. - **Q34** IN 92: ובמילא אינו בהגדרים ד"אין" ו"יש", ולכן ביכלתו לעשות את ה"אינו" "ישנו" — *so He is not within the definitions of "nothing" and "something", and therefore He can make the "is not" into "is"* - **Q71** ML 2740: דשני השמות הוי' ואלקים הם שמותיו של הקב"ה שלמעלה משניהם — *the two names Havayah and Elokim are names of the Holy One who is above both* - **Q72** ML 2740: שגם שם אלקים (צמצום) ענינו הוא לגלות — *the name Elokim (contraction) too is for the sake of revealing*

**Done badly.** Using 'above both' as a way to avoid thinking ('it's a mystery'). The move has to show why each pole needs the higher root.

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M12 (Hold both sides), with the explicit 'above both' step. Tag: stated.*

## DM11 · Ask why at all; answer with desire; land below

**What it does.** Asks why there is a world, rejects the reasons, rests on a desire that has no reason, and then lands that desire in the plain physical deed.

**When it is used.** Purpose, meaning, 'why do mitzvos matter?', 'why would G-d need anything?'

**Steps.** 1. Ask the 'why' as sharply as possible: He lacks nothing (Q85). 2. Show that every reason falls short. 3. Rest on desire: He desired a dwelling below, and about a desire there is no question (Q38). 4. Say what the desire is for: the Essence revealed below as above (Q39), at home as a man is in his house (Q37). 5. Land: the lower the act, the more the essence is expressed (Q83).

**Worked example from the text.** The Rashab opens Samach Vav this way: 'He desired, and about a desire one asks no question'; the dwelling below means the Essence revealed below as above. - **Q38** SV 219 (OCR): נתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים, ומאחר שנתאוה הנה על תאוה אין שואלים קושיא — *the Holy One desired a dwelling below; and since He desired, about a desire one asks no question* - **Q39** SV 3680 (OCR): אבל ענין הדירה בתחתונים הוא להיות גילוי בחי' עצמות אוא"ס למטה כמו למעלה — *a dwelling below means that the Essence of the Infinite Light be revealed below as above*

**Done badly.** Making the desire sound like G-d's need or lack (it is not; Q85). Or stopping at 'He wanted it' without landing in an act the person can do today.

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M14 (Ask why at all) and M15 (The deepest lands lowest). Tag: stated.*

## DM12 · Bring the mashal, then say where it breaks

**What it does.** Uses a picture from the soul or nature (sun and ray, teacher and student, a man's home), then names exactly where it fails, because the failure is where the truth about the Essence shows.

**When it is used.** Every time a mashal is used for tzimtzum, light, or creation.

**Steps.** 1. Give the mashal and what it shows. 2. Name where it breaks: the sun shines by necessity, He by will (Q84, Q86); the teacher's contraction hides from himself for a moment, His does not (Q88, Q89). 3. Draw the conclusion from the break: the break points to the Essence's freedom and to G-d being unchanged (Q85).

**Worked example from the text.** The Tzemach Tzedek: the light comes from Him by His simple will, not like sunlight that spreads by necessity; the belief that the world flows from G-d by necessity is called a falsehood. - **Q84** CH 112: אך כיון שאור זה נמשך ממנו ית' ברצונו הפשוט — *but since this light comes from Him by His simple will* - **Q86** CH 112: כפי השקר של מאמיני הקדמות שהעולם נמשך מהש"י על צד החיוב — *according to the falsehood of those who believe in an eternal world, that the world flows from G-d by necessity* - **Q85** CH 112: ולכן אין שינוי אצלו ית' בין קודם בריאת העולם לאחר הבראו כיון שמקור ההתהוות רק מהארה בעלמא שאינו נוגע להעצמות — *so there is no change in Him between before and after the world was created, since creation comes only from a mere shining that does not touch the Essence*

**Done badly.** Leaving the mashal unbroken, so the listener pictures G-d as a sun or a body.

*Builds on: DEPTH-ENGINE M10 (Where the comparison breaks); meshalim/PRINCIPLES.md. Tag: stated.*

## Worked examples

*The steps name the move or term used at each point and its sources (stated). The plain answers and the shifts are ours, built on those lines.*

## E1 · If G-d is everything, am I even real? Do I matter?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM8 Essence does not push out*: Notice the fear: the unity seems to erase me. That fear comes from treating G-d as a revelation that pushes out what is not itself. [Q07 (IN 99), Q08 (IN 99)] 2. *DM1 Light or Essence?*: Move the claim to the Essence (T01): the essence is the self of each thing. [Q08 (IN 99), Q09 (IN 37)] 3. *T14 yechidah*: Name the self at its deepest: the yechidah, one with the Essence, always whole. [Q48 (IN 64), Q49 (IN 28)] 4. *DM5 lowest proves highest*: Even the feeling 'I exist' is a sign of the Essence. [Q30 (ML 1252)] 5. *DM9 grade the bittul*: Then the practice: bittul hayesh, not self-erasure; that is the vessel. [Q43 (AB 3509)]

**Answer a guide could give** (146 words):

> Chassidus does not answer 'you are nothing'. It asks first: are we talking about G-d's light or G-d Himself? A light is a particular thing, so it crowds out what is not it. G-d's Essence is different. The Rebbe says the essence is 'the very self of each and every thing', so it pushes nothing out. Your deepest point, called the yechidah, is one with Him, and the Rebbe says it can never be stained or broken; it always stays whole. Even your strong sense of 'I am here' is not an accident. Only the One who exists from Himself can make a thing that feels it exists by itself. So you are real, and your realness is from Him. What Chassidus asks is not that you disappear, but that you stop being a 'something for yourself'. That quieter self is exactly the vessel He fills.

- **(a) Beginner:** Stay with 'your realness is from Him' and the yechidah that is never broken; skip light/essence vocabulary.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add the Rashab's ranking of bittul: one's own effort reaches bittul hayesh; the essential bittul comes from the yechidah and from above (Q41, Q42).
- **(c) Doubter:** Argue it: if all being is borrowed from one source, borrowing does not make you fake; it makes you held. Offer DM5 as a reasoned point, not a feeling.

## E2 · If G-d is everywhere, why does the world look so solid and separate?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM2 From whose side?*: The hiding is real toward us, not toward Him. [Q62 (SV 5948), Q15 (AB 5537)] 2. *T04 tzimtzum not literal*: He did not leave; His light shines after the tzimtzum as before. [Q13 (SV 762)] 3. *DM4 Only the unlimited can*: Only the boundless can hide itself; the hiding is His power. [Q61 (SV 5948), Q70 (AB 3544)] 4. *DM5 lowest proves highest*: The world's solidity is a likeness of the Essence that exists from itself. [Q30 (ML 1252), Q27 (AB 4454)] 5. *DM7 two unities*: Choose the lower unity: it exists, and its existence is G-dliness. [Q54 (SV 3173), Q55 (SV 3173)]

**Answer a guide could give** (149 words):

> The texts give two answers that fit together. First, the solid look is a hiding, and the hiding is only on our side. To G-d, the Rashab says, nothing hides at all: His light shines after the contraction just as before. Second, the hiding is not something blocking Him. Only the infinite has the power to hide itself, so the world's quiet, solid face is His own act ('these are His mighty acts'). The Rebbe goes further: why does a stone feel like it exists all by itself? Because it comes from the One who truly exists by Himself. The solidity is His signature. So you do not have to look past the world to find Him. Look at it and say: it is really here, and what it is, underneath, is G-dliness. That is what the Rashab calls the higher of the two ways to see the unity.

- **(a) Beginner:** Use only the signature image: the thing's 'I am here' comes from the One who is.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add Ayin Beis 4454-4455: the light only shows that a source exists; the created 'something' proves the Essence, so the power of the Essence is in the yesh more than in the light.
- **(c) Doubter:** Grant the experience fully (it does look separate) and offer the argument from capacity (DM4) as an inference, not a claim of sight.

## E3 · Why would a perfect G-d make a world at all? He doesn't need anything.

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM11 Ask why at all*: Sharpen the question: He lacks nothing and creation changes nothing in Him. [Q85 (CH 112), Q06 (ML 1983)] 2. *DM11 answer with desire*: The answer is a desire with no reason. [Q38 (SV 219)] 3. *T11 dirah betachtonim*: Name the desire: a home for His Essence in the lowest world. [Q37 (ML 77), Q39 (SV 3680)] 4. *DM5 lowest proves highest*: Why the lowest: the True Being is found in the physical, not the spiritual. [Q33 (ML 1291)] 5. *DM11 land below*: Land: the lower the act, the more the essence shows. [Q83 (IN 106)]

**Answer a guide could give** (146 words):

> You are right: He does not need anything. The Tzemach Tzedek says nothing in G-d changed between before the world and after it. So the Rebbe Rashab opens his great series by asking your question and refusing the usual reasons. His answer is a desire: 'He desired a dwelling below, and about a desire one asks no question.' A desire is not a lack; it is the deepest kind of wanting, with nothing under it. What He desired is a home, and a home is where a man is himself, not just his public face. He wanted to be Himself, His Essence, in this physical, low world. That is why the Rebbe says the 'True Being' shows most in the physical thing, not in spiritual worlds. So the purpose is not somewhere else. It is the plain good act you do today, here, with your hands.

- **(a) Beginner:** Lead with the home image and one concrete act.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add why the lowest: the great torch whose greatness shows far away (ML 1291), and Inyanah 106: the lower Chassidus reaches, the more its essence is expressed.
- **(c) Doubter:** Admit that 'desire with no reason' is where reasons stop, and say the texts claim exactly that: a stopping point, not an evasion.

## E4 · Where is G-d when I suffer? Why does He hide?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM2 From whose side?*: The hiding is toward us, not toward Him; He is not hidden from Himself or absent. [Q62 (SV 5948), Q24 (AB 3544)] 2. *T18 essential hiddenness*: Nothing else hides Him; the hiding is His own deepest power. [Q60 (SV 5948), Q63 (SV 2947)] 3. *G9 guardrail*: Do not say 'the pain is not real' or 'you are nothing'. [Q55 (SV 3173)] 4. *T14 yechidah*: The innermost self is untouched by any flaw. [Q48 (IN 64)] 5. *DM10 above both*: Even the hiding name, Elokim, is for revealing; the hiding will itself shine. [Q72 (ML 2740), Q64 (SV 2947)]

**Answer a guide could give** (142 words):

> Chassidus will not tell you the pain is unreal. It says something more careful. When G-d is hidden, He is hidden from us, not absent. The Rashab says the Essence is found below just as above, because nothing at all can hide it. So the hiding is not a wall someone else put up. It is His own act, and the texts trace it to the deepest, most hidden part of Him. That is why the Rebbe says even the 'hiding' name of G-d is there for the sake of revealing, and why the Rashab says that in the end 'the hiding itself will shine'. In the meantime, there is a place in you the pain cannot reach: the innermost point of your soul, which the Rebbe says is always whole. You can speak to Him from there, even in the dark.

- **(a) Beginner:** Say only: He is hidden from you, not gone; the deepest you is not broken; talk to Him.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add Melukat on tzimtzum as intended, not forced (Q74), and Samach Vav 2947 on knowing Havayah through the hiding itself.
- **(c) Doubter:** Do not argue the pain away. Offer the distinction 'hidden from you' versus 'absent' as a coherent option, and leave room.

## E5 · I prayed and felt nothing. Am I far from G-d?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM1 Light or Essence?*: Feeling is a light; it shows only that the source exists; its absence does not change the source. [Q03 (AB 263), Q02 (AB 398)] 2. *DM9 grade the bittul*: Your own effort reaches bittul hayesh; that is the vessel, not a failure. [Q41 (AB 2501), Q43 (AB 3509)] 3. *T14 essential bond*: The bond is from the yechidah, not from feeling: one cannot be separated. [Q47 (ML 751)] 4. *G10 guardrail*: Do not inflate or shame; name the next honest step. [Q40 (AB 2501)]

**Answer a guide could give** (142 words):

> Not necessarily. Chassidus separates G-d's light from G-d Himself. A feeling in prayer is a light: it shows that He is there, but it is not Him, and when it is gone, nothing in Him is lessened, just as clouds take nothing from the sun. The Rebbe Rashab is honest about what prayer can do by our own effort: even the highest 'melting' is really a setting-aside of ego, and that setting-aside is exactly the vessel that receives. So a quiet, dry prayer, said with your ego set down, is not nothing. Under all feeling is a bond the Rebbe describes in the deepest point of the soul: not something you choose or feel, but that you 'cannot be separated' from His Oneness. Keep praying. Do one small thing after it. The feeling is the light; the bond was never in question.

- **(a) Beginner:** Use the sun-and-clouds image and the encouragement to keep going.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add Ayin Beis 2501: the bittul given from above through Torah is essential, so follow prayer with learning.
- **(c) Doubter:** Grant that no feeling proves anything, then point out that the texts never rested the bond on feeling to begin with.

## E6 · Isn't 'G-d is everything' just pantheism, like Spinoza?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM1 Light or Essence?*: The world is made through a light that has nothing of the Essence in it. [Q01 (AB 398), Q29 (AB 4455)] 2. *G1 guardrail*: Creation makes no change in Him. [Q06 (ML 1983), Q85 (CH 112)] 3. *DM12 mashal and its break*: Not by necessity, like sunlight: by His simple will. [Q84 (CH 112), Q86 (CH 112)] 4. *DM7 two unities*: What is claimed: the world has no being apart from Him; existing, it is G-dliness. [Q54 (SV 3173), Q36 (IN 100)] 5. *DM10 above both*: He is above both 'something' and 'nothing'. [Q34 (IN 92)]

**Answer a guide could give** (141 words):

> No, and the Tzemach Tzedek drew the line himself. Pantheism says the world and G-d are the same thing, and the world flows from G-d by nature, the way light comes from the sun. Chassidus says three different things. First, the world is made through G-d's light, and the light 'has nothing of the essence in it'; the Essence is beyond comparison with it. Second, making the world changed nothing in G-d. Third, the light comes by His free will, and the Tzemach Tzedek calls the view that the world flows from G-d by necessity a 'falsehood'. What Chassidus does claim is that nothing has being apart from Him: things exist, and what they are is G-dliness. That is not 'the world is G-d'. It is 'the world is not anything without Him', while He is above both something and nothing.

- **(a) Beginner:** Usually not needed; if asked, use the sun image and its break.
- **(b) Advanced:** Discuss Ayin Beis 4455: the light is a go-between, and the Essence's power is carried into the 'something'.
- **(c) Doubter:** Lay out the three differences as claims and let the person test them; do not call their view foolish.

## E7 · If there is nothing besides Him, where does evil come from?

**Reasoning.** 1. *G7 guardrail*: Evil is not a second power; nothing has rule of its own. [Q77 (DM 119)] 2. *T18 / DM2*: Its hiding is rooted in His own hiddenness and is real only toward the worlds. [Q63 (SV 2947), Q62 (SV 5948)] 3. *DM4 Only the unlimited can*: A light cannot turn its opposite; only the Essence, which nothing opposes, can turn evil to good. [Q73 (IN 111)] 4. *DM11 land below*: So turning evil to good is where the essence shows most. [Q83 (IN 106)]

**Answer a guide could give** (134 words):

> The texts refuse the idea of a second power. The Tzemach Tzedek says everything is 'appointed over its work' with no rule of its own. The Rashab traces every hiding, including what makes evil possible, back to G-d's own deepest hiddenness, and says it hides only toward the world, not toward Him. Then the Rebbe adds the move that matters most for a person facing evil, in the world or in himself. A light can only fight darkness, because darkness is its opposite. But the Essence of G-d is utterly simple and has no opposite, so only the Essence can turn evil into good. That means the place where you struggle with something bad is exactly where the deepest part of G-d is at work. Not to excuse the evil, but to transform it.

- **(a) Beginner:** Keep it to: there is no rival to G-d; the struggle itself is where He is most at work.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add Melukat 1035: every existence, even the other side, has its root in holiness, and what fell lowest came from highest.
- **(c) Doubter:** Admit this is not a theodicy that explains each evil; it is a claim about what evil is not (a rival) and what can be done with it.

## E8 · How can the infinite G-d be in my small, limited life or one small mitzvah?

**Reasoning.** 1. *DM6 wholeness*: He has power in limit as in the unlimited; otherwise He would be lacking. [Q65 (AB 3691), Q67 (AB 79)] 2. *DM6 guard*: Size is not nearness: a million is no closer than one. [Q69 (CH 211)] 3. *DM3 three tiers*: The Essence is above filling and surrounding, so not tied to great or small. [Q18 (ML 2592)] 4. *DM5 / DM11*: The True Being is found in the physical; the lower, the more the essence shows. [Q33 (ML 1291), Q83 (IN 106)]

**Answer a guide could give** (135 words):

> Chassidus turns the question around. If G-d could only be in big, unlimited things, He would be missing something, and He is missing nothing. So the Rebbe Rashab says: just as He has power in the unlimited, He has power in limit. Limit is not G-d getting smaller; it is one of His powers. The Tzemach Tzedek adds that before the infinite, a million is no closer than one. So a big life is not nearer to Him than a small one, and a grand deed is not nearer than a plain one. The Rebbe says the True Being shows most in the physical, lowest thing. That is why one coin to charity, one candle, one honest word can hold the Essence itself. Your limits stay real. And inside them, the infinite is at home.

- **(a) Beginner:** Use the 'a million is no closer than one' line and one concrete mitzvah.
- **(b) Advanced:** Add Ayin Beis 3691: true simplicity requires multiplicity; Sefer HaChakirah 604: limit stays truly limit with the Infinite clothed in it.
- **(c) Doubter:** Present the wholeness argument as logic (an infinite lacking finite power would be limited) and let it stand on its own.

## Notes and limits

- Line references without a quote (for example ML 1291 on the torch, ML 2574 on the three loves, ML 1035, AB 1678, IN 63, IN 95, ML 77) were read in this pass; their substance is stated, but no Hebrew from them is quoted beyond the index. - The two unities: the Rashab (SV 3173) ranks the second way ('they exist and are G-dliness') higher; the Rebbe (ML 2716) says the lower unity's service reaches higher, and elsewhere (ML 1085) that both are equal. We keep both: equal at the root, the lower one higher in effect. - 'Infinite' (Ein Sof): AB 385 says the name properly applies to the light, since nothing essential spreads. We follow this in G11, but the texts also say 'the Essence of the Infinite Light' freely; the guardrail is against defining the Essence, not against the phrase. - G9 (which unity to give a hurting person) is ours, built on the stated ranking; it is a pastoral rule, not a quote.
