## Conditions a modern person brings that Tier A leaves missing or thin

- **Marriage and home conflict.** No cluster covers shalom bayis: fighting, coldness between spouses, thoughts of divorce. It is one of the commonest things a modern person brings. The Rebbe's letters deal with it constantly: in his Igros Kodesh, 'שלום בית' has 54 hits and 'איש ואשה' 85. Tier A was all devotional texts, so it never reached this.
- **Pain and worry over a child.** The worry cluster names children but never treats them. Nothing covers a struggling or rebellious child, a child who has left observance, or a parent at a loss. One letter (Rebbe's Igros Kodesh, line 66488) cites 'ימין מקרבת' and says to consult 'רופא פסיכולוג' about a relative's behaviour, so this is in the letters and not in Tier A.
- **Waiting: for a partner, or for a child.** Being single, a shidduch that never comes, and infertility are all absent. The letters are full of them: 'שידוך' 355 hits, 'שידוך מתאים' 22, 'זרעא חייא וקיימא' 29.
- **My body is failing: illness, surgery, pain, disability.** This appears only inside the general 'why is this suffering' cluster, and it rests on Likkutei Sichos health letters. The Rebbe's 'רופא מומחה' (88 hits) and 'בריאות הגוף' (143) letters hold a clear stance on the fear, on bitachon, and on doing everything that can be done naturally ('בדרך הטבע', 113). This is the pattern the guide most needs.
- **Thoughts of not wanting to live.** Several gaps flag this and no cluster exists. The corpus is almost silent: the only hit for 'לאבד עצמו לדעת' in the Rebbe's letters (line 133889) is an intermarriage ruling, not care for a suicidal person, and must not be used. This condition needs a safety floor that routes to human help. It cannot be built from the texts, and the map should say so explicitly.
- **Anxiety, panic and sleeplessness as a condition in itself.** 'Dread' (n=5) is thin and the gaps admit there is no method. The Rebbe's short replies do give a repeatable protocol. At line 133919 of his letters: contemplate 'ה' לי לא אירא', follow 'כעצת רופא ידיד', learn Shaar HaBitachon, stay busy ('להיות עסוקה ככל האפשרי'). 'טראכט גוט' appears 21 times and 'חששות' 65.
- **My faith has questions I can't answer.** The doubt cluster (n=7) handles doubt by disowning it. Nothing engages real intellectual questions (science, the problem of evil, contradictions), and the Rebbe answered these directly: 'ספיקות' 130 hits, 'קושיות' 145, 'מדע' 311, 'ספיקות באמונה' 5.
- **Anger at God after catastrophe.** Several gaps name it and no cluster holds it: protest after the Holocaust, the death of a child, or ruin. Toras Menachem has 'למה עשה ה' ככה' 15 times, and the letters have 'עד מתי' (26) and 'הסתר פנים'. The suffering cluster offers only reframes. It does not allow a protest to stand.
- **I can't decide which way to go.** Nothing covers career, where to live, whether to move, or whom to marry. This is a huge share of the letters: 'משנה מקום משנה מזל' 31 hits, 'החלטה' 287, and 'יתייעץ בידיד מבין'. The 'if I moved I'd grow' piece is filed under blame (C 'not my fault'). In the letters it is a real decision question with its own answer (the 17 Elul 5717 letter on loneliness and wanting to move).
- **What will people think: shame, shyness, fear of ridicule.** Absent. The letters have 'מפני המלעיגים' (30), 'מה יאמרו' (15) and 'מדת הביישנות' (2, including a sharp letter saying the yetzer uses shyness to block necessary speech). Social fear is among the most common modern complaints.
- **I can't get myself to start: laziness and heaviness of will.** Laziness and procrastination are never a condition in their own right. They are scattered across 'inspiration fades' and 'nothing changes'. Hits: 'עצלות' 20 in the Rebbe's letters and 9 in Sefer HaMaamarim Rayatz, 'כבדות' 78, 'עצלות וכבדות' 3.
- **I hurt someone: amends and repair.** The gaps of at least five clusters flag this (guilt, forgiveness, fallen too far, crash after prayer, anger). It needs its own condition: seeking forgiveness, repairing harm between person and person. Probes: 'לבקש מחילה' (4 in the letters, 11 in Toras Menachem), 'בין אדם לחבירו' (25/130), 'לפייס'.
- **Aging, retirement and feeling useless.** Absent. The Rebbe's position against retirement and his talks around turning seventy (Toras Menachem 5732, 'גיל שבעים' 7 hits) are exactly this medicine. The letters to elderly chassidim ('זקני אנ"ש' 43) may hold more.
- **Caring for someone and running on empty.** The depression cluster notes caregiver strain as absent. Letters about a family member's illness or behaviour address the carer as well as the patient.
- **Survivors of persecution, war and uprooting.** Trauma is flagged as absent everywhere. The library has letters to refugees ('פליטים' 53 in the Rebbe's letters, 18 in the Rayatz's) and Soviet-era memoirs (Tier C) of people who lived through it.
- **Addiction proper (substances, smoking, compulsive sexual behaviour).** The desire family ('Hooked', 'fraud', 'desires make me bad') explicitly disclaims addiction. Tier B will probably be thin here: 'עישון' appears 0 times in the Rebbe's letters and 1 in Toras Menachem. The map should mark this as a known hole, not fill it by stretching texts about the animal soul.
- **Punishing the body: overwork, fasting and neglect of health.** This is split between 'relentless guilt' and 'work swallowed my life'. The Rebbe's line on the body ('גוף בריא' 82, and 'באופן שלא יחליש בריאותו' in a letter that channels melancholy into study) is a distinct and important reframe against ascetic self-harm and burnout.
- **Thin clusters that need Tier B reinforcement.** Grief (n=2), Depression (n=2), Alone with it (n=2), Trying to help and it isn't working (n=3), Growing yet feeling worse (n=3), Envy, Grudge and Judging (n=4 each), 'Not my fault' (n=4), 'Sad I'm not who I should be' (n=4), 'Lost' (n=4), Dread (n=5), 'Desires make me bad' (n=5). Each rests on one to three readers, and grief and depression rest entirely on Likkutei Sichos letters.

## Under-read in Tier A

- Tanya: Iggeres HaKodesh was grepped, not read. Read the consolation letters written after a tzaddik's passing (the letters near the end, 27-28) for Grief, and the letters on suffering and charity for 'Why is this suffering'. Kuntres Acharon and Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah are unread; Kuntres Acharon has short avodah essays on prayer and bittul, so check it for 'heart feels nothing'. - Derech Mitzvosecha: the mitzvah sections (lines 12-150, 207-371) were not reached. The commandment 'לא תשנא' sits at lines 103-105 next to the love-your-fellow passage that was read. Read the whole interpersonal block for Grudge and Judging (hate, revenge, rebuke). Only the opening of Kiddush Hashem (line 130) was read. - Sefer HaSichos (Rayatz) part 1: the arrest diary (lines 1493-2719) is unread. It is a first-person record of fear, isolation, humiliation and holding firm, and belongs to Dread, Alone with it, and Obstacles too big. The letters at 5031-5135 are also unread. - Sefer HaSichos (Rayatz) part 2: the story material (9740-11107) is unread. It probably holds lived cases of how the Rebbeim treated people and should be mined as Tier C material. - Likkutei Sichos: the reader-noted gaps are the richest part. LS3's general letters (38000-38660) and Yiddish additions, most of the LS2 vol. 35-36 Yiddish letters, and the LS1 vol. 31-32 closing lessons. These are letters to real people and should come before the sicha bodies. - Kuntres Umaayan: maamarim 3-11 and 18-24 were read only in summary, and the Rebbe's notes (685-712) were not reached. - Kuntres HaAvodah: chapters 1 and 3-5 and the appended letter on tzaddikim and baalei teshuvah (438-487) were only skimmed. The letter bears on 'Fallen too far' and 'Can I be forgiven'. - Sha'arei Teshuvah (Mitteler Rebbe): 302-450, 459-588 and 593-858 were not reached, including the Chinuch section. Read it for 'Who am I to do this' and for parenting. - Biurei Tanya: Esterman ch.1-13, 16-25 and 32-48, and Kaidaner ch.2-13 and 15-26, were not reached. Esterman's notes on Tanya ch.32 (love of fellow) serve Judging, and ch.31-34 (joy, the 'broken heart') serve the heavy-mood and remorse clusters. - Sha'arei Orah: about 85% is unread. Sha'arei Avodah: Gates 1-2 were reached only by grep and most of the Torah Gate is unread; that gate is the natural home for 'I can't go deep; my learning is shallow'. - Toras Chaim: very large unread stretches in all four parts: TC2 Toldos, Vayeitzei and Vayishlach; TC3 1990-2030, 2130-2300, 2400-2600 and 2850-3480; TC4 3769-4270 and Ki Sisa. Given the sustained psychology already found (livelihood, falling after prayer), a targeted grep there for Grief, Envy and Anger is worthwhile. - Chanah Ariel and Pelach HaRimon: about 90% of each is unread. The readers suspect more avodah passages sit in the unread lines, so do a density-scored pass rather than a full read. - Likkutei Torah / Torah Ohr: the named unread ranges (TO1 Lech Lecha, Vayera, Vayeshev, Vayigash, Bo; TO2 Yitro, Terumah, Vayakhel, the Esther biur; LT3 Yom Kippur 3967-4028 and Basi Legani 4912-5100) are mostly cosmology. They are lower priority than the letters, but Basi Legani's closing avodah passages are worth one pass. - Sheeris Yehudah: its single private letter of counsel (1066-1081) is the whole basis of 'This is just how I'm built' from that work. Before anything is built on it, check whether the Tzemach Tzedek's reply in his own letters (Igros Kodesh Tzemach Tzedek) explains 'this is not that'.

## Tier C: lived cases worth pulling

- BeChol Beisi Neeman Hu: R. Zalman Gurary's yechidus notes and correspondence with the Rebbe on personal, family and business life. It has 132 'יחידות' hits and 31 'מרה שחורה'. Lines ~9230-9410 record a sustained case of the Rebbe treating one man's melancholy. The Rebbe refuses to 'take it away' ('מרה שחורה נעמט מען ניט אראפ, מרה שחורה איז א ניסיון', line 9247) and follows up in writing for years ('מרה שחורה שאין לה יסוד כלל', 'לחפש ענייני מרה שחורה'). A second passage says worry breeds worry, and that bitachon and joy (from the 'white bile') are the vessel for blessing. This is the best lived case in the library for Depression, Heavy mood, Worry and 'This is how I'm built'. Pull it in full. - Toldos Avraham Chaim: letters between the chassidim R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum, R. Hendel Kugel and his brother on 'עבודת ה', תיקוני המדות ונגעי נפשותיהם, עצבות ומרירות', written from prison. Around line 771 is a striking claim that one who is melancholic from birth is not guilty for it, 'דכמו ענין מחלה הוא אצלו', together with a debate on sadness versus bitterness. It is directly relevant to telling spiritual sadness from temperament, a gap flagged in nearly every sadness cluster. It also has correspondence with the Rashab. - Zikaron Livnei Yisrael (R. Yisrael Jacobson's memoirs, written at the Rebbe's instruction): 47 'יחידות' hits. It covers his first yechidus with the Rashab (2 Cheshvan 5668) and yechidusen through the Rayatz era. Look for how the Rebbeim handled a young man's doubts, poverty and communal burnout (Trying to help; Obstacles). - Admurei Chabad veYahadus Bucharah: 121 'יחידות' hits, including R. Shlomo Eliezerov's letter describing a rare, hours-long yechidus with the Rashab on 'ענינים פנימיים... המכאיבים על כל לב'. Pull it as a model of a Rebbe's time with one person. - Toldos Chabad beRussia HaTzaris: stories of the Alter Rebbe and early chassidim. They include the Liozna takanos that limited yechidus, and the Alter Rebbe's reaction to the crowds ('מה ראו בי'). Search it for yechidus stories on despair, poverty and repentance (Fallen too far; Who am I). - Sefer HaSichos (Rayatz), unread story ranges (lines 9740-11107) and the arrest diary (1493-2719). It has the highest 'יחידות' count of any work (153), and the stories of chassidim and Rebbeim there are the richest source of lived cases for Obstacles, Dread and Alone with it. Treat it as Tier C even though Tier A touched the file. - Lemaan Yeidu Banim Yivaledu (M. Shusterman's memoir) and Zichronosai (Y. Y. Levin, Soviet childhood to escape): survival of persecution, loss of family and years of hiding, told first-person, with encounters with the Rayatz and the Rebbe. Pull them for the Survivors condition and for Suffering, and note how faith was kept or rebuilt. - Avodas HaKodesh: about 700 written answers the Rebbe gave R. Sholom Dovber Levin on his library work. Look for how the Rebbe pushed, corrected and encouraged a worker over decades: Trying to help, Work as service, and Obstacles. The preface also notes that letters thinned after 5725 and 5729 and gave way to short answers (מענות), which explains where in Igros Kodesh the terse protocols sit. - Yoman HaShlichus HaMeyuchedes: the mission to recover the Rebbeim's library from Moscow ('הרבי מלא תקווה'). This is a lived case of persistence against impossible odds, for Obstacles too big and 'I've given up'. - Shiurei Halacha leMaaseh and Otzar Minhagei Chabad: mourning practice ('אבל' has 770 and 132 hits) and yahrzeit customs, the practical side of Grief that the grief gaps say is missing. Pull what the mourner actually does day by day, not rulings.

*What the library actually holds limits Tier B. I checked the index files. Igros Kodesh (Lubavitcher Rebbe) is digital for vols. 1-28 only (about 1928-1973; 134k lines, 25MB). It is by far the richest source, and its later volumes are mostly terse answers (מענות), which is where compact protocols sit. The Rayatz's letters are digital only for vols. 14, 16 and 17, and the Rashab's only for vol. 6, which has almost no pastoral hits. The Rashab's letters (for example, to people in distress) exist only as HebrewBooks scans, a real blind spot. Likkutei Sichos is digital only for vols. 30-39, all of which Tier A already read; vols. 1-29 are scans. Toras Menachem runs through 5733. The Rayatz's war-years Sefer HaSichos is scans only. Catalog error: the folder labelled 'Toras Shalom (Sefer HaSichos of the Rashab)' actually contains שו\"ת תורת שלום, the Rashab's halachic responsa, so do not plan to search it for sichos.

All probe counts above come from exact-string searches with nikud stripped, over the Rebbe's letters, the letters of the other Rebbes, Toras Menachem, Sefer HaMaamarim Rayatz and Sefer HaSichos. Strong probes (dozens to hundreds of hits in the Rebbe's letters): נפילת רוח 55 (almost unique to the Rebbe's letters; 1 in Toras Menachem, none in the other works checked), מרה שחורה 51, מנוחת הנפש 144, רופא מומחה 88, שלום בית 54, שידוך 355, זרעא חייא וקיימא 29, ספיקות 130, מפני המלעיגים 30, טראכט גוט 21, השגחה פרטית 457, גוף בריא 82. Modern-Hebrew phrasings return zero: הערכה עצמית, פחד המות, שאלות באמונה, במקום תעניות, בודד בעולמו, שימוש בסמים. The probe writer should keep to the Rebbe's own idiom and to Yiddish where he wrote Yiddish. Probes marked untested in this plan (for example יתייעץ עם, אל יתבייש, התחלה בפועל, עין רעה, חלקו בחייו, מוסיף והולך, ערך החיים, הפליטים שיחיו, ניצולי חרב, מאסר וגאולה, תעניות וסיגופים, התמדה בלימוד, אין מקום ליאוש, לא תשנא) should be checked before use. A few probes are known to be thin here: טינה בלבו (0 in the Rebbe's letters), לוותר לה (0 in his letters, 4 in Toras Menachem), תורה ומדע (1), לא יחליש בריאותו (1), מדת הביישנות (2), אין מקום לנפילת רוח (2), and in the other letter collections almost everything is 0-10.

Honesty cautions for the map: (1) Suicide: the only hit for לאבד עצמו לדעת in the Rebbe's letters is an intermarriage ruling and must not be repurposed. The guide needs a non-textual safety floor. (2) The Rebbe's consistent pattern on mental and physical illness is to follow an expert doctor, choosing the doctor through 'רופא ידיד'. Next to that he gives bitachon, 'think good', staying busy, and a daily learning or Tehillim portion. He also refers people to a psychologist (line 66488). This pattern is the textual licence for the guide to send people to professionals, and it should be captured as a named truth. (3) Addiction and trauma will probably stay thin even after Tier B, and the map should record them as holes rather than stretch animal-soul texts over them. (4) Every Tier A condition assumes an observant Jewish man. For 'anyone in the world', search Toras Menachem for the Rebbe's Noahide and universal talks (בני נח 118 hits, שבע מצוות 94, אומות העולם 819). These would give the guide a textual basis for addressing non-Jews rather than leaving that step to inference.

Structural suggestions: split 'It's not really my fault' so that 'if I moved I'd grow' goes to a new decision condition. Merge 'Sad that I'm not the person I should be' into Heavy mood / Remorse, as the gaps already suggest. Give 'amends to people I hurt' its own condition, since at least five clusters' gaps point at it.*
