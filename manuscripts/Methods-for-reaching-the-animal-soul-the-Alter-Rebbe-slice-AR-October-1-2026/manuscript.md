## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

The earlier file found the Tanya "thinner" on the animal soul than later works. Read whole, with Torah Ohr, Likkutei Torah and the maamarim, the Alter Rebbe gives a full toolkit. Most of the later Rebbes' methods have their root here.

1. **Two doors, two speeds.** The G-dly soul needs little contemplation; its love is natural. The animal soul needs *long* contemplation, and of a specific kind: creation from nothing, G-d filling the worlds, and the angels' stormy self-nullification. That is "its own root" (AR04-AR06). It is "fed first", before Shema.
2. **It answers to its own good.** The desire faculty turns when it sees G-d as "the source of all pleasures". But only after a selfless first step, or the "good for me" love slides back to self-serving loves (AR07).
3. **The body does what the mind cannot.** Deeds with the limbs, speech aloud and with force, charity from one's labor, eating for heaven's sake, wine in measure, skipping a meal: these reach the vital soul directly (AR20-AR26).
4. **Speaking to oneself.** The beinoni's self-talk ("I refuse to be a fool"), the split "this is only my body; inside is a part of G-d", the staged anger at the animal soul, and the resolve that the animal soul "only listens" in prayer (AR01, AR14, AR17, AR38).
5. **Strategy and timing.** Do not argue with thoughts; name the yetzer's tricks; use the obstacle as fuel; strike while inspiration from above is open; strip the desire's garments first; know your "animal" (ox or sheep); go little by little (AR02, AR27-AR31, AR35-AR36).
6. **Above reason.** The kal vachomer from readiness to die, coercion "until he says I want", stubbornness turned to good, and faith that quiets anger and worry (AR08, AR28, AR37, AR41).
7. **Bitterness in its place.** Set times for humbling, the "bow" of bitter pleading for hidden evil, tears before prayer and joy after (AR15, AR16, AR18). Joy is the winning state, and it is felt in the heart, the animal soul's seat (AR19).
8. **Warnings.** Heat without mind flips into lust; inspiration from above does not touch the animal soul; intellect is weaker than natural traits; humbling does not reach arrogance above reason (section 3).

A new and important contrast: the Alter Rebbe says the long praises before Shema exist *for the animal soul*. "To arouse love in the G-dly soul itself there is no need for so much contemplation" (AR05).

## 2. The methods

### AR01. Tell the heart: I refuse - **Family:** speech - **What it is:** At the moment of desire, the beinoni speaks to his own heart and refuses it, in words. - **How to do it:** 1. Notice the desire as it rises from the heart to the mind. 2. Say to the heart: "I do not want to be wicked even for one moment. I do not want to be cut off from the One G-d in any way." 3. Add: even the lightest Jew gives his life for G-d's unity, and I am surely not less than he. 4. Name the sin a 'spirit of folly' that makes a man forget his hidden love: "I do not want to be a fool like him and deny the truth." 5. Turn the mind fully to the opposite, best of all to holiness. - **Why it works:** The mind rules the heart by birth and by the way man is made. Every person can, with the will in his mind, hold back the spirit of desire in his heart. - **When / for whom:** Every person, at every moment. This is the beinoni's standard and 'every person can be a beinoni'. - **When not:** It does not make the heart despise evil. That depends on great love and is not in a person's free choice (TY 236). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The tzaddik's heart already loathes evil. The beinoni rules only the garments: thought, speech and deed. - **Quotes:** - «באמרו ללבו: אינני רוצה להיות רשע אפילו שעה אחת» — *by saying to his heart: I do not want to be wicked even for one moment.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 232. - «אבל אני, אינני רוצה להיות שוטה כמוהו לכפור האמת» — *but I do not want to be a fool like him, to deny the truth.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 232. - «שכל אדם יכול ברצונו שבמוחו להתאפק ולמשול ברוח תאותו שבלבו» — *every person can, by the will in his mind, restrain himself and rule the spirit of desire in his heart.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 12. TY 209. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR02. Do not argue with the thought - **Family:** strategy - **What it is:** Push a stray desire-thought out at once. Never debate it. Put your strength into what you were doing. - **How to do it:** 1. As soon as it rises, push it away 'with both hands' and turn the mind away. 2. In prayer, act like a man praying while a heckler talks to him: answer nothing, make yourself deaf. 3. Add strength to the kavanah. 4. If it presses hard, humble yourself and ask G-d in thought for mercy, as a father pities his children. 5. Do not deepen thought in the desire. Deepen it in Chassidus instead (MAO 74341). - **Why it works:** One who wrestles with a filthy man gets filthy too. The thought comes from the animal soul, which fights harder exactly when the G-dly soul rises (TY 397). Each push-away is itself 'itkafia' below and above (TY 383). - **When / for whom:** For the beinoni, whose foreign thoughts are his own, from the bad in his own heart. - **When not:** Do not try to 'raise up' the foreign thought by its root. That is only for tzaddikim (TY 395). Do not become sad over it (TY 380-382). - **Quotes:** - «דוחהו בשתי ידים, ומסיח דעתו מיד שנזכר שהוא הרהור רע» — *he pushes it away with both hands and turns his mind away the moment he remembers it is a bad thought.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 12. TY 215. - «אל ישיב מאומה ושום טענה ומענה נגד המחשבה זרה, כי המתאבק עם מנוול – מתנוול גם כן» — *let him answer nothing, no argument or reply, against the foreign thought, for one who wrestles with a filthy one becomes filthy too.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 28. TY 401. - «ישפיל נפשו לה׳ ויתחנן לו יתברך במחשבתו» — *let him humble his soul before G-d and plead with Him in his thought.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 28. TY 402. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR03. Love held in the mind moves the limbs - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** Contemplation that yields love only in the mind and the 'hidden heart' is enough to bring the body to Torah and mitzvos, even when the heart does not burn. - **How to do it:** 1. Contemplate G-d's greatness: all before Him is as nothing. 2. Conclude: so it befits my soul to long for Him and cling to Him, through Torah and mitzvos. 3. Settle this in the mind and the hidden heart. 4. Make 'mouth and heart equal': do in speech and deed what the mind concluded. 5. If even that is hard, picture the love in the mind and act from the picture (TY 668). - **Why it works:** The mind is in a person's power; he can contemplate whatever he wants. The intellectual motive is what drives the body: had he not contemplated, he would busy himself only with his body's needs. - **When / for whom:** For one whose heart stays cold. 'Very near to every person who has a brain in his head.' - **When not:** This love does not lift the deed by itself the way felt love does. G-d 'joins the good thought to the deed' (TY 258-259). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Felt love clothes itself in the deed and gives it wings. Mind-love is higher and cannot clothe itself in the deed without G-d's joining. - **Quotes:** - «ודבר זה ״קרוב מאד״ ונקל לכל אדם אשר יש לו מוח בקדקדו, כי מוחו ברשותו» — *and this is 'very near' and easy for every person who has a brain in his head, for his mind is in his power.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 17. TY 265. - «ולולי שהיה מתבונן בתבונה זו לא היה עוסק בהם כלל, אלא בצרכי גופו לבד» — *had he not contemplated this understanding, he would not engage in them at all, only in his body's needs.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 16. TY 257. - «יכול לעסוק בתורה ומצות לשמן, על ידי ציור ענין אהבה זו במחשבה שבמוחו» — *he can engage in Torah and mitzvos for their sake by picturing this love in the thought of his mind.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 44. TY 668. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR04. Start where its life comes from - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** The animal soul can be shown G-dliness only through contemplating existence-from-nothing: how every thing, its own life included, is made now from the G-dly power. That is its own source. - **How to do it:** 1. In the praises of prayer, dwell on how one word made all creatures, and how each 'something' is null before the power that makes it. 2. Train yourself in this 'seeing of the mind', so that even the animal soul 'sees': 'from my flesh I will see G-d'. 3. 'Eyes below': keep the eyes open to this lower understanding. 4. 'Heart above': let the G-dly soul aim at the Essence. 5. A sense-proof: one sees his own life and existence, and the heavenly hosts bowing westward each day (TO 2252). - **Why it works:** The animal soul's source is only the spreading of G-d's power into creatures. It takes in arousal only from that level (LT 224). - **When / for whom:** Daily, in prayer. - **When not:** If he contemplates only the Essence with 'eyes closed', the animal soul stays hidden. After prayer, when he opens his eyes, he falls and takes the 'something' as primary. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul grasps the Essence itself ('this is my G-d'). The animal soul reaches only a 'vision' (machazeh) of His existence. - **Quotes:** - «וכדי להראות את הנפש הבהמית א"א כ"א ע"י התבוננות יש מאין דוקא» — *and to show the animal soul is impossible except through contemplation of something-from-nothing.* Siddur im Dach (Alter Rebbe's maamar). SD 15091 (OCR). - «ויסתעף מזה ג"כ שיהיה לו נפילות רבות בפתחו עיניו אחר התפלה וישיג את היש לעיקר» — *and from this will come many falls when he opens his eyes after prayer, and he will take the 'something' as primary.* Siddur im Dach (Alter Rebbe's maamar). SD 15123 (OCR). - «לא תקלט בה ההתפעלות אלא דייקא ע״י התבוננות מבחי׳ זו איך שהיש ודבר נפרד הוא בטל לגבי בחינת הזיו המחיה אותו» — *arousal will not be absorbed in it except through contemplating this: how the 'something' and separate thing is null before the radiance that gives it life.* Likkutei Torah, Vayikra (Adam ki yakriv). LT 224. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR05. Feed your animal first - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** The long praises before Shema exist for the animal soul. Lengthy contemplation is needed so that it too agrees. The G-dly soul hardly needs it. - **How to do it:** 1. Before Shema, say and dwell at length on the praises of G-d (Pesukei deZimra, the blessings of Yotzer). 2. Continue until the animal soul and the intellectual soul agree 'that it befits Him'. 3. This 'cuts the thorns' and circumcises the outer heart. 4. Only then come to Shema, the 'meal' of the G-dly soul: 'one may not eat before feeding his animal'. - **Why it works:** The animal soul's love sits in the outer heart, which is covered by its concealment. The inner heart's love is natural and needs no contemplation. - **When / for whom:** Every day of the year. In Elul, compassion from above does what the angels do the rest of the year (LT 3359). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Stated: for the G-dly soul's love 'there is no need for so much contemplation'. - **Quotes:** - «עד שגם נה״ב והשכלית יסכימו שלו יאתה כו׳ אבל בשביל התעוררות האהבה בנפש האלקית עצמה א״צ להתבוננות כ״כ» — *until the animal soul and the intellectual soul too agree that it befits Him; but to arouse love in the G-dly soul itself there is no need for so much contemplation.* Likkutei Torah, Re'eh. LT 3359. - «ותחלה יש להאכיל לבהמה הוא שיסדר שבחו של מקום בפסוד״ז» — *and first one must feed the animal: that is, arranging G-d's praise in Pesukei deZimra.* Likkutei Torah, Bechukotai. LT 1112. - «שצריך להעביר ערלה זו המפסקת ע״י ההתבוננות בכל המבואר בענין פסוד״ז והאופנים» — *one must remove this dividing foreskin through contemplating all that is explained in Pesukei deZimra and 'the Ofanim'.* Likkutei Torah, Ha'Azinu. LT 3971. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR06. Show it its own root: the angels' storm - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Recount how the Ofanim and holy Chayos, whose 'face of the ox' is the animal soul's root, are swept up in 'great noise' and nullify themselves. The animal soul is moved specifically by this. - **How to do it:** 1. In the Yotzer blessing, dwell on the angels' fiery excitement and self-nullification. 2. Consider that your own desire faculty was taken from there, and that its root burns with love. 3. Let that light of love shine on the desire faculty, so the heart pulls away from the physical pleasures it sank into (LT 3376). 4. Then come to 'with both your inclinations' in Shema. - **Why it works:** 'Judgments are sweetened only at their root.' The animal soul cannot be subdued under the G-dly soul except through its own root. Its mode is 'noise' (ra'ash), a loud storm of feeling, and when turned its love is stronger than the G-dly soul's. - **When / for whom:** Daily before Shema. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul needs no angels. It is nullified by its nature 'like a candle before a torch' (SD 11917). - **Quotes:** - «ולכך א"א להכניע ולכבוש את רוח הנה"ב תחת ממשלת הנה"א כ"א ע"י שרשה של הנה"ב» — *so it is impossible to subdue the spirit of the animal soul under the rule of the G-dly soul except through the animal soul's root.* Torah Ohr, Vayeshev. TO 734. - «שמזה דוקא תתפעל הנה״ב» — *for specifically from this the animal soul is moved.* Likkutei Torah, Vayikra. LT 206. - «ולפי שנה״ב שרשה מבחי׳ אופנים וחיות ע״כ יש בה הכח הזה שתתפעל בבחי׳ רעש» — *and since the animal soul's root is the Ofanim and Chayos, it has this power to be moved in the manner of 'noise'.* Likkutei Torah, Nasso. LT 1637. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR07. G-d is the source of every pleasure - **Family:** self-interest and good - **What it is:** The desire faculty itself can turn to G-d when it sees that He is the source of all pleasures and the life of life. - **How to do it:** 1. First a selfless phase: praise G-d only 'to gaze at the King's glory'. The animal soul, which wants only what is for itself, separates. 2. Then come to 'with all your heart': 'nearness to G-d is good for me'. 3. Let the desire faculty, which craves bodily pleasures, want nearness to G-d 'for He is good, the source of all pleasures'. 4. The 'evil angel' then answers Amen against its will and admits the truth. - **Why it works:** The desire faculty is not evil in itself. Its root is holy; only its garments are bad (LT 2117). - **When / for whom:** After subduing, in Shema. - **When not:** Without the first selfless phase there is a fear that this 'good for me' love will draw him to other self-serving loves (MAO 48151). - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's love needs no reason of benefit. - **Quotes:** - «ישוב אל ה׳ ויחפוץ בקרבת אלקים כי טוב שהוא מקור כל התענוגים» — *[the desire faculty] will return to G-d and want nearness to G-d, for He is good, the source of all pleasures.* Likkutei Torah, Nasso. LT 1633. - «מלאך רע בע״כ יענה אמן ומסכים ומתרצה להודות על האמת כי הוא ית׳ חיי החיים ומקור התענוגים» — *the evil angel answers Amen against its will and agrees to admit the truth, that He is the life of life and the source of pleasures.* Likkutei Torah, Nasso. LT 1634. - «ואח"כ יוכל לבא לבחי' ואהבת בכל לבבך שיהי' קרבת אלקים לי טוב ואינו מתיירא שיומשכו מזה לאהבות אחרות» — *and afterwards he can come to 'love with all your heart', that nearness to G-d is good for me, and need not fear being drawn from it to other loves.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken, HaKetzarim. MAO 48151 (OCR). - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR08. Easier than death: the kal vachomer - **Family:** above reason - **What it is:** Use the soul's readiness to die rather than bow to an idol to beat a passing desire, laziness or stinginess. - **How to do it:** 1. Set it to heart: I would accept death rather than be separated from G-d's unity even for a moment. 2. So beating this desire, and even doing its opposite, 'is very easy compared with the suffering of death'. 3. Remember that every sin, even a light one, separates from His unity at that moment like idolatry. 4. Say Shema twice a day to keep the readiness for mesirus nefesh fixed in the heart. 5. Each morning: 'You breathed it into me and will take it; from now I hand it back to You' (TY 621). - **Why it works:** The hidden love inherited from the Patriarchs is above time and above reason. Even the lightest Jews give their lives 'without any reason'. - **When / for whom:** When the yetzer cools a person from toil, charity, prayer with all his strength, or tempts to sin. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Stated: the hidden love works 'without knowledge and contemplation', unlike love born of contemplation. - **Quotes:** - «כשישים אל לבו, שלנצח היצר בכל זה, ויותר מזה, ולעשות הפכו ממש – קל מאד מיסורי מיתה» — *when he sets to heart that to defeat the yetzer in all this, and more, and do its very opposite, is very easy compared with the suffering of death.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 25. TY 362. - «שיזכור תמיד ענין מסירת נפשו לה׳ על יחודו» — *that he always remember the matter of giving his soul to G-d for His unity.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 25. TY 370. - «אלא בלי שום דעת והתבוננות, רק כאלו הוא דבר שאי אפשר כלל לכפור בה׳ אחד» — *but without any knowledge or contemplation, simply as if it were impossible to deny the One G-d.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 18. TY 283. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR09. Fixed times to make evil disgusting - **Family:** habit and repetition - **What it is:** Set times to reflect on what bodily pleasures really are, so that by habit they truly become a little repulsive. - **How to do it:** 1. Set regular times for this, not constant brooding. 2. Use the Sages' counsels: see the body and fine foods for what they become. 3. 'The wise man sees what will be born': all pleasures end as rot and refuse. 4. Alongside, contemplate G-d's greatness to rejoice in Him, even if it feels only imagined. 5. Repeat until it becomes second nature. - **Why it works:** Habit rules everything and becomes a second nature. 'When he accustoms himself to despise evil it will become somewhat despised in truth.' - **When / for whom:** For the beinoni who wants to fulfil the oath 'be a tzaddik'. - **When not:** It will not by itself make evil fully loathsome; that comes with great love (TY 233). - **Quotes:** - «צריך לקבוע לו עתים גם כן לשית עצות בנפשו להיות מואס ברע» — *he must also set times to take counsel with his soul to despise evil.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 237. - «החכם רואה הנולד מהן, שסופן לרקוב ולהיות רמה ואשפה» — *the wise man sees what comes of them: their end is to rot and become worms and refuse.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 237. - «ההרגל על כל דבר – שלטון, ונעשה טבע שני, וכשירגיל למאס את הרע – יהיה נמאס קצת באמת» — *habit rules everything and becomes second nature; when he accustoms himself to despise evil, it will become somewhat despised in truth.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 239. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR10. Say it on the tongue until it is nature - **Family:** habit and repetition - **What it is:** Make the words of love for G-d as Father habitual on the tongue and in the voice, so they wake the heart and mind. - **How to do it:** 1. Speak regularly, aloud, of G-d as 'our real Father and source of our life'. 2. Deepen the thought in it as you say it. 3. Do this constantly: 'habit becomes nature'. 4. If it seems imaginary, do not worry; the love is truly there, hidden. 5. Train the eye too: all you see is the King's garments; 'emunah' means training, like a craftsman training his hands (TY 644). - **Why it works:** The aim is not the feeling but bringing the hidden love into deed, Torah and mitzvos. - **When / for whom:** Always; 'it is very near, in your mouth and in your heart'. - **Quotes:** - «להיות רגיל על לשונו וקולו – לעורר כוונת לבו ומוחו» — *to have it habitual on his tongue and voice, to awaken the intent of his heart and mind.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 44. TY 664. - «ואף אם נדמה לו לכאורה, שהוא כח דמיוני – לא יחוש» — *and even if it seems to him to be imagination, let him not worry.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 44. TY 665. - «אלא העיקר הוא ההרגל, להרגיל דעתו ומחשבתו תמיד» — *rather the main thing is habit: to accustom his mind and thought constantly.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 42. TY 644. - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR11. Carry the prayer's imprint through the day - **Family:** habit and repetition - **What it is:** During the day, re-contemplate briefly what moved you in prayer, bring back the 'picture of love', and use it to bend the yetzer. - **How to do it:** 1. In prayer, contemplate until love comes. 2. During the day, return to the same thoughts, even briefly. 3. Bring the image of that love into the heart (the 'imprint', reshimu). 4. With it, give yourself over and force the yetzer that holds you back from a mitzvah. 5. For fear: one long contemplation daily that G-d fills all and sees, then a light re-contemplation 'at every time and hour' (TY 639). - **Why it works:** Prayer's love passes after prayer (Tanya ch. 13). The imprint can be renewed by review. - **When / for whom:** All day, especially for love. ### AR12. ### AR13. Dig for it: long, hard contemplation - **Family:** understanding - **What it is:** For a coarse soul, fear comes only with great effort. ### AR14. ### AR15. ### AR16. Sword and bow: two weapons - **Family:** fear and bitterness - **What it is:** Two weapons in prayer. The sword (fervent love in Torah and prayer) kills the evil one can feel nearby. ### AR17. Split the 'I': body here, a part of G-d within - **Family:** speech - **What it is:** Speak to the heart in two steps: admit you are far and coarse, then say that this is only the body and its vital soul. Inside is a real part of G-d in exile; free it. - **How to do it:** 1. Say: 'True, I am very far from G-d.' 2. Then: 'But all this is I alone: the body with its vital soul. ### AR18. Weep first, then rejoice - **Family:** fear and bitterness - **What it is:** Clear the heart of sadness by bringing in grief and tears before prayer, then pray in great joy. Pray with great joy in G-d's greatness; at 'Hashiveinu' and 'Selach lanu', cry again. 5. ### AR19. Win with joy, not heaviness - **Family:** feeling and the senses - **What it is:** Joy and quickness defeat the yetzer; sadness and heaviness lose. Joy is also the animal soul's own medium, since it is felt in the heart. - **How to do it:** 1. Clear worldly worries: what seems bad is hidden good (Tanya ch. 26). 2. Rejoice deeply in G-d's true unity and His dwelling with you (Tanya ch. 33). 3. Before prayer, say words of comfort about the redemption and the praises, to arouse true joy (IK 1260). 4. A light joke may open the mind and gladden the heart for G-d (TY 158). 5. Do mitzvos with joy, so that 'the body itself enjoys the joy of the mitzvah and no other joy pleases it' (MA 3415). - **Why it works:** Like two wrestlers: the lazy one loses even if stronger. Joy lifts the soul 'above all obstacles within and without'. The revealed pleasure of the mind is felt as joy in the heart, the animal soul's seat (MA 4126; LT 4961). - **When / for whom:** Always, especially in prayer and Torah; at set times when the soul needs cheering. - **Quotes:** - «אי אפשר לנצחו בעצלות וכבדות – הנמשכות מעצבות וטמטום הלב כאבן, כי אם, בזריזות – הנמשכת משמחה» — *it cannot be defeated with laziness and heaviness, which come from sadness and a heart dull as stone, but with alacrity, which comes from joy.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 26. TY 158. - «ושיהא הגוף ממש נהנה ומתענג בשמחה של מצוה ולא יערב לו שמחה אחרת ותענוג אחר כלל» — *and that the very body enjoy and delight in the joy of the mitzvah, and no other joy or pleasure be pleasant to it at all.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5572, HaAzinu. ### AR20. Do it with the body - **Family:** deed and body - **What it is:** Only mitzvos done with the body bring the Shechinah's light onto the animal soul. Let the G-dly soul in the mind rule; the animal soul then 'answers Amen against its will' and agrees to the deed. 3. Know that nearly all mitzvos were given to be done by the animal soul (MA 3401). - **Why it works:** The G-dly soul is spiritual and the body is physical. The vital soul in the blood is the intermediary that actually does the deed. - **When / for whom:** Always. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Torah study unites the G-dly soul with its inner garments, thought and speech. The deed reaches the body and the animal soul. - **Quotes:** - «אך כדי להמשיך אור והארת השכינה גם על גופו ונפשו הבהמית, שהיא החיונית המלובשת בגופו ממש, צריך לקיים מצות מעשיות» — *but to draw the light of the Shechinah also onto his body and animal soul, the vital soul clothed in his very body, he must fulfil practical mitzvos.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 35. TY 501. - «מאחר דאתכפין לקדושה, ובעל כרחן עונין אמן, ומסכימין ומתרצין לעשיית המצוה» — *since they are subdued to holiness, and against their will answer Amen, and agree and consent to doing the mitzvah.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 35. ### AR21. Speak it aloud, with force - **Family:** speech - **What it is:** Thought alone does not fulfil Shema, prayer or Torah study. Know that Torah in speech weakens the animal soul's hold (LT 2152). - **Why it works:** The G-dly soul cannot speak with physical lips except through the vital soul in the limbs. That vital soul grew from food that became blood; now it is raised with the words. - **When / for whom:** In all learning and prayer. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Kavanah is like the soul and speech like the body of the mitzvah; but speech is what reaches the vital soul. - **Quotes:** - «וכל מה שמדבר בכח גדול יותר – הוא מכניס ומלביש יותר כחות מנפש החיונית בדיבורים אלו» — *and the more force he speaks with, the more powers of the vital soul he brings into these words.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 37. ### AR22. Give from your sweat - **Family:** deed and body - **What it is:** Charity raises the whole vital soul, because all of it went into earning that money. - **How to do it:** 1. ### AR23. Eat for heaven's sake - **Family:** deed and body - **What it is:** Eating in order to serve G-d raises the food's life and turns it into fervor in prayer. ### AR24. Don't give it everything - **Family:** deed and body - **What it is:** Withhold some of what the body demands so it does not get used to full satisfaction. Fasting weakens the animal soul and humbles it. - **How to do it:** 1. In the Ten Days of Teshuvah, fast to lessen 'fat and blood'. 2. On other days do not follow the belly; sometimes skip a meal. 3. Do not give all your strength and labor to the animal soul (TO 161). 4. Enjoy permitted pleasures only 'with coolness' (LT 3375). 5. Fasting weakens the animal soul and brings it to submission (LT 3887). Then the 'uncircumcised heart' submits to the mind. - **When / for whom:** Mainly the Ten Days; small refusals any day. - **When not:** Teshuvah is not fasting. Turned to holiness, it lets even someone whose mind is weak cry out to G-d and refuse to be separated. - **How to do it:** 1. If you cannot contemplate and be moved, use the will above reason: 'I will not rebel against Him.' 2. Cry from the heart over seeing yourself in hiding of the Face. 3. The more you know your faults, the more your heart cries out. - **Why it works:** 'Because it is a stiff-necked people, forgive': the same stubbornness that resists, held to G-d, stands against every obstacle. - **When / for whom:** For one whose mind is not strong. - **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The 'Amalek' stubbornness is the same will turned against Him, resisting even its own teshuvah. - **Quotes:** - «מצד הרצון שלמעלה מן הדעת יוכל כל איש מישראל להיות צעק לבו להוי'» — *by the will above da'as, every Jew can have his heart cry out to G-d.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5568. MAO 10555 (OCR). - **Stated / ours:** stated.

### AR42. Go to the elders; hear, don't only read - **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering) - **What it is:** Hearing guidance spoken is not like reading books. A reader reads by his own mind; if his mind is confused he hardly sees the light. One whose mind is short should lay out his words before the elders of his city. - **How to do it:** 1. Do not rely only on reading. 2. If you cannot draw advice from the text, speak to the great ones of your town. 3. Let them explain it to you. - **Why it works:** Minds differ; one person is not moved by what moves another. - **When / for whom:** For anyone whose 'mind is short'. - **Quotes:** - «שהקורא, קורא לפי דרכו ודעתו ולפי השגת ותפיסת שכלו באשר הוא שם» — *for the reader reads according to his way and his mind, according to the grasp of his intellect where it is.* Tanya, Compiler's foreword. TY 52. - «יפרש שיחתוֹ לפני הגדולים שבעירו והם יבוננוהו» — *let him lay out his words before the great ones of his town, and they will make him understand.* Tanya, Compiler's foreword. TY 61. - **Stated / ours:** stated (general); ours (that it serves the natural mind).

## 3. What the Alter Rebbe says does NOT work, or warns against

1. **Heat without the mind's breadth flips into lust.** After prayer, when the broad mind leaves and only the warmth remains, that same love can at once turn to desire for honor, food and money. - «ונשאר רק בחי' החמימות וההתפעלות שבלבבו בלבד אזי יוכל להתאוה תיכף בכח האהבה ההיא ממש לתאות גשמיים גופניים» — *and only the warmth and excitement in his heart remains, then he can at once desire, with that very love, bodily pleasures.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5566. MA 526.
2. **Inspiration from above does not move the animal soul.** It reaches the G-dly soul only; the animal soul and body are in man's hands (LT 204; MA 3990). - «ולא נתן הבורא ית' שיהי' מתפעלי' מהערה עליונה» — *and the Creator did not grant that they be moved by an awakening from above.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5572. MA 3990.
3. **Intellect alone is weaker than natural traits.** Natural traits are stronger than intellect and do not stop even when intellect opposes them. Only the natural love, rooted above intellect, matches them. Love from human intellect has no lasting power (MAO 75010). - «כי כח המדו' הטבעיי' חזקי' יותר מכח השכל» — *for the power of natural traits is stronger than the power of intellect.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5569. MA 3022. - «ואין להאמונה או לאהבה שמשכל אנושי קיום כי כל שכל אדםי אין לו קיום» — *and faith or love that comes from human intellect has no permanence, for all human intellect has no permanence.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken, Parshiyos. MAO 75010 (OCR).
4. **Contemplating one's lowliness does not touch arrogance above reason.** There is an arrogance that knows it is worthless and still does not bend. Only a revelation of G-dly light above comprehension breaks it. - «הגם שמתבונן היטב בשכלו ורוח בינתו בכל הפחיתות שלו אינה מועלת זאת ההתבוננות בנפשו כלל» — *though he contemplates well with his mind all his lowliness, this contemplation does not help in his soul at all.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken 5572. MA 3846.
5. **Subtle ideas do not move a soul sunk in its animal nature.** Such a soul is drawn after coarse things and is not moved by fine, spiritual matters. - «ואינה מתפעלת מדברים דקים ורוחנים» — *and it is not moved by fine and spiritual matters.* Likkutei Torah, Rosh Hashanah. LT 3776.
6. **'Eyes closed' contemplation of the Essence alone.** It leaves the animal soul untouched; after prayer he falls and takes the world as primary (SD 15123, quoted in AR04).
7. **Self-interest love without a selfless first step.** 'Good for me' love may draw one to other self-serving loves unless the animal soul was first separated by praise 'only to gaze at the King' (MAO 48151, quoted in AR07).
8. **Raising foreign thoughts by their root; arguing with them.** Not for the beinoni; answering them makes him filthy (TY 395, TY 401). - «אל יהי שוטה לעסוק בהעלאת המדות של המחשבה זרה» — *let him not be a fool and busy himself with raising up the traits of the foreign thought.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 28. TY 395.
9. **Heaviness and sadness.** They cannot beat the yetzer (TY 371). Melancholy dressed as piety is a trick (IK 656).
10. **Fasting as teshuvah, and fasting that harms.** Teshuvah is not fasting; one who may be harmed is forbidden to fast much (TY 895, 909). - «ולא כדעת ההמון, שהתשובה היא התענית» — *and not as the masses think, that teshuvah is fasting.* Tanya, Iggeres HaTeshuvah ch. 1. TY 895.
11. **Too much wine.** Sleep and loss of da'at (LT 1863).
12. **Many schemes in divine service.** 'Let a person not seek many schemes and intellectual devices in serving G-d, for many thoughts confuse the mind.' Have one path (MAO 46390). Yet one without ecstatic 'wine' must sit and break his bad traits 'with great schemes' (MAO 46084). Both are stated, in different contexts. - «שלא יבקש אדם תחבולות ושכליים רבים בעבודת ה' כי מחמת מחשבות הרבה יתבלבל השכל» — *let a person not seek many schemes and intellectual devices in G-d's service, for many thoughts confuse the mind.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken, HaKetzarim. MAO 46390 (OCR). - «הוא יושב ומברר ומלבן ומשבר כל המדות רעות שבו בתחבולות גדולות» — *he sits and sifts and whitens and breaks all his bad traits with great schemes.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken, HaKetzarim. MAO 46084 (OCR).
13. **Habitual service may be the animal soul's own nature.** Even devoted learning done by habit may have the animal soul's nature mixed in; only going past habit is pure (MA 2702, quoted in AR26).
14. **The beinoni cannot choose to make evil loathsome.** That depends on great delight in G-d, which is not in one's choice (TY 236). - «ואין לאדם משפט הבחירה בזה כל כך, להתענג על ה׳ באמת, ושיהיה הרע מאוס ממש באמת» — *a person does not have so much choice in this: to delight in G-d truly and for evil to be truly loathsome.* Tanya, Likkutei Amarim ch. 14. TY 236.
15. **The animal soul does not reach the G-dly soul's nullification.** At most it reaches 'nullification of its something-ness' (bittul hayesh). - «שעכ״פ תוכל לבוא גם היא לבחינת ביטול היש» — *that at least it too can come to nullification of the 'something'.* Likkutei Torah, Vayikra. LT 274.

## 4. Gaps and unverified leads

1. **Thinking without feeling suffices (lead).** MAO 75644 (Parshiyos 2, OCR): "it is enough if you think the words said in the small Tanya and the big Tanya, even if you do not feel the bitterness in your heart, for the main thing is the mind." The quote is FOUND, but the piece reads like a letter of counsel and may not be the Alter Rebbe's. Check authorship before using.
2. **Explaining in the animal soul's language.** The Alter Rebbe does not, in what was read, describe "explaining to the animal soul in its own terms" the way the Mitteler Rebbe and Rashab do. His closest images are the father who shrinks his mind for the child and then gives sweets (MAO 51318), and the teacher who must reorder the words (MAO 27075; SD 13593). Both are said about G-d's influence or the sefiros. Using them for the animal soul is ours (AR34).
3. **Niggun.** No passage was found where the Alter Rebbe uses melody to reach the animal soul. Tanya ch. 50 (TY 728) ties the Levites' song to fiery love of the G-dly soul. The word ניגון appears 27 times in MA and over 100 times in MAO; those hits were not read.
4. **The friend and the farbrengen.** Beyond the Tanya foreword (AR42), no Alter Rebbe text on a friend or gathering as a carrier to the animal soul was found here. The "two G-dly souls against one" saying in SOURCES P20 is the Mitteler Rebbe's.
5. **Siddur im Dach.** It mixes the Alter Rebbe's maamarim with editors' notes (Tzemach Tzedek, Maharash and later). Line 2327 (animal soul not moved by Shema's contemplation, moved by the Ofanim) is a note quoting a later Rebbe, so it was left out. Only body text was used.
6. **Wine and prayer.** MAO 68301 (Neviim, OCR) says a drunk may not pray, since refining the animal soul is by chochmah and bittul, not "wine", though he may say Grace. Not verified; worth adding to AR25.
7. **Coverage.** In the 78,856-line MAO, 548 blocks mention the animal soul; 233 were read after filtering for method words. Igros Kodesh OCR, Boneh Yerushalayim, and Tanya Mahadura Kama were not searched.
8. **Order of operations.** The Alter Rebbe gives several sequences: night bitterness → morning praises → anger at coarseness → compassion → Shema (MAO 40457); plowing before rain (LT 3996-4004); subduing before turning (LT 206); selfless praise before "good for me" (MAO 48151). A builder may want one combined daily ladder. That synthesis is ours and is not done here.
9. **Horse and rider.** MAO 38452 says the animal soul, once led, carries the person further than he could go alone. This matches TO and LT on the animal soul's greater power ("bechila yatir"). It could anchor a positive framing for builders: the animal soul as engine, not only enemy.
