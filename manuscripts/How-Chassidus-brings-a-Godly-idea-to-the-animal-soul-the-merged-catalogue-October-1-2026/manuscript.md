## 1. The whole picture in one page

**The animal soul can be taught, but it is a different listener.** All six slices agree: it has a mind, so it can be shown things. But its mind is narrow. It is busy with its own good, its own life, and what it can see and touch. The G-dly soul needs little; the long work is for the animal soul. The Alter Rebbe says the long praises before Shema exist for it; the G-dly soul "has no need for so much contemplation" (AR05). For the Mitteler Rebbe almost all contemplation is "serving G-d with the body" (MR-02).

**What reaches it, in one line each.** 1. **Its own door.** G-d as the life inside the world it can see: creation from nothing, memale, "lift your eyes and see who created these". Not "Hashem is One", not G-d's exaltedness. *All agree; nearly word for word in AR04, TZ03, MR-07, RM05, RS-15* (M03). 2. **Its own currency.** What touches *me*: my life, my good, my pleasure. "He is your life." "Nearness to G-d is good for me." "He is the source of every pleasure." *All agree* (M15-M19). 3. **A translator.** The G-dly soul explains through plain human reason, step by step, with pictures, in detail, until the idea "settles" (M01, M02, M06). *Rayatz and the Rebbe give the full model; the Mitteler Rebbe the pictures; the Rashab the detail.* 4. **Time.** Length, depth, repetition, habit. The first fruit is only agreement; wanting comes later (M07, M08, M41). *All agree that habit becomes nature.* 5. **Its own root and its own feeling.** It is moved before it understands, and more strongly. The angels' storm, melody, joy, pity, its own memory of rescue (M20-M26, M12). 6. **The body and the deed.** The mitzvah done with the limbs, the voice aloud, charity from one's earnings, eating for heaven's sake, small refusals, closing the gates (M27, M31-M39). *Speech aloud: AR, MR, TZ, RS, RM all say it.* 7. **Above reason, when reason fails.** The yoke, holy stubbornness, the simple cry, the soul's readiness to give itself, the yechidah (M66-M71). 8. **People.** A friend makes "two G-dly souls against one"; a mashpia speaks to the one knot; a teacher's words work as much as he lives them (M45-M47).

**Where they agree.** Its door is memale and the physical world (all). It answers to "good for me" (all). Speech aloud wakes the heart (AR, MR, TZ, RS, RM). The praises before Shema are its food (AR, MR, TZ, RS, RY). Habit becomes nature (all). Joy wins and sadness loses (all). Prayer's arousal fades unless it becomes a resolve and a deed (AR, MR, RS, RY). When it is at full strength you do not explain to it (AR, RS, RY, RM).

**Where they differ, or pull against each other.** - **The Mitteler Rebbe and R. Aharon of Strashelye.** The Mitteler Rebbe: the arousal must come from the thing itself, not from wanting to be moved; wanting the excitement is "level zero"; the heart must go back up and deepen the mind (MR-01, MR-03, MR-27). R. Aharon: the first arousal must come "from the side of the *yesh*", the animal soul's own self-mixed feeling; refine it afterwards with knowledge, like smelting ore; "the heart is the main thing" (MR-RA-01, MR-RA-02). The MR researcher notes the gap is narrower than its reputation: both attack those who forbid the heart's arousal; they differ on *which* arousal counts and in what order. Do not present it as "mind vs heart". - **"Don't debate it" vs "explain so a child understands".** The Alter Rebbe, the Rashab and the Rebbe all say: do not answer its thoughts; debate gives it a seat and a win is only for now (AR02, RS-04, RM12). The Rebbe also demands: explain until the animal soul understands, "even a child", even for a person of pure yoke (RM11, RM03), and the Rashab's long road answers each of its arguments (RS-03). *Reconciled (the RM slice's reading and ours):* teach and show it the good; do not open a case where it gets a vote. Which one to use depends on the state: one who can hear reason gets the long road; one who is caught gets the short road (RS-04, RM23). - **Self-interest: door or danger?** Many use self-interest as the door: make it touch yourself; He is your life; start not-for-its-sake; even the yoke is sold as relief from deciding (MR-01, MR-08, RM02, RM13). Others warn: without a selfless first step, "good for me" love slides into other self-serving loves (AR07, MR-RA-03); wanting the feeling for itself is "level zero" (MR-01); the aim must stay on G-d, not the vitality (MR-08). *Ours:* self-interest is the door, not the house (also WAYS.md); it comes with a selfless moment before and a rising reason after (M67, M19). - **Understanding vs habit vs the yoke.** The Tzemach Tzedek: only deep study, felt as da'at, turns the essence (TZ13, TZ02). The Rayatz: understanding alone does not move it out of its habit (RY§3.1). The Rashab: fear comes by remembering and habit, not by long contemplation (RS-09); when caught, use stubbornness, not reason (RS-04). R. Hillel: faith becomes second nature by habit, "without reason or pleasure" (TZ17). The Rebbe: do first, then explain (RM11). *Ours:* they are stages and tools for different states, not rival schools. The Rashab himself says the short road leads back to the long one (RS-04). - **The higher unity.** The Maharash and R. Hillel: the animal soul cannot receive "One" at all (TZ04). The Rashab: it can understand it, dressed in garments, but not feel it; aim only at action (RS-27). - **Breaking.** Several say break it in general first (RM09, RS-25, RS-26, RY13). A chassid's saying warns "break a desire and it becomes two"; uproot it by contemplation instead (RY34). The Rebbe: cancel only the bad *form*; keep the force (RM08). The Rayatz: the stick for the evil, mercy for the animal (RY14). - **Joy by acting.** The Tzemach Tzedek: show joyful movements though you do not feel them; in the end it will be so (TZ23). The Rebbe: not "pinching the cheeks"; joy must reach the animal soul, by habit (RM15). *Ours:* outward acts are a road, not the goal.

**In one line (ours).** Speak to it in its own currency (life, good, what it can see), slowly, in detail, through plain reason and pictures, until it agrees and then wants. Tie every arousal to a deed and a habit. When it cannot hear, do not argue: use the yoke, firmness and the plain cry. Always with joy, never with heaviness.

## 2. The order: one sequence a guide could follow

Each step's content is in the sources. **Stated** means a source itself puts this step before or after the thing it is paired with. **Ours** means we placed it. Joining all fourteen steps into one line is ours: no single source gives the whole sequence (the AR slice notes the Alter Rebbe gives several partial sequences).

1. **See which animal soul you face, and whether it is caught** *(ours)*. Ox, sheep or goat; reasonable or a 'dog'; which layer; which temperament. The diagnosis is stated by several sources. Putting it first in a single sequence is ours. Basis: AR31, RM23, RM10, RS-04, RS-26, MR-26.
2. **Clear the ground first** *(stated)*. Before prayer: drop worries by a reckoning the animal soul accepts, pity your soul, stand before the King. Not at a crisis peak: presence and one practical step come first (the last clause is ours, from PSYCHOLOGY part 5). Basis: RS-31, MR-33.
3. **If it is caught: the short road, then back to the long road** *(stated)*. No argument; holy stubbornness and the yoke; do the opposite. Over time the coarseness weakens and reasoning works again. The Rebbe also: do first, then explain. Basis: RS-04, RS-17, RY16, RM23, RM11.
4. **Break it in general, then refine in detail** *(stated)*. A general breaking and softening (make physicality repulsive, shed added coarseness and habits by forcing) before explaining and sorting trait by trait. Basis: RM09, RY04, RY13, RS-25, RS-26, TZ18.
5. **A selfless gaze before 'good for me'** *(stated)*. First gaze at the King's glory wanting nothing; only then the love that says 'nearness to G-d is good for me'. Basis: AR07, MR-RA-03, RS-29.
6. **Feed the animal first** *(stated)*. In prayer: praises of physical creation and the angels' storm come before Shema, the G-dly soul's meal. Quick praise first, depth from Yotzer on. Basis: AR05, MR-10, TZ03, TZ05, TZ13, RS-23, TZ30.
7. **Explain in its terms: plain reason first, then the G-dliness in it** *(stated)*. Through the thinking soul; at its own price; in detail; with pictures; through memale, its own life and its good. Basis: RY04, RM01, RM02, RS-15, MR-04, RY05.
8. **Agreement, then wanting** *(stated)*. Aim first at agreement (it stops blocking); with long contemplation it comes to want. Basis: MR-02, RS-23, RM25, TZ04, TZ11.
9. **Subdue, then turn** *(stated)*. Itkafia, then is'hapcha: the trait first obeys in deed, speech, thought; then the same force is given a good object and turns. Basis: RM07, AR27, AR07, RM08.
10. **Make it a decision and a deed, at once** *(stated)*. Prayer's arousal fades; what lasts is the resolve, kept in deed, aimed at today's particular trait. Basis: MR-15, MR-16, RY07, AR30.
11. **Through the day: carry the imprint** *(stated)*. Brief re-contemplation, remembering that He sees, small refusals in permitted things. Basis: AR11, RS-09, MR-32, AR26.
12. **After prayer: learn** *(stated)*. Halachah and the Oral Torah after prayer complete the refining. Basis: MR-29.
13. **At night: the reckoning; bitterness at set times, joy by day** *(stated)*. The bedtime court case and set-time bitterness; in the morning, prayer in joy. Basis: RY23, TZ28, AR14, AR18, RS-24.
14. **Again tomorrow, little by little** *(stated)*. The yetzer renews each day; habit becomes nature; drops, not glasses. Basis: AR29, RY25, MR-14, RM15.

Not every step is for every person or every day. Steps 1 and 3 decide which of the rest apply. A person who can hear reason goes 5-9; a person who is caught starts at 3. Steps 10-14 apply to everyone. *(ours)*

## M01 · Explain it through plain reason, step by step

**What it is.** The G-dly soul does not speak to the animal soul directly. It puts the idea into plain human reasoning (the 'thinking soul' or 'middle soul'), which is close to the animal soul, and explains until the idea 'begins to settle' there. First a general rational idea, then the G-dliness inside it.

**How to do it.** 1. Take the G-dly idea. 2. Say it first as an ordinary idea any thinking person follows. 3. Then show the G-dliness in that same idea. 4. Go rung by rung; a little understanding already counts ('a little light pushes away much darkness'). 5. Keep explaining until it 'lies' in the mind. 6. Make it simple enough that 'even a child understands' (RM11). The Alter Rebbe's picture: the father shrinks his mind to the child's mind and talks until school seems right even to the child; if not, he gives sweets (AR34).

**Why it works.** The animal soul has a mind, but it is busy only with its own good, and rational thought is new to it. The thinking soul is natural, so it is near the animal soul, and it is reason, so it can receive from above and pass it on (RY04, RM01).

**When.** For everyone; the Rebbe's main frame for the two souls (RM01). Slow work, in prayer and in study (RY04). **Not when.** Not for the animal soul that can no longer hear reason (see M48, M69). Even at best the thinking soul 'only agrees' (RM01). Without the order of steps it is 'only deceit, or fooling oneself' (RY04).

**Quotes.** - «שעל ידה תוכל נפש האלקית להסביר לנפש הבהמית עד שיתחיל להיות מונח אצלה» — *through it the G-dly soul can explain to the animal soul until [a Godly matter] begins to settle in her.* Toras Menachem, 11 Nissan 5732. `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel Schneerson (Lubavitcher Rebbe) (1902-1994)/Toras Menachem (Brooklyn, New York, 1950-1992).txt`, line 108250. - «ע"י אמצעות של הנפש השכלית הנה הנה"א מבאר ומסביר להנה*ב השגה אלקית, דהנה הנפש השכלית להיותה טבעית יש לה קירוב להנה"ב» — *by means of the intellectual soul, the G-dly soul explains G-dly understanding to the animal soul; for the intellectual soul, being natural, is close to the animal soul.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5700, p. 214. `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (scans, OCR).txt`, line 25339 (OCR). - «דתחלה צריכים לקרב את הנה"ב לענין שכלי בכלל ואח"כ אפשר להביאו לשכל אלקי» — *first one must bring the animal soul close to an intellectual matter in general, and afterwards one can bring it to G-dly intellect.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5696, p. 107. `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (scans, OCR).txt`, line 18806 (OCR).

**In a conversation today** *(ours)*. Say the idea first in everyday words a tired person would nod at; only then name what is holy in it.

## M02 · Use many pictures

**What it is.** Bring the G-dly idea near the natural mind with many analogies (meshalim) and explanations taken from life: the soul in the body, light and its source, a father and a child, a treasure and pebbles.

**How to do it.** Pick pictures from nature and the soul. Explain at length and in width. Only after the length and width of the pictures does one reach the 'depth' inside them (MR-04). Use parables and images from the world (RY04). Picture its loves as a child's pebbles and nuts next to a treasure (TZ08).

**Why it works.** Through 'human pictures' the natural mind grasps 'in every detail' what the G-dly mind grasps above (MR-04). 'Through parables and images the animal soul too understands and grasps G-dliness' (RY04).

**When.** Before and during prayer; in all teaching. **Not when.** If one is moved only by the picture and keeps only what he grasped, it shrinks and dies; the aim is the essence inside the explanation (MR-04). Builders: say where a picture breaks (WAYS).

**Quotes.** - «לקרב ענין אלקי אל השכל עי ריבוי משלים והסברים» — *To bring a Godly matter near the intellect through many analogies and explanations.* Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans). `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai (scans, OCR).txt`, line 63608 (OCR). - «ועוד זאת דע"י המשלים ודמיונות יבין וישיג הנה"ב ג"כ את אלקות» — *and moreover, through parables and images the animal soul too understands and grasps G-dliness.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5692. `Chassidus-txt/R. Yosef Yitzchak Schneersohn (Rebbe Rayatz) (1880-1950)/Sefer HaMaamarim (Riga and Otwock, 1929-1933).txt`, line 559. - «מצמצם האב את שכלו לפי שכלו של קטן ומדבר עמו עד שגם לפי שכלו של קטן יוכשר בעיניו לילך לבית הספר» — *the father contracts his mind to the child's mind and talks with him until, even by the child's mind, going to school seems right to him.* Maamarei Admur HaZaken, Inyanim. `Chassidus-txt/R. Shneur Zalman of Liadi (Alter Rebbe) (1745-1812)/Maamarei Admur HaZaken (scans, OCR).txt`, line 51318 (OCR).

**In a conversation today** *(ours)*. Give one concrete picture instead of a paragraph, then stop.

## M03 · Its door: G-d as the life inside the world it can see

*Taught by:* AR04, TZ01, TZ03, MR-07, RM05, RS-15, TZ26

**What it is.** The animal soul is reached through creation from nothing and G-d filling each thing from inside (memale): how this world, and its own life, is being made now. Not through G-d's exaltedness above the worlds. Several Rebbeim say this almost word for word.

**How to do it.** 1. Take a physical, concrete thing: earth, sea, sun, this body (TZ03, RM05). 2. Ask 'who created these?' and look 'with eyes of flesh' (MR-07, TZ01). 3. See how it is made from nothing now, and is nothing before the power that makes it (AR04). 4. Keep the eyes 'down' on this while the heart aims 'up' (AR04, TZ01). 5. Go slowly; in prayer this is Pesukei deZimra, praises about physical things (TZ03). 6. Choose what the animal soul can understand: the life in things, the good in G-dliness (RS-15).

## M04 · The higher unity: not heard, or heard only in garments

**What it is.** 'Hashem is One' (the higher unity) is not the animal soul's contemplation. The Maharash and R. The Rashab says it can understand even 'One', dressed in examples, but not feel it; so aim only at a practical result.

**How to do it.** If you teach the higher unity at all, dress it in garments (light and its source, speech in its speaker) and aim at the deed: at least not to cut physical things off from G-d by craving (RS-27). **Why it works.** Every trait has a mind; the animal soul's mind can understand that one must love G-d, but not 'One' (TZ04). It understands 'One' only 'not as real knowing and feeling as in the G-dly soul', so its nullification touches only action (RS-27).

## M05 · Feed your animal first: the praises before Shema

*Taught by:* AR05, MR-10, RY09, RS-23, TZ03, TZ13, TZ30

**What it is.** The long praises before Shema exist for the animal soul. 'One may not eat before feeding his animal.' ## M06 · Detail, not generality

**What it is.** Contemplate in particulars: this world, this level, this thing, step by step. General contemplation is only a memory of G-dliness; it does not reach the animal soul's grasp.

## M07 · Aim first for agreement; the wanting comes later

## M09 · Go deep, not quick: cook it through

## M10 · Answer its claims with a true reckoning (the long road)

## M13 · Torah study softens it

## M14 · 'That was not you'

## M50 · Don't debate it

**Quotes.** - «הוא דוקא כאשר עושה התפעלות לב בשר גם כן בבחינת התמימות דוקא בלי הרגש עצמו בזה כלל וכלל» — *Only when the heart of flesh is moved in wholeheartedness, with no self-feeling at all.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut. `Chassidus-txt/R. Dovber Schneuri (Mitteler Rebbe) (1773-1827)/Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (Lubavitch, 1813).txt`, line 875. - «וזה המין שכיח בפשוטים יותר מבמבינים וחכמים» — *This kind is more common among simple people than among the understanding and wise.* Kuntres HaHitpa'alut. `Chassidus-txt/R. ## 4. What does not work

Merged from the slices' warnings. *Who* gives slice entry ids; `XX§3.n` means item n of section 3 in slice file XX.md (those items have no entry id).

1. Debating it. Answering its thoughts point by point gives it a seat at the table, and even a win may be only for the moment. 'One who wrestles with a filthy man gets filthy too.' *Who:* AR02, AR-W08, RM12, RM§3.1, RY14, RS-04.
2. Explaining to the animal soul that can no longer hear reason: the one 'sunk in the thing', the 'dog', the 'iron wall' built by sins. Use the short road, the yoke or the stick. *Who:* RS-03, RS-04, RM23, RM§3.2, RY14.
3. Understanding alone. Natural traits are stronger than intellect. The animal soul can agree with every explanation and stay in its habit. Knowing without feeling is 'only faith' (a believer can still be a thief). Knowledge without the heart 'does nothing'. *Who:* AR-W03, RY01, RY§3.1, TZ02, TZ§3.1, MR-RA-02.
4. Working from the G-dly soul alone. Inspiration from above, contemplation of the Essence 'eyes closed', the G-dly soul's own excitement or natural love, and arousal from its surrounding levels do not touch the animal soul. *Who:* AR-W02, AR-W06, AR30, RS§3, RY§3.3, RM§3.3, TZ§3.3.
5. Subtle or lofty ideas: 'Hashem is One', sovev, G-d's exaltedness. The animal soul is not moved by fine, spiritual matters; at most it is put to sleep. *Who:* AR-W05, TZ04, TZ§3.2, RS-15.
6. General contemplation and general resolutions. A general glow is only a memory of G-dliness; 'from very far G-d appears to him'; a general resolution does not help the particular trait. *Who:* MR-06, MR§3.7, RS-15, RS-16, RY07.
7. Heat without mind and arousal without deed. Warmth left without the mind's breadth turns at once into lust. Wanting the excitement itself is 'level zero'. Shouting and outward movements tear out the G-dly soul's light. Arousal with no deed is a flame in the air. *Who:* AR-W01, MR-01, MR§3.1, MR§3.2, MR§3.5, MR-16, RS§3.
8. Expecting one arousal to change its nature. After prayer he is as he was. One big cry, or the High Holidays' tears without the year's work, returns to the old nature. It does not go away: an old chassid said it tormented him for 67 years. *Who:* MR-15, MR§3.11, MR-32, RS§3, RY§3.2, RY§3.14.
9. Rushing and too much at once. Medicine by the glass; grabbing high rungs; starting at the conclusion; climbing to closeness without leaving evil. *Who:* TZ11, RY25, RS-18, RY§3.4.
10. Heaviness, sadness, melancholy (including sad melodies). They cannot beat it; melancholy dressed as piety is its trick; self-satisfaction and sadness over one's spiritual state are both its traits. *Who:* AR-W09, AR19, AR35, TZ24, RY02, RS-24, RM32.
11. Self-punishment. Teshuvah is not fasting; fasting that harms is forbidden; degrading mussar can bring illness, despair, or the body and animal soul rebelling. Afflict the animal soul, not the body. *Who:* AR-W10, AR24, RM19, RM§3.4.
12. Feeding it: too much wine or mashke, eating for lust, 'giving to the animal soul'. *Who:* AR-W11, AR25, RY12, AR23, MR-30, RM14.
13. 'Good for me' love without a selfless first step; it can slide into other self-serving loves. *Who:* AR-W07, AR07, MR-RA-03.
14. Breaking a desire while the whole will is still in it: 'break a desire and it becomes two'. *Who:* RY34, RY§3.5.
15. Lofty reasons that stop the deed. The animal soul can be pious, 'even Chassidic'. Learning so absorbed that action is forgotten; learning without prayer that swells the ego. *Who:* RM22, RM§3.9, RS§3, RY02.
16. Trusting it after it turns. It may relapse; even a won animal soul hides opposition. *Who:* TZ09, RM§3.13.
17. A soft rebuke: striking the heart with the hand at Ashamnu; the yetzer thinks it is being stroked. *Who:* RY14.
18. Rebuke as the main method for the animal soul itself. The stick is for the image of evil; the animal soul gets mercy and is made a vessel. *Who:* RY14, RY03.
19. A cold, dry self-audit in the middle of a test, especially by the animal soul's instructions. *Who:* RM32.
20. Contemplation against the coarse outer layers (gluttony, bad habits from youth). They are shed by bitterness and actual forcing, not contemplation. *Who:* RS-26.
21. Contemplating one's lowliness against an arrogance 'above reason'. It does not help at all; only light above comprehension breaks it. *Who:* AR-W04, AR15.
22. Fluency and habit without life. Repeating until one can answer questions 'from the lip outward'; habitual service may be the animal soul's own nature; mitzvos done coldly by rote. *Who:* AR-W13, MR§3.4, MR-14, TZ§3.11.
23. Fake or outward joy ('pinching the cheeks'), and leaving the animal soul to itself. *Who:* RM15, RM§3.5, RM§3.6.
24. No broken heart, no contrition, no crushing: then Chassidus is not absorbed and the natural soul's agreement is imagined. *Who:* MR-18, MR§3.9, TZ31, TZ18.
25. Fear alone, or bitterness alone. Fear nullifies but does not refine; bitterness breaks the animal soul's 'is-ness' but does not raise it. *Who:* RS-09, RS-13, RS§3, MR-19.
26. Proofs, wonders and philosophy. Proofs are 'medicine for the sick' only; philosophers grasp G-d with human intellect and are not moved. *Who:* TZ§3.6, TZ§3.7.
27. Anger and 'you are no longer a Jew' toward one who has strayed. *Who:* RY18, RY§3.12.
28. Many schemes in divine service confuse the mind (but elsewhere the Alter Rebbe says one breaks bad traits 'with great schemes'; both stated, in different contexts). *Who:* AR-W12.
29. Trusting the animal soul's own reasoning. Its mind serves its feelings and finds ways to fill the desire. *Who:* RM§3.7, MR-RA-02.
30. Too much worldly cleverness; a heart sunk in business spends the natural soul's 'yechidah' there. *Who:* MR-33, MR§3.15.
