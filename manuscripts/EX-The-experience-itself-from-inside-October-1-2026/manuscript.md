## Five rules the sources give about the inner world

1. **The deeper, the less felt.** "The deeper the arousal of the essence ... the less he feels it" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, line 569 of the `_drive` Sefaria-Kehot file, verified; MAP C6 cites the same words as line 556 of another copy). And "that it is less and he does not feel it comes from added light" (line 217). So a guide never treats intensity as depth. **stated**
2. **Feeling is a ladder, not a price.** Hayom Yom gives three rungs of feeling: understood, felt alive, felt as Godliness. It also says that sometimes, by kindness, Godliness is sensed with no work at all (line 415). **stated**
3. **The test is afterward.** The Mitteler Rebbe's signs of the real thing come later. They are true lowliness, not being stung by insult, and a soul drawn toward Godliness all day in deed (lines 841-846). The Rashab says one is 'a different person' for hours (Kuntres HaAvodah 225). **stated**
4. **Every counterfeit has the same shape.** "There is one who is moved and is taken to be a great something in himself" (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut 560). The arousal becomes the point, not G-d. Visions, the sense that the soul is leaving, 'I saw light!' that vanishes, easy claims of simple delight: all are named. **stated**
5. **Every height comes down.** "If your heart runs, return to the One" (Tanya ch. 50). The return is the main thing (Imrei Binah, MAP C5). Every region here ends in a deed or in the day. **stated**

## EX-01 · Being said right now

*Builds on:* MAP S65 Given right now, S66 Filled from inside; ONE-PROCESS step 3; SPINE F01.

**What it is.** The lower unity, felt. Not the idea that God made the world once, but the felt sense that this room, this hand, this breath is being given being now, from nothing, and would not be here a moment without it.

**From inside.** The solidity thins. Things are still here, but their 'thereness' is lent. The sources' image is the eye: if the eye were allowed to see the life and spirit in each thing, coming from the word of G-d, its physical bulk would not be seen at all. The Alter Rebbe adds a way to feel it in yourself: each night your life withdraws, and each morning you get up a new being; from that you understand that every creature lives like this, running and returning. From inside it is like waking up and noticing you were given back.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Start with yourself, not the cosmos: notice that you woke up this morning, given back (Likkutei Torah). 2. Widen to one object: it is held in being by the word that says it, now (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-3). 3. Stay with 'now'; the sources stress continual, not once. 4. Deepen with detail until it is felt and seen, not only known (Kuntres HaAvodah: 'felt and seen by him how the world lives from the Godly light').

**Signs it is real.** Wonder at plain things. Less grip. A soft gratitude for existing at all. It can be returned to on purpose. (ours, from the sources' after-effects)

**Its counterfeit.** Feeling that the world is unreal, dreamlike or that nothing matters (derealisation). The sources never say the world is not there; they say it is not there on its own. If 'nothing is real' comes with fear or numbness, it is not this.

**Guards.** Not for someone already feeling unreal, dissociated or in a crisis: ground first (MAP S20, Danger D03). Not as a technique to escape a problem that needs a practical step.

## EX-02 · Garments with life inside

*Builds on:* MAP S63 The world held, S66 Filled from inside; ONE-PROCESS step 4; SPINE F04.

**What it is.** The same lower unity, felt in each particular thing: what the eye sees is the outer clothing of the King, and inside each thing is its life from Him.

**From inside.** Everything you look at is a garment, and you remember what is inside it. The tree, the street, the person opposite: the outside is real, and it is a covering. The Rashab describes love 'like water' coming from this: you feel the life in each thing, then how precious that life is, and then you want nothing but it. A soft, flowing closeness, not a fire.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Habit, not a single insight: Tanya ch. 42 says the main thing is to train the mind constantly, so that whatever the eyes see is known as the King's outer garments. 2. 'Emunah' is from 'training', like a craftsman training his hands. 3. In prayer, contemplate the light and life clothed in each creature until it is felt (Kuntres HaAvodah). 4. Then say it in your own words to the heart: this life is the life of the world, and my life too.

**Signs it is real.** Ordinary things become a little tender. Less contempt for the plain. A pull toward the inside of things rather than their surface. (ours)

**Its counterfeit.** Looking 'through' people as if they were only wrappers, which makes them disposable. The garment is His; despising it is not the view from inside. Also: a pretty idea about 'divine sparks' with no change in how one treats the thing.

**Guards.** Not a reason to skip practical care of things or people. Not for someone grieving who needs the person, not the 'inner life', to be honoured first.

## EX-03 · Seeing, not only hearing

*Builds on:* MAP S33 Before Whom, S74 Seen eye to eye; ONE-PROCESS step 6; SPINE F12.

**What it is.** The move from knowing the unity as something heard and understood to having it as something seen, with the certainty of the eye. The sources call this re'iyah, seeing, and say it is the root of all the work.

**From inside.** Hearing is: I understand it, and another argument could still move me. Seeing is: it is so, the way the table is so. The Rebbe says seeing is the certainty that no later argument overturns, as if seeing with eyes of flesh. The Rebbe Rayatz says his father would sit learning and see the Essence of the Infinite with his senses, and that everyone can reach seeing Godliness palpably, only one must work. The Rebbe adds that at Sinai it was 'You were shown', given, not effortful. So from inside: less 'I think', more 'there it is'. Often quiet.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Bind the mind to the idea the way it binds to a physical thing it is looking at (Tanya ch. 42: daas is attachment). 2. Stay with one point long enough that it stops being a thought and becomes a sight. 3. Say it to yourself as plainly as a fact you see: 'He fills this place, here' (Tanya ch. 29). 4. Do not try to force the sight; the deepest seeing is given ('You were shown').

**Signs it is real.** Steadiness: a bad day does not cancel it. Less need to argue it. The 'it is so' returns in odd moments. (stated in part: certainty that is not overturned; ours: the rest)

**Its counterfeit.** Visions, lights, images. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut calls 'seeing frightening visions' a lie of the imagination. Seeing in Chassidus is certainty, not pictures. Also: excitement that says 'I saw light!' and is gone in a moment (EX-04).

**Guards.** Never ask anyone to 'see' or report visions. If a person reports voices or images with distress, that is a matter for care, not this map (D03).

> «להאיר עיניה באמת יחוד אוראיןסוף בראיה חושיית, ולא בחינת שמיעה והבנה לבדה»

## EX-04 · The light reaches the heart

*Builds on:* MAP S39 The heart answers, S40 Mind moved in itself, S26 It goes flat; ONE-PROCESS step 6; KUNTRES-HATEFILLAH.md.

**What it is.** Hargashas Elokus, feeling Godliness: the contemplated truth lights up in the mind and is felt in the heart as itself, not as a conclusion about it. Hayom Yom gives three rungs of 'the ladder of feeling'.

**From inside.** Two ways the heart can be moved. In the outer way, the mind thinks, then only a summary is left ('so, Godliness is good and wonderful'), and the heart is pushed by the summary. It passes quickly. In the inner way the Godly light itself shines in the mind, the explanations are only a passage, and the light flows by itself into the heart: the heart hears and feels the thing the mind grasped. Hayom Yom: learning gives the light of the idea; before prayer, the feeling of the idea's life; in prayer, the feeling of the Godliness in it.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Take time: the Rashab says an hour or more of real effort of mind, with many details, until the idea 'settles well and clean' in the mind. 2. Bind yourself to the thing, not to your understanding of it. 3. Let the heart be moved by itself; do not manufacture the emotion. 4. Climb the three rungs: understood, felt alive, felt as Godliness.

**Signs it is real.** The arousal lasts and returns, because it is 'essential' and not pushed (Kuntres HaTefillah). Not much noise. The heart moves without your arranging it.

**Its counterfeit.** 'Ah, I'm warm, I have seen light!' and then nothing: a flash across mind and heart that leaves everything as before (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). Or an emotion worked up from the bottom-line summary that fades the moment the thought stops.

**Guards.** Not a test a person passes or fails. Hayom Yom says sometimes, by kindness, Godliness is sensed with no work at all; feeling is never the price of being close.

## EX-05 · Held and lifted

*Builds on:* MAP S84 Trust, S63 The world held; being-held/; ONE-PROCESS step 4.

**What it is.** The unity felt as His closeness to me in particular: not a vast impersonal oneness, but the King who comes down to the lowest person, lifts him, brings him in, and holds him. Love answers love.

**From inside.** Tanya ch. 46: a great king comes down from his glory to a lowly man lying in the dung, lifts him up, brings him into the innermost room, and is with him in embrace, spirit cleaving to spirit. From inside, the answer is not effort: even a heart like stone melts and becomes water, and the soul pours out. The felt quality is being received, not achieving.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Use the mirror of water: as a face is reflected, so love wakes love (Tanya ch. 46). 2. Contemplate how far down He came to you, specifically, and what He lifted you from. 3. Do not ask the heart to love; let it see the love shown to it. 4. If the heart stays stone, the text expects that, and says the stone melts.

**Signs it is real.** Softness, relief, sometimes tears. Feeling less alone. A wish to answer with something done for Him. (ours)

**Its counterfeit.** Feeling special or chosen over others; a warmth that wants to be seen. Also a 'held' that is really fusion with a person (a guide, a teacher) rather than with Him.

**Guards.** The 'dung' image must never be used on someone with shame or self-hatred as a description of them; for them use only the lifting. If a person feels held by the chatbot itself, gently point past it.

## EX-06 · Awe that stills

*Builds on:* MAP S45 Lower awe, S46 Higher awe, S33 Before Whom; ONE-PROCESS step 7.

**What it is.** Awe at two depths. Lower awe: He stands over me, looks at me, and I stand as before a king. Higher awe: seeing how each thing is born from nothing by His word, and being hushed before it.

**From inside.** Lower: a straightening. You are seen, and you would not want to break the bond. Higher: Tanya ch. 43 calls it awe as shame, inner awe, coming from the inwardness of Godliness inside the worlds; the wise one 'sees each thing being born from nothing'. It is quiet, like shyness before someone vast and near. Not fear of punishment.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Before learning or a mitzvah, think at length (as much as mind and time allow) that G-d stands over you, His glory fills the earth, and He looks at you (Tanya ch. 41). 2. Let that produce a stillness before the act, as before a king. 3. Then the higher step: look at one thing and see it being born now from His word (Tanya ch. 43). 4. The lower awe is 'the gate and the door'; do not skip it.

**Signs it is real.** Speech slows. Care in small acts. The awe does not frighten; it gathers you. (ours)

**Its counterfeit.** Dread, scrupulosity, fear of a punishing watcher. If 'He sees me' makes a person shrink and hide, it is not the awe the sources mean.

**Guards.** Not for people with religious OCD or trauma tied to a watching, punishing God. For them begin with being held (EX-05) and joy (EX-13).

> *and behold, G-d stands over him, and 'the whole earth is full of His glory', and He looks upon him*

## EX-07 · Less of me

*Builds on:* MAP S56 The self quieted, S59 True lowliness; ONE-PROCESS step 7; SPINE F08.

**What it is.** Bittul hayesh: the self that stands apart and enjoys its own experience goes quiet. The Mitteler Rebbe distinguishes a love where 'there is someone who grasps and loves' from an arousal of bittul where one becomes like a silent stone, not feeling himself.

**From inside.** In the holy side, even the heart's sense of self comes in utter bittul, without feeling itself at all. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut adds the rule: the less one feels it, the more inner light there is. From inside it is lighter, less to defend. Afterward it shows as true lowliness: a sharp word does not sting, because there is less of you to sting.

## EX-16 · One with Him in the deed

*Builds on:* MAP S81 The deed in the hand, S82 The home below; ONE-PROCESS step 14; SPINE F10.

**What it is.** The unity felt in doing a mitzvah: the commandment is His inner will with no hiding, so in the act the person's life is joined with Him, 'a wondrous union with nothing like it'. Mitzvah means joining.

**From inside.** Tanya ch. 35: even a perfect tzaddik who loves Him is still 'a something on his own'; but the life in a mitzvah is not a separate thing at all, it is included in His will and they are truly one. Hayom Yom: mitzvah from 'tzavsa', joining; the one who does it joins with the Essence. From inside: often nothing dramatic. The hand is His for that moment. Sometimes a quiet rightness.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Before the act, a moment: this is His will, and it is open here (Tanya ch. 41-42). 2. Do it with the body, fully. 3. Don't wait to feel it; the union is in the act, not the feeling. 4. Accept His kingship in deed, as the bowing after the Shema (Tanya ch. 42).

**Signs it is real.** Steadiness on days when nothing is felt. A wish to do the next one. Joy 'of a mitzvah' that is quiet.

**Its counterfeit.** Mechanical habit with no 'before Whom' (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut: rote 'commandment of men'); or pride in doing more than others.

## EX-17 · The quiet after

*Builds on:* MAP S48 Returning, S59 True lowliness; ONE-PROCESS section 3 (the descent); KUNTRES-HAAVODAH.md.

**What it is.** The after-effect of real prayer or contemplation through the day: a different person for hours, a trace that keeps the deed, a pull toward Godliness, and less appetite for the coarse. This is where the sources look to tell real from false.

**From inside.** Kuntres HaAvodah: anyone who serves G-d well feels that after prayer, for several hours or even a whole day, 'he is a different person'. What remains of prayer's love is 'only an impression', for keeping the deed all day. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut lists good signs of real bittul and delight: the soul is drawn all day after nearness to G-d in deed; idle chatter feels pained; the world's pleasures lose their hold. The Rebbe Rayatz: old chassidim would fold the siddur at the place they had reached that day, and go on tomorrow.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. End prayer or the sitting with one sentence to carry. 2. Name one deed it goes into today. 3. Notice later in the day what is different, without forcing it. 4. Day after day: the Rashab says the change is not from once or twice but from daily diligence.

**Signs it is real.** Stated: the day is drawn toward Godliness, a different person for hours, coarse things feel coarse. Ours: a gentler tone with people.

**Its counterfeit.** Tanya ch. 12 is honest: after prayer, when the great mind withdraws, the bad stirs again. That is not failure, it is the beinoni. The counterfeit is believing the high must persist, and despair when it doesn't; or a 'glow' that never touches conduct.

**Guards.** Do not measure a person by the after-glow. Some days the only trace is a deed done. That is enough (Kuntres HaAvodah: an impression for keeping the deed).

## EX-18 · The plainest thing

*Builds on:* MAP S64 The plainest thing; ONE-PROCESS step 11; SPINE F02, F09.

**What it is.** The union of the higher and lower unity: the world stays fully here, and is at the same time in utter bittul. The deepest level is the least dramatic.

**From inside.** Samach Vav: the union of the two unities is that even as the world exists, it is in utter bittul and union. And the bittul of chochmah, being bittul in very existence, 'makes no noise at all'. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut's great rule: the deeper the arousal, the less it is felt. Hayom Yom: sometimes Godliness is heard with no work at all. From inside: a cup is a cup, and it is His, and nothing needs to happen.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. After any height, look at something ordinary. 2. Hold both: it is here; it is nothing apart from Him. 3. Do not wait for a feeling; 'no noise' is the sign. 4. Then act normally.

**Signs it is real.** Simplicity, humour, ordinary kindness. Nothing to report.

**Its counterfeit.** Flatness or boredom passed off as 'beyond feeling' (MAP S26). Test: is there tenderness toward the ordinary thing, or indifference?

**Guards.** Not something to tell a beginner they should be at; it reads as 'feel nothing'. Offer it to someone coming down from a high.
