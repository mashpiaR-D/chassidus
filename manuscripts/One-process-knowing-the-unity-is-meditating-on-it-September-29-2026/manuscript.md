## 1. The one sentence

> **In Chassidus, to know that there is nothing but Him is already to meditate on it: knowing (da'at) means holding a truth until it is real, so each rung of the unity is known only by a matching depth of contemplation and of self-giving (bitul), and each one wakes a deeper layer of the soul, until at the bottom of the self, where the mind stops, the heart's simple will is already one with Him; and from there it comes back down, into the body and the day.**

The owner's thesis sits inside it. People are not looking for God. They are looking for themselves. The ladder starts with the self, and at its top the self and He turn out to meet. That is Chassidus's own claim, not only ours: the Tanya says the point is "to understand a little of His Godliness from the soul clothed in a person's flesh", and the Piaseczner says "the knowledge of God is in the knowledge of oneself".

> <small>**Sources.**
> Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 15, line 1197: «שהכוונה היא להבין קצת אלהותו יתברך מנפש המלובשת בבשר האדם» — *the point is to understand a little of His Godliness from the soul clothed in a person's flesh*<br>
> Esh Kodesh, line 542: «שידיעת ד׳ היא בידיעת עצמו» — *the knowledge of God is in the knowledge of oneself*</small>

## Why it is one process, not two

**Knowing is binding.** In the Tanya, da'at is not information. It is binding the mind to a truth "with a strong, very firm bond", as firmly as to a thing you see with your eyes. Without that binding even a wise person gets "only empty imaginings". So there is no knowing the unity first and meditating on it after. The knowing *is* the holding.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 3, line 112: «שמקשר דעתו בקשר אמיץ וחזק מאד» — *he binds his mind with a strong, very firm bond*<br>
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 42, line 629: «כמו שהיא מקושרת בדבר גשמי שרואה בעיני בשר ומעמיק בו מחשבתו» — *as bound as it is to a physical thing he sees with his eyes and thinks deeply into*<br>
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 3, line 113: «לא יוליד בנפשו יראה ואהבה אמיתית, כי אם דמיונות שוא» — *(without that binding) he will not give birth to true awe and love, only to empty imaginings*</small>

**The verse says both halves.** "Know today, and return it to your heart." Kuntres HaAvodah reads this verse as the contemplation of the life in the world, the work of the ruach. Kuntres HaTefillah says the main word in it is *the heart*, and that da'at itself is feeling: a person can understand a thing well and not feel it. The Rebbe: recognize, not only understand.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Kuntres HaAvodah 1, line 38 · *OCR*: «שעז"נ וידעת היום והשבות אל לבביך דוקא» — *of which it says: know today and return it to your heart, precisely*<br>
> Kuntres HaTefillah 5, line 62 · *OCR*: «ולכן כתי' וידעת היום והשבות אל לבבך דהעיקר הוא ההשבה אל הלב» — *'know today and return it to your heart': the main thing is the returning to the heart*<br>
> Kuntres HaTefillah 5, line 61 · *OCR*: «וההרגשה זהו"ע הדעת, כי יכול להיות שמבין את הדבר היטב ומ"מ אינו במורגש אצלו» — *the feeling is what da'at is: a person can understand a thing well and still not feel it*<br>
> Inyanah 17:3, line 93: «שיכיר (ולא רק שיבין) שאין שום נתינת מקום להיש מצד עצמו» — *that he recognize (and not only understand) that the 'something' has no standing of its own*</small>

**The unity lives in the knower.** The Mitteler Rebbe does not only teach the unity and then ask for contemplation. He defines the Godly unity as "a depth of understanding", the something given up to the nothing. His book on contemplation opens by defining it: a strong look into the depth of one matter, standing on it a long time.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Gate of Unity 4, line 140: «שענין היחוד האלקי הוא בחינת עומק ההשגה בביטול היש לאין ביחודא עילאה ויחודא תתאה» — *the Godly unity is a depth of understanding: the something given up to the nothing, in the higher and lower unity*<br>
> Gate of Unity 1:3, line 10: «מהות ההתבוננות בעצם הוא ענין ההסתכלות החזקה בעמקות הענין ולעמוד עליו הרבה» — *contemplation is a strong looking into the depth of a matter, and standing on it a long time*</small>

**Each rung has its own bitul, and its own depth of soul.** The sources pair them. Sefer HaArachim: the bitul of the lower unity comes through contemplation; the bitul of the higher comes of itself. Strashelye: first the unity made through da'at, "know today"; then the main bitul, above da'at. Kuntres HaAvodah lays the soul's levels on the unity's rungs in order: the nefesh by acknowledgment, the ruach by contemplating the life in the world, the neshamah through filling to surrounding, and there the soul comes to union with the Essence.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Sefer HaArachim, Achdus Hashem, line 2353 · *OCR*: «הביטול דיחודא עילאה בא ממילא, והביטול דיחודא תתאה בא ע"י התבוננות» — *the bitul of the higher unity comes of itself; the bitul of the lower unity comes through contemplation*<br>
> Strashelye, Second Gate 40:6, line 558: «ולכך אמרו הדבק במדותיו שהוא היחוד כנזכר לעיל על ידי הדעת» — *so they said 'cleave to His attributes', which is the unity (made) through da'at*<br>
> Strashelye, Second Gate 40:6, line 558: «אז יהיה עיקר הביטול אליו דהיינו להבטל למעלה מן הדעת» — *then the main bitul will be to Him: to be nullified above da'at*<br>
> Kuntres HaAvodah 1, line 29 · *OCR*: «תחלה העבוד' דבחי' נפש בבחי' הודאה» — *first, the service of the nefesh, by acknowledgment*<br>
> Kuntres HaAvodah 1, line 38 · *OCR*: «שעז"נ וידעת היום והשבות אל לבביך דוקא» — *of which it says: know today and return it to your heart, precisely*<br>
> Kuntres HaAvodah 1, line 51 · *OCR*: «נעשה בחי' יחוד ודביקות נפשו בעצמות אוא"ם ב"ה» — *his soul comes to union and cleaving with the Essence of the Infinite (OCR: אוא"ס)*</small>

**At the top the mind stops, and something else holds.** The Rashab: no grasp reaches the Essence, not even grasp by negation; "but by the heart's will the Essence is grasped". The Rebbe: the yechidah is always one with His Essence. Imrei Binah: the yechidah is the power of the Essence in the soul. So the last step of knowing is not a thought. It is the self at its bottom, where it already is one with Him.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Samach Vav, Lech Lecha 5666, line 876 · *OCR*: «דשום השגה, אפי' השגת השלילה, שזהו המדרי' היותר נעלית שבהשגה, אינו שייך באוא"ס» — *no grasp, not even grasp by negation, the highest grasp there is, applies to (the Essence of) the Infinite*<br>
> Samach Vav, Lech Lecha 5666, line 897 · *OCR*: «אבל ברעו"ד נתפס בחי' העצמות» — *but by re'uta deliba, the heart's will, the Essence is grasped*<br>
> Inyanah 11:5, line 64: «מצד היחידה שבנשמה שמיוחדת תמיד בעצמותו ית'» — *from the yechidah in the soul, which is always united with His Essence*<br>
> Imrei Binah, Tzitzit, line 510: «שבחי' יחידה שבנפש הוא כח העצמות שבנפש ממש» — *the yechidah in the soul is truly the power of the Essence in the soul*</small>

**And the top comes down.** Imrei Binah: the return is the main thing; the rising was for the sake of coming back down. Samach Vav: the will in the mitzvah has no reason, so only the mitzvah brings the Essence into place and time. The process ends in life, not above it.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Imrei Binah, the Shema 50, line 283: «והשוב הוא העיקר וכל ההעלא' דרצוא לא הי' רק בשביל הירידה דשוב» — *the return is the main thing; all the rising was only for the sake of coming back down*<br>
> Samach Vav, 5666, line 596 · *OCR*: «ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא» — *the true inwardness of the will is that there is no reason for the will at all*<br>
> Samach Vav, 5666, line 740 · *OCR*: «דהיינו שגם בבחי' מקום וזמן לא יהי' ההגבלה דמקום וזמן» — *so that even in place and time there is no limit of place and time*</small>

**What is ours here.** The sources give the rungs, the stages of contemplation, the levels of the soul and the kinds of bitul, and they pair many of them outright. None of them lays all five on one line of fourteen steps. That line is ours, and so is the order at three points (the self first; the yechidah before "the plainest thing"; two knowings and the hiding as one step). Where a source orders things differently, section 2 keeps it.

## 2. The map

Fourteen steps: eleven going up, a turn at the top, three coming down. Each step is one card with eight parts:

- **What is true here**: the unity at this rung (its face, F01-F12 from SPINE.md, and the rung claims A01-A12, from GUIDE-LAYER.md §2 and the guide's own claims file, where one fits).
- **The move of the mind**: how it is contemplated (Chochmah, Binah or Da'at; breadth, length or depth; the picture; the Piaseczner's quiet or picturing where it fits).
- **From inside**: what it is like, in the sources' own images. Described, never demanded.
- **Which part of you, and what bitul**: the level of the soul (nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, yechidah; mind, heart, will, delight) and the kind of self-giving, where the sources say.
- **The edge**: where the mind stops at this rung, and what lies past it.
- **The counterfeit** at this step.
- **The practice**: the manual's sitting (L01-L14, `manual/part2-the-ladder.md`) and one act.
- **Whose**: which alignments are stated and which are ours. Then the sources, in small print.

<a id="step-1"></a> ### Step 1 · Where you stand: what is already moving *Before the rungs: the door · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F06 The soul as part of the One · Part I 1-2 (L01 opens)*

**What is true here.** Even the scatter is inside the unity. The Mitteler Rebbe calls the daily rush 'the exile of the Shechinah' and 'sleep': life is not gone from the sleeper, only hidden. The Piaseczner says the restlessness itself is the soul reaching out: it is not you who feels this ache, but the spark in you.

**The move of the mind.** No idea yet. Name in one sentence what is moving in you now (a worry, a want, a small joy) and start from it. Then a minute of the Piaseczner's quiet: watch your thoughts and ask, gently, 'what am I thinking?' until the flood slows. Not a faculty of Chochmah, Binah or Da'at yet; only a look at, not in.

**From inside.** A head like a train station. The soul cries and we hear it the way a sleeper stung by a fly dreams that a crate fell on him. After a minute of watching, the head empties a little, and what is left is not empty.

**Which part of you.** The natural soul (nefesh), awake to the world and asleep to itself. **Bitul:** None yet. Its opposite is at the door: wanting arousal 'so as to be a something'. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut does not even count that among its levels.

**The edge.** You cannot make the feeling. Forcing it kills it, and the busy self is exactly what blocks what comes from above.

**The counterfeit.** Chasing a feeling; or taking the quiet for the goal. The quiet is only the table cleared for the line.

**The practice.** Part I, Stages 1-2 (every sitting starts here). Sitting L01 opens from this step. *Act:* Say one sentence to yourself: where I am right now. That sentence is the door.

**Whose.** Stated: the Mitteler Rebbe (distraction is sleep and exile), the Piaseczner (start from what moves; quiet by watching; the self blocks). Ours: calling this the first rung of one ladder.

<a id="step-2"></a> ### Step 2 · Under the layers: the true self *The door: through yourself · F06 The soul as part of the One · L01*

**What is true here.** Under everything you carry, the deepest you is 'truly a part of God above'. The Mitteler Rebbe: the soul in itself is a Godly power. So the search for yourself is already the search for Him. The Piaseczner says it outright: the knowledge of God is in the knowledge of oneself; the first work is to find ourselves.

**The move of the mind.** Breadth, turned on the self. Walk the layers one at a time (what I do, what I feel, what I believe about myself) and see that each one changes, so none is the bottom. Look at each layer, not from inside it; a thought you look at melts. Then ask who is looking out through your eyes.

**From inside.** A floor under the floor. The verdict about yourself goes quiet without being argued away. Sometimes a homesickness that turns out to be love: the candle flame that always leans upward.

**Which part of you.** The Godly soul's nefesh. Kuntres HaAvodah: the nefesh serves by acknowledgment, and the general 'Modeh ani', giving your whole existence to Him, is 'very near to every Jew'. **Bitul:** Acknowledgment (hoda'ah), the first and lowest grade of bitul: a general yes, not yet a grasp. Sefer HaArachim calls the lower unity in every creature 'only acknowledgment'.

**The edge.** The self you know is borrowed ('only the shadow of the world'). The true self is not found by looking in alone; it is found in reaching toward Him. So this step cannot finish by itself; it needs the rungs.

**The counterfeit.** Inflation ('I'm a divine spark, so anything goes') or a warm glow of being special. And the opposite: the verdict 'I am my mistakes', against which the Tanya says, do not be wicked in your own eyes.

**The practice.** Sitting L01, Under everything, the spark. *Act:* Modeh Ani (or a thank-you) before the phone tomorrow; one good act done as if the good in you were the real you.

**Whose.** Stated: the soul as part of God (Tanya), the nefesh's acknowledgment (Kuntres HaAvodah), self-knowledge as God-knowledge (the Piaseczner). Ours: placing the self before the rungs of the world (the manual's order too).

<a id="step-3"></a> ### Step 3 · Being said, right now (rung one) *Rung one: the lower unity (va'ed) · F01 The world said into being now · F02 The higher and the lower unity · claims A10 · L02*

**What is true here.** Each thing you see exists only because His word is saying it now; if the letters withdrew for an instant it would be nothing. This is the lower unity: the world is real and is not a thing on its own. It is Chassidus's own teaching, and within Chassidus it is the first rung, not the summit.

**The move of the mind.** Binah, in breadth: one thing at a time, many details, 'wide as a river'. Not 'the world' in general (the Rashab calls that a false imagination) but this cup, this window, your own hand: how the power of the Infinite is seen in it.

**From inside.** For a second it is not an idea but so: this cup is being said. Then it may go flat just as the details start; that is where the work starts. A quiet love 'like water': seeing how the world lives from the Godly light.

**Which part of you.** Ruach. Kuntres HaAvodah gives this contemplation, the life clothed in the world, to the ruach, and says this, not a higher level, is 'know today and return it to your heart'. The heart's traits answer. **Bitul:** Bitul of the 'something' (bitul hayesh): the thing is nullified to its source but still counts. Sefer HaArachim: continuous creation gives only this, because the creating power is a limited power.

**The edge.** How something comes from nothing 'is not truly grasped by the human mind'. And this rung alone slides back into self; it needs a taste of the next.

**The counterfeit.** 'Nothing is real, so nothing matters' (the world is real and really given). Or a vague, general glow, which the Rashab calls a false imagination; the Mitteler Rebbe: in general, 'from very far off God appears to him'.

**The practice.** Sitting L02, Being said, right now. *Act:* At a moment of worry today, look at one plain object and say: 'Being said.'

**Whose.** Stated: the rung (Tanya, Sefer HaArachim), the particular way of contemplating (the Rashab), its soul-level (Kuntres HaAvodah gives it to the ruach). Samach Vav adds that being renewed is itself the cleaving.

<a id="step-4"></a> ### Step 4 · Held: this particular thing, from inside and all around *Rung one, widened: filling and surrounding · F04 Filling and surrounding · claims A03 · L04*

**What is true here.** He fills each thing from inside, to its own measure, and surrounds all things the same. The surrounding light cannot clothe itself inside a bounded soul; it holds it from head to foot. What you see is the King's outer clothing, with someone inside it.

**The move of the mind.** Length: bring the idea down 'until a small child could grasp it', into the thing you named at the start. Picture: all that the eyes see are the King's outer garments. Through 'within the thing' (filling) the mind reaches 'the thing itself' (surrounding), in Kuntres HaAvodah's words. The Piaseczner's line for it: 'You surround me and fill my body and my spirit.'

**From inside.** The world begins to look like a garment with someone in it. You are held rather than holding up.

**Which part of you.** From ruach toward neshamah. Kuntres HaAvodah: through filling the neshamah comes to surrounding, and there to the knowledge of His wonder. **Bitul:** Still the bitul of the 'something', now reaching past it.

**The edge.** The surrounding is not felt inside you; it holds you from outside. 'In truth he cannot grasp a divine matter with no garment at all.' Every picture here must break.

**The counterfeit.** A cozy 'everything is surrounded by light' in general; or picturing God and the world as a soul in a body, which the Tanya says fails at the root (claim A03).

**The practice.** Sitting L04, Held from inside and all around. *Act:* Say the verse ('I have set God before me always') right before the thing you fear today.

**Whose.** Stated: the pair and its images (Tanya), length (Gate of Unity), filling to surrounding at the neshamah (Kuntres HaAvodah). Ours: making it a separate step.

<a id="step-5"></a> ### Step 5 · Two knowings, and the hiding is His *Rungs one and two, held together · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · claims A02, A12 · L03 + L05*

**What is true here.** From His side, 'before Him', the world is nothing; from our side the created thing really is a separate thing. Both are true, and neither erases the other. The sense that the world runs by itself is His own strongest power, the power to hide, and 'essence does not hide essence'. Once you know He hides there, it is no longer a hiding.

**The move of the mind.** Hold two views at once without collapsing them. Sefer HaArachim's picture: in prayer, the heart above and the eyes below. The mind does not merge them; it keeps both open.

**From inside.** The cover is seen as a cover. The world becomes more solid and more His at the same time, and the question 'then why can't I see it?' gets quieter.

**Which part of you.** The mind holding both. The sources do not assign this step to one level of the soul; placing it between ruach and neshamah is ours. **Bitul:** The higher bitul held as acknowledgment. Kuntres HaAvodah: only lofty souls truly grasp 'all before Him is as nothing', yet every Jew must have this bitul, at least as acknowledgment.

**The edge.** The mind cannot make the two knowings one. Where they meet is above it (step 11).

**The counterfeit.** Korach's error: 'it is all one, so the lines melt'. Or using 'He is hiding' to explain someone's pain; never.

**The practice.** Sittings L03, Two ways of knowing, and L05, The hiding is Him. *Act:* The bedtime Shema with the two thoughts; a blessing said slowly over the thing that seemed emptiest of Him today.

**Whose.** Stated: both knowings (Tanya, Iggeret HaKodesh 20), the hiding as His power (Tanya; Samach Vav), heart above and eyes below (Sefer HaArachim), acknowledgment for everyone (Kuntres HaAvodah). Ours: joining L03 and L05 as one step.

<a id="step-6"></a> ### Step 6 · It is so: binding the mind until the heart answers *The hinge: from understanding to 'it is so' · F12 Knowing the unity · L07*

**What is true here.** 'Know today and return it to your heart.' Knowing here is not having an opinion. It is binding the mind to a truth 'with a strong, very firm bond', as firmly as to a thing you see with your eyes. The Rebbe: to recognize, not only understand. This is the step at which knowing the unity and meditating on it are visibly one act.

**The move of the mind.** Da'at: fasten the thought and do not let it wander. When it drifts, come back without arguing with the stray thought. Say one sentence and repeat it only while it widens. Stay long: the Rashab asks for an hour or more on one idea.

**From inside.** 'I knew this before too, but I only knew it.' Then it is so. The heart answers the way water gives back a face; often only a feather trembles. The Rashab: da'at is feeling. A person can understand a thing well and still not feel it.

**Which part of you.** Neshamah: the mind reaching the heart. The Rashab: the garments of the idea become a passage, and what is felt is the Godly light. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut's fourth level, felt heart arousal, belongs here. **Bitul:** Not yet a kind of bitul, but the ground for it: the one who understands begins to matter less than what is understood.

**The edge.** Where grasp ends, faith carries on (the Tanya: 'only to believe, with a faith above the mind'). Strashelye puts the whole ladder in two sentences: first the unity made through da'at, 'know today'; then the main bitul, above da'at.

**The counterfeit.** Forcing a feeling (without binding, the Tanya says, only empty imaginings). Collecting insights instead of binding one. Knowledge kept as a possession: without prayer, the Rashab warns, even Chassidus learned turns to poison.

**The practice.** Sitting L07, Know it today; and Part I, Stage 6 (binding) inside every sitting. *Act:* Set Him before you at your one fixed moment today: just the line, once.

**Whose.** Stated: da'at as binding (Tanya 3, 42), da'at as feeling (Kuntres HaTefillah), recognize not only understand (the Rebbe), the two stages (Strashelye). Ours: placing the hinge after the rungs of the world and before the rungs above the mind (the manual's order).

<a id="step-7"></a> ### Step 7 · Before Him, nothing: not a thing on my own (rung two) *Rung two: the higher unity (echad), from my side · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F08 Bitul · claims A11 · L06*

**What is true here.** 'Before Him all counts as nothing.' The creature is like a ray still inside the sun: fully light, with no existence of its own. And the root of all separation is to be reckoned 'a thing in itself'. Everyone should reach for a little of this, now and then, even by imagining; without it the lower rung is not true.

**The move of the mind.** Depth: after breadth and length, the idea's very point 'as it is', in the fewest words, then stillness. This is Chochmah, seeing rather than hearing: what one sees leaves no room for doubt. The Piaseczner's daily line: everything is God except me; only I, by my private will, made myself a thing apart.

**From inside.** Less full of yourself. Not crushed, only less in the way. 'Not delight and not joy, only the self nullified.' Still, like a silent stone. The Rashab hears the difference: the Shema is fire and noise, the Amidah is a still small voice.

**Which part of you.** Neshamah at its inward edge. Kuntres HaAvodah: the neshamah grasps the matter stripped of its garments, and the sign is that the natural senses fall away; in the Amidah it is bitul in existence. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut's fifth level, kavanah, whose content is 'all before Him is as nothing', where love and awe 'are not separate from the contemplation at all'. **Bitul:** Bitul in existence (bitul bimetzius), the ray inside the sun, against the bitul of the 'something' at rung one. Sefer HaArachim: the true bitul of the higher unity is given from above, and comes of itself; the lower comes through contemplation.

**The edge.** Bitul reached by thinking fades when the thinking stops, and the self comes back. The real kind is silent. And understanding still leaves someone who understands his own bitul.

**The counterfeit.** Pride in humility (bitul sought as a prize turns into its opposite). Self-hatred dressed as humility. Never on a low day.

**The practice.** Sitting L06, Not a thing on my own (L03's higher knowing belongs here too). *Act:* One mitzvah or one kindness that no one will ever know about.

**Whose.** Stated: the rung (Tanya 20, Shaar HaYichud 10), depth (Gate of Unity), seeing (Sefer HaArachim), neshamah and bitul in existence (Kuntres HaAvodah), kavanah (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut), the fading and the silence (Samach Vav). Disputed: whether contemplation reaches this rung at all (Sefer HaArachim: it is given).

<a id="step-8"></a> ### Step 8 · Weeping and joy in one heart *Above the mind: opposites held in Him · F09 The unity of opposites · claims A05, A12 · L08*

**What is true here.** What cannot meet in you meets in Him: 'the perfection of His Essence bears all opposites'. The test the Mitteler Rebbe sets for real contemplation of the unity is itself a pair: as much joy, exactly that much bitul.

**The move of the mind.** The mind cannot join them. It holds both awake and says both. This is the first rung you cannot think your way through; you can only keep both true.

**From inside.** Weeping lodged on one side of the heart, joy on the other. The bitter cry at your distance and the joy at His nearness are 'one' arousal, only one comes from the far side. Felt, and yet you are not felt in it: 'two opposites in one subject', like a song.

**Which part of you.** The sources do not give this a soul-level of its own. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut sets the cry and the joy 'at that very level' of the Godly soul; placing the step between neshamah and chayah is ours. **Bitul:** Joy and bitul in equal measure (Gate of Unity). Imrei Binah and the Piaseczner at the very top leave only the quiet bitul; see the disagreements.

**The edge.** It cannot be thought through, only held. The joining is His.

**The counterfeit.** 'It's all one, so nothing is really wrong' (Korach again). Or numbing both feelings and calling it peace.

**The practice.** Sitting L08, What can't meet in me meets in Him. *Act:* Pour out both to Him in your own words, in any language you speak, for a few minutes today.

**Whose.** Stated: opposites in His Essence (Kuntres HaTefillah), the test (Gate of Unity), weeping and joy as one (the Zohar in Tanya 34; Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). Ours: its place on the ladder.

<a id="step-9"></a> ### Step 9 · Alone: where the mind stops (rung three) *Rung three: yachid · F05 The Essence beyond both · claims A04, A06 · L09*

**What is true here.** Before Him as He is, He is yachid, 'truly alone', above echad, which still means parts joined. Before the Essence as it is in itself, nothing was made at all. And even the word 'yachid' is said of Him only from our side.

**The move of the mind.** The mind's last moves, in the Rashab's order: grasp by negation (He is not this, not that), then the knowing of wonder, then recognition, then, if it comes, seeing. Kuntres HaAvodah gives the same route. The Shema's 'Echad', with the dalet held long. Picture: the 'one' that is not part of any number.

**From inside.** A hush. Nothing to hold, and no one holding. The Piaseczner: he did not know what his soul saw, or even whether it saw.

**Which part of you.** Chayah. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut's chayah: the point itself is aroused, 'in the very Godly light itself, not from grasping it'. The Rashab's 'nothing apart from You' (efes) stands above the Tanya's 'none besides You' (ayin). **Bitul:** 'Nothing apart from You' (efes): not the ray going out from the sun, but no existence even beside Him.

**The edge.** No grasp reaches the Essence, 'not even grasp by negation, the highest grasp there is'. All contemplation, even 'all before Him is as nothing', sees Him 'from behind'; its use is the thirst it leaves. Do not reach for the Essence with the mind; the main service now is lower down.

**The counterfeit.** Thinking you have reached the top. A cold detachment ('the world doesn't count'). A spacey, unreal feeling: stop, and do something plain with your hands.

**The practice.** Sitting L09, One, and alone. *Act:* Right after the sitting, wash one dish slowly, as the world held and not pushed aside.

**Whose.** Stated: yachid above echad (Gate of Unity, Sefer HaArachim, Imrei Binah), only from our side (Strashelye), the route and its limit (Samach Vav, Kuntres HaAvodah), chayah's arousal not from grasping (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). Ours: pairing chayah with this rung.

<a id="step-10"></a> ### Step 10 · The heart's simple will: where you and He meet *The bottom of the self, at the Essence · F06 The soul as part of the One · F05 The Essence beyond both · L11*

**What is true here.** The yechidah, the single one at the bottom of the soul, is always one with His Essence; it is 'truly the power of the Essence in the soul'. Cleaving is not produced: it is the soul's own life and bond with the Life of life. The point of the heart above knowing, which is never forgotten, lives here: 'God alone is truly your whole life'. This is the owner's thesis at its root: finding your true self is finding Him.

**The move of the mind.** The mind stops. What grasps the Essence now is not thought but re'uta deliba, the heart's simple will: 'by the heart's will the Essence is grasped'. It is an essential bond, like a son's with his father. The mind's part was to make the thirst. What remains is simple: a plain prayer, the Shema said as a leap, a niggun, what you will not give up.

**From inside.** The song that sings itself: it comes of itself, without your knowing. Clearly felt, and you are not there to watch yourself feel it: 'the deeper, the less he feels it'. Sometimes a sudden lifting. For the yechidah, the Rebbe says, that the worlds are nothing is not news; it is simple.

**Which part of you.** Yechidah; will and delight, the most inward of the soul's powers. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut: 'one will in which all wills are included', where the unlearned and the scholars are exactly equal. Kuntres HaAvodah: the soul's natural will is always one with the Essence. Its plainest sign is the martyr's: he may fail in much, but will not be separated from the One. **Bitul:** Essential bitul: silent, not felt as mine, because it is 'not an existence at all'. The later Rebbeim say this, not love or excitement, is what reaches Him as He is.

**The edge.** There is nothing further up to climb. What is beyond is not seen but held, by will. Do not examine it while it happens.

**The counterfeit.** 'I am one with God' turning into grandness or a mission. Watching yourself for signs of the yechidah. Making the arousal the main thing, which the Mitteler Rebbe calls 'serving oneself'; its sign in the Gate of Unity is a fervour in prayer that feels itself and flares into anger when its honour is touched.

**The practice.** Sitting L11, Where you and He meet. *Act:* One act of simple faith: a short prayer in plain words, or a kindness done simply, without explaining it to yourself.

**Whose.** Stated: the yechidah one with the Essence (the Rebbe; Imrei Binah; Kuntres HaAvodah), re'uta deliba above knowing (Samach Vav), the one will and the rule of depth (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). Ours: making this the summit and putting 'the plainest thing' after it (the manual puts L10 before L11).

<a id="step-11"></a> ### Step 11 · The plainest thing: the world held, one with Him (the turn) *Echad above yachid: the world's existence one with Him · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F07 The home below · claims A07, A08, A09 · L10*

**What is true here.** The top of the ladder is not the world erased. The truest unity is that the world's own existence is one with Him, which only the Essence, bearing opposites, can do. The Shema says 'echad', not 'yachid', on purpose. The Rashab: the union of the higher and lower unity is above the higher alone; things exist, only their existence is Godliness; and 'there is none else' is found precisely in the physical 'something'.

**The move of the mind.** Nothing to add. The mind comes back from the top and sees the plain thing as plain and His. Imrei Binah: contemplating how heaven and earth come from nothing is as if one contemplated the very Essence; between yachid and echad there is 'no difference at all'.

**From inside.** Plain. The bread is bread, and it is His. The Rashab warns it is harder than rapture: it is much easier to long to expire than to live below keeping the Torah.

**Which part of you.** The yechidah coming into the body. The Mitteler Rebbe: a simple man's fleshly joy reaches the essential delight in the yechidah. **Bitul:** Bitul that shows inside the 'something' (Strashelye), where the lower stays lower and the higher bitul shines in it (Sefer HaArachim).

**The edge.** This is where the ascent turns. The mind does not hold this; the hand does (steps 12-14).

**The counterfeit.** 'The bread is glowing' (a new high, not the Essence). 'Everything is holy, so I can indulge.'

**The practice.** Sitting L10, The plainest thing. *Act:* Eat one thing slowly, with its blessing or your thank-you.

**Whose.** Stated: the world's existence one with Him (Sefer HaArachim), the union above the higher (Samach Vav), the physical from the Essence (the Alter Rebbe via the Rayatz; Tanya IH 20). Ours: calling it the turn from ascent to descent.

<a id="step-12"></a> ### Step 12 · Coming down: the dark place turned *Coming down: below even the lower unity · F11 Darkness turned to light · L12*

**What is true here.** The unity is shown most fully not by escaping the dark but by turning it: the other side subdued, darkness turned to light. True teshuvah makes good out of the evil itself. The revealed yechidah turns the animal soul 'from opposite to opposite'.

**The move of the mind.** Do not argue with the pull or answer it; act as if you did not hear it, and add strength to what you are doing. Tell Him the small 'no' in your own words.

**From inside.** Sharp, and afterward lighter. A broken heart that becomes a vessel: 'then he becomes a vessel for God's light to dwell within him'.

**Which part of you.** The animal soul itself. Kuntres HaAvodah leaves open whether it ever wants Godliness by its own nature. **Bitul:** Subduing (iskafya), then turning (is'hapcha).

**The edge.** The animal soul by its nature does not want Godliness; all its rising is the Godly soul's work on it (the Rashab marks this 'needs study').

**The counterfeit.** 'Since darkness turns to light, let me go into the dark' (that is the pull talking). Pride in the win. A crushing self-inspection on a low day.

**The practice.** Sitting L12, Darkness turned to light. *Act:* One small 'no' today, told to Him in your own words.

**Whose.** Stated throughout (Tanya 33, 28; Imrei Binah; Kuntres HaAvodah; Kuntres HaTefillah).

<a id="step-13"></a> ### Step 13 · Coming down: the home below, this ordinary day *The purpose: the lowest place · F07 The home below · claims A01, A07 · L13*

<a id="step-14"></a> ### Step 14 · Coming down: the deed in the hand *The unity made actual · F10 Torah and mitzvah · L14*

**What is true here.** The will in the mitzvah has no reason, so it holds no root of limit, and only it brings the Essence itself into place and time. Take part of it and you hold all of it. Its purpose is itself. Holding the etrog as the law says is holding the life in it, one with the Infinite; it is like embracing the king.

**The move of the mind.** The line, done with the hand, slowly. The Mitteler Rebbe: bowing the body in the Amidah is higher than the inner bitul of mind and heart. The Piaseczner: when a good stirring comes, clothe it at once in a mitzvah or in learning.

**From inside.** Afterward, not during: a little quieter, a little less full of yourself. An insult that doesn't sting, 'because he has nothing of his own'. Sometimes: 'I find myself another man.' Awe and love are added.

**Which part of you.** The nefesh in the body again: the lowest power of the soul doing the highest thing. The end holds the beginning. **Bitul:** The servant's yoke: the simple doing that does not carry the work as its own.

**The edge.** None: the circle closes and starts again at step 1, one rung deeper.

**The counterfeit.** Rote doing with the mind elsewhere, called 'the unity made actual'; doing it to be seen; or the reverse, arousal with no deed, 'a flame flying in the air'.

**The practice.** Sitting L14, The unity in your hand; Part I, 'After' (one act, one sentence, the check later). *Act:* The next mitzvah, slowly, with the hand (for anyone: the next kindness, an honest word, a coin given).

## Where the sources place the steps differently (kept, not smoothed)

The ladder above is one arrangement. The sources draw their own ladders, and they do not all agree with it or with each other.

**1. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut: two ladders, not one.** The Mitteler Rebbe gives the natural soul five responses to contemplation: wanting arousal for its own sake (not counted), hearing from afar, good thought joined to deed, felt heart arousal, and kavanah, whose content is "all before Him is as nothing". Then he says all five, "even simple will", are "serving God with the body, not with the soul". The Godly soul has its own five, and they differ in kind: nefesh (the good done by nature, the faith even a thief has), ruach (simple faith; the martyr who will not be separated from the One), neshamah (the heart of flesh sings; the deeper, the less felt), chayah (the point itself, not from grasping it), yechidah (one will including all). Our ladder lays the contemplation-born levels (steps 3-7) and the Godly soul's levels (steps 7-10) on one line. He keeps them as two, and would say the first ladder does not reach the second by climbing. We keep his line and do not merge them in the text; the merging is ours. He also ranks kavanah in the mind above heart arousal (step 7 above step 6), and forbids forbidding arousal while warning against making it "a something".

**2. Kuntres HaAvodah: the nearest thing to this whole map, in one chapter.** The Rashab's first chapter is a stated ladder of soul-levels on rungs of the unity: the nefesh by acknowledgment ("Modeh ani"); the ruach by contemplating the life clothed in the world, which is "know today"; the neshamah by grasping the matter stripped of its garments, its sign that the natural senses fall away; through filling to surrounding, by negation, wonder and the mind's eye seeing; and there the soul's union with the Essence, and in the Amidah bitul in existence. **3. Samach Vav: the heart's will above all knowing, and contemplation as thirst.** The Rashab puts the whole order of prayer, the Shema's "all before Him is as nothing" included, in the "outer heart": it comes from grasping and reaches only "existence". The Essence is grasped by re'uta deliba, the inner heart. So in his map the ladder of knowing does not reach the top; its use is the thirst it leaves ("from behind"). He also gives the mind a route (negation, wonder, recognition, seeing) and says certainty may come "suddenly, from above", only after that toil. And he puts the union of the higher and lower unity above the higher alone. Step 10 and step 11 follow him; step 9's "from behind" is his.

He puts bitul "like a silent stone" above love, and the body's bow above the bitul of mind and heart. Our ladder puts yachid (step 9) below the world held (step 11), which follows him and Sefer HaArachim; but his ranking of yachid above echad stands on the same page.

**5. Sefer HaArachim: the rungs are named, and the higher one is given, not made.** Its ladder is va'ed, echad, yachid, with four levels of the world's nothingness (not a true existence; not an existence for itself; of no account before Him; not at all before the Essence) and the truest unity above them: the world's existence one with Him. It also says the higher bitul "comes of itself" and is "given from above", and that bitul a person makes on his own is still "something". That cuts against the premise that each rung is reached by its matching contemplation. On its view contemplation reaches rung one and prepares for rung two; rung two is a gift. We keep that on step 7's card. It also has its own fold: va'ed, the lowest word, belongs to yachid, above echad.

**6. The Rebbe: the ladder folds.** In Inyanah the Rebbe says that only the yechidah feels simply that the worlds are nothing; the lower soul-levels live inside worlds and feel it as news they must be reminded of. So the first rung (the world said into being, step 3) is held simply only by the deepest level of the soul (step 10). The order of the rungs and the order of the soul are not the same order. This is why the ladder loops (section 3), and why the same line can be sat with at every depth.

**7. The Piaseczner: the door is feeling, picture and body, and the top comes suddenly.** He agrees on the goal (knowing that reaches the heart; a self that gets out of the way) and differs on the road. Steps 1-2 take his door, and step 10's lifting is his. His ladder is not ours: in his books the self is found in reaching toward God, and the self found wants to give itself back and comes back more itself.

## 3. The descent: the top comes back into the body and the day

The ladder is not finished at the top. The sources are nearly unanimous that the climb is for the sake of the return.

**Why it must come down.** Imrei Binah: the return is the main thing, and "all the rising was only for the sake of coming back down". Samach Vav gives the reason at the root: seeing the essence makes one *return*, "for he is nullified from his existence", and can only make vessels below. It adds that holding the world is harder than leaving it: "it is much easier to long to expire than to live below keeping the Torah and mitzvot".

> <small>**Sources.**
> Imrei Binah, the Shema 50, line 283: «והשוב הוא העיקר וכל ההעלא' דרצוא לא הי' רק בשביל הירידה דשוב» — *the return is the main thing; all the rising was only for the sake of coming back down*<br>
> Samach Vav, Ki Ka'asher HaShamayim 5666, line 1985 · *OCR*: «כי מתבטל ממציאותו» — *(seeing the essence, he returns,) for he is nullified from his existence*<br>
> Samach Vav, Ki Ka'asher HaShamayim 5666, line 2016 · *OCR*: «דבאמת בנקל הרבה יותר להיות בבחינת כלות הנפש מלהיות למטה בקיום התורה ומצות» — *it is much easier to long to expire than to live below keeping the Torah and mitzvot*</small>

**Why it comes down into a deed, and not only into a mood.** Samach Vav's central distinction: the will that made the world has reasons and is the root of place and time; the will in the mitzvah has no reason at all, so only the mitzvah brings the Essence itself into place and time. Take part of it and you hold all of it. The Rebbe: the mitzvot are not for another purpose; their purpose is themselves. The Tanya: in doing a mitzvah a person draws the Infinite Light down into the physical world; holding the etrog as the law says is holding the life in it, one with the Infinite.

> <small>**Sources.**
> Samach Vav, 5666, line 596 · *OCR*: «ואמיתית בחי' פנימיות הרצון הוא שאין טעם לרצון כללא» — *the true inwardness of the will is that there is no reason for the will at all*<br>
> Samach Vav, 5666, line 740 · *OCR*: «דהיינו שגם בבחי' מקום וזמן לא יהי' ההגבלה דמקום וזמן» — *so that even in place and time there is no limit of place and time*<br>
> Samach Vav, Ki Ka'asher HaShamayim 5666, line 2005 · *OCR*: «ולכן המשכת בחי' העצמות הוא במצות מעשיות דוקא, שאינו בגדר כלי» — *so the Essence is drawn down precisely through the practical mitzvot, which are outside the category of a vessel*<br>
> Samach Vav, HaShamayim Kis'i 5668, line 6189 · *OCR*: «שכשאתה תופס במקצת מן העצם אתה תופס בכולו» — *when you grasp part of the essence, you grasp all of it*<br>
> Inyanah 16:1, line 84: «כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת כ"א כל תכליתם הוא – הם עצמם» — *His mitzvot are not for some other purpose; their whole purpose is themselves*<br>
> Tanya, Likkutei Amarim 37, line 518: «כי בעשייתה, ממשיך האדם גילוי אור אין סוף ברוך הוא מלמעלה למטה להתלבש בגשמיות עולם הזה» — *in doing it, a person draws the revelation of the Infinite Light down, to clothe itself in the physical world*<br>
> Tanya, Kuntres Acharon 4, line 1574: «הרי זה תופס ממש חיותו המלובש בו מנוקבא דאצילות» — *he is truly holding its life-force, clothed in it (from Malchut of Atzilut)*</small>

**Why the low place is the high place.** The home is here: "even on the earth below there is nothing besides Him, and this is His home". The earth, lowest of all, has the power of the beginning. The soul was bound to the body "to make everything one". The body's bow reaches past the bitul of mind and heart. And Imrei Binah says the future revelation of the Essence has no vessel at all except "the very body of the physical deed of the mitzvah below".

**The three steps down** (cards 12-14 above) take the same order the sources do: first the hardest place in me (the dark turned), then the ordinary house and day (the home below), then the hand (the deed). Each carries the top down with it: the yechidah revealed is what turns the animal soul (step 12); the bitul that showed at the top has to show inside the solid thing (step 13); the will with no reason is what the hand holds (step 14).

**Without the descent, the ascent goes false.** The Mitteler Rebbe: arousal that does not reach action is "a flame flying in the air". The Gate of Unity: Torah and mitzvot without self-giving unite "only on the outside", so the descent also needs the ascent. The Piaseczner: when a good stirring comes, clothe it at once in a mitzvah or in learning.

**The test lives down here.** Not during, but after: lowliness, and an insult that does not sting "because he has nothing of his own"; awe and love added; "I find myself another man". This is how the whole ladder is checked, and it can only be checked in the day.

**And then back to step 1, one rung deeper.** The Rebbe's fold (section 2, no. 6) is why: the first rung is held simply only at the deepest level. So the loop is not a circle but a spiral. The same line ("being said, right now") is sat with again, from further down.

## 4. The same ladder, walked in miniature

Every conversation and every sitting walks the whole ladder in small: **arrive from what is moving → the true self → the line → the edge → the act.** Nothing in this section is a source's own schema; it is ours, built from the steps above and the manual's shape of a sitting.

## In a sitting

| Miniature | Ladder step | Part I stage | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | 1 | Stages 1-2 | One sentence of where you are; a minute of watching thoughts. |
| The true self | 2 | (the one who sits) | A breath of "who is looking out of my eyes": the one sitting is not the verdict. |
| The line | one of 3-11 | Stage 3 | One rung, chosen by what moved you (table below), said softly. |
| Think it through | the rung's own move | Stages 4-5 | Breadth, length, depth; the picture, and where it breaks. |

| From inside | the rung's own state | Stage 7 | Described, never demanded. |
| The edge | the rung's edge | (where the picture breaks; stillness) | Say where the mind stops at this rung; stay a moment there. |
| Words and lips | 10 in small | Stages 8-9 | Your own words; then a verse or the Shema, "Echad" drawn out. |
| The act | 12-14 | After | One act for today, one sentence to carry, the check later. |

So a three-minute sitting touches steps 1, 2, one rung, 6, its edge, and 14. A forty-day course (manual Part III) walks the rungs one by one. Both are the same ladder.

## Which rung to offer, from what is moving

| What the person brings | The rung that meets it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I don't know who I am"; a verdict about oneself | Step 2 (then 10) | The floor under the layers; the self found at the bottom. |
| Worry about tomorrow; the world feels heavy | Step 3 | Nothing is holding itself up; it is being said now. |
| Fear of a particular thing | Step 4 | This thing, held from inside and all around. |
| "If He's here, why can't I see Him?" | Step 5 | The hiding is His; both knowings true. Never used to explain anyone's pain. |
| "I understand it, but I don't feel it" | Step 6 | Bind; stay; da'at is feeling, and it comes by staying. |
| A self that is too big: pride, needing credit | Step 7 | Not a thing on my own. Never on a low day. |
| Torn between two feelings; grief and gratitude together | Step 8 | Both true in one heart. |
| A longing with no name; "there must be more" | Steps 9-10 | Let the mind reach its edge; what is past it is the heart's simple will. |
| Numb; "nothing lands"; simple faith, no words | Step 10 or 11 | The bond is already there; the plainest thing. |
| A habit or pull that keeps winning | Step 12 | The dark place turned, one small "no". |
| "What's the point? My life is ordinary" | Step 13 | The ordinary day is what it was for. |
| High and floating; "it was amazing" | Step 14 | Bring it into the hand before it becomes a flame in the air. |

## In a conversation

The guide's rules stand over this: answer what the person actually said first; short replies, like a friend who gets it; one step inward per reply, the walk deepening across the conversation, never two replies in the same shape; no questions about feelings or how it is landing (GUIDE-LAYER.md; the owner's calls D18-D25 in the guide's claims). Inside those rules, the ladder runs like this:

1. **Arrive from what is moving.** The first reply takes what they brought, as it is. That is step 1: the stirring is already the door. Don't ask for a better feeling.
2. **The true self.** When it fits, one sentence that separates the person from the verdict ("that's something you carry, not who you are"). Step 2.
3. **The line.** One rung, the one that meets what moved them (the table above), in one plain sentence. Not the teaching around it.
4. **The edge.** Say where that rung stops, lightly, so it isn't taken for the whole ("that's the first rung, not the top"; "past that, it isn't thinking anymore"). The edge is what keeps each reply an opening and not a slogan.
5. **The act.** When the conversation settles, one small thing for today. Step 14 in small.

Across a long conversation the rungs deepen, as the ladder does: the same person may be met at step 3 on Monday and at step 10 a month later. The Rebbe's fold means the guide never has to leave the first line behind; it goes deeper into it.

**A sketch** (ours; not a script). Someone writes: "I've been so anxious about work I can't think straight." Reply one meets them where they stand (step 1) and offers the lowest rung that fits (step 3): the meeting, the inbox, and they themselves are being given being this moment, not holding themselves up. Later, if they say "I know that, but I don't feel it", the reply moves to step 6: stay with one thing, and the knowing turns into feeling by staying. If they come back calmer, the next reply ends in step 14: one plain act before the meeting.

## 5. A one-page diagram (description for drawing)

**Shape.** A tall arch drawn like a ladder bent over the top: the left leg rises (steps 1-9), the top is a short flat span (steps 10-11), the right leg descends (steps 12-14). From step 14 at the bottom right, a curved arrow runs under the arch back to step 1 at the bottom left, labelled "again, one rung deeper". Draw the arrow as the start of a spiral, not a closed circle.

**The rungs.** Fourteen rungs, each a short bar across the ladder with its number and name: 1 What is moving · 2 The true self · 3 Being said now · 4 Held · 5 Two knowings, the hiding · 6 It is so · 7 Before Him, nothing · 8 Weeping and joy · 9 Alone · 10 The heart's simple will · 11 The plainest thing · 12 The dark turned · 13 The home below · 14 The deed.

**Five lanes.** Beside the left leg, five thin vertical lanes run parallel to the ladder, one per column of the map, each with a short label at every rung it touches: 1. *The unity* (nearest the ladder): lower unity (3-4), both knowings (5), higher unity (7), opposites (8), yachid (9), the Essence (10), echad held (11). 2. *The mind*: notice (1), breadth (2-3), length (4), hold both (5), Da'at (6), depth and Chochmah (7), hold both awake (8), negation, wonder, seeing (9), stops (10). 3. *The soul*: nefesh (1-2), ruach (3-4), neshamah (6-7), chayah (9), yechidah (10-11). Mark 5 and 8 with a small open circle: "not assigned by the sources". 4. *Bitul*: none (1), acknowledgment (2, 5), of the something (3-4), in existence (7), equal to joy (8), "nothing apart from You" (9), essential and silent (10), inside the thing (11). 5. *From inside*: scattered (1), a floor (2), "it is so" (3, 6), held (4), less of me (7), both at once (8), hush (9), the song by itself (10), plain (11).

The same five lanes run down the right leg, labelled for the descent: the animal soul (12), the house (13), the hand (14); bitul becomes subduing and turning (12), inside the solid (13), the servant's yoke (14).

**Three horizontal lines across the whole drawing.** - At step 6, a solid line labelled **"the heart"**: below it knowing is in the head; above it, it is felt ("know today and return it to your heart"). - Between steps 9 and 10, a dashed line labelled **"where the mind stops"** (no grasp, not even by negation). Above it, the lanes for mind and feeling go faint; only will and bitul continue. - At the very top, over steps 10-11, a soft band (not a line) labelled **"makif: what surrounds and is above the mind"**, drawn as a wide halo over the whole arch, not a place on it, since the surrounding is "not above in place".

**Colour and weight.** A gradient up the left leg from warm and busy (1) to still and pale (10), and back to warm and solid down the right leg. The deed at step 14 is drawn as heavily as step 1: the lowest and the highest have the same weight.

**Margin notes** (small boxes with thin leader lines): - Beside steps 3-10: "Kuntres HaHitpa'alut keeps two ladders here: contemplation's (natural soul) and the Godly soul's. Merged on this drawing." - Beside step 7: "Sefer HaArachim: this rung is given, not made." - From step 10 to step 3, a thin dotted arc: "the Rebbe: only the yechidah feels this rung simply." - Beside step 11: "the turn: the top is here, in the physical."

**Title and caption.** Title: *One process.* Caption: "Knowing the unity is holding it until it is real. Each rung is known by its own depth of mind, soul and self-giving; at the top the mind stops and the heart's will holds; then it comes down into the hand."

## What is ours, what is theirs, and what is unsure

- **Theirs:** every rung of the unity; the stages of contemplation (breadth, length, depth; negation, wonder, recognition, seeing); the soul-levels as Kuntres HaAvodah and Kuntres HaHitpa'alut give them; the kinds of bitul; the inner states in their images; the counterfeits; the return as the main thing; the mitzvah as the only vessel for the Essence in place and time.
- **Ours:** the single ladder of fourteen steps; putting the self first (as the manual does) and the yechidah before "the plainest thing" (unlike the manual); the soul-levels for steps 5 and 8; merging L03 and L05; the miniature (section 4) and the diagram (section 5).
- **Unsure:** whether the two ladders of Kuntres HaHitpa'alut can be laid on one line at all (he suggests not); whether any contemplation reaches step 7 or only prepares for it (Sefer HaArachim says it is given); where exactly the chayah sits (only Kuntres HaHitpa'alut names it in this context).
