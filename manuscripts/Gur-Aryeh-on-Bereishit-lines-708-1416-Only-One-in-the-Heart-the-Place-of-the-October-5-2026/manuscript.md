This is the second half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Bereishit: Yitzchak and Rivka, Yaakov and Esav, the ladder, Lavan, the return, Yosef and his brothers, the descent to Egypt, and Yaakov's blessings and death. As in the first half, most lines are close reading of Rashi. But the Yaakov lines hold some of the Maharal's deepest words on oneness.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **Only One in the heart.** Yaakov's sons said to him: "כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד כך אין בלבבינו אלא אחד" (865): "as there is only One in your heart, so there is only One in ours." Why? "כי דבר שהוא נבדל הוא אחד מכל צד, ולא יצטרף אליו רבוי כלל רק אחדות" (865): "what is set apart is one from every side; no manyness joins it, only oneness."
- **He is the Place of the world.** "כי הוא יתעלה כולל הכל, ולפיכך נקרא בשם 'מקום' מפני שהוא כולל הכל כמו המקום שהוא כולל מה שבתוכו" (1107): "He includes all, so He is called 'Place', since He includes all as a place holds what is in it." And "ודע לך כי הוא יתעלה משותף לכל הבריות" (1107): "know that He is shared by all creatures."
- **Being is good.** "שהמציאות הוא טוב, וההעדר הוא רע, לכך הנוטה אל הרע נוטה אל ההעדר שהוא רע" (1076): "being is good and absence is bad, so one who leans to the bad leans to absence."
- **The ladder joins the worlds.** "לומר כי העולם התחתון קשור בעליונים, ועולים השמימה כסולם הזה שיש מדריגות בה" (871): "the lower world is tied to the upper, and one rises to heaven like this ladder with its rungs."
- **The one who cleaves does not die.** "Yaakov our father did not die." "כי החיים יש להם דביקות אל השם יתברך ביותר" (1409): "life has the most cleaving to G-d." "ואתם הדבקים בה' אלקיכם חיים כלכם היום" (1409).
- **When good comes, cleave to Him.** When Yaakov saw Yosef alive, he said the Shema. "וזהו מדת החסידים אשר יקרה להם טוב מתדבקים אל" (1324) G-d: "this is the way of the pious: when good comes to them, they cleave to" Him.

## 2.1 Only One in the heart

Line 865 is the heart of this range. Yaakov slept on stones, and the stones became one. Why? "Yaakov is the root of oneness." The Maharal quotes the sons at Yaakov's deathbed: "כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד כך אין בלבבינו אלא אחד" (865). Yaakov is holy and set apart, and "כי דבר שהוא נבדל הוא אחד מכל צד, ולא יצטרף אליו רבוי כלל רק אחדות" (865). What is of the mind is not split: "ואין חלוק ורבוי בשכלי, רק אחדות גמור" (865): "in what is of the mind there is no split or manyness, only full oneness." So "כלל הדבר, כל מה ששם יעקב מצטרף לו" (865): "the rule: whatever Yaakov placed with him" became one. The same idea comes back at 1030: "ובדבר שהוא נבדל מן הגוף אין רבוי וחלוק" (1030): "in what is set apart from the body there is no manyness or split," and "וכל קדוש יש לו מעלה נבדלת מן הגוף" (1030).

The lesson: oneness is not made by adding things together. It comes from being set apart, from the body's side, toward the One. Whoever is inwardly set apart gathers all around him into one.

## 2.2 The Place of the world

"And Yaakov refused to be comforted." Rashi: one is not comforted over the living. The Maharal then turns to the name "Makom," Place. "ודע לך כי הוא יתעלה משותף לכל הבריות" (1107): "He is shared by all creatures," every creature has a part in Him. "כי הוא יתעלה כולל הכל, ולפיכך נקרא בשם 'מקום' מפני שהוא כולל הכל כמו המקום שהוא כולל מה שבתוכו" (1107). "כי הוא מקומו של עולם, והוא כולל את הכל" (1107): "He is the place of the world, and He includes all." The world is in Him, not He in the world.

## 2.3 Being is good

Why do the evil eye, the evil inclination and hatred of people take a person out of the world? "כי השם יתברך שברא את העולם בראו בטוב" (1076): "G-d made the world in good." "וכאשר נוטה אל הרע יוצא מן מציאות עולם" (1076): "when one leans to the bad, he goes out of the world's being." "שהמציאות הוא טוב, וההעדר הוא רע" (1076). And at the end of the book: "כי המציאות וההעדר הם שני דברים שאין להם התיחסות וצירוף" (1409): "being and absence have no relation or joining." Good is being. It has no partner in absence.

## 2.4 The ladder

"לומר כי העולם התחתון קשור בעליונים, ועולים השמימה כסולם הזה שיש מדריגות בה" (871). The Temple stands under the ladder, "והוא החבור בין עליונים ותחתונים" (871): "it is the joining of upper and lower." It belongs to both. (Compare 62 in the first half: man ties the worlds.)

## 2.5 Above time

"כי עצם הויה הוא בלי זמן" (794): "coming-to-be itself is without time." "אבל עצם ההפסד הוא שתולה בגלגל" (794): "but loss itself hangs on the wheel." What comes from Him is outside time. Only loss turns with the wheel.

## 2.6 Yaakov did not die

The Talmud says "Yaakov our father did not die." The Maharal gives three reasons. First, father and son are joined, and being and absence cannot be joined (1409). Since Israel lives, the father lives. Second, the middle has no edge: "ודבר שאין לו קצבה אין לו מיתה" (1409): "a thing that has no limit has no death." Yaakov is the middle between Avraham and Yitzchak, and "כי כל דבר יושר אין לו קצה" (1409): "every straight thing has no edge." Third, cleaving: "כי החיים יש להם דביקות אל השם יתברך ביותר" (1409). What keeps most people from full cleaving "זהו שהחומר הוא המבדיל בין השם יתברך ובין" (1409) the mind: "it is matter that separates between G-d and" the mind. Yaakov was set apart from the body, so nothing separated him.

## 2.7 A world of split, and a power of gathering

"כי אין העולם הזה" (1372) "שהוא עולם החלוק והפירוד" (1372): "this world, which is a world of split and parting," is not fit for full oneness. That is why Israel came from two mothers and why sin still happens. Yet "כי יש כח קבוץ עוד בישראל ואחדות" (1372): "there is still a power of gathering in Israel, and oneness." By that power the scattered return, to join with G-d. Rachel on the road is that power (1372).

- **MH-S46 "Only One in my heart" (865, 1030).** "כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד כך אין בלבבינו אלא אחד" (865). Inside: the heart holds one thing. The many thoughts are still there, but they are gathered under one. How to enter: say the Shema slowly. At "One," ask: what else is in my heart right now? Gather each thing under the One, one at a time, the way the stones under Yaakov's head became one. It differs from S62 Only Him, which sees that only He is real. Here the heart, not the eye, is made one. It pairs with the technique `mh_only_one_in_heart` already in the map.
- **MH-S47 "Held in the Place" (1107).** "כי הוא מקומו של עולם, והוא כולל את הכל" (1107). Inside: I am not in a world that has G-d somewhere in it. I am in Him, as a thing is in its place. And "הוא יתעלה משותף לכל הבריות" (1107): I have a share in Him. How to enter: in a place of fear or loneliness, say the name "HaMakom" and think: this room, this street, is in Him. It differs from S67 Surrounded, which feels Him around me. Here He is the place itself, and I have a share in Him.
- **MH-S48 "Close in weakness" (1360).** "ואין לך דכא כמו חולה, ולכך השכינה עמו תמיד" (1360): "no one is as crushed as the sick, so the Shechina is with him always." Inside: when strength is gone, He is closest, above one's head. How to enter: in illness or collapse, do not reach for strength first. Say: He is here, above me, because I am low. It differs from S59 True lowliness, which is a chosen humility. This is the lowliness that came without choice, and it too draws Him near.

## 4.1 Love says His Name (`mh_love_mentions_him`, new)

"כי העובד מיראה את הקב"ה אינו מזכיר את שמו לעולם" (826): one who serves in awe trembles and never says His Name. But "ולפיכך מי שהוא אוהב את אחד תמיד הוא מזכירו, והוא בפיו, ואין זכרו נשכח ממנו" (826). Practice: through the day, speak to Him by name, in short words: "thank You," "help me," "this is for You." Keep Him in your mouth the way one speaks of someone loved. When you feel awe, let it be silent. Both are true service.

## 4.2 What you want is your prayer (`mh_want_is_prayer`, new)

"כי מי שאינו מתפלל על טובה אינו נותן לו" (913): "one who does not pray for good is not given it." "ועוד כי מה שהוא רוצה" (913): "and more: what he wants" is itself his prayer, as it is said, "He does the will of those who fear Him." Practice: notice what you truly want today. Turn it into a sentence to Him. The wanting is already half the prayer.

## 4.3 Cleave at the good news (`mh_shema_on_good`, new)

When Yaakov saw Yosef alive, he did not fall on his neck. He said the Shema. "וזהו מדת החסידים אשר יקרה להם טוב מתדבקים אל" (1324) G-d. "וזהו קריאת שמע שבו נזכר ייחוד מלכות שמים" (1324). Practice: when good news comes, before telling anyone, stop and say the first verse of the Shema. Let the joy go first to the One.

## 4.4 Lean toward being (`mh_being_is_good`, new)

"שהמציאות הוא טוב, וההעדר הוא רע" (1076). The three that take a person out of the world are the evil eye, the evil inclination and hatred of people. Practice: when you feel envy, hatred or the pull to sin, ask: does this make more of the world, or less? Choose the side of being. Be generous with others' good. Be kind even to animals: "אבל מי שהוא נוהג אכזריות עם הבהמה הוא בודאי משום לב רע ואכזריות שבו" (1076).

## 4.5 Do not suspect (`mh_no_suspicion`, new)

Yosef brought bad reports of his brothers. The Maharal says he erred: "שאין זה ממדת הצדיק לחשוד אותם" (1076): "it is not the way of the righteous to suspect them." And "לפי שכל טוב ראוי להגיד" (1191): "all good is fit to be told," while bad is not spoken. Practice: when you catch yourself building a case against someone close, stop. Say one good thing about them instead.

## 4.6 Hang your trust on Him (`mh_trust_at_the_act`, extended)

Yosef sat two more years in prison "שתלה בטחונו בשר המשקים" (1164): "because he hung his trust on the chief butler." The line ends: "אשרי הגבר אשר שם ה' מבטחו" (1164). Add to the practice: do what is needed, ask people for help, but do not rest the heart on them. Say: I did my part. My trust is in Him.

## 4.7 Anything can change (`mh_anything_can_change`, new)

"כי כל דבר אפשר לשנות על ידי בקשה ותחנונים מאת השם יתברך המרחם על הכל" (1336): "anything can be changed by asking and pleading with G-d, who has mercy on all." Yaakov told his sons: "מעתה אינכם חסרים רק תפילה" (1253): "from now you lack only prayer." Practice: when a thing looks fixed and final, do everything you can, and then say: now I lack only prayer. Then pray.

## 4.8 Kindness of truth (`mh_kindness_of_truth`, new)

Kindness to the dead is "שהוא חסד גמור שאינו מצפה לתשלום גמול, והוא חסד של אמת" (1355): "full kindness that expects no repayment, and it is kindness of truth." Practice: once a week, do a kindness no one will repay or know about: visit a grave, say Kaddish or learn for someone who died, help someone who cannot help you back.

## 4.9 See the sin first, then accept (`mh_see_then_accept`, new)

"כי תחלה מראה לו שחטא ומביא עליו המכה" (1079), "כדי שידע למה הוא מכה אותו ויקבל הדין" (1079). He shows the sin first so the person knows why and can accept. Practice (for oneself only, never to judge another): when something hard happens, ask quietly if there is something to fix. If one thing comes to mind, fix it. If nothing does, let it go. Do not invent sins: the Maharal warns that we may not make up sins for the righteous (1006).

## 4.10 Keep the act in order (`mh_ordered_act`, new)

Yosef told his brothers not to quarrel on the road. "שכל פעל שאינו מסודר הוא הרגזה והרעשה" (1310): "every act that is not in order is agitation and noise." Practice: before a trip or a hard task, slow down. Walk, do not rush. Order brings calm, and calm is safer.

## 5. The big human questions

**Where is G-d?** He is the Place of the world. The world is in Him, and every creature has a share in Him (1107).

**How do I make my scattered heart one?** Be set apart from the body's pull, toward the One. What is set apart is one from every side (865). Say the Shema and gather each thought under the One.

**Why is doing bad so bad?** Because it leans to absence. The world was made in good, and being is good (1076).

**What happens when I die?** The one who cleaves to G-d is alive (1409). Matter is what separates; the soul is gathered when the body falls away (760). Life that cleaves has no edge (1409).

**Is G-d near when I am sick and weak?** Nearest of all. The Shechina is above the head of the sick (1360).

**Can things change?** Yes. Anything can be changed by pleading with Him (1336). A promise can change by our sin, so the righteous stay humble (988), but prayer can change a decree.

**Should I trust people?** Ask them, but rest your trust on Him (1164).

**Is it all right to say G-d's Name often?** One in awe may be silent. One who loves always mentions Him (826). Both are good.

**Why is the world so broken?** It is a world of split and parting (1372). But a power of gathering is still in it (1372).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Makom and "He is the place of the world"** (1107) is close to Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 7: He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place; space itself is made by Him and is null before Him. The Maharal adds "He is shared by all creatures," every creature has a share in Him (1107), which is close to Tanya's teaching that the life of every creature is His word within it (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-2).
- **Tzimtzum.** Not named here. But "matter is what separates between G-d and" the mind (1409) and "a world of split and parting" (1372) are the Maharal's words for the hiding. Chabad says the hiding is only from our side (Tanya ch. 48). The Maharal says the one set apart from the body has no separation (1409). Both agree the separation is not in Him.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "What is set apart is one from every side; no manyness joins it" (865) and "in what is of the mind there is no split" (865) use the Rambam's philosophy of the separate intellects, where the separate is simple. The Maharal takes the Rambam's idea and turns it into a path: a person can become set apart and so become one. Chabad (Tanya ch. 2, 18) says the soul is "a part of G-d above," one with Him at the root. The Maharal's Yaakov reaches it by being set apart. Tanya says it is already there and needs to be revealed.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "He includes all" as a place holds what is in it (1107) is the first half. A place is not the thing in it: that is the second half. Here the Maharal gives one image for both halves.
- **Being is good** (1076) is close to the Chabad teaching that the true being of all is G-d's goodness and that evil has no root of its own (Tanya ch. 6-7 on kelipah as having no true being). The Maharal says bad is absence. Tanya says kelipah gets its life from holiness in exile. The two meet: bad has no being of its own.
- **Love mentions Him** (826) fits Tanya ch. 41-44 on love and awe, where awe is the lower and love the higher, but both are needed.
- **Yaakov did not die** (1409): the Tanya (Iggeret HaKodesh 27-28) says the tzaddik's life is faith, awe and love, and after passing his light is even closer to his followers. Both say life is cleaving, not the body.
