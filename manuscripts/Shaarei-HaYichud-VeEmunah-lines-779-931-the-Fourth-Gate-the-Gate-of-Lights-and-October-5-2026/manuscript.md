## The shape of the range

The gate is a single long argument about one question. How can the infinite show itself in something finite without either one cancelling the other? Its tools are the Lurianic terms: kav (the line), igul (the circle), reshimu (the trace), Adam Kadmon, shevirah and tikkun (breaking and repair), parsa (the screen), and heichalot (palaces). But the author keeps turning every term back to one claim. All the grades, screens and differences exist only "toward us". Toward Him, "all is one":

> נמצא ההבדל אינו כי אם לגבי דידן (line 813)

That is why *stated* dominates. A kabbalistic mapping here is almost never left bare. It is closed with a sentence that joins it back to the Essence. The faces also run in a different order from any Tanya range. F05 (the Essence beyond both) leads, because the gate's refrain is that even the hiding, even the vessels, even the power of limit are His Essence. F03 (hiding for revelation) comes next, and F07 (the home below) is third. The home below is strong here: the whole purpose of lights and vessels is that His unity be revealed "precisely" in the lowest yesh. F06 (the soul as part of God) almost vanishes, since this gate is about God and the worlds, not about the soul. The one exception is line 898, where the soul goes down into the body in order to rise.

The gate has three movements. Chapters 1-15 (779-858) set out the structure: two kinds of light, the kav, Adam Kadmon, Atzilut and BYA, and the three "measures". Chapters 16-26 (859-913) turn to service, exile and redemption: what the lower ones add, how, and why it is still hidden. Chapters 27-29 (914-931) bring the two threads together and end with a warning against misreading the Etz Chaim.

## Ch. 1 (779-782): why there are vessels at all

The gate opens with the problem in its sharpest form (line 781, F03 + F05). His Essence "is not in the category of revelation at all", and "there is nothing besides Him, so to whom would He be revealed?" The vessels are the answer:

> אשר על כן היה אצילות הכלים בכדי להסתיר ולהעלים האור בכדי שיתגלה כח הגבול והיש ולייחדם במקורם (line 781)

So hiding is made so that limit and yesh can appear *and then be united in their source*. In the same line, whether the unity shows depends on Israel: "when Israel unite below with self-sacrifice" (F08 + F10). Line 782 then states the gate's strongest F05 claim in its first paragraph. Even the power that contracts is not something other than Him:

> כי אפילו כח הצמצום הוא עצמותו ברוך הוא (line 782)

It follows that there is no change in Him. How He is in every detail and yet unchanged "is beyond a person's power to grasp" (line 782, F12).

## Ch. 2-4 (783-800): two lights, the kav, and the teacher

There are two kinds of light. The light of the essence is the life of each thing, the same everywhere and not revealed. The "ziv and haarah" (radiance) is revealed and differs from place to place (lines 783-786). The soul is the parable: its life is equal from brain to toenail, while its radiance differs in eye, heart and mind. The first light is the kav, and it carries F01 in every chapter of the gate:

> ואלו היה הסתלקות החיות אפילו רגע חס ושלום היה העולם בטל לגמרי והיה כלא היה (line 785)

The kav is "the thinnest of the thin", yet "this extension is from His Essence" (line 785). Chapter 3 places the tzimtzum exactly. It was in the light (the extension), and not in the Essence:

> לכן הצמצום היה בהאור אבל לא חס ושלום בעצמותו (line 787)

The "empty space" is empty only of other extensions, and "the place itself that bears all extensions is His Essence" (line 787). Line 790 gives the same point for sovev (F04). The Essence never left even the kav; it is only not revealed.

Chapter 4 divides radiance into Atzilut and BYA. In Atzilut, by "His power of wonder", He is one with the sefirot (line 794). In BYA "the vessels appear as separate beings, as complete yesh" (line 794). The teacher parable (lines 795-800) carries the chapter. A teacher contracts his wisdom into a parable. From his own side the student's wisdom is joined to his own, while from the student's side the teacher's wisdom is foreign. Line 800 marks where the parable fails, which is the grammar rule G06. A teacher feels the student's wisdom as another thing, but with Him there is no grasping, no feeling and no separate being:

> מה שאין לתאר כן אצלו יתברך כביכול שום תפיסה והדגשה גם איזה מהות נבדל חס ושלום (line 800)

## Ch. 5-7 (801-816): Adam Kadmon, breaking and repair, and "only toward us"

When His simple will rose "to reveal His perfection in all details, even in vessels and yesh" (line 801), the kav held all levels "in complete equality". That equality is Adam Kadmon (lines 802-803). The goal of the whole emanation is stated as F09:

> שיתגלו למהותים נבדלים ואף על פי כן יהיו מיוחדים (line 804)

Breaking is the showing of the parts as separate. Repair is their joining (line 805). The long gloss at line 806 reads AB and SaG, and the "new MaH", as the joining of the limited with the unlimited. It rests on the claim that "head and end are equal to Him". Line 807 names the mechanism. Keter is "wonder", the power "to join two opposites in one subject", as soul and body are joined:

> כח הפלא לחבר שני הפכים בנושא אחד (line 807)

Chapter 7 grades BYA by screens: one screen between Atzilut and Beriah, two before Yetzirah and three before Asiyah. Each lower world is "a likeness" of the one above, and Malchut gives the likeness (lines 809-812). Then line 813 turns the whole ladder around:

> ואצלו יתברך הן הן הכלים דאצילות והן הן הכלים דעשיה בלי הבדל כלל (line 813)

This is why there are ten sefirot and not forty (line 814). Line 815 adds that even the hiding is His: נמצא עצמותו ברוך הוא הוא דייקא אפילו בבחינת הסתר (line 815). Line 816 closes on F12. No creature can grasp how such opposites hold together, "wondrous are Your works".

## Ch. 8-12 (817-844): three measures, and the Essence that bears them

The summary (lines 817-821) gives three measures of the worlds' life. First is the hidden life, the kav. Second is His revelation in the vessels of Atzilut, "and even so He unites with them" (line 818). Third is Malchut of Atzilut, where He is revealed "in actual fact in every single creature". That is "last in deed, first in thought" (line 821, F07). Over all three stands the Essence alone (line 822):

> והרי הדבר עצמו וכל הנמצא הכל הוא כחו לבדו (line 822)

Chapter 9's parable of a man, who is the one who thinks, speaks and acts and bears all of them "in one subject", defines sovev as not a light at all. It is the self that bears everything (line 824, "remember this"). Then comes a strong F07 in F05 terms:

> ואדרבה דייקא כח היש שאין למטה הימנו אשר לבחינת כח היש אנו מעריכים כל ההמשכות הוא דייקא מכח עצמותו ברוך הוא (line 826)

The root of the physical vessels "is reckoned precisely with His Essence" (line 826). Line 827 marks where the parable fails: a man feels his limbs and their pain, but the worlds do not register on Him. Line 828 states F02 outright: מצד העצם הכל הוא עצמותו ברוך הוא ואין עוד מלבדו (line 828). Chapters 10-12 place Adam Kadmon as the "primordial thought". Its "vessels", "breaths", "forehead" and "eyes" are said by borrowing, "to soothe the ear" (lines 829-841). Its inner side "may not be spoken of at all" (line 829). The eyes stand for providence gazing at what will be born. The vessels were the opposite of the light, and so they broke (line 840). The forehead, His will, joins them "though they are truly two opposites" (line 841). Line 843 then concludes:

> ולכן הוא עתיקא הוא זעיר אנפין בלי שינוי כלל כי אם מסטרא דילן שניא (line 843)

## Ch. 13-15 (845-858): why the vessels outrank the light, and the limit on inquiry

Chapter 14 explains the Etz Chaim's claim that the vessels are higher than the light. The vessels come from His Essence, since only the Essence's power can hide so as to make full limit:

> כל יכול להסתיר לעשות כלים בבחינת גבול גמור ויש (line 850)

The wiser a man is, the more he can hide in parables. So "the vessels are from His Essence" while the light is only His radiance (line 852). Yet in revelation the light is greater, since it shows and the vessel hides. In life both are His extension, and without it "the vessels with the light would be as if they never were" (line 852). The father who lowers himself to his son is the parable for why tzimtzum is love of revelation (line 853).

Chapter 15 sets the limit on inquiry. Tzimtzumim are described "lest they understand the tzimtzumim literally" (line 857). They are reckoned only "for the sake of the unity":

> כי אם מצד העולמות בשביל היחוד מוכרחים אנו להעריך אלו הצמצומים (line 857)
> > אבל מרצון המחשבה קדומה ולמעלה אין לנו עסק בזה כלל (line 857)

Line 858 gives the purpose of all this distinguishing: "to raise all worlds to Ein Sof through understanding", and to "understand the negation". That is F12 as a method: one knows the unity by tracing where each attribute comes from and then denying it of the Essence.

## Ch. 16-17 (859-869): service unites; and He is touched, and not touched

Chapter 16 turns to service. Malchut, the Shechinah, is revealed "so that His unity be revealed even in the lower worlds" (line 859). Then line 860 defines all service as that union:

> כי כל עיקר העבודה הוא לייחד קודשא בריך הוא ושכינתיה (line 860)
> > כי אם להבין על ידי הכלים האלו יחודו יתברך אשר אין עוד מלבדו (line 860)

It is done by nullifying one's own yesh through Shema and prayer, and by drawing His will through Torah and mitzvot (F08 + F10). That rouses His will, His Essence, and by that wonder lights join vessels. The lower ones add something. Union comes into the vessels "from His Essence", and "this revelation is from the side of the creatures" (line 863).

Chapter 17 is the boldest passage in the range. Addition and loss do not touch His extension, but they do touch His *intent*:

> כשהתחתונים מטיבים מעשיהם נותנין כביכול עוז וכח לבחינת המשכתו זה בבחינת כלים (line 864)

"Yishmael my son, bless Me"; "give strength to God"; and on the other side, "you weakened the Rock". Yet "if you are righteous, what do you give Him" also holds (line 865). The verses written with aleph (לא) and read with vav (לו) give both at once, and the author calls them opposites held together:

> ושני בחינות אלו שהם הפכים המה אצלו בנושא אחד מצד כחו השוה (line 866)

Chagigah 5b settles where the weeping is. "In secret places My soul weeps" refers to His Essence beyond extension, where His primordial will is, as it were, not fulfilled. "Strength and joy are in His place" refers to the extension into worlds (lines 867-869). The parable is a king who loses a penny's worth that he wanted for a hidden reason (line 868).

## Ch. 18-19 and the great gloss (870-875): the circle, the palaces, and the four confessions

The kav ends in Atzilut, and it stops before the bottom of the circle (lines 870-871). If it reached the bottom there would be no above and below, and no BYA. The gap is left on purpose. It makes room for "the being of the opposite", in order to subdue and turn it (F11):

> יש מקום להתגלות שיהיה נגלה מהות ההיפוך אשר לזה היה הכוונה בכדי לאכפייא ולהפכם (line 871)

Ein Sof "surrounds all worlds and is surrounded by all" (line 873). Line 874 explains the palaces: a house of wood and stone holds up a man's whole life. The gloss then opens "a small door" into the Etz Chaim "so that the power of His unity... be understood". It names service as removing the hiding: ועיקר העבודה להסיר ההסתרה (line 874). It ends on this line:

> וכל ההבדלות הם בערכינו אבל לגביה יתברך אין צמצום מעלים לפניו (line 874)

Line 875 is the heart of the range. It has three parts.

- **The sitra achra (875.5-875.9).** The sitra achra has palaces as well, the reverse of holiness. Its purpose is stated: בכדי לאכפייא לסטרא אחרא ולאהפכא חשוכא לנהורא ומרירו למתיקא. It has no hold in Atzilut. It draws from Zeir Anpin and never from His simple will, where "all is essential good, even from the side of the sitra achra". Reading Jacob and Esau as "true brothers in the power of Atzilut" is marked *reading* (875.8).
- **The four confessions (875.12-875.15).** Yitro gave the yesh of the tzimtzum some reality of its own. Naaman limited the purpose to the earth and to Israel. Rachav granted that "limit and the unlimited are equal to Him", but she left the empty space, because in revelation the tzimtzum seemed to her something added to Him. Moshe alone said "there is no other":

> דהיינו שאין עוד שאפילו בבחינת הצמצומים הכל הוא עצמותו ברוך הוא בבחינת השוואה גמורה (line 875)

- **The work and the future (875.16-875.19).** "The whole intent is that even in revelation there be no other, and this is through Israel's service" (875.16). Now it happens only in secret. "Atzilut no man has ever grasped" (875.18). In the future "His equality will be revealed even in the vessels" (875.19).

## Ch. 20-22 (876-893): the feet of Adam Kadmon, and man made to join Asiyah

Ein Sof is "the life of all and the existence of all" (line 876). All distance is "in our reckoning". From His power "there is no distance at all" (line 882). Adam Kadmon's feet end "on the very ground of Asiyah" in hidden power (line 883). A second gloss (line 884) uses this to settle the Etz Chaim's two endings. It also reads "all is foreseen and freedom is given" through the hidden and the revealed sides. It limits arousal from below: it reaches Atzilut only, and from there it reaches Him "of itself".

Chapter 22 gives the range's clearest statement of F07. The main intent is revelation "in our terms precisely", in the physical vessels of Asiyah:

> שיהיה בשמים ממעל ועל הארץ מתחת אין עוד דייקא בבחינת תחתית העשיה דייקא בבחינתה (line 887)

> ולכן נברא אדם כדי לחבר בחינת עשיה ולייחדם ביחודו יתברך (line 888)

He does it by contemplating "the root of all" until the lower unity matches the higher (line 888). Sinai and the Temple are the proof (lines 891-892). His will was revealed on physical ground and in a voice from between golden cherubim, "before whom yesh and ayin are equal" (line 892).

## Ch. 23-26 (894-913): exile, why the arousal comes from the opposite, and the last exile

In exile the Shechinah still rests on Torah, mitzvot, the ten, and Shema and prayer, where His unity is "felt in body and soul, each according to his measure" (line 894). The four cubits of halacha appear here as they do in Tanya 53, with a stronger claim:

> אשר על ידי התורה ומצות הוא גילוי עצמותו ברוך הוא ממש כי איהו ורצונו חד (line 895)

It is hidden from us, "but certainly in truth it is full revelation" (line 895). Then comes the gate's most original step (lines 897-898). Understanding can rouse only a radiance, since only like rouses like. A soul in the upper Eden and the angels cannot rouse the Essence by their bitul. The arousal that reaches the Essence comes from the opposite:

> אבל עיקר ההתעוררות הוא מצד ההיפוך מצד התגברותו נגד ההיפוך (line 897)

A man sees only the separate yesh and still nullifies himself. "This bitul is not by grasp". It comes from the surrounding light, and so it reaches what is beyond grasp. That is why the soul goes down into the body:

> ועל ידי זה נתעלית הנשמה ליבטל ולהדבק בעצמותו ממש ברוך הוא (line 898)

The father who hides his face to test his son (lines 901-902) gives the reason exile is hidden. The future is described as two opposites held together: "through vessels they will grasp Ein Sof without hiding", and how this can be "is not in the category of grasp" (line 903). The exiles are ordered by what each one sifts. Egypt sifted Binah, "the understanding of His unity and oneness in the worlds" (line 905), against the magicians' understanding of the powers as separate yesh (line 906). Babylon sifted the middot (line 907). The present exile sifts Malchut: bitul inside the yesh itself, "the yoke of Heaven's kingship", "without any understanding or middot" (lines 909-910). This bitul shows more of the Essence than bitul through understanding:

> אבל כשהביטול הוא מצד ההסתרה נגלה יותר בחינת ביטול העצם מצד עצמותו ברוך הוא אשר אין זולתו אפילו בבחינת היש (line 911)

Then "Havayah will be one and His name one in full revelation, and not in the secret of One as now" (line 912).

## Ch. 27-29 (914-931): one union, two blemishes, and the warning

Prayer joins the Holy One (His Essence drawn through the vessels) with the Shechinah (its revelation). Havayah's letters are vessels, "yet the vessels themselves are from His Essence" (line 914). The two unions, Ein Sof with the vessels and the Holy One with the Shechinah, turn out to be one: וכשתעיין בזה הכל הוא בחינת יחוד אחד (line 917). When the lower ones do not arouse, they cause "two blemishes" (line 918). The kav, however, never stops (lines 919, 921-922). The gate then gives its most complete statement of the purpose, in the lowest place and from the lower side:

> כשישראל מבטלים עצמם אליו יתברך ומבטלים העולמות אליו יתברך דייקא מסטרא דתתאין בזה נתגלה שלימותו העצמי (line 924)

The reshimu gives the vessels their high root. It is "the power of limit in His power, equal to Him in complete equality" (line 925). The gate closes by guarding that teaching from misuse. No one should conclude that vessels have "life and existence without the light" (line 931).

## What this book adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The hiding is itself the Essence.** Tanya SH 7 refutes a literal tzimtzum and says He is unchanged. This gate says more. The power that contracts, the screens and Malchut's giving of likeness all come from the Essence, and from a root higher than the light (lines 782, 815, 826, 850, 925). The SPINE (F04 dispute) credits the Tzemach Tzedek with deriving matter from the surrounding light. A close cousin of that claim is stated here in 1820: the root of the physical vessels "is reckoned precisely with His Essence" (line 826).
- **Vessels outrank light.** The Tanya does not discuss the Etz Chaim's rule that the vessels are higher in root than the lights. Here it becomes a statement of F05 and F07 together. The limit is from the Essence, and the Essence shows fully only through it (lines 850-852, 925-931).
- **He is touched, and not touched.** The Tanya's approach rests on "no change" and "all is nullified before Him". This gate adds that the lower ones' deeds "give strength" or "weaken", and that there is weeping in the inner chambers of His will (lines 864-869). It does not drop the Tanya's side. It holds both as "opposites in one subject" (line 866), which is F09 applied to God's relation to service.
- **The four confessions.** The SPINE records Moshe against Rachav only through Magen Avos (F02n). This gate gives a full four-step ladder: Yitro, Naaman, Rachav, Moshe (line 875). Rachav's error is exactly the literal-tzimtzum error, but here it is placed as a grade of faith, not a heresy.
- **The arousal that reaches the Essence comes from the opposite.** Tanya Part I has knowledge brought to the heart as the engine of love and awe. This gate says understanding reaches only a radiance. What reaches the Essence is bitul that is "not by grasp", made against the opposite and inside the yesh (lines 897-898, 910-911). This pushes F11 (itkafia) above F12 as the way to the Essence.
- **Exile as the history of the unity.** Egypt sifted Binah, Babylon the middot, and this exile sifts Malchut (lines 905-913). The Tanya ch. 53 has the Temple and the four cubits, but not a program in which each exile brings out one grade of the unity.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- One line is *none*: 780, the table of contents. Line 779 is marked structural only because it names "the roots of the unity" as its subject. - The parables are *structural*: the soul (784, 823), the teacher (795-797, 927-928), the man with a deep thought (831), the face (834), and the man who is one in speech (915-916). Their applications are *stated*, in the next line, where the text makes the tie itself. - The Lurianic mappings are *structural* where the text adds nothing about the unity: Adam Kadmon's inner side and breaths (803, 829-830, 833-834, 837), the screens between Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah (811-812), Arich (842), and the exile numbers 210, 49, 50 and 70 (907).
- *Reading* is used three times: the teacher's delight when the student grasps, as the lower adding to the higher (798), its reverse (799), and Jacob and Esau as brothers in Atzilut (875.8). - F06 is almost absent (3 lines). The gate does not use "a part of God above". Its person is "the lower ones" and "Israel" in service, not the soul's own nature. Only line 898 (the soul's descent for ascent) and the prophets' refined bodies (892.1) come near. - The text repeats itself. The passage "ועיקר העבודה להסיר ההסתרה ... ועבודה זו היא על ידי קריאת שמע" appears inside the gloss of line 874 and again as lines 889-890. The gloss of line 832 is printed again in brackets in line 833. Both were entered where they stand, and the repetition is noted in the JSONL.

## Disagreements with the Tanya, kept

- **Who reaches Atzilut?** Tanya 39 (Tanya index line 573) makes Atzilut "the abode of the great tzaddikim", who are a chariot. This gate says "Atzilut no man has ever grasped", and that Moshe grasped through the palaces of Beriah (line 875, sentence 18). The two may speak of different things, an abode by bitul against grasping by knowledge. The texts do not reconcile them, and neither does this index.
- **What carries bitul to the Essence: knowing or the yoke?** The Tanya's F12 line (Part I ch. 41-43) and SH 1-7 make contemplation the source of love, awe and bitul. This gate agrees that contemplation is required (lines 858, 888, 910), and it defines its own project as understanding "for the sake of the unity" (line 857). But it says comprehension rouses only a radiance, and that the bitul which reaches the Essence is "not by grasp" (lines 897-898, 911). This is the SPINE's F12 dispute again, now inside one book, and closer to the Maharash's placing of the higher knowledge in faith.
- **Does service change anything above?** The Tanya's "if you are righteous, what do you give Him" stands on one side, and this gate cites it too (line 865). This gate's "give strength to God" and the weeping in the inner chambers (lines 864-869) stand on the other. The author's answer is the aleph/vav reading (line 866). The Tanya range does not make that move, and the difference is left standing.
- **Where the dwelling below is made.** The Tanya names the purpose of the tzimtzumim as the physical body and speech (ch. 49, line 713 there). This gate names it as the revelation of His equality "in the yesh precisely". It adds that this is still "in secret" now and even was in Temple times, "in the secret of One" (lines 896, 912). So the dwelling below is complete only at the end, and until then the gate calls the work in exile "adding lights in Atzilut" (lines 899, 914).
