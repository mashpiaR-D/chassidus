This range goes on through the days of creation, the Garden, the serpent, Cain and Abel and the book of Adam; then comes one of the great set pieces of the Zohar, the **Heichalot** (palaces): seven palaces in the Garden of Eden below for the souls of the righteous (lines 746-801), and seven palaces of holiness through which a prayer rises, spirit into spirit, until "all is one will to Ein Sof" (lines 802-888). The range ends with a short second reading of the days and of Shabbat (889-931). All 321 lines were read in full. Every quote below printed "FOUND near line N" from `find_he.py verify` on the export. A "…" in a quote skips a printed cross-reference or a variant reading in brackets.

## 2. The unity of God

**Light is only in darkness.** «דהא לית נהורא אלא בחשוכא ולית חשוכא אלא בנהורא. ואף על גב דאנון חד אתפרשן בגוונין ועם כל דא אנון חד» (Bereshit 36:10; line 617): "for there is no light except in darkness and no darkness except in light; and though they are one, they are distinguished by colors, and with all this they are one." Deep things come out only from within darkness: «כל אנון טמירין עלאין לא אתגליין אלא מגו ההוא חשוכא» (Bereshit 36:7; line 614): "all the hidden things above are revealed only from within that darkness." Ours: this is the ground under Chassidus's teaching that the hiding is His and that the deepest light comes out of darkness (itkafya, ithapcha; the "yitron ha'or min hachoshech").

**One day.** Rabbi Shimon: the first day goes with all the days, «בגין לאחזאה דלאו בהו פירודא וכלא חד» (Bereshit 71:2; line 894): "to show that there is no separation in them and all is one." And there is «לית יום בלא לילה ולית לילה בלא יום» (Bereshit 71:1; line 893): "no day without night and no night without day."

**The work is always hidden and revealed.** The upper world is hidden, so "Bereshit bara" names no creator; the lower is revealed, so it says "Elohim". «למהוי תדיר עובדא דקודשא בריך הוא סתים וגליא» (Bereshit 58:5; line 775): "so that the work of the Holy One is always hidden and revealed." Ours: a plain, usable form of the teaching that He is in the hiding and the showing at once.

**Separation is the root of death.** The tree of knowledge is death to one who takes it alone: «מאן דנטיל ליה בלחודוי מיית» (Bereshit 43:2; line 693): "whoever takes it by itself dies," because he "separates the plantings." All the other trees may be eaten "in unity" (ביחודא). And when voice is parted from speech, a person goes mute: «דהא קו"ל מדבור לא מתפרשן לעלמין. ומאן דמפריש קול מדבור אתאלם ולא יכיל למללא» (Bereshit 44:12; line 708): "for voice and speech are never separated; whoever separates voice from speech is struck dumb and cannot speak." The Hakdamah's "death is in separation" (line 212) here becomes the story of the Garden.

**The will that cannot be grasped.** At the top of the palaces of prayer, when all the spirits are joined, there is «רעותא דלא אתפס לעלמין» (Bereshit 70:7; line 885): "a will that is never grasped", and then «וכדין כלא רעותא חדא עד אין סוף» (Bereshit 70:7; line 885): "and then all is one will, to Ein Sof." And the thought touches it without knowing: «בר ההוא רעו דמחשבה תפיס ולא ידע מה תפיס» (Bereshit 70:9; line 887): "except that will of thought, which grasps and does not know what it grasps." Ours: this is the Zohar's clearest account of the top of prayer, and the root of Chassidus's "ratzon" above "machshavah" (will above intellect) and of the Rebbe Rashab's "will without reason".

**One spirit.** In each palace spirit is joined to spirit, light to light, «למהוי כלהו רוחא חדא כמא דאת אמר … ורוח אחד לכל» (Bereshit 64:10; line 814): "so that all be one spirit, as it says: and one spirit for all."

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **Arousal from below.** «אתערותא שרי מתתא ולבתר אשתלים כלא» (Bereshit 42:7; line 684): "the arousal begins from below and then all is completed." Smoke rises from the earth and the cloud wakes. «ובתיאובתא דלתתא אשתלים לעילא» (Bereshit 42:7; line 684): "and by the longing below it is completed above." From inside: you do not wait to be moved; your own small longing is the start of the whole. This is the source of "itaruta diletata". Ties: S13 The unnamed longing, S41 The hidden love wakes.
2. **Back to back, then face to face.** Adam and Eve were first made side by side, not facing; God "uprooted and replanted them" and they turned face to face (lines 679-689). The new plantings were "like locusts' horns", their light thin, until they were replanted and grew in light (line 691). Ours: a state of being moved from one's place to be set facing. Ties: S74 Seen eye to eye.
3. **Silent in exile.** Knesset Yisrael says "I was mute with silence": voice has left speech, and praise has become silence, «לך דומיה תהלה» (Bereshit 44:13; line 709): "to You, silence is praise." Ties: S14 Loss and grief, S73 Exile inside. A state the guide should meet without trying to fill it.
4. **The thought that grasps and does not know** (line 887). Near S35 and S36, but this is the top of prayer, reached by joining everything below it. Ties: S61 Will and delight above reason.
5. **Those who suffered and praised.** In the second palace of Eden sit «אנון דסבלו יסורין ומרעין בהאי עלמא בגין לאתתקנא. והוו מודן ומשבחן למריהון כל יומא» (Bereshit 52:2; line 756): "those who bore sufferings and illnesses in this world in order to be repaired, and gave thanks and praise to their Master every day." Ties: S05 Bitter with purpose, S49 Weeping and joy in one heart.
6. **Bitterness that is return.** In the fifth palace are the masters of strong return, «דנשמתהון נפקת בשעתא דאתמרמרו על עובדיהון» (Bereshit 55:2; line 764): "whose souls went out in the hour when they were embittered over their deeds." Even the perfectly righteous cannot enter there (line 765). Ties: S77, S78.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **The order of the palaces: a prayer with the body.** The palaces of prayer give each stage of the rising prayer a bodily act: at the end of the second palace, «הכא הכרעה לאתדבקא במאריה» (Bereshit 65:11; line 825): "here, bowing, to cleave to his Master"; at the end of the third, «הכא השתחואה לאתרצאה במאריה» (Bereshit 66:18; line 843): "here, prostrating, to be accepted by his Master"; at the fourth, «הכא קידה באנפין בארעא לאתגברא על דינא» (Bereshit 67:13; line 856): "here, bowing the face to the ground, to prevail over judgement"; at the fifth, the palace of love: «השתחואה ופרישו דידין לאתדבקא ברחימו דמאריה» (Bereshit 68:10; line 866): "prostration and spreading of the hands, to cleave in love to his Master"; at the sixth, all of them together, to draw the will of the soul of all souls "to Ein Sof" (line 878). Ours: a map of prayer as ascent through love, merit, will, with the body answering at each step. Ties: technique 2 (before Whom), 16 (love and awe), S80 Prayer: mind into heart.
- **Who knows how to order his prayer.** «מאן הוא דידע לסדרא צלותא … דמריה כמשה» (Bereshit 63:1; line 802): "who knows how to order a prayer to his Master like Moses", to lengthen when needed and to shorten when needed, "to join knots" and "unify a complete unity." The one who does is «מתדבק במאריה ובטיל כל גזרין קשין» (Bereshit 66:14; line 839): "cleaves to his Master and annuls all harsh decrees."
- **Below first, then above.** «מתתא בקדמיתא ומלעילא לבתר» (Bereshit 66:13; line 838): "from below first, and from above after." Every palace needs the one below it. Ours: a guard against leaping to the top.
- **The palace of love.** The fifth palace becomes «היכל אהבה היכלא דרחימותא» (Bereshit 68:9; line 865): "the palace of love, the palace of affection", hidden "for whoever needs to cleave to it"; the sixth is the palace of will, where Moses was gathered "with the kisses of love" (line 869). Ties: S44 Love as delight, S60 The essential bond.
- **Handing over the soul at night.** Every night the tree of death rules and all taste death; «אלא אנון בני מהימנותא מקדמי ויהבו ליה נפשייהו בפקדונא» (Bereshit 44:14; line 710): "but the children of faith go first and give Him their souls as a deposit," and so the souls return: "and Your faithfulness in the nights." The same practice as the Hakdamah's line 182.
- **Speech on Shabbat.** Rabbi Shimon, when he saw his old mother talking on Shabbat, would say «אמא, שתוקי, שבת הוא ואסיר» (Bereshit 36:8; line 615): "Mother, be quiet; it is Shabbat, and it is forbidden." The Shabbat is speech, so weekday speech is out of place in it. A practice of guarded speech.
- **Cleave and fear always.** In the first palace of Eden: «רזא דמהימנותא לאתדבקא בר נש במריה ולדחלא מניה תדיר ולא יסטי לימינא ושמאלא» (Bereshit 51:2; line 749): "the secret of faith is that a person cleave to his Master and fear Him always and not turn right or left."
- **Guard: the early sages.** When they reached this place (the measured waters), «מרחשן שפוון דחכימין ולא אמרין מדי בגין דלא יתענשון» (Bereshit 39:3; line 632): "the lips of the wise would move, and they said nothing, so as not to be punished." Some depths are for whispering.

## 5. The big human questions

- **Suffering.** The second palace of Eden is for those who suffered and praised (line 756); the third for the very sick, children who died young, and those who wept for the Temple; the Messiah comforts them (line 758). The fourth is for the mourners of Zion and the martyred, and the Messiah weeps there (line 759). Ours: the Zohar does not explain suffering here; it gives it a palace and a comforter.
- **Why is man in the world?** «ברא לבר נש בעלמא, ואתקין ליה למהוי שלים בפולחניה» (Bereshit 75:1; line 918): "He created man in the world and prepared him to be whole in his service," so that he merit the hidden light. How? Through Torah, and the one who learns every day is counted «כאלו באני עלמין» (Bereshit 75:2; line 919): "as if he builds worlds." The world stands on the breath of those who learn, and most of all on "the breath of schoolchildren."
- **Nothing is outside the repair.** Even demons and harmful spirits: «דהא אנון לתקונא דעלמא הוו» (Bereshit 77:3; line 929): "for they are for the repair of the world," to rebuke the wicked. With the Bereshit I line "all of it is the repair of the world" (line 369), the Zohar's answer to evil is always inside the One.
- **Death "very good".** "Very good" is death (line 915): the second day, when death was made, gets no "it was good", and the sixth, which completes it, gets "very good".
- **Return is always open.** Cain returns and says "from Your face I shall be hidden" (line 717); "if you do well, you will be lifted up" (line 719); "you shall rule over it" is by return (line 723).
- **Face to face as the aim.** The male and female above are joined only when the lower world is set face to face (lines 680-682): "until it was established below, it was not established above." What a person does below completes what is above.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **"The arousal begins from below" (line 684).** The guide already uses the idea; here is its Zohar text. The person's own small longing is where it starts.
2. **The palaces of prayer as a bodily ascent.** A form of prayer the guide lacks: each stage closed by a bodily act (bow, prostrate, face to the ground, spread the hands).
3. **"Grasps and does not know what it grasps" (line 887).** The best description in the corpus of the top of prayer, and a safe one: it is reached only by joining everything below.
4. **Light only in darkness (lines 614, 617)** for the person in the dark.
5. **Silence as praise in exile (line 709)**, to meet a person who cannot speak to God.

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "Grasping without knowing."** Inside: after everything in you has been gathered and joined, your will touches something it cannot hold and does not know, and is filled and made sweet. Enter: only at the end of a prayer that has joined each stage below (lines 885-887). Guard: it cannot be aimed at directly.
- **State, new: "Silence that is praise."** Inside: voice has gone out of your words; you cannot speak to Him, and your silence is itself turned to Him (line 709). Enter: do not force speech; offer the silence. Near S14, S73.
- **Technique, new: "The palaces with the body."** Pray in stages, and close each with a bodily act: bow (cleaving), prostrate (being accepted), face to the ground (over judgement), spread the hands (love), all together (will) (lines 825-878). Guard: below first, then above (line 838).
- **Technique, new: "The first small arousal."** Begin with the longing that is already there, however small; that is the smoke that wakes the cloud (line 684). Ties technique 1 (start from what is already moving).
