## 1. What the range is

Terumah is the longest parashah in the Zohar. Its first half is not really about the Tabernacle at all. The command "take for Me an offering" (Exodus 25:2) opens onto almost everything:

* the hidden point that comes out of Ein Sof (line 2499), and the "canopy" (apiryon) of Shabbat (lines 2510-2523); * what it costs to acquire a mitzvah, a teacher, a friend, and the Holy Spirit (lines 2524-2531); * bringing a wicked man back, which is greater than giving to the poor (lines 2530-2541); * the whole order of daily prayer from evening to morning, read as the furnishing of a room for the Shekhinah (lines 2533-2615); * the Shema as the unity of the upper and lower worlds (lines 2615-2639 and 2732-2739); * Psalm 19 on Shabbat and the speech that is not heard (lines 2674-2697); * whispered prayer that does not let Him go (lines 2711-2715); * David praising in the desert (lines 2743-2751); * the Shekhinah coming down to the Tabernacle "without separation" (lines 2752-2758); * the Land of Israel, the soul after death, and the three parts of the soul bound to Ein Sof (lines 2759-2796); * the Song of Songs as the holy of holies, and the kiss as spirit joining spirit (lines 2803-2873); * the colors of the offering (lines 2874-2903); * the hidden light that feeds the world each day (lines 2904-2906); * the death angel as "very good", the garments of the soul, and Gehinnom (lines 2922-2955).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**The point that comes out of what cannot be known.** The upper world is completed by the letter yod, "the first high point, which comes out of what is closed and hidden and not known." From that closed place one fine, hidden light shines. Then the Zohar says the strangest thing about it: בטש ביה מאן דלא בטש. נהיר ביה מאן דלא נהיר, "He who does not strike struck it; He who does not shine shone in it" (line 2499). The first act is done by One who does not act, in the way we mean acting. **(ours)** This is the Zohar's version of what the Gate of Unity will call the light that is not a change in Him.

**The most revealed place is still a question.** The Shekhinah is called *Mah*, "what?" Why? ואף על גב דאתגליא יתיר מכלא, קיימא לשאלא, מ"ה, "even though she is more revealed than all, she stands for asking: *what?*" (line 2506). What have you seen, what do you know? "For you saw no image" (Deuteronomy 4:15). **(ours)** Even where God is closest and most shown, the right response is not "I have Him" but "what is this?" The question is the knowing.

**Everything one, without separation.** The Tabernacle is built "so that the Shekhinah is included from every side, above and below." The verse says "take for Me an offering," not "for Me and an offering," לאחזאה דכלא חד בלא פרודא, "to show that all is one without separation" (line 2756). The same phrase closes the Shema teaching below (line 2738).

**The Shema makes one above and one below.** "When Israel unify the unity of the secret of *Shema Yisrael* with a whole will" (בשעתא דקא מיחדי ישראל, יחודא דרזא דשמע ישראל, ברעותא שלים, line 2615), one light comes out of the hidden upper world. The six words of *Shema Yisrael* unify the upper world; the six words of *Baruch Shem* unify the lower. אוף הכי אנן צריכין ליחדא עלמא עלאה באחד, וליחדא עלמא תתאה ברזא דאחד, "so we too must unify the upper world in *One* and unify the lower world in the secret of *One*" (line 2635). And: זכאה עדביה וחולקיה מאן דישוי רעותיה להאי, "happy is the lot of one who sets his will on this" (line 2635). Later the letter count is added: 25 letters in the Shema verse, 24 in *Baruch Shem*; one who intends both lifts them to the 49 gates (line 2735). On the old debate between Shammai and Hillel about standing or reclining for the evening Shema, Hillel's house wins because we do not need to separate "this alone and this alone," אלא לאשגחא דכלא איהו חד, בלא פרודא, "but to see that all is one, without separation" (line 2738).

**The world would not last one moment.** The spirit that God breathed from the high point is rooted in this world so that the flow has a vessel below. "If it did not pour into this world, at once it would be lost," ולא יכיל לקיימא אפילו רגעא חדא, "and could not stand even one moment" (line 2853). The same is said of the hidden first light: אלמלי אתגניז מכל וכל, לא קאים עלמא אפילו רגעא חדא, "had it been hidden completely, the world would not stand even one moment" (line 2905). It was hidden like a seed, and each day something comes out of it. The cloud over the Tabernacle is a thread of that light, ואיהו מחדש בכל יומא עובדא דבראשית, "and it renews each day the work of creation" (line 2906). **(ours)** This is the Zohar source for the line Chabad takes from the morning prayer, "He renews in His goodness every day." The Zohar ties it to the hidden light.

**Made on peace.** On the first days of creation each day did the work of the other, light in water and water in fire, לאחזאה דקודשא בריך הוא לא ברא עלמא, אלא על שלום, "to show that the Holy One created the world only on peace" (line 2918).

**He gives what He loves.** A man will not let another take his wife. "But the Holy One is not so," אבל קודשא בריך הוא לאו הכי (line 2643). "Take My offering" is the Shekhinah, the community of Israel, אף על גב דכל רחימו דילה לגביה, ורחימו דיליה לגבה, "even though all her love is toward Him, and His love toward her" (line 2643). He lets her be taken to dwell among us. **(ours)** Unity here is not a fact to be grasped but a love that is willing to give itself away downward.

**Hidden and shown.** The one who kisses in "let Him kiss me" is סתימא דכל סתימין, לית מאן דידע ליה, "the most hidden of all hidden, whom no one knows" (line 2869). From Him goes out one fine thread of light, לזמנין סתים, לזמנין אתגליא, "sometimes hidden, sometimes shown" (line 2869), and even when shown it is not shown at all.

## 3. States of consciousness

**Sitting in His shade (proposed ZS2-16).** R. Shimon and his friends sit under a tree. He says that one who merits the "canopy" merits everything, זכי למיתב בנייחא דצלא דקודשא בריך הוא, "merits to sit in the rest of the shade of the Holy One" (line 2523), "as it says, 'in His shade I delighted and sat'" (Song 2:3). They should see that they sit only in God's shade. Inside: you are not doing anything and not reaching. You are resting, covered. The shade is not yours; it is cast over you. Near S76 (Shabbos: the soul returned) and S67 (Surrounded), but it is a weekday state that can come on any quiet sitting. How to enter: sit still somewhere, and notice that what is over you, covering you, is His (ours).

**The dark light at dawn (proposed ZS2-17).** David praised God "in the desert of Judah" while being hunted. "Even though he was in pain and they chased him, לא הוה שביק שירין ותושבחן מפומיה, he did not let songs and praises leave his mouth" (line 2744). And the Holy Spirit came only because he first roused himself: לא שריא רוח קדשא דלעילא, עד דיתער עליה בר נש מתתא, "the Holy Spirit above does not rest on a person until he rouses himself from below" (line 2744). Then "I seek You early" (*ashachrecha*) is read as "I set right the black light of dawn." The dawn light is dark; one who prepares it, though it is black, merits the white light. דזכי לתרין נהורין. לנהורא דשחרא אוכמא, ולנהורא חוורא דנהרא, "he merits two lights: the black light of dawn and the white light that shines" (line 2750). Inside: you praise while the light is still dark, and the praise itself is what prepares the light. Close to S73 (Exile inside) and S68 (The hiding seen as His), with a practice in it: praise first, before you feel anything. (Contrast S04 Low and heavy: that is the dark without the praise.)

**The whisper that holds Him (proposed ZS2-18).** In the prayer order, when the Glory might rise away, Israel below "hold Him and hold on to Him, and do not let Him go far from them." ועל דא צלותא בלחש, כמאן דמליל ברזא עם מלכא, "so the prayer is in a whisper, like one who speaks in secret with the King" (line 2713), and while he speaks with Him in secret, He does not go far. The goal: דלא ישתבק בר נש מניה, אפילו שעתא חדא, "that a person not be left by Him even one hour" (line 2715). Inside: closeness kept by quietness, like two who lower their voices because they are near. Near S55 (Own words to Him) and S80 (Prayer: mind into heart); new is the sense that the whisper itself keeps Him from leaving.

**Spirit joined to spirit, joy in every limb.** The kiss passage (lines 2861-2868) goes deeper than Mishpatim's (ZS2-14): דלית רחימו דדביקות דרוחא ברוחא, "there is no love of cleaving of spirit to spirit" except the kiss, and וכד נשקין דא לדא, מתדבקן רוחין אלין באלין, והוו חד, "when they kiss each other, the spirits cleave to each other and become one" (line 2861). Four spirits become one fruit, the word *ahavah*, and these are רוחין דרחימו וחדוה דכל שייפי גופא בלא עציבו כלל, "spirits of love and joy of all the limbs of the body, with no sadness at all" (line 2863). This fills in ZS2-14 and ties it to S44 (Love as delight).

**Shame before Him at the falling on the face.** After the Amidah, at "who makes peace," a person should fall on his face: ובעי כל בר נש למכסף מקמי מאריה, "every person must feel shame before his Master," and ולאכללא נפשיה בההוא שמושא דנפשין, "include his soul in that union of souls" (line 2535). The shame is not self-hatred; it is the face covered because one is so near. Tie to S59 (True lowliness) and S33 (Before Whom).

**Joy that needs the right hand.** "All joy found on the left side is found only when the right joins it" (כל חידו דאשתכח מסטרא דשמאלא, לא אשתכח אלא בזמנא דימינא אתדבק בהדיה, line 2811). The Song of Songs is all love and joy (בגין דכל מלוי ברחימו ובחדוה כלא, line 2809); Lamentations is the cup held in the left hand alone. **(ours)** A test for S53 (Glad) against excitement: real joy has kindness in it; heat without kindness turns to complaint.

**The young lion.** "The righteous are bold as a young lion," and why the young lion? דאיהו חלשא וזעירא מכלהו. ולא אתרחיץ בחיליה, "because it is the weakest and smallest of them, and does not trust in its strength" (line 2799). The righteous now trust like that; in the future, like Mount Zion. Fits S84 (Trust): trust that does not lean on one's own deeds.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Setting out with Him (proposed ZT2-06).** "Whoever wants to go on a journey, let him rise at first light and look carefully toward the east" (מאן דבעי למיפק לארחא, יקום בנגהא, line 2565). He will see the letters striking in the sky. If he knows the 42-letter Name and remembers it with the will of the heart, he will see the letters of the priestly blessing in the dawn. וכדין יצלי צלותיה, ויפוק לארחא, ודאי שכינתא אקדימת עמיה, "then let him pray his prayer and go on the road; surely the Shekhinah goes before him" (line 2566). **(ours)** The letter vision belongs to the methods the guide keeps out (TECHNOLOGY technique 25). What the guide can take is the frame: before leaving on a trip or starting a hard day, stand at first light facing east, say the three verses of the priestly blessing slowly, pray, and then go, knowing you do not go alone. Close to technique 2 (Before Whom) and 21 (The act that closes).

**The four furnishings of prayer.** The Shunammite made Elisha "a bed, a table, a chair and a lamp." So we make a room for the Shekhinah each day: evening prayer is the bed (בסדורא דצלותא דערבית, ובתקונא דיליה, הא מטה, line 2611), the morning songs are the table, the Shema said sitting is the chair, the standing prayer with its holiness is the lamp. **(ours)** A simple way to teach the parts of the prayer day: you are setting up a guest room, and He is the guest.

**Entrusting at night.** At the evening prayer, out of awe, אנן מפקידינן גופין רוחין ונשמתין, למלכותא עלאה, "we entrust bodies, spirits and souls to the upper Kingship" (line 2562). A plain practice for sleep: hand over body, feeling and mind before lying down. Tie to technique 24 (The evening look-back).

**Whispered prayer (proposed ZT2-07).** From lines 2713-2715: say the quiet prayer as a secret between you and the King. The aim is not to produce feeling but to keep the closeness from breaking. When joining the blessing of redemption to the prayer, "hold on to Him, and talk with Him in a whisper, in secret, so He does not go far from us." Builds on technique 20 (Your own words to Him) and 12 (Returning the wandering mind).

**The Shema with both halves.** Lines 2635-2739 give a clear Shema meditation: first verse for the upper unity, *Baruch Shem* whispered for the lower, and then the seeing that "all is one without separation" (line 2738). This refines technique 3 (one line) and 16 (love and awe). It does not need a new number.

**Effort, not emptiness.** "One who wants to take up a mitzvah and take up the Holy One," אצטריך דלא ישתדל ביה בריקנייא ובמגנא, "must not do it empty and for free" (line 2524), but pay its full price. And the Holy Spirit comes only "with full payment and very great effort" (ורוח קודשא לאו הכי, אלא באגר שלים, ובאשתדלותא רב סגי, line 2527), with purity of body and home, וברעותא דלביה ונפשיה, "and with the will of his heart and soul" (line 2527). This is a check against any practice that promises quick states.

**Night study.** "Wherever people toil in Torah at night, חד חוטא נפיק מההוא אור גניז, one thread comes out of that hidden light" and is drawn over them (line 2905).

## 5. The big human questions

**How do I know where God is present in a person?** כד חמינן דרעותא דההוא בר נש, "when we see that the will of that person" is to run after the Holy One with his heart and soul, ודאי תמן ידעינן דשריא ביה שכינתא, "there we surely know that the Shekhinah dwells in him" (line 2529). Then you must "acquire him" as a friend at full price. The sign is not knowledge or fame but longing.

**What is the greatest good I can do for another?** Giving to the poor keeps him alive. But כל מאן דאחיד בידא דחייבא, "whoever takes the hand of a wicked man" and works with him to leave the bad road rises three rises (line 2531). He humbles the other side, lifts God's glory, and עביד לההוא חייבא, נפשא אחרא, "makes that wicked man a new soul" (line 2541). **(ours)** The word is "takes the hand," not "rebukes."

**What happens to the soul after death?** The Zohar gives its fullest picture here. The *nefesh* stays near the grave and goes about the world, feeling the pain of the living and asking mercy for them (line 2774). The *ruach* goes into the lower Garden of Eden, dressed in a body like this one. The *neshamah* rises at once to its root. בגין דכל הני תלת, קשורא חדא אינון, "all three are one knot" (line 2779). When the *neshamah* is bound in its place, all find rest: וההיא נשמה אתקשרת באין סוף. וכדין איהו נייחא דכלא, "and that soul is bound in Ein Sof, and then is the rest of all" (line 2792). When people in pain go to the graves, the *nefesh* wakes (האי נפש אתערת, line 2777), wakes the *ruach*, which wakes the fathers, which wakes the *neshamah*, and then God has mercy on the world.

**Why do we die?** "Behold it was very good" (Genesis 1:31) is the angel of death. Why very good? Because people know they will die and return in fear of it. כמה טבא ההיא רצועה לגבי בר נש, "how good that strap is for a person" (line 2922). The deeper answer: the soul has a garment in Eden it took off to come here. At death the angel undresses it of this body, and ולית חדו לרוחא, בר בההוא גופא דתמן, "the spirit has no joy except in that body there" (line 2927). God is kind: דלא אפשיט ליה לבר נש לבושין אלין, עד דאתקין ליה לבושין אחרנין, "He does not strip a person of these garments until He has prepared him others" (line 2928), better ones.

**What if I wanted to change and died before I could?** חמי כמה רחמנותא דקודשא בריך הוא עם בריוהי, "see how great is the mercy of the Holy One with His creatures" (line 2930). A very wicked man who thought of return and died before he could do it is punished, but that will to return is not lost before the King. It comes down, breaks all the locks of Gehinnom, finds him, and wakes him again, and his soul rises. ולית רעותא טבא דיתאביד מקמי מלכא קדישא, "no good will is lost before the holy King" (line 2931). This matches Mishpatim's "nothing is lost" (part2-mishpatim.md).

**What is Gehinnom?** It burns with the same heat by which the wicked heat themselves with the evil inclination, כגוונא דחייביא מתחממן בנורא דיצר הרע (line 2933). When the evil inclination was once locked away, the fire went out. On Shabbat the fire rests, for "the holy King comes and the day is made holy," ואיהו אגין על כלא, "and He shields all" (line 2942). **(ours)** Punishment is described as the inner heat itself, carried on.

**Can my deeds on earth move heaven?** The offering is taken from the angels who raise it, and brought down "in this time, by good deeds, in prayers and requests." Then the colors below win over the colors above, ונצחן גוונין דלתתא, לאינון גוונין דלעילא (line 2876). And the Tabernacle was built only מההוא רעותא ורוחא דלהון, "from their will and spirit" (line 2856). Moses asked who could make it; God said, "not as you think," from every man whose heart moves him.

**What is return, in the body of the angels?** When the judging angel of blue acts, he is called by a harsh name. וכד אתהדרן בני נשא בתיובתא שלימתא, "when people come back in full return," his name turns into Rafael, "for healing comes to them from that same hard judgment" (line 2878).

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **"The most revealed is still a question: *what?*" (line 2506)** is a good line for anyone who wants certainty about God. The Zohar says the nearest place is a question.
2. **"He who does not strike struck; He who does not shine shone" (line 2499)** is the best Zohar line for the Chabad teaching that God's acts are not a change in Him.
3. **The dark light at dawn (line 2750)** is for the one praying in heaviness: the praise before feeling is what prepares the light. A strong line for S04 and S73.
4. **"No good will is lost before the holy King" (line 2931)**, with line 2930, answers the fear of a life ended unfinished.
5. **"Take the hand of the wicked" (lines 2531, 2541)**: helping someone change is placed above charity.
6. **Whispered prayer (line 2713)**, and **entrusting body, spirit and soul at night (line 2562)**, are simple practices the guide can give as is.

Proposed for the map (details in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`): ZS2-16 *Sitting in His shade*, ZS2-17 *The dark light at dawn*, ZS2-18 *The whisper that holds Him*; techniques ZT2-06 *Setting out with Him*, ZT2-07 *Whispered prayer*.

Set aside: the passage on souls of other peoples in the Land (line 2764) is not used in a guide meant for everyone (D28). The long reading of Gehinnom's seven chambers is summarized, not taught.
