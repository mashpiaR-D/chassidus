The second half of Netzach Yisrael turns from exile to redemption. It asks what the "end" is, why it is hidden, what changes when it comes, and what kind of bond with G-d will then be complete.

Three teachings run through it and matter for the guide:

- **Absence comes before being.** Nothing new comes to be unless the old has first fully passed. So ruin can be the first step of a higher being.
- **He is One in Himself always; what changes is the receiver.** "On that day the L-rd will be One and His Name One" does not mean He is not One now. It means that now the receivers take Him in divided ways.
- **Simplicity and the middle.** The simple receives everything, the middle leans to no side, and both have no end.

## 2.1 "On that day He will be One": from the side of the receiver

This is the central unity text of the book. On Zechariah 14:9, "the L-rd will be One and His Name One", the Talmud asks: is He not One now? The Maharal answers:

"כי הוא יתברך מצד עצמו הוא אחד לעולם. אבל הענין הוא מצד המקבלים" (719, 42:3): "for He, from His own side, is always One; the matter is from the side of the receivers."

Now we receive from Him good and what looks bad, so we bless "the Good" over one and "the true Judge" over the other. His Name is written YHVH and read Adonai, "Master", because in this world we stand to Him as a servant, apart. In the future His Name will be read as it is written.

He gives this a clear frame. There are two ways the caused stands to its Cause: "כמו צירוף בן אל האב" (720, 42:4): "like the joining of a son to a father", and like a servant to a master, who is apart. Now the second shows. Then the first will. Even the Shema holds both: "the L-rd our G-d" is said now, from our side, and "כי העלול אינו אחד" (721, 42:5): "for the caused is not one." "The L-rd is One" is said of the world to come, when the joining will be from His side, where He is One.

## 2.2 "Every whole thing is one"

"שכל שלם הוא אחד, והאחד הוא שלם" (717, 42:1): "every whole thing is one, and the one is whole." "The Tabernacle was one" means it was complete. Oneness and wholeness are the same thing seen twice. A thing split into parts is neither.

## 2.3 Two kinds of oneness, and Jacob's Shema

When Jacob's sons said "Shema Yisrael" at his deathbed, they meant: "כשם שאין בלבך אלא אחד, כך אין בלבנו אלא אחד" (748, 44:7): "just as in your heart there is only One, so in our heart there is only One."

The Maharal explains that there are two kinds of oneness. One is "הדבר שהוא בעצמו אחד", a thing that is one in itself. The other is when many parts "נעשים אחד על ידי דבר המאחד אותו" (746, 44:5): "become one through what unites them", as the limbs become one through the soul. The twelve tribes became one through their father, and through him held on to G-d's oneness. Even the letters of אחד show it: aleph (the tribe set apart), chet (eight sons of the wives), dalet (four of the maids).

He applies this to every person: "והיחיד מקבל מלכותו על ידי הכלל" (750, 44:9): "and the single person receives His kingship through the whole." That is why the Shema is said to "Israel", the whole.

## 2.4 Blessing His Name

After the Shema, Jacob said "Baruch shem kevod malchuto", blessing His Name "במה שמורה שמו הגדול על אמתת מהותו" (748, 44:7): "in that His great Name shows the truth of His being." Only the holy may bless this way, so it is said in a whisper, "כמו שיושבת הנשמה בחדרי חדרים" (753, 44:12): "as the soul sits in the innermost rooms." On Yom Kippur, when a person lives like the angels, it is said aloud (749, 753).

## 2.5 "Anochi": His very self

"Anochi, Anochi, I am He who comforts you." The word Anochi, unlike Ani, means the speaker's own self. At Sinai "Anochi the L-rd your G-d" meant "כאילו אמר אני עצמי אלקיך" (793, 48:6): "as if He said: I Myself am your G-d", not handed over to any angel or power. And "כי מאמתת עצמו נברא הכל" (795, 48:8): "for all was created from His very self." Said once at Sinai, the bond could be broken. Said twice in the comfort, it is made necessary.

## 2.6 He includes all, and sharing His glory makes no partner

A human king cannot let anyone ride his horse or sit on his throne, since that would make a partner. G-d gave Elijah His "horse", Moses His staff, Solomon His throne, and the messiah His crown, "ומאחר שמאתו הכל, לא שייך בזה השתתפות" (704, 40:5): "and since all is from Him, sharing does not make a partner." Whatever He gives is still His. And of the two angels who argued (one for kindness, one for judgment): "והוא יתברך כולל הכל" (813, 51:4): "and He includes all."

## 2.7 Mercy is joining

Abraham called the Temple "mountain" (kindness, great and above the line). Isaac called it "field" (judgment, level and straight). Jacob called it "house", and a house is where one lives. Mercy is not only one trait among others: "כי זה ענין הרחמים, האהבה והחבור" (823, 52:3): "for this is what mercy is: love and joining." And "כי כל רחמנות כאשר לבו קשורה בו" (726, 42:10): "for all mercy is when one's heart is bound to him." (He notes that the letters of the bird's whistle, שרק, read backward are קשר, a knot.)

## 2.8 One cause, many ways He is seen

"כי הכל הוא כפי מה שהוא המקבל, הוא האדם" (469, 22:19): "for all is according to what the receiver is, and the receiver is the human being." Adam named every creature, and then named G-d "Adonai". Of the messiah, each school named him by its own name, since one who includes all "נדמה למשיג בו כפי מה שהוא" (713, 41:2): "appears to each one who grasps him according to what that one is." This is the same law as 2.1: He does not change; the one who sees Him does.

## 2.9 No change from His side, none from the receiver's

Bilam's second blessing: "not a man that He should lie". "כי אין שנוי אצל השם יתברך מצד עצמו" (896, 58:2): "for there is no change in G-d from His own side." And "He has not seen iniquity in Jacob" means there is no change from the receiver's side either. So "ה' אלקיו עמו ותרועת מלך בו" (897, 58:3): "the L-rd his G-d is with him": they call and are answered at once. And the Maharal's famous line on divination: "כל אדם שאינו מנחש, מכניסין אותו למחיצתו של הקב"ה" (903, 58:9): "whoever does not practice divination is brought into the Holy One's own chamber", where even angels cannot enter. One who looks to signs has joined outer powers. One who does not stands with Him alone.

## 2.10 Only One heart in the world to come

"כי לימות המשיח יתאחד הרבוי אשר הוא בעולם הזה" (619, 32:4): "for in messianic days the manyness of this world will be made one." Seven strings on the lyre for this world (completeness of the many), eight for the messiah (the one that unites the seven), ten for the world to come (holy and separate). And "ויטו הבריות לב אחד לעבוד את יוצר הכל" (804, 50:1): "creatures will turn one heart to serve the Maker of all." (Ours: this last line is universal as stated.)

Until then, the Maharal admits something honest: "אין עולם הזה ראוי שיהיה בו השם אחד עד לעתיד" (644, 34:2): "this world is not fit for the Name to be one in it until the future." Division is part of how this world is made. That is why, he says, G-d forgives much that division causes.

**Proposed MH-S17 "The ruin as a beginning".** Inside: what has fallen apart is not the end. A thing must fully pass before something higher can come to be; the egg breaks before the chick. "כי ההעדר הוא סבת ההויה" (492, 23:14): "for absence is the cause of coming-to-be." And from G-d, who is the source of being, loss cannot come "אם לא כאשר יעלה אל הויה ואל מציאות יותר במעלה" (557, 26:15): "unless it rises to a higher being." How to enter: look at the loss, and say: "This is the tohu before 'let there be light'." Do not deny the loss. Rabbi Akiva laughed only because the ruin was complete, so the rebuilding was certain (545). Source: 492, 545-558, 665. Caution (ours): this is for one's own losses, held with G-d; it is never a reason to call another person's suffering good.

**Proposed MH-S18 "The well that rises".** Inside: a longing for G-d that comes up from below, from inside me, not only from being drawn down from above. The Maharal: Aaron stands for G-d's wish to join us (the clouds of glory came down), Miriam for our longing for Him: "כי הבאר נובע ועולה מלמטה למעלה" (843, 54:6): "for the well springs and rises from below upward." In the future "נקבה תסובב גבר" (786, 47:6): "the woman will court the man", that is, we will seek Him. How to enter: in prayer, notice any wish for G-d that rises by itself, and follow it up instead of waiting to be moved. Source: 786, 839-845.

## 4.1 Jacob's Shema (`before_whom`; new: `mh_only_one_in_heart`)

How: before the Shema, picture the sons at their father's bed. Say the first verse as "just as in your heart there is only One, so in ours there is only One" (748). Then say "Baruch shem" in a whisper, "as the soul sits in the innermost rooms" (753), knowing you are blessing His Name, which is His very being (748). Source: 742-753.

## 4.2 Joy in the Master, with trembling (`heart_after`)

The Maharal keeps two things together at prayer. Awe: "the caused is apart from the Cause". Joy: "כי העבד יש לו צירוף אל אדון שלו" (481, 23:3): "for the servant is joined to his master." And the tefillin answer: "אני שמח בעלה עצמה, מה שהוא יתברך אדון לי" (483, 23:5): "I rejoice in the Cause itself, that He is Master over me." He warns about joy that means "I am whole": "אבל השמח, הרי בדעתו שהוא שלם" (484, 23:6): "but the one who rejoices thinks in his mind that he is whole", and so seeks nothing more. Keep joy in the heart: "שעיקר השמחה בלב" (487, 23:9): "for the main place of joy is the heart."

## 4.3 Never with drink (guard)

On singing praise to G-d over wine: "והמונע עצמו מזה תבא עליו ברכה" (496, 23:18): "and blessing will come on the one who holds himself back from this." He holds that even in Temple times it was forbidden to speak G-d's praise when drunk. This supports our rule: never drink.

## 4.4 The betrothal (`act`; new: `mh_betrothal`)

"וארשתיך לי לעולם" (781, 47:1): "and I will betroth you to Me forever", in righteousness, justice, kindness, mercy and faithfulness. The Maharal divides it: righteousness and justice are what a person brings, kindness and mercy are what G-d brings, "החסד הוא הטוב שהוא משפיע לעולם, אף שאין הכרחי כל כך לעולם" (783, 47:3): "kindness is the good He gives the world even when the world does not strictly need it." Faithfulness makes the bond complete. How: say these verses slowly each morning (many say them while winding the tefillin strap). At "righteousness and justice" name one fair thing you will do today. At "kindness and mercy" receive. At "faithfulness" rest in the bond. Source: 781-783.

## 4.5 Go back to the one who cut (`own_words`; new: `mh_back_to_the_surgeon`)

"He tore and He will heal." A patient whose wound was opened by a surgeon should go back to that surgeon. "כל מכותיו אינם רק לרפאות את החולה" (949, 61:7): "all His blows are only to heal the sick one." How: when you are hurt, do not go from helper to helper looking for someone who understands. Turn to G-d in your own words and say, "You know this wound; heal it." Source: 948-949. Caution (ours): this is not a reason to refuse doctors. The Maharal himself says doctors are permitted, "מכל מקום ישים עיקר בטחונו בו יתברך" (592, 30:5): "still, let him put his main trust in Him."

## 4.6 Be small to receive (`quieting`; with `mh_remnant`)

"כל אשר הוא פשוט ואין בו דבר, הוא מיוחד לקבל מן העליונים" (550, 26:8): "whatever is simple and has nothing in it is fit to receive from above." Dust receives rain from above because it is small (886), "כי ראוי אל המעלה האלקית הקטנות, יותר מן הגדלות" (888, 57:14): "for smallness fits the divine level more than greatness." "הפשוט מקבל כל הגוונים" (705): "the simple receives all colors." How: before learning or prayer, set aside what you know and what you want; come as the one with nothing in his hands. Source: 550, 705, 886-888.

## 4.7 The three Shabbat meals (`act`)

The three meals express that the world is complete: nothing to add, nothing lacking, no further becoming. "כי ידעתי כי על כל מעשה ה' אין להוסיף ואין לגרוע" (675, 36:8): "for I know that to all G-d's work nothing can be added and nothing taken away." How: at each Shabbat meal, hold one of these three in mind. Source: 675-677.

## 4.8 Torah and kindness in hard times (`act`)

How to be saved from the "birth pangs"? "Let him busy himself with Torah and kindness" (671). Torah is a good beyond the body; kindness is a good in the body. Both are simple, "כי הפשיטות אין בו שנוי" (672, 36:5): "for simplicity has no change", so they carry a person through times of change.

## 5. The big human questions

**Why must things get worse before they get better?** "שלא יתהוה דבר רק כאשר קדם לו העדר צורה לגמרי" (545, 26:3): "nothing comes to be unless a full absence of form came before it." A partial loss is impure; a full loss that makes room for new being "אין זה נקרא הפסד, רק הוא הויה" (665, 35:11): "this is not called loss; it is coming-to-be."

**What is comfort?** It comes from outside, and "כי כל תנחומין נותן חזוק וכח לאשר מקבל התנחומין" (552, 26:10): "for all comfort gives strength and power to the one who receives it." That is why the messiah is called "Menachem son of Chizkiya", comforter son of strength.

**What will I be asked?** The six questions at judgment: did you deal honestly, set times for Torah, raise children, hope for salvation, reason in wisdom, understand one thing from another? And even then, only if "the fear of G-d is his treasure." Why? "כי האדם הוא עלול, ואין לעלול קיום מצד עצמו, כי אם מצד העלה" (586, 29:11): "for a person is caused, and the caused has no standing from itself, only from the Cause." Awe is the knowledge that holds all the rest.

**Is it now or later?** The messiah said "today". Elijah explained: "if you listen to His voice". "והדבר שאינו גשמי אינו תחת הזמן" (573, 28:8): "and what is not physical is not under time." "אבל אין מניעה מצד המשפיע" (761, 45:7): "but there is no barrier from the Giver's side." The delay is only in the receiver. So the end is "הרי הוא קרוב מצד התשובה" (931, 60:3): "near by way of returning."

**Does G-d wait too?** Yes. "If it tarries, wait for it... therefore the L-rd waits to be gracious to you." "והוא יתברך חפץ בשלימות העולם" (599, 31:4): "and He desires the world's completion."

**Can I be good for the sake of reward?** "ולפיכך האדם אם הוא צדיק מפני הטוב, אין זה דבר" (767, 46:4): "so if a person is righteous because of the good he gets, it is nothing." That is why return must come before good times (766); in the days when all is complete, "שהדבר אשר הוא בפעל אינו יוצא עוד לפעל" (764, 46:1): "what is already in act does not come into act again."

**Why do good people have bad traits?** Because they grow from a good root: "הדבר הרע אשר ימצא בהם הוא יוצא ונולד מן תכונה טובה" (532, 25:13): "the bad found in them comes out of and is born from a good disposition." Jealousy comes from a soul that seeks worth; stubbornness from not being easily moved. Use the root rightly. And jealousy would vanish if one rejoiced in another's rise: "לא היה זה פירוד, רק חבור לגמרי" (528, 25:9): "that would not be separation but full joining."

**What is peace?** "השלום הוא מסולק מן ההעדר, מצד השלימות אשר בו" (521, 25:2): "peace is removed from absence, because of the wholeness in it." When people are at one, the power of loss cannot touch them. Elijah will come not only to end quarrels but so that they have "לב אחד" (835), one heart, like parents and children, "וזהו תכלית האחדות" (835, 53:8): "and this is the height of oneness." Aaron's way: he greeted even a wicked man, who then said, "אוי לי איך אשא עיני ואראה את אהרן" (829, 53:2): "woe to me, how can I lift my eyes and look at Aaron."

**Where does a mind come from?** "אין האב נותן השכל לבן, רק השם יתברך נותן השכל" (914, 59:10): "the father does not give the son his mind; only G-d gives the mind." So Torah is not inherited, and it often comes from the children of the poor.

**What is blessing?** "כי אין הקללה רק העדר המציאות, ואין ברכה רק תוספות מציאת" (922, 59:18): "for a curse is only a lack of being, and a blessing is only added being."

**Is G-d with me in trouble?** "בכל צרתם לו צר" (951, 62:1): "in all their trouble He is troubled." And "כי הוא יתברך הוא עצם גאולתן" (953, 62:3): "for He Himself is their redemption." The people are "saved by G-d", like second-tithe fruit redeemed with coins: He Himself is the price.

**What is joy for?** "וידוע כי השמחה היא בשביל השלמה העליונה" (958, 62:8): "it is known that joy is for the highest completion." Mourning is for loss; joy is for completion, and the highest completion is cleaving to Him. And song is what a complete being gives: "והאדם כאשר הוא בשלימות הגמור, אז נותן שירה ושבח למי שממנו השלימות" (735, 43:3): "when a person is fully complete, then he gives song and praise to the One from whom completeness comes."

**What is waiting for me?** Moses at the end: "I do not know what to tell you. אשריכם מה מתוקן לכם" (961, 62:11): "happy are you, what is prepared for you."

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **"He is One now; the change is in the receivers"** (719) is the same principle the Tanya uses in the Gate of Unity: there is no change in G-d, and the whole difference between before and after creation is in how the created receive. The Maharal applies it to the future oneness of the Name. For the Tanya, the world's apparent separateness is already, in truth, nothing before Him. For the Maharal, the world's division is real for now and will be made one later. This is a difference of emphasis worth noting: the Tanya asks us to see the oneness now (by contemplation); the Maharal asks us to hope for it and to live toward it (by faith and deeds), while saying plainly that from His side it is already so.
- **Two relations of caused to Cause** (720: son and servant) match the Tanya's two kinds of service, love and awe, and its image of the son of the King. The Maharal adds that now the servant side shows and the son side is hidden.
- **Jacob's Shema** (748) is a good companion to Tanya ch. 41 (awe before prayer and study) and to the Chabad practice of preparing for the Shema with a sense of the One. The two kinds of oneness in 746 (one in itself, and parts made one) are close to the Chabad distinction between G-d's own simple oneness and the unity of the worlds with Him.
- **Absence before being** (545-558) is not tzimtzum, but it rhymes with it. The Tanya says the light is hidden so that worlds can be; the Maharal says the old form must pass so that a new one can come. Both see hiding or loss as serving a higher being. The guide should not merge them.
- **Bittul and faith** (576-578: "going out of one's own domain") is close to Chabad bittul.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes**: 719 and 896 keep the Rambam's rule that nothing changes in G-d. The Maharal also uses positive words (One, truth, "He includes all"). He never says what G-d is in Himself beyond "One" and "truth" (630: "כי הוא יתברך אמת"), which is close to the Rambam's caution.
- **The Ramak's formula** again does not appear. The nearest is 813 ("He includes all") and 704 (He shares and still has no partner).
