The second half of Vayechi is Jacob's blessing of Joseph's sons and of the twelve tribes, his death, and his burial. It is the longest single range in this volume and one of the richest. The Zohar here gives its plainest statement of unity (thought, understanding, voice and speech are one, and thought is bound to Ayin and never parts from it); the soul as a prince sent to a village to be raised and brought home by the Queen; the days of a life woven into garments; the command to unite the Name "with the mouth, the heart and the soul, like a flame bound to a coal"; the order of prayer; blessing God before blessing anyone; the prayer of the many; Hezekiah turning to the wall; tears that no gate withstands; and Jacob, who "did not die." ## 2. The unity of God

**Thought, understanding, voice, speech: all one, bound to Ayin.** «וכד תסתכל בדרגין, הוא מחשבה, הוא בינה, הוא קול, הוא דבור, וכלא חד … ולא הוי פרוד, אלא כלא חד, וקשורא חד, דאיהו מחשבה ממש אתקשר באין, ולא אתפרש לעלמין, ודא הוא (זכריה י״ד:ט׳) ה' אחד ושמו אחד» (Vayechi 74:2; line 5177): "when you look at the levels, it is thought, it is understanding, it is voice, it is speech, and all is one … there is no separation, but all is one and one bond; for thought itself is bound to Ayin (Nothing) and never parts from it; and this is 'Havayah is one and His name is one.'" Ours: the single strongest unity line in this volume. It maps the Chabad picture of the soul's garments (thought, speech, action) and the Tanya's teaching that the letters of creation never part from their Source (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-2; memory), and it names Ayin, the Nothing above thought, as the root to which all is tied. Ties: F02, S62, S35; with Noach line 1521 (thought, voice, speech one).

**The soul of souls: "He and they are one."** The highest soul, never grasped, wraps itself in radiance and drips pearls that are bound together like the limbs of one body; «והוא אעיל בגווייהו, ואחזי בהו עבידתיה. הוא ואינון חד הוא, ולית בהו פרישו» (Vayechi 72:2; line 5149): "and He enters into them and shows His work through them; He and they are one, and there is no separation in them." Ties: the Tanya's "He and His vitality are one" (ch. 23; memory).

**When things are looked into, all rises to one.** The lower world has separation ("from there it parted"), «ואף על גב דאית ביה פירודא, כד יסתכלון מלי, כלא סלקא לחד» (Vayechi 65:7; line 5066): "and even though there is separation in it, when the matters are looked into, all rises to one." Ours: the practice of unity is the act of looking until the parts rise to one. Ties: technique 14.

**"That day": no parting between day and He.** «והאי יום ההוא, דלית פירודא בין יום ובין הוא» (Vayechi 48:2; line 4897): "and this 'that day' (yom hahu), there is no separation between 'day' and 'He'." The revealed day and the hidden "He" are one.

**One angel, male and female, judgement and mercy, one secret.** The redeeming angel is called male when it gives blessing and female when it is filled with judgement, «וכלא רזא חדא» (Vayechi 44:3; line 4876): "and all is one secret." Ties: Vayetzei line 3290.

**The moon with no light of its own holds every blessing.** David asked why the moon has no light of its own and was not allowed to know (line 4911); yet «אף על גב דלית לה נהורא מגרמה, כל ברכאן, וכל חידו, וכל טיבו, כלהו קיימין בה, ומנה … נפקי» (Vayechi 51:1; line 4912): "even though she has no light of her own, all blessings, all joy, all good dwell in her and go out from her"; so she is called the cup of blessing. Ours: the receiving self is not less; it is where blessing lives. Ties: S59, S65.

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **The prince brought home.** A king sends his son to a village to be raised and learn the ways of the palace; when he is grown, out of love the king sends the Queen, his mother, to bring him in. «נשמתא לא סלקא מהאי עלמא, עד דאתת מטרוניתא בגינה, ואעילת לה בהיכלא דמלכא» (Vayechi 72:6; line 5153): "the soul does not rise from this world until the Queen comes for it and brings it into the King's palace." «אילו הוו ידעין כלהו צדיקייא האי, הוו חדאן ההוא יומא דמטי לון לאסתלקא מהאי עלמא» (Vayechi 72:8; line 5155): "if all the righteous knew this, they would rejoice on the day they come to leave this world." Ours: a way to hold death, one's own or another's, as homecoming. Guard: comfort, never a wish to die. Ties: S84, S65; new state below.
- **Love strong as death.** «כך תקיפו דרחימו דכנסת ישראל לגבי קודשא בריך הוא, כתקיפו דמותא, בשעתא דבעי רוחא לאתפרשא מן גופא» (Vayechi 71:3; line 5140): "so the strength of the love of the Assembly of Israel for the Holy One is like the strength of death, at the hour the spirit must part from the body." And like a seal: though she goes into exile, «בגין דישתאר כל דיוקני בך» (Vayechi 71:2; line 5139), "so that my whole image remains in You." Ties: S13, S43; "Sick with love" (Chayei Sara).
- **Silence everywhere, except the silence of Torah.** Rabbi Shimon was silent an hour, then said: «בכל אתר בעייא שתיקו, בר שתיקו דאורייתא» (Vayechi 71:9; line 5146): "everywhere silence is needed, except the silence of Torah." Ties: "Silence that is praise" (Bereshit II), but with a limit: do not be silent when Torah should be spoken.
- **Sustained by mercy that runs ahead.** «דאי רחמיך לא יקדימו עלייהו דישראל, לא יכלין לקיימא בעלמא» (Vayechi 39:2; line 4819): "if Your mercy did not run ahead of Israel, they could not stand in the world"; He looks at their judgement alone, as a father, and passes the sins one by one until none remain (line 4818). Ties: S84, S78.
- **The donkey's humility in learning.** Issachar is "a strong-boned donkey" because the donkey carries its load, does not kick its master, has no haughtiness, and does not care where it lies: so the one who learns «ולא אית ביה גסות הרוח, כחמור דלא חייש ליקרא דיליה, אלא ליקרא דמריה» (Vayechi 68:1; line 5089): "has no haughtiness of spirit, like the donkey that cares not for its own honor but for its master's." Ties: S59.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Unite the Name with mouth, heart and soul, like a flame on a coal.** Each soul, when it comes down, is sworn by the Assembly of Israel to tell the King of her love; how? «בגין דחיובא על בר נש ליחדא שמא קדישא בפומא, בלבא בנפשא, ולאקשרא כלא, כשלהובא דאתקשרא בטיפסא» (Vayechi 67:3; line 5086): "for it is a person's duty to unite the holy Name with the mouth, with the heart, with the soul, and to bind all together like a flame bound to a coal." Ours: this is the Zohar's own instruction for the Shema and every unification; it pairs with the flame meditation (Bereshit III, line 990). New technique below. Ties: techniques 18 (lips), 2.

**Praise, then prayer, even with a troubled heart.** Rabbi Chizkiyah asked about one whose heart is troubled and cannot order the praise properly; the answer: «אף על גב דלא יכיל לכוונא לבא ורעותא, סדורא ושבחא דמריה אמאי גרע, אלא יסדר שבחיה דמאריה, אף על גב דלא יכיל לכוונא, ויצלי צלותיה» (Vayechi 69:20; line 5122): "even though he cannot aim heart and will, why should the order of his Master's praise be lessened? Let him order his Master's praise, even though he cannot aim, and then pray his prayer." Ours: do not skip the praise because the heart is not in it. Ties: technique 20.

**Bless God first, every morning.** «בכל אתר דברכאן אצטריכו לברכא, בעי קודשא בריך הוא לאתברכא בקדמיתא, ולבתר אתברכו אחרנין» (Vayechi 36:6; line 4784): "wherever blessings are to be given, the Holy One must be blessed first, and afterward others are blessed." «אסיר ליה לבר נש לברכא לחבריה בצפרא, עד דיברך לקודשא בריך הוא» (Vayechi 76:9; line 5203): "a person may not bless his friend in the morning until he has blessed the Holy One." And «מאן דמברך ליה לקודשא בריך הוא, יתברך» (Vayechi 82:10; line 5252): "whoever blesses the Holy One will be blessed." Ties: technique 2.

**Pray with the many.** «וצלותא דיחיד, לא עאל קמי מלכא קדישא, אלא בחילא תקיפא» (Vayechi 53:2; line 4922): "the prayer of a single person does not enter before the holy King except with great force"; He examines it and the person's deeds, which He does not do with the prayer of many. «בגין כך, ליבעי ליה לבר נש, דלצלי צלותא בצבורא» (Vayechi 53:3; line 4923): "therefore a person should pray his prayer with the congregation," for "He does not despise their prayer," even when not all of them pray with intention. Ties: "Among the many" (Vayetzei).

**Search your ways, then pray.** Hezekiah "turned his face to the wall, and prayed": «דמאן דאית ביה חובא, ובעי למבעי רחמי עלוי, יכוין אנפוי ורעיונוי, לאתקנא גרמיה מההוא חובא, ולבתר יבעי צלותא» (Vayechi 37:5; line 4798): "whoever has a sin and wants to ask mercy for himself, let him turn his face and thoughts to repair himself from that sin, and after that ask in prayer," as it says "let us search our ways and examine, and return." And he wept, «דלית תרעא דקיימא קמי דמעין» (Vayechi 37:9; line 4802): "for there is no gate that stands before tears." Ties: technique 24; S77.

**Serve with joy in the morning, song in the evening.** «בעי בר נש למפלח קמיה דקודשא בריך הוא בחדוה, ולאחזאה חדוה בפולחניה» (Vayechi 40:4; line 4825): "a person must serve before the Holy One in joy, and show joy in his service": joy in the morning, song in the evening, like the two daily offerings.

**The afternoon prayer, with more intention.** «בכלהו צלותא בעי בר נש לכוונא דעתיה, ובהאי צלותא יתיר מכלהו, בגין דדינא שריא בעלמא» (Vayechi 41:4; line 4830): "in all the prayers a person must aim his mind, and in this prayer more than all, because judgement rests on the world." The Temple burned at the hour of Minchah.

**Midnight: He longs for their voices.** «השתא עדנא דקודשא בריך הוא תאיב לקלהון דצדיקיא בהאי עלמא, אינון דמשתדלי באורייתא» (Vayechi 44:1; line 4874): "now is the time the Holy One longs for the voices of the righteous in this world who busy themselves with Torah." And «ולית נייחא קמיה, בר בזמנא דמתערי לתתא באורייתא» (Vayechi 43:14; line 4850): "there is no rest before Him except when they wake below in Torah." Ties: the half night practice.

**New words of Torah rise to the King.** «כל מאן דאשתדל באורייתא כדקא יאות, וידע למחדי מלין, ולחדתותי מלין כדקא יאות, אינון מלין סלקין עד כרסייא דמלכא» (Vayechi 68:13; line 5101): "whoever busies himself with Torah properly, and knows how to rejoice in words and renew words properly, those words rise to the King's throne"; the Assembly of Israel hides them, and brings them out before Him in Eden, and He looks at them and rejoices. Ties: technique 22.

**Praise with a sweet voice.** «כל מאן דבעי לשבחא לקודשא בריך הוא בקלא, בעיא ליה קלא נעימותא, דיערב לאחרנין דשמעין ליה» (Vayechi 81:1; line 5232): "whoever wants to praise the Holy One aloud needs a pleasant voice, sweet to others who hear him"; if not, let him not raise his voice.

**Praise matched to worth.** «מאן דמשבח לאחרא, בשבחא דלית ביה, הוא גלי … גנותיה» (Vayechi 47:1; line 4886): "whoever praises another with praise not in him reveals his shame"; so also a eulogy "according to his worth, and no more."

## 5. Big human questions

- **What do my days become?** «ואינון לבושי יקר, דמתלבשא ביה נשמתיה. ואינון יומין הוו, דזכה בהו, ולא חב בהו» (Vayechi 32:6; line 4707): "and they are garments of honor in which his soul is clothed; they are the days in which he was worthy and did not sin." Days lost to sin leave holes in the garment (line 4708). And yet: Adam returned, «וקודשא בריך הוא קביל ליה, ועביד ליה מאנא … לבושא אחרנין, ולא מן יומוי» (Vayechi 32:8; line 4709): "and the Holy One accepted him and made him other garments, not from his days." Ours: return makes new clothing where the old was lost. Ties: S77.
- **Why do souls come down if they will only die?** Rabbi Elazar asked it (line 4943). The answer: a "cistern" does not flow by itself; when a soul completes itself in this world and rises, the cistern becomes a well of living water (lines 4944-4945). Ours: each life fills something above that could not be filled otherwise.
- **Why was I made to suffer illness?** When a person is sick and cannot pray, his soul rises to be judged, and the body is pressed; if the judgement is for good, the sweat breaks and the soul returns (lines 4769-4771). And God judges for good even the one not yet worthy «והוא חמי דהא לבתר זכי» (Vayechi 35:5; line 4771): "for He sees that afterward he will be worthy," or that he will have a righteous child.
- **Were we known before we were born?** «כל נשמתין דנחתין לעלמא, עד לא ייחתון, כלהו קיימי קמיה דקודשא בריך הוא, בדיוקנא דקיימי בהאי עלמא, ואקרון בשמהן» (Vayechi 35:10; line 4776): "all souls that come down to the world, before they come down, all stand before the Holy One in the image they have in this world, and are called by name." Ties: S65.
- **Is there death at all for the righteous?** «יעקב לא מית, בגין כך לא אתמר ביה מותא» (Vayechi 79:3; line 5215): "Jacob did not die; therefore death is not said of him," only "he expired and was gathered"; the sun was gathered to the moon (same line).
- **Do the living and the dead still speak?** In a time of distress, when a Torah scroll is carried out and people pray at graves, the nefesh tells the ruach, the ruach the neshamah, the neshamah the Holy One, and He has mercy (lines 4727-4728, 4739). Rachel at the crossroads does more than all (line 4740).
- **Why is kindness withheld?** Solomon thought, once sun and moon had joined, that the right hand (kindness) was no longer needed; «אנת דחית ימינא, חייך, אנת תצטרך לחסד בני נשא, ולא תשכח» (Vayechi 82:7; line 5249): "you pushed away the right; by your life, you will need people's kindness and not find it." Ours: no level is so high that it no longer needs kindness.
- **Why are there rich and poor?** (See also Vayigash line 4336.) Zebulun who fed Issachar is blessed first: «מאן דסעיד למריה דאורייתא, נטיל ברכאן מעילא ותתא» (Vayechi 66:13; line 5083): "whoever supports a master of Torah receives blessings from above and below."

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Thought, understanding, voice, speech, all one, bound to Ayin (line 5177).** The strongest unity sentence in Zohar vol. 1.
2. **Unite the Name with mouth, heart and soul, like a flame on a coal (line 5086).**
3. **The prince brought home by the Queen (lines 5152-5155).** Death as homecoming.
4. **Praise then prayer, even with a troubled heart (line 5122); bless God first (lines 4784, 5203).**
5. **No gate stands before tears (line 4802); search your ways, then pray (line 4798).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "Flame on the coal."** At the Shema or any unification: say the words (mouth), feel them (heart), give yourself into them (soul), and hold the three bound together like a flame that does not leave its coal (line 5086). Pairs with "The flame" (Bereshit III). Ties techniques 18, 2.
- **Technique, new: "Thought to speech, one."** Before speaking a holy word, trace it back: the word, the voice, the understanding, the thought, and the Nothing the thought is bound to; then speak it as one (line 5177). Ties technique 14; S35.
- **State, new: "Brought home."** Inside: thinking of death (one's own, or a loved one's) as the Queen coming to bring the grown son into the palace; grief remains, but under it a quiet gladness (lines 5152-5155). How to enter: with the grieving, tell the parable; for oneself, at bedtime. Guard: comfort only. Near S84.
- **Technique, new: "Praise anyway."** When the heart is troubled and prayer will not come, still say the order of praise first, then ask (line 5122). Ties technique 20.
- **Technique, new: "Search, then ask."** Before asking mercy for yourself, first turn "face and thoughts" to repairing the thing you did; then pray; let tears come if they come (lines 4798, 4802). Ties S77, technique 24.
- **State, new: "Cup of blessing."** Inside: knowing you have no light of your own, and feeling that very emptiness as the place where every blessing gathers (line 4912). Near S59, S65.
