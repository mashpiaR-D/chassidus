This range is the Maharal's handbook of prayer and of giving. The Path of Service (Avodah) walks through the day: waking, the Shema, the Amidah, bowing, Amen, blessings over food, grace after meals, and the Shema at bedtime. Then come the paths of chesed (lovingkindness), tzedakah (charity), judgment and truth.

It is the richest range in the book for practice. Nearly every custom of prayer is read as a way of standing before the One: as an effect before its Cause, as a receiver before the Giver, as nothing before the All.

## 2.1 Service shows He is One

The Path of Service opens with this: "כאשר מקריבין אליו קרבן מורה זה כי הכל שלו וכאשר הכל הוא שלו אם כן אין זולתו" (412, Avodah 1:1): "when one brings Him an offering it shows that all is His, and when all is His there is none besides Him."

Prayer does the same from the human side. It "מורה שהאדם נתלה בו יתברך וצריך אליו ואין לו קיום זולתו, והנה זהו אלהותו יתברך" (421): "shows that a person hangs on Him and needs Him and has no standing without Him, and this is His divinity." For the Maharal, "divinity" means just this: that everything hangs on Him.

At night too: "מפני שהוא אחד הכל שב אליו כי אין זולתו יתברך" (423): "because He is One, all returns to Him, for there is none besides Him." The day prayers come from the world that exists. The evening prayer comes from the fact that there is nothing besides Him at all.

## 2.2 "Hashem is One": oneness with no joining

Why does the Shema say "Hashem our G-d" and then "Hashem is One"? "Our G-d" is His relation to us. "One" is more: "כי אחדותו בעצמו מבלי צרוף כלל" (444): "for His oneness is in Himself, with no joining at all." He is One not only as our G-d but in Himself.

And the length of the word: "מצד אחדותו הוא בלתי תכלית והוא נצחי כי במה שזהו ענין שהוא אחד כי אין זולתו" (447): "from His oneness He is without end and eternal, for this is what it means that He is One: there is none besides Him." What is not one lives in a slice of time. What is One has no edge. So we lengthen "echad", until "המליכו למעלה ולמטה" (447): "you have crowned Him above and below" and on four sides.

## 2.3 Bowing: nothing besides Him

The high point of the range is the teaching on bowing (454-457). One bows because "כי אין נמצא עם השם יתברך שום מציאות והכל אפס זולתו יתברך" (454): "for with G-d no being exists, and all is nothing besides Him." Bowing is handing the self over and cancelling one's own being before Him. Rising at the Name is the other side: all beings stand from Him.

Then comes a fine point. At the blessing of the Fathers each person bows by himself, since each has his own relation to G-d as Father. At Modim the prayer leader and all the people bow together. Why? Because "אין בזה הבדל בין איש לאיש ואינו כמו ההויה שהיא לכל אחד ואחד בפ"ע" (454): "in this there is no difference between one person and another, and it is not like being, which belongs to each one by himself." *Being is private. Nothingness is shared.* Before the One, all are equal because none is anything.

And the pace: bow at once, rise slowly, since "כל הויית האדם הוא בזמן ובטול שלו בלא זמן כלל" (457): "all a person's coming-to-be is in time, and his nullifying is with no time at all."

## 2.4 Twoness and the One

The Shema at bedtime guards against harm because "כאשר קורא ק"ש דבק באחדות ונצול מן השניות" (516): "when he reads the Shema he cleaves to oneness and is saved from twoness." Harm comes from division. The One who "מקשר ומאחד הדברים אשר הם מחולקים" (441): "binds and unites the things that are divided" is why we mention night in the day blessing: to deny two powers.

The thanksgiving offering brought both leaven and matza, since "ומי שהוא אחד כולל הכל אף שני ההפכים" (551): "and the One includes everything, even two opposites." An angel rules water or fire. Only the One rules both.

## 2.5 The world is one because all things borrow

Lending shows oneness: "ההלואה זה מורה על שהם אחד כאשר זה מקבל מזה וזה מקבל מזה" (593): "lending shows that they are one, when this one receives from that and that one from this." Day lends to night, the moon to the stars. And "שהעולם נמצא מן הש"י שהוא אחד ולכך העולם הוא אחד" (593): "for the world comes from G-d who is One, and so the world is one." If creatures did not need each other, people would say there were two powers. So refusing to lend or give is a kind of twoness, and the Maharal calls closing one's eyes to charity like idolatry (566).

## 2.6 Truth is one

"כי אין דבר שהוא אחד רק האמת הוא אחד" (618): "for nothing is one except truth, which is one." Ask what a person is. Every false answer (a beast, a bird) is one of many. Only the true answer is one. So G-d's seal is truth. And "והיו"ד שהיא ראשונה בשמו הגדול ית' מורה על שהוא אחד" (626): "and the yud, first in His great Name, shows that He is One." The yud is the one letter you cannot divide.

## 4. Meditation and practice

The Path of Service is a full practice manual. The Maharal's own words give these steps.

## 4.1 Two doorways (new: `mh_two_doorways`)

"האחד הסלוק מכל הדברים, והשני להתייחד עם השם יתברך" (426): "the first, stepping away from all things, and the second, being alone with G-d." One without the other fails. If you turn to Him but do not first step out, your thought is mixed. How: before you begin, name what you are putting down. Then turn. Source: 426. Close to `arrive` and `before_whom`.

## 4.2 Stand as an effect before its Cause (new: `mh_stand_as_effect`)

- Pray from a low place (430). - Feet together: no spreading out, since "כי ההתפשטות הוא הסרה והסתלקות מן העלה" (431): "for spreading out is turning away and leaving the Cause." - Hands folded over the heart; eyes down in awe, heart up in longing (432). - "As if the Shechina is facing me" (433): this is `before_whom`. - "וכאשר האדם עושה עצמו כבשר שהלכה הצורה ממנו נחשב כאלו אינו כלל ואז תפלתו נשמעת" (434): "when a person makes himself like flesh whose form has left it, he counts as if he were not at all, and then his prayer is heard." Even a blade of grass has a form. Let go even of that.

## 4.3 Speak your lack aloud (`lips`, `own_words`)

"צריך שיהיה מבקש חסרונו בדבור" (417): "he must ask for what he lacks in speech." A person is a speaking being. To receive, bring the lack out in words. Whisper what is hidden (418); speak what is revealed.

## 4.4 Lengthen "echad" to the six sides (`mh_six_sides`, from Gevurot Hashem)

447: hold the word until you have crowned Him above, below and on four sides. Not too long; once is enough; do not split the letters.

## 4.5 Bow at once, rise slowly (new: `mh_bow_at_once`)

457: bow like a rod, all at once, because letting go takes no time. Rise like a snake, slowly, because coming into being takes time. At Modim bow together with everyone (454).

## 4.6 Amen with all your strength (`mh_silent_trust`)

460: answer Amen with all your heart. Faith that He can do all gives rest, "ולכן לא ימצא אצלו צער" (460): "and so no pain is found in him". The believer is "like a peg in a sure place".

## 4.7 Silence (`quieting`)

"סמא דכולא משתוקא" (465): "the medicine for everything is silence." Too much praise pulls Him down into words. The Maharal also objects to adding long praises daily and to prayers addressed to angels (466-468).

## 4.8 Thanks and blessing (`mh_hodaah`)

"שכל הודאה מוסר עצמו אל השם יתברך" (512): "for in every thanks one hands oneself over to G-d." Bless before you enjoy (475): the world is His; the blessing is how you receive it from Him.

## 4.9 Give like a spring (new: `mh_spring`)

"וכל שמוסיף לקלח המעין לחוץ המקור יותר נותן לו" (563): "the more the spring pours outward, the more the Source gives it." And He is "מקור חיים אשר משפיע תמיד" (564): "the Source of life, which gives always." To give at all times is to be like Him. How: give something each day, even small, and give words of comfort too, which the Maharal says are worth more than a coin (576).

## 4.10 Bedtime Shema (`letters`, `hold`)

516: read the Shema in bed to cling to oneness. Sleep is handing the soul back to its treasury; waking is being born anew (423). Pray on rising.

## 5. The big human questions

**Needing.** A person's need is not a shame. It is where G-d's divinity shows: we hang on Him (421), "אין לאדם קיום מצדו רק מצד הש"י" (500): "a person has no standing from his own side, only from G-d's."

**Being equal.** Being is private; nothingness is shared (454). Pride divides; bowing unites.

**Love and awe.** Love unites: those who serve in love "מתאחדים ביחד מצד הש"י שהוא אחד" (474): "become one together from the side of G-d, who is One". Awe separates: "כל אחד עושה רצון קונו בפני עצמו" (474): "each one does his Maker's will by himself". Both belong; love is higher.

**Why the world exists.** The Maharal gives a striking reason. The world was made so that the created could also give: "כי בזה דומה המושפע אל הש"י המשפיע העולם כי הדומה משפיע הדומה" (528): "for in this the receiver becomes like G-d who gives to the world, for the like gives the like." We are made to receive, and then to give like Him.

**Copying G-d.** Only in chesed, "כי מדה זאת היא מדת השם ית' היא מדה עצמית" (523): "for this trait is G-d's trait, a trait of His very self." Judgment is not ours to copy; we err. Chesed we can. And chesed is endless because it comes "from His own side" (508), not from the receiver's worth.

**The world's footing.** "והעולם דבק בו ית' לגמרי מצד החסד אבל לא מצד המשפט" (537): "and the world cleaves to Him wholly through chesed, but not through judgment." Judgment sets Him apart like a king judging; chesed joins.

**Suffering and poverty.** Why doesn't G-d feed the poor Himself? So that we may give and be saved (575). The poor person does more for the giver than the giver for him (588).

**Marriage.** "והיא הויה אלקית עד שאין זה הויה טבעית" (550): "and it is a divine coming-to-be, not a natural one." By nature man and woman are different; they are made one by the Name between them.

**Receiving the Shechina in a person.** Welcoming a guest, rising for a mother, blessing the new moon: each is receiving the Shechina, "כמו שיתאחד הניצוץ עם הנר" (473): "as the spark unites with the flame". The human face is the image of G-d.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Nothing besides Him** (454) is the same claim as the Gate of Unity: "all is nothing before Him." The Maharal reaches it through bowing and through cause and effect. He does not use tzimtzum or the constant word of creation.
- **"Hashem is One" as oneness with no joining** (444) agrees with the Rambam's negative path: His oneness is not a oneness of parts or of relation. The Maharal keeps "our G-d" (relation) and "One" (no relation) side by side. The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7) reads "Hashem our G-d" as the higher unity above "Hashem is One". Different order, same two levels.
- **Lengthening echad to six sides** is the Talmud's own instruction. The Ramak's formula (He is the knower, the known and the knowing) does not appear. The Maharal's "knowing is joining" (from the Path of Torah) is near it.
- **Bowing and Modim**: the Maharal's "nothingness is shared" is not found in the Tanya. It fits Chabad's teaching that ahavat Yisrael comes from the soul's root, but grounds it in shared nothingness, not shared root.
- **The spring**: the Tanya (ch. 34-37) speaks of tzedakah as drawing G-d's light below. The Maharal's spring is the same flow from the other end: the giver is fed by the Source.
