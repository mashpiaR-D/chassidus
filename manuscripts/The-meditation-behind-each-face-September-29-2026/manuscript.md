## Why meditation

Every concept in Chassidus points to one thing, the unity of God, and that unity cannot be understood: by definition it is beyond the mind. The Tanya says so and also says what to do about it. Knowing, *da'at*, is binding the mind to a truth until it is as real as a thing seen with the eyes; without that binding, even a wise understanding breeds only imaginings (Likkutei Amarim 3 and 42). Where the mind ends, faith carries on. So what a concept gives is not information but a glimpse, and the glimpse comes in contemplation (*hisbonenus*). Every concept is really a meditation.

Below, for each of the twelve faces the one idea takes across Chassidus, is the meditation behind it. The method is Jewish, the way Chabad contemplates (hisbonenus), never borrowed from mindfulness. Each meditation gives one line of teaching as the object, how to think it through (detail by detail, then down into your own life), the Tanya's own picture and where it breaks, binding the mind to the line and bringing it back when it drifts, what it is like from inside (described, never demanded), saying it with the lips in words of Torah or prayer, and one mitzvah or act to carry it into the day. This is the shape of a sitting in `Chabad Library/hisbonenus/paths/CRAFT.md`. Beside it stand other Jewish practices the sources give: setting God before you ('I have set God before me always', Shulchan Aruch HaRav OC 1:5), the Shema's 'One' drawn out with its own thought (OC 61:5-6), and pouring out the heart in one's own words (Likutei Moharan 52). Six faces already have a full verified path, linked. Four do not yet: the Essence (F05), opposites (F09), Torah and mitzvah (F10), and darkness turned to light (F11). Their meditations here rest on the face's own anchors and are the outline for paths still to be built.

## F01 The world said into being now

*Every created thing, stone and dust included, exists only because the letters of God's speech stand in it at this moment and continuously bring it from nothing into being. If they left for an instant it would return to absolute nothing. Providence is this same continuous creation, seen in each particular.*

**The line.** The word that made this is saying it right now.

**Think it through.** The table, the window, the street outside, your own hand: each one is being said this second. Not 'was made', but 'is being made'. If the word stopped for an instant, it wouldn't fall or break; it simply wouldn't be. Then make it particular: you, here, now, are being said.

**The picture.** His word never leaves Him the way our words leave us (Tanya 21), so there is no second you are outside Him. Only a picture; His speech is not like ours at all.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

**From inside.** Being held, not holding. (Described, never demanded; if nothing moves, the thinking still counts.)

**Say it.** 'There is nothing besides Him.'

**Do.** Touch the mezuzah on the way out with the line in mind.

**Full path.** ein_od_milvado

## F02 The higher and the lower unity

*From His side ('before Him', from above downward) the world is nothing at all, not even 'another' thing beside Him. That is the higher unity of Shema. From the world's side (from below upward) the world is a real, separate thing that He fills and rules in time and space through Malchus. That is the lower unity of Baruch Shem. Both are true, and the second exists for the sake of the first.*

**The line.** From His side there is only Him; from ours, He fills everything.

**Think it through.** With the Shema: cover your eyes and say 'One', drawing it out as the Alter Rebbe teaches, on its ches that He reigns in the heavens and the earth, on its dalet that He alone is in His world and rules the four directions. Everything folds into it: not one among many, the only. Then 'Blessed be the name of His glorious kingdom forever', and the world comes back, real, full of Him, ruled by Him.

**The picture.** A ray inside the sun has no existence of its own (Shaar HaYichud 10). Only a picture.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

**From inside.** Lightness, then a world full of Him. (Described, never demanded; if nothing moves, the thinking still counts.)

**Say it.** The Shema's first verse and 'Blessed be the name…'.

**Do.** The bedtime Shema, with these two thoughts.

**Full path.** ein_od_milvado

## F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation

*The world's apparent independence is a concealment worked by God's own power (gevurah, the Name Elokim, 'nature'). It conceals only toward the creatures and is itself a kindness. Its purpose is revelation: there is no king without a people who stand apart from him. Once one knows He is hiding there, it is no longer a hiding.*

**The line.** The hiding is Him hiding, out of love.

**Think it through.** Take the part of the day that feels emptiest of Him: what seems to run by itself, the hard thing. The screen is His own power, the Name that means 'nature'; it hides only toward us. He set His great light aside and hid it for love of the lowly person, to raise him (Tanya 49). Once you know He is hiding there, it is no longer a hiding.

**The picture.** A king who comes down from his palace to lift a man from the dust and bring him inside, room after room (Tanya 46). Only a picture.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

**From inside.** Finding what was never gone, and the heart answering back. (Described, never demanded; if nothing moves, the thinking still counts.)

**Do.** Say a blessing slowly over the thing that seemed empty of Him.

## F04 Filling and surrounding

*He is present in two ways that are not the same relation. As 'filling' (memale), He is the vitality clothed inside each creature, contracted to its measure. As 'surrounding' (sovev), He is the light that is not revealed within any creature. That light is not 'above' in place, and it holds all equally. The very 'somethingness' of matter comes from the surrounding light, not from the filling light.*

**The line.** He fills each thing to its measure and surrounds all things alike.

**Think it through.** The life in a leaf fits a leaf, the life in you fits you, contracted to each one's size. Around all of it is the light no thing can hold, not 'up there' but around the near and the far the same; the very solidity of the chair comes from it.

**The picture.** Held from inside and from all around. Only a picture; He has no inside or outside.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

**From inside.** Being carried, with no edge. (Described, never demanded; if nothing moves, the thinking still counts.)

**Say it.** 'I have set God before me always.'

**Do.** Say it with the lips before the thing you fear.

**Full path.** surrounded

## F05 The Essence beyond both

*Beyond higher and lower, infinite and finite, 'nothing' and 'something', is the Essence. It is defined by neither, and so it alone can create a 'something' from absolute nothing and be present in the finite. The physical, which feels uncaused, comes from the Essence. Even the whole encompassing order counts as 'one detail' next to it.*

**The line.** Only He is simply there, so the plainest thing points to Him.

**Think it through.** Lights and levels are His radiance. Bread does not feel like radiance at all; it is just there. Only the Essence makes a something from absolute nothing (Iggeret HaKodesh 20), so the plain just-thereness of things points past every level to Him. The lowest points to the highest.

**The picture.** Chosen, not for anything reached. Only a picture.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

## F06 The soul as part of the One

*The divine soul is literally a part of God above, drawn from His wisdom, which is one with Him. Its innermost level, the yechidah, is always united with the Essence, so no impurity reaches it (hence Modeh Ani before washing). Because all souls share one Father, Jews are literally brothers, and love of one's fellow is rooted in that unity.*

## F07 The home below

*The purpose of the whole chain of worlds is not the upper worlds but this lowest, physical one. God desired a dwelling here, where His Essence is 'at home' and revealed below as above. Nothing changes in Him; the change is only in the receivers. The dwelling is made by our deeds during exile, as the 'something' becomes nullified to Him and a vessel for Him.*

**The line.** He made the world to live in plain places like mine.

**Think it through.** The whole chain of worlds was made for this lowest one (Tanya 36). This kitchen, this Tuesday, this work: each is a room He wanted to live in. The deeds done here are what make it His home.

**The picture.** The joy of a plain man whose king moves into his little house to live with him (Tanya 33). Only a picture.

**Bind the mind.** Say the line again, slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought, and keep thinking it through; the pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.

## F09 The unity of opposites

*What cannot be joined in created things (fire and water, love and fear, good and evil, forbidding and permitting) is joined in Him, because He is the 'nothing' that joins them. The perfection of His Essence 'bears all opposites'. Only the Essence, which has no opposite, can turn evil into good.*

**The line.** What can't meet in me meets in Him.

**Think it through.** Fire and water make peace when the light that is no trait at all shines in them (Iggeret HaKodesh 12). Weeping lodged on one side of the heart, joy on the other (Tanya 34).

## F11 Darkness turned to light

*The unity is shown most fully not by escaping the dark but by transforming it. The 'public domain' of the many becomes the 'private domain' of the Single One, and the evil inclination is 'spiced' into good. The power to transform comes only from the Essence, so the transformation is where the Essence is most revealed. Hence the last and darkest generations carry the greatest task.*

**The line.** The dark I turn becomes the brightest light.

**Think it through.** The unity is not shown by running from the dark but by turning it. Each time the pull is refused, His glory rises from the very place it pulled (Tanya 27, 33); light that came out of darkness shines more.

**The picture.** The advantage of light that comes out of darkness. Only a picture.

## F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart'

*The unity must be known by real comprehension, not only believed. It must also be brought from the mind to the heart and into prayer, until one sees Ein Sof resting in the lowest physical thing.
