## What this range is

It begins with grief. The Midrash HaNe'elam on Eichah is a correspondence between the exiles in Babylon and those left in the Land of Israel, each saying they have more reason to weep; then God weeps, the patriarchs and Moshe rise from their graves, and Rachel will not be comforted. At midnight the Shechinah walks through the ruined Temple and speaks to her bed and her altar.

Then it turns to the Tikkunim. The Sifra Tanina ("second book") and the Tikuna Kadma'ah ("first tikkun") open, like the Tikkunei Zohar, with "and the wise will shine like the radiance of the sky". They read the letters, vowel points and cantillation of the Torah, Ezekiel's chariot, the order of prayer, tefillin and the Shema as ways to raise the Shechinah from exile. Among the letter mysticism are some of the clearest lines on the Cause of causes in the whole Zohar corpus. The additions close with free will and a hymn to the hidden letters.

## The unity of G-d

**One without partner and without number.** The Tikuna Kadma'ah reads "See now that I, I am He, and there is no god with Me":

> דאיהו יחיד קדם כלא, ולית לה שתפא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 91, line 4380)
> *For He is single before all, and has no partner.*

> חד בלא חשבן ובלא שתוף (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 91, line 4380)
> *One without number and without partnership.* (Unlike male and female, who are one "in partnership".)

**No measure, no division.** The Sifra Tanina, on the Cause of causes:

> ולית לה מדה ושעור (ZC Sifra Tanina 104, line 4286)
> *And He has no measure and no size.*

> מסטרא דעלת כל העלות. אחד בלא שתוף (ZC Sifra Tanina 104, line 4286)
> *From the side of the Cause of all causes: one without partnership.*

> לית תמן נדות ולא רחוק, ולא קצוץ, ולא פרוד (ZC Sifra Tanina 105, line 4287)
> *There is no distance there, no being far, no cutting and no separation.*

> ומאן דמיחד לה בה, כאלו מיחד לה ואמליך לה על כל עלאין ותתאין (ZC Sifra Tanina 105, line 4287)
> *Whoever unifies Him in her [the Shechinah] is as if he unified Him and crowned Him over all the upper and lower ones.*

> ולאו אנון כגונא דבר נש דגופה מינא חדא, ונשמתה מינא אחרא, אבל לעלא כלא חד (ZC Sifra Tanina 102, line 4284)
> *They are not like a person whose body is of one kind and whose soul of another; above, all is one.*

**He, His Name and His titles are one.**

> קודשא בריך הוא הוא ושמה וכנויה כלא חד (ZC Sifra Tanina 40, line 4222)
> *The Holy One, He and His Name and His titles are all one.* (Not like a person whose name and titles are separate from him.)

> ואיהו לאו איהו כנוי (ZC Sifra Tanina 92, line 4274)
> *And He is not a title* (all the titles and names are His, and He is none of them).

**Inside as outside.** In the additions under Shelach:

> הכי איהו מלגאו דכל עלמין כמו מלבר דכל עלמין לא אשתני (ZC additions, Shelach 18, line 4410)
> *So He is within all worlds as He is outside all worlds; He does not change.*

> ומלכא דכלהו איהו אחד (ZC additions, Shelach 18, line 4410)
> *And the King of them all is One.*

This is "fills all worlds and surrounds all worlds" (F02) in a single sentence, with "He does not change" added.

> הוא בכל אתר ודאי (ZC additions, Shelach 12, line 4404)
> *[The Name of the Lord] is surely in every place.*

> יהו'ה איהו בכל ספירה וספירה (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 32, line 4321)
> *YHVH is in every single sefirah.* (So one may pray to Him through any of them; all goes to YHVH "who is in every place".)

**Like lighting a lamp from a lamp.** The Tikuna Kadma'ah switches to Hebrew for this:

> כמדליק נר מנר, ואינו חסר מן הראשונה (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 44, line 4333)
> *Like lighting a lamp from a lamp; the first is not lessened.* (So He descended into each sefirah "to no end and no limit".)

> ומי שמכיר אותו בזאת, כאלו מכיר אותו בעליונים ובתחתונים (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 44, line 4333)
> *And whoever recognizes Him in "this" [the Shechinah] is as if he recognized Him in the upper and lower worlds.*

> דודאי לא ברא קודשא בריך הוא מלתא דלאו הוא צריך (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 93, line 4382)
> *For surely the Holy One created nothing that was not needed.*

**The last vision is no vision.** Ezekiel saw nine visions; the tenth, the Crown, he did not describe:

> ואפל על פני ולא אמר מאי חזא (ZC Sifra Tanina 40, line 4222)
> *"And I fell on my face": and he did not say what he saw.*

**Seen with closed eyes.** The hymn of the letters at the very end:

> בסתימו דעינין חמאן לה (ZC additions, Vayera 6, line 4418)
> *With closed eyes they see it* (with stopped ears they hear it; with open eyes it hides).

> כל סוכלתנו בה הדרא לאחורא (ZC additions, Vayera 6, line 4418)
> *All understanding turns back from it.*

> חד הוא חד להוי חד אקרי (ZC additions, Vayera 7, line 4419)
> *He is One, He will be One, He is called One.*

## States of consciousness

**The mother who comforted (S73 Exile inside; S13).** The exiles remember the Shechinah:

> וממללת על לבנא, כאמא לברה (ZC Eichah 3, line 4062)
> *And she spoke to our heart, like a mother to her son.*

> בגין לאגנא עלנא (ZC Eichah 5, line 4064)
> *To shield us.* (When the King raised the strap, she stood in front and took the lashes.)

> ורוחנא אזלת אבתרה (ZC Eichah 58, line 4117)
> *And our spirit went after her* (and she flew off, and we were left bewildered).

We propose (ours): **Z-S22 Missing the Mother.** The ache for the Presence that once comforted and is now gone. Different from S13 (the unnamed longing) because it knows what it misses. The Zohar Chadash does not rush to fix it. It lets it be named and then speaks of hope (4138).

> איכה יחידתא דילי, דאתיחדא עמי ביחודא (ZC Eichah 12, line 4071)
> *"How", My only one, who was united with Me in unity.*

> שכינתא מבכה על בנהא (ZC Eichah 47, line 4106)
> *The Shechinah weeps for her children.*

**She is with us and cannot remove our troubles.**

> ולא יכלא לאעדאה מינן עאקין (ZC Eichah 54, line 4113)
> *And she cannot take the troubles from us* (yet she comes down with us in bitterness and sees us every day).

This is a rare honest line. The Presence in exile shares the pain but does not always remove it. For someone who asks "if God is with me, why is this happening?", it gives a true answer and not an easy one.

> הכי שבקנא דיוקני בך (ZC Eichah 73, line 4132)
> *So I left my image in you* (like a seal that leaves its form in the wax).

> כי יש תקוה לאחריתך (ZC Eichah 79, line 4138)
> *"For there is hope for your future."*

**Held by love (the tzaddik bound with them).** The complete tzaddik in exile:

> איהו אסור וחבוש עמהם ברחימו (ZC Sifra Tanina 95, line 4277)
> *He [God] is bound and imprisoned with them in love.*

> זכאה נשמתא דאיהי קן דילה (ZC Sifra Tanina 97, line 4279)
> *Happy is the soul that is a nest for her* (when she nests there she fears no accuser).

> ואם אשכחת צדיק בעלמא אתדבקת בה (ZC Sifra Tanina 98, line 4280)
> *And if she finds a righteous one in the world, she clings to him* (the dove who wanders the six days).

This confirms the Tikkunim's Z-S02 (making her a nest, part 4 report 1), now felt from inside (ours): **being a nest**, the quiet sense that one's mouth, prayer and soul have become a place where the Presence can rest. It is the lower counterpart of F07: not a Temple, but a person.

> דאם לית חכמה לית דחילו (ZC Sifra Tanina 92, line 4274)
> *For if there is no wisdom there is no fear* (and if no fear, no wisdom).

## Meditation and practice

**Prayer as a ladder (ties to 18 lips, 20 own words).**

> זכאה צלותא דבר נש דאיהי אית עבידת סלם (ZC Sifra Tanina 43, line 4225)
> *Happy is a person's prayer that becomes a ladder* (for the hosts of the King and Queen to go up and down).

> סליקת בחשאי בצלותא בחשאי (ZC Sifra Tanina 42, line 4224)
> *She rises in silence, in silent prayer.*

> אין עבודה אלא תפלה (ZC Sifra Tanina 32, line 4214)
> *There is no service but prayer.*

**Raise the thought to the Hidden.** The heart of the Tikkunim's prayer teaching:

> וסלקין לה במחשבה עד, ההוא מפלא (ZC Sifra Tanina 87, line 4269)
> *And they raise it in thought up to that Hidden One.*

> מיד נענים (ZC Sifra Tanina 87, line 4269)
> *At once they are answered.*

And the Tikuna Kadma'ah adds: "to no end and no limit" (line 4322). We propose (ours): **Z-T22 The stone and the sling.** Put the words of prayer (the stone) in the mouth (the sling) and throw them, in thought, past every name and level to the Hidden One who has no partner. Do not stop at any image. The Zohar Chadash gives this image four times; the key line is 4268:

> זכאה איהו מאן דשוי לה בקירטא דילה, דאיהי פומוי, בקריאת שמע (ZC Sifra Tanina 86, line 4268)
> *Happy is one who puts it in his sling, which is his mouth, in the Shema.*

It is technique 3 (one line) with a direction: through, not to.

**Stand as a poor man at the gate (ties to 20 own words).**

> צריך בר נש למהוי איהו כעני לתרעא דמלכא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 29, line 4318)
> *A person must be like a poor man at the King's gate* (in the standing prayer, for the sake of the Shechinah).

**Pray from love and fear, not for reward.**

> דלא מצלין אלא מגו רחימו ודחילו דמאריהון (ZC Sifra Tanina 71, line 4253)
> *Who pray only out of love and fear of their Master.*

> זכאה איהו מאן דלא נטיל איהו אגרא לגרמה (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 2, line 4291)
> *Happy is one who does not take the reward for himself.*

> אל תהיו כעבדים המשמשים את הרב על מנת לקבל פרס (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 23, line 4312)
> *Do not be like servants who serve the master in order to receive a reward.*

> דלא ליקרא דילי עבידנא אלא ליקרא דשכינתא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 33, line 4322)
> *For not for my own honor do I do this, but for the honor of the Shechinah.*

> אלא במה שלבו חפץ (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 23, line 4312)
> *Only in what his heart desires.* (Some hearts desire the secrets, some the plain meaning.)

**Look at the words and at what they hang on.**

> ואסתכלין במלי דאוריתא ברעיתא בהרהורא דלבא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 14, line 4303)
> *And they look into the words of Torah with will, with the musing of the heart.*

> בגין דלית מלה דלא תליא על רזא אחרא עלאה (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 14, line 4303)
> *For there is no word that does not hang on another, higher secret.*

**Every limb a gift.** One of the most beautiful lines in this range:

> דלית דורונא חביבא קדם קדשא בריך הוא כדורונא דשכינתא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 58, line 4347)
> *For there is no gift dearer before the Holy One than the gift of the Shechinah.*

> דבגינה נחית קדשא בריך הוא לשריא בכל אבר דילה (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 58, line 4347)
> *For through it the Holy One comes down to dwell in each of his limbs* (every limb that does a mitzvah).

We propose (ours): **Z-T23 The limb as gift.** Before a mitzvah, notice the hand or foot or mouth that will do it and offer that limb as a gift; after, rest a moment in the sense that He now dwells there. It is the practical side of Z-T09 (crown Him limb by limb) and Z-T17 (Shema through the body).

> בלבא בפומא ובמחשבה (ZC additions, Shelach 10, line 4402)
> *With the heart, with the mouth, and with thought* (we say "Hear, Israel" every day).

> במדה שאדם מודד, בה מודדין לו (ZC Sifra Tanina 50, line 4232)
> *By the measure a person measures, so he is measured.* (As he crowns the Shechinah in this world, so his soul is crowned.)

> הכי קודשא בריך הוא אמליך לה על שמיא וארעא (ZC Tikuna Kadma'ah 102, line 4391)
> *So the Holy One crowns him over heaven and earth* (as he crowned Him over heaven, earth and the four directions).

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus agrees and depends on much of this. "One without number and without partnership" (4380) is exactly the Rambam's and the Tanya's "one, but not in number". "He, His Name and His titles are one" (4222) and "He is not a title" (4274) are what Chassidus means by the Essence that is beyond names yet present in them. "Within all worlds as outside all worlds, He does not change" (4410) is the Tanya's memale and sovev with the key words "does not change". "Like lighting a lamp from a lamp" (4333) is the classic image for emanation that loses nothing. The Shechinah in exile and the call to raise her (4183, 4240, 4246) is the ground of the Chassidic avodah of "for the sake of unifying the Holy One and His Shechinah".

Where Chassidus goes further: the Tikkunim here ask that we "raise the Shechinah" by learning secrets and praying with intention. Chassidus, following the Baal Shem Tov, says this is done also in plain acts done with love, eating, talking, working, which the Zohar Chadash only hints at (ZC Shir HaShirim 629, report 5; the King's table, report 6). And where the Midrash on Eichah lets the Shechinah say "she cannot take our troubles away" (4113), Chassidus insists on bitachon: that the good will be revealed. The guide should hold both: honesty about pain and trust.

What the guide should take (ours): the lines on the Cause of causes (4380, 4286-4287, 4222, 4274, 4410, 4333) as the Zohar Chadash's core unity teaching; Z-S22 and Z-S02 (being a nest); Z-T22 and Z-T23; lines 4113 and 4138 as a pair for someone in exile inside; the teshuvah cluster (4381, 4208, 4412, 4392, 4365) for someone who has fallen; and 4413 (free will) for someone who fears they were made wicked. Leave out the letter permutations and angel names (4185-4186, 4210, 4263, 4282), the magic of names, the claims about "ignorant" prayer (4237), the date calculations for the end (4351-4358), the passage on the foreskin buried in earth (4389) and the lines against the nations (4412, second half).
