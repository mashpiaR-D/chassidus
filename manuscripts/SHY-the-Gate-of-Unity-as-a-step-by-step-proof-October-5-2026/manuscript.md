## Summary: how this text reasons (one page)

Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah (the Gate of Unity and Faith, Tanya part 2) is not a list of claims about G-d. It is a proof, built in order, where each chapter answers a question the last chapter left open. That is its mark.

It starts with a purpose, not a thesis (Chinuch Katan): the love every person can reach is "made" by thinking, and the first thing to think about is His unity. Then it starts the proof from a word in a verse that seems too strong ("Know today... there is nothing else": who thinks there is a god under the sea?). It answers with one premise taken from the Baal Shem Tov: G-d's word did not create once and leave; it stands inside every created thing now, down to a stone. It defends that premise against the best rival picture (the silversmith whose cup outlives his hands) by pointing to the root difference: something from nothing, not something from something. Then it draws the inference: what exists only through the power inside it counts as nothing next to that power, the way a sunbeam inside the sun is not a "thing".

Here is the move that makes it a real proof and not a sermon: the author tells you where his own parable fails. The beam on earth looks real because the sun is far away. But the creature is never away from its source. So why does it not vanish? He builds the second half of the book to answer that. The hiding (tzimtzum, the Name Elokim, nature) is a power of G-d, as far beyond us as creation itself. It is kindness inside restraint, and it is one with the Name that gives being (Havaya). So it hides only from us, never from Him. Before Him the earth is not even an "also". Why hide at all? Because a King needs a people that feels separate, and that is what space and time are for. So there are two true sentences, and he keeps both: before Him everything is nothing (the upper unity), and He is found in this place and this hour exactly as in heaven (the lower unity).

Then the book climbs past every picture it used. He knows the world by knowing Himself, so nothing is added to Him. That is the knock-down answer to those who read tzimtzum literally, since they too believe He knows everything. Every name we give Him is His one simple Self. Wisdom, the top of every creature, is to Him like a deed of the hand, so even "He is too deep to grasp" is the wrong kind of sentence. Here reasoning hands over to faith, at a point the reasoning itself has marked. Last, the parables come back, now licensed with two qualifiers ("exactly so, and more than this"; "but not really in this way"). Speech, letters, utterances: all these words are true only from our side. The book ends where it began, at the stone, now seen as light of light of light, still His.

How it reasons, in short (ours): (1) find the pressure point in a verse; (2) lay one premise and test it by imagining the source withdrawn; (3) take it to the hardest case; (4) beat the rival picture at its root; (5) build a parable and mark its break; (6) let the break drive the next step; (7) turn every obstacle into His power; (8) say on whose side each truth stands; (9) stop at the edge of grasp and name the faith that goes further; (10) bring it back to the lowest thing, and to the heart. R. Aharon of Strashelye turns step 8 into a rule for every Kabbalistic word, and pushes step 7 further: even the power that contracts is His Essence. The Tzemach Tzedek (Derech Mitzvosecha, the mitzvah of unity) names the whole Gate's goal as a mitzvah: know that Havaya is one with Elokim. He adds an argument the Gate implies: no chain of causes can make a 'something', only the Infinite can. And he names the fruit: to make the 'I' into nothing, "and this is the truth of Chassidus".

## Chinuch Katan · Why prove it at all (lines 770-779)

**Claim (stated).** There are two loves of G-d. One is a gift of the righteous. The other is 'made' in the heart by thinking about what wakes love, and anyone can reach it. That is why love can be commanded. The first and founding thing to think about is pure faith in His unity.

**Moves.** SHY-M01 Press the verse until it says more; SHY-M18 Hand over to faith at the marked edge

**Parable.** None. The opening is a verse question (why does the Torah say 'to DO' love?).

**Its job in the proof.** Sets the goal of the proof. It is not information, it is love that is made. The proof is the raw material for that love. (stated: lines 777-779; 'the goal of the proof' is ours)

## Chapter 2 · Not the silversmith's cup (lines 792-798)

**Claim (stated).** Those who deny that G-d watches each detail picture Him as a craftsman whose vessel outlives his hands. But craft is something from something (it changes a shape). Creation is something from nothing. Even the split Sea stood only while the wind blew. So by a 'how much more' argument, the Maker's power must be inside the made thing always. 'You give life' means 'You give being'.

**Moves.** SHY-M04 Name the rival picture and find its root error; SHY-M05 'How much more', from a known wonder

**Parable.** Silversmith and vessel (an anti-parable, the rival's picture). The Sea and the east wind (a true parable). **Where it breaks.** The Sea parable is weaker than the case it teaches: water's nature to flow was itself created, and a stone wall stands with no wind. Creation from nothing is above nature, so the argument goes 'how much more'. The text marks this itself (line 794).

**Its job in the proof.** Defends the premise against the strongest rival model by finding that model's root error. (stated)

## Chapter 4 · The sun and its sheath (lines 804-811)

That is His greatness (chesed), His goodness spreading without end. Elokim is the sheath. It is His gevurah, the power to contract and hide that life, so the creature seems a thing in itself. We cannot grasp the hiding any more than we can grasp creation from nothing. It is His might. The hiding is called 'vessels'; the life is called 'light'.

**Moves.** SHY-M08 Turn the obstacle into His power

**Parable.** The sun and its sheath (nartik), so creatures can bear it. A vessel covering what it holds. **Where it breaks.** A sheath is a second object outside the sun. Elokim is not a second thing; chapter 6 corrects this ('Havaya is Elokim'). The text does not call this a break here; it fixes it in ch. 6. (ours, built on line 822)

**Its job in the proof.** Answers the break of ch. 3 by adding a second divine power. The concealment is not a gap in G-d but one of His powers. (stated)

## Chapter 7 · As much below as above (lines 823-848)

**Claim (stated).** Why hide at all? 'No king without a people': kingship needs beings that feel separate and far. Malchut (the Name Adnut) makes space and time and keeps the world a full 'something'. Malchut is one with His Essence, so space and time are nullified before Him: the upper unity. The life in a creature is contracted to fit it, through combinations, substitutions and numbers of letters, down to the stone.

His knowing surrounds each creature in actual fact, since it is its life. Stated (line 840): unlike a soul, He is not held in the world and takes no change from it.

**Its job in the proof.** The centre of the proof. It gives the reason for the hiding, separates the two unities, and proves that nothing changed. (stated)

## Chapter 10 · Light that has no name (lines 862-867)

**Claim (stated).** Since 'the Torah speaks in human language', the sages may speak of the sefirot by parable and call them 'lights'. The attributes are one with Him like the light inside the sun's globe, which has no existence of its own: 'exactly so, and more than this'; and yet 'not really in this way'. The attributes carry names only toward creatures. The days of creation are the attributes revealed (chesed made light, gevurah made the firmament), each holding the others, 'so that the dry land appear, and a person on it to serve Hashem'.

**Moves.** SHY-M17 Speak by licensed parable, with two qualifiers; SHY-M10 Say on whose side it is true

**Parable.** The source of light (ma'or) and the light (or) inside the sun's globe. **Where it breaks.** Stated (line 863): 'but not really in this way, only in a way far and wondrous from our grasp'.

**Its job in the proof.** Returns to the sun parable, now with permission and with limits. Turns the unity of the attributes into the story of creation. (stated)

## Chapter 11 · What was hidden, now spoken (lines 868-875)

**Claim (stated).** The 'Ten Utterances' are called 'utterances' only relative to creatures. **Moves.** SHY-M17 Speak by licensed parable, with two qualifiers; SHY-M16 Climb the inner ladder, then change category

**Parable.** A person's kindness, clothed in thought, then speech (a king who commands his servants to give). **Where it breaks.** Stated (line 870): they are not like the letters of our thought, G-d forbid.

## Chapter 12 · Moonlight, down to the stone (lines 876-882)

**Claim (stated).** Creatures differ by changes in combination, substitution and exchange of letters. A word draws more than its letters: a general power above them that binds them. The first letter in a combination rules. Lower creatures come from letter-exchanges, like moonlight that is light of the sun's light, down and down until stones and dust.

**Moves.** SHY-M19 Measure the descent; SHY-M03 Go to the hardest case

**Parable.** Moonlight on the earth: light of the light of the sun.

**Its job in the proof.** Comes back to the stone of ch. 1, now with the whole chain shown. The proof closes at the lowest point. (stated)

## Terms

*Each term as the Gate uses it. Relations are stated unless marked. Sources are quote ids (full text in the last section).*

## SHY-T01 · Letters of speech; the Ten Utterances · אותיות הדבור · עשרה מאמרות

The divine 'words' (like 'Let there be a firmament') that stand inside each created thing and keep making it.

- **Relations:** *give being to* every creature (SHY-T02); *are clothed in* the creature, as its life and 'soul'; *descend by* combinations, substitutions and numbers (SHY-T18); *are called 'letters' only* relative to creatures; they are flows from His attributes (SHY-T19); *are one with* His attributes, which are one with Him
- **Misreadings:** That G-d 'talks' with a mouth (ruled out, line 797). That speech is a one-time event in the past (ruled out, line 785). That the letters are like our letters of thought (ruled out, line 870).
- **Sources:** Q3 (SHY 1:3), Q5 (SHY 1:6), Q6 (SHY 1:9), Q83 (SHY 11:1), Q84 (SHY 11:3), Q86 (SHY 11:7) · **stated**

## SHY-T02 · Something from nothing · יש מאין

Making a thing's very existence, not reshaping something that was already there.

- **Relations:** *is the opposite of* something from something (craft, line 793); *requires* the Maker's power inside the made thing always (SHY-T03); *is beyond* every created mind (line 807); *comes only from* the Infinite; no chain of causes can make a 'something' (Tzemach Tzedek, D4)
- **Misreadings:** That 'from nothing' means from some raw stuff called 'nothing'. That once made, a thing has its own being (the silversmith picture).
- **Sources:** Q9 (SHY 2:2), Q10 (SHY 2:3), Q11 (SHY 2:4), D4 (DM Achdus HaShem 2), D5 (DM Achdus HaShem 2) · **stated**

## SHY-T03 · Continuous creation · כח הפועל בנפעל תמיד · מהוה

The Maker's power must be in the made thing every moment, giving it being now; that is what the Name Havaya means.

- **Relations:** *is the meaning of* Havaya, 'brings into being' in the present (SHY-T08); *is shown by* the withdrawal test (SHY-M02); *grounds* the nullity of the creature (SHY-T04); *answers* those who deny providence over details (line 792)
- **Misreadings:** That the world would 'collapse' if He withdrew. The text says it would be as if it had never been (line 785).
- **Sources:** Q3 (SHY 1:3), Q11 (SHY 2:4), Q22 (SHY 4:2), Q95 (SHY 4:2) · **stated**

## SHY-T04 · Nullified in existence; the ray in the sun · בטל במציאות · כאור השמש בשמש

A thing whose whole being is a radiance of its source does not count as a 'thing' next to that source, like a sunbeam inside the sun's globe.

- **Relations:** *is true of* every creature, relative to the word that makes it (line 802); *is hidden by* the contraction (SHY-T10); *is 'before Him'* the upper unity (SHY-T14); *is the parable for* the attributes' unity with Him, 'and more' (line 863); *is not the same as* 'not existing' for us: for the lower beings the world is a full something (line 825)
- **Misreadings:** That the world is an illusion for us. The text says it looks like a something to eyes of flesh, and that the hiding is meant so time and space are real even for the lower beings (line 831). That nullity means worthlessness.
- **Sources:** Q15 (SHY 3:2), Q17 (SHY 3:3), Q18 (SHY 3:4), Q77 (SHY 10:1) · **stated**

## SHY-T05 · Nothing and naught; nothing apart from Him · אין ואפס · אפס בלעדו

The status of a creature measured against its source: 'nothing', and 'there is nothing apart from Him'.

- **Relations:** *follows from* continuous creation (SHY-T03), by SHY-M06; *is said* relative to the Maker's power, 'le-gabei' (line 799); *is sharpened by* the Tzemach Tzedek: 'as not', not 'not' (D8)
- **Misreadings:** Taking 'nothing' in an absolute sense, with no 'relative to'.
- **Sources:** Q13 (SHY 3:1), Q16 (SHY 3:2), D7 (DM Achdus HaShem 2), D8 (DM Achdus HaShem 2) · **stated**

## SHY-T06 · Eyes of flesh; the appearance of 'something' · עיני בשר · נראה ליש

Things look solid and self-standing because we do not see the power inside them, not because they are.

- **Relations:** *is the place of* the name 'something' (line 802); *is produced by* the contraction (SHY-T10), for the lower beings; *would end if* the eye were allowed to see (line 800)
- **Misreadings:** That the fault is in our eyes and should be fixed. The text treats the appearance as intended (ch. 7).

## SHY-T07 · Havaya (the Name of being) · שם הוי״ה

The Name that means He brings everything into being now; His greatness and kindness spreading without end.

- **Relations:** *is shielded by* Elokim (SHY-T09); *is one with* Elokim (line 818); *is* chesed / gedulah (line 806); *is above* time: was, is and will be in one moment (line 829); *maps onto* the surrounding (sovev) in the Tzemach Tzedek (D6)

## SHY-T24 · Names only toward creatures · לגבי הנבראים

## How the terms fit together (ours, on the stated relations)

- **The making:** Letters of speech (T01) give being now (T03) from nothing (T02) to every creature, down to the stone through combinations (T18). This is the Name Havaya (T07), His kindness (T08).
- **The status:** what is made this way is nullified in existence before its source (T04), 'nothing apart from Him' (T05). It looks like a 'something' only to eyes of flesh (T06).
- **The hiding:** His gevurah (T10), the Name Elokim (T09), 'nature', covers the light with vessels (T11). It is kindness inside restraint, so Havaya is Elokim. It hides only from the lower beings.
- **The why:** Kingship (T13) needs a people that feels separate. From it come space and time, the 'world'.

- **The no-change:** He knows all by knowing Himself (T17). He fills without being held and surrounds in fact (T20).
- **The edge:** His attributes are His Essence (T19). Wisdom is 'action' before Him (T21). Names hold only toward us (T24). Past this edge is faith above intellect (T22), the root of made love (T23).

## SHY-M01 · Press the verse until it says more

**What it does.** Finds a word in a verse that is too strong for the plain meaning, and uses that excess as the question the whole proof must answer. **When.** At the start. Whenever a familiar truth ('G-d is one') is being agreed to too easily. **Cross-ref.** DEPTH-ENGINE M1

**Steps.** 1. Quote the verse whole. 2. Give the plain meaning (no other gods). 3. Ask: if that is all, why the strong wording ('know today', 'bring it back to your heart')? 4. Let the question stand open. The answer must explain the extra force.

**Worked example from the text.** 'Know today and bring it back to your heart that Havaya is Elokim... there is nothing else.' Who would think there is a god living in the water under the earth? So 'nothing else' must mean more than 'no other god'. It is answered only in ch. 6: not even an 'else'. The Chinuch Katan does the same with 'to DO love'.

## SHY-M02 · The withdrawal test

**What it does.** Tests whether a thing has being of its own by imagining its source taken away for one moment. **When.** To show that something is held in being now, not made once. **Cross-ref.** new (SHY)

**Steps.** 1. Name the thing (the sky, a stone, you). 2. Name what gives it being (the word of G-d inside it). 3. Imagine that source leaving 'for one moment'. 4. Ask: does it stay, like a cup after the smith goes home, or does it become 'as if it never was'? 5. If the second: its being is borrowed this moment. Conclude continuous creation.

**Worked example from the text.** If the letters left the sky for one moment and returned to their source, the heavens would be absolutely nothing, as before 'Let there be a firmament'.

## SHY-M14 · Prove 'no change' through His knowing

## Worked examples

*Real questions, reasoned the Gate's way. Each step names the move and terms; the answer is a plain reply a guide could give (120-180 words). The step order and wording are ours; each step's content is stated in its sources.*

## SHY-E01 · "If G-d is everywhere, why does the world look so separate from Him, and why can't I feel Him?"

1. **SHY-M02** (The withdrawal test; Continuous creation): Everything you see is being given being now; take away the word inside it and it would be as if it never was. [Q3]
2. **SHY-M06** (From 'depends on' to 'nothing next to'; Nullified in existence; the ray in the sun, Eyes of flesh; the appearance of 'something'): So next to that word it counts as nothing; it looks solid because eyes of flesh do not see the power inside it. [Q13, Q14, Q18]
3. **SHY-M07** (Build the parable, mark the break, let the break ask the next question; Nullified in existence; the ray in the sun): But the source is always here, so why the look of separateness? The parable breaks. [Q20]
4. **SHY-M08** (Turn the obstacle into His power; Contraction and hiding; gevurah, Elokim (the Name that shields)): The look of separateness is made by His power to hide, as wondrous as creation. [Q25, Q26]
5. **SHY-M12** (Ask why at all; Kingship; the Name Adnut; 'world' as space and time): Why? A King needs a people that feels separate. [Q43, Q44]
6. **SHY-M10** (Say on whose side it is true; Upper unity, Lower unity): The hiding is real for you and is not there for Him; and He is here as much as anywhere. [Q40, Q49]

**Answer.** The Alter Rebbe asks your question in chapter 3, about his own parable. A sunbeam looks like a real thing out in the open, because the sun is far away. But you are never far from your Source. The word that makes you is inside you right now. So why don't you dissolve into it, and why don't you feel it? His answer: the hiding is not a place where G-d is missing. It is one of His powers, and as hard to grasp as creating something from nothing. He hides on purpose, because a King wants a people, beings who stand on their own and choose Him. So the feeling of separateness is real for you, and it is wanted. From His side it hides nothing. And He is here, in this room, this hour, exactly as much as in heaven. Not feeling it is not proof He is absent. It is the shape His closeness takes so that you can be you.

- **Beginner:** Skip the Names and the letters. Use the sunbeam and the King: 'He is closer than the light. He hides a little so there can be a you to love Him.'
- **Advanced:** Go to ch. 6-7: Elokim equals nature and is one with Havaya; Malchut makes space and time and is one with the Essence. Add Strashelye: all differences are in our reckoning (S8). Then the Tzemach Tzedek: the mitzvah is to know Havaya is Elokim (D1).
- **Doubter:** Start from the rival picture (SHY-M04). 'Fine, if the world were a cup and G-d a smith, you would be right to feel nothing. But a cup is reshaped silver. Nothing here was reshaped from anything. What holds it in being now?' Let the question work; do not push the conclusion.

## SHY-E02 · "Did G-d create the world and step back? Does He actually care about the small details of my life?"

1. **SHY-M04** (Name the rival picture and find its root error; Something from nothing): The 'stepped back' picture is the silversmith. Its root error: treating creation like craft. [Q7, Q8, Q9]
2. **SHY-M05** ('How much more', from a known wonder; Continuous creation): Even the Sea stood only while the wind blew; how much more creation from nothing. [Q10, Q11]
3. **SHY-M03** (Go to the hardest case; Letters of speech; the Ten Utterances, Combinations, substitutions, numbers): This reaches the smallest, lowest thing, even a stone. [Q4, Q5]
4. **SHY-M14** (Prove 'no change' through His knowing; His knowledge): He knows every creature by knowing Himself, and that knowing is its life. [Q52, Q62]

**Answer.** The Alter Rebbe says this exact doubt, that G-d made the world and left, has a root, and he names it. We picture G-d like a silversmith. Once the cup is made, it sits on the table without him. But a smith only reshapes silver that was already there. G-d made the silver too, from nothing. Something made from nothing has no being of its own to fall back on. Think of the Sea at the Exodus: it stood up only while the wind kept blowing. Creation needs that, only far more. His power is inside every made thing at every moment, down to a stone. And His knowing is not a report He gets from far away. He knows you by knowing Himself, and that knowing is what keeps you in being. Chapter 2 says this is the answer to those who deny He watches over details. The details are where He is busiest.

- **Beginner:** Use only the Sea: 'The water stood because the wind kept blowing. You are here because He keeps giving you being, now.'
- **Advanced:** Add the Tzemach Tzedek: no chain of causes can make a 'something'; an effect melts back into its cause, so the solid thing comes from the Infinite itself (D4). Providence is built into what being is.
- **Doubter:** Grant the smith picture its strength first. Then ask what, in a universe with no outside, keeps anything existing from one moment to the next. Leave it as a real question.

## SHY-E03 · "If everything is 'nothing' before G-d, am I real? Does my life matter, or am I just an illusion?"

1. **SHY-M06** (From 'depends on' to 'nothing next to'; Nothing and naught; nothing apart from Him): 'Nothing' is said relative to the power that makes you, not on its own. [Q13]
2. **SHY-M10** (Say on whose side it is true; Upper unity, Lower unity): From your side the world is a full 'something', on purpose. [Q45, Q50]
3. **SHY-M12** (Ask why at all; Kingship; the Name Adnut; 'world' as space and time): Kingship needs separate beings; that is you. [Q43, Q44]
4. **SHY-M13** (Hold both unities; Lower unity): And He is found in your place and your hour. [Q48]
5. **SHY-M18** (Hand over to faith at the marked edge; Made love): The point of knowing this is love: 'Hashem is his true soul and his very life'. [CK3]

**Answer.** You are real. The Gate never says you do not exist. It says something more careful: next to the power that is giving you being right now, you do not count as a second thing. Like sunlight inside the sun, you are its light, not its rival. But the same book says G-d hides His power on purpose, so that time and place, and you inside them, stay fully real from your side. Why? Because He wants to be a King, and a king needs people who stand on their own and can turn to him freely. Your separate life is not a mistake to be erased. It is what He made the whole hiding for. And He is not far off. He is found in your place and your hour as much as in heaven. Chinuch Katan says the goal is that you feel He is 'your true soul and your very life'. That makes your life matter more, not less.

- **Beginner:** Say only: 'You are real, and you are held. G-d gives you being every moment because He wants you.'
- **Advanced:** Bring in D8 (the Tzemach Tzedek): the world was 'not' and is now 'as not', which is not the same. And Strashelye S15: the aim is that bitul show in the 'something' itself, not that the something vanish.
- **Doubter:** Take the worry seriously: some teachings do make people feel unreal. Show that the text guards against that (time and space stay real 'even for the lower beings'). Do not argue metaphysics with someone in distress; see ON.md rule 4.

## SHY-E04 · "So is G-d just the universe? Is nature G-d?"

1. **SHY-M09** (Unite the opposed names by the rule of His attributes; Elokim (the Name that shields), Havaya (the Name of being)): Elokim equals 'nature' in number: nature is the Name that hides. And Havaya is Elokim. [Q30, Q31, Q33]
2. **SHY-M11** (Test the near parable at its root; 'Else' (od), Surrounding and filling): He fills the world but is not held in it; not like a soul in a body. [Q39, Q61]
3. **SHY-M16** (Climb the inner ladder, then change category; Wisdom as 'action' before Him): He is above even wisdom, the highest thing in creation. [Q69, Q70]
4. **SHY-M17** (Speak by licensed parable, with two qualifiers; Names only toward creatures): Even His names are only toward creatures. [Q80]

**Answer.** No, and the Gate shows why with care. It does say something close to your question: the Name Elokim has the same number as the Hebrew word for nature. Nature is how G-d hides, so the world looks as if it runs by itself. And it says that hiding Name is one with the Name that gives being. So nature is not a second power beside Him. But the reverse is not true: G-d is not nature. He fills the world without being held in it. A soul in a body feels the body's pain and changes; He takes no change from summer, winter, day or night. The book climbs further. Even wisdom, the highest thing in creation, is to Him like a simple deed of the hand. So the best sentence is: nothing is outside Him, and nothing contains Him. Nature is His mask, worn for us. It is not His face.

- **Beginner:** 'Nature is how G-d hides, not what G-d is. He is inside everything and bigger than everything.'
- **Advanced:** Use the Tzemach Tzedek: Havaya is the surrounding and Elokim the filling, and 'the surrounding is the filling' (D2, D6). And Strashelye's dilemma (S18): either His Essence became physical or the world is outside Him. The Gate escapes both: the world is not outside Him (it is nothing apart from Him) and He did not become it (He is not held in it).
- **Doubter:** Concede what pantheism gets right: no outside, nothing self-made. Then show the cost: a G-d who is the world changes with it and cannot be addressed. The Gate's G-d gives being without being the thing given.

## SHY-E05 · "Why would a good G-d hide Himself, when the hiding lets so much pain and evil happen?"

1. **SHY-M08** (Turn the obstacle into His power; Contraction and hiding; gevurah): The hiding is His power, beyond our grasp like creation itself. [Q26, Q27]
2. **SHY-M09** (Unite the opposed names by the rule of His attributes; Greatness / kindness (chesed), Contraction and hiding; gevurah): The restraint is held inside kindness: it is how a world can be built. [Q32, Q23]
3. **SHY-M12** (Ask why at all; Kingship; the Name Adnut; 'world' as space and time): The reason: a King wants a people who are free and separate. [Q43]
4. **SHY-M10** (Say on whose side it is true; Upper unity): A world of pure hiding could not stand, so He joined mercy: revelation through tzaddikim and Torah. [Q29, Q97]
5. **SHY-M18** (Hand over to faith at the marked edge; Faith above intellect): Past this, we do not grasp the measure of His restraint; we believe it is His. [Q27, Q54]

**Answer.** The Gate does not explain away pain. It says something smaller and stronger. The hiding comes from G-d's own power, and it sits inside His kindness. Without restraint there could be no world at all, only endless light where nothing separate could stand. 'The nature of the Good is to do good', and part of that good was to hold Himself back so that you could exist and choose. Why that hiding takes the shape it takes in your life, the book says plainly that no created mind can grasp. That is as hard to grasp as how He makes something from nothing. It also says a world of pure restraint could not last, so He joined mercy to it: people who show Him, and Torah that feeds the soul. So the honest answer is: the dark is not a place outside Him, and it is not His last word. You do not have to understand it to refuse to be alone in it.

- **Beginner:** Do not explain at all at first. Listen. Then only: 'Even here, you are not outside Him.'
- **Advanced:** Add Strashelye: the deepest arousal comes from overcoming the opposite (Fourth Gate, line 897, per SHAAREI-HAYICHUD.md), and 'nothing else' is revealed from inside the hiding (S13). Keep the Gate's honesty: the measure of His restraint is not known (S4).
- **Doubter:** Do not offer a theodicy. Name the limit the text itself marks (line 810). Say the Gate claims only that the hiding is not a rival power, not that every pain has a reason we can see.

## SHY-E06 · "If G-d knows everything, doesn't the world change Him? And how can He know me, one small person?"

1. **SHY-M14** (Prove 'no change' through His knowing; His knowledge): He knows all by knowing Himself, so nothing is added to Him. [Q51, Q52, Q55]
2. **SHY-M15** (Refute from what the other side already believes; Contraction and hiding; gevurah): This is also why He did not withdraw: if He knows you, He is not absent. [Q58]
3. **SHY-M11** (Test the near parable at its root; Surrounding and filling): His knowing surrounds each thing in actual fact, because it is its life. [Q62]
4. **SHY-M18** (Hand over to faith at the marked edge; Faith above intellect): How this is, no mouth can say; we believe it. [Q54]

**Answer.** The Gate takes this from the Rambam. We know things from outside: first there is me, then I learn something, and the knowledge is added to me. G-d does not work that way. He knows every creature by knowing Himself, because every creature is His power, given now. So knowing the world adds nothing to Him and changes nothing. He was alone before creation, and He is alone after it, with no change. And this is not a cold knowing. Chapter 7 says our thoughts surround only a picture of a thing, but His knowing surrounds each creature in fact, because that knowing is what gives it being. So He knows you not as one item on a long list. Your being known by Him and your being here are the same thing. The Rambam adds that no mouth can say this and no heart grasp it fully. We reason to here, and then we believe.

- **Beginner:** 'G-d knows you the way the sun knows its own light: you are not news to Him.' (Say it is a picture.)
- **Advanced:** Add the note to ch. 9 and Strashelye S11: 'He is the knower' applies after the contraction into vessels of wisdom; from His side, 'knowing' does not apply at all.
- **Doubter:** Show the dilemma first: either knowing changes the knower (then G-d is composite) or He does not know (then providence fails). The Gate's answer, knowing by being the source, is the third way. Ask if they have a better one.

## SHY-E07 · "I can't understand G-d at all. Why should I even try to think about this?"

1. **SHY-M16** (Climb the inner ladder, then change category; Wisdom as 'action' before Him): Right: He is not in the category of grasp. But that is a conclusion you reach by thinking. [Q70, Q71]
2. **SHY-M18** (Hand over to faith at the marked edge; Faith above intellect): Faith begins where thinking marks its own edge. [Q74, Q75]
3. **SHY-M01** (Press the verse until it says more; Made love): The thinking is for the heart: love that is 'made' by thinking, which anyone can reach. [CK2, CK1]

**Answer.** You are in good company. The Gate itself says no created mind can grasp Him, and then it goes further than you did. Even saying 'He is too deep to understand' is the wrong kind of sentence, like saying a deep idea is too deep to touch with your hands. He is not in the category of understanding at all. But notice how the book gets there: through twelve chapters of careful thinking. The thinking is not meant to capture G-d. It is meant to clear away wrong pictures, like G-d as a craftsman who left, or G-d as a soul stuck in a body. Then a true faith has room. And the opening chapter, Chinuch Katan, gives the real reason to think. There is a love anyone can reach, 'made' in the heart by thinking about what wakes it. The first thing to think about is His oneness. So think, not to finish the subject, but to let it reach your heart.

- **Beginner:** Give one thought only (the word that keeps everything in being) and one minute a day with it.
- **Advanced:** Add Strashelye: unity through understanding still leaves someone who understands (S10); the main bitul is above Da'at (S17). The Gate's own 'faith above intellect' points the same way.
- **Doubter:** Agree that a G-d you could fully grasp would not be G-d. Then ask whether 'I can't grasp it' is a reason to stop, or the reason the question is worth asking.

## SHY-E08 · "What am I actually doing when I say the Shema twice a day?"

1. **SHY-M13** (Hold both unities; Upper unity, Lower unity): 'Hear O Israel' is the upper unity; 'Blessed be the Name' is the lower unity. [Q1, Q42]
2. **SHY-M06** (From 'depends on' to 'nothing next to'; Nullified in existence; the ray in the sun): Upper: before Him everything, even space and time, is nullified like the ray in the sun. [Q47]
3. **SHY-M13** (Hold both unities; Kingship; the Name Adnut; 'world' as space and time, Lower unity): Lower: His Essence is found here, in space and time, equally. [Q48, Q49]
4. **SHY-M01** (Press the verse until it says more; Made love): The Shema is the root of the 'made' love; the Gate is built on its first section. [CK1, Q98]

**Answer.** The Gate opens with the Zohar's answer and spends twelve chapters earning it. The first line, 'Hear O Israel, Hashem is our G-d, Hashem is One', is the upper unity. It is how things are before Him: everything, even space and time, is as nothing, like a sunbeam inside the sun. The second line, 'Blessed be the Name of His glorious kingdom forever', is the lower unity. Through His kingship He makes space and time, and His very Self is found in them. He is on the earth below exactly as in the heavens above. The Zohar notes that 'va'ed' (forever) has the same letters as 'echad' (one), switched. So twice a day you say both. First: there is nothing but Him. Then: and He is fully here, in this place and this hour. Neither sentence cancels the other. The title page of this part says it is built on the first section of the Shema, the source of the love it is trying to make.

- **Beginner:** 'First line: only He. Second line: He is right here. Say both and mean both.'
- **Advanced:** Bring in the shiluv of Havaya and Adnut (line 829) and the Tzemach Tzedek's reading of the Zohar: the mitzvah of unity is to know that Havaya is one with Elokim, and the Kabbalists' Havaya-in-Adnut is close to it (D1).
- **Doubter:** Present it as a claim about two perspectives, not a mystery: one from the source, one from inside the world. Ask which one they live in, and whether the other could be true too.

## The procedure: running the Gate on any question (ours)

*A checklist built from the moves, in the Gate's own order. Not every question needs every step; never skip 7-8 when hiding is involved, or 5's 'relative to'.*

1. Find the pressure point. What in the person's question (or the verse) is too strong, or does not fit the easy answer? Keep it open. (SHY-M01)
2. Lay the one premise: everything is being given being now, by His word inside it. Test it with the withdrawal test. (SHY-M02, T01, T03)
3. Take it to the hardest case the person cares about: the stone, the body, the boring day, the bad news. (SHY-M03)
4. If they hold a rival picture (the smith who left, the soul in the body), find its root error; do not just deny its conclusion. (SHY-M04, SHY-M11)
5. Draw the inference with its 'relative to': nothing next to the power that makes it. Put the look of solidity in the viewer. (SHY-M06)
6. Give one image, then mark where it breaks, and let the break be the next question. (SHY-M07)
7. Re-file the hiding as His power, held inside kindness, one with the Name that gives. (SHY-M08, SHY-M09)
8. Say on whose side each sentence is true, and keep both sides. (SHY-M10)
9. If asked why: kingship needs a people; the separate self is wanted. (SHY-M12)
10. Hold both unities: before Him, nothing; here, He is found, equally. (SHY-M13)
11. If the question touches change or knowledge: He knows by knowing Himself; nothing is added. (SHY-M14, SHY-M15)
12. At the edge, change category, then hand over to faith and name what is believed. (SHY-M16, SHY-M18)
13. Speak of the attributes only with both qualifiers and 'toward us'. (SHY-M17)
14. Come back down: to the stone, to this hour, and to the heart. The aim is love made by thinking. (SHY-M19, T23)
15. Before answering, run the guardrails G01-G14. Fail any one: rewrite.

## What Strashelye and the Tzemach Tzedek add, in one place

- **The goal named as a mitzvah.** The Tzemach Tzedek reads the mitzvah of unity three ways: no other power (plain); Havaya is one with Elokim (the Zohar, the Gate's ch. 4-6); and, 'by the depth of the Baal Shem Tov's foundation', no other existence at all, citing Gate ch. 3 by name (D1, D3, D7, D12). This is the Gate's own ladder, and already in ON.md (ON-01, 02, 06). New here: his third level is exactly the Gate's ch. 3 inference (SHY-M06), and his second is its ch. 6 (SHY-M09). **stated**
- **Why no chain can make a 'something'.** An effect melts back into its cause, so the solid being of a thing comes only from the Infinite, which no limit binds (D4, D5). This sharpens SHY-M19: the letter chain shapes; it does not by itself give being. **stated**
- **'As not', not 'not'.** Before creation: not. Now: as not (D8). This sharpens the 'relative to' of SHY-M06 and guards G07. **stated**
- **The fruit.** 'Not to say I, but to make the I into nothing', and 'this is the truth of Chassidus' (D9, D10). Read with the Gate's ch. 7 (the people is wanted), this is bitul of the claim to stand alone, not erasure (G08, ours).
- **The parable law.** Strashelye turns the Gate's ch. 6 into a law: a parable may speak only from the side of revelation and never 'from every side' (S2, S14), and quotes ch. 6 for it. **stated**
- **The break of ch. 3, restated.** The sun and the world's space are 'an essence in itself', so 'something' falls there (S3). **stated**
- **The hiding pushed further.** Even the power of contraction is His Essence (S6); the contraction was in the light, not the Essence (S7); the literal reading is 'a grave sin' (S5). **stated**
- **The side-rule.** 'All differences are in our reckoning' (S8): the Gate's SHY-M10 made universal. The lower unity at full strength: His power in limit as without limit (S9). The aim: His perfection revealed 'precisely from the side of the lower ones' (S12), bitul shown in the 'something' itself (S15). **stated**
- **Where he differs.** He says the vessels do appear as 'od' in the soul-body unity of Atzilut (S13), where the Gate says the earth is not even 'od'. He makes the main bitul one above understanding (S10, S17), where the Gate ends in faith and made love. Kept, not smoothed. **stated**; 'differs' is our comparison.
