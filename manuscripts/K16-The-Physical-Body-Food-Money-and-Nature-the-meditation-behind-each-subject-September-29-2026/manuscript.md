## Why a meditation

Every concept in Chassidus points to the unity of God, and that unity cannot be understood by definition. It can only be glimpsed, in contemplation (*hisbonenus*). So behind each subject below is a sitting, and the sitting, not the idea, is what matters.

## How to sit with a section

This is *hisbonenus*, the Chabad way of contemplation, not mindfulness. There is nothing here to watch in the body and nothing to empty out.

1. **Hold** the one line of teaching. It is the object for the whole sitting.
2. **The move.** Think it through slowly: open it detail by detail, bring it down into your own day, take the source's own picture and remember that it is only a picture, and make it particular: you, here, now.
3. **Stay.** Bind the mind to it, the Tanya's *da'at* (Tanya 3): say the line again slowly. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought. The pauses are time to think, not silence to watch anything.
4. **From inside** describes the glimpse. It is never asked for. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts: what you held in your mind carries the act that follows.
5. **Back into the day.** Say it with your lips in words of Torah or prayer, do the one mitzvah or act of Torah in your own measure, and carry the return line through the day.

**Tie** says whether the move rests on derivation steps the entry marks *stated*, or on our own *reading*; the steps are listed. **Thin** marks subjects whose tie to the unity the entry calls partly thin (3 of 12 here); their sittings are kept modest and say why. **Kept** records a disagreement the entry keeps between the sources; it is left standing, not smoothed. **Before you start** is a safety line where a subject touches health, fasting or sleep.

## S186 · Serving God in the physical · עבודה בגשמיות

*Face F07 The home below. Also touches F11 Darkness turned to light, F09 The unity of opposites, F01 The world said into being now. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The plain acts of your day, eating, working, paying, can be joined to Him, and that joining is how this lowest world becomes His home.

**Hold.** '"In all your ways know Him": know means join, in all one's deeds, even in the physical things one does.' (Tzava'at HaRivash)

**The move.** Open it. The whole earth is full of His glory, and in everything is His vitality, so a person puts knowing into each physical thing 'to raise it, bind it, and join the tent to be one'; the Alter Rebbe sets the measure: done for the body's wish alone, even its real need, the permitted thing stays in nogah, the mixed realm, but eaten to widen the mind for God, the same meat and wine are refined and 'rise to God like an offering'; and the Rebbe: refining permitted things for Heaven completes the dwelling below. Bring it down: one plain act you will do today anyway, lunch, the drive to work, paying a bill. The Tanya's picture is the offering on the altar; only a picture, since there is no altar at your table, only what you intend. You, today, at that one act: it can be joined.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'in all your ways know Him.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The joy of the plain man whose king lives with him: the ordinary is the point, not a detour from it. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'In all your ways know Him.' Before one meal today, say its blessing slowly and eat it for the strength to serve. Through the day, return to: 'know means join'.

**Kept.** How much of the physical can be raised, and by whom? Tanya 7: only what is done for Heaven. Ruzhin: serving God in pleasure itself is a path for the chosen few, after the common road of Torah and mitzvot. Ropshitz: He delights in being served by lowly things. The earlier Chassidim broke desire by refusing permitted things they craved. The Alter Rebbe: spirituality and physicality are opposites in essence. Kept side by side.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tzava'at HaRivash 94:1 (line 194): בכל דרכיך דעהו. זהו כלל גדול. דעהו לשון חיבור … בכל מעשיו אפילו בדברים גשמיים שעושה The Besht's rule: 'know Him in all your ways' means joining (the letters of the Name), even in physical acts. - Keter Shem Tov 1:69:3 (line 114): כי צריך ליתן הדעת בכ"ד גשמי להעלותו ולקשרו ולחבר האהל להיות אחד Da'at is to be put into every physical thing to raise it, bind it and 'join the tent to be one': the physical act as unification. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 7:4 (line 135): אזי, נתברר חיות הבשר והיין שהיה נשפע מקליפת נוגה, ועולה לה׳ כעולה וכקרבן Eaten to widen the mind for God, the meat and wine are refined out of nogah and rise to God like an offering.

## S187 · Eating · אכילה

*Face F01 The world said into being now. Also touches F11 Darkness turned to light, F05 The Essence beyond both, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** What feeds you is not the bread by itself but the word of God standing in it now. Eating joins the word in the food to the word in you.

**Hold.** 'Not by the bread alone, in itself, does man live, but by all that comes from the mouth of Havayah, clothed in the bread.' (Likkutei Torah, Tzav)

**The move.** Open it. The word 'let the earth bring forth' entered the fruit as its inner life and stands in it now, and that life, woken by the blessing, is the soul's food; a plant can feed a speaker because its spark is higher than man's, rooted in Tohu, the world before the vessels broke; and whatever one eats for health rises in the hour of Torah and prayer. Bring it down: the bread on your plate at the next meal, this slice, this morning. The Keter Shem Tov's picture is the king's lost jewel, which only his son may find; only a picture, since He loses nothing, and the picture only settles the ear. You, now, before the blessing: you are about to be fed by His word.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'by all that comes from the mouth of Havayah.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Being fed, not feeding yourself: the word in the bread never left Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the blessing at your next meal slowly with your lips, knowing what is in the food. After eating, learn one line of Torah, so that what you ate rises in it. Through the day, return to: 'not by bread alone'.

**Kept.** How much can be raised? Tanya 7-8: only food eaten for Heaven; forbidden food cannot rise at all save by a very great teshuvah, and gluttony drags the permitted down 'for the time being'. Ruzhin: serving God in pleasure itself is a path for the few. The earlier Chassidim refused permitted things they craved.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Likkutei Torah, Tzav 7:8 (line 430): אלא כי לא על הלחם לבדו מצד עצמות הלחם יחיה האדם, אלא על כל מוצא פי הוי׳ שמלובש ממש בהלחם The Alter Rebbe: bread (plant) can sustain man (speaker) only by the word of God literally clothed in it. - Keter Shem Tov 1:159:1 (line 222): ונכנס המאמר ההוא בחיות פנימי להחיות הרקיע כל ימות עולם שיעמוד כמ"ש לעולם ה' דברך נצב בשמים … וזה החיות הוא מזון הנשמה The Ari via Keter Shem Tov: the utterance that created the fruit stands in it as its inner life, and that life, woken by the blessing, is the soul's food. - Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / והבריח התיכון כו' / קמו:2 (line 452): לפי שיש בהמאכל ניצוץ אלקי גבוה מבחי' ניצוץ האדם וע"כ הוא מחי' את האדם The Maharash: food feeds man because its divine spark is higher than man's, rooted in Tohu.

## S188 · Sleep · שינה

*Face F09 The unity of opposites. Also touches F05 The Essence beyond both, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F06 The soul as part of the One, F01 The world said into being now. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 7). Thin: partly.*

**Before you start.** This is not a sleep technique and not a remedy for sleeplessness.

**The glimpse.** At night the dividing mind goes back into the soul's root, where what is split by day is one; and each morning you are given back new, like the world.

**Hold.** 'Sleep is the withdrawal of the mind into the essence of the soul.' (Torah Ohr, Miketz)

**The move.** Open it. In sleep the dividing mind withdraws, so a dream can join two opposites in one thing, and its root is above, in the 'circles', where everything divided below is included and united without separation; exile is this sleep, and at the redemption 'we were like dreamers' will be revealed; and the Rashab: on waking a person is renewed in the light and life of the soul, as God renews creation every day, 'like a new creature'. Bring it down: your bed tonight, the day's arguments left behind, and tomorrow's first moment awake. The dream is Torah Ohr's own picture; only a picture, since the dream is not the unity, it only points to its root. You, tonight: going back to the root; tomorrow, given back new.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'into the essence of the soul.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Like waking renewed, 'another person', given back and not self-made. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the Shema at bedtime tonight with your lips, with 'into Your hand I entrust my spirit'. Tomorrow on waking, say Modeh Ani before anything else. Through the day, return to: 'a new creature'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: in Chabad the tie is stated (Torah Ohr, the Rashab), but outside Chabad sleep is practice or a metaphor for smallness of mind, with no stated tie to the unity.

**Kept.** What is sleep? Torah Ohr: the mind's withdrawal into the essence of the soul, the dream rooted where all is one; yet the upper 'sleep' is what gave the nations dominion. The Rashab: night is concealment, waking a renewal. R. Nachman: 'sleep' is the mind fallen into smallness, to be woken from.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Torah Ohr, Miketz 6:16 (line 876): דהיינו כמו ענין השינה שהוא הסתלקות המוחין אל עצמות הנפש Sleep is the withdrawal of the mind into the essence of the soul; the parallel above is the light withdrawing into the Essence of Ein Sof. - Torah Ohr, Vayeshev 3:8 (line 678): ושם הוא בבחי' עגולים שאין בהם בחי' מעלה ומטה רק הכל בהשוואה אחת ובהתכללות והתאחדות כל הענינים בלי שום פירוד The dream's root is in the 'circles', where all things divided below are included and united without separation. - Hemshech Ayin Beis, חלק שני / בס"ד. ש"פ ויחי, העת"ר / תתד:2 (line 3199): כמו"כ האדם הוא בכי"ו כברי' חדשה שמתחדש באור הנשמה שנותנים לו …so a person is every day 'like a new creature', renewed in the light of the soul given him; hence 'who returns souls' and Modeh Ani.

## S189 · Money and livelihood · פרנסה וממון

*Face F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity. Also touches F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation, F07 The home below, F05 The Essence beyond both. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 4). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** You make the vessel; the livelihood is His to give. The money you earn holds your life, and given away it carries your whole life up to Him.

**Hold.** 'A person only has to make a vessel for his livelihood.' (Hayom Yom)

**The move.** Open it. All sustenance is His, and the stars and nature it comes through are 'only messengers of the flow'; idolatry began when people bowed to sun and moon because food comes from them; the Alter Rebbe told a Chassid who had lost his wealth that his task was to light up his surroundings with Torah and prayer, and livelihood is what God must give him; and money earned by toil holds the whole vital soul that earned it, so given to charity the whole vital soul rises to God. Bring it down: your pay, your accounts, next month's bills. The Rashab's picture is the axe in the builder's hand; only a picture, and it only settles the ear. You, now: the vessel is yours to make, clean and honest; the filling is His.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'only a vessel.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Lightness: the vessel is yours to make, and the holding up is not. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'You open Your hand and satisfy every living thing with favour.' Give one coin today from what you earned. Through the day, return to: 'only a vessel'.

**Kept.** Effort or trust? Hayom Yom 5 Tammuz: livelihood is God's task, yours is to light your surroundings. Hayom Yom 27 Sivan: make a vessel. The Rashab: effort is needed because blessing comes through the vessels of Asiyah. R. Nachman: lust for money is want of faith. Emphases, not flat contradictions.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Hayom Yom, סיון / כז סיון:1 (line 342): האדם עליו רק לעשות כלי לפרנסתו, ולהשתדל בכל כחו אשר הכלי יהי' טהור מכל סיג ופסולת דאונאה Hayom Yom: one only makes a vessel for livelihood, kept pure by Torah law; the blessing is from above. - Kuntres Umaayan, מַאֲמָר כג. / פֶּרֶק א:4 (line 475): דעם היות שנמשך ההשפעה על ידם הרי באמת אינם אלא שלוחי השפעה לבד The Rashab: idolatry bowed to sun and moon as the source of food; they are only messengers, an axe in the builder's hand. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:19 (line 513): הרי כל כח נפשו החיונית מלובש בעשיית מלאכתו או עסק אחר שנשתכר בו מעות אלו, וכשנותנן לצדקה – הרי כל נפשו החיונית עולה לה׳ Tanya 37: money earned holds the whole vital soul that earned it; given as charity, the whole vital soul rises to God.

## S190 · Work and commerce · משא ומתן / עסק

*Face F02 The higher and the lower unity. Also touches F07 The home below, F12 Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart', F09 The unity of opposites. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** In the Shema you say He is One in heaven and on earth. A working day run as if it were separate from Him says the opposite.

**Hold.** 'All worldly matters and the business of livelihood should not be as if done for themselves … so that even in earthly matters they do not separate them from the One.' (Iggeret HaKodesh 9)

**The move.** Open it. Morning and evening a Jew testifies that 'Havayah is One' in heaven above and on earth below, so running one's business as if for itself, apart from the true One, is false testimony in the Shema; the remedy is to work in order to give life to others, and 'in this we make the form like its Maker, Havayah is One', who does kindness all day; and the whole business day becomes a place for His dwelling through the charity given from its earnings. Bring it down: tomorrow's working day, the desk, the till, the calls, the deal. The Iggeret HaKodesh's picture is the form made like its Maker; only a picture, since He has no form. You, at work tomorrow: the same 'One' you said in the Shema covers this desk.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'even in earthly matters, not separate.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The lightness of a ray inside the sun; then a working day that is full of Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before work tomorrow, say the Shema's first line with your lips, drawing out 'One' (Echad): on its ches think that He reigns in the heavens and the earth, on its dalet that He alone rules the four directions. Set aside one coin from the day's earnings for charity. Through the day, return to: 'One, here too'.

**Kept.** Effort or trust? The Rashab: effort is needed because blessing comes through the vessels of Asiyah, hence 'in all that you do'. Hayom Yom 5 Tammuz: livelihood is God's task. Emphases, not flat contradictions. Torah Ohr also warns that prayer's love and a day absorbed in gain are joined only as a dream joins opposites, yet it forbids calling the arousal in prayer vain.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 9:1 (line 1091): כל מילי דעלמא ועסקי פרנסה לא יהיה כאלו דעבדין לגרמייהו … שגם בעניני ארץ לא יפרידו IH 9: business must not be 'for themselves'; one who separates earthly affairs from the One bears false witness in the Shema. - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 9:2 (line 1092): שבזה אנו מדמין הצורה ליוצרה, ה׳ אחד, אשר ״חסד אל כל היום״ Business done to give life to others makes the form like its Maker, 'Havayah is one', who gives life every moment. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 34:6 (line 467): וגם שאר היום כולו, שעוסק במשא ומתן, יהיה מכון לשבתו יתברך, בנתינת הצדקה שיתן מיגיעו Tanya 34: the whole business day becomes a place for His dwelling through the charity given from its earnings.

## S191 · The body · גוף

*Face F05 The Essence beyond both. Also touches F07 The home below, F09 The unity of opposites, F06 The soul as part of the One. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** This body, which on its own is no vessel for Godliness, was chosen freely by His Essence; only the One who does wonders holds it and the soul together.

**Hold.** 'The Holy One chose the body of Israel with His free choice, and this choice is from His very Essence.' (the Rebbe)

**The move.** Open it. Body and divine soul are utterly apart, and only 'the One who does wonders, who binds spiritual to physical' joins them; 'You chose us from every people' refers to the physical body, which looks like any other; body and animal soul are not vessels for Godliness by their own definition but become vessels 'by the revelation of the power of the Essence'; and in a mitzvah the limbs become a chariot for the Supreme Will. Bring it down: your hand as it reaches for the cup of kiddush, your feet on the way to shul. The Chernobyler's picture: 'when you move a limb, who moves it but the Creator?'; only a picture, since the Creator is not a hand behind your hand. You, now, in this plain body: chosen, not for anything you reached.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'chosen, from His very Essence.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Being chosen, not for anything you reached; He is in the plainest place. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the blessing 'who formed man' (Asher Yatzar) slowly with your lips today, dwelling on 'who does wonders'. Do one mitzvah with your hands, a coin given or hands washed for bread. Through the day, return to: 'who does wonders'.

**Kept.** Break the body, or refine it? The Besht: refine it, not with mortifications. Amdur: break the body always. R. Nachman: mercy on the body, and breaking its brazenness. The earlier Chassidim refused permitted things they craved, to break desire.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ג / בס"ד. ש"פ שמיני, מבה"ח וער"ח אייר ה'תשכ"ח:15 (line 2107): מצד זה שהקב"ה בחר בהגוף דישראל, בבחירתו החפשית, שבחירה זו היא מעצמותו ממש The Rebbe: God's bond with Israel through the body comes from His free choice of the body, and that choice is from His very Essence. - Hemshech Ayin Beis, חלק שני / בס"ד. ש"פ ויחי, העת"ר / תתג:4 (line 3197): ובאמת הריחוק ביותר שהגוף והנה"ט הן מק"נ משכא דחויא The Rashab: body and divine soul are utterly apart; only 'the One who does wonders' joins them. - Me'or Einayim, Vayigash 1 (line 286): כי כל כחותיו וחיותו הוא הבורא ברוך הוא … וכשאתה מנענע אזה אבר מי הוא המנענע אותו כי אם הבורא ברוך הוא The Chernobyler: all a person's powers are the Creator, who binds spiritual to physical; who moves a limb but He?

## S192 · Health and healing · רפואה

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F01 The world said into being now, F07 The home below, F09 The unity of opposites. Tie: stated (derivation steps 2, 4). Thin: partly.*

**Before you start.** This is not medical advice and it does not explain illness.

**The glimpse.** The doctor and the medicine are the natural way, and inside the natural way He is making and guiding the world every moment. The doctor is the Healer's messenger.

**Hold.** The doctor is 'the messenger of the Healer of all flesh who does wonders, who is Havayah our God'. (the Rebbe, in a letter)

**The move.** Open it. See the specialist, 'that is the natural way', and inwardly, 'every moment He creates the world and guides it'; a person must be healthy in body too, so that the mission for which the soul came down to this lowest world is fulfilled; in every matter there must be a hold in the ways of nature, so follow the doctors, but without a divided heart; and the Alter Rebbe writes that the life of soul and body flows from the Father's face, His will. Bring it down: the next appointment on your calendar, the medicine on the shelf, the doctor's name. The Rebbe's picture is a messenger and the One who sends him; only a picture, since the Healer is not far off but making you now. You, now, with that appointment: the natural way, and the Healer inside it.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'the messenger of the Healer of all flesh.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** The relief of finding what was never gone: the natural way is His messenger, not a stranger. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips the close of Asher Yatzar: 'who heals all flesh and does wonders.' Keep the next medical step as the natural vessel, the appointment or the dose. Through the day, return to: 'the Healer's messenger'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: the Rebbe's letters, the Degel and R. Nachman state ties, but most of the material is counsel, and the tie of healing as such to the soul-body bond is the entry's reading.

**Kept.** Effort or trust? The Rebbe: follow the doctor as a natural vessel, without a divided heart. Iggeret HaKodesh 22 protests those who 'run from town to town seeking counsel from afar' and do not turn to God. Emphases, not flat contradictions.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Igros Kodesh (Lubavitcher Rebbe), כרך ז / ב'עב:5 (line 28807): הנה כשהרופא עצמו יודע אשר הוא הוא שלוחו של הרופא חולי כל בשר ומפליא לעשות הוא ה' אלקינו The Rebbe to a physician: when the doctor knows he is the messenger of the Healer, the danger that people forget their Maker is gone. - Igros Kodesh (Lubavitcher Rebbe), כרך ד / תתקפט:5 (line 17183): זהו בנוגע לדרך הטבע. ובנוגע לפנימיות הענינים … הנה בכל רגע ורגע הוא בורא את העולם ומנהיגו כפי רצונו … כדי שהוא ועל ידו תתמלא השליחות שבשבילה ירדה נשמתו לעוה"ז התחתון הגשמי והחומרי The Rebbe: see a specialist, that is the natural way; inwardly, He creates the world every moment and governs it; and a Chassid must be healthy in body too, so that the soul's mission in this lowest world is fulfilled. - Igros Kodesh (Lubavitcher Rebbe), כרך ט / ב'תתצב:4 (line 37489): ואף שבכל ענין צריכה להיות אחיזה בדרכי הטבע וכמ"ש בכל אשר תעשה The Rebbe: in every matter there must be a hold in the ways of nature, 'in all that you do' (citing Kuntres Umaayan), so follow the doctors, but without a divided heart.

## S193 · Nature and miracle · טבע ונס

*Face F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Also touches F04 Filling and surrounding, F05 The Essence beyond both, F02 The higher and the lower unity. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Nature is the hiding Name, making the world look as if it runs by itself; miracle is the Name that is; and the Essence above both joins them, so that nature too is a wonder.

**Hold.** 'Elokim is also the numerical value of "hateva", nature, because it hides the light above that brings the world into being and gives it life.' (Shaar HaYichud 6)

**The move.** Open it. The Name of contraction hides the light that makes the world, so the world seems to stand and run by nature, and it is a shield, so that creatures are not nullified; Sfas Emes: 'olam', world, is from 'he'elem', hiding, and through the hiding itself His Godliness is recognized, for 'all creation is a wonder'; and the Rebbe: nature is rooted in Elokim, the filling light, miracle in Havayah, the surrounding light, and the Essence above both joins them. Bring it down: something that happens by itself every day, the sunrise, the kettle boiling, the seed coming up. The Shaar HaYichud's picture is a shield; only a picture, since this shield is His own kindness and not a wall between. You, this morning: the kettle is a wonder in hiding.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'all creation is a wonder.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** The relief of finding what was never gone: the hiding itself is where He is recognized. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Havayah is the God (Elokim).' Say one blessing today over something ordinary, with full attention: over water, 'by whose word all came to be'. Through the day, return to: 'all creation is a wonder'.

**Kept.** Is nature to be broken, changed, or joined to miracle? Amdur: a person's own nature is to be broken. The Degel: by wisdom a person makes himself a new nature. R. Nachman: prayer changes nature, and at the end nature will be nullified. Radzin: 'nature' is only a creation that has forgotten its beginning. The Rebbe: nature and miracle joined by the Essence. The texts do not answer each other.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:1 (line 787): ולכן הוא גם כן בגימטריא ״הטבע״, לפי שמסתיר האור שלמעלה – המהוה ומחיה העולם SH 6: Elokim equals 'nature' because it hides the light that creates and enlivens the world. - Sefat Emet, Genesis, Bereshit 27:3 (line 100): העולם נקרא ע"ש ההעלם שהטבע מכסה פנימיות חיות אלקות … וע"י ההסתר גופי' נמצא הכרת אלקותו ית"ש Sfas Emes: nature covers the inner divine vitality, and through the hiding itself God is recognized. - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ד / בס"ד. ש"פ חוקת-בלק, י"ב תמוז ה'תשכ"ט:18 (line 2579): כי שורש הטבע הוא משם אלקים, ממלא, ושורש הנסים הוא משם הוי', סובב The Rebbe: nature is rooted in Elokim (memale), miracle in Havayah (sovev); the Essence above both joins them.

## S194 · Vitality in all things · חיות

*Face F01 The world said into being now. Also touches F04 Filling and surrounding, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** The life in every thing, a stone included, is His word bringing it into being now. 'You give life to all' means 'You bring all into being'.

**Hold.** '"And You give life to all": read not "give life" but "bring into being".' (Shaar HaYichud 2)

**The move.** Open it. Vitality here is not biological life but being, given every moment from nothing; so even stones, dust and water have 'a soul and spiritual vitality', the letters of the Ten Utterances clothed in them; that light and vitality keep the stone from returning to nothing; and the Tzemach Tzedek: the main vitality comes from the surrounding light. Bring it down: the stone on the path outside, the water in your glass, the table under your book. The Tanya's picture is speech and the one who speaks; only a picture, since our words leave us and His do not. You, now, with this glass in your hand: it is being brought into being now, and so are you.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'You bring all into being.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Being held, not holding: His word never leaves Him, so there is no moment the stone, or you, stands outside Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips, from the morning prayer: 'And You give life to all.' Say the blessing over water once today, 'by whose word all came to be', slowly. Through the day, return to: 'bring into being'.

**Kept.** What is vitality? Shaar HaYichud 2: being, created from nothing. The Tzemach Tzedek: its main source is the surrounding light. Strashelye: a stone's soul is its being and existence. R. Nachman: the main vitality is the mind. Hayom Yom: Chassidus is the vitality one brings into everything.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 2:5 (line 768): ועל זה נאמר: ״ואתה מחיה את כולם״ – אל תקרי ״מחיה״, אלא ״מהוה״ SH 2: 'You give life to all' means 'You bring all into being': vitality is creation from nothing. - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:5 (line 759): שגם בדומם ממש, כמו אבנים ועפר ומים, יש בחינת נפש וחיות רוחנית SH 1 (from the Ari): even stones, dust and water have a soul and spiritual vitality: the letters of the Ten Utterances. - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 38:3 (line 526): ואפילו אבנים ועפר הדומם ממש – יש בו אור וחיות ממנו יתברך, שלא יחזור להיות אין ואפס כשהיה Tanya 38: light and vitality in stones keep them from returning to nothing.

## S195 · Mineral, plant, animal, speaker · דומם צומח חי מדבר

*Face F01 The world said into being now. Also touches F11 Darkness turned to light, F07 The home below. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 4, 5). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** Stone, plant, animal and person each stand by His word now, and the lowest is rooted highest; holy sparks lie in all four for a person to raise.

**Hold.** 'Even this physical earth and the inanimate itself: were the letters to withdraw from it for an instant, it would return to nothing.' (Shaar HaYichud 1)

**The move.** Open it. Every kingdom lives by the word standing in it, the mineral too, with 'a soul and spiritual vitality'; in the shattering, holy sparks fell into four parts, mineral, plant, animal and speaker, for man to raise; mineral, plant and animal are rooted in Tohu, before the world of repair where man is rooted, so bread can feed a person; and the Rebbe: letters are 'the mineral in man's powers', rooted higher even than the plant, so the service of letters reaches higher than the service of the mind. Bring it down: four things within reach, a stone, a loaf, a bird at the window, and you, speaking. The Rebbe's picture is Yaakov, who 'took of the stones of the place': the letters are the stones; only a picture, since letters are not stones. You, now, saying one word of Torah: the mineral in you, reaching highest.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'it would return to nothing.' When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing.

**From inside.** Being held, not holding: the stone, the plant, the bird and you stand by the same word. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say one line of Torah aloud with your lips today, word by word, since letters are the mineral in man and rooted highest. Say the blessing over one fruit slowly, raising its spark. Through the day, return to: 'by the word in it'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:4 (line 758): ואפילו ארץ הלזו הגשמית ובחינת דומם ממש, אילו היו מסתלקות ממנה כרגע חס ושלום האותיות SH 1: the inanimate too would return to nothing if the letters left it for an instant. - Keter Shem Tov 1:159:1 (line 222): נפלו ניצוצין קדישין לד' חלקים שהם דומם צומח חי מדבר שראוי לאדם להעלותם Keter Shem Tov: holy sparks fell into the four kingdoms, mineral, plant, animal, speaker, for man to raise. - Likkutei Torah, Tzav 7:9 (line 431): דבחי׳ דצ״ח שרשן הוא מבחי׳ עולם התהו שקדם לבחי׳ עולם התיקון The Alter Rebbe: mineral, plant and animal are rooted in Tohu, prior to Tikkun, where man is rooted.

## S196 · Procreation · פריה ורביה

*Face F05 The Essence beyond both. Also touches F04 Filling and surrounding, F07 The home below, F06 The soul as part of the One. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4). Thin: partly.*

**The glimpse.** Bringing a child into the world is like making something from nothing, which is the power of God Himself. In every person there are three partners, and one of them gives the soul.

**Hold.** 'Begetting, generation after generation, reveals the infinite power, especially since begetting resembles creation from nothing, which is by the power of the Essence.' (the Rebbe)

**The move.** Open it. In every person there are three partners, father, mother, and the Holy One, who gives the soul, 'a part of Havayah'; the purpose of the soul's descent is to draw the surrounding light 'from concealment into inward revelation', and begetting brings a soul into the world for that; and the Rebbe: the dwelling below begins 'from the first mitzvah, be fruitful and multiply'. Bring it down: a child in your family, a grandchild, or yourself as your parents' child. The Rebbe's own comparison is creation from nothing; only a likeness, as he says 'resembles', not the thing itself. You, now: a soul was given through you, or to you, by the third partner.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'three partners.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** Being chosen, not for anything you reached: the third partner gave the soul. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'Be fruitful and multiply, fill the earth and subdue it.' Today bless one child, or call your parents. Through the day, return to: 'three partners'.

**Thin.** The entry marks this partly thin: in Chabad the tie is stated (Derekh Mitzvotekha; the Rebbe), but outside Chabad the sources tie procreation to the soul's descent and the needed physicality, not to the unity.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ד / בס"ד. ש"פ תצא, י"ג אלול ה'תשמ"א:8 (line 2993): כידוע דבענין ההולדה (להוליד דור אחר דור) הוא גילוי כח הא"ס, ובפרט שענין ההולדה הוא (גם) דוגמת בריאה יש מאין שהיא בכח העצמות The Rebbe: begetting reveals the infinite power and resembles creation from nothing, which is the Essence's power alone. - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of procreation 2 (line 2): וזאת היא תכלית ירידת האדם לעוה"ז להמשיך גילוי אוא"ס ב"ה הסוכ"ע מההעלם אל הגילוי בבחי' פנימית ממש The purpose of the soul's descent: to draw the surrounding light into inward revelation. - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. יום שמחת תורה ה'תשמ"ב:5 (line 667): מתחיל ממצוה הראשונה פרו ורבו ומלאו את הארץ וכבשוה The Rebbe: the soul's descent to make a dwelling below begins with the first mitzvah, be fruitful and multiply.

## S197 · Place and space · מקום

*Face F02 The higher and the lower unity. Also touches F04 Filling and surrounding, F05 The Essence beyond both, F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. Tie: stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 6). Thin: no.*

**The glimpse.** He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place. Space is nothing before Him, and still He is found right here in it.

**Hold.** 'He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place.' (the Sages, in the Mitteler Rebbe's Shaar HaYichud)

**The move.** Open it. Space and time come into being from His kingship, and before His Essence they are nullified in existence, yet 'though above space and time, He is found below within space and time'; the Rebbe reads 'the place of the world' as the filling light that gives it life, and 'the world is not His place' as His not being caught by it; and the Keter Shem Tov: He is the place of the world because He is infinite, and 'if so, there must be none else'. Bring it down: the room you are in, its four walls, the distance from your chair to the door. The Shaar HaYichud's picture is sunlight inside the sun; only a picture, since the sun has a place and He does not. You, here, in this room: held in its Place.

**Stay.** Say it again, slowly: 'the place of the world.' When the mind drifts, come back to the words without arguing.

**From inside.** The lightness of a ray inside the sun, with nothing of its own to hold up; then the room again, full of Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips: 'There is none else besides Him.' Touch the mezuzah as you leave this room today. Through the day, return to: 'the place of the world'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own verified passages, copied exactly): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:6 (line 800): גם בחינת המקום והזמן בטילים במציאות ממש לגבי מהותו ועצמותו יתברך SH 7: space and time are nullified in existence before His Essence, like sunlight in the sun. - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ד / בס"ד. ש"פ דברים, שבת חזון, ד' מנחם-אב ה'תשל"ו:4 (line 2784): דהוא מקומו של עולם קאי על אור הממלא, שהוא מחי' את העולם, ואעפ"כ אין העולם מקומו, שאינו נתפס בזה The Rebbe: 'the place of the world' is the filling light; 'the world is not His place' is that it is not caught by it. - Keter Shem Tov 1:55:5 (line 88): פי' שהוא מקומו ש"ע דהוא א"ס א"כ צ"ל אין עוד Keter Shem Tov: He is the place of the world because He is infinite; if so, there must be 'ein od', none else.
