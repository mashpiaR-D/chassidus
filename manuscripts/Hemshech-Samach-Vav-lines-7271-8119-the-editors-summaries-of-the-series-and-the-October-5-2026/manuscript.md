## The shape of the range

Because the editor boils each discourse down to its conclusion, this range is the densest statement of the series' thesis anywhere in the book. The thesis comes through the same way in both the chapter-heads and the summary. The world, the light, even Ein Sof as the Kabbalists name it, are all "name and glimmer". What God wants is not that glimmer made bright. He wants the **essential concealment** (העלם העצמי) of Ein Sof, the light "included in His very Essence", to be revealed *below*, in a lower being that has itself become its vessel. The way there runs through what reveals *least*: the "no" of a prohibition, prayer "from behind", the simple servant who has no self, toil over a hard legal question.

That is why F05 (the Essence) leads the face count, with F10 (Torah and mitzvah) and F08 (bitul) close behind. F12 (knowing) is low. The range keeps saying that knowledge reaches only the radiance, and that the Essence is reached by self-giving rather than by grasp.

## Chapter-heads, Shavuot to Nasso (7271-7292): forgetting, and the "no" that reaches the Essence

The range opens on "Zion said, God has abandoned me". From the side of Havayah there is only "abandonment". From the side of Adnut, which covers Havayah so that creatures can exist, there is also "forgetting" (line 7273, F03): > כיסוי והעלם שם הוי' שיוכל להיות התהוות הנבראים (line 7273)

Yet the surrounding light never forgets, because it does not contract into the worlds. In the soul, that surrounding light is the point of the heart that stays unchanged even in exile (line 7274, F04 + F06): > ומ"מ אין שכחה לפני כסא כבודך - בתי' סוכ"ע, מקיף, כיון שאינו מתצמצם ומתלבש (line 7274)

The way to the inner face is the hidden one (line 7276, F03): > כמשל האב המסתיר פניו מבנו, ואעפ"כ מתאמץ הבן לרוץ אחרי אביו (line 7276)

The Nasso chapter-heads give the two surroundings. The "near" surrounding still belongs to the chain of worlds. The "far" surrounding "does not belong to the chain at all" (line 7283) and is "the essence of light as it is to itself" (line 7284, F05). Positive mitzvot draw the near one and prohibitions draw the far one, because a "no" is known only by negation (line 7290): > אלא בבתי' ידיעת השלילה, מקיף הרחוק שאינו בבחי' גילוי כלל, העלם העצמי (line 7290)

So exile can withhold only light that belongs to revelation (line 7292): > משא"כ בעצמות האור שלמע' מגילוי אין שייך מניעה (line 7292)

## UReisem to Ki Merosh (7293-7335): the will present in every limb, and prayer as the back

The yechidah's will has no counterpart in the other side (line 7299, F06 + F11): > הרצון דיחידה שנמשך מבחי' פני' ועצמ' א"ס - אין לנגדו לעו"ז (line 7299)

Vayikach Korach gives the clearest F04 picture in the range. The will moves every limb without being clothed in any of them. It is "two opposites" (line 7310, F04 + F09): > וא"כ ה"ז ב' הפכים -- שהאברים מתפעלים מהרצון, והוא אינו מתפעל מהם (line 7310)

It follows that above (line 7312): > איהו תפיס בכולהון ולית מאן דתפיס בי', ולכן נמצא הארתו בכולם בשוה (line 7312)

Az Yashir and Ki Merosh teach the mediators. Z'A and Malchus let "the Essence of the Emanator reach and join bounded creatures" (line 7318), and creatures take only "the barest edge" (line 7321, F01). Bitul comes before union (line 7329): > צ"ל בתכלית השפלות והצמצום, נקודה תחת היסוד (line 7329)

Prayer is "after God". Its contemplation knows only kingship, radiance "that does not touch the Essence" (line 7338). Its fruit is thirst (line 7339, F12 + F05): > שכל הגילוים הם שם כבוד מלכותו, ולכן נעשה תשוקה וצמאון להעצמות (line 7339)

## Titen Emes to Tiku (7336-7396): son, faithful servant, simple servant; the end wedged in the beginning

This is the longest thread of the range. The son serves from nature. The faithful servant serves from love and draws added light into Havayah. The simple servant serves from the yoke alone, and so "has nothing of his own" (line 7349) and is "no existence to himself" (line 7359, F08). His bitul is "incomparably greater" (line 7368), and his low work of refinement is the novelty (line 7370, F11 + F07): > ועי"ז ממשיך התחדשות מאין ליש -- המשכה חדשה שלא בהדרגה (line 7370)

Why the lowest work reaches highest: only light "as it was before the tzimtzum, above the source of the line" is new (line 7375). Only Malchus can create from nothing, because the "beginning" (the Essence, "from which all were found", line 7383) is wedged in the end (lines 7384-7386, F05 + F01): > שרק בכחו ויכלתו להוות מאין ליש (line 7386)

The whole chain was made for the dwelling below (line 7391, F07): > שנתאווה דירה בתחתונים, ובשביל זה הי' כל סדר ההשתלשלות (line 7391)

A drawing from above that leaves the lower being unchanged does not fulfil it (lines 7393-7395): > לא נשלמה הכוונה שהתחתון עצמו יהי' כלי לאלקות (line 7393)

## Kadosh Atah to Kol HaShoneh (7397-7481): Keter's nothing, and "truth of truth"

Keter's "nothing" differs from Chochmah's "nothing". Chochmah only "takes up no room". Keter is "bitul in actual existence, since Ein Sof itself shines there" (line 7408). The range turns this into a precise statement of the unity (line 7415, F02 + F05): > וכמו החילוק בין אין זולתך (שמלבד העצמות אין מציאות, אבל עמו, יש (line 7415)

"There is none besides You" leaves room for a light that exists *with* Him. "There is nothing besides You" leaves none.

Kol HaShoneh Halachos carries this into law. "These and these are the words of the living God" is Atzilus shining "only diminished" (line 7473). But "living Elokim is only Elokim, not the Essence" (line 7474), "Godliness, not God" (line 7476). Havayah is the necessary being "whose existence is from itself" (line 7477), and so (line 7478): > ואין שום דבר זולת העצמות (line 7478)

Only His essential name is "truth of truth" (line 7480). The law reaches the essential concealment, and the law follows the humble (line 7497): > ולא להעלם העצמי דא"ס, ששם שרש ההלכה (שלזה באים ע"י הביטול דוקא) (line 7497)

The decider can hold both views because "in Tiferes the Essence of Ein Sof, which bears all opposites, shines" (line 7483, F09).

## Im Bechukosai to Ein Omdin (7482-7545): higher and lower knowledge; souls as engraved letters

Line 7488 restates the lower and higher knowledge of Shaar HaYichud. Line 7491 gives the higher (F02): > ולכן אלקות הוא היש, אמיתית הענין, והעולמות אין תופסים מקום כלל (line 7491)

The range then goes one step past the Tanya's usual top (line 7493): > ואמת לאמיתו - אין עוד שלמע' גם מהביטול דכולא קמי' כל''ח (line 7493)

It follows that the lights are only a name (line 7517), and that from the light "we know only that His existence is from Himself" (line 7518). Souls are "Godliness that became a 'something'" (line 7515). Engraved letters, unlike ink, are the stone itself (line 7532). At the root, in the hidden sefirot (line 7544, F06 + F05): > ומאיר בהם אוא"ס הבל"ג ממש, ומיוחדים עמו (line 7544)

## 5668 and the closing section (7546-7609): descent, "kodesh", and the three-in-one of Rosh Hashanah

The soul's root shines only when it descends (line 7550). It rises through Torah and mitzvot above even that root, into the light "included in His very Essence" (lines 7564, 7577). "Single, one, primordial" name three levels of the light (line 7574, F02 + F04 + F05).

The closing section reads Rosh Hashanah as unity. Malchiyos is filling, "He and His organs are one" (line 7601). Zichronos is the surrounding revealed in the filling (line 7602). The shofar draws from the place where both are one (line 7603): > כדי להיות יחוד סובב וממלא צ"ל המשכה מעצמות א"ס ששם כלולים שניהם (line 7603)

## The summary (7610-7706)

The summary says the same things more briefly, and so more baldly. Its first sentence is the thesis (lines 7610-7611, F07 + F05): > פנימיות ועצמות אוא"ס עד שיהי' גילוי אוא"ס למטה (line 7611)

The Garden of Eden, for all its ascents, "never reaches the Essence". Only "the end of the act, first in thought" does (line 7612). The will of the mitzvot, having "no source of limit", is the one will that *can* be revealed in time and place (line 7617). The summary also keeps two things the chapter-heads blur. Ein Sof as the Kabbalists use the term is "a level other than the Essence, the light" (line 7639). The surrounding light is boundless even after tzimtzum, while "Chochmah of Atzilus and even Adam Kadmon are called something from nothing" (line 7638). The closing conclusion (7700-7705) ends on a balance, not on the servant alone (line 7705, F05 + F09): > כדי שהעצמות (העלם העצמ5י) יהי' בגילוי, צריכה להיות גם העבודה בבחי' הגילויים (line 7705)

## What this adds to what the Tanya says, or says differently

1. **The unity is above bitul.** In the Tanya (SH 3-6) the height is "all before Him is as nothing", which is the higher unity. Here that is still "of the light", and "truth of truth" is "there is nothing else" above it, reserved for the future (lines 7493, 7415). The Tanya's highest unity becomes a middle level.
2. **"Ein Sof" is not the Essence.** The Tanya uses "Or Ein Sof" as the top term. Here "Ein Sof" names the light, "a level other than the Essence" (line 7639). The light "reveals nothing of the Essence" except that its existence is from itself (line 7518).
3. **The "no" outranks the "yes".** Tanya ch. 4 and 23 locate the unity in the positive mitzvah as the clothing of the will. Here the prohibitions, prayer "from behind" and the yoke reach what the positive mitzvot and Torah study with delight cannot (lines 7290, 7332, 7703).
4. **The lowest servant is the highest.** Tanya ch. 41 has "servant" and "son" as two motives to hold together. Here the simple servant, who "has nothing of his own", reaches the Essence, and the faithful servant and the son do not (lines 7400-7402, 7421).
5. **Toil in law as the dwelling below.** Tanya ch. 5 says the halacha is the will clothed in wisdom. Here the Oral Torah reached through toil and bitul is "truth of truth", above "these and these" (lines 7480-7481, 7497).

## Where the tie is thin

- The whole range is secondhand. It is an editor's paraphrase, and a stated tie here means the editor states it. - The 413 index lines carry no tie. They are marked none even where the headword is a unity term. - The Kabbalistic mapping lines (partzufim, the hair of the skull, the four levels of flow from Z'A to Malchus: 7281, 7315, 7326-7328, 7453-7458) are marked structural. They carry the argument but not the unity itself. - Line 7363 (the servant's work "as if the master did it", read as the dwelling below) is the one *reading*. - The maamar labels at 7452-7504 are inferred and may be off by one.

## Disagreements kept

- **With the Tanya on the top of the ladder** (point 1 above). The Tanya's "all is as nothing before Him" is not the last word here. The series marks it as belonging to the light, below "there is nothing else".
- **With the Tanya on knowing.** Tanya ch. 42 and SH make contemplation the path to the higher unity. Here contemplation reaches "only radiance and glow" (line 7333), and the Essence comes through bitul, yoke and thirst (lines 7339, 7497). This does not deny the Tanya: contemplation still leads to the thirst. It does demote it.
- **Inside the series, on who draws the Essence.** The chapter-heads praise the simple servant above the son (7400-7402). The summary's conclusion says revelation needs the sons and Torah too (line 7705). The editor himself notes (line 7597) that one might rank the son-turned-servant highest, but "all Israel have a share", so his advantage is only that he has both. The tension is kept, not resolved.
- **On creation.** The chapter-heads place the power of creation in Malchus alone, since the Essence is wedged in the end (7386, 7672). The summary says creation "is from the light, being like the luminary" (line 7640). Both are in the text. The series' own answer is that the hidden power is in Malchus and its revelation is through the light (line 7590). That reconciliation is the text's own. Applying it to these two lines is ours.
