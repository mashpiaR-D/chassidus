## The shape of the range

The Idra Rabba ("the great gathering") is a single scene. Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai calls ten companions into a field, sits them in a circle, and asks each in turn to stand and "set in order" one feature of a vast face. First comes the face of Atika, the Ancient One, also called Arikh Anpin, the Long Face: His skull, the dew that drips from it, His hair, His forehead, His one open eye, His nose, and the thirteen parts of His white beard. Then comes the face of Zeir Anpin, the Small Face: a skull, three hollows of mind, black hair, a forehead, eyes of four colors, a nose that smokes, ears, and a beard of nine parts. Then the male and female are drawn out of one body, and the whole is called Adam, the human form. At the end three of the ten die in joy, their souls leaving "by a kiss" (line 2675).

An editor's note at the head of the Idra (line 2321) warns that every bodily word here (skull, hair, nose, ear) is a symbol for hidden inner things and not a picture of a body. The Idra itself says the same in its closing summary: the form is not this form, only "like" this form (line 2617).

## The unity of G-d

**1. It is all one, and the difference is only from our side.** The Idra's own summary of everything it has said:

> כללא דכל מלין, עתיקא דעתיקין, וזעיר אפין, כלא חד. כלא הוה. כלא הוי. כלא יהא. לא ישתני. ולא משתני (Zohar, Idra Rabba 41:1; line 2617)
> The sum of all these words: the Ancient of Ancients and the Small Face are all one. All was, all is, all will be. He does not change and is not changed.

> ומסטרא דילן הוו שניין דא מן דא (Zohar, Idra Rabba 41:3; line 2619)
> It is from our side that they differ one from the other.

The same point is made about time. The "one day" of the end is neither day nor night, because day and night exist only from where we stand (לית יום אקרי, אלא מסטרא דילן, line 2484). So the two faces, mercy and judgment, day and night, are real to us and one in Him. This is very close to what Chabad later says in Shaar Hayichud: the many exist "from our side," while from His side nothing is added (ours).

**2. Known and not known.** Atika is "known and not known" in His arrangements (ובתיקונוי ידיע ולא ידיע, line 2334). He "sits and is not found" (יתיב ולא שכיח, line 2485). The Idra never lets the face become an object. Each feature is described, and then withdrawn.

> כלא הוא, והוא סתים מכל סטרוי (Zohar, Idra Rabba 9:1; line 2382)
> All is He, and He is hidden on every side.

**4. In the Ancient there is no left.** (לית שמאלא בהאי עתיקא סתימאה, כלא ימינא, line 2351) At the deepest level there is no judgment at all, only right, only giving. Right and left, light and dark, mercy and judgment hang on the Small Face, "and not on the Ancient" (ולא בעתיקא, line 2516). Judgment comes only when the inner will is hidden: "Who causes this judgment? The will of wills that is not revealed" (מאן גרים להאי דינא. רעוא דרעוין דלא אתגלי, line 2549). He does not change (אני ה' לא שניתי); what changes is how much of the will shows.

**5. The one eye that holds the world.**

> אי עינא דא אסתים רגעא חדא, לא יכלין לקיימא כלהו (Zohar, Idra Rabba 8:11; line 2370)
> If this eye closed for one moment, none of them could stand.

It is an eye with no lid, like the eye of a fish, that never sleeps; "all are fed from its watching" (ומאשגחותא דהאי עינא מתזנן כלהו, line 2369). This is the Idra's version of the world held in being each moment (map S63).

**6. One body, one name.** When the upper and lower are joined, "they became one body," and "in all of them only One is seen" (ובכלהו לא אתחזי אלא חד, line 2659), which is why the angels say "holy, holy, holy... the whole earth is full of His glory." At the end the companions are told the verse of Moshe: "Hashem is the G-d" (Devarim 4:39), read as the Ancient and the Small Face being one: "and all is one, blessed be His name" (וכלא הוא חד, בריך שמיה, line 2649).

**7. The one soul through all.** The human form was made so that one breath of life would pass through every level "so that all would be in one unity." Whoever cuts this unity is like one who says there is another soul besides this one:

> ולמהוי כלא ביחודא חד. ומאן דפסיק האי יחודא מן עלמא, כמאן דפסיק נשמתא דא, ומחזי דאית נשמתא אחרא (Zohar, Idra Rabba 41:7; line 2623)
> So that all would be in one unity. Whoever cuts this unity from the world is like one who cuts off this soul and shows that there is another soul besides it.

## States of consciousness

- **Love, not fear, as the door to the secret.** Habakkuk heard and feared. "With us the matter hangs on love" (אנן בחביבותא תלייא מלתא, line 2327). The Israelites in the desert asked "Is Hashem among us or not (ayin)?" and were punished because they did it "not in love but as a test" (משום דלא עבדו בחביבותא, אלא בנסיונא, line 2352). Maps to S41-S46 (love). The Idra is the one place in this volume where the deepest secret is explicitly opened by love and closed by testing.
- **The settled spirit that can hold a secret.** One whose spirit is not settled hears a thing and it rolls in him "like a thorn in water until he throws it out" (כחיזרא במיא, line 2328); one whose spirit is lasting keeps it. "The world stands only on secrecy" (line 2329). Close to S32 (quiet). *Proposed state: the settled spirit.*
- **Under the open eye.** Being seen by an eye that is "always open, always smiling, always glad" (פקיחא תדיר. חייכאן תדיר. וחדאן, line 2377). The lower eye is washed in the white of the upper eye (line 2372), and when the white is revealed all other colors sink and only white is seen (line 2536-2537). This is a being-seen that is pure kindness. Near S33 (before Whom) and S63, but with a different feel: not awe before a King, but being looked at by a face that is laughing with you. *Proposed state: under the open eye.*
- **The hour of favor.** The forehead of the Ancient is "the will of wills" (רעוא דרעוין, line 2354). When it is revealed, prayers are accepted and anger calms. Rabbi Elazar names the time: Shabbos afternoon at Mincha (line 2355). Maps to S76 (Shabbos), with a new point: there is a weekly hour where the whole of judgment rests.
- **Softness as the condition for wisdom.** Wisdom flows only through soft hair: "wisdom does not come out of a person who is hard and a master of anger" (לית חכמתא נפקא מבר נש דאיהו קשישא … ומארי דרוגזא, line 2414), and "words of the wise are heard in gentleness." Maps to S32; the point is that anger closes the channel of mind itself.
- **Quiet wisdom like wine on its lees.** The mind of the Ancient is "quiet and still like good wine on its lees" (שקיט ושכיך כחמר טב על דורדייה, line 2599). The Small Face's wisdom flows and speaks; the wisdom of wisdom is still. A picture for S32 at its highest.
- **Death by a kiss.** The three who died "while their souls were sweetened by those words" left "by a kiss" (נפקא נשמתייהו בנשיקה, line 2675), because they had entered and not come back out. Rabbi Shimon's rule is that these secrets are only for those "who entered and came out" (דעאלו ונפקו, line 2616): whoever enters and does not come out, better for him not to have been created. This is the Idra's run and return, and it is the Zohar's form of running out and coming back (ratzo vashov), which the Tanya makes the rule of the whole service.

## Meditation and practice

- **The Shabbos Mincha "et ratzon."** Say "ואני תפלתי לך ה' עת רצון" at Shabbos Mincha with the knowledge that this is the hour when the will of wills is uncovered and anger calms (line 2355). Ties to `before_whom` and `lips`. *Proposed technique: the hour of the will of wills.*
- **Praying to the Long-Patient One.** When Rav Hamnuna the Elder prayed he said: "To the Master of the nose I pray, to the Master of the nose I plead" (לבעל החוטם אני מתפלל, לבעל החוטם אני מתחנן, line 2390). The "nose" of the Ancient is endless patience, "long of breath." Before asking, one turns to the part of G-d that does not get angry. Ties to `before_whom` and `own_words`. *Proposed technique: turning to the Patient One.*
- **Remembering the first kindness in the middle of anger.** "In anger remember mercy" is read as: remember the upper kindness of the Ancient, in which mercy wakes for all (line 2566). Kindness of the Ancient is "kindness of truth," given "for the life of the soul, not the life of the body" (line 2460). Ties to `hold` and `edge`.
- **Sin given a passage.** Under the Ancient's nose there is a path with no hair, so that sin can "pass through" to the holy mouth that says "I have forgiven" (line 2446). "Merit, and it passes; do not merit, and it stays sin" (זכו עובר, לא זכו פשע). When this path is revealed "all the accusers are silent," and the person who puts a finger to the lips to signal silence marks this path (line 2449). A practice of letting a fault pass through to forgiveness rather than clinging to it. Ties to `heart_after` and S77.
- **The ear that waits.** The ear is made with a bent passage "so the voice is held back from going into the mind, and is discerned in the mind, not in haste" (line 2555, in the variant). The ear and the heart are both "testers" from one source (line 2561). A practice of hearing slowly. Ties to `discern`.
- **Speaking so the words rise.** Rabbi Shimon sees each part of the face waiting for the word to leave a companion's mouth, and then rising and being crowned (lines 2488, 2441). The words of Torah said here are heard above with joy (line 2490). Ties to `lips` and `chazarah`.

## The big human questions

- **Why is there judgment if G-d is all good?** Because the inner will is hidden, not because He changes (line 2549). Judgment is real only at the level of the Small Face, "from our side."
- **Why did the first worlds fail?** The kings of Edom "died" because the human form was not yet set; "the form of Adam includes all and can settle all" (line 2497). They did not vanish; "died" means they went down from their first level, and once Adam was set they got new names and were sweetened (line 2499). Brokenness is a stage before the form that can hold both sides.
- **What is a person?** The form "Adam" is the whole of upper and lower, male and female, mercy and judgment joined (lines 2618, 2633). Only what joins this body is holy; the spirits that do not join it wander and are unclean (lines 2655-2657). A human being is the shape in which G-d's unity can be seen.
- **What of death?** For those who cling, death is a kiss. "You who cling to Hashem your G-d are all alive today" (line 2676). The verse says cling "in Hashem, literally," not "to Hashem" (ולא כתיב הדבקים ליי, אלא ביי ממש, line 2568).
- **Who may know this?** Only those who can enter and come back out, and only in love.

## For the guide (ours)

1. The Idra gives the guide its clearest ancient source for "from our side": the two faces, day and night, mercy and judgment are one in Him and differ only as seen by us (lines 2484, 2617, 2619). This sits well beside Shaar Hayichud.
2. The "open eye that laughs" is a strong, warm image for being held in being. It should enter as a state near S63 and S33, offered to people who meet G-d's watching only as surveillance.
3. The Idra's rule, love opens and testing closes (lines 2327, 2352), is a clear teaching for anyone who approaches the question "is He here or not" as an experiment.
4. The requirement to "enter and come out" belongs with the map's return current. The Idra is also the plain warning that going in without coming back is not the goal of the path.
5. The Shabbos Mincha hour and "to the Master of the nose I pray" are simple, concrete practices from this range that anyone can use.
