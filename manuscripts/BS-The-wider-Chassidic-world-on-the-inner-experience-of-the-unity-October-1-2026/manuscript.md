## What this file is

The guide leads people into "there is nothing but Him, and He is fully here", the Chabad way. Chabad grew out of a wider world. That world has its own words for what the unity feels like from inside, and its own doors into it. This file maps them, so the guide can use what fits and know where Chabad parts ways.

It builds on three existing files and does not repeat them: - `being-held/sources-baal-shem-tov-school.md`: the Besht school on being held, trust, providence, equanimity's basic texts, hiddenness. Cited here, not copied. - `PIASECZNO.md` and `piaseczno/`: the Piaseczner's full method, states and counterfeits. Entries BS-19 to BS-27 distil it into the entry format and point back to its sections. - `consciousness-map/MAP.md` and `ONE-PROCESS.md`: each entry names the nearest map states (S-numbers) and currents (C-numbers).

**Verification.** Every Hebrew line below was checked with `find_he.py verify FILE "…" --line N` and came back FOUND (77 of 77). All texts are Sefaria-derived, not OCR. Breslov and Tanya files carry vowels; quotes are given without them. **stated** = a source says it; **ours** = our synthesis. Each entry's "Whose" line says which parts are which.

## The short answer

1. **The Besht's centre is dveikus: a thread, not a peak.** The mind keeps resting on Him while the hands work. It is trained in small things, comes and goes like fire, and its first fruit is equanimity (BS-01 to BS-03, BS-07).
2. **"No place empty of Him" is applied to the mind itself.** Even the thought you drifted into is not outside Him; He is there "with His back turned" (Maggid). Even a pleasure has a spark you can follow home (Besht, Vitebsk). Even the bridge you are afraid on has no spot without Him (R. Nosson) (BS-04, BS-05, BS-17).
3. **Ayin is a place, not a thought.** Where nothing breaks (Maggid), where every real change passes (Kozhnitz), where you ask what "I" refers to and find His life (Berditchev) (BS-08, BS-09).
4. **The deepest is unfelt.** Felt love and awe are the road; the real attachment is beyond feeling, held by faith (Vitebsk, Piaseczno). Chabad says the same in Kuntres HaHitpa'alut (BS-10).
5. **Joy is the floor.** All of them: the Besht (awe without joy is black bile), Breslov (force yourself, even with silliness; one hour for the broken heart), Chabad (rejoice over the unity as over an inheritance) (BS-12, BS-13).
6. **Breslov's own door is speech.** Hisbodedus: your own words, alone, every day; at night it can undo the self into His unity "and the whole world with it" (BS-14 to BS-16, BS-18).
7. **The Piaseczner is the psychologist of the way in.** Start from what is already moving; quiet by watching; one line that fills the room; a picture that falls away; a hum that starts singing itself; a small quiet group; test by fruit, afterwards (BS-19 to BS-27).

## The entries ### BS-01 · A thread that stays with Him

**What it is.** Dveikus, the Baal Shem Tov's centre: the mind keeps resting on G-d while the hands and mouth do other things. Not a peak. A thread that runs through the day.

**From inside.** In prayer it is like not wanting to let go of a word: you stretch the word out because you do not want to part from it (Tzava'at HaRivash 70:1). The Besht's parable, as R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk tells it: two pieces of silver weld only where the rust is scraped off; then they become one. Dveikus is the same: nothing in between (Peri HaAretz, Ki Tisa 1). He adds that strong pleasure holds the mind without effort, and that is what attachment is like: the mind is simply not free to wander.

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Start gently. Do not spend all your strength at the start of prayer; the attachment comes in the middle (Tzava'at HaRivash 32:1). If it does not come, say the words with plain intention and strengthen yourself little by little until He helps (32:2). 2. Train it in small things: while attached, ask Him for some household need, or do or say an ordinary thing, so that you get used to staying attached while doing worldly things (81:1). 3. When you must talk of worldly matters, be like a man who steps out of his house meaning to come straight back (84:5; already in being-held, 'Where your thought is').

**Signs it is real.** Praise and insult start to weigh the same, because the mind is busy elsewhere (Tzava'at HaRivash 10:1, stated). Ordinary talk and work no longer cut the thread. One keeps learning; the attachment does not replace it.

**Its counterfeit.** Idle chatter excused by 'I am in dveikus'. The text warns exactly here: one who speaks idle words and relies on his dveikus should take great care not to fall from it (30:2). Also a private glow that makes one feel above others (12:1).

**Guards.** Not a reason to stop learning: if he does not learn, his dveikus will be cancelled (29:2). Not a badge: he must not tell himself he is greater than his friend because he serves in dveikus (12:1). For the guide (ours): offer it as a thread to pick up again, never as a state to hold by force.

**Chabad.** Agrees on the goal: Tanya ch. 41 asks one to know that 'G-d stands over him' and the whole earth is full of His glory. Differs on the road (ours): Chabad reaches and keeps the awareness through hisbonenus, with Torah and mitzvos as the vehicle, and is wary of training a felt state (see PIASECZNO.md section 7 on Kuntres HaHitpa'alut). Map: S33 'Before Whom', C5.

- «שמחמת דביקות אינו רוצה לפרוד עצמו מהתיבה ולכך מאריך באותה תיבה» (Tzava'at HaRivash 70:1; `Chassidus-txt/Baal Shem Tov (R. Yisrael ben Eliezer) (1698-1760)/Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770).txt` line 126). Because of attachment he does not want to part from the word, so he lengthens that word. - «שצריך לגרור מעצמותו שלא יהיה עליו שום חלודה ומסך מבדיל» (Peri HaAretz, Ki Tisa 1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk (1730-1788)/Peri HaAretz (Vitebsk and Tiberias, 1752-1788).txt` line 81). He must scrape from his very self, so that no rust and no separating screen is on him. - «ידבק עצמו בבורא יתברך ויבקש דבר מצרכי בית» (Tzava'at HaRivash 81:1; `Chassidus-txt/Baal Shem Tov (R. Yisrael ben Eliezer) (1698-1760)/Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770).txt` line 149). Let him attach himself to the Creator and ask for some household need [to train himself].

**Whose.** stated (images, steps, warnings); ours (the 'thread' framing, the guide's use, the Chabad comparison).

**In a chat today.** "While you do the dishes, keep one small thread to Him. Not a big feeling. Just: You're here. When the thread drops, pick it up again. No fuss."

## BS-02 · Alone in a full room

**What it is.** Being alone with G-d without leaving people. The Besht's 'small' attachment: no high worlds, only one plain thought.

**From inside.** Sometimes when he is attached he can be alone even in a house full of people (Tzava'at HaRivash 63:2). It can be like a small child with a small mind: he only thinks that the whole earth is full of His glory and that He is near him, and still the attachment is great (67:1; also in being-held). The Kozhnitzer Maggid: it should be as certain as a friend standing in front of you, 'for so it surely is' (Avodat Yisrael, Avot 1:16).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Do not wait for a quiet room. 2. Hold one plain thought: He fills this place, and He is near me. 3. The Kozhnitzer's step: picture, with no doubt at all, that the Creator is present before you, the way your friend is present when he stands facing you. He calls this 'the essence of the service'. 4. If even that is too much, hold only the certainty, not a picture.

**Signs it is real.** The noise of the room stops mattering. Nothing about you looks different. You treat the people around you better, not worse.

**Its counterfeit.** Withdrawing behind a blank face; a cool distance from people that calls itself inwardness.

**Guards.** (ours) The Besht also advises not to look at the faces of people whose thoughts are not attached (Tzava'at HaRivash 50:1). The guide does not pass this on: in a chat today it would breed contempt. The picture is never a form of G-d (see BS-22).

**Chabad.** Agrees on the fact: 'G-d stands over him... and looks at him' (Tanya ch. 41). Differs in tone (ours): Tanya's frame is awe, like one standing before the king; Kozhnitz's is the certainty of a friend's presence; the Piaseczner (a Kozhnitz descendant) makes it a son before his father. Map: S33, S85.

- «לפעמים כשהוא דבוק יכול להתבודד עצמו אפילו בבית שיש ב"א» (Tzava'at HaRivash 63:2; `Chassidus-txt/Baal Shem Tov (R. Yisrael ben Eliezer) (1698-1760)/Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770).txt` line 118). Sometimes, when he is attached, he can be alone with himself even in a house where there are people. - «לצייר תמיד באמת ובתמים מציאות הבורא ית' בלי שום ספק כלל וכמו שהוא עומד בפני חבירו» (Avodat Yisrael, Avot 1:16; `Chassidus-txt/R. Yisrael Hopstein, Maggid of Kozhnitz (1737-1814)/Avodat Yisrael (Kozienice, Poland, 1750-1810).txt` line 471). To picture always, truly and wholeheartedly, the presence of the Creator without any doubt, just as one stands before his friend. - «והנה ה׳ נצב עליו» (Tanya ch. 41; `Chabad Library/_drive/01 Alter Rebbe — R. **Whose.** stated (Besht, Kozhnitz, Tanya); ours (the comparison of tones, the guard about faces).

**In a chat today.** "You don't have to leave the room. Right where you are, think one plain thought: He fills this room, and He's near me. Stay with it for three breaths."

## BS-03 · A fire that comes and goes

**What it is.** Fervour (hislahavus) is real attachment, but by nature it cannot be constant. It pulses.

**From inside.** The Besht: serving with fervour is complete attachment, and that cannot be always; it is 'touching and not touching', like fire. When you blow on a fire it first goes out, then it grows; the flame itself rises and falls and moves. 'Constant pleasure is not pleasure' (Tzava'at HaRivash 111:2).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Expect the pulse. 2. When it dips, do not chase it; keep saying the words with simple intention (32:2). 3. When it comes, let it come; do not grab. 4. Begin quietly; the fire takes in the middle (32:1).

**Signs it is real.** Coming and going without panic. The dip is followed by a return. Something of it stays in the day.

**Its counterfeit.** Trying to keep the flame at full height: shouting, swaying harder, forcing tears. The Piaseczner's test: stop all movement; if everything goes quiet and you could chat, it was compulsion (Hakhsharat 5:33). Insisting on feeling kills it (Hakhsharat 3:4).

**Guards.** Never measure a person by how long the fire lasted. For someone who has just 'lost it', this is first of all comfort.

**Chabad.** Agrees fully (ours): this is ratzo vashov, running and returning (MAP C5, S47, S48). Chabad adds that the 'return' goes down into deed and the world, which the Besht's image leaves implicit.

- «וזה א"א להיות תמיד רק מטי ולא מטי כדרך אש» (Tzava'at HaRivash 111:2; `Chassidus-txt/Baal Shem Tov (R. Yisrael ben Eliezer) (1698-1760)/Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770).txt` line 228). And this cannot be constant, only touching and not touching, the way of fire. - «כי תענוג תמידי אינו תענוג» (Tzava'at HaRivash 111:2; `Chassidus-txt/Baal Shem Tov (R. Yisrael ben Eliezer) (1698-1760)/Tzava'at HaRivash (Medzhibozh and Mezritch, c. 1740-1770).txt` line 228). For constant pleasure is not pleasure. - «אל יתעקש להתעורר דוקא» (Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 3:4; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 59). Let him not insist on being aroused.

**Whose.** stated (the image and the rule); ours (the link to ratzo vashov and the guide's use).

**In a chat today.** "It's meant to come and go. A fire that never flickers isn't a fire. When it fades, that isn't failure. It's the breath out."

## BS-04 · He is in the drift too

**What it is.** No place is empty of Him, applied to the mind: even the thought you drifted into is not outside Him. He is there 'with His back turned'.

**From inside.** The Maggid: a person who wanted to cleave, and whose thought went off to worldly things, is where his thought is; and since no place is empty of Him, wherever he is he can find attachment to the Creator from the very place he is. He is there in hiding, His 'back'. If he is wise-hearted he can find, in that very thought, the Godliness in it; then he is a returner, and it gives G-d great delight, like a father whose little son pulls away the cover his father hid behind (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 75:1). The Besht: all a person's thoughts have His being in them (Keter Shem Tov 1:29).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Notice you drifted. 2. Do not jump away in fright: you have not left His world. 3. (Maggid) Find what is alive in the thought (a love, a fear, a delight) and turn that toward its source. 4. (For most people, Chabad's way, below) Do not engage the content; simply turn back.

**Signs it is real.** Drift stops being a disaster. Returning becomes quick and light, even playful, like the child finding the father's face.

**Its counterfeit.** Entertaining a craving 'in order to raise it'. That is the trap the Alter Rebbe names.

**Guards.** Tanya ch. 28 (stated): one should not be foolish and try to raise the foreign thoughts; that was said only of tzaddikim, whose stray thoughts come from others, not from their own heart. So the guide uses the Maggid's teaching as comfort (you have not fallen out of His world) and the Chabad method in practice (turn back without commentary) (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees on the ground (no place empty of Him); differs on method, explicitly (Tanya ch. 28). Map: S01 'Scattered', S68 'The hiding seen as His'.

- «וכ"מ שהוא האדם ימצא שם דביקות הבורא ית' מהמקום אשר הוא שם» (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 75:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dov Ber, Maggid of Mezritch (c.1704-1772)/Maggid Devarav leYaakov (Mezritch, Volhynia, 1760-1780).txt` line 171). **Whose.** stated (Maggid; Tanya); ours (how the two are combined for the guide).

**In a chat today.** "You drifted. That's all right. You haven't left Him; He's there in the drift too, just facing away. Don't argue with the thought. Turn around."

## BS-05 · Tracing a pleasure to its root

**What it is.** Every enjoyable thing has a spark of holiness in it. Its sweetness is borrowed from Him. So a pleasure can be a doorway, if you follow it back.

**From inside.** The Besht: if he hears something funny and joy is born in him, let him think: this is a part of the world of love. If he sees or eats something he enjoys: this is a part of the world of delight; and let him take great care that the delight not turn coarse (Tzava'at HaRivash 87:9). There is nothing empty of sparks, even wood and stones (141:1). When he eats or uses a thing, even for his body's need, and then serves G-d with the strength it gave him, he repairs the sparks (109:2). R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk: the taste in every food is the Shechinah in exile; 'in all your ways know Him' (Peri HaAretz, Vayikra 14).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Catch the moment of pleasure: a joke, a taste, music, a view. 2. Ask where its sweetness comes from. 3. Say thank you (a blessing is the ready-made form). 4. Use the strength it gave you for something good.

**Signs it is real.** Gratitude grows; the pleasure is enjoyed more, not less, and it does not swallow you. You can stop when you have had enough.

**Its counterfeit.** 'Raising sparks' as a spiritual name for indulgence: overeating, or lingering on a forbidden attraction, while telling oneself it is holy work.

**Guards.** (ours, following Tanya ch. 28) The guide uses this only with innocent pleasures (food, laughter, music, nature), never with compulsive or forbidden desire. For someone struggling with an addiction, it does not suggest 'elevating' the craving.

**Chabad.** Agrees on eating and enjoying for the sake of Heaven, with blessings as the vehicle (ours). Differs where the Besht turns a bad love back toward its source (87:8): Tanya ch. 28 reserves that for tzaddikim. Map: S29, S81, S82.

**In a chat today.** "That taste you're enjoying: the sweetness didn't make itself. Stay with it a second longer and thank the One who put it there."

## BS-06 · The good eye

**What it is.** A way of looking. You see a thing as nothing in itself, only His life resting in it. That look, says the Maggid, blesses the thing. Its opposite, the admiring look that makes a thing stand on its own, cuts it from its root.

**From inside.** The Maggid on 'the good of eye shall be blessed': when a wise person looks at a thing he knows it is as nothing before Him, truly nothing apart from the Godliness resting in it, and by such looking he draws more life into it. The 'bad eye' looks at the thing and marvels, 'how lovely this is', and makes it a thing on its own, and so cuts it from its root (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 43:1). R. Nachman: always look at the meaning (sechel) in each thing and bind yourself to it, so it lights your way to come close to G-d through that very thing (Likutei Moharan I 1:2).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Look at one thing: a tree, a cup, a face. 2. Drop 'how lovely this is', as if it stood alone. 3. Instead: this is His life, here, now. 4. Ask what this thing is saying to you about Him (Breslov: its sechel). 5. Notice the warmth that comes.

**Signs it is real.** You love the thing more, not less. People feel looked at kindly. Beauty stops being something to grab.

**Its counterfeit.** A cold 'it's all illusion' that looks through people and dismisses them. The good eye blesses; it never belittles.

**Guards.** Not for someone in a state of unreality (MAP S20): 'it is nothing' can frighten. With them, stress 'He is giving it life', not 'it is nothing' (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees closely (ours): Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah sees the creative word inside each thing, and Iggeret HaKodesh 11 asks one to picture each being coming to be from nothing every moment. The Maggid adds an effect on the thing seen (it is blessed); Chabad stresses the effect on the one who sees. Map: S63 'The world held', S72, S85.

- «כשמסתכל בדבר יודע שהדבר ההוא כאין נגדו ית'» (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 43:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dov Ber, Maggid of Mezritch (c.1704-1772)/Maggid Devarav leYaakov (Mezritch, Volhynia, 1760-1780).txt` line 111). When he looks at a thing he knows that this thing is as nothing before Him. - «ומתמיה עליו דהיינו מה נאה דבר זה ועושה אותו דבר לדבר בפ"ע» (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 43:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Dov Ber, Maggid of Mezritch (c.1704-1772)/Maggid Devarav leYaakov (Mezritch, Volhynia, 1760-1780).txt` line 111). Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 4). To look at the meaning in each thing.

**Whose.** stated (Maggid, R. Nachman); ours (steps, guard, Chabad comparison).

**In a chat today.** "Look at that tree, or at your child's face, for a moment. Not 'how lovely', as if it stood on its own. Rather: this is Him, here, giving it life right now. Do you love it more?"

## BS-07 · Equanimity from inside

**What it is.** Hishtavus: praise and insult, good food and plain food, what you wanted and what you did not, all weigh the same. Here: what it is like from inside, and its Breslov and Chabad forms. (The basic texts, Tzava'at HaRivash 2 and 10, are in being-held, 'Equanimity'.)

**From inside.** Not numbness. The Besht says it comes from dveikus: the mind is too busy with Him to weigh the rest (Tzava'at HaRivash 10:1). The Vitebsker: when you pray for something you lack, first let the prayer wake you; then cling to the root, until the wish for the thing falls away by itself (Peri HaAretz, Nasso 7). R. Nachman: when a person knows that everything that happens to him is for his good, that is a taste of the world to come; today we bless 'the Good' on good news and 'the true Judge' on bad; in the future it will all be 'the Good' (Likutei Moharan I 4:1).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. (Besht) Say of each event: this is from Him. 2. (Vitebsk) Pray for what you need; when the prayer has warmed you, rest in Him until the wanting quiets. 3. (Breslov) Practise the one blessing: try to see this too as 'the Good'. 4. (Chabad) Contemplate that you are being brought into being from nothing this very moment, by the source of all good; then how can you say 'it is bad for me'? (Iggeret HaKodesh 11).

**Signs it is real.** Lightness, not flatness. You still care and still act. Insult stings less. Joy is easier.

**Its counterfeit.** Indifference; suppressed pain; 'nothing matters'; using 'it's all from Him' to skip a real grief or a real wrong.

**Guards.** Never tell a grieving or frightened person that it is all the same. Equanimity is a fruit, not a demand. Breslov itself gives the broken heart its set hour (Likutei Moharan II 24:2). Esh Kodesh shows that in catastrophe one weeps with G-d (see BS-26).

**Chabad.** Agrees, stated: 'in truth no evil comes down from above, and all is good' (Iggeret HaKodesh 11), and so 'first of all, a person should rejoice'. The ground differs (ours): for the Besht it grows from dveikus, for Chabad from contemplating creation from nothing, for Breslov from faith that all is good, held as a foretaste. Map: S84 'Trust', S85.

And what causes this is constant attachment to the Creator. - «אבל אחר שיתעורר ידבק אל שורשו שיבטל ממנו חפץ השגת החסרון» (Peri HaAretz, Nasso 7; `Chassidus-txt/R. **In a chat today.** "You don't have to feel it's all the same. Try one thing: when the praise or the insult comes, say quietly, 'this too came from Him', and see if it weighs a little less."

## BS-08 · Where nothing breaks

**What it is.** Ayin (nothingness) as an experience: holding yourself as nothing before Him. Not worthlessness. A place in you below the level where things can be hurt or broken, and the gate every real change passes through.

**From inside.** The Maggid: in something at the level of ayin there is no breaking, because breaking belongs to a measure or a vessel. One who holds himself as nothing because of his attachment is like the greatest minister standing before the king: in his own eyes he is small, he wants no honour, all his traits are nullified (Maggid Devarav leYaakov 22:2). Ayin is also what joins opposites: in G-d love and fear are together (3:1). The Kozhnitzer: when a thing is to change from one state to another it must come for a moment to ayin, like the egg that decays before the chick takes form (Avodat Yisrael, Vayishlach 1).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. When you are hurt or stuck, notice the 'someone' that is hurt. 2. Stand before Him, like the minister before the king: that someone is small here. 3. Let the old shape loosen, for a moment. 4. Then ask what new thing wants to grow.

**Signs it is real.** Insult has less grip. Love and awe can be felt together. Real change follows; you are not just calmer, you are different.

**Its counterfeit.** Self-erasure and worthlessness. The Piaseczner: one who feels himself worthless behaves worthlessly (Esh Kodesh 541, in PIASECZNO.md section 6). Also depression or dissociation dressed up as 'ayin'.

**Guards.** Not for someone already crushed or self-hating: offer them BS-13 first (find the good point). Ayin is held 'because of attachment', in His presence, never alone with one's failures (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees, and shares the source (the Maggid is the Alter Rebbe's teacher). Bitul is the axis of Chabad (MAP C4, S56 'The self quieted', S59 'True lowliness'), and Tanya ch. 33 says that even on the earth below there is nothing else.

## BS-09 · Who is saying 'I'?

**What it is.** An inquiry into the word 'I'. The aliveness you find when you look is His; only He can truly say 'I'.

**From inside.** R. Levi Yitzchak: when one says 'I' he must look at himself and know what he is saying 'I' about. The life in him is the Creator; were that life withdrawn he would be nothing. So no angel or person can truly say 'I'; only G-d can. King David cleaved to the trait of ayin and knew that all his life and movement was the part of G-d in him (Kedushat Levi, Shemot 18). 'G-d is King', in the present tense, is ayin: He is giving us life now, 'we are nothing, only G-d gives us strength' (Bereshit 1). The Besht describes prayer moving from spoken letters to the letters of thought, and then to 'the trait of ayin, where all his bodily powers are nullified' (Keter Shem Tov 2:51).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. In a quiet moment say 'I' slowly. 2. Look at what it points to: the body? the thoughts? 3. Notice the aliveness under them. 4. Say: this is being given to me right now. 5. Then act from it.

**Signs it is real.** Lightness, gratitude, less defending of oneself. The 'I' does not vanish in fear; it softens into 'given'.

**Its counterfeit.** Depersonalisation; panic that 'I don't exist' (MAP S20). Or a clever, cold 'there is no self' that changes nothing.

**Guards.** Stop if it frightens; return to the body and a simple deed. The Piaseczner's balance: there is also a unique self to reveal (Tzav VeZeruz 30, PIASECZNO.md section 5). Not for someone in acute anxiety or unreality (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): Tanya ch. 2 calls the soul 'a part of G-d above', and Chabad bitul is exactly this. Chabad would add that the true 'I', the divine soul, is found, not lost (MAP S58 'A ray in the sun', S60).

- «בשעה שאומר אני צריך להסתכל על עצמו ויודע שעל מה אומר אני» (Kedushat Levi, Shemot 18; `Chassidus-txt/R. Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev (1740-1809)/Kedushat Levi (Berdychiv, Ukraine, 1772-1798).txt` line 371). ## BS-10 · Closer than feeling

**What it is.** The deepest attachment is not felt. Felt love, awe and delight are the way there, not the place.

**From inside.** R. Menachem Mendel of Vitebsk: no grasp of mind can hold true attachment to the Infinite, surely not feeling or delight. All felt awe, love and delight of attachment are preparation; the true attachment beyond the felt is the unfelt, held by faith without feeling, a nullifying of existence. It is reached only after first using all that can be grasped (Peri HaAretz, Vayakhel-Pekudei 3). The Piaseczner at his peak: 'not delight and not joy do you feel at this time, only the nullification of selfhood' (Chovat HaTalmidim, Three Essays 3:15); 'afraid even to fear, afraid to love' (Tzav VeZeruz 28:2).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Do not skip the felt stages; use love and awe fully. 2. When feeling thins and you are still there, do not chase it back. 3. Stay, by faith, and do the next good thing.

**Signs it is real.** Quiet steadiness. You are less interested in your own state. The fruit shows in conduct.

**Its counterfeit.** Numbness or depression called 'beyond feeling'; using this teaching to skip the work of the heart.

**Guards.** The Vitebsker himself says it comes only after the felt stages. Never offer it as a reason not to try. For a numb person, start with BS-19 and BS-16 (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees exactly (PIASECZNO.md section 7): Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, 'the deeper the arousal, the less it is felt'. (Background, ours: R. Menachem Mendel was the Alter Rebbe's senior colleague in the Maggid's circle.) Map: S57 'No self to watch', S36, S64.

## BS-11 · Into the letters

**What it is.** Prayer as entering the words: putting all one's strength into each letter until the self is forgotten. The Besht's chief method in prayer.

**From inside.** The Besht: put all your strength into the words, go from letter to letter until you forget your body, and think how the letters join one another; this is a great delight. Then you come to the letters of thought and do not hear what you are saying. Then to the trait of ayin, where all bodily powers are nullified (Keter Shem Tov 2:51). Sometimes a person prays with love, awe and great fervour with no movement at all, so that others think he has no attachment; this is the better service (Tzava'at HaRivash 105:1).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Choose one short line. 2. Say it slower than ever, word by word, putting your whole strength into each. 3. Linger on a word you do not want to leave (70:1). 4. Let the body grow still. 5. If the words fall silent inside, let them.

**Signs it is real.** Stillness instead of display. Time passes unnoticed. Afterwards, gentleness.

**Its counterfeit.** Trance-chasing; chanting for an altered state; or the opposite, theatrical movement. The Piaseczner's test applies: stop moving; does anything remain? (Hakhsharat 5:33).

**Guards.** Not while driving or in charge of others. For someone prone to dissociation: eyes open, attention on the meaning (ours).

**Chabad.** Differs in emphasis (ours): Chabad prayer centres on contemplating the meaning (hisbonenus) in Pesukei deZimra and the Shema; letter-absorption is not its method. Agrees that inner fervour without outer show is higher (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, in PIASECZNO.md section 7). Map: S80 'Prayer: mind into heart'.

## BS-12 · Joy as the ground

**What it is.** Joy is not a reward for closeness. It is the floor closeness stands on. All four schools say so, each with its own reason.

**From inside.** The Besht: serve with awe and with joy, 'two friends who never part'; awe without joy is black bile (Tzava'at HaRivash 110:1). Weeping is very bad, unless it is weeping from joy (45:1). The Besht's parable as his grandson tells it: a musician plays so sweetly that the listeners dance almost to the ceiling; a deaf man walks in, sees them leaping and thinks them mad; had he heard the music he would be dancing too (Ba'al Shem Tov anthology, Yitro 55). R. Nachman: it is a great mitzvah to be always joyful; human nature pulls toward gloom, so force yourself, even with silliness (Likutei Moharan II 24, II 48).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. (Breslov) Set one hour for the broken heart; the rest of the day, joy (II 24:2). 2. Start from outside if you must: a tune, a step, a smile. 3. (Chabad) Then give it its real reason: rejoice over the true unity as over an inheritance you did not earn (Tanya ch. 33). 4. (Besht) Learn and pray with joy; it thins stray thoughts (Tzava'at HaRivash 51:1).

**Signs it is real.** Joy that makes room for others; joy that can sit beside sorrow; the 'music' heard, not only the dancing seen.

**Its counterfeit.** Forced, manic cheer; denial; joy at one's own spiritual success.

**Guards.** Do not press joy on a mourner or on someone in shock. Breslov's own order: the broken heart has its hour, and a broken heart slides into gloom more easily than joy slides into frivolity (II 24:2). Silliness for joy is offered gently and never mocks the person (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees strongly, stated: Tanya ch. 33 (joy over the unity) and Iggeret HaKodesh 11 ('first of all, a person should rejoice'). Differs in the engine (ours): Chabad's joy comes out of contemplation; Breslov allows it to be willed from the outside in. Map: S53 'Glad', S49, S50.

**In a chat today.** "You don't need a reason to start. Joy can come from the outside in: a tune, a silly step, a smile you put on. Then let it find its real reason: you're His, and He's here."

## BS-13 · The good point that sings

**What it is.** R. Nachman's 'Azamra': find one good point in yourself, then another. From the good points, melody is made. The door to joy for someone who feels bad about himself.

**From inside.** Seek and search to find in yourself some merit, some good point; with that little good, rejoice and strengthen yourself, and do not leave your place even if you have fallen (Likutei Moharan II 48:4). 'I will sing to my G-d with what is still in me': songs and melodies are made by gathering the good points (I 282).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Find one good thing in you, however small, even from today. 2. Hold it; do not argue it away. 3. Find another. 4. Let a tune or a word of thanks come from them. 5. From there, pray.

**Signs it is real.** Self-hatred eases; prayer becomes possible again; you turn toward Him instead of away.

**Its counterfeit.** Self-flattery; ignoring real wrongs that need repair.

**Guards.** This is for the one who condemns himself, not for the one who is pleased with himself. It never replaces repair of harm done to others (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees on refusing despair (ours; Tanya chs. 26-31 on sadness). Differs in method (ours): Chabad lifts the self-condemned through the divine soul and the beinoni's standard, not by collecting merits. The Piaseczner: 'a king's child with some dirt on him' (PIASECZNO.md section 8). Map: S11 'The verdict: I'm my mistakes'.

## BS-14 · Talking it out with Him

**What it is.** Hisbodedus: a set time alone, speaking to G-d in your own language, as to someone present. R. Nachman calls it higher than everything.

**From inside.** Set at least an hour, alone in a room or a field, and pour out your talk between you and your Maker, with arguments and excuses, with words of grace and pleading, in the language you speak (Likutei Moharan II 25:1). Even when the words are stopped and you cannot open your mouth, the readiness, standing before Him wanting to speak, is itself very good; and make a prayer of that very thing (II 25:1). Speech is the main thing, though one can do it in thought (Sichot HaRan 232:5). The Kozhnitzer: hide yourself away and be alone with your Maker (Avodat Yisrael, Avot 5:8). The Piaseczner: pour out your heart in any language you understand (Tzav VeZeruz 4:11).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Fix a time and place; outside among grasses is best (Sichot HaRan 227). 2. R. Nachman told one man to take two hours: one to long and prepare himself to speak, one to speak (232:8). 3. Speak plainly, in your own language. 4. If nothing comes, say that. 5. Turn what you learned today into a prayer: 'when will I come to this?' (II 25:2). 6. The rest of the day, joy (II 25:1).

**Signs it is real.** You end nearer and quieter, with a resolve. The talk turns from complaint toward longing.

**Its counterfeit.** Venting into the air; rumination; a self-pity loop with no 'You' in it.

**Guards.** (ours) Keep to the set time; for someone who ruminates, keep it short and end with one small act. In real danger, the owner's rule applies first.

**Chabad.** Differs (ours): spontaneous daily speech to G-d is not a named Chabad practice; Chabad's daily core is hisbonenus and the set liturgy. Agrees that the heart must open and that the plain cry counts (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, in PIASECZNO.md section 7). Map: S55 'Own words to Him'.

- «ההתבודדות הוא מעלה עליונה וגדולה מן הכל» (Likutei Moharan II 25:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 6344). Hisbodedus is a supreme level, greater than everything. - «ההכנה שהוא מוכן ועומד לפניו יתברך, וחפץ ומשתוקק לדבר, אך שאינו יכול, זה בעצמו גםכן טוב מאד» (Likutei Moharan II 25:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 6350). Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Sichot HaRan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1803-1810).txt` line 1489). One hour to walk and long and prepare himself to speak. - «ושופך לפניו את לבך בשיח ותפילה כפי שעולה על לבך בכל לשון שאתה שומע» (Tzav VeZeruz 4:11; `Chassidus-txt/R. **Whose.** stated (Breslov, Kozhnitz, Piaseczno); ours (Chabad note, guards).

## BS-15 · Becoming nothing, held in One

**What it is.** R. Nachman's account of where hisbodedus leads: the self's 'somethingness' is undone, and the soul, and the whole world with it, is included in G-d's unity.

**From inside.** The essence of bitul, where a person nullifies his 'yesh', becomes ayin and is included in G-d's unity, comes only through hisbodedus (Likutei Moharan I 52:5). Its time is night, when the world is free of this world's chase; even if you are not busy, a busy world makes it hard (52:3). Its place is a road no one walks. There one can empty the heart of everything and come to nullify all 'yesh'; then he is included in the Necessary Existence, his soul and the whole world with it (52:4-5).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Night, when others sleep. 2. Alone, somewhere no one passes. 3. Speak until the day's business is emptied out (BS-14). 4. When nothing is left to say, stay. 5. Let what remains be held in Him.

**Signs it is real.** The self thins without fear. The world is felt as held, not lost. Morning comes with lightness.

**Its counterfeit.** Exhausted blankness; a 'void' that is lonely rather than full; dissociation.

**Guards.** (ours) Safety first: the guide does not send anyone to a lonely road at night; a quiet room with the lights low is enough. Not for the anxious, the newly bereaved alone, or anyone prone to unreality.

**Chabad.** Agrees on the goal (ours): bitul and 'nothing but Him' are Chabad's summit (MAP S62 'Only Him', S72 'Atzilus: nothing apart'). Differs on the road, sharply: R. Nachman says 'only through hisbodedus'; Chabad reaches bitul through contemplation in prayer.

- «שעקר הבטול, שאדם מבטל ישותו ונעשה אין, ונכלל באחדות השם יתברך, אין זה אלא עלידי התבודדות» (Likutei Moharan I 52:5; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 2709). The essence of bitul, where a person nullifies his 'yesh', becomes ayin and is included in the unity of G-d, comes only through hisbodedus. - «אך עקר ההתבודדות הוא בלילה, בעת שהעולם פנוי מטרדת העולם הזה» (Likutei Moharan I 52:3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 2697). But the main time of hisbodedus is at night, when the world is free of the busyness of this world. - «ונעשה נפשו וכל העולם בבחינת מחיב המציאות» (Likutei Moharan I 52:4; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 2706). And his soul and the whole world become included in the Necessary Existence.

**Whose.** stated (R. Nachman); ours (the safety guard, the Chabad comparison).

**In a chat today.** "When the house is asleep, sit for a while with the lights low. Tell Him whatever is left of the day, until there's nothing left to say. Then just stay: a little less you, a little more held."

## BS-16 · The silent scream

**What it is.** A cry with no sound: R. Nachman's way to cry out fully when you cannot, or may not, make noise.

**From inside.** One can scream in 'a still small voice', a very great scream that no one hears, because no sound comes out. Picture the scream in your mind, with its melody, the way people scream, and go into it until you truly scream inside (Sichot HaRan 16:1). He insists it is not imagination but a real scream carried into the brain (16:2). The Piaseczner's parallel: reaching on without words, 'only in mind and heart', again and again, until one feels the stone on the heart split (Derekh HaMelekh 860, in PIASECZNO.md section 3).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Name what you would cry out about. 2. Picture the cry and its tune. 3. Let it rise inside, fully, with no sound. 4. Direct it to Him. 5. Rest afterwards.

**Signs it is real.** Release; the heart unblocks; tears may come; then quiet.

**Its counterfeit.** Rehearsing panic; an inner scream that tightens rather than releases.

**Guards.** (ours) For the numb and stuck (MAP S03, S16), not for someone already panicking. If it tightens the chest or brings fear, stop and breathe.

**Chabad.** Agrees that the simple cry counts (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut, in PIASECZNO.md section 7). The technique itself is not Chabad (ours). Map: S51 'The sudden cry'.

- «שיכולין לצעק בקול דממה דקה בצעקה גדולה מאד ולא ישמע שום אדם כלל» (Sichot HaRan 16:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Sichot HaRan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1803-1810).txt` line 70). One can cry out in a still small voice, with a very great scream, and no one will hear at all.

**Whose.** stated (R. Nachman, Piaseczno); ours (guards).

**In a chat today.** "If you can't say it out loud, scream it inside. Picture the cry, the tune of it, and let it go all the way up, with no sound. Nobody hears it but Him."

## BS-17 · The narrow bridge, without fear

**What it is.** 'The whole world is a very narrow bridge, and the main thing is not to be afraid at all.' R. Nosson ties the absence of fear directly to 'no place empty of Him'.

**From inside.** R. Nachman: a person must cross a very, very narrow bridge, and the rule and the main thing is not to be afraid at all (Likutei Moharan II 48:2). R. Nosson explains why one need not fear: 'for G-d is with us, for the whole earth is full of His glory' (Likutei Halakhot, Netilat Yadayim 6). And: there is no despair in the world at all, for in every place one can find G-d (Likutei Halakhot, Chanukah 3).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Admit the bridge is narrow; do not pretend. 2. Say: there is no place on it where He is not. 3. Take the next step only. 4. If you have fallen, say: there is no falling out of His world (R. Nosson's 'no despair at all').

**Signs it is real.** Fear loosens while care stays. You keep walking.

**Its counterfeit.** Recklessness; denial of real danger; bravado.

**Guards.** 'Not to be afraid' does not mean ignoring danger. In real danger the guide acts on the owner's rule first (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): this is trust (bitachon) resting on the unity; see being-held for the Chabad and Besht texts. Map: S84 'Trust', S02 'Holding it all up'.

- «שהאדם צריך לעבר על גשר צר מאד מאד, והכלל והעקר שלא יתפחד כלל» (Likutei Moharan II 48:2; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 6527). A person must cross a very, very narrow bridge, and the rule and the main thing is not to be afraid at all. - «שהעקר שלא יירא ולא יתפחד כלל כי ה' אתנו. כי מלא כל הארץ כבודו» (Likutei Halakhot, Orach Chaim, Netilat Yadayim 6:84; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nosson of Breslov (1780-1844)/Likutei Halakhot (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1814-1844).txt` line 1631). The main thing is not to fear or be afraid at all, for G-d is with us; for the whole earth is full of His glory. - «אין שום יאוש בעולם כלל, כי בכל מקום יכולין למצא השם יתברך» (Likutei Halakhot, Orach Chaim, Chanukah 3:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nosson of Breslov (1780-1844)/Likutei Halakhot (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1814-1844).txt` line 4315). There is no despair in the world at all, for in every place one can find G-d.

**Whose.** stated (R. Nachman, R. Nosson); ours (steps, guard).

**In a chat today.** "The bridge is narrow; that's real. But there's no spot on it where He isn't. You don't have to stop being careful. Only stop being afraid."

## BS-18 · The field prays with you

**What it is.** Prayer or hisbodedus outdoors, where everything that grows joins in. Seeing the One in everything, as company rather than as an idea.

**From inside.** When a person prays in a field, all the grasses come into the prayer, help him and give strength to his prayer (Likutei Moharan II 11:1). It is better to do hisbodedus outside the town, among grasses, because the grasses awaken the heart (Sichot HaRan 227:1). The Piaseczner: go alone to the forest if you can, and sing with the sun and the birds (Tzav VeZeruz 48, in PIASECZNO.md section 3).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Go out, if you can, to grass or trees. 2. Look around first: all this is alive from Him. 3. Then speak or sing what you have. 4. Notice that you are not alone in it.

**Signs it is real.** Less loneliness in prayer; the heart wakes; the world feels like a congregation.

**Its counterfeit.** Nature-mood with no 'You' in it; worshipping the view.

**Chabad.** Agrees in principle (ours): Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah sees each creature held in being by His word. Differs in practice: Chabad has no outdoor prayer method. Map: S63, S85.

- «כשהאדם מתפלל בשדה, אזי כל העשבים כלם באין בתוך התפלה» (Likutei Moharan II 11:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Likutei Moharan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1802-1808).txt` line 6212). When a person prays in a field, all the grasses come into the prayer. - «כי העשבים גורמים שיתעורר הלב» (Sichot HaRan 227:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Nachman of Breslov (1772-1810)/Sichot HaRan (Bratslav, Ukraine, 1803-1810).txt` line 1469). For the grasses cause the heart to awaken.

**Whose.** stated (R. Nachman, Piaseczno); ours (steps, Chabad note).

**In a chat today.** "If you can, go outside, somewhere with grass or trees. Say what you need to say out there. Notice it doesn't feel like you're saying it alone."

## BS-19 · Start from what is already moving

**What it is.** The Piaseczner's first rule: do not manufacture a feeling. Find what is already stirring and use it as the key.

**From inside.** 'Every arousal, even a bodily one, is a key to the soul' (Bnei Machshava Tova 8:8): a business worry, a joy, a sigh. 'You need only try to know yourself and what is happening in you' (Hakhsharat 4:6). The soul is already crying and singing; we do not hear it (PIASECZNO.md section 4).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Ask: what is moving in me right now? 2. Take it, even if it is small or worldly. 3. Begin with the clearer and gladder feelings, not the soul's pains (Hakhsharat 89, in PIASECZNO.md). 4. Turn it toward Him: 'You'.

**Signs it is real.** Prayer starts from truth, not performance. The stirring grows when turned toward Him.

**Its counterfeit.** Waiting for a 'holy' feeling and despising the ones you have; or staying with a worldly feeling and never turning it.

**Guards.** If the heaviness is hunger, tiredness or a hurt, say so first (Derekh HaMelekh 937, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Chabad.** Agrees on the start (ONE-PROCESS step 1, 'what is already moving'). Differs (PIASECZNO.md section 7): Kuntres HaHitpa'alut wants 'only Godly arousal, not the arousal of the flesh'; the Piaseczner lets any arousal in and redirects it. Map: S13 'The unnamed longing', S38.

- «כל התעוררות אפלו אם גופנית, היא מפתח לנפש» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 8:8; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 100). Every arousal, even a bodily one, is a key to the soul. - «ועליך להשתדל רק להכיר אותך ואת אשר בך מתרחש» (Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 4:6; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 87). You need only try to know yourself and what is happening within you.

**Whose.** stated (Piaseczno, via PIASECZNO.md); ours (framing for the guide).

**In a chat today.** "Before we try to feel anything: what's already there right now? A worry, a want, a sigh? We start there."

## BS-20 · Quieting by watching

**What it is.** Hashkatah: watch your thoughts until the head empties, then plant one verse softly. (Known only from a student's record; the Rebbe's own seeds are 'waiting an hour' and 'calm yourself and watch'.)

**From inside.** He will slowly feel his head emptying, and his thoughts stopping their usual flood (Derekh HaMelekh, Inyan HaShkatah). The reason: a person's own 'somethingness' is what resists inspiration from above (same). In the older first-hand form: if a person halted for an hour the whole flood of his thoughts and wishes, the divine part would appear to him without a garment (Derekh HaMelekh, Bereshit 18). Watching stray thoughts 'with a restful heart', they thin until 'as with a broom you have swept your mind' (Hakhsharat 9:64-65).

**How one enters or deepens it.** Full steps in PIASECZNO.md section 3, step 2: 1. Watch your thoughts for a few minutes: what am I thinking? 2. Feel the head slowly empty. 3. Say one verse, such as 'Hashem Elokim Emet'. 4. Ask simply for what you need. 5. Softly, never forcefully. 6. Positively, not 'don't'. 7. Or watch the small hand of a clock. 8. Close with a verse in melody.

**Signs it is real.** A hollow, clean head that is not empty of Him; then one holy thought that takes root.

**Its counterfeit.** Blankness for its own sake; drowsiness; forcing the quiet.

**Guards.** Never forcefully: force wakes the ego. Cite as a student's record, not the Rebbe's own text (PIASECZNO.md section 9).

**Chabad.** Differs (PIASECZNO.md section 7): Chabad settles the mind by filling it with one concept extended in length and breadth; hashkatah settles by emptying first. Both reject fighting thoughts. Map: S32 'Quiet', S56.

- «אז ירגיש לאט לאט שראשו מתרוקן ומחשבותיו עמדו משטפן הרגיל» (Derekh HaMelekh, Inyan HaShkatah 4 (a student's record); `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931).txt` line 1556). Then he will slowly feel his head emptying and his thoughts stopping their usual flood. - «כי הישות של האדם הוא המתנגד להשראה ממרום» (Derekh HaMelekh, Inyan HaShkatah 3 (a student's record); `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931).txt` line 1555). For a person's 'somethingness' is what opposes inspiration from above. - «אבל לא שיאמר זאת בחזקה» (Derekh HaMelekh, Inyan HaShkatah 4 (a student's record); `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931).txt` line 1556). But not that he say it forcefully. - «היה חלק אלקות נראה לו בלא לבוש» (Derekh HaMelekh, Bereshit 18; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931).txt` line 90). The divine part would appear to him without a garment.

**Whose.** stated (the student's record and the Rebbe's own seeds); ours (the guide's use).

**In a chat today.** "For a minute, just watch your thoughts go by. Don't push them. Only ask: what am I thinking now? If your head gets a little emptier, good. Now say one short line, gently."

## BS-21 · One sentence that fills the room

**What it is.** The Piaseczner's daily affirmations of the unity: one line, said slowly, until it widens and fills the space around you.

**From inside.** 'And You surround me and fill my body and my spirit' (Chovat HaTalmidim 9:3), turned over until his room and everything around him fill with this thought (Instructions 34). In the street: 'the whole world is Godliness: the grain of sand under my feet, the air I breathe inside me' (Bnei Machshava Tova, Daily Instructions 7:1). And the daily recognition: everything is G-d, 'and only I, by my private will, made myself into a thing existing on its own' (Principles 14:1).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Pick one line. 2. Say it calmly; repeat only while it grows (Hakhsharat 110, in PIASECZNO.md). 3. Think of Him, not the words. 4. Let it spread from the head to the chest to the room. 5. Carry one phrase into the street.

**Signs it is real.** The line stops being words; the room feels full; you walk differently.

**Its counterfeit.** Mechanical repetition; a mantra used to numb.

**Guards.** Stop repeating when it stops growing (Hakhsharat 110). The 'except me' line is said as an invitation to come back in, never as self-condemnation (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): these are hisbonenus objects, and Tanya ch. 33's 'even on the earth below there is nothing else' is the same unity. Differs in method: Chabad unfolds the line in detail with the intellect; he repeats it simply and pictures it. Map: S25 'The line takes hold', S66 'Filled from inside', S67 'Surrounded'.

- «ואתה מקיף אותי וממלא את גופי ורוחי» (Chovat HaTalmidim 9:3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Chovat HaTalmidim (Warsaw, 1928-1932).txt` line 129). And You surround me and fill my body and my spirit. - «עד שגם חדרו אף כל סביבו מתמלאים מן מחשבה זו» (Chovat HaTalmidim, Instructions 34; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Chovat HaTalmidim (Warsaw, 1928-1932).txt` line 228). Until his room too, and everything around him, fills with this thought. - «כל העולם אלקות הוא, גרגיר חול אשר תחת רגלי, האויר שאני נושם בקרבי» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Daily Instructions 7:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 286). The whole world is Godliness: the grain of sand under my feet, the air I breathe within me. - «ורק אני ברצוני הפרטי הוצאתי את עצמי לדבר נמצא בפני עצמו» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 14:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 182). And only I, by my private will, have made myself into a thing existing on its own.

**Whose.** stated (Piaseczno); ours (guard, Chabad note).

**In a chat today.** "Say this slowly, as many times as it keeps growing: 'You surround me, and You fill me.' Then look at the room. Is it fuller than it was?"

## BS-22 · Before Him, a picture that falls away

**What it is.** A permitted picture: standing before His throne like a child before a loving father. A handrail for a person of flesh, which drops away by itself.

**From inside.** 'Picture that you stand before the Throne of His glory and pray and ask of Him, simply like a son who stands and pleads with his father' (Bnei Machshava Tova 7:13). Then 'this bodily picture falls away by itself' (7:12). The Kozhnitzer before him: picture His presence with certainty, like a friend standing before you (BS-02).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Eyes closed, or face the wall. 2. Picture only the standing-before, not any form of Him. 3. Speak as a child to a father. 4. When the feeling is there, let the picture go; do not hunt for more images (Hakhsharat 184, in PIASECZNO.md). 5. If you cannot picture it, come at it sideways through an ordinary memory (Mevo HaShe'arim 395, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Signs it is real.** The picture fades and the presence stays.

**Its counterfeit.** Imagining a figure of G-d; visions and voices; a picture one clings to.

**Guards.** He has no image; the picture is of you standing, not of Him. Straining kills it (Mevo HaShe'arim 390, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Chabad.** Agrees on 'before Him' (Tanya ch. 41). Differs in that imagery is his engine and not a named Chabad technique (PIASECZNO.md section 9, item 9). Map: S33, S74 'Seen eye to eye'.

- «ציר לך שאתה עומד לפני כסא כבודו» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 7:13; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 91). Picture to yourself that you are standing before the Throne of His glory. - «אז התמונה הציור גופני הזה ממילא יתבטלו» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 7:12; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 90). Then this bodily image and picture will fall away by itself.

**Whose.** stated (Piaseczno, Kozhnitz); ours (the guide's wording).

**In a chat today.** "Close your eyes. Don't picture what He looks like; He has no look. Picture only this: you're standing right in front of Someone who loves you, the way a child stands before a father."

## BS-23 · The song that sings itself

**What it is.** Melody as a door: you hum to wake the soul, and after a while the soul is humming by itself.

**From inside.** 'Take some movement of a tune, turn your face to the wall or just close your eyes... little by little you will feel that your soul has already begun to sing by itself' (Hakhsharat 9:33). No new tunes needed: 'there is one who sings only a whispered hum, and his voice is heard on high' (Bnei Machshava Tova 18:15). R. Nachman: melody is made from gathered good points (BS-13).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. A tune you already know. 2. Wall or closed eyes. 3. Picture yourself before Him. 4. Hum, even in a whisper. 5. Let words come out while singing, even words not in the siddur (Hakhsharat 281, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Signs it is real.** The moment when you are no longer singing it; it is singing.

**Its counterfeit.** Music as mood-management, with no 'You'.

**Guards.** None special; a whisper is enough where others are near.

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): niggun is central in Chabad too (MAP S52 'The song that sings itself'). Hisbonenus itself is silent and conceptual (PIASECZNO.md section 7).

- «מעט מעט תרגיש שנפשך התחילה כבר לנגן מעצמה» (Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 9:33; `Chassidus-txt/R. **In a chat today.** "If a tune comes to you, hum it, even in a whisper. Keep going a while. Notice if, at some point, it starts singing by itself."

## BS-24 · The holy group

**What it is.** A small fellowship: a few people who meet to help each other's souls wake, each with one confidant. Quiet, not public.

**From inside.** Each takes a friend before whom he reveals all the secrets of his heart (Bnei Machshava Tova, Proposed Amendments 9:2). 'Every service in which the soul is revealed opposes noise and publicity' (10:1). They talk together about G-d 'even if they have nothing new to tell each other' (Chovat HaTalmidim, Instructions 17).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Two or three people. 2. Meet regularly; learn a little guidance slowly and apply it to yourselves. 3. Each has one confidant. 4. Talk of Him even with nothing new to say. 5. Keep it quiet. (His group also had a little drink, a song, a dance; the guide never recommends drink: PIASECZNO.md section 8.)

**Signs it is real.** You are seen, so you see yourself; the warmth outlasts the meeting.

**Its counterfeit.** A clique; public display; a group that feeds on its own excitement.

**Guards.** No drink, ever (owner's rule). The guide encourages a real person to share with; it is not a substitute for one (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): the farbrengen and the mashpia are Chabad's equivalents. Differs: Chabad hisbonenus is individual, his method social (PIASECZNO.md section 7, item 13).

Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 325). Each of them should take a friend for himself before whom he reveals all the secrets of his heart. - «כן כל עבודה שיש בה התגלות הנפש מתנגדת היא לשאון ופרסום» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Proposed Amendments 10:1; `Chassidus-txt/R. ## BS-25 · The leap in the Shema

**What it is.** Saying the Shema ready to give oneself completely: a single joyful leap 'through the fire into the Infinite'.

**From inside.** 'And in joy you make one leap through the pyre into the Infinite' (Bnei Machshava Tova 14:7). After a Shema said this way, 'you will feel you are another man' (Hakhsharat 436, in PIASECZNO.md).

**How one enters or deepens it.** Per PIASECZNO.md section 3, step 10: 1. Stop a few moments before the Shema. 2. Say aloud first that you accept His kingship and are ready to give yourself. 3. Pause; only then begin. 4. Only some days a week, so it does not wear out (Hakhsharat 436).

**Signs it is real.** Joy, not dread; you come out of it different.

**Its counterfeit.** Morbid fascination with death; dramatics.

**Guards.** (ours) The guide speaks of giving oneself, never of dying. Not for anyone with thoughts of self-harm; there it speaks only of being held (see being-held).

**Chabad.** Agrees (ours): Chabad also places self-giving (mesirus nefesh) in the Shema, and teaches that the soul's bond with G-d is above reason (MAP S60 'The essential bond', S83 'Accepting the yoke'). His 'leap in joy' is his own image.

- «ובשמחה אתה עושה קפיצה אחת דרך המוקד אל האין סוף» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 14:7; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 188). And in joy you make one leap through the pyre into the Infinite.

**Whose.** stated (Piaseczno); ours (guards, Chabad note).

**In a chat today.** "Before you say 'Hear O Israel', stop for a moment. Tell Him: all of me is Yours. Then say it, like stepping off a ledge into arms that are already there."

## BS-26 · When nothing is felt and all is broken

**What it is.** Esh Kodesh: the inner life when method fails, in the Warsaw ghetto. Faith that is present but not felt; weeping together with G-d.

**From inside.** 'He believes even then, only he does not feel it' (Esh Kodesh, HaChodesh 3). In the inner rooms G-d weeps, and one who comes there 'weeps and wails with Him, as it were, together' (HaChodesh 10). The Rebbe at his son's bedside: 'no feeling and no thought happens, only darkness and sorrow' (Tzav VeZeruz 174, in PIASECZNO.md).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. Do not push for feeling. 2. Say: faith can be here unfelt. 3. You are not weeping alone; He is weeping with you. 4. Hope and surrender together: 'may it be better soon; and He is G-d' (Esh Kodesh 557, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Signs it is real.** Company in the dark; the person keeps going, however slowly.

**Its counterfeit.** Forcing a 'spiritual' meaning onto someone's suffering; pressing joy or equanimity on them.

**Guards.** Never explain suffering. Never press BS-07 or BS-12 here. In real danger, the owner's rule first (ours).

**Chabad.** Differs by absence (PIASECZNO.md section 7, item 14): no Chabad manual in the index addresses this state as directly. Kuntres HaHitpa'alut's 'the simple cry counts' is the nearest. Map: S14 'Loss and grief', S73 'Exile inside'.

- «מאמין הוא גם אז רק שאינו מרגישה» (Esh Kodesh, 5702, HaChodesh 3; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 1941).txt` line 578). He believes even then, only he does not feel it. - «ושם בוכה ומילל עמו כביכול יחד» (Esh Kodesh, 5702, HaChodesh 10; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Esh Kodesh (Warsaw Ghetto, 1941).txt` line 585). And there he weeps and wails with Him, as it were, together.

**Whose.** stated (Esh Kodesh, Tzav VeZeruz); ours (guards).

**In a chat today.** "You don't have to feel anything right now. Faith can be there without being felt. And you're not crying alone. He's crying with you."

## BS-27 · The honest check, afterwards

**What it is.** How the Piaseczner tells the real from the false: arouse first, test later, at a set time, by fruit and not by glow.

**From inside.** Not in the middle. Afterwards, one question: 'am I truly more master of myself now than before?' (Bnei Machshava Tova 19:7). Excitement without a vessel may be 'only empty imagination and irritated nerves' (Chovat HaTalmidim, Three Essays 2:42). Fervour made by movement: 'try to stop all your movements' (Hakhsharat 5:33). And the other side: real states often come suddenly, 'he suddenly feels lifted above the world' (Derekh HaMelekh, Shavuot 4:7), and what you could not have produced on purpose you did not make up (PIASECZNO.md section 6).

**How one enters or deepens it.** 1. During: do not investigate; it spoils the elevation (Esh Kodesh 541, in PIASECZNO.md). 2. After, at a set time: Do I rule myself more? Are my traits softer? Is a longing or a purity left? 3. If it was noise, drop it without despair: 'among ten false fervours you will find one true' (Bnei Machshava Tova 247, in PIASECZNO.md).

**Signs it is real.** This entry is the sign-test for all the others.

**Its counterfeit.** Two: trusting every glow; and killing every stirring by over-checking.

**Guards.** Never examine in the middle. Never use the check to shame (ours).

**Chabad.** Agrees, found independently (PIASECZNO.md section 7): the Rashab's 'has your dominant trait changed?' and Chabad's warning against watching yourself in the state. Map: S17 'Feeling fake', S18 'Chasing the high', S19.

- «אם באמת מושל אני על עצמי עתה יותר מאשר לפנים» (Bnei Machshava Tova, Principles 19:7; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Bnei Machshava Tova (Piaseczno, 1917-1923).txt` line 252). Whether I truly rule myself now more than before. - «לא יהי׳ בטוח שאין זה רק דמיון שוא וגרוי העצבים בלבד» (Chovat HaTalmidim, Three Essays 2:42; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Chovat HaTalmidim (Warsaw, 1928-1932).txt` line 324). Let him not be sure that this is not only empty imagination and irritated nerves. - «נסה נא לחדול מן כל תנועתיך» (Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim 5:33; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Hakhsharat HaAvrekhim (Warsaw, 1930-1940).txt` line 145). Try now to stop all your movements. - «פתאום מרגיש שהונף והורם מעל לעולם אל היכלי עליון» (Derekh HaMelekh, Shavuot 4:7; `Chassidus-txt/R. Kalonymus Kalman Shapira of Piaseczno (1889-1943)/Derekh HaMelekh (Warsaw, 1931).txt` line 1396). Suddenly he feels lifted and raised above the world into the supernal chambers.

**Whose.** stated (Piaseczno); ours (the guide's wording).

**In a chat today.** "Don't ask now whether it was real. Later today, ask one thing: am I a little more my own master than this morning? Is something left, a longing, a little quiet?" ## For the guide: an order of doors (ours)

Not a ladder. A rough order of which door to offer, by where the person is.

- **Scattered, drifted, ashamed of the drift:** BS-04 (He is in the drift too), then BS-01 (pick up the thread).

- **Afraid:** BS-17 (the narrow bridge), with being-held.
- **Hurt by someone, stuck in a pattern:** BS-08 (where nothing breaks), BS-07 (equanimity, offered, never demanded).
- **Wants to talk:** BS-14, outdoors if possible (BS-18).
- **Already warm, wants more:** BS-11 (into the letters), BS-21, BS-22, BS-25.
- **Quiet and steady, wondering why the feeling is gone:** BS-10, BS-03.
- **Crushed:** BS-26 only.
- **Afterwards, always:** BS-27.

Rules that come from these sources (stated where cited): never force a feeling (Hakhsharat 3:4; hashkatah "not forcefully"); never examine in the middle (Esh Kodesh 541); test by fruit, later (Bnei Machshava Tova 19:7); joy is the floor, but the broken heart has its hour (Likutei Moharan II 24:2); for most people, do not engage a craving in order to "raise" it (Tanya ch. 28); no drink; no pictures of G-d.

## Gaps and unsure points

1. **Only the main books were read for each master.** Kedushat Levi, Avodat Yisrael, Peri HaAretz and Likutei Halakhot are large; this strand used targeted searches (ripgrep and `find_he.py grep`) on the strand's themes and read the passages around each hit. Other passages surely exist.
2. **Sources overlap with being-held.** Tzava'at HaRivash 2:1, 10:1, 67:1, 84:2-5 and 137 are already quoted there; here only 10:1 and 67:1 are re-used, with a new angle (from inside). New in this strand: the Maggid's "good eye" (43:1), "in the drift too" (75:1), "nothing breaks" (22:2); Vitebsk on unfelt dveikus, the silver weld, the taste in food, and prayer that quiets the wanting; Berditchev's "on what do I say I"; Kozhnitz on picturing the presence and on change through ayin; Breslov on hisbodedus, bitul at night, the silent scream, the field, the bridge, Azamra; R. Nosson's "no despair in the world".
3. **Compiled works.** Keter Shem Tov and Tzava'at HaRivash are compilations of the Besht and the Maggid; some passages are the Maggid's. The Ba'al Shem Tov anthology's parable of the deaf man is the Degel quoting his grandfather. Sichot HaRan is R. Nosson's record.
4. **The Kozhnitz-Piaseczno line** (the Piaseczner as a descendant of the Kozhnitzer Maggid) is background knowledge, not checked in the library; it is marked "ours".
5. **Tanya ch. 28** is the clearest written disagreement found. Other Chabad-Breslov or Chabad-Besht differences in this file are our reading of emphasis, not a stated dispute, and are marked "ours".
6. **R. Nachman's "only through hisbodedus"** (Likutei Moharan I 52) stands against Chabad's road to bitul through contemplation. The file states both and does not reconcile them.
7. **Hashkatah** is a student's record (Derekh HaMelekh appendix), as PIASECZNO.md section 9 explains.
