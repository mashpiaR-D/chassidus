## What this range is

Rabbi Shimon and his circle read the Song of Songs as the love of God and the Community of Israel, and, inside that, as the love of the soul for its source. The node reads "the song of songs" as a union of four radiances; "let him kiss me" as spirit joining spirit; "draw me after you" as running with desire. It stops for long stories: David in the wilderness looking up at the sky, the incense that purifies the heart, the soul in the lower Eden before birth, and the moon that asked to be equal to the sun. It ends with a long piece on letters, vowel points and cantillation, read as the body, soul and mind of the Torah.

## The unity of G-d

**Every love below is for joining above.**

> בגין דכל רחימו דעלמא תתאה, לאו איהו אלא לאתחברא בעלמא עילאה (ZC Shir HaShirim 177, line 2832)
> *For all the love of the lower world is only in order to join the upper world.*

> כל נשיקין דרחימו, לאו אינון אלא למהוי כללא חדא (ZC Shir HaShirim 153, line 2808)
> *All kisses of love are only in order to be one whole.* (The line before: until they all were one, and the Holy One one, with no separation at all.)

> אתדבקותא דרוחא ברוחא (ZC Shir HaShirim 119, line 2774)
> *The cleaving of spirit to spirit* ("let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth").

**To unify all with love.** On "the song of songs which is Solomon's":

> ולייחדא כולא בחביבו ברעותא (ZC Shir HaShirim 124, line 2779)
> *And to unify all in fondness, with the will* (so that it be one above and below).

**All the rungs are one knot.** From the incense:

> למהוי כולהו קשורא חד, וחיבורא חד, וייחודא חד (ZC Shir HaShirim 362, line 3017)
> *So that all of them be one knot, one joining, one unity.*

> בגין דלא ישתארון תתאין בלא אשגחותא אפילו רגעא חדא (ZC Shir HaShirim 136, line 2791)
> *So that the lower ones will not be left without watching, not even for one moment.*

**He hides in the heart.** The highest of the four radiances, Ehyeh:

> אבל קאים בסוכלתנו דלבא (ZC Shir HaShirim 75, line 2730)
> *But it stands in the understanding of the heart.* (It is not revealed at all, yet the heart knows it and looks at it.)

**No image, and no one knows Him.** At the end of the long piece on letters and points:

> ולית מאן דידע ביה כלום (ZC Shir HaShirim 636, line 3291)
> *And there is no one who knows anything of Him.* (There is no image or form above at all.)

**Everything was one point in His thought.**

> רישא דכל רעותין וכל מחשבין דעלמא (ZC Shir HaShirim 505, line 3160)
> *The head of all the wills and all the thoughts of the world* (said of the one hidden point in which all rose in thought before creation).

**He will not enter above until we enter below.**

> בקרבך קדוש ולא אבוא בעיר (ZC Shir HaShirim 496, line 3151)
> *"The Holy One in your midst, and I will not come into the city."* (He swore not to enter the upper Jerusalem until Israel enters the lower.)

## States of consciousness

**Run and return (ratzo vashov).** The third radiance:

> תיאובתיה לאסתלקא לעילא, ומיד אהדר (ZC Shir HaShirim 73, line 2728)
> *Its longing is to rise above, and at once it returns.*

**Thirst in the body (S13 The unnamed longing).** David in the wilderness:

> למהוי נפשא ובשרא דבקא לגבך (ZC Shir HaShirim 335, line 2990)
> *That soul and flesh both cleave to You* ("my soul thirsts for You, my flesh longs for You").

> אנא חמי לך, לאתדבקא בך (ZC Shir HaShirim 336, line 2991)
> *I see You, to cleave to You* ("so I have seen You in the holy place": even here in the wilderness).

We propose (ours): **Z-S18 Seeing Him in the wilderness.** Seeing God in a dry and empty place, as if in the Temple. Not the comfort of feeling close in a holy place, but the stubborn inner sight in the place of lack. It belongs beside S73 (exile inside) as its answer.

> ובכל עאקו דהוה ליה, הוה משבח ומצלי (ZC Shir HaShirim 333, line 2988)
> *And in every trouble he had, he praised and prayed.*

**One heart toward the Father.** The incense purified:

> ולא הוה ליה אלא לבא חדא, לקבל אבוי דבשמיא (ZC Shir HaShirim 342, line 2997)
> *And he had only one heart, toward his Father in Heaven.*

We propose (ours): **Z-S19 One heart.** The heart no longer divided. The Zohar Chadash says it comes after the heart is "sifted in clarity and light, in joy and will" (the same line). Close to S62 Only Him, but felt as a heart made whole rather than a world seen as Him.

**Held like a child (S84 Trust).** Even in exile:

> כאמא על בנין (ZC Shir HaShirim 101, line 2756)
> *Like a mother over children.* (His love is not withheld; His throne rests over them.)

> דלא ינשי לון, ולא ישבוק לון לעלמין (ZC Shir HaShirim 217, line 2872)
> *That He will not forget them and will not leave them, ever.* (He set His Shechinah among them as a pledge.)

> איהו בחשאי (ZC Shir HaShirim 117, line 2772)
> *It is in silence* ("to You silence is praise").

And the bride silenced in the middle of her song at the sea:

> איך שכיך לבה ורעותה (ZC Shir HaShirim 524, line 3179)
> *How her heart and will are hushed.* (When her beloved does not want the praise yet, her love goes quiet; later he reminds her of that silence with tenderness.)

> מאן דמנח תפילין, אצטריך למחדי (ZC Shir HaShirim 208, line 2863)
> *Whoever puts on tefillin must rejoice.*

> דהא כל חדוא ורעוא לאו איהי אלא בך (ZC Shir HaShirim 232, line 2887)
> *For all joy and delight is only in You.*

> ובגו חדוה דא אית לי לבכייא (ZC Shir HaShirim 473, line 3128)
> *And within this joy I have reason to weep* (for the two Temples).

**The day of falling is the day of rising.**

> ביומא דנפילה קימה משמע (ZC Shir HaShirim 414, line 3069)
> *On the day of falling, rising is meant.* ("Do not rejoice over me, my enemy, for I fell, I rose.")

## Meditation and practice

**Look at His work in the wilderness (ties to 11 seeing the garment).**

> הוה מסתכל התם במדברא עובד אומנותא דקודשא בריך הוא (ZC Shir HaShirim 331, line 2986)
> *He would look there in the wilderness at the craft of the Holy One* (and praise).

> והוה אסתכל בשמיא, בסיהרא וכוכביא (ZC Shir HaShirim 332, line 2987)
> *And he would look at the sky, at the moon and the stars* (and he always feared, and praised, and exalted).

This is the full form of Z-T11 (lifting the eyes, report 1). The Zohar Chadash gives David's night practice in detail: sit outside, look at the moon and stars, fear, praise, and exalt.

**Intention in every act (ties to 16 love and awe, 21 the act that closes).**

> לשוואה רעותא ולבא בכל עובדא ועובדא (ZC Shir HaShirim 243, line 2898)
> *To set the will and the heart in every single act.*

**Keep her before your eyes.** Of the husband and the Presence:

> דלא תסתלק מעינא רגעא חדא (ZC Shir HaShirim 279, line 2934)
> *That she not leave the eye for one moment.*

We propose (ours): **Z-T19 Not one moment out of sight.** Choose one thing to keep before the eyes all day, the way a lover keeps the beloved in view: a Name, a verse, a simple "You are here". Technique 3 is one line. This is the same line kept visible, not recited.

> התעורר לנגד הקדוש ברוך הוא בשיר ותעורר האהבה (ZC Shir HaShirim 50, line 2705)
> *Awaken before the Holy One with song, and you will awaken the love.* (Tie to 19 melody.)

> ואתערו לא סלקא אלא מתתא לעילא (ZC Shir HaShirim 88, line 2743)
> *And the awakening rises only from below to above.*

> אתערו דלעילא, לא אתער (ZC Shir HaShirim 533, line 3188)
> *The awakening above does not awaken* (until the adornments of this world are set right first).

> ניהב חלקא לכל חד וחד מההוא חדוא דילן (ZC Shir HaShirim 291, line 2946)
> *Let us give a share of our joy to each and every one.*

> קודשא בריך הוא, שאלין ליה זעיר, והוא יהיב סגי (ZC Shir HaShirim 174, line 2829)
> *Of the Holy One, one asks little, and He gives much.*

**Comfort with good words.** Joseph was taken from the pit:

> דארצו ליה במלין טבין (ZC Shir HaShirim 222, line 2877)
> *For they ran to him with good words* (because he was sad from that pit).

> מאן דאוליף סניגוריא על בנוי (ZC Shir HaShirim 102, line 2757)
> *[His will is for] one who argues in defense of His children.* (Ties to Z-T13.)

## The big questions

**Who am I? Where from? Where to?** The Zohar Chadash lists four knowings a person needs. The second:

> ולאשתמודע מאן איהו. ואיך אתברי, ומאן אתי. ולאן יהך (ZC Shir HaShirim 479, line 3134)
> *And to know who he is, how he was made, where he comes from, and where he is going.*

The first is to look into the secret of one's Master; the third, to know the soul within; the fourth, to look at this world and what it is for. And after all of it, to know one's Master. This is the Zohar Chadash's plainest list of the big questions, and it names them in the same order people still ask them.

> אפילו אית ביה עובדין טבין סגיאין (ZC Shir HaShirim 481, line 3136)
> *Even if he has many good deeds* (whoever goes to that world without knowing is put out of all its gates).

This is a hard line, and the guide should hold it with care. The point is not to frighten but to say that knowing Him is part of the task.

> כל אינון בני עלמא דלית בהו כיסופא (ZC Shir HaShirim 340, line 2995)
> *All the people of the world who have no shame* (have no share in the world to come).

> ובכי על עובדוי (ZC Shir HaShirim 395, line 3050)
> *And it weeps over its deeds.*

**Why does God feed those who anger Him?** The Shechinah says "I am black":

> כד חזינא כמה חייבין דמרגיזין למארי כולא, ואנא זן לון (ZC Shir HaShirim 406, line 3061)
> *When I see how many sinners anger the Master of all, and I feed them.*

**Is everything I do in daily life part of this?**

> ודרכו יחפץ, אפילו במילי דעלמא (ZC Shir HaShirim 629, line 3284)
> *"And He delights in his way": even in worldly matters.*

> קודשא בריך הוא אתרעי ביה, כאילו הוא דיליה ממש (ZC Shir HaShirim 630, line 3285)
> *The Holy One desires him as if he were truly His own* (if a person sets his mind, will and heart toward the holy King).

> כל מחשבין ורעותין דעלמא דאינון גו לבא, כולהו אתחזון באנפין (ZC Shir HaShirim 540, line 3195)
> *All the thoughts and wills that are in the heart, all of them show in the face.*

> זולתי שערי דמעות שלא ננעלו (ZC Shir HaShirim 54, line 2709)
> *Except the gates of tears, which were not locked.*

**What is the lamp?** While Israel was set like a wick:

> אור של מעלה היתה דולקת עליהם (ZC Shir HaShirim 48, line 2703)
> *The light from above was burning over them* (and singing, never still).

And the outer altar yearning for the inner:

> ולעולם לא שכיך מלזמרא (ZC Shir HaShirim 46, line 2701)
> *And it never ceases from singing.*

**Is dispute a failure?** The disputes of the sages over the law:

> וכל דא איהו תיקונא וקישוטא דכלה (ZC Shir HaShirim 163, line 2818)
> *And all this is the adornment and ornament of the bride.*

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus reads the Song of Songs the same way, and this node is a quarry for it. "All the love below is only for joining above" (2832) is the principle of Tanya chapter 49 on love. "Spirit cleaving to spirit" (2774) is the Alter Rebbe's reading of "let him kiss me", which Tanya chapter 45 and Likkutei Torah on Shir HaShirim build on. The Ehyeh radiance that "stands in the understanding of the heart" though it is not revealed (2730) is the Chassidic idea that the heart knows what the mind cannot see.

Where Chassidus goes further: the Zohar Chadash's list of four knowings (3134-3135) ends with knowing one's Master. Chassidus would add that the knowing is not only information but daat: binding the mind to the thing until the heart is moved. The Zohar Chadash hints at this with "one heart" (2997) but does not name it.

What the guide should take (ours): David's night practice as the model for Z-T11; Z-T19; states Z-S18 and Z-S19; the four knowings (3134) as a short frame for anyone asking "who am I"; line 3069 for someone after a fall; line 2709 for someone who can only cry; line 2829 for someone who does not know what to ask for. Leave out the incense protection claims (3000-3001, 3012-3014), the lists of angel names, and the line on the nations and the dregs (2834-2835).
