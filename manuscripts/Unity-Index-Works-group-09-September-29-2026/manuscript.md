## Faces

- **F08 Bitul** (thin): ביטול 3 lines, all 'bitul chametz' (lines 44, 49, 54), the halachic nullification, not the chassidic one. Counted but not evidence.
- **F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation** (minor): Line 101 (from Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim): maror sits in the middle, not on the left side of gevurah, because bitterness over one's distance from Godliness arouses great mercy. Stated as a reason for the Seder-plate layout.

## Signature (reading)

The Haggadah is a book of law, custom and textual sources, and on most of its pages the one idea is not stated. Its angle is the Rebbe's method of 'taamim uminhagim': every custom of the night gets a reason, and a few of the reasons reach down to the root. There God redeemed 'in His glory and Himself' is read as the Essence of Ein Sof before the tzimtzum (line 313, stated), and the matzah is read as the food through which 'the physical is not separate from the spiritual' in a Jew (line 408, stated). What it adds is placement: the Essence and the transformation of the physical are put inside the halachic order of the Seder, next to the source for the egg and the charoses, not in a separate discourse. That the whole Seder is therefore an enactment of the home below is our reading; the book does not say so.

## Structure

Preface (Divrei Shalom's method); laws of Shabbat HaGadol and bedikat chametz; the Seder plate; then the fifteen simanim of the Seder, each with the Rebbe's notes (reasons, customs, sources) and the modern commentary. The Chassidus comes in short quoted notes at particular points (Maggid on 'not by an angel', Motzi Matzah, the plate). The structure is halachic and liturgical.

## Signature (reading)

The responsa do not carry the one idea in any real way. The Tzemach Tzedek writes here as a posek, arguing from the Talmud, Rishonim and Acharonim on treifot, tefillin, niddah and idolatry, and the vocabulary of unity appears only as halachic homonyms. The work belongs to the index as the other half of the Tzemach Tzedek's life: the same author who defines the commandment of unity as knowing 'that He is one and there is nothing besides' (Derekh Mitzvotekha) rules here on the kashrut of a lung. That the two are one expression (halacha as His will and wisdom, F10) is the spine's general claim, and it is our reading for this book. One halachic distinction in it has the shape of the idea: a single tefillin house must be 'truly one' from a single hide, not many pieces joined, because 'one' there means 'יחידי ממש' (line 410). The analogy to yachid/echad is ours.

## Structure

Responsa ordered by the four sections of the Shulchan Aruch (Orach Chaim, Yoreh Deah in this file), each a letter to a questioner, followed by indexes. The structure is halachic; nothing in the arrangement points to the unity.

## Faces

- **F12 Knowing the unity** (minor, stated in passing): Line 1088 (OC 113:8, on adding praises in the Amidah): the view that all perfections 'are defects in Him' is explained 'as is known to those who know the matter of His simple unity, simple in the utmost simplicity'. - **F10 Torah and mitzvah** (the work's whole domain, tie unstated): Tefillin contain 'the unity of His Name' and show 'that He has the power and rule over the upper and lower beings' (line 384). The deeper tie (a mitzvah draws Or Ein Sof into the physical, Tanya 37) is not stated here.

## Signature (reading)

The Shulchan Arukh HaRav is halacha, and it carries the one idea only at its edges. It opens with 'shivisi Hashem lenegdi tamid' as 'a great principle in the Torah' (line 71): a person should act always as before a great King who 'fills heaven and earth'. It rules on the Shema as the acceptance of the kingdom of heaven. Once, explaining why one may not multiply praises of God, it defers to 'those who know the matter of His simple unity, simple in the utmost simplicity' (line 1088). That phrase is the Alter Rebbe's own language for the unity, dropped into a halachic argument and left unexplained: the code acknowledges the idea and does not teach it. The work also gives a useful contrast. It permits Modeh Ani before washing because the formula has no Divine Name (line 72), a purely halachic reason; the Rebbe later reads the same practice as proof that the yechidah is never defiled (Inyanah 11:4-5). Both stand. The reading of the whole code as 'the will of the Essence made law' (F10) is the spine's, not the book's.

## Structure

Halachic order of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch (siman and se'if). Opening laws (rising, Modeh Ani, shivisi) come first. The Shema, tefillin and prayer sections are the only places where the unity appears in content, as the object of the mitzvah. The rest is law.
