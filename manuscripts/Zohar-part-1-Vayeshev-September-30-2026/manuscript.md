Vayeshev is Joseph's fall: the coat, the dreams, the pit, Tamar, Potiphar's wife, the prison and the dreams of the butler and baker. The Zohar reads it around one hard question: why do the righteous suffer? Its answers here are among the most tender in the book: the broken share the moon's lack and she dwells in them; "sufferings of love"; the Shechinah goes with the righteous into the valley of death. Around that: the evil inclination that comes first and the good one that comes at thirteen; bad thoughts driven off by Torah; a nightly accounting; the human image that beasts fear; Joseph looking at his father's image; dreams that follow their interpretation; and comfort for the childless. ## 2. The unity of God

The unity teaching in Vayeshev is quiet; it comes through the Shechinah's presence in pain.

**Near to the broken-hearted, because they share her lack.** Souls born when the moon is in her flaw are broken in body; and yet «ובגין כך איהי שריא בגווייהו תדיר דלא שבקא לון» (Vayeshev 4:4; line 3759): "and so she dwells within them always and does not leave them"; "Havayah is near to the broken-hearted", «לאינון דסבלי עם סיהרא ההוא פגימו, אינון קריבין לה תדיר» (same line): "to those who bear that flaw with the moon, they are always near to her." And «אינון דסבלי עמה יתחדתון עמה» (same line): "those who suffer with her will be renewed with her." Ours: this is unity felt from below; the broken are not far from God but inside the Shechinah's own exile. Ties: S73, S86; the Chabad teaching that the Shechinah is with Israel in exile (Tanya ch. 17, Iggeret HaKodesh; memory).

**He goes with them into the valley.** «בכל אתר דצדיקיא אזלי, שכינתא אזלא עמהון ולא שביק לון» (Vayeshev 20:2; line 3924): "wherever the righteous go, the Shechinah goes with them and does not leave them." Joseph went down "into the valley of the shadow of death", and "Havayah was with Joseph."

**All is in His will.** Hosts praise at night, Israel by day, three times each, «ועל דא כלא הוה … ברשותיה קיימא, וכלא איהו ברעותיה» (Vayeshev 19:8; line 3921): "and so all stands in His power, and all is in His will."

**Zion and Jerusalem, judgement and mercy, one.** «דא דינא ודא רחמי, ותרוייהו חד» (Vayeshev 14:3; line 3866): "this is judgement and this is mercy, and the two are one." From the hidden "Most High" one voice comes out and only then do judgement and mercy appear (line 3867).

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **Suffering of love.** «דהא קודשא בריך הוא רחים עליה ברחימו לקרבא ליה לגביה, מתבר גופא בגין לשלטאה נשמתא» (Vayeshev 3:2; line 3745): "for the Holy One loves him with love, to bring him near; He breaks the body so the soul may rule." These are «יסורין של אהבה» (Vayeshev 4:5; line 3760): "sufferings of love", and those who bear them «אינון חברים משותפים בהדה» (same line): "are partners, companions with her." Guard: the Zohar itself says this holds only when "He checked and found no sin" (line 3747); it is a way to carry pain, never a reason to seek it or to judge another's pain. Ties: S59, S86.
- **Joy is where the Shechinah rests, and the broken one?** The Zohar asks it straight: «שכינתא לא שריא באתר עציבו אלא באתר דאית ביה חדוה» (Vayeshev 3:5; line 3748): "the Shechinah does not dwell in a place of sadness, only where there is joy"; Jacob lost the Shechinah while he grieved for Joseph and his spirit lived again with the news. Then where is joy for the broken? The answer is the line above: she dwells in the broken because she is broken with them. Ours: the Chabad split between sadness (atzvut, heavy and closed) and bitterness (merirut, broken and open) (Tanya ch. 26, 31; memory) answers the same question. Ties: S73 vs. joy states.
- **Pure heart, new spirit.** «לב טהור ברא לי אלהים ורוח נכון חדש בקרבי»: a pure heart is "a listening heart" and "a good heart that feasts always"; the new spirit is new like the moon (lines 3992-3994). Ties: S77.
- **The evil inclination as mountain or hair.** In the end the righteous will see it «כחד טורא רברבא» and wonder how they overcame it, and the wicked will see it «דקיק כחוטא דשערא» and wonder how they could not; «אלין יבכון ואלין יבכון» (Vayeshev 22:22; line 3959): "these will weep and those will weep." Ties: Tanya ch. 1 and 27 on the beinoni's lifelong struggle (memory).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Bad thoughts: turn to Torah.** Rabbi Chiya saw a student with a pale face, knew he was thinking of sin, and drew him into words of Torah «עד דאתיישב רוחיה בגויה» (Vayeshev 22:4; line 3941): "until his spirit settled within him." «כד חמי בר נש דהרהורין בישין אתיין לגביה, יתעסק באורייתא, וכדין יתעברון מניה» (Vayeshev 22:5; line 3942): "when a person sees bad thoughts coming to him, let him busy himself with Torah, and then they will pass from him." Ties: technique 12 (returning the wandering mind); Tanya ch. 12 and 28 on pushing away stray thoughts (memory).

**The nightly accounting.** «בכל יומא ויומא, כד אתי ליליא, בעי לאסתכלא ולמבדק, בכל מה דעבד כל ההוא יומא. בגין דייתוב מנייהו» (Vayeshev 23:10; line 3969): "every day, when night comes, he must look and examine all he did that whole day, so that he may return from them." Two witnesses go with him from waking: "let your eyes look straight ahead", "weigh the path of your feet" (line 3968). Ties: technique 24 (evening look-back), directly.

**Bedtime: crown Him, then a verse of mercy.** «כד סליק בר נש לערסיה, מבעי ליה לאמלכא עליה מלכותא דשמיא בקדמיתא, ולבתר יימא חד פסוקא דרחמי» (Vayeshev 7:5; line 3805): "when a person goes up to his bed, he must first crown over himself the kingdom of heaven, and then say a verse of mercy"; for in sleep the soul leaves and rises, each by its own path. Ties: the bedtime Shema; technique 24.

**Morning hands.** The body tastes death in sleep; on waking one does not touch the eyes until the hands are washed, from a vessel above to a vessel below, and the water is poured where no one walks (lines 3833-3837). Ours: a first act of the day that says "the night is over; I return to holiness."

**Look at your teacher's image.** Elisha asked for a double spirit; Elijah said "if you see me taken". «האי מאן דאסתכל במה דאוליף מרביה, וחמי ליה בההוא חכמתא, יכיל לאתוספא בההוא רוחא יתיר» (Vayeshev 24:5; line 3980): "whoever looks at what he learned from his teacher, and sees him in that wisdom, can gain more of that spirit." Joseph in every act «הוי חמי ברוחא דחכמתא לההוא דיוקנא דאבוי» (same line): "would see with the spirit of wisdom that image of his father," and his spirit grew. New technique below. Ties: the Chassidic practice of picturing the Rebbe's face (Tanya ch. 2 on bond to the sages; memory); technique 9 (picture).

**Be strong against it, from the side of strength.** «ובגין דההוא יצר הרע תקיף, בעי בר נש דיהא תקיף מיניה» (Vayeshev 21:2; line 3930): "because that evil inclination is strong, a person must be stronger than it." And in the hour of return or learning, when it comes most: be «תקיפא כאריא בכל סטרוי» (Vayeshev 22:19; line 3956), "strong as a lion on every side"; like Joseph, leave the garment in its hand and run outside (line 3958). Ties: Shulchan Aruch's opening "strong as a lion"; technique 17.

**Hand the interpretation to God.** Dreams follow the mouth that reads them (line 3804); so tell a dream only to one who loves you (line 3809), and read it as Joseph did: «לפקדא פישרא לקודשא בריך הוא, בגין דתמן איהו קיומא דכלא» (Vayeshev 23:15; line 3974): "to entrust the interpretation to the Holy One, for there is the ground of all."

## 5. Big human questions

- **Why do the righteous suffer and the wicked prosper?** Several answers side by side: love that breaks the body so the soul can rule (line 3745); the wicked paid their good here so they have nothing there (line 3747); the hour of the soul's birth, when the moon was flawed, and «בר דצלותא בטיל כל גזרי דינין» (Vayeshev 3:8; line 3751): "except that prayer cancels all decrees"; cleansing for the world to come (line 3755). Ours: the Zohar does not give one tidy answer; it gives several and keeps prayer open.
- **Why do the righteous die young?** «כדין קודשא בריך הוא נטיל זכאה דאשתכח בינייהו, בגין דישרי דינא על כלהו אחרנין» (Vayeshev 1:15; line 3734): the righteous one is taken because while he lives judgement cannot fall; he is gathered before the evil comes.
- **What if I have no children?** Hard lines about the one who will not have children stand beside comfort for the righteous who tried and could not, or whose children died: «עובדוי וההיא אורייתא, קא מגינן עליה לההוא עלמא» (Vayeshev 16:16; line 3889): "his deeds and that Torah protect him in that world," and to them is promised "a name better than sons and daughters" (line 3890). And God «נטע אילנין בהאי עלמא אי אצלחו יאות, לא אצלחו אעקר לון ושתל לון אפילו כמה זמנין» (Vayeshev 16:22; line 3895): "plants trees in this world; if they succeed, good; if not, He uproots them and replants them, even many times." Ours: no life is thrown away; there are more chances than we see.
- **For whom do I labor?** The one alone who rises early and works day and night, "his eye not sated with wealth", never asks «למי אני עמל ומחסר את נפשי מטובה» (line 3888): "for whom am I laboring and depriving my soul of good?"
- **Does anything learned get lost?** At the resurrection «ההיא אורייתא נטירא מההוא זמנא ותיעול במעייהו כמלקדמין» (Vayeshev 13:5; line 3843): "that Torah is kept from that time and will enter their inner parts as before", and even what he struggled with and could not grasp will enter him set right (line 3844).
- **Can one come back?** Reuben tried to save Joseph and did return for his own sin; «דכל מאן דעביד תשובה, קודשא בריך הוא קיים ליה בעלמא דין ובעלמא דאתי» (Vayeshev 13:20; line 3858): "for whoever does return, the Holy One keeps him in this world and the world to come."
- **What makes us human?** Beasts fear the human image while a person keeps it; sin changes it; Daniel's image did not change, and that image was "the angel that shut the lions' mouths" (lines 3961-3965).
- **In exile, who atones?** «אורייתא היא מכפרא עלייהו, ועובדין דכשרן, בגין דשכינתא עמהון בגלותא» (Vayeshev 23:11; line 3970): "the Torah atones for them, and good deeds, for the Shechinah is with them in exile"; and whoever learns and does good «גרים לה לכנסת ישראל, לארמא רישא בגו גלותא» (Vayeshev 23:12; line 3971): "causes the Assembly of Israel to raise her head inside the exile."

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **The broken are near her; those who suffer with her will be renewed with her (line 3759).** The Zohar's deepest word to the one in pain.
2. **Wherever the righteous go, the Shechinah goes (line 3924).**
3. **The nightly accounting (line 3969) and bedtime crowning (line 3805).** Evening practice in the Zohar's own words.
4. **Bad thoughts pass through Torah (lines 3941-3942).**
5. **Look at your teacher's image in what you learned (line 3980).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "Broken with her."** Inside: pain that does not feel like distance from God but like sharing His Presence's own lack; knowing you will be renewed with her (lines 3757-3760). How to enter: in suffering, instead of asking "why am I far," say "I am with her in her exile." Guard: never offered to someone else as an explanation of their pain. Near S73, S86, S59.
- **Technique, new: "The teacher's face."** When learning or deciding, recall the teacher (or parent) from whom this wisdom came and see them in it; let their spirit add to yours (line 3980). Ties technique 9.
- **Technique, new: "Crown, then mercy, then sleep."** At bedtime: accept His kingship aloud, say one verse of mercy, and give over the soul for the night (lines 3805-3807). Pairs with the nightly accounting (line 3969). Ties technique 24.
- **Technique, new: "Draw him into Torah."** When you see a friend (or yourself) in bad thoughts, do not rebuke; open a word of Torah and stay with it until the spirit settles (lines 3941-3942). Ties technique 12.
- **State, new: "Replanted."** Inside: after failure or loss, knowing that God replants a tree many times and nothing is final (line 3895). Near S77, S78.
