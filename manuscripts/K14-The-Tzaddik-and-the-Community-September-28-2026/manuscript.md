## What this cluster shows

These eleven subjects are the one idea as a community. They start with one person in whom the yesh has become ayin (the tzaddik), run through the lines that bind souls to him and to Moshe, and reach the body of Israel as one stature, the couple, the child, the peace between opposites, the table of friends, and the teaching carried outward. The spine judged the doctrine of the tzaddik 'thin / forced' (pillar map, 03). This pass revises that for the tzaddik himself. The Maggid, Noam Elimelech, the Degel, Me'or Einayim and Likutei Moharan all state that the tzaddik's work is the unity: he makes ayin from yesh, walks 'in the true unity', unites all worlds with God, or makes himself 'mah' and draws down 'Havayah is Elokim'. Tanya 10 states it for the Chabad tzaddik as transformation of darkness. What stays thin is the tzaddik as provider of children, life and sustenance. There the unity is his condition, not the source from which these functions are derived.

The strongest stated ties are in Tanya. Chapter 2 grounds the bond with the sages in the soul's root in Chochmah 'which is one with Him'. Chapter 32 grounds love of Israel in one Father and says the whole Torah is to draw Or Ein Sof into Knesset Yisrael 'one in One'. Chapters 23 and 34 define the Avos' chariot as bitul to His unity. Chapter 42 makes Moshe the one who draws da'at 'united with its Emanator'. IH 12 makes peace the revelation of Ein Sof among opposites. The second part of Tanya, the gate of the unity, is itself titled 'Chinuch Katan'. The Rebbe adds the leader who 'binds Israel with Or Ein Sof' and the wellsprings that show their essence only 'outside', which is the spine's own oil image applied to spreading. Real differences are kept: intercessor against rank, dispute as fall against dispute as creation, and the Avos as summit against the Avos as creatures. The farbrengen is only partly stated: its tie runs through love of Israel and one line of the Rayatz.

## S163 · The tzaddik · צדיק

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F11 Darkness turned to light · F07 The home below · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Search terms:** צדיק יסוד עולם · צדיקים · הצדיק · צדיקי הדור

> The tzaddik is the unity of God as a person: one in whom the yesh has become ayin, so that through him the worlds are joined to their Source and darkness is turned to light.

## Derivation

1. The spine's own anchor: heaven and earth were made yesh from ayin, and 'tzaddikim make ayin from yesh', even in eating, by raising the sparks to their source (Maggid 6:2, F08). The tzaddik is creation run in reverse. (*stated*)
2. The Alter Rebbe defines the complete tzaddik by transformation, not suppression: his evil is 'turned to good', and the bnei aliyah are 'those who turn darkness to light' (Tanya 10:1-6, F11). His service is not for his own thirst but 'to unite the Holy One and His Shechinah in the lower realms' (10:7, F07). (*stated*)
3. Tanya also gives the tzaddik a love of delight that is 'the reward of tzaddikim', a gift that cannot be commanded (Chinuch Katan 7, 11). It places the great tzaddikim in bitul bemetziut, 'a chariot to Ein Sof' (39:13; see S166). (*stated*)
4. The Besht's circle makes the tzaddik the joint between the worlds. 'Tzaddik yesod olam' is the one who 'binds and unites all the worlds to the Holy One and draws divine vitality into them', 'holding heaven and earth' as one (Degel, Korach 2). The complete tzaddik is a channel of blessing only as one who 'walks always in the true unity' (Noam Elimelech, Noach 5). He is 'bound to Him until he is, as it were, one with Him' (Me'or Einayim, Chayei Sarah 13, a passage that also states 'ein od milvado'). (*stated*)
5. Breslov puts the same point as the tzaddik's own bitul: he makes himself 'mah', cleaves to Ein Sof, and on returning draws down the knowledge 'that Havayah is Elokim', so that he blesses 'the Good and Beneficent' over everything (Likutei Moharan 4:10:19-21, G14). (*stated*)
6. The Rashab, as the Rayatz reports him (Hayom Yom 14 Kislev), ranks the tzaddik below the yashar, the tamim and the chassid. The tzaddik draws revelations within the order of the worlds; the chassid unites the Holy One and His Shechinah below. In Chabad the tzaddik is a rank, and the chassid's own service is the higher term. The word shifts, though: in Hayom Yom 'tzaddik' is named for keeping the positive commandments, while Tanya 10:7 gives the complete tzaddik exactly the chassid's service ('who deals kindly with his Maker', uniting the Holy One and His Shechinah below). The ranking is terminological. It does not rank the Lizhensk or Chernobyl tzaddik below the chassid. (*stated*)
7. That the tzaddik's work as intercessor, bringing 'children, life and sustenance', is an expression of the unity is our reading. The texts read here treat his unity as the condition of the flow (Noam Elimelech, Degel: 'only when the world is bound to the tzaddik'). They do not derive the flow from the unity. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 1 and 10: the complete tzaddik has turned his evil to good, and is one of the few 'bnei aliyah' who turn darkness to light. Tanya 2: in every generation the tzaddikim and sages are 'head and brain' to the souls of the people. Chinuch Katan 7-11: the tzaddikim's love of delight is a gift, not a commandment. Tanya 39: great tzaddikim serve as a chariot in Atzilut. IH 15:28 names 'tzaddik yesod olam' as the soul bound to God 'with cleaving and desire' (found, not quoted).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5632, Aseh Lecha Teivas (line 4331): 'whoever guards the covenant is called a tzaddik', and this is the 'turn from evil' through which the soul is purified before it is sanctified. A narrow, halachic-moral definition, not a doctrine of leadership.
- **Rashab.** As reported by the Rayatz (Hayom Yom 14 Kislev): tzaddik, yashar, tamim, chassid. The tzaddik is named for keeping the positive commandments and draws revelations within the order of the worlds. The chassid 'deals kindly with his Maker' by uniting the Holy One and His Shechinah below.
- **Rayatz.** Transmits the Rashab's ranking (above).
- **the Rebbe.** Not located as a separate teaching on the tzaddik in this pass. See S165 for the leader of the generation.
- **Other schools.** Maggid: 'tzaddikim make ayin from yesh' (6:2), and the tzaddik's pain arouses God's compassion for all Israel (5:1). Degel: the tzaddik unites heaven and earth, but only when the world is bound to him. Lizhensk: the tzaddik's flow of blessing is conditioned on his walking 'in the true unity', and he reverses creation, bringing things up to Chochmah where they become ayin (Terumah 7:4). Chernobyl: bound to God 'as it were one with Him', he sweetens judgments at their root. Breslov: bitul as 'mah', drawing down 'Havayah is Elokim'. The real difference: Lizhensk and Chernobyl build an intercessor whose power rests on his unity, while Chabad keeps the tzaddik as a rank of the soul, puts the chassid's own service in the foreground, and ranks the chassid above him (Rashab; but there 'tzaddik' is a narrower term, see step 6). The Concepts pillar's Kotzk critique and its grades of tzaddik (Dinov, Izhbitz, R. Tzadok) were not re-verified here.

## Thin or forced?

Not thin in the texts read here, which revises the spine's pillar map (pillar 03: 'thin / forced'). Noam Elimelech, the Degel, Me'or Einayim and Likutei Moharan state the tie in so many words, and Tanya 10 states it for the Chabad tzaddik. What stays thin is the tzaddik as wonder-worker and provider of 'children, life and sustenance'. In what was read, the unity is his condition, not the source from which these functions are derived.

## S164 · The bond with the Rebbe · התקשרות

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity **Search terms:** התקשרות · מקושר · אמונת חכמים · להתקשר

> Hitkashrut is the unity of God as a line of souls: a disciple bound to his Rebbe is a branch bound to the root through which it is one with God's wisdom.

## Derivation

1. The divine soul is 'literally a part of God above', drawn from His wisdom, which is one with Him (Tanya 2:1-2, F06). (*stated*)
2. Souls come down in grades. The life of the souls of the simple 'is drawn from the souls of the tzaddikim and sages, the heads of Israel in their generation', as the nails draw their life from the brain (Tanya 2:5-10). (*stated*)
3. Cleaving to the sages therefore re-unites the simple soul 'with its first essence and root in supernal Chochmah, for He and His wisdom are one'. That is why cleaving to the sages 'is as cleaving to the Shechinah' (Tanya 2:11). The bond is a unity, not an allegiance. (*stated*)
4. 'Da'at' itself means hitkashrut: binding the mind to God (Tanya 3:13, 42:4). The same word names binding the mind to God and binding the disciple to his master, but whether Tanya intends the link is our reading. (**reading**)
5. The bond outlasts death. The Rebbe's own faith, love and awe spread into each disciple 'according to his hitkashrut and closeness to him, in life and after death, with great love' (IH 27:14-18). Breslov says the same from 'one root' (Likutei Moharan 66). (*stated*)
6. The bond can be refused. For the Maggid, the complete redemption needed 'the hitkashrut of all', and Dasan and Aviram would not bind themselves to Moshe (6:1); he gives no reason for the refusal. In Me'or Einayim, faith in Moshe brought the people to faith in God, and 'what destroys faith is pride' (Vayetzei 33, F08). That pride is what breaks the bond is stated only there. Reading the Maggid's Dasan and Aviram the same way is ours. (*stated*)
7. The Rayatz moves the bond from the face to the Torah. True hitkashrut is learning his maamarim, farbrenging with friends and keeping his requests (Hayom Yom 24 Sivan). A chassid who acts as the Rebbe's emissary is 'bound in everything, walking, eating, sleeping' (8 Iyar). He grounds it in 'the essential bond of the soul with Godliness, equal in all Israel' (Sefer HaMaamarim 5689). Torah is one with God (F10), so the bond runs through the unity itself. The texts state the facts; the F10 link is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2:11: cleaving to the sages re-unites the soul with its root in Chochmah. IH 27: after the tzaddik's passing his 'essential spirit' spreads into his disciples according to their hitkashrut, and his light shines on them from above as well.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** In Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (OCR) the word hitkashrut is used for binding the mind in contemplation ('ha'amakat vehitkashrut da'ato') and for binding one's thought and speech to God's in Torah study, in Tanya's sense. In the lines found it is not used for the bond with the Rebbe.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 24 Sivan: hitkashrut is through his Torah, farbrengens and requests, not through seeing his face. Hayom Yom 8 Iyar: chassidim are the Alter Rebbe's emissaries, and 'an emissary is one with the sender'. Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (Zos Toras HaBayis): the essential bond of the soul is equal in all Israel, and the bond with 'Moshe' of the generation works through it. Sefer HaSichos 5688: the monthly 'maamad' is 'the foundation of the hitkashrut of anash'. This is practical and not derived from the unity.
- **the Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5741 (Tetzaveh): the essential bond of the soul is revealed through the shepherd of the generation (see S165).
- **Other schools.** Maggid: hitkashrut of all to Moshe is needed for redemption. Chernobyl: hitkashrut is one of the ten middot in every Jew, 'Yosef, the attribute of the tzaddik bound to the Creator'. Torah teaches what to bind oneself to against 'many Yosefs in the market' (Beshalach 14, read). Emunat chachamim leads to faith in God (Vayetzei 33). Degel: the tzaddik can bind the world to God only when the world is bound to him. Breslov: disciples and tzaddik share one root (Likutei Moharan 66). R. Nosson's emunat chachamim (pillar 03) was not re-verified. The difference: in the Polish, Galician and Breslov texts the bond is the machinery of the channel. In the Rayatz it is carried by the disciple's own learning and action.

## Thin or forced?

Not thin in Chabad: Tanya 2 and IH 27 state it. The institutional side (the maamad, reporting births to the Rebbe, Sefer HaSichos 5688) is tied by the Rayatz to 'the inner matter' of hitkashrut. It is not derived from the unity, and there the tie is thin.

## S165 · Moshe and the leader of the generation · נשיא הדור / אתפשטותא דמשה

**Faces:** F12 Knowing the unity · F05 The Essence beyond both · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** נשיא הדור · אתפשטותא דמשה · רעיא מהימנא · משה רבינו

> The leader of the generation is the unity of God as knowledge given to a people: Moshe and his extension in every generation join Israel to Or Ein Sof by drawing da'at and faith into them.

## Derivation

1. Da'at is hitkashrut: the mind bound to God 'as it is bound to a physical thing seen with the eyes' (Tanya 42:4, F12). (*stated*)
2. Moshe is the sum of 'the seven shepherds who draw vitality and Godliness to all the souls of Israel'. He is the 'faithful shepherd' who draws da'at to all Israel. His soul is rooted in supernal Da'at, 'united with their Emanator, for He and His knowledge are one' (Tanya 42:2). (*stated*)
3. Every Jewish soul has something of Moshe. In every generation sparks of Moshe's soul clothe themselves in the sages of the generation 'to teach the people knowledge' (42:2-3). After his passing his light spreads 'in every generation to the 600,000 souls', hidden like the sun shining under the earth on the stars (IH 27:19, F03). (*stated*)
4. Moshe is the one who said 'ein od' and left no remainder in the vacated space, unlike Rachav (Mekor Mayim Chayim via Magen Avos, F02). That the leader therefore carries the higher unity is our reading: the passage sets Moshe's confession against Rachav's and says nothing of his office as leader. (**reading**)
5. The Rayatz: the power of 'the extension of Moshe in every generation' is the 'Atah' in Moshe, 'the Essence of the Essence of Ein Sof' (Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, F05). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: 'Moshe and his extension in every generation bind and join Israel with Or Ein Sof', by feeding faith itself and revealing 'the essential bond of the essence of the soul' (F06). In exile this shows most as mesirat nefesh, so Mordechai was 'Moshe of his generation'. By citing that Midrash, 'the author of the maamar ruled on himself' that he is the revealed shepherd of his generation (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5741). That the author meant is the Rayatz is our identification. (*stated*)
7. Breslov gives Moshe a different office: the tzaddik who is 'Moshe', silence above speech, alone may enter the confusions of the vacated space, to raise the souls fallen there (Likutei Moharan 64:3). Chabad's Moshe draws knowledge down; Breslov's goes down into the place of no knowledge. (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 2: in every generation there are 'heads of the thousands of Israel'. Tanya 42: Moshe draws da'at, and his sparks are in the sages of every generation. Tanya 44: the love of the 'faithful shepherd' shines, hidden, in every generation through 'itpashtuta'. IH 27: his light spreads after his passing to the 600,000.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.

## S166 · The Patriarchs as the chariot · האבות הן הן המרכבה

**Faces:** F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F07 The home below · F02 The higher and the lower unity · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** הן הן המרכבה · מרכבה · אברהם יצחק ויעקב · האבות

> The Patriarchs as chariot are the unity of God as a human body: limbs and minds so nullified to Him that nothing in them carries anything but His will.

## Derivation

1. A chariot has no will of its own. The limbs that do a mitzvah 'become a chariot to the Supreme Will'. The Avos were this with all their limbs, 'holy and set apart from this world', 'only to the Supreme Will alone, all their days' (Tanya 23:6-7). (*stated*)
2. Tanya 34 follows directly on the unity of chapter 33. The Avos never stopped, 'even for one hour', binding mind and soul to the Master of the worlds 'in the bitul mentioned above to His unity' (Tanya 34:1, F08). (*stated*)
3. To be a chariot is 'bitul bemetziut' and inclusion in His light, the service of great tzaddikim in Atzilut. Others reach it only at moments, as in the bowings of the Amidah (Tanya 39:13-14). (*stated*)
4. What the Avos were in themselves, the Mishkan made public as 'the revelation of His unity'. After the Temple it is the four cubits of halacha. One who cannot be a chariot makes Him a dwelling through fixed times of Torah (Tanya 34:3-4, F07). (*stated*)
5. The chariot is inherited. The Avos drew down souls for their children for all time (Tanya 18:4), and the hidden love in every Jew is 'an inheritance from our fathers'. The Maharash names the inheritance: 'the longing and thirst to be nullified and included in Or Ein Sof itself' (Toras Shmuel 5631). (*stated*)
6. A limit kept. The Maharash says the Avos, though always in bitul, 'were still creatures'. God showed Himself to them as Shaddai, the source of limit, and they knew Havayah only by knowing, not by union. That came only at Sinai (Toras Shmuel 5632). The Maharash alone sets this limit. The Sfas Emes line that can look like one (Bamidbar 5:5: 'the Avos are the chariot; if so, their cleaving to God Himself is above the chariot') does not place anything above the Avos. In context it says that Israel, unlike the nations, cleave to God Himself beyond the ordered levels, and that this comes in the merit of the Avos, who 'brought themselves close'. Our reading of an elliptical line. (*stated*)
7. Ger places the Avos at the root of the unity: 'the covenant of the Avos is at the root of oneness, above and above'. 'One' is above 'first', so Shabbat, unity above time, belongs to the Avos (Sfas Emes, Bechukotai 19:4, F02). (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 18 (the hidden love, inherited from the Avos because they are the chariot), 23 (the definition), 34 (bitul to His unity; the dwelling through Torah for those who cannot be a chariot), 39 (Atzilut; bitul bemetziut).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5631: the inheritance of the Avos is the thirst to be included in Or Ein Sof, by which Israel raise Torah ('Adam') to what is 'not Adam'. Toras Shmuel 5632: the Avos were in constant bitul yet remained creatures, so the revelation of Havayah in union waited for Sinai. His 42 uses of the phrase were not all read.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689 (Zos Toras HaBayis): every Jew may say 'when will my deeds reach those of my fathers', because the essential bond of the soul is equal in all.
- **the Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Sfas Emes: the covenant of the Avos at the root of the unity (Bechukotai 19:4); Israel's cleaving is 'above the chariot' (Bamidbar 5:5). Me'or Einayim (Vayetzei 33): the purpose of choosing Israel is that they become a chariot and throne for His glory, as the Avos were. The difference: Tanya sets the Avos as the model most people cannot reach. The Maharash places something above them, Sinai. The Sfas Emes does not: Israel's cleaving 'above the chariot' comes in the Avos' merit (Bamidbar 5:5), and their covenant is at the root of the unity (Bechukotai 19:4).

## S167 · Love of Israel · אהבת ישראל

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F09 The unity of opposites · F04 Filling and surrounding **Search terms:** אהבת ישראל · ואהבת לרעך · אחדות ישראל · אהבת רעים

> Love of Israel is the unity of God among His children: souls that are one at their root in the One God, loved across the bodies that divide them.

## Derivation

1. The divine soul is a part of God (Tanya 2, F06). (*stated*)
2. All souls 'are matched, and have one Father'. So all Israel are 'literally brothers', from the root of their souls 'in the One God'; only the bodies are divided (Tanya 32:2, the spine's F06h). (*stated*)
3. So the love depends on bitul of the body. Those who make the body primary cannot truly love (32:1-2). The Rayatz, citing the Alter Rebbe: 'love your fellow' is the vessel for 'love Havayah your God', because 'in holiness there is bitul and unity, in kelipah selfhood and division' (F08). (*stated*)
4. It is 'the whole Torah' because the root of the Torah is to draw Or Ein Sof into Knesset Yisrael 'one in One'. God does not rest where souls are divided (Tanya 32:3; see S168). (*stated*)
5. The hard case keeps opposites distinct in conduct. Toward a sinner, hate and love 'are both true': hate for his evil, love for 'the spark of Godliness in him'. Compassion for that spark, in exile within him, cancels the hate (Tanya 32:6, F09). (*stated*)
6. The Tzemach Tzedek builds the mitzvah on the Arizal: all Israel is one body, the soul of Adam. Its limbs are included in one another through Shem Mah, drawn from Ein Sof, 'which includes all'. Hillel's negative form means covering the other's fault as one's own love covers one's own, 'as a makif' (Derekh Mitzvotekha, F04). (*stated*)
7. The Alter Rebbe: love of God and love of Israel 'are engraved as one' in every soul, and love of Israel is greater, 'for one loves what the Beloved loves' (Hayom Yom 28 Nissan). (*stated*)
8. Outside Chabad the love is also derived, but from the one Creator rather than the soul's part in God. Izhbitz: it 'branches from the recognition that one God created us, one Maker for all of us' (Beit Yaakov, Lech Lecha 15:1). The Besht's mesirat nefesh even for a Jew never seen (Hayom Yom 15 Kislev) is stated. That it rests on F06 is our reading. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 32. Hayom Yom 3 Adar I: love even a Jew one has never seen. Hayom Yom 2 Tammuz: in the Siddur, accept the mitzvah before prayer, as the gate to standing before God. Hayom Yom 28 Nissan: both loves engraved as one.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Hayom Yom 18 Av, in the Alter Rebbe's name: love of Israel must go 'until the soul is exhausted'.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Ahavat Yisrael: one body of Adam's soul, joined through Ein Sof; love covers faults like a makif. Hayom Yom 24 Teves: the 'ways of Chassidus' are that chassidim be one family in love.
- **Maharash.** Hayom Yom 8 Av: 'what is the use of Chassidus and fear of Heaven if the main thing, love of Israel, is lacking'. Toras Shmuel 5632: slander is the reverse of 'love your fellow', the sum of all the mitzvot.
- **Rashab.** Not located. The ahavat Yisrael passages in his Sefer HaMaamarim (OCR) are the Rayatz's maamar and diary printed there.
- **Rayatz.** Maamar 'Man de'ihu ze'ir' (printed in the Rashab's Sefer HaMaamarim 5659, OCR): love of one's fellow is the vessel for love of God; holiness is bitul and unity. Sefer HaSichos 5708 (Pesach): 'the two loves, of God and of Torah, are in love of Israel', even to a simple Jew.
- **the Rebbe.** Not located as a separate teaching in this pass (Inyanah has no hits).
- **Other schools.** Besht: mesirat nefesh for love of Israel (Hayom Yom 15 Kislev), and his love 'beyond measure' (16 Tammuz). Izhbitz: derived from the one Creator and the divine breath in each person. Pillar 22 records Polonoye (one stature of 613 limbs), Sochatchov, the Sfas Emes and Piaseczno (love rests on faith); not re-verified here. The difference: Tanya derives the love from the soul's identity with its source. Izhbitz derives it from common creatureliness and the wish to give good away. The pillar reports Piaseczno making it a function of faith.

## S168 · Knesses Yisrael: one body · כנסת ישראל / כלל ישראל

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F07 The home below · F04 Filling and surrounding **Search terms:** כנסת ישראל · כלל ישראל · קומה אחת · ששים ריבוא

> Knesset Yisrael is the unity of God as a people: one stature whose limbs, nullified to one another, become the place where His unity rests.

## Derivation

1. All souls have one Father (S167, Tanya 32:2). (*stated*)
2. Together they form 'one complete stature' of ten levels, from 'your heads' to 'your water-drawers', and 'the totality of their souls is called Knesset Yisrael' (Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 1:1). The Tzemach Tzedek: one body, the soul of Adam, its limbs included in one another through Ein Sof (Derekh Mitzvotekha). (*stated*)
3. The root of the whole Torah is 'to draw Or Ein Sof into Knesset Yisrael, the source of the souls of all Israel, to be one in One', not when souls are divided, for 'the Holy One does not rest in a defective place' (Tanya 32:3, F07). (*stated*)
4. The head is not complete without the feet. So each person is lowly before every other, and 'by this bitul they become one for the indwelling of His unity'. Whoever thinks himself separate 'falls into the world of division' (Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 1:2-3, F08). Gathered, they become 'the private domain of His unity' (1:4). (*stated*)
5. Holiness is one and kelipah many: Yaakov's seventy are called one 'nefesh', Esav's six 'nefashot' (the Rayatz's maamar in the Rashab's 5659 volume). (*stated*)
6. Sochatchov: a person may seek closeness to God only through the klal. Amalek's work is to make him 'reckon himself by himself' and so despair (Shem MiShmuel, Tetzaveh 7:21). The wording echoes Tanya 22 ('regarding oneself as a thing in its own right'), but Sochatchov speaks of separation from the klal, not from God's unity. The F08 link is our reading. (**reading**)
7. The 600,000 are the number of the root souls, to which Moshe's light spreads in every generation (IH 27:19; see S165). The sacred sums and 'one body' language are stated. That Knesset Yisrael is also Malchus, the Shechinah and the bride (Torah Ohr, see S169) is one being and not two is our joining of passages. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya 32:3 (Or Ein Sof in Knesset Yisrael, one in One). Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 1 (one stature, bitul, reshus hayachid). Torah Ohr, Shemot supplements: Knesset Yisrael in exile as a woman in labor who gives birth to Mashiach.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Ahavat Yisrael: all Israel one body, and hence mutual responsibility (arevut) and the Arizal's confessing sins in the plural.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** The maamar printed in the Rashab's 5659 volume: Yaakov's one 'nefesh' against Esav's 'nefashot'.
- **the Rebbe.** Toras Menachem 5719: Knesset Yisrael is the bride of the Holy One, taking on His mission in the world (see S169).
- **Other schools.** Sochatchov: closeness to God only through the klal; Saul fell when he went in his own strength and not in the strength of the klal (Tetzaveh 7:21-22). Pillar 22 records Polonoye's half-shekel (one stature of 613, whoever joins the whole is credited with all) and the Sfas Emes; not re-verified here. The difference: Chabad reads the one body as the place of His indwelling, made by bitul. Sochatchov reads it as the only access to God for the individual, whose separateness is Amalek.

## S169 · Man and woman; marriage · איש ואשה / נישואין

**Faces:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F09 The unity of opposites · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below **Search terms:** איש ואשה · חתן וכלה · נישואין · קידושין · אשת חיל

> Marriage is the unity of God as a union of two: the supernal groom and bride are Havayah and Elokim, the surrounding and the filling light, whose union draws down what is above both, and the couple below is drawn from them.

## Derivation

1. The Alter Rebbe maps the supernal groom and bride ('chatan vekallah ha'elyonim') onto the two modes of presence; the human wedding is the image he starts from. 'The groom is the Holy One, sovev kol almin, called Havayah; the bride is the Name Adnut, the power of the maker in the made, memale kol almin' (Torah Ohr, Shemot supplements 3, F04). 'Chatan' is from 'nechit', descent: it is a great descent for sovev to contract into Adnut to bring yesh from ayin (4). (*stated*)
2. Divine male and female are 'the union of Havayah and Elokim' (Mah and Ban). When the two are included in each other a level higher than both is drawn, the delight of Ab. This is 'as with the chuppah into which groom and bride enter, bringing revelation from the makifim' (Torah Ohr, Vayechi 5:9; G14, F09). The human chuppah is a parenthetical image for the supernal union here, not something derived from it. (*stated*)
3. The order runs both ways. First the bride receives light from the groom; then 'through the bride he gladdens the groom', adding light 'from opposite him', and she becomes her husband's crown (Torah Ohr, Bereshit 5:15). The lower adds to the higher (G05). (*stated*)
4. The Mitteler Rebbe: the joy of groom and bride exceeds that of all mitzvot, because the power to beget is yesh me'ayin 'from the essence of the soul itself' (F05). The bodily union carries the highest 'by the rule that the higher a thing, the lower it can descend' (Derushei Chasunah, G05). (*stated*)
5. The Rebbe: 'man and woman, groom and bride' below derive from the Holy One as groom and Knesset Yisrael as bride. The meaning of the match is that the soul takes on His mission in the world (Toras Menachem 5719, F07). When a home is built on Torah, 'the Shechinah between them' unites husband and wife, and this makes the revealed marriage of God and Israel below (5727). (*stated*)
6. The Maharash: forbidden unions are the reverse of Torah, for Torah is 'the day of His wedding', the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah (Toras Shmuel 5639). (*stated*)
7. The Besht uses the joy of groom and bride as the analogy for letters and worlds 'united in a true union with Godliness'. Where the analogy fails, groom and bride remaining two while the world and its source are not (G06), is our marking; the text does not say it. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Torah Ohr, Bereshit 5 (the two wedding blessings, 'gladdens groom and bride' and 'groom with the bride'); Vayechi 5 (Havayah and Elokim as male and female, the chuppah); Shemot supplements (the joy of groom and bride, sovev and memale). Likkutei Torah, Tazria 3 (God called 'ish', Knesset Yisrael 'ishah'; found, not read in full).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Maamarei Admur HaEmtzai, Derushei Chasunah: joy from the essence of the soul brings out what is hidden, yesh me'ayin, and this is the root of the power to beget.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Derekh Mitzvotekha, Arayos (a woman and her sister): the unions above between masculine and feminine sefirot use kinship names forbidden below. They are permitted in Atzilut and forbidden in Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah. Leah and Rachel are the hidden and revealed worlds. The reason is given from the difference between Atzilut and the lower worlds (the passage was not read to its end).
- **Maharash.** Toras Shmuel 5639: Torah is 'the day of His wedding', the union of the Holy One and the Shechinah; forbidden unions are its reverse.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, Asher Bara (among the maamarim of the wedding of 14 Kislev 5689; the preceding Lecha Dodi is dated to it): the seventh blessing includes the ten utterances, 'Israel merit below as above'. The gathering to gladden groom and bride is also love of Israel (see S172).
- **the Rebbe.** Toras Menachem 5719 (kiddushin and gittin in the service of man); 5727 (peace in the home and the revealed marriage of God and Israel).
- **Other schools.** Besht's letter: 'the joy of groom and bride in smallness and physicality, all the more so in this supernal level'. Eshet chayil was not traced. The difference: the Besht uses marriage as an analogy for unification. The Rebbe (5719) derives the couple below from the supernal groom and bride, and the Mitteler Rebbe derives the wedding joy from the essence of the soul. Torah Ohr maps the supernal couple and uses the human wedding as its image, which is nearer the Besht's direction than a derivation.

## S170 · Children and education · חינוך

**Faces:** F12 Knowing the unity · F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F11 Darkness turned to light **Search terms:** חינוך · חנוך לנער · קטנים · בנים

> Education is the unity of God planted early: the child is trained in faith in His unity, so that the love built on it holds when he is grown and when he falls.

## Derivation

1. The Alter Rebbe calls the second part of Tanya, the Shaar HaYichud VeHaEmunah, 'Chinuch Katan', and opens it with 'Train a youth according to his way' (Chinuch Katan 1-4). The book of the unity is named as a book of education. (*stated*)
2. There are two loves. The tzaddik's flame of delight is a gift. The love 'every person can reach', by contemplating 'that He is our very life', is the one that can be commanded and trained (Chinuch Katan 7-11). (*stated*)
3. Even a tzaddik falls between levels, and what remains is 'the love in which he was trained and habituated from his youth, before he reached the rank of tzaddik'. So 'even when he is old he will not depart from it' (Chinuch Katan 12). (*stated*)
4. The foundation of that love is 'the pure and faithful belief in His unity'. The gate of the unity (Shaar HaYichud 1-7) follows immediately (F12). (*stated*)
5. Piaseczno: 'chinuch' is a beginning that brings a hidden capacity from potential to act. In a Jewish child 'the breath of God is hidden and stored', and the educator must bend down into the child's smallness to reach that spark (Chovat HaTalmidim, Introduction 5-6, F06). He tells the child that God sees even his play (1:5). (*stated*)
6. The Rebbe: education is also of oneself, daily and with mesirat nefesh. It comes from bitul, feeling like a child before the truth (F08), and the Besht's 'everything one sees is an instruction' means educating oneself from everything (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5738, G12). (*stated*)
7. The Rayatz: Torah and kosher education are 'the pillar of light that lights the darkness of the coarse world' and bring the purpose for which the soul came down (Sefer HaSichos 5709, F11, F07). The Rayatz does not name the unity here. Reading the light in the darkness as F11 and the soul's purpose as F07 is ours. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Tanya, Chinuch Katan: the second part of Tanya is named for education, and education is grounded in faith in the unity.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located as a teaching of his own. The preface printed with Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (OCR, scan p. 9) calls the yeshiva 'a house of chinuch' to form 'a whole Jew' in whom 'Israel, Torah and the Holy One' are joined. Its author was not established in this pass, so it is not cited as the Rashab's.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaSichos 5709: kosher education is the pillar of light in the dark world. Sefer HaSichos 5708: talk of Judaism and education, and bring the yeshiva into the home.
- **the Rebbe.** Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5738 (Purim Katan): mesirat nefesh for education, of children and of oneself; 'out of the mouths of babes You have founded strength' silences the enemy.
- **Other schools.** Piaseczno, Chovat HaTalmidim: the child's hidden divine soul is to be revealed, each by his own way, and the child should know God sees even his game. The difference is one of emphasis: Tanya starts from the content taught (faith in the unity); Piaseczno from the child's hidden soul. Both are stated.

## S171 · Peace and dispute · שלום ומחלוקת

**Faces:** F09 The unity of opposites · F04 Filling and surrounding · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F01 The world said into being now **Search terms:** שלום · מחלוקת · עושה שלום · שלום בית

> Peace is the unity of God among opposites: fire and water stay themselves yet become one when the Ein Sof that is no attribute is revealed in them; dispute is either the space in which a world is made or the collapse of His two modes into one.

## Derivation

1. 'He makes peace in His heights': Michael is water, chesed, the flow down; Gavriel is fire, gevurah, the withdrawal upward. 'They are opposed and contrary' while they are only attributes (IH 12:1-2). (*stated*)
2. God makes peace between them by revealing Or Ein Sof, 'which is not an attribute at all'. Then the opposites 'are included in their source and root and become literally one, nullified in His light'. The gevurot are sweetened in the chasadim through Tiferet, which is why the plain Name Havayah denotes Tiferet (IH 12:3-5, F09). (*stated*)
3. Below, this is awakened by tzedakah and giving life to 'one who has nothing of his own' (IH 12:6). (*stated*)
4. The Besht: 'seek peace and pursue it' means making peace between the physical thing and 'the divine vitality that gives it life' (Hayom Yom 5 Av, F01). (*stated*)
5. Dispute as error. Korach claimed that the all-blue tallit, the surrounding light, needs no threads, the contracted inward flow. His dispute with Aharon was a denial that the makif must come through the contracted, inward light (Likkutei Torah, Korach 1:33-37, F04). The maamar speaks of makif and inner light; calling them sovev and memale is our mapping. (*stated*)
6. Dispute as creation. For Breslov, the dispute of the sages makes 'the vacated space' in which the world is created by their words. Without it 'all would be Ein Sof'; but too much speech breaks the vessels (Likutei Moharan 64:4, F03). For the Degel, a dispute 'for the sake of Heaven' (shamayim, esh-mayim) is fire and water made heaven, and it ends in the covenant, which is peace (Korach 1). The Degel joins the opposites in 'heaven' and the covenant; that they are joined in Him (F09) is our reading. (*stated*)
7. The Rebbe: there is no peace except that founded on Torah (F10). Peace between husband and wife makes the revealed marriage of God and Israel (Toras Menachem 5727; see S169). The communal side, bitul of each to the other as the ground of unity, is S168. (*stated*)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** IH 12 (peace from revealed Ein Sof). Likkutei Torah, Korach (Korach's dispute as denying the contracted flow). Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim (bitul among people as the ground of unity).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Not located in this pass.
- **Maharash.** 'Oseh shalom' occurs about 50 times in Toras Shmuel; not read in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rayatz.** Not located as a separate teaching in this pass.
- **the Rebbe.** Toras Menachem 5727: 'the whole Torah was given to make peace in the world'; peace founded on Torah; the home and the revealed marriage above.
- **Other schools.** Besht (Hayom Yom 5 Av): peace between the physical and its divine life. Degel: a dispute for the sake of Heaven as fire and water becoming heaven. Lizhensk: the tzaddik makes 'peace and unity in the upper and lower worlds' (Terumah 7:4). Breslov: dispute as the vacated space of creation. The difference is real. Chabad reads Korach's dispute as a collapse of the two modes of presence and peace as their inclusion in Ein Sof. Breslov gives dispute a positive, creative role through the vacated space, whose literal sense the Alter Rebbe rejects (spine F03 disagreement).

## S172 · The gathering of friends · התוועדות / אהבת חברים

**Faces:** F06 The soul as part of the One · F08 Bitul: the person's side of the unity · F01 The world said into being now **Search terms:** התוועדות · התועדות · חברים · דיבוק חברים

> The gathering of friends is the unity of God practised at a table: souls that are one at their root meet, each lowly before the other, and in each one God is heard.

## Derivation

1. The farbrengen is 'one of the foundations of the ways of the chassidim and of Chassidus, and the entrance to the foundational mitzvah of love of Israel' (Hayom Yom 24 Tishrei). Its tie to the unity runs through S167 (Tanya 32, F06). (*stated*)
2. The Tzemach Tzedek: 'the ways of Chassidus' are that all chassidim be as one family in love, alongside 'Chassidus is chayus' (Hayom Yom 24 Teves). (*stated*)
3. Friends need one another because each has what the other lacks. So each is lowly before every person, and 'by this bitul they become one for the indwelling of His unity' (Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 1:2-3, F08). The passage speaks of all Israel; applying it to a farbrengen is our reading. (**reading**)
4. The Rayatz, on the gathering at a wedding feast and, from it, on any gathering where friends 'arouse themselves and arouse one another': this is 'then those who fear God spoke to one another, and God listened'. At that moment 'each one hears God, and in each one God is heard' (Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, F06, F01). 'The very gathering' of those who learn and serve 'is a great and essential matter'. (*stated*)
5. Rebuke at a farbrengen is 'with love and great affection', never with shaming (Hayom Yom 24 Tishrei:2). Sochatchov: whoever does not find closeness to God should blame his poor attachment to the klal and strengthen love of Israel and 'cleaving of friends' (Shem MiShmuel, Tetzaveh 7:21). (*stated*)
6. That the customs of the farbrengen (mashke, niggun, stories) express the unity was not found stated in anything read. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** Hayom Yom 3 Adar I: love of Israel includes joining the congregation where one lives. Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim: each needs the other.
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Not located in this pass.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** Hayom Yom 24 Teves: chassidim as one family.
- **Maharash.** Not located in this pass.
- **Rashab.** Hayom Yom 20 Tishrei records a saying of his 'at one of the farbrengens' (on the heaviness before prayer); nothing on the gathering itself was located.
- **Rayatz.** Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, Asher Bara: the gathering to arouse one another, 'each hears God'. Hayom Yom 24 Tishrei (foundation and gateway, loving rebuke) and 24 Sivan (hitkashrut includes farbrenging with friends). Sefer HaSichos 5708: at farbrengens they say that the two loves are in love of Israel.
- **the Rebbe.** Compiled the Hayom Yom entries. The many 'dibbuk chaverim' hits in his Igros Kodesh and Toras Menachem were not read in this pass.
- **Other schools.** Sochatchov: cleaving of friends as the remedy when closeness to God fails. Pillar 22 records R. Asher of Stolin (Beit Aharon: cleaving of friends as part of Shabbat's holiness); not re-verified. The difference: Chabad makes the gathering itself an institution and a gateway; Sochatchov prescribes it as a remedy.

## Thin or forced?

Partly thin. The stated tie runs through love of Israel (S167). The Rayatz's 'each hears God, and in each God is heard' is the one direct statement found. The customs of the farbrengen carry no stated tie.

## S173 · Spreading the wellsprings · הפצת המעיינות

**Faces:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F05 The Essence beyond both · F07 The home below · F06 The soul as part of the One **Search terms:** הפצת המעיינות · יפוצו מעינותיך · שליחות · הפצה

> Spreading the wellsprings is the unity of God carried outward: the Essence, separate from everything like oil, shows itself when its teaching permeates the 'outside' until the outside is found to be wellsprings.

## Derivation

1. The Besht's letter: Mashiach will come 'when your teaching is published and revealed in the world and your wellsprings spread outward, so that they too can make unifications and ascents like you' (Ben Porat Yosef). What is spread is the power to unify. (*stated*)
2. The Rebbe dates the beginning to the Alter Rebbe's liberation on 19 Kislev. The Tzemach Tzedek held that the printing of Likkutei Torah fulfilled the promised date of 5608; the Maharash answered that 'we need Mashiach below ten handbreadths' (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5746, a disagreement kept). (*stated*)
3. The oil. Because the inner Atik, which Chassidus draws down, is 'separate from everything, like oil that does not mix', it permeates everything. So after 19 Kislev it is the wellsprings themselves, not only their water, that are found outside (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5726:16-17). This is the image the spine takes from Inyanah 7-8 for all of Chassidus. (*stated*)
4. 'The essence of the wellsprings of Chassidus is expressed only when they spread outward.' The aim is 'to turn the outside into wellsprings', down to the lowest, and this is the vessel for Mashiach (Inyanah 22:3-23:1; F11, G05). (*stated*)
5. Spreading reveals that 'the true existence of the outside itself is the wellsprings' (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5726:16, F05). In 5729 the 'one' above the ten sefirot is to be revealed in Malchus and in this physical world, which completes the dwelling 'for His Essence' (F07). (*stated*)
6. The one who spreads is an emissary, and 'an emissary is one with the sender' (Hayom Yom 8 Iyar). The Mitteler Rebbe: the inner Torah is fire and people fear fire, so the teacher must go to the receiver (Hayom Yom 16 Av). The emissary's oneness is stated. Reading it as F06 is ours. (**reading**)
7. Outside Chabad the verse is used ethically. Polonoye, quoting Olelot Efraim (not a chassidic work): learning should be public, to teach others, while deeds stay hidden (Toldot, Ki Teitzei 136). No tie to the unity is made there. (**reading**)

## The Rebbeim and the schools

- **Alter Rebbe.** The liberation of 19 Kislev as the beginning of 'spreading outward' (as the Rebbe reads it).
- **Mitteler Rebbe.** Hayom Yom 16 Av: explaining the Besht's journeys, the teacher must go to the receiver.
- **Tzemach Tzedek.** As the Rebbe tells it (Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5746): the end of 5608 was fulfilled by the printing of Likkutei Torah, the spreading of the inner Torah.
- **Maharash.** His answer to the Tzemach Tzedek: 'we need Mashiach below ten handbreadths'. The spreading is not the goal in itself.
- **Rashab.** Cited by the Rebbe (5726): the revelation after Petersburg is like the olive, which gives its oil when crushed.
- **Rayatz.** Hayom Yom 8 Iyar: chassidim are emissaries, and the emissary is one with the sender. Sefer HaSichos 5709, citing the Besht: every Jew comes into the world with a mission of Torah light.
- **the Rebbe.** Inyanah 22-23; Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat 5726 (Vayeshev), 5729 (13 Tammuz), 5746 (Zos Chanukah).
- **Other schools.** The Besht's letter (in Ben Porat Yosef). Polonoye (Toldot, quoting Olelot Efraim): the verse as an ethic of teaching. The difference: in the Besht's letter the content spread is the power of yichudim, and in the Rebbe it is the Essence permeating the outside. Polonoye's reading carries no unity content. The spine's disagreement stands: the Rashab warns that Chassidus without the work of prayer becomes selfhood, while the Rebbe stresses action and spreading outward.

| Intercessor or rank? | Lizhensk, Degel and Chernobyl: the tzaddik is the channel of blessing and unites the worlds, his power resting on his unity with God (Noam Elimelech, Noach 5; Degel, Korach 2; Me'or Einayim, Chayei Sarah 13).
