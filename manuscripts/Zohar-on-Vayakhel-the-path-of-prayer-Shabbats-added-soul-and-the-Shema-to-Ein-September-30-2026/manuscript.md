## 1. What the range is

Vayakhel is the Zohar's great parashah of **prayer and Shabbat**. Its main parts:

* Moses gathers Israel apart from the mixed multitude after the calf (lines 3755-3796), with a passage on the angel of death at funerals that we set aside; * Elijah and Jonah; Jonah as the soul's voyage through life and death (lines 3797-3851); * the poor man as God's gift, and Jacob's trust (lines 3815-3827); * **the order of prayer** as the Queen adorned and brought to the King, ending in giving over the soul (lines 3861-3872); * **the ascent of prayer** through the air and the seven firmaments, received by the angels of each gate (lines 3875-3904), and the six commandments fulfilled in prayer (lines 3905-3910); * **Shabbat**: the fire of the other side hidden, the added spirit, the three meals, the Torah reading, Havdalah and its spices (lines 3911-4032); * the lower Garden of Eden: its firmament, gates, letters, garments, river and pillar (lines 4037-4128); * the ark, the covenant, and the tzitz on Aaron's forehead (lines 4129-4224); * the incense (lines 4220-4238); * **Rav Hamnuna Sava's unification of the Shema** (lines 4173-4182) and the four repairs of prayer (lines 4163-4165); * the doe who longs for water, and the resurrection in the Galilee (lines 4239-4250).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**The Shema lifted to Ein Sof.** The unification that Rav Hamnuna Sava received, teacher from teacher, back to Elijah: first "Shema" joins the name to the seventy names, and "Israel" joins the woman to her husband. Then join all the limbs and the two dwellings ברעו דלבא, "with the will of the heart," לאסתלקא בדבקותא דאין סוף, לאתדבקא כלא תמן, למהוי רעותא חדא עלאי ותתאי, "to rise in cleaving to Ein Sof, to cleave everything there, so that upper and lower are one will" (line 4175). At "One": לסלקא רעותיה בדחילו ורחימו לעילא לעילא עד אין סוף ולא ישתבק רעותא מכל אינון דרגין ושייפין, "to raise his will in awe and love higher and higher, up to Ein Sof, and not let the will leave any of the levels and limbs," ולמהוי כלא קשורא חדא באין סוף, "so that all is one knot in Ein Sof" (line 4179). That is why ולהכי אנן מאריכין באחד, "we lengthen the word 'One'" (line 4181). **(ours)** This is the Zohar text behind the Chabad practice of dwelling on *echad*, and behind the Tanya's reading of the Shema as bittul of all into the One.

**Where all wills are fixed.** In prayer, ובעוד דפומיה ושפוותיה מרחשן, לביה יכוון, ורעותיה יסתלק לעילא לעילא, "while his mouth and lips murmur, let his heart intend, and his will rise higher and higher," to unify all in the secret of secrets, דתמן תקיעו דכל רעותין ומחשבין ברזא דקיימא באין סוף, "where all wills and thoughts are fixed, in the secret that stands in Ein Sof" (line 4120). And he should cleave his will אדבק רעותיה כנורא בגחלתא, "like a flame to a coal" (line 4119).

**The firmament with no color.** Above all the heavens is one that has no appearance: ביה לית גוון, לא אתחזי, ולא אתגלייא, האי איהו דאפיק כל נהורין, "in it there is no color; it is not seen or revealed; it is what brings out all the lights" (line 4116). Even the souls of the righteous see its light only כמבתר כותלא, "as from behind a wall" (line 4116). Past this, no wise one can know; happy is the one who "enters and comes out" and cleaves to Him (line 4118). **(ours)** A good picture of the Ein Sof light that fills and surrounds without itself being a thing seen.

**Prayer makes all one.** The prayer that rises through the seven palaces joins them למהוי כלא חד כדקא יאות, "so that all is one as it should be" (line 3903). And God does not complete His crown alone: איהו מחכה עד דיסתיימון כל צלותהון דישראל, "He waits until all the prayers of Israel are finished" (line 3904). **(ours)** Each person's prayer is waited for.

**Shabbat is the name of the union.** Shabbat is *shin* (the three fathers) and *bat* (the daughter, the lower point): ש' בת, למהוי כלא חד, "*shin*, *bat*, so that all is one" (line 3934).

**The Shekhinah was never divorced.** In exile she dwells with Israel to protect them, אבל תרוכין לא הוות לעלמין, "but there was never a divorce" (line 4185). That is why we mention the Exodus every day: to show that she is free and joined to her husband.

## 3. States of consciousness

**Given over (proposed ZS2-20).** After the first three blessings (the King's right hand, His left under her head, and the embrace), מאן דאצטריך למשאל שאלתין, ישאל. דהא כדין שעתא דרעותא איהו, "whoever needs to ask, let him ask, for this is the hour of will" (line 3866). Then, at the falling on the face, one must ולממסר נפשיה, בשעתא דאיהי נקטא נפשין רוחין, "hand over his soul, at the hour when she is gathering souls and spirits" (line 3867). If he gives himself in that will and it is accepted, he is bound in the bundle of life, and קודשא בריך הוא קרי ליה שלום לתתא, "the Holy One calls him 'Peace' below" (line 3870). The six commandments of prayer end the same way: למסור נפשיה לגביה, ולמיהב ליה נשמתא ברעותא דלבא, "to hand over his soul to Him and give Him the soul with the will of the heart" (line 3908). Inside: having asked for what you need, you stop holding and let your life go into His hands. Near S83 (Accepting the yoke) and S60 (The essential bond); new is the quiet after asking, where nothing more is asked. This is the Zohar source for the Chabad practice of *mesirat nefesh* in *nefilat apayim*.

**The added soul: no sadness and no anger.** On Shabbat eve an added spirit comes to each person, and with it נשיין כל עצבא וחימתא, ולא אשתכח בר חדוה, "all sadness and anger are forgotten, and there is only joy" (line 3938). האי יומא, יומא דנשמתין איהו, ולאו יומא דגופא, "this day is a day of souls, not a day of the body" (line 3960). This fills in S76 (Shabbos: the soul returned). When it leaves, the soul is left bare, and the spices of Havdalah are for לאתישבא נפשיה בההוא ריחא, "settling the soul in that scent" (line 4030).

**The soul new each morning.** At night all souls are gathered in, and in the morning ונשמתא איהי חדתא השתא כמלקדמין, "the soul is new now, as at first" (line 4127): "new every morning; great is Your faithfulness." A state worth naming for anyone who wakes heavy: this is a new soul.

**Broken for Him.** "Happy is he whose hope (*sivro*) is in the Lord": read *shivro*, "his breaking," דניחא להו לצדיקייא, לתברא גרמייהו, ולאתברא תבירו על תבירו, "for it is pleasing to the righteous to break themselves, breaking upon breaking" (line 3821), all for God. And the righteous are God's vessels: תבירין אינון בהאי עלמא, בגין לקיימא אורייתא, "they are broken in this world to keep the Torah" (line 4209), and God uses only these vessels. Fills in S59 (True lowliness), and joins Yitro's "the King dwells in broken vessels" (part2-yitro.md).

**One heart toward the Father.** Whoever smelled the incense had his heart cleared, ולא הוה ליה אלא לבא חדא, לקבל אבוה דבשמיא, "and he had only one heart, toward his Father in heaven" (line 4220). The two hearts of the Shema become one (see ZT2-03 in part2-vaera-bo.md).

**The distracted face.** R. Shimon sees R. Yosi thinking about worldly matters and says: יוסי קום אשלים דיוקנך, דאת חד חסר בך, "Yosi, get up and complete your image, for one letter is missing in you" (line 4199). When he turns to the Torah, his image is whole. **(ours)** A gentle word for the wandering mind (technique 12, Returning the wandering mind): the self is incomplete while elsewhere.

**A glimpse, then only a face.** When a righteous person stood before the tzitz, the priest saw at the first glance the letters shining on his face; when he looked again he saw only a lit face, דהא רעותא דקודשא בריך הוא הוה ביה בההוא בר נש, "for the will of the Holy One was on that person" (line 4202). The vision above shows itself only for a moment. This adds to ZS2-13 (*The glimpse that calls you back*).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Rav Hamnuna Sava's Shema (proposed ZT2-11).** From lines 4173-4182, step by step:

1. "Shema": the Name gathered from the seventy names; hold that all is one gathering.
2. "Israel": the bride joined to her husband.
3. "Hashem Elokeinu Hashem": join the limbs and the two dwellings with the will of the heart.
4. "One": lengthen the word. Raise the will in awe and love, higher and higher, up to Ein Sof, and do not let go of any level on the way; tie all into one knot there.

**(ours)** This is the fullest contemplative Shema in volume 2. It fits technique 8 (Depth), 14 (Stopping at the edge of understanding) and 16 (love and awe). What is new is the instruction not to leave any level behind: the rise carries the whole person, and the whole world, with it.

**Jonah's four questions (proposed ZT2-12).** The soul is Jonah in the ship of the body; the captain is the good inclination, who says: מה לך נרדם קום קרא אל אלהיך, "why are you sleeping? Get up and call to your God" (line 3837). Then four questions: מה מלאכתך דאת עסקת בה בהאי עלמא, "what is your work that you did in this world?" and confess it; "where do you come from?" אסתכל מאין באת, מטפה סרוחה, ולא תתגאי קמיה, "look where you came from, a putrid drop, and do not be proud before Him"; "what is your land?" you came from earth and will return to it; "of what people are you?" do you have merit of the fathers to protect you (line 3838). **(ours)** A ready frame for technique 24 (The evening look-back), short enough to do in bed.

**The order of prayer as a walk to the King.** Songs and praises adorn the Queen; the seated prayer readies her maidens; at *emet veyatziv* all is ready; at *ga'al Yisrael* stand, for the King comes out to meet her; the first three blessings are His right hand, His left, and the kiss; פומיה ולביה ורעותיה כחדא, "his mouth, heart and will as one" (line 3865); then ask; then the last three; then give over the soul (lines 3862-3871). And the six commandments fulfilled in words: fear (in the psalms), love (at *ahavat olam*), blessing, unity (at the Shema), the priestly blessing, and handing over the soul (lines 3905-3908).

**Four repairs.** צלותא איהי תקונין מתקנן דמתתקנן כחדא, ואינון ארבע, "prayer is repairs that are repaired together, and they are four" (line 4163): of oneself (through mitzvah and the offerings), of this world (through the psalms of praise for each creature), of the upper world (through the angels' praise), and of the holy Name (through the Amidah).

**Whispered prayer (fills in ZT2-07).** The angels called "masters of ears" listen only to those דמצלאן צלותהון בלחישו, ברעותא דלבא, דלא אשתמע ההוא צלותא לאחרא, "who pray their prayer in a whisper, with the will of the heart, so that no one else hears it" (line 3893). If a human ear hears it, no one above does. This adds to ZT2-07 (*Whispered prayer*, part2-terumah-1.md).

**Pray before you eat.** One who eats before praying is like a diviner: at night the soul went up, and on waking only the life of the blood remains in charge. וכד יצלי בר נש צלותא דפולחנא דמאריה, כדין מתיישבא חילא דדמא באתריה, "when a person prays the service of his Master, the life of the blood settles in its place" (line 4159), and the soul takes its seat.

**Blessing after food with joy.** ולא ישתכח עציב, אלא דיברך בחדוה, "he must not be sad, but bless with joy" (line 4214) and with a good eye, as one giving. Adds to ZT2-08 (*The table as an altar*).

**Silence at the Torah reading.** When the scroll is raised, all stand in awe כמה דהשתא קיימין על טורא דסיני לקבלא אורייתא, "as if they now stood at Mount Sinai to receive the Torah" (line 3977), and no one speaks, not even words of Torah. Before it, the prayer *Brich Shmei*: לא על אינש רחיצנא, ולא על בר אלהין סמיכנא, אלא באלהא דשמיא, "not on man do I trust, nor on any angel do I lean, but on the God of heaven" (line 3979).

**Friday night witness.** Say *Vayechulu* as a witness, בעי בר נש למסהד סהדותא דא, בחדוה, ברעותא דלבא, "a person must bear this witness with joy and the will of the heart" (line 4007); whoever does, it atones for his sins.

**Night.** בליליא ישוי רעותיה, דהא אתפטר מעלמא דא, "at night let him set his will that he is taking leave of this world" (line 4121) and giving his soul back to the Master of all. Supports the entrusting at night in part2-terumah-1.md.

**The poor man at the door.** דההוא מסכנא דורונא דקודשא בריך הוא הוי, דשדר ליה, "that poor man is a gift the Holy One sent him" (line 3815); receive him with a good face. Give from what is yours: לחם לא כתיב, אלא לחמך. ההוא דילך מממונך, "it does not say 'bread' but 'your bread,' what is yours, from your own money" (line 3817), not what was taken wrongly.

## 5. The big human questions

**Is it in my power to bring God near?** "If you say this is not in a person's power": מאן דיתרעי לביה, ימשיך לה לשכינתא לגביה, "whoever's heart is willing will draw the Shekhinah to himself" (line 3826). The will rises first to the heart, ההוא רעותא סליק בקדמיתא על לבא (line 3825), then to all the limbs, and together they draw her radiance.

**Does my prayer get through?** דהא צלותא דבר נש בקעת אוירין, בקעת רקיעין, פתחת פתחין, "a person's prayer splits the air, splits the heavens, opens doors" (line 3877). The Zohar then follows it gate by gate. The prayer of those in trouble, "from a broken heart," is kissed by the angel of the south, who says: "may the Holy One pity you" (line 3886).

**Is the soul after death only punished?** The soul is washed in the river of fire like a salamander-cloth that is cleaned only in fire. When it is bruised by that fire, God sends the "sun of righteousness with healing in its wings," ומטא לההיא נשמתא ואתסיאת, "and it reaches that soul and it is healed" (line 4085). The garments of the soul in the upper Garden are made not from deeds but from will: אלין לא תליין אלא ברעותא דרוחא, "these depend only on the will of the spirit" (line 4063), the intention of the heart in Torah and prayer. The soul there longs for the light כהאי בר נש דתאיב למשתי בתאיבו למיא, "like a person who longs with thirst to drink water" (line 4068).

**Who carries our pain?** In the Garden is a "palace of the sick." The Messiah enters, calls all the pains of Israel onto himself, ואלמלא דאיהו אקיל מעלייהו דישראל, ונטיל עליה, "and if he did not lighten them from Israel and take them on himself," no one could bear them (line 4090). **(ours)** The guide can use the image carefully: the suffering of the world is carried, not only suffered.

**Does the way of the righteous change the place?** "The path of the righteous is like the light of dawn," כאור נגה: דנהיר ואזיל, ולא אתחשך, "shining and going on, not darkening" (line 4145). A path (*orach*) is where no one walked before; even a common road becomes new when the righteous walk it, דשכינתא אזלא בההוא אתר, מה דלא הוות מקדמת דנא, "for the Shekhinah walks in that place, as she did not before" (line 4148).

**Is love or fear higher?** Service from fear is precious but does not rise to cleave above; love does. דהא לית מאן דשליט על דרגא דיראה, אלא אהבה, "for nothing rules over the level of fear except love" (line 4172).

**When should I bless my soul?** "Bless the Lord, my soul": עד לא תפוק מן עלמא, בעוד דאנת אשתכחת עם גופא, "before you leave the world, while you are still with the body" (line 3851).

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **Rav Hamnuna Sava's Shema (lines 4173-4182)** is the deepest meditation in volume 2 and should be offered as a full practice.
2. **"Ask, for this is the hour of will," then give over the soul, and He calls you "Peace" (lines 3866-3870)** gives the guide a shape for the end of prayer.
3. **"The firmament with no color that brings out all lights" (line 4116)** is a strong image for Ein Sof.
4. **"All sadness and anger are forgotten" (line 3938)** and **"the soul is new each morning" (line 4127)** are for the heavy-hearted.
5. **Jonah's four questions (lines 3837-3838)** make an evening look-back anyone can use.
6. **"Broken upon broken, for Him" (lines 3821, 4209)** meets the person who feels broken: God's vessels are broken ones.

Proposed for the map (details in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`): ZS2-20 *Given over*; techniques ZT2-11 *Rav Hamnuna Sava's Shema* and ZT2-12 *Jonah's four questions*. Added sources to ZS2-13, ZT2-07 and ZT2-08.

Set aside: the angel of death among women at funerals and the advice to turn away from them (lines 3780-3796), the harsh passage on burial of those who damaged the covenant (lines 4137-4143), the daily reading of the incense passage as protection from sorcery and plague (lines 4222-4230; the guide may mention the custom but not promise results), the star and hidden-fire imagery of Shabbat eve (lines 3913-3927), the fingernails at Havdalah (lines 4021-4028), and the land and nations passages (lines 4041-4045). The "palace of the sick" is kept only as an image of pain carried.
