Vayigash is Judah stepping forward to Joseph, the brothers' reunion, and Jacob's spirit reviving. The Zohar reads "and Judah drew near" as the drawing near of two worlds, king to king, and of the soul's levels to each other. Inside it: nefesh, ruach and neshamah as three rungs a person climbs; David who slept "sixty breaths" so as never to taste death and rose at midnight, "living to the Living"; the prayer spoken in a whisper that is always accepted; Elijah's still small voice; the Shechinah weeping for her children; and the command to mourn with her so as to rejoice with her. ## 2. The unity of God

**Redemption and prayer, hand and head: see that all is one.** «אין מפסיקין בין גאולה לתפלה, כמה דלא מפסיקין בין תפלה של יד לתפלה של ראש. דבעי למחזי דכלא חד» (Vayigash 1:9; line 4282): "we do not interrupt between redemption and prayer, as we do not interrupt between the hand tefillin and the head tefillin, for one must see that all is one." Ties: technique 2; F02.

**The soul's levels, one.** «נפש אתקריב ברוח, ורוח אתקריב בנשמה, וכלא חד» (Vayigash 2:12; line 4294): "nefesh draws near to ruach, and ruach draws near to neshamah, and all is one." Ours: the soul's parts are not three souls; the same drawing-near that joins the worlds joins the self. Ties: Tanya ch. 1-2 on the soul (memory).

**Drawing near so that all is one.** "And he drew near": «תקרובתא דעלמא בעלמא, לאתאחדא דא בדא למהוי כלא חד» (Vayigash 2:13; line 4295): "the drawing near of world to world, to be joined one with another, so that all may be one."

**He and no other.** Judgement on Egypt was done in the very form of the other side, killing and taking souls, but by Him: «כי אני ה' אני הוא ולא אחר» (Vayigash 12:12; line 4407): "for I am Havayah, I am He and no other." Ours: even the harsh side has no power of its own.

**The Shechinah goes down with them.** «ובכל אתר דישראל אתגלו, שכינתא אתגליא עמהון» (Vayigash 11:15; line 4393): "and in every place where Israel was exiled, the Shechinah went into exile with them." Ties: S73, S86.

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **Nefesh, ruach, neshamah as a climb.** A person begins with nefesh; «כיון דאתי בר נש לאתדכאה בהאי דרגא, אתתקן לאתעטרא ברוח» (Vayigash 2:7; line 4289): "once a person comes to be purified at this level, he is made ready to be crowned with ruach"; and when he rises in both and enters his Master's service, «כדין שריא עליה נשמה» (Vayigash 2:8; line 4290): "then neshamah dwells on him." Ties: the map's staged states; C-currents of ascent.
- **Living to the Living.** David watched his sleep so that the holy crown should not find him bound to death at midnight: «חי לגבי חי, ולא נאים בשינתא לטעמא טעמא דמותא» (Vayigash 5:6; line 4312): "living toward the Living, not sleeping a sleep that tastes death." And «בגין דאצטריך לשבחא חי לחי» (Vayigash 5:19; line 4325): "for the living must praise the Living"; «ומאן דאתקריב לגביה, איהו חי» (same line): "and whoever draws near to Him is alive." Ties: S60; the state proposed below.
- **Mourning with her, rejoicing with her.** When the kingdom of heaven is bent low, «אית ליה לכל בר נש, לאתאבלא עמה, ולאתכפיא עמה. ובגין דכד איהי יזדקפא, ועלמא יתחדי, יתחדי איהו בהדה» (Vayigash 11:6; line 4384): "every person must mourn with her and be bent low with her, so that when she is raised and the world rejoices, he will rejoice with her." Ties: S73, S86; Tikkun Chatzot.
- **Weeping by the holy spirit.** Joseph wept for the two Temples and the exiled tribes; his brothers did not weep «דלא שרא עלייהו רוח קודשא» (Vayigash 9:9; line 4369): "for the holy spirit did not rest on them." Ours: some tears are a sign of seeing further. Ties: S73.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Whisper the prayer.** «ומאן דאשמע קליה בצלותיה, צלותיה לא אשתמע» (Vayigash 10:2; line 4371): "whoever makes his voice heard in his prayer, his prayer is not heard." Prayer is not the heard voice but «קלא אחרא, דתליא בקלא דאשתמע» (Vayigash 10:3; line 4372): "another voice that hangs on the voice that is heard." So «לצלאה בלחש, בההוא קלא דלא אשתמע. ודא היא צלותא דאתקבלת תדיר» (Vayigash 10:4; line 4373): "to pray in a whisper, in that voice that is not heard; and this is the prayer that is always accepted," as Hannah, "her voice was not heard"; when it is done with will and intention and uniting His name every day. New technique below. Ties: technique 18 (lips); the silent Amidah.

**Morning holds the right, afternoon the left, every day.** «בצפרא, לאחדא בימינא דקודשא בריך הוא. במנחה, לאחדא בשמאלא» (Vayigash 10:2; line 4371): "in the morning, to hold the right of the Holy One; at Minchah, to hold the left"; prayer and request are needed every day "to be held to Him."

**Praise first each day.** «אצטריך ליה לאקדמא ליה ברכאן בכל יומא ויומא» (Vayigash 10:1; line 4370): "he must bring blessings before Him first, each and every day"; so "my soul longs, even faints, for the courts of Havayah; my heart and flesh sing to the living God." Ties: Vayishlach's "praise, then ask."

**Midnight praise and the day's kindness.** «מאן דמשבח ליה לקודשא בריך הוא בארעא בליליא, רעי ביה … וכל אינון מלאכין קדישין … כלהו צייתין לההוא דקא משבח ליה בליליא» (Vayigash 5:16; line 4322): "whoever praises the Holy One on earth at night, He delights in him … and all the holy angels who praise the Holy One all listen to the one who praises Him at night." Then the day is his salvation (line 4323). Ties: the half night practice (Lech Lecha).

**Grace after meals, shown full.** «ואכלת ושבעת וברכת»: the morning blessing is of prayer and unity; this blessing is of food, to show the level of faith "full as it should be," so that it too will be filled (line 4327). For «קשין מזונא דבר נש קמי קודשא בריך הוא, כקריעת ים סוף» (Vayigash 6:3; line 4328): "a person's food is as hard before the Holy One as the splitting of the Sea."

**Sustaining a soul.** «וכל מאן דזן ומפרנס למאן דאצטריך ליה, וכל שכן ביומא דכפנא. הא אתאחיד באילנא דחיי» (Vayigash 8:3; line 4347): "whoever feeds and supports one in need, all the more in a day of famine, is held to the tree of life." Ties: charity practices (Vayetzei line 3165).

## 5. Big human questions

- **Why are there rich and poor, wise and foolish?** «וכלהו בגין למזכי אלין באלין, למזכי צדיקיא עם רשיעיא, למזכי חכימין עם טפשין, למזכי עתירין עם מסכנין» (Vayigash 7:2; line 4336): "all so that they may merit through one another: the righteous with the wicked, the wise with the foolish, the rich with the poor." Ours: difference is for giving. Ties: S86 (other held).
- **Does evil come from God?** When a person walks to the right, His guarding is always with him and evil cannot rule; when he clings to evil, the guarding leaves and evil takes its chance (line 4348). Evil rules where His guarding has been pushed away.
- **Where is God when the world burns?** In the still small voice: after the wind, the earthquake and the fire, «ואחר האש קול דממה דקה, דא הוא אתר פנימאה דכלא, דמניה נפקין כל נהורין» (Vayigash 8:13; line 4357): "and after the fire a still small voice; this is the innermost place of all, from which all lights go out." Ties: S62, silence states (Bereshit II "Silence that is praise").
- **What is sleep?** Sleep is "one sixtieth of death"; David slept only "sixty breaths" (lines 4309-4311). Ours: a call to wake at midnight, not a sleep rule for the sick or weak.
- **Why the Temple?** «מה צואר, איהו שפירו דכל גופא, הכי נמי בי מקדשא, איהו שפירו דכל עלמא» (Vayigash 9:3; line 4363): "as the neck is the beauty of the whole body, so the Temple is the beauty of the whole world"; when it was cut off, the whole world darkened (line 4367).
- **Does anything start above first?** «מכאן, דדרגא דלעילא לא אתער לעילא, עד דאתער בקדמיתא לתתא» (Vayigash 11:13; line 4391): "from here: the level above is not aroused above until it is first aroused below." Jacob's spirit revived first; then "Israel" spoke.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Whisper your prayer; the voice not heard is the one accepted (lines 4371-4373).**
2. **Nefesh, ruach, neshamah as a climb, and all one (lines 4289-4294).**
3. **Living to the Living (lines 4312, 4325).** Why praise wakes the soul.
4. **Mourn with her, rejoice with her (line 4384).**
5. **The still small voice as the innermost place (line 4357).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "The unheard voice."** Pray with moving lips and a whisper only you can barely hear; put the weight of the prayer into the inner voice that "hangs on" the whisper; end by uniting His name (lines 4371-4374). Ties technique 18.
- **State, new: "Living to the Living."** Inside: at midnight or early morning, awake while the world sleeps, a sense of being alive toward the One who is life; praise feels like breathing (lines 4312, 4322, 4325). How to enter: rise at midnight or before dawn and say a psalm aloud softly. Near S60, S76.
- **State, new: "Climbing nefesh, ruach, neshamah."** Inside: the felt difference between keeping the body's life holy (nefesh), a spirit that rules it (ruach), and a higher soul that rests on you once you truly serve (neshamah) (lines 4289-4292). How to enter: purify at the level you are on; do not reach past it. Near the map's staged ascent.
- **Technique, new: "Mourn with her."** At midnight or on a fast day, sit low and grieve for the Shechinah's exile and the world's brokenness, knowing that the one who mourns with her will rejoice with her (lines 4380-4384). Ties Tikkun Chatzot; S73.
