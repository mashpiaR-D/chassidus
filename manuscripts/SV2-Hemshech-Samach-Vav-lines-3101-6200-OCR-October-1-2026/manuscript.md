## What this slice adds (one page)

Hemshech Samach Vav, lines 3101-6200 (OCR), runs from Shavuos 5666 to Shemos 5668. It reads like one long argument about who reaches G-d's Essence and how. The answer keeps turning upside down. Feeling G-d in prayer is good. The child who runs after a hidden father is deeper. The plain servant who serves under the yoke, with no light, no knowing and no love, only because he was told to, is deeper still: he is 'not a being for himself', and his work counts as the Master's own hand. The 'no' reaches higher than the 'do'. Toil and even despair inside honest work act like a rotting seed. The lowest things (earth, the body, the plain mitzvah done with the hand) carry the power of the very first, because 'their end is wedged in their beginning'. Three corrections to the current depths. (1) Past 'all is as nothing before Him' there is 'there is none else'. In 'as nothing' someone is still there feeling 'He alone'. The world is real, not an illusion, but it is not true existence. The way down is the servant's self-giving and 'Modeh ani', not more thought. (2) Bittul has a sound. Noise means something still burns. Bittul made by thinking ends when the thinking stops. Essential bittul is silent. (3) Being hidden is not distance. G-d hides by His own will, and a willed hiding is not a hiding. His help is mostly a hand behind the child. The descent goes the same way. The soul's root shines only below, in a body lower than an animal's: the lever is gripped at its lowest end. The guide can take a person down by these steps: hiding, the narrow place, the 'no', the yoke, the 101st time, silence, none else. Then back up: the deed in the hand, 'Modeh ani', the next mitzvah. The insights are our plain English. Every argument, parable and order of steps is the text's own (stated). Links to D1-D14 and the guards are ours.

## SV2-C01 · When G-d feels far away in prayer, and nothing moves you.

*Where:* ד"ה השמים מספרים / זכרתי לך חסד נעוריך, Shavuos 5666 (lines 3253-3278), with ד"ה כי מראש צורים (3733)

1. **A father hides from his small son to test him. A foolish child thinks the hiding is real. He cries, gives up, and soon forgets his father.** (M9) — *how:* The text sets the parable up before applying it: the foolish child's crying is real but short, because he believes the distance. «ואם התינוק טפש הוא, לא יבין שהרחקה זו אינה אמיתית» — *And if the child is foolish, he will not understand that this distancing is not real.* · line 3255 (OCR)
2. **A wise child knows it is a test. He runs after his father on the paths his father walked, seeing only his back, never his face.** (M7) — *how:* Same parable, second child: he does not need to see the face to keep going; he follows the footsteps. «ומתאמץ לרוץ בהם לראות את אביו מאחוריו, ואעפ"י שלא יראה פניו» — *He strains to run on them, to see his father from behind, even though he will not see his face.* · line 3261 (OCR)
3. **When the father sees how hard the child runs in the dark, he turns around with double love. The love was there all along, hidden inside the hiding.** (M4) — *how:* The turn: the distancing was the father's love in disguise; the doubled love was its purpose from the start. «שהיתה האהבה טמונה ומכוסה בהעלם בכוונת ההרחקה» — *For the love had been buried and covered, hidden within the intention of the distancing.* · line 3261 (OCR)
4. **So the walls you hit in prayer, when you cannot feel anything, are that hiding. The wise child knows it is not real distance.** (M11) — *how:* The nimshal: the obstacles in prayer are the father's hiding; the remedy is the wise child's knowledge, not a feeling. «והבן חכם מבין אשר הרחקה זו אינה אמיתית» — *And the wise son understands that this distancing is not real.* · line 3263 (OCR)
5. **Keep going even if your heart is not on fire. Think it through until it is clear that it should be so, and let at least your outer will move toward Him.** (M16) — *how:* He names this 'outer will' and still calls it real service: understanding firmly 'that it ought to be so' is enough to keep walking. «ואף שאין לבו נתפעל בזה ברשפי אש באמת» — *And even if his heart is not truly stirred by this in flames of fire.* · line 3263 (OCR)
6. **All day, hold on to Torah and mitzvos because they are His wisdom and will, even when you feel nothing. It is true even if you do not feel it.** (M15) — *how:* The walking 'behind Him' continues outside prayer: Torah study and mitzvos held on the strength of knowing, not feeling. «ואף שהוא אינו מרגיש, אבל האמת כן הוא» — *And even though he does not feel it, the truth is so.* · line 3278 (OCR)
7. **Distance is how the deepest closeness comes. Like dancers who step apart and then come together closer than if they had never moved apart.** (M12) — *how:* Stated as a general rule with its own parable of the dancers; the far point is the cause of the near point. «שמסיבת תכלית הריחוק יבוא תכלית שלימות הקירוב» — *That from the utmost distance comes the utmost completeness of closeness.* · line 3733 (OCR)

**Bottom:** The bottom: the love was inside the hiding. Not feeling Him is His way of drawing you closer (stated, 3261). **Return:** Down: the father 'turns his face' as double kisses in prayer and as 'face-light' in Torah (3265); in the day it is plain study and mitzvos kept without feeling (3278).

## SV2-C02 · 'G-d has forgotten me.' Can He forget?

*Where:* ד"ה ועשית חג שבועות, Shavuos 5666 (lines 3183-3234)

How can He forget?** (M1) — *how:* A clash of two verses opens the matter. «איך נא'י ואד' שכחני, והלא אין שכחה לפני כסא כבודך» — *How can it say 'and the L-rd forgot me'? Is there not 'no forgetting before Your throne of glory'?* · line 3183 (OCR)
2. **There are two kinds of forgetting. One is just not remembering, because you did not tie your mind to it. The other is when something else pushes it out, the way hunger wipes out the memory of being full.** (M2) — *how:* From the Zohar's 'he did not remember... and he forgot': lack of attention versus an active forgetter. «שהרעב משכח את השבע» — *That hunger makes one forget satiety.* · line 3187 (OCR)
3. **For us the active forgetter is chasing this world's pleasures. It wipes away every light and every G-dly feeling in the soul.** (M9) — *how:* Applied to the person: first one stops feeling the G-dly pleasure in Torah and prayer, then something else crowds it out entirely. «שזה משכח ממנו כל אור וכל הרגש אלקי שבנפשו» — *That this makes him forget every light and every G-dly feeling in his soul.* · line 3196 (OCR)

5. **That light shines in every Jew all the time, in the point of the heart above reason, even in exile, even in sleep. 'I sleep, but my heart is awake.'** (M8) — *how:* It is in the person as the 'point of the heart', the 'with all your might' above reason; it never stops. «הנה אור זה מאיר בקביעות ותמידות בלי שינוי והפסק כלל» — *This light shines steadily and constantly, without any change or interruption at all.* · line 3223 (OCR)
6. **'My image stays in you forever.' Your feelings and thoughts may wander here and there. The deepest point of you is not dressed in anything, so it cannot be forgotten.** (M10) — *how:* The Zohar's seal on the heart: the lower levels go 'here and there'; the point itself stays. «דיוקני, היינו עצם בחי' נקודת המל', בחי' כתר שבה, אשתאר בך לעלם» — *'My image': the essence of the point of Malchus, its crown, 'remains with you forever'.* · line 3223 (OCR)
7. **When the soul's essence shines, there is no weakness and no forgetting. The task is to lift your small, forgettable love into that remembering.** (M15) — *how:* Return: 'tash-koach' (weakened strength) comes from the body; the fix is the essence of the soul shining into mind and heart. «אבל כאשר מאיר מבחי' עצמות הנשמה, אז אין בה תשישות כח» — *But when it shines from the essence of the soul, there is no weakness of strength in it.* · line 3234 (OCR)

**Bottom:** Bottom: the point of the heart above reason, sealed in you, which cannot forget and cannot be forgotten. **Return:** Return: in prayer, raise 'the kindness of your youth', the small love that can be forgotten, into steady remembering; in the day, guard against the pleasures that make you forget.

## SV2-C03 · Why would a 'don't' reach higher than a 'do'?

*Where:* ד"ה נשא את ראש בני גרשון, Naso 5666 (lines 3336-3398)

1. **The 365 prohibitions are tied to the higher letters of G-d's name. Why would 'don't' be higher than 'do'?** (M1) — *how:* The question from 'My name with yud-heh is 365; My remembrance with vav-heh is 248'. «אבל שרש המלת גבוהים משרש מ"ע» — *But the root of the prohibitions is higher than the root of the positive commandments.* · line 3373 (OCR)
2. **A 'do' builds a vessel and draws down light that already fits vessels. It is like a garment cut to your size. A 'don't' builds nothing. It touches what is beyond fitting, like a house that surrounds you from afar.** (M2) — *how:* Positive mitzvos make vessels; vessels draw light already on its way down. Prohibitions are an absence of vessel. «אמנם לבוש הוא כמדת האדם, ובית הוא מקיפו מרחוק» — *But a garment is the measure of the person, and a house surrounds him from afar.* · line 3346 (OCR)
3. **Think of a great king. Small matters do not touch him at all; his soul is too high to be caught in them.** (M9) — *how:* The mashal from the human soul: what is beneath you does not draw you. «וכמו רוממות נפש המלך, שלא תתפס נפשו בדברים קטני הערך» — *Like the exaltation of the king's soul, whose soul is not caught by small matters.* · line 3378 (OCR)
4. **But a thing he cannot stand moves his whole self. Pushing away what he hates reaches deeper into him than doing good does.** (M7) — *how:* The turn of the mashal: refusal reaches the inner self where doing does not. «כי הלאו מגיע יותר בפנימיות ועצמות הנפש» — *For the 'no' reaches more into the inwardness and essence of the soul.* · line 3378 (OCR)
5. **So by the 'no' you know Him more truly: not by seeing what He is, but by knowing what He is not and cannot stand. Knowing by negation still knows the essence.** (M10) — *how:* He limits the claim: it is 'knowledge of negation' only, not revelation; still it knows the essence. «וממילא ע"י הלאו יודעים יותר עצם מהותו» — *And so through the 'no' one knows more of His very essence.* · line 3383 (OCR)
6. **That is why the prohibitions are kept by sitting still and not doing. The strength is in holding back.** (M13) — *how:* The halachic form ('sit and do not do') is read as the mark of their root in strength and withholding. «בבחיי שב ואל תעשהי, בבחי' תוקף הכח והגבורה» — *As 'sit and do not do', as the strength of power and might.* · line 3378 (OCR)
7. **Nothing can block the essence. That is why the prohibitions still apply fully in exile, while many positive mitzvos have fallen away. In the darkest time, your 'no' still reaches Him.** (M15) — *how:* Positive light can be held back by concealment; the essence has no blocker, so its channel stays open in exile. «ע'"ז אינו שייך שום מונע ומעכב» — *Against this no preventer or withholder applies.* · line 3398 (OCR)

**Bottom:** Bottom: the essence that no light reaches is reached by refusal, by the strength to not do. **Return:** Return: in exile and darkness the 'no's and the fences are what keep love above reason alive (3394); the deed is simply holding back from what He does not want.

## SV2-C04 · Is my bond with G-d stronger than my wants?

*Where:* ד"ה וראיתם אותו, Shlach 5666 (lines 3432-3453)

1. **The forces against holiness also have a fierce will, even fiercer. So why say they have no 'shadow of a shadow', no deepest level?** (M1) — *how:* Talmud: demons have a shadow but not a shadow of a shadow; he asks why, given their strong will. «ולמה אמרו בבואה דבבואה לית להו» — *Why did they say: a shadow they have, a shadow of a shadow they do not have?* · line 3448 (OCR)
2. **Because the holy will cannot be otherwise. Your soul cannot be separated from G-d in any way at all.** (M2) — *how:* The difference is not strength of will but its kind: 'cannot be otherwise'. «שאינו יכול להיות נפרד מאלקות בשום אופן» — *That he cannot be separated from G-dliness in any way.* · line 3448 (OCR)
3. **That is why a Jew gives his life when it comes to it, even though he wants to live. He does not choose death. He simply cannot do otherwise.** (M9) — *how:* Mesirus nefesh happens 'by itself'; the text explicitly keeps the person's wish to live. «כי רוצה הוא לחיות» — *For he wants to live.* · line 3448 (OCR)
4. **The other side has many wants, each strong. Many wants confuse and weaken each other. The soul's deepest will is one simple will.** (M13) — *how:* Strength by unity: many wills cancel; one will cannot be split. «הרצונות מבלבלים זא"ז ומחלישים זא"ז» — *The wills confuse each other and weaken each other.* · line 3448 (OCR)
5. **You see this in the deed itself: in the plain doing of a mitzvah, all mitzvos are equal. That sameness is the one will showing.** (M15) — *how:* Details differ in the intentions; in the bare deed there are no levels. «אבל בעצם העשיה כולם שוים» — *But in the essence of the doing, they are all equal.* · line 3453 (OCR)
6. **Against this, nothing stands. Opposites exist only among revealed things, not against the essence.** (M5) — *how:* This will comes from the Essence, and 'against it there is no opposite'. «דנגד בחי' זו לא יש לעו"ז» — *For against this level there is no opposite.* · line 3453 (OCR)
7. **This is why a sudden thought of return comes to a person and he does not know where from. It reaches everyone; whoever wants closeness at all, feels it.** (M16) — *how:* Return to daily experience: unbidden stirrings come from the surrounding light, felt by whoever generally wants to be near. «ולא נודע לו מאין בא לו זאת» — *And he does not know from where this came to him.* · line 3432 (OCR)

**Bottom:** Bottom: one simple will that cannot be otherwise; nothing stands against it. **Return:** Return: it shows in the plain deed (all mitzvos equal there), and in the sudden stirrings of teshuvah and the morning wish to serve.

## SV2-C05 · Where is G-d in my plain, ordinary body?

*Where:* ד"ה ויקח קרח, Korach 5666 (lines 3535-3560)

1. **Decide to stretch out your hand. It moves at once, with no delay at all. So your will has no organ of its own that it must pass through.** (M9) — *how:* From the body: if the will passed down through organs, there would be a lag. «בלי איחור ושיהוי כלל» — *Without any delay or lingering at all.* · line 3535 (OCR)

**Bottom:** Bottom: a will that holds and is not held, the same everywhere. **Return:** Return: every movement of the hand is a small proof; the deed is moved by the same will as the highest thought.

**Hisbonenus (the text's steps, our wording):** 1. Notice how your many wants pull against each other. 2. Look under them for the one you could not trade away. 3. See that it is not a feeling: it is that you cannot be otherwise. 4. See it in the plain deed, where all mitzvos are equal.

**Inside:** Even while wanting to live; quiet, not dramatic. **Lands:** The plain doing of a mitzvah, in which there are no levels. **Extends (ours):** Sharpens D8 (the bottom is a bond): the bond's mark is 'cannot be otherwise', not intensity.

## SV2-D4 · No one left to take credit

**Teaching (stated, in our words):** The plain servant under the yoke is not a separate thing; he is like his master's limb. His only joy is his master's joy, he does not care whose name is on the work, and his work counts as the master's own hand. That total self-giving reaches the Essence. Its bittul is a contraction, but it is not a goal of self-loss; it is service.

**Hisbonenus (the text's steps, our wording):** 1. Do one thing you do not feel like doing, only because He asked. 2. Do not look for light or reward in it. 3. Let it be 'this is how it has to be'. 4. Notice: whose work is it? His, through your hands. 5. Let His pleasure be your pleasure.

**Inside:** Contraction and steadiness; chopping wood the same as handing a cup. **Lands:** The plain task in front of you, at work, at home, at night. **Extends (ours):** Corrects D2 (no one left to watch): the self is not erased but given away; extends D11 (called by the command): kabbalas ol is the self's highest stance, not its lowest.

## SV2-D7 · Past 'nothing but Him': there is none else

## Guards (ours)

- SV2-D4 (the servant) and SV2-D7 (none else) are never presented as self-erasure. The text frames the yoke as *service* and keeps the self as the one who serves. For anyone with dissociation or depression, use DESCENT's guards. - SV2-D6 (despair as a rotting seed) is meaning *inside honest toil*. It is never praise of despair and never offered to someone in clinical depression. - SV2-D2 and SV2-D5 (the 'no', the 101st time) are personal practices. They are never a lever for anyone else's authority. - The text speaks of the Jewish soul. Offering these depths to anyone is the owner's reading and is said humbly.
