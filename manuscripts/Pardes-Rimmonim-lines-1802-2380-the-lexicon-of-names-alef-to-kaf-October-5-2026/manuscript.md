*Range:* lines 1802-2380: Gate 23 (Erchei HaKinuyim, the Values of the Cognomens), letters alef through yud and the start of kaf

---
Gate 23 is a dictionary: every word the Zohar and the Tikkunim use for a sefirah, in the order of the alef-bet, with what it means and where. It is the longest gate and the one later kabbalists used most. Much of it is technical ("which sefirah is 'arm', which is 'thigh'"). But a dictionary of God's names is also a dictionary of how a person meets Him, and scattered through the entries are some of the Ramak's plainest and most usable lines: on love that begins in fear, on God returning like a deer, on the pit and the well, on salt in the bitter, on joy as the fruit of judgment, on the hundred daily blessings, on kindness as what no one forces you to do. This report reads the first half (alef to the start of kaf). The name of the book itself comes from an entry here (line 2112).

## 2. The unity of God in this range

**Ayin: the answer is Nothing.** Keter is called Ayin, "because there is no grasp there at all; to one who asks about it they answer, the grasp of it is Nothing" (והשואל עליו ישיבו לו ההשגה ביה היא אין, line 1911). Ein Sof means "there is no end or limit to His exaltedness, and nothing contains Him" (line 1914). And Keter is "the ceasing of thought, for there thought ends" (כי שם כלתה המחשבה, line 1991), and Sefer Yetzirah's "if your heart runs, return" applies there.

**He is the place of the world.** Chochmah is called "house of the world", "for He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (והוא מקומו של עולם ואין העולם מקומו, line 2060).

**The flame was always in the coal.** How does one light come out of another without anything new? "Like a flame bound to a coal: in the coal's power the flame was there from the start, and nothing was added except bringing the flame out from hidden to revealed" (כי בכחה היתה השלהבת מעיקרא ולא נתוסף בה כי אם הוצאת השלהבת מהעלם אל התגלות, line 2106). Emanation is revealing, not adding.

**One is two made whole.** "Echad" is said of Tiferet and Malchut together: "a male without a female is called half a body, and a half is not one; when they join, two halves become one body" (דדכר בלא נוקבא פלג גופא אקרי ופלג לאו הוא חד, line 1895). The Ramak's unity is not a bare point; it is a union. And "Ein Sof rests His presence only in a whole place, not in a damaged one" (ואין הא"ס משכין שכינתו אלא במקום שלם לא במקום פגום, line 1961).

**Faith is faith in the union.** Emunah (faith) is named from the Shechinah when she is joined to Truth; "and this is the faith of Israel, to believe in this union"; the Tikkunim: "one who unites Him in her, it is as if he unites Him and crowns Him over all above and below" (ומאן דמייחד ליה בה כאלו מייחד ליה ואמליך ליה על כל עילאין ותתאין, line 1960).

**Nothing in the world is outside life.** Sha'arei Tzedek, quoted with approval: "wherever 'life' is said, there is no evil there at all, little or much" (כי בכל מקום שנקראים חיים הוא כי אין שם רע לא מעט ולא הרבה, line 2252); "with life comes good, with death comes evil", and "those who know this secret understand the 'expiring and gathering' said of the righteous".

## 3. States of consciousness

**The deer returns (RK-S17, proposed).** The Zohar on "my strength, hasten to help me": "as a hart or a deer, when they go and move far off, at once return to the place they left, so the Holy One, though He goes up higher and higher to the Infinite, at once returns to His place, because Israel below hold on to Him and do not let Him be forgotten or go far" (מה איל וצבי בשעתא דאזלי ומרחקי מיד אהדרן לההוא אתר דשבקו... בגין דישראל לתתא אתאחדן ביה ולא שבקן ליה לאתנשיא מיניה, line 1909). Inside: He feels far, gone up and away; and I know it is the way of a deer, who goes far and comes straight back, and my holding on is part of why. How to enter: in a time of distance, do not let go; say it to Him: "I am still holding". Near S73 (Exile inside) and S84 (Trust); new is that our holding draws Him back.

**Salt in the bitter (RK-S18, proposed).** The "Salt Sea" is judgment with salt in it. Salt does not take away the bitterness; it makes it bearable: "were it not for salt, the world could not bear the bitterness" (מלחא לא יכיל עלמא למסבל מרירא, line 2351). Inside: the hard thing is still hard; it has not been sweetened away, but something in it lets me carry it. How to enter: stop waiting for the bitterness to vanish; look for the salt, the one thing that makes it bearable today. The map has comfort states; this one is honest that the pain stays.

**Love that begins in fear.** "The beginning of love comes through fear, in the secret of 'his left hand under my head', and afterwards through the right, 'his right hand embraces me'" (תחלת האהבה הוא ע"י הפחד בסוד שמאלו תחת לראשי, line 1854). And "many waters cannot quench love": the kindness that follows does not put out the love that fire began. Love is also "the fire of Gevurah" that wakes affection (line 2126), and Netzach is "burning light, which brings a burning love into the heart of the one who longs" (line 1882). Ties S45 (Lower awe) to S43 (Love like fire). No new id; the order is the teaching.

**Joy is the laughter of judgment turned.** Yitzchak (Isaac, "he will laugh") is Gevurah: "joy and laughter are on the side of Gevurah, and the great proof is the Levites in song" (השמחה והשחוק בצד הגבורה וראייה גדולה מן הלוים בשירה, line 2362), but only "the wine that gladdens, not the wine that makes drunk" (line 2362), and the future tense means "judgment that will be turned into mercy and laughter" (דין שיהפך לרחמים ולצחוק, line 2362). Joy here is not the absence of strength; it is strength that sings. Near S53 (Glad) and S49 (Weeping and joy in one heart).

**Being sought, being held.** Holiness "is always with a person" through the covenant (דאשתכח תדיר עמיה דבר נש, line 2185); "Zeh" and "Zot" are words of pointing, "as one who points with a finger: this". David said "in Zot I trust" (בזא"ת אני בוטח, line 2183). Ties to S84.

**Dryness.** When sin breaks the channels, the Shechinah is called "dry land"; "until Tiferet flows into her and through him the dry is moistened" (ועל ידו מתלחלח היבש, line 2330). And "Eretz" (land) is from "ratzon" (will): "what was dry land became earth", "a land fully willing" (ומה דהות יבשה אתעבידת ארץ, line 2013). Dryness is a stage that ends in willingness.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**A hundred blessings (RK-T18, proposed).** "Israel must complete a hundred blessings every day" (ישראל חייבים להשלים בכל יום מאה ברכות, line 1916). The Ramak reads it as the tithe chain: a hundred from Israel feeds the ten of the Levite feeds the one of the Priest; "if there is no hundred there is no ten, and no ten no one". What it is for: to make the day a steady rising of thanks that feeds the flow from above. How: count your blessings literally, a hundred short berachot over the day, each said slowly enough to mean it. "Baruch", he notes, is "the secret of the upper Source... which is blessed always, its waters never stop" (line 2077). Guard: counting is a help to attention, not a quota.

**After midnight (RK-T19, proposed).** Malchut is "night" when she draws from judgment, and "midnight" when she draws from mercy, "that is, from midnight onward" (בשעת יניקתה מן הרחמים דהינו מחצות ומעלה, line 2287). What it is for: to meet the turn of the night toward mercy. How: rise once after midnight (once a week if not more), say words of longing for the Presence, then learn. The Tanya makes this Tikkun Chatzot "the joy that comes after sadness... like the advantage of light that comes precisely from darkness" (כיתרון האור הבא מן החשך דוקא, Tanya line 373). Guard: not at the cost of health or the next day's duties; the Tanya itself allows once a week.

**Turn the oy into a hoy.** Two cries of grief: "Oy" when the flow has withdrawn all the way up and "teshuvah does not annul the decree"; "Hoy" when it has withdrawn only to Binah, and "the matter still hangs on teshuvah, and teshuvah annuls" (וכאשר נאמר הוי עדיין הדבר תלוי בתשובה ותשובה מבטלת, line 2157). Practice (ours): when grieving, ask whether this is still a "hoy", something teshuvah can still change, and act while it is. Ties to `cheshbon`.

**Be a little ink.** "The World to Come is grasped only by one who makes himself black like ink in this world" (ואין עולם הבא מושג אלא על ידי שמשחיר אדם עצמו בעולם הזה, line 2129): humble, hidden, spent on the work. And "humility is the highest wisdom" (כי הענוה היא חכמה עליונה, line 2364), above even fear. This is a guard on all the techniques: they are for a humble person.

**Kindness is what no one forces.** Sefer HaOrah's definition: kindness is "anyone who does a thing that he is not obliged to do by law, but does it by his own wish and will, with nothing forcing him" (כל העושה דבר שאינו מוכרח לעשות מצד הדין אלא שעושה אותו בחפצו ורצונו מאין מכריח, line 2277). And grace is "beyond the line of law altogether" ( line 2276). And a limit: "an ignorant man cannot be a chasid", for kindness needs wisdom (ולא עם הארץ חסיד כי צריך חכמה בחסד, line 2281). This gives RK-T16 its inner rule: do one unforced good, wisely.

**Silence above, speech below.** Chashmal is "silent" (chash) and "speaking" (mal): above, in the four-letter Name, "there is no speech, only silence"; below, in Adonai, speech explains (כי למעלה בשם בן ד' אין דבור אלא שתיקה, line 2302). Moses asked why R. Akiva died so, and was told "Be silent, so it rose in thought", "for silence is in Chochmah" (כי שתיקה בחכמה, line 2335). Ties to `quieting`, `edge`: at the top, silence is the right answer.

## 5. The big human questions

**Suffering of the righteous.** "Even bochan", the testing stone: Malchut tests the righteous, "and this is the secret of sufferings of love, by which she chastens the righteous" (וזה סוד יסורים של אהבה שהיא מייסרת הצדיקים, line 1815). Not punishment, not chance: testing that is love.

**God in our pain.** "The children of Israel sighed from the work": the Zohar reads it that above "Tiferet sighed over them, for they had no strength to raise a sigh upward" (שהת"ת מתאנח עליהם שלא היה בהם כח לעלות אנחה למעלה, line 1972): "in all their trouble He is troubled". When we cannot even sigh, He sighs for us.

**Tears.** "When the Holy One remembers His children, He lowers" His two tears of judgment "into the great sea" of Binah, to sweeten them and turn judgment into mercy (וכשהב"ה זוכר את בניו מוריד אותם לים הגדול, line 2139). Remembering us is what turns judgment.

**Who belongs?** The Temple mount is called "mountain" because "as a mountain is open to anyone in the world who wants, so this holy place is open to receive anyone in the world who wants" (אוף אתר דא קדישא הפקרא לקבלה לכל מאן דבעי בעלמא, line 2168); the convert comes "under the wings of the Shechinah". This is the Ramak's own warrant for the guide's D28: the door is open to whoever wants.

**The well and the pit.** The Shechinah is a "pit" when "she has no spring and source within her", only gathered water, "and she is in poverty"; a "well" when living water springs from within her, and what springs is the souls of the righteous (line 2047). Ours: a person, too, can be a pit that only holds what is poured in, or a well.

**Renewal and fullness.** Malchut is the "curtain that renews each day the work of creation" (ומחדש בכל יום מעשה בראשית, line 2179). The book's title: the seven streams flowing into her make her "an orchard of pomegranates... full of mitzvot like pomegranates" (line 2112).

**Longing.** Binah is "Asher", "the place that all praise and long for, above and below" (דעילאי ותתאי מאשרין ליה ונכספין ליה, line 2032). Tiferet is "Asher" in another sense, "running and returning, rising to Binah and descending to Malchut" (line 2034). And the redemption comes from Binah, "for the accusers' judgments rise only up to Binah and not into it" (line 2089): there is a place above all accusation.

**Shabbat and sadness.** "Do not let there be trouble and sorrow on the day of our rest", "for trouble and sorrow are from judgment, and on Shabbat there is no judgment" (כי הצרה והיגון מצד הדין ובשבת אין דין, line 2279).

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **Midnight and joy.** The Ramak's "mercy from midnight onward" (line 2287) is the cosmic ground of Tikkun Chatzot; the Tanya turns it into the rhythm of sorrow then joy, "light that comes from darkness" (Tanya line 373). Full agreement; the Tanya adds the psychology.
- **Joy from Gevurah.** The Ramak locates joy on the left, in Yitzchak and the Levites' song (line 2362). Chabad usually puts joy with the revelation of the Essence and with bitul. The two meet in the Levites (in Chabad, the Levites' song is also "ratzo vashov", Tanya line 728): joy is strength turned to song.
- **Ayin.** The Ramak's Ayin is "no grasp" (line 1911): an epistemic Nothing. In Chassidus Ayin becomes a state of the person (bitul, "I am nothing") and the true being from which the world is "yesh". Same word, a real shift from what God is to what we become before Him.
- **Place of the world.** Line 2060 and Chassidus agree; Chassidus adds that the world is not only in Him but is nothing apart from His speech in it.
- **Humility above fear.** "Humility is the highest wisdom" (line 2364) matches the Chassidic ranking of bitul above awe. The Ramak puts it in a lexicon entry; the Maggid and the Alter Rebbe build a path on it.
