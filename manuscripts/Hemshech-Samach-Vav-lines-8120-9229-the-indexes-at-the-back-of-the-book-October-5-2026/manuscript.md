## A method note first

The two ranges before this one treated the subject index as a list and marked every entry *none*. This range does not. A subject index entry here is a headword followed by page numbers, and most page numbers carry a short gloss in brackets. Many glosses are one-line summaries of the Rashab's point on that page: "the Essence carries all and is not found as an existence", "the servant is no existence of his own". Where a gloss states a tie to the unity, this range marks it *stated* and opens the `how` with "Indexer's gloss". Where it names a building block (a soul analogy, a definition), the row is *structural*. Where the glosses are practical or technical (water, the numbers, the altar, angels' names), the row is *none*. The voice is the editors'. A *stated* row here means the index states the tie; it does not mean the Rashab's own sentence is quoted. The page cited would have to be read to confirm the gloss. That was not done in this pass.

In the other indexes (verses, sayings, prayers) a line gives only a phrase and page numbers. Those lines are *none*, except where the phrase itself is a known anchor of the unity ("the whole earth is full of His glory", "He and His life are one"). Those are *structural*, with a `how` that says the line is a locator only. They are useful as a map of where in the book the series leans on each anchor. They say nothing on their own.

## The shape of the range

Almost all of the content is in the first 388 lines. The subject index from מ to ת happens to cover most of the series' key terms: מלכות, ממלא and סובב, מצות, נשמות, נתאוה (the dwelling below), עבד, עצמות, צמצום, קו, רצון, רצוא ושוב, תורה, תשובה. Read in a row, the glosses give a compressed map of the whole series. F05 (the Essence) leads by far, as in the rest of the book. F10 (Torah and mitzvot) is second. Nearly every entry that touches the Essence ends by saying how the Essence is drawn: by the mitzvot, by Torah under the yoke, by the simple servant, by return.

The verse, sayings and prayer indexes are a long run of locators. Their only value for this index is which anchors the series cites and how often. Among the sayings, "Israel rose in thought" (line 8932) and "He and His life are one, He and His vessels are one" (9035) are cited most, about twenty pages each.

## The Essence (עצמות, עצם, עילה וסיבה)

The entry עצמות (8321-8322) is the richest single line in the range. The Essence carries everything and is not itself "found" as one of the things that exist: > נושא הכל ולא במציאות נמצא (line 8321)

The entry takes a side against the Ramak (R. Moshe Cordovero). If Ein Sof were the Essence, the worlds would come from the Essence, which does not change. So there must be a level that is "found from the Essence" and is not the Essence, and creation comes from that. Before the Essence, all is nothing: > הכל אין ואפס לגבי ע. (line 8321)

His ability is "to shine and not to shine", both at once (8321.3, F09). "You" in prayer means the Essence above hiding and revealing (8322). Two further glosses say how the Essence is reached. The mitzvot are rooted in it, so doing them draws it. Reaching the Essence itself is only through the souls of the lower worlds serving with the yoke: > להגיע לעצמות ממש זהו דוקא ע"י נשמות דב"ן בעבודה בקבעומ"ש (line 8322)

The entry עצם (8317-8318) gives the rule that makes this possible. A ray has grades; an essence is not divided, so whoever grasps part of it grasps all of it: > כשאתה תופס במקצתו אתה תופס בכולו (line 8318)

The entry עילה וסיבה (8305) calls the Essence "the cause of all causes" (סיבת כל הסיבות), a cause that does not show itself or clothe itself in its effect. Line 8190 lists מציאותו מעצמותו ("His existence is from His essence") as its own headword.

## Filling, surrounding, and what is above both (8153-8163, 8261)

The index keeps three levels apart. Filling is revealed and surrounding hidden (8157). Filling is bounded and cannot make a something; surrounding is unbounded and the something comes from it. The editors give the three-level structure its own heading: > ממכ"ע סוכ"ע ושלמעלה מממלא וסובב (line 8162)

Even the surrounding light is not the Essence. The entry סוכ"ע (8261) says it is found everywhere and clothes itself nowhere, yet it is "only His name, a ray". Knowledge by negation reaches only the surrounding light, "not the Essence of Or Ein Sof" (8163). The union of the two (8161, 8372) is made mainly by the mitzvot: > יחודם: גילוי סוכ"ע בממכ"ע -- בעיקר ע"י המצות (line 8372)

The same line asks the obvious question. If the two were already joined at creation, what do the mitzvot add? The index gives the question and does not give the answer here.

## Creation (מלכות, נבראים, צירופים, עשרה מאמרות)

Malchut is the source of something from nothing (8150-8151). The power to create lies in Malchut alone, because the Essence is "wedged in the end": > עצמות א"ס במלכות המהווה יש מאין (line 8227)

Creatures cannot exist without their source. They are only a ray, and their nullification is that of the sun's ray in the sun (8215). Divine speech counts as a deed (8332). A new letter combination is drawn every moment (8356), which is F01's continuous creation in the index's words. Human action, by contrast, is "not renewal and not contained in him" (8344).

## The soul (נשמה, נשמות ישראל, נר"נ ח"י)

The glosses on souls are strongly F06. The soul is a being of its own "and yet cleaving and united" (8246). Its root is "Godliness of Atzilut itself" (8247). In its descent, and only there, its root is drawn into it as it is in the Essence: > דוקא בירידתה למטה נמשך בה שרשה כמו שהיא בעצמות (line 8247)

The purpose of the descent is the union of surrounding within filling, and drawing the Essence below (8249). Above, the soul's service reaches only the filling light; below, it reaches the Essence (8251). The souls preceded everything, and all the tzimtzumim are for them (8255): > דירה בתחתונים -- לדור ולשכון בנש"י (line 8255)

## The dwelling below (8256-8257)

The headword is lost in OCR, but the glosses are the entry נתאוה (the desire for a dwelling below). It is a will "without any reason or cause" (8256). All the worlds unfold for it. It is completed by refinement, and by the simple servant who draws from the Essence. It is "for Himself", the Essence revealed below: > לעצמו כביכול -- גילוי עצמות למטה (line 8257)

## The person's side (עבד, קבלת עול, רצוא ושוב, תשובה)

The servant entries (8275-8285) grade the forms of service. The faithful servant serves with love and delight but "does not reach the Essence" (8280). The simple servant serves only from the yoke, and his service counts "as if the master himself worked" (8284). The servant as such "is no existence of his own, only the master's existence" (8275). The yoke is the service of "there is nothing else", to be revealed in the future (8292): > קבעומ"ש הו"ע העבודה דבחי' אין עוד (line 8292)

Ratzo (running) touches only the ray; shov (returning) reaches the Essence too (8409). Return from the depth of the heart reaches the essential delight "as it is in the Essence" (8505).

## Torah and mitzvot (8182-8186, 8478-8493)

The Torah entry quotes "the Torah and the Holy One are all one" (8478). The Torah joins Or Ein Sof with the worlds, and it is the end of the whole intent (8480): > תורה היא תכלית הכוונה -- גילוי אוא"ס בעולם (line 8480)

The index keeps a fine division between Torah and mitzvah. Through Torah the rise is into "the essence of the light"; only through the mitzvot is it into "the essential hiddenness" (8484). The mitzvah entry says the physical itself is made into a mitzvah (8185). Toil in the oral Torah under the yoke, when the bright light of the written Torah is hidden, reaches the Essence (8493).

## Hiding and darkness (צמצום, קו, פרסא, עננים)

Tzimtzum cannot apply to the Essence. It is only "in the outwardness of Or Ein Sof", and after it the light shines as before (8361). The partition between worlds "does not hide with respect to the Essence" (8346). In the line (קו) one feels "there is nothing besides You" (8370). In the cloud the darkness itself shines (8314): > שהענן והחשך עצמו מאיר (line 8314)

## The other indexes (8508-9229): where the anchors are cited

These lines only locate. The *structural* rows mark the anchors the series uses. For F04: "the whole earth is full of His glory" (8643), "as the soul fills the body so the Holy One fills the world" (8996), "He grasps all worlds and none grasps Him" (9032). For F05: "before the world was created there was He and His name alone" (8997), "You are one but not in number" (9038), "no thought grasps You" (9039), "there is one and no second" (8740). For F01: "forever, God, Your word stands in the heavens" (8710), "with ten utterances the world was created" (8894). For F02: "He and His life are one ... in Beriah He and His life are not one" (9035). For F06: "Israel rose in thought" (8932). The books index adds four lines from the Rambam's Laws of the Foundations of the Torah (9052-9055), the philosophical frame of F05: > שהוא לבדו אמת ואין לאחר אמת כאמיתתו (line 9055)

The index of expressions has one line (9183) that reads like the index's own thesis: "all comes to one style, and one root and cause for them". It is a locator for p. שסה, and the index does not say what the "all" is. It is marked *structural* and left there.

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **The Essence is not the source of the worlds.** The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud) speaks of Ein Sof creating through speech and does not draw a line inside Ein Sof. The index states the Rashab's line clearly (8321-8322): the Essence "is not a source for worlds", and creation comes from a level "found from" it. The Tanya does not say this.
- **Surrounding is also only a ray.** The Tanya places sovev above memale. The index adds a level above both and calls even sovev "only His name, a ray" (8261).
- **The simple servant above the faithful one.** The Tanya ranks love above awe and prizes the servant who serves with love. The index grades the faithful servant, who serves with love, below the simple servant, who serves only from the yoke, because only the latter reaches the Essence (8280, 8284).
- **Torah and mitzvah differ in reach, the other way round.** The Tanya (ch. 4-5, 23) makes both a union with the supreme will, and puts Torah study higher because the mind grasps it and is grasped by it. The index ranks their reach the other way: through Torah the rise is into the essence of the light, through the mitzvot into the essential hiddenness (8484). It adds that Torah studied under the yoke holds both advantages.
- **The dwelling is for the souls.** The Tanya ch. 36 says the dwelling below is in the physical world. The index adds "to dwell in the souls of Israel" (8255).

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **All stated rows in this range are the editors' summaries.** They are faithful to the series as the earlier ranges read it, but a gloss like "the world is not a total lie" (8295) is one line with no argument. The page it cites would have to be read to know how far the Rashab takes it.
- **Headwords lost in OCR.** Some key entries (8161, 8256, 8302) begin mid-list without their headword. The headword was inferred from the glosses (8256 is surely נתאוה, 8302 surely עושין רצונו של מקום). The inference is in the `how`, not in the quotes.
- **Merged columns.** Lines 8487-8496 fuse two or three columns of the index (oral Torah, 'beginning', 'purpose', resurrection). They were read piece by piece, and quotes were taken only from phrases that run unbroken.
- **Locators are not ties.** The 74 *structural* locator rows in the other indexes show only that the book cites an anchor. They were given faces so the index can find them. They carry no teaching.
- **F09 is thin.** It is tagged where the gloss joins contraries ("spreads and does not spread", "to shine and not to shine", "above time and place within time and place" at 8446). 'Ratzo and shov' (8409) was not tagged F09, because the gloss ranks the two and does not join them. 'Will and delight' (8415) was tagged F09 only because the gloss says the two "unite"; they are two powers, not opposites, so the tag is weak.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **With the earlier ranges of this book.** Lines 6712-7019 and 7271-8119 mark the first half of this same subject index as *none* throughout. This range marks the glosses. The two methods are not reconciled here. If the index is to be consistent, either the earlier half should be re-read for glosses, or these rows should be taken only as pointers to the pages they cite.
- **With the Ramak.** The index records the Rashab's disagreement with the Ramak outright (8261, 8321). For the Ramak the Essence is the surrounding light, or Ein Sof is Keter. For the Rashab neither holds. This is a disagreement inside the Kabbalistic tradition, and it is kept as the index states it.
- **Is the world real?** Line 8441 says reward and punishment prove "that the world is a real existence", and 8295 that it is "not a total lie". Other glosses say that before the Essence "all is nothing" (8321). These are the SPINE's two knowledges (G02), and they stand here side by side without the text joining them.
- **Was the Essence revealed at Sinai?** The index says both. "I am" revealed "the essence of Ein Sof" (8212). The same line says "the truth of the Essence was not revealed at Sinai", and that everything was drawn in hiddenness. The gloss holds both and does not choose.
