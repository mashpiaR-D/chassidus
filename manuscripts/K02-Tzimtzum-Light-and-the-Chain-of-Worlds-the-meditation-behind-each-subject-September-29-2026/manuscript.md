## S014 · Tzimtzum · צמצום

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F05 The Essence beyond both · F08 Bitul **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** He did not withdraw from the world; He held back His own light so that a finite world could stand and receive Him, and He did it out of love. The hiding is toward us only; before Him nothing is hidden.

**Hold.** 'The contraction and the hiding are only for the lower beings; before the Holy One all is as nothing, like sunlight in the sun' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 6).

**The move.** Open it: His light is without end, and had nothing been held back no finite world could stand at all; so He contracted it, and the contraction is His own power, not an absence. It happened in the light, not in Him: the light held back is simply gathered into its source, as before. The Maggid gives the reason in one word, love: the love for Israel is itself the contraction, and without it His Godliness could not be revealed in worlds. You, here, now: the covering over your world is there toward you only; before Him it is like sunlight inside the sun, though that is only a picture, and He is no sun.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'hidden only toward us'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The relief of finding that nothing was taken away, only held back out of love; and something in you answering, as a face answers its reflection in water. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'ein od milvado'. Then give a coin to charity quietly, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'hidden only toward us'.

**Disagreement kept.** The Alter Rebbe and Strashelye reject the literal reading, Strashelye calling it 'a grave offense'; the Besht's circle speaks of Him 'clearing a place in Himself', and the Rebbe writes that even the literal view does not contradict serving Him with understanding. R. Nachman holds that the void is two opposites, crossed by faith rather than understanding.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:8 (line 794): אך אין הצמצום וההסתר אלא לתחתונים, אבל לגבי הקדושברוךהוא – ״כולא קמיה כלא ממש חשיבי״, כאור השמש בשמש - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 33:2 (line 94): שאהבה שאהב את ישראל הוא בעצמו הצמצום … ואלולי זה לא היה יכול להתגלות אלהותו ית' בעולמות - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of having faith in God 6:1 (line 109): ובבחי' האור הזה הוא שהי' הצמצום האמור ולא בעצמותו ח"ו … והנה האור שנסתלק מלהאיר היינו שנכלל במאור כמו שהי' מקדם

## S015 · Line and impression · קו ורשימו

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding · F01 The world said into being now **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4)

**The glimpse.** Even where He seems withdrawn, a trace of Him holds everything, and a measured line of His light reaches down into each thing.

**Hold.** 'Because this trace contains everything in hiding, it is what lets everything come into the open through the line, down to the end of Asiyah' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 13).

**The move.** The Tzemach Tzedek's picture is writing that was erased, leaving only a trace on the page; a picture only, since, as the Maharash says, a holy thing is never wholly uprooted. After the first contraction such a trace stayed, holding everything in hiding, and from it a line of light was drawn, measured to each world, down to the very end of the lowest one. You, here, now: that line shines in every creature as a radiance of a radiance of a radiance, in this room and in you. The Mitteler Rebbe says the whole of it is only a limit He sets within Himself so that everything can come into the open.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'it holds everything in hiding'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Finding a trace where it looked blank: what was withdrawn was never wholly gone. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips the Maharash's words: a holy thing is never wholly uprooted. Then pour out a few words to Him in your own language from wherever the day feels blank (hisbodedus), in your measure. During the day, return to: 'a trace remains'.

**Thin.** The line and the trace are technical Lurianic doctrine; the tie to the unity is stated by the Mitteler Rebbe and the Rayatz, so this meditation holds only the simplest picture and does not open the rest.

**Disagreement kept.** For the Mitteler Rebbe, understanding the void and the trace in detail is itself the way to the true unity; for R. Nachman no inquiry finds Him in the void, and only faith crosses it.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of having faith in God 6:1 (line 109): התהוות הכלים הנ"ל פ"ה הוא מן הרשימו והתהוות האורות … הוא מן הקו … ולכן נק' רשימו כמו הכותב דבר ומוחקו שנשאר רשימו - The Gate of Unity 13:55 (line 760): כי ברשימו זו להיותה כוללת הכל בהעלם על כל פנים, היא הנותנת שיוכל לבא הכל בהתגלות על ידי בחינת הקו עד סוף העשיה - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:21 (line 1271): וכן, גם ה״קו״ בעצמו המלובש בסיום וסוף נצחהודיסוד ד״אדם קדמון״ … והארה דהארה דהארה, הוא בכל הנבראים ונוצרים ונעשים

## S016 · Light and luminary · אור ומאור

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F05 The Essence beyond both · F04 Filling and surrounding **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 4, 5, 7)

**The glimpse.** The ray has no being apart from the sun; so everything that comes from Him is one with Him, and from His side the world is as sunlight inside the sun.

**Hold.** 'The light shows that there is none besides You, for there cannot be light without a luminary' (the Rashab, Hemshech Ayin Beis).

**The move.** Open the picture: in the sun, the ray is not a second thing beside the sun; it is the sun's own and has no existence of its own. So, and more, His will, His wisdom and His attributes are one with Him, and before Him the whole world is as sunlight inside the sun. But it is only a picture, and the sources say where it fails: it is 'not really in this way, but in a way far beyond our grasp', and above, the light does not pour out by necessity as the sun's does; it is willed. You, here, now: you and everything around you are like that light, held in being by its Source, and what that shows is 'there is none besides You'.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'there is none besides You'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The lightness of a ray inside the sun, with nothing of its own to hold up. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'ein zulasecha', there is none besides You. Then say one blessing slowly today. During the day, return to: 'none besides You'.

**Thin.** The image is stated in Chabad and thin outside it; the meditation uses it as Chabad does and says where it fails.

**Disagreement kept.** Iggeret HaKodesh 20 calls the light 'His very Essence'; the Maharash calls creation 'only a mere radiance'. The entry keeps the tension.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Hemshech Ayin Beis, second night of Sukkot 5670, 676:1-2 (line 2710): ולמעלה הרי האור אינו בדרך הכרח ח"ו מן המאור … רק האור מגלה איך שאין זולתך כי א"א להיות אור בלי מאור - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 10:1 (line 834): כי הזיו והאור שם – עצם אחד ממש עם גוף המאור המאיר, ואין לו שום מציאות כלל בפני עצמו - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 10:2 (line 835): אך לא ממש בדרך זה, רק בדרך רחוקה ונפלאה מהשגתינו

## S017 · Lights and vessels · אורות וכלים

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F09 The unity of opposites · F10 Torah and mitzvah **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 5, 6, 7)

**The glimpse.** The limits that hold His light are not a second thing set against Him: they are His own power to measure, and where a vessel is wholly given over to its light, He and His vessels are one.

**Hold.** 'The contraction and hiding of the life is called vessels, and the life itself is called light' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 4).

**The move.** Open it: the vessel is not a second thing set against the light; it is His own power to hide and measure, and the power to limit belongs to the One who can do anything, so the vessel's limit comes from Him Himself. Because creation passes through vessels, things are many, divided and finite; and a vessel is one with its light when it is wholly set aside to it, while a broken vessel becomes a thing on its own. You, here, now: a mitzvah is a vessel like that, measured, a coin, a candle, a cup of wine, and through it the limitless comes into limit.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the hiding is called vessels, the life is called light'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Limits no longer read as distance from Him: the measure is His, and so is what it holds. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Make Kiddush this Shabbos, or say a blessing with your lips over a cup of water today, slowly: the cup is a vessel. During the day, return to: 'measured, and holding the limitless'.

**Thin.** The pair is technical, and its tie to the unity is drawn only in Chabad and Strashelye; the meditation keeps to what those sources state plainly.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:8 (line 783): והנה, בחינת הצמצום והסתר החיות – נקרא בשם ״כלים״, והחיות עצמו – נקרא בשם ״אור״ - The Gate of Unity 29:28-32 (line 1630): כי כל יכול יש בו כח המגביל גם כן … והיינו דאיהו וגרמוהי חד, מכח דאין סוף עצמו - Likkutei Torah, Matot 2:5 (line 2600): שגם הכלים הם אלקות ממש כי הם בבחי׳ ביטול לגמרי ממש לגבי האור א״ס המלובש בהן

## S018 · Inner and surrounding light · אור פנימי ואור מקיף

**Face:** F04 Filling and surrounding. **Also touches:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 7)

**The glimpse.** His light is in each thing in two ways: measured to it and grasped inside it, and present in it equally but not shown.

**Hold.** 'All of the Infinite Light is called surrounding the earth, although it is truly within it' (Tanya, ch. 48).

**The move.** Open it: the life clothed inside each thing, cut to its measure, is the inner light; the light not revealed inside it is called surrounding, not because it is above in any place, but because it does not show. The Rashab's picture is your own will: it is in your foot as fully as in your head, the same everywhere, while sight is only in the eye and hearing only in the ear; it is only a picture, since He has no body. You, here, now: the surrounding light holds this room and the farthest worlds equally, and there is no place empty of Him, even in the physical earth. It is called surrounding only because it is not revealed; in truth it is within.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'truly within'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Held without an edge: present at the near and the far alike. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'leis asar panuy minei', no place is empty of Him. Then give a coin to charity where you are today. During the day, return to: 'truly within'.

**Disagreement kept.** Breslov sets the empty void between the filling and the surrounding light, crossed only by faith; Chabad has no such void, since the surrounding light is truly within.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:7 (line 681): וכל אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא נקרא סובב עליה, אף שהוא בתוכה ממש - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:22 (line 1272): וסובב כל עלמין בשוה … ו״לית אתר פנוי מיניה״ אף בארץ הלזו הגשמית, רק, שהוא בבחינת מקיף וסובב - Hemshech Ayin Beis, Shavuot 5672, 3:3 (line 11): והנה ידוע דהרצון הוא בחי' אור מקיף … דבזה מחולק כח הרצון מכל הכחות שכולם באים בבחי' אור פנימי בבחי' אור בכלי

## S019 · Limit and limitlessness · גבול ובלי גבול

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F09 The unity of opposites · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 4, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** The limited is as nothing beside the limitless, and yet the power to set a limit comes from the Essence itself and is itself without limit; the measured world is His, not a place He cannot reach.

**Hold.** 'The power of limit and measure comes precisely from the Essence of the Infinite Light' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 15).

**The move.** Open it: between the measured and the unmeasured there is no ratio at all; the measured is as nothing beside it. And yet the power to measure does not come from somewhere smaller: it comes from the Essence itself, and that power of limit is itself without limit, so that even the limitless can come into a measure. You, here, now: a measured day, a measured cup, the exact words of a blessing, each limit drawn by a power that has none. The Rashab adds that unity and many-ness are not opposites in the Essence.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the power of limit comes from the Essence'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Measured things no longer stand against the endless; they are held in it. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the blessing over whatever you eat next with your lips, word by word, no more and no less. Then set a fixed time, however short, to learn Torah today. During the day, return to: 'the limit is His'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - The Gate of Unity 15:53 (line 887): וכן מעצמות אור אין סוף דוקא בא כח הגבול והמדה אשר בבחינת רשימו שבקו - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:3 (line 677): בכדי שיהיו בבחינת גבול ותכלית, שהיא הארה מועטת מאד וממש כלא חשיבי לגבי בחינת הארה בלי גבול ותכלית, ואין ביניהם ערך ויחס כלל - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, Basi LeGani 5737:21 (line 1450): דגם כח הגבול הכלול בעצמותו הוא בלי גבולי ואינו אלא משלימות עצמותו … אלא כשם שיש לו כח בבלי גבול כך יש לו כח בגבול

## S020 · Adam Kadmon · אדם קדמון

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F06 The soul as part of the One **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** The first will for worlds reaches down to the very bottom, so the plain earth shows His creative power more openly than the heavens; the end of everything is set in its beginning.

**Hold.** 'The end of all, Malchus of Malchus of Asiyah, is embedded in the beginning of all' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 7).

**The move.** Of Adam Kadmon we are told only a little and told to go no further: 'we have no business with hidden things', only faith that He and His attributes are one. What is said is that it is still the Infinite, and that its 'feet' reach the very bottom of the lowest world, with the surrounding light shining beneath them; 'body' and 'feet' are borrowed words, Strashelye says, and there is no body there. You, here, now: that is why the plain earth under your house shows His power to make something from nothing more openly than the heavenly hosts do. The end of everything is set in its beginning.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the end is set in the beginning'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Nearness at the bottom: the lowest ground is where His power shows most openly. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'na'utz sofan bitchilasan', their end is set in their beginning. Then say a blessing slowly over bread or fruit, the plain yield of the earth. During the day, return to: 'the end in the beginning'.

**Thin.** Adam Kadmon is a technical stage most sources refuse to open ('we have no business with hidden things'); this meditation holds only the stated point that its end is set in its beginning, and goes no further.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - The Gate of Unity 7:20, 7:24 (line 308): ואחר כך נמשך על ידי צמצום, מלכות דאין סוף להיות כתר דאדם קדמון … ונמצא סוף הכל שהוא מלכות דמלכות דעשיה, נעוץ בתחלת הכל - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:25-26 (line 1276): היא מראה כחה ויכלתה ביסוד העפר הגשמי בגילוי עצום … משום דרגלי ״אדם קדמון״ מסתיימים בתחתית עשיה, ותחת רגליו מאיר אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא הסובב כל עלמין - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 9:6-7 (line 832): וצמצום אדם קדמון, וסוד הדיקנא, שסוד כל הצמצומים לצמצם האור שיתלבש בבחינות כלים די׳ ספירות

## S021 · The chain of descent · סדר השתלשלות

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 6)

**The glimpse.** The long way down, garment over garment until it is dark here, was not a drifting away from Him; it was made for this lowest place, and before Him nothing has changed.

**Hold.** 'The purpose of the chain of worlds and their descent from level to level is not for the sake of the higher worlds', but for this lowest one (Tanya, ch. 36).

**The move.** Open it: level under level, garment over garment, each hiding the light more, down to this world where the dark is doubled. Yet before Him nothing has changed since before creation; the change is only in those who receive, through the garments. You, here, now: the queue, the form to fill in, the long drive, the far end of the whole chain, is the place it was made for, where light can shine in place of dark. The Maggid adds that nothing down here is without a root above, and the root of all roots is His first will to make worlds.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'not for the higher worlds'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The relief of finding that the long way down ends here on purpose. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** In the dullest stretch of the day, say with your lips one line of Torah or a blessing; that is the mitzvah there, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'for this lowest place'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:4-5 (line 487): וזהו ענין השתלשלות העולמות וירידתם ממדרגה למדרגה, על ידי ריבוי הלבושים המסתירים האור … והנה, תכלית השתלשלות העולמות וירידתם ממדרגה למדרגה – אינו בשביל עולמות העליונים - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 50:1 (line 118): אין דבר למטה שאין לו שרש למעלה … ושורש כל השרשים הוא רצון הקדמון שעלה ברצונו לברוא את העולמות - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:4 (line 691): ותכלית כל הצמצומים הוא – כדי לברוא גוף האדם החומרי, ולאכפייא לסטרא אחרא … כי זה תכלית השתלשלות העולמות

## S022 · Tohu and tikkun; the breaking of the vessels · תהו ותיקון / שבירת הכלים

**Face:** F11 Darkness turned to light. **Also touches:** F08 Bitul · F09 The unity of opposites · F07 The home below **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 3, 4, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** What broke did so because each part stood alone; the work is to draw those great lights into vessels that make room for each other, and that inclusion is the unity at its fullest.

**Hold.** The breaking was 'destroying in order to build' (the Rebbe, Likkutei Sichos 35).

**The move.** Open it: in tohu each light stood by itself, too great for its vessel and not including the others; the vessels broke, and the broken pieces became things in their own right, the separate 'something' of our world, while in tikkun each includes the others. Likkutei Torah's picture is a broken jar, which no longer serves its wine; only a picture, since the fallen sparks are still there to be raised. You, here, now: through mitzvot those great lights are drawn into vessels that hold together, so the mitzvah you do today is where that mending happens. The Rebbe calls the goal, tohu's lights in tikkun's vessels, 'the ultimate unity'.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'destroying in order to build'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The sweetness of light that came out of darkness: what broke, gathered back and holding together. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say a blessing with your lips over food today, slowly; the Besht's circle counts raising the fallen sparks, even in eating, as the purpose of a Jew's service. Then do one mitzvah together with another person, making room for them in it. During the day, return to: 'destroying in order to build'.

**Thin.** The tie is stated in Chabad (the Rebbe: tikkun is 'the ultimate unity'); outside Chabad its link to the unity is the entry's reading, so this meditation keeps to the Chabad line and leaves the other moods standing.

**Disagreement kept.** Two moods are kept: for R. Nachman the breaking stays a catastrophe, the source of the husks and the external wisdoms; for R. Chaim of Chernovitz and the Rebbe it is a purpose.

## S023 · The four worlds · אצילות בריאה יצירה עשיה

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F04 Filling and surrounding **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4)

**The glimpse.** The four worlds are not four places but four degrees of one hiding; in all of them it is the same Godliness of Atzilus, and to Him first and last are the same.

**Hold.** 'All their particulars are united with the Essence of the Infinite Light in the four worlds … and to Him there is no difference at all between first and last' (the Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 8).

**The move.** Open it: three great contractions for three lower worlds, Beriah, Yetzirah and Asiyah, while Atzilus is Godliness itself; the little light in each world is still 'without end' to the one below, and can reach it only by more hiding. So a world is a degree of hiding, not a place, and Strashelye says that in truth it is the same ten sefiros of Atzilus in all of them, His power alone, unchanged. You, here, now: this world of doing, where you cook and work, is the most covered, and it is still the same Godliness, only covered. To Him there is no difference between first and last.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'no difference between first and last'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The same light behind every covering: nothing lost on the way down, only covered. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the Shema's 'One' with your lips, thinking that it is the same Godliness in all four worlds. Then do one mitzvah of doing today with your hands, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'first and last, the same to Him'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - The Gate of Unity 8:38-39 (line 389): כולם מיוחדים ומקושרים כל פרטיהם בעצמיות אור אין סוף שבאבי''ע … ואין הפרש לגבי עצמו בין ראשון לאחרון כלל - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:1 (line 688): הם שלשה מיני צמצומים עצומים כלליים. לשלשה מיני עולמות כלליים … כי עולם האצילות – הוא אלהות ממש - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 49:3 (line 690): כי אור מעט מזער המתלבש בעולם הבריאה, עדיין הוא בבחינת איןסוף לגבי עולם היצירה, ואי אפשר להתלבש בו, אלא על ידי צמצום והעלם

## S024 · Atzilus and the screen · אצילות / פרסא

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F12 Knowing the unity · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** Behind the screen, He and His vessels are one; on our side of it things stand apart, yet the outside of the curtain is holy too, and His light breaks through it.

**Hold.** 'Though the curtain separates, outside the curtain too is holy' (the Maharash, Toras Shmuel).

**The move.** Open it: behind the curtain is Atzilus, where 'He and His life are one, He and His vessels are one'; how He is one with His attributes is beyond us, and the Zohar calls them 'the secret of faith'. Created minds could not take that light unscreened, so there is a screen, rooted in the Name that hides; no cloth hangs there, and the word is only a picture. You, here, now, are outside the curtain, and outside the curtain is holy too. And the light that reaches the screen breaks through it with the vessels of Malchus, and shines in the lower worlds as in Atzilus itself.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'outside the curtain too is holy'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Not needing to see behind the curtain: what comes through it is the same light, and faith carries the rest. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** When you pass the ark in shul or the mezuzah at home, say with your lips: 'outside the curtain too is holy'. Then learn one halacha or give a coin to charity, in your measure. During the day, return to that line.

## S025 · Concealment and revelation · העלם וגילוי

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F12 Knowing the unity · F09 The unity of opposites · F11 Darkness turned to light **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** The hiddenness is Him hiding, by His own power; once you know He is hiding there, it is no longer a hiding, even where you did not know you were hidden.

**Hold.** 'If a person knows that the Holy One is hiding there, it is no hiding' (the Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov).

**The move.** Open it: the hiding is His own power, the life hidden in each thing so that it seems a thing on its own; and the same Name that hides Him is how He is seen, as letters cover a writer's mind and so carry it to you. The Besht's picture is a king who surrounded himself with walls, towers and gates that were only an illusion, and for whoever knows it no wall stands between him and the king; a picture only, for He has no palace. You, here, now: the part of your day that seems to run by itself is that hiding, and knowing He is hiding there already undoes it. R. Nachman adds a hiding inside the hiding, where one no longer knows one is hidden; even there He is, and knowing it turns the hiding into knowledge.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'if one knows He is hiding there, it is no hiding'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The walls go thin: the relief of finding what was never gone. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the Besht's line with your lips once more. Then pour out a few words to Him in your own language about the part of the day that feels empty of Him (hisbodedus), in your measure. During the day, return to: 'He is hiding here'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Keter Shem Tov 1:55:9 (line 92): אמנם כ' הבעש"ט שאם ידע האדם שהקב"ה מסתתר שם אין זה הסתרה - Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3 (line 56): שהיה מלך א' חכם גדול ועשה באחיזת ענים חומות ומגדלים ושערים … ואין שום מחיצה מבדיל בין האדם ובינו ית' בידיעה זאת - Hemshech Ayin Beis, second night of Sukkot 5670, 675:4 (line 2708): וההתהוות בבחי' יש זהו ע"י ש' אלקים דוקא שמעלים ומסתיר על האור דש' הוי' כו', אך עי"ז דוקא נראה ונגלה גדלות ש' הוי' המהווה

## S026 · Angels and the heavenly hosts · מלאכים

**Face:** F08 Bitul. **Also touches:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F06 The soul as part of the One · F02 The higher and the lower unity **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4)

**The glimpse.** An angel loves and fears by nature and cannot choose; a soul in a body chooses, and by choosing to hold back what pulls it, rises above the angels.

**Hold.** 'Because they have no choice, and their fear and love are natural to them … therefore the righteous are greater than they' (Tanya, ch. 39).

**The move.** Open it: the angels live from the outer side of His life, the letters, and even the highest of them are still a 'something' before Him; their love and awe come by nature, and they cannot choose. A soul, clothed in a body and pulled the other way, can choose, and by choosing to subdue what pulls it, rises above them. The Iggeret HaKodesh says that where ten Jews stand together the Shechinah rests on them so strongly that an angel standing there could not remain. You, here, now: the small choice in front of you today, to hold your tongue or to give, is the thing the angels cannot do.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the righteous are greater, by choice'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The dignity of a small choice. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** When you say Kedushah with a minyan, say with your lips 'Kadosh, kadosh, kadosh', the angels' words in the mouths of people who choose. Then make one small choice against the pull today, quietly, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'by choice'.

**Thin.** Angels serve here mostly as a foil, showing by contrast what a soul in a body can do; the meditation keeps to that and claims nothing about angels beyond the entry's lines.

**Disagreement kept.** Piaseczno holds that the angels' self-nullifying service is holier than man's choice, yet man, choosing, is greater; Tanya 39 puts the righteous above them because of choice.
