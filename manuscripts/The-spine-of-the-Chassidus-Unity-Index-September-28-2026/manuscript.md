**The index's claim:** Chassidus is not 220 books but one idea in 220 ways. This file sets out what that idea is, according to the Rebbeim themselves, the twelve faces it takes across the corpus, and the grammar by which any subject is shown to be one of those faces. It also says where the claim is the Rebbeim's own, where it is ours, and where it is thin.

## 1. The thesis

> Chassidus is one point: the Essence of God (Ein Sof, atzmus), drawn down and revealed as it is present in everything. Put as unity, the point reads: 'there is nothing besides Him', true even in the lowest earth, and that is His dwelling below. The point is not one subject among others. It is separate from every subject and so present in every subject down to the smallest, and it shows most fully at the lowest point (the animal soul, the physical world, the last generation).

## Precision, before anything else

- Unity here is the Essence's own unity, and it is neither the many added up into one nor the world erased. It has rungs: the lower unity (the world is real and is given being each moment), the higher unity (before Him it counts for nothing), and above both, before Him as He is in Himself, nothing made at all. For Him as He is, the truest word is 'yachid', alone, beyond any relation to parts. The Rebbe distinguishes it from 'echad', which unifies parts (Inyanah 5:5). The Rayatz reads 'Havayah echad' as 'the essential One whose meaning is yachid' (Limud HaChassidus p. 2). The Mitteler Rebbe puts yachid above 'the joining of separate things called echad' (Gate of Unity 10:12-13). The Tzemach Tzedek says the Essence may be called only yachid (Sefer HaArachim vol. 9, line 3912). Yet the Shema says 'echad', not 'yachid', on purpose. Its news is that the many, and the world's own existence, are one with Him while they stand, and only the Essence, which bears opposites, can do that (Sefer HaArachim vol. 9, lines 749, 356, 1205, 1884). Even 'yachid' is said of Him only from our side (Strashelye, Second Gate 27:7). So 'the unity of God' is a faithful name for the spine when it means the Essence present as itself in all things and one with their very existence. - The claim that all of Chassidus is one point is stated explicitly by the Lubavitcher Rebbe (Inyanah 2:1-3, 1965). The Mitteler Rebbe says it of his own book: 'the unity of Godliness in every single detail' of the chain of worlds. The Rayatz, citing the Maharash and the Mitteler Rebbe, calls that book 'the key to the teaching of Chassidus'. The claim for the whole corpus therefore rests on the Rebbe's statement, with the Mitteler Rebbe and the Rayatz as its warrant. Nothing earlier states it for the whole corpus. - Each Rebbe names the point from a different side: the unity in every detail (Mitteler Rebbe); the true unity that is His dwelling below (Alter Rebbe); vitality, 'Chassidus is chayus' (Tzemach Tzedek); the Essence, 'Anochi', beyond mere radiance (Maharash); knowing God so as to love and fear Him (Rashab); joining form and matter (Rayatz); the Ein Sof point that permeates everything (the Rebbe). The Rebbe himself says the earlier definitions name branches and are still true, because the essential appears in every branch (Inyanah 18:4-19:3). That reconciliation is his reading. It is recorded here, not assumed.

## How each Rebbe states it

**Mitteler Rebbe.** His Shaar HaYichud is about 'the unity of Godliness in every single detail'. The whole task of a person is to join every detail, head to foot, to the simple unity, and to reach it in the lowest physical thing.

> **The Gate of Unity, Introduction 1** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 1)
> מהות הקונטרס הלז מדבר בענין היחוד אלקות בכל פרט ופרט בסדר ההשתלשלות
> — The Mitteler Rebbe defines his book (which the Rayatz calls the key to all Chassidus) as the unity of Godliness in every single detail.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:10** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 296)
> הנה זה כל האדם בהתבוננות כל פרטי פרטיות מראש לסוף, לחבר הכל ליחוד הכללי דאחדות הפשוט הנקרא ראשון
> — The whole task of a person: to join every detail, head to foot, to the general unity of the simple One.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:12** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 298)
> וזהו עיקר קוטב היסוד המוסד בהתבוננות הפרט דוקא, שיומשך הכל אל הכלל
> — The axis of contemplating a particular is that everything be drawn to the whole.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:44** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 330)
> עד שברגע א' ממש בהתבונן בנפשו בסוף הכל
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: train until, in one moment, contemplating the lowest physical thing…

> **The Gate of Unity 7:46** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 332)

**Rayatz (from the Maharash and Mitteler Rebbe).** Shaar HaYichud is the key to the teaching of Chassidus.

> **Kuntres Limud HaChassidus, printed p. 30** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Limud HaChassidus; line 1539 · *OCR*)

## Limits of the thesis

- The thesis is a Chabad claim about Chabad Chassidus. Of the 220 works, 96 are outside Chabad. There the one idea is present, strongly so in the founding circle (see faces F01, F03 and F09), but it is often not the organizing center. Breslov's emunah and hisbodedus, Izhbitz's will of God and the Polish doctrine of the tzaddik are organized around other centers. This index does not claim that they are organized around this one. - The Rashab warns against those who engage only in 'the deep matters', the Essence and what precedes the tzimtzum: they 'turn things upside down' and coarsen them with the selfhood of comprehension (Kuntres HaTefillah, anchor G21). An index built on a single idea runs exactly this risk. The grammar below is meant to work subject by subject, not to repeat a slogan.

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 18 ¶2** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 73 · *OCR*)
> יתאונן על העוסקים בענינים עמוקים דוקא. והוא לפיבת העדר התמסרותם לעיין בעומק דברי רז"ל ומהם עוד מהפכים הדברים ומגשיטים בישות דהשגה
> — The Rashab's warning: those who engage only in the deepest matters (the Essence, before the tzimtzum) turn things upside down and make them coarse with the selfhood of comprehension (OCR).

## F01 · The world said into being now

**לעולם ה׳ דברך נצב בשמים · מהוה · יש מאין בכל רגע · השגחה פרטית**

Every created thing, stone and dust included, exists only because the letters of God's speech stand in it at this moment and continuously bring it from nothing into being. If they left for an instant it would return to absolute nothing. Providence is this same continuous creation, seen in each particular.

*Named by the sources as:* The Besht's reading of 'Forever, O God, Your word stands in the heavens' (SH 1); 'the power of the maker in the made' (Maggid); 'Havayah' as present-continuous (SH 4); 'renews the world every moment in His particular providence' (Hayom Yom).

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:3** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 757)
> ופירש הבעל שם טוב זכרונו לברכה … כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש
> — The Besht via SH 1: the words of 'Let there be a firmament' stand in the heavens now; if they left for an instant, nothing.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:4** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 758)

> — Only the yechidah feels this simply; the lower soul-levels feel it as a novelty.

> — The error it corrects: the craftsman model of creation (and with it the denial of providence).

> — Providence extends: each particular movement bears on the whole purpose of creation.

**Where the sources pull apart.** The Rebbe adds a distinction: only the yechidah feels continuous creation simply. The lower soul-levels (nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah) live inside worlds, so to them it is a novelty they must be reminded of (F01h). This is the reason the idea needs contemplation at all.

**Thin or forced?** None. This face is stated in so many words by the Besht, the Maggid, the Alter Rebbe, the Rayatz and the Rebbe.

## F02 · The higher and the lower unity

**יחודא עילאה · יחודא תתאה · שמע ישראל · בשכמל״ו · ב׳ ידיעות · קמיה**

From His side ('before Him', from above downward) the world is nothing at all, not even 'another' thing beside Him. That is the higher unity of Shema. From the world's side (from below upward) the world is a real, separate thing that He fills and rules in time and space through Malchus. That is the lower unity of Baruch Shem. Both are true, and the second exists for the sake of the first.

*Named by the sources as:* The Zohar's 'yichuda ila'ah / yichuda tataah' (SH 1, 7; Mitteler Rebbe 7:30-33); the Maharash's two knowledges (Toras Shmuel); IH 20's 'kamei' versus 'from below upward'; the Tzemach Tzedek's 'commandment of unity'.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 755)
> הוא ״יחודא עילאה״, ו״ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד״ – הוא ״יחודא תתאה״
> — SH opens with the Zohar's pair: Shema = higher unity, Baruch Shem = lower unity.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 791)
> היא אין ואפס ממש לגבי הקדוש ברוך הוא, כי שם ״אלהים״ אינו מעלים ומצמצם אלא לתחתונים
> — Higher unity: from His side even the earth is nothing, because Elokim conceals only toward the lower ones.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:8** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 802)
> וזהו יחודא תתאה … הכל מלא מאור אין סוף
> — Lower unity: His Essence fills the earth within time and space, joined with Malchus.

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1255)
> היינו ״קמיה״ דוקא, שהיא ידיעתו יתברך מלמעלה למטה
> — IH 20: 'nothing before Him' is true of His knowledge from above downward.

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1256)
> אבל בידיעה שממטה למעלה – היש הנברא הוא דבר נפרד לגמרי
> — From below upward the creature is genuinely a separate thing. Both knowledges stand.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:31** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 317)
> יהו״ה אחד, אחדות פשוט ממש שלפני הצמצום הראשון
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: the first verse of Shema is the simple unity before the first tzimtzum.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:33** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 319)
> וכוונה כללית דברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד ביחודא תתאה הוא בחינת מלכות דאין סוף אחר הצמצום
> — Baruch Shem = Malchus of Ein Sof after the tzimtzum, down to Malchus of Asiyah.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 32 ¶3** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 201 · *OCR*)
> דהוי' ואלקי' כולא חד שהאלקי' אינו מעלים ומסתיר באמת על ש' הוו'
> — The Rashab: the mitzvah of unity is that Havayah and Elokim are one; Elokim does not truly conceal.

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:2** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 715)
> ד"ת הוא שלמטה היש ולמעלה האין … אבל בחי' ד"ע הוא איך דכולא קמי' כלא חשיב קמי' דוקא
> — The Maharash: two knowledges. The lower (below is 'something', above is 'nothing'); the higher (all is as nothing before Him), rooted in Anochi.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 34 ¶1** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 212 · *OCR*)
> ורוב הגשמות דעכשיו עבודתם הוא בבחי' הביטול דיחו"ת
> — The Rashab: most souls now serve in the bitul of the lower unity (OCR: הנשמות).

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 34 ¶3** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 214 · *OCR*)
> דבעבודה דיחו"ת בנקל הוא שיפול ממדריגתו ח"ו ויהי' יש גמור
> — Yet the lower unity alone easily collapses into full selfhood, so everyone must touch the higher.

> **Kuntres Limud HaChassidus, printed p. 3** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Limud HaChassidus; line 103 · *OCR*)
> והוי' אחד פירושו מיוחד, והוא אחד העצמי דענינו יחיר
> — The Rayatz: 'Havayah echad' is the essential One whose meaning is 'yachid' (OCR יחיר).

> **Kuntres Limud HaChassidus, printed p. 3** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Limud HaChassidus; line 109 · *OCR*)
> הם בטלים אל האל"ף דמורה על אין סוף ב"ה אלופו של עולם
> — 'Echad' teaches the unity of all created levels, nullified in their source.

> **Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 1** (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; line 119)
> ומצווים במצות היחוד לידע האמת כי הוא אחד וזולתו אין עוד
> — The Tzemach Tzedek: the commandment of unity is to know that He is one and there is nothing besides.

> **Mekor Mayim Chayim on Baal Shem Tov, Vaetchanan 6:2** (Mekor Mayim Chayim on the Baal Shem Tov (anthology, 1938); line 1605)
> וזהו שמשה רבינו עליו השלום שאמר אין עוד, שמו בחללו של עולם
> — Moshe's 'ein od' versus Rachav's partial confession: two grades of the unity (via Magen Avos, a later reading of the Besht).

**Where the sources pull apart.** Who can hold the higher unity? The Rashab says most souls today serve with the bitul of the lower unity and very few truly reach the higher, yet everyone must touch it at least for the hour, because the lower alone 'easily falls' into full selfhood (F02i, F02j). The Rebbe places the higher feeling in the yechidah that every Jew has (F01h, F06c). Magen Avos, cited in Mekor Mayim Chayim, frames it as Moshe's 'ein od' against Rachav's partial confession (F02n). Pillar 01's architecture adds the Yismach Moshe's floor-and-ceiling version; that was not re-verified in this pass.

**Thin or forced?** None for Chabad. Outside Chabad the formal two-unity vocabulary is rarer: about a third of the rate (see coverage).

## F03 · The hiding for the sake of revelation

**הסתר פנים · צמצום · גבורה הכלולה בחסד · אלקים = הטבע · אין מלך בלא עם**

The world's apparent independence is a concealment worked by God's own power (gevurah, the Name Elokim, 'nature'). It conceals only toward the creatures and is itself a kindness. Its purpose is revelation: there is no king without a people who stand apart from him. Once one knows He is hiding there, it is no longer a hiding.

*Named by the sources as:* 'Gevurah included in chesed' and 'Elokim = hateva' (SH 4-6); 'there is no king without a people' (SH 7; Maggid); the Besht's 'if one knows the Holy One is hiding there, it is no hiding' and the parable of the illusory walls; 'the first tzimtzum's intent is revelation' (Rashab via Rayatz; the Rebbe).

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 780)
> כי אם בהסתר פנים, שהחיות מסתתר בגוף הנברא, וכאילו גוף הנברא הוא דבר בפני עצמו
> — Gevurah: vitality hides inside the creature so it seems a thing in itself.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 4:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 781)
> הן הן גבורותיו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, אשר כל יכול, לצמצם החיות והרוחניות הנשפע מרוח פיו ולהסתירו
> — The hiding is itself His omnipotence, not an absence.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 787)
> ולכן הוא גם כן בגימטריא ״הטבע״
> — Elokim = nature: the shield that makes the world look self-running.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 788)
> וזו היא בחינת גבורה הכלולה בחסד
> — The contraction is itself kindness: gevurah included in chesed.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 796)
> כי תכלית בריאת העולם הוא בשביל התגלות מלכותו יתברך, ד״אין מלך בלא עם״
> — Why hide: kingship needs a people who are 'other'.

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:55:9** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 92)
> אמנם כ' הבעש"ט שאם ידע האדם שהקב"ה מסתתר שם אין זה הסתרה
> — The Besht: once one knows God is hiding there, it is no longer a hiding.

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:39:3** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 56)
> שהיה מלך א' חכם גדול ועשה באחיזת ענים חומות ומגדלים ושערים
> — The Besht's parable of the king's illusory walls.

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 100:1** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 216)
> לפיכך עשה צמצם אחר צמצם עד שיכולים לסובלו … כמשל האב שמצמצם דעתו ושכלו בשביל בנו
> — The Maggid: contraction after contraction, as a father narrows his mind for his child's delight.

> **Hayom Yom, אדר ב / כז אדר ב:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 193)
> דער צמצום הראשון איז כוונתו הגילוי
> — The first tzimtzum's intent is revelation (Rashab via Rayatz).

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד. מוצש"ק פ' בא, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשל"ז:19** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1448)
> דכוונת הצמצום הוא בשביל הגילוי
> — The Rebbe (Basi LeGani 5737): the intent of the tzimtzum is for revelation.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 29:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 154)
> שגם כאשר נמצאים אנו בשפל המצב, מ"מ הרי הוא ית' הוא השוכן אתנו
> — The Rebbe: even at the lowest point of exile, He dwells with us; the Shechinah is the one who dwells in hiding.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:15** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 809)
> והבינו ענין הצמצום המוזכר שם כפשוטו, שהקדוש ברוך הוא סילק עצמו ומהותו חס ושלום מעולם הזה
> — The dispute: the Alter Rebbe rejects a literal tzimtzum.

> **Tzava'at HaRivash 137:3** (Baal Shem Tov (and Maggid circle), Tzava'at HaRivash; line 286)
> והוא עשה צמצום ופנה בעצמו מקום לברוא באותו מקום העולמות
> — Contrast: Tzava'at HaRivash speaks of God 'clearing a place in Himself', spatial wording the Alter Rebbe would not take literally.

> **Mekor Mayim Chayim on Baal Shem Tov, Vaetchanan 6:1** (Mekor Mayim Chayim on the Baal Shem Tov (anthology, 1938); line 1604)
> וכיון שהצמצום הוא ענין העלם, אם כן מובן שזה רק לגבי הנבראים
> — A later reading of the Besht (Magen Avos in MMC): tzimtzum is concealment and holds only toward creatures.

**Where the sources pull apart.** Is the tzimtzum literal? The Alter Rebbe calls the literal reading an error (F03k). Tzava'at HaRivash speaks of God 'clearing a place in Himself' (F03l), spatial wording that may or may not be meant literally; that it conflicts with SH 7 is our reading. The later anthology Mekor Mayim Chayim, via Magen Avos, reads the Besht as holding that tzimtzum is concealment 'only toward the creatures' (F03m). The Rebbe says the first tzimtzum was a 'complete removal' of the first light, of light and not of Essence, and that this is what makes vessels possible (F03j).

**Thin or forced?** None. The concealment vocabulary is common to all schools (coverage F03). Only the reason given for it varies: love (Maggid), kingship (SH), revelation (Rashab, Rebbe).

## F04 · Filling and surrounding

**ממלא כל עלמין · סובב כל עלמין · לית מאן דנפיק מרשותיה לבר · עיקר החיות הוא מהסובב**

He is present in two ways that are not the same relation. As 'filling' (memale), He is the vitality clothed inside each creature, contracted to its measure. As 'surrounding' (sovev), He is the light that is not revealed within any creature. That light is not 'above' in place, and it holds all equally. The very 'somethingness' of matter comes from the surrounding light, not from the filling light.

*Named by the sources as:* The Zohar pair from Ra'aya Meheimna (SH 7; Tanya 48); the Tzemach Tzedek in Derekh Mitzvotekha, Mitzvat Achdut HaShem; the Rashab in Kuntres HaTefillah.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:17** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 811)
> איהו סובב כל עלמין כו׳, ולית מאן דנפיק מרשותיה לבר, איהו ממלא כל עלמין
> — SH 7: the Zohar pair, surrounding all worlds / filling all worlds.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:20** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 814)
> ״ממלא כל עלמין״ – היא בחינת החיות המתלבשת תוך עצם הנברא
> — Filling = the vitality clothed inside each creature, contracted to its measure.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 48:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 679)
> ואין הפירוש סובב ומקיף מלמעלה בבחינת מקום חס ושלום
> — Surrounding is not spatial 'above'; it is the light whose influence is not revealed within.

> **Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 2** (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; line 120)
> רק שבאמת מ"מ עיקר החיות הוא מהסובב … אלא ע"י הארת הסובב שהוא כל יכול
> — The Tzemach Tzedek: the very existence of matter (yesh) cannot come from cause-and-effect filling light, only from the surrounding, omnipotent light.

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 10 ¶4** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 44 · *OCR*)
> הן בענין בחי' טמכ"ע אשר כל משכיל יכול להבין הדבר לאשורו נם בשכל הטבעי דנה"ב
> — The Rashab: filling light can be grasped even by the animal soul's natural mind; surrounding light by the divine soul. OCR: ממכ"ע, גם.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:36** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 322)
> שהוא בחינת מקיף הכללי דעגול שמקיף לקו
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: the whole line, head to foot, is itself one detail within the encompassing circle.

**Where the sources pull apart.** The Rashab rates filling light as graspable 'even by the natural mind of the animal soul', while the surrounding light and what is above it need the divine soul (F04e). The Tzemach Tzedek makes the surrounding light the source of matter itself (F04d). That runs against the natural picture in which the lowest, physical level comes from the most contracted light.

**Thin or forced?** Strongly Chabad. Outside Chabad the pair appears at about a ninth of the Chabad rate (coverage F04).

*Contemplated:* ONE-PROCESS.md, step 4, "Held": length, brought down into one particular thing; from ruach toward neshamah; through filling to surrounding.

## F05 · The Essence beyond both

**עצמות · אנכי מי שאנכי · יחיד · הוי׳ אחד (ולא הוי׳ יחיד) · כשם שיש לו כח בבלי גבול כך יש לו כח בגבול · התהוות הגשמיות מהעצמות**

Beyond higher and lower, infinite and finite, 'nothing' and 'something', is the Essence. It is defined by neither, and so it alone can create a 'something' from absolute nothing and be present in the finite. The physical, which feels uncaused, comes from the Essence. Even the whole encompassing order counts as 'one detail' next to it.

*Named by the sources as:* 'He alone, by His power, can create yesh from absolute nothing' (IH 20); 'Anochi is His Essence, not a mere radiance' (Maharash); 'the physical comes into being from the Essence' (Alter Rebbe, as told by the Rayatz); 'as He has infinite power so He has finite power' (Avodat HaKodesh via the Rebbe); 'one detail relative to the Essence' (Mitteler Rebbe).

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 17:2** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 92)
> הכח שבמלכות לחדש יש מאין הוא דוקא מעצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מגדר שייכות לעולמות, ובמילא אינו בהגדרים ד"אין" ו"יש"
> — The Rebbe: the power to create something from nothing comes only from the Essence, which is defined neither as 'nothing' nor 'something'.

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:11** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1261)
> ולכן הוא לבדו בכחו ויכלתו לברוא יש מאין ואפס המוחלט ממש
> — IH 20: only He, whose being is from Himself, can create yesh from absolute nothing.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 7 ¶12** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 46 · *OCR*)
> אני התפללתי עם הסטעגדער, דהתהוות הגשמיות הוא מהעצמות
> — The Alter Rebbe's answer: I prayed 'with the lectern' — the physical comes into being from the Essence (OCR סטענדער).

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד. מוצש"ק פ' בא, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשל"ז:21** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1450)
> אלא כשם שיש לו כח בבלי גבול כך יש לו כח בגבול
> — Avodat HaKodesh via the Rebbe: as He has infinite power, so He has finite power; both belong to the Essence's perfection.

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשי"ט:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1290)
> לפי שהתהוותו הוא מהעצמות, אשר מציאותו מעצמותו
> — The Rebbe: the created 'something' feels uncaused because it comes from the Essence.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:37** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 323)
> וגם הוא פרט אחד יחשב לגבי עצמיות אור אין סוף שלפני הצמצום
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: even the encompassing whole is 'one detail' relative to the Essence of the light before the tzimtzum.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:38** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 324)
> שלמעלה מהיות גם בחינת כלל לפרט
> — The Essence is beyond being even a 'whole' to parts (compare the Rebbe's yachid/echad).

> **Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of the unification of God 2** (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; line 120)
> כי מטבע העלול להתכלל בעילתו בביטול ולא להיות בחי' יש
> — Why: an effect naturally dissolves into its cause; independent 'somethingness' needs the omnipotent.

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:1** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 714)
> שאנכי בחי' עצמותו ית' שאינו בחי' הארה בעלמא, ומצד בחי' זו נמשך להיות אשר הוצאתיך מאמ"צ
> — The Maharash: 'Anochi' is His Essence, not a mere radiance; only from it comes the exodus from limits.

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלה:3** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 713)

**Where the sources pull apart.** Where does creation ex nihilo come from? The Maharash says 'only from a mere radiance', the last level, speech (F05j). IH 20 and the Rebbe say the power to create yesh is the Essence's alone (F05a, F05b). The Rebbe's own formula in Inyanah 17:2 fits both: in practice creation comes through Malchus, but the power in Malchus is from the Essence. He is not answering the Maharash there, so applying it to this tension is our reading. The two formulations stand in the sources.

**Thin or forced?** The term 'atzmus' is Chabad-heavy (coverage counts include the ordinary sense 'bones', so the non-Chabad figure is inflated).

## F06 · The soul as part of the One

**חלק אלוה ממעל ממש · מאן דנפח מתוכיה נפח · יחידה · אב אחד לכולנה**

The divine soul is literally a part of God above, drawn from His wisdom, which is one with Him. Its innermost level, the yechidah, is always united with the Essence, so no impurity reaches it (hence Modeh Ani before washing). Because all souls share one Father, Jews are literally brothers, and love of one's fellow is rooted in that unity.

*Named by the sources as:* Tanya 2 and 32; the Rebbe on the yechidah in Modeh Ani; the Rayatz on man as the 'middle creation', dust and a part of God; Hayom Yom on trials revealing the yechidah.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 2:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 64)
> היא חלק אלוה ממעל ממש
> — The divine soul is literally a part of God above.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 2:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 65)
> דאיהו חכים ולא בחכמה ידיעא, אלא הוא וחכמתו אחד
> — The soul is drawn from His wisdom, which is one with Him (the Rambam's unity applied to the soul).

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 11:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 64)
> מצד היחידה שבנשמה שמיוחדת תמיד בעצמותו ית'
> — The yechidah is always united with the Essence; no blemish reaches it.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 11:4** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 63)
> לפי שכל הטומאות שבעולם אינן מטמאות את ה"מודה אני" של יהודי
> — Hence Modeh Ani is said before washing, even with impure hands.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 14:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 75)
> אין בה שום נתינת מקום לשום ענין זולתו ית'
> — The yechidah gives no room to anything besides Him.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 10 ¶5** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 69 · *OCR*)
> ונשמתו מן העליונים ביותר שהוא חלק אלוקה ממעל
> — The Rayatz: man is joined of the lowest (dust) and the highest (a part of God), and grasps Godliness with a physical mind.

> **Hayom Yom, תשרי / יז תשרי, א דחוהמ"ס:4** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 516)
> על ידי העבודה דנסיונות מתגלה בחי' יחידה שבנפש
> — Trials reveal the yechidah.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 32:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 444)
> שכולן מתאימות, ואב אחד לכולנה
> — Tanya 32: all souls are matched, with one Father; hence 'brothers', the root of ahavat Yisrael.

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:55:8** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 91 · **reading**)
> הצדיק יש לו נשמה חלק אלוה ממעל
> — Keter Shem Tov attributes the 'part of God' soul to the tzaddik; Tanya extends it to every Jew. The difference in scope is noted here, not resolved.

**Where the sources pull apart.** Keter Shem Tov gives the 'part of God above' soul to the tzaddik (F06i). Tanya gives it to every Jew. Whether the Keter Shem Tov passage restricts it or is only describing an example is our reading, so it is marked reading.

**Thin or forced?** Ahavat Yisrael rests on a stated tie in Tanya 32. In the Polish schools the same love is argued from other grounds (see pillar 22), and tying it to this face there is our reading.

## F07 · The home below

**נתאוה הקב״ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים · דירה לעצמותו · תכלית הבריאה · למטה כמו למעלה**

The purpose of the whole chain of worlds is not the upper worlds but this lowest, physical one. God desired a dwelling here, where His Essence is 'at home' and revealed below as above. Nothing changes in Him; the change is only in the receivers. The dwelling is made by our deeds during exile, as the 'something' becomes nullified to Him and a vessel for Him.

*Named by the sources as:* Tanya 33 and 36-37; the Rashab (Eitz HaChaim 1); the Maharash ('below as above'); the Rayatz ('to make it a place for His dwelling'); the Rebbe ('a dwelling for His Essence: a dwelling is where the self is').

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 484)
> שתכלית בריאת עולם הזה הוא – שנתאוה הקדוש ברוך הוא להיות לו דירה בתחתונים
> — Tanya 36: the purpose of this world is that God desired a dwelling in the lowest realm.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:3** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 486)
> קודם שנברא העולם היה הוא לבדו יתברך יחיד ומיוחד, וממלא כל המקום הזה שברא בו העולם, וגם עתה כן הוא לפניו יתברך
> — The dwelling is not a change in Him; the change is only in the receivers.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 488)
> אלא התכלית הוא עולם הזה התחתון
> — The upper worlds are not the goal; this world is.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 495)
> תלוי במעשינו ועבודתנו כל זמן משך הגלות
> — The completion depends on our deeds throughout exile.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 19 ¶10** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 131 · *OCR*)
> אך תכלית בריאת יש מאין הוא כדי לחיות חיש כטל לאין
> — The Rashab: the purpose of creating yesh is that the yesh become nullified to ayin (OCR: להיות היש בטל).

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 19 ¶11** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 132 · *OCR*)
> תכלית חכוונה בבריאת העולמות משום שנתאווה הקכ"ה לחיות לן דירה
> — The Rashab joins dirah and bitul: the lower beings become a vessel and dwelling through bitul.

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק א / בס"ד. יום ב' דראש השנה ה'תשמ"א:16** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 77)
> דירה לעצמותו ית' [וכידוע דענין הדירה הוא מה שהעצם נמצא בהדירה]
> — The Rebbe: a dwelling for His Essence, 'a dwelling is where the self is'.

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשי"ט:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1290)
> הנה הוא דוקא דירה לעצמותו ית'
> — The physical 'something' specifically is the dwelling for the Essence, now in truth and in the future revealed.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 10 ¶2** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 66 · *OCR*)
> להאיר מחשכי עולם התחתון באור תורה ומצות. ולעשותו מכון לשבתו ית' ויתעלה
> — The Rayatz: man's mission is to make the lowest world a place for His dwelling.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 28:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 146)
> ש"נתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו (ית') דירה בתחתונים"
> — The Rebbe (Acharon shel Pesach 5730): the final generations complete the dwelling below.

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:1** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 714)
> בכדי שיהי' בחי' דירה בתחתונים והיינו שיהי' נמשך גילוי אלקות בתחתונים למטה כמו למעלה
> — The Maharash: the dwelling below means Godliness revealed below as above, which only the unlimited Essence can do.

**Where the sources pull apart.** Is the dwelling made by nullifying the 'something' (the Rashab: the purpose of yesh is that it become batel, F07e) or by the 'something' itself being the true 'something' of the Essence (the Rebbe: the created yesh is the dwelling for the Essence, F07h)? These are two emphases of one doctrine, and each Rebbe states his own.

**Thin or forced?** This is the most distinctively Chabad face: 66 Chabad works against 16 others, and a rate eighteen times higher (coverage F07). Attaching it to non-Chabad works is our reading.

## F08 · Bitul: the person's side of the unity

**ביטול · בטל במציאות · יש → אין · גסות הרוח כעבודה זרה · חמץ ומצה**

If there is nothing besides Him, then the root of all separation, and of idolatry itself, is to regard oneself as a thing in one's own right. Holiness rests only on what is nullified to Him. Bitul is the human side of the unity: the righteous turn 'something' into 'nothing', as God made 'something' from 'nothing'.

*Named by the sources as:* Tanya 22 and SH 3; the Maggid's 'reckoning oneself as ayin' and 'tzaddikim make ayin from yesh'; the Rashab's bitul of the higher and lower unities; the Alter Rebbe's 'chametz is selfhood, matzah is bitul' (Rayatz); Hayom Yom: 'how small he is and how high he can rise'.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 22:8** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 301)
> כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו … אין קדושה עליונה שורה אלא על מה שבטל לו יתברך
> — Tanya 22: the root of idolatry is regarding oneself as a separate thing; holiness rests only on what is nullified to Him.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 3:4** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 774)
> ולכן הם בטלים במציאות לגבי מקורם
> — SH 3: creatures are nullified in existence relative to their source, as the ray in the sun.

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 19:1** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 66)
> מחשיב את עצמו לאין ואי"ן נקרא חכמה
> — The Maggid: through awe a person reckons himself as nothing, and ayin is Chochmah, the source of life.

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 6:2** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 18)
> כי מעשה שמים וארץ הי' יש מאין וצדיקים עושים מיש אין
> — Heaven and earth were made yesh from ayin; the righteous make ayin from yesh.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 36 ¶1** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 225 · *OCR*)
> ה"ה מתבטל מישותו לגמרי לפי שעה ומתייחד ביחודו ית'
> — The Rashab: contemplating His unity, a person is nullified, at least for the hour, and united with His unity.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 21 ¶4** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 176 · *OCR*)
> להוציא את החמץ שהוא ישות וגסות הרוח, ולהכניס מצה שהוא ביטול

> **Hayom Yom, אייר / יט אייר, לד לעומר:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 288)
> חסידות איז א ג-טליכער פארשטאנד, דער פארשטאנד וואס ווייזט דעם מענטשן ווי קליין ער איז, און ווי גרויס ער קען זיך דערהויבן
> — Chassidus is a godly understanding that shows a person how small he is and how high he can rise.

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 21 ¶1** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 81 · *OCR*)

**Where the sources pull apart.** Learning Chassidus without the work of prayer can turn into more selfhood (the Rashab, F08h). The Rebbe instead stresses bringing it into action and outward (G11). ## F09 · The unity of opposites

**התכללות ההפכים · נושא אחד · אי״ן המחבר · שלימות עצמותו נושא כל ההפכים · אלו ואלו**

What cannot be joined in created things (fire and water, love and fear, good and evil, forbidding and permitting) is joined in Him, because He is the 'nothing' that joins them. The perfection of His Essence 'bears all opposites'. Only the Essence, which has no opposite, can turn evil into good.

*Named by the sources as:* The Besht: the Shechinah holds good and evil 'in one subject', and evil is a throne for the good; the Maggid on ayin as the joiner; the Rashab: 'the perfection of His Essence bears all opposites'; SH 6 on attributes included in one another; the Rebbe on the Essence having no opposite.

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:17:1** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 24)
> ואיך כוללת ב' הפכים בנושא אחד טו"ר … כי הרע הוא כסא להטוב
> — The Besht: the Shechinah, the true unity, holds good and evil in one subject; evil is a throne for the good.

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 3:1** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 14)
> אמנם בהש"י יש אהבה ויראה כאחת כי הוא המחברם וכח הפועל בנפעל
> — The Maggid: in God love and fear coexist, because He joins them; the joiner is ayin.

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 11 ¶2** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 46 · *OCR*)
> עד מה ששלימות עצמותו נושא כל ההפכים והכל בבחי' יחיד ממש
> — The Rashab: the perfection of His Essence bears all opposites, all in true singularity.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:3** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 789)
> לכן הן מתייחדות זו בזו וכלולות זו מזו
> — SH 6: His attributes are included in one another because they are one with Him.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 20:2** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 111)
> ורק עצמותו ית' שהוא פשוט בתכלית הפשיטות ומושלל מכל הציורים וגם אין שייך שום מנגד אליו
> — The Rebbe: only the Essence, which has no opposite, can transform evil to good; revelations can only fight it.

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:39:2** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 55)
> אבל למעלה בבינה שנק' אלהים חיים הכל אחדות אחד
> — The Besht on 'these and these': above, in Binah, the forbidding and permitting are one unity.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 32:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 448)
> ושתיהן הן אמת: שנאה – מצד הרע שבהם, ואהבה – מצד בחינת הטוב הגנוז שבהם
> — Tanya 32: hate and love toward the same sinner are both true.

> **Hayom Yom, סיון / ל סיון, ר"ח:2** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 347)
> רוחניות וגשמיות זיינען הפכים בעצם מהותם
> — Counter-voice: the Alter Rebbe tells a young prodigy that spirituality and physicality are opposites in their essence; what is a virtue in one is a flaw in the other.

**Where the sources pull apart.** In the life of service the opposites stay opposites. The Alter Rebbe tells a young prodigy that 'spirituality and physicality are opposites in their essence; what is a virtue in one is a flaw in the other' (F09h). Tanya 32 holds love and hate toward the same person as both true (F09g). The union of opposites belongs to the Essence; it does not license blurring them in conduct.

**Thin or forced?** Evenly spread across schools (coverage F09 is the one face with equal rates), but the counted term 'opposites' is generic, so the count is weak evidence.

## F10 · Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual

**אורייתא וקוב״ה כולא חד · רצון העליון · יחוד נפלא · מצוה = עמוד · פנימיות התורה כנשמה**

Torah is His will and wisdom, one with Him, so knowing a halacha is a union 'unlike any other'. A mitzvah draws the revelation of Or Ein Sof into a physical object. Because the mitzvot are His will, they have no purpose beyond themselves. The inner Torah is the soul of the revealed Torah. Chassidus understands every halacha in its essential root and in avodah.

*Named by the sources as:* Tanya 5, 23 and 37; SH 1:9; the Rebbe on Torah's essence being its unity with Ein Sof and on mitzvot as their own purpose; the Rashab on pnimiyus as soul; Hayom Yom 11 Adar II.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 5:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 112)
> והוא יחוד נפלא שאין יחוד כמוהו ולא כערכו נמצא כלל בגשמיות
> — Tanya 5: knowing a halacha is a union with His will and wisdom unlike any other.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 23:9** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 310)
> וזהו שכתוב ד״אורייתא וקודשא בריך הוא – כולא חד״
> — Tanya 23: Torah and God are entirely one.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 23:10** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 311)
> כי אין שום דבר נפרד, כי אם בהסתר פנים
> — Separateness exists only through the hidden face, which is absent in Torah study.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 37:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 496)
> כי בעשייתה, ממשיך האדם גילוי אור אין סוף ברוך הוא מלמעלה למטה להתלבש בגשמיות עולם הזה
> — A mitzvah draws the revelation of Or Ein Sof into physical things.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:9** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 763)
> ד״אורייתא וקודשא בריך הוא כולא חד״
> — SH 1 closes the account of the letters with the same formula.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 3:4** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 18)
> מהותה של תורה הוא מה שהיא "מיוחדת לגמרי באוא"ס ב"ה המלובש בה בתכלית היחוד"
> — The Rebbe: the essence of Torah is its unity with Or Ein Sof, not its sustaining the worlds.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 16:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 84)
> כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת כ"א כל תכליתם הוא – הם עצמם
> — Mitzvot are His will, one with Him, so their purpose is themselves.

> **Hayom Yom, אדר ב / יא אדר ב:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 170)
> הבנת כל הלכה בשרשה ומקורה העצמי ברוחניות
> — Chassidus includes understanding every halacha in its essential spiritual root, and in avodah.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 47 ¶3** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 303 · *OCR*)
> יופים אשר פנימיות התורה היא כנשמה לחיצוניות התורה הגוף, ונותנת בה חיים
> — The Rashab: the inner Torah is the soul of the revealed Torah and gives it life (OCR יופים=יוסיף).

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשי"ט:4** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1289)
> לפי שהם ממשיכים ומחברים אוא"ס ב"ה עם העולמות ממש
> — Mitzvot are 'pillars' joining Or Ein Sof with the worlds.

**Where the sources pull apart.** The Rashab warns that Torah learned without love and awe can become 'a potion of death', and that learning alone moves a person only for the hour (G20, F08h). So the unity in Torah is not automatic. It depends on the learner's bitul, which ties F10 to F08.

**Thin or forced?** Stated in general. The halachic works themselves (Shulchan Aruch HaRav, responsa, novellae) do not state the tie on their pages, so linking a given ruling to this face is our reading, as the Rebbe's own four-cubits example shows (F11e).

## F11 · Darkness turned to light

**אתכפיא · אתהפכא חשוכא לנהורא · רשות הרבים → רשות היחיד · יתרון האור מן החשך · חשך כפול ומכופל**

The unity is shown most fully not by escaping the dark but by transforming it. The 'public domain' of the many becomes the 'private domain' of the Single One, and the evil inclination is 'spiced' into good. The power to transform comes only from the Essence, so the transformation is where the Essence is most revealed. Hence the last and darkest generations carry the greatest task.

*Named by the sources as:* Tanya 33 and 36; the Rebbe on 'I created the evil inclination, I created Torah as its spice' and on doubled darkness into doubled light; the Rayatz: 'the physical becomes a vessel for Godliness'; Hayom Yom: 'the world itself is truly good'; the Besht: 'the advantage of light from darkness'.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 33:9** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 458)
> דאתכפיא סטרא אחרא ממש ואתהפך חשוכא לנהורא
> — Tanya 33: faith in the unity subdues the other side and turns darkness to light.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 33:12** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 461)
> ונעשים ״רשות היחיד״ ליחודו יתברך, באמונה זו
> — The 'public domain' of the many becomes the 'private domain' of the Single One.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 20:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 110)
> מכיון שהכח לתבל את יצר הרע ולהפכו לטוב , הוא מצד עצמותו ית' דוקא
> — The Rebbe: Torah's essence shows specifically in being 'spice' for the evil inclination; transforming evil comes only from the Essence.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 27:3** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 143)
> להפך את החשך כפול ומכופל – לאור כפול ומכופל
> — The last generations' task: turn doubled darkness into doubled light.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 22:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 120 · **reading**)
> ורק כשהכחות באים לברר איזה דבר גשמי שמחוץ להאדם
> — The Rebbe's halachic example: the yechidah shows in the four cubits only when refining something physical outside the person.

> **Hayom Yom, טבת / כה טבת:3** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 65)
> אז וועלט אליין איז באמת גוט, ווייל דאס איז דאך רצונו ית'
> — The Rashab via the Rayatz: Chassidus's exodus is from the world's limits within the world; the world itself is truly good because it is His will.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 16 ¶6** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 129 · *OCR*)
> והבכן הוא הגברת החומר על הצורה עד אשר נהפך החומר לצורה, והגשמי נעשה כלי לאלקות
> — The Rayatz: Chassidus's effect is that matter turns into form and the physical becomes a vessel for Godliness (the text reads חומר על הצורה, apparently inverted).

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:18:2** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 26)
> כי יש יתרון האור מן החשך
> — The Besht: Avram goes down to Egypt; the advantage of light comes from the darkness.

**Where the sources pull apart.** There are two exits, 'breaking and leaving' (the exodus from Egypt) and 'refining and repairing' within the world (Chassidus's exodus, F11f). The sources prefer the second without denying the first.

**Thin or forced?** The four-cubits halacha (F11e) is the Rebbe's illustration and is marked reading.

*Contemplated:* ONE-PROCESS.md, step 12, "The dark place turned", the first step down.

## F12 · Knowing the unity: 'know today and return it to your heart'

**וידעת היום והשבות אל לבבך · לידע · התבוננות · אמונה למעלה מהשכל · שיכיר ולא רק שיבין**

The unity must be known by real comprehension, not only believed. It must also be brought from the mind to the heart and into prayer, until one sees Ein Sof resting in the lowest physical thing. Where comprehension ends, faith holds what is beyond it. The aim is to 'recognize, not only understand'.

*Named by the sources as:* SH 1's question 'would you think there is a god in the waters?'; the Rambam's 'to know' as read by the Rayatz and the Tzemach Tzedek; the Mitteler Rebbe's 'in one moment'; the Rashab's 'all study is preparation for the main thing, contemplation in prayer'; the Rebbe's 'recognize, not only understand'.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 756)
> וכי תעלה על דעתך שיש אלהים נשרה במים מתחת לארץ
> — SH 1: why must the unity be 'returned to the heart'? The question that begins the work.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:12** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 806)
> רק להאמין באמונה, שהיא למעלה מהשכל ומהשגה
> — SH 7: the unity of His Essence and knowledge cannot be grasped, only believed.

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 9:7** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 833)
> והנה, אין לנו עסק בנסתרות, אך ״הנגלות לנו״, להאמין אמונה שלימה
> — SH 9: 'we have no business with hidden things'; what is revealed to us is to believe fully in 'He and His attributes are one'.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 14 ¶3** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 107 · *OCR*)
> ואינו אומר להאמין שיש שם מצוי ראשון, לפי שצריכים לידע דבר זה
> — The Rayatz: the Rambam says 'to know', not 'to believe'.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 14 ¶5** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 109 · *OCR*)
> תחלה צריך לייגע ולהטריה את שכלו להשכיל ולהבין עד כמה שביכולתו להבין, ואת אשר לא יבין יאמין באמונת פשוטה
> — Toil to understand as far as possible, and believe what remains beyond.

> **Kuntres Limud HaChassidus, printed p. 18** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Limud HaChassidus; line 889 · *OCR*)
> גם לפטור מלימוד הקבלה בטענת אין לנו עסק בנסתרות
> — The Rayatz rejects 'we have no business with hidden things' as an exemption from Chassidus.

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 11 ¶5** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 49 · *OCR*)
> כי כל הלימוד והידיעת הקודמת היא כהכנה אל העיקר
> — The Rashab: all prior study is preparation; the main thing is contemplation in prayer.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:44** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 330)
> עד שברגע א' ממש בהתבונן בנפשו בסוף הכל
> — The Mitteler Rebbe: train until, in one moment, contemplating the lowest physical thing…

> **The Gate of Unity 7:46** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 332)
> שבזה ממש שורה בבחינת אין סוף דוקא מבחינת תחלת הכל
> — …one sees that Ein Sof rests precisely there, from the beginning of all.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 17:3** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 93)
> שיכיר (ולא רק שיבין) שאין שום נתינת מקום להיש מצד עצמו
> — The Rebbe: to recognize, not only understand, that the yesh has no standing of its own.

> **Derekh Mitzvotekha, The commandment of having faith in God 1:1** (Tzemach Tzedek, Derekh Mitzvotekha; line 95)
> זו מ"ע לידע ולהאמין שיש אלוה
> — The Tzemach Tzedek: the first commandment is to know and to believe.

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:2** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 715)
> כי הגם שאין אנו משיגים זה בהשגה גמורה, מ"מ אנו מאמינים באמונה שלימה
> — The higher knowledge is held by faith, not full comprehension.

**Where the sources pull apart.** How much can be known? The Alter Rebbe says the unity of His Essence and knowledge is beyond comprehension and only to be believed, adding 'we have no business with hidden things' (F12b, F12c). The Rayatz says the Rambam wrote 'to know' and rejects 'we have no business with hidden things' as an excuse from Chassidus (F12d, F12f). The Maharash places the higher knowledge in faith (F12l). The Tzemach Tzedek writes 'to know and to believe' (F12k). The Rayatz's own resolution is to toil to understand, then believe the remainder (F12e). The positions differ in emphasis; they are not simply contradictory, because the Alter Rebbe's 'hidden things' concerns how He unites with His attributes, not Chassidus as such.

**Thin or forced?** None. This face is the method of the Ein Od path (00_EIN_OD_PATH, earlier work), which is built on SH 1-7.

*Contemplated:* ONE-PROCESS.md, step 6, "It is so", the hinge where knowing visibly becomes meditating; and da'at as the method of every step (ONE-PROCESS.md §1).

## 3. The grammar

These are the recurring moves by which a subject in Chassidus is shown to be an expression of the one idea. Each move is one the sources themselves make. The worked example is theirs.

## G01 · Trace the thing to its source in divine speech

**שמו בלשון הקודש · צירופי אותיות · עשרה מאמרות**

*Move:* Take any creature and show that its Hebrew name is a permutation of letters descending from the Ten Utterances. That name is its life, and without it the thing is nothing.

*Example:* A stone is never named in the Ten Utterances, yet its life comes through the 231 gates of letter permutation until the name 'even' is formed (SH 1:6-9).

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 760)
> ואף שלא הוזכר שם ״אבן״ בעשרה מאמרות שבתורה

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:3** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 757)
> ופירש הבעל שם טוב זכרונו לברכה … כי אילו היו האותיות מסתלקות כרגע חס ושלום וחוזרות למקורן, היו כל השמים אין ואפס ממש

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 1:9** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 763)
> ד״אורייתא וקודשא בריך הוא כולא חד״

## G02 · Hold both knowledges: nothing before Him, something to itself

*Move:* Say what the subject is from above (nothing, not even 'another') and what it is from below (a real, separate thing). Do not collapse either into the other.

*Example:* IH 20: the created yesh is 'as nothing before Him' in His knowledge from above, and 'a completely separate thing' in knowledge from below. The Maharash: the lower and higher knowledges (Toras Shmuel 5627).

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1255)
> היינו ״קמיה״ דוקא, שהיא ידיעתו יתברך מלמעלה למטה

> **Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:6** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 1256)
> אבל בידיעה שממטה למעלה – היש הנברא הוא דבר נפרד לגמרי

> **Toras Shmuel, תרכ"ז / מאמרים / מצות יאכל כו' / רלו:2** (Rebbe Maharash, Toras Shmuel (Matzos Yei'achel 5627); line 715)
> ד"ת הוא שלמטה היש ולמעלה האין … אבל בחי' ד"ע הוא איך דכולא קמי' כלא חשיב קמי' דוקא

## G03 · Show that its concealment serves revelation

**גבורה הכלולה בחסד · אין מלך בלא עם · אם ידע שהקב״ה מסתתר שם**

*Move:* Find the concealment in the subject (nature, law, suffering, exile, the evil inclination). Show that it is His power, a kindness, and there for the sake of revelation, and that knowing this undoes it.

*Example:* Elokim is numerically 'nature' and is the shield of Havayah; the contraction is gevurah included in chesed (SH 6). The Besht: if one knows the Holy One hides there, it is no hiding (Keter Shem Tov 1:55).

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 787)
> ולכן הוא גם כן בגימטריא ״הטבע״

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 788)
> וזו היא בחינת גבורה הכלולה בחסד

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:55:9** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 92)
> אמנם כ' הבעש"ט שאם ידע האדם שהקב"ה מסתתר שם אין זה הסתרה

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:2** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 796)
> כי תכלית בריאת העולם הוא בשביל התגלות מלכותו יתברך, ד״אין מלך בלא עם״

## G04 · Join the particular to the whole, and the whole to what is beyond whole and part

**חיבור הפרט עם הכלל · לחבר הכל ליחוד הכללי**

*Move:* Do not contemplate the unity 'in general'. Go through the details one by one, then join each to the whole chain, and finally see that the whole chain is one detail before the Essence.

*Example:* The Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud 7: 'to join everything to the general unity of simple oneness'; the whole joined chain is 'only like one detail' before the encompassing light, and that too is one detail before the Essence.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:2** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 288)
> בחיבור הפרט עם הכלל דווקא

> **The Gate of Unity 7:10** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 296)
> הנה זה כל האדם בהתבוננות כל פרטי פרטיות מראש לסוף, לחבר הכל ליחוד הכללי דאחדות הפשוט הנקרא ראשון

> **The Gate of Unity 7:12** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 298)
> וזהו עיקר קוטב היסוד המוסד בהתבוננות הפרט דוקא, שיומשך הכל אל הכלל

> **The Gate of Unity 7:36** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 322)
> שהוא בחינת מקיף הכללי דעגול שמקיף לקו

> **The Gate of Unity 7:37** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 323)
> וגם הוא פרט אחד יחשב לגבי עצמיות אור אין סוף שלפני הצמצום

## G05 · Find the highest in the lowest

**נעוץ תחלתן בסופן · כל הגבוה גבוה ביותר יורד למטה מטה ביותר**

*Move:* Look for the subject's root not at its top but at its bottom. The end is embedded in the beginning, so the Essence rests specifically in the lowest physical thing and the lowest person.

*Example:* The Mitteler Rebbe: 'in this very place Ein Sof rests, from the beginning of all' (Shaar HaYichud 7:46). The Rebbe: the created physical yesh is the dwelling of the Essence (Basi LeGani 5719). Hayom Yom 27 Adar II: the higher a thing, the lower it descends.

> **The Gate of Unity 7:8** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 294)
> ונעוץ תחלתן בסופן וסופן בתחלתן והיו לאחדים ממש כשלשלת

> **The Gate of Unity 7:51** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 337)
> אבל באמת קרוב מאד בלי הפסק והסתר כלל באמצע

> **The Gate of Unity 7:46** (Mitteler Rebbe, Shaar HaYichud (The Gate of Unity); line 332)
> שבזה ממש שורה בבחינת אין סוף דוקא מבחינת תחלת הכל

> **Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד, יו"ד שבט, ה'תשי"ט:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat; line 1290)
> הנה הוא דוקא דירה לעצמותו ית'

> **Hayom Yom, אדר ב / כז אדר ב:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 193)
> כל הגבוה גבוה ביותר יורד למטה מטה ביותר

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 19:4** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 106)
> הרי כל מה שהחסידות נמשכת בבחי' תחתונה יותר … מתבטא אז ביותר עצם מהותה

## G06 · Explain by analogy from the soul, and mark where the analogy fails

**מבשרי אחזה אלוה · לשכך את האזן · אין המשל דומה לנמשל**

*Move:* Use the soul's powers (thought, speech, will, delight) as the analogy for the divine. Then state where the analogy breaks. The soul and body are two, whereas the world and its source are not.

*Example:* SH 6:7: 'the analogy is not like the thing at all, for the soul and body are truly separate in their root'. SH 10:1: speaking of sefirot as 'lights' is permitted to soothe the ear. The Rebbe: 'from my flesh I see God' as Chassidus's route to making the hidden part of Torah graspable.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 1:8** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 8)
> "מבשרי אחזה אלוקה"

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 10:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 834)
> לכך ניתן רשות לחכמי האמת לדבר בספירות בדרך משל

## G07 · Add life, not a fifth interpretation

*Move:* Read the subject in all four layers (pshat, remez, drush, sod), then show how the yechidah of Torah enlivens and clarifies each layer from within, without adding a new explanation beside them.

*Example:* Modeh Ani: pshat (thanks for the soul's return, and it must be a Jewish soul); remez (resurrection, since all creation is renewed every moment); drush (return a deposit, since mitzvot are their own purpose); sod (Malchus with Yesod, the Essence's power in Malchus). Inyanah 9:3-19:2.

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 14:2** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 76)
> אין החסידות מוסיפה עוד פירוש – נוסף על הפירוש הפשוט

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 8:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 44)
> לימוד החסידות מכניס חיות בכל ענין וענין (בכל חלקי הפרד"ס שבו) שלומדים בתורה

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 14:4** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 78)
> כי בכל רגע ורגע מתהוית כל הבריאה מחדש ממש כמו במעשה בראשית

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 16:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 84)
> כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת כ"א כל תכליתם הוא – הם עצמם

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 17:2** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 92)
> הכח שבמלכות לחדש יש מאין הוא דוקא מעצמות אוא"ס שלמעלה מגדר שייכות לעולמות, ובמילא אינו בהגדרים ד"אין" ו"יש"

## G08 · Show the human work in it as bitul

**יש → אין · אין קדושה שורה אלא על מה שבטל**

*Move:* Find where the subject asks the person to stop being a thing in his own right. That is where the subject touches the unity.

*Example:* Gassus haruach 'is literally idolatry', because idolatry's root is regarding oneself as separate (Tanya 22). The Maggid: tzaddikim make 'nothing' from 'something'.

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 22:8** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 301)
> כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו … אין קדושה עליונה שורה אלא על מה שבטל לו יתברך

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 6:2** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 18)
> כי מעשה שמים וארץ הי' יש מאין וצדיקים עושים מיש אין

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 36 ¶1** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 225 · *OCR*)
> ה"ה מתבטל מישותו לגמרי לפי שעה ומתייחד ביחודו ית'

## G09 · Show its purpose as the home below, or as its own purpose

*Move:* Ask what the subject is for. The answer the sources give is that it serves the dwelling below, or, for a mitzvah, that it has no purpose beyond itself because it is His will.

*Example:* Tanya 36: the purpose of the chain of worlds is this lowest world. The Rebbe: returning a deposit is binding even against the depositor's debts, because 'the return itself is the purpose' (Inyanah 15:4-16:2).

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:1** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 484)
> שתכלית בריאת עולם הזה הוא – שנתאוה הקדוש ברוך הוא להיות לו דירה בתחתונים

> **Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 36:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 488)
> אלא התכלית הוא עולם הזה התחתון

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 16:1** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 84)
> כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת כ"א כל תכליתם הוא – הם עצמם

## G10 · Show opposites joined in Him

*Move:* Where the subject contains a tension (love and fear, kindness and judgment, good and evil), show that it is joined in the 'nothing' or the Essence, while keeping the tension in conduct.

*Example:* The Maggid: fire and water, love and fear, are joined by ayin (Maggid Devarav LeYaakov 3). The Rashab: the Essence bears all opposites (Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 11).

> **Maggid Devarav leYaakov 3:1** (Maggid of Mezritch, Maggid Devarav LeYaakov; line 14)
> אמנם בהש"י יש אהבה ויראה כאחת כי הוא המחברם וכח הפועל בנפעל

> **Kuntres HaTefillah, scan p. 11 ¶2** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres HaTefillah; line 46 · *OCR*)
> עד מה ששלימות עצמותו נושא כל ההפכים והכל בבחי' יחיד ממש

> **Keter Shem Tov 1:17:1** (Baal Shem Tov, Keter Shem Tov; line 24)
> ואיך כוללת ב' הפכים בנושא אחד טו"ר … כי הרע הוא כסא להטוב

> **Hayom Yom, סיון / ל סיון, ר"ח:2** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 347)
> רוחניות וגשמיות זיינען הפכים בעצם מהותם

## G11 · Begin from the effect; end in action

**מהמאוחר אל המוקדם · להביאו בפועל · הפעולה מאמתת**

*Move:* Creatures grasp from the later to the earlier, so start from what the thing does. An idea is true only when it produces an act. Only through action is the essence of Chassidus grasped.

*Example:* The Rayatz: 'every explanation begins from the action' (Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan p. 8). The Rebbe: 'every matter learned in Chassidus must be brought into practice' (Inyanah 19:5).

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 8 ¶6** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 53 · *OCR*)
> ולהיות כי חוק השגת הנבראים הוא מהמאוחר אל המוקדם, מההשגה אל ההשכלה, מההתפשטות אל הנקודה, ומהמציאות אל המהות, לכן הנה תחלת הביאור בכל דבר הוא בהפעולה

> **Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 19:5** (Lubavitcher Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus (1965); line 107)
> שכל ענין שלומדים בחסידות צריך להביאו בפועל, כי ע"י העבודה בפועל דוקא תופסים במהות החסידות

## G12 · Read every event as instruction and providence

**בכל דרכיך דעהו · כל דבר הוראה · השגחה פרטית**

*Move:* Treat each thing seen or heard as a teaching and each movement as bearing on the purpose of creation. 'Know Him' means 'join': even in physical acts.

*Example:* Tzava'at HaRivash 94: 'know Him in all your ways, a great principle; know means joining'. Hayom Yom 24 Elul: everything seen was an instruction. Hayom Yom 28 Cheshvan: a particular movement bears on the whole purpose.

> **Tzava'at HaRivash 94:1** (Baal Shem Tov (and Maggid circle), Tzava'at HaRivash; line 194)
> בכל דרכיך דעהו. זהו כלל גדול. דעהו לשון חיבור

> **Hayom Yom, אלול / כד אלול:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 468)
> יעדער זאך וואס מ'האט געזעהן - א הוראה אין עבודה און הדרכה

> **Hayom Yom, חשון / כח חשון:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 578)
> אלא עוד זאת דתנועה פרטית של נברא יש לה יחס כללי לכללות כונת הבריאה

> **Hayom Yom, תשרי / ז תשרי:1** (Hayom Yom (compiled by the Rebbe from the Rayatz, 1942); line 492)
> האדם השם לבו ודעתו על כל הנעשה עמו ומסביב לו, רואה הוא אלקות במוחש

## G13 · Take it through force, light and life

**כח · אור · חיות · מוסר · חקירה · חסידות**

*Move:* Any trait or subject can be treated at three rungs: breaking the coarse (mussar, force), knowing the higher (chakirah, light), and joining form with matter (Chassidus, life). Chassidus's mussar and chakirah are of another order, because life includes force and light. Study proceeds from below upward.

*Example:* The Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus, scan pp. 13-17: mussar of Chassidus, then chakirah of Chassidus, then 'the vitality that is the essence of Chassidus'.

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 17 ¶4** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 138 · *OCR*)
> ואחרי כן הוא החיות שהוא העצמיות שבחסידות

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 13 ¶9** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 104 · *OCR*)
> א-ל אחד בראם, ולכוונה אחת לגלות אור קדושת חביון עוזו ית' נבראו

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 16 ¶6** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 129 · *OCR*)
> והבכן הוא הגברת החומר על הצורה עד אשר נהפך החומר לצורה, והגשמי נעשה כלי לאלקות

## G14 · Show Havayah is Elokim in it

**הוי׳ הוא האלקים · הוי׳ שלמעלה מהעולמות יהיה אלקיך**

*Move:* Show that the finite, natural aspect of the subject (Elokim) and its infinite, creating aspect (Havayah) are one. Then bring the Name above the worlds into the person's own 'God', his limits.

*Example:* SH 6:4: 'the two Names are literally one'. The Rashab: this is the mitzvah of unity. The Maggid, as told by the Rayatz: teshuvah must reach until 'Havayah above the worlds becomes your Elokim'.

> **Kuntres Eitz HaChaim (scans, OCR), id 15741, scan p. 32 ¶3** (Rebbe Rashab, Kuntres Eitz HaChaim; line 201 · *OCR*)
> דהוי' ואלקי' כולא חד שהאלקי' אינו מעלים ומסתיר באמת על ש' הוו'

> **Kuntres Toras HaChassidus (scans, OCR), id 15658, scan p. 23 ¶6** (Rebbe Rayatz, Kuntres Toras HaChassidus; line 198 · *OCR*)
> כי עבודת התשובה צריבה להיות עד אשר שם הוי' שהוא למעלה מהעולמות יהי' אלקיך

> **Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:5** (Alter Rebbe, Tanya; line 791)
> היא אין ואפס ממש לגבי הקדוש ברוך הוא, כי שם ״אלהים״ אינו מעלים ומצמצם אלא לתחתונים

## Applying the grammar to a new subject

1. Name the subject's concealment (G03) and its source in speech or will (G01, G09).
2. Say what it is from above and from below (G02). Show Havayah as Elokim in it (G14).
3. Join its detail to the whole and to the Essence (G04). Look for the highest in its lowest point (G05).
4. Find where it asks for bitul (G08), where it joins opposites (G10), and what act it produces (G11).
5. Record whether each link is **stated** (a text says it) or **reading** (we say it). If no step is stated, say that the subject's tie is thin.

## 5. Where the tie is thin or forced

- Doctrine of the tzaddik outside Chabad (pillar 03): no stated tie in the verified anchors. Forced if pressed. - Hitbodedut, niggun, dance, story-telling: no specific stated tie. The general license ('everything seen is an instruction', Hayom Yom 24 Elul) is not a specific link. - The halachic corpus (Shulchan Aruch HaRav, responsa, novellae on the Talmud): the tie is stated in general (Tanya 5, 23; Hayom Yom 11 Adar II) but not on the halachic page. The Rebbe's own halachic parallels (Yom Kippur, the four cubits) are illustrations and are marked reading. - Customs and calendar entries (Hayom Yom), administrative letters (Igros Kodesh), histories and institutional volumes (Library of Agudas Chassidei Chabad): thin. At most the general 'home below / every detail' reading applies. - The detailed phenomenology of states (Kuntres HaHitpa'alut): the purpose is stated (contemplating Godliness), and most passages are about the psychology of arousal (605 of 912 lines are structural). But the tie is not mostly ours: the line-by-line reading found 222 lines that state it in the text's own words (Godly arousal against self-arousal, bitul, the soul as part of God, the bond to the Essence, separation from the One) and only 9 that are our reading. What is thin is the side it is stated from: the soul's, almost never the worlds' (F01, F02 and F04 appear on only 11 lines, mostly as the content of contemplation). Corrected 2026-09-29 from `KUNTRES-HAHITPAALUT.md`; the earlier wording said the link of each passage was our reading. - Partzuf and sefirah mechanics in the maamarim: the Mitteler Rebbe gives the reason to learn them (join detail to whole, G04), but most passages do not state the tie themselves. The tie is stated at the level of method, not in each passage. - The Rayatz's polemical and historical writing (the Tzemach Tzedek and the Haskalah): thin.
