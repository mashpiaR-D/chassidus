This is the first half of the Maharal's commentary on Rashi's Devarim: Moshe's rebuke in hints, the judges, the spies recalled, the journeys past Edom and Moav, Moshe's prayer to enter the Land, the Ten Commandments again, the Shema and its second part, the laws of the chosen place, and "you are children to Hashem." Some passages about nations and women are left out under D28. The unity teaching in this half is among the richest in all of Gur Aryeh.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **He is all, and nothing is outside Him; so the person in His image holds his whole world.** "וכיון שנברא בצלם אלקים, הנה כמו שהשם יתברך הוא הכל ואין חוץ ממנו, כך ראוי הוא שהאדם שהוא נברא בצלמו הוא כולל כל עולמו" (131). "כי כל דבר אלקי אינו מחולק ולא יוגבל" (131).
- **The Shema: He is One now, and in the end all will see it.** "אדרבה, זהו עיקר יחודו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, שבזה נראה אחדותו של הקב"ה שהוא עתה אחד" (148). The powers of the angels are only lent: "לפי שהוא אינו רק דבר שהוא שאול להם לזמן, ולא יהיה לנצח" (148).
- **His voice does not stop.** "אלא קולו של הקב"ה הוא השפעת התורה מאתו" (146). "כי אצלו אין שינוי, רק השינוי מצד המקבלים, והוא יתברך משפיע תמיד" (146).
- **Love makes two into one; fear keeps apart.** "כי אהבה הוא מתדבק עם האוהב עד שנעשה אחד עם האוהב" (189). So "all is in Heaven's hands except fear of Heaven": fear that G-d forced would not be fear.
- **Serve from love, not fear.** "שמע מינה שעיקר המצוה בעבודת הקדוש ברוך הוא הוא האהבה, ולא היראה" (149). And love comes through Torah: "ואת דרכיו כמה הם טובים, ושבחו מכיר, ויבוא לאהבה" (152).
- **Because He is One, His ways are not pure strict law.** "ומפני שהוא אחד אין מדותיו דין גמור, שהרי יש בו רחמים ודין" (293).

## 2.1 He is all, and nothing is outside Him (131)

This is the strongest unity line in the range. The sages say Adam at first reached from earth to sky and from east to west. Why? Because he was made in the image of G-d: "וכיון שנברא בצלם אלקים, הנה כמו שהשם יתברך הוא הכל ואין חוץ ממנו, כך ראוי הוא שהאדם שהוא נברא בצלמו הוא כולל כל עולמו" (131). "Just as G-d is all and there is nothing outside Him, so the person made in His image includes his whole world." The reason: "אין דבר אלקי חלק בלבד, כי החלק הוא לגשם שמתחלק" (131). "כי כל דבר אלקי אינו מחולק ולא יוגבל" (131). A body has parts. What is divine is whole.

And the image is not a shape: "שאין צלם אלקי נאמר על התואר והתמונה בלבד, רק נאמר שדבק בצורת האדם ענין אלקי" (131). Man completes the lower world: "שהאדם השלמת כל הנמצאים התחתונים" (131). After the sin he was made smaller, "אבל לא הוסר ממנו צלם אלקי, רק שמעטו" (131). Why smaller and not gone? "כי אין חטא בדבר שהוא אלקים, והחטא הוא בגוף" (131).

## 2.2 The Shema: One now, and One in the end (148)

"Hashem who is our G-d now will be One." Some asked: then the Shema speaks only of the future? The Maharal answers the opposite: "אדרבה, זהו עיקר יחודו של הקדוש ברוך הוא, שבזה נראה אחדותו של הקב"ה שהוא עתה אחד" (148). Now He gives power to angels and to the heavenly princes of the nations. Does that lessen His oneness? No: "לפי שהוא אינו רק דבר שהוא שאול להם לזמן, ולא יהיה לנצח" (148). A thing lent is still the lender's. "ולבסוף שישוב הכל אל אחדותו נראה שהוא אחד ברוך הוא, ומיוחד בפי הכל" (148).

## 2.3 The voice that does not stop (146)

"A great voice, and it did not stop." What does that mean? "אלא קולו של הקב"ה הוא השפעת התורה מאתו" (146). He is the source of life that always gives. At Sinai He opened their ears to receive it. But the giving itself never stopped: "כי אצלו אין שינוי, רק השינוי מצד המקבלים, והוא יתברך משפיע תמיד" (146). "With Him there is no change; the change is only on the side of those who receive."

## 2.4 Love makes one; fear keeps apart (189)

"All is in Heaven's hands except fear of Heaven." Why can G-d not make a person G-d-fearing? Because fear that is forced is not fear, "אם הוא מוכרח ואנוס על החטא, כן אינו ירא שמים כשהוא מוכרח להיות צדיק" (189). The Maharal goes deeper. Fear is separation: "והיפך זה המתירא, שהוא נבדל מן אותו שהוא מתירא, וזהו עצם היראה" (189). Love is the opposite: "וזהו היפך האהבה, כי אהבה הוא מתדבק עם האוהב עד שנעשה אחד עם האוהב" (189). Now, whenever G-d acts on something, "הרי יש דביקות לפועל במתפעל" (189): there is already a bond. So G-d cannot put fear, which is distance, into a person by His own act. Only the person can stand back in awe. (Ours: the Maharal is saying that all G-d's acts on us are closeness.)

## 2.5 One G-d, so not pure strict law (293)

"You are children to Hashem your G-d; do not cut yourselves" over the dead. Two reasons. First: "אבל נראה כי האיסור הוא מפני כי ישראל הם מציאות אמיתי, שהרי הם עלולים מן השם יתברך" (293). Children of G-d are true being, and destruction does not fit them. Second: "ועוד יש לך להבין, שהשם יתברך הוא אחד" (293), "ומפני שהוא אחד אין מדותיו דין גמור, שהרי יש בו רחמים ודין" (293). One who destroys himself over a death acts as if all were pure strict law. If that were so, "היה חס ושלום השם יתברך נוטה לגמרי למדה אחת, ואינו כלול מרחמים ודין, ואם כן יש כאן שניות" (293). "Then there would be twoness." And "כי העלול מורה על העלה" (293): what is caused shows its cause. A people split into pieces would show a split in its source. "כי כל חילוק הוא השחתה" (293).

## 2.6 Every mitzvah is to Him alone (228)

Why is idolatry like denying the whole Torah? "שאין מצוה רק אל השם יתברך" (228). All the mitzvot are addressed to One.

## 2.7 No jealousy in Him (125)

"A jealous G-d" does not mean He feels jealousy: "לא שיהיה הוא יתברך בו קנאה, שאין בו קנאה" (125). It means He repays those who break His will.

## 2.8 All their needs from Him (172)

In the desert even their clothes grew with them, "שאין לומר שיהיה קצת צרכיהם על פי ה', וקצת צרכיהם אנושי" (172). When He provides, He provides all.

- **MH-S68 "I hold my whole world" (131).** "כך ראוי הוא שהאדם שהוא נברא בצלמו הוא כולל כל עולמו" (131). Inside: I am not a small part of a big world. The divine in me is whole, as He is whole, so my soul holds my world. How to enter: stand still and say: "You are all, and nothing is outside You. You made me in Your image. What is divine in me is whole." It differs from S60 The essential bond, which is the tie between the soul and G-d. This is the soul's wholeness, taken from His.
- **MH-S69 "The voice still speaking" (146).** "והוא יתברך משפיע תמיד" (146). Inside: the voice of Sinai did not end. When I open a holy book, I am hearing it now. What changes is only my hearing. How to enter: before learning, say: "Your voice did not stop. Open my ears." It differs from S65 Given right now, which is about the world given each moment. This is Torah given each moment.
- **MH-S70 "One now" (148).** "שבזה נראה אחדותו של הקב"ה שהוא עתה אחד" (148). Inside: every power I meet, in nature or in people, is lent. None is its own. He is One now, not only at the end. How to enter: when some force seems to rule your life, say the Shema and think: "This power is lent. You alone are." It differs from S62 Only Him, which is the quiet sense that nothing else is. This is seeing the powers that seem to rule, and knowing they are borrowed.
- **MH-S71 "Love that makes one" (189, 149).** "כי אהבה הוא מתדבק עם האוהב עד שנעשה אחד עם האוהב" (189). Inside: I do not serve Him from a distance, afraid. I come close, and closeness makes us one. How to enter: before a mitzvah, think: "I do this to come close to You." It differs from S44 Love, which is the feeling. This is love as the act of joining. The awe that stands back is mine to bring; the love that joins is His way with me.
- **MH-S72 "Held in mercy and law" (293).** "ומפני שהוא אחד אין מדותיו דין גמור, שהרי יש בו רחמים ודין" (293). Inside: a loss has come. But this is not a world of pure strict law. The One who took is also the One of mercy, and both are in Him. How to enter: at a loss, say: "You are One. Your law and Your mercy are one in You." Mourn fully, within the Torah's measure, and do not destroy yourself. It differs from S84 Trust, which is calm reliance. This is holding on in grief, knowing He is not only din.

## 4.1 Stand as His image (`mh_whole_as_image`, new)

The practice for MH-S68 (131). Once a day, stand still. Say: "You are all, and nothing is outside You." Then: "I am made in Your image. What is divine in me is not divided." Guard: this is about the soul, "כי אין חטא בדבר שהוא אלקים, והחטא הוא בגוף" (131). It is not a reason for pride.

## 4.2 Hear the voice now (`mh_hear_the_voice`, new)

The practice for MH-S69 (146). Before learning, say: "Your voice did not stop. You are giving Torah now. Open my ears." Then learn one line slowly, as one who hears it spoken.

## 4.3 Say the Shema over the powers (`mh_only_one_in_heart`, extended)

Add 148 to the Shema practice. When a power seems to rule you, a boss, an illness, a market, say the first verse and think: "This power is lent for a time. You are One now."

## 4.4 Serve to come close (`mh_serve_from_love`, new)

The practice for MH-S71 (149, 189). one who serves only from fear finds the service hard (149), and so "ומניח והולך" (149): he leaves it and goes. Practice: before each mitzvah, say: "I do this to come close to You." Let awe keep you careful, and let love make you stay.

## 4.5 Learn to love (`mh_learn_to_love`, new)

"What is love? These words on your heart." "לפי שעל ידי דברי תורה מכיר" (152) the Holy One "ואת דרכיו כמה הם טובים, ושבחו מכיר, ויבוא לאהבה" (152). Practice: when you learn a law, ask: "What does this show about how good His ways are?" Let the answer lead to love. And let the words stay sharp in your mouth, so that "הדברים שהם על לבבו הם לו למשוך אותו בעבותות אהבה" (154).

## 4.6 Thank Him for every measure (`mh_thank_every_measure`, new)

"With all your might" means with every measure He measures to you: "שמחוייב להודות להקדוש ברוך הוא על כל מדה ומדה, אפילו נותן לו דבר פורענות חייב להודות" (151). Practice: at night, name one good thing from the day and one hard thing. Thank Him for both.

## 4.7 Ask as a free gift (`mh_ask_free_gift`, new)

"שהצדיקים אינם מבקשים אלא מתנת חנם כאשר מתפללים על עצמם, שאינם מתגאים הצדיקים לומר שיש להם זכות" (93). And "כל התולה בזכותו" (93) has it hang on others' merit. Moshe knew "שהקדוש ברוך הוא נותן מתנת חנם" (94). Practice: when you pray for yourself, ask as for a gift: "Give it to me by Your goodness, not by what I did." When you pray for another, recall their good.

## 4.8 Din, then mercy (`mh_ask_free_gift`, same practice)

Moshe prayed "Adonai" (the judge) and then "Hashem" (the name of mercy): "בשם של רחמים רחם עלי" (98). And he recalled that His hand "פשוטה לקבל שבים" (100). Add to 4.7: begin your plea by admitting He is the judge. Then ask for mercy.

## 4.9 A mitzvah draws a mitzvah; new each day (`mh_every_day_leaving`, extended)

"שהרי מצוה גוררת מצוה" (207): if you keep the old, you will hear the new. And "שיהיו כל המצות עליך כאילו היום נצטוו" (208). Add to the practice: each morning, pick one mitzvah and do it as if it were given to you today. Watch it lead to the next one.

## 4.10 Look for the lesson (`mh_take_the_lesson`, new)

"שמי שיודע דבר ורואה, ואינו לוקח מוסר, הוא עבירה בפני עצמה" (9). Practice: when you see something happen to someone, ask: "What is this teaching me?" Not taking the lesson is itself a fault.

## 4.11 Mourn within the measure (`mh_mourn_within_measure`, new)

The practice for MH-S72 (293). The Torah says do not cut yourselves over the dead. Practice: in mourning, keep the laws of mourning fully, and within them grieve. Do not harm yourself in body or soul. Say: "You are One; in You are mercy and law." Guard: this is a teaching for one who grieves, never a word to hurry someone out of grief.

## 5. The big human questions

**What is G-d?** All, with nothing outside Him (131). One now, not only at the end (148).

**What am I?** Made in His image, a divine thing that is whole, not a part (131). Sin is in the body, not in the divine in you (131).

**Did G-d stop speaking after Sinai?** No. His voice is the flow of Torah, always; only our hearing changes (146).

**Why serve from love and not fear?** Fear alone makes the service hard, and one leaves it (149). Love joins until one (189).

**How do I come to love G-d?** Through Torah: learn how good His ways are (152).

**Can G-d make me G-d-fearing?** No. Forced fear is not fear. That is yours to give (189).

**Why do I think G-d hates me?** "חשבו כי הוא יתברך היה שונא את ישראל" (59): the spies' generation hated Him, so they thought He hated them. (Ours: what we feel toward Him can color what we think He feels toward us.)

**How do I pray for myself?** Ask a free gift, not a wage (93, 94).

**What happens when I do one mitzvah?** It draws the next (207).

**How should I grieve?** Fully, within the Torah's measure, knowing the One who took is also the One of mercy (293).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Tzimtzum.** "With Him there is no change; the change is only on the side of those who receive" (146) is very close to the Chabad reading of tzimtzum: the hiding is only from our side, and from His side nothing changed (Tanya ch. 48; Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; Iggeret HaKodesh 20). The Maharal says it of the voice of Sinai. Chabad says it of the whole of creation. And "a great voice that did not stop" is the same verse Chabad uses for the word of G-d that keeps creating each moment (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-2).
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "No jealousy in Him" (125) and "anything divine is not divided and not limited" (131) are the Rambam's way: G-d has no feelings and no parts (Yesodei HaTorah 1:7-11; Guide I:35, I:54). The Maharal then uses the negation for a positive teaching about man: what is divine in man is also undivided.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "כמו שהשם יתברך הוא הכל ואין חוץ ממנו" (131) is the first half almost word for word. The second half is also here: the powers of angels are only "lent" (148), not His being.
- **The Shema** (148) matches Tanya ch. 20 and Shaar HaYichud ch. 1 and 7: "Hashem is One" means there is nothing besides Him. The Maharal adds that the lent powers are the reason it can be said now.
- **The soul in His image** (131) is close to Tanya ch. 2: the soul is "a part of G-d above, truly." The Maharal says the divine is not a part at all. Chabad's "part" and the Maharal's "whole" say the same thing from two sides: the soul is of His essence, and His essence has no parts.
- **Love and fear** (149, 189) fit Tanya ch. 41-44: fear is the base, love is the goal, and love is closeness. The Maharal's claim that G-d cannot give fear is close to the Talmud line Tanya uses (ch. 41), and his idea that all His acts on us are bonds fits Tanya ch. 46 on love as a reflection of His love.
- **Mourning within the measure** (293) fits the Chabad teaching that death is not the end of the soul (Iggeret HaKodesh 27-28).
