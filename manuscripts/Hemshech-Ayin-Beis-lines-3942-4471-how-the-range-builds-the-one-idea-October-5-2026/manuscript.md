## The shape of the range

This is one long argument carried across nineteen maamarim, week by week through the summer of 1915. The Rashab picks up each week exactly where he stopped ("ולהבין זה ילה"ק משנת"ל", "to understand this, first recall what was explained"). The thread is **how the Essence comes to be in the lower world, and what in the person and the world is the vessel for it.** The key words move in order: vessel and light, running and returning, refinement, delight, rest, gift, and at the end *yesh* and *ayin*. F05 (the Essence) leads by far. F08 (bitul) and F07 (the dwelling below) come next, because the whole range asks what kind of bitul makes a vessel, and for what.

1. **The vessel first (3942-3968).** Absence comes before coming-to-be. The vessel came first in the thought, and light is drawn for the vessel's sake. But the vessel must be in bitul.
2. **Running and returning (3969-4049).** Everything runs and returns: souls, angels, vessels, and above all the light itself. The light is a glow that clings to its source. So it runs back to it, and it is renewed from the hidden light.
3. **Refinement (4050-4166).** Two ways of refining (from below, from above). Then a third, "food from food": what is already good is refined into a higher bitul. The rooster, the bread and the heifer all serve this. Pinchas draws from the Essence by giving his body.
4. **Manna and Shabbat (4167-4285).** Bread from the earth comes by refinement. Manna, bread from heaven, is above refinement, from the dew of Atik. Why it did not fall on Shabbat and came day by day. Unfelt essential delight.
5. **The dwelling as delight (4286-4337).** Creation has a delight of rest and a delight in its inner intent. The intent is that separate creatures be nullified to Him, a dwelling below.
6. **The end deed above the start (4338-4402).** "The end of the deed was first in thought" means the finished deed rises *above* the start of thought, into the essence of delight. A gift well received delights the giver more than the giving.
7. **Havayah is Elokim (4403-4435).** Tzimtzum and revelation are included in each other, and so "truly one". Service unites them until Ein Sof is revealed.
8. **The true yesh (4436-4471).** Only the Essence truly exists. Created *yesh* is real only because its source is hidden. And yet only the created *yesh* proves the Essence, and the Essence's power is more in it than in the light.

## Shavuot, second night and day (3942-3968): absence before being, the vessel first

**Brokenness brings light (3942-3946).** Only a broken heart receives the light:

> ואז דוקא יאיר לו אור ה' ויבא על הענין ואדרבה בריבוי גילוי אור ביותר (line 3942)

The soul above is "standing", light in measure. Only below, through being pushed away, does it become "walking", which is the Essence beyond measure:

> ומהלכים הוא גילוי בחי' עצמות אוא"ס שלמע' מבחי' מדה וגבול (line 3944)

Tzimtzum alone makes nothing (3944). Egypt's slavery, and this last exile, are the absence that comes before being. The trembling at Sinai made bitul so that essential light could be revealed (3946).

**The vessel came first in thought (3948-3966).** The tzimtzum is secondary to the revelation but comes before it (3948). Before the tzimtzum, the light related to worlds was mixed into the inner light. The tzimtzum hid everything so that this light could be found (3951). In the chain, light comes before vessel. In the thought, the vessel comes first, because the aim is the vessel:

> אך זהו מפני שבמח' הכלי קדמה והיא תכלית הכוונה ע"כ היא ממשיך את האור (line 3962)

So also the earth came first in the thought, "the end of the deed rose first in thought" (3954). But a vessel that is *yesh* works against the aim:

> ואם הכלי היא בבחי' יש ומציאות הרי אינה כלי לאור ואדרבה מעלמת על האור וה"ז היפך הכוונה לגמרי (line 3963)

On Shabbat, physical pleasure is divine pleasure (3965). The inner aim is that light above the vessel shine inside the vessel (3966).

## Naso to Shelach (3969-4049): running and returning

**Everyone runs (3970-3992).** Running is thirst "to be included in the living God" (3976). The contemplation that kindles it: all the worlds live only from Malchut, "His name", a glow, and even Atzilut is only a glow:

> וכמו"כ הוא גם בעולמות עליוני' כמו בעולם אצי' ולמע' מאצי' שהכל הוא רק הארה לבד וכולא קכל"ח (line 3978)

This sets the heart aflame to be included in the Essence, with no delight even in divine light (3979-3980). The return is Torah and bitul, "only to fulfil His will below" (3981). Creation needs distance: "a *yesh* exists only by distance" (3989).

**The light's running (3994-4021).** The light is only the revealing of the essence, with no measure beside it (4004). So it changes nothing in the Essence:

> ולכן אין האור פועל שינוי כלל בהעצם וכמ"ש אני ה' לא שניתי (line 4007)

The source is separate in measure from the revelation (4010). Yet the aim is precisely that light be revealed (4011). When it shines in vessels, the inner aim is fulfilled (4012). The light is like its source yet beyond measure from it. That is why it is called Ein Sof, not "without beginning" (4014). Three loves follow: eternal love (from seeing that all is a glow), great love (from the wonder of Ein Sof itself), and the silent love of delights (4016-4018).

**Renewal (4023-4049).** The light in the worlds must always be renewed, because it came through the "leap" of the tzimtzum (4026). The hidden light of the first day renews it:

> דהאור הגנוז הוא המחדש את האור וע"י הוא הקיום (line 4030)

Every grasp of Godliness, however small, is grasp, because the whole chain exists "so that they may know Him" (4042-4043). Practical mitzvot draw the Ein Sof above the chain (4048).

## Korach to Pinchas (4050-4166): refinement, and food from food

**Running is bitul (4053).** Renewal comes by running, and running is bitul. The good of the animal soul is its desiring power, which can desire Godliness (4054). The food one lives on, when one prays with its strength, is included in Godliness (4058). Refinement turns darkness into a vessel, and that reveals the higher delight (4063):

> ואין מלך בלא עם שהוא בחי' נפרדים דוקא, ומזה שהם מתבטלים ומקבלים עליהם עול מלכותו ית' יהי' זה לנח"ר ועונג כבי' לפניו ית' (line 4064)

In the outer will, darkness and light are the same before Him. The distinction comes from delight (4071). Korach's mistake was to grasp the tzimtzum for its own sake (4075).

**Two ways, and a third (4083-4136).** The first way removes coarse dross first. The second draws the good out by revealed light (4093). Then comes the third, which is Shabbat's: refinement of "food from food". Even Atzilut needs a higher bitul (4100). The river of fire before upper Gan Eden is bitul, not punishment. There one enters only as "nothing":

> רק מי שהוא בחי' אין ומה בעצם שבלתי מרגיש א"ע כלל שנק' ביטול עצמי למע' מביטול היש לאין (line 4107)

The red heifer's ash is the essence left after the *form* of evil is burnt. Service then is without a vessel, and draws from the Essence (4110). Upper Gan Eden knows by negation, which leads to "seeing the essence" (4118-4119). "God will circumcise your heart" removes even felt love. What is left is the soul's essential love, "rooted and bound in the Essence" (4126).

**Pinchas (4140-4166).** In the Essence, repair comes before breaking (4143). Man was made to refine the earth (4151). The *yesh* lives only by its bitul to the *ayin*:

> באמת מה שהיש מקבל חיות מהאין זהו דוקא ע"י הביטול של היש אל האין (line 4154)

Only the name Ban nullifies physical *yesh*, even its outside. Eliyahu's body flew (4156). Pinchas, by giving his body, drew from the Essence as a gift (4165).

## Matot to Ekev (4167-4285): manna, Shabbat, unfelt delight

The manna came from the dew of Atik, the head "not arranged", where "one does not say You but He" (4174). It was measured by the omer and yet not measured, "in measure and without measure" (4219). Above Atzilut there are no sefirot at all (4205). Even Chochmah in Ein Sof is only the negation of its absence (4210). The sefirot of Atzilut are unlimited compared with the lower worlds, but they are measure compared with the Essence (4211). Division that comes from strength is no real division:

> וכמו התו' שבאה במדידה בבחי' הגבלה בריבוי פרטים והכל הוא מהות העצמות ובלתי מוגבל כלל בעצם (line 4214)

The light that lights worlds is Ein Sof that "measured itself" (4217). Shabbat is the King's delight in His own Essence (4222). Mishneh Torah joins the higher and lower unity (4228). Va'etchanan warns against dividing the upper waters from the lower:

> והאמת אינו כן דהכל הוא אלקו' (line 4254)

> דכאשר תופס את האמת שהעיקר הוא האלקו' והוא בטל לאלקו' שזהו אחד דיחו"ת (line 4254)

What is essential does not change as it comes down, even when it is felt (4260-4262). Manna came day by day because it was a gift, not earnings (4266). In the future the Essence itself will be revealed in Torah (4275-4276). Revealed and hidden Torah are one:

> הכל א' הוא דהכל הוא בבחי' מהוע"צ א"ס (line 4276)

## Re'eh and Shoftim (4286-4337): the delight in the dwelling

Creation is God's "power of action", Malchut and the letters (4294). He made it without weariness. His power of limit is also Ein Sof:

The inner aim is that separate creatures be nullified to Him (4303). The final aim is the lowest world:

> ותכלית הכוונה הוא בעולם התחתון ממש כד אתכפי' סט"א ואתהפכא חשול"נ (line 4305)

Mitzvot refine *nogah* until it shines "without any hiding of the face" (4306). The chief revelation will come in the future, and below (4309). The hand that gives charity will become a vessel for Ein Sof (4310). Before creation, His delight was hidden in His Essence, "for in what would He delight?" (4319). Yet even the delight in the dwelling is only a particular delight, a glow of the essence of delight:

> דבחי' מקור התענוגים הוא בחי' עצמות אוא"ס והיינו תענוג שהוא עצמי ואינו מורכב בשום דבר (line 4330)

## Ki Tetze and Ki Tavo (4338-4402): the end deed rises above the start

A particular delight rises only to its own start. But the deed done well rises into the Essence itself (4350). The receiver comes first in root, before even the root of the giving (4361):

> וסומ"ע זו עולה בבחי' עצמות התענוג ממש (line 4362)

God "delights in the quality of the service of Jews below" (4363). Its root is "Yachid above Echad" (4363). Creation's purpose is creatures who know Him. His perfection needs nothing:

> וזהו מ"ש הרמב"ם שכל הנמצאים צריכים אליו והוא אינו צריך להם (line 4357)

Service above the order of the chain rises into the Essence. That means "we will do" before "we will hear", teshuvah, and self-sacrifice in this generation's darkness:

> הרי כ"ז עולה בבחי' עצמות אוא"ס ב"ה שמתענג בעצמותו כבי' שהוא בחי' עונג עצמי ופשוט שאינו מורכב בשום דבר (line 4377)

On Shabbat, prayer is in the soul's essence, with no requests. Its "return" is the soul going back to the Essence (4392-4394).

## Nitzavim (4403-4435): Havayah is Elokim, truly one

> ואם הי' ח"ו הכוונה לצמצם ממש הרי הי' העלם לגמרי (line 4408)

> ומאחר דהוי' ואלקים כלולים זב"ז מובן לנו עוד איך דהוי' ואלקים כולא חד ממש (line 4411)

Both names are kindness. Elokim is the severity inside the kindness (4411). Service reverses the tzimtzum and unites the two names "until Ein Sof is revealed" (4413-4414). In exile even "who created these" cannot be seen (4418). Created things feel a life, but not that it is Godliness (4424). The Shechinah went into exile for Israel's sake (4427). Egypt was left by faith. On Rosh Hashanah acceptance of the yoke makes all the powers Godliness (4420).

## Rosh Hashanah 5676 (4436-4471): the true yesh

> דהנה כל מה שנתהווה אינו מציאות אמיתי (line 4441)

> שהרי מציאותו הוא רק מהתעלמות מקורו ואם הי' מקורו מתגלה עליו הי' מתבטל מציאותו (line 4442)

Even the light is not true in itself (4449). Only the Essence is. And it "exists without being an existent" (4450). But only the Essence can make a thing whose source is not felt (4452). So only creation points to the Essence:

> ונמצא דהיש הנברא דוקא מכריח את בחי' יש האמיתי (line 4454)

> א"כ כח העצמות הוא דוקא במציאות היש (line 4455)

> ובמדרי' זו מציאות היש הוא מציאות אמיתי והיינו דלעתיד יהי' הגוף הגשמי אלקות (line 4456)

So a practical mitzvah, done in acceptance of the yoke, surpasses Torah (4456). The shofar must be a deed, because a deed reaches the Essence (4457). The last lines divide the mediating *ayin* in two. The essence of the light is the *ayin* of the true *yesh*. The spreading light is the *ayin* of the created *yesh* (4463-4470).

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **Two opposite verdicts on the *yesh*, both kept.** The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud 3) says creatures only *seem* to exist. The Rashab repeats this and sharpens it: they are not true existence even while they exist (4441-4448). Then he turns it around. The created *yesh* is the one thing that proves the Essence. The Essence's power is more in the *yesh* than in the light. And at that level the *yesh* is true existence (4454-4456). The Tanya does not say the second half.
- **The light is not the Essence.** In the Tanya, "light" is how the unity is described. Here light is "the lowest level in Ein Sof" (4454). It does not point to the Essence, and it is not true in itself (4449-4450).
- **Deed above Torah.** The Tanya ranks Torah's knowledge high, as a union of minds. Here practical mitzvot surpass Torah, because deed reaches the Essence and Torah is light (4456-4457). The Rashab adds that Torah is essential light.
- **The end above the start.** "The end of the deed was first in thought" is read more strongly than binding end to start. The finished deed, well received, rises *above* the start of the will, into essential delight (4350-4363).
- **The receiver before the giver.** The vessel came first in thought (3953, 3962). The receiver is prior in root to the giving (4361). The Tanya does not say this.
- **Unfelt delight as the peak.** The Tanya's highest service is love of delights. Here there is a level above even that felt essential delight: unfelt delight, "where one does not eat" (4383-4388, 4395).
- **Havayah and Elokim "truly one" because both are kindness.** The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud 4-6) makes them one as sun and shield. Here the reason is that each holds the other's intent (4408-4411).
- **Food from food.** Even Atzilut, "Godliness itself", needs refinement into a higher bitul (4100, 4134). The Tanya's refinement is of *nogah*.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 19 lines are *none*: the maamar headings (3947, 3969, 3993, 4022, 4050, 4079, 4112, 4137, 4167, 4199, 4229, 4257, 4286, 4313, 4338, 4370, 4403, 4436, 4461). - 86 line entries carry no face. They are the opening questions and the steps inside long analogies: the embryo, the heart and blood, the map drawn on white, the builder, the rooster, the river and pool. There are also Kabbalistic mechanics with no line about the unity in them (the three Shabbat meals, Binah as "seventh", the fifteen foundations). - No row is *reading*. Where the tie is only ours, the line is marked structural and given faces only by its subject. - F05 (181) overstates how often the Divine Essence itself is the subject. Many tags mark "essential light", "essential delight" or "the soul's essence", which the Rashab carries upward by analogy. - Some long lines hold several ties. Only six were split into sub-entries. Other lines' second ties are named in `how` but have no quote of their own.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Is the *yesh* true or not?** It is "not true existence" (4441-4448), and it is "true existence" at the level where the Essence's power is (4456). Both are the Rashab's, a few lines apart.
- **Light or *yesh*: which holds the Essence?** The light clings to its source and is "like" it (4014, 4455). Yet the Essence's power is "more" in the *yesh* than in the light (4455).
- **Does the tzimtzum hide?** Elokim does not truly hide ("one essence does not hide another", 4441), and the aim of revelation is included in it (4408). Yet created things do not feel that their life is Godliness (4424), and in exile the Shechinah is bound "like one in a sack" (4426).
- **Does anything change in Him?** "I have not changed": the light makes no change in the Essence (4007, 4013). Yet the aim is incomplete without revelation in vessels (4011). And He "delights" in the service below (4363).
- **Gift or work?** Manna, Shabbat's inner delight and the love of delights are gifts, above arousal from below (4266, 4382). Yet "if he had not given Him pleasure He would not have given", so service is still needed (4256, 4270, 4392).
- **Shabbat meals.** Shabbat eating is refinement of "food from food" (4100, 4134). Elsewhere it is not refinement at all but drawing from above (4139). The Rashab records both.
- **Against the Tanya on Torah and deed.** Torah is essential light (4456), yet deed surpasses it (4456-4457). This is kept as the Rashab's own ranking.
