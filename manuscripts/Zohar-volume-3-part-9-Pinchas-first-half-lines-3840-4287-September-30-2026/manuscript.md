## The shape of the range

Pinchas opens with the zealot's reward and turns fast to the inner life.

- **The main story.** Rabbi Abba and Rabbi Yosi rise at midnight to learn. A shadow walks through the house, and it is the soul of Rabbi Pinchas ben Yair (3923-3972). Later Rabbi Shimon meets the living Rabbi Pinchas on the road, under a tree by a spring, with an eagle and a rose (3997-4237).
- **The Raya Mehemna layer.** Woven through the story, it covers: - reincarnation and levirate marriage (3884-3900); - the sefirot and the chariot, read through the tzitzit and tefillin (4087-4183); - the body as a map of the upper world (heart, brain, lungs, liver, spleen, windpipe) (4201-4281).
- **The great statement on the Cause of causes.** It sits in the middle of the section on sacrifices (4062-4066). It is the classic source of the phrase "surrounds all worlds, fills all worlds."

## The unity of G-d

**1. Surrounds all worlds and fills all worlds.** This is the passage the Chassidic masters quote most from the whole Zohar:

> איהו סובב על כל עלמין, ולית סובב לון לכל סטרא, עילא ותתא, ולארבע סטרין, בר מניה. ולית מאן דנפיק מרשותיה לבר. איהו ממלא כל עלמין (Zohar, Pinchas 40:11; line 4065)
> He surrounds all worlds, and there is none that surrounds them on any side, above or below or on the four sides, except Him. And none goes out of His domain. He fills all worlds.

> איהו שליט על כלא, ולית מאן דשליט ביה. איהו תפיס בכלא, ולית מאן דתפיס ביה. ואיהו לא אתקרי ידו"ד, ובכל שמהן, אלא באתפשטות נהוריה עלייהו (Zohar, Pinchas 40:10; line 4064)
> He rules over all, and none rules over Him. He grasps everything, and nothing grasps Him. He is not called by the four-letter Name or by any name, except as His light spreads over them.

"When He withdraws from them, He has no name of His own from them" (line 4064). No light can look at Him without going dark, "even the Supreme Crown" (line 4065). He gives life to all, "and there is no other G-d above Him to give Him life" (line 4066). Sins do not reach Him "to separate His letters," for in Him there is no separation (line 4064). *Ours:* here is the Chabad pair of *sovev* ("surrounds") and *memale* ("fills") in its own ancient words. The Tanya's chapters 41 and 48 and all of Shaar Hayichud rest on this line. Chabad adds one thing the Zohar does not spell out here: that "surrounds" does not mean "outside," but a presence too great to be held in any vessel.

**2. The Cause of causes rides and is not ridden.** "The Cause of causes unites all, orders all, and shines in all. His light passes through soul, body and garment."

> ולית ביה שנוי ושותפו וחושבן ותמונה ודמיון (Zohar, Pinchas 55:6; line 4182)
> In Him there is no change, no partner, no number, no image and no likeness.

"All the upper and lower levels are rider and chariot to Him, and none rides upon Him" (line 4182).

**3. Do not split "I" from "He."** "Woe to one who separates *Ani* ('I') from *Hu* ('He')" (ווי ליה למאן דאפריש אני מן הוא, line 4017), "for all is one without separation" (דכלא חד בלא פרודא, line 4017). The verse "See now that I, I am He" joins them (line 4017).

**4. Voice and speech in every word.** "The Holy One and His Shechinah are the voice and speech of every angel, of every voice and word of Torah, of every voice of prayer, of every commandment":

> בכל אתר שולטנותיה בעלאין ותתאין, איהו חיים דכלא, איהו סביל כלא (Zohar, Pinchas 49:9; line 4132)
> Everywhere is His rule, above and below. He is the life of all; He bears all.

"There is no *Adonai* without the four-letter Name, just as there is no speech without voice" (line 4133). This is true in the world of emanation. "In the world of separation there is voice without speech" (line 4133).

**5. Inside and outside the same.** He is hidden inside the Torah, and is known through the commandments, which are His Shechinah, "His likeness." "He is humble, and she is humility; He is faithful, she is faith..." The outside "does not differ from the inside, to show that she is His emanation, and there is no separation there at all" (ולא אשתניאת איהי דלבר, מההוא דלגו, line 4171). That is why "one whose inside is not like his outside may not enter the house of study" (line 4171). In this world He is written one way and read another. In the world to come He will be written and read the same, "to be mercy on every side" (line 4172).

**6. All one on Shemini Atzeret.** The festival order runs from Rosh Hashanah to the last day of Sukkos. On Rosh Hashanah the left arm wakes: judgment, and the need for full return. On Yom Kippur the left is placed under the head. On Sukkos the right embraces, "and then all faces shine," so rejoice "in every kind of joy" (line 3871). On the eighth day the body joins, "and then all is called One, without separation" (כדין כלא אקרי אחד, בלא פרודא, line 3868).

## States of consciousness

- **The flame that sways.** Why do Jews sway when they learn? Rabbi Shimon weeps before he answers. The soul is "the lamp of Hashem"; once it is lit from the Torah above, its light never rests:

> נהורא דשרגא כיון דאתאחדא גו פתילה, ההוא נהורא לא שכיך לעלמין, אלא מתנענעא נהורא לכאן ולכאן (Zohar, Pinchas 16:2; line 3954)
> The light of a lamp, once it has caught in the wick, never rests; the light sways here and there.

"When he says one word of Torah, the light is lit, and they cannot be still" (line 3955). **Proposed state: the lit flame.** *Ours:* it names the bodily restlessness of a soul that has caught fire in learning or prayer as a sign of life, not a problem to fix. Near S53 and S41. - **Joy shared with Him.** "Any joy of Israel in which they do not share the Holy One is not joy," and the accuser turns it to weeping (line 3957). But one who shares his joy with Him: if trouble comes, G-d shares the trouble, "I am with him in distress" (line 3958). And more: when a person includes G-d in his joy, G-d goes to the Garden of Eden and brings the person's dead father and mother to be there with him, "and people do not know." But in a person's distress:

> אבל בעקו דבר נש, קודשא בריך הוא זמין לגביה בלחודוי, ולא אודע לאבוהי ולאמיה (Zohar, Pinchas 18:5; line 3964)
> But in a person's distress, the Holy One comes to him alone, and does not tell his father and mother.

**Proposed state: joy with the parents present.** *Ours:* this is a tender teaching for mourners at a wedding or a birth. It also holds a second teaching: G-d keeps our pain between Himself and us. It maps to S53 and S86. - **The closed rose.** "As long as the rose is closed it has no scent" (כל זמן דשושנה אטימא, לית לה ריחא, line 4220). "When they open in return, the scent rises at once" (line 4220). Maps to S77. - **Heart up, eyes down.** In prayer, a person's "heart is above, toward the Name, and his eyes below, toward *Adonai*" (line 4161), like the angels, whose backs are high and whose sight is low. A precise posture of attention: reaching up and humble at once. Near S33 and S41. - **Held in sickness.** The Shechinah surrounds the sick person "like the sand around the sea," so the fever does not burst out: "Hashem will support him on his sickbed" (line 4252). For a fully righteous person she surrounds the whole body, head to foot (line 4253). Maps to S63 and S86. - **Joy of the hour of favor.** When an eagle circles over them, Rabbi Pinchas says "now is a time of favor; at this hour the gates of mercy open for all the sick." The eagle is the most merciful of all creatures to its young (4225-4226). Maps to S84.

## Meditation and practice

- **Rising at midnight to greet the King's face at dawn.** "All who merit to toil in Torah from midnight, and come with the Queen when day shines to greet the face of the King, hold fast to the Shechinah and inherit her," and a thread of kindness rests on them (line 3843). David "did not let midnight pass in sleep," and "I will rise" means "I will bind myself to You forever" (line 3851). Continues the midnight rising (part 1).
- **The servant in three places.** One should be a servant three times in prayer. First, "like a servant who arranges his praises before his master." In the middle, like one who asks a gift. At the end, like one who thanks for the gift and goes (line 4019). Ties to the four postures of prayer (part 8).
- **Bowing at "Blessed," standing at the Name.** "Whoever bows, bows at *baruch*; whoever straightens, straightens at the Name" (4143). Standing in the Amidah raises the fallen Shechinah (line 4144). "Greater is one who answers Amen than the one who blesses" (line 4151). Ties to `act` and `lips`.
- **Whispered prayer.** "Only her lips moved, and her voice was not heard." Each person prays so that the one beside him does not hear (line 4177). "Whoever makes his voice heard in his prayer is of little faith" (line 4177). Ties to `quieting`.
- **Speaking without stumbling.** The five parts of the mouth should be "open without sticking," so the words come out without delay. "If my prayer is fluent in my mouth, I know it is accepted" (line 4118). Ties to `lips`.
- **Answering Amen with all your strength.** "Whoever answers *Amen, yehei shmei rabba* with all his strength": wake all the limbs with strength, "while the lamp still burns over your head" (3973-3974). Ties to `act`.
- **Confess, and be judged by the King alone.** On Rosh Hashanah a person should name his sins one by one:

> מאן דמפרש חטאוי, לא אתמסר דיניה, אלא בידא דמלכא, קודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי. ומאן דדאין ליה קודשא בריך הוא, איהו לטב (Zohar, Pinchas 60:1; line 4194)
> Whoever names his sins, his judgment is handed to no one but the King, the Holy One alone. And whoever the Holy One judges is judged for good.

Speak first, "and leave no room for another to speak" (line 4196). Ties to `cheshbon` and `heart_after`. **Proposed technique: confessing into His hands.** - **Repair where you broke.** "A person must repair his soul only in the place where he sinned. Whoever does not do so has no true repair" (line 3915). Ties to `act`. - **Change of place.** "A person who lives in a town of bad people, where he cannot keep the Torah or succeed in it, should change his place and be planted among good people" (line 3930). The same holds for change of name and change of deeds (line 3926). Ties to `choose_line`. - **Visiting the sick.** Do not sit at the head (where the Shechinah is) or at the feet (line 4253). *Ours:* keep the plain point. Sit with care, and know that the sick person is held.

## The big human questions

- **Why does the righteous person suffer for others?** A doctor lets blood from one arm to heal the whole body. "When the Holy One wants to give healing to the world, He strikes one righteous person among them" (line 3949). One is enough, unless the sins are heavy (3951-3952). The accuser is so glad to rule over a righteous man that "he forgets the whole world" (line 3950). The shepherd gives the wolf the fattest ram, leads the flock home, and then comes back to save the ram (line 3932). *Ours:* this belongs in the guide only as the Zohar's own view, said with care. It must never be used to explain another person's suffering to them.
- **Is life ruled by the stars?** "Before the Torah was given, all creatures hung on fate, even children, life and food. After the Torah was given, He took them out from the rule of stars and fates," for one who learns in order to do (3904-3905).
- **What is the heart?** Israel among the nations is like the heart among the limbs: "soft and weak," the one that "knows pain and grief," and the one nearest the King (line 3992). The heart "tells good blood from bad, like one who picks food out of waste" (line 4250).
- **What is anger?** "Whoever is angry is as if he served idols." All fever comes from the gall. Anger is like the sea rising to break the world, and the Shechinah is the sand that holds it in (line 4252).
- **Is study enough?** "Not study but action is the main thing" (line 4171). Torah without commandments is barren (line 3931).

## For the guide (ours)

1. Line 4065 ("surrounds all worlds, fills all worlds") and line 4064 ("He grasps all and nothing grasps Him") are the Zohar's own words for the center of Chabad teaching on unity. The guide should cite them directly beside Tanya 41 and 48.
2. "The lit flame" (3954) is a warm, bodily state. It treats movement and restlessness in prayer as life, not failure.
3. Two linked teachings belong together in the guide: share your joy with G-d, and your departed parents are brought to it; in your distress, G-d comes to you alone (3957-3964).
4. "Confessing into His hands" (4194-4196) gives confession a gentle purpose. You name the fault yourself so that only the loving Judge judges it.
5. "Heart up, eyes down" (4161) and the whispered prayer (4177) are two plain instructions for prayer that anyone can follow.
