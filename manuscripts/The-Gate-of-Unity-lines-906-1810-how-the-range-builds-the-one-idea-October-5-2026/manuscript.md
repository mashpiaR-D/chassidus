## The shape of the range

This range is the reverse of the Tanya range 561-840. There, *stated* made up two thirds of the entries. Here it is about one line in six. These seventeen chapters are the Mitteler Rebbe's walk through the chain of worlds: the primordial will, the kav and the circles, Adam Kadmon, tohu and tikkun, the three lines, Atik and Arich, Abba and Imma, lights and vessels, and the start of Zeir Anpin. Most lines are steps in that mechanism. They are honestly *structural*, and more than half of them carry no face at all. The SPINE already warns about this (section 5): in partzuf and sefirah mechanics the Mitteler Rebbe states the tie at the level of method, not in each passage. This range confirms the warning line by line.

The tie does surface, and when it does it surfaces hard. It comes in a few places. The simple will 'included in His very Essence' (ch. 16-17). The joining of opposites (ch. 20-22), which is why F09 leads the count. The Essence as distinct even from pleasure (ch. 24). The Zohar's 'He and His life are one, He and His causes are one' as the whole point of the lights and vessels (ch. 27-30). And the chain 'bound in one moment' from the essential will down to the breath of the heart (ch. 32). F05 is the leading stated face. F12, F11, F01 and F02, which lead in the Tanya's Shaar HaYichud, are rare here.

## Ch. 16-17 (906-1002): two wills, the kav, and the circles

The chapter's model is a human will. A decided will can exist inside a person before it has any 'general and particular', 'as if it were not' (line 916). Only when it turns toward a deed does it become a general thought that holds the whole deed 'in one glance'. The first of the two wills is the Essence's own: > שנכלל בעצמותו ממש (line 906)

It stays so even when joined to what spreads out from it: > נשאר כלול בהעלם עצמות דאור אין סוף (line 925)

The second will, after the tzimtzum, is the kav, the 'hyle thread' holding every particular drawing (line 928). Its first circle is the general surround of all four worlds, 'surrounding them equally' (line 939, F04). Then the range gives its first plain statement of the book's program, unity in every detail (F04 with G04): > שאין פרט היותר אחרון שלא קדם להיות ברצון ובמחשבה זו (line 952)

It adds F07 in the Mitteler Rebbe's terms. The end rose first: > סוף מעשה, כמו בסוף אלף הז', עלה במחשבה קדומה זו תחלה (line 953)

Chapter 17 explains why the circles run 'highest best' while the straight line runs 'inner best' (lines 945, 978-985). The key sentence for the unity is where Etz Chaim's 'above and below' stops: > רק אחדות פשוט בתכלית (line 992) > > לית מחשבה תפיסא ביה, אפילו מחשבה הקדומה (line 997)

The world of Ein Sof has no above and below, and no thought grasps the Essence, not even the primordial thought. That is F05 as a limit on everything that follows.

## Ch. 18-19 (1003-1101): creation from nothing within the soul; Atzilut as revelation

Chapter 18 finds yesh me'ayin inside the soul. A 'will for a will' (the will to study or to trade) comes and goes. So its source must itself 'come as creation from nothing' (line 1017, F01). The same is true of Adam Kadmon, which is 'Adam of Beriah': > בריאה יש מאין האמיתי דאור אין סוף עצמו (line 1007)

The kav, by contrast, knows everything only in itself: > שכולל במחשבת עצמו עדיין ולא בהתהוות רצון במחשבה שחוץ ממנו כלל (line 1045)

In a parenthesis, line 1046 gives this in the Rambam's words, בידיעת עצמו ולא בידיעה שחוץ ממנו. Chapter 19 says what Atzilut is. It is not an effect spreading out from a cause but 'ziv and light' (line 1054), like colour to an apple or motion to a living thing. The Mitteler Rebbe marks where Sefer Yetzirah's flame-and-coal image fails: the flame comes from the coal's own heat, and that is not true revelation (line 1058, G06). The conclusion: > שיצאו מהעלמם לגלוי בלבד (line 1080)

Line 1074 states the pattern the whole book applies: many particular wills, והכל ממקור אחד.

## Ch. 20-22 (1102-1269): tohu, tikkun, and the joining of opposites

This is the range's densest statement of F09, and it is not the SPINE's simple version. Tohu's sefirot are 'points', pure essences. They cannot join: > כי אין להם כלי שיוכלו להתכלל בו ב' הפכים כאלה (line 1123)

A person whose essential nature is kindness cannot bear someone essentially strict. Line 1125 gives the example of Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel: one side can be silenced but not included in the other's view. Joining happens only in the 'compound' (murkav), when a trait spreads out beyond its essence (lines 1126-1144). That is what a partzuf is: > שהוא בחינת ההתכללות מכל ההפכים (line 1146) > > ולא בעצם מהותן (line 1149)

Chapter 21 adds the joining from above. The middle line can join right and left because > שעולה עד בחינת פנימית הכתר, שלמעלה מהתחלקות הקוין (line 1174)

Prayer, 'yehi ratzon, from before You', reaches the level above the division: > לשנות מרצון של דין לרצון של חסד (line 1192)

Chapter 22 separates two kinds of inclusion. In one, a thing is nullified in its opposite (sweetening, line 1206). That is possible only because each opposite already holds some of the other in hiding (line 1213). In the other, severity serves kindness from within, and the result is 'the force of kindness, more than kindness itself' (line 1232). Here tzimtzum appears as a kindness: rain and the flow 'come contracted so that they can be received' (lines 1228-1229, F03). The range's strongest F11 closes the chapter. Essential good and essential evil cannot be included at all (lines 1261-1263), and yet: > שנהפך גם עצם הרע לטוב, וכן זדונות עצמן נעשו כזכיות (line 1264) > > מצד מסירת נפש בעצמות אור אין סוף, שלמעלה גם מהתחלקות עצם החסד וגבורה (line 1265)

The point of the chapter: only the Essence, which is above even the essential attributes, 'to Him and not to His attributes' (line 1269), can turn essential evil into good. The person reaches it by mesirut nefesh.

## Ch. 23-24 (1270-1384): will and pleasure; the Essence beyond pleasure

Chapter 23 is all analogy (52 structural lines, no stated line). Will and pleasure are 'one essence', and 'it is not known which came first' (line 1319). The Mitteler Rebbe corrects the early Kabbalists who made pleasure the soul's essence: > ואין האמת כן (line 1296)

Chapter 24 applies this above. The Essence of Or Ein Sof is the 'King's delights in His Essence' (line 1324), and still: > ועם כל זה באמת הרי עצמות אור אין סוף מובדל גם מבחינת תענוג פשוט (line 1327)

It 'only shines as pleasure' (line 1328). This pleasure-and-will from the Essence gives F04 in both of its forms: > מלובש תוך כל ספירה וספירה בפרטי פרטות, שבלעדו אין לו קיום כלל (line 1336) > > וגם הוא מקיף את כולם (line 1337)

Line 1354 is F01 in this vocabulary: the absence of the light of pleasure 'is the absence of the light of their existence'. The same chapter keeps a distance, though. The lights of the emanated are 'separate from the Essence' (line 1346). Line 1372 gives Torah's many strict laws a root: דאורייתא מחכמה עילאה. Line 1384 gives the Essence above time, which makes time 'eternal' when it shines in it (F05 with F09).

## Ch. 25-26 (1385-1509): Abba and Imma, Shabbat, and the Shema with mesirut nefesh

Most of these two chapters are partzuf mechanics and are marked structural: the mazalot, the 'mountains of darkness', Yisrael Saba and Tevunah. The stated points are few. The 'droppings of supernal wisdom are Torah' is praise, because their root is higher than revealed wisdom (line 1411). Silence is 'a fence for wisdom' because it raises the bitul of koach mah to the source of all insight (line 1470, F08). The key line for the person's side of the unity is the parenthesis on the Shema: > והיינו במסירת נפש דקריאת שמע, מה שאין כן בתורה ומצות בלא מסירת נפש אין היחוד רק בחיצוניות (line 1481)

The Ten Martyrs raised 'feminine waters' to the inner level 'only through mesirut nefesh' (line 1482). The parable of the great sage who can teach a small child because he knows the idea 'from its very source' (line 1494) is marked *reading* for F07. It is the book's own G05 move, but the text uses it here only for how souls are born.

## Ch. 27-30 (1510-1712): lights and vessels, and 'He and His causes are one'

This is where the range states its theme most directly. The Zohar's formula divides the unity: > איהו וחיוהי חד הן אורות, ואיהו וגרמוהי חד הן בחינת הכלים (line 1513)

The lights are 'without what' (line 1514), and the vessels are 'something' (line 1516). Matter is not foreign to form. It is form's own last level made coarse (line 1552), and matter and form grow 'as one, truly' (line 1556). The first 'something' is 'the yesh of the light' (line 1585). Chapter 29 then says what all this detail is for: > אין כללות ועיקר המכוון בהם רק בענין זה דאיהו וחיוהי חד וגרמוהי חד, שבאמת אין הפרש בין איהו וחיוהי חד לאיהו וגרמוהי חד (line 1601)

That is G04 and F12 in one sentence. The argument that follows is the range's most original F05. The root of the vessels, of limit and of 'something', comes before the root of the lights: > מצד עצמות המאציל הרי עשה שורש להתהוות הכלים קודם שעשה שורש להתהוות האורות (line 1602)

Limit comes from the Essence as much as light does: > כי כל יכול יש בו כח המגביל גם כן (line 1628) > > בשקול וכח ומקור אחד ממש, שהוא בחינת אין סוף ממש (line 1644)

The limiting vessels are also what let anything reach below (lines 1595, 1649, F03 with F07). Chapter 30 draws the practical point. The one who meditates on the Name in the Amidah should aim at the source of the vessel, and the light follows (lines 1657-1658, F10). The mitzvot are the King's 248 limbs. Their three vessels are intention, 'love, awe and bitul', and the deed 'only to do the King's will' (lines 1698-1703). Each mitzvah draws down revealed Godly light through the kav, אנת ממלא כל שמהן (line 1707). The unity has one stated limit here: 'He and His causes are one' is said of the vessels and not of the garments of thought, speech and deed (line 1674). Exile is 'sleep', in which only a trace of life remains (line 1693, F03).

## Ch. 31-32:44 (1713-1810): the vessel's advantage, and one moment from will to heart

Chapter 31 turns F09 toward the lowest level. Vessels can hold opposites that lights cannot: > כי יש בכלים דוקא בחינת וענין ההתכללות מדבר והיפוכו, מה שאין כן באורות (line 1720)

A legal ruling for practice has to include kindness in judgment (lines 1740-1741). The outer vessel, the one that acts, is where the joining mainly happens: > ונמצא שמצד בחינת חיצוניות הכלי, שהוא למעשה, שם הוא עיקר בחינת ההתכללות (line 1743)

The text then raises its own objection. This joining comes from the vessel's bitul to the light (line 1745, F08), which should put it in the inner vessels. It answers that the outer vessels come from the limiting power in the surrounding light (makif) (line 1754). That is why miracles happen there, 'above nature within nature' (line 1757). Division is the condition of union (line 1762), and the 'Adam of tikkun' is formed 'in a body of matter specifically' (line 1763, F07).

Chapter 32 opens with 'from my flesh I see God' (line 1772). It places Zeir Anpin's vessels still inside the world of Ein Sof, because of איהו וחיוהי וגרמוהי חד (line 1770). Every new level needs a contraction first (line 1794). Yet the heart's traits match the mind's 'without deviation even of a yud's tip' (line 1799). Even the heart's outermost vessels are הכל עדיין בבחינת אין סוף (line 1804). The range ends on the Mitteler Rebbe's signature image of the unity, taken from the soul: > מתאחד ומתקשר ממקור הרצון העצמי עד הבל הלב ברגע אחד, כאילו הוא מהות אחד ממש (line 1809)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The unity of the vessels, not only of the lights.** The Tanya's SH relies on the light and life in each thing (F01). Here the 'something' itself (limit, vessel, matter) has its own root in the Essence, and that root comes before the light's (lines 1602, 1628, 1643-1644). The SPINE records the Tzemach Tzedek's claim that the somethingness of matter comes from the surrounding light, and the Alter Rebbe's, via the Rayatz, that the physical comes from the Essence. This range gives the mechanism in the Mitteler Rebbe's words. Linking it to those two sayings is ours.
- **F09 has a location.** The Tanya's SH 6 says the attributes are joined because they are one with Him ('You bind and unite them'). This range says where joining can and cannot happen. It cannot happen between pure essences (tohu, the points, lines 1123 and 1261). It can happen in the compound, in the vessels, and above all in the outer, acting vessel (lines 1149, 1720, 1743). Or it can come from above the division: inner Keter, and the Essence reached through mesirut nefesh (lines 1174, 1265).
- **Teshuvah as the Essence's work.** Essential evil turned to good is credited to mesirut nefesh reaching the Essence, 'above even the division of essential kindness and severity' (line 1265). The Tanya range has F11 as itkafia and ithapcha. It does not ground the transformation of essential evil in the Essence in these terms.
- **The Essence beyond pleasure.** Lines 1296 and 1327 say plainly that neither the soul's essence nor the Essence of Or Ein Sof is pleasure. It is only what pleasure shines from.
- **Mesirut nefesh sets how deep the union goes.** Torah and mitzvot unite, but without mesirut nefesh only 'in the outer' (line 1481). The Tanya range also says that service without love and awe does not rise (Tanya line 576). The Mitteler Rebbe's line is sharper and names the level reached.
- **The method stated, not assumed.** Line 1601 is the G04 principle written into the middle of the mechanics: all this detail is for one thing only.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- About 83% of line entries are structural and 525 of them carry no face. Chapters 23 (will and pleasure), 25 and 26 (Abba, Imma, the mazalot) and the anatomy in 27-28 and 30 are mechanics. The unity is the reason for learning them (line 1601), but most lines do not say it. No face was forced on them. - Eight lines are *none*. They are illustrations and a transition: the bribe (971, 1018, 1432, 1435), the ill loser (1303), the bursting skin bottle (1409), the eye healed through the foot (1723) and the closing formula of ch. 16 (942). - Two lines are *reading*: 1186 ('be silent, so it arose in thought' as bitul) and 1494 (the sage and the child as the highest reaching the lowest). - Several faces are tagged at the structural level only by analogy. Examples are F06 for the yechidah analogy (1010, 1773-1775) and F10 for Shabbat and Yom Tov mapped onto the partzufim (1455-1458). The text uses these as illustrations, not as claims about the soul or the festivals. - F12 barely appears (4 lines). This part of the book is the content to be contemplated, not the instruction to contemplate. The instruction is in chapters 1-7, outside this range.

## Disagreements kept

- **Is Atzilut one with the Essence, or separate?** The Tanya (Tanya line 568) and the Zohar formula here (line 1513) say one. The same range also says the emanated lights are 'separate from the Essence' (line 1346), and that Atzilut is only 'appearance and colour' (line 1564). It also says Atzilut is 'still united with its very source' (line 1562) and 'still the end of the world of Ein Sof' (line 1770). The text holds both: separate as revelation, one by root. We do not merge them.
- **Where do opposites join?** The SPINE's F09 says 'in Him', in the 'nothing' or the Essence. This range agrees for the Essence (line 1265). It also puts the joining in the lowest place, the outer vessel and the deed (line 1743), and denies it to pure essences (line 1261). Both are the Mitteler Rebbe's. They are kept side by side, not reconciled.
- **Pleasure and essence.** The early Kabbalists said 'nothing is above pleasure' and made it the soul's essence. The Mitteler Rebbe says 'the truth is not so' (line 1296). He then keeps their saying as true of how the Essence shines, not of what it is (lines 1328-1329). The text names this disagreement itself.
- **Knowing the Essence's knowledge.** The Tanya (SH 7, Tanya line 834) says the unity of His Essence and knowledge is only to be believed. This range glosses the kav's self-contained thinking with the Rambam's 'knowing Himself, not by a knowledge outside Him' (line 1046). It gives this as an illustration in a parenthesis and does not claim to comprehend it. Whether that differs from the Alter Rebbe is not settled here. It is recorded, not resolved.
