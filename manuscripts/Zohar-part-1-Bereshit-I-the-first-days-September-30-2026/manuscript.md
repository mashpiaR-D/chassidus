This is the Zohar's reading of the first verses of Genesis, and it is the densest cosmology in the whole work. It opens with the "spark of darkness" (botzina dekardinuta) and the point that breaks out of Ein Sof, walks through the six days as the unfolding of the sefirot, then holds several long passages in the later style of the Tikkunei Zohar and Ra'aya Meheimna (lines 444-557: prayer and the shofar notes, the mixed multitude, Moses and the Shechinah in exile), and returns to "Bereshit" in a second, parallel commentary (lines 553-610). All 350 lines were read in full. Every quote below was checked with `find_he.py verify` against the export and printed "FOUND near line N". A "…" in a quote skips a printed cross-reference or a variant reading in brackets.

## 2. The unity of God

**Ein Sof has no trace.** The range gives the Zohar's plainest statement that the root is beyond any grasp: «אי"ן סו"ף לית ביה רשומא כלל ולא תליא שאלתא ביה ולא רעיונא לאסתכלותא דמחשבה כלל» (Bereshit 12:1; line 397): "Ein Sof has no mark at all; no question reaches it, and no thought for the mind to look at at all." From "the hidden of the hidden" a thin light shines, hidden "like the point of a needle", and only then do letters begin.

**Many forms, one.** The Alef at the head of everything «ולא אקרי אלא אחד. לאחזאה דאף על גב דאית ביה דיוקנין סגיאין לאו איהו אלא חד» (Bereshit 12:2; line 398): "is called only One, to show that though there are many forms in it, it is only one." Rabbi Yosei says the same of the "near" gods (plural) of Deuteronomy 4:7: «וגבורות סגיאין אנון דנפקין מחד וכלהו חד» (Bereshit 35:9; line 606): "and many powers go out from One, and all of them are one."

**The Cause above all causes has no second.** In "Let us make man" the Zohar sets two levels. The "cause of causes" consults with what is under it; but the Cause above all causes consults with none: «איהו חד בלא חושבן ולא שתוף» (Bereshit 15:4; line 431): "He is One without number and without partnership." There is a "one" that is one of a pair, male and female; He is not that one. And in His presence every light is darkness: «ועלת … העלות לית נהורא קיימא קמיה דכל נהורין מתחשכן קמיה» (Bereshit 15:9; line 436): "and before the Cause of causes no light can stand, for all lights darken before Him." This is the source of the Chabad teaching that "He is one, but not in the count" (echad ve'lo bechushban) and that before the Essence the lights are as nothing.

**The whole world is kernel and shell.** After the first light was hidden, a shell was created for the kernel, and that shell made another. The Zohar then generalizes: from the first point to the end of all levels, «כלהו איהו דא לבושא לדא, ודא לדא» (Bereshit 9:9; line 366): "all of it is this a garment for that, and that for another": one inside the other, until even the garment becomes a kernel for the level below. And its verdict on the whole order, shells included: «וכלא איהו תקונא דעלמא» (Bereshit 9:12; line 369): "and all of it is the repair of the world." Ours: this is the frame for the Tanya's "levush" (garment) and for the Chabad claim that concealment is also His.

**Two unities.** The gathering of the waters "to one place" is the knot of the upper world: «וכל שייפין מתכנשין תמן והוו כלהו ביה חד בלא פירודא כלל» (Bereshit 7:3; line 328): "and all the limbs gather there and all become one in it with no separation at all." There the revealed world is united with the hidden world. And the lower unity is its match: «ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד יחודא דלתתא. יחודא עלאה … שמע ישראל יהו"ה אלהינו יהו"ה אחד» (Bereshit 7:7; line 332): "'Blessed be the name of the glory of His kingdom' is the lower unity; the upper unity is 'Hear O Israel, Havayah our God, Havayah is One'." This is the very text the Tanya (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 7) builds its two unities on. The key of knowing the hidden from the revealed: «ומגו האי בתראה אשתמע בסוכלתנו ההוא דאתכסי» (Bereshit 7:2; line 327): "and from within this last one, the hidden one is heard by understanding."

**Mi stands for asking, not for knowing.** In the second commentary: the upper world «קיימא לשאלתא ולא למנדע ביה» (Bereshit 30:22; line 574): "stands to be asked about, and not to be known." You ask only about the place from which things come out, to know that; not to know Him, "for they cannot."

**Separation is for the sake of union.** Even the dividing of light and darkness is not a real split: «והבדלה הוה מסטרוי לאסתכלא אפין באפין ולאתדבקא דא בדא למהוו כלא חד» (Bereshit 32:5; line 591): "the separation was from its sides, so that they look face to face and cleave to each other, so that all be one."

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **Renewed every morning.** Every night the spirit goes up out of its garment and is "eaten" by the fire above, and it comes back new: «בני נשא דאנון חדשים בכל יומא ויומא» (Bereshit 8:21; line 355): "people, who are new each and every day." The vast place above takes in all souls and is not filled, like the sea that the rivers enter (line 356). From inside: waking as being given back. Ties: S65 Given right now.
2. **The moon that made herself small.** The moon complains that two kings cannot share one crown; God says "go and make yourself small." She lowers herself to be head of the lower ones, and from then has no light of her own (lines 373-374): «כדין אזעירת גרמה למהוי רישא לתתאי» (Bereshit 10:3; line 373): "then she made herself small, to be head over the lower ones." Ties: S59 True lowliness. The Zohar also knows the pain in it: the fourth day is "the stone the builders rejected" (line 378).
3. **The dispute that ends in love.** The left side wakes dispute "in strength and anger", and Gehinnom comes from it; when the anger cools, "a dispute of love" wakes (lines 311-313). «ודא איהו ארחהון דצדיקייא שירותא דלהון בקשיו וסופא דלהון בנייחא» (Bereshit 6:9; line 312): "and this is the way of the righteous: their beginning is in hardness and their end is in rest." Korach's dispute was not for the sake of heaven and "denied the work of creation" (line 308). Ties: S15 Angry at someone (as the beginning), S86 The other held (as the end); C7.
4. **The spirit refined like dross.** Tohu and Bohu are the dross of the snow in the waters, refined "once and twice" until no dross is left, and from it come wind, quake, fire and the "still small voice" (lines 277-279). Ours: a picture of the inner refinement before the quiet: first the storm, then the quiet voice. Ties: S32 Quiet, S20 (as the storm).
5. **Before Him all lights go dark.** Line 436. The state of total bittul before the Essence: S57 No self to watch, S62 Only Him.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **Prayer as the poor man at the gate.** In the Tikkunim material: «צריך בר נש למהוי איהו כעני לתרעא דמלכא בצלותא דעמידה» (Bereshit 17:6; line 449): "a person must be like a poor man at the King's gate in the standing prayer," wrapped in the tallit like a poor man, in tefillin like a pauper, for the Shechinah's sake. Ties: technique 2 (before Whom), S59 True lowliness.
- **Raise the thought like a thrown stone.** The shofar note "zarka" (thrown) teaches that «הכי צריך לסלקא מחשבתיה בצלותיה» (Bereshit 18:2; line 461): "so must he raise his thought in his prayer," like a stone thrown to a known place; and even if a serpent is wound round his heel he must not stop. And more: «וצריך לסלקא … עד אין סוף» (Bereshit 18:3; line 462): "he must raise it to Ein Sof," and in bowing bring it down "to no end", not stopping above or below. Ties: C5 Running and returning; technique 12 (returning the wandering mind) as its guard.
- **Torah and prayer with love and awe.** Prayer is the Shechinah, Torah is the Holy One; there must be no break between them, «וצריך לסלקא תורה ומצוה ברחימו ודחילו» (Bereshit 17:12; line 455): "and one must raise Torah and mitzvah with love and awe." This is the Tanya's "two wings" (ch. 40-41) in its source.
- **Teshuvah and Torah lift a person out of the elements.** When a person returns and learns, God takes him out of the four elements, «אפריש נפשיה מתאוה דילהון ושוי ליה בגנתא דיליה דאיהי שכינתא» (Bereshit 24:2; line 520): "separates his soul from their desire and sets him in His garden, which is the Shechinah," to work it with the positive commands and guard it with the negative. Ties: S77, S79.
- **Mitzvot dress the Shechinah.** «וכל מאן דמקיים פקודין דאורייתא כאלו הוא לביש לשכינתא בלבושהא» (Bereshit 16:7; line 443): "and whoever keeps the commandments of the Torah, it is as if he dresses the Shechinah in her garments"; whoever sins strips her (line 442). Ours: a motive for the deed that is not about oneself at all. Ties: S81 The deed in the hand, S82 The home below.
- **Guard: keep to the edges.** The rings and poles of the Ark: whoever approaches, approaches by the poles «ולא במה דלגו» (Bereshit 34:3; line 597): "and not by what is inside", as the Nazirite is told: go around the vineyard, do not come near. Only those fit to serve inside may enter. A guard for any deep practice.
- **Guard: the Pardes.** Of the four who entered, Ben Azzai and Ben Zoma entered "the shells of the Torah" and were struck; Rabbi Akiva entered the kernel and came out in peace (line 511). Ours: the danger is not depth but entering by the shell.
- **Guard: anger.** «כל הכועס כאלו עובד עבודה זרה» (Bereshit 25:1; line 525): "whoever is angry is as if he worships idols." The Tanya repeats this (Iggeret HaKodesh 25).

## 5. The big human questions

- **Why was man made able to sin?** The companions ask: would it not be better if man could not sin? Rabbi Shimon: without good and evil inclinations, light and darkness, there would be no merit and no guilt. And the Torah was made for him, with reward and punishment. They answer: «דודאי לא ברא קודשא בריך הוא מלתא דלאו איהו צריך» (Bereshit 16:5; line 441): "for surely the Holy One did not create a thing that is not needed." And: if man sins, repentance was made before him (line 439). Ties: S34 Two voices, S10 Torn in two.
- **The hidden light.** The first light, in which Adam saw from one end of the world to the other, was hidden for the righteous; Moses used it for his first three months and again from Sinai, and it was on his face (lines 608-610). The good that is stored, not lost.
- **A good thought is joined to the deed.** Of Moses who wished to bring the mixed multitude back: «דקודשא בריך הוא מחשבה טובה מצרפה למעשה» (Bereshit 28:2; line 546): "for the Holy One joins a good thought to the deed." Ties: S29 Good thought joined to deed.
- **Face to face.** The earth, parted from heaven, was "tohu and bohu" because she wanted to cleave to heaven as before and saw the heaven shining while she was dark; the light came to her «ותבת באתרה לאסתכלא בשמיא אפין באפין» (Bereshit 32:4; line 590): "and she returned to her place to look at the heavens face to face." Ours: longing that is not cured by merging but by facing. Ties: S13 The unnamed longing, S74 Seen eye to eye.
- **The broken lights of exile.** David in exile "took pain" for the Shechinah, dry and parched; when he saw Israel return in joy he set ten kinds of song (line 447). Grief for the Shechinah, then song.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **"All of it is the repair of the world" (line 369).** Kernel and shell all the way down, and the shells too are part of the repair. The guide can use this for the person who feels his outer life is only covering.
2. **Beginning in hardness, ending in rest (line 312).** A shape for anger and conflict that the guide does not otherwise name so plainly: the first dispute burns; the second, after it cools, is a dispute of love.
3. **The moon's smallness** as a real state with its pain.
4. **Two unities with the Zohar's own text** (lines 328, 332), under the Tanya's.
5. **Stopping at the edge by keeping to the poles (line 597)**: a guard the guide can give in one line.

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "Face to face after parting."** Inside: you were close, were set apart, and ache, seeing the other lit while you are dark; then light comes and you return to your place and look face to face. Enter: by accepting the separation as the condition for facing (lines 590-591). Near S13 and S74.
- **State, new: "The dispute of love."** Inside: the anger has cooled; you still disagree, but now for the sake of heaven, and the disagreement holds you together. Enter: wait until the heat goes down; do not settle in heat (lines 311-313). Near S86.
- **Technique, new: "The thrown stone."** In prayer, throw the thought up past every level "to Ein Sof", and in bowing bring it down "to no end"; do not stop if something grips the heel (lines 461-462). Guard: technique 12.
