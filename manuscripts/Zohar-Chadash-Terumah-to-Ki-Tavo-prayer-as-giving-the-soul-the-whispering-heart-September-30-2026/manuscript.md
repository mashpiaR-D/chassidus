## What this range is

Twelve nodes from Exodus to Deuteronomy. Terumah opens with the morning order of prayer read as a ladder, and the Mishkan as a dwelling below. Ki Tisa reads the Ten Commandments through thought, and makes fear the gate. Tzav has one short passage on turning a bad thought into a pure one. Achrei Mot has the Shema in the limbs, midnight, and a Shabbat home. Chukat reads Psalm 45 with the Song of Songs and David's "my heart whispers". Balak has the midnight verses of the early pious and God weeping over His children. Vaetchanan is the Kav HaMiddah, the "Measuring Line", one of the deepest pieces in the Zohar Chadash on the Infinite. Ki Tavo says the curses of Deuteronomy are wrapped in love.

## The unity of G-d

> וכד כנסת ישראל בגלותא, לא איקרי אחד (ZC Ki Tisa 3, line 1934)
> *When the Community of Israel is in exile, He is not called One.* (The line before: the Holy One and the Community of Israel together are called one.)

This is the Zohar's teaching that "One" is also a union to be made, not only a fact.

**Witness that He is One.** God says to Israel, "My people, what have I done to you?":

> ותסהידו בי דאנא אחד (ZC Achrei Mot 58, line 2104)
> *[Only that you go to the synagogues] and bear witness that I am One.*

**No one but Him.** Israel was not given to angels or to hosts:

> אלא לקודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי (ZC Balak 68, line 2398)
> *Only to the Holy One alone* ("for the Lord's portion is His people").

> כל אסוותא דעלמא, בידא דקודשא בריך הוא (ZC Balak 49, line 2379)
> *All the healing in the world is in the hand of the Holy One.*

**The Measuring Line: from the Infinite to the last rung.** The Kav HaMiddah reads the Shema as the whole of existence:

> מאתר דלית ביה שיעורא דאקרי אין סוף (ZC Vaetchanan 7, line 2481)
> *From the place that has no measure, which is called Ein Sof* (to the last end of all rungs).

> בר לעילא לעילא, דלית שיעורא (ZC Vaetchanan 21, line 2495)
> *Except for the highest of high, which has no measure* (and it does not stand to be looked at or known).

**The hidden heart.** Why so few know the unity of the Shema:

> לבא אטים הוא מניה (ZC Vaetchanan 2, line 2476)
> *The heart is closed off from it.* (Many wise men labor and do not know and do not cleave.)

**The Mishkan: dwelling below as above.**

> למהוי דיורא דלתתא, כגוונא דדיורא דלעילא (ZC Terumah 36, line 1889)
> *So that there be a dwelling below like the dwelling above* (joined like soul and body, to be all one).

This is the root of F07 (a dwelling for Him in the lowest world) as the Zohar Chadash states it.

## States of consciousness

**Giving the soul in love (S62 Only Him, and the self-offering of prayer).** In the morning order, at the blessing "Shield of Abraham":

> כשלהובא בגחלת, ברחימו דמאריה (ZC Terumah 6, line 1859)
> *Like a flame in a coal, in love of his Master.* (To be bound up in Him in the heart.)

And at nefilat apayim, falling on the face:

> ברעותא דלבא ישוי גרמיה לקודשא בריך הוא, דמסר ליה נפשיה ברחימו (ZC Terumah 14, line 1867)
> *With the will of the heart he should set himself before the Holy One as one who hands Him his soul in love.* (And the Holy One counts it as if He took his soul, and gives it back.)

We propose (ours): **Z-T15 Handing over the soul.** In the moment of falling on the face after the Amidah (or any short moment of bowing), hand the soul over in love, as one who is ready not to take it back, and then receive it back as a gift. The Chabad liturgy already has the intent. The Zohar Chadash gives the inner movement plainly and says God counts it as real.

**The whispering heart.** David could not even move his lips:

> ואפילו בשפוותיה לא אתייהיב ליה רשו לרחשא, אלא בלביה (ZC Chukat 63, line 2255)
> *Even with his lips he was not given leave to whisper; only in his heart.* ("My heart whispers a good word.")

We propose (ours): **Z-S16 The heart that whispers.** Prayer that has gone below the lips. Different from silence and from blankness: a word is moving, but only in the heart. It is the inner side of technique 18 (lips), for when the lips cannot or should not move.

> כחדוה דנשמתהון דצדיקייא דאינון קריבין ליה (ZC Chukat 109, line 2301)
> *[There is no joy before Him from all His chariots] like the joy of the souls of the righteous who are near Him.*

**Become like a child (joy against anger).** On "serve the Lord in joy", the Zohar Chadash says the cherubim have faces like children, and anyone angry who has a child come to him will calm:

> לית רחימו וחביבו בר ברבייא (ZC Terumah 74, line 1927)
> *There is no love and fondness except with a child.*

We propose (ours): **Z-S17 The child's face.** The softening that comes when one meets God as a child meets a father; joy that dissolves anger. It ties to S54 Compassion but faces upward.

**A Shabbat home (S76 Shabbos).** Coming home from the synagogue, the angels and the Shechinah see:

> ויחזו שרגא נהרא, ופתורא מתתקנא (ZC Achrei Mot 97, line 2143)
> *And they see a lamp shining and a table set* (and husband and wife in joy).

> זה שלי הוא (ZC Achrei Mot 97, line 2143)
> *This one is mine* (says the Shechinah).

**The heart moved to cleave (S13).** Of charity:

> וידבק ביה בקודשא בריך הוא (ZC Behar 7, line 2178)
> *And he will cleave to the Holy One* (when he sets his heart and will).

**Expansion of the heart.** The Kav HaMiddah ends by saying the measuring stands:

> גו רעותא, ואתפשטותא דלבא, ומוחא, ורוחא (ZC Vaetchanan 58, line 2532)
> *Within the will, and the expansion of the heart, the mind, and the spirit.*

## Meditation and practice

**Turning a bad thought (ties to 12 returning the mind).** The olah is for thoughts. The movement:

> ואתפרש בר נש מגו מחשבה רעה, ואתדבק במחשבה טהורה (ZC Tzav 6, line 2044)
> *And the person separates from the bad thought and cleaves to the pure thought* (until all cleaves to the hidden upper thought, and all is one).

We propose (ours): **Z-T16 Three steps of thought.** When a bad thought comes, do not fight it head on. Leave it for a pure thought (a verse, a Name, a good memory of someone), and from the pure thought rise to the "hidden thought", the simple sense of Him. Technique 12 returns the mind. This adds the ladder: bad, pure, hidden, one.

**The Shema into the limbs (ties to 7 detail by detail; 18 lips).**

> כל תיבה ותיבה משפיע בכל אבר ואבר דיליה (ZC Achrei Mot 61, line 2107)
> *Each and every word flows into each and every limb of his.* (The 248 words of the Shema and the 248 limbs.)

We propose (ours): **Z-T17 Shema through the body.** Say the Shema slowly and let each word land in the body, as if it went into a limb. It is not magic and needs no count. The point is that the Shema is said by the whole person, not only the mind. It pairs with the Tikkunim's "crown Him limb by limb" (Z-T09).

**The midnight verses of the early pious.** The node gives an exact short order:

> ה' אלהי אתה ארוממך אודה שמך (ZC Balak 2, line 2332)
> *"Lord, You are my God; I will exalt You, I will praise Your Name"* (Isaiah 25:1, then "I thank You for I am awesomely made" and "a river goes out of Eden").

> שבחא דחסידי קדמאי, כד מתערו בפלגות ליליא (ZC Balak 2, line 2332)
> *The praise of the early pious ones when they woke at midnight* (and then they set out their praises and learned Torah).

We propose (ours): **Z-T18 Midnight waking verses.** On waking in the night, say these three verses with the heart's intent, then learn a little. For a person who wakes at night with worry, this gives the waking a direction.

> זכאה איהו כל בר נש דיקום בפלגות ליליא לאתעסקא באורייתא (ZC Achrei Mot 52, line 2098)
> *Happy is anyone who rises at midnight to study Torah.*

**Learning on the road joins the Shechinah.**

> נימא מילי דאורייתא, ונתעסק בה, ונשתתף בשכינתא (ZC Balak 24, line 2354)
> *Let us speak words of Torah, engage in it, and join the Shechinah.*

**Blessing with the heart.** The priests' hands rise lightly:

> ובריך לון ברעו דלבא (ZC Terumah 65, line 1918)
> *And he blesses them with the will of the heart.*

**Honoring the image.** Going to prayer in tallit and tefillin, angels announce:

> הבו יקרא לדיוקנא דמלכא קדישא (ZC Terumah 1, line 1854)
> *Give honor to the image of the holy King.*

> איבעי ליה לאשפלא גרמיה (ZC Chukat 21, line 2213)
> *He must lower himself* (after he has cleaved to Torah and sat on the heights).

## The big questions

> דהא על ידא דאורייתא אוליף בר נש פולחנא דמאריה (ZC Ki Tisa 34, line 1965)
> *For through the Torah a person learns the service of his Master* (without it people would not know Him).

> מאן דחשיב למעבד מצוה, כאלו עשאה (ZC Ki Tisa 45, line 1976)
> *One who thinks to do a mitzvah, it is as if he did it.*

> רישא למנדע לקודשא בריך הוא במחשבתיה (ZC Ki Tisa 50, line 1981)
> *The beginning: to know the Holy One in his thought* (the end: not to let thought and will stray to the other side).

> ויהבא למאן דלית ליה זכותא מדיליה (ZC Ki Tisa 68, line 1999)
> *And she gives to one who has no merit of his own.*

> כל בר נש דלא דחיל חטאה, לית ליה רשו למיעל להאי פתחא דמהימנותא (ZC Ki Tisa 81, line 2012)
> *Any person who does not fear sin has no leave to enter this gate of faith.*

**Will I be forgiven if I keep remembering my sin?**

> קודשא בריך הוא מחיל ליה, ולא גבי מניה (ZC Ki Tisa 102, line 2033)
> *The Holy One forgives him and does not collect from him* (one who returns and fears that sin every day).

> דהא לית רחימו לקודשא בריך הוא בהדי בר נש בעלמא, אלא למאן דתב מחוביה (ZC Balak 38, line 2368)
> *The Holy One has no love for a person in the world like for one who returns from his sin.*

> כמה היא עילאה חילא דתיובתא על כלא (ZC Chukat 11, line 2203)
> *How high is the power of teshuvah over everything.*

> אתי לאקמא לה מעפרא (ZC Chukat 128, line 2320)
> *He comes to raise her from the dust* (when they return with a whole heart).

> לא נשארו בגלות (ZC Achrei Mot 68, line 2114)
> *[If they returned,] they would not remain in exile* (even one whole day).

**Does God suffer with us?** In Balak God weeps over His children and says to them:

> אנא ואתון נגלי (ZC Balak 88, line 2418)
> *I and you, we will go into exile.*

And later the Shechinah goes after her son:

> כאימא דאזלא בתר ברה, בשווקים (ZC Balak 112, line 2442)
> *Like a mother who goes after her son in the markets* (for fear someone will harm him).

> לא חשיב ליה קודשא בריך הוא חובה, בגין דלאו איהו בדעתיה (ZC Achrei Mot 101, line 2147)
> *The Holy One does not count it as a sin, because he is not in his right mind.* (Said of the poor man who speaks against God in his distress.)

**Is the robbed man bitter?** A Jew robbed on the road says:

> דאנא לא חיישנא עלי (ZC Balak 40, line 2370)
> *For I do not worry about myself* (only about my old parents and the poor bride whose money I carried).

> ומלקיותא כלילין ברחמי (ZC Ki Tavo 9, line 2624)
> *And the lashes are wrapped in mercy.* (Like a father who loves his son, holds the strap, and cries out loud.)

> מאן דאית ליה לבא, יסתכל וידע לאתבא למאריה (ZC Ki Tavo 20, line 2635)
> *Whoever has a heart, let him look and know to return to his Master.*

> ווי ליה, למאן דאתא למפתי למאריה, בלבא רחיקא (ZC Nasso 3, line 2191)
> *Woe to one who comes to flatter his Master with a distant heart.*

> מאן דשצי חסד מעלמא, אשתיצי הוא (ZC Matot 7, line 2474)
> *One who wipes kindness out of the world is himself wiped out.*

**Is the struggle with the evil inclination normal?**

> וכל שייפין דגופא בצערא בינייהו (ZC Ki Teitzei 6, line 2574)
> *And all the limbs of the body are in pain between them* (between the soul and the evil inclination, every day).

**What does the soul know after death?**

> ומשגת מה שלא תוכל להשיג בעודה בגוף (ZC Ki Teitzei 43, line 2611)
> *And it grasps what it could not grasp while in the body.*

> ולית חפשי אלא כל גבר דידע שמא (ZC Achrei Mot 12, line 2058)
> *And no one is free except a man who knows the Name* (as it should be known).

**Why is a minyan so important?** The Shechinah comes first to the synagogue and asks:

> מדוע באתי ואין איש (ZC Behar 16, line 2187)
> *Why have I come and there is no one?*

> דכל חד וחד מישראל ידביק אדבקותא בחכמתא עילאה (ZC Chukat 138, line 2330)
> *That every one of Israel will cleave with a cleaving to the upper wisdom* (the secret of His Name).

> זכאה איהו, מאן דזכי דישבוק ברא בעלמא (ZC Achrei Mot 113, line 2159)
> *Happy is one who merits to leave a son in the world* (who learns Torah; the story is of a sinner saved by the child someone else raised).

**Why is the Temple mourned?** At every watch of the night:

> אוי שהחרבתי את ביתי (ZC Achrei Mot 42, line 2088)
> *Woe that I destroyed My house.*

## Chassidus and the Zohar Chadash here (ours)

Chassidus agrees closely. "When the Community of Israel is in exile He is not called One" (1934) is the Zohar root of the Chassidic teaching that our service "makes" the unity of the Holy One and the Shechinah felt below. The Mishkan as a dwelling below like above (1889) is the dirah betachtonim of Tanya chapter 36. Handing over the soul at nefilat apayim (1867) is exactly how the Alter Rebbe explains that moment. The movement from a bad thought to a pure one to the hidden thought (2044) matches the Tanya's advice not to wrestle with foreign thoughts but to turn away to holiness.

Where Chassidus goes further: the Kav HaMiddah (2481, 2495) speaks of a line drawn from Ein Sof. The Arizal will make the kav the center of his teaching on tzimtzum, and the Rebbe Rashab and the Rebbe will read "the highest of high that has no measure" (2495) as the Essence beyond both line and light. The Zohar Chadash names the place and stops.

What the guide should take (ours): Z-T15, Z-T16, Z-T17, Z-T18 as practices; Z-S16 and Z-S17 as states; lines 2147 and 2370 for people in poverty or loss; line 2368 for someone who thinks their past has made them unloved; line 2624 for someone who reads suffering as rejection. Leave out the shed yehudai at 2066-2067, Balak's sorcery at 2389-2397 and the messianic scenario at 2436-2467, apart from the mother image at 2442.
