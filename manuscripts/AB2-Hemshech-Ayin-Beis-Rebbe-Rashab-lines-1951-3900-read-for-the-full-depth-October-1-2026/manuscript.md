## What this slice adds (one page)

The Rebbe Rashab in this stretch of the Hemshech is a master of the *inside* of avodah. The ideas (or and kli, tzimtzum, screens, Atzilus and BYA) are the frame; again and again he stops to say what it is like in a person, often in Yiddish, and where a person goes wrong. What it adds to 'Go deeper':

1. **Knowing is not yet seeing.** Understanding shines only while you think; then 'the love passes and goes'. Seeing stays, like a beautiful thing you once saw. You get there not by a new idea but by pressing the same one 'with strength' until it becomes recognition, and the place to start is low: the real sky and earth (C01, C18).

2. **How to tell the real from the false.** A longing to rise with no accounting of what is wrong; a storm over sins that leaves 'not even a mark'; a cry that is 'only feeling himself, without feeling G-d'; a ratzo without bittul (Nadav and Avihu's 'strange fire'); anger at sinners after fiery prayer. Each is named, and the true form given (C02, C03, C14, C15).

3. **The stillness under love.** 'Love is not bittul, for there is one who loves.' Two depths of 'only You': 'with You there is something' and 'even with You, nothing'. The mind that 'does not lose itself' is moved most deeply where it does not show. Inside the heart a fire 'smolders very deep', and the inner wind is just that 'he lives in it' (C09, C11, C16).

4. **Bottoms the text itself finds.** A will for G-d in the soul's essence that never stops, seen in a simple Jew and surfacing even in joy (C05); the test that has no being of its own, 'G-dliness iz a zach' (C04); the mind's blank, astonishment, as the bridge to a new insight (C08); the soul's taste for G-dliness that clings without understanding (C10); the name, which calls the self from under all its powers (C17); the child who says 'Abba' without knowing what a father is (D9).

5. **The returns are plain.** Modeh Ani as setting yourself down; a second page of learning; charity beyond your will, in secret; eating in order to learn and pray; praying for the Shechinah's lack and not your own; being after prayer as in prayer (C06, C12, C20, avodah).

6. **The lowest gets the plainest mercy.** The 'heel' soul, for whom contemplation stays on the surface, has 'a wonderful rise' through honest bitterness and pity on its own soul, because 'it is the nature of the exalted to be drawn to the lowly' (C13).

**How to use it with DESCENT (ours).** AB2-D1 sharpens D3 into two named steps (ein zulascha / efes bilt'cha). AB2-D3 and AB2-D11 sharpen D2: the deepest closeness has no lover standing in it, and its fire does not show. AB2-D4 extends D5: the mind's blank is the doorway, not the wall. AB2-D5 goes under D8: a will that never stops. AB2-D6 extends D9: the bottom of distance is where the plainest mercy falls. AB2-D7 extends D6: under all your names is the one that, called, turns all of you. AB2-D8 extends D13: plainness reversed. AB2-D2 and AB2-D10 are new or extend D10: the test is not there; 'it does not touch Him' and 'He wants it' are both true.

**Guards (ours, from the text's own balances).** The text never makes self-loss the goal: Nadav and Avihu's sin was not wanting to come back (C14), and 'throwing life away' is real only for the animal soul (C02). The G-dly soul must not fall into 'the rot of sadness' (C02); joy is 'the sign of G-dly life' (C03). Do not skip steps in prayer (avodah). Anger, even for Heaven's sake, is 'harsh severity' (C15). Every bottom is followed by deed.

## AB2-C01 · Balak 5674 (lines 2180-2183); Shemini 5675 (3717-3718); Emor 5675 (3815-3820)

**Start.** You have understood that G-d makes the world from nothing. You were moved while you thought about it. An hour later it is gone. Why?

1. **Understanding, even good understanding, shines only while you are thinking. When you stop, it leaves, and the love that came from it leaves with it.** (M1) *How:* The text asks why love born of contemplation passes. Its answer: understanding is like hearing; it does not settle into the inner soul. «ורק בשעת ההתבוננות אז מאיר ונרגש אצלו, ואח"כ מסתלק ממנו וממילא גם האה' חולפת ועוברת» — *Only while he contemplates does it shine and is it felt; afterwards it leaves him, and so the love too passes and goes.* · line 3816

2. **Moshe already knew about the golden calf. G-d told him plainly. He broke the tablets only when he saw it. Seeing does something that knowing, even sure knowing, does not.** (M9) *How:* The proof from Moshe: clear knowledge from G-d's own mouth did not move his hands; the sight did. «והלא ידע זאת מקודם בבירור שא"ל הקב"ה סרו מהר» — *But he knew this beforehand, clearly, for the Holy One told him, 'They have turned aside quickly'.* · line 2180

3. **Think of something beautiful you have seen. It stays in front of your eyes. A thing you only heard about you picture for a moment, and then it fades.** (M9) *How:* A plain mashal from ordinary life: the seen stays, the heard is forgotten. «וכמו כאשר רואה אדם איזה דבר טוב ויפה הרי הדבר עומד תמיד נגד עיניו» — *As when a person sees something good and beautiful: it stands always before his eyes.* · line 3817

4. **So the place to start seeing is low and close: the real sky and the real earth. Look at them until you see that they exist only from G-d's power. That plain looking makes a deeper bittul than lofty ideas you only hear about.** (M15) *How:* The text says that in the physical heaven and earth the infinite power is seen 'with the senses', because something from nothing is not in the nature of creatures; and Isaiah gives this-world contemplation, not angels, for that reason. «דכשירגיל עצמו בראי' זו בשמים וארץ גשמי' לראות ביטולם לאלקות שזהו בחי' ראי' חושי' ממש» — *When he trains himself in this seeing, in the physical heaven and earth, to see their nullity to G-dliness, which is real sense-sight.* · line 3717

5. **How do you get from understanding to seeing? Not by a new idea. By pressing your mind hard into the same idea until it becomes recognition. Then it stays and does not leave.** (M16) *How:* Seeing is not a gift outside the work; it comes from 'deepening the daas with strength' in the grasp you already have. «וע"י ההעמקה שמעמיק דעתו בחוזק בההשגה עד שבא לידי הכרה ולידי ראי' שרואה אמיתי' הענין» — *Through the deepening, that he deepens his mind with strength in the grasp, until he comes to recognition and to seeing, that he sees the truth of the matter.* · line 3816

6. **Even seeing can still be a rush. You are thrilled because it is new to you, and it is new because you are still someone apart. Moshe saw face to face and stayed calm, because he was not a separate self; his seeing settled into doing the mitzvos.** (M12) *How:* The text turns on seeing itself: the burning that comes with it shows the seer is 'still a thing of his own'. The settled seeing of Moshe comes out in Torah and mitzvos. «מפני שהוא בעצמו הוא מהות דבר בפ"ע עדיין שאינו בבחי' ביטול ממש להיות בבחי' כלי אל האור» — *Because he himself is still a thing of his own, not truly nullified so as to be a vessel for the light.* · line 3819

**Bottom.** Seeing that stays: the truth of the matter standing before you always, like a beautiful thing you once saw (3816-3817); and under it, Moshe's calm seeing that is no longer an event (3820).

**Return.** Down to the sky over your head and the ground under your feet (3717), and to doing the mitzvos in deed, which is where settled seeing lives (3820).

## AB2-C02 · Re'eh 5674 (lines 2458-2475)

**Start.** You feel a strong pull to G-d: 'It is not good for me down here. I want to throw it all away.' Is that teshuvah?

1. **Ask yourself: what exactly is not good? A longing to rise with no accounting of what is wrong is not yet a turn to G-d.** (M1) *How:* The text's first false arousal: he feels it is bad to be below, but has 'no accounting in his soul' of what is bad. «והיינו שאין לו חשבון בנפשו מה לא טוב לו היותו למטה (וואס איז עם ניט גוט)» — *He has no accounting in his soul of what is not good for him in being below (what is not good for him).* · line 2458

2. **If you counted, you would find two things. If it is the narrowness of the soul down here, then work to understand as well as you can here, because the deepest drawing of Torah happens only below. If it is the hiding, then be bitter about it and fix your ways.** (M2) *How:* Each answer to 'what is not good' gives its own work; the soul's advantage below is that Torah's essential light is drawn only here. «מ"ש וירם קרן לעמו בחי' המשכה עצמיות דתו' שזהו דוקא למטה» — *'He raises the horn of His people': the essential drawing of Torah, which is only below.* · line 2459

3. **The second false arousal is a storm over your sins. You want to throw everything away. But it is an outburst, not an inner hearing, and afterwards not even a mark of it is left.** (M4) *How:* The text names it in Yiddish: 'a hispaalus-dike derher', not 'a pnimiyus-dike derher'; the wish to throw it all away 'is really only imagined'. «שבאמת הוא רק מדמה לבד שמדמה להשליך הכל מנגד» — *That in truth he only imagines; he imagines throwing everything away.* · line 2461

4. **Real teshuvah has to reach the animal soul too. Picture a channel of water that was dammed. When it breaks through, it breaks the dam and carries the dam along with it.** (M9) *How:* The mashal of the dammed channel: the G-dly soul bursting out of its narrow place breaks the coarseness of the animal soul, and the animal soul itself must be moved. «וכידוע המשל מאמת המים כשסותמין אותה ה"ה שוברת את הסתימה (לבד מה שלוקחת הסתימה אתה)» — *As in the known mashal of a channel of water: when it is blocked it breaks the blockage (besides taking the blockage along with it).* · line 2464

5. **Only the animal soul is allowed to feel that it can find no comfort at all, because it is the guilty one. The G-dly soul's pain is greater, but it cannot fall into that kind of collapse; that would be like the rot of sadness.** (M10) *How:* Here the comparison breaks: the G-dly soul hurts more yet must not be crushed; the crushing belongs only to the animal side, and that is what changes it. «אבל מ"מ אינו שייך בו שפלות זו (דיא ארא(גיפאלינקייט) שלא ימצא נוחם לנפשו כלל (שזהו כמו רקבון דעצבות» — *Yet this lowness (the fallen-down-ness) does not belong to it, that it should find no comfort at all (which is like the rot of sadness).* · line 2465

6. **So contemplate to quiet the excitement, not to stir it. Hold your powers back so the feeling goes inward, where even your natural self can feel it.** (M7) *How:* The text reverses what we expect: the aim of contemplation here is to calm the soul's excitement so the matter settles inside. «כ"א אדרבא להשקיט התפעלות הנפש ולעצור בעד כחות הנפש שלא יתפעלו כ"כ ויהי' בבחי' הרגש פנימי יותר» — *Rather, the opposite: to quiet the soul's excitement and hold back its powers so that they are not so stirred, and it becomes a more inward feeling.* · line 2473

7. **Where does real comfort come from? Not from fainting toward G-d. From a strong decision in Torah, mitzvos and G-d's ways. Exactly there you find rest for your soul.** (M15) *How:* Even if the soul expires in longing, that is not its comfort; the outcome is a firm resolution, and 'in this specifically' he finds comfort. «המצות ובעסק התורה ובהלוך בדרכי ה' ובזה דוקא ימצא נוחם נפשו» — *In the mitzvos, in Torah study and in walking in G-d's ways; and in this specifically he will find comfort for his soul.* · line 2475

**Bottom.** The animal soul's own 'no comfort at all', which breaks it in its essence (2465); the G-dly soul is kept from that collapse.

**Return.** A plain, strong resolution about tomorrow's Torah and mitzvos, which is where the soul rests (2475).

## AB2-C03 · Bo 5675 (lines 3326-3329)

**Start.** You feel dry. Torah has no taste, prayer has no life, and you cry out about it. Surely that cry is holy?

1. **There is a real cry that comes from spiritual distress: no life in Torah or prayer, and you beg to have your G-dly soul back.** (M3) *How:* The text first honors the cry from the narrow place, whether out loud or the silent cry of the heart. «שחפץ ומבקש שתשוב רוחו ונפשו האלקי' אליו ויחי' בחיי האלקות» — *He wants and begs that his spirit and G-dly soul return to him and he live the life of G-dliness.* · line 3326

2. **But if the cry is only 'it hurts me', and you do not feel what you are far from, it is still cold. It is feeling yourself without feeling G-d.** (M4) *How:* The turn: the cry comes from the heart's point, yet without feeling the greatness he is far from, it is self-feeling. «כי זה רק הרגש עצמו בלא הרגש אלקי» — *For this is only feeling himself, without feeling G-d.* · line 3327

3. **You can tell by the result. Such a cry leaves you stuck in sadness, with no joy, or close to despair. Joy is the sign of G-dly life.** (M2) *How:* The text gives a test: the self-feeling cry does not save the soul; it stays in its sorrow. «וע"כ הוא נשאר במיצר ועוצב זה ואין לו שמחה בנפשו (שזהו סימן על חיות אלקי» — *So he remains in this narrowness and sorrow, and has no joy in his soul (which is the sign of G-dly life).* · line 3327

4. **Why does it happen? A person can serve with his open mind and heart while his soul's very point is wrapped inside other wants, without his knowing it.** (M11) *How:* The root of what blocks: the inner point is covered by other wills, even hidden ones, as in 'I do not know by which road they lead me'. «דעם היות שעובד את ה' בכחות הגלוים במוח ולב מ"מ יכול להיות אשר עצם נפשו הוא בתוך הקלי'» — *Even though he serves G-d with the open powers of mind and heart, still his very soul can be inside the kelipah.* · line 3328

5. **The fire that is real comes from thinking about how great G-d is until there is one want only, like a wife's desire for her husband. A love like that has to be true, or it is no love at all.** (M5) *How:* Higher still: fiery love from contemplation is not a natural love, so it exists only when it is real. «כי אם לא תהי' האהבה באמת הרי אינה אהבה כלל» — *For if the love is not true, it is no love at all.* · line 3329

6. **That fire eats the other fire. Each time you strengthen the holy fire, it cools and burns away the natural heat. At first it only forces it down; deeper, it turns the darkness itself to light.** (M15) *How:* 'Fire consuming fire': the G-dly heat cools the animal heat; in the outer heart by force, in the inner heart by turning it over. «ובכל פעם שמגביר בחי' אש האלקי ה"ז מקרר ומכלה את האש הטבעי» — *Each time he strengthens the G-dly fire, it cools and consumes the natural fire.* · line 3329

**Bottom.** The soul's own point found wrapped in other wills (3328): the dryness is not the bottom, the covering is.

**Return.** Back to the next contemplation of His greatness, and to the small daily act of strengthening the holy fire (3329).

## AB2-C04 · Shabbos Chol Hamoed Sukkos 5675 (lines 2730-2732); Ki Sisa 5675 (3540)

**Start.** Something is pulling at you that you know is wrong. It feels huge and real. Is it?

1. **Every Jew holds a simple faith: there is nothing outside G-d. Nothing at all stands apart from Him.** (M3) *How:* The foundation is not a proof but the simple faith in every Jew, which needs no sign. «שהרי אין לך דבר שחוץ ממנו כו', שזהו האמונה פשוטה שבכאו"א מישראל באחדות ה'» — *For there is nothing outside Him; this is the simple faith in every Jew in G-d's oneness.* · line 2730

2. **Then what is the thing that pulls you away from Him? Because it is cut off from His oneness, it has no real existence. Only the infinite power can hold up something that is not real, so that it looks real, and even very real.** (M4) *How:* The but-in-truth: kelipah's being separated from Him is exactly why it is not a being at all. «והוא רק כח הא"ס להעמיד דבר שאינו מציאות כלל רק דבר נדמה בבחי' דמיון בלבד שיהי' נראה למציאות» — *It is only the power of the Infinite that can hold up a thing that is no existence at all, only a seeming, an image, so that it looks like existence.* · line 2731

3. **That is why the Torah says 'G-d is testing you'. The test is a big thing to you, but the thing itself is nothing; it was set up only to be a test. When you stand firm, it is cancelled and leaves no mark.** (M2) *How:* The text's question: how is 'it is only a test' an answer, when a test is hard? Answer: the test-thing is not an existence in itself. «הנסיון אינו שום מציאות בעצמו), וכאשר האדם עומד בנסיון ה"ה מתבטל לגמרי ואינו נשאר שום רושם כלל» — *The test is no existence in itself; and when a person stands up to the test, it is cancelled entirely and no mark at all remains.* · line 2731

4. **Where is G-d's purpose, then? Not in the thing. In you, the one who stands and does not do it.** (M7) *How:* The divine intent is moved from the object to the person: unlike permitted things, the test-thing has nothing G-dly in it. «כ"א בהאדם שעומד בנסיון שלא יעשה הדבר בזה הוא הכוונה אלקית» — *Rather in the person who stands up to the test and does not do the thing: in this is the Divine intent.* · line 2732

5. **G-dliness is a real thing. Whatever is cut off from G-dliness is not a thing at all. Permitted things, used for a G-dly purpose, are real.** (M13) *How:* The text gathers it into one Yiddish line: G-dliness 'iz a zach'. «כי אלקות הוא דבר (איז א זאך) ומה שנפרד מאלקות אינו במציאות דבר כלל» — *For G-dliness is a thing ('is a thing'), and what is separated from G-dliness is no thing at all.* · line 2732

6. **But knowing this takes a G-dly feeling. Without it, you will take the test for a real wall. With it, you know it is only a test and you stand. Even so, it is not easy; it takes great strength of soul.** (M12) *How:* Both sides held: the test is nothing, yet standing against it is real work that needs 'great strength'. «וכאשר יש הרגש אלקי בנפשו ה"ה יודע שזהו רק נסיון לבד ועומד נגד המונע כו', אבל אין זה עבודה קלה כלל» — *When there is a G-dly feeling in his soul he knows it is only a test, and he stands against the obstacle; but this is no easy work at all.* · line 3540

**Bottom.** G-dliness 'is a thing' and nothing else is (2732): the pull has no being of its own.

**Return.** To the next real moment of pull: stand, and it leaves no trace (2731); the purpose was you, standing (2732).

## AB2-C05 · Vayeitzei 5675 (lines 3005-3018)

**Start.** You love G-d when you think about Him. When you stop thinking, where is the love?

1. **There are two kinds of love. A love that comes from thinking exists only while you think. A love that is part of you, like a parent and child, is always there, even when it is not showing.** (M2) *How:* The text defines essential love by the parent-child mashal: its arousal is only its showing. «דאהבה עצמיות היא שישנה תמיד גם כשאינה בהתגלות וכמו אהבת האב אל הבן או הבן אל האב» — *Essential love is there always even when it is not revealed, like the love of a father for a son or a son for a father.* · line 3005

2. **Your soul has that second kind toward G-d: a will to its source that never stops. It does not show because a person, as he lives, is mind and heart, and the work is with them.** (M4) *How:* In the essence of the soul there is no contraction or hiding, so its will is constant; it is hidden because 'the form of man' is mind and emotions. «אמנם אית רצון עצמי וטבעי שנמשך לשרשו, דבעצם הנשמה לא יש צמצום והעלם, והיא תמידית» — *There is an essential, natural will drawn to its root; in the essence of the soul there is no contraction or hiding, and it is constant.* · line 3008

3. **You can see it in plain people. A simple Jew who has nothing to do with the work of the heart still wants G-d, from the point of his heart.** (M9) *How:* The proof is from what 'we see with our senses' in every Jew. «וכמו שאנו רואין בחוש בכאו"א מישראל גם איש פשוט שאינו שייך לעבודה שבלב כלל ה"ה רוצה באלקות» — *As we see plainly in every Jew, even a simple man who has nothing to do with the service of the heart: he wants G-dliness.* · line 3017

4. **Even someone sunk deep can have it surface, and not only in trouble. It can come out exactly in a happy moment, when he finds himself close to G-d-fearing people. Then it is true through and through.** (M15) *How:* The surprise: the essential will is revealed in joy as well as in distress. «לא דוקא ע"י המיצר והדוחק כ"א אדרבא יכול להיות בעת השמחה דוקא שמתקרב ביותר ליראי אלקים» — *Not only through distress and pressure; on the contrary, it can be exactly at a time of joy, when he comes closer to those who fear G-d.* · line 3017

5. **Another door is distance. When you honestly see that you are not what you should be, and it truly touches your heart, the will rises with force to leave the physical and be drawn to Him.** (M11) *How:* The text lists three revealers; this one starts from knowing 'he is not like that, and it is not good for him'. «וכאשר הענין נוגע לו ללבו ה"ה מתעורר בתוקף הרצון שבנפש לצאת מכל ענינים החומריים ולהימשך לעצמות אוא"ס» — *When the matter touches his heart, he is stirred with the strength of the will in the soul to leave all physical things and be drawn to the Essence of the Infinite Light.* · line 3011

6. **When this will comes out, it reaches all your powers. In the heart it is a quiet seriousness, 'ernstkeit': it leans only toward G-d. In deed it means never turning good into bad, whoever pushes you.** (M8) *How:* Up and down the inner ladder: the truth in the will spreads into mind, heart and deed as temimus. «והו"ע התמימות (ערנשטקייט) שלבבו נאמן (ערנשט) לאלקות» — *This is temimus (seriousness), that his heart is faithful (serious) to G-dliness.* · line 3018

**Bottom.** A will in the soul's essence that has no hiding and never stops (3008), true 'because it never breaks off' (3017).

**Return.** Into a heart that leans one way and hands that do not turn good into bad (3018).

## AB2-C06 · Tavo 5674 (lines 2495-2496); Chayei Sarah 5675 (2949-2954)

**Start.** You wake up groggy. Your mind is thick and your soul feels tiny. Is this the worst moment to start serving G-d?

1. **When you wake, your heart and mind are thick and coarse, and your soul is as small as a newborn baby.** (M3) *How:* The text begins with the plain state on waking, after sleep when the inner powers withdrew. «וגם מיד בקומו משנתו לבו ומוחו מגושם ומטומטם ומצד נשמתו הוא בתכלית הקטנות כתינוק שנולד» — *Right when he gets up from sleep, his heart and mind are coarse and dulled, and from the side of his soul he is utterly small, like a newborn baby.* · line 2950

2. **Exactly then, when you are most physical and smallest, you need the higher light most. ## AB2-D1 · 'With You there is something' and 'even with You, nothing' (extends D3)

**Teaching.** There are two kinds of 'only G-d'. In the first, nothing exists apart from Him, but with Him there is something: a light that feels it is His. In the second, even with Him there is nothing: the light as it is inside Him is not a light at all. Each is real; the second is deeper.

- Think: everything you are comes from Him. Apart from Him you are nothing. - Now notice that you still are, as His. 'With You there is something.' - Picture a flame inside a coal: there is no flame there, only the coal. That is the light as it is in Him. - Think: 'even with You, nothing'. Do not try to vanish. Only see that there is nothing to add to Him.

**From inside.** The text's own Yiddish: 'mit dir iz faran a zach', with You there is a thing. The seraphim quench their thirst in the very wish to be included.

**Where it lands.** In prayer, it is the step from Shema's love to the Amidah's standing silence (2822). In the day it means: you exist, as His.

- «דאין זולתך הוא שזולתך אין שום מציאות כלל אבל אתך יש מציאות (מיט דיר איז פאראן א זאך)» — *'There is none but You': apart from You no existence at all, but with You there is existence (with You there is a thing).* · line 2294 - «ואפס בלתך הוא שגם עם העצמות אין שום מציאות כלל, וזהו בהאור הכלול בעצמותו שאינו בבחי' מציאות אור כלל» — *'Nothing besides You': even with the Essence there is no existence at all; this is the light included in His Essence, which is not an existing light at all.* · line 2294

## AB2-D2 · The test is not there (extends D10)

**Teaching.** The thing that pulls you from G-d has no existence of its own. Only the Infinite can make a nothing look real, even very real. When you stand, it is cancelled and leaves no mark. G-d's purpose was never in the thing; it was in you, standing.

- Remember the simple faith: there is nothing outside Him. - Ask: then what is this pull? It is cut off from His oneness, so it is not a being at all. - Notice how real it looks. Only His power can make a nothing look like that. - Stand. Watch it leave no trace. - Know that the point of the whole thing was you, the one who stood.

**From inside.** It looks like a wall; it is a picture of a wall. The text: G-dliness 'iz a zach', is a thing; what is apart from it is no thing at all.

**Where it lands.** The very next temptation. Permitted things, used for a G-dly aim, are real; the test-thing is not (2732). And it still takes 'great strength of soul' (3540).

- «והוא רק כח הא"ס להעמיד דבר שאינו מציאות כלל רק דבר נדמה בבחי' דמיון בלבד שיהי' נראה למציאות» — *Only the power of the Infinite can hold up a thing that is no existence at all, only a seeming, so that it looks like existence.* · line 2731 - «כי אלקות הוא דבר (איז א זאך) ומה שנפרד מאלקות אינו במציאות דבר כלל» — *G-dliness is a thing ('is a thing'); what is separated from G-dliness is no thing at all.* · line 2732

## AB2-D3 · Love still has a lover (extends D2)

**Teaching.** Love is not the deepest closeness, because in love there is still someone who loves. Wisdom's bittul is not bowing before something greater; it is nullity itself, with no one standing apart to feel it. Even fear and love at their deepest are not 'emotions'.

- Feel your love for G-d. Notice the one who loves. - Think: a candle in daylight is still a candle; a small sage before a great one still is a sage. - Now let the seeing come without a lover in it: only that all is nullified before Him. - Do not make it a feeling. Let it be the quiet of standing in the Amidah.

**From inside.** 'A still small voice' (2822). Like the man of real mind: not moved where no need, moved most deeply where needed, and none of it shows (3832).

**Where it lands.** The Amidah, where legs and body stand as one: the bittul reaches deed (2822). In the day: a calm that is not coldness.

- «ה"ז בבחי' קול דממה דקה בבחי' ביטול שלמע' מאהבה כו', דאהבה אינו ביטל דיש מי שאוהב» — *This is a 'still small voice', a bittul above love; for love is not bittul, since there is one who loves.* · line 2822 - «ואהוי"ר שאינן בבחי' יש אינן במדרי' המדות וכמו אה' עצמי' שזהו התקשרות עצמי» — *Love and fear that are not a 'something' are not at the level of emotions, like essential love, which is an essential bond.* · line 3832

## AB2-D4 · The blank before the new (extends D5)

**Teaching.** After deep effort the mind goes stunned and empty, as if it has stopped. This blank is not failure. It is the mind emptying before its source, and right after it a new insight falls in. To receive a higher light, the vessel must be emptied first.

- Work on one deep idea as hard as you can: how is it, what is it? - When your mind goes blank, do not run from it. This is the emptying. - Remember Rabbi Zeira, who fasted to forget what he knew so he could receive more. - Wait. Let the new insight fall in, in plain words: 'it is like this'.

**From inside.** 'Es vert im lichtig', it begins to get light; 'es hert zich rechtkeit', rightness is heard before it is understood (3529). Then astonishment, 'like a complete stopping of the mind' (3530).

**Where it lands.** In study and in contemplation before prayer. Also in life: the pause between mind and heart where feeling goes quiet before it becomes love (3511, 3475).

- «והשתוממות זאת היא ממוצע שעי"ז בא על ההשגה הנפלאה» — *This astonishment is a go-between through which he comes to the wondrous grasp.* · line 3530 - «והענין הוא דבכדי לקבל גילוי אור עליון צ"ל ביטול הכלי דוקא» — *To receive a revelation of higher light there must be the vessel's bittul specifically.* · line 3531

## AB2-D5 · The will that never sleeps (extends D8)

**Teaching.** Under the love that comes and goes with thinking, there is a will in the soul's essence for G-d that never stops, like a parent's love for a child. Even a simple Jew far from inner work has it. It surfaces at times, not only in distress but in joy, and when it surfaces it is wholly true.

- Notice the love that leaves when you stop thinking. - Look under it. Find the simple wanting G-d that was there before you thought. - Remember a happy moment among good people when it came up by itself. - Let it reach your heart as quiet seriousness: leaning only one way.

**From inside.** 'Ernstkeit', seriousness: a heart faithful to G-d (3018). True 'because it never breaks off' (3017).

**Where it lands.** Into deed: never turning good into bad, whoever pushes (3018).

- «משא"כ הרצון שהיא אהבה ישנה תמיד גם כשאינו מתעורר, כמו אהבת האב והבן» — *But the will, which is a love that is there always even when not aroused, like the love of father and son.* · line 3008 - «וזהו שנק' אמת מפני שאינו נפסק לעולם שגם כשאינו בהתגלות יש בו הרצון בעצם» — *It is called truth because it never breaks off; even when not revealed, the will is in him in essence.* · line 3017

## AB2-D6 · The lowest gets the plainest mercy (extends D9)

**Teaching.** There is a mercy that comes from knowing and feeling, and a simpler mercy above all reasons: it is the nature of the high to be drawn to the low. So the soul that is farthest, 'the heel', for whom contemplation stays on the surface, has a wonderful rise: when it lowers itself and pities itself, it wakes that plain mercy.

- Admit it plainly: my understanding stays outside; it does not enter my heart. - Feel the bitterness of that, without despair. - Have mercy on your own soul, which came down so far. - Think: the Exalted is drawn to the lowly by His very nature. You are where His mercy falls most.

**From inside.** The desert: no seed, no water, and the strongest thirst. 'Would that in the holy place I had such love' (3891).

**Where it lands.** Self-rebuke that is honest, and being careful even in permitted things (3888); the ascent goes in order: first mercy on the body's coarseness, then on the soul's lowness (3893).

- «רק מפני שטבע המרומם להיות נמשך אל השפל כו', וע"כ עיקר הרחמים הוא דוקא על השפל במדרי'» — *Only because it is the nature of the exalted to be drawn to the lowly; so the main mercy is on the one who is low.* · line 3887 - «וז"ע מדבר שהוא תוקף הצמאון מפני העדר הגילוי» — *This is the desert: the strength of thirst, from the absence of revelation.* · line 3891

## AB2-D7 · Called by name (extends D6)

**Teaching.** A name is not felt even by its owner, yet it reaches deeper than all your powers: calling a fainted man's name brings him back, and calling a man 'wise' or 'kind' wakes power from his hidden source. It works because, under everything, he knows what he is. The soul's depth answers being called.

- Think of your name. You never feel it. Yet when called, all of you turns. - Remember: a fainted man revived by his name. - Ask: what in me turns when called? Something under my mind and heart. - Hear G-d calling you by name. Let all of you turn.

**From inside.** 'Penei elai', turn to me: the whole self turns to the caller (3801). The river that stopped, dug again at the depth of the spring (2535).

**Where it lands.** Teshuvah from the depths (2535); on Rosh Hashanah, calling 'HaMelech' (2536). With people: praise them into their goodness (ours, from 3802).

- «וכמו בהתעלפות ר"ל שיכולים להשיב נפשו ע"י שקוראין אותו בשמו» — *As in fainting, where they can bring back his soul by calling him by his name.* · line 2534 - «וכמו שע"י קריאת שם חכם ממשיכים כח חכמתו מהעלמו העצמי» — *As by calling him 'wise' they draw his power of wisdom from its essential hiddenness.* · line 2536

## AB2-D8 · What is plain, reversed (extends D13)

**Teaching.** Today the world is plain to us and G-d is news we have to learn. Grasp that comes as news can always be set another way. When the infinite light shines, it reverses: G-d is plain, the world is the novelty, and there is no room for another view. In the Temple a person felt G-d and did not feel himself. In the future grasping G-d will be natural.

- Notice that the world needs no proof to you. It is just there. - Notice that G-d, to you, comes as an idea you learned. - Picture the reverse: G-d just there, needing no proof; the world the surprising thing. - Remember Avraham, who saw a lit palace and asked: can it have no owner? The owner looked out.

**From inside.** 'Es leigt zich op der inyan zeyer gut', it settles very well, yet another setting stays possible (3674). In the Temple, 'the feeling of G-dliness and not the feeling of himself at all' (3677).

**Where it lands.** Now, by toil; the text says the seeing that is given 'comes through toil and great preparation' (3683). It lands in the plainness of a Jew's simple faith.

- «אבל כאשר תפיסת האלקו' הוא בפשיטות אז אין מקום להנחה אחרת» — *When the grasp of G-dliness is plain, there is no room for another setting.* · line 3674 - «ומי שהי' בביהמ"ק הי' בו הרגש האלקות ולא הרגש עצמו כלל» — *Whoever was in the Temple had in him the feeling of G-dliness and not the feeling of himself at all.* · line 3677

## AB2-D9 · The child who says 'Abba' (extends D7)

**Teaching.** A baby who has tasted grain calls 'Abba'. He knows nothing about his father, not why or how he is his father. Yet he recognizes him, from the essence, and is drawn after him with great longing. Matzah is the soul's recognition of the Essence in that way: knowing without knowing what.

- Do not look for an explanation of G-d right now. - Be like the child: you know He is your Father. - Let the longing come by itself, not from reasons. - Then eat the matzah, guard it from swelling up: small, flat, close.

**From inside.** 'Great longing' (3667). Matzah is both 'food of healing' for the ego and 'food of faith' (3669).

**Where it lands.** On Pesach night; and every morning in Modeh Ani, which is the same general recognition (2495).

- «שהרי אינו יודע איך שהוא אביו ואין בזה שום התגלות רק שהוא בהכרה שמכיר שהוא אביו ונמשך אחריו בגעגועים גדולים» — *He does not know how he is his father, and there is no revelation in it; only he recognizes that this is his father and is drawn after him with great longing.* · line 3667 - «ובחי' מיכלא דמהימנותא הו"ע ורעה אמונה» — *And 'food of faith' is 'shepherd faith'.* · line 3669

## AB2-D10 · Both true: it does not touch Him, and He wants it (extends new)

**Teaching.** On the outside of His light, nothing we do makes a difference to Him. In His inner Essence is rooted the intent that there be service. Both are true and do not contradict. So too forgiveness: it comes only from the Essence, where sin leaves no mark at all, and yet it comes only through teshuvah.

- Think: He lacks nothing. My service adds nothing to Him. - Think: and yet He wants it, from His very inside. - Hold both. Do not resolve them. - Serve, not for yourself, since service by its nature is not for oneself.

**From inside.** 'Mah ichpas lei', what does He care, and 'specifically slaughter it properly' (3632): two truths in one breath.

**Where it lands.** In the details of mitzvos as Torah gives them (3632). In teshuvah that knows it is forgiven from where sin cannot reach, and still does the work (3634).

- «ומ"מ לא איכפת לי' ושניהם אמת ואינם סותרים זל"ז» — *And still it does not matter to Him; both are true and do not contradict.* · line 3633 - «דסליחת עוונות הוא דוקא מעצמות א"ס שאין העון נוגע ואינו עושה שום רושם כלל» — *Forgiveness of sins comes only from the Essence of the Infinite, where sin does not touch and makes no mark at all.* · line 3634

## AB2-D11 · A fire too deep to see (extends D2)

**Teaching.** Felt wind, noise and fire in prayer are still 'not G-d', because they are felt. Inside the point of the heart there is a fire so deep it does not show, a trembling no one else notices, and a life that is not excitement: you simply live in it.

- Notice the excitement. Let it pass. - Look under it, at the point of the heart. - Feel a burning that does not need to show. - Let it become plain life: you live in this, the way a living man is alive in whatever he does.

**From inside.** Yiddish: the heart 'smolders inside, very deep' (3275); 'er lebt in dem', he lives in it (3277). 'All the people in the camp trembled' versus 'they moved and stood far off' (3277).

**Where it lands.** In Shema and the Amidah; then in the day, as ordinary aliveness.

- «שלא נראה וניכר מגודל עומקו כו' (בל"א דיא הארץ קלייעט אינוועניק טיף זייער» — *Unseen and unnoticed because of its great depth (the heart smolders inside, very deep).* · line 3275 - «וכמו איש חי בעצם היינו שבכל ענין הוא חי ואין זה בהתפעלות» — *Like a man who is alive in essence: in every matter he is alive, and it is not excitement.* · line 3277

## AB2-D12 · A taste without understanding (extends new)

**Teaching.** What words cannot say, song says, the way a kiss says love. The song of the point is one sudden movement holding all tunes. Deepest of all, in everyone: you hear a G-dly idea you do not understand and your soul clings anyway, because it simply has a taste for G-dliness.

- Hear a G-dly idea you cannot follow. - Notice that something in you clings anyway. - Do not explain it. That clinging is the soul's own taste. - If a tune comes, let it be one movement, not a show.

**From inside.** Rabbi Akiva singing 'every kind of tune' in prayer; Moshe's voice, above the Levites' ordered song (3292-3293).

**Where it lands.** In the listening itself; and in niggun during prayer. Still tied to learning: only 'when he hears some G-dly understanding' (3294).

- «ופי' בכל מיני ניגון היינו כמו תנועה אחת הכוללת כל מיני ניגון פרטי» — *'With every kind of tune' means like one movement that includes every particular tune.* · line 3293 - «ער האט בעצם א געשמאק אין אלקות שזהו סיבת הדביקות שנדבקה נפשו בזה גם שאינו מבין» — *He has, in essence, a taste for G-dliness; that is why his soul clings even though he does not understand.* · line 3294
