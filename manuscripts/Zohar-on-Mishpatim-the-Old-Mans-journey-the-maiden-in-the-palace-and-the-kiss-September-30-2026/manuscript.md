## 1. What the range is

Most of this range (lines 1910-2279) is the **Sava deMishpatim**, one of the Zohar's great set pieces. R. Chiya and R. Yosi meet an old donkey driver on the road, a man R. Yosi thought was full of empty riddles. At the inn he wraps himself in his cloak and, over one long night, reads the laws of the Hebrew slave and the sold daughter ("these are the judgments") as the secrets of the soul: its coming down into a body, its oppression, its return, reincarnation and levirate marriage, the souls of converts, the death of children. He weeps again and again, talks to himself ("old man, old man, you have gone into the great sea"), and at the end tells them he is Rav Yeiva Sava (line 2275).

Then comes a **Raya Mehemna** (lines 2280-2408) in which Moses, R. Shimon, Elijah and the heads of the heavenly academies speak together on the commandments of the section (the lost object, cities of refuge, oaths, judges, the laws of damages). A short passage on holiness and on insult (lines 2409-2428) is followed by the **Idra deMishkena** (lines 2429-2449), a brief account of the "face of the King", and by the end of the parashah: the three festivals, the angel sent before Israel, the kiss, meat and milk, and the covenant at Sinai (lines 2450-2490).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**He sees the dark from within the light.** "The one who dwells in light cannot see into darkness. But the Holy One is not so": ידע מה בחשוכא, אף על גב דנהורא עמיה שרא. מגו נהורא, אסתכל בחשוכא, "He knows what is in the darkness, though light dwells with Him; from within the light He looks into the dark" (line 2035). **(ours)** A line for a person who feels his darkness is where God cannot see him.

**Nothing is lost.** The Sava's account of souls rests on one principle: דהא לית מלה אפילו זעירא בעלמא, דלא הוי ליה אתר ודוכתא לאתטמרא ולאתכנשא תמן, ולא אתאביד לעלמין, "there is nothing in the world, even the smallest, that does not have a place where it is hidden and gathered, and it is never lost" (line 2018). "Even the breath of the mouth has a place" (line 2028), ואפילו מלה דבר נש, ואפילו קלא, לא הוי בריקנייא, "even a person's word, even a voice, is not in vain" (line 2028). This is a unity of total holding: everything that has ever been is kept.

**His great Name is built only with us.** In exile the Name Elokim is broken: its "Mi" (Who, the upper mother) has gone up, and "Eleh" (These, the children) have fallen (lines 2113-2114). So in the Kaddish we pray "may His great Name be magnified". מאן שמיה רבא. ההוא קדמאה דכלא, בגין דלית ליה בניינא אלא בהדן, "What is His great Name? The first of all; for it has no building except with us" (line 2115). **(ours)** This is one of the boldest lines in the range: the completeness of the Name waits on the people who say it. It is F07 (the home below) and F02 (the lower unity) in the Zohar's own voice.

**Two unities in the soul and in God.** In a person, body and soul are two, "matter and mind, life and death"; but in God אבל קודשא בריך הוא חיים, ושכינתיה חיים, "the Holy One is life and His Shekhinah is life", הכא גופא ונשמתא כלא חד. מה דלאו הכי בבר נש, "here body and soul are all one, which is not so in a person" (line 2364).

**Judgment below and above are one act.** "When judgments are set right below, they are set right above" (line 2445); when not, "as it were", the mother withdraws from the children (line 2446). The justice of a human court is part of the divine unity.

**From within darkness, light.** "Even though God made 'this opposite that', מגו חשוכא, נפיק נהורא, from within darkness light goes out" (line 2312). And the other side always comes first, "grows and guards the fruit", and then is thrown away (line 2174): F11 as a law of growth.

## 3. States of consciousness

**Torah as a hidden lover (proposed new state ZS2-13; ties to S79 Learning: mind clothed in His wisdom, S44 Love as delight).** The Sava's famous parable (line 2008): a beautiful maiden hidden in her palace has one lover whom no one knows. He passes her gate all day, looking everywhere. She opens a small window, shows him her face for a moment, and hides again. Only he sees it, "and his heart and soul and being go after her". So the Torah: she reveals herself "only to her lover", וגליאת אנפהא לגבי רחימאה, ומיד אתהדרת ואתכסיאת, "she shows her face to her lover and at once turns back and hides" (line 2008), ועל דא, אורייתא אתגליאת ואתכסיאת, ואזלת ברחימו לגבי רחימהא, לאתערא בהדיה רחימו, "so the Torah reveals and conceals, and goes in love toward her lover, to awaken love with him" (line 2008). The stages (lines 2009-2011): first a hint, and "if he understands, good"; if not, she calls him "simple" and speaks to him from behind a curtain (derash), then behind a thin veil (aggadah), and at last אתגליאת לגביה אנפין באנפין, ומלילת בהדיה כל רזין סתימין דילה, "she reveals herself to him face to face and speaks with him all her hidden secrets" (line 2010). Then he sees that the plain sense itself, כמה דאיהו, דלאו לאוספא ולא למגרע אפילו את חד, "as it is, is not to be added to or taken from by even one letter" (line 2011). Inside: study as courtship, a glimpse that makes one keep coming back. **(ours)** The guide can use this for anyone who studies and feels it dry: the glimpses are the invitation.

**The cleaving kiss (proposed new state ZS2-14; ties to S60 The essential bond).** "Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth": מאי נשיקות אדבקותא דרוחא ברוחא, "what are kisses? the cleaving of spirit to spirit" (line 2460), for the mouth is the source of the breath, and so lovers kiss mouth to mouth and their spirits cleave "and do not part". So מאן דנפיק נשמתיה בנשיקה, מתדבק ברוחא אחרא. ברוחא דלא מתפרש מניה, "one whose soul leaves in a kiss cleaves to another spirit, a spirit that never parts from him" (line 2461). Jacob kissed Rachel and wept: באתדבקותא דרוחא בה, לא יכיל לבא למסבל, ובכה, "in the cleaving of spirit with her the heart could not bear it, and he wept" (line 2463). "As long as the Holy One walks with Israel, as it were, spirit cleaves to spirit" (line 2464). Inside: a closeness so full that it brings tears. It differs from S60 in that it is felt as a meeting of two, breath to breath.

**Carried out of the dark by the hand (ties to S77 The lower return, C7).** On teshuvah: God sees a man walking in darkness and says, "I must take him by the hand": אנהיג ליה קודשא בריך הוא בארח מישר, כמאן דאתקיף בידא דאחרא, ואפקיה מגו חשוכא, "the Holy One leads him on a straight way, like one who grasps another's hand and brings him out of the darkness" (line 2143). The two angels who mourned him are then comforted too (line 2145). **(ours)** Return here is not only an act of the person; it is being led out by the hand.

**The broken spirit as offering (ties to S59 True lowliness).** "The sacrifices of God (Elokim) are a broken spirit": קרבנא דאלהים, עציבו, ותבירו דלבא, "the offering to Elokim is sadness and a broken heart" (line 2170). The Zohar adds that one who had a bad dream should show sadness, וההוא עציבו מסתייה לחלמא בישא, "and that sadness is enough for the bad dream" (line 2170). But the proud heart is the one that "despises" (line 2171). **(ours)** Chassidus will insist on joy and warn against sadness (Tanya 26-31). The Zohar here gives sadness one small, bounded place: a sadness offered, not dwelt in.

**The King's jester in pain (proposed new state ZS2-15).** "David was the King's jester (badicha): even when he was in pain, once he stood before the King he went back to his jesting, to cheer the King" (line 2155). Inside: grief does not stop a person from giving joy to the One he loves. **(ours)** A rare and humane picture of joy chosen for the sake of Another.

**The heart that sees (ties to S37 Depth, S40 Mind moved in itself).** The Raya Mehemna: prophets see with the eyes, but the wise see בעין השכל דלבך, "with the eye of the mind of your heart", and see everything (line 2331). הלב רואה, והלב שומע, "the heart sees and the heart hears", it understands and knows (line 2333). To Moses: "your thought, which has no end, ובה תסתכל, בההוא, דלית ביה סוף, and with it you will gaze at the One who has no end" (line 2334). And God enters a person through the windows of eyes, ears, nostrils and mouth, כמלכא דייעול באתכסייא לחדרי חדרים, למללא עם בריה, "like a king who enters in disguise into the inner rooms to speak with his son" (line 2335).

**Grief that is held (ties to S14 Loss and grief).** The Sava explains why a remarried widow cannot rest with her second husband: the first husband's spirit stirs in her, וכדין איהי דכירת ליה תדיר, ובכאת עליה, או אתאנחת עליה, "and so she remembers him always, and weeps for him, or sighs for him" (line 2056). And the husband's spirit left with a widow who did not remarry "stays there twelve months, and every night goes out and visits her soul", until at her death they are joined again (line 2067). **(ours)** Whatever one makes of the metaphysics, the Zohar honors the grief: love leaves something of the beloved inside, and it does not vanish.

**Bound to a teacher after death.** R. Abba dreamed of R. Shimon and woke "in that delight, weeping and laughing", and did not eat for three days, from joy and from not meriting to see him again; ביה אתקשרנא תדירא. דהא כד נהירא לי שמעתתא, חמינא דיוקניה, "yet I am always bound to him, for when a teaching shines for me, I see his image" (line 2449).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Return is always open.** דלית מלה בעלמא, ולית מפתחא בעלמא, דלא תבר ההוא דתב בתיובתא, "there is nothing in the world, and no lock in the world, that one who returns does not break" (line 2135); לית מלה בעלמא דקיימא קמי תשובה, "nothing stands before teshuvah" (line 2137), not even an oath of God. Those who return stand where the completely righteous cannot (lines 2136, 2146).

**The daily unity at the edge of death.** The poor who accept silently confess and "unify the Holy One every day, so that their death be in 'One'" (line 2372). Each night, "into Your hand I deposit my spirit" (line 2381). **(ours)** A nightly practice: give the spirit into His hand, with the word "One".

**Study with the one who studies.** "Wherever words of Torah are spoken, the Holy One and the Assembly of Israel are there and listen" (line 1990). "There is no shame in saying words of Torah before anyone" (line 1929).

**Pray much, and say "I love my Master".** The slave who says "I love my master" is read as one who multiplies prayers every day, and by that he comes to love the Holy One (line 2195).

**Words that wound.** "It is forbidden to call a friend by a shaming name" (line 2421); one who calls another "wicked" is judged; but "like the wicked" is allowed, as "the Lord was like an enemy, and not an enemy" (line 2423).

**Guard what you let in.** The Sava's rule about the other side's "shell" is also practical: judges should not judge after eating (line 2427), and the body's openings are windows for the King (line 2335).

## 5. The big human questions

**Why must the soul come down?** Before creation all souls stood before Him in their future form. One refuses: "it is enough for me in this world; I will not go to another world to be enslaved and dirtied". God answers: מן יומא דאתבריאת, על דא אתבריאת למהוי בההוא עלמא, "from the day you were created, you were created for this, to be in that world" (line 1961). The Torah then tells people: "see how the Holy One pities you: a precious pearl He had, He sold you for nothing, to be a servant among you in this world" (line 1962), and pleads: when your time comes, לא תפוק מתטנפא בחובין, תפוק בת חורין, ברירה נקיה, "do not go out soiled with sins; go out free, clear and clean" (line 1963). **(ours)** The purpose of life in one image: a pearl lent to us, to be returned clean.

**Is God a Father to the soul?** The soul that leaves pure is received "as a father does to his beloved daughter: he kisses her, embraces her, and gives her gifts" (line 1972), in "the palace of love" (line 1970). A person who grows in merit is called "a son of the Holy One" (line 1921).

**Why do children die? (the hardest question in the range).** The Sava gives several answers, some hard (the child would have soured, and was taken while still fragrant, lines 1951-1956). But the most striking passage refuses to smooth the pain. On "the tears of the oppressed, and they have no comforter": these are "the little ones who die while nursing", דלית דמעין דנפקי מלבא, כהני דמעין, "there are no tears that come from the heart like these tears", and everyone asks, אן דינא דקשוט, דעביד מארי עלמא, "where is the true justice that the Master of the world does?" (line 2266). The child born of a forbidden union cries, "if my fathers sinned, what sin did I do?" (line 2265), and the verse answers: "they have no comforter". Only then the consolation: there is a place in the world to come prepared for those children where even the completely righteous cannot stand, וקודשא בריך הוא רחים לון, ואתדבק בהו, "and the Holy One loves them and cleaves to them" and makes His own academy with them (line 2267). **(ours)** The guide should keep the order the Zohar keeps: first the tears and the question, with no answer rushed in; only then the love.

**Mercy on everyone.** "His mercies are on all His works, even on beasts and birds" (line 2139); מאן בעי אסוותא, אינון מארי כאבין, "who needs healing? those in pain", that is, the wicked (line 2140), וקודשא בריך הוא לא שביק רחמוי מנייהו, "and the Holy One does not withdraw His mercy from them" (line 2140).

**Insult and providence.** When Shimei cursed David, David "knew that the word had come down from another, higher place" (line 2160), and it brought him to complete return.

**Being engraved in each other.** At the Old Man's parting, R. Chiya reads "set me as a seal on your heart": a seal leaves its whole image where it pressed, "so that even if I go here or there, you will find my image engraved in you, and remember me" (line 2276). And their prayer: יהא רעוא, דדיוקנא דילן, תהא חקוקה בלבך, כמה דדיוקנא דילך חקוק בלבן, "may it be His will that our image be engraved in Your heart, as Your image is engraved in ours" (line 2278).

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **The maiden in the palace (lines 2008-2011)** is the best picture in the Zohar of study as love.
2. **"There are no tears like these... where is true justice?" (line 2266)**, followed by "He loves them and cleaves to them" (line 2267), shows how to hold the worst question without a quick answer.
3. **"Nothing is lost, not even a breath" (lines 2018, 2028)** is a deep comfort for loss and for effort that seems wasted.
4. **"His great Name is built only with us" (line 2115)** makes a person's prayer part of God's own wholeness.
5. **"Like one who takes another's hand and leads him out of the dark" (line 2143)** gives return its tender form.
