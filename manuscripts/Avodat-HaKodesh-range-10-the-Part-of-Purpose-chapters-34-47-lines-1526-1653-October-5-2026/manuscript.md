## The shape of the range

This range is ibn Gabbai's own answer to the Rambam's "Guide", word by word. The Rambam explained the Bible's human words for G-d ("see", "hear", "place", "throne", "sit", "stand", "rock") as figures of speech for created things or for G-d's actions. Ibn Gabbai explains each one as a name for **unity**: every "seeing" or "hearing" or "sitting" of G-d is the joining of His lights. And each is caused, in part, by us.

1. **The burning bush and "I will be"** (1526-1547). Israel's return below woke the Return above. The bush burns and is not eaten: clinging to the Fire gives life. "I will be with you": with you, as you rise.
2. **"Show me Your ways, show me Your glory"** (1548-1570). Moses asked to know how to *unite* Him. His glory and He are one.
3. **Seeing** (1571-1583). G-d's "seeing" is His lights looking into each other, "eye in eye".
4. **Hearing** (1584-1596). "Hear, O Israel" means "gather". Hear the old and you will hear the new.
5. **Man and woman; son** (1597-1604). Israel are sons by the soul and servants by the body.
6. **Place, throne, sitting, rising, standing, rock** (1605-1645). His place is His Name; the throne is not other than the One on it.
7. **The arousal from below** (1646-1653). The upper world was not complete until man was made. The righteous and the Righteous One draw each other like a magnet.

## The unity of G-d

**He is all, and nothing contains Him.** "Place" (Makom) is a name of G-d because its number equals the Name. "He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (line 1605):

> אחר שכלם חלק והוא הכל, איך יגביל החלק את הכל (Avodat HaKodesh 3:40:2; line 1606)
> Since they are all a part and He is the all, how could the part contain the all?

And more: by letter-exchange, the letters of the Name are the soul of the letters of "Makom", and "place" is like their body:

> נמצא אם כן שהשם הוא המקום והמקום הוא השם (Avodat HaKodesh 3:40:4; line 1608)
> It follows that the Name is the place, and the place is the Name.

"Though the Great Name is separate in the utmost separation from the place, which is all created things, still He is joined and attached to them, to watch over them" (line 1608).

*Ours.* This is a sharp statement of the paradox that Chabad makes central: He is utterly beyond the world, and the world's very being is His Name (Shaar HaYichud ch. 3-6; Tanya ch. 20-21). The guide can hold both lines as a single meditation: **the part and the all**.

**He is One in all His attributes.** Of the Thirteen Attributes:

> ואף על פי שאנו אומרים מדת רחמים ומדת הדין אין להעלות על לב חלילה שיש בו שתי מדות הפכיות כי אינו כן, אבל הכל אחד מיוחד בתכלית הייחוד (Avodat HaKodesh 3:35:20; line 1567)
> And though we say "the attribute of mercy" and "the attribute of judgment", one must not think, G-d forbid, that there are two opposite attributes in Him, for it is not so; but all is one, united in the utmost unity.

"We cannot say it with our mouths or grasp it truly", and this is exactly what Moses asked to see: "how He is one in His Name and His goodness" (line 1567). The Zohar he brings: "He alone is all ... all of them rise and are bound and united in one, and all is He, and He is one" (line 1638).

**He and His glory are one.** "In the passing of My glory ... until I pass": "to say that He is His glory and His glory is He, and to warn that He and His glory are one" (שהוא וכבודו אחד, line 1559). "'Here is a place with Me': My place is secondary to Me, and I am not secondary to My place" (line 1560).

**"I am the whole of all, closed and sealed."** Ibn Gabbai reads "Ehyeh asher Ehyeh" as the unfolding of the Thought. The first Ehyeh is Wisdom, where all was hidden:

> אני כלל הכל ואני הוא הכל סתום וחתום (Avodat HaKodesh 3:34:22; line 1547)
> I am the whole of all, and I am the all, closed and sealed.

Then "asher Ehyeh" is Binah, where all is ready to come out; then the last Ehyeh, "now all will be drawn out in detail" (line 1547).

## Unity is the upper will, and we can do it

**What Moses really asked.** "Let me know Your ways, and I will know You" (Ex. 33:13): "and I will know You" means "I will unite You" (ואדעך איחדך, line 1550).

> כי הרצון העליון הוא הייחוד, והמיחד את השם הגדול בכבודו, מוצא חן בעיניו ועושה רצונו (Avodat HaKodesh 3:35:3; line 1550)
> For the upper will is the unity; and one who unites the Great Name with His glory finds favor in His eyes and does His will.

"Happy is the one born of woman who knows how to coax his Creator and please Him" (line 1551), so that his will is done too, for Israel's sake. *Ours.* A good line for the guide, since it names prayer as a loving art, not a demand.

**Why Moses asked to *see*.** He wanted the knowledge of unity to be complete, and seeing makes it permanent:

> כי ישאר חקוק ומצוייר בלבו ... אף כשיסתר מעיניו (Avodat HaKodesh 3:35:9; line 1556)
> For it would remain engraved and pictured in his heart ... even when it was hidden from his eyes.

*Ours.* This is the goal of Chabad meditation (hitbonenut): to see the idea until it is "engraved" and stays with one all day (Tanya ch. 42: "to accustom his mind and thought ... until it is fixed"). Moses asked for it by sight; we reach it by long seeing with the mind.

**"I will be with you": with you as you rise.** "'With you': He hinted that this depends on him, that he wake himself to receive this being and completion ... for, as it were, He needs help":

> עמך עם מה שתתעורר אתה אז תשרה הברכה בך (Avodat HaKodesh 3:34:18; line 1543)
> "With you": with what you awaken yourself; then the blessing will rest on you.

And why take Israel out? "When you take the people out of Egypt you will serve G-d on this mountain; and service is a need on high ... so the exodus is a need of the Godhead" (line 1543).

**Our return wakes the Return above.** The groaning in Egypt: "their return woke the Return, and it returned to them, and the awakening came from head to end" (line 1527).

> כי התעוררות השלמות והייחוד העולה מלמטה סבת הייחוד למעלה, והייחוד למעלה סבת הישועה והגאולה למטה (Avodat HaKodesh 3:34:3; line 1528)
> For the awakening of wholeness and unity that rises from below is the cause of the unity above, and the unity above is the cause of salvation and redemption below.

**The upper world was waiting for man.** "No shrub of the field was yet ... for the Lord G-d had not made it rain on the earth, and there was no man to work the ground" (Gen. 2:5). The Zohar: the upper world was not set right until the lower was (line 1647). "A mist went up from the earth": "first smoke rises from the earth, and the cloud wakes, and all joins together" (line 1649). Ibn Gabbai:

> ולזה לא נתגלה העליון עד שנברא התחתון כי בו השלמות (Avodat HaKodesh 3:47:1; line 1646)
> So the upper was not revealed until the lower was created, for in it is the completion.

The righteous "raise the mists to the upper earth by their Torah, good deeds and prayers" (line 1651).

**The magnet.** The righteous and the upper Righteous One:

> והרי הם כאבן השואבת את הברזל שזה מושך את זה, וזה נאחז מזה ואינם נעזבים זה מזה (Avodat HaKodesh 3:47:6; line 1651)
> And they are like the magnet and iron, this one draws that one, and that one holds to this one, and they do not leave each other.

Only an outside cause parts them (line 1651). Rabbi Nechunya sat gazing at the Chariot, "his thought clinging and bound to the upper ones and they to him", until his companions had to use a trick to call him back (lines 1652-1653).

*Ours.* This is the Chabad doctrine of "the arousal from below" (itaruta diltata), stated as ibn Gabbai's whole theology. And the magnet is a lovely picture for the guide: **drawn and drawing**. When I cling, I am drawn; and He is drawn to me. It pairs with the mountain that longed for Moses "more than Moses longed to cling to it" (line 1533).

## Seeing and hearing

**G-d's seeing is uniting.** "And G-d saw the light": "He wrapped Himself and united with it"; His seeing is never of "something outside Himself" (line 1571). "Eye to eye" means "the upper sight joined with the lower sight ... this one seen within that one, and both are one" (line 1572). Israel are G-d's eye: "My eye weeps for My eye" (line 1573).

**Coming to see is causing the faces to shine.** "Whoever comes to unite the Holy Name and does not intend it with heart, will and awe, so that the upper and lower be blessed by it, his prayer is thrown outside" (line 1577). And "you come to be seen before Me" means:

> העבודה בשלמות הייחוד בכוונה הרצויה גורמת הראות הפנים פני המלך והאדם והתייחדם (Avodat HaKodesh 3:36:8; line 1578)
> Service in complete unity, with the desired intention, causes the faces to be seen, the face of the King and of man, and their uniting.

> כל לשון שמיעה המיוחסת לשם יתברך הוא ענין אסיפה וחבור כענין שמע ישראל (Avodat HaKodesh 3:37:1; line 1584)
> Every word of "hearing" said of G-d means gathering and joining, as in "Hear, O Israel".

When a person unites, "it is with a voice, and that voice rises and gathers and unites all the emanation from the head of the Thought to its end" (line 1584). And when we do not hear, He does not hear: "as I called and they did not hear, so they will call and I will not hear" (line 1585).

**Hear the old, hear the new.** "If you hear the old you will hear the new":

> כי הייחוד וההשמע למעלה אין לו הפסק ואין לו סוף, וצריך העובד השלם לחזור תמיד על השמיעה ההיא ולשמוע (Avodat HaKodesh 3:37:9; line 1592)
> For the unity and the gathering above have no pause and no end, and the complete servant must always return to that hearing and hear.

So "Torah scholars have no rest, not in this world and not in the next; they go from strength to strength" (line 1592). *Ours.* Chabad's "ascending from level to level" (maalin bakodesh) and the Rebbe's teaching that true rest is itself growth. For the guide, the point is gentle: repetition is not stale. Hear the old line again today, and it opens new.

## States of consciousness

- **Drawn and drawing** (lines 1533, 1651). Proposed below.
- **Engraved** (line 1556). The fruit of long contemplation: the unity stays pictured in the heart when the eyes close. Ties to Tanya ch. 42 and the guide's settled-contemplation states.
- **Burning and not consumed** (line 1531). "Clinging to the Lord your G-d, who is fire, will not consume you but add to your life." Proposed below.
- **Rising with Him** (line 1543). The sense that His "I will be" moves as I move. Ties to "alive in the task" (range 5).
- **The part and the all** (lines 1606-1608). An insight state; ties to Shaar HaYichud nullification states.

## Meditation and practice

- **Take off both shoes** (line 1531). Before coming near: clean the body of bad deeds and the soul of false beliefs; "one shoe is not enough, both must be removed". Proposed below.
- **Hear the old, hear the new** (line 1592). Proposed below.
- **Ask for the cause first** (line 1594). Daniel asked "let Your face shine" first, then for the Temple. In prayer, ask first for His unity, then for the need.
- **Pray from the depth** (line 1618). "One who wants to pray before the holy King must ask from the depth of all": aim prayer at the root (Yud of the Name). Ties to "reaching the source of the Will" (range 7).
- **Bold prayer** (line 1620). "Since Your unity depends on us, why forget us forever?" A prayer the guide can teach: *You need us whole, so make us whole.*
- **Look to the rock you were hewn from** (line 1643). When tempted, recall where the soul came from and do not soil it.

## The big human questions

**Does G-d see and hear me?** His seeing and hearing are His joining with us; when we see and hear Him, He sees and hears us (lines 1578, 1585).

**Is G-d changeable, angry then kind?** No; all is one in Him. The change is on the side of those who receive (lines 1567, 1633).

**Why was I made?** Because the upper world was waiting for someone to work the ground (line 1646).

**Is my yearning answered?** Yes. The mountain wanted Moses more than he wanted it (line 1533).

## Lines that did not bear on the four questions

Lines 1532-1537 (the midrash on Moses and Mount Horeb, the thornbush as the oppressing powers), 1545-1546 (Albo on "Ehyeh"), 1557-1566 (the reading of "back and face" and the cantillation marks), 1579-1583 (the pilgrimage, the sapphire pavement and the colors of the bow), 1597-1601 ("man of war" and the Zohar on the hair of judgment), 1611-1615 (the thrones in the spheres, Amalek), 1629-1637 (the terms "stand" and "planted"), 1639-1642 (commentators on "the Rock, His work is perfect") and 1644 (the land as the place of service) were read in full. They serve the argument above.
