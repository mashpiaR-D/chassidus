## How the range builds the idea

This is the stretch where the Tanya states the one idea most openly. Chapter 20 defines the unity. Chapters 21-22 explain hiding and idolatry by it. Chapters 23-25 make Torah and sin a matter of union and separation. Chapters 26-31 turn it into the beinoni's daily psychology. Chapter 33 gives the spine's own sentence, and chapters 35-38 end in the dwelling below, made by physical deeds.

**Ch. 18 end (281-284).** Faith is above knowledge because "the One God" lives in the soul's chochmah. The simplest Jew gives his life for the unity without reasoning. F06 and F12 are stated: the unity is held first by faith, below comprehension, not by it.

**Ch. 19 (285-298).** The soul is a flame that wants to rejoin its source even if that means it is extinguished there (F08, bitul as the soul's own nature). Holiness is defined as what is "not a thing in itself" (291), and kelipah as wanting to be yesh (292). The love is "hidden", not lost. It is asleep in the wicked and wakes in a test of faith, when all kelipot become "as if they never were before God" (296). Line 294 comes close to the spine's F06 claim that no impurity reaches the soul's root, though the Tanya puts it as chochmah that is asleep, not as yechidah.

**Ch. 20 (299-306).** The formal definition: God is "yachid u-meyuchad", unchanged by creation, "משום דכולא קמיה כלא חשיב, וכאין ואפס ממש" (301). Creation and its continued existence are only His word clothed in the worlds (302, F01). Lines 303-306 build the analogy of speech, thought and the soul's faculties (G06). They are structural steps.

**Ch. 21 (307-315).** The analogy is used and then broken: a man's speech separates from him, but "there is nothing outside Him" (308). His speech stays united with His Essence after creation as before, and the change is only toward the creatures (312). The contractions are hester panim toward the creatures, and before Him "כחשכה כאורה" (314), because the garment is of Him, "Havayah is Elokim". This is the Tanya's own statement of F02 and F03 together, and of G03 and G14.

**Ch. 22 (316-323).** The Torah calls it real "speech" because that is truly how life descends to the creatures, so the lower view is kept (G02). The concealment is strong enough to sustain kelipot, which feed from the "back" of the will. The chapter ends in the sharpest F08 sentence in the book: "כי עיקר ושרש עבודה זרה, הוא מה שנחשב לדבר בפני עצמו" (323). Arrogance is idolatry because holiness rests only on what is nullified to Him.

**Ch. 23 (324-337).** The mitzvot are the "limbs of the King", nullified to His will as limbs are to a soul. Torah is more: "אורייתא וקודשא בריך הוא – כולא חד" (332), because the will is the halacha itself (331). In study, sovev kol almin is revealed in the soul, which is more than in the upper worlds (334, F04 and F05 at the lowest point). Line 337 records a limit: not every mind can bear the awe this brings, and its absence does not hold back action.

**Ch. 24 (338-355).** This is the unity's negative image. A sin separates a person from His unity more than kelipah, which "knows its Master", or even a gnat (340-348). Lines 349-350 and 352 are halachic or eschatological detail with no real tie.

**Ch. 25 (356-370).** "The thing is very near": the hidden love for "the One God" can be woken at any moment (356). The union made by Torah and mitzvah is eternal above and bound by time below (365-366). The Shema fixes the readiness to die "for His unity" in the heart (370). Lines 368-369 are halachic detail.

**Chs. 26-31 (371-464).** This is the psychology of the beinoni. Much of it is practical counsel (joy, alacrity, foreign thoughts, fixed times for contrition), and it is marked structural wherever the text does not itself tie the counsel to the unity. The ties it does state are strong: - F11. Itkafya and ithapcha are the two kinds of gratification above (387). The yetzer is "spiced" into a delicacy (388). Every small subduing raises His glory above all (383-384, 391). "Perhaps for this he was created" (385). - F12. The rebuke to the animal soul in 421 asks how long it will hide the Or Ein Sof that fills all worlds "גם במקום זה שאני עליו". Line 422 calls seeing the unity "בראיה חושיית, ולא בחינת שמיעה והבנה לבדה" the root of all service. This is the Tanya's version of G05 and of 'know today'. - F06 and F07 in ch. 31. "Within me is literally a part of God" (455). The descent of a light "that fills and surrounds all worlds" into a snake-skin body is explained as "אין זה, כי אם ירידה זו – היא צורך עליה" (463). - A limit is kept at 395. Elevating the middot of foreign thoughts, a Baal Shem Tov practice, "was said only of tzaddikim". The transforming power is not given to everyone, and the index should not flatten F11 into a universal technique.

**Ch. 32 (465-471).** Ahavat Yisrael rests on "ואב אחד לכולנה" (466): souls are one in their root and only bodies divide. Hillel's "the whole Torah" follows because the Torah's root is drawing Or Ein Sof into Knesset Yisrael "to be one within One" (467). Loving and hating the same person, for the hidden spark and for the evil, is held without reduction (470, F09). Lines 468 and 471 are halachic qualifications.

**Ch. 33 (472-483).** The spine's chapter. It gives the contemplation of the true unity (472-474), "the whole of man and the purpose of all worlds", which is His dwelling below (475). Then comes the sentence the index rests on: "אשר אפילו בארץ מתחת – אין עוד מלבדו, וזו היא דירתו בתחתונים" (477). The chapter closes on the "public domain" of this world becoming "רשות היחיד ליחודו יתברך" through this faith (483, F11). Faith in the unity is here the one mitzvah that contains the 613 (478-479).

**Ch. 34 (484-492).** The Patriarchs, Moshe and Sinai were bitul in existence (F08). Since the Temple, His dwelling, "which is His unity", is the four cubits of halacha, and charity makes the rest of the day a dwelling too (487-490, F07 and F10). Joy and a broken heart are held at once, from the divine and the animal soul (491-492, F09 stated).

**Ch. 35 (493-505).** Why do beinonim exist? The Shechinah rests only on what is wholly nullified. Even a perfect tzaddik's soul stays "רק הוא דבר בפני עצמו – ירא ה׳ ואוהבו" (496), while a mitzvah, being His will without hester panim, is not separate at all. Hence deeds are the oil for the light (F08 and F10 joined, G08 and G09). The Maggid's gloss grounds this in Ein Sof as "the true One" (499).

**Ch. 36 (506-516).** The dwelling below in full. "Above and below" do not apply to Him (507). The descent through concealing garments ends in this world's "doubled and redoubled darkness" (509). The purpose is this lowest world: "אלא התכלית הוא עולם הזה התחתון" (510), where light is revealed out of darkness. Sinai was a foretaste of ein od milvado seen with the eye (514). The messianic era is the completion (512, 516).

**Ch. 37 (517-545).** The dwelling is made by our deeds in exile. "כי הגורם שכר המצוה – היא המצוה בעצמה" (518), because the mitzvah draws Or Ein Sof into physical things under kelipat nogah. The whole vitality of the world rises through the 600,000 souls (526-533). The 248 positive commandments are named "as He desired a dwelling below" (527). Charity raises the whole vital soul (535). The chapter keeps a tension it does not resolve. Torah's light is incomparably higher (540, 543), yet "the deed is the main thing" because the purpose is the dwelling below (542).

**Ch. 38 to 38:15 (546-560).** Why speech without intention discharges a duty and thought does not: the soul needs no repair, the body does (547, the halacha read through F07). Even stones have His light lest they return to nothing (548, F01 stated in passing). The four levels of inanimate, plant, animal and speaker correspond to the letters of Havayah (552). Intention is to the deed as soul to body, yet one simple will "without change or multiplicity" is in both (556, F05). The range ends on the two levels of intention: contemplative love and fear against natural, inherited love (559-560).

## Where the tie is thin, forced or disputed

- **None (14 lines).** 349-350, 352, 359, 368-369, 444, 446, 449, 468, 471, 513, 519-520 are halachic or eschatological qualifications. They protect the argument but do not express the idea.
- **Structural (49 lines).** Most of the beinoni's counsel in chs. 26-30 (sadness, hecklers, market-sitters, study beyond one's nature) is psychology that serves the unity only through its place in the argument. The text does not name the unity in these lines, and they are not forced to.
- **Reading (9 lines).** These ties are ours: 355, 372, 378, 399, 414, 445, 459, 469, 524. Line 344 is marked stated for what it says (this world's evil is the dregs of kelipah, 'the end of the refinement'), but tying it to the dwelling below is our reading, and its entry says so.
- **F12 disagreement kept.** The Tanya does not require comprehension. The awe of ch. 23 is "not for every mind" and does not hold back action (337), and natural hidden love is a valid, lower intention (560). This fits the Alter Rebbe's "believe" side against the Rayatz's "to know" (SPINE F12). At the same time, 422 calls seeing the unity by "sensory sight", not understanding alone, the root of all service. Both are in this range.
- **F05 is mostly not named as atzmus.** The Tanya speaks of "Or Ein Sof", "yachid u-meyuchad" and "His essence and being" (312, 556). The spine's Essence language (atzmus beyond sovev and memale) is the later Rebbeim's reading of these passages, and the entries tag F05 only where the text itself points past revealed light: 300, 312, 334, 360, 497, 499, 505, 556.
- **F11's limit.** Transformation (ithapcha) belongs to tzaddikim. Beinonim subdue (itkafya), and full transformation is placed in the messianic future (386-387, 395, 462). The spine's "darkness turned to light" should be read with this ranking kept.
