## The shape of the range

The earlier range (lines 1-280) was mostly anthropology, and there the unity came in only at a few points. This range is the reverse. Much of it teaches the unity outright. Chapters 41-53 turn the soul's love and awe into contemplation of Ein Sof. Chapter 48 defines sovev and memale. Chapters 49 and 53 name the purpose of all tzimtzumim and of all worlds. Shaar HaYichud 1-7 then sets out the core doctrine of F01, F02 and F03 step by step. That is why *stated* dominates. The cosmological faces (F01-F05), which were rare in chapters 1-18, are dense here. F12 leads the count because nearly every chapter of Part I in this range is about how knowing becomes love and awe.

## Ch. 39-40 (561-597): where service rises, and why Torah is the unity

Chapter 39 is a ladder of worlds and souls, and most of it is structural. Its stated points are these. Service rises because through it the sitra achra is subdued (line 567, F11). Torah and service done with love and awe are included in sefirot where Or Ein Sof "is united with them in complete union" (line 568, F10, G04): > ואוראיןסוף מתייחד בהן בתכלית היחוד (line 568)

Great tzaddikim are a chariot, "nullified in existence and included in His light" (line 573, F08). Service without awakened love and awe "remains below in the world of separation" (line 576). That is F12 from the negative side: knowledge that has not reached the heart leaves the act outside the unity. Line 575 already uses the phrase ואין לנו עסק בנסתרות ("we have no business with hidden things"), but to set aside the chariot-tzaddikim as a model, not to limit knowledge.

Chapter 40 gives F10 its fullest statement in the range. Torah and the Holy One are one, and He fills all worlds equally, so the worlds differ only in their recipients (line 587). The revelation of the supernal will in the mitzvah waits "until the end of days, when the world is raised from its physicality" (line 590, F07 as the end). The gloss at line 591 says the will clothed in the mitzvah "is itself the halacha he studies": > והוא הוא ﬠצמו הדבר הלכה והתורה שעוסק בה (line 591)

The Talmud and Kabbalah levels of line 595 are kept structural. The observation that the plainer study reaches lower is ours.

## Ch. 41 (598-625): contemplation before the act

This is the practical chapter. Before tallit, tefillin or study, one contemplates that Or Ein Sof, sovev and memale, is clothed in these very letters and threads, and "draws His light" onto the part of God in the body (line 602, F10 + F04 + F06 + F08): > ובקריאתו או בלבישתו הוא ממשיך אורו יתברך עליו (line 602)

Line 613 is F01 in the first person. The Shechinah that vivifies all worlds is what gives him "this very power of speech": > והיא היא – המשפעת בו כח הדבור הזה (line 613)

Line 623 gives continuous creation by the hour: "each hour is a different drawing from the upper worlds to vivify the lower". Lines 618-621 join the soul's union with the union of sovev and memale above (F04), and Elokai Neshama becomes the daily act of handing the soul back (F08). Lines 605-608 are marked structural (servant and son, two mitzvot). Reading line 606 as ch. 6's "holiness rests only on what is nullified" is marked *reading*.

## Ch. 42-43 (626-656): Da'at as binding; seeing through the garments

Chapter 42 is F12's charter within Part I. Da'at is not book knowledge. It is binding the mind "with a strong and firm bond": > עד שתהא מחשבתו מקושרת בה׳ בקשר אמיץ וחזק (line 629)

It holds G06 openly. The analogy of a person who feels his own toenail (line 635) is "only to soothe the ear" (line 637). Line 638 adds the Alter Rebbe's reserve on inquiry: > אך כל ישראל מאמינים בני מאמינים בלי שום חקירת שכל אנושי (line 638)

Lines 641-644 are the clearest G03 in the range. One fears a king's inner life by seeing his clothes, and "all one sees... are the outer garments of the King" (F03 + F01). The same line defines emunah as habituation: > הכל הם לבושים החיצונים של המלך הקדושברוךהוא (line 644)

Chapter 43 distinguishes lower fear, which comes from the worlds as garments (line 647), from higher fear. Higher fear comes from seeing how each thing "is born and comes into being from nothing" (line 648, F01), which makes heaven, earth and oneself nullified like sunlight in the sun (line 649, F02 + F08). Lines 654-655 (love before fear, the providential teshuvah of R. Elazar ben Durdaya) are structural, and their tie is thin.

## Ch. 44-47 (657-696): the inherited love, the King's descent, the gift of Himself

F06 is stated repeatedly. One love is equal in every Jew (line 659), and He is "our life" (line 660): > כי הלא אב אחד לכולנו (line 662)

This matches F06's "one Father". Line 666 names F07 as the content of the pleasure given to God: > או ״להיות לו דירה בתחתונים״ (line 666)

Line 673 keeps the other side of F12. The inherited love, though higher, does not exempt anyone from toil: צריך לטרוח בשכלו להשיג. The Alter Rebbe therefore holds both sides of the F12 dispute within this range: comprehension is required (lines 629, 673, 674), and faith carries what comprehension cannot (lines 638, 834). Chapter 45 has mercy on the spark in the "serpent's skin" and on the Shechinah "who dwells with them amid their impurity" (line 676, F03 + F11). Chapter 46's parable of the king raising the man from the dunghill (line 681) is marked *reading* for F07. Its fulfillment is stated. In Egypt: > אלא הקדושברוךהוא בכבודו ובעצמו ירד לשם (line 683)

That is F05 and F07 together with G05. Line 686 defines holiness as sovev. Line 689 says the body's matter does not block the union, "even darkness does not darken for You": > כי אין החומר מונע יחוד הנפש באוראיןסוף ברוךהוא (line 689)

Lines 690-691 take Shabbat and chametz, halachic severity, and ground it in the holiness shining on every soul. The first is stated. The second is carried by the first and marked structural. Chapter 47 makes Torah the gift of the Essence: > והרי זה כאלו נתן לנו את עצמו כביכול (line 695)

## Ch. 48-50 (697-731): sovev defined; the purpose of the tzimtzumim

Chapter 48 is F04's defining text. Sovev is not spatial: > ואין הפירוש סובב ומקיף מלמעלה בבחינת מקום חס ושלום (line 701)

The earth is the example: > וכל אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא נקרא סובב עליה, אף שהוא בתוכה ממש (line 703)

His knowledge "surrounds" the globe and "brings it into being from nothing thereby" (line 709). The Tzemach Tzedek's claim, recorded in the SPINE, that the somethingness of matter comes from sovev is present here in seed. Linking the two is ours.

Chapter 49 states F07 as the purpose of every tzimtzum: > ותכלית כל הצמצומים הוא – כדי לברוא גוף האדם החומרי (line 713)

Line 714 reads the tzimtzum as love, "as water face to face" (F03 as kindness), and that is what calls for the person's self-giving. Line 719 reads "ahavat olam" as the contraction of infinite light into "olam" for love of Israel. Line 720, the choice of the material body, is marked *reading*: the text states the choice, but reading it as the Essence's choice is later. Line 723 joins F07, F10 and F11 with G05 and G11. Speech is required because it draws Or Ein Sof down into the vital soul made of mineral, plant and animal, so that > להיות כבוד ה׳ מלא כל הארץ הלזו דוקא בבחינת גילוי (line 723)

Chapter 50 gives the "shov" after the "ratzo" (F07 + F08): > להיות דירה בתחתונים לאחדותו יתברך בבחינת גילוי (line 730)

Line 726, love from gevurah, is marked *reading* for F09.

## Ch. 51-53 (732-763): the Shechinah through the worlds into halacha

The question at line 732 is how the Shechinah can "rest" anywhere when no place is empty of Him. The soul-in-body analogy answers it (lines 733-735). Then comes G05, stated: > כמו שמצוי שם – כך נמצא בתחתונים ממש (line 737)

Material things "seem dead" yet are continuously created (line 740, F01 + F03). Chapter 52 carries the Shechinah through each world's Holy of Holies into the Talmud and Mishnah. Permitting and forbidding both come from His middot (lines 748, 751, F09 + F10). The bare mapping lines (749, 752, 755) are structural. Chapter 53 is the range's strongest F07 in time. The First Temple held a revelation greater than the upper worlds (line 757), and since the destruction: > אין לו להקדושברוךהוא בעולמו אלא ד׳ אמות של הלכה בלבד (line 759)

Part I ends on F11. The Shechinah rests on the divine soul through the animal soul being consumed like a wick "and turned from darkness to light" (line 762). In a tzaddik the soul itself is turned; in a beinoni, at least its garments.

## Chinukh Katan (768-779): faith in the unity as the root of love

The headings are *none*. The introduction distinguishes the love that is a self-rising flame, the reward of tzaddikim, from the love that can be commanded, made by contemplation (lines 774-778). It ends by naming the gate's premise: > היא האמונה הטהורה ונאמנה ביחודו ואחדותו (line 779)

Here "faith" names the foundation, and Shaar HaYichud then sets out to explain it for understanding. This is the F12 tension again, placed at the gate's threshold.

## Shaar HaYichud 1-7 (783-840): the core doctrine, step by step

- **SH 1 (783-791), F01.** The Besht's "Your word stands in the heavens" (line 785). The stone and its name show G01 at work: והוא חיותו של האבן (line 788).
- **SH 2 (792-798), F01 as providence.** The smith analogy is refuted. Creation from nothing is a greater wonder than the split sea, and needs "the power of the Maker in the made" always.
- **SH 3 (799-803), F02, higher unity.** Creatures are nullified like sunlight in the sun, and "yesh" only to eyes of flesh: אם כן, אפס בלעדו באמת (line 800). The text marks where its own analogy fails (line 803). That is G06, and it leads to SH 4.
- **SH 4-6 (804-822), F03 and the joining of the Names.** Havayah is present-continuous creation (line 805). Gevurah hides so that the creature is not nullified (lines 808-810). Elokim equals "hateva" (line 815), and וזו היא בחינת גבורה הכלולה בחסד (line 816). The middot unite because they are one with Him, "You bind and unite them" (line 817, F09 stated). So "Havayah is Elokim" (line 818, G14). The earth is not even called "od" (line 819). That is 'yachid', not a whole with dependent parts. The soul-body analogy is refused at its root (line 821, G06).
- **SH 7 (823-840), the lower unity, the Essence, and the dispute.** "There is no king without a people" (line 824). Malchut and Adnut make space and time (lines 825-827). His Essence fills the earth "as much below as in heaven above" (line 830). The phrase matches F07's "below as above", but this passage uses it for the lower unity, not the dwelling. Then comes F05: no change before and after creation, and His knowledge is His Essence (lines 832-835). This is known only by faith: > רק להאמין באמונה, שהיא למעלה מהשכל ומהשגה (line 834)

F03's kept disagreement is stated from the Alter Rebbe's side: those who "understood the tzimtzum literally" (line 837) are refuted by their own belief in providence (line 838). Line 839 gives the Raya Mehemna's "He binds and unites kind to kind... no closeness among the four elements except by the Holy One among them". That is F09's "ayin that joins", stated.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Five lines are *none*: the colophon, the two editorial markers and the two Part II headings (764, 766, 768, 769, 781). - The kabbalistic mappings are structural and carry no face: line 593 (the bird of Metatron), lines 725 and 728 (silver and gold, the Levites), and lines 749, 752 and 755 (the Shechinah's path through each Holy of Holies). - Halachic detail appears at lines 581-582 (neutral study), 608 (awe and service as two mitzvot), 622 (lishmah as for a get), 691 (a speck of chametz) and 760 (lips moving count as a deed). Each is marked structural, since the text uses it inside the argument. Only line 690 is *stated*, because there the text itself gives the reason from the soul's holiness.
- *Reading* is used seven times, where the face is ours rather than the text's: lines 579-580 (Torah in exile), 584 and 606 (bitul), 681 and 720 (F07 read into a parable and into the choice of the body), and 726 (love from gevurah as F09). Sub-entries add three more: 599.2, 730.3 and 840.3. - Within this range the text states only one side of the F03 dispute, literal against non-literal tzimtzum (lines 837-838). The other side (Tzava'at HaRivash) lies outside the range, as the SPINE records.
