The second half of Be'er HaGolah finishes the aggadot about G-d (Well 4), then defends sayings that look like tall tales (Well 5), sayings that seem to clash with science (Well 6), and laws and prayers that seem harsh to outsiders (Well 7). Again and again the Maharal says the sages speak of the essence of a thing, not its bodily size, and that every "change" in G-d is on the side of the receiver.

Five teachings in this half matter most for the guide:

- **His will joins, He does not.** "G-d does not become one with the beings, and He is apart from them, only His will becomes one with them" (281). That joining of will is the essence of joy.
- **The key.** "All these things are not about His true self" (308). G-d prays, wears tefillin, is "helped", is "sad": all of it is from the receiver's side. "Take this key in your hand."
- **One makes all one.** "Because He is One and there is none besides Him, so He makes all one" (317). Things become one only through another; He alone is one in Himself.
- **A lacking world proves one Maker.** If the world were whole, it would need two makers, each doing half. "When the caused is lacking and not finished, it shows that the Maker is One" (467).
- **Found as you are found.** "G-d is found to a person as the person is found with G-d" (291). One who prays wrapped, turning to no side, finds Him turned to him.

## 2.1 He does not join the beings; His will does

G-d "plays with Leviathan" (275-283). The Maharal explains laughter: "אין השם יתברך מתאחד עם הנמצאים, והוא נבדל מהם, רק שרצונו מתאחד עמהם, וזהו עצם השחוק" (281, Well 4 9:7): "G-d does not become one with the beings, and He is apart from them, only His will becomes one with them, and this is the essence of laughter." Joy is will joined to a thing. So in the end "אז ימלא שחוק פינו" (282, Well 4 9:8): "then our mouth will be filled with laughter." He is also the one who renews: "ובכל יום ויום הקב"ה מחדש מעשה בראשית" (279, Well 4 9:5): "and each and every day the Holy One renews the work of creation," so each day counts as a creation of its own. And He wants to give more than we want to take: "המשפיע יותר חפץ להשפיע, ממה שהמקבל חפץ לקבל" (283, Well 4 9:9): "the giver wants to give more than the receiver wants to receive." "יותר ממה שהעגל רוצה לינק, הפרה רוצה להניק" (283): "more than the calf wants to suck, the cow wants to nurse."

## 2.2 The key: all is from the receiver's side

This is the rule for every strange aggadah about G-d. Nature is His messenger ("הטבע הוא שלוחו יתברך", 254, Well 4 7:4), and "ברוך שכחו וגבורתו מלא עולם" (253, Well 4 7:3): "blessed is He whose strength and might fill the world." When He seems to weep or grieve: "הכל הוא מצד המקבל" (256, Well 4 7:6): "all is from the receiver's side." He is found to the receivers as they are ready to receive. "ודבר זה הוא יסוד גדול מאוד" (256): "and this is a very great foundation." So "בצרתם לו צר" (273, Well 4 8:8): "in all their trouble He was troubled": He is found as they are. When they do His will, "יגדל נא כח ה'" (274, Well 4 8:9): "let the power of G-d grow." The receiver "helps" only because "אין הפועל פועל רק כאשר יש כאן מקבל מוכן" (285, Well 4 10:2): "the doer does not act except when there is a ready receiver."

Then the Maharal gives the key outright. "ישמעאל בני ברכני" (307, Well 4 15:4): "Yishmael My son, bless Me" means that He wants to be found to creatures in the trait of blessing, beyond strict law. But: "כל הדברים האלו אינם באמתת עצמו יתברך" (308, Well 4 15:5): "all these things are not about His true self." In Himself He is the source of blessing and needs nothing: "בהיכלו כולו אומר כבוד" (308): "in His palace all say glory." G-d praying, G-d wearing tefillin, G-d blessed: all is from the side of the receiver who lacks. "ומפתח זה תקח בידך" (308): "and take this key in your hand."

## 2.3 Twoness is opposition; tie the world to His oneness

Why is G-d "angry" each morning? Because at sunrise kings bow to the sun. "כל שניות הוא התנגדות" (286, Well 4 11:1): "all twoness is opposition." The sun rules the lower world, and people who stay inside nature "ואינם מקשרים את העולם הזה באחדותו יתברך" (288, Well 4 11:3): "and they do not tie this world to His oneness." To live under nature alone, with no tie to Him, is itself a kind of bowing to the sun. The first hours of the morning are for accepting His kingdom.

## 2.4 He is One, so He makes all one

"What has G-d done since creation? He pairs couples" (315). The only new thing under the sun is the joining of particular things. Only the One can join: "במה שהוא יתברך אחד ואין זולתו, לכך מאחד הוא יתברך הכל" (317, Well 4 17:3): "because He is One and there is none besides Him, so He makes all one." Man joins the lower world, and G-d joins man. "הם מתאחדים על ידי זולתם, כי בעצמם אינם אחד" (318, Well 4 17:4): "they become one through another, for in themselves they are not one."

## 2.5 Near as a son, apart as a Cause

Heaven was made "from the light of His garment", earth "from the snow under the Throne" (320-327). The caused has two sides toward its Cause. It is near: "כמו שיש לבן חבור וצירוף אל האב שבא ממנו" (323, Well 4 18:4): "as a son has a joining and bond with the father he came from." And it is far: "העילה הוא נבדל מן העלול" (323): "the Cause is apart from the caused." The garment is the near side; the Throne (His kingship over what is below) is the far side.

## 2.6 One world, from one Maker

Rabba bar bar Chana's frog, snake and raven (333-341) teach that even the impure edges of the world are tied in. "מי יתן טהור מטמא לא אחד" (334, Well 5 2:2): "who can bring pure from impure? Not One?" "וראוי שיהיה העולם אחד, לפי שהוא מפועל אחד" (336, Well 5 2:4): "and it is right that the world be one, since it is from one Maker." The world is like one tree from one root, and even what is off to the side is carried by it: "ועם כל זה הם מתקשרים ומתאחדים עם עצם המציאות עד שהכל אחד, ובזה העולם אחד" (338, Well 5 2:6): "and even so they are tied and joined to the core of being until all is one, and so the world is one."

## 2.7 A lacking world shows one Maker

"The north wind He made and did not finish: whoever is a god, let him come and finish it" (467). "מי שהוא אלוה, יבא ויגמור אותו" (467, Well 6 11:6): "whoever is a god, let him come and finish it." A caused thing is always lacking. If the world were whole, two makers would each have done half. "וכאשר העלול חסר ולא יושלם, מורה שהפועל הוא אחד" (467): "and when the caused is lacking and not finished, it shows that the Maker is One." The lack "מוכיח ומחייב שהוא יתברך אחד ואין זולתו" (467): "proves and requires that He is One and there is none besides Him."

## 2.8 The world stands by cleaving to Him

What does the earth stand on? "העולם כולו השם יתברך מקיים אותו שלא יתמוטט" (435, Well 6 8:2): "the whole world, G-d keeps it standing so it does not fall," and it stands "על ידי הדביקות שיש לעולם בו יתברך" (435): "through the cleaving the world has to Him." The chain goes up through people, Torah, the wise, holy spirit, prophecy, to His arm: "זה התעלות מן המטה עד הדביקות בו יתברך, והוא יתברך קיום הנמצאים" (442, Well 6 8:9): "this is a rising from below up to the cleaving to Him, and He is what keeps beings standing."

## 2.9 The end: One and His Name One

The world was made for an end. "יהיה ה' אחד ושמו אחד" (386, Well 5 12:2): "G-d will be One and His Name One." That is why the spirit of Mashiach was made on "day one" (the Torah says "one day", not "first day"): "מפני שעל ידו יהיה כל העולם אחד" (390, Well 5 12:6): "because through him the whole world will be one." The angels were made on day two, so that no one would think they were partners.

**Proposed MH-S25 "Found as I am found".** Inside: the whole self turned to Him with nothing to the right or left, the way a poor man or a person in danger calls, and the sense that He is turned the same way to you. "השם יתברך נמצא אל האדם כמו שהאדם נמצא אצל השם יתברך" (291, Well 4 12:1): "G-d is found to a person as the person is found with G-d." The verse "תפלה לעני כי יעטוף" (292, Well 4 12:2): "a prayer of the poor when he wraps himself," shows what wrapping means: "כאשר האדם מכוין לגמרי מבלי פנות ימין ושמאל, גם כן השם יתברך נמצא אליו" (292): "when a person aims fully without turning right or left, G-d too is found to him." "ואז האדם דבוק בו יתברך במדת טובו" (292): "and then the person cleaves to Him in His trait of goodness." How to enter: see 4.1. Map: near S33 Before Whom, S51 The sudden cry and S60 The essential bond.

**Proposed MH-S26 "The middle point".** Inside: standing at the center, leaning to no edge, with everything around you turned toward the One in the middle. In the dance of the righteous, G-d sits in the middle of the circle, and each one points and says "הנה אלקינו זה" (295, Well 4 13:3): "behold, this is our G-d." The middle is apart from the circle, and every point faces it; the joining is "from every side". The tzaddik is the world's one pillar because "שאין הצדיק יוצא מן האמצע אל שום קצה" (443, Well 6 8:10): "the righteous one does not go out from the middle to any edge." Edges are where loss lives: "כל דבר שהוא קצה, הוא הולך אל הקצה, אשר בקצה דבק ההעדר" (472, Well 6 13:2): "whatever is an edge goes to the edge, and at the edge nothingness clings"; the middle is "הדבר השוה, שאינו יוצא לשום קצה" (472): "the even thing, which goes out to no edge." How to enter: see 4.2. Map: near MH-S11 The simple one, MH-S5 Undivided now, S64 The plainest thing; technique `mh_simple_no_edge` (Gevurot Hashem).

## 4.1 Wrap yourself in prayer (`before_whom`; new: `mh_wrapped_prayer`)

G-d "wrapped Himself like a prayer leader" (291-292). Wrapping is praying with no turning to right or left, as the poor man prays when he has nothing else. Practice: before a prayer, put your hands over your eyes or pull the tallit (or a coat) over your head. Say to yourself: "Nothing else now." Then speak to Him as one who has no other help. Hold the line: "He is found to me as I am found to Him" (291).

## 4.2 The circle and the middle (`bittul`; new: `mh_circle_and_middle`)

Picture a circle of people, each facing the center. The center is apart from all of them and equally near to each. That is Him. Point (inwardly, or with a finger) and say "הנה אלקינו זה": "behold, this is our G-d" (295). Then notice where you lean to an edge (anger, craving, fear) and step back to the middle (443, 472). The dance at 293-297 adds the body: joy done in act, lifting yourself off the body at "Kadosh".

## 4.3 Tie the morning to Him (`before_whom`; new: `mh_tie_the_morning`)

The anger of the morning is at those who meet the sun and "do not tie this world to His oneness" (288). The first hours are for accepting His kingdom. Practice: before checking news or messages, say "Modeh ani" and the first line of the Shema, and name the day as His. The sun, the work and the plans are His messengers ("nature is His messenger", 254).

## 4.4 Stand as the caused (`accept`; with `mh_stand_as_effect`)

"בעל יראת השם יתברך, הוא עושה עצמו עלול אל השם יתברך, והעלול יש לו קיום מצד העילה" (440, Well 6 8:7): "one who fears G-d makes himself caused by G-d, and the caused stands by its Cause." "יראת ה' היא אוצרו" (440): "the fear of G-d is his treasure." Practice: when you feel shaky, say: "I am made by You; I stand because You hold me." Let the awe itself be the floor.

## 4.5 Pray the sins end, not the sinners (`heart_after`; new: `mh_pray_for_the_sinner`)

Beruria to Rabbi Meir: "מי כתיב 'חוטאים', "חטאים" כתיב" (524, Well 7 6:8): "is it written 'sinners'? 'Sins' is written." "אלא בעי עלייהו רחמי דלהדרו בתשובה" (524): "rather, ask mercy for them that they return." The reason: "מאחר שהאדם מעשה ידיו של השם יתברך" (524): "since a person is the work of G-d's hands." Practice: when someone hurts you, pray by name that the harm leaves their heart and that they come back. Do not pray for their fall. Guard: this does not mean staying near a person who is dangerous.

## 4.6 Let the other speak (`own_words`; new: `mh_let_them_speak`)

Truth is not served by shutting a mouth. The one who argues should be "חומל עליו, ומדבר עמו בנחת" (525, Well 7 7:1): "has pity on him and speaks with him calmly." Silencing the other "אין זה רק בטול וחולשת הדת" (526, Well 7 7:2): "this is only undoing and weakness of the faith." Beit Hillel won "מפני שנוחין הן ועלובין הן" (330, Well 5 1:3): "because they were gentle and let themselves be shamed"; one "מכת המנצחים, אין דבריו נמשכים אחר האמת" (330): "from the camp of those who must win, his words do not follow the truth." And "לא תשנא את אחיך בלבבך" (528, Well 7 7:4): "do not hate your brother in your heart": say it out, so he can answer. Practice: in a dispute, state the other side's best case first, in their words, before your own.

## 4.7 Cleave to the wise (`act`; with `mh_gathering`)

"הדבק בתלמידי חכמים כאילו דבק בשכינה" (499, Well 7 1:4): "cleave to the wise, as if cleaving to the Shechina." All people are like one body: "והנה כל בני אדם נחשב כמו אדם אחד פרטי" (506, Well 7 3:2): "and all people count as one single person," and life reaches each limb through the heart. And no one is shut out: "כל אדם הוא בכח להיות תלמיד חכם" (500, Well 7 1:5): "every person is able to become a scholar"; "יגעתי ולא מצאתי, אל תאמין" (500): "'I toiled and did not find': do not believe it." Practice: keep one living teacher, and spend time near him or her each week.

## 4.8 Learn aggadah to know Him (`own_words`)

"אם רצונך להכיר את יוצר הכל, עסוק באגדה" (485, Well 6 15:8): "if you want to know the Maker of all, busy yourself with aggadah." But law comes first, because "ותכלית התורה הוא המעשה" (485): "and the aim of the Torah is the deed." Practice: read one short aggadah a day, and ask the Maharal's question: what does this say about the essence, not the size?

## 5. The big human questions

**Does G-d need anything from me?** No. "All these things are not about His true self" (308). But He wants to give more than you want to take (283), and He is found to you as you are ready (256, 285).

**Why is the world broken?** Because anything made lacks something, and that lack is itself proof that one Maker made it (467). The sages do not say the world is perfect. They say "אין חסרון במעשה ידיו" (342, Well 5 3:1): "there is no lack in the work of His hands", meaning the lack comes from the receiver, not from Him (342-346).

**Why does science and Torah seem to clash?** The sages speak of "סבת הסבה": "ועל אותה הסבה הטבעית יש סבה אלקית, והוא סבת הסבה" (393, Well 6 1:3): "and over that natural cause there is a divine cause, the cause of the cause." And "לא עיינו חכמים רק במהות, ולא בגשמי" (416, Well 6 5:2): "the sages looked only at the essence, not at the bodily."

**Why is there sin?** It is a leaning toward nothing: "העושה החטא הוא נוטה אל ההעדר הגמור" (400, Well 6 2:7): "one who does the sin leans toward full nothingness." And the world was set to our level: "השם יתברך סדר העולם במדריגה הראוי לבני אדם" (396, Well 6 2:3): "G-d set the world at the level fitting for people."

**Is my one life worth much?** "המקיים נפש אחד, כאילו קיים את כל העולם כולו" (426, Well 6 6:2): "whoever keeps one soul alive, it is as if he kept the whole world alive."

**Is every person made in G-d's image?** Yes. Standing upright is the image: "והצלם הזה כולל כל בני אדם" (436, Well 6 8:3): "and this image includes all people." "חביב האדם שנברא בצלם אלקים" (437, Well 6 8:4): "beloved is man, who was made in the image of G-d." And in judging people, "הכל הולך אחר המעשה" (508, Well 7 3:4): "all goes by the deed."

**Can I rise from very low?** "שהוא מרומם ומנשא את האדם מתוך השפלות היותר גדול" (371, Well 5 8:4): "that He raises and lifts a person up out of the greatest lowness."

**When I cannot understand?** Leave it "עד כי יערה עליו רוח ממרום" (459, Well 6 9:16): "until a spirit is poured on him from on high," and do not think badly of the wise.

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **"Only His will becomes one with them"** (281). This is the Maharal's clearest line on how G-d relates to the world: He stays apart; His will joins. The Tanya goes further. In the Gate of Unity the creatures are nullified in His light, and "the will" (ratzon) is not separate from Him. For Chabad, His will joined to the world is Him. The Maharal keeps a gap. This is a real difference; mark it.
- **The key of the receiver** (256, 308) is the same principle as the Gate of Unity ch. 7: all change and all "hiding" are on the side of the receivers, not in Him. Tzimtzum in the Tanya is exactly this kind of hiding for the receivers' sake. The Maharal does not use the word tzimtzum.
- **"Each day He renews creation"** (279) matches the Tanya's teaching that creation is renewed each moment (Gate of Unity ch. 1-2). The Maharal says "each day", the Tanya "each moment", and the Tanya builds on it the claim that the world has no being of its own. The Maharal does not draw that conclusion here.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** "The Cause is apart from the caused" (323) and the rider apart from what he rides (468) are the Rambam's language, which the Maharal cites. He adds the other side: the caused is joined like a son to a father. That pairing is close to the **Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"): near as a son (all is from Him), far as a Cause (He is not the world). Still two sentences, not the Ramak's one.
- **"He is One, so He makes all one"** (317) and "the world stands by cleaving to Him" (435, 442) are close to the Tanya's view that the world has being only through its tie to the divine word. The Maharal says the tie keeps it standing; the Tanya says the tie is its whole being.
- **A lacking world proves one Maker** (467) has no direct parallel in the Tanya. It is a proof by the lack of the caused. Chabad would add that the lack is by design, so that man can finish it (the home below, S82).
- **The middle point** (295, 443, 472) matches the Chabad teaching of the "middle line" and of the tzaddik as the one who does not lean to kindness or strictness alone.
