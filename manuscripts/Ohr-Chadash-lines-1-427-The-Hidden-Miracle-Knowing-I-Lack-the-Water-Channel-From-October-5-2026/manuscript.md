Ohr Chadash ("New Light") is the Maharal's commentary on Megillat Esther. It asks why a book with no open miracle and no written Name of G-d is still a book of His hand. This first half covers the long introduction and the first two chapters: the king's feast, Vashti, and the rise of Mordechai and Esther.

Six teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The miracle was hidden because it was so great.** "מה שלא נעשה נס נגלה במגילה הזאת הוא בשביל גודל הנס" (81): "there was no open miracle in this Megillah because the miracle was so great." It came from a high, hidden place. "ומטעם זה לא נכתב גם כן השם בנגלה בכל המגילה הזאת" (81): "for this reason the Name is not written openly in the whole Megillah."
- **Know that you lack.** "כי האדם צריך שיהיה תמיד בדעתו שאינו בשלימות, וצריך השלמה, ואז האדם מקבל השלמה מן השם יתברך" (22): "a person must always know he is not complete and needs completing, and then he receives completion from G-d."
- **What is from G-d is not in time.** "אבל דבר שהוא מן השם יתברך, פעל זה נעשה בלא זמן נמשך" (45): "what is from G-d is done with no stretch of time." Time is change, and change is loss (87).
- **One start, spreading like water.** "כי העולם הזה יש לו התחלה אחת, מפני שהוא נברא מן השם יתברך שהוא אחד" (178): "this world has one start, because it was made by G-d, who is One." It spreads "כמו אמת המים" (179), "like a water channel."
- **To be fed is to be kept in being.** "והקיום הוא ההזנה בעצמו" (222): "the lasting is the feeding itself." He feeds all and takes nothing (243), and He does not take back what He gave (246).
- **A door open upward.** "ויש לאדם פתח פתוח לעלות עד עולם העליון על ידי שהוא צדיק" (234): "a man has an open door to rise to the upper world."

## 2.1 The hidden face, and a miracle too great to show

The Megillah has no open miracle and no written Name. The Maharal does not see this as a lack. He says the miracle came from so high that it could not show. "מה שלא נעשה נס נגלה במגילה הזאת הוא בשביל גודל הנס" (81): "there was no open miracle because the miracle was so great." "ומטעם זה לא נכתב גם כן השם בנגלה בכל המגילה הזאת" (81): "for this reason the Name is not written openly."

The hiding was double, "hide, I will hide" (40). So the one who would save had to come from hiddenness too. Esther's name means "hidden." "לכך הגיע תפילתה גם כן אל מקום עליון הנסתר" (40): "so her prayer reached the high hidden place." Like the incense, which is offered in secret, "יבא דבר שבחשאי, שהוא הקטורת" (41): "let a thing done in secret, the incense, come" and atone.

Every "the king" in the Megillah means two kings: the king of Persia and the King of the world. "וכל הדברים היו נעשים למטה ולמעלה" (82): "all things were done below and above." (The Maharal comes back to this in the second half, 834.)

## 2.2 What is from Him is not in time

"It came to pass" (vayehi) is a word of sorrow, says the Talmud. The Maharal explains: "והויה שהיא בזמן היא השתנות, מפני שעצם ההויה הוא השתנות מענין לענין, וכל שינוי רע" (87): "being in time is change, since coming-to-be is change from one state to another, and all change is bad." "כי כל מצטער הזמן לו ארוך" (88): "for one in pain, time is long." But "וכל הויה שלימה היא שמחה, וההפסד הוא אבל וצער" (92): "all whole being is joy; loss is mourning and pain."

His act is not like this. "אבל דבר שהוא מן השם יתברך, פעל זה נעשה בלא זמן נמשך" (45): "what is from G-d is done with no stretch of time." "מפני כי הוא מן השם יתברך, ואינו תחת המשך הזמן" (45): "because it is from G-d, and not under the stretch of time." That is why the dawn leaps. Before dawn is the darkest hour: "אותה שעה אין חשך גדול הימנו" (44): "at that hour there is no darkness greater." "והאדם משתוקק אל האור" (43): "and a person longs for the light."

## 2.3 One world, one start

The Maharal reads the 127 provinces as three worlds. The upper world is one hundred. "ומפני כי עולם העליון אין בו פירוד וחלוק, ולכך כנגד זה מספר מאה, כי מספר מאה הוא כמו אחד נחשב" (167): "the upper world has no split or division, so it is one hundred, for one hundred counts as one." "אבל עולם התחתון הוא עולם הרבוי והפירוד" (168): "but the lower world is the world of the many and the split." "כי העולם העליון אין בו חלוק כלל" (170): "the upper world has no division at all."

Yet even the lower world has one root. "כי העולם הזה יש לו התחלה אחת, מפני שהוא נברא מן השם יתברך שהוא אחד" (178): "this world has one start, because it was made by G-d, who is One." "העולם נברא מן השם יתברך כמו אמת המים, שהולך ומתפשט מן השם יתברך" (179): "the world was made from G-d like a water channel that goes and spreads out from G-d." The many is the same water, spread out.

## 2.4 Kept in being: to be fed is to exist

The king's feast is a picture of how He feeds the world. The Maharal says feeding is not an extra. "כי אף אחר שנבראו מן השם יתברך, צריכים הם לקיום שלהם מן השם יתברך, והקיום הוא ההזנה בעצמו" (222): "even after they were made by G-d, they need their lasting from G-d, and the lasting is the feeding itself." As He "זן את כל העולם, אף הנשמה זנה כל הגוף" (222): "feeds the whole world, so the soul feeds the whole body."

He gives and does not take. "שהוא יתברך מפרנס הכל ואינו מקבל מן בריותיו" (243): "He provides for all and takes nothing from His creatures." And "והרי השם יתברך אחר שנתן דבר מה לאדם, אינו חוזר ונוטל ממנו" (246): "after G-d gives a man something, He does not take it back."

## 2.5 The caused shows the Cause

"והעלול הוא מורה על שבחו של העילה, כי אין עלול בלא עילה, ויורה העלול על מציאות עילה" (68): "the caused shows the praise of the Cause, for there is no caused without a Cause, and the caused shows that the Cause exists." (The Maharal says this of Israel's praise; we keep only the general rule.) The blessing comes down to the world from the six sides, and "ומשם מקבל העולם הברכה מן השם יתברך" (210): "from there the world receives blessing from G-d."

## 2.6 "Yehudi" is "yechidi": one who says He is One

Mordechai is called "Yehudi." The midrash reads it as "yechidi," the one alone. "זה שמייחד שמו יתברך וכופר בעבודה זרה, הוא יחידי" (365): "one who declares His Name one and denies idols is 'yechidi.'" The righteous man keeps the order of creation: "כי הצדיק מציאותו ומהותו אינו יוצא מן הסדר שסדר השם יתברך בבריאה" (367): "the righteous man's being does not leave the order G-d set in creation," "לפי שדומין לבוראן" (368): "because they are like their Creator."

## 2.7 Kingship and the many

Why does a proud man want to be alone? Because man was made alone. "ולכך כל אדם רוצה לבלעות את חבירו, שלא יהיה נמצא אחר זולתו" (127): "so every man wants to swallow his fellow, so that no other will exist but him." The cure is His kingship. "וכאשר האדם מקבל מלכותו יתברך, אז ראוי שיהיה הרבוי, מצד שהשם יתברך חפץ ברבוי" (128): "when a man accepts His kingship, the many are fitting, because G-d wants the many." Only the One is alone. When I stop trying to be the one, there is room for everyone.

- **MH-S41 "Knowing I lack" (22).** Inside: I know I am not finished, and this does not crush me. It opens me. "כי כאשר נשלם דבר אחד, דבק בו ההעדר" (22): "when a thing is complete, absence clings to it." So I do not want to be complete in myself. I want to be completed by Him. How to enter: in prayer, name one place you are not whole. Say: I need completing, and He completes. It differs from S59 True lowliness (seeing oneself as small). This state is being open, like an empty vessel, ready. The Maharal comes back to it in 669-670 (second report).
- **MH-S42 "Held in the hidden" (40-41, 81-82).** Inside: nothing looks like a miracle. His Name is not written anywhere on my day. And I feel that He is working all the more, from a place too high to show. How to enter: look at a day when things turned, and nothing was open. Say: "all things were done below and above" (82). The hiding is not His absence. It differs from S85 Seeing Him in what happens, which sees Him in the event. This state trusts Him in what does not show.

## 4.1 Know that you lack (`mh_know_i_lack`, new)

Before prayer or learning, say: I am not complete. "כי האדם צריך שיהיה תמיד בדעתו שאינו בשלימות, וצריך השלמה, ואז האדם מקבל השלמה מן השם יתברך" (22). This is not self-blame. It is the stance of one who can receive. A full cup takes nothing. This is MH-S41.

## 4.2 Pray into the hidden (`mh_secret_prayer`, new)

When nothing shows, pray quietly, alone, with no one knowing. Esther's prayer "reached the high hidden place" (40) because it came from hiddenness. The incense was offered in secret (41). A hidden prayer fits a hidden hour. This is MH-S42.

## 4.3 The darkest hour before dawn (`mh_darkest_before_dawn`, new)

When things are at their worst, remember: "at that hour there is no darkness greater" (44), and the dawn leaps. What is from Him comes "with no stretch of time" (45). Let the darkness turn into longing: "and a person longs for the light" (43).

## 4.4 Room for the many (`mh_room_for_others`, new)

When you feel the pull to be the only one, the best, the one who counts, name it: every man "wants to swallow his fellow" (127). Then take on His kingship. Say: He is the One; He wants the many (128). Then look at the other person as someone He wants to exist.

## 4.5 Fed by Him, now (`mh_fed_by_him`, new)

Before eating, think: the food keeps me alive, and "the lasting is the feeding itself" (222). He feeds and takes nothing back (243, 246). My being right now is His giving right now. This joins S65 Given right now.

## 4.6 The door upward (`mh_door_upward`, new)

"A man has an open door to rise to the upper world" (234). "Man stands in the lower world, where he lives, and has a tie to the upper world" (235). In a small, low place, picture the courtyard with its door open upward. You do not have to leave the courtyard. You have to keep the door open.

## 4.7 Joy for the Presence (`mh_joy_for_the_shechina`, new)

"כי אין השכינה שורה רק מתוך שמחה" (284): "the Shechina rests only out of joy." And "אבל הטובה האמיתית היא בלתי גשמית" (284): "true good is not bodily." Joy here is the joy of wholeness (14, 92), not of the feast. Never from drink.

## 4.8 Offer yourself (`mh_offer_yourself`, new)

"כל המקריב מנחה כאילו הקריב נפשו" (314): "one who brings an offering is as if he offered his soul." In prayer, which stands in place of the offerings, give yourself, not only words.

## 4.9 Slow speech (`mh_silence_for_mind`, extended)

"אבל הגדול דבור שלו שכלי, והשכלי אינו ממהר לצאת אל הפעל" (319): "the great man's speech is of the mind, and the mind is slow to come out." Before speaking in anger, wait. The one who jumps first is the common man.

## 5. The big human questions

**Where is G-d when nothing shows?** At work from a place too high to show (81). The hidden face is not an empty face.

**Why does a hard time feel so long?** "For one in pain, time is long" (88). Being in time is change (87). What is from Him is not under time (45), so help can come all at once.

**Is it bad to feel I am not complete?** No. One who knows he lacks is ready to be completed (22). What feels whole already draws loss.

**Why do I want to be the only one?** Man was made alone, so he wants to swallow others (127). When he takes on His kingship, the many are good (128).

**Does G-d take back what He gave?** No (246). He feeds all and takes nothing (243).

**Is anything by chance?** No (101). G-d "does not leave those who serve Him" (102).

**Who hurts me most?** "Each can harm himself more than he can harm another" (145).

**What if I was cut off from my family?** "והשם יתברך מגביה שפלים" (389): "G-d raises the low." He does not "עוזב השפלים המדוכאים" (389): "leave the low and the crushed."

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Constant creation.** "The lasting is the feeding itself" (222) is very close to Gate of Unity ch. 1-2: the word of G-d must stand in the thing always, or it returns to nothing. The Maharal says it through feeding. Chabad says it through speech. Both say the world is kept in being from moment to moment.
- **One start, spreading like water** (178-179) is close to the Chabad picture of the chain of worlds (hishtalshelut) flowing from one source. The Maharal does not add that the flow is not real from His side. He does say the upper world "has no division at all" (170).
- **Tzimtzum.** Not named. The nearest idea is the hidden face (40, 81): He hides, and the hiding is His work. Tanya ch. 48 and Gate of Unity ch. 7 say tzimtzum hides Him from us, not from Himself. The Maharal also puts the hiding on the receiver's side: the miracle did not show because it was too high for us to see (81).
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The Maharal here knows Him through what He does: the caused shows the Cause (68), and the feeding shows the Feeder (222). This is the Rambam's way too (attributes of action). The Maharal's disagreement with the Rambam is in Derekh Chayyim 1164-1173, not here.
- **The Ramak's formula** ("He is all, and all is not He"). "The world was made like a water channel spreading from G-d" (179) is close to its first half. The second half comes through "the lower world is the world of the split" (168).
- **Knowing I lack** (22) is close to Tanya ch. 29-31 on the broken heart that opens the way to joy. The difference: the Maharal says completeness itself draws loss, so knowing you lack is the safe place to stand.
- **"Yehudi" as "yechidi"** (365) is the same reading the Chabad teachings use for self-giving over G-d's oneness (mesirut nefesh).
