## What this range is

The longest continuous run of the book: Elijah's praise of Rabbi Shimon's mouth as Sinai; the Ten Commandments as one word; the Shechinah's garments embroidered with every creature; "set me as a seal on your heart" read eight ways; the heart as a nut, a wine jug and a sick bride whose pulse only the Faithful Shepherd can read; Tikkun 30's cry "wake up, you sleepers"; the three kinds of fear; Ruth and Naomi as body and soul; the pardes of four rivers; Adam's sin and the two trees; Tikkun 57 on the angels and the Name that fills every place; the tree of knowledge and the warning against star-magic; gilgul, mazal and why the righteous suffer; the lame master who leaps with the 42-letter name; and the breath (hevel) of prayer.

## The unity of G-d

**"There is no place empty of Him."** Tikkun 57 (91b) is the page Chassidus quotes more than any other in the Tikkunim after Patach Eliyahu. The context is the angels: each angel is rooted in a sefirah, and each angel's name changes by its errand (lines 2653-2657). Then:

> ולית מלאכא דלא אשתכח ביה שם יהו"ה, דאשתכח בכל אתר, כגוונא דנשמתא דאשתכחת בכל אבר ואבר (TZ 91b:6, line 2658)
> *There is no angel in which the Name is not found, for it is found in every place, as the soul is found in every single limb.*

> דלית אתר פנוי מניה לא בעלאין ולא בתתאין (TZ 91b:6, line 2658)
> *For there is no place empty of Him, not in the upper worlds and not in the lower.*

And the line that follows, often left out when the famous phrase is quoted:

> יהו"ה לא אתקרי ביחודא דארבע אתוון אלא בעלת העלות דמיחד לון (TZ 91b:6, line 2658)
> *The Name is called by the unity of its four letters only through the Cause of causes, who unites them.*

So the practice the text draws is: a person must "crown the Name in all the sefirot, in all the thrones, in all the angels, and in every limb of a person" (same line). There is no name greater "up to Ein Sof and down to no end" (לעילא עד אין סוף, ולתתא עד אין תכלית, line 2659). The soul-in-body picture returns a few folios earlier: God speaks so that His voice is heard in throne, angels, heaven and earth, "as the soul rules in the whole body, even in the smallest limb, and no limb is empty of it":

> כנשמתא דשלטנותה בכל גופא אפילו באבר זעירא ולית אבר פנוי מינה (TZ 79a:6, line 2500)

**Our reading.** The Tanya (Likkutei Amarim ch. 51, Iggeret HaKodesh 25, Shaar HaYichud) builds its whole answer to "how is He in every place?" on this pair: no place empty of Him, as the soul in the limbs. The Tikkunim give it as a claim about names and angels and the body; Chassidus turns it into a meditation on space itself. What the Tikkunim add, and Chassidus keeps, is that the presence in every place is the work of the one Cause who unites the letters (line 2658): the fullness is a unity, not a spread.

**He carries everything and nothing carries Him.** At Sinai He spoke from all four directions,

> דאיהו סביל עלאין ותתאין, ואיהו סמיך עילא ותתא וכרסיא יקרא, ומלאכין ונשמתין, איהו סביל כלא, וקשיר כלא, ומיחד כלא, ולית מאן דסמיך ליה (TZ 64b:4, line 2292)
> *He bears the upper and the lower; He supports above and below, the throne of glory, the angels and the souls. He bears all, ties all, unites all, and there is none who supports Him.*

**No name, and every name for your need.** He is called by every name to show His strength through each rung, "but He has no known name" (וליה לית שמא ידיעא, line 2293). He is called One when He rests in a rung, and has no number when He rests on all worlds. Why so many names? לאחזאה לכל חד מישראל מאתר דקראן ליה לפום צרכייהו, "to show each person the place from which to call Him, according to their need" (line 2293). The many names are for the one who prays.

**The Place of the world.** קודשא בריך הוא הוא אתריה דעלמא, "the Holy One is the place of the world" (line 2390), and again with the Talmud's words: דקודשא בריך הוא אתקרי מקומו של עולם ואין העולם מקומו, "the Holy One is called the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (line 2535).

**He does not change; only the wicked see Him change.** Rabbi Elazar asks: if God appears in so many forms, what of "I the Lord have not changed"? Rabbi Shimon:

> ואיהו לא אשתני ולא אסתתר מינה, אבל לגבי חייביא אשתני קודשא בריך הוא ואסתתר מנייהו (TZ 71b:6, line 2391)
> *He does not change and does not hide from her (the Shechinah); but toward the wicked the Holy One changes and hides from them.*

And: ואית שנויין אוחרנין לטב לכל חד כפום עובדיה, "and there are other changes, for good, to each according to his deeds" (line 2392); אבל לאלין דתליין מניה ומשכינתיה לא אשתני לעולם, "but to those who hang on Him and on His Shechinah He never changes" (line 2393). He appears as lion, ox, eagle or old man (line 2393); the one who clings sees no disguise. Elsewhere: אני יהו"ה לא שניתי, דאיהו רחום וחנון, לית ביה דינא כלל ולא שנויין, "I the Lord have not changed: He is merciful and gracious, there is no judgment in Him at all and no changes" (line 2682). Judgment belongs to the garments.

**The Torah of Atzilut is He.** There is a Torah of creation and a higher Torah "in which is not called creation, only His emanation, and she and He are all one" (ואית אורייתא עלאה דלא אתקרי בה בריאה אלא אצילותא דיליה, והיא והוא כלא חד, line 2284). Whoever toils in Torah, His name is there. (Source of face F10, "Torah and the Holy One are all one".)

**Inside everything, hinted in nothing.** Within the Shechinah the Holy One shines "like a soul in a body; inside everything, the one who holds all and ties all, who is not hinted in any hint, and all is hinted in the Shechinah" (לגו מכלא ההוא דאחיד כלא וקשיר כלא, דלא אתרמיז בשום רמיזו, וכלא אתרמיז בשכינתא, line 2303). Her garments carry the shape of every creature, "so that He looks at every creature through her, to have mercy on them" (בגין לאסתכלא מינה בכל בריין לרחמא עלייהו, line 2301). Our reading: every creature is on the garment God looks at when He looks with mercy.

**He is known by His deeds.** Some look at a king's clothes, some at his body, but ובודאי בעובדוי אשתמודע מאי ניהו מלכא, "surely by his deeds is it known what the king is" (line 2300).

## States of consciousness

**No place empty (ties S62 Only Him, S63 The world held, S72).** From inside: wherever attention goes, a limb, a star, an angel, a room, the Name is there as the soul is in the hand. Entry: the practice the text gives, crowning the Name in every sefirah, throne, angel and limb (line 2658). The guide already has S62; the Tikkunim add the bodily form of it: my own limbs are the first place I learn there is no empty place.

**The imprint that does not leave (Z-S03, from the previous reports, confirmed).** Line 2304 repeats it: דאף על גב דאנת תסתלק מני בגלותא, חותמא דילך אשתאר עמי, ולא אתעדי מני לעלם, "even though You withdraw from me in exile, Your seal remains with me and never leaves me". And the soul's likeness: the angels go up and see the soul's image engraved above, come down and see its image below, וחזאן דלא אשתני מדיוקנא דלעילא ודחלין מניה, "and they see that it has not changed from the image above, and they stand in awe of it" (line 2309). Ties to F06 (the soul as part of the One).

**When the soul wakes in prayer, the throne wakes above (ties S80).** בזמנא דאיהי אתערא לתתא בצלותא, כרסייא אתער לעילא (line 2312). And the general law: ואתערותא דלתתא גרים אתערותא דלעילא, "an awakening below causes an awakening above" (line 2890); אתערותא צריכא מתתא לעילא, "the awakening must go from below upward" (line 2598).

**Asleep, and my heart awake: the sick bride (proposed Z-S09, "the heart that waits for the right voice").** The Shechinah in exile is a bride sick with longing. All the doctors (the sages) bring her perfumes of apple and pomegranate; nothing helps, and her pulse does not return

> ולא אתחזרת רוחא לגבה בהון בגלותא במרעא דילה, ולא דפיקו אתחזרת לגבה, עד דייתי רעיא מהימנא רחימו דילה (TZ 70b:6, line 2379)
> *Her breath did not return to her through them in the illness of her exile, nor did her pulse return, until the Faithful Shepherd came, her beloved.*

He brings an apple to her nose, she smells it and knows him; "the comforter who restores my soul is far from me" turns to comfort, and at once breath and pulse return. Later the same story: a great physician reads her pulse in ten kinds, "and she recognized the physician and said: the voice of my beloved knocks" (ואיהי אשתמודעא באסיא, ואמרת (שם ה ב) קול דודי דופק, line 2830). From inside: a numbness that no general comfort reaches, and then one voice that is recognized. Ties to S03 (Numb, the stone heart) and S13 (The unnamed longing). Our note: in Chassidus the "faithful shepherd" in every generation is the teacher whose word is recognized by the soul; the guide should not claim that role, only point to the voice the person recognizes.

**Wake up: she is among you (ties S16 Knowing it, not feeling it; S12 The flat world).** Tikkun 30 (73b), the passage the Chassidic masters cite for their mission: a spirit blows on those who learn Torah and cries

> קומו ואתערו לגבי שכינתא, דאית לכון לבא בלא סכלתנו למנדע בה, ואיהו ביניכו (TZ 73b:5, line 2419)
> *Get up and wake toward the Shechinah, for you have a heart without the understanding to know her, and she is among you.*

"All the kindness they do, they do for themselves, even those who toil in Torah" (כל חסד דעבדין לגרמייהו עבדין, ואפילו כל אינון דמשתדלין באורייתא, line 2420). From inside: the wake-up is not to a far God but to one who is already in the room.

**Stripped of every other thought (proposed technique Z-T07, "one thought in the Shema").** The strongest meditation instruction in the range:

> זכאה איהו מאן דסליק אמונה דקודשא בריך הוא דאיהי שכינתיה כלילא מעשר אמירן, במחשבתא חדא ברעותא חדא בלא ערבוביא כלל (TZ 97b:6, line 2728)
> *Fortunate is one who raises the faith of the Holy One, His Shechinah made of ten sayings, in one thought and one will, with no mixture at all.*

"Many are the thoughts in a man's heart": these are shells, and a person must strip them all off, as a man takes off his clothes to be one with his wife,

> הכי צריך לאפשטא מניה כל מחשבין אחרנין, בזמנא דמיחד לקודשא בריך הוא בכל יומא תרין זמנין (TZ 97b:6, line 2728)
> *so he must strip off from himself every other thought when he unites the Holy One twice every day* (in the Shema).

And: no barrier between him and the wall, which the text reads as the walls of the heart (דלא יהא דבר חוצץ בינו לבין הקיר, line 2664). Ties to technique 12 (returning the wandering mind) and 3 (choosing the one line). The Tikkunim's picture is not emptiness but nakedness before the Beloved: nothing between.

## Meditation and practice

**1. Mouth and heart the same.** ומאן דאתעסק באורייתא כדין צריך דיהא פומא ולבא שוין, "one who learns Torah must have mouth and heart equal" (line 2285), with the Talmud's "whoever is not inside as outside may not enter the study hall". The heart is a jug for the wine of Torah: rinse it inside and out; וכד איהו קנקן דאיהו לבא דכיא מלגאו ומלבר, שריא תמן חכמה, "when the jug, the heart, is clean inside and out, wisdom rests there" (line 2406). Ties to S17 (Feeling fake) as its cure.

**2. Break the shell of the heart.** ועוד לבא הוא אגוז, ומאן דתבר קליפה דיליה דאיהו יצר הרע, עליה אתמר (תהלים נא יט) לב נשבר ונדכה אלהי"ם לא תבזה (line 2361): "the heart is a nut; whoever breaks its shell, the evil inclination, of him it is said: a broken and crushed heart God will not despise". The proud heart, whole in its shells, the Shechinah does not rest on. Ties to S59 True lowliness; C4.

**3. Keep the heart a Shabbat home: no sadness, no anger.** וצריך דלא יתער (נ"א לאתערא) עציבו וקטטה מסטרא דטחול, אלא דתהא לב נטיר מכלהו, "let no sadness or quarrel be woken from the spleen's side; let the heart be guarded from all of them" (line 2363). "Fortunate is one who keeps a dwelling for Shabbat, which is the heart, so that the sadness of the spleen and the anger of the gall do not come near" (line 2577), for דכל מאן דכעיס כאלו אוקיד נורא דגיהנם, "whoever is angry, it is as if he lit the fire of Gehinnom" (line 2577). Ties to S15 Angry at someone; S04. (Ours: the Tikkunim locate sadness and anger in the body's organs; the guide keeps the practice, a guarded heart on Shabbat, and not the anatomy.)

**4. Love and awe in both good and bad.** Fear of God so that my children live or my money grows is not the root:

> אבל רחימו ודחילו עיקרא דיליה בין טב ובין ביש (TZ 73b:8, line 2422)
> *But love and awe at their root are in good and in bad alike.*

Three levels (line 2470): the complete tzaddik fears Him "in good times and in distress" (בין בטיבו בין בעאקו); the beinoni in good times only; the wicked in neither. Love and fear for a reward "are of the maidservant" (lines 2423-2424). And: ולית פקודא דלא אתייהיב בדחילו ורחימו, "there is no mitzvah that was not given in awe and love" (line 2436). Ties to technique 16, S43, S46; the Tanya's "love that depends on nothing" (ch. 41-44).

**5. Pray before you speak.** Before a hard teaching Rabbi Shimon stands, sits a moment, and prays: רבון עלמין דאנת איהו מארי מלכין וגלי רזין, יהא רעוא דילך לסדרא מלין בפימאי, "Master of the worlds, Lord of kings and revealer of secrets, may it be Your will to set words in order in my mouth", "that I not come to shame before You" (line 2411). Proposed technique Z-T08 (ours): before speaking to someone about God, a one-line prayer for the right words. Ties to technique 20.

**6. Prayer is a ladder; the breath carries it.** זכאה איהו מאן דסליק צלותין ביה, דהא צלותא איהי סלם, "fortunate is one who raises prayers by it, for prayer is a ladder" (line 2550), on which the angels go up and down. The breath (hevel) of prayer and good deeds is one thing; the breath of idle, empty words is another: ואית הבל דמלין בטלין מלין ריקנין (line 2855). And: זכאה איהו מאן דמחבר לון כחדא, ואפיק לון מפומוי ברחימו ודחילו, "fortunate is one who joins them (voice and breath) together and brings them out of his mouth in love and awe" (line 2896). Ties to technique 18.

**7. Partner in creation through speech.** Read "you are My people" (ammi) as "you are with Me" (immi), in partnership:

> הכי אנת בשותפו עמי, דבמלולך אתעבידו שמיא וארעא (TZ 104a:2, line 2806)
> *So you are in partnership with Me, for by your speech heaven and earth are made.*

God takes the words of prayer "and builds worlds with them" (line 2828). And one who works in His service has all creation helping him: דכל בריין דעלמא אינון בסיעתא דיליה (line 2828). Ties to S29 (Good thought joined to deed); F01.

**8. Lend to the poor as the moon borrows from the sun.** הכי שכינתא אמרת לוו עלי ואני פורע, דהכי צריך בר נש למהוי מלוה למסכנא, "so the Shechinah says: borrow on My account and I will repay; so must a person lend to the poor" (line 2547).

**9. Hide in teshuvah.** When the destroyer has leave, a person should hide; ובמאי נסתר בר נש ... בתיובתא, "and where does a person hide from these judgments? In teshuvah" (line 2689), "who dwells in the shelter of the Most High". But from God Himself no one hides: וכי יכיל בר נש לאתכסיא מניה, "can a person hide from Him?" Adam hid his face for shame (line 2687). Ties to S84, S68.

**10. A guard against magic.** Woe to those who leave the Torah to "busy themselves with the angels over the stars, sun and moon, and those over spirits and demons, to be like gods knowing good and evil" (line 2717); this is the fruit of the tree of knowledge and caused the Temple's fall (lines 2718-2723). "Whoever pictures the Holy One above in any form or image" is clothed in that image when he leaves the world (דכל מאן דצייר לעיל לקודשא בריך הוא, line 2724). The guide's rule against letter-magic and imagery of God (technique 25) is the Tikkunim's own.

## The big questions

**Why were we made, if Adam would sin?** Rabbi Elazar asks the question directly (line 2730). The answer:

> דיוקנא דלעילא לא הוה שלים עד דברא לאדם (TZ 98a:2, line 2730)
> *The form above was not complete until He created Adam.*

As a soul cannot act without a body, the upper form needed a form below "to bring out action through it". Our reading: this is the Tikkunim's version of the "dwelling below" (F07): the purpose of the human is to be the hand of the above. And free choice is the point: the body was made of good and evil בגין למהוי ליה אגרא ועונשא, "so that he would have reward and punishment", or he would be like a beast (line 2732); and God showed him both roads.

**Death is not His decree.** וכמה טפשין אינון בעלמא דאמרין הא קודשא בריך הוא גזר על בר נש דימות, "how foolish are those who say the Holy One decreed that man must die" (line 2735); in Atzilut "there is no sin and no death" (line 2736).

**Suffering, gilgul and the stars of birth.** The righteous sometimes suffer "as the deeds of the wicked": the cause is gilgul, a debt carried from before (lines 2785, 2897). "Children, life and livelihood depend not on merit but on mazal" (lines 2753-2759), and a person may be bound before birth to a hard hour; each soul has its time in gilgul (דכל נשמתא אית לה עת וזמן בגלגולא, line 2777). Our note: the guide should hold these carefully. They explain suffering without blaming the sufferer, which is their mercy; they can also sound like fate, which is their danger. The Tikkunim themselves keep the door open: God troubles to bring souls back "for the honor of the righteous, to remove sin from their children" (line 2750); a tree cut down "grows again from its roots, and new branches rise as before" (line 2791); and the tzaddik goes down even into Gehinnom "to bring out every soul that only thought of teshuvah and died young, and he takes trouble for it" (line 2467).

**Love beyond the grave.** Ruth is the nefesh, Orpah the body. When the body does teshuvah, the soul says to it "where you die I will die, and there I will be buried" (באשר תמותי אמות ושם אקבר (שם א יז), ולא תזוז נפשא בקברא מניה, line 2453): the soul does not leave it even in the grave. God "does not let the work of His hands be lost" (וכלא בגין דלא ישתצון עובדי ידוי, line 2456).

**Each one carries the world.** לעולם יראה האדם על עצמו כאלו כל העולם תלוי עליו, "a person should always see himself as if the whole world hangs on him" (line 2793). And at Sinai "no one was missing" (איש לא נעדר לא חסר מנייהו חד מחשבנא, line 2586), all souls of all generations were there.

**Suffering loved turns to mercy.** ואלין דחביבין יסורין אתהפך לון מדינא לרחמי, "for those to whom sufferings are dear, (the Name) turns from judgment to mercy" (line 2695). But suffering when the Shechinah is absent, from lies and empty speech, is דלאו אינון יסורין של אהבה, "not sufferings of love" (line 2895).

**The fallen are raised.** "Though she has no power to rise by herself, the Holy One will raise her" (line 2700). And truth holds up the world: דאמת הוא סביל עלאין ותתאין (line 2694); one who calls Him in falsehood is far from Him (line 2691).

**The end: no wall between.** "In the future the Holy One will remove these shells and show Himself to Israel in the kernel within ... and there will be no partition between the Holy One and His Shechinah and Israel" (ולא יהיה מחיצה בין קודשא בריך הוא ושכינתיה ובין ישראל, line 2865). The shells are "a partition between Israel and their Father in heaven" (line 2864), and He clothes Himself in them now "to guard Israel", "in all their distress He is distressed".

## Chassidus and the Tikkunim here (ours)

- **The core text of Chabad's unity doctrine is here** (line 2658), and so is its body-picture (line 2500). Chabad reads "no place empty of Him" literally and radically (Shaar HaYichud ch. 7, against a literal tzimtzum). The Tikkunim's own sentence ties the fullness to the angels and names and says the unity is made by the Cause of causes; that is the Chabad reading's strongest support.
- **"He does not change, only toward the wicked"** (2391) is the germ of the Chassidic teaching that the hiding is only from our side (F03): He is unchanged; the garments change.
- **Awe and love in good and bad** (2422, 2470) is the Tanya's ideal of love not bound to a cause.
- **Where the Tikkunim differ:** mazal and gilgul govern livelihood even for the righteous (2753-2759). The Baal Shem Tov's school moves the weight toward providence over every detail and trust; the Tikkunim leave more to the hour of birth. The guide should keep the Tikkunim's mercy (no blame) and Chassidus's trust.
