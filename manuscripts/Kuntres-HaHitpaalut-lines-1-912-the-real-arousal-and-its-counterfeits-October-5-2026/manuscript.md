## The shape of the range

This book does not teach the unity. It teaches how to tell whether the unity has reached you. It is a field guide to arousal (hitpa'alut): what the real thing is, what it is mistaken for, and how to tell them apart. So the faces that lead are the three faces of the person's side: - F08: bitul, the absence of self-feeling. - F12: knowing that moves from mind into heart. - F06: the soul as a part of God.

The cosmological faces hardly appear: F01 twice, F02 five times, F04 six times. When they do appear, it is as the *content* of the contemplation (sovev and memale, "all before Him is as nothing") and never as the subject.

The book's thesis is one sentence at line 135. The arousal must be "only Godly arousal and not the arousal of the life of flesh". Everything else unpacks the word "Godly". An arousal is Godly when: - its source is the part of God in the soul, not the self; - it is aroused by the thing itself, not by the wish to be aroused; - it leaves no feeling of self behind.

That is the tie to the unity, and the text states it. It says it in the language of the soul, not of the worlds.

## First letter (1-115): the soul asleep

The letter opens with a complaint. Worry over livelihood has emptied the chassidim's minds and hearts. Chassidus is heard in one hour and the body is served in another, "with no joining at all, rather utter separation" (line 16, *reading* F12). The one teaching in the letter is about sleep. The life is not gone from a sleeper. It is hidden, and it comes back into view when he wakes (lines 10-11). Then the text names this hiding:

> כך טרדת הפרנסה שנקרא גלות השכינה נקרא שינה (line 12)

That is F03, stated: the distraction is the exile of the Shechinah. The book closes on the same image at line 899: "this is the essential exile, likened to sleep". The whole tract sits inside it.

The letter also states the goal. Heart arousal is "the essence of the revelation of Godliness in the congregation of Israel" (line 47), and it is "the root that brings the perfection of the days of Moshiach" (line 48, F07). Lines 33 and 61 give the F12 pairing, mind first and then heart, "return it to your heart", and line 33 names what that produces:

"God within me, literally." The second half of the letter (89-115) is about travel to Liadi and gathering in towns, and it is *none*. Three lines on communal "complete oneness" (98, 99, 102) are marked *reading*. The text means unity among chassidim; linking it to God's unity is ours.

## The rule (116-159): Godly arousal, and the bond beneath it

The second letter begins by naming two opposite errors (126-128). Some hear and grasp and think that is all of Chassidus. Others forbid any arousal, because a sudden one "is feeling oneself" (line 127). The book's work is to show that this second group has mistaken the real thing for its counterfeit.

The rule itself comes in lines 134-135. The Alter Rebbe's whole aim was that Godliness be revealed in the chassidim's souls:

> שיהיה התפעלות נפשם רק התפעלות אלקות דוקא ולא התפעלות חיי בשר (line 135)

Then comes the ground of the rule. Love (ahavah) and cleaving (devekut) differ. Love is an arousal that needs toil. Cleaving is already there:

> שהדביקות הוא חיות והתקשרות הנפש בחיי החיים עצמות אור אין סוף ברוך הוא (line 144)

This is F05 and F06 together. The soul is bound to the Essence itself, "of itself, in an essential and constant bond" (line 146). This bond is the yechidah, Rashbi's "one knot" (147, 153). It is in every Jew, even one whose soul is only nefesh of Asiyah (148, 154). That is why the Shema comes first, "to give the soul over into the One" (149), and "you shall love" only after it.

The proof offered is ordinary experience (156-159). A simple person hears an explanation of sovev and memale and is deeply stirred. That comes from the Godliness in his soul, drawn "as a spark after the flame" (158), "and this is called Godly arousal proper" (159).

## The counterfeit (160-224): strange fire, and two opposites in one subject

At once the text turns to what the real thing is confused with. There is a cleaving that looks exactly the same (164). It is the outward cry of the heart of flesh, "strange fire from the heat of the blood" (175). Its name gives it away:

"This is called serving oneself." It ends in the boast "I, and none besides me" (line 181). The text names the phrase. Reading it as the self taking the place of "none besides Him" is ours (*reading*). Yet even here, line 192 keeps F03: "a little mixture of Godly light, in great concealment" is inside the counterfeit.

The key to the distinction is a teaching of the Alter Rebbe on holiness and nogah (197-204). In holiness, the vessel, the light's own "somethingness", is in complete bitul. In nogah, the vessel is felt and spreads beyond its light:

> שבקדושה בחינת הכלי שהוא בחינת הישות של האור הוא בבחינת הבטול בתכלית (line 197)

The consequence runs against intuition: "the more it is reduced and unfelt, the more light" (204). That creates the puzzle the rest of the book answers. How can a real arousal be felt in the heart of flesh and yet unfelt?

"Two opposites in one subject." That is F09, stated, at the centre of the book. The answer is melody (208-219). The niggun comes suddenly, of itself. It is felt, but the one who sings does not feel himself singing. It resembles the joy that claps the hands at good news, because the arousal is "true only from the good thing itself to which his soul is bound" (218).

## The natural soul's five levels (225-316)

This is the most structural stretch: 80 of its 92 lines are *structural*. It is a ladder of the natural (intellectual) soul's response to contemplation.

1. **Wanting arousal for its own sake.** This is not counted among the five (304). All he wants is "some vitality so as to be a yesh" (228, F08 stated). Even so, the hidden love is inside it (229-230).
2. **Hearing from afar** (233-241). He acknowledges the truth, but "it does not belong to him". It is "not for himself but for Godliness alone" (240), and that makes it the true beginning.
3. **Good thought joined to deed** (242-267). Thought that touches him ("moving from its place", 249) gives birth to love and awe for action, "that Godliness be revealed in good deeds" (266).
4. **Felt heart arousal** (268-288). This is "the main mitzvah of the service of the heart, to toil in thought until the heart is aroused" (271). It is hard, because the Godliness in contemplation stays surrounding (makif) the mind and does not enter the heart (275).
5. **Kavanah** (289-302). This is intent that holds the whole length of the idea. Heart arousal turns out to be a contraction of it, and its content is:

At this level love and awe "are not separate from the contemplation at all" (298). Knowing and feeling are one here, which is F12 at its fullest.

Then the turn (312-314). All five levels, even the simple will above reason, belong to the natural soul. They are "serving God with the body, not with the soul":

> כי בחינת הנשמה מצד עצמה הרי היא כח אלהי ממש (line 313)

## Why the Godly soul's arousal differs in kind (317-436)

This is the densest *stated* stretch outside the Godly soul's levels, and F06 leads it. The reasoning goes step by step: - Some God-fearing people have no "sense of hearing" at all, so the sense cannot come from a good heart. It comes "from the soul itself, which has Godly power" (323). - The Godly soul's comprehension is Godliness itself, "even the comprehension" (328). - The natural soul is also moved only by the Godliness in what it grasps. But its grasp comes through a garment that hides (329, split into three).

> אבל השגה זו מבחינת לבוש דנוג״ה המסתיר על עצם האור האלהי (line 329)

The picture of Eden explains the difference (339-369). There the soul enjoys Godly light "without concealment or contraction" (342). Its comprehension is "in the Godly ayin" (344), because it is "actually a part of God" (353). Even in Eden it wears a garment, only "so that it not be nullified out of existence" (366). That is F03 in its classic role: the hiding lets the creature go on existing.

The book gives a practical sign of the hiding:

> מזה מורה על היות שבחינת אלהות שבהשגה זו מוסתר בה מאד (line 372)

If a moment passes between hearing and being moved, the Godliness in the idea is hidden. The summary sits at line 379. The Godly soul's arousal is not "the arousal of something separate from Godliness". Line 427 gives F01, stated, inside the psychology. The natural soul can be moved by Godliness at all only because nogah too draws "from the Godly power that brings all into being":

> מצד שיש לבחינת נוגה גם כן שרש יניקה מכח אלהי שמהוה את הכל (line 427)

## The Godly soul's five levels (437-625)

The ladder now runs through the Godly soul, and it starts at the bottom, with deeds done by nature.

**Nefesh: the good deed done by nature (437-494).** Every Jew keeps Shabbat, avoids idolatry and eats matzah "from his essential Jewishness", not from choice (443, 446). "Here the one who serves is equal to the light one" (444). The root of this is faith, and here the book gives its plainest statement of the unity. It puts that statement in the mouth of a thief who prays for success at the tunnel:

> שה׳ הוא העוזר לכל כי הוא ברא את הכל והוא אחדות פשוט (line 470)

The faith is real and natural. The theft comes from the natural soul, and the two sit side by side as "two separate things" (469). That is F12's gap at its most extreme: the unity believed and fully cut off from the deed.

**Ruach (495-537).** The Exodus shows it: they left Egypt "by the simple faith in their soul" (497), like a child after its father (503). Converts show it: sparks scattered by the breaking of the vessels, stirred by "a Godly spirit that he himself does not know" (505-507, F11). Yom Kippur penitents show it: God is revealed to every spark, and it weeps of itself (516-518). Martyrdom shows it most:

> אך ליפרד לגמרי מאחד בזה מעורר כח היותר פנימי ועצמי (line 531)

A man who might commit many sins will die rather than be separated from the One. The power comes "only from the essential bond of the Godly soul with its source, which cannot be separated in any way" (533, F05 + F06).

**Neshamah (538-563).** Here "my heart and my flesh sing to the living God" (541). The heart of flesh itself sings, and without choice, like melody. The confusion is named precisely:

> עד שיעשה עיקר מן ההתפעלות ולא מן האלהות כלל (line 548)

"Until he makes the arousal the main thing, and not Godliness." Then comes the rule of the whole book:

> וזהו הכלל גדול דכל שההתפעלות העצמות עמוקה יותר (line 556)

The deeper the arousal, the less it is felt. This level "is the beginning of the true revelation of Godliness in the heart of every Jew" (561).

**Chayah: doubled song (564-598).** Here the point of the idea is itself what is aroused (572), "in the essence of the Godly light itself, not from comprehending it" (580). This is Shabbat prayer. Weekday prayer is for refining the natural soul, and that happens only when the Godly soul reaches the physical heart:

> שתפלה העיקרית היא לברר ולזכך לנפש הטבעית (line 583)

That is F11 and F07 together: the purpose of prayer is the lowest part of the person.

**Yechidah: simple song (599-625).** "The essence itself rises in song" (600). It is one will, before chesed and gevurah divide:

> אלא רק רצון אחד שכל הרצונות נכללים וטפלים בו (line 606)

That is F09 and F05. Opposites are included in the one will that comes before them. The parable is a man saving his life, for whom wife, children and livelihood become "as nothing" (608, *reading* for F02). This level is rare, but it shines hidden in every Jew. Every Jew is drawn when he hears a Godly matter, even without understanding, "so the unlearned and scholars are exactly equal" (621-623).

## The groups of chassidim (626-889)

The last third applies the map to real people. It is mostly *structural* diagnosis, and F08 leads.

- **Cold hearers and the dulled (640-652).** They cannot bear the toil of memale and sovev (640). They spend the yechidah of the natural soul on trade (644, *reading*: devotion to the Essence turned toward business). Yet "the essence of his Jewish nature truly remains" (647).
- **Simple teshuvah (653-669).** Seeing that he does not hear, such a man cries out without contemplation. The book defends the cry:

> ונמצא אחת היא רק שזה מצד המנגד (line 662)

Bitter weeping and joyful singing are one arousal. The weeping comes "from the opposing side". The book grounds this in the Zohar that the Tanya quotes (Likkutei Amarim 34):

> בכיה תקיעא בליבאי מסטרא דא וחדוה מסטרא דא (line 664)

At the same level at which the Godly soul rejoices, it weeps "over its distance from the One" (666, F09 stated). - **The flash (670-731).** He says "Aha, I have seen the light", and it ends there (675-678). The cause is the lack of a broken heart (689). Here the book cites the Maggid, by way of the Alter Rebbe. No one receives the deep comprehension of Or Ein Sof without a natural melancholy (702), and then:

> אז ישכון בו ממקור כל החיים מקורא דכולא להחיות רוח נדכאים (line 704)

> ואז נהפך האנחה שלו והמרה שחורה הטבעיות הנזכר לעיל לשמחה ועונג רק מצד אלהות ששורה על נפשו ממש (line 708)

That is F07 in the person, and F11 as sigh turned to joy. - **Heart without mind; mind without deed (732-797).** Do not make a yesh of your arousal (741). Let the arousal feed understanding, until the heart is "included and united in the mind" (748). Then the F10 argument:

> להיות ידוע שאין לאור אחיזה ותפיסה רק בכלי (line 769)

The deed is the vessel. Without it, arousal is "a flame flying in the air" (778). - **False bitul (798-853).** This is the book's sharpest passage on confusion. A young man feels a deep "kvell" in the mind and thinks it is essential bitul. The sign that it was not:

> בא לכלל הרגשת העצמיות מזה עצמו דוקא (line 808)

Afterward he feels himself, from that very experience. He wanted bitul "as the one thing he lacked" (814). Bitul sought as an acquisition is its own opposite. The true sign is the reverse: true lowliness afterward, and indifference to insult, "for he has nothing of his own" (829). Line 849 gives the rule:

> וכל שלא היה בו בחינת הזזה וביטול אמיתי לא יוכל לקרב לאלהים קירוב אמיתי לעולם (line 849)

- **Yechidah and the simple (854-889).** Most who claim the simple pleasure are imagining it (861-863). But one heart-of-flesh arousal can be wholly true. It comes from the heart's lowest point, the "beast" below knowledge:

> שנקרא בחינת בהמה שלמטה מן הדעת ששרשה למעלה מן הדעת (line 871)

> הרי חיות עונג בשרי זה מגיע לבחינת עונג העצמית שביחידה (line 882)

The lowest in the soul is rooted in the highest (F09 and F05, and F07 in miniature). Its condition is wholeness (temimut) "without any self-feeling" (875). It is found "more among simple people than among the understanding and wise" (887).

## What this book adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

1. **A theory of the counterfeit.** The Tanya describes love and awe and how to produce them. This book describes what looks like them and is not: strange fire, false devekut, arousal sought for vitality, the "kvell" that ends in self-regard. It gives signs for each: the delay (372), the aftermath (808), the response to insult (813, 829), and whether the love reaches deeds (833).
2. **Unfelt feeling as the mark of the real.** "The deeper, the less felt" (556). The Tanya does not make the *absence of self-feeling* inside a felt arousal the test. Here it is the whole test, stated as a unity of opposites (207).
3. **Contemplation's love belongs to the natural soul.** All five levels born of contemplation, up to simple will, are "serving God with the body" (312). The Godly soul's own arousal is different in kind (419-430). The Tanya (ch. 12, Chassidus-txt Tanya line 185) has the Godly soul's ten powers ruling during Shema and prayer. It treats the love born of contemplation in prayer as the Godly soul's. The two are not flatly opposed, since the Kuntres says the Godly soul's arousal is clothed inside the natural one (414). But the division of labour differs.
4. **Weeping and joy as one arousal.** The Tanya quotes the same Zohar (Likkutei Amarim 34:9). This book uses it to defend the unlearned man's cry as Godly arousal "from the opposing side" (660-664).
5. **The simple above the wise.** At the yechidah the unlearned and scholars are equal (623), and the true fleshly pleasure is found "more among simple people" (887). The Tanya's ch. 18-19 hidden love is also in every Jew. What is new is that the book ranks the simple higher in this one respect.
6. **The unity stated in a thief's faith.** "He created all and is simple oneness" (470) is the book's only plain cosmological statement of the unity. It is there to show how faith can live fully cut off from the deed.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- **76 lines are *none*.** Most of the first letter (the travel plan, 89-115), the formal apologies (68-88), and the closing (894-912).
- **The ladders are structural.** 605 lines, two-thirds of the book, are steps, signs, parables (the treasure of another, the good news, cooked and raw meat, the flame in the air) and kabbalistic mappings (570, 594-596). They serve the distinction. Only the lines that name Godliness, the Godly soul, bitul or the One were marked *stated*.
- **Nine lines are *reading*.** They are lines 8, 14, 16, 98, 99, 102, 181, 608 and 644. The communal "complete oneness" of 98 and 102 is the text's word, but it means unity among chassidim.
- **The cosmological faces are nearly absent.** F01 appears at 427 and 470. F02 appears at 291, 344, 181 (reading) and 608 (reading). F04 appears only as the content of contemplation (59, 156, 275, 291, 581, 640). Line 275's "surrounding in the mind" uses F04's word for the psyche. The text does not draw the parallel.
- **Faces are sometimes carried by vocabulary.** "Godly arousal" is tagged F06 because the text grounds it in the soul as "part of God" (353, 359, 419). Where a line only says "Godliness" and gives no ground, it was left structural.
- **Line 546 repeats line 545 word for word.** This is a duplication in the file.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **With the SPINE.** SPINE §5 says that in this book "most passages are about the psychology of arousal" and that "the link of each passage to the unity is our reading". The first half is right: 605 lines are structural. The second half is too strong. 222 lines state their tie in the text's own words: Godly arousal against self-arousal, bitul, part of God, the bond to the Essence, separation from the One. Only 9 ties are ours. The honest version is that the book states the unity from the soul's side, and almost never from the worlds' side.
- **With the Tanya, on how much heart arousal matters.** Tanya ch. 16 says that love hidden in the mind and heart's understanding (tevunah), clothed in deed, already gives the mitzvot wings (Tanya line 235). This book calls that level "good thought joined to deed". It ranks that level third of five, says heart arousal "is the essence of the revelation of Godliness" (47), and says that "the light of God never reached" those who pray dry (34). The Tanya consoles the one who cannot feel. This book warns him.
- **Within the book itself.** Heart arousal is "the essence of the revelation of Godliness" (47, 61). Yet kavanah in the mind is higher, and heart arousal is "smallness" and a contraction beside it (290-292). The book also forbids forbidding arousal (743) and warns against making a yesh of it (741). Both sides stand in the text, and neither is resolved into the other.
- **Contemplation or simplicity.** Most of the book demands toil in contemplation (271, 640, 728). It also says the highest Godly arousal needs no understanding, and that the simple reach the yechidah more often than the wise (621-623, 887). This is the SPINE's "simplicity or comprehension" dispute, and it is kept here inside a single book.
- **With the Gate of Unity.** The Mitteler Rebbe's Gate of Unity says real contemplation yields joy and bitul "in equal measure" (Gate line 199). This book agrees that bitul is the test. It makes that test harsher, though: bitul that is felt as an achievement is its opposite (814).
