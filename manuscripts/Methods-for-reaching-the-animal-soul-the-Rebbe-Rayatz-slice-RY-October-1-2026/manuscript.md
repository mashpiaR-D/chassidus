## 1. Overview: what this slice adds

The earlier files took from the Rayatz the core claims: the animal soul has a mind (RS 1360), its mind is narrow (RS 555-556), explain first and use the stick second, and the "friendly yes-man" counterfeit. This slice adds the **practical psychology** that fills the sichos and letters, and a clearer **sequence** from the maamarim.

1. **The animal soul is a clever craftsman, so the first skill is detection.** Chassidus's intellect exists "to know how to get around the animal soul with its little wisdoms" (RY01). Ask "who is speaking" (RY02). It speaks through pride and through gloom alike.
2. **There is an order for explaining to it** (RY04-RY07): make physicality repulsive; bring it to rational thought in general; go through the intellectual soul, the "middle soul"; explain the idea, then the G-dliness in it; start from what it already loves, its own life (RY05); show it what is mortal (RY06); aim at your own particular weak trait and at today (RY07).
3. **It is reached by feeling more than by argument.** It is moved before it understands (RY08). Praise lifts it "from outside" (RY09). Song "brings it out" (RY10). A farbrengen puts "two G-dly souls against one" and the animal soul "senses the depth from which the words come" (RY11). Mashke "softens the hide" (RY12). A mashpia should make the student smell his own stink (RY15).
4. **The stick is for the evil; mercy is for the animal.** The Rayatz separates the yetzer hara ("the image of evil": rebuke it, throw it out) from the animal soul ("only the power of desire": have mercy on it, make it a vessel) (RY14, section 3).
5. **When understanding fails, the deed and the yoke carry it** (RY16-RY21, RY30): fence and deed by kabbalas ol from the yechidah; standing firm with mesirus nefesh weakens it; tactics such as "show it a good thing, then seize it"; closing the gates; doing the opposite of what it wants.
6. **Time and dose:** drops, not glasses (RY25); fifteen minutes a day (RY22); a nightly court case (RY23); habit that becomes nature (RY33).
7. **Stories** (marked STORY) show the methods at work: the Rashab and the milk (RY19), the 600 "fasts" (RY22), R. Pinye's shame before simple Chassidim (RY29), R. Hendel crying at Modeh Ani after 67 years of the animal soul tormenting him (section 3).

## 2. The methods ### RY01. Outwit the clever one

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** The animal soul is a craftsman at its trade. It comes to each person with its own small wisdoms. The intellect of Chassidus is given to you mainly so you can see through them and get around them.
- **How to do it:** - Learn Chassidus as a tool for avodah, not as knowledge to own. - Expect the animal soul to answer every good intention with a clever reason. - Smell out where the trick lies. Check yourself first: often the bad smell you blame on someone else comes from you.
- **Why it works:** The animal soul is 'an artisan at its work' and comes to everyone with its little wisdoms; 'for this' Chassidus gave intellect and understanding. Chassidim are called clever because Chassidus lets them twist free of those wisdoms.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always. Most needed by learned people, because the clever one 'sharpens its teeth to bite each according to his level: this one with intellect, that one with avodah'. **Not:** Intellect alone is 'nothing'. Seeing through the trick is not yet changing the trait.
- **Quotes:** - «וויסן ווי ארומצוגיין דעם נפש הבהמית מיט זיינע חכמה'לאך און המצאה'לאך» — *to know how to get around the animal soul with its little wisdoms and little inventions.* Sefer HaSichos 5707, Pesach talks. `SH:7018` (typed) - «ווייסן זיי אוסצודרייען זיך פון די חכמה'לאך פון נה"ב, אבער השכלה אליין איז דאך גארניט» — *by the intellect of Chassidus they know how to twist free of the animal soul's little wisdoms; but intellect alone is nothing.* Sefer HaSichos 5707, Pesach talks. `SH:7020` (typed) - «דערשמעקן זיי וואו דער עכבר ליגט, אמאל דאכט זיך אז עס שמעקט פון יענעם און באמת שמעקט גאר פון זיך אליין» — *[Chassidim] sniff out where the mouse lies. Sometimes it seems the smell comes from the other fellow, and in truth it smells from oneself.* Sefer HaSichos 5709, Simchas Torah eve before hakafos. `SH:8709` (typed)

## RY02. Ask who is speaking

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** Before acting on any thought, ask which soul is saying it. The animal soul dresses up. It can appear as a pious man, a pauper, a jester or a nobleman. It speaks through both pride and gloom.
- **How to do it:** - With every matter, stop and ask: who is the speaker? The G-dly soul, the intellectual soul, the natural soul, or the animal soul? - Treat both self-satisfaction with your spiritual state and sadness about it as its voice. - Know its costumes, so you recognise it.
- **Why it works:** Its first move is never open sin. It agrees with you, befriends you, and argues from good things (for example: pray quickly so you can teach your guests). Its aim is to stop Torah, prayer and refining the traits. The same trick raises one person and lowers another.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always, and especially for students, when 'one meets the animal soul face to face'. The melancholic is shown everything in black; the sanguine is shown everything in bright white.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul is 'the true wise one'. Questions like 'who am I to do this?' are asked by the clever one, not by the G-dly soul (SH 9171).
- **Quotes:** - «װער איז דער זאָגער צי דער נפש אלקית צי דער נפש השכלית אָדער דער נפש הטבעית אָדער גאָר דער נפש הבהמית» — *one must always, with every thing, know who is the speaker: the G-dly soul, the intellectual soul, the natural soul, or even the animal soul.* Sefer HaSichos 5703, p. 77. `SHo:4055` (OCR) - «ער פארשטעלט זיך אמאל פאר א ארימאן, פאר א עושר, פאר א בדחן, אמאל גאר פאר א פריץ, אבער מען דארף אים קענען מיט אלע זיינע התחכמות'ן» — *he disguises himself sometimes as a pauper, a rich man, a jester, sometimes even as a nobleman; but one must know him with all his clevernesses.* Sefer HaSichos, Simchas Torah night 5707 (retelling the Rebbe Rashab, 5658). `SH:6651` (typed) - «השמחה - צופרידענקייט - פון זיך במעמד ומצב הרוחני, וכן העצבות ממעמד ומצב הרוחני, הן שתי מדות רעות של הנה"ב» — *Self-satisfaction with one's spiritual state, and sadness over one's spiritual state, are two bad traits of the animal soul.* Igros Kodesh vol. 9, letter. `IKo:29382` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY03. Tell it to its face

- **Family:** speech
- **What it is:** Speak to the animal soul (or the yetzer hara) directly, in the second person, with firmness. Name its trick. Order it about.
- **How to do it:** - Stand firm and say: 'I know you, with all your little wisdoms by which you want to fool me.' - On the festival, address it: 'Nu! You took the body and started giving orders. Crawl into the sukkah and work with your feet.' - Ask yourself 'until when?' with full force, without favouritism to yourself, until the arrow reaches the heart of the animal soul, wrapped in the silk of excuses about place and time.
- **Why it works:** Once it is named, it loses its disguise. The 'until when' must be drawn like a bow, 'with intellect and counsel, with strength and might', because the animal soul's heart is wrapped in excuses.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** After Yom Kippur and on Sukkos the old Chassidim spoke to it so. When it is dressing itself up as a tzaddik. **Not:** The Rayatz distinguishes: the yetzer hara is to be thrown out entirely; the animal soul is to be made a vessel (see RY14, section 3).
- **Quotes:** - «מ'דארף זיך שטעלן מיט א תוקף און זאגן צום יצה"ר: איך קען דיך מיט אלע דיינע חכמה'לעך וואס דו ווילסט מיך אריינארען» — *one must stand with firmness and say to the yetzer hara: I know you, with all your little wisdoms by which you want to fool me.* Sefer HaSichos, Purim 5709. `SH:8965` (typed) - «אז עס קומט סוכות זאגט ער צום נפש הבהמית: נו! וואס, דו האסט גענומען דעם גוף און הויבסט אן צו קאמאנדאווען, קריך אריין אין סוכה און ארבעט מיט די פיס» — *when Sukkos comes he says to the animal soul: Nu! What, you took the body and started giving orders? Crawl into the sukkah and work with your feet.* Sefer HaSichos, Shabbos Chol HaMoed Sukkos 5708 (saying of old Chassidim). `SH:7588` (typed) - «הוא הקשת אשר צריכיס למתוח היתר על היתד בהשכל ומועצות, בכח וגבורה, עד אשר חציו, יגיעו גם ללב הנה"ב המעוטף בבגדי משי הן המה טענות המקום והזמן» — *['until when'] is the bow that one must draw tight with intellect and counsel, with strength and might, until its arrows reach even the heart of the animal soul, wrapped in silk garments: these are the excuses of place and time.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter. `IKo:10552` (OCR)

## RY04. Explain in steps, through the middle soul

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** The G-dly soul explains G-dliness to the animal soul, but not directly and not all at once. It goes through the intellectual soul, which is natural and so close to the animal soul. First comes a general intellectual matter, then the G-dly matter. First the plain concept, then the G-dliness in it. Parables and images help.
- **How to do it:** - First make physicality repulsive to it. This breaks its overall being. - Then bring it close to intellect in general: explain natural, rational matters it can follow. - Then show it that natural intellect sees only the 'is-ness' of a thing, while the truth of the thing is its G-dly life. - In each subject: explain the intellectual idea first, then 'the G-dliness in this matter'. - Use parables and images taken from the world. - Even a little understanding counts: a little G-dly light pushes away much darkness.
- **Why it works:** The animal soul is mostly emotion, but it has a mind. That mind is busy only with its own good. Rational thinking is new to it; physicality is 'plain' to it. So you must raise it rung by rung. The intellectual soul can receive from the G-dly soul and pass it on.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For everyone who prays with contemplation. It is slow work. **Not:** Without the order of steps it 'is not avodah at all; it is only deceit, or fooling oneself' (SMo 18806). Many grasp G-dliness well 'according to their level' and are not changed.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul grasps only the spiritual. Through the natural soul it grasps G-dliness 'tangibly' (SM 559). When the animal soul is moved by G-dly good, that is mesirus nefesh on its side, since all its effort is for physical good (SMo 28498).
- **Quotes:** - «דתחלה צריכים לקרב את הנה"ב לענין שכלי בכלל ואח"כ אפשר להביאו לשכל אלקי» — *first one must bring the animal soul close to an intellectual matter in general, and afterwards one can bring it to G-dly intellect.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5696, p. 107. `SMo:18806` (OCR) - «ראשית צריכים למאס אצלו ענין החומריות דזה עושה שבירה בכללות מהותו» — *first one must make physicality repulsive to it; this makes a break in its whole being.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5696, p. 107. `SMo:18806` (OCR) - «ע"י אמצעות של הנפש השכלית הנה הנה"א מבאר ומסביר להנה*ב השגה אלקית, דהנה הנפש השכלית להיותה טבעית יש לה קירוב להנה"ב» — *by means of the intellectual soul, the G-dly soul explains G-dly understanding to the animal soul; for the intellectual soul, being natural, is close to the animal soul.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5700, p. 214. `SMo:25339` (OCR) - «ועוד זאת דע"י המשלים ודמיונות יבין וישיג הנה"ב ג"כ את אלקות» — *and moreover, through parables and images the animal soul too understands and grasps G-dliness.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5692. `SM:559` (typed) - «הנה מעט אור אלקי דוחה הרבה מחשך הנה"ב» — *a little G-dly light pushes away much of the animal soul's darkness.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5692, p. 197. `SMo:15326` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated. The ordering into one ladder is ours.

## RY05. Start from its own life and good

- **Family:** self-interest and good
- **What it is:** The animal soul understands only 'good', and only physical good. So the G-dly soul explains to it the good of G-dliness, starting from what it already loves: being alive. Everything it craves, it craves while the thing is alive. So life is the main thing. Then: the world too has a life, and that life is G-dly.
- **How to do it:** - Let it notice that it feels its own vitality, and that vitality is what matters. - Show it that even its cravings are for living things; a dead thing it does not want. - Bring it from there to the G-dly life in the world, 'You give life to them all' (Pesukei deZimra). - Then: 'love the L-rd your G-d, for He is your life'.
- **Why it works:** 'The root of intellect is pleasure.' The G-dly soul's mind is in the good of G-dliness because its delight is there; the animal soul's mind is in physical good because its delight is there. You reach a mind through its delight.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Pesukei deZimra is the place for this (SMo 16031). Good for the animal soul because it starts with its own experience. **Not:** This love is 'limited by his grasp and by what is felt in his soul' (SMo 28498). It is not the essence's 'run' beyond vessels.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul 'knows no other good' than G-dliness. The animal soul knows no good of G-dliness until it is explained (SM 229).
- **Quotes:** - «דזהו כל ענין העבודה של הנה"א להתלבש בהנה"ב להבינו ולהסבירו הטוב דאלקות» — *this is the whole work of the G-dly soul: to clothe itself in the animal soul, to make it understand and to explain to it the good of G-dliness.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Shabbos Bereishis 5692. `SM:229` (typed) - «דכל תשוקתו הוא ג"כ כאשר הוא חיוני א"כ העיקר הוא החיות» — *all its craving too is when [the thing] is alive; so the main thing is the vitality.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5682-5683, p. 66. `SMo:1547` (OCR) - «דכמו שמרגיש בעצמו שיש בו חיות והעיקר הוא החיות כן', הנה מזה יבין גם בעולם שיש בו חיות האלקי המחי' אותו» — *just as he feels in himself that he has vitality and the vitality is the main thing, from this he will understand that the world too has G-dly vitality enlivening it.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5686, p. 248. `SMo:6739` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY06. Show it what is mortal

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** Let the natural soul see, in its own terms, that the physical world by itself is death, and that craving it is worse than death. It can understand this, because it feels it in itself.
- **How to do it:** - Contemplate the greatness of G-d in the coming-into-being of worlds and creatures, and how all is secondary to G-dliness. - Bring it down to what the natural soul can feel: the body dies, things decay, craving ends in loss ('See, I have set before you life and good, death and evil'). - Let the natural soul be moved, so its traits are nullified. - Add: guard the mouth (from overeating and bad talk), and keep words of Torah engraved in the mind.
- **Why it works:** Its thoughts, speech and deeds are the spreading-out of its bad traits. So you must nullify the traits, and the way is a contemplation it can grasp 'by feeling it in itself'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Yom Kippur night; daily prayer.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul is alive in itself; the body is dying from the day it is born (SMo 1443). The G-dly soul does not need to be told this.
- **Quotes:** - «דגם בהנה"ט יובן ויושג שגשמיות העולם מצ"ע הוא מות, והתאוה והחמדה הוא רע שעוד קשה ממות» — *the natural soul too understands and grasps that the world's physicality in itself is death, and lust and craving are an evil worse than death.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Motzaei Yom Kippur 5692. `SM:126` (typed) - «שכ"ז יבין גם הנה"ט ע"י שמרגיש זה בנפשו» — *the natural soul too understands all this by feeling it in itself.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Motzaei Yom Kippur 5692. `SM:126` (typed) - «והעצה להיות ד"ת חקוקים במוחו» — *the advice: that words of Torah be engraved in his mind.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Motzaei Yom Kippur 5692 (summary). `SM:127` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY07. Aim at your own animal, and at today

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** General contemplation is not enough. Work on the deficits of your own animal soul, and during the contemplation think about the particular trait you must fix and the coming day.
- **How to do it:** - Know the details of your own animal soul's make-up first. - In prayer, pick the trait that is your weak point. - While contemplating, think about how this applies to that trait and to today's dealings. - Then the contemplation 'touches actual deed in the work of the whole day'.
- **Why it works:** Otherwise the natural soul may be moved during contemplation, and 'when he comes into worldly matters after prayer, the whole matter is forgotten'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Every day, in prayer.
- **Quotes:** - «צ"ל שיחשוב בזה בעת ההתבוננות, ואז הנה יהי' זה נוגע אל הפועל ממש בהעבודה דכל היום» — *he must think about it [the trait] during the contemplation, and then it will touch actual deed in the work of the whole day.* Sefer HaMaamarim, 13 Tishrei 5692. `SM:138` (typed) - «דעבודה פרטית הוא כאשר העבודה הוא בשני ענינים הא' דעבודתו תהי' באותם הענינים שהם חסרוני הנה"ב שלו» — *particular avodah is when the work is in two things; the first, that his work be in those matters that are the deficiencies of his own animal soul.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5699-5700, p. 317. `SMo:23382` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY08. Use its excitability; surround one trait

- **Family:** feeling and the senses
- **What it is:** The animal soul is moved before it understands, and it is moved more strongly than the human mind. Use this. Bring all your emotions to bear, with excitement, against one particular trait, as a general surrounds an enemy on three flanks.
- **How to do it:** - Choose one particular trait. - Take the whole of the emotions (love, fear, compassion) against it. - Do it with feeling and excitement, not coldly. - At Neilah, let the body and natural soul be stirred, as they are by nature.
- **Why it works:** 'In an animal the excitement comes first.' The animal soul's root is in the Ofanim, whose praise is noise and storm; so excitement acts on it. The Alter Rebbe learned this tactic from the Seven Years' War: victory came by surrounding on three flanks. The Tzemach Tzedek called it 'attacking'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** In prayer, after the stage of seeing the good of G-dliness (see RY10, second stanza). **Not:** Excitement that stays at the level of the outer heart is only for 'lighting the darkness of the animal soul' (SM 138).
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Man (and the G-dly soul) first understands and then is moved. The animal is moved first (SM 129).
- **Quotes:** - «ובבהמה הרי תחלה היא ההתפעלות, וכן הוא בהנה"ט שההתפעלות היא ביותר» — *in an animal the excitement comes first; and so in the natural soul, the excitement is greater.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Motzaei Yom Kippur 5692. `SM:129` (typed) - «ובעבודה הענין װאס מען נעמט דעם גאנצען חג"ת כנגד איין מדה פרטית» — *in avodah it means one takes the whole of the emotions against one particular trait.* Sefer HaSichos 5703, p. 124. `SHo:4485` (OCR) - «דער ארומנעמען איז מיט התפעלות, ווייל דער נה"ב איז ענינו התפעלות» — *the surrounding is done with excitement, because the animal soul's whole nature is excitement.* Sefer HaSichos 5703, p. 124. `SHo:4485` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY09. Praise lifts it from outside

- **Family:** speech
- **What it is:** Saying praises aloud (Pesukei deZimra) lifts the animal soul, the way praising someone lifts the one who speaks. It 'prunes the thorns' of the animal soul. But it is an engraving from outside, not an inner change.
- **How to do it:** - Say Pesukei deZimra aloud and with feeling. - Let the praise warm it, lift it, and take it a little out of its physicality. - Then go further: the 'storm' of the Ofanim in the blessings of Shema reaches it inwardly; the 'fire' of Ahavah Rabbah and Shema burns it.
- **Why it works:** Praise lifts the speaker even though nothing inward happened. 'Zimra' is from 'cutting', cutting the thorns of the animal soul.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** At the start of prayer. **Not:** Alone it is not enough: 'not in the wind is G-d'.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The G-dly soul's work is the 'fire' and the still small voice of the Amidah; the praise is for the animal soul.
- **Quotes:** - «וכמו"כ הוא בהנה"ב שע"י התשבחות דפסוד"ז מתרומם ומתעלה במדרי' עליונה יותר» — *so too the animal soul, through the praises of Pesukei deZimra, is lifted and raised to a higher level.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Tetzaveh 5692. `SM:459` (typed) - «ומ"מ לא ברוח ד' לפי שהוא רק חקיקה מבחוץ» — *yet 'not in the wind is G-d', because it is only an engraving from outside.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Tetzaveh 5692. `SM:459` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY10. The niggun

- **Family:** feeling and the senses
- **What it is:** A melody makes a contemplation come alive. Song in prayer has an intrinsic power to 'bring out' the animal soul. The Mitteler Rebbe's Kapelye niggun maps the four stages of refining the animal soul.
- **How to do it:** - Contemplate with a melody, not in silence. - Sing in prayer, as 'those with a ring (shir) go out with it': the song is the ring on the rope that leads the animal out. - Sing the four-stanza niggun calmly and hear its four stages: (1) giving yourself over to the work, even for a short idea; (2) feeling that G-dliness is good, and that this alone does not drive out the animal soul; (3) going deep into the badness of physicality and surrounding the animal soul; (4) fear of the dangers in the work, trust in help from above, with strong Chassidic joy.
- **Why it works:** 'Every melody is movement, and every movement brings warmth.' A contemplation without melody does something, but not visibly.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** In prayer; at farbrengens; before, during and after davening (the same niggun is the longing before, in, and after).
- **Quotes:** - «הנה קול ניגוז הוא שעושה התפעלות הנפש» — *the sound of a niggun is what makes the soul's excitement.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5680-5681, p. 25. `SMo:110` (OCR) - «השיר והזמרה בשעת התפלה יש בהס משוס סגולה עצמית להוציא את הנפש הבהמית» — *song and melody during prayer have an intrinsic power to bring out the animal soul.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 5, p. 191 (Alter Rebbe's teaching on 'with what may an animal go out'). `LD:8398` (OCR) - «װאס זיינען מתאים מיט די פיר דרגות אין דעם סדר העבודה פון דער נפש האלקי' װאס בעדארף מברר זיין דעם נפש הבהמית» — *[the four stanzas] correspond to the four levels in the order of service of the G-dly soul, which must refine the animal soul.* Sefer HaSichos 5703, p. 123. `SHo:4480` (OCR)

## RY11. Two G-dly souls against one

- **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering)
- **What it is:** At a farbrengen and with a friend there are two G-dly souls against one animal soul. The animal soul feels the depth from which honest words come, and they act on it, even when the talk seems to do little.
- **How to do it:** - Farbreng often, even if 'it does not seem to work much'. - Speak from the heart; the animal soul senses where words come from. - Sit with a friend so that two G-dly souls face one natural soul. - Hear Chassidus only from someone who works on himself (a ba'al avodah).
- **Why it works:** Words from the heart act on the hearer. The intellectual souls of both get a taste for what the G-dly soul says and feels, and 'the animal soul too becomes more refined'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Often, especially for young people. **Not:** Not from a 'Chassidic liar' who says Chassidus and does not do it (SH 7026).
- **Quotes:** - «צוויי נפש אלקיתין אויף איין נפש הטבעית» — *two G-dly souls against one natural soul.* Igros Kodesh vol. 10 (from the Rayatz's notes of Elul 5652). `IKo:30653` (OCR) - «איז חוצ דעס ווערט דער נפש הבהמית אויך איידעלער» — *besides this, the animal soul too becomes more refined.* Igros Kodesh vol. 10. `IKo:30653` (OCR) - «דער נפש הבהמית בכלל און דער יצה"ר בפרט דערשפירן דעם עומק פון וואנען די הוראה הנהגה ווערטער גייען און עס פועלט» — *the animal soul in general and the yetzer hara in particular sense the depth from which the words of instruction come, and it works.* Igros Kodesh vol. 17, letter (in the Rebbe's name, by the secretary). `IK:13234` (typed)

## RY12. Mashke that softens the hide

- **Family:** deed and body
- **What it is:** At a farbrengen a little Chassidic mashke 'soaks the hide', as an animal is given drink before slaughter so its skin comes off easily. With the hide softened, plain truths ('turn from evil' includes needless permitted things) finally sit in the mind.
- **How to do it:** - Take a little mashke at a farbrengen, for this inner purpose only. - Use the softened state to take in truths the animal soul usually resists. - Never get drunk.
- **Why it works:** The 'hide' covers the truth. Softened, it lets the truth through. The start of avodah is stripping the hide.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** At a Chassidic farbrengen. **Not:** Being drunk feeds the animal soul. 'One must not give to the animal soul; one must gladden the G-dly soul' (Purim 5707). Chassidus in general calls for great care with wine.
- **Quotes:** - «הנחה זו באה ליד טעימת משקה חסידי כשעור הבהמה של הנפש הטבעית מתרכך» — *this premise comes, beside the tasting of Chassidic mashke, when the 'animal hide' of the natural soul softens.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 5, p. 43. `LD:7416` (OCR) - «מ'דארף ניט געבן דעם נפש הבהמית, מ'דארף משמח זיין דעם נפה"א» — *one must not give to the animal soul; one must gladden the G-dly soul.* Sefer HaSichos, Purim 5707. `SH:6886` (typed)

## RY13. A general blow, then particular sorting

- **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering)
- **What it is:** Work on the animal soul needs 'a strong introduction': a general blow that brings a general nullification. At a farbrengen this happened when a beggar called the town's rich man by his pet name, or plain Chassidim greeted the young prodigy as 'Leizerke'. Then comes the slow sorting of good from bad in the person's wealth or learning.
- **How to do it:** - First, a general humbling of the pride of wealth or learning. - Then explain the good side: the chance to do great things with wealth. - Then show the bad side: wealth blinds him to how coarse he is.
- **Why it works:** Pushing away and nullifying are 'good only for a time, as a general introduction to the work of refining'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Farbrengen among Chassidim; the mashpia with a proud person. **Not:** Pushing away alone is not the refining.
- **Quotes:** - «ביחוד העבודה של הנפש הבהמית וסייעתה זוקוקה להקדמה חזקה, המתבטאת בהכאה הכללית» — *especially the work on the animal soul and its helpers needs a strong introduction, expressed in a general blow.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 5, p. 207. `LD:8525` (OCR) - «לאחר הביטול הכללי צריך לבוא סדר של בירור פרטי» — *after the general nullification must come an order of particular sorting.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 5, p. 207. `LD:8527` (OCR)

## RY14. Stick for the evil, mercy for the animal

- **Family:** fear and bitterness
- **What it is:** Builds on SOURCES P14. The stick (harsh words, thunder) is for the yetzer hara, 'the image of evil'. Mercy is for the animal soul itself, which is 'only the power of desire'. When it is at full strength, do not argue; thunder at it and stop the act. Strike the heart at Ashamnu with something hard: a soft hand only pets it.
- **How to do it:** - Know which you face: the shape of evil (stick) or plain desire (mercy, and making it a vessel). - When it is strong, do not enter explanations; refuse the act itself, whether food, drink or speech. - At Ashamnu, strike the heart 'not with the hand but with a dry stick'.
- **Why it works:** 'A servant is not corrected by words', especially when it is in full strength and 'not a vessel to hear anything'. A soft blow is taken as a caress.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For the yetzer, and when the animal soul is in full strength. Rebuke is only 'engraving from outside' (SMo 29699). **Not:** Not as the main method for the animal soul itself, which is to be made a vessel (SH 8966).
- **Quotes:** - «מקל הוא הענין של מבטשין ליה ליצר הרע שהוא ציור הרע, וענין הרחמיס הוא על הנפש הבהמית שהיא רק כוח המתאוה» — *the stick means beating the yetzer hara, which is the image of evil; and the mercy is for the animal soul, which is only the power of desire.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 3, p. 110. `LD:4901` (OCR) - «ואינו נכנס עמו בדברים והסברים, כי בדברים לא יוסף עבד» — *he does not enter into words and explanations with it, for 'a servant is not corrected by words'.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5682-5683, p. 259. `SMo:2163` (OCR) - «צריכיס להכות על הלב לא עס היד אלא עס עצ יבש. כשמכים עם היד חושב היצר הרע שמלטפיס אותו» — *one must strike the heart [at Ashamnu] not with the hand but with a dry stick. ## RY15. Make it smell itself

- **Family:** fear and bitterness
- **What it is:** The mashpia's work is less to give the student a taste for the idea and more to make him feel 'the untastiness of himself', the stink of self-love. Avodah is tanning: a raw hide is coarse and stinks, and so is physical good.
- **How to do it:** - Look honestly at your self-love, pride and lies until they disgust you. - Let the mashpia point them out. - Then work the hide: refine the body and animal soul by practice in fixing traits.
- **Why it works:** Without it, Chassidic learning becomes 'impure from their ego and pride'. The stench 'twists the head and darkens the eyes'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For students and young men who are learning.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** Contrast: giving a 'taste' in the idea feeds the mind; the animal soul changes only when it smells itself.
- **Quotes:** - «אבער אין דעם אמת'ן האבן זיי בעדארפט טאן אין דעם אז די תלמידים זאלן דערהערן דעם ניט געשמאק פון זיך» — *but in truth they should have worked on this: that the students feel the untastiness of themselves.* Sefer HaSichos 5707, Pesach talks. `SH:7031` (typed) - «א פעל איז בטבעה גראב און שמעקט שטארק» — *a hide is by nature coarse and smells strongly.* Sefer HaSichos, Shabbos Nitzavim 5707. `SH:7420` (typed)

## RY16. Accept the yoke: fence and deed

- **Family:** above reason (yoke, faith, mesirus nefesh, yechidah)
- **What it is:** When understanding does not reach, start with kabbalas ol, which is the same for every soul. It has two moves: a fence (conquer your wants in all physical things, including eating and drinking) and a deed (fixed times to learn, the positive mitzvos, care with the prohibitions). Both by compulsion.
- **How to do it:** - Decide firmly to be exactly as you have resolved, 'without reckoning with anything in the world'. - Fence: say no to the coarseness in thought, speech, deed and eating. - Deed: set study times; do the mitzvos. - When bad thoughts fall in, do not despair; push them away and strengthen the study times. Teaching others also works salvation in your soul.
- **Why it works:** It comes from the yechidah, the point that 'carries Your yoke' with mesirus nefesh. 'The depth-height of the yechidah is revealed in the depth-below of actual deed.'
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For one who cannot reach the work of mind and heart, or who is downcast by bad thoughts. It is 'an entrance and introduction'.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** The main way is mind and heart (study and contemplation, prayer). Kabbalas ol is open to all, from the greatest mind to the simplest.
- **Quotes:** - «הקבלת עול היא דבר השוה לכל נפש, ובכל ענין» — *kabbalas ol is equal for every soul, and in every matter.* Sefer HaSichos, 5 Iyar 5689. `SH:4086` (typed) - «הקבלת עול הוא המסירה ונתינה בכח ועוז ובתוקף גדול, מבלי התחשב עם כל דבר בעולם» — *kabbalas ol is the handing-over and giving with strength and great force, without reckoning with anything in the world.* Sefer HaSichos, 5 Iyar 5689. `SH:4088` (typed) - «ענינו לכבוש רצונותיו ותאוותיו בכל אשר יחפוץ ויתאווה להם בכל הענינים החומרים» — *[the fence] means to conquer his wants and desires in all physical things he may want and desire.* Sefer HaSichos, 5 Iyar 5689. `SH:4093` (typed)

## RY17. Stand firm: mesirus nefesh weakens it

- **Family:** above reason (yoke, faith, mesirus nefesh, yechidah)
- **What it is:** There are many ways of contemplation to make the mind rule the heart. But the war is won by actual service with mesirus nefesh: not moving a hair from the Torah's law, paying no attention to any hindrance. Standing firm itself weakens the animal soul, as a soldier who throws his life forward frightens the enemy.
- **How to do it:** - Learn in order to do. Let each law 'touch' you. - When the animal soul and yetzer hinder with tricks, do not attend to them; just do the mitzvah. - Be the soldier: 'yiras Shamayim shines in their heart'.

## RY18. Show it a good thing, then seize it

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** A tactic: show the animal soul something good, lead it up to it, and once there, take hold of it entirely with force. The context is two givers of charity: one gives at once; the other first struggles not to give and then gives 'out of avodah'.
- **How to do it:** - Offer the animal soul a good it can want. - Walk it there. - At the point of action, take full control with gevurah. - A related tactic with someone far away (R. - **When / for whom, and when not:** When the animal soul will not move by plain argument.

## RY19. Show it 'enough' (story)

## RY21. Close the gates

- **Family:** deed and body
- **What it is:** Some of the animal soul's wants cannot be repaired; they are broken first. Shut the mouth from what the natural soul wants to say, the ear from what it wants to hear, the eye from what it wants to see. Repair begins in the middle soul, the intellectual soul.
- **How to do it:** - Break: close mouth, ears and eyes to what the natural soul wants. - Meanwhile prepare the 'tools' (the intellectual soul's work). - Later, replace the broken parts with new ones.
- **Why it works:** Like a craftsman who first breaks the spoiled parts of a vessel and only later fits new ones.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** When a trait is bad at root (see darkei C 1.15: pride, anger, lying). **Not:** Breaking is 'not avodah at all'; avodah is the repair. It is only a holding stage.
- **Quotes:** - «ישבר הוא את הרצונות הטבעיס להיות בולס פיו מלדבר מה שחפצ נפש הטבעית ולהיות אוטם אזנו משמוע ועוצס עינו מלראות במה שחפצה נפש הטבעית» — *he should break the natural wants: to shut his mouth from speaking what the natural soul wants, to stop his ear from hearing and close his eye from seeing what the natural soul wants.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter. `IKo:12921` (OCR) - «התחלת עבודת התקון היא בנפש האמצעיי שהיא הנפש השכלית» — *the beginning of the work of repair is in the middle soul, the intellectual soul.* Igros Kodesh vol. 3, letter. `IKo:12922` (OCR)

## RY22. The fast of fifteen minutes (story)

- **Family:** habit and repetition
- **What it is:** STORY. A Chassid complained to the Rebbe Maharash that he was a schemer who took everything with cunning. He was told to fast 600 fasts. ## RY23. The nightly court case

- **Family:** speech
- **What it is:** The bedtime Shema is a court case between the G-dly soul and the animal soul. The animal soul pleads its good deeds (I did favours, I did not take revenge). Old Chassidim spent hours on it. In truth one comes then to recognise that it all came from the animal soul.

## RY24. Tehillim and tears, with a limit

## RY25. Medicine in drops

- **Family:** habit and repetition
- **What it is:** Medicine is taken in drops, not by the glass. One who drinks it by the glass shows how sick he is; in spiritual things, the animal soul has a hand in it. Refining goes in order and gradation, slowly.
- **How to do it:** - Take on small, fixed amounts. - Keep timing and measure. - Be suspicious of sudden huge resolutions.
- **Why it works:** The refining must be 'in order and gradation, slowly slowly'; this is why the yetzer comes first in life and the good inclination later.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always. **Not:** Big jumps; 'excitement from excitement'.
- **Quotes:** - «א רפואה נעמט מען טראפענסווייז, ניט גלעזערווייז» — *medicine is taken by drops, not by glasses.* Sefer HaSichos 5709, sichah. `SH:8814` (typed) - «אז מען מוז וויסן אז דער נה"ב האט א יד אין דעם» — *one must know that the animal soul has a hand in it.* Sefer HaSichos 5709, sichah. `SH:8814` (typed) - «והבירור צ"ל בסדר והדרגה דוקא לאט לאט» — *and the refining must be in order and gradation, slowly slowly.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Pekudei 5692. `SM:504` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY26. Harness its strength

- **Family:** strategy
- **What it is:** The animal soul has power: force, stubbornness, the strength of the ox. Do not only push it away; take its good and use it in Torah, mitzvos and prayer.
- **How to do it:** - Drive out its bad. - Acquire its good: its force and stubbornness. - Put them to work in Torah, mitzvos and prayer.
- **Why it works:** 'Much produce comes by the strength of the ox.' Its pull is stronger than other pulls (SHo 5096).
- **When / for whom, and when not:** The level of 'mine is yours and yours is mine' (G-dly soul clothed in the animal soul).
- **Quotes:** - «ומשתמשת במעלת הכח והתוקף שבנה"ב כמ"ש ורב תבואות בכח שור» — *and [the G-dly soul] uses the advantage of the strength and force in the animal soul, as written 'much produce comes by the strength of the ox'.* Sefer HaSichos, additions, reshimah (on the four types). `SH:10172` (typed) - «קונה זיין דעם טוב פון כחות נה"ב, כמו דעם תוקף און עקשות וכדומה, און אויסנוצען דאס אין תורה ומצות ועבודה שבלב» — *acquire the good of the animal soul's powers, like its force and stubbornness and the like, and use it in Torah and mitzvos and the service of the heart.* Sefer HaSichos 5703, p. 124. `SHo:4484` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY27. Torah as spice

- **Family:** self-interest and good
- **What it is:** The animal soul wants physicality, and physicality is bitter. Torah is a spice that turns the bitter sweet, so that even physical things are felt for their true value. With much engagement in G-dly matters, the animal soul's pleasure itself can move to G-dliness.
- **How to do it:** - Do not starve the animal soul of all taste; season its world with Torah. - Engage much and deeply in G-dly matters, so its pleasure turns.
- **Why it works:** 'I created the yetzer hara and I created Torah as its spice.'
- **When / for whom, and when not:** In daily life.
- **Quotes:** - «דער נפש הבהמית וויל דאך גשמיות, וואס גשמיות איז דאך ביטער — מרירות, איז דורך דעם תבלין פון תורה הערט זיך די מעלה פון גשמיות» — *the animal soul wants physicality; physicality is bitter; through the spice of Torah one feels the virtue of physicality.* Sefer HaSichos, Pesach 5708 (Baal Shem Tov's message to the Alter Rebbe). `SH:7995` (typed) - «יכולים לפעול שגם הנה"ב יהי' כל תענוגו באלקות» — *one can bring about that the animal soul too will have all its pleasure in G-dliness.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5687, p. 149. `SMo:7696` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY28. Help another against his animal soul

- **Family:** others (friend, teacher, gathering)
- **What it is:** Helping a friend carry out his matter against his animal soul is charity with his G-dly soul, and it lifts and strengthens the G-dly soul of the helper.
- **How to do it:** - Notice where a friend is stuck. - Help him do the thing his animal soul is blocking.
- **Why it works:** 'Charity lifts up.'
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Among friends.
- **Quotes:** - «וואס מען קומט איס צוהילף דורכצופירן זיין ענין בא זיין נפש הבהמית דאס איז מרומס ונותן כח להנפש אלקית של העוזר» — *when one comes to help him carry out his matter with his animal soul, this elevates and gives strength to the G-dly soul of the helper.* Igros Kodesh vol. 5, letter. `IKo:15796` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY29. Simple faith outruns the natural mind (story)

- **Family:** above reason (yoke, faith, mesirus nefesh, yechidah)
- **What it is:** STORY. R. Pinye of Shklov, a great scholar, eight years with the Alter Rebbe, watched the plain sons of R. Moshe (a village tailor and merchant) and felt ashamed: his natural mind still overpowered his G-dly mind, while in them, who came only out of fear of heaven and acceptance of the yoke, form ruled matter by simple faith.
- **How to do it:** - Let shame before simpler people move you. - Rely on kabbalas ol and simple faith where understanding has not reached.
- **Why it works:** In simple faith 'the G-dly intellect shines'.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For the learned.
- **Quotes:** - «ואף על פי כן החומר שלי מתגבר על הצורה, התגברותו של השכל הטבעי על השכל האלקי» — *and nevertheless my matter overpowers the form: the natural intellect overpowers the G-dly intellect.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 2, p. 52. `LD:2743` (OCR) - «ואצלס ישנה ההתגברות של הצורה על החומר, בהסם דוקא מאיר השכל האלקי ברגש של אמונה פשוטה» — *and with them there is the triumph of form over matter; in them specifically the G-dly intellect shines with a feeling of simple faith.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 2, p. 52. `LD:2743` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated (story)

## RY30. The mitzvah's own power

- **Family:** deed and body
- **What it is:** Every mitzvah has its own special power to subdue the animal soul and detach it from worldly things. Faith means believing in what you do: the deed works even when you do not understand it. Charity done with devotion redeems a person from the prison of the animal soul.
- **How to do it:** - Do the mitzvah, even without understanding. - Give charity with real devotion: then 'the whole body is a chariot'.
- **Why it works:** Each mitzvah joins a person to the Essence and has a particular refining power.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Always.
- **Quotes:** - «כל מצוה ומצוה יש לה סגולה פרטית לאכפייא להנה"ב ולנתקו מעניני העולם ולבררו» — *every single mitzvah has a particular property to subdue the animal soul, detach it from worldly matters and refine it.* Sefer HaMaamarim, Shemini Atzeres 5692. `SM:192` (typed) - «אמונה משמעה להאמין במה שעושים, ועל ידי הפעולה, למרות שלא יודעיס ולא מבינים בהשגה -- נעשה הדבר בפועל» — *faith means to believe in what one does; and through the action, though one does not know or understand it, the thing is accomplished in deed.* Likkutei Dibburim vol. 3, p. 109. `LD:4899` (OCR) - «ולפדות עצמו ממאסר הנה"ב ולרחוץ עצמו» — *and to redeem himself from the prison of the animal soul and wash himself.* Igros Kodesh vol. 17, letter. `IK:12010` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY31. Teshuvah the animal soul feels

- **Family:** fear and bitterness
- **What it is:** True teshuvah is when the animal soul too is distressed about its own matters, not only the G-dly soul about the animal soul. Like water that breaks a dam and carries the stones away with it.
- **How to do it:** - Let the regret reach the body's own wants, not stay in the G-dly soul's sorrow.
- **Why it works:** Then it breaks the coarseness of the animal soul.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** Teshuvah, Elul, Yom Kippur.
- **Contrast with the G-dly soul:** G-dly soul's distress alone is not complete teshuvah.
- **Quotes:** - «כ"א שהנהיב יהי' צר לו מאד מעניניו כו', והיא אמיתית התשובה כשהוא בשניהם בנה"א ובנה"ב» — *but the animal soul should feel great distress over its own matters; and that is true teshuvah, when it is in both, in the G-dly soul and the animal soul.* Sefer HaMaamarim 5682-5683, p. 138. `SMo:1714` (OCR)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY32. March with joy

- **Family:** feeling and the senses
- **What it is:** Do not be thrown by the animal soul's lies. A soldier goes into danger with a knapsack and a song of joy, and that makes him a victor.
- **How to do it:** - Know it lies: its laughing and weeping are both lies. - Do not be impressed; go forward singing.
- **Why it works:** Joy makes the victor; sadness is one of its tools.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** When the animal soul raises doubts like 'who am I?'.
- **Quotes:** - «א סאלדאט גייט מיט א ראניעץ אין פאחאד, חאטש ער גייט דאך אין א מקום סכנה, גייט ער מיט א ניגון פון שמחה» — *a soldier goes with a knapsack on the march; though he goes to a dangerous place, he goes with a song of joy.* Sefer HaSichos, Pesach 5709. `SH:9172` (typed) - «ער גייט מיט שמחה און דאס מאכט אים פאר א מנצח» — *he goes with joy, and that makes him a victor.* Sefer HaSichos, Pesach 5709. `SH:9173` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated

## RY33. Train the body until you can trust it (story)

- **Family:** habit and repetition
- **What it is:** STORY. The Rebbe Rashab told the Gerer Rebbe (5685) that from childhood he trained his body not to want the forbidden and to want each limb's mitzvah. By much habit it became nature, until he trusted his body. (Also in darkei C 1.19.)
- **How to do it:** - Train early and long. - Aim at the body's wanting itself, not only its doing.
- **Why it works:** Habit becomes nature.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** From childhood; for a lifetime.
- **Quotes:** - «אני הרגלתי את גופי לא לרצות את האסור» — *I trained my body not to want what is forbidden.* Sefer HaSichos 5685, summer. `SH:708` (typed) - «וברוב רגילותי הוטבע ונעשה טבע, עד אשר הנני מאמין להגוף» — *and by much habit it was impressed and became nature, until I trust the body.* Sefer HaSichos 5685, summer. `SH:709` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated (story)

## RY34. Uproot the source, do not just break it

- **Family:** understanding
- **What it is:** From the Agudas library (R. Avraham Chaim Rosenbaum, a Chassid). Chassidim said: 'break a desire and it becomes two'. Breaking it while the whole will is still in it leaves the desire, and adds a second desire: pride in the breaking, and wanting the world to come too. The Chassidic way is to uproot the source of the desire by contemplation: the source of all pleasures is with G-d; the pleasures of this world are nothing. Long contemplation in prayer leaves a mark on the whole day.
- **How to do it:** - Contemplate 'with You is the source of life': all pleasures descend from Him through countless contractions. - Do this at length, as in prayer. - Let the love and fear born from it rule the day.
- **Why it works:** A little light pushes away much darkness; like searching for chametz by candlelight.
- **When / for whom, and when not:** For one who has begun the Chassidic path. **Not:** Not a mockery of plain itkafia: for most of us, someone who holds back from sin is to be respected.
- **Quotes:** - «אז מי צו ברעכט א תאוה ווערט צוויי» — *if one breaks a desire, it becomes two.* Toldos Avraham Chaim, ch. 9, 'Breaking desires by the way of Chassidus'. `TAC:859` (typed) - «לעקור ולשרש את פורה ראש ומקור תאוות הגוף ונה"ב ע"י התבוננות» — *to uproot and root out the poisonous source of the desires of the body and the animal soul by contemplation.* Toldos Avraham Chaim, ch. 9. `TAC:864` (typed)
- **Stated / ours:** stated (a Chassid, not the Rayatz)

## 3. What the sources say does NOT work, or warn against

1. **Understanding alone does not move it out of habit.** The animal soul may agree with every explanation, through the intellectual soul, and stay where it is. «אמנם זהו מצד השכל בלבד אבל עם זה אינו פועל הזזה לצאת מרגילותו» — *but this is from the intellect alone, and it does not move him to leave his habit* (SMo:30069, 5704, OCR). «וכן הוא בהנה"ב הגם שיודע מה שהנה"א מסביר לו אבל אינו פועל עליו לצאת ממיצר הגוף ותאוותיו» — *so too the animal soul: though it knows what the G-dly soul explains to it, this does not make it leave the narrowness of the body and its desires* (SMo:30069, OCR). See also RY01: "intellect alone is nothing" (SH:7020).
2. **Prayer's excitement that does not last.** «אמנם כ"ז הוא בעת התפלה דוקא, ואין זה מתקיים אחר התפלה» — *all this is during prayer only, and it does not last after prayer* (SMo:4947, 5685, OCR). The remedy is RY07.
3. **Contemplation that leaves the animal soul out.** People who pray with good contemplation "from the soul alone" are careless in practice: «וטעם וסבת הדבר הוא מפני שהתפלה אינה בהתעסקות עם הנה"ב כ"א מצד הנשמה לבד» — *the reason is that their prayer does not engage the animal soul, only the soul* (SM:551, 5692). The fix: «ויבאר דהעבודה הוא ההתעסקות בבירור הנה"ב ע"י התבוננות בענינים המובנים גם להנה"ב שאז מתחבר הכוונה והפועל» — *the work is refining the animal soul through contemplating matters the animal soul too can understand; then intention and deed join* (SM:553, summary).
4. **Rushing to closeness.** Climbing to closeness without leaving evil by one's own effort: «ותהי' עבודתו בהקירוב רק מצד הנה"א והטבעי' דנה"ב ישאר בתקפו» — *his service in closeness will be only from the G-dly soul, and the natural traits of the animal soul will remain in their strength* (SM:724, Shavuos 5692).
5. **Breaking a desire while still wanting it.** "Break a desire and it becomes two" (TAC:859); breaking can itself be "a trick of the yetzer" (TAC:863). See RY34.
6. **Soft rebuke.** Hitting the heart with the hand at Ashamnu: the yetzer thinks it is being stroked (LD:5298). Arguing with it when it is at full strength (SMo:2163). See RY14.
7. **Too much at once.** Medicine by the glass is a sign of the animal soul's hand (SH:8814). See RY25.
8. **Mashke to excess** feeds the animal soul (SH:6886). See RY12.
9. **A mashpia who only gives "taste"** in ideas, without making the student feel his own untastiness (SH:7031). See RY15.
10. **Self-satisfaction and sadness** are both its traits (IKo:29382); «כך היא דרכה של הנה"ב להעציב את האדס» — *such is the way of the animal soul: to sadden a person* (IKo:26684, OCR). It raises one person and lowers another (SH:8822). The Rayatz also notes that "it comes and talks him into it" through holy-sounding reasons: «קומט דער נה"ב און רעדט עם איין» — *the animal soul comes and talks him into it* (SH:554, Simchas Torah 5685).
11. **Natural-soul longing that confuses.** The natural soul's longing, when it cannot attain its object, «וכשאינו יכול להשיגו מתבלבל או מתיאש» — *when he cannot attain it he becomes confused or despairs* (SM:1007, Noach 5693, summary), unlike the G-dly soul's orderly longing.
12. **Anger and "you're not a Jew"** to someone who has strayed (TAC:827). See RY18.
13. **A tension the sources leave open.** At Purim 5709 the Rayatz says «מיט דעם נפש הבהמית קען מען גאר ניט מאכן, אים קען מען ניט מתקן זיין» — *with the animal soul nothing at all can be done; it cannot be repaired* (SH:8964), then two lines later «מיט דעם נפש הבהמית קען מען עפעס מאכן ער זאל זיין א כלי צום נפש האלקית» — *with the animal soul something can be done: that it be a vessel for the G-dly soul* (SH:8966), while the yetzer hara must be thrown out entirely. A letter says the natural soul's bad wants cannot be cleaned, only replaced: «אשר על כן הנה בנפש זו אינו שייך ענין התקון או הנקיון כי אס צריכיס להחליפה באברים חדשיס לגמרי» — *in this soul repair or cleaning does not apply; one must replace it with entirely new limbs* (IKo:12919, OCR). *Ours:* read together, the animal soul is not "fixed" as it stands; it is broken where it is evil, replaced, and its raw power is made a vessel (RY21, RY26).
14. **It does not go away.** R. Hendel at Modeh Ani: «עס איז שוין 67 יאהר ווי ער — הנה"ב — מאטערט מיר» — *it's already 67 years that he, the animal soul, torments me* (SH:7554, Sukkos 5708, STORY). Every morning it is back. *Ours:* no method in this slice is a one-time fix.
