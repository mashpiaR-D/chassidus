# The animal soul in contemplation: what the sources say

*Research file for builders. Question from the project owner: "We made the G-dly soul a bit; now we need to translate it to the animal consciousness." How does Chassidus explain the unity to the animal soul, the natural soul and the natural mind? How does that differ from contemplation for the G-dly soul?*

**How to read this file.** - Every Hebrew quote below was checked with `find_he.py verify FILE "…" --line N` and came back FOUND at that line. - Line numbers refer to the `.txt` file named in the source key (§0). Where the text is an OCR scan, the quote keeps the OCR spelling, and the work is marked **(OCR)**. OCR errors are glossed in brackets: e.g. נם = גם. - **Stated** means the source says it. **Ours** means it is our reading, our bridge, or our application to the project's ladder.

Terms used throughout: - **Animal soul** (נפש הבהמית, נה"ב): the soul that gives the body life and wants what the body wants. Chassidus also calls it the **natural soul** (נפש הטבעית, נה"ט) and the **living soul** (החיונית). - **Natural mind** (שכל הטבעי, השכל הטבעי דנה"ב): the animal soul's own intelligence. It is real intelligence, but it works only on physical things and on what serves the self. - **Human intellect** (שכל אנושי): ordinary human reasoning. Some sources put it in the animal soul. The Rebbe makes it a middle soul between the two (§2, P4). - **G-dly soul** (נפש האלקית, נה"א): the soul that is "a part of G-d above". It wants only G-d. - **Memale** (ממלא כל עלמין): G-d's light that fills each thing from inside, as its life. **Sovev** (סובב כל עלמין): the light that surrounds all things and does not enter them in a measured way. - **Subduing** (אתכפיא, itkafia): the animal soul is overpowered and held down. **Turning** (אתהפכא, ishapcha): the animal soul itself changes and wants G-d.

## 1. The answer in one page

**The animal soul is not deaf. It thinks.** Every desire has a reason behind it. The animal soul does not want what it does not know to be good. So it has a mind, and a mind can be shown something (Rayatz, RS 1360). Its mind is narrow. It understands only physical things, its own needs, and the "is-ness" of things (AB 5699). It grasps creation from nothing only a very little, and only in a coarse way. What is above creation it does not grasp at all (RS 555-556).

**So there are two kinds of contemplation.** The Rashab names them plainly in Kuntres HaTefillah (KT 77, OCR): - When prayer contemplates things the natural mind *can* understand, the G-dly soul works on the animal soul until it too "understands and feels the Godly matter", and is moved to love and awe. This **turns** it. - When prayer contemplates things *above* the natural mind, the G-dly soul's love only **overpowers** the animal soul's traits. This **subdues** it.

**What the natural mind can hold is memale: G-d as the life inside things.** "Any thinking person can understand it, even with the natural mind of the animal soul" (KT 44). The way in is life. Every person feels that something gives life to his body. The world is the same: a big body with a life in it (KA 129). Contemplating "He is your life", the life of the world and of your own soul, is what makes the animal soul want G-d (AB 5090). Sovev, and what is above both, is grasped by the G-dly soul's mind (KT 44).

**What moves it is what touches itself.** The Mitteler Rebbe builds the natural soul's ladder out of business. A cold thought about a fortune that belongs to someone else does not move you. A thought about a deal that is *yours* grips the whole mind (KH 246-252). Contemplation reaches the natural soul when it is held "like a matter that touches oneself" (KH 252). The Rashab: the natural mind is made to see that G-d is "the ultimate good" and the physical is "not good at all" (KA 142). That is the animal soul's own language: good for me, bad for me. The Alter Rebbe gives it as a question of wanting: if there is truly nothing else, "then whom would it want besides Him?" (LT Matot, LT 2660).

**It needs time, proofs, and a patient teacher.** The G-dly soul has to "busy itself with the natural mind" until the point "settles well" there (AB 5701). Sometimes "more proofs and demonstrations" are needed (KA 152). It starts as mere *agreement* (הסכם): the animal soul stops getting in the way. Only with steady work does it come to want (KA 142). The Rebbe adds a lower floor: the animal soul may understand only "that it must be so", even though the thing itself is beyond it (TM 107862, citing the Rashab's Samach Vav).

**Why it must reach the animal soul.** The G-dly soul needs no repair. It came down only to refine the animal soul (KT 77; RS 1359). If the G-dly soul's excitement does not reach the animal soul, nothing in the person's life changes (KA 255; AB 5702). "And you shall love … with all your heart" means with both inclinations (בשני יצריך). That verse is quoted in almost every source below.

**But the animal soul never wants G-d by its own nature.** By itself it wants only to live (KA 180). Even its love of G-d is the G-dly soul's mind clothed in its mind. Its opposition stays hidden underneath (MM 683). And the natural mind's grasp, alone, only takes the *taste* (געשמאק) out of physical things. Real love in it needs the G-dly soul's fire (AB 5703; KA 152).

**When explaining fails, you rebuke it.** If "with words a servant is not corrected", one must "discipline it with a stick", meaning harsh words (RS 1360; Tanya ch. 29). When the animal soul is very strong, you cannot even begin with understanding. You start with accepting the yoke (KA 94).

**And watch for its piety.** The animal soul is "a clever fox". Sometimes it dresses as a humble tzaddik. Sometimes it gives you a great urge to learn Chassidus, just to keep you from praying (HY 23 Sivan). "There can be a pious animal soul, never mind a chassidic one" (HY 335).

**In one line (ours):** for the animal soul, the unity is taught as *life* and *good*. It is taught in its own terms, slowly, with proofs, until it agrees, then until it wants. Above that it is not taught but overpowered, by the G-dly soul's fire and, at the root, by the yechidah.

## 2. The principles, one by one

### P1. The animal soul has a mind, so it can be taught **Claim.** The animal soul is not only appetite. Every trait rests on a reason, because no one wants what he does not know to be good. Since there is a mind in the animal soul, it can understand a Godly matter too. **Stated** (Rayatz).

- «וכל מדה הרי בהכרח שיש לה שכל המחייב את המדה» — *every trait must have a mind that requires it.* RS 1360 (Sefer HaMaamarim 5689, "Tzidkas Pirzono"). - «ומאחר שיש שכל גם בנה"ב הרי יכול להבין גם כן ענין אלקי» — *and since there is a mind in the animal soul too, it can understand a Godly matter as well.* RS 1360. - «ושתהי' ההתבוננות באופן כזה שיהי' מושג בשכל הטבעי דנה"ב וירגיש הנה"ב ג"כ הטוב דאלקות» — *the contemplation should be such that it is grasped in the animal soul's natural mind, and the animal soul too feels the good of Godliness.* RS 1360.

The Rebbe says the same in a three-level map. The animal soul has its own "neshamah" (understanding), "ruach" (feelings) and "nefesh" (deeds). Its understanding works only on physical things (TM 21802, Purim 5715): - «כי אם להבנה והשגה בענינים גשמיים וחומריים» — *only to understanding of physical, material matters.* TM 21802.

### P2. What the natural mind cannot grasp **Claim.** The natural mind knows things *after* they exist, as things. It sees creation from nothing only a very little, and coarsely. What is above creation it does not grasp at all. **Stated** (Rayatz, Rashab).

- «הרי ההשכלה היא בגשמי' הדבר ובהטבע שהוטבע בנבראים לאחר שנתהוו» — *its understanding is of the physical side of a thing, of the nature set in creatures after they came to be.* RS 555 (Sefer HaMaamarim 5692). - «ה"ה מעט מזעיר מאד מה שהנה"ט עצמה תשכיל בזה, ואף גם זאת בא אצלה בהגשמה מאד» — *what the natural soul itself understands of this [how the nothing makes the something] is very, very little, and even that comes to it very coarsely.* RS 556. - «ומכש"כ בעניני אלקות שלמעלה מהתהוות שלא תשכיל ולא תשיג כלל» — *how much more Godly matters above creation, which it does not understand or grasp at all.* RS 556. - «והשכל דנה"ב הוא רק בצרכי עצמו החומריים וההשכלה רק בישות הדבר» — *the animal soul's mind is only for its own material needs, and its understanding is only of the thing's "is-ness".* AB 5699.

The Rayatz also says what the animal soul's knowing *is*: an acquired knowing, like an animal that learns where its food is. - «וזה שהבהמה אין לה דעת הרי אין הכוונה שאין לה דעת כלל, כ"א שהדעת שבה הוא דעת הנקנה ע"י אכו"ש והדומה» — *"an animal has no da'at" does not mean none at all, but that its da'at is acquired through eating, drinking and the like.* RS 555.

**Ours.** This is the sharpest boundary for builders. The animal-soul track can go as far as "the life in things". It cannot reach "before Him all is nothing" as an experience of its own. At most it reaches "it must be so" (P8).

### P3. Memale is the natural mind's door; sovev is the G-dly soul's **Claim.** The light that fills (memale) can be understood "even with the natural mind of the animal soul". Sovev and what is above it can be grasped in the G-dly soul's mind. **Stated** (Rashab).

- «אשר כל משכיל יכול להבין הדבר לאשורו נם בשכל הטבעי דנה"ב» [נם = גם] — *which any thinking person can understand properly, even in the natural mind of the animal soul.* KT 44 (OCR). - «שגם ענינים אלו יכולים לבוא לידי השגה והנחה טובה» — *[of sovev and above:] that these too can come to grasp and to "a good settling" (a gutter leyg) in the G-dly soul's ChaBaD.* KT 44 (OCR). The words "in the G-dly soul's ChaBaD" (בחב"ד דנה"א) come right after the quote in the same line.

How memale is taught to the natural mind, in the Rashab's own steps: - «ומבשרי אחזה אלקה שכל אדם מרגיש בעצמו. שיש בו חיות המחי' אותו» — *"from my flesh I see G-d": every person feels in himself that there is a life giving him life.* KA 129 (OCR). - «נרגש ונראה אצלו איך שהעולם חי מאור האלקי» — *it is felt and seen by him how the world lives from the Godly light.* KA 129 (OCR). - «לאהבה את ה' אלקיך כי הוא חייך איך שהוא חיי העולם בכלל וחיי נפשו בפרט» — *to love G-d "for He is your life": how He is the life of the world in general and of one's own soul in particular.* AB 5090. The same line goes on: when this is grasped in the natural mind too, "it makes bitul in the animal soul, so that it wants Godliness".

**Ours.** The path is: your own body is alive → something gives it life → the world is a body and is alive the same way → that life is G-d → He is *your* life. Each step is something the natural mind already knows from inside. The Tanya's ch. 42 gives a sense-based twin: we fear a king because of his inner life, which we do not see; we see only his body and clothes. So too we see heaven and earth with eyes of flesh, and know His light is clothed in them to give them life: - «ככה ממש יש לו לירא את ה׳, על ידי ראיית עיני בשר בשמים וארץ וכל צבאם» — *so exactly should he fear G-d, by seeing with eyes of flesh the heavens and earth and all their hosts.* TY 641 (Tanya ch. 42). The Tanya does not say this is "for the animal soul". **Ours** to file it here.

### P4. Who does the understanding: one soul's mind inside the other **Claim.** The G-dly soul's ten powers are clothed in the animal soul's ten powers: mind in mind, feeling in feeling. So the G-dly soul thinks *through* the natural mind. That is both why its grasp below is coarser, and why it can reach the animal soul at all. **Stated** (Mitteler Rebbe, Rashab).

- «ומכל מקום מלובשים יו״ד כחות דנפש האלהית ביו״ד כחות דנפש הטבעית» — *nevertheless the ten powers of the G-dly soul are clothed in the ten powers of the natural soul.* KH 412. - «מ"מ אופן החפץ והרצון דנה"א הוא כפי אופן ההטבעה דנה"ב» — *the G-dly soul's way of desiring follows the shape of the animal soul's nature.* AB 5701. The Rashab's example: a person whose physical nature leans to kindness will serve G-d through kindness.

There are three versions of where "human intellect" sits: - **Mitteler Rebbe:** the natural soul *is* the thinking soul. «הכל הוא בנפש השכלית שנקרא נפש הטבעית החיונית» — *it is all in the intellectual soul, which is called the natural, living soul.* KH 312. And: «והכל הוא בנה"ט שמתבוננת בשכל אנושי ומכיר בוראו ומתפעל בלב בשר דוקא» — *it is all in the natural soul, which contemplates with human intellect, recognizes its Creator, and is moved in the heart of flesh.* IB 270. - **Tzemach Tzedek:** human intellect belongs to the animal soul, and that is *why* the animal soul can be turned. «וגם נפש הבהמית תתהפך עי"ז לדעה זו שהרי הוא משיג ומתבונן בשכל האנושי שמנפשו הבהמית» — *the animal soul too will be turned to this view by it, since he grasps and contemplates with the human intellect, which is from his animal soul.* DM 54 (Mitzvah of eating matzah). - **The Rebbe:** a third, middle soul, the "intellectual soul". It is natural human reason. Because it is rooted in the divine image, it can rise to understand memale, sovev, and even what is above both. It can then "explain it to the animal soul". «והיינו שכל טבעי וגשמי הנקרא שכל אנושי, שכל ענינו הוא להשכיל בענינים הטבעיים» — *a natural, physical mind called human intellect, whose whole business is natural things.* TM 101889. «אך מצד שרשו בבחי' הצלם יש ביכלתו להשכיל בשכל אנושי גם בעניני אלקות» — *but because it is rooted in the divine image, it can also understand Godly matters with human intellect.* TM 101889. «ביכלתה לבאר ולהסביר זאת גם לנה"ב» — *it can explain and clarify it to the animal soul too.* TM 101889.

**Ours.** For the project, "the human brain" in the owner's question is this middle layer. The Rebbe's model gives builders three listeners, not two: the G-dly soul (sees), the thinking self (understands and translates), and the animal soul (has to be persuaded in terms of life and good). Note the conflict: the Rebbe's intellectual soul can grasp even sovev, while the Rashab keeps sovev for the G-dly soul's ChaBaD (KT 44). They may be speaking of different things: understanding a concept vs. having it settle in a soul. **Not resolved in the sources we read.**

### P5. Turned vs. subdued: the Rashab's rule **Claim.** Contemplation the natural mind can follow lets the animal soul itself understand, feel and love. Contemplation above it only overpowers the animal soul's traits. **Stated** (Rashab).

- «אם העכודה היא בהעניינים שגם השכל הטבעי דנה"ב יכול להבין זאת הרי הנה"א פועל בו» [העכודה = העבודה] — *if the work is in matters that even the animal soul's natural mind can understand, the G-dly soul works on it…* KT 77 (OCR). - «שגם: הוא יבין וירגיש את. הענין האלקי» — *…so that it too understands and feels the Godly matter.* KT 77 (OCR). - «ואם העבודה היא בענינים שלמעלה מהשגת השכל המבעי» [המבעי = הטבעי] — *and if the work is in matters above the natural mind's grasp…* KT 77 (OCR) → «מתגבר. על המדות טבעים דנח"ב להכניעם» [דנח"ב = דנה"ב] — *…it overpowers the animal soul's natural traits to subdue them.* KT 77 (OCR).

The Rashab says the same in Ayin Beis, with the reason. The G-dly soul's own excitement is simply not the animal soul's business: - «כי ההתפעלות של הנה"א מצ"ע מה שהנה"א בעצמו מתפעל על אלקות הרי אין זה שייך להנה"ב» — *the G-dly soul's own excitement, what it is moved by in itself, has nothing to do with the animal soul.* AB 2431 (Shabbos Re'eh 5674). - «וא"ז שפועל עליו בבחי' מקיף לבד כי באופן כזה לא יפעל בנה"ב שירצה באלקו'» — *not that it acts on it only as something surrounding, for that way it will not make the animal soul want Godliness.* AB 3848.

**Ours.** "Subdue" is not failure. The Tanya's beinoni lives there. But only the first kind of contemplation changes what the animal soul wants.

### P6. Bring the matter itself down, or dress it in pictures? (the Rashab holds both) **Claim.** There are two statements, and builders need both. 1. In prayer the contemplation should be dressed in "garments of grasp and natural matters" so the natural mind can follow. It should not be the soul's own lofty grasp. **Stated** (AB 2430). 2. Elsewhere: what is meant is *not* putting the matter into more tangible words and explanations. It is bringing the matter as it is into the natural mind, which *raises* the natural mind. **Stated** (AB 5090).

- «שאין זה בערך השגת שכל הטבעי דנה"ב להשיג ענינים אלו» — *these are not in proportion to what the animal soul's natural mind can grasp.* AB 2430. - «כ"א שההשגה תהי' מלובשת בלבושי השגה ובענינים טבעים בכדי שיהי' מושג גם בשכל הטבעי» — *rather the grasp should be clothed in garments of grasp and in natural matters, so that it is grasped in the natural mind too.* AB 2430. - «ואין זה שמביא את הענינים בהתלבשות יותר היינו בדברי' והסברים מוחשים יותר בכדי שיובן גם בשכל הטבעי» — *this is not bringing the matters in more clothing, that is, in more tangible words and explanations so that the natural mind understands.* AB 5090. - «ובאמת הוא בבחי' העלאה שמעלה את השכל הטבעי שגם בו מושג ומורגש הענין» — *in truth it is a raising: it raises the natural mind, so that in it too the matter is grasped and felt.* AB 5090.

Analogies help and also hide (AB 5701). The G-dly soul grasps the Godly matter "through" physical analogies. Then it strips the analogy off, and the light shines more.

**Ours.** These fit together as two stages. First choose *which* matters to contemplate (natural ones: life, creation, providence) and use pictures from nature. Then do not stay in the picture. Lift the natural mind until it holds the real thing. An animal-soul track that only multiplies analogies has missed the second half.

### P7. The G-dly soul must work with the natural mind, and it takes proofs and time **Claim.** It is not enough that the G-dly soul understands *through* the natural mind. It must deliberately work on the natural mind until the point sits well there. Sometimes that needs extra proofs. **Stated** (Rashab).

- «כ"א שמשתדל ומתעסק עם השכל הטבעי דנה"ב שיבין ג"כ את הענין ויונח אצלו בטוב» — *rather, he strives and busies himself with the animal soul's natural mind, so that it too understands the matter and it settles well with it.* AB 5701. - «ה' שלא להסתפק בהשנת הנה"א לבד ובהתפעלות שלו, כ"א להשתדל שיובן הענין גם בהשכל המבעי» [בהשנת = בהשגת; המבעי = הטבעי] — *[the servants of] G-d should not settle for the G-dly soul's grasp and excitement alone, but strive that the matter be understood in the natural mind too.* KA 152 (OCR). - «ולפעמים צריכים לזה ראיות והוכחות יותר בכדי שיונח. הענין בהשבל הטבעי» — *sometimes more proofs and demonstrations are needed for this, so the matter settles in the natural mind.* KA 152 (OCR). - «וית' לקמן שזה עיקר גדול בעבודה להביא ההתבוננות לידי הבנת השכל הטבעי» — *it will be explained below that this is a great principle in avodah: to bring the contemplation to the natural mind's understanding.* KA 142 (OCR).

The Rashab also says (KA 152) that this depends on "drawing the natural soul near and giving it over" to the contemplation. If the preparation is only to understand with the G-dly mind, it does little to the natural soul.

The Mitteler Rebbe gives the time and the effort: «וכמו כן רחוק מנפש הטבעית הגופנית להתפעל מעניני אלהות רק על ידי יגיעה רבה» — *likewise the bodily natural soul is far from being moved by Godly matters, except through great toil.* KH 426.

**Ours.** For a meditation builder this means: the animal-soul track is longer. It repeats. It brings arguments. It asks the inner skeptic "do you agree?" and waits for a yes.

### P8. Steps inside the animal soul: agreement, then wanting; and "it must be so" **Claim.** The animal soul's change has stages. First it agrees and stops resisting. Later it wants, and lives the Godly matters with energy. **Stated** (Rashab). The Rebbe, citing the Rashab's Samach Vav, names a floor below that: understanding only that "it must be so". **Stated** (the Rebbe; Samach Vav itself not checked).

- «ותחלה הוא בדרך הסכם שאינו מונע להאהבה ולהרצון בפועל דנה"א» — *at first it is by way of agreement: it does not block the G-dly soul's actual love and will.* KA 142 (OCR). The same line continues: through diligent work the will arises in the animal soul too, and "even from the physical side he lives in Godly matters, and does everything with will, desire and great life". - «שתבין עכ"פ שבהכרח לומר כן, אף שהענין עצמו אינו מושג אצלה» — *that it understands at least that one must say so, even though the matter itself is not grasped by it.* TM 107862 (Shabbos Re'eh 5733), citing Hemshech Samach Vav. - «ע"י אריכות ההתבוננות, גם נפש הבהמית תסכים לזה» — *through lengthy contemplation, the animal soul too will agree to this.* TM 107862.

The Mitteler Rebbe's lowest counted rung for the natural soul is the same "agreement" (P10).

### P9. What moves the animal soul: what touches itself, life, good, pleasure **Claim.** The natural soul is moved by what touches its own interest. Contemplation reaches it when it is held "like a matter that touches oneself". **Stated** (Mitteler Rebbe). The Rashab frames it as good vs. bad, and as taste. The Alter Rebbe frames it as "whom else would it want". **Stated.**

The Mitteler Rebbe's two kinds of thought: - «והמחשבה הב׳ היא מחשבה שחושב בענין טוב הנוגע לעצמו, כמו שמרויח במשא ומתן טוב» — *the second kind is a thought about a good thing that touches oneself, as when one profits from a good deal.* KH 248. - «וכך יובן בהתבוננות אלהות במחשבה כזאת שנדבק היטב בה כענין הנוגע לעצמו» — *so too in contemplating Godliness with such a thought, that clings to it well, as to a matter that touches oneself.* KH 252. - «כמו שאדם עוסק במשא ומתן בהתפעלות לבו ממש» — *as a man does business with his heart truly aroused.* KH 283. (Love of G-d is the source of the positive commands, fear of the negative ones, like eagerness for profit and care to avoid loss.)

Good vs. bad, for the natural mind: - KA 142 (OCR), the content to bring into the natural mind: Godliness is the main thing and the physical secondary; Godliness is "the ultimate good", while the physical "is not good at all". The line's verified opening is «ונם ‏ ע"י החלישות דנה"ב נעשה מוכשר לקבל את האור והוא שגם השכל הטבעי יבין את הענין האלקי» [ונם = וגם] — *also, by weakening the animal soul it becomes fit to receive the light: that the natural mind too understands the Godly matter.*

Taste: - «נוטל ממנו התענוג (דער געשמאק) מהענינים החומריים» — *it takes from it the pleasure ("the geshmak") in material things.* KA 150 (OCR). - Hayom Yom gives the positive side: «עס איז ניטא אזא קאלטע זאך ווי שכל הטבעי און שכל אנושי» — *there is nothing as cold as natural intellect and human intellect.* And «און ווען דער שכל הטבעי פארשטעהט א השגה אלקית» — *and when the natural intellect understands a Godly concept* (and its feelings are moved by the intellectual taste), "that is the true resurrection of the dead". HY 315 (11 Sivan, from the Rashab's talks).

Wanting (the Alter Rebbe): - «שישוב היצה״ר ממש לאהבת ה׳ ע״י ההתבוננות איך שבאמת אין עוד ממש וא״כ למי יחפוץ זולתו ית׳» — *that the evil inclination itself return to love G-d, by contemplating how in truth there is nothing else at all; and if so, whom would it want besides Him?* LT 2660 (Likkutei Torah, Matos).

Life (P3): "He is your life" (AB 5090; KA 129).

**Ours.** These are the animal soul's four handles: *my* interest, good for me, pleasure, my life. A builder can phrase each contemplation in one of them. "You want life. Here is where life comes from." "You want what is good. This is the only lasting good." "You want pleasure. Here is the pleasure under every pleasure." None of these lines is a quote. They are our translation of the stated method.

### P10. The natural soul's ladder (Mitteler Rebbe) **Claim.** Contemplation produces five levels of arousal *in the natural soul*, matched to nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah and yechidah. A level below them, wanting arousal for its own sake, is not counted. **Stated** (KH 225-311). All five, even the top one, are "serving G-d with the body", not with the soul. **Stated** (KH 312).

The ladder (KH, plain English): 0. **Not counted.** He wants only to be aroused and get life from it, "to be a something". It is close to "strange fire" (KH 226-228, 304). «הא׳ הגרוע שבכולם והוא שכל עיקר חפצו אינו רק להיות מתפעל בלבד ויהיה לו מזה חיות» — *the first, the worst of all: his whole wish is only to be aroused, and get vitality from it.* KH 226. 1. **Nefesh: agreement from a cold thought.** He hears and understands, and it is true to him, "it sits very well in the mind". But "the matter is far from him, and he asks what use it will be to his soul" (KH 234-235). It gives birth only to shame and to agreement to avoid evil (KH 255-257). «ולא נולד מזה רק בחינת ההסכם להיות סור מרע» — *all that is born from it is agreement to turn from evil.* KH 257. Mapped to nefesh (KH 306). 2. **Ruach: the "good thought".** The thought grips him like news of a deal that is his. Love and fear arise in thought and reach action (KH 242-267). 3. **Neshamah: felt in the heart.** The heart is moved. "This is the main commandment of the service of the heart: to toil in thought until the heart is moved" (KH 271). «אבל בעבודת ה׳ לא בקל יומשך התפעלות בלב מהתפעלות המחשבה» — *but in serving G-d, the heart is not easily moved by a moved thought.* KH 274. These are "natural love and fear" (KH 277). 4. **Chayah: kavanah.** Above heart-arousal. The whole length of the contemplation is held in mind and heart and is not shortened into a feeling. This is "intellectual love and fear", which are not separate from the contemplation: «ודחילו ורחימו שכליים אינו נבדל מעצם ההתבוננות כלל, אלא ממילא ומאליו באה בהכרח» — *intellectual love and awe are not separate from the contemplation at all; they come by themselves, of necessity.* KH 298. Its sign is constancy (KH 300). 5. **Yechidah: the simple will.** Above contemplation altogether (KH 302, 311).

Then the line that changes everything: - «הכל הוא בנפש השכלית שנקרא נפש הטבעית החיונית, שגם היא תסכים ותודה בהודאה והתפעלות במוח והתפעלות בלב» — *it is all in the intellectual soul, called the natural living soul, which can also agree and acknowledge, with arousal in mind and heart.* KH 312. - «וכל זה נקרא עובד ה׳ בגופו ולא נקרא עובד ה׳ בנשמתו» — *and all this is called serving G-d with his body, not serving G-d with his soul.* KH 312.

**Ours (important for builders).** In the Mitteler Rebbe's map, *everything contemplation produces* is, first of all, the natural soul's. The project's ladder treats contemplation as the G-dly soul's work. KH reverses the emphasis: contemplation is mainly how the *natural* soul is brought along. The G-dly soul's own arousal is of a different kind (P11). The existing summary already flags this (`KUNTRES-HAHITPAALUT.md`, "Contemplation's love belongs to the natural soul").

### P11. The G-dly soul's ladder is a different kind **Claim.** The G-dly soul is "a Godly power, literally". It is moved by Godly things the way the natural soul is moved by bodily things: by its own nature (KH 424). Its arousal is clothed in the natural soul's, which hides it (KH 407). **Stated** (Mitteler Rebbe).

- «כי בחינת הנשמה מצד עצמה הרי היא כח אלהי ממש» — *for the soul in itself is a Godly power, literally.* KH 313. - «וכמו שהנפש הטבעית תתפעל מענינים גופנים בבחינת עצמית וטבעית כי משם לוקחה» — *just as the natural soul is moved by bodily matters, essentially and by nature, since it was taken from there [so the G-dly soul by Godly matters].* KH 424. - «אך שהתפעלות השגה דנפש הטבעית כמו לבוש בלבד להתפעלות השגה דנפש האלקית המסתירה ומעלימה» — *but the natural soul's arousal from grasp is only like a garment for the G-dly soul's, which hides and conceals it.* KH 407.

The G-dly soul's own ladder (KH 437-625, from the summary and checked lines) starts from good deeds done by nature and climbs through faith that is never lost (the thief who prays at the tunnel, KH 470), self-sacrifice, the heart of flesh that sings, up to the yechidah's single will. Its arousal comes "of itself" and is not toiled for. The natural soul's arousal comes "only through great toil" (KH 426).

The Mitteler Rebbe also notes that the two souls vary separately. One person has a high G-dly soul and a coarse natural soul. Another has a lower G-dly soul but a "finer" natural soul, more given to bitul (KH 820-821). - «ולהיפוך יש שנשמתו נמוכה ורחוק ערכו בהתפעלות אלהות מזולתו, אבל גופו דנפש הטבעית דקה יותר» — *conversely, one whose soul is lower and far from another's in Godly arousal, but his body, the natural soul, is finer.* KH 821.

### P12. Both mind and heart are needed in the animal soul; the mind alone only takes the taste away **Claim.** If the natural mind does not understand, the G-dly soul's love does nothing to the animal soul, except weaken it. If the natural mind understands but there is no heart-arousal in the G-dly soul, the animal soul's traits do not change either. "Both are needed." **Stated** (Rashab, AB 5702-5703).

- «לא יפעול מאומה בהנה"ב שאינו ניזוז ממקומו» — *it will do nothing to the animal soul, which does not budge from its place.* AB 5702. - «ויכול להיות רק בדרך חלישות שנחלש כחו מפני גילוי האור דנה"א» — *at most by weakening: its strength weakens before the revealed light of the G-dly soul.* AB 5702. - «וצ"ל שניהם דוקא» — *both are needed.* AB 5702.

And a surprising advantage the animal soul has: - «ובנה"ב כאשר משיג בשכלו ענין אלקי והוסכם אצלו בשכלו הטוב דאלקות ה"ז פועל עליו לצאת מן החומריות» — *in the animal soul, when it grasps a Godly matter in its mind and agrees that Godliness is good, this moves it to leave materiality.* AB 5702. The Rashab explains: the G-dly soul's mind can stay "in its own heights", far from action. The animal soul's mind exists only to serve its feelings, so when it understands, it acts. - «והיינו שלוקח ממנו את החיות מהענינים הגשמיים» — *that is, it takes away its vitality in physical matters.* AB 5703. - «אבל בכדי להיות לו אהבה לאלקות זהו דוקא ע"י התפעלות האהבה דנה"א» — *but for it to have love for Godliness, that comes only through the G-dly soul's arousal of love.* AB 5703.

The same split in Kuntres HaAvodah: water-love (from seeing the life in the world) by itself only weakens the animal soul, unless one works with the natural soul so it understands. Fire-love acts on the animal soul by itself: - «משא"כ האהבה דכרשפי אש שפועלת בעצמה על הנה"ב» — *unlike love like flames of fire, which acts on the animal soul by itself.* KA 152 (OCR).

The image the Rashab uses: oil and wick. The wick is the G-dly soul. The oil is the animal soul's natural mind. The oil follows the wick and is burned into light: - «וזה"ע השמן שנמשך אחר הפתילה היינו בחי' השכל הטבעי דנה"ב שנמשך אחר השכל דנה"א» — *this is the oil drawn after the wick: the animal soul's natural mind drawn after the G-dly soul's mind.* AB 5703. He adds that the animal soul is "oil" because its root is in Tohu, the essence of things. So every bit of it that is refined adds much light to the G-dly soul (AB 5703; also AB 3409).

**Ours.** For builders: the animal-soul track needs a *mind* part (it agrees) and a *heart* part (the G-dly soul's love, which it catches). An explanation with no warmth will cool its desires but will not make it love.

### P13. Why it must reach the animal soul **Claim.** The soul came down only to refine the animal soul. The days of a person's life are measured by the refinements he must do. Prayer is the main place for it. **Stated** (Rashab; Rayatz; Mitteler Rebbe).

- «שתפלה העיקרית היא לברר ולזכך לנפש הטבעית הנקרא עבודה ובירור» — *the main prayer is to refine and purify the natural soul; this is called service and refining.* KH 583. - «עד ואהבת פועל יוצא להביא אהבה גם בנפש הטבעית» — *until "and you shall love", which acts to bring love into the natural soul too.* KH 588 (on the Shema). - RS 1359: the G-dly soul needs no repair; it was already bound to G-d before descent; it comes down only to refine the animal soul and the body. - «שעושה מדבר שאינו כלי להיות כלי» — *it makes a thing that is not a vessel into a vessel.* TM 20179 (2nd day Rosh Hashanah 5715). The Rebbe: the G-dly soul's own work reaches only "lights and revelations". When it "thinks about matters that belong to the animal soul and explains them to the animal soul", it does something new and draws new light. Line: «אך כאשר נה"א חושבת ומתבוננת בענינים השייכים לנה"ב ומסבירה אותם לנה"ב» — *but when the G-dly soul thinks and contemplates matters that belong to the animal soul, and explains them to the animal soul.* TM 20179. - «כאשר נפש האלקית מתבוננת בענין אלקי באופן שגם נפש הבהמית יכולה להשיג» — *when the G-dly soul contemplates a Godly matter in a way the animal soul too can grasp.* TM 44255 (2nd day Rosh Hashanah 5720). There the Rebbe calls this "returning light", which reaches the Essence, while the G-dly soul's own work is "direct light".

The test, by the Rashab: if prayer does not touch the animal soul, the two souls were never joined (KA 255, P16).

**Ours.** This is the project's answer to "why translate at all". The animal soul is not a side audience. In these sources, reaching it is the point of the descent.

### P14. Speaking to it and rebuking it **Claim.** You may, and sometimes must, speak to the animal soul directly. First explain. If it will not learn, rebuke it in harsh words. **Stated** (Rayatz; Tanya).

- «אך אם ההתבוננות אינו פועל בהנה"ב ובדברים לא יוסר עבד, אזי צריכים לרדותו במקל» — *but if contemplation does not act on the animal soul, and "a servant is not corrected by words", one must discipline it with a stick.* RS 1360. The stick is "harsh words" from the hidden store of fear of heaven. - «לרגוז על נפש הבהמית, שהיא יצרו הרע, בקול רעש ורוגז במחשבתו» — *to rage at the animal soul, his evil inclination, with a loud, angry voice in his thought.* TY 421 (Tanya ch. 29).

The content of the Tanya's rebuke is *the unity itself*. He tells the animal soul that the Infinite light fills this very place as before creation, and: - «מכחיש האמת הנראה לעינים, דכולא קמיה כלא ממש באמת, בבחינת ראייה חושיית» — *you deny the truth that is visible to the eyes, that all before Him is truly as nothing, as sense-sight.* TY 421.

And who benefits: - «והנה, על ידי זה יועיל לנפשו האלהית, להאיר עיניה באמת יחוד אוראיןסוף בראיה חושיית» — *through this he will help his G-dly soul, to light its eyes with the truth of the unity of the Infinite light, as sense-sight.* TY 422.

**Ours.** Two different ways of speaking to the animal soul: - The Rashab/Rayatz way: *explain*, in its terms, until it agrees (P7-P9). - The Tanya ch. 29 way: *confront* it with the unity it denies. This is not an explanation it is expected to accept. It humbles it, and it clears the G-dly soul's sight.

The Rayatz puts them in order: explain first, the stick second. The Tanya also says why rebuke works: the animal soul has no real existence of its own. Its life comes from holiness, so it gives way before the light (TY 423).

Hayom Yom adds a striking image for the same thing: striking the animal soul's stone makes the spark that lights the G-dly fire. - «דורך הכאה בכח אין דעם אבן של נפש הבהמית» — *by striking hard on the stone of the animal soul [a spark comes out that lights the Godly fire].* HY 314 (10 Sivan, the Alter Rebbe).

### P15. When it is too strong, start with the yoke, not with understanding **Claim.** A person ruled by his natural powers cannot begin with service by reason. First he accepts the yoke of heaven and leaves his habits. That giving of himself makes him fit for contemplation. **Stated** (Rashab).

- «א"א לו להתחיל בעבודה שע"פ טו"ד» — *he cannot begin with service based on reason and understanding.* KA 94 (OCR). - «וע"כ ראשית כל דבר הוא לקבל עליו עול מלכות שמים» — *so first of all he must accept the yoke of the kingdom of heaven.* KA 94 (OCR). The same line tells of a great chassid who, after his first visit to the Tzemach Tzedek, decided: "whatever I want by nature, I won't do". - The Rebbe: one can also force the animal soul "before he explains to it and it does not yet understand", by accepting the yoke; this reaches higher still. «שעוד לפני שמסביר לו ואינו מבין עדיין, הרי הוא כופה אותו בקבלת עול» — *even before he explains to it, while it does not yet understand, he forces it by accepting the yoke.* TM 20179. - The Tanya on habit: to change a habit "one must arouse love of G-d by contemplating G-d's greatness in his mind, to rule over the nature in the left chamber". «צריך לעורר את האהבה לה׳ על ידי שמתבונן בגדולת ה׳ במוחו, לשלוט על הטבע שבחלל השמאלי» TY 249 (Tanya ch. 15).

### P16. The three kinds of animal soul (goat, ox, sheep) **Claim.** Animal souls differ. The Rashab names three general kinds, in KT 77 (OCR). **Stated.** - **Goat**: the hardest. It does not move at all and does not even show its strength. You see its stubbornness only when you try to change it. «דעז הוא עז שבבחמות-בבחי' תקיפות וחוזק ביותר שאינו ניזוז ממקומו כלל משום דבר» [שבבחמות = שבבהמות] — *the goat is the boldest of animals: the most force and strength; it does not budge from its place at all for anything.* KT 77 (OCR). - **Ox**: the goring ox. It shows its strength, butts and kicks. But it is easier to move toward good, because it moves at all. «ושור הוא שור נגח שמראה תוקפו וחזקו לנגח ולבעוט» KT 77 (OCR). «ובזה נקל יותר. לפעול הזזה לטוב מפני שיש בו תנועת התפעלות» — *with this it is easier to move it to good, because there is movement of feeling in it.* KT 77 (OCR). The Rashab's reason: its "is-ness" is weaker. - **Sheep**: soft by nature, easy to bend and to turn. «וכשב טבעו רך וכקל לכפותו ולהפכו» [וכקל = ונקל] KT 77 (OCR).

And work on the particular trait: «וכפי אשר ידץ אינש בנפשי' מדותיו הרעים באיזה מדה פרטיות ביותר» [ידץ = ידע] — *as a person knows his own bad traits, in which particular trait most…* KT 77 (OCR). He must arouse in prayer the holy trait that opposes it.

The Mitteler Rebbe's "goat" case, though he does not use the word: the man who understands sovev and memale and is not moved at all, "a heart of stone", from the animal soul's coarseness: - «שאע"פ שלבבו יבין האמת, מ"מ יתעקש בעקשות קשה» — *even though his heart understands the truth, he stubbornly insists.* TC 4646 (Toras Chaim, Tetzaveh).

**Ours.** For a builder: the goat needs the yoke and the rebuke (P14-P15) more than explanation. The ox can be reached by explanation, because its passions already move. The sheep is turned easily. The sources do not give separate contemplations for each kind.

### P17. Da'at is feeling, and feeling is what sorts good from bad **Claim.** Refining the animal soul is done mainly through da'at. Da'at is *feeling*: as pain is felt in the brain through the nerves, and a child or an animal feels pain less for lack of da'at. One chooses good and rejects bad only by feeling it. **Stated** (Rashab, KT 77-78; Alter Rebbe, LT).

- «נמצא דההבחנה לבחור בטוב ולמאוס ברע הוא ע"י הרגש הדעת דוקא» — *so the discernment to choose good and despise bad comes only through the feeling of da'at.* KT 78 (OCR). - «כי עיקר בירור המדות הוא ע״י המשכת הדעת שהוא חיות המדות» — *for the main refining of the traits is by drawing down da'at, which is the life of the traits.* LT 2660.

**Ours.** This explains why "understanding" for the animal soul has to end in *feeling* good and bad. It is the bridge to the project's step 6 (da'at).

### P18. The yechidah turns what contemplation cannot **Claim.** When the yechidah, the soul's core, is revealed (as in a real test of self-sacrifice), it changes the animal soul's very essence, "from one extreme to the other". **Stated** (Rashab; Hayom Yom). But the animal soul still does not, by its own nature, want G-d. **Stated** (Rashab, KA 180).

- «שהנה"ט מצ"ע רוצח רק לחיות» [רוצח = רוצה] — *the natural soul, by itself, wants only to live.* KA 180 (OCR). - «אבל לא שהנה"ב בעצמו בטבעו ירצה באלקות» — *but not that the animal soul itself, by its nature, wants Godliness.* KA 180 (OCR). - «נהפך יחידה שבנפש הבהמית לאלקות» — *the yechidah of the animal soul is turned to Godliness.* HY 516 (17 Tishrei).

The Mitteler Rebbe shows where the natural soul's "yechidah" sits in a busy man: in his business. - «ויעסוק בבחינת יחידה שבנפש הטבעית במשא ומתן ושאר צרכי גופו» — *he deals with the yechidah of the natural soul in business and his other bodily needs [as if they touched the apple of his eye].* KH 644.

**Ours.** The animal soul has an essence, and its essence is set on staying alive and on its own affairs. Contemplation can redirect its mind and feelings. Only the revealed essence of the G-dly soul turns the essence of the animal soul.

### P19. Even when it loves, its opposition is still hidden underneath **Claim.** The animal soul's love of G-d, born of understanding, is really the G-dly soul's mind clothed in its mind. Its hidden opposition remains. Only light far beyond it, "from above to below", nullifies it. **Stated** (the Rebbe, MM 683, Shabbos Vayetzei, 10 Kislev 5722).

- «הוא, שההתבוננות דנפש האלקית היא באופן שגם נפש הבהמית תבין ותשיג את הענין האלקי» — *[refining the animal soul] means the G-dly soul contemplates in a way that the animal soul too understands and grasps the Godly matter.* MM 683. - «ע"י שהשכל דנה"ב בא להבנה שצריך לאהוב את ה'» — *through the animal soul's mind coming to understand that one must love G-d.* MM 683. (Not because its existence was nullified.) - «נשאר בהעלם ההתנגדות שלה לנה"א» — *its opposition to the G-dly soul remains hidden.* MM 683.

**Ours.** A guard against overclaiming. "The animal soul now loves G-d" is a true stage, not the end.

### P20. Other carriers: the friend, the habit, the food, the count **Claim (stated, short).** - **A friend.** "When one talks with another about avodah and they learn together, it is two G-dly souls against one natural soul" (the Mitteler Rebbe, in Hayom Yom). «איז דאס צוויי נפש אלקית אויף איין נפש הטבעית» HY 56 (20 Teves). The Rebbe repeats it: «שאז יש שתי נפשות אלקיות כנגד נפש בהמית אחת» TM 108753. - **Habit.** If you leave the animal soul alone and don't deal with it, it will have no part in holiday joy. If you work with it and accustom it, "habit becomes nature", until it is turned and shares the joy. «כאשר מניחים את נה"ב לנפשה ולא מתעסקים עמה» … «הרגל נעשה טבע» TM 110285. - **Food and the body.** For eating to be truly for G-d, first comes prayer "with contemplation that the animal soul too understands", so it grasps that one must want G-d and not the world's material things. «צ"ל תחלה העבודה דתפלה בהתבוננות כזו שמובנת גם לנה"ב» MM 1340 (10 Shevat 5729). - **Slowly, by counting.** The omer (barley, animal food) is for the animal soul. The light is drawn into it "slowly, slowly" by counting, so that «שגם נפש הבהמית תוכל למסור נפשה באחד» — *the animal soul too can give itself over in "One".* DM 61 (Tzemach Tzedek). - **Learning "that belongs to them".** The Rebbe: there is a learning that draws da'at into the animal side, «שגם נפש הבהמית והגוף יבואו לביטול היש ע"י ההתבוננות השייכת אליהם» — *so that the animal soul and body too come to nullify their self-ness, through the contemplation that belongs to them.* TM 36420 (Purim 5718). - **Prayer as offering.** The Alter Rebbe: one contemplates Godliness in prayer and is moved, «ומתפעל בזה עד שגם נה"ב מתפעל ביותר ותרצה להדבק ולמסור נפשה לה' אחד» — *and is moved until the animal soul too is greatly moved and wants to cleave and give itself to the One G-d.* MA 66373 (OCR, Maamarei Razal). - **"Before Him nothing" yields the animal will.** «שיתהפך גם הרצון של הנה"ב הנק' יצה"ר להיות לה' לבדו» — *so that the will of the animal soul, called the evil inclination, also turns to be for G-d alone.* TO 2307 (Torah Ohr, Vayakhel). «ורצון זה ואהבה זו נולדים ונמשכים מהשכל» — *and this will and love are born from the mind*, from contemplating how "all before Him is as nothing". TO 2307.

## 3b. The project's 14 rungs, approached for the animal soul

Steps from `ONE-PROCESS.md` §2. For each: what the sources say about the animal soul here, and what is ours.

1. **Where you stand.** *Stated:* the worst level is wanting arousal to "be a something", and it is not counted (KH 226-228, 304). The animal soul arrives as a friend who agrees with everything you do (RS 1359: «ער ווערט מיט אים א גוטער פריינד» — *he becomes a good friend to him*; «דתחלת הזדווגות היצה"ר אל האדם שמסכים לו על כל אשר יעשה» — *the evil inclination's first bond with a person is that it agrees with whatever he does*). The busy merchant's whole self is in his business (KH 644). *Ours:* for the animal soul, step 1 is to notice what it wants right now, without a fight. That want is the material.
2. **The true self.** *Stated:* the animal soul's own self wants only to live (KA 180). It has no part of G-d to discover. *Ours:* for the animal soul, this rung is replaced by "you are alive; something gives you life" (KA 129). This is the animal soul's honest version of looking under the layers.
3. **Being said right now (memale).** *Stated:* this is the natural mind's own rung (KT 44; AB 5090; KA 129). Contemplate the life in each creature and in yourself. Water-love: drawing near. *Ours:* the main rung of the animal-soul track.
4. **Held, inside and around.** *Stated:* "inside" (memale) yes. "Around" (sovev) is grasped in the G-dly soul's ChaBaD (KT 44). The Rebbe says the human intellect can understand even sovev (TM 101889). *Ours:* for the animal soul, stay with "filled from inside". Present "surrounded" as something it can accept as necessary (P8), not as something it will feel.
5. **Two knowings.** *No source found for the animal soul.* *Ours:* the animal soul lives entirely in the "lower" knowing (the world as it looks). It should not be asked to hold both.
6. **Da'at: bind until the heart answers.** *Stated:* da'at is feeling, and it is what sorts good from bad (KT 78). In worldly things the heart answers at once; in G-d's service it does not answer easily (KH 273-274). The natural mind's agreement must meet the G-dly soul's heart (AB 5702). Proofs as needed (KA 152). *Ours:* this is the rung where the animal-soul track spends the most time.
7. **Before Him, nothing.** *Stated:* in the animal soul, contemplation brings only "nullification of the something" (KA 145: «ונראה דבנה"ב פועל רק בחי ביטול היש» — *it seems that in the animal soul it brings only bitul of the something*). At most, the animal soul understands "it must be so" (TM 107862). The Alter Rebbe's form for the animal soul: "if there is nothing else, whom would it want?" (LT 2660). *Ours:* phrase this rung as a question of *wanting*, not of seeing.
8. **Weeping and joy.** *Stated:* lower teshuvah, bitterness at one's distance, "breaks the self-ness and materiality" of the animal soul but does not raise it (KA 150: «וזהו שגורם שבירת הישות והחומריות», «אבל לא שיעלה גם הוא ויהי' נכלל בקדושה»). Fire-love raises it (KA 150, 152). Holiday joy reaches the animal soul only if you have worked with it (TM 110285). *Ours:* bitterness is a tool for the animal soul, but it is a breaking tool, not a turning tool.
9. **Alone, where the mind stops.** *Stated:* above creation, the natural soul "does not understand or grasp at all" (RS 556). Contemplation there can only subdue it (KT 77). *Ours:* for the animal soul, this rung is not a contemplation. It is the G-dly soul's fire acting on it.
10. **The heart's simple will.** *Stated:* the natural soul has its own top level: a simple will above reason (KH 302, 311). The revealed yechidah turns the animal soul's essence (KA 180; HY 516). *Ours:* the animal soul can be turned here, but only from above.
11. **The plainest thing: the world held, one with Him.** *Stated:* every bit of the animal soul refined adds much light, because its root is in Tohu (AB 5703; AB 3409). Light "from above to below" is what finally nullifies the animal soul's hidden opposition (MM 683). *Ours:* the turn of the ladder is also the turn of the animal soul's role. From obstacle it becomes oil.
12. **The dark place turned.** *Stated:* the animal soul's home rung. Subdue first, then turn (KT 77). Goat, ox, sheep (KT 77). Rebuke in Tanya ch. 29 (TY 421). Explain, then the stick (RS 1360). Work on the particular trait (KT 77). *Ours:* build this rung's practice around the three kinds.
13. **The home below.** *Stated:* making "a thing that is not a vessel into a vessel" (TM 20179). Eating and the body (MM 1340). Habit becomes nature (TM 110285). *Ours:* the animal soul's daily life is the vessel.
14. **The deed.** *Stated:* when the animal soul agrees, "this moves it to leave materiality" (AB 5702). Its mind exists for action. With a strong animal soul, the yoke comes before understanding (KA 94; TM 20179). The 101st repetition changes habit (TY 248-249). *Ours:* for the animal soul, the deed is the proof that the contemplation reached it.

## 4. Guards and counterfeits the sources name

1. **The pious animal soul.** "The evil inclination is called the animal soul not because it is a beast. Sometimes it is a clever fox, and great wisdom is needed to understand its tricks. Sometimes it dresses as a simple, humble tzaddik with good traits" (HY 332, the Tzemach Tzedek to the Maharash). «כי לפעמים הוא שועל פקח שבחיות וצריכים לחכמה מרובה להבין תחבולותיו» and «ולפעמים הוא מתלבש בלבוש צדיק תמים ענו ובעל מדות טובות» HY 332. The Maharash: «עד אז לא ידעתי אז עס קען זיין א פרומער נה"ב» — *until then I did not know there could be a pious animal soul* (HY 335).
2. **The urge to learn instead of pray.** «ויש אשר מתעורר חשק גדול ללמוד חסידות או להתבונן היטב באיזה השכלה» — *sometimes a great desire arises to learn Chassidus or contemplate some concept well*, and it is only the animal soul's trick to keep you from the work of prayer (HY 333). Rule: anything that blocks a thing that leads to actual service, even if the block is something lofty, is the animal soul's trick (HY 334).
3. **"You're above all that."** The Rayatz: «זיין נפש הבהמית — דער חכם להרע — זאָגט אים אַז ער איז אַ חסיד» — *his animal soul, the one "wise to do evil", tells him he is a chassid*: working on prayer and good traits is trivia; he stands above it (SS 7034, Pesach 5707).
4. **The friendly yes-man, then the cooling.** The animal soul does not come to make you sin. It comes to befriend you, agree with you, and give reasons: health, being "a mensch", "everything has a measure". Then it cools your mitzvos into rote (RS 1359-1360).
5. **Wanting the arousal, not G-d.** Level zero, "strange fire" (KH 226-228, 304).
6. **Excitement that rips the G-dly soul out.** The Rashab describes a person who contemplates only generally. «ער רייפט אוים דעם נה"א» — *he tears the G-dly soul out* [of the animal soul], with great noise. «דההתפעלות דנה"א אינו פועל מאומה על הנה"ב» — *the G-dly soul's excitement does nothing to the animal soul* (KA 255, OCR). The test: does anything change in the animal soul?
7. **Weakening mistaken for change.** The animal soul can be weakened by revealed light without being changed (AB 5702; KA 142, 152). And in the beinoni its essence may stay at full strength (Tanya ch. 12-13; see `darkei-hachassidus/sources-a-alter-rebbe-rashab.md` §3.2).
8. **Cold agreement that does not last.** The busy man's agreement "is very cold and lasts only for a while … in an hour or two it passes and is as if it never was" (KH 643).
9. **"I understand it" mistaken for the animal soul being moved.** The man whose heart "understands the truth" and stubbornly stays put (TC 4646). And the Rashab: the G-dly soul's own understanding, without work on the natural mind, does not touch the natural soul except to weaken it (KA 152).
10. **The animal soul's own claim to want G-d.** It does not, by its nature (KA 180). Its love is borrowed, its opposition hidden (MM 683).
11. **No taste for inner Torah.** Some people, when the inner Torah is explained, "have no geshmak in it" and do not even understand it. This is the animal soul overpowering, since it does not relate to anything not physical (TM 21802). «אין להם בזה שום געשמאַק» TM 21802. *Ours:* a builder should treat "this does nothing for me" as information about the animal soul, not as a verdict on the content.
12. **Despair.** The sources here do not name despair as the animal soul's trap in the passages read. Tanya ch. 26-31 and Kuntres HaAvodah on bitterness («שבאמת זהו עצבות» — *which in truth is sadness*, KA 248, OCR) are the places to look. *Not verified in this pass.*

Also a guard from the Tanya's foreword, against one-size explanations: «הנה ספרי היראה הבנויים על פי שכל אנושי, בוודאי אינן שוין לכל נפש» — *books of fear built on human intellect are surely not equal for every soul*, since one man's mind is not moved by what moves another's (TY 53). *Ours:* the animal-soul track has to be personal, as the Rashab says (work on *your* particular trait, KT 77).

## 5. Gaps: what the sources do not say

1. **No full script.** No source gives a step-by-step contemplation written *for* the animal soul. The Rashab gives the rule (what to contemplate, that it must settle in the natural mind, that proofs may be needed). The Mitteler Rebbe gives the ladder and the business analogy. The wording of an actual animal-soul meditation is ours.
2. **Goat, ox and sheep have no separate methods.** The Rashab names the three kinds and says the ox is easier because it moves. He does not give a contemplation for each.
3. **Sovev for the animal soul.** The Rashab keeps sovev for the G-dly soul (KT 44). The Rebbe says the intellectual soul can understand even sovev and above (TM 101889). The Rebbe also reports, from Samach Vav, that the animal soul understands "it must be so" without grasping it (TM 107862). We did not find a source that reconciles these. We also did not verify the Samach Vav passage itself (OCR search failed); it is cited only through the Rebbe.
4. **Where human intellect lives.** Mitteler Rebbe: in the natural soul. Tzemach Tzedek: from the animal soul. The Rebbe: a middle soul. The difference matters for design and is not settled here.
5. **The "two knowings" rung (step 5).** Nothing found on the animal soul here.
6. **Despair as the animal soul's trap.** Not verified in this pass (see §4.12).
7. **The Rashab's open question.** The Rashab himself leaves open whether the natural soul has any hidden power of self-sacrifice of its own (KA 180, OCR «וצ"עץ אם יש בהנה"ט» [= וצ"ע], *it needs study whether the natural soul has…*). He concludes it is all the G-dly soul working on it, but marks it.
8. **Tanya is thinner here than the later works.** The Tanya speaks of subduing (ch. 9, 12-15), rebuking (ch. 29), and turning (ch. 9 for the tzaddik, the "harlot" parable: the animal soul's real wish is to be overcome, TY 188: «אך נפש הבהמית שמהקליפה, רצונה להפך ממש – לטובת האדם שיתגבר עליה וינצחנה»). It does not set out "contemplation the natural mind can follow" as a method. That method is the Rashab's and the Mitteler Rebbe's. *Ours:* do not attribute it to the Tanya.
9. **Not checked.** Derech Chaim and Sha'arei Teshuvah (Mitteler Rebbe) gave no direct hits on these terms in our searches. Kuntres Umaayan was not re-read for this file (the existing summary has its line on the natural soul). Likkutei Sichos was not searched. The ChassidusIntelligence tools were not used.
