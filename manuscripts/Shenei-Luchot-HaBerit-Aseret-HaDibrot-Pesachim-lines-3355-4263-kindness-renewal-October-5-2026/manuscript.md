Pesach is the pillar of kindness (*chesed*); "Pesach" means "He had pity" (line 3356). The tractate holds the laws of Rosh Chodesh, Pesach and the Omer; a long section on kindness (weddings, the sick, the dying, guests); six sermons for Shabbat HaGadol that the Shelah calls *Matzah Ashirah* ("rich matzah"); his Haggadah commentary *Matzah Shemurah* ("guarded matzah"); and a short ethics chapter. The sermons are among the richest pages in the book on the unity of G-d.

## 1.1 No reality but His, renewed each moment

The clearest unity line in the whole book so far comes in the sixth sermon:

> אמנם אמיתית האמונה היא הש"י מחדש בטובו בכל יום תמיד מעשה בראשית בכוונת שופע שפעו, ואלו היה מונע רגע אחד, היה הכל כלא היה בטל המציאות (line 3938)

"The truth of faith is that G-d renews in His goodness every day, always, the work of creation, by pouring out His flow; if He held it back one moment, all would be as if it had never been." Then he reads the verse "know this day... there is none else" (Deut. 4:39):

> אלא רצה לומר, שאין עוד מציאות בעולם זולת מציאותו יתברך, כי בהסתרו יאבד הכל (line 3938)

"It means there is no other reality in the world but His reality, for if He hid it, all would be lost." The stars and angels have only "the readiness to receive, as the eye is ready to see when sight flows into it" (line 3938), and "nothing ever acts but Him" (לעולם אין שום דבר בפועל זולתו יתברך, line 3941). This is, almost word for word, the argument of the Tanya's Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah ch. 1-3 and its reading of "there is none else". The Shelah wrote it about 1620.

## 1.2 "I am all"

He quotes the Zohar on "you shall know no god but Me": "do not say it is another; rather, I am all" (אלא אנא הוא כלא, line 3603). And from Avodat HaKodesh: "the foundation of foundations... that the L-rd is G-d, there is none besides Him" (line 3610).

## 1.3 Good and bad from one source

Before anything was revealed, all things were mixed in the hidden thought, "like silver and dross". When the will is made clear, "one sees that the good is the heart of the will, and the bad is only to complete the good, to strengthen and keep it; so all is hewn from one source" (line 3610). Even the husk (*kelipah*) has a root in holiness, "for without this there would be, heaven forbid, two" (line 4113), and its poison will be taken away while its root remains. His providence is simple: "the change is only from the side of the receivers... from His side, all is one oneness" (line 4110). "No bad comes down from Him" (line 3903).

## 1.4 Nature and providence are one

Elokim is the name of nature and law; Havayah (the four-letter Name) is the name that is above nature and can change it (line 4071). They look like opposites, "still, inwardly they are one oneness, drawing near to each other" (line 4071); in their inner root "the two names Havayah and Elokim return to one oneness" (line 4075). This matches Shaar HaYichud ch. 6 ("Havayah is Elokim").

## 1.5 Soul of all worlds; cause and effect embrace

"He is the soul of all creatures and worlds, as it is written: You give life to them all" (line 3886). "Cause and effect cleave and embrace each other and become one"; the whole chain from the first point to the last is "one knot and one unity... His flow spreading into all that exists" (line 4118). "All are held and tied together, and they are one thing" (line 3830); "the revealed is the hidden, and the hidden is the revealed" (line 3831).

## 1.6 Beyond grasp; known only by His deeds

"Just as His essence cannot be grasped, so His will cannot be grasped" (line 4102). We praise Him only from His deeds: "we have no leave to praise and exalt except from His deeds, not from His essence" (line 4026), which is why daily Hallel without a miracle is improper. He is "Makom" (Place) from His deeds, since He is the place of the world, and hidden in His essence (line 4180). His light "pierces down" to see heaven and earth, yet He is not joined in essence with creatures the way the sefirot are (line 4226).

## 1.7 He needs no world

"He does not need His world; He made it only so there would be creatures to receive the good" (line 3560). He is King in His essence, before any creation: "not like a king of flesh and blood, for there is no king without a people; but He needs no people, for His own being is enough" (line 3649). He is called Father only once there is creation, cause and effect.

## 1.8 The dwelling below

"When the Holy One made His world He desired to have a dwelling below as He has above" (line 4079). Sins pushed the Presence up seven heavens; Abraham to Moses brought it down.

## 3.1 The inner search for leaven (proposed: `inner_search`)

The Shelah reads the laws of the search for leaven as an inner practice (lines 3943-3952). "Let a person search his deeds and check the leaven that shows, and also the hidden leaven" (line 3946), by the light of a single candle, that is, for G-d alone. "Search every hole and crack, for the Holy One tests hearts": the wrongs we have trodden so often we forgot them, and the wrongs between people, forgiving what cannot be found (line 3947). "The purses need the most searching, for most wrongs are in money" (line 3948). Check your study and prayer too (line 3949). Mixed leaven is a good deed mixed with ego, "done for show, or without intent" (line 3950); hardened leaven is mere habit (line 3951). *Tie:* `cheshbon`, S77. *Ours:* a yearly deep search, and a small one any night.

## 3.2 Four rungs of service

From lowest to highest (lines 3657-3663): serving for the reward of the world to come (allowed, "ordinary things made in the purity of the holy", line 3661); inner fear, keeping His word; inner love, the son who serves with joy; and for the few, innermost awe, the servant who is a chariot. "The nearer one comes, the more ready he is for service" (line 3664): the High Priest bows at every blessing, the king throughout. *Tie:* S83, S43. *Ours:* a map for the guide to show a person where they stand, without shame for the lower rungs.

## 3.3 Tell His wonders (proposed: `tell_wonders`)

"By telling His wonders a cleaving is made between Him and us, and the darkness and cloud that divide Him from us are lifted" (line 4174). From the Zohar (line 4172): one who tells of the Exodus with joy, G-d gathers His household to hear, and "a person must always tell before the Holy One all the miracles He did". And tell your faults one by one before the accuser does. *Tie:* `own_words`, `lips`. *Ours:* a daily spoken telling of the good one has seen, as a way into closeness.

## 3.4 Every day as leaving Egypt

"We remember leaving Egypt every day, as if we left today" (line 3700), and each day's holiness hangs on that going out. *Tie:* S65, S78.

## 3.5 Kindness done as He does it

"Whoever wants to cleave to the Presence, let him learn from the ways of his Creator" (line 3460): clothe the naked, visit the sick, comfort mourners, bury the dead. Three rungs: gladdening a couple (maybe for return), visiting the sick (maybe no return), burial (no return, the true kindness), and the rule: no thought of repayment (line 3473). Charity with a kind face (line 3473). "No day without some act of kindness", in body, money, or by teaching (lines 3533, 4262). Guests: feed them at once, a bright face, "even if there is worry in his heart, let him put it aside" and do not tell them your troubles (line 3522); the best bed; walk them out with food for the road (lines 3523-3524). At the sickbed, sit wrapped as in shul, "for the Presence is above the head of the sick" (line 3478). *Tie:* `act`, S86.

## 3.6 Say the aim, do it glad

"Before each mitzvah... and so all year with every mitzvah let him say [for the unity...], and then do the mitzvah in joy and a good heart" (line 4164; cf. line 3390). *Tie:* `lips`, S53.

## 3.7 The seder meal before the King

"Let him and his household sit as those who eat before the King", with no idle talk (line 4221); the meal in the middle of Hallel "joins the physical and refines it to cleave to the spiritual". The seder night: husband and wife as king and queen, children as princes, "not parted from cleaving to G-d for a moment" (line 3416). Once-a-year mitzvot: "honor them, love them, cleave to them" (line 3419). *Ours:* the guide does not carry the four cups of wine; it keeps the posture of the meal.

## 3.8 Remember death, gently (proposed: `death_in_hand`)

"Let a person always think that his life and health are all in G-d's hand... let the time of death be always a reminder" (line 3536). The confession of the sick: "my healing is in Your hand and my death is in Your hand" (line 3486), said early, while the mind is clear (line 3481): "many confessed and did not die". *Ours:* the guide uses this for clarity and return, never to frighten; it does not give medical or end-of-life advice.

## 3.9 Contentment

Psalm 23 read against the love of money (lines 4050-4056): "neither poverty nor riches"; the grasping man always lacks; "even one kindness... surrounds him as if he were full of all good" (line 4052); "I will fear no evil, for You are with me", since the Presence is lowly with us in trouble (line 4055). "Let him be glad in his portion even if his food is scant, for it is in G-d's hands" (line 4030). *Tie:* S84.

## 4. The big human questions

- **Why the world?** He needs nothing; He made it to give good (lines 3560, 4102).
- **Why suffering?** The Shelah gives four reasons (lines 4002-4007); the one the guide keeps is "I may be wrong": "I did what is good in Your eyes, not good in my eyes, for I may be wrong" (line 4003), and "what you think is judgment is mercy" (line 4245). Exile in Egypt was "good news" (line 3902). *Ours:* never offered to a person in fresh pain as an answer.
- **Death.** In His hand; accepted in love (lines 3486-3490); "precious in G-d's eyes is the death of His devoted" (line 4250).
- **Money.** Most wrongs are in money (line 3948); contentment is wealth (line 4052).
- **Worth.** A broken heart is not a flaw: an afflicted cantor is fine, "he is more humble" (line 3961). The fiercest pull comes at the holiest souls (line 4214).
- **What is reward?** The mitzvah itself, its spirit, not a payment by agreement (lines 3833, 3575).

## 5. With Chabad (kept)

- **Renewal and "none else".** Line 3938 is the thesis of Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-3 in full: continuous renewal, the world would return to nothing if the flow stopped, and "none else" means no other reality, not merely no other god. The Shelah presents it as "the truth of faith". This is the strongest agreement found so far.
- **Havayah and Elokim.** Lines 4071-4075 match Shaar HaYichud ch. 4-6: Elokim is nature and hides; Havayah gives being; they are one.
- **Tzimtzum.** No full tzimtzum teaching here, but "if He hid it, all would be lost" (line 3938) and "the Presence dresses in the husks" (earlier, line 3340) fit Chabad's reading that the hiding is itself held in being by Him.
- **The Rambam's negative attributes.** The Shelah keeps the Rambam's rule that we know Him only by His acts (lines 4026, 4110, 4180) but, following Pardes, reverses him on the body words: "eye" is the true name of His providence, and our eye is called eye by borrowing (line 3827). Chabad takes the same Kabbalistic line (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud 9-10).
- **The dwelling below.** Line 4079 is the midrash the Tanya builds ch. 36 on. The Shelah uses it as history (the Presence brought down); Chabad makes it the purpose of each day's work.
- **The Ramak.** "All is hewn from one source" and "without this there would be two" (lines 3610, 4113) sit with the Ramak's "He is all that exists"; the guard "not joined in essence as with the sefirot" (line 4226) and "not true partnership, heaven forbid" (line 3795) keep "not all that exists is He".
- **A nuance.** Line 3649 says He is King with no need of a people; Chabad (Shaar HaYichud 7; Likkutei Torah) stresses "there is no king without a people" for the level of Malchut. Both hold both; the Shelah stresses the essence, Chabad the revealed kingship. And line 4118 ("a cause cannot be called cause without an effect") gives the Shelah's own form of the Chabad side.
- **Not carried.** The lines that call the nations husks and Israel the fruit (4104, 4186, 4233-4236), the exclusion of non-Jews from tzara'at (3876), fasting on the eve of Rosh Chodesh (4260), the four cups and wine meanings, the halachic details of chametz, matzah baking and the seder, the kavanot of Names, and the Ari's material on Adam's sparks are read and not carried into the guide.

## 6. What the guide should take (ours)

1. **"There is no other reality but His"** with continuous renewal (line 3938): the book's central unity line.
2. **The inner search for leaven** (lines 3946-3952).
3. **Tell His wonders aloud** to lift the cloud (line 4174).
4. **Kindness for its own sake, one every day** (lines 3473, 3533).
5. **Contentment: one kindness is enough to be full** (line 4052).
6. **The four rungs of service** as a map without shame (lines 3657-3664).
7. **The cord of merits** and **the struggle as a sign** (lines 4214-4215).
8. **"I may be wrong about what is bad"** (line 4003), offered with care.

## 7. Thin places

- The sermons (3622-4153) are long and often turn on number-play and Names; the plain teachings above were drawn out of them. - Lines 3374-3458 and 4264 onward are law; the deathbed prayers (3485-3558) are liturgy and were read, not analyzed. - Much of Torah Ohr is quoted from Toldot Yaakov, Avodat HaKodesh, Pardes, the Ari's circle and Tomer Devorah; the Shelah's own voice is clearest at 3401-3419, 3469-3473, 3560, 3657-3664, 3700, 3938-3941, 3943-3952, 4050-4056, 4110, 4174, 4215.
