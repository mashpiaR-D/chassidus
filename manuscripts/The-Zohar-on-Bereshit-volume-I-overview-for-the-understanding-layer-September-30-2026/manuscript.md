## 1. Short answer

Volume I of the Zohar teaches that God is one "without number and without partnership," that everything that seems many (the levels, the lights, the names, the worlds, even the dark and the shells) is His garment and His work, and that the way into this knowing is through the heart and the body at prayer: a thought thrown up to Ein Sof, a flame bound to its coal, a Shema that crowns Him on every side, a whisper, tears, the Shechinah carried on the road. Its unity is not cold. It is the unity of a flame and its wick, a spring and its well, a body and its limbs, a King and a Queen who long for each other. It adds to the guide a large set of felt states, many of them about pain (the broken near the Shechinah, sufferings of love, light little by little, the limp after the night) and about death (the prince brought home), and a set of daily practices that Chabad later took up almost word for word: rise at midnight, bless God first, pray with the many, forgive before sleep, account for the day at night, unite the Name with mouth, heart and soul.

## 2. The core unity teaching

The volume says the same thing in many voices. In order from the plainest to the deepest:

**There is no other.** «בגין דלית אלהא אחרא בר קודשא בריך הוא בלחודוי» (Introduction 15:1; line 151): "for there is no other god besides the Holy One alone." And in the Midrash HaNe'elam, in plain Hebrew: «שהוא אדון על הכל, בבת אחת וברגע אחד הוא מניע לכל וכלם כאין נגדו. רבי יצחק אומר על שתים אותיות משמו להורות שהוא הכל ואין אחר בלתו» (Chayei Sara 19:13; line 2728): "for He is Lord over all; at once and in one instant He moves everything, and all are as nothing before Him ... to teach that He is all and there is none other beside Him." The Ra'aya Mehemna in the Addenda adds that He is not less present anywhere: «ועלת כל כלא דאיהו אדון על כלא ולית אלהא בר מניה ולא אשתכח בכל עלאין ותתאין פחות מיניה» (Addenda, Volume I 4:3; line 5302): "and the Cause of all, who is Lord over all, and there is no God besides Him, and He is not found less in any of the upper and lower beings."

**One without number, beyond grasp.** «איהו חד בלא חושבן ולא שתוף» (Bereshit 15:4; line 431): "He is One without number and without partnership." «אי"ן סו"ף לית ביה רשומא כלל ולא תליא שאלתא ביה ולא רעיונא לאסתכלותא דמחשבה כלל» (Bereshit 12:1; line 397): "Ein Sof has no trace at all; no question reaches it, no idea for the gaze of thought." Its will is «רעותא דלא אתפס לעלמין» (Bereshit 70:7; line 885), "a will never grasped", and yet at the end of true prayer «וכדין כלא רעותא חדא עד אין סוף» (same line): "then all is one will, up to Ein Sof."

**All the levels are one chain, bound to Ayin.** The volume's strongest sentence: «וכד תסתכל בדרגין, הוא מחשבה, הוא בינה, הוא קול, הוא דבור, וכלא חד … ולא הוי פרוד, אלא כלא חד, וקשורא חד, דאיהו מחשבה ממש אתקשר באין, ולא אתפרש לעלמין, ודא הוא (זכריה י״ד:ט׳) ה' אחד ושמו אחד» (Vayechi 74:2; line 5177): "when you look at the levels, it is thought, it is understanding, it is voice, it is speech, and all is one ... there is no separation, but all is one and one bond; for thought itself is bound to Ayin (Nothing) and never parts from it; and this is 'Havayah is one and His name is one.'" Noach says the same of creation: «אשתמע דכלא איהו ההיא מחשבה סתימא דהות לגו וכלא חד» (Noach 41:9; line 1521): "it is heard that all is that hidden thought that was within, and all is one."

**The giver and the receiver are one.** «שמשא בסיהרא, חדא אינון בלא פירודא, וסיהרא אף על גב דאיהו תחות שמשא, כלא איהו שמשא, בלא פירודא» (Vayetzei 2:4; line 3061): "the sun in the moon are one, with no separation; and the moon, though it is below the sun, all of it is sun." «ולית לאפרשא בין מקורא ובירא וכלא חד» (Toldot 14:5; line 2947): "there is no separating between the source and the well, and all is one." «הוא ואינון חד הוא, ולית בהו פרישו» (Vayechi 72:2; line 5149): "He and they are one, and there is no separation in them."

**Hidden and revealed, one.** «ומעלמא דא עד עלמא דא, כלא חד» (Vayetzei 24:8; line 3290): "from this world to that world, all is one." «מאן דידע שמיה על בורייה, ידע דהוא ושמיה חד הוא» (Chayei Sara 19:23; line 2738): "whoever knows His name truly knows that He and His name are one." And the two unities of the Shema, the Tanya's text: «ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד יחודא דלתתא. יחודא עלאה … שמע ישראל יהו"ה אלהינו יהו"ה אחד» (Bereshit 7:7; line 332).

**Even the dark and the shells are His work.** «דהא לית נהורא אלא בחשוכא ולית חשוכא אלא בנהורא. ואף על גב דאנון חד אתפרשן בגוונין ועם כל דא אנון חד» (Bereshit 36:10; line 617): "there is no light except in darkness and no darkness except in light; and though they are one, they are distinguished in colors, and with all that they are one." Kernel and shell: «כלהו איהו דא לבושא לדא, ודא לדא» (Bereshit 9:9; line 366), "all of it is this a garment for that," «וכלא איהו תקונא דעלמא» (Bereshit 9:12; line 369), "and all of it is the repair of the world." And «מכאן שאין הקדוש ברוך הוא עושה רעה לשום אדם, אלא כשאינו משגיח בו הוא כלה מאליו» (Vayera 27:13; line 2454): "the Holy One does no evil to any person; when He does not watch over him, he perishes of himself."

**Knowing it is the work.** «וכד ינדע בר נש דכלא חד ולא ישוי פירודא, אפילו ההיא סטרא אחרא יסתלק מעל עלמא» (Introduction 24:4; line 211): "when a person knows that all is one and makes no separation, even the other side will depart from the world." «אור בחבור דאתוון. מות בפירודא» (Introduction 24:5; line 212): "light in the joining of the letters, death in separation." And the heart that clings to the One is made one: God wanted Adam «בגין דישתכח יחידאי, ובלבא יחידאי, ובאתר דדבקותא יחידאי, דלא אשתני, ולא מתהפך לעלמין» (Vayechi 28:6; line 4645): "to be single, with a single heart, in a single place of cleaving that does not change and never turns over."

**Known as far as each heart can hold.** «דאיהו אתידע ואתדבק לפום מה דמשער בלביה כל חד כמה דיכיל לאדבקא ברוחא דחכמתא» (Vayera 9:3; line 2207): "He is known and cleaved to according to what each one measures in his heart, as far as he can cleave by the spirit of wisdom." Ours: the Zohar's unity is never only a doctrine; it is known in the measure of the heart, and it is felt most in the flame: «מאן דבעי למנדע חכמתא דיחודא קדישא. יסתכל בשלהובא דסלקא מגו גחלתא. או מגו בוצינא דדליק» (Bereshit 86:3; line 990): "whoever wants to know the wisdom of the holy unity, let him look at the flame that rises from a coal or a burning lamp."

## 3. States and meditations added

The seventeen reports propose 32 states and 40 techniques (listed in the JSON below). They group into seven families (ours):

1. **The edge of knowing.** Mah: searched to the end; the seeker answered; grasping without knowing; longing that is praise; known in my own gate; seeing in the heart. With techniques: the thrown stone, the flame, the lamp of the soul, thought to speech one, still to learn.
2. **Prayer's body and voice.** The palaces with the body; taking the fathers with you; the first small arousal; praise, spell it out, then the heart; praise anyway; the unheard voice; silent, shouted, wept; flame on the coal; crowning in the One; giving the soul in the Shema; good thought upward; among the many.
3. **Trust.** Leaning, not naming; the knot before the pit; before the road; ask before you eat; pray, then let go into Torah; glad in all that comes; waiting for His kindness; held after cleaving.
4. **Pain held inside the Presence.** Broken with her; nothing of my own; needle's-eye light; the limp after; the driven sea; gone down and not caught; replanted; cup of blessing; mourn with her.
5. **Return.** Near in one moment; enemy made servant; remembering the forgiven sin; search, then ask; call the day back; the last day; days drawn near without shame.
6. **Night and day.** The half night; living to the Living; handing over the deposit; crown, then mercy, then sleep; no one goes to bed unforgiven; under the sukkah of peace.
7. **The other person.** Pleading for another; praying for the one who hurts; the poor man as a gift; speak well of His children; draw him into Torah; the teacher's face; the dispute of love; face to face after parting; the one who holds.

And one state about death: **brought home** (the prince in the village, lines 5152-5155).

Overlaps with Part 2 (ours): "ask before you eat" = ZT2-04 (the daily asking); "the unheard voice" = ZT2-07 (whispered prayer); "the half night" = ZT2-09 (rising at midnight); "before the road" = ZT2-06 (setting out with Him). The two volumes teach these independently; the guide should merge them.

## 4. Where it agrees and differs with Chabad

**Agreement, often word for word (the Zohar as source).**

- "He is all and there is none beside Him" (line 2728), "all are as nothing before Him" (same line), "no God besides Him, not less in any" (line 5302): the ground of Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah and Tanya ch. 20-21, 48 (memory). - The two unities of the Shema (line 332) are the text Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 builds on (memory). - "One, not in the count" (line 431); "all lights darken before Him" (line 436): the Chabad "echad ve'lo bechushban." - Thought, speech and action as garments; thought, voice and speech one and bound to Ayin (lines 1521, 5177): the frame of Tanya ch. 4 and of the letters that never leave their Source (Shaar HaYichud ch. 1-2; memory). - Love and awe as the two wings that raise Torah and mitzvah (line 455; Tanya ch. 40-41, memory); a mitzvah without thought does not rise (line 5632). - Joy: "no service except from joy" (line 4525); the Shechinah does not rest in sadness (lines 3748, 4524); broken heart distinct from sadness (lines 3759, 4201). The Tanya's split between merirut and atzvut (ch. 26, 31; memory) answers the Zohar's own question at line 3748. - The awakening from below draws the awakening from above (lines 3413, 4391, 4940); mitzvot as garments and a "dwelling below" (Tanya ch. 35-37; memory). - The practices: midnight rising, the bedtime Shema and forgiveness, blessing God first, the Shema as self-offering (line 2628), praying with the congregation, guarding thought, the daily accounting (line 3969).

**Where it differs (kept as differences).**

- **No tzimtzum as a doctrine.** Volume I has the hidden point, the "lamp of darkness" (botzina dekardinuta) that sparks from the dark (line 3572), the light that does not shine (line 3238), the hidden first light (lines 4237-4238, 5579-5585). It never teaches the Lurianic withdrawal, so it does not raise the question the Gate of Unity answers (tzimtzum not literal). Ours: the Zohar's pictures of hiding fit the Chabad reading well (the hiding is His, lines 366, 369, 617), but they are not the same claim.
- **Unity as unfinished work.** «כל זמנא דכנסת ישראל בגלותא, שמא קדישא לאו שלים הוא» (Vayetzei 20:7; line 3204): "as long as the Assembly of Israel is in exile, the holy Name is not whole"; the King "without the Queen is not a king" (line 5400). Chabad says the same of the revelation (the Shechinah in exile) but insists nothing changes in Him ("I Havayah have not changed," Shaar HaYichud ch. 7; memory). The guide should keep both: He is one; the showing of it is our work.
- **The other side as a real power.** The Zohar speaks of the serpent, Samael, the harlot, the black rooster, the shells, with a vividness that can sound dualist. Its own lines hold it inside the unity (lines 211, 369, 2454, 4407: "I am He and no other"), as Tanya ch. 6 and 22 do (memory), but the guide should never teach the other side as a rival god.
- **Suffering.** The Zohar gives many answers side by side (love that breaks the body, the hour of birth, cleansing, the wicked paid here), and it says outright that the Shechinah dwells in the broken (line 3759). Chabad tends to one answer (all is good, hidden good). Ours: keep the Zohar's plurality; never use "sufferings of love" to explain someone else's pain.
- **Israel and the nations; male and female; the covenant.** Much of the volume speaks of Israel against the nations, of sefirot as male and female, and of the covenant of the flesh. The Chabad works translate these into inner terms more than the Zohar does. The guide (D28, universal) reads "Israel" as the one who clings to the good (as line 4272 itself narrows it to "the pure of heart") and leaves out passages against other peoples, anatomical sefirot, and sexual prohibitions except where a line teaches something about the heart.
- **The body.** The Zohar honors the body more than the Tanya's first chapters seem to: body and soul are "lovers and friends" (line 2872); the soul mourns its body seven days (lines 4579, 4754); the same body will rise and learn what it could not (lines 3843-3844). The guide should keep this warmth.
- **Death.** The Zohar tells much about death (the herald, thirty days, relatives who come, the added spirit, the seven days, the year, the prince brought home). Chabad works say less. Ours: use it for comfort, gently; never as threat.

## 5. What the guide should take (ours)

1. **For the unity itself:** lines 431, 2728, 5177, 3061, 2947, 5302. Short, plain, and each gives a picture: One without number; He is all; thought to speech bound to Ayin; all of it is sun; source and well; not less in any.
2. **For the Shema:** the two unities (line 332); giving the soul (line 2628); flame on the coal (line 5086); crowning in the One (lines 5401-5406).
3. **For prayer:** the thrown stone (lines 461-462); the palaces with the body (lines 825-878); praise then request, and the heart's unspoken words (lines 3510-3515); praise anyway (line 5122); the whisper (lines 4371-4373); pray with the many (lines 4922-4923, 3486); the prayer of the poor (lines 3504-3506); bless God first (lines 4784, 5203); prayer as the ladder (lines 5630-5631).
4. **For the heart in pain:** broken with her (line 3759); wherever the righteous go, the Shechinah goes (line 3924); light little by little (lines 3532-3533); mended as before (line 3694); tears tear the decree (lines 4682, 4802); the limp after (line 3538).
5. **For trust:** leaning, not naming (line 2961); do not lean on the butler (line 4011); rejoice in all that comes (line 4059); do not watch your prayer (lines 4213-4215).
6. **For the day and the night:** the nightly accounting (line 3969); crown, then mercy, then sleep (line 3805); forgive before sleep (line 4192); the half night and "living to the Living" (lines 4312, 4322, 4874); return each day as if the last (line 4617).
7. **For the other person:** speak well of His children (line 5363); pray for the one who hurts (line 2254); the poor man as a gift (line 2221); rich and poor so that each can merit through the other (line 4336); "if there is one, I am he" (line 5378).
8. **For death and grief:** the prince brought home (lines 5152-5155); Jacob did not die (line 5215); the soul mourns its body and rises (lines 4579-4583).
9. **Leave out:** physiognomy and palm reading, star and dream omens, sorcery, letter-number mechanics, the detailed mapping of offerings to sefirot, passages against other peoples, and threats about the unrepentant as teaching (keep them only as the Zohar's own weight behind return).

## Notes and gaps (kept honest)

- **A later insert.** Lines 4409-4512 (Vayechi 1:1-15:5) are printed in the Zohar but end with the printers' note «עד כאן אינו מן הזהר» (Vayechi 15:5; line 4512), "until here it is not from the Zohar." It was read and reported; its lines are cited as "printed in the Zohar, marked as not original."
- **Mixed layers.** The Hakdamah and Bereshit contain Tosefta, Sitrei Torah and Midrash HaNe'elam passages; the Addenda contain Ra'aya Mehemna, Zohar Yashan and Bahir fragments. The reports name the layer where it matters.
- **Overlap.** The Addenda to volume I (lines 5269-5659) were included here as part of volume I. If another reader also covered them, the two readings should be merged.
- **Tanya and Chabad references** in all seventeen reports and here are from memory and were not verified against the Tanya text.
- **Verse citations** inside quotes were checked as part of the quote; free-standing English renderings of verses are not quoted Hebrew and were not run through the checker.
