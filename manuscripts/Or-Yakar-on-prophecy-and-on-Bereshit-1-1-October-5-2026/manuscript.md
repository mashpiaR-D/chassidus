Or Yakar (Precious Light) is the Ramak's huge commentary on the Zohar. Only two short portions were found in open typed form: a section on prophecy and a section on the first verse of Genesis. Both were read in full.

## 2. The unity of God in these portions

**Nothing flows without union.** "No prophecy can be drawn to a prophet except in the secret of unity" (ואי אפשר להמשך שום נבואה לנביא אלא בסוד היחוד, line 13): Ein Sof clothed in Keter, Keter in Chochmah, down to Malchut. Every gift from above comes through a joining.

**One King, many windows.** The King is one; the seers differ. Picture a house with one man inside and many windows of colored glass. "One who looks through white glass says the King is white" (והרואה בעששית לבנה אומר המלך לבן, line 40), another says green, another red. "Each looks according to his pane and hangs it on the King" (כל א' וא' צופה כפי בחינת העששית ותולה במלך, line 40). The difference is in the glass, not the King. (Ohr Ne'erav line 481 uses the same lamp image for the sefirot.)

**The Something hides the Nothing.** In the Bereshit portion: "the Something does not tell of the Nothing, it hides it" (ואין היש מגיד האין אלא מעלים; Or Yakar on Bereshit, segment 7). And Ein Sof is "a soul to Keter" (ולזה אין סוף נשמה לכתר; Or Yakar on Bereshit, segment 7), the soul inside all the vessels.

## 3. States of consciousness

- **Seeing through my own pane** (new, RK-S35). I know that what I see of God is true and also colored by who I am. "The matters were not found the same, just as souls are not the same" (לכך לא נמצאו הענינים בשוה כמו שהנשמות אינם משתוים, line 40). Some see "in fear and trembling, some calmly, like a man speaking to his friend" (לפי' מהם רואים בפחד ברתת מהם בנחת כמדבר איש לרעהו, line 37). The state: humble certainty. I trust my glimpse and do not make it the only one. Links S86 (the other held) and D28.
- **Seeing without eyes.** It is called seeing, "not that there is sight of a physical eye" (לא שתהיה שם ראיית עין גשמי, line 41), but seeing of the mind. A guard against S20 (unreal, frightened) and S18 (chasing the high).

## 4. Meditation and practice

**RK-T47. Your window, your ladder** (new). "All souls are hewn from under the Throne of Glory, so each receives according to his own soul" (וכל הנשמות הם חצובות מתחת כסא הכבוד ולכך כל א' וא' שופע לו לפי בחינת נשמתו, line 35), "and through this window he looks, by this chain he is drawn, and by this ladder he rises" (ודרך חלון זה הוא צופה ודרך שלשלת זו הוא משתלשל ודרך הסולם הזה הוא עולה, line 36). The practice: notice which way God comes to you most (awe or love, song, rebuke, study, kindness, as David sang, Solomon taught, Daniel saw the end, Job argued). Go up by that ladder first. Do not force yourself into another person's window.

**Prepare the vessel.** The prophets' preparation, as far as it fits a person today: - Be glad in your lot. Even a rich man who is busy with his money "is not called rich for prophecy, unless he adds to his wealth that he is glad in his portion" (אמנם גם העשיר אם יטריד אחר ממונו לא יקרא עשיר לנבואה אלא צריך שיצרף אל עושרו שהוא שמח בחלקו, line 4). - Joy. "They also needed joy, as in 'bring me a musician'" (וזה שהיה עוד צריך אליהם שמחה כענין קחו לי מנגן, line 6). - Seclusion. "They would go into seclusion as far as they knew how" (והיו מתבודדים כפי ידיעתם להתבודד, line 6). - Place. The prophets went out to the valley, to the river, to the Tent. - Then wait. Even with every preparation, it comes only by God's will (line 7, Baruch ben Neriah).

Links arrive, quieting, and RK-T44 (reach by love, not by force).

**Not for the guide.** The six levels of prophecy as a ladder to climb; Bilaam's prophecy from the shells; angels seen with the eyes (lines 29-33). The guide makes no claims to prophecy.

## 5. The big human questions

**Why do good people see God so differently?** Because each looks through his own window (line 40). The glass differs; the King does not. This is the Ramak's answer to religious difference inside the tradition, and the guide can carry it further (ours, D28): every honest seeker sees the one King through a real, colored pane.

**What do I need to meet God?** A clear mind, strength to bear hard things, enough to live on, gladness in what you have, joy, a quiet place, and then patience for His will (lines 4-7).

**Why can I not see the Nothing behind things?** Because the Something hides it (Bereshit segment 7). What is, covers what gives it being. That is why the soul must look through things, not at them.

## 6. Against Chabad (ours)

- **The colored windows.** Chabad teaches that each soul has its own portion in Torah and its own way of service. The Ramak's image (one King, many panes) is simpler and more generous, and makes a good guide image.
- **The Something hides the Nothing.** This is the heart of the Gate of Unity: the world looks like "something" and hides that it is nothing apart from the word that makes it (Tanya line 302, checked in the Sefer Yetzirah report). The Ramak states it in one line.
- **Joy before seeing.** "Bring me a musician" as preparation fits the Chabad rule that joy opens the heart and sadness closes it (Tanya chapters 26 and 31). Full agreement.
- **Tzimtzum.** Not in these portions.
