## The shape of the range

The range opens in the middle of *Bayom HaShmini Atzeres* and ends at the start of *Kol HaShoneh Halachos*. The maamarim are:

Between 4899 and 4900 the dates jump from Marcheshvan to Iyar. The maamarim between them are not in this file.

Across all seven maamarim there is really one argument, told from several sides. It goes like this: - Light is not a thing next to the Essence. It is the Essence revealed, and that is why it can unite with everything. - But all light, even Atzilus, even the Or Ein Sof before the tzimtzum, is still "only light". It is not the Essence itself. - The Essence is reached from below, by what a person does against his own grain: the simple servant's yoke, the deed of a mitzvah, toil over a contradiction in a halachah, standing firm in a trial, turning round the animal soul. - Torah is the channel. It is unlike every other mediator, because it joins the two essences themselves. But its Written form gives only what was already prepared. The Oral Torah, won by toil and fear, draws a truly new light from the essential hiddenness.

That is why F10 (Torah and mitzvah) and F05 (the Essence) lead the face counts by a wide margin. The cosmological faces (F01, F02, F04) are rare. They appear only where the Rashab stops to say how the worlds come to be (4929, 4960, 5071).

## Bayom HaShmini Atzeres (to 4587): the essential colour, "none besides You" and "nothing apart from You"

The range opens on a parable. White is an "essential colour". Red or green is added to a thing. White is the thing itself, as linen is white, and washing only uncovers it. So an essential colour is "ayin", not a separate something (4519, F05). The light is like this. It is not a thing apart from the Essence, only the Essence revealed: > לפי שאינו בבחי' מציאות דבר בפ"ע מן העצמות, אלא הוא בחיי גילוי העצם (line 4521)

Because it is "ayin", it can unite with every vessel, even vessels that are opposites (F09). The Name Havayah likewise joins every other Name: > להיותו בבחי' אין ובבחי' העדר המציאות, לכן הוא מתאחד עם כל המדריגות שהן הפכים זמ"ז (line 4521)

Chochmah is "white" too, and so it can be turned in many ways. But it is still some kind of existence, and that is why division and disagreement begin in Chochmah (4527). Keter has no colour at all (4537). Then comes the range's most exact statement of F05 and F08 together. "There is none besides You" (ein zulatecha) is the light. It exists only from its source, and so it feels that He alone is: > דאין זולתך קאי על האור, שמציאותו הוא רק מצד המאור (line 4538)

"Nothing apart from You" (efes biltecha) is higher. Even "with You" there is no existence at all: > אבל אפס בלתך (הוא) היינו שגם עמך ית' לא יש שום מציאות כלל (line 4538)

The Rashab ties this to Tanya ch. 35: "He alone is and there is none besides Him, and this is the level of Chochmah". So Tanya's formula names the bitul of Chochmah. The bitul of Keter is "efes", beyond it. Maimonides' "were He to cease, all would cease" is what the light *feels* (4538, F01).

The soul's side follows from Tanya ch. 19. The soul, like a flame, longs to leave the wick and be nullified in its source (4539, 4542, F06). Chochmah is that longing. Keter is the inclusion itself: > אבל בחי' הכתר הוא בחי' ההתכללות ממש באוא"ס בבחי' אין ואפס ממש, שמתבטל במציאות לגמרי (line 4542)

Keter is silence, the bitul of the yechidah. Silence is above even the "mah" of Chochmah.

Next the Rashab applies this to Torah and mitzvot (4551-4570). Torah comes from Chochmah, so it divides like the hairs of the skull, one light to each hair, and even its inner part divides. The mitzvot are the 613 "paths" of the bare skull, rooted in the essential ayin of Keter. So in the *doing* all mitzvot are equal: > דעצם עשיית המצות הוא למעי מבחי' התורה ולמעלה מכוונת המצות (line 4567)

So "Yaakov My servant", the simple servant who serves by the yoke alone, stands above the son and the faithful servant (4570, F08). The Rashab adds a warning. Atzilus is Godliness itself ("He and His life are one"), yet before the Essence it counts as "made": > מ"מ, הנה לגבי עצמות אוא"ס נחשב אצילות לבחי' עשיי (line 4559)

Shemini Atzeres is when the Essence drawn down on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur is taken in inwardly. It can be taken in only through the yoke of Heaven (4580). Circling the bimah is a deed, and a deed is greater than reading. Simchas Torah belongs to the second tablets, the Oral Torah that comes by toil (4586).

## Amar R' Akiva (4588-4689): the son who supports himself, and the three knots

This maamar holds a long parable (4593-4602). A perfect son runs his father's estate but still lives from it. That is "something from something". True perfection is to go far away, serve a craftsman, and live by one's own craft, "from nothing to something". The analogue: the souls of Atzilus are Godliness itself (4604, F06), and through Torah they become "like the One above" and light the Name Havayah even in the darkness of kelipat nogah (4610, F11). Yet all of that is still the father's: > וא"כ, כל מה שמשכיל ומתחכם בתורה ובידיעת אלקות, אין זה מכח עצמו, אלא בכח עליון שבו (line 4612)

Only toil in the Oral Torah, innovating "like something from nothing", reaches "God, except You", the essential hiddenness (4621). Yosef flourished only when he was sold as a servant (4631).

The core of the maamar is the Zohar's three knots: Israel, Torah and the Holy One (4657-4665, F10). The simple reading is a chain, each link's head tied to the tail of the one above (4658). The Rashab rejects that as the whole truth. At Sinai the transmission was "face to face", inner to inner. So the hidden of Torah and the hidden of the Holy One: > והיו לאחדים ממש, בלי השתל' וירידות מראש לסוף (line 4660)

Even the letters are in complete union with the inner light of Ein Sof. So reading them aloud binds the reader to the hidden of souls, of Torah and of the Holy One (4660). Then comes the key distinction. Every other mediator passes on only the outside of what is above it, and so it separates. Torah joins the two essences themselves: > אבל בבחי' ממוצע דתורה הוא שמחבר ב' המהותים יחד, וממילא ההתחברות הוא בעצם מהותם ממש (line 4662)

The Rebbe's footnote narrows this. Torah is that kind of mediator only between Or Ein Sof and the souls. Between Or Ein Sof and the worlds it separates, and the worlds are joined only through Israel's toil in Torah: > הרי הם פועלים החיבור בעולמות ע"ד שהוא בנש"י (line 4664)

The mikveh is read the same way. Tevilah means habitul, nullification. It is the bitul and inclusion of the revealed soul in the hidden, up to the hidden of the Holy One (4675, F08). Prayer purifies from below and Torah from above.

## Lech Lecha (4690-4782): what the Written Torah cannot do, and two refinements

The Written Torah is "clear wisdom", already clarified in primordial Chochmah. Everything that flows into the worlds, even the life of the kelipot, is measured by it (4702, F01, F03): > דכל אופני ההמשכות בעולמות בבחי' הפנימיות וחיצוניות, הכל הוא ע"פ המדה ושיעור שנמדד באור החכמה דתורה (line 4702)

But everything in it is "only light", even the hidden of the Holy One, even the light before the tzimtzum. Drawing the Essence itself needs toil (4708, F05): > אמנם המשכת בחי' עצמות א"ס, היינו בחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס ממש כו וזה נעשה דוקא ע"י עסק (line 4708)

Here the range explains F03 through study. When a sage who is sure of a matter must rule on it in practice, doubts and contradictions rise up. "Every question is from the other side." These concealments belong to the Oral Torah only. Toiling through them back to the same truth reaches the Essence: > הנה ביגיעה והעמקה זאת ה"ה מגיע לבחי' עצמות א"ס ממש, היינו בחי' ההעלם העצמי דא"ס (line 4718)

What comes of that toil is new, "creation from nothing indeed" (4725). It branches without end, and it concerns physical doing: what is permitted and can be raised (4727).

Then two refinements (F11). Direct light drives out darkness from above, because "nothing hides Or Ein Sof" (4738). Contemplation of Godliness makes the evil of the natural traits fall away by itself. Returning light works from below. The animal soul itself comes to understand "the good of Godliness" and to want it (4749, 4751). The Rashab ranks the second higher, because it completes the purpose of creation: > נשלם ע"י הבירור מלמטה למעלה דוקא, מפני שעושה את הדברים התחתונים כלים לאלקות (line 4753)

Avraham reached his root by trials. Every trial hides, and standing firm nullifies the hiding (4769). "I will make your name great" is His essential Name, drawn by that toil (4781).

## HaMechaseh (4783-4899): help from behind, and the son who became a servant

Help from above usually comes hidden. A toddler walks because his father holds him from behind (4786). The higher light works unseen while the person thinks he walks alone (4795, F04). In exile God hides entirely and still "stands behind our wall" (4814, F03). Open help is different in kind. It is the Essence lowering itself, the father lifting the child onto his shoulder: > דבאופן הא' הוא המשכת הארה לבד, לא השפלת העצמות, ולכן הוא בהעלם לבד, וכאן הוא בחי' השפלת העצמות (line 4805)

Three parables (the teacher's short way, the teacher who gives the essence of a subject, seeing the master's face) show that even with such help the result can be the student's own new idea (4815-4852). The Oral Torah is "woman". It receives from the Written Torah, yet it gives birth by its own power, because its root is higher (4859). Iggeret HaKodesh 20 is cited: the earth brings forth "something from nothing always".

The maamar ends on fear. Study done for the pleasure of the reasoning reaches only Chochmah. Study done in dread of missing the Supreme Will is the "yoke of Torah", and it reaches the essential hiddenness (4869, 4875, F08). A hard question follows: if hidden Eden is only for scholars, what of the unlearned? And the Essence is drawn only by the deed of a mitzvah, not by its intent (4887). The answer: > דבאמת גם ע"ה ואנשים פשוטים מגיעים בעבודתם לבחי' העלם העצמי דא"ס (line 4887)

They reach it by setting their own self aside under the yoke. Torah study reaches the same place only when the son becomes a servant.

## VeHayah Or HaLevanah (4900-5012): hairs, the skull, and the will that never changes

The halachot are "hairs". They are nourished from the brain (Chochmah) but grow from the skull (Keter). That is why they can descend all the way into physical doing (4915, F05, F07): > וזהו כח הירידה וההמשכה של ההלכות שירדו למטה מטה, שזהו דוקא לפי ששרש המשכתם הוא מבחי' עצמות אוא"ס (line 4915)

"The higher a thing is, the lower it can lower itself" (4917). So providence equal in heaven and on earth comes only from "who is enthroned on high" (4926).

Here the range gives its clearest F01 statement, citing Iggeret HaKodesh 20. The worlds come to be through the light, "because it is like the luminary". Malchus makes them a real "something" (4929, 4943). Beriah-Yetzirah-Asiyah are infinitely apart from Atzilus, and still: > ומ"מ, התהוות בי"ע הוא מהאלקות דאצילות (line 4960)

The sefirot, even in their source, are only "the secret of His Name". A name has no bearing on the essence of the one named (4974, F05): > שכל הע"ס הן רזא דשמי (line 4974)

The skull holds the will with no reason at all. That is the will in the mitzvot, which never changes (4999). An animal's will never changes either. So when the animal soul's will turns to good it reaches higher than the human's (4990, F11). This is the Omer: barley, animal food, waved "before Havayah", "an omer per skull" (4997). Wrestling with contradictions until each halachah stands in its place ("turn it and turn it") reaches the essential ayin of Keter. The world rises with the one who does this (5007, F07).

## Ko Amar Hashem (5013-5105): plain Elokim and living Elokim, and Torah as covenant

Prayer starts from the lowest bitul, acknowledgement. It is a creature reaching for its own root (5024, 5027). Torah comes from above. Within the refining of halachot, the Rashab sets two ways side by side: by reason and pleasure, and by the yoke. Only the yoke reaches the essential ayin (5050, F08): > אבל כאשר עסק התורה בבירור ההלכות הוא בבחי' קבעומ"ש, עי"ז דוקא מגיע לבחי' האין העצמי ממש שלמעלה מעלה מבחי' האין דע"ס (line 5050)

Then comes the range's fullest F03 and F02 passage. "These and these are the words of the *living* God." Plain Elokim hides Havayah, because otherwise there could be no worlds: > לא הי' אפשר להיות עולמות בע"ג, אלא הכל הי' בטל במציאות ממש (line 5071)

"You are Havayah alone": *Atah* is the Essence, above hiddenness and revelation. *Hu* is Or Ein Sof. "Alone" is the light included in the Essence (5071). "Living Elokim" is a contraction that is not total, "a little that holds much", like sunlight through a small hole rather than through a screen (5080, 5085). The difference turns on what the light is clothed in. A garment that is not Godliness hides the light. A garment that is Godliness does not (5096). So in the halachot before us the essence of the Chochmah of Atzilus is diminished, not hidden (5082, 5096). That is why Torah is "My covenant". It joins Or Ein Sof with the worlds, and it joins Zeir Anpin (day) with Malchus (night, plain Elokim). This fulfils the purpose of creation (F07): > דתכלית הכוונה דבריאת והתהוות העולמות הוא להיות גילוי אוא"ס בעולם, דנתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים (line 5103)

## Kol HaShoneh Halachos (5106-5139): even living Elokim is not the Essence

The range closes on a limit. Even "living Elokim" is only expansion: > דהיינו רק בחי' התפשטות לבד, ולא בחי' העצמות ממש (line 5113)

The proof comes "from my flesh". In a person, intellect and feeling change with age, so they are not the soul's essence. The soul itself does not change with the body (5119). Above the mind is the "air" under the skull, which binds the essence of the soul. It can compel, where revelations cannot. Yet even it is not the essence (5130). So too the sefirot, their hidden source, and whatever moves that source: > שכ"ז אינו בחי' עצמיות, כ"א הכל רק בחי' התפשטות הארה לבד (line 5131)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say (or says differently)

- **The Tanya's "none besides Him" is ranked, not final.** Tanya ch. 35 (gloss) makes "He alone is and there is none besides Him" the level of Chochmah. The Rashab accepts that and then puts "nothing apart from You" (efes) above it (4538). Even "with You" nothing exists; that is Keter, silence, the yechidah. The Tanya's formula of the unity becomes a middle rung.
- **Light is the Essence revealed, not an emanation apart.** The white-colour parable (4519-4521) gives a reason why one light can unite with opposite vessels. The Tanya states that it does, in Shaar HaYichud ch. 7 and Iggeret HaKodesh 20. The reason offered here is that the light adds nothing to the Essence.
- **Torah joins two essences.** Tanya ch. 5 calls the union through Torah study "a wondrous union", because the intellect grasps and is grasped. Here Torah is the mediator that alone does not separate (4662), and the three knots are inner to inner, not a chain (4658-4660). But this union is still "only light". The Essence is drawn only by toil under the yoke (4708).
- **Pleasure in study reaches less than fear.** The Tanya praises Torah study that grasps God's wisdom (ch. 5) and study with love and awe (ch. 40). Here, study moved by reason or pleasure reaches only Chochmah or its source. Study moved by fear of missing the truth, "in order to do", reaches the Essence (4869, 5050, 5062). The unlearned simple servant reaches it too (4887).
- **Refinement from below completes the dwelling.** Tanya ch. 36 names the purpose, the dwelling below. Here the Rashab says which refinement completes it: returning light, which makes the animal soul itself a vessel, not the direct light that only lifts it (4753).
- **Concealment inside Torah.** The Tanya treats the other side as outside Torah and mitzvot. Here the contradictions within the Oral Torah are themselves the concealment ("every question is from the other side", 4718), and toil through them is the way to the Essence.

## Where the tie is thin

- **Footnotes.** 337 of 621 lines are the editor's apparatus, and 325 of them are pure references.
- **Long parables.** The perfect son (4593-4602), the two ways of teaching and seeing the master (4815-4852), the small and great sage (4949-4958), and the silver refining (4754-4758) build the argument but state no unity until their analogue. They are marked structural, most with no face.
- **Kabbalistic anatomy.** Hairs, skull, the new Netzach-Hod-Yesod, the growth of Zeir Anpin, the air under the skull (4912-4913, 4945-4948, 4975-4980, 5130) are structural. The tie arrives only where the Rashab names the Essence or the Name.
- **No readings.** Every tie in the range is the text's own or structural. The Tanya comparisons above are ours; they are kept here and out of the `how` fields.
- **OCR damage.** Page headers are fused into text lines (4526, 4542, 4559, 4579, 4612, 4621, 4665, 4706, 4725, 4767, 4859, 4926, 5130). Stray "י" marks, asterisks and numerals sit inside words ("וכידועפ", "בח"", "הי""). A double quotation mark stands for an apostrophe at 4658 ("בבחי" פנים"). Quotes keep all of these, and some quotes were shortened to avoid the worst runs.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Is the Oral Torah's innovation truly "from nothing"?** The text says the matters come "as true creation from nothing" (4725) and "truly new light" (4722). The Rebbe's footnote says they are only "like something from nothing", since they follow the rules by which Torah is expounded and the veteran student's insight was already given at Sinai (4622-4629).
- **"We have no real creation from nothing."** The Rashab cites Iggeret HaKodesh 20 on the earth bringing forth "something from nothing always" (4859). In the same line he adds that in truth we have no real creation from nothing, only its likeness.
- **Where does Torah join essences?** The text says Torah joins "the two essences" (4662) and is the covenant joining Or Ein Sof with the worlds (5103). The Rebbe's footnote: between Or Ein Sof and the worlds Torah is a separating mediator, and only Israel's toil effects the joining (4663-4664).
- **Deed or study?** The earlier rule is that the Essence is drawn only by the deed of mitzvot, not their intent (4887, citing Toldos Noach). Yet this range says Oral Torah study reaches the Essence. The Rashab resolves it through the yoke (study as a servant). He also concedes that the unlearned reach the Essence without study.
- **Two statements reconciled, not erased.** One earlier maamar says refining halachot reaches the hidden power of Chochmah. The second Lech Lecha says it reaches above that power. The Rashab reconciles them by motive, reason against yoke (5053-5060).
- **Atzilus: Godliness or "made"?** Atzilus is "Godliness itself" (4559, 4960) and also, before the Essence, "Asiyah" (4559) and "only expansion" (5113-5114). Both are said, from different vantage points.
