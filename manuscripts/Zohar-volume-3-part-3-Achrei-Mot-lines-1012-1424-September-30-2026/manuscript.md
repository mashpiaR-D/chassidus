## The shape of the range

Achrei Mot is the parasha of Yom Kippur, and the Zohar reads it through one story: Rabbi Shimon and the companions walk to Rabbi Pinchas ben Yair, and on the way and at his table they open the service of the day. The high priest's entry into the Holy of Holies becomes a teaching about uniting the Name, about times of favor, about the scapegoat that turns the accuser into a defender, and about teshuvah as the depth from which one calls. Then the forbidden unions of Leviticus 18 are read, one by one, as the exile of the Shechinah: the King without the Matrona, the house destroyed, the wife who is never divorced.

- 1012-1068: fear first, then joy; the death of Nadav and Avihu as atonement; the incense and the cherubim. - 1069-1121: the journey to Rabbi Pinchas; the companions' love; souls engraved above before birth. - 1122-1150: the two goats; the accuser who becomes a defender (with the Raya Mehemna). - 1151-1212: praise at the hours of the day; Ehyeh; the high priest's steps and prayer. - 1213-1287: "my soul longs for You at night"; the love of the whole soul; the three dwellings of the soul after death; the living who go to the graves. - 1288-1424: Tamar and the hidden-and-revealed Torah; the forbidden unions as exile; the man with the mark on his face; the One who is "not now One".

## The unity of G-d

**"Is He not One now? No."** The plainest statement in the volume of the Zohar's second "One":

> ביום ההוא יהיה יי' אחד וגו'. ואי תימא השתא לאו הוא אחד. לא, דהא השתא חייבי עלמא גרמו, דלא אשתכח חד (Zohar, Achrei Mot 63:1; line 1384)
> "On that day the Lord will be One." And if you say: now He is not One? No: for now the wicked of the world have caused it, that He is not found one.

The Matrona is far from the King; the Upper Mother withholds her streams; "as it were, He is not found one" (כביכול לא אשתכח חד, line 1385). When she returns to the palace "all will be joined as one without separation" (1386). And the letters of the Name are one knot: "yud is tied to heh, heh to vav, vav to heh, and heh is tied to all; all is one knot and one word; they never part. As it were, whoever causes separation, it is as if he destroyed the world" (1383).

**He is them, as the flame is held in the coal.** The ten crowns are His, and they are not other than He:

> קודשא בריך הוא, אפיק עשר כתרין ... דאתעטר בהו, ומתלבש בהו, והוא אינון, ואינון הוא, כשלהובא דאחידא בגומרא, ולית תמן פרודא (Achrei Mot 41:6; line 1259)
> The Holy One brought out ten crowns ... He is crowned with them and clothed in them. He is they, and they are He, like a flame held in a coal, and there is no separation there.

*Ours.* This is the Zohar's great image of the sefirot, the one the Tanya and the whole Chabad literature carry ("He and His lifeforce are one", Tanya ch. 2 and Iggeret HaKodesh 20, with the flame and coal from Sefer Yetzirah). Here it is said of the crowns above, next to a list of ten impure crowns below that hang on "the dirt of the nail" of Wisdom (1259). The unity is not flat: there is a side that is not He, and it lives on scraps.

**Ehyeh: the whole, hidden, then the detail.** Rabbi Shimon teaches Rabbi Elazar the Name at the burning bush. "Ehyeh is the whole of all, when the paths are hidden and not yet parted and are all included in one place: then it is called Ehyeh, the whole of all, hidden and not revealed" (1176). "Asher Ehyeh" is the Mother who has conceived and is ready to bring out the details. Then comes YHVH, "the detail and the existence" (1180). "And in that hour Moses knew the secret of the holy Name, hidden and revealed, and he cleaved as no one else in the world cleaved" (1180). The tip of the yud "points to Ayin", the Nothing (קוצא דיו"ד דלעילא, רמיזא לאין, line 1182).

**Hidden and revealed, all the way down.** Three levels are bound one to another: the Holy One, the Torah and Israel, and "each is level above level, hidden and revealed" (1310). "There is no word in the Torah in which the upper holy Name, hidden and revealed, is not marked" (לית לך מלה באורייתא, דלא רשים בה שמא קדישא עלאה, דסתים וגליא, line 1299). The first letter a child learns, alef, is a thing "no one can cleave to with understanding or raise with the will, nor speak with the mouth; even the highest angels cannot", for the worlds hang on its tip (1314).

**Above waits on below.** "As it is done below, it is done above ... the priest above is not found above until he is found below, and from below the holiness of the upper King begins to rise, and all the worlds are one before the Holy One" (1187). "Joys do not enter before Him, and service is not done before Him above, until Israel do it below" (1189).

**The Name is one, and He is His Name.** "I and My Name are one" (דהא אנא ושמי חד הוא, line 1052). The priest must not come in "at any time" but "with zot", the time that is bound to the Name.

## States of consciousness

**Weeping in one side of the heart and joy in the other.** When Rabbi Shimon opened the secret of the destroyed house, "Rabbi Shimon wept and Rabbi Elazar wept", and Rabbi Elazar said:

> בכייה תקיעא בלבאי מסטרא חדא, וחדוותא בלבאי מסטרא אחרא. דהא שמענא מלין, דלא שמענא עד השתא (Achrei Mot 54:8; line 1346)
> Weeping is lodged in my heart on one side, and joy in my heart on the other, for I have heard words I have not heard until now.

This is S49 (weeping and joy in one heart) in the Zohar's own voice, and it gives the cause of each: grief for the Presence, joy at the truth heard.

**The King looking at His house.** "The vav came back down to its place, looked at His house and it was destroyed; sought the Matrona and she had gone far; saw His palace and it was burned; sought the people and they were exiled ... then the Lord God of Hosts called on that day to weeping" (1345). A state for the guide's grief work: not "I have lost", but seeing the loss with Him, as He sees it.

**Wife, never divorced.** In exile the Shechinah is called "your father's wife", not "your mother", because she is far from Him. But: "she is the wife of the holy King, not sent away with a writ of divorce ... though she is far from the King, the King's watching is on her always" (אשגחותא דמלכא בה תדירא, line 1349). *Ours:* this is a strong line for S12 (the flat world) and S68 (the hiding seen as His): the distance is real, the bond is not broken, and His eye has not left.

**He is with them in exile.** "When Israel are in exile, as it were, the Holy One is with them in exile, for the Shechinah never departs from them" (1193). "The Lord your God walks in the midst of your camp: this is the Shechinah, found among Israel, all the more in exile, to shield them always" (1350). Enemies can harm only when Israel weaken her strength; when they return, she breaks theirs (1351).

**Longing at night: "You are my soul."** "The Holy One is the spirit and soul of all, and Israel say: You are my soul and my spirit; therefore I long for You, to cleave to You, and I seek You, to find Your will" (1213). This is S41-S43 given its object: the longing is for the soul of one's soul.

**The one who delights in Torah fears nothing.** "Whoever delights in Torah does not fear anything" (1217). Rabbi Shimon is "free", "raises his head to reveal and does not fear" (1418); "he fears nothing from above or below" (1078). *Ours:* S84 (trust) as boldness.

**Fear first, then joy.** "All service a person would do before his Master needs fear first ... and from the fear of his Master he will come afterward to do the commandments in joy" (1013). Rejoicing much in the things of this world is forbidden; in Torah and mitzvot one must rejoice (1014). Fear "is the beginning, from below upward" (1015). This is the order of S45 (lower awe) before S53 (glad), and it matches the Tanya's placing of fear as the start of service.

**The companions' love after the argument.** "At first they look like warriors who want to kill each other; afterward they return in brotherly love", and "gam" (also) includes the Shechinah with them, and He listens and delights (1076). *Ours:* S86 (the other held) inside a learning partnership. The fight over Torah is not a failure of love.

## Meditation and practice

**The high priest's steps: close the eyes and bind above.** The fullest account of an inner entry in this range:

> נטיל ג' פסיען אחרן ... אסחר לדוכתיה. נטיל ג' פסיען, אסתים עיינין, ואתקשר לעילא (Achrei Mot 32:6; line 1210)
> He took three more steps ... and turned to his place. He took three steps, closed his eyes, and bound himself above.

He hears the cherubim's wings singing; he burns the incense and the wings fall still, "and cleave in a whisper". Then, if he is worthy, "a will of light goes out, fragrant with the pure balsam mountains above", "the scent enters his nostrils, and the heart is settled" (ואתיישבא לבא, 1211), "all is in a whisper", and "the priest opened his mouth in prayer, with will and with joy" (1211). *Ours:* this is a whole sequence: steps, closed eyes, binding above, silence, the settled heart, and only then prayer. It matches `before_whom`, `quieting` and `hold` in the guide, and it adds the order: the heart settles first, the words come after. *Proposed technique: the priest's entry.*

**Pray from the depth.** "Before the Holy One created the world He created teshuvah", and "every hour teshuvah stands ready for people" (1255). When is a person cleansed? "When he returns before the upper King and prays from the depth of the heart: from the depths I called You" (1256). And more:

> ממעמקים קראתיך יי', אתר גניז הוא לעילא ... וההוא עמיקא דעמיקתא אקרי תשובה. ומאן דבעי לאתבא ולאתדכאה מחובוי, בהאי עומקא אצטריך למקרי לקודשא בריך הוא (Achrei Mot 41:4; line 1257)
> "From the depths I called You, Lord": it is a hidden place above ... and that depth of depths is called teshuvah. Whoever wants to return and be cleansed of his sins must call to the Holy One from this depth.

**Love Him with the love of your soul.** "The complete service a person must do": to love the Holy One "with the very love of the nefesh, and that is complete love, the love of his soul and his spirit; as these cleave to the body and the body loves them, so a person should cleave to love the Holy One" (1227). And "from the love of the Holy One, a person must rise every night to toil in His service, until morning wakes" (1228). The world stands on such people (1228-1229).

**Listen to Torah from anyone.** "Whoever listens to words of Torah is fortunate in this world, and it is as if he received the Torah at Sinai. And one must hear words of Torah even from any person" (1240). *Ours:* a practice of humility for the guide's learning path.

**Serve the scapegoat's way: give the dog a bone.** On Yom Kippur the goat is sent to the other side; the accuser "sees the goat, desires it", returns to find Israel without sins, "rises and praises them before the Holy One", and "the accuser becomes the defender" (קטיגורא אתעביד סניגורא, 1129, 1149). *Ours:* this is not a technique for the guide but a picture of how judgment is sweetened without war: give the harsh side its small share and it turns.

**Grieve for the righteous.** "Whoever grieves over the loss of the righteous, or sheds tears for them, the Holy One proclaims over him: your iniquity is removed" (1040).

**The penitent's mirror.** A man whose face was marked by a grave sin tells how he returned: "every day I look at my face in a mirror and weep before the Holy One, Master of the worlds, over that sin, and I wash my face with those tears" (1356). Rabbi Abba reads "your iniquity is removed" over him three times, and the mark goes. *Ours:* a real and simple practice of daily return, with its guard in the same passage: it was done "from that day" of the healing of the soul, and it ended in Torah "day and night" (1357), not in endless self-punishment.

**Cast it on Him.** "Cast your burden on the Lord": "on" exactly, because sustenance hangs on the upper source (1422). *Ours:* S84 (trust) with a verse to say.

**Guards.** Times of favor and of judgment come in years, months, weeks, days and hours: "seek the Lord while He may be found"; "sometimes He is far ... sometimes near" (1050-1051). In the afternoon, when Isaac's judgment wakes, "a person must be careful in the Mincha prayer" (1158). Going to the graves of the righteous in a time of trouble is only "with teshuvah and fasting" and a broken heart (1278, 1283).

## The big human questions

**The soul before and after.** "All the souls of people, before they come down to the world, are all engraved before Him in the sky in that very image they have in this world, and all they learn in this world they knew before they came" (1106). The souls of those who will be wicked rush the hour and come down; "if he is worthy later and returns before his Master, he takes that very portion" (1107).

**The three dwellings.** The nefesh of the righteous stays in this world "to look and know and shield the generation"; the ruach dwells in the lower Eden; the neshamah in the "bundle of life". When the world needs mercy, the nefesh tells the ruach, the ruach the neshamah, and the neshamah the Holy One (1269-1275). "The tzaddik, though he departs this world, is not gone from any of the worlds, for he is found in all the worlds more than in his life" (1284).

**Suffering and the exile.** The Raya Mehemna reads the pains of the sages in exile as the birth pangs of the Shechinah, from which the Messiah is born (1222-1225). "Those who guard the Torah suffer" is not the end of the story.

**Why He is patient.** "If you knew how many hosts are held back for your sake, you would know you are not worthy to stand in the world one hour; and with all that ... I have not rejected them" (1190). He "leaves His own, and does not collect one in a hundred" (1188).

**Teshuvah and who we are.** "Nothing stands before teshuvah" (1363, 1405). "When I was far, the Holy One greeted me first with peace, so that I would be near" (1367).

**Torah as healing.** The Holy One is like the apple: "as the apple heals all, so the Holy One is healing for all", in color, scent and sweetness (1329).
