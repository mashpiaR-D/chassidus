Vayera is Abraham at the tent door: the three guests, the escort, the plea for Sodom, Lot and his daughters, Abimelech, the birth of Isaac, Hagar, and the binding. The Zohar layers in much Midrash HaNe'elam (the "hidden midrash"), which reads the same verses as the soul at death and at the resurrection, and a vision of the end. All 513 lines were read in full. ## 2. The unity of God

**Known in the gates, as each measures in his heart.** No one has ever reached His wisdom; "blessed be the glory of Havayah from His place": the lower ones say He is above, the higher ones say He is below. Yet "her husband is known in the gates": «דאיהו אתידע ואתדבק לפום מה דמשער בלביה כל חד כמה דיכיל לאדבקא ברוחא דחכמתא» (Vayera 9:3; line 2207): "for He is known and cleaved to according to what each one measures in his heart, as far as he can cleave by the spirit of wisdom." And the analogy that the Tanya will build on: «תא חזי, דהא נשמתא דבר נש לאו איהו … מאן דיכיל למנדע לה, אלא בגין אלין שייפין דגופא … בגין כך אתידע ולא אתידע. כך קודשא בריך הוא אתידע ולא אתידע בגין דאיהו נשמתא לנשמתא» (Vayera 9:5; line 2209): "Come and see: a person's soul, no one can know it, except through these limbs of the body; … so it is known and not known. So the Holy One is known and not known, for He is the soul of the soul." Ours: this is the source of the soul-and-body analogy for God's relation to the world, and of the idea that each person knows God "in the gates" of his own understanding.

**Judgement and mercy in one moment.** Even while judging the world He does not change: He sits in judgement on this one and in mercy on that one, «וכלא ברגעא חדא ובשעתא חדא» (Vayera 13:1; line 2259): "all in one instant and one hour." And He does everything at once, which no messenger can do: gives life and brings down to the grave, raises light and brings rain, feeds, helps the poor, props the fallen, raises kings and removes them, «והכל בזמן אחד וברגע אחד ובבת אחת» (Vayera 27:26; line 2467): "all at one time, in one instant, at once."

**The potter's wheel.** God turns the course of things like a potter at his wheel, making one vessel and then another; and the wheel turns by human deeds: «וטקלא קיימא בעובדין דבני נשא» (Vayera 21:9; line 2357): "and the wheel stands on the deeds of people." When they do good it turns to the right; when they do evil, to the left, until they return. «וכלא נפקא מעקרא ושרשא דלעילא» (Vayera 21:10; line 2358): "and all comes out of the root and source above." Ours: providence that answers, within one hand. Ties: S85 Seeing Him in what happens.

**He does no evil to anyone.** «מכאן שאין הקדוש ברוך הוא עושה רעה לשום אדם, אלא כשאינו משגיח בו הוא כלה מאליו» (Vayera 27:13; line 2454): "from here: the Holy One does no evil to any person; but when He does not watch over him, he perishes of himself" ("You hide Your face, they are terrified"). Ours: the Zohar's version of the Tanya's teaching that everything is sustained each moment by His attention; withdrawal, not attack, is what we call harm.

**In all their trouble He is troubled.** «בכל צרתם לא צר» is written with Alef and read with Vav: «בגין דקודשא בריך הוא עמהון בעקו» (Vayera 36:1; line 2563): "because the Holy One is with them in trouble," and even to the highest place, where there is no anger, the trouble of Israel reaches. The Shechinah is with them in every exile (line 2564).

## 3. States of consciousness

1. **The will that draws its own spirit.** From a book of the sons of the East, which Rabbi Abba warns against, a true principle: «דהא כגוונא דרעותא דבר נש איכוון ביה בהאי עלמא, הכי אמשיך עליה רוח מלעילא כגוונא דההוא רעותא דאתדבק ביה» (Vayera 5:6; line 2135): "as the will a person aims at in this world, so he draws on himself a spirit from above like the will he cleaves to." It depends «במלין ובעובדא וברעותא לאתדבקא» (Vayera 5:7; line 2136): "on words, on deed and on the will to cleave." And after death «כמה דבר נש אתמשך בהאי עלמא הכי נמי משכין ליה» (Vayera 5:10; line 2139): "as a person is drawn in this world, so he is drawn there." Ties: C3, S29. Ours: this is also a guard: what you aim at, you become.
2. **Seeing with the closed eye.** Three kinds of seeing: from afar, where the eye cannot hold what it sees; with the eye half closed, taking a little; and the "rolling of the closed eye", where the shining mirror is seen only when the eye is shut and turned (lines 2099-2101). The prophets saw in these; only Moses saw "what is not seen at all" (line 2102). Ties: S35. Ours: an image of inward seeing, not an instruction to press the eyes.
3. **Love that is seen in everything done.** «בשעתא דבר נש רחים ליה לקודשא בריך הוא. קודשא בריך הוא רחים ליה בכל מה דאיהו עביד ונטיר ארחוי» (Vayera 23:23; line 2418): "when a person loves the Holy One, the Holy One loves him in all he does and guards his ways." Ties: S60 The essential bond, S84 Trust.
4. **Not questioning at the binding.** Abraham saw Jacob "from afar" and did not reason further, «ולא בעא להרהר אבתריה דקודשא בריך הוא» (Vayera 34:4; line 2552): "and did not want to question after the Holy One." Ties: S83 Accepting the yoke, S36 Faith above knowing.
5. **The merciful father who became cruel.** «מאן … חמא אבא רחמנא דאתעביד אכזר» (Vayera 33:10; line 2547): "who ever saw a merciful father become cruel?" Abraham, all water, took in fire so that he would be whole. Ours: a state of holding a quality opposite to one's nature for His sake. Ties: C4, S10.
6. **Laughter to come.** In the future «ואזי יהיה שחוק בעולם מה שאין עכשיו» (Vayera 27:7; line 2448): "then there will be laughter in the world such as there is not now": Isaac is named for it. Ties: S53 Glad.

## 4. Meditation and practice

- **Hospitality even in pain.** Abraham, in pain from the circumcision, ran to the guests «בגין דלא למגרע (מה) דהוה עביד מקדמת דנא» (Vayera 6:3; line 2149): "so as not to lessen what he used to do." Ties: S86.
- **Escort the guest.** «כד בר נש עביד לויה … לחבריה איהו אמשיך … לשכינתא לאתחברא בהדיה ולמהך עמיה בארחא לשזבא ליה» (Vayera 10:11; line 2230): "when a person escorts his friend, he draws the Shechinah to join him and go with him on the road, to save him."
- **The poor man is a gift.** «בשעתא דקודשא בריך הוא רחים ליה לבר נש, משדר ליה דורונא, ומאן איהו, מסכנא, בגין דיזכי ביה» (Vayera 10:2; line 2221): "when the Holy One loves a person, He sends him a gift: who is it? a poor man, so that he may gain merit through him." Then a thread of kindness is drawn over him that the destroyer respects. Ties: S86. Ours: a reframing the guide can give at once.
- **Tie the knot of faith before you go down.** A man who must go down into a deep pit and fears he will not come up ties a rope above first: «עד לא ייחות תמן קשר קשרא דמהימנותא בקדמיתא לאתתקפא ביה, ולבתר נחת» (Vayera 23:16; line 2411): "before he went down there, he first tied the knot of faith to hold onto, and then he went down." Ours: a practice for anyone entering a hard place (a job, a hospital, a painful conversation): first tie the knot. Ties: S84 Trust.
- **Do not rely on miracles.** «לא ליבעי ליה לבר נש לסמכא על ניסא» (Vayera 23:2; line 2397): "a person should not rely on a miracle"; and in time of plague or danger, flee before being caught (line 2424). A guard against spiritual recklessness.
- **Say the offerings and the incense with the heart.** Whoever says in the synagogue the passages of the offerings "and sets his heart and will on them", the angels who recall his sins recall him for good (lines 2172, 2175). In a plague, gather and say the passage of the incense «ברעות נפשא ולבא» (Vayera 6:30; line 2176): "with the will of the soul and the heart." Rabbi Acha stopped a plague so, and then was told: now make them return (line 2182). Guard: the recitation does not replace return.
- **Pray for the wicked to return, never for them to die.** «מצוה לו לאדם להתפלל על הרשעים כדי שיחזרו למוטב … אסור לו לאדם להתפלל על הרשעים שיסתלקו מן העולם» (Vayera 11:24; line 2254): "it is a mitzvah to pray for the wicked that they return to good, … and forbidden to pray for the wicked that they be taken from the world": if Terah had been taken, there would have been no Abraham.
- **Guard: know the smooth-tongued scout.** The evil inclination is like the bandits' scout who meets travelers as a servant, wins their trust with sweet words and leads them to the ambush (line 2380); «הפקחין כשרואין אותו מכירים אותו» (Vayera 21:32; line 2380): "the wise, when they see him, recognize him," and take another road. Ties: technique 17 (testing the arousal).
- **Guard: food and wine.** The evil inclination is roused through eating and drinking; «צדיק אכל לשבע נפשו. ואינו משתכר לעולם» (Vayera 21:24; line 2372): "the righteous eats to satisfy his soul, and never becomes drunk." (This matches the guide's rule: never drink.)
- **Guard: do not calculate the end.** Rabbi Yosei and Rabbi Yehudah find Adam's book and begin to read the end; fire strikes their hands and it is lost; Rabbi Shimon: «לית רעותא דקודשא בריך הוא בדא דיתגלי כל כך לעלמא» (Vayera 30:22; line 2514): "it is not the Holy One's will that this be revealed so much to the world."

## 5. The big human questions

- **Death.** One does not leave the world before seeing the Shechinah (line 2084); the soul confesses what the body did (line 2085); life seen from its end is "a blink" (line 2096); the soul warns: be light as a deer to do your Creator's will (line 2098). Ours: the guide keeps these as the Zohar's view of dying and never uses them to press a person.
- **Judged only on what is.** «דהא לא דיינין ליה לבר נש על בישין דאיהו עתיד וזמין למעבד» (Chayei Sara 1:6; line 2573, read with this range): "a person is not judged for the evil he will do in the future," but he may be saved by the good he will yet do. The boy Ishmael was heard "where he is" (line 2526).
- **Why the wicked live.** God looks at every wicked person before birth to see if he will leave a righteous child, or save a Jew from a strange death, or do one good thing, and for that He brings him into the world (line 2473).
- **Joy as the heart of service.** "Because you did not serve with joy": the Zohar reads the verse as the joy of the priests, the good heart of the Levites and Israel between them (line 2489).
- **Suffering shared.** "In all their trouble He was troubled" (line 2563).
- **Rabbi Akiva at "sick with love".** When Rabbi Eliezer, dying, taught him the Song of Songs and reached «כי חולת אהבה אני», Rabbi Akiva «לא יכיל למסבל … וארים קליה בבכייתא» (Vayera 3:5; line 2090): "could not bear it, … and raised his voice in weeping." Ties: S51 The sudden cry, S44.

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **Known and not known, like the soul in the limbs (lines 2207-2209).** The analogy the guide uses for God and the world, in its Zohar source.
2. **The knot of faith before going down (line 2411).** One of the most usable images in the whole Zohar for daily life.
3. **The poor man as a gift of love (line 2221).**
4. **What you aim at draws its spirit (lines 2135-2139).**
5. **Pray for the wicked to return, not to die (line 2254).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **Technique, new: "The knot before the pit."** Before entering any hard place, stop and tie the knot: one sentence of faith said aloud ("You are with me here"), then go down (line 2411). Close: on coming out, return to the knot and thank. Ties S84.
- **Technique, new: "The poor man as a gift."** When someone in need appears, say inwardly: this is a gift sent to me by One who loves me; give as receiving (line 2221). Ties S86.
- **State, new: "Known in my own gate."** Inside: you know Him exactly as far as your heart can measure, and no further, and that is truly Him (lines 2207-2209). Enter: contemplate the soul known only through its limbs. Near S27 and S71.
- **Technique, new: "Praying for the one who hurts."** Pray that the one doing wrong return, never that he be removed (line 2254). Ties S15 Angry at someone, S86.
