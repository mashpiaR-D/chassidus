## The shape of the range

The range continues the word-by-word reading of the Shema. It has three movements.

- **Sections 70-83 (352-401): the letters of "echad" and "va'ed", and what they spell.** "Va'ed" is "echad" with letters exchanged: the higher unity carried into the lower. The aleph becomes a vav, a hidden line of the surrounding light that is equal in every world. The chet becomes an ayin, the outside of Chochmah. The dalet stays a dalet. Then come the large ayin and dalet ("ed", witness), the small shin-mem, "ashmach" ("I will rejoice"), and the tefillin. F04, F05, F02 and F09 lead.
- **Sections 84-89 (402-421): two ways of walking.** The surrounding light drawn down into the inner (Shema, Torah study, this world) and the inner raised to the surrounding (the mitzvot, the second paragraph, the World to Come). In the end the Essence makes the two one ("I will walk among you", "two statures"). F04, F05, F07, F09 and F10 lead.
- **Sections 90-95 (422-437): the mitzvah as the union.** Every mitzvah makes the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah. It does this through bitul and through the yoke, which awaken His humility and His gevurah. The range weighs study against deed, reads the destruction of the Second Temple, and sets now against the time to come. F10, F08, F05 and F11 lead.

Compared with the previous range of this book (266-351), F04 (filling and surrounding) rises sharply here. That is because the vav of "va'ed" is read as the general surrounding light, present equally below and above. F10 also rises, because the last third of the range is about Torah and mitzvot.

## Sections 70-73 (352-366): "va'ed" is "echad" with its letters exchanged

The range starts from the Zohar's "kegavna". As the sefirot unite above, Malchut unites below "in the secret of One". The higher unity of Atzilut is the source of the lower unity of Malchut (line 352):

> דבחי' יח"ע דאצי' הוא בחי' מקור ליח"ת דמל' בבריאה (line 352)

Why "in the secret of One" and not "as One"? The answer is "I am first and I am last" (Isaiah 44:6). "Ani" has the letters of "ayin", the light of Keter. Keter shines in Malchut, the end, exactly as it shines in Chochmah, the head:

> פי' אני שבכת' הוא אני שבמל' באין שינוי כלל (line 353)

So the aleph of "echad" becomes the vav of "va'ed". The vav joins above and below, "no place is empty of Him" (F04, F02). It is the general Keter, where the Essence before the first tzimtzum rests (F05):

> שבו שורה עצמות אא"ס שלפני הצמצו' הראשון (line 353)

This light is hidden. A revealed light would thin out at each step, like mind passing into feeling and then into deed. A hidden drawing comes down straight, like the shape of a vav, and so it is the same everywhere. That is why every creature, from the highest to the lowest, has bitul, not graded by rank (line 354, F08). It is also why the surrounding light is in this world as fully as in Atzilut:

> גם בעוה"ז הנפרד כמו בעולם האצי' ממש (line 354)

The soul analogy (G06) is the simple will. It is present in the physical act as fully as in the spiritual mind, because it is separate from both. A person does not feel it; he thinks his mind moves him (lines 354-355).

The chet of the higher unity becomes an ayin because Chochmah's inner light *does* lessen as it comes down. If the inner Chochmah of Atzilut were revealed in Beriah, the creatures would cease to exist (line 357, F03 + F08). Creation from nothing comes through Binah, "Elokim". Only the outside of Chochmah is hidden in the stone as its first matter (line 357, F01). The inner Chochmah holds the Essence, the true nothing, which cannot appear as a something. Yet souls, unlike angels, can receive it. In the World to Come the essential nothing is felt as a something (line 358, F06 + F09):

> שיבא בחי' האין העצם בבחי' מורגש בבחי' יש (line 358)

Sections 72-73 ground the ayin (both a letter and the word "eye") in sight. Sight is outer, but the gaze of love, "to gaze at the glory of the King", reaches the essence of the soul. When Israel looks upward, God's eyes look downward "from the pleasure of the Essence" (line 361, F12 + F05). Malchut of one line rides "the light cloud" into Egypt: the hiding of Godliness in the exile of the seventy princes (line 365, F03). Her gevurot keep the world from the outside forces, and her kindness is what lets any divine light reach the worlds at all (F11 + F09).

## Sections 74-75 (367-373): love from far, fear from near; the Torah in a garment

Section 74 brings the letters into prayer. Love grows from distance: the further the Essence is beyond grasp, the more the soul longs to be included in Him. Fear grows from nearness, up to bitul in existence:

> עד שנעשה כאבן דומם בלי הרגשת עצמו כלל (line 367)

The higher fear is Chochmah itself (line 367, F08 + F12). Love from distance can reach self-sacrifice beyond reason. It is the aleph of Keter, and so the light of Ein Sof is found in the farthest distance as in the nearest nearness:

> ונמצא תוקף אורו למטה דוקא בריחוק כמו למעלה ממש בקירוב היותר פנימי (line 368)

The nearness of Chochmah comes only in steps. So bitul differs from world to world (line 368). In Atzilut "He and His life are one". In Beriah there is only the nullification of the something.

Section 75 asks how the Torah, rooted in inner Chochmah, came down revealed. The answer is that it came clothed. Light of the Essence reaches below only in a foreign garment, as a deep idea reaches a child in a parable (F10 + F03 + F05):

> וכל אור שהוא מן העצמות ממש א"א שיבא למטה בגלוי כמו שהוא כ"א ע"י התלבשות בענין לבוש זר (line 370)

The garment is not matter to a form, so the Torah is one Torah in Atzilut and in Assiyah (line 371). In the time to come the garment will reveal, not hide:

> הרי הלבוש דתור' אינו מעלים כלל אדרבה ע"י יראו אור העצמי (line 371)

The Torah was given to Israel and not to the angels, "because Israel rose in the thought of inner Abba" (line 371, F06). The dalet does not change, because "their beginning is wedged in their end": Malchut is Keter and Keter is Malchut, "upper is lower and lower is upper" (lines 372-373, F05 + F09).

## Sections 76-83 (374-401): witness, name, joy, tefillin, prophecy

Sections 76-78 are mostly letter-maps (structural, no face). Two ties stand out. First, "ed" (witness) applies only to what is hidden. The witness comes from Binah, the hidden world, to the revealed world, Israel (line 379). Second, Malchut (shin-mem, "name") goes down small, at night, hidden in nogah, to sort the 288 sparks (line 381, F11 + F03). When the sorting is complete, evil will be turned to good "as if it had never been" (line 383, F11 + F07):

> שזהו לאחר תשלום כל הברורים דוקא שנהפך הרע לטוב כאלו לא הי' במציאת מעולם (line 383)

By the sorting, "turning darkness and subduing the other side, the glory of the Holy One is raised", as a king's name grows by conquering the farthest (line 382).

Sections 79-81 read the Zohar's praise of "the one who makes this unity every day". In "Shema" and "echad" the fixed order of the unions is reversed. Speech rises into the mind, and the heart gives to thought. Malchut, the receiver, rises higher than Z'A, the giver (line 391, F09). The glory of Malchut is to be hidden (line 389):

> וזהו כבוד אלקי' שהוא בחי' כבודו של אלקי' הוא בבחי' המל' כשבא ועולה בבחי' הסתר דבר (line 389)

The joy that waits is hidden joy from the Essence (line 392, F05). A king used to every treasure delights only in something new, and more in something turned end to end. In "ashmach", Malchut stands enclosed between the aleph and the chet (line 393, F02):

> משום שאין יחוד אמיתי יותר ממנו (line 393)

The word ends in the right order, so the outside forces get no nourishment: "in God alone, and no stranger there" (line 393, F11).

Sections 82-83 map the Shema onto tefillin. The letters of the passages really clothe the surrounding light (line 396, F10 + F04). A person is moved only when contemplation takes the form of letters in thought (line 397, F12, structural). Prophecy is the Essence speaking in a person "without his feeling himself at all" (line 400, F05 + F08):

> אבל הנביא הוא שבו נגלה עצמו' אור האלקי דלית בי' מחשבה והשגה (line 400)

## Sections 84-89 (402-421): the two walkings, this world and the next, and the Essence where they meet

The whole Torah is compared to tefillin because the Torah is the inclusion of left in right (line 402, F10 + F09). It is the wisdom that sorts, in deed. "You will see My back" is the Torah that came down to sort. The inner glory shines now only hidden, in the tefillin on the head.

Section 84 names the two walkings. "Walk before Me" draws the surrounding into the inner: the Shema, "with all your might". "Walk after God" raises the inner to the surrounding: the mitzvot. The descent is for the ascent:

> וירידה זו שיוגבל הבלתי מוגבל מא"מ לאו"פ יריד' זו רק לצורך עלי' (line 403)

Neither changes its nature. The unlimited dwells in the limited vessel and stays unlimited (line 403, F09). Torah study is the drawing down of the Essence of Ein Sof through all the worlds (line 406, F10 + F05):

> שהוא גלוי המשכת עצמות הא"ס מלמעלה מעלה למטה מטה באבי"ע (line 406)

"Doing His will" (section 86) means awakening the simple will in the Essence, which stands between the Essence and the worlds. "He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" (line 409, F04 + F05). Accepting the yoke of Heaven makes the union of sovev and memale (line 410). The mitzvot in deed count as "doing His will" only when the sorting is complete. Then their pleasure reaches the Essence itself, and below and above will be as equal as before the tzimtzum (lines 410-411, F07):

> יתגלה עצמו' התענוג והרצון שהי' לו בזה כי נתאוה להיות לו (line 410)

> שיהי' למטה ולמעל' שוה כמו שהי' לפני הצמצו' ממש (line 411)

Section 87 is the range's clearest F11. Only in this world is there teshuvah, which turns evil to good "from end to end" (line 412). Only here can the upper fall and the lower rise, because this is the time of sorting, like wine while it ferments. The root is F04. Before the general surrounding light "upper is lower and lower is upper", like a sphere (line 413):

> וידוע דלגבי אור המקי' הכללי העליון תחתון והתחתון עליון (line 413)

Sections 88-89 answer a contradiction. The World to Come is both an ascent ("who will ascend the mountain of God") and a descent ("the glory of God will be revealed"). Both are true. First comes the full raising. Then comes a drawing from the Essence, so that the tzimtzum is removed (line 416, F05 + F03 + F07):

> שיוסר הצמצום הזה ויהי' גלוי עצמו' אא"ס שלפני הצמצום למעלה ומטה בשוה (line 416)

In the Essence, ascent and descent are not two (lines 417-421, F05 + F09):

> אבל קמי' עצמותו ממש שניהם כא' שוין (line 418)

The soul's yechidah feels will and deed "in one glance" (line 419, F06). That is the soul's image of the Essence that holds both. The Essence does not lessen as it descends. It widens (line 421):

> אלא בחינת העצמות הוא שמתגלה למטה כמו למעלה (line 421)

## Sections 90-95 (422-437): the mitzvah makes the union; study, deed and the Temple

The true intent of every mitzvah is the upper union, the Holy One and His Shechinah, and it comes through bitul of the will (line 422, F10 + F08 + F02). The Shema's higher unity is "You are One but not in number... not of these attributes at all". The line of light is "as nothing" before the Essence (line 423, F05):

The Essence contracts Himself through humility and through gevurah (line 423, F03 + F09). His humility comes from His greatness. The Essence is the true nothing and dwells only in lowliness (line 424, F08):

> דבחי' רוממות העצמות הרי הוא בחי' אין האמיתי שלא יוכל לשכון בבחי' יש וגיאות רק בבחי' אין דשפלות (line 424)

A gloss ranks fear above love. The servant's total bitul reaches the Essence more than wondrous love does. The penitent's weeping soul is the proof, and so "Moshe My servant" is above the son (line 424):

> שבטול הנפש דמצד היראה יותר מגיע בעצמות מקירוב הנפש באהבה ודביקות היותר נפלאה (line 424)

The mitzvot have two sides: bitul of the will, and the yoke ("my Father in heaven decreed"). The yoke, subduing oneself against nature, is higher (line 426, F08 + F11). Bitul below awakens His humility, and gevurah below awakens His gevurah. So the mitzvot bring the Essence down into the worlds:

> גורם למעלה בחי' ירידת עצמות אא"ס בנאצלים ונבראי' (line 426)

The mitzvot are "the clothing of God's simple Oneness in physical things" (line 427):

> בחי' התלבשות אחדו' ה' הפשוטה בדברים גשמיי' למטה (line 427)

Section 92 settles study against deed. The deed is greater at its root above, and study is greater below, as the light of the deed. The Torah is where the Essence, the true nothing, first shines (line 428):

> ע"כ בה דוקא ראשית גלוי אור העצמו' הנק' אין האמיתי (line 428)

Section 93 orders the Shema: love (running), study (returning), then tefillin. Its aim is binding the soul "to the simple Oneness", to give itself up "in One" (line 431, F08 + F12 + F10). Sections 93-94 read the Temples. The mitzvot are the King's limbs. Sin lets the inner light go out to the outside forces, as blood leaves a damaged limb (line 432). In exile He rises "to His place" and "darkness is like light", so even the outside can take the inner (line 433). Before His Essence even Israel and the nations are equal, which is why we say He *chooses* (line 434, F05):

Torah is the medicine that draws new light into every damaged limb (line 435). Neglect of Torah damages the head, which includes all the limbs (line 436). Section 95 turns the order around for the time to come. The mitzvah, which is will beyond reason, will be above the Torah (line 437, F07 + F05):

> אבל לעתיד יהי' להיפך דבר מצוה יהי' למעלה מאור דתור' (line 437)

## What this range adds to the Tanya (or says differently)

- **The surrounding light is equal everywhere now, not only at the end.** The Tanya (Shaar HaYichud 7) uses "below as above" for the lower unity. Here the general makif is present in this world "as in Atzilut itself" right now, only hidden (lines 353-355). That is why every creature has bitul without rank. The revealed equality is kept for the time to come (lines 411, 416).
- **The tzimtzum comes from humility and gevurah.** The Tanya (chapter 49) says the tzimtzum is for love of Israel. SH 4 says gevurah hides. This range adds humility that comes from greatness, and gevurah as self-mastery, "as His kindness has no limit below, so His power to contract Himself has no limit above" (line 423). It then ties both to the person: bitul of will awakens His humility, and the yoke awakens His gevurah (line 426).
- **A mechanism for the mitzvah making the union.** The Tanya (chapters 40-41) says the mitzvah joins the doer to Ein Sof. Here the mitzvot are the King's limbs, vessels built by Israel. Sin makes the light "bleed" to the outside forces (lines 427, 432-433).
- **Study and deed, now and later.** The Tanya (chapter 37) makes the practical mitzvot the purpose. This range agrees at the root: the deed is greater above, and in the time to come the mitzvah will be above the Torah (lines 428, 437). Below and now, study is greater, as the light of the deed (lines 428-429).
- **Ascent and descent one in the Essence.** The Tanya does not say this. Here, only in a measured circle is there up and down. In the Essence, running and returning are one (lines 417-421). The yechidah is the soul's image of this (line 419).
- **Before the Essence, Israel and the nations are equal** (line 434). The Tanya speaks of the choice of Israel. This range says why the choice is needed: before the Essence "darkness is like light".

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Twelve line entries carry no face. They are letter-mechanics (lines 359, 374, 380), the psychology of speech and deliberation (362, 375-377), the prophets by temperament (401), tefillin straps as thighs (398), and questions that only set up the next answer (366, 387, 408). - Line 399's knot of tefillin as "a union of kindness and severity" is a union of sefirot. Its tie to the unity of God is thin, and it is marked structural.
- *Reading* is used five times. Line 364, the wide mind that "bears a thing and its opposite", is read as an image of F09. Line 372.5, the completion of the first will in the last deed, is read as F07. Line 385.3, the reversed order of the unions, is marked reading for F09 before the text develops it. Line 391.2, "the end of the deed rose first in thought", is read as F07. The text's own statements are elsewhere (for example 410.3 for F07). - Line 414 is a consequence (only the worthy receive in the World to Come). Its one tie, Torah as "an elixir of life or death", is thin.

## Disagreements kept

- **Love and fear: far or near?** Line 367 says love comes from distance and fear from nearness. A gloss notes that elsewhere it is the reverse. Line 368 resolves this: one pair is from the giver's side and the other from the receiver's. Both accounts stand.
- **Fear above love.** Line 424 puts the servant's bitul above "wondrous love" as reaching the Essence, against the Zohar's "no service like love", which it quotes and qualifies. The Tanya does not rank them this way. This is kept as a difference of emphasis, not smoothed.
- **Is Malchut built from gevurot or from kindness?** Lines 365 and 426 say both are true. The gevurot are for her own building and for judging the worlds. The kindness of Z'A is for the worlds' sake.
- **Two readings of the same letters.** "Shin-mem included in the large ayin" is speech rising into Binah (lines 386-389) or Binah's feelings coming down into speech (line 395). The text keeps both. In line 393, shin-mem stands between aleph and chet (Z'A) or, "more correctly", between Keter and Chochmah.
- **Which paragraph of the Shema is higher?** The first "does His will above" (line 409). The second, through the mitzvot, will make below and above equal, "and in this the second paragraph is higher" (line 411).
- **Why the Second Temple fell.** Line 434 says the First Temple's generation studied Torah and the Second's did not. A gloss records the opposite view elsewhere: the Second Temple's martyrs died for Torah, and its sin was baseless hatred. Line 435 then says its own first answer is "not enough", and line 436 gives another. All three are kept.
- **Study or deed.** The Talmud's "study is greater because it leads to deed" is read as study greater below and deed greater at the root (line 428), and as the reverse in the time to come (line 437).
