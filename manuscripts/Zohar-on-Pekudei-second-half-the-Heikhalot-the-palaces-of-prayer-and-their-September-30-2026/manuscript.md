## 1. What the range is

The **Heikhalot** of Pekudei are the Zohar's map of the ascent of prayer and of the soul. There are two sets.

**Seven palaces of holiness** (lines 4721-5085): (1) the palace of the "sapphire brick," where prayers and souls are received or turned back, and the gate of tears; (2) the palace of "the essence of heaven," where the garments of the soul are kept and the reward for seeking wisdom is given; (3) the palace of "radiance," with gates for decrees, for life and death, for illness, and for healing, and the watchers who see every good thought; (4) the palace of "merit," where all judgments are weighed; (5) the palace of "love," where souls come from and acts of kindness are crowned; (6) the palace of "will," the place of the kiss, where R. Akiva died in love; (7) the holy of holies, with no image and no body. A second pass (lines 5039-5085) lays the Amidah on the palaces blessing by blessing and ends with "thought, will, voice and word."

**Seven palaces of impurity** (lines 5086-5187): the pit, the ditch, silence, the mire, Sheol, the house of the strange woman of Proverbs 7, and the dregs of wine. They are named against the seven names of the evil inclination and the seven chambers of Gehinnom, and each gathers a kind of sin: lust, anger, evil speech, cursing, vanity.

It closes with R. Shimon's prayer on the will that is never grasped, and the righteous as a true offering (lines 5192-5209).

## 2. The unity of G-d

**Prayer rises to where all wills are fixed.** The palaces exist for two orders of praise, בין סדורא דקיימא במלה בין סדורא דקיימא ברעותא, "the order that stands in words and the order that stands in will" (line 4721), the second being the heart's intention לאסתכלא לעילא לעילא עד אין סוף, "to look higher and higher up to Ein Sof," where there is תקיעו דכל רעותין ומחשבין, ולא קיימין במלה כלל, "the fixing of all wills and thoughts, and they do not stand in words at all" (line 4721).

**The will that is never grasped.** R. Shimon: ארימית ידי בצלותין לעילא, "I raised my hands in prayer on high" (line 5196). The high will rests on a will that is not known and never grasped. The nine palaces of thought are not lights, not spirits, not souls; תפסין בה ולא תפסין, "they grasp and do not grasp" (line 5199). The light from which highest thought shines is called Ein Sof, ועל דא כלא קאים, "and on this everything stands" (line 5201). And in the seventh palace: וההוא דלא אתיידע, ולא עאל בחושבנא, רעותא דלא אתפס לעלמין, כדין כלא סליק עד אין סוף, "that which is not known and does not enter any count, a will never grasped: then everything rises to Ein Sof" (line 5021), where the light אנהיר ואתבסם ותפיס ולא תפיס, "shines and is sweetened, grasps and does not grasp" (line 5022). This repeats and seals the passage of part2-pekudei-1.md (lines 4352, 4610-4611).

**The seventh palace has no image.** רוחא דכל רוחין, רעוון דכל רעווין, "spirit of all spirits, will of all wills" (line 5002), with no form and no body. *Olam* (world) is read from *he'elem* (hiding): the world אתגלייא בסתירה, "is revealed in hiding" (line 5004). **(ours)** The Chabad reading of *olam* as *he'elem* has its source here.

**Holy and blessed are one bond.** Those on the right say "holy" and join those who know how to sanctify; those on the left say "blessed" and join those who do not know how; וכלהו כלילן אלין באלין ביחודא חדא, "and all are included in each other in one unity" (line 4782). **(ours)** Those who know and those who do not are held in one bond.

**Below wakes above.** On evil speech: ולא ידעי דהא באתערו דלתתא, תליא אתערו דלעילא, בין לטב בין לביש, "they do not know that on the awakening below hangs the awakening above, for good or for bad" (line 5125). And whoever learns Torah wakes the "holy tongue" above (line 5130).

## 3. States of consciousness

**The black flame reaching (fills in S13).** מאן דאיהו בחשוכא, תאיב תדיר למהוי בנהורא, "whoever is in darkness always longs to be in light"; שלהובא אוכמא דלתתא, אתער תדיר לגבי, "the black flame below always wakes toward" the white flame above, to cleave to it (line 4959). **(ours)** The unnamed longing (S13) is described here as the natural pull of the dark part of the flame to the white. The longing itself is the proof of the bond.

**Love from below meets love from above.** In the palace of love, angels "bring love between Israel below and the Holy One above," וכדין רחימו דלתתא, גו רחימו עלאה, אתדבק דא בדא, "and then the love below cleaves inside the love above" (line 4895). Every act of kindness rises and is crowned there: וכל אינון דעבדי חסד בעלמא, אינון חסדין סלקין ועאלין גו האי היכלא (line 4896). Tie to S42 (Love like water) and S86 (The other held).

**Dying in love (R. Akiva).** Of the four who entered the orchard, only R. Akiva came in and out in peace. When he reached the palace of love, אתדבק ביה ברעו דלבא, "he cleaved to it with the will of the heart" (line 4917), and said this palace must be joined to the one above; ואיהו סליק ואשלים אהבה זוטא באהבה רבא, "and he rose and completed the small love in the great love"; ועל דא מית באהבה, ונפק נשמתיה בהאי קרא, "and so he died in love, and his soul went out on this verse, *ve'ahavta*" (line 4917). **(ours)** "Small love completed in great love" names S44 (Love as delight) at its peak, and the Chabad reading of *ahavah rabbah*.

**Joy in the soul, sorrow in the body.** Of the ten martyrs: ועל דא גופא דלהון הוה בעציבו, ונשמתא בחדוה, "their body was in sorrow and their soul in joy" (line 4927). Tie to S49 (Weeping and joy in one heart).

**After the extra soul.** Angels go out with the Shabbat soul to remove from people כל עציבו, וכל יגיעא, וכל מרירו דנפשא, וכל רוגזא דעלמא, "all sadness, all weariness, all bitterness of soul, and all the anger of the world" (line 4950). Fills in S76.

**The voice in the study hall.** A strong spirit tempts one who learns: מה את קאים הכא, "what are you standing here for?" (line 5138); better go with the proud and the pleasure-seekers. **(ours)** A precise picture of S34 (Two voices): the voice that pulls you out of the room just when you sit down.

**Rest and anger have two sources.** בגין דכל סטרא דנייחא, איהו מסטרא דימינא, ומסטרא דמהימנותא, "every side of calm comes from the right, from the side of faith," וכל סטרא דרוגזא, איהו מסטרא אחרא בישא, "and every side of anger from the evil other side" (line 5101). Whatever a person throws in anger is taken up as an offering to the other side.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Thought, will, voice and word (proposed ZT2-13).** The closing teaching on prayer: prayer must come מגו מחשבה, ורעותא דלבא, וקלא, ומלה דשפוון, "from thought, and the will of the heart, and voice, and the word of the lips" (line 5082). מחשבה. ורעותא. וקלא. ומלה. אלין ארבע מקשרין קשרין, "thought, will, voice, word: these four tie the knots" (line 5083). מחשבה אפיק רעותא, "thought brings out will"; רעותא דנפיק מגו מחשבה, אפיק קלא דאשתמע, "the will that comes out of thought brings out the voice that is heard" (line 5084), and the voice ties the lower palaces to the upper and draws blessing down in a whisper. **(ours)** A four-step way to begin any prayer: (1) think of what you are about to say and to Whom; (2) let a want for it form; (3) let the want become a quiet voice; (4) let the voice become words. Fills a gap between technique 2 (Before Whom) and technique 18 (Saying it with the lips). Also line 4827: prayer must come "out of thought, and in the will of speech and breath."

**Ask first to know Him (proposed ZT2-14).** In the Amidah, the first request is for knowledge: שירותא דשאלתין למנדע למאריה, לאחזאה דתיאובתיה לגביה, "the first of the requests is to know one's Master, to show that one's longing is for Him" (line 5062). מכאן ולהלאה, יתפרש זעיר זעיר, וישאל שאלתוי מה דאצטריך לשאלא, "from there he should part little by little and ask what he needs" (line 5062), and כל שאלתוי יהון בתחנונים ובעותין לקמי מאריה, "all his requests should be in pleading before his Master" (line 5063), not in self-justification. **(ours)** For anyone with a list of needs: ask first to know Him; then ask for the rest, slowly.

**Opening with merit (proposed ZT2-15).** The palace of merit: בגין דלית שלימו דדינא, אלא בזכותא, "for there is no wholeness of judgment except with merit" (line 4971). The Sanhedrin אצטריכו למפתח בזכותא, "had to open with merit" (line 4974), and so on Rosh Hashanah we join merit to judgment. **(ours)** A practice for judging others and oneself: before any verdict, first find and say the merit. Tie to technique 24 (The evening look-back) and to S11.

**Leaving prayer.** When a person leaves the last palace, ישוי גרמיה, כמאן דנפיק מחברותא דמלכא, "he should see himself as one leaving the King's company," humble; אבל יחדי גרמיה, "but let him be glad" (line 5073), for he is among "those who think on His name."

**The seventh palace is a whisper.** The Amidah begins at the seventh palace: רזא דרזין בלחישו, ולא אשתמע קלא. הכא איהו רעותא דלבא, "the secret of secrets, in a whisper, no voice heard; here is the will of the heart" (line 5045), ולסלקא רעותא מתתא לעילא, עד אין סוף, "to raise the will from below to above, up to Ein Sof" (line 5045). Adds to ZT2-07.

**When to be long and when to be short.** Moses prayed long for Israel and five words for his sister. Lengthening in the right place makes the prayer accepted: וכד אריך באריכו, באתר דאצטריך, קודשא בריך הוא קביל צלותיה (line 5025).

**Kindness in hunger.** In famine, מאן דעביד חסד עם בני נשא, ויהיב לון למיכל ולמשתי, "whoever does kindness with people and gives them food and drink" (line 5149) pushes back the spirits of "ruin and famine."

**Curse the day, not yourself.** Words a person says to curse himself in anger or in an oath are taken up by the "cursers of the day." ועל דא לייט איוב יומיה בצעריה, ולא גופיה, "so Job, in his pain, cursed his day, and not himself" (line 5145). **(ours)** A practical rule for the guide: in pain, say the day is bad; do not say you are.

## 5. The big human questions

**What happens to a prayer that was not good enough?** A prayer turned back is not thrown away. An angel takes all the rejected prayers, ונטיל כל הני צלותין דאתדחיין, דאקרון צלותי פסילאן וגניז לון, "and takes all the prayers that were pushed away, called unfit prayers, and hides them away" (line 4736). אי תב לגבי מאריה כדקא יאות, וצלי צלותא אחרא זכאה, "if he returns to his Master properly and prays another, worthy prayer," the angel lifts the old ones up to meet it, וסלקין ואתערבון כחדא, ועאלין קמי מלכא קדישא, "and they rise and mix together, and enter before the holy King" (line 4737). **(ours)** One of the most comforting lines in the Zohar: every poor prayer you ever said is kept, waiting to go up with your next good one.

**Are tears ever wasted?** Above the gate is another gate, opened three times a day and never locked, for those who return in tears (line 4739). The prayer rises, ואינון דמעין אשתארו הכא, ורשימין בהאי פתחא, "and those tears remain here, engraved on this gate" (line 4740). The tears shed for the Temple and for the righteous who died are kept on the chariots until "God wipes the tears from every face."

**Does a good intention I could not carry out count?** The watchers of the third palace see all who think of a mitzvah but cannot do it (ועל כל אינון דמחשבי מחשבה דמצוה, אף על גב דלא יכלי למעבד, line 4833); all who comfort a poor man or grieve for him when they cannot give (לכל אינון דמנחמי למסכנא, או דצערי לבייהו עליה, אף על גב דלא יכלי למיהב ליה, line 4834); all who wanted to do wrong and did not (לכל אינון דחשיבו למעבד בישין, ולא עבדי, line 4835); and those who visit the sick and gently help them look at their lives and return (ואודעו ליה דיסתכל בחובוי ובעובדוי, ויתוב מנייהו למאריה, line 4836). And the "eyes of the Lord" note deeds done in secret and those done with a whole heart, ואף על גב דלא אתעבידו כדקא יאות, "even though they were not done properly" (line 4869).

**Is healing possible through pain?** The gate of healing brings in the prayers of those in pain and illness (line 4804). And: ההוא מרע כד אתיא ליה אסוותא, מגו דחיקו סגיא אתיא ליה, "when healing comes to the sick, it comes out of great distress" (line 4911).

**What is the greatest reward?** דלית אגרא בההוא עלמא, כאינון דמשתדלי בחכמתא, לאסתכלא ביקרא דמאריהון, "there is no reward in that world like that of those who toil in wisdom to gaze on the glory of their Master" (line 4780), and it is given ואפילו מכל בר נש, "to any person at all" who learns wisdom to know his Master (line 4777).

**Where do the righteous of the nations go?** In the gates of the seventh palace of the other side, light from the holy side shines, and there is a place made ready לאינון חסידי דשאר עמין, "for the pious of the other nations" who dealt honestly (line 5183). **(ours)** Even in its darkest map the Zohar leaves a lit place for good people of every nation (D28).

**What does a righteous life accomplish?** ועל דא צדיקא כפרה איהו בעלמא, וקרבנא ממש, "the righteous person is atonement for the world, a real offering" (line 5206). And the breath of children learning Torah becomes a guardian spirit of the world (line 4943). Money spent on Shabbat and on the poor, כל מאי דאפיק למיכל ולמשתי, הוא ילונו לקודשא בריך הוא בהלואה, "is a loan to the Holy One" (line 4926), which He repays double. The righteous die "by a kiss" (בגין דתפוק נשמתיה בנשיקה, line 4954).

**Why does the body pull?** אית מלין דגופא אתהני בהו, ועיילי לגופא, ולא לנשמתא, "there are things the body enjoys that enter the body and not the soul" (line 4997), and things the soul enjoys and not the body. The sixth palace of the other side is the seductress of Proverbs 7, sweet at first and bitter at the end.

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **The rejected prayers kept and raised with the next good one (lines 4736-4737)** should be taught to anyone who feels their prayers are empty.
2. **The watchers who see good intentions not carried out (lines 4833-4836, 4869)** answer the fear that only results count.
3. **Thought, will, voice, word (lines 5082-5084)** is a clear, universal way into prayer.
4. **"Ask first to know Him" (line 5062)** puts the longing before the needs.
5. **"Open with merit" (lines 4971, 4974)** is a practice for judging self and others.
6. **R. Akiva, "small love completed in great love" (line 4917)**, and **"the black flame always reaches for the white" (line 4959)**, name the heart's deepest movement.
7. **"Curse the day, not yourself" (line 5145)** is a simple, protective rule for pain.

Set aside: the names and numbers of the angels at each gate, the ministering of the other side's palaces in detail, the passages on Lilith and the mirror (lines 5168-5170), on children who die (lines 4799-4803, 5121; not used as an explanation of any child's death), on sexual sins and their punishments (lines 5104-5111), on the mamzer (line 5177), and on nations as persecutors (lines 4973, 5149-5150, 5185). The camel dream-sign (line 5191) is not used.
