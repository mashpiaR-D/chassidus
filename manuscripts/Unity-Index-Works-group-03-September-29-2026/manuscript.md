**Group summary.** Group-03 spans the whole map. Two Chabad works carry the idea as their stated center: Likkutei Sichos proves it on Rashi and the Rambam and maps the two unities onto two Torah genres, and Kuntres HaTefillah brings it from knowledge into prayer. Likkutei Biurim (badly OCR'd) turns bitul inward against spiritual ecstasy. The Besht circle (Ketonet Pasim, Midrash Pinchas, Ohr HaMeir, Irin Kadishin) states the idea in short, strong form: opposites one above Binah, the hiding that is no hiding, letters of Torah in all speech, vitality in all things. These works restrict service through the physical to the few. Sod Yesharim states the higher and lower unity almost as Tanya does, but draws an Izhbitz conclusion about choice. Likutei Halakhot affirms 'ein od mamash' but holds the empty space by faith, and is organized around the tzaddik. Tzav VeZeruz is mostly thin: the idea appears in a few entries as experience.

## Signature (reading, resting on stated passages)

Likkutei Sichos is the one idea proven on the text of Torah itself. Each sicha takes a single problem in a Rashi, a Rambam or a Midrash, solves it first in plain sense, and then shows that the plain sense and the inner sense are one point. Its angle is G07 (add life to each level, do not add a fifth) and G02 (hold both knowledges). What it adds to the corpus is the claim that the two standpoints of the unity are built into the genres of Torah. The halachic Rambam must say 'no true existence like His', because halacha needs a world to be fulfilled in. Rashi's 'He is Yachid' speaks from the Essence, for which there is no existence at all (vol. 24, 111037-41, stated). The goal is not the world's abolition but its being a dwelling 'while remaining in its own existence' (vol. 4, 11578, stated), and the ground of that is the Essence, for which 'the existence of the world itself is Godliness' (vol. 25, 120979, stated). The work also keeps a disagreement open in a footnote: those who hold tzimtzum literally must, the Rebbe says, differ with the Besht too, and 'more likely' they hold that renewal each moment comes from His providence, not from His Essence (120981, stated). That every sicha, including the many on customs, dates and textual details, is one expression of the idea is our reading. The Rebbe states the tie often, but a large share of the Rashi and halachic sichos solves a textual question and names the inner point only in the last section or in a footnote.

## Structure

Volumes follow the yearly cycle: Bereishis to Devarim, then festivals and special days, then letters. A typical sicha has numbered sections: (1) a question on the plain sense, (2-4) the proposed answers and their difficulties, (5-8) the resolution in pshat or halacha, (9-10) 'the inner meaning' (be-pnimiyus ha-inyanim), and (11) 'the directive' (horaah). The grammar G11 is built in: from the textual effect to its root, ending in action. The footnotes carry the Chassidus sources (Tanya, Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, the Rashab's hemshechim), so the unity doctrine often sits under the line rather than in it (e.g. 120976-120986).

## Disagreements kept

- Literal vs non-literal tzimtzum: kept by the Rebbe himself (120981) as a difference with the Besht's continuous creation; he offers a charitable reading of the other side (renewal from providence, not Essence). - 'Ein od milvado' as 'no true existence like His' (Rambam) vs 'no existence at all' (Rashi's inner reading, the Essence): presented as both true from two standpoints, not reconciled into one.

## Signature (stated)

Kuntres HaTefillah is the one idea on its way from the book into the person. It does not teach the unity; it assumes the chassid already knows the contemplations of memale and sovev, and of what is above sovev (44). It asks why that knowledge changes nothing. Its answer is the Rashab's side of the spine's disagreement on where the unity is brought down: in prayer, by detailed contemplation with effort of the mind, until 'the Godly light itself shines in his mind' and so, of itself, in the heart (66, stated). Knowledge left general stays 'makif', and the emotions of the animal soul remain 'in their strength as at birth' (50). It adds two things: a demand that is the same for every Jew, the acceptance of the yoke of Heaven that 'His kingship is unified over each one in particular' (111), and a warning that those who deal only in 'the deep matters' coarsen them with the selfhood of comprehension (73). The metaphysics is stated in passing and at the top: 'the perfection of His Essence bears all opposites, and all is truly yachid' (46). The rest of the letter is method.

## Structure

Chs. 1-2 set the standard (contemplation in prayer, in detail); chs. 3-6 distinguish true from imagined arousal and define daas as feeling; chs. 7-9 on those who learn much Chassidus and pray little; chs. 10-14 on preparation, the less gifted, and the young; ch. 15 on kabbalas ol for all; chs. 16-17 on businessmen (from the Mitteler Rebbe) and daily Shulchan Aruch; ch. 18 the charge: 'to learn in order to do', that is, to serve in prayer (122). The order itself is G13's force-light-life compressed into prayer, ending in action (G11).

## Disagreements kept

- Where the unity is brought down: prayer (this work, the Rashab) vs action and spreading outward (the Rebbe, Sichos Kodesh / Likkutei Sichos). ## Faces

- **F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation** (primary): צמצום 358 lines, חלל הפנוי 89. Every day is a new creation through the tzimtzum of the empty space (2506). The questions that come from the empty space have no answer 'because if one answered them he would find God there' (2505).
- **F12 Knowing the unity** (primary (the faith side)): אמונה 1292 lines. The empty space is 'two opposites, yet both are true, and impossible to understand now', so one must 'strengthen oneself in faith alone' (3349). This is Breslov's side of the spine's knowledge-or-faith disagreement.
- **F10 Torah and mitzvah: the unity made actual** (secondary): The method of the whole work: from every law one must draw counsels to come close to Him (Intro 10). Chukim, mitzvos without reason, are given to strengthen faith against the questions of the empty space (5492).
- **F02 The higher and the lower unity** (secondary): Stated at 9217-9218: the essential purpose is to know 'ein od milvado, ein od mamash', nothing exists but He, 'and after creation it is as before creation'.
- **F01 The world said into being now** (minor): יש מאין 43, השגחה 204; creation renewed each day (2506).

## Signature (reading, resting on stated passages)

Likutei Halakhot carries the one idea as faith rather than as contemplation. Its angle is the paradox of the empty space. God withdrew His Godliness 'for the sake of creating the world, for without it everything would have been one' (2505). The empty space is at once empty of Him and filled by Him, 'two opposites… yet both are true' (3349, stated). That paradox cannot be understood now, only believed. So the unity is affirmed at full strength: 'ein od milvado, ein od mamash… after creation as before creation' (9217-18, stated). It is reached not by the Chabad route of comprehension (F12, the Rayatz's 'to know') but by faith, the tzaddik and his counsels. Its method is G03 and G07 at scale: every law of the Shulchan Aruch is opened to a counsel for coming close to Him (Intro 10, stated). What it adds to the index is a second center. The unity is stated in the text, but the organizing center of the work is the tzaddik (2904 lines) and emunah (1292), not the unity. That the whole Shulchan Aruch commentary is one expression of the idea is our reading, and the spine's limits section already warns that Breslov is organized around other centers.

## Structure

Follows the Shulchan Aruch order law by law. Each law gets one or more discourses (numbered 'halacha' 1-6+), each opening from a torah of Likutei Moharan and then reading the law's details through it. The same few torahs (e.g. LM I 64 on the empty space, LM II 12 'Ayeh') return across distant laws, so the unity theme surfaces wherever those torahs are the key (Afternoon Prayer, Joining Domains, Idolatry, Circumcision).
