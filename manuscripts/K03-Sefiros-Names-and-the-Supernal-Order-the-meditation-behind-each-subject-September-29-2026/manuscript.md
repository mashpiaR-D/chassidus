## S027 · The ten sefiros · עשר ספירות

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F02 The higher and the lower unity · F09 The unity of opposites **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 7)

**The glimpse.** The one light is clothed in ten measures so that measured things can come from it; the ten are one with Him, and He Himself is not one of the ten.

**Hold.** 'You are One, but not in the count of the ten sefiros' (the Tikkunim, as the Mitteler Rebbe reads the Shema, Shaar HaYichud 7).

**The move.** Open it: so that finite things could come from infinite power, the Infinite Light clothed itself in ten measures and joined them so fully that 'He, His life and His vessels are one'. The picture is your own soul, whose ten powers, thinking, understanding, loving, holding back and the rest, are many while you are one; but the picture stops, since what makes it impossible for a Jew to be torn from Him comes from the Essence, above the ten altogether. You, here, now: through everything you think and feel today, the One you turn to is not in their count.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'One, not in the count'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Beneath all your powers, a bond to Him that none of them makes and none can undo. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say the first verse of the Shema with your lips, slowly, thinking at 'One' that He is not one of the ten. Then learn one line of Torah, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'One, not in the count'.

**Thin.** The tie is stated at the level of doctrine (Iggeret HaKodesh 20, Shaar HaYichud); most working passages on the sefiros do not restate it, so this meditation keeps to the doctrine and does not map the ten.

**Disagreement kept.** Whether the ten are in the Essence divides the sources: the Mitteler Rebbe says that even there, 'alone', the ten are included in Himself, hidden; the Rashab, citing the Alter Rebbe, says there are no sefiros in the Essence at all, and the ten of Atzilus are a new thing.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - The Gate of Unity 7:32 (line 318): למעלה מי׳ ספירות כו' וכמאמר אנת הוא חד ולא בחושבן י׳ ספירות - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:12 (line 1262): נתלבש אוראיןסוף בכלים די׳ ספירות דאצילות, ומתייחד בתוכן בתכלית היחוד, עד ד״איהו וגרמוהי חד״, לברוא בהן ועל ידן ברואים בעלי גבול ותכלית - Hayom Yom, סיון / כא סיון:1 (line 329): ענין המסירת נפש על אלקות, וואס א איד ניט ער וויל און ניט ער קאן זיין ח"ו א נפרד מאלקות, זהו מעצמות א"ס ב"ה, שלמעלה מענין הספירות

## S028 · Keser: Atik and Arich · כתר / עתיק ואריך

**Face:** F05 The Essence beyond both. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F07 The home below **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** Between Him and everything there is a meeting point, the crown: the last touch of the Infinite and the first root of all things in one; and the life in your daily bread comes from its innermost part.

**Hold.** 'Keser mediates between the Emanator and the emanated, and holds the last level of the Infinite' (Iggeret HaKodesh 20).

**The move.** Open it: the Infinite is not defined as wisdom or kindness, so for it to become measured attributes there must be something between, and that is Keser, the crown, whose upper side is the last touch of the Infinite and whose lower side is the root of all that is made. The Mitteler Rebbe's picture is from the soul: its two sides are delight and will, the pleasure inside a wanting and the wanting itself; only a picture, since His will is not a want like ours. You, here, now: the Rashab says the very substance of a person's food and life comes from Atika Kadisha, the innermost of the Infinite, so the bread on your table is drawn from the highest place. Their end is set in their beginning.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'the last of the Infinite, the root of all'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The plainest things, bread and a day's living, come from the innermost place, not from far off. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before you set out to earn your living today, say with your lips 'ein od milvado'. Then give a coin to charity from what you earn, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'from the innermost'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:14 (line 1264): כי כתר הוא ממוצע בין המאציל לנאצלים, ויש בו בחינה האחרונה של האין סוף - The Gate of Unity 17:21 (line 963): והוא בחינת עתיק יומין ואריך אנפין דאצילות, שהרצון והתענוג בא בגלוי - Kuntres Umaayan, מַאֲמָר כד. / פֶּרֶק א:2 (line 493): דגוף ועצם השפע הוא מעתיקא קדישא שהוא פנימיות ועצמיות אין סוף

## S029 · Chochmah and Binah above · חכמה ובינה / אבא ואימא

**Face:** F06 The soul as part of the One. **Also touches:** F08 Bitul · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4, 6)

**The glimpse.** The soul is drawn from His wisdom, which is one with Him; that is why the plainest Jew, who cannot explain it, will not be torn from Him.

**Hold.** 'Chochmah is koach mah, what is not yet grasped or understood, and therefore the Infinite Light is clothed in it' (Tanya, ch. 18).

**The move.** Open it: Chochmah, the first flash, is the power of 'what?', not yet grasped, so the Infinite, which no thought can hold, rests in it; it is the level of 'He alone, and nothing besides'. Binah is where the flash takes shape and becomes something, and their union is peace above: the something of understanding made like the nothing of wisdom. The soul is drawn from His wisdom, the Tanya says, as a child from his father's mind, and He and His wisdom are one; a picture only, since He has no mind or body. You, here, now: that is why the simplest Jew, who could not explain it, will not deny the One; it rests in you before any thought takes shape.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'not yet grasped, and so He rests in it'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** A core older than your thoughts, that no argument reached and none can take; a homesickness that is really love. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say Modeh Ani with your lips tomorrow on waking, before your first thought takes shape. Then say the first verse of the Shema slowly, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'before the thought, He'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 18:10 (line 258): וזהו לשון חכמה – ״כ״ח מ״ה״, שהוא מה שאינו מושג ומובן, ואינו נתפס בהשגה עדיין, ולכן מתלבש בה אוראיןסוף ברוךהוא - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 35:7 (line 477): שאוראיןסוף ברוךהוא אינו מתייחד אפילו בעולם האצילות אלא ﬠל ידי התלבשותו תחלה בספירת חכמה, והיינו משום שאיןסוף ברוךהוא, הוא אחד האמת, שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו, וזו היא מדרגת החכמה - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 2:2 (line 65): דאיהו חכים ולא בחכמה ידיעא, אלא הוא וחכמתו אחד

## S030 · Upper and lower knowledge · דעת עליון ודעת תחתון

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F12 Knowing the unity · F04 Filling and surrounding **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 4, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** There are two true ways of knowing: from below, the world is solid and its source is a hidden 'nothing'; from above, He is the true Something and the world is as nothing before Him. Both are rooted in Him, and both are true.

**Hold.** 'Both knowledges are true, for both have their root in the Essence of the Infinite Light' (the Rebbe, 19 Kislev 5715).

**The move.** Open the two: the lower knowledge sees the world below as something and its source above as 'nothing', hidden and beyond grasp; that is creation from nothing. The higher sees it the other way: above is the true Something, and the world, real as it is, is as nothing before Him. Each has its own setting-aside: the lower lets go of 'I am something', and the higher goes further, to Moshe's 'what are we', which the Rebbe says is in every Jew. You, here, now, stand in the lower and are asked to touch the higher; the Rebbe insists that both are true, because both are rooted in the Essence.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'both are true'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The lightness of a ray inside the sun; then the table again, solid and His. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips Moshe's words 'v'nachnu mah', and what are we. Then do one mitzvah today without looking for credit, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'both are true'.

**Thin.** Stated in Chabad, but the frame shifts inside it (by source in Likkutei Torah, by viewpoint in the Maharash and the Rebbe), and outside Chabad the phrase is mostly Bilaam's; this meditation uses the viewpoint form and keeps the other standing.

**Disagreement kept.** In Likkutei Torah the Alter Rebbe defines the two by their source, the surrounding and the filling light; the Maharash and the Rebbe define them by viewpoint. Whether that is a dispute or two framings is the entry's reading.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ב / בס"ד. י"ט כסלו ה'תשט"ו:7 (line 876): דשתי הדיעות הם אמת, דשתיהם יש להם שרש בעצמות אוא"ס - Toras Shmuel, תר"ם ח"ב / הנחות מכ"ק אדמו"ר מהורש"ב / והוא כחתן כו' / תשצ:1 (line 13069): ד"ת הוא בריאה יש מאין שלמטה יש ולמעלה אין, איך שיפורש אין אם הארה או שאינו מושג כו', וד"ע הוא שלמטה אין ולמעלה יש אמיתי - Sefer HaMaamarim Melukat, חלק ד / בס"ד. ש"פ מטות-מסעי, מבה"ח מנחם-אב, ה'תשי"ג:7 (line 3087): דעת עליון הוא שלמעלה יש ולמטה אין, היינו, שרואים בראי' חושית שלמעלה הוא היש האמיתי ולמטה אין

## S031 · The emotive attributes and Zeir Anpin · מדות עליונות / זעיר אנפין

**Face:** F09 The unity of opposites. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 4, 5, 7)

**The glimpse.** In Him kindness holds judgment and judgment holds kindness, because each is one with Him; and when a person's love and fear are bound to Him, they stop being scattered branches.

**Hold.** 'Because they are wholly one with Him, they are united with one another and included in one another' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 6).

**The move.** Open it: His kindness includes judgment, and His judgment kindness; they do not pull apart, and that inclusion is the sign that they are one with Him. The Maggid shows the other side in a person: traits tied to things are scattered branches, since what you love you do not fear and what you fear you do not love; tied to God they become one, because the 'nothing' at their root joins them. The picture from the soul fails, and Likkutei Torah says where: the light in His attributes changes nothing in Him, 'not like the soul, which is itself clothed in the body'. You, here, now: your love and your firmness with someone close to you, a child or a friend, can be one when both are for Him.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'one with Him, and so included in one another'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** A heart wide enough for both. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips the words of Patach Eliyahu, 'v'ant hu d'kashir lon u'meyached lon', You bind them and unite them. Then do one kindness today for someone you must also be firm with. During the day, return to: 'You bind them and unite them'.

**Disagreement kept.** Likkutei Torah holds both sides: in His Essence 'He is none of these attributes', and yet 'He Himself is the Merciful and the Kind'. The entry keeps both.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:3 (line 789): מהתכללות המדות זו בזו, נראה לעין ד״איהו וגרמוהי חד״, שהן מדותיו, כי מאחר שהן ביחוד גמור עמו, לכן הן מתייחדות זו בזו וכלולות זו מזו - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 47:1 (line 115): כי כשהמדות דבוקים לדברים גשמיים הם ענפים מפורדים כי מה שאוהב אין מתירא ממנו ומה שמתיירא אין אוהבו וכאשר הם דבוקים להש"י הם הכל באחדות כנודע שבחי' אי"ן הוא המיחדת הכל - Likkutei Torah, Shir HaShirim 8:3 (line 4421): ומ״מ אין זה פועל שום שינוי והתפעלות בעצמותו ית׳ כי גם האור המתלבש בע״ס הוא רק כענין זיו והארה לבד אבל עצמותו ית׳ קדוש ומובדל כו׳ ואינו כדמיון הנשמה שהיא עצמה מתלבשת בגוף

## S032 · Kindness, judgment and the sweetening of judgments · חסד וגבורה / המתקת הדינים

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F09 The unity of opposites · F11 Darkness turned to light **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 4)

**The glimpse.** Judgment and kindness are not two powers: the strictness that holds back is part of the kindness that builds, and brought back to its root a judgment turns out to be kindness.

**Hold.** 'Judgments are sweetened only at their root' (Tzava'at HaRivash).

**The move.** Open it: judgment is the power that holds back and hides, and that very holding back is the kindness by which the world is built; judgments are separate only as they spread out. The Maggid's picture is speech: the five parts of the mouth are separate and dark until one voice passes through them all and gives them a single life; a picture only, for He has no mouth. You, here, now: the irritation that rises when something goes wrong is judgment spreading out, and love and joy, the Tzava'at HaRivash says, lift the anger to its root, where it is sweetened. The Besht adds that even knowing 'I am in smallness now' sweetens it at its root.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'sweetened only at the root'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Sharpness met at its root rather than fought: firmness with kindness inside it. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** When irritation rises today, say with your lips the Besht's words 'I am in smallness now'. Then do one kind act before you answer, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'at the root'.

**Disagreement kept.** Whether sweetening is contemplation or the tzaddik's act divides the sources: for the Maggid and the Tanya it happens when the unity or the supernal will is revealed in the judgment; for Noam Elimelech and Breslov it is a work of the tzaddik, and in Breslov a practice too (the pidyon, midnight prayer, taking counsel).

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tzava'at HaRivash 132:5 (line 272): שכאשר האהבה והשמחה גוברות וגורמות להכעס והזעם שיעלה למעלה אל שרשו ושם הוא נמתקה כידוע שהדינים אינם נמתקים אלא בשרשם - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 6:2 (line 788): והרי – בחינת גבורה זו וצמצום הזה, הוא גם כן בחינת חסד שהעולם יבנה בו, וזו היא בחינת גבורה הכלולה בחסד - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 49:1 (line 117): אבל כאשר נכנס בהם החיות אזי החיות שבהם אחדות פשוט כי הבל א' מכניס חיות בכולם ונמתקו הדינין

## S033 · Malchus: no king without a people · מלכות

**Face:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation. **Also touches:** F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 6)

**The glimpse.** The distance is on purpose: there is no king without a people who stand apart from him, and He made space, time and your separateness so that He could be King over you.

**Hold.** 'There is no king without a people; am, people, is from omemus: things that are separate, foreign and far from the king' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 7).

**The move.** The picture is a king: many sons or ministers close to him do not make him a king, only a people, far off and separate, does; it is only a picture, since His kingship is one with His Essence, and the space and time it makes are as nothing before Him. So He made the world hidden and set apart, with its space and its time, a full 'something', so that there can be a people. You, here, now, ordinary and far off, are exactly the one His kingship is for, and He is King over the separate ones when they keep His commandments. The Maggid says the delight He would have when Israel crown Him was first in His will.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'no king without a people'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Distance that is not rejection: the far-off one is the one a king reigns over. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say one blessing slowly today, and when your lips reach 'Melech ha-olam', King of the world, crown Him from where you are. Then keep one commandment on purpose, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'no king without a people'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:2 (line 796): כי תכלית בריאת העולם הוא בשביל התגלות מלכותו יתברך, ד״אין מלך בלא עם״, פירוש ״עם״ – מלשון ״עוממות״, שהם דברים נפרדים וזרים ורחוקים ממעלת המלך - Tanya, Part II; Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah 7:6 (line 800): ולפי שמדת מלכותו יתברך מיוחדת במהותו ועצמותו יתברך בתכלית היחוד, כמו שיתבאר, הלכך, גם בחינת המקום והזמן בטילים במציאות ממש לגבי מהותו ועצמותו יתברך - Tanya, Part IV; Iggeret HaKodesh 20:18 (line 1268): רק יהיה היש דבר נפרד מאלקות, בכדי שיהיה המאציל ברוךהוא מלך על כל הנפרדים, עלידי שיקיימו מצותיו שיצוה עליהם

## S034 · The Shechinah · שכינה

**Face:** F07 The home below. **Also touches:** F08 Bitul · F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 6)

**The glimpse.** He dwells in all things to give them life, rests openly on what is set aside for Him, and does not leave even at the lowest point.

**Hold.** 'It is called Shechinah because it dwells and is clothed within all worlds, to give them life and keep them in being' (Tanya, ch. 41).

**The move.** Open it: He dwells in all the worlds to give them life, and rests openly only on what is wholly set aside for Him, so that 'God is One' is revealed in it. Even a great tzaddik is still 'someone' who loves, so the Shechinah rests on him through the mitzvot, which are His will with no hiding in them. You, here, now, even at your lowest: the Rebbe says that when we are at the lowest point He is the one who dwells with us, and He gives the strength to end the exile.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'dwelling within, to give life'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Not alone at the lowest point: the one who dwells with you there is Him. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say with your lips 'ein od milvado'. Then do one mitzvah in the plainest place you spend time today, a blessing in the car or a coin at the checkout, so He rests there openly. During the day, return to: 'He dwells with us'.

**Thin.** Not thin in Chabad; in Radomsk the tie to the unity is our reading, so this meditation keeps to the Chabad line and leaves Radomsk's call to feel the Shechinah's pain as its own center.

**Disagreement kept.** Which level is called Shechinah divides the Rebbeim: for the Alter Rebbe, the beginning of the revelation of the Infinite; for the Mitteler Rebbe, even the line, relative to the Essence; for the Tzemach Tzedek, Malchus as it becomes Atik for Beriah. The Rebbe collects all three; the reconciliation is his.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 41:16 (line 591): הנקרא בשם ״שכינה״, על שם ששוכנת ומתלבשת תוך כל עלמין להחיותן ולקיימן - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 35:5 (line 475): והנה, ענין השראת השכינה, הוא גילוי אלהותו יתברך ואור איןסוף ברוךהוא באיזה דבר, והיינו לומר, שאותו דבר נכלל באור ה׳ ובטל לו במציאות לגמרי - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 29:5 (line 154): שגם כאשר נמצאים אנו בשפל המצב, מ"מ הרי הוא ית' הוא השוכן אתנו

## S035 · The union of the Holy One and His Shechinah · יחוד קודשא בריך הוא ושכינתיה

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F04 Filling and surrounding · F10 Torah and mitzvah **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** When a plain act is joined to the thought of Him, the light that surrounds all and the light that fills all become one, through you.

**Hold.** 'Leshem yichud Kudsha Brich Hu u'Shechintei', for the sake of the union of the Holy One and His Shechinah, in the name of all Israel (said before a mitzvah; Tanya, ch. 41).

**The move.** Open it as the Tanya does: the words mean drawing His light onto the source of your soul and of all Israel's souls, the Shechinah, which gives you the strength for this very word or act. The Holy One, source of Torah and mitzvot, is the light that surrounds all worlds; the Shechinah, source of the soul, is the light that fills them; the mitzvah joins them. You, here, now: the Komarna works bring it to every act, so that thought joined to deed is a union 'face to face', even over a drink of water, if you believe that in every thing there is the being of God.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'for the sake of the union'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** The closeness of an embrace: your plain act and His light, met. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before your next mitzvah, say with your lips 'leshem yichud', for the sake of the union. Then do it, even a blessing over a glass of water, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'thought and deed, one'.

## S036 · The thirteen attributes of mercy · י"ג מדות הרחמים

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F11 Darkness turned to light · F10 Torah and mitzvah **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 4, 5)

**The glimpse.** His great mercy comes from where everything is nothing before Him, and from that height it comes all the way down, measured, to the place of wrongdoing itself, to lift it.

**Hold.** 'Great humility: to lower Himself down even to the place of sins, to carry the sin and lift it, so that darkness is turned to light' (Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim).

**The move.** Likkutei Torah's picture is a king whose very height makes him pity everyone below him; a picture only, for He has no body. So, from where all is as nothing before Him, even the upper worlds, His mercy is very great, and since boundless mercy could not fit the worlds, it comes through thirteen measures that let it be received. You, here, now: it is a humble mercy, lowering itself even to the place of wrongdoing, carrying it and lifting it, so that darkness turns to light. Through it the lower letter of the Name returns to be one with the others.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'He carries it and lifts it'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Being carried: the mercy does not wait above but comes down to where you are. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** The next time the thirteen attributes come in prayer, say them with your lips slowly: 'Hashem, Hashem, E-l rachum v'chanun'. Then give a coin to charity, mercy in your measure. During the day, return to: 'He carries and lifts'.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Likkutei Torah, Nitzavim 4:38 (line 3606): והיא ענוה גדולה ועצומה להשפיל עצמו למטה מטה גם למקום העוונות להיות נושא עון ומגביהו שיהיה אתהפכא חשוכא לנהורא - Likkutei Torah, Eikev 1:2 (line 3041): וככה ממש כביכול באור א״ס ב״ה שהכל בטלים במציאות נגדו ית׳ וכולא ממש כלא חשיבא קמיה ואפי׳ עולמות עליונים כדכתיב הן שמים לא זכו בעיניו ולכן הרחמנות גדולה מאד עליהם - Likkutei Torah, Eikev 1:4 (line 3043): והנה המשכות בחי׳ רחמים רבים הנ״ל הוא ע״י י״ג מדות והיינו שלפי שהם רחמים רבים בלי גבול א״א להתלבש תוך העולמות אם לא ע״י צמצומים דרך י״ג מדות

## S037 · The divine names · שמות הקדושים / הוי' ואלקים

**Face:** F02 The higher and the lower unity. **Also touches:** F03 The hiding for the sake of revelation · F10 Torah and mitzvah **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 6)

**The glimpse.** One Being called by many names: Havayah, who brings everything into being now, and Elokim, the name of nature that shields it; and the name that hides hides nothing before Him.

**Hold.** 'He and His name are one, for His attributes are His names' (Shaar HaYichud VehaEmunah, ch. 6).

**The move.** Open the names: Havayah means He is bringing everything into being now; Elokim, equal in number to 'nature', shields us from it as a sheath shields the sun; and 'Havayah is Elokim', for the name that hides is His kindness and hides nothing before Him. Torah Ohr's picture is a man's name, Avraham or Yaakov, which does not touch what he is; so the names turn toward us, and He Himself is beyond them. You, here, now: the 'nature' around you, the weather, the bus, the price of bread, is the name Elokim, and Havayah is the life inside it.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'He and His name are one'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** Nature no longer standing on its own: behind the name 'nature' is the One bringing it into being now. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Say a blessing today and hear the two names side by side, 'Baruch atah Hashem Elokeinu'; then say with your lips 'Hashem hu ha-Elokim'. During the day, return to: 'Havayah is Elokim'.

**Disagreement kept.** Torah Ohr says the sefiros are only 'His name' and do not touch His Essence; Likkutei Torah says that too, and also that 'He Himself is the Merciful and the Kind'. The entry keeps both.

## S038 · Supernal will and delight · רצון ותענוג העליון

**Face:** F10 Torah and mitzvah. **Also touches:** F07 The home below · F05 The Essence beyond both **Tie:** stated (derivation steps 2, 3, 4, 6, 7)

**The glimpse.** A mitzvah is His will with nothing hiding it, and His will is one with Him; inside that will is His delight, which lies in your wish to please Him.

**Hold.** 'Since all the mitzvot are the will of the Holy One, and He and His will are one … His mitzvot are not for the sake of any other purpose' (the Rebbe, Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus 16).

**The move.** Open it: the world comes from His will too, but through many coverings, so it can stand as a thing apart; a mitzvah is His inner will with no covering, and what lives in it is one with that will. Inside every will is a delight, and the Maggid says the world was made only for His delight in the deeds of the mitzvot, which lies in a person's burning wish to please Him. You, here, now: the coin in your hand for charity, or the candles before Shabbos, has no purpose beyond itself; doing it is the whole point. Yet the Mitteler Rebbe adds that His Essence is separate even from delight; it only shines through it.

**Stay.** Say it again slowly: 'He and His will are one'. When the mind drifts, come back to the line without arguing with the stray thought.

**From inside.** An embrace: holding His will with nothing between. If nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.

**Back into the day.** Before a mitzvah today, say with your lips 'asher kideshanu b'mitzvosav', who made us holy with His commandments. Then give one coin to charity only because He wants it, in your measure. During the day, return to: 'He and His will are one'.

**Disagreement kept.** Which is higher, will or delight, and whether He receives delight, divides the sources: for the Tzemach Tzedek and the Mitteler Rebbe delight is the inside of will, yet the Essence is separate even from delight; for the Maggid the world was made for His delight.

**Anchors** (the entry's own passages, verified there): - Inyanah Shel Toras HaChassidus, 16:1 (line 84): ומכיון שכל המצות, ענינם הוא – רצון הקב"ה, שהוא ית' ורצונו אחד, הרי כשם שבו ית' אין שייך לומר שהוא בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת ח"ו, כן גם מצותיו אינם בשביל איזו תכלית אחרת - Tanya, Part I; Likkutei Amarim 35:4 (line 474): מה שאין כן המצות, שהן פנימית רצונו יתברך, ואין שם הסתר פנים כלל – אין החיות שבהם דבר נפרד בפני עצמו כלל, אלא הוא מיוחד ונכלל ברצונו יתברך - Maggid Devarav leYaakov 63:1 (line 139): הנה העולם לא נברא רק בשביל שעשוע הקב"ה ותענוג כביכול במעש' המצות
