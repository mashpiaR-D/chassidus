This is chapter 3 of Pirkei Avot in the Maharal's commentary. It opens with "look at three things and you will not come to sin" and closes with the straight road of halacha (Jewish law). In between is some of the Maharal's clearest teaching on how the One relates to the many.

Five teachings matter most for the guide:

- **The evil inclination is twoness.** "שהיצר הוא השניות" (428): "for the inclination is twoness." From G-d's side, who is One, it should not exist. So the Shema (the declaration that G-d is One) drives it away.
- **See yourself as the caused, clinging to the Cause.** "כאשר מחשב האדם עצמו שהוא עלול, ויש לו התדבקות בעלה, אינו בא לידי חטא" (443): "when a person thinks of himself as caused, and cleaves to the Cause, he does not come to sin."
- **One from the Maker's side, many from the receiver's side.** What flows from Him "has oneness from the side of the Maker, and it divides from the side of what receives" (614). And "כי האחד הוא הכל כאשר אין זולתו" (616): "for the One is all when there is none besides Him."
- **He is the Place of the world.** "ופירושו שהוא יתברך מקומו של עולם" (495): "He is the place of the world." All places are under the Place.
- **The image of G-d is a light on the face.** "כי דבק בפנים שלו זיו, וניצוץ עליון דבק בו, ודבר זה הוא צלם אלקים" (634): "a shine cleaves to his face, a spark from above cleaves to him, and this is the image of G-d."

## 2.1 The evil inclination is twoness

"ויצר הרע מתגבר באדם מכח גבהות הלב" (424): "the evil inclination grows strong in a person from the height of his heart." It needs a self that feels important: "כי אין יצר הרע לאדם רק כאשר יחשוב עצמו בריאה חשובה" (433): "a person has an evil inclination only when he thinks himself an important creature." But "אבל כאשר רוחו ולבו נשברה בקרבו, אין כאן יצר הרע שיהיה מסית" (424): "when his spirit and heart are broken within him, there is no evil inclination to entice."

Then the root: "ומצד השם יתברך אשר הוא אחד, אין ראוי להיות נמצא היצר הרע, שזה נחשב שניות" (428): "from the side of G-d, who is One, the evil inclination should not exist, for it counts as twoness." And "שהיצר הוא השניות" (428): "for the inclination is twoness." So the Shema, which says He is One, pushes it away. "כי מצד השם יתברך הסתלקות יצר הרע לגמרי" (445): "for from G-d's side the evil inclination is fully removed."

## 2.2 The caused and the Cause

The Maharal's main reading of Akavya's three things: a person comes from nothing and goes to loss, and he stands before the King. So he is the caused, and G-d is the Cause. "כאשר מחשב האדם עצמו שהוא עלול, ויש לו התדבקות בעלה, אינו בא לידי חטא" (443): "when a person thinks of himself as caused, and cleaves to the Cause, he does not come to sin." Why? "כי אין החטא והיצר הרע נמצא רק כאשר האדם הוא סר מן העלה" (443): "sin and the evil inclination are found only when a person turns away from the Cause."

The same frame explains Eden (659-661). Before the sin, "ותמיד דבק העלול בעלה שלו, והוא הטוב בעצמו" (660): "the caused always cleaved to its Cause, which is the Good itself." So man knew only good. "כי האדם יש עלה עליו, וצריך שיהיה דבק בעלתו יתברך, שהוא הטוב" (660): "a person has a Cause over him and must cleave to his Cause, who is the Good." To know good and evil is to have turned from the Cause. Even freedom is a gift that is not simply good for us: "יותר טוב שיהיה ברשות העלה" (665): "it is better to be in the Cause's power."

And the Cause wants the caused: "כי אין עלה בלא העלול, לכך השם יתברך חפץ להיות עם אשר הוא עלה להם" (534): "there is no cause without the caused, so G-d wants to be with those whose Cause He is." Wisdom lasts only from Him: "יש אל העלול קיום מצד עלתו" (556): "the caused has lasting from its Cause," and "והוא יתברך בית החכמה" (557): "He is the house of wisdom."

## 2.3 Man created single

"לפיכך נברא האדם יחידי, שעתה מורה העולם, שהוא עולם אחד, על פועל אחד" (455): "so man was created single, so that the world, being one world, would point to one Maker." A single origin "מאחד אותם ומקשר אותם עד שהם אחד" (454): "unites them and binds them until they are one." If there had been two first people, "each one wants to be all" (459) and they would swallow each other.

## 2.4 Torah is one because it is necessary

The world begins with bet (two). The Ten Commandments begin with alef (one). "אבל התורה היא אחת" (469): "but the Torah is one." It is one because it follows from His truth, "והמחויב הוא מיוחד" (469): "and what is necessary is single." "ולכך התורה מביאה האדם אל השם יתברך" (468): "so the Torah brings a person to G-d." "כי השם יתברך עם התורה לגמרי" (470): "for G-d is fully with the Torah."

## 2.5 The Place of the world

Three who eat and speak Torah eat at G-d's table (479). "ושלשה יש להם התאחדות כאילו הם דבר אחד" (483): "three have a union as if they were one thing": two lines stay apart, three close a shape. "ולפני השם יתברך אין שייך רק שלחן אחד" (486): "before G-d there can be only one table."

G-d is called Makom (Place). "ופירושו שהוא יתברך מקומו של עולם" (495): "He is the place of the world." "וכל המקומות תחת המקום, הוא השם יתברך, אשר הוא כולל כל המקומות" (495): "all places are under the Place, who is G-d, who holds all places." The world is in Him; He is not in the world as in a place.

## 2.6 Ten, and the One who is all

With ten the Shechina rests, because ten is whole: after ten, counting starts again (517). "והוא יתברך שהוא שלם, הוא נמצא עם השלם" (518): "He who is whole is found with what is whole." But He is also with five, three, two and one, "ומפני כי הוא יתברך הכל" (521): "because He is all." Five is four and a middle, and "כי כל אמצעי, בשביל שהוא אמצעי, מקשר ומחבר הכל" (522): "every middle, because it is the middle, binds and joins all." "והוא יתברך כולל הכל ומקשר הכל" (522): "and He holds all and binds all."

The deepest lines come with the tithe (the tenth part given to G-d, 612-619):

- "שיש בו אחדות מצד הפועל, ומתחלק מצד בחינת המושפע" (614): "it has oneness from the side of the Maker, and it divides from the side of what receives." - "כי כל אשר הושפע מן השם יתברך נוטה מן האחדות אל הרבוי, כאשר הוא יתברך בלבד אחד" (614): "all that flows from G-d leans from oneness to many, since He alone is One." - Nine is division; the tenth stands for all. "כי האחד הוא הכל כאשר אין זולתו, והבן זה מאד" (616): "for the One is all when there is none besides Him; understand this well." - "כי הנקודה היא אחת באמצע העיגול מקשר כל העיגול, עד שהוא אחד" (618): "the point is one in the middle of the circle and binds the whole circle until it is one." - So "כי הכלל הוא ברכה, שאין בכלל חסרון" (613): "the whole is blessing, for the whole has no lack." Giving the tenth ties the nine to the One, and that brings blessing.

## 2.7 "The whole earth is full of His glory"

"Give Him of His, for you and yours are His." G-d is not removed from this world: "לכך כל מה שהוא אל האדם הוא אל השם יתברך" (536): "so all that is a person's is G-d's." The verse "the whole earth is full of His glory" means the fullness itself "הוא כבודו, ולכבודו נברא" (540): "is His glory, and for His glory it was made." That is why we bless before we benefit: it is holy until the blessing lets it out for use. And "ומידך נתנו לך" (541): "and from Your hand we have given You": the wealth was never in our hand.

## 2.8 The world as a garment

"העולם הזה הגשמי הוא כמו מלבוש אל עולם הנבדל" (630): "this physical world is like a garment to the separate world." A garment shows the shape of the one who wears it, not his true self. So "כי כאשר יתבונן האדם בציור גופו, יכול האדם לעמוד לדעת את השם יתברך" (630): "when a person looks closely at the form of his body, he can come to know G-d." This is "from my flesh I see G-d" (Job 19:26).

## 2.9 Oneness in judgment

"כי מצד שהוא טוב רוצה בטוב, ואינו חפץ ברע" (669): "because He is good He wants the good and does not want evil." Even judgment comes from His goodness: "אצל השם יתברך אף הרע הוא מן צד הטוב שבו" (670): "with G-d even the bad comes from the side of the good in Him." So one blesses on bad news too. And "ומן הטוב לא יצא רק הטוב" (680): "and from the Good only good comes out": all is prepared for the feast.

- **MH-S35 "Caused before the Cause" (443, 660).** Inside: I do not stand on my own. I came from nothing and I go on only from Him. I feel myself held from above, the way a ray is held by the sun. In that state there is no room for the pull to sin. How to enter: say "I am caused; He is my Cause," and rest there until it is felt, not only known. It differs from S65 Given right now (a gift in this moment) and from MH-S15 Held from the other side. Here the stress is on the direction: I lean up to my Cause.
- **MH-S36 "The light on the face" (634).** Inside: looking at a person and seeing, past the features, a shine that is not bodily, "a separate, G-dly light." How to enter: before you speak to someone, look at his face and say: the image of G-d is here. It differs from S86 The other held. That is holding someone in care. This is seeing G-d's light in him.

## 4.1 Caused and Cause (`mh_caused_and_cause`, new)

When a pull toward sin comes, stop. Say three lines from Akavya: where did I come from (nothing), where am I going (loss), before whom will I stand (the King). Then the Maharal's line: I am caused, He is my Cause, I cling to Him (443). The pull has no ground when the self is not "an important creature" (433). Guard: "because he came from a putrid drop, let him do mitzvot and gain a rise, and not stay in his lowness" (434): "בשביל שבא מטפה סרוחה יעשה מצות, ויקנה לו מעלה, ולא ישאר בשפלותו." Lowliness must lead to doing, never to despair.

## 4.2 Shema against twoness (`mh_shema_against_twoness`, extended)

Already in the Be'er HaGolah report. Here is its reason: the inclination is twoness (428), and from His side it is fully gone (445). When torn two ways, say the Shema slowly, and on "One" let the two pulls fall into one will.

## 4.3 Learn with another, out loud (`mh_learn_with_another`, new)

"אצל היחיד הדבור והמחשבה שוה" (466): "for one alone, speech and thought are the same." With two, the word goes out and becomes something between them, and the Shechina is there. "ובשנים יש צלם אלקים ביותר" (506): "in two the image of G-d is more." Practice: learn with a partner and say the words aloud.

## 4.4 G-d's table (`mh_gods_table`, new)

Say words of Torah at every meal. Three who do so eat "at G-d's table" (479-486), and three who bless together are one thing. Practice: at a meal with others, one short teaching, then bless together.

## 4.5 Bless before you take (`mh_bless_before_taking`, new)

All is His, and the fullness of the earth is His glory (536, 540). A blessing lets it out for use. Practice: before any food or pleasure, a short blessing, said as "this is Yours, and You give it."

## 4.6 From my flesh I see G-d (`mh_from_my_flesh`, new)

The body is a garment that shows the shape of the one who wears it (630). Practice: sit still. Look at your hand, how it is formed and how it moves at your will. Say: as my soul is in my body and not seen, so He is in the world. Stop at the likeness; the Maharal says the garment shows only the shape, "not the true self" (630).

## 4.7 See the light on the face (`mh_see_the_image`, new)

The image of G-d is a light on the face (634), and shaming a friend "destroys the image of G-d" (583). Practice: before you speak to someone, look at his face and name the light. Then speak to it. This is MH-S36.

## 4.8 Give the tenth to tie the nine (`mh_tenth_to_the_one`, new)

Nine is division; the tenth is the One who is all (614-616). Giving a tenth binds the rest to the One, and "the whole is blessing" (613). Practice: when you give charity, say: this ties the rest to Him.

## 4.9 The middle point (`mh_circle_and_middle`, extended)

Already in the Be'er HaGolah report. Add 618: the one point in the middle binds the whole circle until it is one. Picture yourself and all that pulls at you as a circle, with Him as the point in the middle.

## 4.10 Bless the true Judge (`mh_goodness_in_judgment`, new)

On hard news, say "Blessed is the true Judge." The Maharal: even the bad comes from the side of the good in Him (670). It is not a denial of pain. It is placing the pain inside His goodness. Guard: this is not a way to push down grief or to skip real help.

## 5. The big human questions

**Where does temptation come from?** From a self that feels important (433), and from twoness, a split in the heart (428). When a person sees himself as caused and clings to the Cause, temptation has no place (443).

**Why am I free, if it is better to be held?** Freedom is likeness to G-d: "he is in his own power, like G-d who does what He wills" (658). But the Maharal says it is not simply for our good (665). Freedom is a dignity, and cleaving is the good.

**If G-d is One, why is the world many?** From His side all is one; from the receiver's side it divides (614). He is all when there is none besides Him (616).

**Where is G-d?** He is the Place of the world (495). The earth's fullness is His glory (540). And there is a light on every face (634).

**Why do bad things happen?** He wants the good (669). Even judgment is from the side of the good in Him (670). All is prepared for the feast (680).

**Is the body bad?** No. Bodily mitzvot please Him (582), and "this world is a corridor to the World to Come" (582). The body is a garment that shows Him (630).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **Twoness and the Shema** (428) is close to Tanya ch. 12 and 13 on the struggle of the two souls, and to the teaching that saying the Shema with thought makes the animal soul yield. The Maharal's word is "twoness" itself: the inclination is not a second power, it is the split.
- **Cause and caused** (443, 534, 556). The Tanya (ch. 3-4) uses light and its source; the Maharal uses cause and caused. Both say the caused has no lasting apart from its Cause (556). The Maharal adds a warm side: "there is no cause without the caused," so He wants to be with us (534).
- **One from the Maker's side, many from the receiver's** (614). This is the Maharal's steady answer to what tzimtzum answers in Chabad. Gate of Unity (ch. 7) says the many is a hiding, and from His side there is no change. The Maharal says the leaning to many is "from the side of what receives." The two meet: the many is real to the receiver, and not a change in Him.
- **"The One is all when there is none besides Him"** (616). This is as near as the Maharal comes to the Ramak's formula. He says the One "is all." He does not add, with the Ramak, "and all is not He," but his "from the receiver's side" does the same work: the many is not He as they are many.
- **Makom** (495). "He is the place of the world, and the world is not His place" is Midrash Bereishit Rabbah 68. The Maharal reads it as "He holds all places." Chabad (Gate of Unity ch. 7) uses the same saying for the Or Ein Sof filling all. The guide can join them here.
- **The Rambam's attributes.** "Only G-d is simple with the utmost simplicity" (635) is the Rambam's language. But the Maharal speaks of His will, His wanting the good (669), His being with the caused (534). He does not limit us to what He is not.
- **"The fullness of the earth is His glory"** (540) matches Tanya ch. 33 and 36: the lower world as where His glory is shown, and the joy of seeing that the world is filled with Him.
- **The light on the face** (634) meets the Chabad teaching of the G-dly soul in every Jew and of loving one's fellow (Tanya ch. 32). The Maharal says it of every man made in the image. The guide should keep it universal.
