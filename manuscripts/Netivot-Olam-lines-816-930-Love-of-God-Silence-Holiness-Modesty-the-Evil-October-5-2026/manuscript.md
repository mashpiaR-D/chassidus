## 2.1 Love is possible because He is One

How can there be love between a person of flesh and the Infinite? Love is between likes. The Maharal's first answer: "כי כל דבר הוא אוהב דבר אשר הוא השלמתו והוא ית' השלמת האדם" (816, Ahavat Hashem 1:1): "for everything loves what completes it, and He is the completion of man."

His second and deeper answer comes from Rabbi Akiva, who died drawing out the word "echad":

- "כי הכל שב אליו ית' ואין דבר זולתו רק השם ית' והוא אחד ואין זולתו" (817): "for all returns to Him, and there is nothing besides Him but G-d, and He is One and there is none besides Him." - "ומצד שהוא ית' אחד ואין זולתו הכל שב אליו, ובזה נשמתו הוא שב אל הש"י ומפקיד נשמתו ביד הש"י" (817): "and because He is One and there is none besides Him, all returns to Him; and in this his soul returns to G-d and he entrusts his soul into G-d's hand."

That is why one who reads the Shema at bedtime need not also say "Into Your hand I entrust my spirit": saying "One" already is entrusting.

And love of G-d is more complete than any human love. Two human lovers each keep their own being. Love of G-d goes "עד שאין לאדם מציאות בעצמו ובזה הוא מתדבק לגמרי בו" (817): "until the person has no existence in himself, and in this he cleaves to Him wholly." And "כי מאחר שאין זולתו איך יהיה סר מאתו" (820): "for since there is none besides Him, how could one turn away from Him?" The Shema's "One" leads straight into "you shall love".

## 2.2 One Source, and it is good

Why bless on the bad as on the good? "להודיע כי אין כאן רק התחלה אחת טובה" (823): "to make known that there is only one Source here, and it is good." The heretics saw good and bad in the world and said there must be two sources. The Maharal: the bad is from the side of the receiver. The One who is good hates evil and removes it, and that removal feels bad to the one it touches.

## 2.3 The evil inclination makes the world look two

The Path of the Evil Inclination has the book's sharpest link between inner work and unity. When there is an evil inclination opposite a good one, "נראה שיש כאן ב' התחלות ח"ו" (895): "it looks, G-d forbid, as if there were two sources." So when the yetzer will not leave, read the Shema, "כי השם ית' שהוא אחד מסלק היצר הרע" (895): "for G-d, who is One, removes the evil inclination."

"He will be One on that day": is He not One now? He is, but "לא נראה אחדותו כאשר יש רע בעולם" (896): "His oneness is not seen while there is evil in the world." So one who overcomes his yetzer "הוא מאחד הש"י לגמרי" (908): "makes G-d wholly one."

## 2.4 Evil has no being

The yetzer is "renewed every day" because it is not part of the person and has no being of its own: "כי אין מציאות אליו כי אין לרע מציאות" (891): "for it has no existence, since evil has no existence." What has no being must be renewed to act at all.

## 2.5 The light is one; the righteous is one

The world was made for one righteous person, since "והאור הזה הפשוט הוא אחד כי הדבר הנבדל לא יתרבה ולא יתחלק כלל" (912): "and this simple light is one, for what is separate does not multiply or divide at all." Many only comes from a material carrier. So "הטובים הם על דרך אחד והרעים על דרכים הרבה" (916): "the good are on one road and the bad on many roads." The straight line is one; the ways off it are endless.

**Proposed MH-S12 "Entrusting in One".** Inside: saying "One" I let go of my soul into His hand, as one does at night. How to enter: at bedtime, say the Shema and on "echad" rest the whole self in Him, as Rabbi Akiva did (817). Source: Netivot Olam 817; Gevurot Hashem's six sides (`mh_six_sides`).

## 4.1 Echad as entrusting (new: `mh_echad_entrust`)

817: the Shema at bedtime replaces "Into Your hand". How: lie down. Read the Shema slowly. On "echad", let the breath out and give the soul back to Him for the night. Nothing is needed after that. Source: 817.

## 4.2 Bless on the bad (`heart_after`, `discern`)

823: say "Blessed is the true Judge" and mean: there is one Source and it is good. The Maharal adds Rabbi Akiva's "all that the Merciful does is for the good" (824): the donkey, the rooster and the lamp he lost stood in for his body, soul and mind, and saved him. This is not denying pain. It is refusing to split the world in two.

## 4.3 Read the Shema against the yetzer (`letters`, `hold`)

895: when the pull does not leave, first stir the good against it, then learn, then read the Shema, then remember the day of death. The Shema works because it declares one Source; the yetzer lives on the look of two.

## 4.4 Do not fight the yetzer as a rival (new: `mh_not_a_rival`)

"אין חבוש מתיר עצמו מבית אסורים" (900): "a prisoner cannot free himself from prison." And "ושנים שהם מתנגדים זה לזה גובר האחד על השני" (901): "and when two oppose each other, one overcomes the other." One who sets himself up as the yetzer's rival ("an arrow in Satan's eye") gets attacked. Fight with all your strength, but say "May the Merciful One rebuke you, Satan": the removal is His. How: when pulled, do not argue with the pull. Turn and ask Him to remove it, then act. Source: 900-901.

## 4.5 Starve the craving (`hold`)

"משביעו רעב מרעיבו שבע" (888): "feed it and it is hungry, starve it and it is full." Feeding desire shows and deepens lack. Starving it makes one like the patriarchs, who were whole and had no yetzer (887). This is not fasting for its own sake (the Maharal elsewhere rejects self-torment, 1056); it is not feeding a craving.

## 4.6 The embryo's posture (new: `mh_folded_notebook`)

"כי האדם בפרט בבריאתו כפנקס מקופל" (927): "for man in particular, at his forming, is like a folded notebook." Nothing sticks out; the body is gathered like one point. The Maharal reads each detail as a way to live (928-929): hands on the temples (think over every act), heels to the seat (kneel like a servant before his Master), "וראשו מונח בין שתי ברכיו" (929): "and his head rests between his two knees" (no pride), mouth closed (eat for need, not for pleasure). How: sit gathered, hands to the temples, head bowed, and review the day's acts before Him. Source: 927-929. Close to `cheshbon`.

## 4.7 Silence that stays with people (`quieting`)

"חושך אמריו יודע דעת" (836): "one who holds back his words knows knowledge." But also "כי צריך האדם שיהיה דעתו מעורב עם הבריות" (838): "for a person's mind must be mixed with people." Silence is not withdrawal; it is speaking only when the word brings something good into being.

## 5. The big human questions

**Why we want what is bad.** "האדם הזה הוא נברא חסר בעצמו וכל חסרון הוא רע ולפיכך משתוקק האדם אל הרע תמיד" (887): "this person was created lacking in himself, and all lack is bad, and so a person always longs for the bad." Craving is not who you are. It is the voice of lack. The more whole a person is, the less it pulls. This is a gentle and freeing account of temptation.

**Why the good feel it more.** The yetzer is strongest in the scholar and the one who reaches high, because a high level cannot be held fully in this world and the gap is a lack (887). To the righteous the yetzer looks like a mountain; to the wicked like a hair (890). Feeling the struggle is a sign of height, not failure.

**Love that does not depend.** "כי האהבה שאינה תלויה בדבר אף אם בטל הדבר לא בטל האהבה" (835): "for love that does not hang on anything, even if the thing is gone, the love is not gone." Serving for reward is a servant's way; serving because He was good to me is higher; the full love stays when trouble comes.

**Love as one's nature.** "כי מה שמשתוקק האש לעלות למעלה הוא דבר עצמי אליו ואין בטול לדבר עצמי" (824): "for the fire's longing to rise is of its very self, and what is of the self cannot be cancelled." Love of G-d is like that in a person. Awe can be worn down by burdens; love cannot.

**Holiness.** "כי אין הקדושה רק שהוא נבדל מדברים הגופנים" (844): "for holiness is nothing but being separate from bodily things." Not rejecting the body but not being swallowed by it: "sanctify yourself in what is permitted".

**Good and evil are real.** "כי המדות אינם בהסכמה מבני אדם" (866): "for the traits are not a matter of human agreement." Good traits are good in themselves. We could learn modesty from the cat and honesty from the ant. This is universal.

**Wisdom and hiddenness.** "כי החכמה בעצמה היא צנועה ונסתרת" (864): "for wisdom in itself is modest and hidden." So learn and give in modesty.

**Humility without self-hatred.** At birth the soul is made to swear: "ואמר אף אם כל העולם יאמרו לו שהוא צדיק יהיה בעיניו כמו רשע" (930): "and even if the whole world tells him he is righteous, let him be in his own eyes like one who is wicked." The Maharal gives the reason: a person in this world is never fully in act; there is always more good to gain. Measured against what he could still become, he is not yet righteous. This is a call to growth, not a verdict. For the guide it must be framed that way, never as "I am bad" (see S11).

## 6. With Chabad (ours)

- **"There is nothing besides Him, so all returns to Him"** (817) is the Gate of Unity's claim, and the Tanya's ch. 20. The Maharal reaches it through Rabbi Akiva's "echad", not through tzimtzum or the letters of creation.
- **Love with no being of one's own** (817) is the Tanya's "love like water" and "bittul" joined: in Chabad terms it is the love that comes from knowing one is nothing before Him. The Maharal says human lovers keep their own being; love of G-d does not. The Tanya says the same about "kiss" love (ch. 45-46).
- **Love from essence, fear not** (824) is the opposite order from some Chabad texts that call awe the root. The Maharal holds that love, like fire rising, is the soul's own nature. The Tanya (ch. 18-19) agrees: the hidden love is the soul's inheritance and cannot be lost.
- **The yetzer makes two sources seem real** (895) is not in the Tanya in these words. The Tanya's "the other side" is given life by G-d against its will; the Maharal says it has no being and must be renewed each day. Both deny it any standing of its own.
- **Rambam**: 836-839 quotes the Rambam's five kinds of speech at length and approves, but adds that idle talk is actually forbidden. 863 on the holy tongue argues with the Guide: the tongue is not holy because it lacks crude words; it lacks crude words because it is holy.
