## The shape of the range

These seventeen maamarim are one argument. It asks what makes the difference between Tohu and Tikkun, and how opposites can become one. The answer the Rashab builds step by step is *bitul*, and the root of bitul is the Essence. That is why F05 (the Essence) and F08 (bitul) lead the count by a wide margin. F12 (knowing) comes third, because the second half of the range is a long study of how knowledge works: hearing and seeing, comprehension and sight, speech and the face. Each is used to show how much of the Essence a revelation carries.

1. **Incense, scent and teshuvah (1743-1858).** Sacrifices reach only the outer Keter. Incense reaches the inner Keter. Teshuvah from the depths reaches the Essence itself. Hidden Chochmah is the one intermediary through which the emanated and the souls can reach the Essence.
2. **Exaltation and existence (1859-1974).** Two kinds of exaltation: self-raising, which must cancel the other, and essential exaltation, which gives the other a place though it has none of its own. This is the range's clearest statement of the higher and lower unity (1915-1922).
3. **Mind, emotion and inclusion (1975-2141).** Surrounding and inner light, Shabbat, the stages of growth, how the mind weakens and then includes the emotions. It ends with a unity "without head or end".
4. **The new Mah, the face and the three bitul (2142-2315).** Speech against the face, hearing against sight, the new Mah that "breaks through", Moshe as the one who unites existence and bitul, and the three bitul of light: *ayin*, *ein zulatcha*, *efes biltecha*.

## Shemini Atzeres, Noach, Lech Lecha, Vayera (1743-1858): how far each service reaches

**Bitul is the measure of light (1743-1756).** In holiness the light is strongest where bitul is greatest. Atzilut shines most because its vessels are wholly batel, "included still in the Ein Sof": > דבאצי' הוא בחי' תוקף האור והגילוי לפי שהכלים דאצי' הן בבחי' תכלית הביטול באוא"ס (line 1748)

True bitul does not feel itself at all: > וכ"ה בקדושה ביטול עצמי ופנימי הוא שאינו מרגיש כלל שהוא בטל (line 1744)

The other side is the mirror image. Where holy light is strong, there is no room for selfhood (1749, F08, F11). The incense removes the "taste" for worldly things at its essence. That takes service with the soul's surrounding and essential powers (1751). It draws "oil", the essential light "utterly apart from the order of worlds" (1752).

**Sacrifice, incense, Avraham (1758-1787).** Sacrifices are "bread": they strengthen the inner life. Incense is "scent": it restores the soul from its very bond with the body (1758). The second refinement, from above, makes the sparks "Godliness itself": > והבירור השני דשם מ"ה מלמעלמ"ט הוא שנכלל בקדושת האלקות דאצי' ונעשה אלקות ממש (line 1764)

The bond through Atik joins Ein Sof to worlds by a separating intermediary. With Israel it is different: > משא"כ ההתקשרות עם נש"י הוא בחי' התקשרות עצם בעצם (line 1784)

After the flood a wholly new light was needed. Only self-sacrifice that nullifies one's very essence draws it (1785).

**Unlimited love and the nose of Atik (1789-1818).** Reasoned love settles in the heart, quenches its thirst and leaves the nature unchanged. The will of wills overflows the heart and changes the nature at its essence (1793-1805). The Ein Sof light before Atzilut can shine in the lower worlds as it does in Atzilut (1801). True cleaving is in the Torah and mitzvot that come after prayer: > הרי עיקר הדבקות והגילוי הוא בקיום המצות (line 1794)

Yom Kippur's incense is teshuvah. The bitterness of distance reaches higher than delight: > דעומק ופנימי' הנפש מגיע בבחי' עומק ופנימי' א"ס (line 1812)

**Hidden Chochmah as the one intermediary (1820-1858).** Atik is part of the Essence and Arich is will, so neither can mediate. Hidden Chochmah is wisdom like the emanated and yet hidden in essence. That is why it can join them to the Essence: > ועי"ז מגיע גם לבחי' העצמו' שלמע' מבחי' ממוצע (line 1823)

Its covering is an *essential* concealment. The delight itself becomes wisdom and is hidden by that very becoming (1830). From it comes exile's darkness, and its cause is love: > והיינו דסבת הגבו' שהוא ההסתר הגדול הוא האה' עצמי' (line 1834)

> והו"ע נתאווה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה כו', שהוא גילוי בחי' עצמו' א"ס ממש בבחי' פנימי' הקו (line 1838)

The soul came down to show the body the Ein Sof light through creation from nothing. Things perish, so they are not self-existent (1843-1846). When this becomes as certain as sight, the animal soul is nullified in its essence: > דכאשר אצלו מתאמת ענין התהוות היש מאין ואחדות ה' וכן כל עניני אלקי בבחי' ראי' חושי' כו', ה"ה מתבטל בעצם מהותו (line 1846)

In the future grasp will reach the essence itself, "the true yesh" (1852-1853). The body will be the surrounding Ein Sof and the soul will receive from it (1850).

## Bamidbar and Shavuot (1859-1974): two exaltations, and the truth of "Havayah is one"

**Torah is His craftsman's tool (1860-1880).** The Torah draws "the King's delight in His Essence" into every level (1862). Atzilut exists "so that they may know Him" (1866). Mah, the name of the line, joins opposites: > דענין ש' מ"ה הוא לעשות התכללו' והתחברות ההפכים (line 1873)

**Tohu and the lowly (1881-1889).** Tohu broke because each sefirah was a king, a thing in itself. Tikkun's traits make room for their opposites through bitul (1883). Only bitul is a vessel for the Ein Sof light: > ובד"כ כ"ד שבבחי' מציאות א"ז כלי לאוא"ס כ"א בחי' הביטול דוקא הוא כלי לגילוי אוא"ס ב"ה (line 1887)

**The core of the range (1892-1922).** Existence begins with the tzimtzum: > דבאוא"ס שלפה"צ לא יש ענין המציאות כלל (line 1892)

The Rashab then asks why the exalted cannot bear the other. His answer: the cause is not that the other exists. It is that the exalted one is himself an existence. Essential exaltation has no existence of its own, and before it "truly all is as nothing": > אבל כאשר הרוממות הוא בבחי' בלי מציאות בעצמו אז באמת כל דבר כלא וכאין קמי' וכן נראה כל דבר בבחי' העדר המציאות (line 1894)

The same exaltation, as it spreads, gives place to everything. The place is given, not owned: > דזה שנותן לו מקום אין זה שהדבר תופס מקום אצלו, דבאמת אין הדבר מצ"ע תופס מקום כלל רק מה שהוא נותן לו מקום וערך (line 1916)

Then he applies this to God, in the range's plainest statement of the two unities: > דבאמת כל העולמות אינם במציאות כלל, ומה שהן במציאות זהו רק מה שהאוא"ס נותן להם מקום ומציאות אבל לא שהן בבחי' מציאות מצ"ע (line 1918)

> משא"כ בידיעתו ית' מה שנתהוו הוא רק שהוא ית' נותן להם מקום דבאמת אינם במציאות כלל (line 1918)

> וביחוד דמלמעלמ"ט הוא שהוי' הוא אלקי' והיינו שמציאות הנבראים הוא שהן אלקות (line 1919)

> וזהו אמיתית היחוד דהוי' אחד שהעולמות נבראו ומ"מ ה"ה בבחי' אחד לפי שהם כלולים בעצמותו ובטלים ומיוחדים (line 1920)

"Elokim spoke" at Sinai because the higher knowledge was first revealed there. Elokim is still present, but only as the Ein Sof giving place. Kingship follows the same pattern. Real royal might is utter bitul, as with David (1912). Essential exaltation is "absence of existence" (1908).

**Why the higher gives to the lower (1924-1974).** Tohu would not receive, because receiving implies lack. Tikkun's bitul feels that each part is incomplete alone, and this comes from bitul to the Essence's own wholeness: > והיינו מצד עצם הביטול לבחי' שלימות העצמית דעצמות א"ס שיהי' בכל ההפכים דוקא (line 1934)

Light descends into vessels from bitul to the Intent, and so it gains: > דאמיתית הכוונה הוא להיות התלבשות האור בכלי כו' דלא לתהו בראה אלא לשבת יצרה (line 1947)

> וזהו אנכי הוי' אלקיך שע"י יחוד אורות וכלים בבחי' התיישבות וגילוי בבחי' או"פ עי"ז אנכי ה' אלקיך מאיר גילוי בחי' אנכי (line 1950)

Drawing the Essence loses nothing: > דכשם שבהמקבל הרי כשאתה תופס במקצת מן העצם אתה תופס בכולו, כמו"כ בהעצמות אין בזה שום חסרון ח"ו בזה שנמשך (line 1965)

## Naso to Korach (1975-2141): mind, emotion, and a unity without head or end

**Surrounding light as the Essence revealing itself (1978-2012).** > כ"א הוא בחי' התפשטות והתגלות העצמות, ובבחי' המשכת האור לאחר הצמצום הוא שהעצמות מגלה אור (line 1981)

The tzimtzum did not touch that light. It only hid it from the worlds (1980). On Shabbat the surrounding enters within, and the higher knowledge is served: > וכך הנה העולם נראה ליש ודבר, ובאמת הוא בטל במציאות לגבי אור הוי' המהווה אותו (line 2000)

> וזהו ביטול ההגשמה שהכל הוא אלקות (line 2001)

He sets 'yachid' above 'echad': > דאחד הוא בסדר השתל' שכולל ו"ק ויחיד הוא בחי' עצמות אוא"ס שלמע' מהשתל' (line 2004)

> אמנם ש' הוי' דלעילא הוא בחי' עצמות אוא"ס שלמע' מהשתל' דאינו שייך שם שום מדה והגבלה כלל ה"ז בחי' יחיד שמיוחד בעצם (line 2006)

**Inner light makes, and needs vessels (2013-2033).** Actual creation comes from the inner light, and only through a vessel: > דהאור בעצם אינו בערך להוות מציאות יש רק ע"י הכלי (line 2016)

The vessel's descent is the Essence's own intent (2027). Both light and vessel give only through bitul (2028).

**Mind and heart (2034-2093).** Emotions are the soul's essence and are stronger than the mind. Yet love comes only from knowing: > דאיך יהי' לו אהב' לאלקות כאשר אינו יודע את אלקות (line 2057)

> דהעיקר הוא שההתבוננות איך שהוי' אלקים כולא חד יהי' בבחי' השבה אל הלב שיאיר ויורגש הענין בלבו דוקא (line 2062)

The divine soul's intellect grasps "how every creature truly is the Divine nothing in its very being" (2072). Creation holds two opposites: > ולכן יש בענין הבריאה שני הפכים דהרי ההתהוות צ"ל בבחי' קירוב ובבחי' שייכות האין אל היש כו', ובהיפך הוא בדרך העלם והסתר הבורא מהנברא (line 2085)

**The mind weakens, then includes (2094-2141).** Chochmah weakens the emotions by itself, because the true "ayin" rests in it: > ואמנם ג"ז מה שיש בחכ' בחי' הביטול זהו מפני שבחכ' מאיר מבחי' אין אמיתי שהוא בחי' עצמות אוא"ס (line 2103)

> וזהו ג"כ מה שהחכ' הוא בבחי' התאחדות ממש באוא"ס דאיהו וחיוהי חד בבחי' חד ממש (line 2104)

In will, loving good and rejecting its opposite are one will (2114). Suffering in this world is not true distance, because the surrounding Ein Sof is present here in particular (2120). Then comes the highest inclusion, where each part is completed only by the others: > אבל בהתכללות הזאת שיש כאן פרטים וכולם מתכללים זע"ז עד שהן כדבר א' ממש (line 2133)

> ועוד זאת שהאחדות היא מבלי אשר ימצא ראש וסוף כלל (line 2135)

This inclusion comes "not from reason but from bitul to the Essence above Chochmah" (2136).

## Chukat to Vaetchanan (2142-2315): face, sight, the new Mah, and the three bitul

**Speech and face (2146-2175).** Speech carries the soul's inner life with no break, but only the powers' essence. The face carries the soul's essence itself, by breaking through its physical vessel. "Panim" (face) is from "turning from His essential place" (2170). The red heifer makes the impure pure. That is the Essence reaching into wisdom: > כי גם בחי' העצמות נמשך בחכ' רק שזהו בעצם למעלה מן החכ' (line 2173)

**Hearing and sight (2178-2209).** Hearing grasps existence, and sight grasps essence. In the physical world the power of Ein Sof is seen with the senses: > דבשמים וארץ הגשמיים נראה כח הא"ס בראי' חושיות ממש (line 2181)

Even the higher unity should be contemplated as though seen. That is the job of every soul, not only the souls of Atzilut: > וכמו בהתבוננות דיחו"ע איך דכולא קמי' כלא חשיב שא"א להיות בבחי' ראי' ממש רק בנשמות היותר גבוהות שהן נשמות דאצי' כו', מ"מ צריך כאו"א להשתדל כמה שיש באפשרי להיות בזה בחי' הראי' (line 2183)

> ובאמת נהפך הוא דאצי' הוא בחי' יש וכל דיירי ארעא כלא חשיבי (line 2184)

**The new Mah breaks through (2213-2256).** Every breaking through is a small radiance of an overwhelming light. The dawn is brighter than the sun (2219-2220). Souls are the essence of Godliness (2224). They sing the Shema, not the angels' "the whole earth is full": > ושירות הנשמות הוא שמע ישראל כו' ה' אחד שזהו בחי' עצמות א"ס (line 2226)

The Baal Shem Tov's "part of the essence is all of it" is read as the essence hidden: > דזהו כמו שהעצם בא בהתעלמות שבאמת הוא תופס בכל העצמות רק מפני שמתעלם ה"ז נק' מקצת מן העצם (line 2227)

In the future the Essence will be revealed to every creature (2240). That comes through present service (2243).

**The Essence bears all (2257-2267).** > דהנה שלימות העצמות הוא שנושא הכל כבי', והכל הוא בבחי' אחדות פשוטה ממש (line 2257)

> וזהו אמיתית השלימות שנמצא הכל ואין שם בחי' הריבוי כלל ח"ו וח"ו כ"א הכל בבחי' אחדות פשוטה בתכלית הפשיטות לגמרי (line 2258)

Tikkun's bitul is existence with no self, and Moshe is its example: > ועניו הוא שהוא בבחי' מציאות והוא בבחי' ביטול מציאותו לגמרי (line 2263)

**Food, Shabbat, vows (2268-2274).** Shabbat's physical delight rises into Godly delight (2270). Releasing a vow lets the physical "yesh" be included in holiness (2273).

**The three bitul (2276-2315).** Creatures, like light, are radiance. They are not self-existent: > ולכן באמת כל הנבראים אינם בבחי' מציאות לעצמן להיותן בבחי' אור וזיו המתפשט (line 2277)

Light feels its source, and so it is batel as "none besides You": > דבהאור נרגש איך שהוא לבדו הוא ואין זולתו וזהו הביטול שבהאור (line 2278)

He distinguishes two ways of saying "there is nothing besides Him": > דאין זולתך הוא שזולתך אין שום מציאות כלל אבל אתך יש מציאות (line 2294)

> ואפס בלתך הוא שגם עם העצמות אין שום מציאות כלל (line 2294)

The higher knowledge belongs to the Da'at under inner Keter: > והוא הרגש בחי' העצמות שלמעלה מבחי' מקור להשתל' דשם הוא הדיעה האמיתית דלמעלה יש ולמטה אין דכולא קמי' כל"ח (line 2299)

The range ends with the rule that opposites unite only when each contains the other: > והיינו שמצד בחי' ההתכללות שכלול כ"א מבחי' מנגדו יוכלו להתייחד כא' ממש (line 2315)

## What this range adds to the Tanya, or says differently

- **Why the higher unity is true: the giver is no existence.** Shaar HaYichud gives the higher unity as "all before Him is as nothing". Here the Rashab explains why this is true while worlds still exist. Worlds have no place of their own. They exist only because the Ein Sof gives them place. In His knowledge they are not existent; in their own knowledge they are (1918). What makes this possible is that essential exaltation is itself "absence of existence" (1894-1895, 1908). Elokim is "included in His Essence" in exactly this sense (1919). The Tanya's own image (Part II ch. 3) stays in the background. The analysis of *giving place* is new.
- **Three levels of "nothing besides Him".** The range separates *ayin* (light is nothing, like the essence), *ein zulatcha* (without You nothing exists, "but with You there is something") and *efes biltecha* (even with You there is nothing). It places them in revealed light, in outer Keter and Chochmah, and in inner Keter (2281, 2294-2299). The Tanya's "ein od" is not graded this finely.
- **Bitul as the root of unity between opposites.** Tohu broke from selfhood. Tikkun unites opposites because each part is batel and feels incomplete alone (1883, 1934). The highest inclusion, "without head or end", is from bitul to the Essence, not from reason (2133-2136). The Essence's own wholeness is to "bear all" in simple oneness (2257-2259). This joins F09 to F08 and F05 more tightly than the Tanya does.
- **Knowing as seeing.** The Tanya's F12 is contemplation that reaches the heart. That is here too (2062, "know today and return it to your heart"). But the range adds a scale: hearing grasps existence, sight grasps essence (2180). Even the higher unity should be contemplated "as though seen" (2183). Future comprehension will be of the essence, "the true yesh" (1853).
- **Creation is from the inner light through vessels, yet the Essence alone makes "yesh".** "Light in itself cannot bring a something into being, only through the vessel" (2016). "The coming-into-being of the something is from the Essence alone" (1911, 2259). Both are stated. The Rashab joins them through the vessels' root in the Essence (1948, 2027).
- **Concealment as essential, from love.** Exile's darkness is "essential darkness", rooted in what is hidden in essence. Its cause is the essential love for Israel (1834, 1837). This goes beyond the Tanya's F03, where concealment serves revelation.

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- 17 lines are *none*: the maamar headings (1757, 1788, 1819, 1859, 1890, 1923, 1952, 1975, 2041, 2068, 2110, 2142, 2176, 2210, 2246, 2275, 2304). - 42 line entries carry no face. Most are opening questions, links in an analogy, or bare kabbalistic mapping (the Ofanim, tefillin and Z'A, the names Ab, Sag, Mah). - Only 3 rows are *reading*: 1864 (the aleph "counts as one"), 1906 (Israel's height is from their essence, read as F06) and 1935 (trade between lands "counted as one", read as F09). Everywhere else the tie is stated in the line or is a structural step the text itself uses toward it. - Long analogies are marked structural even though they carry the argument: pleasure from opposites (1760-1762), the translator (1782-1783), the child's emotions and the adult's (2046-2051), the face and the king (2157-2170), the barrel and the hair (2218-2219), the candle at noon (2286-2289). The text uses them to build the teaching. It does not say how each line bears on the unity. - Many F05 tags mark "the essence of the soul" in the analogies, not the Divine Essence. They are kept because the Rashab draws the parallel himself ("so too above"). But the count of F05 (230) overstates how often the Divine Essence itself is the subject.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Comprehension against sight.** In ch. 227 future *comprehension* of the essence ranks above sight, "alei ayin" (1851-1853). In ch. 275 comprehension settles inside and sight does not, yet sight "is far above comprehension" (2248-2250). The Rashab holds both. The ranking depends on whether comprehension is of existence or of essence.
- **Tohu's bitul.** In ch. 277 Tohu had bitul too, even more openly than Tikkun (2265). Earlier Tohu is simply "selfhood" with no bitul (1881, 2030). The Rashab reconciles them himself: Tohu's bitul was total non-existence, which is also against the Intent (2266, 2276). Both statements are kept.
- **Where creation comes from.** The inner light, through vessels, makes things exist (2013, 2016). The surrounding light is "the main coming-into-being and life" (1969). The Essence alone creates "yesh" (1911, 2259). The range states all three and does not rank them in one place. This is the SPINE's F05 tension again (F05a/b against F05j).
- **The emotions and the mind.** Emotions are rooted above the mind and are firmer than it (2051-2055). Yet the mind rules them, and love is only real through contemplation (2056-2058). The Rashab reconciles them himself: the mind rules only weakened emotions, and it weakens them through the Ein Sof light that rests in Chochmah (2061, 2102-2104).
- **Tanya 19 against Tanya 35.** The Rashab reads these as two levels of Chochmah's bitul: the wish to be included, and "He alone is" (2300). The Tanya does not rank them this way. This is the Rashab's reading of the Tanya, recorded as his.
