## What this slice adds to the full depth (one page)

The Tzemach Tzedek adds three things to the full depth. First, he takes the ladder into the act of addressing G-d: no name reaches the Essence; only 'You' (Atah), said face to face, does; we pray to Him, never to His attributes; of the Essence the right word is not even 'One' but 'Alone', while 'One' is His oneness inside a world that is really there. Second, he shows that the bottom is not calm. Where knowing ends, faith is a separate sense (you cannot see with your foot), and the true response to not-grasping is love-sickness and longing. The deepest love, 'with all your might', is a love that breaks YOUR own limit, and its fuel is distance: the body that hides G-d is what makes the love burst like dammed water; the soul came down to stop 'standing' and start 'walking'. It wants not Gan Eden but Him alone ('I want nothing; not Your Gan Eden; only You'). Third, he grounds the return in the lowest: matter comes straight from the Infinite, so a fly's wing shows His power more than the sefiros; His greatness is His humility, bending equally to heaven and earth; the highest is 'dry land inside the sea', separate and pointing 'this is my G-d', which only the Essence can do; newness comes through shrinking like the moon; today alone is the place of turning.

**For the guide (ours).** Four things change in 'Go deeper' after this slice:

1. **A new bottom-of-the-mind move: turn from describing to addressing.** When the person reaches 'He cannot be named', the next tap is not another description but 'You' (TZ-D1, TZ-C08). This is the Tzemach Tzedek's own reading of 'Baruch Atah'.
2. **The edge of knowing is an ache, not a rest.** DESCENT's D5 ('where knowing ends') should open into longing (TZ-D3, TZ-C15) and into faith as a separate sense (TZ-D9, TZ-C01), not into a quiet 'I don't know'.
3. **Distance is fuel.** For people who feel far, the deepest love is built from the distance itself (TZ-D2, TZ-D8, TZ-C10, TZ-C21). This is the most healing teaching in the slice, and the one DESCENT lacks.
4. **Guard against spiritual greed.** 'Not Your Gan Eden, only You' (TZ-D5) corrects the hidden wish to collect deep experiences, including from this very feature.

**Guards (ours).** Do not hand 'the world is nothing' to someone who feels unreal: the Tzemach Tzedek himself adds 'not that there is no world here at all' (DM 235). Do not press 'bitterness over distance' on someone depressed: he pairs it at once with joy (DM 259) and the Rebbeim warn 'let Your terror not frighten me' (MA 676).

## TZ-C01 · Derech Mitzvosecha, Mitzvas Ha'amanas Elokus, ch. 1-2, 4 (DM lines 96-103)

**Starts from:** What exactly are we commanded to believe? That G-d exists?

1. **You do not need faith to know that G-d is there. You can see it the way you know your own soul is in your body: the body by itself is as still as meat in a pot, yet it lives.** *(M9)* How the text gets there: The kasha (from the Bahag and R. Chasdai): how can 'believe in G-d' be a commandment, when every command already assumes Him? The Tzemach Tzedek answers that His existence and His giving life are not a matter of faith at all. They are 'seen' by the mind, as surely as you know you have a soul, though you have never seen it. > «שהרי הגוף אינו חי מעצמו לבד שהרי בעצמו הוא דומם» · *for the body is not alive by itself; by itself it is inanimate* · DM line 96

2. **The whole world is like one great body. Look at the turning sky, the growing grass, the feeling animals: that is the life of G-d inside it. Look this way and you 'see' Him.** *(M6)* How the text gets there: He widens the mashal from one body to the world: nothing in it lives by itself, so the life in it is His. That is 'from my flesh I will see G-d': a seeing by the mind, as firm as the eye's. > «כך אחזה אלוה ממש בראותו כל העולם חי וקיים» · *so I truly 'see' G-d, when he sees the whole world living and standing* · DM line 96

3. **So what is commanded is not to know it once. It is to go deep into it and not let your mind slip away. A passing thought does nothing.** *(M16)* How the text gets there: If it is seen, how can it be commanded? Because a person can look away. The command is depth: to sink the mind into it, as against a 'passing thought' (hirhur), which the Zohar says does nothing. > «ואפי' שיחשוב זאת במחשבה והרהור לבד אינו כלום אלא צריך להעמיק דעתו דוקא ואז יפתחו המדות» · *even if he thinks it with a mere passing thought, it is nothing; he must deepen his mind, specifically, and then the feelings open* · DM line 99

4. **Knowing that is felt (da'as) is the key to the heart. Faith on its own sits above you like a crown and does not move your feelings: the thief at the tunnel prays to G-d for success and still breaks in.** *(M10)* How the text gets there: He contrasts da'as (knowing that is felt, 'like Adam knew') with emunah (a crown that surrounds the head and does not enter). The proof is the Talmud's thief: his faith is real, yet his hands are not moved by it. > «ולכן גנבא אפום מחתרתא רחמנא קריא» · *so the thief at the mouth of the tunnel calls on the Merciful One* · DM line 98

5. **So go as far as knowing can go, and you become a different person, because your heart opens.** *(M15)* How the text gets there: 'Know the G-d of your father': know, specifically. Whatever can be reached by da'as, reach it and go deep. > «ואז תהפך לאיש אחר כי יפתחו מדותיך לאהבה את ה' וליראה אותו» · *and then you will turn into a different person, for your feelings will open to love G-d and fear Him* · DM line 98

6. **Where knowing ends, faith begins, and faith is not a weaker knowing. It is the only organ that fits Him, as only the eye fits light: you cannot see with your foot.** *(M4)* How the text gets there: His Essence is not intellect at all, so 'graspable' and 'ungraspable' do not even apply, the way 'touchable' does not. The tool for what is beyond mind is faith. > «וכמו שלא יוכל לראו' ברגלו כך לא יוכל לתפוס בשכלו» · *just as one cannot see with his foot, so one cannot grasp with his mind* · DM line 103

**Where it bottoms out:** What can be known, know; what cannot be known, believe (DM 99). Faith is a sense of its own, the organ for the Essence.

**How it returns down:** The return is to da'as again: deepen what can be known so the heart opens and the deed follows (DM 98-99).

## TZ-C02 · DM Emunah ch. 3-4 (lines 100-103), with the Maharal

**Starts from:** Is G-d the greatest mind, the Knower who is also the Known (Rambam)?

1. **Calling G-d 'pure intellect' quietly gives Him a definition, and anything defined has an outside.** *(M1)* How the text gets there: He quotes the Maharal against the philosophers: a mind knows a thing as it is; to say His Essence is mind is to put Him in a category. > «וכי הנשמה שבגוף האדם יוכל לעמוד על אמתתה» · *can a person even grasp the true nature of the soul in his own body?* · DM line 100

2. **Because He has no definition at all, there is nothing outside Him. A defined thing is separated from what is not it; He is not defined, so nothing is separate from Him.** *(M4)* How the text gets there: The Maharal's turn, which the Tzemach Tzedek embraces: His utter simplicity is not emptiness but the reason He knows all and can do all. > «ומזה בעצמו שהוא פשוט בתכלית הפשיטות אין דבר נבדל ממנו» · *from this itself, that He is utterly simple, nothing is separate from Him* · DM line 100

3. **So 'I' in 'I am the L-rd your G-d' means 'I who am I', the Essence itself, which no thought holds; and faith is how we hold it.** *(M2)* How the text gets there: The command to believe is aimed at this Essence, higher than wisdom by myriad levels. > «אנכי מי שאנכי שהוא מהותו ועצמותו» · *'I': who I am, which is His being and Essence* · DM line 100

4. **Even 'unknowable' is too small a word. Before Him, 'known' and 'unknown' are like 'touchable': the category does not reach Him.** *(M5)* How the text gets there: He climbs past the negative: not only is He not grasped; the question of grasping is a bodily measure that does not apply. > «וכמו שאינו נופל עליו לומר שאינו נתפס במישוש ח"ו כך ממש לא נופל ל' השגה מושג או בלתי מושג» · *just as one cannot say of Him that He is not touched, so the word 'grasped or not grasped' does not apply* · DM line 103

5. **And yet both Rambam and Maharal are right: in the sefiros, where He clothes Himself, He truly is Knower and Known; in Himself He is beyond.** *(M12)* How the text gets there: The vessels are not separate like an axe in a hand; how the light becomes one with them is itself beyond knowing, so the sefiros are called 'the secret of faith' (line 104). 'Both are words of the living G-d' (line 107). > «ושניהם דברי אלקי' חיים» · *and both are words of the living G-d* · DM line 107

**Where it bottoms out:** The Essence is not something, not even 'the unknowable something'. It is simple being, and because of that, nothing stands outside it.

**How it returns down:** Back down: since nothing is outside Him, every creature is known by Him in particular, with personal providence (DM 108).

## TZ-C03 · DM Emunah ch. 5 (line 108)

**Starts from:** If G-d does everything, why does He not change, the way a craftsman changes when he works?

1. **A worker who pours himself into his work is pulled out of himself: the hand tires, the mind tires. That is 'flow' (shefa).** *(M1)* How the text gets there: He sharpens the kasha with ordinary experience: giving that comes from your substance moves you from your place. > «מלת שפע נופל על ענין פעולה הבאה בהתפשטות כח מעצם המשפיע עד שעצמותו נטרד וזז ממקומו» · *'flow' is said of an action that spreads from the giver's own strength, until his very self is pulled and moved from its place* · DM line 108

2. **Light is different. The sun shines and loses nothing; cover it with clouds for a thousand years and it gains nothing.** *(M3)* How the text gets there: This is why the Kabbalists said 'light', not 'flow': light leaves its source unchanged. > «ולכן אם יהיה ענן ולא יומשך זיוו בחלל העולם לא יתרבה כחו עי"ז מאם יהיה זיוו מאיר אלף שנה» · *if a cloud stops its rays, its strength is not increased, compared with shining a thousand years* · DM line 108

3. **But the sun cannot hold back its light; a giver chooses. G-d has both perfections: He shines like light and gives by choice.** *(M10)* How the text gets there: The mashal breaks and the break teaches: in Him, the unchanging of light and the freedom of a giver are one. > «אבל בו ית' יש בו שני השלימיות» · *but in Him there are both perfections* · DM line 108

4. **So the fullness of faith holds two things at once: every single thing that happens comes from Him, personally; and He does not change at all.** *(M12)* How the text gets there: He quotes the Ramak: the vessels color the light like water in colored glass; the change is not in the water. Hence both pillars of faith stand. > «וזהו שלימות האמונה שנאמין היות כל הפעולות השונים באים ממנו דוקא בהשגחה פרטית לכל נברא בפרט» · *this is the fullness of faith: to believe that all the different acts come from Him, specifically, with personal care for each creature* · DM line 108

**Where it bottoms out:** He is fully present to each creature and fully unmoved. Neither cancels the other.

**How it returns down:** In practice: what happens to you today is from Him, addressed to you, and He is not tired of you.

## TZ-C04 · DM Emunah ch. 6 (line 109) and Shoresh Mitzvas HaTefillah ch. 35 (line 253)

**Starts from:** How can the Infinite 'withdraw' (tzimtzum) without changing?

1. **Close your eyes. Sight does not leave you; it folds back into you.** *(M9)* How the text gets there: The mashal from one's own body: when the eye shuts, the power of sight is gathered into the soul. > «כשמעצים העין נכלל כח הראי' בעצמות הנפש ולא שחלף הלך לו לגמרי» · *when one closes the eye, the power of sight is gathered into the soul itself, and has not gone away* · DM line 109

2. **Even a blind man has sight inside him: that is why he can father a child who sees.** *(M7)* How the text gets there: From the outer eye to the inner soul: the power is there even where it never shows. > «וכן אפי' בסומא שאין הכח ראי' מתפשט היינו שכלול בעצמיות הנפש ולכן הוא מוליד אדם רואה» · *even in a blind man, whose sight does not spread out, it is held in the soul, so he fathers a seeing child* · DM line 109

3. **Or think of a tractate you know by heart. While you learn something else, it is all there, whole, 'as if in a box', only not shining in words.** *(M3)* How the text gets there: The second mashal fixes what changes and what does not: only the showing in letters stops. > «שכולם שגורים בפיו וכאילו מונחים בקופסא» · *all of it fluent in his mouth, as if lying in a box* · DM line 253

4. **So the withdrawal is only toward us. What is left showing is a trace, like the mark left when writing is erased.** *(M4)* How the text gets there: But in truth the light 'is always held in its source'; the contraction is only from the side of the receivers. > «ולכן נק' רשימו כמו הכותב דבר ומוחקו שנשאר רשימו» · *it is called a trace, like one who writes a thing and erases it, and a trace remains* · DM line 109

**Where it bottoms out:** Nothing of Him ever left. What we call hiding is light folded into its source.

**How it returns down:** The person in darkness is not abandoned: what is not shining is whole, as in the closed eye (ours: applied to a person's own dark times).

## TZ-C05 · DM Emunah ch. 7, 10 (lines 110, 114) and Tefillah ch. 9 (line 225)

**Starts from:** Why do we praise G-d, if everything is nothing before Him?

1. **The question is real: does He need our praise? Everything on earth counts as nothing before Him.** *(M1)* How the text gets there: He states the kasha bluntly before answering it. > «וכי הוא ית' צריך לשבחים שלנו שאנחנו משבחים אותו והלא כל דיירי ארעא כלא חשיבי'» · *does He need our praises? All dwellers of earth are as nothing* · DM line 225

2. **For Him to put on 'greatness' and 'kindness' at all is a lowering. His greatness is itself His humility.** *(M4)* How the text gets there: The Maggid's reading of 'where you find His greatness, there you find His humility': the very attribute of greatness is a bending down. > «לפי שהגדולה עצמה ענוה גדולה היא להשפיל עצמו כ"כ» · *for greatness itself is great humility: to lower Himself so far* · DM line 110

3. **He is so high that heaven and earth are the same distance below Him. So He bends equally to see the earth, and does not leave it to angels.** *(M12)* How the text gets there: 'Who is like our G-d, who sits so high, who bends so low to see in heaven and earth': the nations say His glory is above the heavens (in the spiritual), and He left the earth. We say: mind and matter are equal before Him. > «שהשכל עם העשי' גשמיות שוים אצלו» · *for intellect and physical action are equal before Him* · DM line 114

4. **So spiritual is not closer to Him than physical.** *(M5)* How the text gets there: He draws the conclusion against the instinct that 'spirit is nearer to G-d'. > «ואין לומר כלל שמהות הרוחני' קרוב לו יותר מהגשמיות» · *one cannot say at all that the spiritual is closer to Him than the physical* · DM line 114

5. **That is why praise matters: praising a wise man for his wisdom wakes his wisdom. Our praise draws Him into the attributes that act for us.** *(M9)* How the text gets there: The mashal is from people; 'Hallel' is from 'when His lamp shone' (behilo): praise is light. > «וכמשל האדם החכם שכשמשבחים אותו איך שהוא חכם עי"ז מתעורר כח חכמתו» · *like a wise man: when they praise how wise he is, his wisdom wakes* · DM line 225

**Where it bottoms out:** His height is the reason for His nearness to the lowest. Greatness and humility are one act.

**How it returns down:** Praise in prayer is not flattery; it draws Him down. And the earth, your physical day, is where He bends to look (DM 114: 'in the midst of the land').

## TZ-C06 · DM Emunah ch. 8-9 (lines 111-112)

**Starts from:** If nothing is outside Him, how can He be moved by us, glad or angry? And how can a prayer change His will?

1. **In His Essence He is not moved at all. 'If you were righteous, what do you give Him?'** *(M4)* How the text gets there: The kasha: from whom could He be moved, if nothing is outside Him? The first answer is that in Himself He is not. > «ובערך זה נאמר אם צדקת מה תתן לו» · *about this level it says: if you were righteous, what do you give Him?* · DM line 111

## TZ-D1 · Only 'You' reaches Him

**Teaching.** No name reaches the Essence. Every name of G-d names Him as He acts through some attribute. The only word that reaches Him as He is, before any attribute, is 'You', said face to face. (stated: DM 222-223)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Notice that every word for G-d (Merciful, Judge, Creator) says what He does for you.
2. The Tzemach Tzedek: before He clothes Himself in attributes, He has no name at all.
3. But in each blessing we say 'Baruch Atah', 'Blessed are You'. 'You' is not a description. It is turning toward Him.
4. Say 'You' slowly, without adding anything after it. Do not picture; address.
5. Then add the name of what you need ('who heals the sick'), knowing that this is how the same 'You' comes to you.

**From inside.** Not an idea of G-d but a turning toward Him. The mind stops describing; the whole person faces. (ours: description; the text gives the grammar: 'to the face', lenochach.)

**Where it lands.** Lands in the first two words of every blessing, many times a day.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D5 (where knowing ends) and D6 (under all your names): under all His names, too, there is no name, only 'You'. new.

> «דאתה היינו למהו"ע כמו שהוא טרם התלבשותו במדותיו, שאין שם המורה עליו רק באמרו אתה לנוכח» · *'You' means His Essence before He clothes Himself in attributes; no name points to Him except saying 'You', face to face* · DM line 223

## TZ-D2 · Your own 'too much'

**Teaching.** 'With all your might' (me'odecha) is not a love that is truly infinite. It is a love that goes past your own limit, a 'very much' that is yours. Because it breaks your measure, it wakes the true Infinite. And it is powered by distance: the body and animal soul that hide G-d are what make the love break its banks. (stated: DM 232, 258-259)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Find the edge of your love: where it usually stops, how much it usually holds.
2. Remember a time something touched the inner point of your heart and you acted beyond reason (DM 232).
3. Now look honestly at the distance: how your days go to livelihood and things, and ask, is this why my soul came down? (DM 259)
4. Do not push the bitterness away. It is the board holding the water back.
5. Let the love go one step past your usual measure, small or large; it need not be a tzaddik's measure, only past yours.
6. Close with joy in a mitzvah (the right chamber of the heart).

**From inside.** Water held back by a board bursting through; a soul that 'stood' starting to 'walk'. (stated images)

**Where it lands.** Lands in money: 'with all your might' means 'with all your property'; give charity from your own toil (DM 258-259).

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D8 (the bottom is a bond) and D9 (the turn): the distance itself is the fuel for the deepest love. Corrects any reading of D2 that makes the self only something to dissolve: here the self's limit is the place where the Infinite is woken.

> «ולפיכך נק' מאדך מאד שלך שאצלך הוא בלי גבול» · *so it is called 'your might': yours, which for you is without limit* · DM line 232

> «אבל בכל מאדך היא האהבה הבאה מצד הריחוק והמנגד» · *'with all your might' is the love that comes from distance and opposition* · DM line 259

## TZ-D3 · Love-sickness: when knowing ends, longing begins

**Teaching.** When the mind really sees that the power making things from nothing cannot be grasped, the right response is not calm. It is love-sickness: a longing that cannot be filled, the way an animal cries when its food is held back. Longing (cheshek) is higher than love: the longer's thought never leaves the one he longs for. (stated: CH 597-619)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Look at the unbroken flow: six thousand years and the giving of life never paused (CH 598).
2. See that it comes out counted and separate: no two blades of grass joined, no two stars (CH 603).
3. Want to grasp the source of that flow. Try. Notice you cannot.
4. Do not turn the 'cannot' into a quiet idea. Let it hurt, like hunger.
5. Hold on to plain Torah and halachah meanwhile: 'sustain me'.

**From inside.** Like a sick person who takes pleasure in nothing and cannot keep food down; like a deer crying for water. (stated images)

**Where it lands.** Lands in prayer (prayer is 'chilu', sickness), and in plain study of halachah as the support.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Corrects and deepens D5 (where knowing ends): the end of knowing is not only that 'the soul's root knows', but an ache of longing that keeps the person moving. Extends D14.

> «יחלה מזה ויתאוה תאוה להשיג על בורי' את כח השופע מאין» · *he will grow sick and long to grasp clearly the power that flows from nothing* · CH line 606

> «שאין מחשבת החושק נפרדת מן החשוק כלל» · *the thought of one who longs is never apart from the one he longs for* · CH line 606

## TZ-D4 · Alone, not only One

**Teaching.** Of the Essence itself, 'One' says nothing: there is no second to deny. The right word is 'Alone' (Yachid). 'One' (Echad) is said of Him inside a world that is really there, which is like the ray inside the sun. He is Alone now just as before the world. (stated: DM 229, 235)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Say 'Echad' and think of the seven heavens, the earth and four directions (the letters' numbers): He is one inside all of that.
2. Now notice: the world is still here. The text insists: 'not that there is no world'.
3. Then go under the word: before Him there is nothing to count, so not even 'one'. He is Alone.
4. Hold both: Alone in Himself; One inside the world.

**From inside.** Before Him darkness and light are alike, up and down equal. (stated image)

**Where it lands.** Lands in the first verse of Shema each day, which ends in 'a dwelling below'.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Sharpens D3 and D4: 'nothing' and 'one' apply only where there is a world; the Essence is beyond both. Guards against D3's danger: the world is not unreal.

> «אמנם לגבי עצם מהות הא"ס ב"ה אין שייך לקרותו אחד רק יחיד» · *of the very Essence one cannot say One, only Alone* · DM line 235

> «ולא שאנו אומרים שאין כאן עולם כלל ח"ו» · *not that we say there is no world here at all* · DM line 235

## TZ-D5 · Not Your Gan Eden: only You

**Teaching.** The deepest love does not want any pleasure, not even spiritual ones, not even Gan Eden, which is 'for those who love being something'. It wants Him alone. The Alter Rebbe said it in Yiddish in his deveikus. (stated: DM 255-256)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Notice the wish behind spiritual effort: to feel uplifted, to feel close, to 'get' something.
2. Go up the ladder (DM 255): this world's pleasures are the waste of the lower Eden; the lower Eden must be forgotten to enter the higher.
3. Then even the higher: it is still a pleasure for a self that stays itself.
4. Say the Alter Rebbe's words: I want nothing at all; not Your Gan Eden; only You.

**From inside.** Better to be nullified and touched by Him than to remain whole and enjoy His light. (stated: 'better to take shelter in G-d than trust in man')

**Where it lands.** Lands in plain service with joy, even when one feels 'barren' of arousal (DM 256).

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D8 (what you cannot let go) and corrects a hidden counterfeit of the whole descent: seeking depth as an experience. new.

> «איך וויל זע גאר ניסט איך וויל ניט דאיין ג"ע איך וויל ניט דאיין עוה"ב» · *I want nothing at all; not Your Gan Eden; not Your World to Come* · DM line 256

> «ולפיכך נק' הג"ע יש להנחיל אוהבי יש» · *Gan Eden is called 'something, for those who love something'* · DM line 256

## TZ-D6 · Dry land inside the sea

**Teaching.** The highest is not vanishing like a fish in the sea, nor living apart like a land creature. It is both at once: still separate, with an evil inclination, and pointing with the finger 'This is my G-d'. Two opposites in one subject can come only from His Essence; that is why the Sea, and every true marriage, is 'hard'. (stated: MA 247-270)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Picture fish: they die the moment they leave the water (the hidden worlds, the angels).
2. Picture land creatures: they live from the earth yet walk apart from it (souls, you).
3. Now Israel at the Sea: walking on dry ground inside the sea. Separate people, and seeing.
4. Say 'this' about something in front of you, pointing, as they did.

**From inside.** Each one pointing with his finger 'this'. (stated)

**Where it lands.** Lands in a marriage, a body, a weekday: fully yourself and His.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D10-D12 (the return): the return is not a compromise but the one thing only the Essence can do.

> «וכדי שיהי' שני הפכים אלו בנושא אחד ממש» · *for these two opposites to be in one subject* · MA line 268

> «ובנ"י הלכו ביבשה בתוך הים» · *Israel walked on dry land in the midst of the sea* · MA line 262

## TZ-D7 · The moon goes dark before it is new

**Teaching.** A truly new light, not the daily renewal of the old, comes only through becoming nothing: the moon shrinks to a dot and disappears; the seed rots before it sprouts. (stated: MA 271-279)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Notice the daily 'sun' of your life: things renewed as they were.
2. Ask for something truly new.
3. Then accept the shrinking: less of you, a smaller dot.
4. Begin with awe, the foundation; build on it.

**From inside.** A seed rotting in the ground; the moon black. (stated images)

**Where it lands.** Lands in learning each day 'as new', and in Shabbos.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D2 (stillness given) and D14 (no bottom: each return renews): the newness is the fruit of the shrinking.

> «שהתבואה אם לא ירקב מקודם אזי לא יצמיח כלל» · *grain that does not rot first will not sprout* · MA line 275

> «כ"א ע"י בחי' ביטול מכל וכל» · *only through complete nullification* · MA line 274

## TZ-D8 · Salt water made sweet by the earth

**Teaching.** The way to the deepest will is 'from the narrow place', and each person knows his own sore. The soul's passage through the body is like salty deep water breaking up through thick earth: by passing through, it becomes living water. But the well must be dug with a heavy head and a broken heart. (stated: OH 4092-4098)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Name your own narrow place: the sore you know.
2. See it as the earth the water must pass through, not as a wall.
3. Dig: a heavy head, a broken heart, in prayer.
4. Then strike fire from the stone heart, and give it a page of halachah.

**From inside.** Bitter water turning sweet as it rises through soil. (stated image)

**Where it lands.** Lands in prayer first, then Torah, then deeds (OH 4095).

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D9 (the bottom sends you back) and D11: the descent is what makes the water living. new image.

> «וענין המיצר מה שידוע לכאו"א נגעו» · *the narrow place is what each knows as his own sore* · OH line 4093

> «והנה מי התהום הם מלוחים בעצם וכשבוקעים ויוצאים דרך עובי העפר הכדור הם מתבררים מיד» · *the waters of the deep are salty; breaking through the thick earth they are cleaned at once* · OH line 4094

## TZ-D9 · Faith is a sense

**Teaching.** Faith is not weak knowing. It is the organ for what is beyond mind, as the eye is for light: you cannot see with your foot. Before Him, even 'graspable/ungraspable' does not apply, any more than 'touchable'. (stated: DM 96-103)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. First know all that can be known: the world as a body, alive from Him (DM 96).
2. Go deep until the heart opens (DM 98-99).
3. Arrive at the edge: 'graspable' does not apply.
4. Do not force the mind further. Switch organs: rest in faith, the way you rest in sight.

**From inside.** Like a rich man glad of the pearls in his pouch though he is not looking at them. (stated image, DM 97)

**Where it lands.** Lands in steadiness: faith does not move feelings by itself (the thief), so keep the knowing working too.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Sharpens D7 (simple faith): faith is a distinct sense, and it does not replace da'as; both are needed.

> «וכמו שלא יוכל לראו' ברגלו כך לא יוכל לתפוס בשכלו» · *as one cannot see with his foot, so one cannot grasp with the mind* · DM line 103

> «כמו ששמח העשיר מהון מרגליות הצרור ומונח אצלו אע"פ שאיננו רואה בעינו כל רגע» · *as the rich man rejoices in the pearls tied up beside him, though he does not see them every moment* · DM line 97

## TZ-D10 · Today only

**Teaching.** Only here, where good flows even where evil is, can a person change from one end to the other. In the world to come each thing is in its place. One true thought of return turns a life. (stated: DM 261-264)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Say: today, in this world specifically, I can change my taste from end to end.
2. Remember the ruling: even a total sinner who says 'on condition I am righteous' may have thought of teshuvah.
3. Make that thought now.
4. If not now, when?

**From inside.** Mixed ground, where change is still possible. (ours: image)

**Where it lands.** Lands every morning (DM 264: 'every day').

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D12 (the deed) and D14 (begin again): the possibility of beginning again is a property of this world.

> «לכן זאת ישים אדם על לבו בכל יום איך שהיום בעוה"ז דוקא הוא יכול לשנות טעמו מהקצה אל הקצה» · *let a person put on his heart each day that today, in this world specifically, he can change his taste from end to end* · DM line 264

## TZ-D11 · His greatness is His humility

**Teaching.** For Him to be King over creatures who are nothing before Him is like a man showing off before a fly: a huge lowering. His greatness is itself humility, and He bends to see the earth exactly as the heavens. What moves Him to it is our giving up of our will. (stated: DM 110, 114, 338-340)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. Feel how small you are before Him: less than a fly before a man.
2. Then turn it: He chose to be King over exactly this.
3. Give up one want of yours now, as 'soul' means will.
4. Know that by this He 'gives up' His and wants to be here.

**From inside.** A king who bends his head; a king's son crying from captivity. (stated images)

**Where it lands.** Lands in small surrenders of will in the day, and in Baruch Shem after Shema.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D11 (called into being: the command): being commanded is His humility, and our yielding draws His kingship. new.

> «וכמו שאין שייך כלל לומר באדם שיתפאר לפני זבוב» · *as a person would not glory before a fly* · DM line 338

> «לפי שהגדולה עצמה ענוה גדולה היא להשפיל עצמו כ"כ» · *greatness itself is great humility, to lower Himself so* · DM line 110

## TZ-D12 · After vanishing, a lover is given back

**Teaching.** There are two kinds of giving oneself: 'kallah' as consuming (nothing left, in 'One') and 'kallah' as pining (someone still loves). The lower comes after the higher because 'Baruch Shem' is said between, giving back a world and a self. (stated: DM 257)

**The hisbonenus, as the text gives it.**

1. In 'Echad', let everything go: no wants left.
2. Whisper 'Baruch Shem': the world, His kingdom, comes back.
3. Now you are someone again: love Him 'with all your heart'.
4. Notice the love is not less for coming after; it is the purpose.

**From inside.** A bride: once consumed, once pining. (stated image)

**Where it lands.** Lands in the words of Shema each day, and in the day that follows.

**Relation to DESCENT.** Extends D9-D11: the return gives back a lover, not just a world.

> «הא' מלשון כליון שמכלה ומבלה הכל» · *the first from 'consumption', consuming everything* · DM line 257

> «שהכלות הנפש הוא בבחי' יש עדיין יש מי שאוהב» · *pining is still 'something': there is someone who loves* · DM line 257

## Avodah: what the texts say to do

- **Do not settle for a passing thought about G-d; sink the mind into it, and keep it from slipping away. What can be known, know; what cannot, believe.** «מה שאפשר לידע ידע דייקא ומה שא"א לידע יאמין» · *what can be known, know; what cannot be known, believe* · DM line 99
- **Do not chase the feeling of love. Do the thinking; the love comes by itself.** «והציווי הוא על ההתבוננות והיגיעה במוח בלבד» · *the command is only on contemplation and effort of the mind* · DM line 417
- **In each blessing, say 'Atah' as a real turning, face to face, before naming the need.** «שאין שם המורה עליו רק באמרו אתה לנוכח» · *no name points to Him except saying 'You' face to face* · DM line 223
- **Pray to Him, not to an attribute: 'Father of mercy, roll Your mercy onto us'.** «ולפע"ד הי' ראוי לומר אב הרחמים גלגל עלינו מדת הרחמים» · *one should say: Father of mercy, roll the attribute of mercy upon us* · DM line 223
- **Each day, think: today I can change from end to end.** «אבל אם לא עכשיו ח"ו אימתי» · *if not now, when?* · DM line 264
- **Fill both chambers of the heart: bitterness over the distance (where do my days go?) and joy in the mitzvos.** «וכי בשביל זה ירדה נשמתו בעוה"ז» · *was it for this that the soul came down?* · DM line 259
- **Give charity from your own toil: it turns 'something' into 'nothing'.** «וזהו יסוד כל המצות שתכליתם הוא בחי' לעשות מיש אין שמזה נעשה צחוק ותענוג למעלה» · *the foundation of all mitzvos, whose aim is to make 'nothing' out of 'something', which is laughter and delight above* · DM line 259
- **Do not greet people or start the day's business before prayer; let the soul spread into the body first.** «וקודם התפילה הנשמה באפו דווקא ואינו מתפשט בכל הגוף» · *before prayer the soul is only in the nostrils* · MA line 741
- **Stand to pray with a heavy head; dig the well with a broken heart; then study a halachah.** «והנה הבאר לא הי' מעצמו בלתי שיחפרו אותו תחלה, והיינו ענין כובד ראש לב נשבר ונדכה» · *the well is not dug by itself: a heavy head, a broken heart* · OH line 4095
- **When fear starts to make you fall from serving, stop it: 'let Your terror not frighten me'.** «ואימתך אל תבעתני» · *let Your terror not frighten me* · MA line 676
- **Review your learning once more than usual: the hundred-and-first time is the toil that brings Him down.** «וגם אינו דומה שונה פרקו מאה פעמים למאה פעמים ואחד» · *one who reviews a hundred times is not like one who reviews a hundred and one* · MA line 676
- **Serve with joy in each mitzvah, even when you feel barren of arousal: joy breaks the measure.** «ע"י שמחה גורמים גילוי הפנימית ואז יוצא מהגדר והמדה» · *through joy the inner is revealed and it goes beyond border and measure* · DM line 256
