Vayechi is the book of Jacob's last seventeen years and his death. This first part opens (lines 4409-4512) with a text in an unusual Aramaic about exile and redemption that ends with the printed note «עד כאן אינו מן הזהר» (Vayechi 15:5; line 4512): "until here it is not from the Zohar." It was read in full and is reported below, marked as a later insert. The Zohar proper (line 4513 on) then teaches about joy as the only way to serve; Adam leaving the one unchanging tree for the tree that turns; the days of a life that "draw near" to the King without shame; each day as a witness that stands outside until one returns; the thirty days before death, the dying person's added sight, the seven days of the soul's mourning; the four species; and Rabbi Shimon holding back the angel of death from Rabbi Yitzchak. All 298 lines were read in full. Every quote below printed "FOUND near line N" from `find_he.py verify` on the export. A "…" in a quote skips a printed cross-reference or variant in brackets.

## 2. The unity of God

**Cleave to the one tree that does not change.** God raised Adam «בגין דישתכח יחידאי, ובלבא יחידאי, ובאתר דדבקותא יחידאי, דלא אשתני, ולא מתהפך לעלמין» (Vayechi 28:6; line 4645): "so that he would be single, with a single heart, in a single place of cleaving that does not change and never turns over"; «בההוא קשורא יחידא, דכלא ביה אתקשר» (same line): "in that one bond in which all is bound." Adam left it and clung to the place that turns «מטב לביש ומביש לטב» (Vayechi 28:7; line 4646), "from good to bad and bad to good"; «ודאי כדין אתהפך לבייהו» (Vayechi 28:8; line 4647): "and surely then their hearts turned." Ours: this is the Zohar's clearest link between unity and the felt life: cleaving to the One makes the heart one; cleaving to the many makes it turn. Ties: F02, S62; Tanya ch. 24-25 on the Divine will as one (memory).

**All is one in the pleasantness.** "To gaze on the pleasantness of Havayah" and "delight on Havayah": «אמר רבי שמעון, כלא חד» (Vayechi 22:4; line 4588): "Rabbi Shimon said: all is one." Ties: S76.

**Deeds below wake what is bound above.** «דהא באינון עובדי … דאיהו עביד לתתא קטיר לון במלין עלאין דלעילא, וכד נטלין לון לתתא ועבדי בהו עובדא, אתער ההוא עובדא דלעילא דקטיר בה» (Vayechi 27:1; line 4618): "for the works He made below He bound to higher things above; and when they are taken below and a deed is done with them, the deed above bound to it is awakened." So the four species: «במלין ועובדא בעיין לאחזאה מלה, בגין לאתערא מלה אחרא» (Vayechi 27:2; line 4619): "by words and by deed a thing must be shown, to awaken another thing." Ties: Tanya ch. 35-37 on mitzvot as drawing the Light down (memory).

## 3. States of consciousness (ours, tied to the map)

- **No service except from joy.** «לית פולחנא דקודשא בריך הוא, אלא מגו חדוה» (Vayechi 16:13; line 4525): "there is no service of the Holy One except from within joy," for the Shechinah does not dwell within sadness; Elisha called for a musician. And «שכינתא לא שריא, אלא באתר שלים, ולא באתר חסר, ולא באתר פגים, ולא באתר עציב, אלא באתר דאתכוון, באתר חדו» (Vayechi 16:12; line 4524): "the Shechinah dwells only in a whole place, not a lacking one, not a flawed one, not a sad one, but in a place set right, a place of joy." Ties: Tanya ch. 26, 31 (memory); joy states on the map.
- **Days that draw near without shame.** "The days of Israel drew near": at death all his days are counted before the King; «זכאה חולקיה דההוא בר נש, דיומוי אתקריבו גבי מלכא בלא כסופא» (Vayechi 29:3; line 4655): "happy is the man whose days draw near to the King without shame." Ties: S65 (given right now); the state proposed in the Hakdamah report.
- **The added spirit at the end.** When judgement rests on a dying person, «אתוסף רוחא עלאה ביה, מה דלא הוה ביומוי» (Vayechi 21:11; line 4575): "a higher spirit is added to him that he did not have in his days," and he sees what he did not merit to see while living. Ties: S35, S36.
- **Seventeen good years.** Jacob, whose whole life was trouble, looked at Joseph and it was «כאילו חמא לאמיה דיוסף» (Vayechi 16:9; line 4521): "as if he saw Joseph's mother"; «והוה דמי בגרמיה כמה דלא אעבר עליה צערא ביומוי» (same line): "and it seemed to him as if no sorrow had passed over him in his days." Ours: late consolation can undo the felt weight of a whole life. Ties: S78.
- **Wisdom settles only in stillness.** Rabbi Shimon got down from his donkey: «ודאי חכמתא לא מתיישבא, אלא כד בר נש יתיב ולא אזיל, אלא קאים בקיומיה» (Vayechi 31:5; line 4688): "surely wisdom does not settle except when a person sits and does not move, but stands in his place." Ties: technique 14; stillness states.
- **At the end, a thousand years is one day.** «אפילו אי בר נש קיים אלף שנין, ההוא יומא דאסתלק מעלמא, דמי ליה כאילו לא אתקיים בר יומא חד» (Vayechi 31:18; line 4701): "even if a person lived a thousand years, on the day he leaves the world it seems to him as if he lived only one day." Ties: S65.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Return every day as if it were the last.** «אבל זכאי חסידי, בכל יומא ויומא מסתכלי בלבייהו, כאלו ההוא יומא מסתלקי מעלמא, ועבדין תיובתא שלימתא קמי מאריהון» (Vayechi 26:15; line 4617): "but the pious, every single day, look into their hearts as if that day they are leaving the world, and do complete return before their Master." New technique below. Ties: technique 24; "return one day before your death."

**Each day is a witness.** The days of a person's life are real: «ואזלין וטאסין בעלמא, נחתין ואזהרן לבר נש, כל יומא ויומא בלחודוי» (Vayechi 32:3; line 4704): "they go and fly in the world, and come down and warn a person, each day by itself"; a day in which one sinned «סליק בכסופא, ואסהיד סהדותא, וקאים בלחודוי לבר» (same line): "rises in shame, bears witness, and stands alone outside," and waits until the person returns; then it goes back to its place (line 4705). Ties: technique 24; S77.

**Tears tear the decree.** Leah wept at the crossroads not to be given to Esau; «כל בר נש דאושיד דמעין קמיה דקודשא בריך הוא, אף על גב דאתגזר עליה עונשא, יתקרע, ולא יכיל ההוא עונשא לשלטאה ביה» (Vayechi 30:24; line 4682): "every person who sheds tears before the Holy One, even though a punishment was decreed on him, it is torn, and that punishment cannot rule over him." Ties: "Silent, shouted, wept" (Chayei Sara). Guard: tears come; they are not forced.

**Thought, too, is before Him.** «אפילו ההוא … מה דחשיב בר נש ואסתלק ברעותיה, כלא אשתכח קמי קודשא בריך הוא, ולא אתאביד מניה» (Vayechi 30:11; line 4669): "even what a person thought and what rose in his will, all is found before the Holy One, and nothing is lost from it." Ties: guarding thought (Vayetzei).

**The four species: hold them, and ask.** The species are bound to the holy name above; we hold them «בגין לאתערא חדוה ההוא דאחיד ביה» (Vayechi 27:2; line 4619): "to awaken the joy of what it is bound to." They are guests, «ובהו בעי בר נש למלכא בעותיה» (Vayechi 27:18; line 4635): "and through them a person should put his request before the King." On Sukkot Israel go out carrying "the King's signs," the sign of who won the judgement (lines 4636-4638). Ties: technique 11.

**Days of Awe: the notes can still be torn.** On Rosh Hashanah the notes are written; if a person returns, «קרעין פתקין דעליה» (Vayechi 26:8; line 4610): "they tear the notes against him"; Yom Kippur is the day of return; if not complete, they hang until the eighth day of the festival (lines 4611-4612).

## 5. Big human questions

- **What happens when we die?** A herald calls for thirty days; the soul goes up each night and sees its place, and the person does not know (line 4551). At death «אבוי וקריבוי משתכחין תמן עמיה, וחמא לון ואשתמודע לון» (Vayechi 20:7; line 4558): "his father and relatives are there with him, and he sees them and knows them," and they go with his soul to its place. For seven days the soul goes from house to grave and mourns the body (line 4579); then enters Eden and, if worthy, rises (lines 4580-4583). Ours: the Zohar's account is warm and personal; the guide should give it gently to the grieving, never as a threat.
- **Can the angel of death be held back?** Rabbi Shimon saw the angel of death dancing before Rabbi Yitzchak, took his hand, decreed "whoever is used to entering, enter," and learned Torah with him; the angel left (lines 4556-4560). Rabbi Yitzchak dreamed of his father: his place was ready, and he was held in the branches of the great tree that is Rabbi Shimon (line 4561). Ours: the bond to a righteous teacher and Torah learned together hold a person in life.
- **What is return?** «וכד תייבין בני עלמא … ואמא תבת וכסיא על בנין, כדין אתקרי תשובה. מאי תשובה. דא תשובה דאמא» (Vayechi 22:3; line 4587): "when people return … the mother comes back and covers her children; that is called teshuvah. What is teshuvah? The mother's return." Ours: return is the Mother coming back to us as much as our coming back to her. Ties: S77, S78.
- **Is toil worth it?** "What profit has a man in all his toil under the sun?" Torah toil for its own sake «דלעילא מן שמשא הוא» (Vayechi 31:18; line 4701): "is above the sun"; Torah learned for honor stays under it (same line).
- **Why did Jacob not want to be buried in Egypt?** So they would not make a god of him; because the Shechinah would dwell with his children in exile anyway; and so his body would lie with his fathers (line 4666).
- **The later insert (lines 4409-4512).** Its best line: «דלא איתייהיבת נבואתא, אלא לתבירי לבא» (Vayechi 1:11; line 4419): "prophecy is given only to the broken-hearted." It is printed in the Zohar but marked by the printers as not part of it; the guide should cite it as "printed in the Zohar, marked as not original."

## 6. What this range gives the guide (ours)

1. **The one unchanging tree and the single heart (lines 4645-4647).** Unity as the cure for a heart that keeps turning.
2. **No service except from joy (lines 4524-4525).**
3. **Return every day as if it were the last (line 4617); each day a witness (lines 4704-4705).**
4. **Tears tear the decree (line 4682).**
5. **Death told warmly: the relatives who come, the seven days, the rising (lines 4551-4583).**

## Proposed states and techniques from this range

- **State, new: "Single heart."** Inside: when attached to the One, the heart stops flipping between good and bad, mercy and anger; a steadiness not of numbness but of one bond (lines 4645-4650). How to enter: before a decision or in prayer, say "one tree, one heart, one bond," and let every pull be placed under the one will. Near S62, S60.
- **Technique, new: "The last day."** Each day (or each night), look into the heart as if today you leave the world; do complete return for what that reveals (line 4617). Pair with "No one goes to bed unforgiven" (Miketz). Ties technique 24.
- **Technique, new: "Call the day back."** For a day you know went wrong, picture it standing outside in shame; return, and let it go back to its place (lines 4704-4705). Ties S77.
- **State, new: "Days drawn near without shame."** Inside: the sense that one's days so far can be brought before Him, none of them pushed away (lines 4654-4658). How to enter: at the evening look-back, ask of today "can it come near?" Near S65.
- **Technique, new: "Still to learn."** Before learning something deep, sit down and stop moving; let wisdom "settle" (line 4688). Ties technique 14.
