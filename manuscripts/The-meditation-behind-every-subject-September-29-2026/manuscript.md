*Chassidus Unity Index. 197 subjects in 16 clusters, each with the meditation that stands behind it. Built from each subject's own entry in `../subjects/` and its verified passages. Checked by `_build/meditations_index.py`.*

## Why

Every subject in Chassidus points to one thing, the unity of God. That unity can't be understood, only glimpsed, in contemplation (hisbonenus). So the meditation, not the idea, is what matters, and each subject below has its own.

## How to use one

This is hisbonenus, the Jewish way of contemplating as Chabad does it, never mindfulness. One subject per sitting.

- **Hold:** one line of teaching. Say it, and fix the mind on it.
- **The move:** think it through slowly, detail by detail, down into your own day. The sources' own picture, and a few words on where the picture breaks.
- **Stay:** say the line again. When the mind drifts, come back to it without arguing with the stray thought.
- **From inside:** what the glimpse is like. It is never asked for; if nothing is felt, the thinking still counts.
- **Back into the day:** say it with the lips in words of Torah or prayer, do one mitzvah or kind act, and keep one line for the day.

## Before any sitting

If you are in danger, or having thoughts of not wanting to be alive, stop here and reach a person now: your local emergency number or a crisis line where you are. Some subjects are not for a low day; they carry their own line, *Before you start*.
