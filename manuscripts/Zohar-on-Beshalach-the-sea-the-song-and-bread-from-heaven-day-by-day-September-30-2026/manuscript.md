## 1. What the range is

Beshalach follows Israel from Egypt to the sea, through the Song, into the desert, to the manna and to the war with Amalek. The Zohar's main blocks:

* **Habakkuk and the Shunammite (lines 849-875).** Why "a prayer of Habakkuk": he was the Shunammite's son, twice embraced, who died and was revived. Then the broken spirit of those who left Egypt. * **The pillars of cloud and fire; midnight study (lines 876-912).** * **"The Lord will fight for you, and you be silent" (lines 913-937).** On Shabbat, and on keeping still. * **The splitting of the sea (lines 938-1032),** with R. Shimon's long reading of the three verses of 72 letters from which the 72-letter Name is drawn (lines 1008-1025), and the parable of the King whose face always shines (lines 1017-1019). * **The Song of the Sea (lines 1033-1192),** read verse by verse. * **Marah, the manna, Shabbat (lines 1193-1267),** with the five kinds of food and the daily asking. * **Rephidim and Amalek (lines 1268-1334).**

## 2. The unity of G-d

**He does not change; all is held in Him.** R. Shimon's parable (lines 1017-1019): a perfect king's face always shines, "and when he judges, he judges for good and for bad". The fool sees the shining face and is not careful. The wise man says, "the king is whole, his mind is whole", אנא חמי דבההוא נהירו, דינא יתיב ואתכסיא, "I see that within that light, judgment sits hidden" (line 1017). So God: "I the Lord have not changed", לא דליגנא לאתר אחרא, בי אתכליל כלא, "I did not leap to another place; in Me all is included" (line 1019). Judgment and mercy are not two powers that take turns; they are held in one unchanging whole. And "the Holy One does not change His ways, only the wicked change them, and turn mercy to judgment" (line 1254).

**Judgment wrapped in mercy.** Even the drowning at the sea was "judgment in mercy": the sea covered them, then gave them up, and God held out His right hand so the earth would take them in for burial (line 925). "For even though He judges, He has pity on His works" (line 929). The Tetragrammaton "everywhere is mercy, and even when it fights and does judgment, that judgment is in love" (line 924).

**Mercy on all, not only on Israel.** "Who is like You among the mighty" (line 1165): the great tree has seventy branches, the peoples of the world; when Israel's portion rules, it guards them all and gives peace to all seventy, which is why seventy bulls are offered on Sukkot (line 1164). מי כמכה דיעביד כעובדך וירחם על כלא, "who is like You, who acts as You do and has mercy on all" (line 1165), נטיר לכלא... ולא בעיא למעבד עמהון גמירא, "guarding all... and not wishing to destroy them" (line 1165). **(ours)** This is one of the range's universal lines, and the guide can use it.

**Water, Torah, and He are one.** In the desert "they went three days and found no water": "there is no water but Torah", ואין מים אלא תורה, ואין תורה אלא קודשא בריך הוא, "and there is no water but Torah, and no Torah but the Holy One" (line 1194). F10 in its bluntest form.

**"Is the Lord among us, or Ayin?" (lines 1282-1284).** The people who had just seen the Shekhinah at the sea were not fools. They wanted to know the difference between the Hidden Ancient One, "called Ayin (Nothing)", and the Name YHVH (line 1283). They were punished על דעבידו פרודא, "because they made a separation" (line 1284): they tested the two as if they were two. **(ours)** This is the Zohar's own warning against the very thing a contemplative mind is tempted to do: to set a higher "Nothing" against the God who is present, and ask which one is really there.

**Everything hangs on the Ancient One.** "Why do you cry to Me? To Me precisely: all hangs on Atika" (line 1028), and at that hour the Ancient Holy One was revealed "and there was favor in all the worlds" (line 1028). When the Name above is unified, "the walls of darkness split, and the faces of the King are seen and shine for all" (line 1127).

## 3. States of consciousness

**The broken spirit that cannot rejoice (ties to S04 Low and heavy, S03 Numb).** When they left Egypt, רוחיהון הוה תביר בגווייהו, והוו שמעין אינון תושבחן, ולא יכלין למחדי, "their spirit was broken inside them; they heard the praises and could not rejoice" (line 873). The Zohar adds a general rule: בר נש כד איהו שביק פולחנא, כדין ידע תבירו דגרמוי, תבירו דרוחיה, "when a person leaves his labor, then he feels the breaking of his bones, the breaking of his spirit" (line 874). They had "tasted death", and God healed them: the paths gave off healing scents, וקל תושבחן דהוו שמעין, הוו חדאן ונייחין ברוחיהון, "and the sound of the praises they heard made them glad and rested in their spirit" (line 874). **(ours)** An exact picture: the moment the pressure lifts is often when a person first feels how broken he is; and song heard, not song sung, is what heals.

**Too full to move: the longing to keep gazing (proposed new state ZS2-07; ties to S19 After the high).** At the sea "they gazed as if eye to eye", and when the words ended "they all were sweet in their souls", ותאבון למחמי ולאסתכלא, ולא הוו בעאן לנטלא מתמן, מסגיאות תיאובתא, "and they longed to see and to gaze, and did not want to move from there, from the greatness of their longing" (line 1190). Moses had to lure them into the desert with a glimpse of glory, and "Shur" means "gazing there" (line 1192). Then: they went looking, "and did not find Him" (line 1194); at Marah "their soul was not sweetened as before" (line 1195). Inside: the high was real; the next day is bitter; I keep looking for it. It is S19 seen whole, with its cure: a "tree" was shown, and "the accuser became the defender" (line 1196).

**Be still (proposed new state ZS2-08; ties to S32 Quiet, S83 Accepting the yoke).** "The Lord will fight for you and you be silent": God did not want Israel to stir anything below, because the fathers had already stirred it above; תחרישון ודאי, ולא תתערון מלה, דלא אצטריך לכו, "be silent, surely, and do not stir a thing; you do not need to" (line 923). If they had stirred, the Name of mercy would not have acted, and the judgment would not have been done in mercy (line 926). And to Moses at the sea: "why do you cry to Me... this is not a time for prayer" (line 937). Inside: the stillness of one who knows the work is being done by Someone else. **(ours)** This is the counterweight to the Zohar's rule that everything needs "an awakening from below" (Vaera, line 592). There are hours for effort and hours for silence.

**Held by a brother born for trouble (ties to S84 Trust).** "A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for trouble" (Proverbs 17:17): בשעתא דעקתא יולד בעלמא, אח יהא לקבלך, לשזבא לך מכל אינון דעקין לך, "in the hour when trouble is born into the world, a brother will be there for you, to save you from all who press on you" (line 1091).

**The poor man's broken heart (ties to S59 True lowliness).** "The Lord hears the needy" because they are closest to the King: "a broken and crushed heart God will not despise", ולית לך בעלמא, דאיהו תביר לבא כמסכנא, "and there is no one in the world with a heart as broken as the poor man" (line 1215). Others appear before God in body and soul; the poor man only in his soul, וקודשא בריך הוא קריב לנפשא יתיר מגופא, "and the Holy One is closer to the soul than to the body" (line 1215).

**The faithful son who asks each day (ties to S84; proposed new state ZS2-09).** See the practice below. The state it builds: one who has food and still asks for it, and waits "until it is given from the King's house" (line 1238), "is called a faithful son" (line 1234). Its opposite is named sharply: those without faith "wear themselves out every day after food, day and night, lest they not get a slice of bread" (line 1247). **(ours)** This is S84 set against worry (S07 Restless wanting) in one sentence.

**Rest of the spirit and breaking of the spirit (proposed new state ZS2-10).** Every act done in His service becomes a breath that rises: ולית לך הבל, דלית ליה קלא, דסליק ואתעטר לעילא, ואתעביד סניגורא, "there is no breath that does not have a voice, which rises and is crowned above and becomes an advocate" (line 1168). Every act not in His service becomes a breath that "wanders in the world", איהו תבירא דרוחא, "and that is the breaking of the spirit" (line 1170). The first is called נייחא דרוחא, "the rest of the spirit"; the other, "striving after wind" (line 1173). Inside: at the end of a day, some hours feel gathered and some feel scattered. The Zohar names both.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Ask for your bread every day, even when you have it (proposed technique ZT2-04; ties to technique 20, Your own words to Him).** The Zohar is explicit: ואף על גב דאשתכח עמיה, בעי למשאל קמי קודשא בריך הוא, ולצלאה צלותא על מזונא כל יומא, "even though he has it with him, he must ask before the Holy One and pray for his food every day" (line 1233), so that blessing is drawn through him every day. Do not cook today for tomorrow (line 1234). R. Yeisa Sava would not prepare his meal until he had asked; then he waited an hour, and said, "now it has been given from the King's house; from here on, prepare the meal" (line 1238). What it is for: to know, each day, where it comes from. Guard **(ours)**: this is not a rule against planning (the Zohar itself sets aside a double portion on Friday, lines 1262-1263); it is a daily act of receiving.

**Pray from the depths (ties to technique 8, Depth).** "From the depths I called You": one who prays should ask מעמקא דלבא, בגין דישתכח לביה שלים בקודשא בריך הוא, ויכוין לבא ורעותא, "from the depth of the heart, so that his heart be whole with the Holy One, and direct his heart and will" (line 1257), and aim it at "the depth of all, the depth of the well, from which the springs go out" (line 1258).

**Prayer from a narrow place.** A synagogue is needed, with windows, because prayer must go out straight toward Jerusalem "from within narrowness": אצטריך אתר דחיק בעאקו, לשדרא בגויה ההוא רוחא, דלא יסטי לימינא ולשמאלא, "a tight place is needed, to send the breath through it, so that it does not turn right or left" (line 1185), like the shofar's sound pushed through its narrow end. "From the straits I called Yah." **(ours)** The narrowness a person feels can itself be the shape that sends the prayer straight.

**Unify the Name with heart, awe and love.** One who prays and "knows how to unify the Holy Name" is "near to all who call Him in truth" (line 1124); a thread of grace is drawn over one who studied at midnight and prays at dawn (line 1122). But one who comes to unify ולא יתכוון ביה ברעותא דלבא, בדחילו ורחימו, "without directing it with the will of the heart, in awe and love", has his prayer thrown out (lines 1126, 1129).

**Midnight Torah.** Study "day and night" so as to be whole, כמה דלית יומם בלא ליליא, "as there is no day without night" (line 892); the praise that rises "from this world, which is far", is the most pleasing (line 898).

**Invite the Shabbat like a guest.** "Call the Shabbat a delight": invite it, "as one invites a guest, with a set table, a house in order" (line 919); speak no weekday talk, because ההוא מלה דנפיק מפומיה דבר נש, סלקא ואתער אתערותא לעילא, אי לטב, אי לביש, "the word that leaves a person's mouth rises and stirs something above, for good or for bad" (line 921). Set the table on Friday night, "for blessing does not rest on an empty table" (line 1263).

**Say the Song of the Sea each day, with intention** (line 1068).

**Do not leave the many.** In a time of judgment do not set yourself apart, because "at all times the mercy of the Holy One is on the people all together as one" (line 859).

**Be with them in their pain.** Moses sat on a stone during the battle, בגין דישראל שריין בצערא, ויהא עמהון בצעריהון, "because Israel were in distress, so that he would be with them in their distress" (line 1313). And his hands could not stay raised when Israel below stopped praying (line 1315).

**Give to the poor.** "Whoever makes the poor man whole" adds a letter and turns judgment (tzedek) into charity (tzedakah) (line 1227). The sick are fed "only by the Holy One Himself" (line 1228).

## 5. The big human questions

**Why does He seem far?** R. Chizkiyah: "Why, Lord, do You stand far off?" When sins cause it, "the Holy One rises up and up, and people cry and shed tears and there is no one who looks at them" (line 1156). The Zohar does not soften it. It also says Israel "does not seek the Holy One in a time of ease, only when it is pressed" (line 914), and then He is "filled with mercy".

**Love is the only service.** R. Yeisa: "how much a person must love the Holy One, for there is no service of Him but love" (line 1093); "whoever loves Him and serves in love, the Holy One calls him 'beloved'" (line 1093). And "do not let your foot be too often in your friend's house" means: guard your inclination from heating up against "your friend's house", which is "the holy soul that your Friend placed inside you" (line 1094). **(ours)** The soul is the house God lent us. Gentle and exact.

**The wars of Torah end in love.** "All the wars of the world are strife and ruin, and all the wars of Torah are peace and love" (line 1100).

**Drawn by love.** Before giving the Torah, God drew Israel "with many drawings of love, like a man who draws his son to school" (line 1210).

**The world stands on the ones who make themselves a remnant,** who keep nothing for themselves (line 1065).

**Weeping for the Temple.** Whoever weeps and raises his voice over the ruin of His house "will merit to see it settled in joy" (line 1097).

**His honor and ours.** God overlooks insults to Himself but not to the righteous: קודשא בריך הוא תבע עלבונא דצדיקייא יתיר על דיליה, "the Holy One demands the insult of the righteous more than His own" (line 1270).

**The sickness of holding.** The "sick evil" of Ecclesiastes is the stinginess that sits on a person "like a sick man who does not eat or drink" (line 1295): he cannot give, cannot even use his own wealth.

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **The daily asking (lines 1233-1238, 1247)** is a complete answer to worry about money, with a practice: ask each day, even when you have; then receive.
2. **The broken spirit healed by song (lines 873-874)** explains why relief often feels like collapse, and what helps.
3. **"Be still, you do not need to" (line 923)** balances the whole tradition's call to effort.
4. **The King's shining face with judgment hidden in it (lines 1017-1019)** is a mature picture of the unity of mercy and judgment.
5. **The soul is "the house your Friend placed in you" (line 1094)** is a sentence a person can carry.
