## The shape of the range

This range is almost all doctrine, and the text itself says it is about the unity. The introduction names the book's subject: the Alter Rebbe taught only to fix "the simple unity" in mind and heart, and this book is on the higher and lower unity of the Shema. Petach HaShaar then works through one verse, the first verse of the Shema, from every side. It covers the two directions of the unity (ascent and descent), the geometry of "place", the Ark in the Holy of Holies, darkness before light, the soul's two hearts, Shema against prayer, Baruch Shem, Torah as the vessel for the Essence, and knowledge against faith. That is why *stated* is so high (70 of 98). It is much higher than in the Mitteler Rebbe's Gate of Unity, where most lines are map.

The face profile is his, not the Tanya's. The Essence (F05) leads. It is followed by Torah and mitzvah (F10) and the two unities (F02), which is natural for a book on the Shema and prayer, and then by opposites joined (F09). "Thing and its opposite" is this range's most repeated phrase. The home below (F07) is much stronger here than in the Gate of Unity, because the range keeps arguing that the lowest and most bounded point is the highest.

## Introduction (1-8): why the book exists

The Alter Rebbe's whole teaching had one aim: > הכל היה לקבוע אחדו' ה' הפשוטה שהוא בחי' עצמות אא"ס ב"ה במוח ולב כל א' (line 3)

The unity is named as the Essence itself, and its content is the higher and lower unity: Havayah and Elokim, sovev and memale, the Shema and Baruch Shem. By the Zohar these are "the root of all mitzvot": > יסוד כל המצות שהן רק יחוד קוב"ה ושכינתיה (line 3)

Line 4 is the book's charter for F12. Knowing the Essence is now "faith above knowing". But the point of the Shema is that this faith come down into knowing: > שיבא כח האמונה זו שלמעלה מן הדעת לידי גלוי בדעת והבנה (line 4)

Moshe is the intermediary who carries the unity that is above knowing into Israel's knowing (line 4). The author warns in so many words: > ולא יטעו בו לומר שהאמונה בבחי' סובב למעלה מן הדעת לבד (line 4)

To make the Shema faith alone is "the complete opposite of the truth" (line 5). No one may excuse himself by humility. Even a simple reader must toil in the unity of sovev and memale, "and this is the whole man" (line 5). The same line says that not knowing is "the cause and root of all harsh judgments": the "I will hide My face" of exile, poverty and persecution. The hiding here is the punishing kind, not the purposeful tzimtzum of F03, and the entry says so. Line 6 turns on the kabbalists. All the kavanot rise to one place, and God wants one intention only: > כי לא חפץ ה' באלה כ"א בכונה אחת לבד לקשר נפשו אל אמתת עצמותו ית' דוקא (line 6)

The unity dwells only in bitul (F08): > ולא זו הדרך ישכון בה אחדות ה' שהוא דוקא בבחי' הבטול והעדר הרגשת עצמו מכל וכל (line 6)

Lines 1-2 and 7-10 are headings, the closing blessing and the signature.

## Petach HaShaar 1-2 (11-15): the two unities of "Echad"

Section 1 sets out the range's main theme. The kabbalists read the Shema as ascent: the ten sefirot included in their Emanator, with self-giving "to Him and not to His attributes". The Sages read it as descent: "make Him king in heaven and earth and the four directions". Each letter of Echad gets a place: chet for the heavens and earth, dalet for the four directions, and alef for Chochmah, the "master of the world" (line 11). The Mitteler Rebbe keeps both readings: > שני אופני היחודים הנ"ל ענין א' הוא והא בהא תליא (line 11)

"Is He not one now?" Now the alef is hidden in the six directions, and in the future it will be revealed (line 11, F03 with F07). Alef reversed is pele, "wonder": Chochmah still hidden in Keter, which "counts as the Essence" (line 12). How can alef come from its opposite? "He is the place of the world and the world is not His place" holds both at once: > א"כ הכל א' ממש והא בהא תליא דוקא (line 12)

Section 2 (lines 13-15) is structural. Bereishit is Chochmah and "also a saying" (line 13, F01). Light and darkness are chesed and tzimtzum, and they were "used in mixture" until Chochmah came out of hiding: > וב' אלה הפכיים שרשם תחלה בחכמ' (line 14)

## Sections 3-5 (16-23): place, and the lowest as the highest

Lines 16-21 build the geometry of "place": length, breadth and height, point, line and plane (yod, vav, dalet), and the teacher and the small student. This is structural map and carries no face. Line 19 is the range's one *reading* line entry. The influence comes down "as it is above". Reading that as the Essence below as above is ours; the text is speaking of a teacher. Line 22 turns the geometry into doctrine. The dalet of Echad is lengthened because all four directions gather at the lowest point, and that lowest point is the "above" that comes before everything: > והוא בחי' מטה ממש שנעוץ תחלתן בסופן והוא בחי' מעלה שקדם להכל (line 22)

"Make Him king" is a descent from the Essence, which is above being a source (line 22). Line 23 answers the pele-alef question and gives F01 as its proof: > וצריך בכל יום ויום להיות בחי' חידוש דמאמ' בראשית מאין הנעלם (line 23)

## Section 6 (24-26): the Ark that took no space

This is the range's central image for F09. The Ark stood in the measured Holy of Holies and took up no space. That is a greater wonder than divine light being above place. "He grasps all worlds and none grasps Him" is > זהו דבר והיפוכו בנושא א' (line 24)

The answer comes in two levels (line 25). In the first, pele becomes the master of the world, held yet not held: "the heavens are My throne". In the second, the Essence as it is comes below into bounds. "What house will you build Me" means Me literally: > לי ממש כענין נתאוה להיות לו ממש דירה בתחתונים (line 25)

There the paradox dissolves: > ששם אין זה ב' הפכים כלל וכלל (line 25)

The bound itself takes on the unbounded, which is why the Ark took no space. The same line puts the four cubits of halacha "at the place of the Ark and tablets". In the future Israel will be a boundless vessel (line 26, F07 with F06): > ולע"ל יבא בגלוי למטה בכנ"י בבחינות בלתי גבול כמו למעלה ממש (line 26)

## Sections 7-8 (27-30): the great dalet, the lower unity, and the Temple's west

The six words of the Shema's first verse are the higher unity, Baruch Shem is the lower unity, and the great dalet is Malchut of Atzilut (line 27). The middle line, "the line of measure", is itself not of measure: > והוא עצמו אינו מן המדה כי יש בו מעצמות אא"ס הבלתי גבול וכולל גם בחינת הגבול ב' הפכי' (line 27)

The lower unity is built exactly like the higher: "as they unite above, so she unites below" (line 28). In the future: > ולע"ל יהיה הויה אחד ושמו אחד יח"ע ויח"ת שוין ממש (line 28)

Lines 29-30 explain why the Shechinah was in the west of the Temple. A seal in wax reverses right and left: once the influence is received, the receiver is primary, "their beginning wedged in their end" (line 30). The same line covers the Oral Torah ("even if they tell you left is right") and the moon-based calendar, which makes it F10 as well as F02.

## Sections 9-11 (31-39): Yachid above Echad; darkness before light, and the darkness that will shine

Line 31 lowers everything said so far: it is "only the bitul of something to nothing". Then comes the range's sharpest support for the SPINE's precision on "yachid": > עד לפני הצמצום דקו"ח שנק' יחיד שלמעלה מאחד (line 32)

Why is a day "evening then morning"? Because creation began with judgment. A boundless chesed cannot make "something" without a tzimtzum first: > כ"א עפ"י צמצום האור העצמי תחל' ואח"כ יומשך אור חסדו לחדש הבריאה מאין האלקי (line 32)

So every creature joins two opposite middot (line 32, F09). Line 33 carries this into the service of the heart (F08, F11): > וזהו כמו שא"א לאור בלא חשך הקדום כך א"א לאהבה ושמחה בלא מרירות והכנעה קודם (line 33)

Lines 34-36 apply the rule to souls entering Gan Eden, to learning and livelihood, and to exile. The last exile is the darkest because the light coming after it is the greatest: > כך כל מה שיתחשך בהסת' גדול יותר זהו מפני שצריך לבא אור גדול ביותר (line 36)

This is F03 as the SPINE states it, hiding for the sake of revelation, and here the text says it outright. Then the future breaks the pattern (lines 37-39): > זה יהיה עיקר החידוש לע"ל מה שבחי' הצמצום והחשך העליון יאיר (line 38)

The reason is "the light of the Essence will be revealed below just as above" (line 38). The first will to create was His essential chesed. It was hidden in the Essence, light and darkness together, and it had to be hidden to become the line. Even time will change: > שהזמן יהי' נצחי בבחי' נצחיו' דאא"ס ממש (line 38)

## Sections 12-14 (40-47): "God of knowings", two hearts, R. Akiva

Influence comes two ways: from nothing to something, and from "the true something" of the Essence (line 40). In the future both will be equal, because the Essence is all-capable: > דבר והיפוכו סובל יחד שזהו בא מאמיתת עצמותו ממש שהוא כל יכול (line 40) > > וזהו כי ה' הוא האלקים כולא חד ממש (line 40)

The same line goes further. The hiding itself, when it shines, surpasses the light (F03, F11): > כשההעל' עצמו יאיר יהי' בו יתרון מעצם האור (line 40)

"Unite our hearts" joins the outer heart's bounded love, which comes from the intellect, to the inner heart's unbounded love from the soul's essence. That is "two opposites in one subject" (line 41). Light and darkness divide only from Chochmah downward (line 43). Lengthening "Echad" is the unity of the ten sefirot hidden in the Essence: > שיחוד זה הוא בע"ס שבעצמו' שלמעלה הרבה מיחוד שאא"ס מתייחד בע"ס דאצי' (line 43)

All the kabbalistic unions of the Shema belong after the tzimtzum: > ובכ"ז אין יוצאי' י"ח היחוד האמיתי שהוא במוה"ע ממש (line 43)

Yet every new blessing, even for livelihood, comes only from the essential middot (line 43, F10). The soul's root in the Essence (F06) is why any Jew can give his life: > ומזה הטעם יוכל להיות מס"נ על קדה"ש בכל פחו' שבישראל לפי שקדה"ש הוא למוה"ע דוק' (line 44)

R. Akiva lengthened "Echad" "until his soul left and was included in the simple One" (line 44). Adam's soul was put in a body "for the sake of ascent above its source" (line 46). The work of this world is to bring the hidden heart into the light: > אך עיקר העבודה בזה העולם אינו אלא להוציא בחי' תעלומות הלב לבא לידי גילוי אור דוקא (line 46)

Line 47 turns "long days" into two things. One is the daily drawing of light into all of life, "even his livelihood, so it rises to the One". The other is eternal life from the soul's root in the Essence.

## Section 15 (48-50): love, fear, and the two servants

The Shema is "the first mitzvah, the root of all mitzvot" (line 48). The servant of Elokim serves in fear and refines the 288 sparks (line 50, F11). The servant of Havayah does the mitzvot, "the very ways of Havayah". The line then says: > שאף על פי שהוא י"ת שלימותא דכולא מ"מ בחר בעמו ישראל שיהי' שלימותו ע"י תו"מ (line 50)

The text says "perfection", not "dwelling". Reading this line as F07 is marked as ours.

## Sections 16-19 (51-63): Shema and prayer, two opposites that are one

"Cleave to His attributes" and "to Him, not to His attributes" are both true, because cleaving goes two ways (line 52). Self-giving goes only to the Essence: > לפי שכל העלאה ובטול והתכללות אינו אלא למוה"ע ממש דוקא (line 52)

Walking in His ways is cleaving to the middot, and even there the aim is "Ein Sof Himself who is clothed in them" (line 52, F04). Shema and prayer are > אך באמת הק"ש והתפלה המה ב' הפכי' ממש ומ"מ הא בהא תלי' והי' לאחדי' ממש (line 53)

because > דכל העלאה גורם המשכה לפי ערך ההעלאה ממש (line 53)

Prayer's blessings each draw on a particular sefirah, marked by the vowel under the Name (lines 55-57). The point is never the sefirah itself: > ולא בספירה אור החכמה עצמה תליא מילתא אלא דוקא בבחי' אא"ס שמלובש במדה וספירה זו דחכמה (line 56)

"The Name" in the singular is His essential Havayah, the "Havayah Echad" of the Shema (line 59). So He > שומע תפלת כל פה אעפ"י שאינו יודע לכוין בשמות כלל (line 59)

The Essence is all-capable and can send chesed through gevurah (line 60). The inner bitul of the Shema is what gives the outer bitul of prayer its force (line 61). True self-giving reaches only the Essence, and the Torah never commands it, because it is the soul's nature (line 62). The constant unions above only renew the old measure. A truly new light comes through the Shema alone: > אבל כדי שיהי' אור חדש ממש נגלה ממוה"ע שלא לפי ערך המדה הראשונה (line 63) > > שמצד גילוי אור הנעלם ומרומם ביותר מכולם יבא האחדו' והשלום וחיבור גם בין ב' הפכי' (line 63)

## Sections 19-21 (64-73): a new east each day; the unbounded in number

The Sages spoke of physical directions on purpose. Space itself is renewed each day (F01): > וא"כ בכל יום מתחדש מזרח חדש שלא היה אתמול (line 64)

The ascent must start from the lowest: > ולפ"ז עיקר היחוד תלוי בו"ק הגשמיי' שכאשר התחתון עולה ונכלל בעליון ממילא יעלה הכל עד רום המעלות (line 64)

Kingship spreads further the more separate the people (line 65). Baruch Shem is where the Essence is present in Malchut hidden in the lower worlds (line 66): > לפי שעצמות אא"ס ממש שורה במדת מל' דאצי' שמסתתר בבי"ע (line 66)

"Lift your eyes and see who created these" (lines 66-73) is the book's F12 turned on the bounded world: "the action resembles the Actor" (line 66). The letters of the sayings are "the power of the Actor in the acted" (line 67). Number and bound come from the same infinite power: > מאותו הכח עצמו הבלתי בע"ג הוא כל יכול ית' להוציא במספר וגבול דוקא (line 70)

The daily renewal never stops, like a spring measured into vessels (line 68). From this the text draws its boldest claim: > ונמצא דיח"ת דאיהו וגרמוהי חד שבמל' שרשו יותר עליון מיח"ע (line 70) > > שהאותיות די' מאמרות שבאים בהגבלה ממש בתוך כל נברא ממש הוא יותר עליון בשרשו מעצמות אור הראשון שבחכמה (line 72)

The world to come is bodies refined to receive the Essence itself in matter (line 71). "See" is sight, above the "hearing" of the Shema (line 73).

## Sections 22-23 (74-82): sick with love; Torah as the vessel; the dwelling below

In exile Israel is "sick with love". The soul longs, but the vessel is too weak, because now there is only > שנק' השגת המציאות ולא השגת המהות (line 74)

We know Him only from His actions, the daily renewal (line 74). In the future His essence will be revealed down to this world, and bodies will live from it (line 75). For now the essence is reached through Torah and mitzvot. Torah's root never fell in the breaking, and > ואינה מסתרת כלל עצמות אורו אדרבה היא בחי' כלי להכיל עצמות אורו ממש (line 76)

Halacha refines the husk (line 76, F11). The Shema's "world is not His place" is set against Torah: > אבל ד"א של הלכה שנק' היכלא עלאה הוא מקומו והיכלו הפנימי העצמי (line 77)

Baruch Shem inwardly is Israel taking on the yoke of the mitzvot, and here F07 is stated in its own words: > וזהו עיקר מה שא' שנתאוה הקב"ה להיות לו דירה בתחתונים דוקא ולא בעליונים (line 78) > > כי לולי בחי' הכלי דמ"צ במצות בפ"מ לא היה גלוי אא"ס בע"ס דאצי' ובי"ע (line 79)

The love that comes first must itself be a love for the descent, for His light revealed below, since > שכל העלאת מ"נ צ"ל בדוגמא של המשכת מ"ד דוקא (line 79)

Torah is the intermediary in every world, and "the root of the life" of every creature it speaks of (line 80). It joins the Essence to the deed (line 81). Thought, speech and deed are > שהן ג' מיני כלים לאחדות הפשוטה (line 82)

and deed, the outermost of the three, rises above all (line 82).

## Sections 24-26 (83-90): knowledge and faith, memale and sovev

Faith does not mean believing that God enlivens the worlds. That is seen with the mind, as a person sees his own soul's life: "from my flesh I see God" (line 83). Memale is da'at, a felt knowing: > וא"כ כמו שירגיש בחיי נפשו הוא יכול להרגיש עד"ז בחיי החיים כולם (line 84)

Faith is for sovev, His essential kingship, which is not clothed in the worlds (line 84). It is unchanged: > כי אין חילוק כלל בין קודם שנבה"ע לאחר שנבה"ע (line 85)

"The earth will be full of knowledge": sovev, which now reaches the earth only as faith, will come into knowing (line 86). The Shema already begins it, since > כי עיקר ההמשכה דבחי' סובב צריך להיות בארץ דוקא (line 86)

Contemplating sovev makes love grow beyond the fixed hidden love, which never grows by itself (line 87). The vessel is raised by practical mitzvot (line 88). "Know today" means sovev itself coming into da'at through Torah and mitzvot (line 89). Teshuvah works only "today", in this world, because sovev rests here (line 90). The range ends with speech that never leaves the speaker: > וע"כ כמו בשמים ממעל אין עוד כך על הארץ מתחת אין עוד (line 90)

## Shaar Kriat Shema 1-2 (92-98): the heart and the firmament

The new gate opens with questions (line 93). It then builds a long analogy: the two hearts, the diaphragm, and the firmament as a mediator made of both extremes (lines 93-98). All of it is structural. The only faces are F09 (two opposite flows from one place, line 96) and, at line 98, F03: the firmament over the chayot coarsens the Shechinah's radiance > הגמשת אור זיו השכינה שלא יתבטלו במציאותם לגמרי (line 98)

## What this range adds that the Tanya does not say, or says differently

- **Yachid above Echad, stated.** Line 32 calls the level before the tzimtzum of the line "Yachid, which is above Echad". It then makes lengthening "Echad" a matter of reaching it: the ten sefirot hidden in the Essence, far above Ein Sof's union with Atzilut (line 43). The Tanya range indexed so far does not draw this line in these words.
- **The lower unity above the higher.** The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud treats the higher unity as the deeper view (the worlds nullified like sunlight in the sun). Here the lower unity, Baruch Shem, Malchut's letters and the mitzvot, is said to have "a higher root" (lines 70, 72, 78), on the rule "the more bounded, the higher its root" (line 70). It will be said aloud in the future (line 66). Both books keep both unities. The weighting is reversed.
- **The hiding will shine, and surpass the light.** The Tanya's F03 is hiding for the sake of revelation. Here the future novelty is that the tzimtzum itself becomes light (line 38), with an advantage over the light itself (line 40).
- **Kabbalistic kavanot demoted.** All the unions of the Shema according to the kabbalah belong after the tzimtzum and "do not fulfill the true unity" (line 43). God wants one intention, to bind the soul to the Essence (line 6). The Tanya does not argue this.
- **Shema and prayer as two opposites that are one** (lines 52-63). The Tanya does not treat the Shema-prayer relation this way. Every new blessing in prayer, even healing and livelihood, is a new light from the Essence that only the Shema can draw (lines 43, 63).
- **Space renewed daily.** "A new east that was not yesterday" (line 64) carries F01 from things to the directions of space.
- **The four cubits of halacha.** The Tanya (ch. 53) says that since the destruction He has only the four cubits of halacha. This range agrees and says more. The four cubits are "His place" at the site of the Ark and tablets (line 25) and "His inner, essential palace" (line 77), set against the Shema's unity, of which "the world is not His place".

## Where the tie is thin (kept thin)

- Seven lines are *none*: the headings (1, 2, 9, 10, 92), the signature (8) and the colophon (91). - Pure map with no face: lines 15, 16, 18 and 20 (the geometry of place), and 94-95 (the physiology of the heart). Lines 13, 17, 21 and 96-98 are structural, with a face only as scaffolding.
- *Reading* is used twice, both times at line 19 (the line entry and sub-entry 19.2). They read a teacher's influence arriving "as it is above" as the Essence below. - Two stated sub-entries carry F07 as a secondary face that is ours, and each "how" says so. At 50.2 the text says Israel completes His "perfection", and "dwelling" is our word. At 64.4 the text says the whole ascent hangs on the lowest, physical directions, and linking that to the home below is ours. - Where a line is stated but one of its faces is ours, the "how" says so. Examples are 5.3 (the punishing hiding, not the purposeful tzimtzum) and 50.2.

## Disagreements kept, not smoothed

- **Faith and knowledge of sovev, inside this book.** The introduction says it is an error to hold that faith in sovev is above knowing only, and "the complete opposite of the truth" to make the Shema faith alone (lines 4-5). Section 24 says that now sovev reaches souls "only as faith" and that knowing it belongs to the future (lines 84, 86). Section 26 then makes "know today" the bringing of sovev into da'at now, through Torah and mitzvot (line 89), and line 87 grants "a revealed glimmer of sovev" in the Shema even now. The book resolves this by stages: faith now, drawn toward knowing by contemplation and deed, and full knowing later. The two emphases are both on the page, and the index keeps both.
- **Against the Tanya's reserve.** The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud 7 says His knowledge being His Essence is "only to be believed, with a faith above intellect" (Tanya index, line 834). This range insists the Shema's whole purpose is to bring the faith above knowing into knowing (line 4). The two passages concern different objects, His knowledge in the Tanya and sovev here. The emphasis still pulls the other way, and it matches the Rayatz's side of the SPINE's F12 dispute.
- **The soul-body analogy.** The Tanya's Shaar HaYichud refuses the soul-in-body analogy at its root (Tanya index, line 821). This range uses it openly. The soul in the body is the model for memale, "from my flesh I see God" (lines 83-84). The soul's essence, which surrounds the body and is untouched by its events, is the model for sovev (lines 84-85). The Mitteler Rebbe marks the analogy's limit only by setting sovev above it. That is a use, not a refusal.
- **What Sinai gave.** Line 89 says "you were shown to know" at Sinai was memale only, and line 75 that at Sinai they "only heard the voice". This ranks Sinai below what practical mitzvot will achieve. The Tanya does not rank Sinai this way.
