## The shape of the range

1. **Cain, Abel and Seth** (1943-1948). Good and evil come from one root, and the Messiah comes when evil turns to good.
2. **What unity means** (1949-1953). The lower flame yearns for the upper one. The one who unites holds up the Presence in exile.
3. **Looking into the Chariot** (1954-1963). Turning the heart from the world to the Chariot counts as praying all day. The patriarchs were the Chariot.
4. **Ezekiel's vision, verse by verse** (1964-2023). Why the vision came in exile, the shells around the nut, the flash of the chashmal (a shining that comes and goes), and the garments that cover the Names.
5. **Prophecy** (2024-2088). The two inner conditions (wisdom and cleaving), the four levels (Moses, the prophets, the holy spirit, the heavenly echo), and why prophecy left with the Temple.

The center of the range, for the guide, is chapter 21 (lines 2033-2051). It says the whole purpose of creation once more, from the side of prophecy: a person was built in the image of the upper form so that he could unite the Name, and when he does, the Presence rests on him.

## The unity of G-d

**One root under the opposites.** Cain and Abel came from one womb:

> להעיד על כי מסבה אחת פשוטה יצאו ההפכים (Avodat HaKodesh 4:14:4; line 1946)
> To testify that from one simple cause came the opposites.

And the Messiah comes from Ruth, from a line that passed through Moab. Ibn Gabbai:

> וכשהרע נהפך לטוב אין שבח גדול מזה (Avodat HaKodesh 4:14:6; line 1948)
> And when the bad is turned to good, there is no praise greater than this.

*Ours.* This is the same as the Chabad teaching that turning darkness to light is the highest praise (Tanya ch. 27, the "is'hapcha" of the tzaddik). The guide can hold it plainly: the part of me that pulled away is not outside G-d's one root. When it turns, that is the greatest praise.

**The flame reaching up.** Unity is not an idea but a yearning. "The black of the lamp is always stirred and yearning for the upper white flame, to unite and cleave to it" (line 1950). This is the same image the Zohar uses and Chassidus uses for the soul as "the candle of G-d" (Proverbs 20:27; Tanya ch. 19). Ibn Gabbai says the full way of unity was lost, and what remains is this: to unite everything up to the Infinite "with intention of the heart and a mind clear of every stray thought" (line 1953). He adds that one must unite judgment and mercy, "to know that all is one" (line 1953). And:

> ומארי ייחודא רשימין במחשבתא סתימא (Avodat HaKodesh 4:15:5; line 1953)
> And the masters of unity are inscribed in the hidden Thought.

**The one who unites holds up the Presence.** On "sustain me with flagons" (Song 2:5): the one who knows how to unite the great Name "supports and holds up Knesset Yisrael (the Presence as the soul of Israel) in this exile" (line 1953). *Ours.* This is tzorech gavoah (the need on high) in its most human form. The Presence in exile leans on the one who unites.

**He never leaves Israel in exile.** Why did Ezekiel reveal the Chariot at all, when its secrets are hidden? Because the exile in Babylon was the hardest, and Israel thought they were forsaken. So G-d showed the prophet that His hosts had gone down to Babylon with them (line 1969). Israel heard, "and each one set his will in his heart, with great love, to give himself over for the holiness of his Master" (line 1969). And:

> ומעלמין לא שביק קב"ה לישראל בגלותא (Avodat HaKodesh 4:18:6; line 1969)
> And never does the Holy One leave Israel in exile.

*Ours.* The guide can use this as a first answer to the lonely question. The vision of the Chariot was given as comfort, to people in exile.

**The flash that comes and goes.** The chashmal of Ezekiel is read as an inner shining:

> לפי שעה מתנוצץ בלב וברגע מסתלק (Avodat HaKodesh 4:19:10; line 1980)
> For a moment it flashes in the heart, and in an instant it departs.

Ibn Gabbai reads this with the Talmud's "at times silent, at times speaking": there are times of divine will and times without it (line 1980). The upper creatures are "running and returning" like sunlight glinting on water in a bowl, which "does not rest in one place" (line 2001). *Ours.* This is exactly what Chassidus calls ratzo vashov (running and returning), and it gives the guide a way to speak to a person whose moment of closeness has faded. The flash is real, and its going is part of its nature, not a failure.

**Garments over the Names.** The Names cannot be approached bare:

> מפני כי אין לגשת אליהם בלי לבוש (Avodat HaKodesh 4:19:24; line 1994)
> For one cannot approach them without a garment.

The proof: we do not read the Name YHVH as written but through its garment, Adonai (line 1994). And the hands under the wings are windows where the prayer of those who return comes in, through the garments "merciful and gracious" (line 1991). *Ours.* The guide can say: the way in is always through a garment. A word, a mitzvah, a name of mercy. This agrees with the Chabad teaching that G-d's light is received through garments (Tanya ch. 4 and 5, the garments of thought, speech and action).

**Each goes only toward the face that shines on it.**

> כל אחד ואחד אינו מתנהג אלא על סבת הפנים המאירים אליו (Avodat HaKodesh 4:19:29; line 1999)
> Each one is led only by the face that shines toward it.

**Action comes only when they are held in each other.** "The wheel within the wheel": action happens only "when they are included in each other and cleave to each other", as Abraham was included in Isaac and Isaac in Abraham at the Binding (line 2005). *Ours.* Kindness and strength are not two powers. The deed is born where they hold each other.

## Tzorech gavoah: the purpose of creation, from the side of prophecy

Chapter 21 gives the second condition for prophecy: walking with G-d, service, cleaving, and "gazing at the hidden unity always, his thought bound there without a break" (line 2033). Then it gives the reason this works:

> והחפץ היה בבריאה שיהיה זה הכח ביד אדם פקידו לתקן הכבוד במעשיו המתוקנים (Avodat HaKodesh 4:21:1; line 2033)
> And the desire in creation was that this power be in the hand of man, His appointed one, to repair the Glory by his well-made deeds.

The limbs of a person match the limbs of the Chariot:

> כי בהיותו מקדש ומטהר איבריו למטה הנה התקון ההוא עולה גם למעלה (Avodat HaKodesh 4:21:3; line 2035)
> For when he sanctifies and purifies his limbs below, that repair rises above as well.

So the righteous are "called by His name" (Isaiah 43:7): the tzaddik below who builds the world is called "all", like the upper tzaddik (line 2034). And when a person has made himself in this likeness, the upper form finds its own kind in him:

> הנה הוא כסא כבוד ומרכבה אליהם ומעוררם לבא לשרות עליו ולנבאו כי מצא מין את מינו ונעור (Avodat HaKodesh 4:20:9; line 2032)
> He is a throne of Glory and a chariot for them, and he wakes them to come and rest on him and give him prophecy, for kind found kind and was stirred.

The real person is the inner form, not the body:

> כי הגוף הוא הלבוש והוא בשר אדם לא אדם (Avodat HaKodesh 4:21:12; line 2044)
> For the body is the garment; it is the flesh of a man, not the man.

And the mitzvot are engraved in the upper form, and the right intentions have their source there:

> כי הכל בא משם והכל שב לשם (Avodat HaKodesh 4:21:15; line 2047)
> For all comes from there and all returns there.

Solomon, seeing this, said "this too I saw as wisdom under the sun, and it was great to me" (Eccl. 9:13): that the upper world's repair was left in the hand of a creature under the sun (line 2037).

*Ours.* This is the same claim as Tanya ch. 23 and 34, that the limbs doing a mitzvah are a chariot, and that a person can make himself a dwelling for the Presence. The difference stays the same as in the rest of the book. Ibn Gabbai speaks of repairing the Kavod (the Glory, the lower attributes) and uniting it with the great Name. Chabad speaks of a desire in the Essence itself for a home below (Tanya ch. 36). Both agree that the person's deed is what G-d wanted when He made the world.

**Never mistake the servant for the Master.** A prophet sees the form "like a man" on the throne, but knows it is caused and that "the true authority is above it" (lines 2049-2051). The prophets walk in the King's palaces and know every servant there:

> ולא ימירו האדון בעבד חלילה (Avodat HaKodesh 4:21:19; line 2051)
> And they do not exchange the Master for the servant, G-d forbid.

*Ours.* A clear safety line for the guide. No image, feeling or light is itself G-d. A person who has a strong experience should honor it and keep going past it.

## Prophecy

> הנבואה אשר היא האושר וההצלחה האמיתיים (Avodat HaKodesh 4:20:1; line 2024)
> Prophecy, which is the true happiness and success.

**Wise, strong, rich, humble.** The Talmud says the Presence rests only on one who is wise, strong, rich and humble. Ibn Gabbai reads these as a match to the upper Chariot: wisdom and understanding, kindness and strength, beauty (line 2026). And:

> והענוה עיקר הכל (Avodat HaKodesh 4:20:3; line 2026)
> And humility is the root of all.

**The closed gate.** Moses was given forty-nine gates of understanding, but not the fiftieth, "the gate of silence, which is the gate of the essence" (line 2029). *Ours.* This matches the Chabad teaching that the Essence is beyond grasp even for Moses, and is reached by bittul (self-nullification), not by knowing.

**Close your eye and roll it.** From the Zohar: the hidden colors can be seen only with closed eyes:

> סתום עינך ואסחר גלגלך (Avodat HaKodesh 4:22:7; line 2058)
> Close your eye and turn your eyeball.

Ibn Gabbai uses the same picture at line 2001: the upper light is seen only "in the closing of the eye as it rolls". *Ours.* We do not give this to the guide as a technique. It is a picture of how the hidden is known: not by staring, but by closing the outer eye.

**Moses never needed music.** Moses was always full, "enough for himself and overflowing to others" (line 2072). Other prophets needed music to bring the spirit back, because it came and left (lines 2072-2073). David woke the upper world with song:

> כי כפי מה שאני מעורר למטה יתעורר למעלה (Avodat HaKodesh 4:24:2; line 2077)
> For as I arouse below, so it is aroused above.

David's harp played by itself at midnight. Its secret is the lamp: "the blue light in the lamp yearns and moves to join above with the white light" (line 2078). *Ours.* Music is a real door in this book. It "gladdens the upper heart" (line 2073). The guide can offer a niggun (wordless melody) before contemplation, as Chabad does.

**All Torah is one seal.** All of Torah, Prophets and Writings are held in the upper Thought and the hidden unity, "nothing goes outside, for all is within the seal of truth" (line 2084).

**The generation matters.** The sages heard a heavenly voice say that Hillel and Shmuel HaKatan were fit for the Presence, "but the generation was not fit" (line 2085). Ibn Gabbai explains:

> הדור גורם גדול להמנע השלימות מן הראויים אליו (Avodat HaKodesh 4:24:13; line 2088)
> The generation is a great cause in holding back completion from those fit for it.

*Ours.* Nobody grows alone. What a person reaches depends in part on the people around him. This supports the guide's call to find a community and a teacher.

## States of consciousness

- **The flash in the heart** (line 1980). A moment of closeness that comes and goes. Ties to ratzo vashov states in the index. Proposed below.
- **The yearning flame** (lines 1950, 2078). The lower light reaching for the upper. Ties to the Tanya ch. 19 "candle of G-d" state already in the index.
- **Holding up the Presence** (line 1953). Uniting as support for the Presence in exile. Proposed below.
- **Kind finds kind** (lines 2032, 2043, 2047). The resting of the Presence on one whose form matches the upper form. Ties to the chariot state (Tanya ch. 23) already in the index.
- **Not alone in exile** (line 1969). Proposed below.

## Meditation and practice

- **Turning the heart from the world to the Chariot** (line 1954): the Bahir says it counts as praying all day. The guide keeps this as a short turn of the mind in the middle of the day.
- **Uniting judgment and mercy** (line 1953): when something hard happens, hold that the hard and the kind are one. Proposed below.
- **Purifying the limbs** (line 2035): each limb kept holy is a repair above. Ties to the existing chariot technique.
- **A niggun before contemplation** (lines 2072-2078). Proposed below.

## The big human questions

**Am I alone when things are dark?** "Never does the Holy One leave Israel in exile" (line 1969).

**Why did my closeness fade?** Because a flash "for a moment shines in the heart and in an instant departs" (line 1980). Its going is part of how it comes.

**What is a human being for?** To be the one in whose hand the repair of the Glory was placed (line 2033).

**Who am I, really?** The inner form, not the body; "the flesh of a man, not the man" (line 2044).

**Can my bad be redeemed?** When the bad turns to good there is no greater praise (line 1948).
