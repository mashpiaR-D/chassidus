## 1. What the range is

* **Jethro (lines 1335-1395).** Why the Torah could be given only after Jethro, the chief priest of every idol, came and said "now I know that the Lord is greater than all gods": when the other side bows, "the Holy One rules alone above and below". Balaam and Job as Pharaoh's other two advisers. The mountain open to anyone who wants. * **A Tosefta on the hidden trace (lines 1368-1372).** * **The "Raza deRazin", the secret of secrets (lines 1396-1568).** A long, unique block reading "this is the book of the generations of Adam" and "you shall see (techezeh) from all the people" as a science of reading a person: hair, forehead, eyes, face, lips, ears, and the lines of the hand, each tied to a letter. * **Before Sinai (lines 1569-1629).** The month of Twins, Moses' ascent, the herbalist and his herb, the eagle's wings, the third day. * **"All the people saw the voices" (lines 1630-1718).** Sinai compared with Ezekiel; the tablets; the souls that flew out and the Torah that brought them back. * **The Ten Commandments (lines 1719-1909)**, with the unity of the Name, the poor man's prayer, Shabbat and its three meals, "He" and "You", honor of parents, and a Raya Mehemna on oaths, Shabbat and the intention of the heart.

## 2. The unity of G-d

**"All is the Holy One. All is He. All is one."** In the middle of the commentary on "I am the Lord your God", R. Yitzchak says it in three short sentences:

> כלא קודשא בריך הוא. כלא הוא. כלא חד (line 1731)

"All is the Holy One. All is He. All is one." Nothing in the range is plainer. The Zohar adds that "these words are revealed to the harvesters of the field", the companions who work the mystical field.

**He and His Name are one; the Torah is His Name.** "Other gods" excludes the Shekhinah; "before My face" excludes the King's faces, "in which the holy King is seen, and they are His Name, and He is they", הוא ושמיה חד הוא, "He and His Name are one" (line 1738). "The whole Torah is one Name, the holy Name of the Holy One itself" (line 1831), and so "one who merits the Torah merits the holy Name; R. Yosi said, he merits the Holy One Himself, for He and His Name are one" (line 1832). All the commandments are joined "in the body of the King" ולית מאן דנפיק מן גופא דמלכא לבר, "and none goes out of the King's body" (line 1726). F10 in the Zohar's own words.

**The hidden trace (Tosefta, lines 1368-1372).** "In the hiddenness of the hidden, a trace was traced that is not seen and not revealed", ההוא רשימו, רשים ולא רשים, "that trace, traced and not traced". The masters of understanding cannot grasp it. איהו קיומא דכלא, "it is the existence of all" (line 1368). It wanted to cover itself and made itself a palace, stretched it out, opened fifty gates, hid inside it, and the palace filled with light (lines 1369-1370). That palace is פקיחא דעיינין, דלא ניים, "the open eye that does not sleep" (line 1372), always watching to shine below. And the hidden will stays "hidden and concealed, not revealed", קיימא ולא קיימא, "existing and not existing" (line 1372). **(ours)** This short passage has the whole later doctrine in seed: a first "trace" (reshimu) that is and is not, which hides itself in order to shine, and a watching eye that never sleeps. Chassidus will build its account of tzimtzum and the hidden light on terms like these.

**"He" and "You".** Why do we speak to God as "You" and call Him "Father"? "The one who is hidden and has no beginning we call He (Hu); from the place where a beginning is found, we call You (Atah), and He is called Father. And all is one" (line 1822, verified in two pieces). **(ours)** This is a small key to prayer: the blessing formula moves from "You" to "He" (Barukh Atah... asher kideshanu) because it moves from the One who can be addressed to the One who cannot, and they are one.

**The one Voice.** At Sinai there was "a great voice that did not cease" (Deuteronomy 5:19): והאי קלא דכליל שאר קלין ביה, לא אתפסק לעלמין, "this voice, which holds all the other voices in it, never stops" (line 1625). A voice is fire, wind and water in one (line 1626).

**Pure from within impure (F11).** "All is joined each to each, pure and impure", לית טהור אלא מגו טמא, "there is no pure except from within the impure" (line 1386). Shell and kernel rise together until the dead rise: then "the shell will break, and the light will shine in the world without covering, from within the kernel" (line 1387). And even the other side has "a thin, small light from the side of holiness" in it, like one grain of wheat in a heap of straw (line 1384).

**Everything is true, and all in His hand.** "All that the Holy One made above and below is truth", ולית לך מלה בעלמא דבעי בר נש לדחיא ליה מניה, ולאנהגא ביה קלנא, "and there is nothing in the world that a person should push away or treat with contempt, for all are works of truth, and the world needs all of them" (line 1363). A snake bites only when it is whispered to from above; and sometimes by the same thing He saves people; וכלא בידא דקודשא בריך הוא תליא, וכלא איהו עובדי ידוי, "all hangs on the hand of the Holy One, and all is the work of His hands" (line 1365). "Very good" includes the angel of death, "and all is one secret" (line 1366).

**The other side's acknowledgment completes the unity.** When the "king" and "priest" of the other side bow, כדין קודשא בריך הוא שליט בלחודוי עילא ותתא, "then the Holy One rules alone above and below" (line 1346). So the Torah waited for Jethro. **(ours)** The unity is not complete while something holds out against it; it is complete when even the opponent says "now I know".

## 3. States of consciousness

**The heart that flies out, and is held (proposed new state ZS2-11; ties to S20 Unreal, spacey or frightened, and S14 Loss and grief).** Why did Moses first "speak to them" before God spoke? Because "when joy comes to a person, or pain, before he knows of it he cannot bear it, for the heart flies away for a moment; and when he knows of it, he stands firm and can bear it" (line 1710). Even so, at the first Word their souls flew out "up to His throne of glory, to cleave there" (line 1711). The Torah pleaded for them, and אורייתא אתקיפת, ואחידת בהו בנשמתייהו, "the Torah grew strong and took hold of their souls" and brought them back: "the Torah of the Lord is perfect, restoring the soul", restoring it literally (line 1712). Inside: shock, the self gone for a moment, then held and brought back. **(ours)** Two lessons for the guide: prepare a person before hard or great news; and the soul that flies out in great closeness needs something to hold it, here the Torah.

**The poor man's prayer, and the King who lives in broken vessels (ties to S59 True lowliness, S55 Own words to Him).** "All the prayer of Israel is prayer, וצלותא דעני עלאה מכלהו, and the prayer of the poor man is higher than all" (line 1748), because it rises to the throne. God hears others sometimes, but the poor always, משום דדיוריה דמלכא בהני מאני תבירי, "because the King's dwelling is in these broken vessels" (line 1750); and "one who rebukes a poor man rebukes the Shekhinah" (line 1751). **(ours)** "The King lives in broken vessels" is one of the most usable sentences in the range for a person who feels broken.

**Coming near is met (ties to C4 From self-enclosed to held).** "One who comes to purify himself is helped": Moses went up, and then "the Lord called to him", דמאן דבעי לאתקרבא, מקרבין ליה, "one who wants to come near, they bring him near" (line 1592). And "all the idols of the world go down; one who cleaves to the Holy One goes up" (line 1391).

**The joy that is not only for mercy (proposed new state ZS2-12; ties to S50 Joy and bittul together, S49 Weeping and joy in one heart).** "I will greatly rejoice in the Lord, my soul shall exult in my God": אי ברחמי אתי עלנא, שוש אשיש ביי'. אי בדינא, תגל נפשי באלהי, "if He comes to us in mercy, I rejoice in YHVH; if in judgment, my soul exults in Elokim" (line 1835). R. Yehudah: בכלא חדוה על חדוה, "in everything, joy upon joy" (line 1837). Inside: a gladness that does not wait for things to go well, because both names are His. It is close to S50, but its cause is different: not self-nullification but knowing that the hard name is also "my God".

**Shabbat rest (ties to S76 Shabbos: the soul returned).** On Shabbat a person is given "another soul, a higher soul, a soul in which all wholeness is, like the world to come" (line 1794). The souls of the righteous delight, and a spirit spreads through the worlds, ונייחין בנייחא שלים, מתנשי מכלהו, כל רוגזין, כל דינין, וכל פולחנין קשין, "and they rest in complete rest, and forgotten from them are all angers, all judgments, and all hard labors" (line 1802). At Shabbat afternoon, when on weekdays judgment rules, "the will of wills is found, and the Holy Ancient One reveals His will, and all judgments are subdued" (line 1797). Moses died at that hour, into that will (line 1798).

**The springs of the heart open.** Near R. Shimon, "the springs of the heart are open on every side and all is revealed; when we leave him, we know nothing and all the springs are shut" (line 1746). The Zohar knows that a teacher's presence opens what solitude cannot.

**Everyone sees according to himself.** At Sinai כל חד וחד חמא, כדקא חזי ליה, "each one saw as was fitting for him" (lines 1649, 1680), standing in rows by rank. No one saw the same thing, and all received.

## 4. Meditation and practice

**Do the deed even when you cannot bring the heart (ties to technique 21, The act that closes; proposed technique ZT2-05).** The Raya Mehemna is honest about intention. God wants "the heart and the will" (line 1897). One who does a mitzvah without intention has still done his Master's command, though not like one who does it "in the will of gazing at his Master's glory" (line 1896). And for those who cannot: David prayed "establish the work of our hands", אף על גב דלית אנן ידעי לשואה רעותא, אלא עובדא בלחודוי, "even though we do not know how to set our will, only the deed alone", establish it above (line 1898). How: do the act, and pray that it be completed where your heart could not reach. **(ours)** This is a gift for the guide: permission to act without feeling, with a prayer instead of a demand.

**Awe with a broken heart.** "A person cannot draw the spirit from above... except in awe", ויכוין לביה ורעותיה בדחילו ותבירו דלבא, "and let him direct his heart and will in awe and brokenness of heart, and then he draws down the spirit from above" (line 1378). And true awe is found only where there is wholeness: לית דחילו אלא באתר דהוי שלימותא שכיח, "there is no awe except in a place where wholeness is found" (line 1582).

**Silence.** The "still small voice" (kol demamah dakah): "demamah" means "one must be silent from it and close his mouth"; דממה איהי שתוקא דלא אשתמע לבר, "demamah is a silence that is not heard outside" (line 1640). **(ours)** An inner silence that is not the absence of voice but a voice kept in.

**Shabbat: three meals as three joys.** Set the table on Friday night; eat three meals with joy, each for one face of the divine: the first "of the Field of holy apples", the second "of the Holy Ancient One", the third "of Ze'ir Anpin" (lines 1785-1791). R. Shimon at each meal said "prepare the meal of the upper faith, prepare the meal of the King, and sat and rejoiced" (line 1788). On festivals, "if he rejoices alone and does not give to the poor, his punishment is great" (line 1793). Guard **(ours)**: the guide can take the joy and the giving; the detailed sefirotic map of the meals is the Zohar's, not ours to impose.

**Honor parents with melody.** "Honor the Lord with your substance (mehonekha)": read "from your throat (mechonekha)", בחדוה דנגונא, למחדי לבא, "with the joy of melody, to gladden the heart, for this is the joy of the heart, like the melody of the whole world" (line 1889). A son's good deeds gladden his parents' hearts.

**Speak to each person in his way.** The herbalist: "a person must speak with another according to his ways" (line 1603).

**Do not invent Torah.** "Whoever says in words of Torah what he does not know and did not receive from his teacher" makes a graven image (line 1762).

**Do not trust wisdom over Him.** The herbalist's teaching, confirmed by R. Shimon: God hid the powers of plants "so that they would not turn from His ways and trust in that wisdom and forget Him" (line 1611). A guard against making any technique the object of trust.

## 5. The big human questions

**Who is the person?** "Skin and flesh You clothed me": these are only garments, "vessels of the person, and they are not the person"; דהא ודאי האדם לאו איהו אלא נשמתא, "for surely the person is nothing but the soul" (line 1460). When the person departs, he takes off those garments.

**Reading faces, and why the guide does not (ours).** The long "secret of secrets" (lines 1396-1568) teaches the masters of wisdom to read hair, forehead, eyes, face, lips and hands. Its deepest claims are about the soul, not the body: "a spirit is in a person, with the secrets of the letters engraved in it" (line 1428), and the face shows it; one who walks in truth has a face such that "the eyes of the heart love him" (line 1432). The lines change with a person's deeds: דהא כפום מנהגא דבר נש, הכי מתחלפי שרטוטין, מזמן לזמן, "according to a person's conduct the lines change, from time to time", and אסוותא להאי תיובתא, "the cure for this is return" (line 1479). The purpose it states is care: the wise are "to warn people, and to give healing to people, and to heal their souls" (line 1509). The guide takes only these two things, that the inner life shows, and that it can change; it does not read or judge anyone's face or hands.

**Why were we created, and what is this world?** "This world, next to the world to come, is only like a corridor before the hall" (line 1667). The delight of the righteous is "to gaze at that radiance from which all radiance comes" (line 1668).

**Is anything outside Him?** No: "nothing should be pushed away or scorned" (line 1363); "all is one" (line 1731).

**Is the mountain open to all? (D28).** Abraham's "mountain of the Lord": "as a mountain is ownerless to anyone in the world who wants, so this holy place is open to receive anyone in the world who wants" (line 1392). The peoples will come "to walk until their feet ache, to enter under the wings of the Shekhinah" (line 1391). The range also holds many lines that set Israel apart from the nations (for example lines 1739-1741); we keep them as the Zohar's and do not carry them into the guide.

**Exile.** Ezekiel's vision was given "so that they would know that the Holy One does not leave them, and wherever Israel are scattered in exile, He dwells with them" (line 1648).

## 6. For the guide (ours)

1. **"All is the Holy One. All is He. All is one" (line 1731)** can stand at the head of the Zohar's unity.
2. **The King dwells in broken vessels (line 1750)** for a person who feels broken.
3. **The heart that flies out and the Torah that holds the soul (lines 1710-1712)** for shock, grief, and overwhelming closeness alike.
4. **"Even though we only know the deed" (line 1898)** as permission to act without feeling.
5. **Joy in YHVH for mercy and in Elokim for judgment (lines 1835-1837)** as a joy that does not depend on events.

Proposed for the map (details in `ZOHAR-PART-2.md`): ZS2-11 *The heart that flies out and is held*, ZS2-12 *Joy upon joy, in both names*; technique ZT2-05 *The deed with a prayer for the heart*.
