# Overview

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is early work.** It is about one month into the research, and there is a lot more to come. Studies here will be corrected, finished and added to, and some findings will change as the reading goes deeper. Read it as a first look at work in progress, not a finished book.

This overview describes the <!-- n:families -->47<!-- /n --> results in the collection, area by area. Each description says what the result shows, in plain words, and points to the manuscript to start with. The [manuscript map](CONTENTS.md) lists every manuscript with its abstract.

**The one claim.** Chassidus says of itself that it is one point: the Essence of G-d, drawn down and present in everything. Put as unity, it reads "there is nothing besides Him", true even in the lowest place, and that is His dwelling below. The research takes that claim seriously and tests it. The Unity Index (area I) files the whole library under it. The line-by-line readings (areas II and III) check it against single books, every line. The contemplation studies (area IV) follow it from knowing to living, because in Chassidus to know a truth is to hold it until it is real. Areas V and VI ask what it does for a person, and how to say it to anyone.

**How to read the marks.** *Stated*: the text says it. *Ours* or *reading*: the tie is the research's own. *Thin*: the tie is weak and is not forced. *OCR*: quoted from a scanned book exactly as the scan reads.

**The six areas.**

1. **The Unity Index.** The thesis, its 12 faces and 14 moves, 197 subjects, 220 works, and the meditation behind each.
2. **The Chabad works, line by line.** The Tanya, the Mitteler Rebbe, the Rebbe Rashab's series and tracts, R. Aharon of Strashelye, and the life the teaching asks for.
3. **The sources Chassidus stands on.** The Rambam, the Kuzari, the Zohar, ibn Gabbai, the Ramak, the Maharal, the Shelah, and the Piaseczner Rebbe beside them.
4. **Contemplation.** The works read as meditation, a map of inner states, a manual, the full depth, and how a maamar reasons.
5. **The person.** The animal soul, the feeling of holding everything up, a clinic, everyday problems, and how change happens.
6. **Saying it plainly.** The mashal, the teacher, a translation for anyone, and a ladder for someone starting from zero.
