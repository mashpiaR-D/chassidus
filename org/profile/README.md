## Only One research

We study Chassidus with AI, and publish what we find so anyone can read it and check it.

This is early: about a month of work so far, with a lot more to come.

The question at the center: is all of Chassidus really one point, the Essence of G-d, present in everything, so that "there is nothing besides Him" holds even in the lowest place? We read the Chabad library and the books it stands on, line by line, and ask of every line how it carries that one idea, and what it does to a person who sits with it.

- **[chassidus](https://github.com/mashpiaR-D/chassidus)**: the research collection. 459 manuscripts in 47 results, from the Unity Index to line-by-line readings of the Tanya, Samach Vav and Ayin Beis, with every Hebrew quote checked against its source.

Every quote is checked. Every claim says whose it is: the text's (*stated*) or ours.
